### STORY 2: Sinner May Skip Asian Swing

**Long-Form Post [12:30 PM ET]**

There's been a significant new development in the Jannik Sinner injury story — and it's not the update many fans were hoping for.

Former Italian ATP professional Stefano Pescosolido told Tuttosport this week that the world No. 1 could bypass the entire Asian swing, including the China Open in Beijing where he is the defending champion, and instead make his comeback at the Vienna Open in late October.

"I think he's a little more careful from a physical standpoint. He wants to avoid relapses," Pescosolido said.

Sinner has been sidelined since winning Wimbledon in July with a right-knee tendon injury. He is still on the China Open entry list and has not officially withdrawn. But with his ATP Finals spot already secured as No. 2 in the Race to Turin, the calculus appears to be shifting toward protecting long-term health.

The smart move? Probably. The disappointing one for fans? Absolutely.

What do you think — should Sinner skip Asia and come back healthy in Vienna, or try to defend his title at the China Open?
