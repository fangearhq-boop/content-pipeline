Andreeva Cruises Into Singapore Open Quarterfinals With 6-0 Second-Set Demolition

By Ryan Calloway | September 23, 2026

WTA | Singapore Open | Match Report

Mirra Andreeva is building momentum at exactly the right time. The top seed and Roland Garros champion swept past Aliaksandra Sasnovich 7-5, 6-0 on Wednesday at the WTA Singapore Open, advancing to the quarterfinals with a performance that underlined her status as the tournament favourite.

The second set was especially commanding. Andreeva broke serve in the opening game and never looked back, winning six straight games to close out the match. Sasnovich, a former top-30 player and never an easy draw, had no answer for the Russian's movement and ball-striking from the back of the court.

Form and Context

Andreeva, ranked No. 5 in the world, enters Singapore as the clear favourite after a breakthrough 2026 that included her first Grand Slam title at Roland Garros in May. She was elevated to the No. 1 seed here after Jessica Pegula withdrew from the draw before the start of the tournament.

The Singapore Open was upgraded to WTA 500 status this year — a significant boost in prestige and prize money — and the field reflects it. Mertens, Krejčíková, and Alexandra Eala are among the quarterfinal contenders still in the draw, though Andreeva's straight-set run through the rounds so far suggests she is operating at a level above the rest of the field.

The Wider Tournament Picture

Elise Mertens had a scheduled second-round match against Barbora Krejčíková on Wednesday — a match between two former Grand Slam champions that adds intrigue to the lower half of the draw. Oleksandra Oliynykova also advanced with a dominant 6-0, 6-1 win over Maja Chwalińska.

Alexandra Eala, the Filipino No. 3 seed, has been the tournament's crowd favourite in the region and will be among the players to watch as the quarterfinals take shape Thursday and Friday. The draw is on indoor hard courts at the OCBC Arena — a surface that tends to favour aggressive baseliners, which suits Andreeva's game.

The Korea Open Runs Alongside

The WTA 250 Korea Open in Seoul is also underway this week, running September 21-27. Ostapenko is the top seed there, and the parallel event gives the WTA's mid-tier players additional ranking opportunities before the October 1000s begin in Beijing and Wuhan.

What's Next

Andreeva's quarterfinal opponent at the Singapore Open will be determined by the remaining second-round results. The quarterfinals are expected Thursday. If she continues this form, Andreeva could enter the WTA fall swing's two biggest events — the China Open and the Wuhan Open — with a title and genuine confidence heading into WTA 1000 territory.
