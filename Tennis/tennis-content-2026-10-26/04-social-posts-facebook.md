### STORY 1: Medvedev Wins Almaty

**Long-Form Post (8:00 AM ET)**
Daniil Medvedev finally did what three previous opportunities couldn't produce — he defended a title.

### STORY 3: Wawrinka Farewell at Basel

**Long-Form Post (12:30 PM ET)**
Tonight at the Swiss Indoors in Basel, Stan Wawrinka takes the court one last time.

It is fitting that the final chapter is in Switzerland. Wawrinka has won this event three times — 2006, 2015, and 2016 — and Basel has been home to some of his greatest indoor performances. At 41, the three-time Grand Slam champion faces Jiří Lehečká in the first round.

Career highlights: three Slam titles (2014 Australian Open, 2015 Roland Garros, 2016 US Open), an Olympic doubles gold in 2008 alongside Roger Federer, and a career-high ranking of No. 3.

He was never the favorite. That was always the charm. He made a career out of rising to moments most people expected others to own. The last of Federer's generation is taking his bow tonight.

### STORY 5: WTA Finals Preview

**Long-Form Post (10:30 AM ET)**
The 2026 WTA Finals have an unusual backdrop — and an elite field.

For the first time since the event's move to Saudi Arabia, the WTA Finals return to a familiar location: the Indian Wells Tennis Garden. The Saudi contract ended early amid safety concerns linked to the ongoing regional conflict, and Indian Wells stepped in to host November 8–15.

The eight players who earned their spots: Elena Rybakina (No. 1, defending 2025 champion), Aryna Sabalenka, Mirra Andreeva, Jessica Pegula, Coco Gauff, Elina Svitolina, Linda Noskova, and Karolína Muchová.

Rybakina arrives as the top seed and defending champion, fresh off her Ningbo Open title this week. The draw is expected around November 5.

This is the most compelling WTA Finals field in years. Who makes the final?
