Medvedev Rises to No. 5 in ATP Race; Vienna and Basel Could Decide Everything

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | October 26, 2026

Three players have already clinched their spots at the ATP Finals in Turin. Five spots remain. And after Daniil Medvedev's Almaty title on Sunday, the standings entering this week's Vienna and Basel events are tighter than ever at the bubble.

The Confirmed Three and the Open Five

Alexander Zverev, Carlos Alcaraz, and Ben Shelton have all secured their places in Turin. Zverev leads the Race standings comfortably. Alcaraz and Shelton follow. From No. 4 onward, nothing is settled.

Medvedev's Almaty win moves him to No. 5 in the Race with 3,660 points — a position that technically puts him inside the qualification line. But Flavio Cobolli sits at No. 6 with 3,640 points, just 20 points behind. The margin between an automatic qualifier and a player watching from home is, at this moment, roughly the equivalent of a second-round win.

Arthur Fils, named the ATP Mover of the Week for October 26, holds the No. 7 spot with 3,425 points. Frances Tiafoe is at No. 8 with 3,380. Beyond them, Alejandro Tabilo Jódar, Alex de Minaur, and Novak Djokovic are all mathematically in play. Seven players. Five spots. Three events left.

What This Week Offers

Vienna and Basel each award 500 Race points to the champion — 250 to the finalist, 180 to each semifinalist, and so on down the bracket. Running simultaneously this week, they together offer 1,000 points to champions and distribute meaningful points at every stage.

For Medvedev, who is already in the Vienna draw as the No. 3 seed, a deep run could put enough distance between himself and the rest of the field to enter Paris with security. For Cobolli, Fils, and Tiafoe — all competing in one or both events — the same logic applies in reverse. A quarterfinal run while others reach semis or finals can shift the standings by 100 or more points in a single week.

Djokovic is in the Basel draw on a wildcard and needs to compete aggressively across both remaining 500s and Paris to secure his position. He is currently in the bubble but not comfortably inside it.

Paris Masters: The Final Decider

Vienna and Basel wrap up November 1. Paris Masters begins October 31 and concludes November 8 — a 1,000-point event that offers the most significant final redistribution of the season. The ATP Finals in Turin start November 15, meaning the Race is effectively settled by Paris Sunday.

Players who make deep runs in Vienna or Basel and then perform in Paris could leapfrog multiple positions. Players who exit early in both 500s are likely dependent on Paris alone to save their Turin hopes. The arithmetic varies by player, but the general shape of the race is clear: everything from No. 4 downward is still undecided.

What's Next

Vienna and Basel run through November 1. Watch for midweek results in both draws to begin reshaping the standings in real time. The ATP Race table will look different by Friday. After that, Paris Masters from October 31 to November 8 closes the season's final major scoring window. The ATP Finals in Turin begin November 15 — and the final five spots will be claimed somewhere between now and Paris Sunday.
