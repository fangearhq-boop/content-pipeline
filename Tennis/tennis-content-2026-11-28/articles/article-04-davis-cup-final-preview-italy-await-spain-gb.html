Davis Cup Final Sunday: Italy Await Spain or Great Britain in Bologna

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | November 28, 2026

Italy are where they almost always seem to be these days: in a Davis Cup final. Their 2-1 win over Czech Republic on Friday put them into Sunday's decider for the fourth consecutive year, and they will find out their opponent on Saturday when Spain face Great Britain in the second semifinal in Bologna. Whatever the result, the final shapes up as a compelling conclusion to one of the best Finals weeks in recent memory.

The question on everyone's mind when Italy qualified for the final was who they would rather face. It is an interesting one. Spain beat Italy 0-2 in last year's final, with Italy winning comfortably in straight sets across both singles rubbers. Italy and Spain have history in this format, and Spain's depth — especially if Carlos Alcaraz is fit to play singles — makes them arguably the sterner test.

Italy vs Spain: A Familiar Final?

The 2025 Davis Cup final in Bologna ended with Italy winning 2-0 against Spain. It was not a dramatic final by the numbers, but it was a defining moment in Italy's dynasty: they beat Spain, the most successful Davis Cup nation in modern times, without needing the doubles. If Saturday goes Spain's way and they face Italy again, the storylines write themselves — rematch, Alcaraz seeking revenge, Italy defending their dominance over their biggest rival.

Spain have additional motivation if they reach Sunday. They last won the Davis Cup in 2019. In the years since, they have watched Italy build a dynasty in a format that Spain once dominated. Alcaraz specifically has yet to lift the Davis Cup for Spain, and at 23 years old, winning the title this week — if fit — would be a significant moment in his career alongside his Grand Slam victories.

Italy vs Great Britain: History in the Making?

If Great Britain beat Spain on Saturday, Sunday's final takes on a different kind of significance. Great Britain have not been in a Davis Cup final since 1937. For a nation with a complicated relationship with its own tennis history — shaped so much by Tim Henman, Andy Murray, and years of near-misses — reaching a Davis Cup final in 2026 would be an extraordinary achievement.

Arthur Fery's win over Alexander Zverev in the quarterfinal has already given Great Britain one result that will be talked about for years. But the ultimate prize of a final is something else entirely. Captain Leon Smith's team would need to beat Spain first, which is not a small ask, particularly if Alcaraz returns to full fitness.

Italy's Position

Regardless of the opponent, Italy enter Sunday's final as favorites. Three-time defending champions, playing in front of a home crowd in Bologna, with Lorenzo Musetti coming off one of the most significant individual performances in Davis Cup this year. Filippo Volandri's squad has found solutions at every stage of this tournament, even without Sinner, and there is nothing in front of them that suggests they cannot do it again.

A fourth consecutive Davis Cup title would put Italy in rarefied company. The United States and Australia have had long periods of dominance in the Davis Cup's history, but four in a row in the modern Finals era — with its eight-team knockout format — would represent something genuinely historic.

What's Next

Spain vs Great Britain gets underway on Saturday in Bologna. The winner meets Italy in Sunday's Davis Cup final, November 29, 2026. Full match times and streaming details will be posted on daviscup.com. For Italy, the wait begins. They have done their part.
