Hangzhou Open: Medvedev's R16 Opponent Set as Royer Beats Walton

By Ryan Calloway | September 24, 2026

ATP | Hangzhou Open

Daniil Medvedev's first opponent at the ATP 250 Hangzhou Open has been confirmed. Valentin Royer of France defeated Adam Walton of Australia 6-3, 6-4 in the Round of 32 on Wednesday, setting up a Round of 16 meeting with the tournament's No. 1 seed. That match is scheduled for Friday, September 25.

Medvedev received a first-round bye and has yet to hit a competitive ball at the $1,039,090 Hangzhou hard-court event. Friday's match against Royer will be his tournament debut.

The French Presence

Royer's run represents the broader French influence on this week's draw in Hangzhou. Five players from France feature in the tournament — an unusually strong national contingent for an ATP 250. The Hangzhou Open has historically been a tournament where lower-ranked French players have had success, and the absence of the top 10 (mostly in London for Laver Cup) makes the 2026 edition even more navigable for players who have historically needed that kind of opportunity.

Royer beating the No. 1 seed would be a significant result. Medvedev, however, is a different challenge than Walton. The Russian is No. 1 seed for a reason, and his clay and hard-court game — while not at the very peak of recent seasons — remains well above the Hangzhou field's average level.

Open Draw, Wide Opportunity

Beyond the Medvedev matchup, the Hangzhou draw is notable for its openness. Andrey Rublev is the No. 2 seed, Tomás Martín Etcheverry the No. 3, and Quentin Halys the No. 4. With virtually all top-10 ATP players at Laver Cup in London this week, an ATP 250 in China is the opportunity of the season for ranked players outside the elite.

If Medvedev reaches the final and wins, it would be a routine result for a player of his standing. If someone unseeded or lightly seeded makes a deep run, Hangzhou 2026 becomes one of those results people remember.

What's Next

Round of 16 play in Hangzhou begins Thursday and continues Friday. Medvedev vs. Royer is one of the featured R16 matches on Friday. The Hangzhou Open runs through Sunday, September 29. Follow atptour.com for draw updates and live scores throughout the week.
