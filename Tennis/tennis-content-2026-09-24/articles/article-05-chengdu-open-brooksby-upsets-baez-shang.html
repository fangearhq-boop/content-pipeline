Chengdu Open: Brooksby Upsets No. 8 Seed Báez as Home Favourite Shang Awaits Court

By Elena Voss | September 24, 2026

ATP | Chengdu Open

The ATP 250 Chengdu Open has produced its first upset. American Jenson Brooksby defeated No. 8 seed Sebastián Báez of Argentina 7-5, 7-5 on Day 1, eliminating one of the top-eight players in the draw on opening day and sending a signal about how competitive this week's field intends to be.

Brooksby won in straight sets without ever being broken — a composed performance that underlines his value as a consistent hard-court player when healthy and in form. For Báez, whose clay credentials have carried him into the top 15, hard courts in China remain a difficult proposition.

Vacherot and Tabilo Still to Come

The two highest seeds in the Chengdu draw have not yet played. Top seed Valentin Vacherot and No. 3 seed Alejandro Tabilo — the defending champion — both received byes into the Round of 16. Their tournament debuts come later in the week. Tabilo won the 2025 Chengdu Open title and arrives as one of the players best suited to defend on this surface, having built his reputation as a hard-court specialist with serious depth and heavy groundstrokes.

The Brooksby result means Tabilo's section of the draw has one fewer dangerous floater. But the larger point is that upsets in the early rounds are exactly what makes an open ATP 250 draw compelling — and Chengdu has shown it will deliver them.

The Shang Factor

The most anticipated storyline of the early rounds involves Shang Juncheng, the 21-year-old Chinese star who won the 2024 Chengdu Open title. Shang received a wildcard into this year's event and will face 38-year-old Adrian Mannarino in his opening match. The crowd context for that match will be loud and heavily one-sided — Chinese fans have embraced Shang as a standard-bearer for the next generation of Chinese tennis, and playing at home in Chengdu carries its own kind of pressure and energy.

Other notable names in the draw include Denis Shapovalov, a former top-10 player working his way back through the rankings, and Alejandro Davidovich Fokina, who adds an entertaining Spanish presence to the field. Seventeen-year-old American Ulrich Kouame, who received a Next Gen Accelerator wild card, is one of the youngest players competing this week.

What's Next

The Chengdu Open continues Thursday and Friday with more Round of 32 matches. Vacherot and Tabilo both enter in Round of 16 play later in the week. The tournament runs through Sunday, September 29 at the Sichuan International Tennis Centre. Follow atptour.com for scores and draw updates.
