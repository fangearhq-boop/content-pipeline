Paris Masters Semifinal Day: Shelton vs. Van Assche, Alcaraz vs. Ruud

By Elena Voss | November 7, 2026 | Tennis Fanrecap

The Rolex Paris Masters arrives at its penultimate day with two semifinals that could not have been scripted better. Ben Shelton meets local wildcard Luca Van Assche in the first match of the afternoon, followed by Carlos Alcaraz against Casper Ruud. Both matches are at Accor Arena in Nanterre, with the first not before 2:30 PM ET and the nightcap not before 5:00 PM ET.

The prize: a Paris Masters final on Sunday, November 8, and 600 ranking points for each finalist — with 1,000 points and the title on the line for the winner.

Shelton vs. Van Assche: Power Meets Magic

Ben Shelton, the third-seeded American, reached the last four by defeating Tommy Paul 6-4, 4-6, 6-3 in the quarterfinals, hitting 19 aces and converting two of four break points. This is the 24-year-old's first Paris Masters semifinal, and he arrives with momentum and a serve that few players in the world can handle.

Standing across the net will be Luca Van Assche — 21 years old, Parisian, and playing on a wildcard. Van Assche's run to the last four has been the defining story of the week. He opened against Mariano Navone, winning 6-3, 7-5 without dropping serve. He then eliminated 15th seed Learner Tien 7-6(4), 6-4, saving nine of the ten break points he faced. In the round of 16, he beat Lorenzo Musetti — the same Musetti who had just stunned world No. 1 Alexander Zverev in straight sets. And in the quarterfinals, Van Assche edged Rinky Hijikata 6-4, 6-7(4), 7-5 to reach uncharted territory.

The crowd at Accor Arena will be an overwhelming factor. Van Assche has described Paris as "the Masters 1000 with the best atmosphere," and playing at home in the last four will only amplify that support. The question is whether he can sustain his level against Shelton's power game.

Alcaraz vs. Ruud: The Man Who Keeps Finding a Way

Carlos Alcaraz's Paris Masters record is a curiosity unlike any other in his career. Despite winning 28 ATP titles — including Grand Slams and multiple Masters 1000 events — the Spaniard had never reached a Paris Masters semifinal across nine previous appearances. That changes today, one way or another.

Alcaraz earned his spot in the most dramatic fashion possible. Against Taylor Fritz in the quarterfinals, Alcaraz was a set and a break down and facing two match points in the second set. He erased both, took the set 7-5, and ran away with the third 6-3, turning certain elimination into a 6-7(10), 7-5, 6-3 victory. The resilience was vintage Alcaraz.

Casper Ruud, meanwhile, has been quiet but effective. The Norwegian defeated Nuno Borges 7-6(5), 6-4 in the quarterfinals, maintaining the consistency that has been his hallmark throughout the indoor hard-court season. Ruud is dangerous in this format and enters the semifinal fresh.

Alcaraz leads their head-to-head record and enters as the strong favorite. But he will need another high-energy performance to continue his Paris redemption arc.

What's Next

The Paris Masters final takes place Sunday, November 8, also at Accor Arena. The champion earns 1,000 ranking points, and with the ATP Finals less than a week away (Turin, November 15-22), form in Paris matters. For Alcaraz and Shelton — both already qualified for Turin — a Paris title would be the perfect final tune-up. For Van Assche and Ruud, winning this semifinal would be the biggest result of their respective years.
