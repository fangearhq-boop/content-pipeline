ATP Finals Field Complete: Tiafoe's Dream, Jodar's History

By Ryan Calloway | November 7, 2026 | Tennis Fanrecap

The 2026 Nitto ATP Finals field is officially complete. Eight players will contest the year-end championship in Turin, Italy, from November 15-22 — and the final two spots were confirmed in dramatic fashion on Friday evening during the Paris Masters quarterfinals.

The full field: Alexander Zverev, Carlos Alcaraz, Ben Shelton, Daniil Medvedev, Flavio Cobolli, Arthur Fils, Frances Tiafoe, and Rafael Jodar.

Tiafoe: A Long Road to Turin

Frances Tiafoe clinched the seventh spot in the field when Tommy Paul fell to Ben Shelton in the Paris quarterfinals. With Paul eliminated, Tiafoe's points tally locked him into a top-eight finish in the Race to Turin — securing his first-ever ATP Finals appearance.

Tiafoe had been on the edge of qualification for weeks, holding his position despite a second-round exit at Paris, where he lost to Quentin Halys after holding four match points. The American's consistent season across multiple events accumulated enough points to hold on. Making the ATP Finals has long been a stated goal of his; after years of near-misses, he will walk into the Inalpi Arena in Turin next week having achieved it.

Jodar: The Most Historic Qualification in ATP History

Rafael Jodar's spot was sealed in equally dramatic fashion — but he did not have to lift a racket to confirm it. When Carlos Alcaraz defeated Taylor Fritz 6-7(10), 7-5, 6-3 in the quarterfinals on Friday evening, Jodar's spot in Turin was mathematically secure. Fritz had needed to continue in Paris to overtake Jodar; with his elimination, the final door closed.

What makes Jodar's achievement remarkable is the context: 2026 is his first full year on the ATP Tour. No player in the history of the ATP Rankings era had ever qualified for the year-end Finals in the same year as their tour-level debut — until now. Jodar becomes the first.

The young Spaniard's season included results good enough to accumulate Turin-qualifying points across the Race, and he held on through the final weeks of the season as more experienced players chased him down. Fritz, who had been in contention for weeks, could not close the gap.

Fritz Misses Out

For Taylor Fritz, the elimination from Paris ended what had been an agonizing race. The American had been in contention for a Turin spot for months, but a 6-7(10), 7-5, 6-3 quarterfinal loss to Alcaraz — in which he held two match points and lost both — meant the door closed on his 2026 ATP Finals campaign. Fritz will have to wait at least another year for his chance to compete at the year-end event.

What's Next

The Nitto ATP Finals run November 15-22 at the Inalpi Arena in Turin, Italy. The eight-man field is now set, with Zverev entering as world No. 1. Alcaraz, Shelton, and Medvedev are expected to be the key contenders. For Tiafoe and Jodar, the event will be an experience — and perhaps a springboard — unlike any they have had before.
