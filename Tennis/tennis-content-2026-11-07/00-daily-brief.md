### STORY 2: ATP Finals Field Complete — Tiafoe and Jodar Clinch Last Two Spots
**Tier:** 1 | **Type:** Race Clinch / Historic | **Byline:** Ryan Calloway

The 8-player field for the Nitto ATP Finals (Nov 15-22, Turin) is now complete. Frances Tiafoe clinched his spot when Tommy Paul fell to Shelton in the QF — Tiafoe's first-ever ATP Finals appearance. Rafael Jodar qualified without playing: Taylor Fritz's QF loss to Alcaraz sealed Jodar's spot, making him the first player in ATP history to qualify for the Finals in the same year as his tour-level debut. Final 8: Zverev, Alcaraz, Shelton, Medvedev, Cobolli, Fils, Tiafoe, Jodar.

**Angles:** Historic Jodar qualification; Tiafoe's long road to Turin; Fritz's Paris QF exit dashes his Finals hopes.

### STORY 4: Van Assche's Paris Fairytale — French Wildcard Reaches Home Semifinal
**Tier:** 1 | **Type:** Feature / Underdog Story | **Byline:** Elena Voss

Luca Van Assche, 21, has captivated Paris on a wildcard run that includes wins over Mariano Navone, 15th seed Learner Tien, Lorenzo Musetti (who had just shocked No. 1 Zverev), and Rinky Hijikata. **Angles:** Wildcard-to-SF path; home crowd factor; beat the man who beat the No. 1; youth of French tennis.

### STORY 5: Zverev and Djokovic Both Exit Paris — No. 1 and 10-Time Champion Gone
**Tier:** 2 | **Type:** Recap / Results | **Byline:** Ryan Calloway

Two of the biggest names in Paris exited in shocking fashion on Day 3 (Nov 4). World No. 1 Alexander Zverev, in his first match since officially climbing to No. 1, lost to Lorenzo Musetti 7-6(5), 6-2 — becoming the fifth player since ATP rankings began to lose his opening match as world No. 1, and the first to do so in straight sets.
