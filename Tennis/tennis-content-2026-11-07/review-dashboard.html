### Long-Form Post The two most anticipated names at this year's Paris Masters are already gone, and both departed in historic fashion. Alexander Zverev walked onto court on Wednesday as the world's No. 1 player for the first time in his career. He had won the French and US Opens in 2026, reaching the summit of men's tennis at last. His first match at No. 1? A 7-6(5), 6-2 defeat to Lorenzo Musetti. His Paris Masters story may not be finished — but this chapter closed earlier than anyone expected.
