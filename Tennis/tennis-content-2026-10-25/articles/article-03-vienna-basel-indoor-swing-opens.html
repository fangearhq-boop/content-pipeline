Vienna and Basel Open Sunday — The European Indoor Season Is Here

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | October 25, 2026

After weeks of hardcourt action in Asia, the tennis world shifts indoors this week as both the Vienna Erste Bank Open and the Swiss Indoors in Basel launch their main draws on Sunday. Two simultaneous ATP 500 events means a week of wall-to-wall elite men's tennis — and with the Race to Turin very much alive, every result will carry added weight.

Vienna: Alcaraz and Zverev in Opposite Halves

The Vienna draw has the ingredients for a classic final if the top seeds navigate their sections. Top seed Alexander Zverev opens against Spanish wildcard Martín Landaluce, while No. 2 seed Carlos Alcaraz — who joined the draw on a late wildcard — faces Karen Khachanov in his opener. Alcaraz leads that head-to-head 6-0, so the question is less about the outcome than the manner of it.

The draw setup means Zverev and Alcaraz could only meet in the final, which would be must-watch tennis. Alcaraz has been one of the best players in the world across both 2025 and 2026, and Zverev has been the year-end No. 1 by a significant margin. Their matchups always deliver something memorable.

The rest of the Vienna field includes Flavio Cobolli, Alex de Minaur, Frances Tiafoe, Tommy Paul, and Jakub Mensik — all players with legitimate upset potential on fast indoor hardcourts.

Basel: Shelton, Djokovic, and a Star-Studded Field

Basel is headlined by No. 1 seed Ben Shelton, who has been among the most exciting players on tour this year and arrives in Switzerland looking to add to his 2026 titles. The draw also includes Novak Djokovic on a wildcard, Holger Rune, and Arthur Fils — making this one of the stronger Basel fields in recent years.

Djokovic's participation adds intrigue. The Serb is still in contention for a spot in the ATP Finals in Turin, and a deep run in Basel would significantly boost his Race to Turin standing. His indoor record is excellent, and the crowd in Basel has always been one of the warmest in the sport for him.

The Stakes: ATP Race to Turin

With three players already confirmed for the ATP Finals — Zverev, Alcaraz, and Shelton — the remaining five spots are up for grabs. Medvedev, Cobolli, Tiafoe, Fils, de Minaur, and Djokovic are all in the hunt, and Vienna and Basel represent the most significant points opportunities before the Paris Masters (October 31–November 8).

Every round matters, every upset changes the math, and the Race to Turin officially enters its most critical phase this week.

What's Next

Both Vienna and Basel run through November 1. First-round action begins Sunday and Monday, with the draws expected to heat up toward the latter part of the week as the bigger names progress. By the time the finals are played on November 1, the picture of who is heading to Turin will be considerably clearer.

For fans, this is one of the best weeks on the men's calendar — two venues, two fields, and a Race that turns every match into appointment viewing.
