ATP Race to Turin: Vienna and Basel Open the Final Push

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | October 25, 2026

Three men have secured their places at the ATP Finals in Turin (November 15–22). Five spots remain. And with Vienna and Basel opening their main draws on Sunday, the Race to Turin has officially entered its most critical stretch of the season.

Who's In, Who's Chasing

Alexander Zverev, Carlos Alcaraz, and Ben Shelton have clinched their spots and will be in Turin regardless of what happens between now and the Paris Masters. The remaining five places are contested by a cluster of players who have all had strong years but have not yet done enough to lock in their berths.

Based on October standings, the chasing pack is led by Flavio Cobolli (fourth), Frances Tiafoe (fifth), and Arthur Fils (sixth), with Daniil Medvedev (seventh), Novak Djokovic (eighth), and Alex de Minaur (alternate) all in serious contention. The margins between these players are close enough that a quarter-final exit in Vienna or Basel could drop a player out of position entirely.

What Vienna and Basel Mean for the Race

ATP 500 events offer significant Race points — 500 for a title, 300 for a finalist, 180 for a semifinal, and 90 for a quarter-final. For the players currently sitting on the bubble, this week is not just about winning titles; it's about every round won and every round lost.

Medvedev, who could add points from the Almaty final on Sunday, is particularly well-positioned to make a move this week. He is seeded in Vienna and will want to run deep, ideally reaching the final or winning the title. Fils and Djokovic are both in Basel, meaning their results will directly affect each other's Race standings.

Paris Remains the Biggest Prize

The Paris Rolex Masters (October 31–November 8) is the final ATP Masters 1000 of the season and offers the largest remaining points haul — 1,000 points for a title. With Paris coming directly after Vienna and Basel, players who go deep this week could arrive in Paris either energized or tired, depending on how the draws play out.

The Race will effectively be decided across the next three weeks. All five open spots are expected to be settled by the time the Paris Masters concludes, with the ATP Finals in Turin beginning just one week later.

What's Next

Vienna and Basel both run through November 1. First-round results will begin reshaping the Race picture from Sunday onwards. Following the results in real time, and checking racetotur.in for the updated standings, is the best way to track how the picture evolves.

After Paris, the final invitations to Turin will be sent. The race for those five open spots is one of the best ongoing storylines in men's tennis right now — and it is just getting started.
