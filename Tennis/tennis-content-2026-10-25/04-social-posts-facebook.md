### STORY 2: Almaty Final — Basilashvili vs. Medvedev

**Long-Form Post (8:00 AM ET)**
Every now and then, tennis gives us a story like this.

Nikoloz Basilashvili came into the 2026 Almaty Open as a qualifier — ranked No. 128 in the world, not even in the main draw until someone else dropped out. He had to win qualifying matches before the tournament even started. And on Saturday, he beat No. 7 seed Stefanos Tsitsipas 6-4, 6-7(5), 6-3 to reach his first ATP Tour final in more than four years.

Waiting for him in Sunday's final: top seed and defending champion Daniil Medvedev, who has won this tournament before and who handled his semifinal without breaking much of a sweat (Bublik retired early).

Basilashvili was once a top-10 player. He knows how to win at this level. But Medvedev is still one of the best hard-court players alive. This final has all the ingredients for a compelling story — the qualifier chasing a comeback, the champion defending his crown.

The Almaty final takes place Sunday at 5:30 PM local time. Who are you rooting for?

### STORY 4: WTA Finals Preview

**Long-Form Post (12:30 PM ET)**
The 2026 WTA Finals field is complete, and it's a good one.

Eight players will compete at the Indian Wells Tennis Garden in California from November 8 to 15 — and for the first time, this premier season-ending event is in the United States after years in Riyadh. The field: Elena Rybakina (world No. 1), Aryna Sabalenka, Mirra Andreeva, Jessica Pegula, Coco Gauff, Elina Svitolina, Linda Noskova, and Karolina Muchova.

Rybakina is the clear favorite. She won today in Ningbo, she's the world No. 1, and she won last year's WTA Finals final against Sabalenka. Sabalenka, who has been one of the most consistent players of the past several years, will be hunting for a reversal of that result. Gauff brings the local energy — she's based in Florida but her fan base across California is massive.

The tournament moves to round-robin format before the semis and finals, so every match counts and there's no margin for a slow start.
