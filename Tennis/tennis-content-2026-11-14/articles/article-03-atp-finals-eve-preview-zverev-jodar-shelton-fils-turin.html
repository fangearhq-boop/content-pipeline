ATP Finals Opens in Turin Tomorrow — Zverev vs. Jodar Leads a Compelling Day 1

By Ryan Calloway | November 14, 2026 | Tennis Fanrecap

After months of building, the 2026 Nitto ATP Finals opens Sunday at the Inalpi Arena in Turin, and the first day delivers two John McEnroe Group matches that set the tone for the week. Year-end world No. 1 Alexander Zverev faces Rafael Jodar not before 2:00 PM CET. Ben Shelton and Arthur Fils close out the evening at 8:30 PM CET. Carlos Alcaraz, the second seed and form favorite, waits until Monday's Day 2 when he faces Frances Tiafoe.

Zverev Opens Against Jodar

Zverev arrives in Turin having claimed year-end No. 1 for the first time and seeking a third ATP Finals title after wins in 2018 and 2021. He is the highest seed in the McEnroe Group and the most experienced player in the field at this event. His record at the ATP Finals — the depth of runs, the consistency in group stage and knockout rounds — makes him the structural favorite.

His first opponent is Jodar, who made history this season by qualifying for the Finals in his debut year on the ATP Tour — the first player ever to do so. That is not an easy achievement to put in context. It means Jodar, in his first full season as a tour-level player, finished among the eight best in the world. He beat Zverev during the indoor season and enters Turin with nothing to lose and the kind of loose, aggressive game that upsets prefer.

Zverev leads their career head-to-head, but head-to-heads at the Finals are notoriously difficult to weight. Everyone here is in form. Everyone here is dangerous.

Shelton vs. Fils in the Night Session

The evening McEnroe Group match between Ben Shelton and Arthur Fils is a generational showdown between two players who both want to prove they belong at the top of the game. Shelton reached the Paris Masters final two weeks ago before losing to Alcaraz. Fils is making his ATP Finals debut and comes in as one of the tour's rising powers.

Both are big servers who play best on hard courts with pace. The key variable in indoor conditions — where the ball stays low and the bounces are faster — will be who can dictate from the return. The player who gets into rallies on their own terms will likely win the opening set.

The Lendl Group Waits for Monday

Carlos Alcaraz opens his title campaign Monday against Frances Tiafoe. Daniil Medvedev, Flavio Cobolli (also a debutant), and Tiafoe round out the Ivan Lendl Group. The Alcaraz-Medvedev group clash is the marquee Lendl Group match — two players who have faced each other in Grand Slam finals and understand each other's games deeply.

But all of that is for Day 2 and beyond. Sunday belongs to Turin and the McEnroe Group, and the season's final chapter finally starts at 2:00 PM CET tomorrow.

What's Next

The ATP Finals runs through Sunday, November 22, with semifinals on Friday and Saturday and the final on Sunday. The two group winners and two second-place finishers with the best records advance from each group to the knockouts. Zverev, Shelton, Fils, and Jodar all begin their bids for a place in the semifinals starting tomorrow. The best indoor clay-hard court tennis of the year is about to be played.
