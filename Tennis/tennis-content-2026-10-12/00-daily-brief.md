### STORY 1: Zheng Qinwen Stuns Andreeva, Makes WTA Beijing History
**Tier:** 1 | **Type:** FOLLOW-UP (Oct 10 S1: Andreeva advances to WTA Beijing final)
**Byline:** Marcus Cole

Zheng Qinwen won the 2026 China Open (WTA Beijing) on October 11, defeating top seed and finalist Mirra Andreeva 7-5, 6-4. Zheng entered as a wildcard ranked No. 54 — the lowest seed ever to win a WTA 1000 event in the modern era. She became the first Chinese player to win the China Open and claimed her 6th WTA title overall, returning from a right elbow injury that had sidelined her since Tokyo 2024. Andreeva, who was chasing her 4th title of 2026 and what would have been a move to World No. 3, fell in 1:43.

**Key Facts:**
- Zheng def. Andreeva 7-5, 6-4 (1:43)
- Zheng entered as wildcard ranked No. 54 — lowest-ranked WTA 1000 champion in modern era
- First Chinese player ever to win the China Open WTA 1000
- Zheng's 6th career WTA title; first since Tokyo 2024 (returning from right elbow injury)
- Andreeva's 5th final of 2026; would have been her 4th title + World No. 3 ascent
- Zheng dropped 78 games across 7 matches (most by a WTA 1000 champion since format began 1990)
- On her run: def. No. 6 seed Svitolina 6-3, 7-6(6); def. No. 13 seed Mertens 7-5, 6-2 in SF
