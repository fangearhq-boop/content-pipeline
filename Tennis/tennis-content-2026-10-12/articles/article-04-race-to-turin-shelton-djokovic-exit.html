Race to Turin Reshuffled: Shelton and Djokovic Lose Shanghai Points

By Marcus Cole | October 12, 2026

The ATP Race to Turin for the final spots at the year-end championships shifted significantly after Ben Shelton's third-round exit and Novak Djokovic's second-round loss at Shanghai Masters.

Shelton's Exit Costs Him Dearly

Ben Shelton came to Shanghai needing a strong run. The American was sitting near the bubble — approximately 4,440 Race points — which placed him in the fight for one of the final spots at the ATP Finals in Turin. A deep run in Shanghai, a Masters 1000 offering up to 1,000 points, would have gone a long way toward securing his place.

Instead, Shelton fell in the third round to 21-year-old Arthur Gea, losing 7-6(5), 7-6(3) in his first-ever Masters 1000 main draw match. The defeat means Shelton picks up minimal points from Shanghai and his Race standing is now far more precarious heading into Vienna and Paris-Bercy.

Djokovic: Beijing Points but Shanghai Deflation

Novak Djokovic's ATP Race had received a significant boost from his 7th Beijing title earlier in October — he climbed six places in the Race standings after that win. But his second-round exit at Shanghai leaves him with the minimum points from one of the tour's richest events.

For Djokovic, the calculus is now clear: he will need very deep runs in Vienna and Paris-Bercy, the two remaining events before Turin, to climb into the top 8. At 39, managing schedule and physical load is as much a priority as chasing points — the Race math may simply not work in his favor this year.

Who Is Advancing

While Shelton and Djokovic saw their Race campaigns falter, others advanced to improve their positions. Alex de Minaur reached the Round of 16, where he faces Karen Khachanov. A win would push de Minaur further up the Race standings. Daniil Medvedev, also still alive, is a serious contender to accumulate points as he advances through the draw.

Frances Tiafoe was another bubble casualty, losing to Albert Davidovich Fokina 7-6(4), 4-6, 7-6(6). Stefanos Tsitsipas, meanwhile, picked himself up with a convincing upset of No. 11 seed Taylor Fritz 3-6, 7-5, 6-3, injecting himself back into Race conversations.

What's Left: The Math

Alexander Zverev and Carlos Alcaraz have already qualified for Turin. That leaves six spots in the ATP Finals for the remaining field. Shanghai, Vienna, and Paris-Bercy are all still to be completed. A player who reaches the Shanghai final could earn up to 800 points; the winner earns 1,000. Vienna (ATP 500) and Paris-Bercy (ATP Masters 1000) offer additional hauls.

The Race is still mathematically open for Shelton, Djokovic, Tiafoe, de Minaur, Medvedev, Tsitsipas, and several others. But every early exit narrows the window further, while every deep run opens the door wider. The coming weeks in Shanghai's later rounds will be decisive.

What's Next

The Shanghai Round of 16 takes place October 13-14. The quarterfinals and semifinals will follow in the days after. With Vienna beginning October 26 and Paris-Bercy starting November 2, the Race will accelerate toward its conclusion over the next three weeks.
