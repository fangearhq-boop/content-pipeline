US Open Draw Ceremony Preview: Thursday at Noon ET — Everything You Need to Know

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | August 25, 2026

The 2026 US Open main draw gets its structure on Thursday, August 27, when the draw ceremony takes place at approximately noon ET. Until that moment, seeds are confirmed but matchups are unknown. On Thursday, the brackets fill in — and then the real conversation about the 2026 US Open can begin.

The Confirmed Seeds

Seedings for the 2026 US Open were determined by August 24 rankings, coinciding with the draw ceremony on the 27th. The top line on the men's side belongs to Alexander Zverev, who assumes the No. 1 position in the absence of Jannik Sinner. Carlos Alcaraz is seeded No. 2. Novak Djokovic is No. 4.

On the women's side, Aryna Sabalenka holds the No. 1 seed. Iga Swiatek is seeded No. 2. Coco Gauff — the defending US Open champion — is seeded No. 3. Jessica Pegula, Cincinnati finalist and fan favorite at Flushing Meadows, is seeded No. 4.

Alexandra Eala of the Philippines enters the draw as a seed for the first time in her Grand Slam career — a historic moment for Filipino tennis.

The Withdrawal Factor

More than fourteen players have withdrawn from the 2026 US Open field. Sinner's absence is the most significant — the world's top-ranked player is out with a right knee injury, reshaping the bottom half of the men's draw considerably. Emma Raducanu and Bianca Andreescu have also confirmed withdrawals due to stress fractures.

When that many players withdraw, the draw does something unusual: it creates pathways where fourth-seed or lower players can realistically reach the second week without facing anyone from the top half of the draw until the quarterfinals. The ceremony on Thursday will reveal whether the bracket falls in ways that make those pathways obvious or obscure.

What to Watch in the Draw

Three questions the draw will answer: Which quarter does Djokovic land in — and does a favorable section give him a realistic path to a semifinal without facing Zverev or Alcaraz until the final? Does Gauff face any of the top four women before the semifinals as defending champion? And where does Arthur Fils — arriving fresh from his Cincinnati Masters 1000 title — land in a bracket he could legitimately threaten?

The women's draw is arguably deeper despite the withdrawal list. Sabalenka, Swiatek, Gauff, and Pegula are four players who could each plausibly win the tournament. The quarter assignments will reveal who has the harder path to the final.

What's Next

The draw ceremony is Thursday, August 27 at noon ET. First-round main draw matches begin Sunday, August 30. The women's final is scheduled for Saturday, September 12. The men's final is Sunday, September 13. The 2026 US Open carries a record $108 million prize pool — the largest in the tournament's history.
