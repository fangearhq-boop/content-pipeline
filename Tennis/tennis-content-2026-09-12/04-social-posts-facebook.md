### STORY 5: Arthur Ashe Legacy Feature

**Long-Form Post**
**Window:** 12:00 PM ET

Ben Shelton will play in the US Open final on Sunday at Arthur Ashe Stadium.

The last Black American man to reach a US Open final was Arthur Ashe — in 1972. The court where Shelton plays Sunday is named after him.

The last Black American man to reach any Grand Slam final was MaliVai Washington at Wimbledon in 1996 — thirty years ago.

Shelton and Frances Tiafoe both reached the semifinals of the same draw. Whatever happened on Friday night — and whoever lifts the trophy on Sunday — this US Open will be remembered.

The game has always been big enough. Sometimes the moment catches up to it.
