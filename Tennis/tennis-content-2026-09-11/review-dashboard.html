This is his third US Open semifinal in five years. Tonight is the match. Both are playing for more than themselves. ---

With Ben Shelton and Frances Tiafoe both winning their quarterfinals, the 2026 US Open men's final is guaranteed to be all-American. Carlos Alcaraz, the defending champion, fell at 3:33 AM to a 22-year-old from Atlanta who had never beaten a top-3 player before that night. The draw opened up, and two Americans walked through. Shelton is 22. Tiafoe is 28. Different generations of the same dream. Tonight they play each other, and on Sunday, one of them makes history. ---
