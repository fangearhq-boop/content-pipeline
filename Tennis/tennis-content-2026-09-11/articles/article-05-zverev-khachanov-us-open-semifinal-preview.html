Zverev vs. Khachanov: Roland Garros Champion Eyes Second 2026 Grand Slam in Men's SF

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | September 11, 2026

Before the all-American night session at Arthur Ashe, the first men's semifinal of the day gives the tournament's No. 1 seed a chance to make his own history. Alexander Zverev of Germany, the reigning Roland Garros champion, faces Karen Khachanov of Russia in a match scheduled no earlier than 3 PM ET on Friday at Arthur Ashe Stadium.

Zverev's 2026 Season

This has been the year Alexander Zverev finally put it all together at Grand Slam level. He won the 2026 French Open in June — his first major title — after years of falling short in the sport's biggest moments. That win at Roland Garros seemed to unlock something in the German. He has carried that confidence into the hard court season and arrives in Flushing Meadows as the world No. 1 seed.

A victory today would put him one match from winning two Grand Slam titles in a single calendar year. That is territory very few players in the modern era have reached. Zverev's path to the semifinal included a dominant quarterfinal victory over Botic van de Zandschulp, 6-2, 7-5, 6-1 — a performance that showed his full range and left no doubt about his fitness entering the final stretch of the tournament.

Khachanov's Third Grand Slam Semifinal

Karen Khachanov is in unfamiliar territory in a familiar way. This is his third Grand Slam semifinal — he reached the last four at the 2022 US Open and the 2023 Australian Open — and on both previous occasions he was unable to advance further. A strong, physical baseliner with a significant serve, Khachanov is capable of troubling anyone when he is hitting cleanly and his first serve is on.

His route to the semifinals raised some questions. His quarterfinal opponent, No. 28 seed Alexander Blockx, retired from the match when trailing 6-2, 7-5, 2-2 in the third set. Whether that abbreviated match sharpened or rusted Khachanov's match instincts is one of the key narratives heading into today.

The Key Matchup

Zverev's serve and his ability to control points from the baseline make him the heavy favorite. The critical variable, as it often is with Zverev, is the opening set. When he takes the first set and gets on the right side of the scoreline early, his opponents rarely come back. Khachanov's best chance lies in forcing a slow, grinding match where his physical presence can wear down his opponent over five sets.

What's Next

Today's winner faces Ben Shelton or Frances Tiafoe in Sunday's men's final at Arthur Ashe Stadium. For Zverev, a second Grand Slam title in 2026 would cement him as the year's dominant player on the men's side. For Khachanov, reaching a Grand Slam final for the first time would be the defining result of his career. The match starts no earlier than 3 PM ET at Arthur Ashe — Friday's opening act before the all-American evening showdown.
