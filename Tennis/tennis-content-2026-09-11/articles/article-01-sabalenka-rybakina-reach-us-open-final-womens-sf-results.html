Sabalenka, Rybakina Reach US Open Final After Beating Pegula and Gauff

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | September 11, 2026

The 2026 US Open women's final is set. No. 1 seed Aryna Sabalenka defeated No. 3 Jessica Pegula 7-5, 6-2, and No. 2 Elena Rybakina came back from a set down to defeat No. 4 Coco Gauff 3-6, 6-4, 6-4 on Thursday at Arthur Ashe Stadium. Both semifinals were decided in straight sets, and the women's final — Sabalenka vs. Rybakina — is scheduled for Saturday.

Sabalenka Dismantles Pegula

Aryna Sabalenka was methodical and relentless in her 7-5, 6-2 victory over Jessica Pegula. She attacked Pegula's second serve from the first game, using her serve-plus-one strategy to repeatedly get on the front foot and dictate rallies from the baseline.

The first set was competitive — Pegula held and fought — but Sabalenka's aggression eventually told. She broke to take a 6-5 lead and then held to close the set. From there, the match was never in doubt. Sabalenka took the second set 6-2 in comfortable fashion, breaking at will and giving Pegula very little to work with.

Pegula had made three consecutive US Open semifinals and was one of the most experienced players in the tournament. She simply ran into a Sabalenka who was playing some of the best tennis of her career. The Belarusian advances to her fourth consecutive US Open final.

Rybakina Rallies Past Gauff

The second semifinal told a different story. Coco Gauff, the 2023 US Open champion, came out fast and took the first set 6-3, serving notice that the new world No. 1 was in for a fight. Gauff's movement was sharp and her groundstrokes had purpose — she looked, for a set and a half, like a player who might pull off a stunning upset.

But Elena Rybakina steadied. The Kazakh, who secured the world No. 1 ranking for the first time in her career when she won her quarterfinal earlier in the week, found her timing and began to impose herself. She took the second set 6-4, and then in the third, it was Rybakina who looked the fitter, sharper, and calmer player. She won the deciding set 6-4 to advance.

For Gauff, her US Open run ends in the semifinals. She leaves Flushing Meadows having pushed the tournament's best player hard in a tournament where she reached the last four.

What's Next

Saturday's women's final at Arthur Ashe Stadium features Sabalenka against Rybakina — their third Grand Slam final meeting, with the previous two split 1-1. Sabalenka is bidding to become just the third woman in the Open Era to win three consecutive US Open titles. Rybakina, the 2026 Australian Open champion and incoming world No. 1, is seeking to complete a sweep of the two hardcourt majors in a single calendar year. The match is appointment viewing for any tennis fan.
