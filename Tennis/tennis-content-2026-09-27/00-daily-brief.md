### STORY 2: Singapore Open — Fernandez Wins 6th WTA Title, Defeats Gibson 7-5, 6-0
**Type:** FOLLOW-UP (Sep 26 S2: Gibson in final after Prozorova controversy → FINAL RESULT)
**Tier:** 1 (WTA 500 title result; Fernandez's 6th career title; strong result story)
**Byline:** Elena Voss
**Angle:** Leylah Fernandez defeated Talia Gibson 7-5, 6-0 in the WTA 500 Singapore Open final on Sunday to claim the sixth title of her career. Gibson, an Australian qualifier who had advanced to her first WTA final after semifinalist Tatiana Prozorova was withdrawn under the concussion protocol, took a set point in the opening set but could not convert it. Fernandez then won nine consecutive games to close out the match in straight sets and claim the title at the OCBC Arena. The 23-year-old Canadian's path to the title included a quarterfinal victory over No. 1 seed Mirra Andreeva and a three-set semifinal comeback against Maja Chwalinska. This is Fernandez's first title since the Japan Open in October of the prior year, and it comes as the Asian swing heats up ahead of bigger events in Beijing and Wuhan. Gibson, who had beaten Maria Sakkari in the quarterfinals and advanced through the controversy-heavy draw, exits with her best-ever WTA finish.
