Chengdu Open Final Set: Shapovalov and Hurkacz to Meet Tuesday After Ending Two Cinderella Runs

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | September 27, 2026

The 2026 ATP 250 Chengdu Open will produce a final between Denis Shapovalov and Hubert Hurkacz on Tuesday, after both players defeated the surprise runs of the tournament's second week. The final brings together two established tour names who have the pedigree to win at this level — but they had to work through some of the tournament's more compelling storylines to get there.

Shapovalov Ends Mannarino's Run

Adrian Mannarino entered Sunday's semifinal as one of the week's great stories. The 38-year-old Frenchman had beaten Shang Juncheng in the opening rounds, then eliminated No. 3 seed Alejandro Tabilo (the defending champion) and No. 1 seed Valentin Vacherot in back-to-back quarterfinals — a run that nobody in the draw had predicted when the week began.

Shapovalov put an end to it in three sets: 6-7(6), 6-3, 6-2. Mannarino won the opening set in a tiebreak and appeared to have found his rhythm, but Shapovalov's level lifted considerably in the second and third sets and he closed the match out with control. The Canadian, No. 7 seed in Chengdu, has been building form during the Asian swing and his semifinal performance showed the kind of clean ball-striking that puts him on the short list of players who can win titles on hard courts.

Hurkacz Survives Harris

The other semifinal was nearly as compelling. Qualifier Lloyd Harris — who had beaten No. 1 seed Vacherot in the quarterfinals — pushed Hurkacz hard across three sets before the Polish player closed it out 4-6, 6-3, 6-4. Harris took the opening set, and for the second time in consecutive rounds, the qualifier from South Africa was competing past expectations. Hurkacz found his footing and accelerated through the second and third sets to book his spot in Tuesday's final.

Hurkacz enters the final with 13 career ATP titles to his name and the experience of having competed at the sport's highest levels for several years. He is chasing his first title since earlier in the 2026 season.

What to Watch in the Final

Shapovalov and Hurkacz represent a contrast in playing styles that should make for an engaging hard-court final. Shapovalov's lefty attack can be devastating when he's hitting through the court; Hurkacz's defensive and serve-first game gives him a reliable baseline for big-match play. This would be Shapovalov's second career ATP Tour final if he wins, while Hurkacz would add to his already substantial title count.

What's Next

The Chengdu Open final takes place Tuesday, September 29. The winner adds an ATP 250 title and the associated ranking points heading into the stretch run of the 2026 season. The Asian swing continues immediately with the China Open (ATP 500) in Beijing, which begins September 30. Both Shapovalov and Hurkacz have entries in the Beijing field depending on final draws — the ATP 500 draw is released Monday.
