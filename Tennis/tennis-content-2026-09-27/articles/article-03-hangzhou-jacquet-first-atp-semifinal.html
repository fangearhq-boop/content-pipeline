Kyrian Jacquet Reaches First ATP Tour Semifinal in Hangzhou, Faces Rublev on Monday

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | September 27, 2026

Kyrian Jacquet's remarkable run through the ATP 250 Hangzhou Open continued Sunday when the French player defeated No. 6 seed Fabian Marozsan 7-6(12), 6-4 to reach the first ATP Tour semifinal of his career. The victory came one day after Jacquet eliminated No. 3 seed Tomás Martín Etcheverry, making him the rare player to topple two seeded opponents in back-to-back rounds at the same tournament.

The tiebreak against Marozsan gave Jacquet his stiffest test of the week, stretching to [12-10] before he claimed it. The opening set alone was a test of nerves; once Jacquet broke through, the second set was notably more controlled as he closed out the match 6-4 to earn Monday's semifinal berth.

The Context of a Breakthrough

Jacquet entered Hangzhou as a player with limited ATP Tour exposure at the semifinal level. His wins over Etcheverry and Marozsan represent career-best performances for a player who is now set to break into the world's top 90 rankings as a result of this run. The wins didn't come in soft brackets — both Etcheverry and Marozsan are ranked in the top 50 and arrived in Hangzhou as genuine contenders in a field that was missing most of the top 10 due to the Laver Cup.

His Monday SF opponent, No. 2 seed Andrey Rublev, came through his own Sunday quarterfinal in a hard-fought battle against Hugo Gaston — a match where Gaston reportedly saved five set points before eventually falling. Rublev enters the semifinal as a significant favorite but will be aware that Jacquet has already beaten two opponents ranked higher than him this week.

Full Semifinal Picture

No. 1 seed Daniil Medvedev's quarterfinal result had not been confirmed at research time. If Medvedev advanced, the other semifinal would be Medvedev against a fourth player who had progressed through the opposite QF. The final is scheduled for Tuesday, September 29. Prize money for the tournament stands at $1,039,090.

What's Next

Jacquet faces Rublev in Monday's semifinal at the Hangzhou Open. A Jacquet win would mark one of the most significant upset runs at any ATP 250 event this season, sending an unheralded Frenchman to his first career ATP Tour final. The tournament concludes Tuesday with the final. Hangzhou's results feed directly into the Asian swing calendar that continues with the China Open (ATP 500) in Beijing beginning September 30.
