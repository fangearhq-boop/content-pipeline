Singapore Open — Fernandez Wins 6th WTA Title, Defeats Gibson 7-5, 6-0
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Leylah Fernandez is a WTA 500 champion today, having defeated Australian qualifier Talia Gibson 7-5, 6-0 in the final of the Singapore Open. It was not a straightforward win. Gibson, who had gone on an improbable run through the draw — including a quarterfinal over Maria Sakkari — made Fernandez work in the opening set. Gibson had a set point and nearly pulled the first set out from under the defending favorite. Fernandez converted her own opportunity and then didn't look back. She won nine consecutive games to close the match in what became a dominant straight-set victory. For Fernandez, this is her sixth WTA career title. The full tournament week was a reminder of what she looks like when she's clicking: she beat No. 1 seed Mirra Andreeva in the quarterfinal, fought back from a set down against Maja Chwalinska in the semifinals, and then dispatched Gibson without drama once she found her groove. The Singapore Open's final will also be remembered for the controversy surrounding Tatiana Prozorova's semifinal withdrawal on Saturday — a situation that continues to generate debate about WTA concussion protocol. But the trophy goes to Fernandez, and it's a well-earned one. One day after producing the biggest win of his career over No. 3 seed Etcheverry, Jacquet did it again. Sunday's tiebreak against Marozsan went all the way to [12-10] before Jacquet claimed it — a sign of just how hard this run has been to earn. He now faces No. 2 seed Andrey Rublev in Monday's semifinal. Rublev advanced through Sunday's quarterfinal against Hugo Gaston in what sources described as a lengthy, physical match where Gaston saved five set points before eventually falling. Both players will have run plenty of miles by the time Monday's SF kicks off. ### Long-Form Post The China Open draw gets released tomorrow, and the conversation around the ATP side of it is already dominated by one question: can Alexander Zverev use this week to finally become World No. 1? Jannik Sinner — who entered the year as the consensus No. 1 and has been one of the most dominant players in the history of the sport during 2026 — officially withdrew from Beijing with a knee injury. His absence creates a vacuum in the draw and, more importantly, in the Race points chart. Zverev currently leads the ATP Race to the Finals.
