Gauff vs. Andreeva at Arthur Ashe: The Women's US Open Quarterfinal the Sport Has Been Waiting For

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | September 9, 2026

US Open 2026 | Coco Gauff | Mirra Andreeva | Women's Quarterfinals | WTA

Coco Gauff and Mirra Andreeva will meet at Arthur Ashe Stadium on Wednesday, starting no earlier than 12:30 PM ET, in a women's quarterfinal that needs no additional billing. The 2023 US Open champion against the 2026 French Open champion. A rematch of this year's French Open semifinal, which Andreeva won. One of the tournament's most anticipated matches, now arriving at the right time.

Whoever wins this match advances to the US Open semifinals on Thursday to face No. 1 Aryna Sabalenka, who is chasing a third consecutive US Open title. That's the prize. But to get there, one of women's tennis's most compelling young rivalries will produce another chapter.

The Backstory

Gauff won the 2023 US Open at age 19, making her one of the youngest Grand Slam champions in recent memory. She came into 2026 as a player who had the talent, the experience, and the drive to add more titles. The French Open semifinal against Andreeva was a setback — losing to an opponent who subsequently went on to win the title in Paris.

Andreeva, also 21, has lived up to every expectation since that Paris win. She has continued playing aggressive, decisive tennis throughout 2026, and her road to the US Open quarterfinals has been excellent. She enters Wednesday's match as the more recent major champion between the two, but Gauff has the home crowd advantage and a particular motivation that comes from having lost this specific rivalry's last major meeting.

The Matchup

Gauff's game is built on court coverage, consistent groundstrokes, and an ability to absorb pressure and redirect it. She moves exceptionally well and rarely gives opponents the angle they want. Her serve has improved significantly over the last two years, and she is a genuinely difficult opponent for anyone who tries to take her off her preferred game plan.

Andreeva plays with a different kind of energy — direct, aggressive, and willing to take risks that most players at her level would decline. When her forehand is working and she is getting her first serve in, she is capable of dispatching opponents before they have found their footing. She beat Gauff at Roland Garros by playing her own game without flinching.

What's Next

The winner of today's match advances to the US Open semifinals on Thursday. Sabalenka is waiting, having already dispatched Linda Noskova in the quarterfinals on Tuesday. A Gauff vs. Sabalenka semifinal would be a rematch of multiple previous Grand Slam encounters. An Andreeva vs. Sabalenka semifinal would be a battle of two of the most aggressive baseliners in the women's game.

Either outcome sets up a must-watch Thursday. But first, Wednesday belongs to Gauff and Andreeva — two 21-year-olds playing for a seat at the US Open final table.
