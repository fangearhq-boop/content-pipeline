US Open QF Day 2: Zverev Eyes Semis, All-American Women's Quarterfinal Guarantees U.S. Woman in Final Four

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | September 9, 2026

US Open 2026 | Alexander Zverev | Jessica Pegula | Emma Navarro | Quarterfinals

The 2026 US Open quarterfinals continue Wednesday evening with two matches that will complete the final four in both the men's and women's draws. At 6:30 PM ET, No. 1 Alexander Zverev faces Botic van de Zandschulp for a men's semifinal berth. Immediately following, No. 3 Jessica Pegula meets No. 26 Emma Navarro in an all-American women's quarterfinal that guarantees at least one American in the women's final four.

Zverev vs. Van de Zandschulp

Defending champion Alexander Zverev enters this match as the clear favorite. The German No. 1 seed has been one of the most consistent players at this tournament, and he arrives at the quarterfinals in excellent form. He has won this title before — he knows how to handle the pressure of these rounds.

But Van de Zandschulp has been one of the tournament's compelling underdog stories. The Dutchman has shown resilience and belief throughout his run, and Zverev himself has acknowledged the threat, noting that Van de Zandschulp has always had a particular affinity for this tournament. That is not the kind of comment a player makes about an opponent they take lightly.

If Zverev advances, he will face the winner of Friday's all-American men's semifinal — Tiafoe or Shelton. The men's final is beginning to take shape, and Zverev will want to be in it.

Pegula vs. Navarro — A Rematch With Stakes

The 2024 US Open produced a significant moment when Emma Navarro eliminated Jessica Pegula in that tournament. Now the two Americans meet again at the same stage — the 2026 quarterfinals — with the same prize on offer: a place in the final four.

Pegula, the No. 3 seed, is one of the most reliable competitors in women's tennis. Her game is built on consistency, aggressive returning, and the ability to outlast opponents in long rallies. She has reached this stage before and is capable of going even further.

Navarro, at No. 26, arrives with the confidence of someone who already proved she can beat Pegula on this court. She plays with a boldness that can trouble more experienced opponents, and the memory of 2024 gives her belief that the draw is not impossible.

Either way, an American woman will be in the US Open semifinals. The winner will eventually face the winner of Gauff vs. Andreeva — meaning the women's final four could feature multiple Americans alongside world No. 1 Sabalenka.

What's Next

Wednesday's matches complete the US Open quarterfinal round. By the end of the night, all four semifinal spots will be filled — men's and women's. The women's semifinals are scheduled for Thursday, the men's for Friday. The US Open has reached its most intense stretch, and every match from here carries final implications.

Tune in Wednesday evening on ESPN and CBS for the full coverage. The Ashe is loud. The stakes are high. And after Tuesday night's 3:30 AM finish between Shelton and Alcaraz, the bar for drama has been set very high indeed.
