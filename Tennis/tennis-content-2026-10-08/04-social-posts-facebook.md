**Long-Form (8:00 AM ET)**
Novak Djokovic has done it again — and this time, history came with a retirement.

It was one of the strangest tournament endings in recent memory — and Djokovic still had to win the final.

The question now: How many more? And can anyone beat him in Beijing — ever?

### STORY 2: WTA Beijing QF Set

**Long-Form (10:30 AM ET)**
The WTA Beijing draw has completely flipped — and Coco Gauff is going home early.

In one of the most surprising results of the WTA 1000 season, Elise Mertens defeated defending champion and No. 3 seed Coco Gauff 6-3, 6-3 in the Round of 16. It was the first time Mertens had beaten Gauff in six career meetings. Gauff, speaking to reporters afterward, described the online abuse she receives as a player as "draining" — a reminder that the pressure on elite athletes extends far beyond the court.

Meanwhile, Iga Swiatek quietly made history. Her 6-1, 6-3 win over teenager Iva Jovic was Swiatek's 148th WTA 1000 main-draw victory — tying Serena Williams' all-time record. Swiatek is now in the QF against Mertens on October 9. One more win, and she stands alone.

There were thrills elsewhere too. Karolina Muchova edged Naomi Osaka 7-5, 1-6, 7-6(7) in a 2-hour, 52-minute thriller. Elina Svitolina and Mirra Andreeva also advanced.

No top-3 seed remains. The QF draw is wide open.

Who do you think takes the Beijing title?

### STORY 3: Shanghai Masters Day 2

**Long-Form (12:30 PM ET)**
The Shanghai Masters has started — and the tournament's first day already had its signature upset.

On Day 1 (Wednesday), Daniel Altmaier — a veteran German not typically in the conversation for Masters wins — knocked out No. 7 seed Holger Rune 1-6, 7-6(5), 6-4. A classic first-set collapse, a tiebreak save, and a dominant third set. Altmaier next faces Ben Shelton, who is in the tournament's dangerous middle tier.

The storylines entering the second week: Alexander Zverev as top seed in the absence of Sinner and Alcaraz, Djokovic building momentum off his Beijing title, and Shelton and de Minaur looking to move up the Race to Turin standings.

Shanghai runs through October 18. The best tennis is still to come.

### STORY 4: ATP Race to Turin

**Long-Form (3:00 PM ET)**
The ATP Race to Turin just got a whole lot clearer — for one man.

With Jannik Sinner confirmed absent from Shanghai (he withdrew October 3 and hasn't played since Wimbledon in July), Alexander Zverev's path to Year-End No. 1 is essentially a formality. Zverev entered Shanghai with approximately 8,740 Race points. Sinner, at ~7,950, is about to shed roughly 3,550 of those points by not defending his 2025 Shanghai title. The projected gap after this tournament: more than 4,500 points.

That would make Zverev the first German to finish a season ranked No. 1 in the Open Era.

For the rest of the field, the Race to earn the remaining spots in the eight-man Turin field is very much alive. Ben Shelton (4,430 pts) holds spot No. 3 right now, with Carlos Alcaraz (~4,100) just behind him. Medvedev, Arthur Fils, Alex de Minaur, and Jiří Menšík are all within range. The final spots will be decided over the last three weeks of the season.

Where do you see the Race settling when the Shanghai dust clears?

### STORY 5: Swiatek WTA 1000 Record

**Long-Form (6:00 PM ET)**
There's a record on the line in Beijing tomorrow — and it belongs to one of the sport's all-time greats.

Iga Swiatek's Round of 16 victory over Iva Jovic gave her 148 WTA 1000 main-draw wins — exactly matching Serena Williams' career record. One more win, when she faces Elise Mertens in the quarterfinals on October 9, and Swiatek claims sole ownership of the mark.

It's a remarkable stat for a 25-year-old who entered this year as a controversial No. 8 seed in Beijing. Swiatek's 2026 season has had its ups and downs — she's currently sitting just outside the WTA Finals bubble, in 10th place with 3,584 points, needing 651 more to crack the top eight. A deep Beijing run would go a long way toward keeping her season alive.

Tomorrow's quarterfinal is against the same Mertens who just upset Coco Gauff. Swiatek will be motivated. History awaits.
