Gauff Out in Beijing Shock as Swiatek Ties Serena Williams' WTA 1000 Record

By Ryan Calloway | October 8, 2026

Beijing, China

Coco Gauff's bid to defend her WTA China Open title ended in a shock Round of 16 exit Wednesday, while Iga Swiatek quietly reached a milestone tied to one of the sport's most legendary names.

Elise Mertens defeated the No. 3 seed Gauff 6-3, 6-3 in straight sets — the Belgian's first victory over Gauff in six career meetings. The result eliminated the defending champion and left the Beijing draw without any of the top three seeds for the quarterfinals.

Mertens, the 13th seed, was clinical throughout. Gauff, who had been one of the tournament's favorites, made uncharacteristic errors and appeared unable to find her rhythm against Mertens' flat, aggressive ball-striking. Speaking to reporters after the match, Gauff acknowledged that the constant online criticism she receives takes a personal toll. "It's draining," she said.

Swiatek Ties the Record

While the Gauff exit dominated headlines, Iga Swiatek's efficient 6-1, 6-3 win over 18-year-old Iva Jovic carried a historic footnote: it was Swiatek's 148th WTA 1000 main-draw victory, matching Serena Williams' all-time record for the most WTA 1000 wins in the Open Era.

The win was also Swiatek's 31st WTA 1000 quarterfinal, further evidence of her sustained excellence at the sport's biggest events. At 25, she now faces Mertens in the quarterfinals on October 9 — and a victory would give her sole ownership of the all-time record.

Swiatek entered Beijing as the No. 8 seed, ranked 10th in the WTA Race to Finals with 3,584 points. She sits approximately 650 points outside the bubble for the year-end championships in Riyadh, making a deep run in Beijing essential for her season hopes.

Muchova Wins Thriller, QF Set

Elsewhere in the Round of 16, Karolina Muchova delivered arguably the day's most entertaining match, defeating former No. 1 Naomi Osaka 7-5, 1-6, 7-6(7) in two hours and 52 minutes. The third set tiebreak went deep before Muchova held on, advancing to her quarterfinal against an opponent to be confirmed.

The quarterfinal bracket now includes: Swiatek (8) vs. Mertens (13), Qinwen Zheng vs. her opponent, Elina Svitolina (6) in her half, and Mirra Andreeva (4) and Ekaterina Alexandrova (19) in theirs. With Gauff, Rybakina, and Sabalenka all eliminated before the quarterfinals, the trophy is legitimately anyone's.

The semifinals are scheduled for October 10, with the final on October 11.

What's Next

The central storyline entering the quarterfinals is whether Swiatek can claim sole ownership of the WTA 1000 wins record against the same player who just ended Gauff's campaign. Mertens has momentum; Swiatek has history — and a record — riding on every match from here.

For Gauff, the loss continues a mixed stretch after her US Open run earlier this season. She has already confirmed she will not compete in the subsequent Wuhan Open, giving her time to reset before the WTA Finals qualifying window closes.
