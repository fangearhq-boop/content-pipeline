Shanghai Masters R1: Rune Falls to Altmaier, Hurkacz Sets Up Djokovic Clash

By Elena Voss | October 8, 2026

Shanghai, China

The Rolex Shanghai Masters is two days old and has already delivered its signature upset: Daniel Altmaier, ranked outside the top 60, defeated No. 7 seed Holger Rune 1-6, 7-6(5), 6-4 in the first round, setting a combative early tone for the ATP's final Masters 1000 event of the year.

Altmaier's victory on Day 1 (Wednesday, October 7) had the hallmarks of a classic comeback win. After dropping the opening set without a fight, the German dug in, forced a tiebreak in the second, and then dominated the decisive third. His reward: a second-round match against Ben Shelton, who brings power and momentum of his own. Shelton is currently third in the Race to Turin at 4,430 points.

Day 2 Results

Thursday's action (Day 2) continued filling in the draw. The headline matchup to watch: Hubert Hurkacz defeated James Duckworth 6-3, 7-6(4) and is now lined up to face Novak Djokovic in the second round. Djokovic, who arrived in Shanghai fresh off his seventh Beijing title, enters with a bye and has not yet played in the tournament.

Stefanos Tsitsipas showed good form in a 6-3, 7-5 win over qualifier Kimmer Coppejans. Jenson Brooksby defeated Jaume Munar 7-5, 6-4. Sebastian Baez came back from a first-set loss to win 1-6, 6-3, 6-4. Pablo Carreno Busta advanced 4-6, 7-5, 6-3. Nuno Borges, Matteo Arnaldi, Zachary Svajda, Pavel Kotov, and Thiago Agustin Tirante also won their first-round matches.

In an entertaining sidelight, Bernard Tomic made a tour return and lost to Arnaldi 6-3, 6-1. Tomic reportedly told Arnaldi post-match that he had simply been "tired."

The Draw Landscape

With Jannik Sinner and Carlos Alcaraz both absent — Sinner withdrew October 3 after his injury return timeline extended past the Asian swing, while Alcaraz played the Japan Open instead of Shanghai — Alexander Zverev steps into the top seed role for a tournament he has never won.

Djokovic, seeded but not No. 1, represents the tournament's most dangerous entrant given his form: a 34-0 record in Beijing entering this week. The Shanghai draw sets up a potential Zverev-Djokovic semifinal in what would be a marquee matchup for the Race to Turin standings.

The Masters 1000 points available in Shanghai are significant, particularly for the Race to Turin bubble. Shelton, Alcaraz, Medvedev, Fils, de Minaur, and Menšík are all fighting for the six non-guaranteed spots in the eight-player year-end field.

What's Next

The top seeds — Zverev, Djokovic, and others with byes — enter the draw in the second round beginning this weekend. The full field will be in action through October 18. Key second-round matchups to watch: Djokovic vs. Hurkacz, Altmaier vs. Shelton, and Tsitsipas continuing his push through the draw.
