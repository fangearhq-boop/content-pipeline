Medvedev vs Tiafoe: The Lendl Group's Semifinal Spot Comes Down to Friday

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | November 19, 2026

The Ivan Lendl Group at the 2026 ATP Finals will be settled on Friday, November 20, when Daniil Medvedev and Frances Tiafoe play the last group match with genuine stakes at this year's tournament. Both players are 1-1. Carlos Alcaraz has already won the group at 2-0. Friday's other match — Alcaraz against the eliminated Flavio Cobolli — has no bearing on the standings. Everything comes down to Medvedev and Tiafoe.

How Both Players Arrived at 1-1

Medvedev started his group stage with a 7-6(4), 6-3 win over Cobolli on Monday. He did not concede a break point in that match, playing with the controlled baseline efficiency that makes him difficult to beat in best-of-three indoors. Wednesday, against Alcaraz, was a different story. The Spaniard won 6-2, 6-4 without facing a break point, and Medvedev left the court without having made a meaningful dent in Alcaraz's service games.

Tiafoe lost his opener to Alcaraz on Monday — a 6-2, 6-2 defeat that lasted just 69 minutes and offered little for the American to take forward. Wednesday brought a very different performance. He faced two set points down in the tiebreak against Cobolli, saved both, won the breaker 8-6, and then closed out the second set 6-0. His first ATP Finals win came emphatically, and he arrives at Friday's match with momentum that his opponent may lack.

The Head-to-Head Picture

Medvedev leads their career head-to-head record 4-0. All four of those wins came on hard courts, the same surface they will play on Friday. That number is substantial, and it makes Medvedev the clear favourite based on historical record alone. At the same time, the Tiafoe who beat Cobolli on Wednesday — saving set points, then bagelling the second set — is not a player who will walk into the arena without confidence.

Medvedev has also had a difficult week for different reasons. His loss to Alcaraz was a reminder that, against the best players on fast indoor courts, his defensive game plan can run out of room. He will need a performance closer to his Monday win over Cobolli to advance.

What's Next

The winner of Friday's match advances to the ATP Finals semifinals on Saturday, where they will face Alexander Zverev. Zverev is the McEnroe Group winner at 2-0 and already confirmed in the last four. The semifinal would take place at the Inalpi Arena in Turin on Saturday, November 21. The ATP Finals final is scheduled for Sunday, November 22. Regardless of Friday's result, the Alcaraz-Medvedev group-stage meeting only deepens the narrative around what a potential Alcaraz-Medvedev semifinal rematch — in a knockout match — would look like. Friday, however, decides who actually gets that chance.
