Tiafoe Saves Two Set Points, Then Bagels Cobolli to Win First ATP Finals Match

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | November 19, 2026

Frances Tiafoe has been to the ATP Finals before and left without winning a match. That ended on Wednesday in Turin. His 7-6(6), 6-0 win over Flavio Cobolli at the Inalpi Arena was a performance built in two very different halves — a tense, nearly lost first set and an utterly dominant second — and it set up a Lendl Group decider against Daniil Medvedev on Friday.

The result eliminates Cobolli, who finishes his ATP Finals debut at 0-2 and exits without a group win despite playing in front of a home crowd in Italy throughout the week. For Tiafoe, now 1-1 in the group, the path to the semifinals is still open.

The Match That Almost Slipped Away

Cobolli had the first set in his hands. At 6-4 in the tiebreak, the Italian held two set points — either of which would have put him in front and given the Turin crowd reason to celebrate. Tiafoe saved both. He then won the next four points to claim the tiebreak 8-6.

What followed suggested the first-set drama had something definitive to it. Tiafoe did not need a tiebreak rescue in the second set because Cobolli had nothing left to offer. Tiafoe took all six games, 6-0, in a second set that was over in a manner that made the first-set tension feel like a distant memory. The match lasted 1 hour and 32 minutes in total.

Cobolli's reaction — playing in front of his home supporters, leading in the tiebreak — made the collapse of the second set particularly difficult. The match might have felt like a turning point in the right direction after saving the opening set, but Tiafoe took control immediately after and never gave it back.

Tiafoe's First ATP Finals Win

This was Tiafoe's first victory at the ATP Finals across all his appearances at the tournament. The year-end event has historically been a difficult venue for him — the fast indoor conditions, the best-of-three format with little margin for error, and the quality of opposition are all factors. Wednesday's win breaks that sequence and gives Tiafoe something to build on heading into Friday.

The manner of it also matters. Saving set points under pressure and then producing a 6-0 second set requires both resilience and the ability to shift gear quickly. Tiafoe showed both against an opponent who, though eventually outclassed, did give him a real test in the first set.

What's Next

Tiafoe plays Daniil Medvedev on Friday in the Lendl Group's decisive final match. Medvedev is 1-1 after losing to Alcaraz 6-2, 6-4 on Wednesday. The winner reaches the ATP Finals semifinals and will face Alexander Zverev. Medvedev leads their career head-to-head 4-0, which makes Tiafoe the underdog on paper. But the American will arrive on Friday with momentum from his first ATP Finals win, a 6-0 second set in his legs, and an opponent who also needs the win just as badly.
