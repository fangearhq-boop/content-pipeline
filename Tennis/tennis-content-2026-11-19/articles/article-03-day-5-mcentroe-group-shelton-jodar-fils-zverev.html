ATP Finals Day 5: McEnroe Group Closes Out — What's at Stake for Shelton and Fils

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | November 19, 2026

Thursday at the Inalpi Arena in Turin settles the John McEnroe Group at the 2026 ATP Finals. Two matches, one remaining semifinal spot, and two players — Ben Shelton and Arthur Fils — whose fates are linked by the results of matches they're not even playing against each other. Alexander Zverev, already through, opens the day's scheduling board as a confirmed semifinalist. The other spot still belongs to whoever earns it today.

Shelton vs Jodar — Not Before 2:00 PM CET (8:00 AM ET)

Ben Shelton (1-1) faces Rafael Jodar (0-2) in the afternoon session. The situation for Shelton is clean: win, and he is in the semifinals. The situation for Jodar is different — eliminated after losing both his group matches, he is competing for professional pride and the experience of finishing his debut year-end appearance on a positive note.

Shelton's record in the group so far reflects his quality and his challenges. He beat Fils in a three-setter on Day 1 to earn his first ATP Finals win, then lost to Zverev on Day 3. The Zverev loss was expected given Zverev's 7-0 all-time lead going in, but it meant Shelton had to reach the final day at 1-1 rather than wrapped up. Wednesday's work now comes down to this afternoon's match.

Jodar, despite his 0-2 record, is not someone to dismiss entirely. He is the first player to qualify for the ATP Finals in the debut year of his tour career. He pushed Zverev in his first match and fell ill during his loss to Fils, which added context to that result. He will want to close his week with a win.

This is the first meeting between Shelton and Jodar at the ATP level.

Zverev vs Fils — Not Before 8:30 PM CET (2:30 PM ET)

The evening match is Arthur Fils (1-1) versus Alexander Zverev (2-0). For Zverev, this is a final group-stage match to close out a perfect record. For Fils, the mathematics are more complicated.

If Shelton wins his afternoon match, Fils must beat Zverev to have any chance of drawing level on record and forcing a tiebreak. If Shelton loses, Fils can advance even by losing to Zverev in three sets, depending on how the sets and games figures align. The clearest path for Fils is to win outright regardless of Shelton's result.

Fils has been one of the more compelling stories of this group stage. His comeback win over Jodar on Day 3 — losing the first set before winning the next two 6-2 — showed composure and fight that many Frenchmen at the ATP Finals have not been able to demonstrate. He now has a chance, however dependent on circumstances, to reach the semifinals of the year's last major event.

What's Next

The McEnroe Group's final standings will be known by Thursday night, confirming who joins Zverev in the semifinals. Zverev will play the Lendl Group runner-up — to be determined by Friday's Medvedev-Tiafoe match. Carlos Alcaraz, the Lendl Group winner, will face the McEnroe Group runner-up on Saturday. The ATP Finals final is scheduled for Sunday, November 22.
