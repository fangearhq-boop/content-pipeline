### Long-Form Post Elena Rybakina ends 2026 at the top of the WTA rankings, cementing a historic year with a distinction no player representing Kazakhstan has held before. She is the 17th player in WTA history to close a season ranked No. 1 in the world. Rybakina clinched the position on October 29 at the Pan Pacific Open in Tokyo, finishing the year with 8,099 ranking points. She receives the Chris Evert WTA No. 1 Trophy. Mirra Andreeva, who won the 2026 WTA Finals in Indian Wells without dropping a set, closes the season at No. 2 with 7,806 points. Iga Swiatek, who held the year-end No. 1 position in 2022, 2023, and 2024, finishes 2026 at No. 9. It is the kind of season that reshapes how people talk about the sport. Rybakina is at the top of the game. Who do you think makes the biggest leap in the WTA rankings in 2027?
