### STORY 2: Tiafoe Beats Cobolli 7-6(6), 6-0 — First ATP Finals Win

**Long-Form Post (10:30 AM ET)**

Frances Tiafoe has been to the ATP Finals before. He never won a match there — until Wednesday.

He beat Flavio Cobolli 7-6(6), 6-0 in Turin, and the story of the match was written in the first-set tiebreak. Cobolli led 6-4, holding two set points. Tiafoe saved them both, then won the tiebreak 8-6. After that, the second set was one-sided: 6-0, with Tiafoe winning every game.

It was a tough night for Cobolli. He was playing in his home country, in front of a Turin crowd that wanted him to do well. He finishes the group stage 0-2, without a win, having led in the tiebreak of his second match and still come up short.

Tiafoe is now 1-1. He plays Daniil Medvedev on Friday for a place in the semifinals.

### STORY 5: Rybakina Year-End WTA No. 1

**Long-Form Post (9:00 PM ET)**

Elena Rybakina ends 2026 at the top of the WTA rankings, cementing a historic year with a distinction no player representing Kazakhstan has held before.

She is the 17th player in WTA history to close a season ranked No. 1 in the world. Rybakina clinched the position on October 29 at the Pan Pacific Open in Tokyo, finishing the year with 8,099 ranking points. She receives the Chris Evert WTA No. 1 Trophy.

Mirra Andreeva, who won the 2026 WTA Finals in Indian Wells without dropping a set, closes the season at No. 2 with 7,806 points. Iga Swiatek, who held the year-end No. 1 position in 2022, 2023, and 2024, finishes 2026 at No. 9.

It is the kind of season that reshapes how people talk about the sport. Rybakina is at the top of the game.
