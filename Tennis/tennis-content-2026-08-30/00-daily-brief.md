### STORY 1: Buse Wins Winston-Salem — Historic Peruvian Champion Heads to US Open
**Tier:** 1 — Breaking/Trending
**Platforms:** X, Facebook, Article
**Byline:** Marcus Cole

Ignacio Buse (PER, World No. 36) defeated Arthur Fery (GBR) 6-3, 6-2 in the 2026 Winston-Salem Open final on Saturday, Aug 29. The win gives Buse his second ATP Tour title of 2026 (first was Hamburg ATP 500, clay, May 2026) and his first on hard courts. At 22 years and 5 months, Buse is the youngest player ever to win the Winston-Salem title, surpassing Hubert Hurkacz who was one month older when he won in 2019. The victory also makes him the first Peruvian player to win an ATP Tour final since 2004. He heads directly to the 2026 US Open, which begins today.
