No Norrie, No Problem: Great Britain Qualify for Davis Cup Final 8

By Elena Voss | September 20, 2026

Davis Cup | Great Britain | Ecuador

Great Britain have secured their place at the Davis Cup Finals in Bologna — and they did it without their top-ranked singles player.

Cameron Norrie withdrew from the tie through injury ahead of the weekend, forcing GB captain to reshuffle. Arthur Fery and Toby Samuel stepped into the breach at the Copper Box Arena in London, winning their opening-day rubbers to put Great Britain 2-0 ahead against Ecuador. The squad completed the job on Day 2 to book a Final 8 spot.

It was the kind of squad depth that Davis Cup ties sometimes demand — and the British team delivered when it mattered.

Fery and Samuel Step Up

On Day 1, Toby Samuel opened the tie against Andres Andrade and battled to a 4-6, 6-4, 6-1 win after losing the first set. The turnaround from the second set onward suggested a team that was settled and focused despite the absence of their captain's pick at No. 1.

Arthur Fery was more emphatic. The British No. 1 in Norrie's absence, Fery dominated Alvaro Guillen Meza 7-5, 6-0 in a clinical display that left Ecuador needing to win three of the remaining rubbers — a near-impossible ask.

Leading 2-0, Great Britain needed just one more rubber from the remaining three to qualify. The team closed out the tie on Day 2 to complete the job.

Depth Rewarded

Norrie's absence is not insignificant — the left-hander has been one of GB's most reliable performers in Davis Cup for several years. But Fery, 22, has been growing into his role as a big-match player, and Sunday's performance against Ecuador confirmed he can lead this team when required.

Harry Wendelken, brought in as a replacement for Norrie, was also part of the squad and available to contribute. The British team's ability to prepare alternatives speaks to the depth LTA performance programmes have built in recent years.

Bologna Awaits

Great Britain joins a strong field for the Davis Cup Finals in Bologna (November 24-29), alongside confirmed qualifiers Canada, Czechia, South Korea, and Austria. The Final 8 format will determine paths and potential matchups.

GB's Davis Cup history at the Finals has been a mixed story in recent years — reaching Bologna is significant, but winning there requires another level again. The hope is that Norrie returns to fitness in time to strengthen the squad for November.

What's Next

For the GB players, the Davis Cup chapter closes until November. Fery and the squad will return to the ATP Tour for the fall swing — the China Open, Shanghai Masters, and other events — before the Finals demand their attention again in Bologna. A draw in their favor in Bologna and a fully fit squad could make Great Britain genuine contenders for the title.
