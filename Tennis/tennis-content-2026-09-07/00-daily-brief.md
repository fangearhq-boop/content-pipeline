### STORY 1: Three Americans in US Open QF — Historic Run for U.S. Men's Tennis (TIER 1)
**Status:** FOLLOW-UP (2026-09-05 S4: Four American men in R4)
Ben Shelton, Frances Tiafoe, and Alex Michelsen all advanced to the US Open quarterfinals on Sunday, giving the U.S. three men in the QF of a major for the first time since 1995. Shelton routed Tsitsipas 6-2, 6-3, 6-4; Tiafoe outlasted Medvedev in back-to-back tiebreaks 7-6(1), 6-4, 7-6(6); Michelsen dispatched Etcheverry 7-6, 6-4, 6-4. Now: Shelton faces defending champion Alcaraz; Tiafoe meets Michelsen in an all-American QF. U.S. men haven't won a major since Roddick at the US Open in 2003.
