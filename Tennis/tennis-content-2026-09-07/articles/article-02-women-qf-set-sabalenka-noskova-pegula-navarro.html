Women's US Open QF Set: Sabalenka Chases Three-Peat, All-American Battle in Pegula vs. Navarro

By Ryan Calloway | September 7, 2026

Aryna Sabalenka swept into the quarterfinals with a win over Taylor Townsend, setting up a blockbuster clash with Wimbledon champion Linda Noskova. Meanwhile, Pegula and Navarro guarantee an American in the women's semifinal.

Four women's quarterfinal spots are confirmed at the 2026 US Open, and the matchups are compelling: Aryna Sabalenka vs. Linda Noskova, and Jessica Pegula vs. Emma Navarro. Four more spots will be decided Monday, but two storylines already dominate the conversation entering week two.

Sabalenka on the Verge of History

Aryna Sabalenka defeated Taylor Townsend 6-4, 6-3 in Sunday's fourth round, advancing to the quarterfinals without dropping a set all tournament. The two-time defending US Open champion is two wins from the final, where a victory would make her the first woman to win three consecutive US Open titles since Serena Williams did it from 2012 to 2014.

The path runs directly through Wimbledon champion Linda Noskova. Noskova, who collected her first Grand Slam title at the All England Club this summer, survived a tough three-set match against Ukraine's Marta Kostyuk — 7-5, 5-7, 6-4 — to reach the quarterfinals. The Sabalenka-Noskova matchup is the most anticipated women's match of the tournament so far: the defending champion on a historic run against the most dangerous player in the draw.

All-American QF: Pegula vs. Navarro

Jessica Pegula and Emma Navarro will meet in the quarterfinals after both won their fourth-round matches on Saturday and Sunday. Pegula, a fan favorite from Buffalo, handled Sorana Cirstea 6-3, 6-4 in a clean performance. Navarro eliminated Anna Kalinskaya 6-4, 6-2.

It is the second all-American women's quarterfinal at the US Open in three years. One of them is guaranteed to be in the women's semifinal, adding to what is shaping up as a landmark week for American tennis — with multiple Americans in both the men's and women's final eight.

What's Next

Four more women's quarterfinal spots will be decided Monday when Iga Swiatek, Coco Gauff, Elena Rybakina, and their opponents complete the round of 16. The women's quarterfinals are scheduled for Wednesday, September 9, and Thursday, September 10.

On the line for Sabalenka: a place in tennis history as one of the few players — man or woman — to win the same Grand Slam three consecutive times. On the line for Pegula or Navarro: a shot at the first American women's US Open finalist since Serena Williams.
