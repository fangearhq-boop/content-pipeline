US Open Day 9: Swiatek-Zheng Rematch, Gauff-Jovic, Rybakina-Osaka Headline Monday's Blockbuster Schedule

By Elena Voss | September 7, 2026

Monday's order of play completes the women's R16 and sets the full quarterfinal picture. Three marquee women's matches and a pair of compelling men's fourth-round contests make this one of the best days of the tournament.

Day 9 at the US Open is a full schedule of fourth-round tennis, completing the lineup of quarterfinalists. When the day is over, all eight women's and all eight men's quarterfinalists will be known. Here is what to watch.

Swiatek vs. Zheng Qinwen — 11:30 AM ET, Arthur Ashe Stadium

The marquee afternoon match is a rematch that tennis fans have been waiting for. In the Paris 2024 Olympics, Zheng Qinwen upset Iga Swiatek in the semifinals on her way to gold. Two years later, they meet again on a hard court in New York, with a US Open quarterfinal spot on the line.

Swiatek is the world's top women's player and a five-time Grand Slam champion. Zheng came to Flushing Meadows with a point to prove. This match has the ingredients of a classic: two elite baseliners, a rivalry with Olympic history, and the high-stakes US Open stage.

Rybakina vs. Osaka — Not Before 2:30 PM ET, Louis Armstrong Stadium

Elena Rybakina entered this US Open with significant questions about her ankle, having not practiced fully since Cincinnati. She has answered every one of those questions over the past eight days, reaching the fourth round without a retirement. Now she faces Naomi Osaka, who has had one of the most compelling comeback seasons in recent memory.

Osaka reached the fourth round by beating players who tested her from every angle. Rybakina is a different challenge — a heavy, flat-hitting baseliner who tends to neutralize Osaka's power. This is the most physically demanding matchup of the women's round of 16.

Gauff vs. Jovic — Not Before 7:00 PM ET, Arthur Ashe Stadium

The night session features an all-American clash with significant stakes. Coco Gauff, the No. 4 seed, faces Iva Jovic, who defeated Alexandra Eala in a three-set thriller in the third round at this same stadium Saturday night. Jovic has been one of the best stories of the tournament — rolling through the draw as the No. 14 seed with growing confidence in every round.

Gauff is the heavy favorite, but Jovic is playing the best tennis of her career and has the crowd's attention. An Arthur Ashe night session atmosphere that favors an upset-minded American challenger is the kind of stage Jovic thrives on.

Men's Fourth Round

On the men's side, No. 1 seed Alexander Zverev faces 21st seed Luciano Darderi in the Arthur Ashe evening session. Zverev has spent significant time on court in his first three matches and will be tested by Darderi's clay-court-style game on the hard courts of New York.

Also Monday: 20-year-old Learner Tien — the youngest American man in the US Open round of 16 since Andy Roddick in 2002 — faces 2022 US Open semifinalist Karen Khachanov. A Tien win would add a fourth American to the men's quarterfinals alongside Ben Shelton, Frances Tiafoe, and Alex Michelsen.

What's Next

Monday's matches are on ESPN and Tennis Channel. Quarterfinals begin Tuesday for the men and Wednesday for the women. By Monday night, every quarterfinal matchup will be confirmed and the second week storylines will be set.
