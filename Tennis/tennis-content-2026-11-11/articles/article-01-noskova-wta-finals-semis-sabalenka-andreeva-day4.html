Noskova First Into WTA Finals Semis; Sabalenka and Andreeva Meet in Wednesday's Decisive Clash

By Elena Voss | November 11, 2026 | Tennis Fanrecap

Linda Noskova arrived at the WTA Finals as the No. 8 seed, the youngest qualifier in the field, and an acknowledged longshot against three players ranked ahead of her in Group 1. Through three days in Indian Wells, she has played as if none of that matters.

The 21-year-old Czech beat Coco Gauff 6-3, 6-3 on Day 3 to become the first confirmed semifinalist of the 2026 WTA Finals. It was her second straight-sets win of the round robin. She has not dropped a set. In her WTA Finals debut.

How Noskova Clinched It

The match against Gauff was controlled from the first game. Noskova disrupted Gauff's rhythm early with her flat, pace-heavy groundstrokes, winning the opening four games to build a lead she never gave back. The second set followed the same pattern — a quick break, clean hold after clean hold, no second-serve hesitation, no visible nerves.

Gauff, the defending Wimbledon champion and one of the favorites entering the week, could not find a foothold. This is now two losses to Noskova in the round robin after a first-round defeat to Noskova as well, in a tournament where Gauff's season has promised more than it has delivered.

Noskova is here as a Grand Slam champion — she won Wimbledon in July — but she is playing like someone who has nothing to prove and everything to gain. That combination is dangerous.

Group 1 Picture: Pegula and Gauff Fight Thursday

Jessica Pegula kept her semifinal hopes alive the same day, defeating Marta Kostyuk 6-2, 7-5 in a steady performance that eliminated the Ukrainian from contention. Kostyuk ends her WTA Finals debut at 0-2.

That sets up the final Group 1 match on Thursday: Pegula vs. Gauff, both at 1-1, with the second semifinal spot on the line. These two have met regularly throughout the season; they know each other's games well. Whoever wins joins Noskova in the last four.

Wednesday's Main Event: Sabalenka vs. Andreeva

Group 2 has produced some of the best tennis of the tournament so far, and Wednesday's featured match — Aryna Sabalenka against Mirra Andreeva — has the look of a semifinal before the semifinals have even started.

Both players are 1-0 after commanding opening wins. Sabalenka beat Karolina Muchova 7-6(2), 7-5 in a tight match that required her to solve a tiebreak Muchova had not lost all season. Andreeva dismissed Elina Svitolina 6-4, 6-0, winning the final seven games of the match.

This is the Group 2 decider in all but name. Whoever wins today clinches a semifinal spot. The loser will need a win on Thursday to stay alive — and their fate may then depend on what happens in the other Group 2 match.

Sabalenka leads the head-to-head 4-2, but Andreeva, in her first WTA Finals, has shown no intimidation so far. For the No. 2 seed, this is an opportunity to put the bracket on her terms. For the No. 3 seed, it is a chance to announce herself on the biggest stage of her career.

What's Next

Wednesday's Day 4 schedule also includes Svitolina vs. Muchova in Group 2, which will help determine Thursday's stakes. The WTA Finals runs through November 15, with the semifinal and final rounds concluding the week in Indian Wells.

With Noskova already through, the remaining three semifinal spots will be settled by the end of Thursday's play. The tournament is halfway done, and the picture is becoming clearer — Noskova is the first name on the semifinal sheet, and the rest are still chasing.
