ATP Finals Draw Day Tomorrow: Sinner Out, Eight Players Set for Turin Bracket on Nov 12

By Marcus Cole | November 11, 2026 | Tennis Fanrecap

The 2026 Nitto ATP Finals draw ceremony takes place Thursday, November 12, at 12:00 PM. Twenty-four hours from now, the bracket for the season-ending event in Turin will be set, and the storylines that have been building all autumn will finally have a structure around them.

The tournament runs November 15-22 at the Inalpi Arena in Turin. Eight players. Two round-robin groups of four. The top two from each group advance to the semifinals. For the winner, it means ending one of the most competitive men's seasons in recent memory on the highest possible note.

The Field

The eight qualified players for the 2026 Nitto ATP Finals are Alexander Zverev, Carlos Alcaraz, Ben Shelton, Daniil Medvedev, Flavio Cobolli, Arthur Fils, Frances Tiafoe, and Rafael Jodar.

Zverev arrives as the year-end No. 1 — the first time in his career he has held that honor — after winning Roland Garros and the US Open in 2026. Alcaraz arrives on a 10-match winning streak following back-to-back Masters 1000 titles in Vienna and Paris. Shelton, who lost the Paris Masters final to Alcaraz just three days ago, is the highest-energy player in the draw. Medvedev, a former No. 1 who has won at this event before, brings consistent hard-court excellence.

Cobolli, Fils, Tiafoe, and Jodar round out the field. Tiafoe is making his first ATP Finals appearance. Jodar is making his, too — and in his debut year on the ATP Tour, which is a first in the event's history.

The Absent Presence: Jannik Sinner

No conversation about the 2026 ATP Finals field can ignore who is not in it. Jannik Sinner, the defending year-end No. 1 and the winner of this event last year, will not return to the ATP Tour before 2027. A knee injury ended his season prematurely, and with it, the possibility of defending the title he won in Turin twelve months ago.

Sinner's absence removes one of the most compelling rivalries from the draw. His matches against Alcaraz and Zverev have been among the defining contests of the past two years. Without him, Turin will be a different kind of tournament — but not a lesser one. The remaining eight players are more than capable of filling the gap.

What to Watch in the Draw

The key question for Thursday is groupings. The draw will determine whether Alcaraz and Zverev meet in the round robin or the final, and whether the debutants get a smoother path or a brutal one.

No one in this field is a soft draw. Cobolli and Fils both had strong autumn swings. Jodar qualified on merit, not reputation. Medvedev at this specific event has a track record that says ignore him at your peril. Any group could produce three players with legitimate semifinal ambitions.

What's Next

The draw ceremony is Thursday at 12:00 PM. First-round matches begin at the Inalpi Arena on Saturday, November 15. The final is Sunday, November 22.

Whatever the bracket brings, the 2026 ATP Finals will close one of the most eventful seasons in men's tennis in years — with a year-end No. 1 defending his ranking in a tournament, a form player seeking a first Finals title, and a generation of newer names ready to announce themselves on the season's biggest indoor stage.
