WTA Toronto Round 3: Sabalenka Faces Zhang Shuai, Swiatek Eyes Quarterfinal Berth

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | August 6, 2026

The US Open begins in 18 days. At the National Bank Open in Toronto, the two women most likely to hold the trophy in New York are in Round 3 action on Thursday — and the results will matter far beyond Canada.

Aryna Sabalenka, the world No. 1 and the tournament's top seed, faces Shuai Zhang in what is her third match of the week. Iga Swiatek, who opened with a dominant 6-0, 6-3 win over Sara Bejlek, also takes the court Thursday as the tournament enters its most significant round before the quarterfinals.

Sabalenka's Hard-Court Streak

Since the summer swing began, Sabalenka has been close to unbeatable on hard courts. She has now won 13 of her last 14 hard-court matches in 2026, and her opening win in Toronto — a 6-3, 6-3 dismantling of Moyuka Uchijima — was exactly the kind of controlled, dominant performance a champion plays when everything is working.

Zhang Shuai, her Round 3 opponent, advanced when Jelena Ostapenko retired from their match trailing 6-0, 4-0. Sabalenka leads the head-to-head 4-1 and enters Thursday as a heavy favorite. But Zhang has the experience to compete at this level, and any opponent who takes Sabalenka out of her rhythm can make a match of it.

Swiatek Building Toward New York

Iga Swiatek's first match in Toronto was convincing. She gave Bejlek nothing in the opening set — a 6-0 bagel — and backed it up with a 6-3 second set to advance comfortably. The former world No. 1 is in the kind of form that suggests she is using Toronto as a tune-up rather than a destination in itself.

Swiatek is targeting a deep run before the US Open. The Flushing Meadows hardcourt suits her power game, and the Canadian summer has historically been good preparation for her. Thursday will tell us whether she is ready to challenge the top seeds or still finding her best form.

The Bigger Picture

The WTA Toronto field has seen several results that have thinned the draw already, and the path to the final for both Sabalenka and Swiatek is clearer than it might have been expected at the tournament's start.

If both players win Thursday and continue through the bracket, a potential Sabalenka-Swiatek semifinal would be the biggest match in women's tennis before the US Open — and an early preview of what could be the defining clash of the 2026 Grand Slam season's final chapter.

What's Next

Thursday's Round 3 results will determine the Toronto quarterfinal lineup. The quarterfinals are scheduled for Friday, August 7, followed by the semifinals on Saturday and the final on Sunday, August 9. Both Sabalenka and Swiatek will then turn their attention to the Cincinnati Open (August 13-23) and the US Open (August 24).
