### STORY 1: Griekspoor Upsets No. 1 Zverev in Montreal

**Tier:** 1
**Type:** FOLLOW-UP
**News Hook:** No. 1 seed Alexander Zverev eliminated in Montreal opener by Tallon Griekspoor 6-7(3), 6-2, 6-4. First NBO upset of a top seed in the first match since Kyrgios-Medvedev 2022.
**Key Narrative:** Griekspoor, who entered the match having lost his last 4 meetings with Zverev and trailing 3-9 in career H2H, put on a dominant display — 20 forehand winners, 17/18 net points — to knock out the Roland Garros champion and tournament top seed.
**Why This Matters:** Zverev was Montreal's biggest draw. His early exit blows open the top half of the draw and gives outsiders like Griekspoor a genuine path to the semifinals.
**Audience Angle:** Upset shock factor; tournament drama; Griekspoor career moment; draw implications.
**Content Direction:** Lead with the scoreline and the historical context (first to do this since 2022). Build out the net dominance and forehand stats. Close with what's next (Griekspoor into R3, open draw).
