Fils Destroys Tirante as Cobolli Beats Paul With Help From M&Ms — French-Italian SF Looms

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | August 21, 2026

One quarterfinal was clinical. The other required a snack. Both produced results. Arthur Fils and Flavio Cobolli are into the Cincinnati Open ATP semifinals — and they'll face each other on Saturday in a matchup of two rising European stars.

Fils: 64 Minutes of Controlled Domination

Arthur Fils made Thiago Agustin Tirante look ordinary. The French No. 21 seed won 6-3, 6-2 in exactly 64 minutes — a performance so efficient it bordered on clinical. Fils didn't panic, didn't give Tirante any foothold, and left the court looking every inch a semifinalist who believes he can win the whole thing.

It is Fils' third Masters 1000 semifinal of 2026, a year in which he has announced himself as one of the most consistent performers on tour. His baseline game — flat, penetrating, physically oppressive — has proven adaptable across surfaces, and on the hard courts of Mason, Ohio, he looked as dangerous as he has all season.

Tirante had been one of the stories of this Cincinnati week, having upset Novak Djokovic in the round of 16. But Fils gave him no opportunity for a second consecutive upset, closing out the match with the kind of assurance that makes him a credible title threat.

Cobolli Rallies, M&Ms and All

Flavio Cobolli's quarterfinal against Tommy Paul was a different kind of story. Paul won the first set 6-2 and looked in control. Cobolli looked to be in trouble. Then came the changeover, and a bag of M&Ms.

"I love M&Ms," Cobolli reportedly told his team, reaching into his bag for the chocolate candy as Paul was preparing to serve out a dominant win. What followed was one of the more improbable turnarounds of this Cincinnati week. Cobolli won the second set 6-3 and the third 6-4, advancing to his first career Masters 1000 semifinal.

Whether the M&Ms were the cause or merely a coincidence, Cobolli's energy visibly shifted after that mid-match break. The 24-year-old Italian — a clay-court specialist who has found a second gear on hard courts this summer — showed the composure of a player ready to compete at the very top level. Paul, who beat No. 1 seed Zverev in the round of 16, ran out of steam as the match progressed.

Saturday's ATP Semifinal

The Fils vs. Cobolli semifinal pits the French No. 21 seed against the Italian who entered this tournament unseeded. Both are in their mid-20s, both are among the most physically impressive ball-strikers on tour, and both have demonstrated this week that they belong at this level. Their head-to-head record and stylistic matchup make Saturday's clash genuinely difficult to predict.

The other ATP semifinal will emerge from the Fritz vs. Nakashima and Tiafoe vs. Musetti quarterfinals being played Friday. Winners from those two matches will face each other in the second semifinal slot.

What's Next

The ATP Cincinnati semifinals are scheduled for Saturday, August 22. Fils vs. Cobolli is confirmed. The second semifinal will be determined by Friday's remaining quarterfinal results between Fritz/Nakashima and Tiafoe/Musetti. The Cincinnati final is Sunday, August 23.
