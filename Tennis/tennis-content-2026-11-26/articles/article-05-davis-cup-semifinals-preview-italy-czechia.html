Davis Cup Semifinals Set: Italy Face Czech Republic Friday, Saturday Tie TBD

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | November 26, 2026

The 2026 Davis Cup Finals are down to the final four, and the semifinals are beginning to take shape. After Thursday's quarterfinal results — including Italy's dramatic 2-1 win over South Korea and Czech Republic's clinical 2-0 dismissal of Canada — one semifinal is already confirmed: Italy vs Czech Republic on Friday in Bologna.

The second semifinal, also in Bologna, will be decided by Thursday afternoon's remaining results. The winner of Great Britain vs Germany faces the winner of Spain vs Austria on Saturday, Nov. 28, with the final on Sunday, Nov. 29.

Italy vs Czech Republic — Friday

Italy come in as the defending champions — having won in 2023, 2024, and 2025 in the modern Finals format — and despite the absence of Jannik Sinner, they remain the team to beat. Their quarterfinal win over South Korea was hard-fought, requiring a doubles comeback in a tiebreak that stretched to 11-9 in the second set, but they got through.

Czech Republic, meanwhile, impressed with a 2-0 shutout of a depleted Canada. Jiří Lehečká, now ranked No. 14 in the world, was composed and efficient, and Tomáš Macháč added another singles win to give the Czechs a perfect quarterfinal record. The Czechs are a genuinely dangerous team and will not be overawed on this stage.

The key matchup will be whether Italy's Musetti and Berrettini can handle Lehečká and Macháč in singles. If the tie goes to doubles, Italy's Vavassori partnership is a genuine weapon. Czech Republic will likely counter with Lehečká, who has shown an ability to lift his game in big moments.

Saturday's Semifinal — TBD

The other side of the bracket is still being decided. Great Britain lead Germany 1-0 after Norrie's win over Struff, with Fery vs Zverev in progress at time of writing. Spain face Austria in the afternoon session, potentially without Alcaraz in singles.

All four teams — Great Britain, Germany, Spain and Austria — are capable of making Friday's final. Great Britain haven't been to a Davis Cup Final since 2015. Germany would have Zverev leading them. Spain have Davis Cup pedigree like few nations in the world.

What's Next

Italy vs Czech Republic kicks off at 10:00 AM CET on Friday, Nov. 27, at SuperTennis Arena in Bologna. The Saturday semifinal follows at 12:00 PM CET. The final is Sunday, Nov. 29.
