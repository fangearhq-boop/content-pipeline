Italy Survive South Korea Upset Bid, Advance to Davis Cup Semifinals on Doubles Heroics

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | November 26, 2026

Italy's bid for a fourth consecutive Davis Cup title lives on, though it took a remarkable doubles performance to get them through. The Italians defeated South Korea 2-1 on Wednesday at the SuperTennis Arena in Bologna, with Matteo Berrettini and Andrea Vavassori winning an extraordinary deciding doubles rubber to seal the quarterfinal victory.

Lorenzo Musetti set Italy on their way with a dominant opening performance, crushing Seongchan Hong 6-0, 7-6(5) in Rubber 1. The first set was an exhibition of controlled aggression from the Italian No. 2, and while Hong showed more fight in the second set tiebreak, Musetti closed it out at 7-5 to give Italy a 1-0 lead in the tie.

South Korea then leveled through Soonwoo Kwon, who beat Flavio Cobolli in Rubber 2 to set up a winner-takes-all doubles. It was the kind of resilience that made South Korea's debut at the Davis Cup Finals so compelling — the first time the country had ever reached the Final 8.

Doubles Thriller

With the tie on the line, the pressure fell on Berrettini and Vavassori, and they delivered. The Italian pair took the first set in a tiebreak, winning it 7-6(2). The second set went even deeper — all the way to a 7-6 tiebreak that stretched to 11-9 before Italy finally closed it out.

It was the kind of dramatic conclusion that Davis Cup is built for. Berrettini, a former Wimbledon finalist who has reinvented himself as one of tennis's most reliable doubles players at this level, combined brilliantly with Vavassori to hold Italy's title defense together at its most precarious moment.

Italy advance to face Czech Republic in Friday's semifinal. The Czechs eliminated a depleted Canada 2-0 in their quarterfinal, with Jiří Lehečká and Tomáš Macháč both winning in straight sets.

Italy Without Sinner

The result is all the more significant given who is not in the Italian squad. Jannik Sinner, who won his fifth Grand Slam at Wimbledon this summer, has been absent since August with a knee injury, compounded by tonsil surgery in November. His recovery timeline puts his Australian Open return at risk, and his presence has been sorely missed throughout the year-end season.

Yet Italy continue to find ways to win. Without their best player, they have relied on collective effort, with Berrettini and Musetti carrying the singles load and the doubles pair providing a crucial backstop. It is a testament to the depth of a squad that has now won three consecutive titles in the modern Finals format.

What's Next

Italy face Czech Republic in Friday's Davis Cup semifinal in Bologna. The match is scheduled to start at 10:00 AM CET. For Italy, a win would put them in Sunday's final and within reach of a historic fourth consecutive title. The Czechs, meanwhile, will fancy their chances against an Italy side that has needed to dig deep to get this far. This one has all the makings of a classic.
