### STORY 5: Cincinnati Preview: Alcaraz Returns, Sinner Targets Historic Sweep

#### Long-Form Post — 3:00 PM ET

Cincinnati is eight days away — and it might be the most fascinating Masters 1000 of the year.

Jannik Sinner arrives having won every Masters 1000 he entered in 2026. Five straight. A sixth in Cincinnati would be unprecedented in the Open Era for a single season. He also defended his Wimbledon title. The Italian is simply playing at a different level right now.

But Cincinnati is not a formality. Carlos Alcaraz — the defending champion — will make his first appearance since shutting his season down in April with a right wrist injury. Five months off, building up, motivated. When Alcaraz is healthy and hungry, he is the one player who can match Sinner rally for rally.

Then there is Alexander Zverev, who won his first Grand Slam at Roland Garros and has been in the best form of his career. He is world No. 2 and for the first time the race for the top ranking feels genuinely competitive.

On the women's side, Sabalenka, Rybakina, Swiatek, Gauff, and a dozen other stars make the WTA draw one of the strongest of the year outside the Grand Slams.

The US Open starts August 24. Cincinnati is the final chapter before that. Who do you think comes through?
