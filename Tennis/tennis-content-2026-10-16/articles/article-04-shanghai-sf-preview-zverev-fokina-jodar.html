Shanghai Masters Semifinals Set: Zverev Faces Fokina, Jodar Into Final Four

By Ryan Calloway

October 16, 2026 — Shanghai, China

The Shanghai Masters is entering its final weekend, and the semifinal lineup is coming into full view. The tournament that began with 64 players is down to four, and the matchups on Saturday will decide who contests the title on Sunday.

Alexander Zverev — the year-end World No. 1 and defending champion — will face Alejandro Davidovich Fokina in the first semifinal. In the other half of the draw, Rafael Jodar has confirmed his place in the semifinals after defeating Casper Ruud on Friday.

Zverev vs. Davidovich Fokina

The first Shanghai semifinal brings together the tournament's top seed — Zverev — and the player who has benefited most from the week's unexpected results. Davidovich Fokina advanced on Thursday after Arthur Gea, the young Frenchman who had captivated the Shanghai crowd with wins over Shelton and Mensik, retired after 22 minutes with a right leg injury.

That path to the final four was fortunate, but Fokina is not simply a passenger. The Spaniard has reached four career Masters 1000 semifinals and knows how to compete at this level. His aggressive, unpredictable game can trouble any opponent, including the world No. 1.

Zverev, meanwhile, has been the picture of dominance throughout the tournament. He has not dropped a set. He dismantled Alex de Minaur in under 80 minutes in the quarterfinals, and the ankle concerns that followed him into the week have appeared to diminish with each passing round. If he plays the tennis he is capable of, he is a very short favorite to reach the final.

Jodar: Cinderella in the Final Four

Rafael Jodar's presence in the semifinals is one of the more improbable things to happen at a Masters 1000 this season. The 12th seed arrived with a game suited to the surface and a willingness to fight from behind — and he has exercised both qualities in every match this week.

After defeating van de Zandschulp, Lehecka, and now Ruud — all from a set down — Jodar faces the winner of the Medvedev vs. Paul quarterfinal, which was also played Friday. Medvedev, the fifth seed, is a former Shanghai champion and the slight favorite. Paul has been exceptional all season at hard-court Masters events.

Either opponent is a significant step up from Ruud. But Jodar has not flinched from bigger challenges before.

The Road to the Title

A Zverev title in Shanghai would be a statement conclusion to his extraordinary 2026 season — year-end World No. 1, French Open champion, US Open champion, and now potentially a Shanghai title. He would enter Saturday as the prohibitive favorite.

But Jodar and his potential opponent — Medvedev or Paul — are not simply names making up the numbers. Jodar's run has already defied expectations. Medvedev knows how to win in Shanghai. Paul has been one of the form players in North American and Asian hard-court tennis this fall.

The semifinals and final are the reason tournaments run ten days. Saturday and Sunday are where reputations are made.

What's Next

Alexander Zverev faces Alejandro Davidovich Fokina in the first Shanghai Masters semifinal on Saturday, October 17. Rafael Jodar faces the winner of Medvedev vs. Paul in the second semifinal, also Saturday. The Shanghai Masters final is Sunday, October 18, at the Qizhong Tennis Center.
