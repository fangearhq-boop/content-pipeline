Ostapenko Overpowers Shnaider to Reach Wuhan Open Semifinals

By Elena Voss

October 16, 2026 — Wuhan, China

Jelena Ostapenko had to work for her place in the Wuhan Open semifinals, but she got there. The Latvian defeated Diana Shnaider 7-6(6), 6-1 in a quarterfinal that required a tight first set before Ostapenko took full control.

The match lasted 105 minutes, and the contrast between the two sets told the story of how the match unfolded. Ostapenko was tested in the first, pushed to the limit of a tiebreak before converting at 7-6. In the second, there was no such drama — she was precise, aggressive, and dominant in a 6-1 statement.

A Testing First Set

Shnaider, a young Russian who has been one of the more intriguing players on the WTA Tour in the past two seasons, made Ostapenko work for every point in the opening set. The tiebreak went to 7-6 before the Latvian found the decisive moment.

Ostapenko's game is built around aggression — heavy groundstrokes hit with conviction and pace that can make sustained rallies a painful experience for opponents. Shnaider is capable of matching that approach, and in the first set she did so well enough to force a tiebreak.

But Ostapenko's experience in close situations — she is a former Roland Garros champion who has been in tight matches at the biggest events — proved the difference at the sharp end of the first set.

Dominance in the Second

The second set told a different story. Ostapenko found a higher level — sharper on her backhand, more penetrating on her serve — and Shnaider had no answer. A 6-1 second set closed out the match and sent Ostapenko into the semifinals without a great deal of energy expended.

That efficiency matters at this stage of a tournament. Ostapenko has played deep into the draw all week, and her matches have tested her in different ways. The ability to shift into a dominant gear when the moment called for it, as she did in the second set against Shnaider, suggests she is in the right physical and mental shape for what comes next.

The Semifinal: Ostapenko vs. Kartal

Ostapenko's semifinal opponent is Sonay Kartal, the British qualifier who earlier Friday defeated Elina Svitolina in one of the biggest upsets of the Wuhan draw. Kartal arrived at the tournament ranked No. 170 in the world and has beaten players far above her ranking in consecutive rounds.

The matchup is a fascinating contrast of styles. Ostapenko is an established player with a powerful, risk-heavy game. Kartal is a rising player who has shown composure and precision in moments where many others would have crumbled. On paper, Ostapenko is the favorite. But Kartal has made a habit of ignoring what is on paper this week.

The Wuhan semifinal will be one of the more compelling stories of the fall swing regardless of the result.

What's Next

Jelena Ostapenko faces Sonay Kartal in the Wuhan Open semifinals. The Wuhan final is Sunday, October 18. Ostapenko will look to advance with the same attacking game that has carried her through the draw — and perhaps a touch more precision in the opener than she showed in the first set against Shnaider.
