Nakashima Stuns Fritz to Set Up All-American Cincinnati Semifinal

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | August 22, 2026

Brandon Nakashima put together one of the more remarkable upsets of the Cincinnati Open on Friday, beating No. 6 seed Taylor Fritz 7-6(9), 4-6, 6-3 to advance to his second straight Masters 1000 semifinal — and setting the stage for the first all-American men's Cincinnati SF since 2010.

Fritz held a 4-1 head-to-head advantage heading into the match. By the end, Nakashima had flipped the script and earned a semifinal berth against Frances Tiafoe.

A Tiebreak That Changed Everything

The first set was as tightly contested as any tennis produces. Fritz was playing at a high level — 14 aces, and just three points lost behind his first serve across the set. By those metrics, he should have won it comfortably. Instead, the tiebreak ran all the way to 9 points on Nakashima's side.

The decisive moment came at 4-4 in the tiebreak, when a 32-shot rally — an extraordinary exchange even by the standards of hard-court Masters tennis — ended in Nakashima's favor. He won six of the next seven points, taking the tiebreak 11-9 and, with it, whatever psychological grip Fritz had built through the first set.

Fritz leveled by taking the second set convincingly, but the third told a different story. He broke early to open it, and for a moment it looked like his superior consistency might carry the day. But Nakashima broke back at 3-3, broke again for 5-3, and served out the match.

Nakashima's Summer in Numbers

The win pushed Nakashima's hard-court win total on the season to 22 — the most on the ATP Tour. He now has back-to-back Masters 1000 semifinals for the first time in his career, following his runner-up finish in Montreal earlier this month.

He is ranked No. 27 in the world, but his actual form over the past several weeks has placed him among the best players on the hard-court swing. His career-high ranking of No. 22, set after Montreal, may well improve further depending on how Cincinnati concludes.

An All-American Semifinal in Cincinnati

On Saturday, Nakashima meets Frances Tiafoe, who also won his Friday quarterfinal — defeating Lorenzo Musetti 7-6(2), 7-5 in a match where the Cincinnati crowd played a defining role. It will be the first all-American men's Cincinnati semifinal since Andy Roddick and Mardy Fish met in 2010.

Earlier in the week, four Americans reached the quarterfinals simultaneously — the first time that had happened since 1995. Now two of them are a win away from the final, with the other semifinal matching Italy's Flavio Cobolli against France's Arthur Fils.

For American tennis, this is a summer that keeps getting better. Tiafoe and Nakashima are both heading into the US Open in a week's time with enormous momentum behind them. The draw ceremony is August 27. Whatever happens in Saturday's semifinal, both have earned their place as serious contenders for Flushing Meadows.

What's Next

Nakashima faces Frances Tiafoe in the Cincinnati Open men's semifinal on Saturday, August 22. The winner advances to the final, scheduled for Sunday, August 23.
