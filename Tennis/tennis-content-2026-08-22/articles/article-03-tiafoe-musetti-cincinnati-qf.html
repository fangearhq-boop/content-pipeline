Tiafoe Beats Musetti to Reach His First Masters 1000 Semifinal in Two Years

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | August 22, 2026

Frances Tiafoe has always known how to feed off a Cincinnati crowd. On Friday night, with his first Masters 1000 semifinal in two years on the line, he did it again — beating Lorenzo Musetti 7-6(2), 7-5 to advance to Saturday's all-American showdown with Brandon Nakashima.

His previous appearance in a Masters 1000 semifinal was also at Cincinnati, in 2024. That piece of history feels fitting. The home-crowd energy in Mason, Ohio seems to bring out something extra in Tiafoe that harder venues cannot replicate.

Tiebreak Dominance, Second-Set Drama

Musetti held the early upper hand in the first set, leading 3-2 before Tiafoe steadied. The set reached a tiebreak, and there Tiafoe was in complete control — he won it 7-2, converting with the precision and confidence that went missing in his Montreal retirement weeks earlier.

The second set produced the match's most dramatic swing. Musetti broke back at 5-5, and the match teetered. Tiafoe broke straight back. Serving at 6-5, he held for the match amid what commentators described as one of the loudest crowd roars of the entire Cincinnati week.

The final score was 7-6(2), 7-5. Two sets, two hours of tense play, one decisive result.

The Comeback Context

The result carries extra weight given what came just weeks before it. At the Montreal Open, Tiafoe retired mid-match against Rinderknech with a hand issue and underwent subsequent surgery in New York. He came back to Cincinnati with his fitness as a question mark and has answered that question emphatically.

He is now one win away from a Masters 1000 final. His run through Cincinnati has included quality victories and, on Friday night, the kind of clutch performance under pressure that defines tournament contenders. He carries a 7-6(2), 7-5 win over a top-10 opponent into a semifinal where the crowd in Mason will once again be firmly behind him.

Facing Nakashima

Saturday's semifinal pairs Tiafoe against Nakashima — fellow Americans whose contrasting playing styles make for a genuinely unpredictable matchup. Nakashima, who stunned No. 6 seed Taylor Fritz in the other quarterfinal, is carrying his own momentum from back-to-back Masters 1000 SFs and a Tour-leading 22 hard-court wins on the season.

It is the first all-American Cincinnati semifinal since Andy Roddick and Mardy Fish in 2010. For a sport looking for its next generation of American stars, Saturday's match is exactly the kind of high-stakes showcase the US game needs, with the US Open just over a week away.

What's Next

Tiafoe faces Brandon Nakashima in the Cincinnati Open men's semifinal on Saturday, August 22. The winner advances to Sunday's final.
