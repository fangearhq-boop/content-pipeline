### STORY 1: Sinner Withdraws From 2026 US Open

**Confidence: HIGH** (multiple major outlets, ATP Tour confirmed, player statement)

- **Jannik Sinner (ITA, World No. 1)** officially withdrew from the 2026 US Open — announced August 21, 2026
- Injury: right knee tendon inflammation — no surgery required but needs more time
- Player quote: "very sad and disappointed"
- This is his **first-ever Grand Slam withdrawal** since his 2019 US Open debut
- 2026 season record: **44-3**, six titles
- Won the first five Masters 1000 events of the year (Indian Wells, Miami, Monte-Carlo, Madrid, Rome)
- Already withdrew from both Montreal and Cincinnati with same knee issue
- Next target: **Asian hard-court swing** — Beijing/Shanghai in October
- Context: Third consecutive Grand Slam where either Sinner or Alcaraz has been absent (Alcaraz missed RG and Wimbledon; Sinner now misses US Open)
- ATP Rankings as of Aug 20: Sinner No. 1 (13,450 pts), Alcaraz No. 2 (8,160 pts)

**Sources:** ATP Tour, CBS Sports, Al Jazeera, Yahoo Sports, Olympics.com (all Aug 21-22, 2026)

### STORY 2: Nakashima def. Fritz; All-American ATP SF

**Confidence: HIGH** (ATP Tour confirmed, Tennis Majors detailed match report)

- **[27] Brandon Nakashima def. [6] Taylor Fritz 7-6(9), 4-6, 6-3**
- Fritz H2H heading in: 4-1; Nakashima pulled off the upset
- First set tiebreak went to 11-9 (Nakashima); 32-shot rally at 4/4 was the turning point
- Fritz hit 14 aces and won just 3 points behind first serve in first set, still lost the tiebreak
- Third set: Fritz broke to open, Nakashima broke back at 3-3, then broke again for 5-3 and served out
- Nakashima now has **22 hard-court wins this season — Tour-leading**
- **Back-to-back Masters 1000 SFs for the first time in his career** (Montreal was first)
- ATP SFs Saturday: Tiafoe vs. Nakashima; Cobolli vs. Fils
- **First all-American men's Cincinnati SF since 2010** (Roddick and Mardy Fish)
- Career-high ranking No. 22 (set after Montreal runner-up finish)

**Sources:** ATP Tour, Tennis Majors, Tennis.com (Aug 21-22, 2026)
