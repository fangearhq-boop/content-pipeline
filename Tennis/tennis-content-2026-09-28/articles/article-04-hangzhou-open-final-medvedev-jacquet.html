Hangzhou Open Final: Jacquet's Remarkable Run Sets Up Career Moment Against Medvedev

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | September 28, 2026

Kyrian Jacquet arrived in Hangzhou last week ranked outside the top 100. On Monday, September 29, he will play in an ATP final against Daniil Medvedev. It is one of the Asian swing's best stories, and it arrives at exactly the moment the tennis world needed a reminder that the draw can produce something unexpected.

Jacquet's run began with a Round of 16 win over No. 3 seed Tomás Martín Etcheverry — a 6-2, 6-3 result that stood as the week's signature upset. He then continued through the quarterfinals and into the semifinals, where he beat his way into the final match. Medvedev, who reached his 68th career semifinal in Hangzhou, defeated Coleman Wong to book his place across the net from Jacquet on Monday.

The Context Around This Tournament

The Hangzhou Open field was shaped by the simultaneous Laver Cup, which pulled most of the top-10 ATP players to London for the team event. Medvedev — who was not part of the Laver Cup format — entered as the clear No. 1 seed in a draw that was always going to produce a less familiar finalist. But Jacquet's win over Etcheverry was not a fluke result explained entirely by the weaker field; Etcheverry was a genuine contender in Hangzhou and Jacquet beat him convincingly.

The tournament runs on hard courts in Hangzhou, China, with a prize fund of $1,039,090. It is an ATP 250 event and wraps up on September 29, the same day as the Chengdu Open final.

What a Jacquet Win Would Mean

A victory for Jacquet would be his first ATP title. It would also represent one of the more improbable runs in Asian swing history — a player coming from outside the top 100 to win an ATP title against a former Grand Slam champion and established top-10 player. Medvedev has won ATP Masters 1000 events and a US Open title; the gap in career achievement between the two finalists is significant.

But finals are finals. Medvedev has lost them before, and Jacquet arrives having beaten players he had no business beating by ranking alone. The match is scheduled for Monday evening Hangzhou local time.

What's Next

The Hangzhou Open final concludes the tournament's run alongside the Laver Cup week. Both finalists will return to the circuit with either a title or a runner-up result adding to their season records. Attention shifts next to the China Open in Beijing — beginning Wednesday, September 30 — where both Medvedev (No. 3 seed) and a range of players from the Asian swing week will enter the main draw with form and momentum.
