Chengdu Open Final: Hurkacz and Shapovalov Through as Week's Upsets Produce Marquee Title Match

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | September 28, 2026

The Chengdu Open final is set: Hubert Hurkacz faces Denis Shapovalov on Monday, September 29, in a championship match that emerged from one of the most upside-down weeks in the ATP 250 event's history.

Neither finalist arrived as the headline name. Valentin Vacherot — the top seed — was eliminated in the quarterfinals by South African qualifier Lloyd Harris. Alejandro Tabilo — the defending champion from Chile — was sent out in the quarterfinals by 38-year-old Frenchman Adrian Mannarino. And then in the semifinals, both surviving narratives closed off: Shapovalov defeated Mannarino 6-7(6), 6-3, 6-2 in a three-set comeback, and Hurkacz edged Harris 7-6(10), 6-4 in a tight semifinal that went deep into the first-set tiebreak.

How They Got Here

Shapovalov's week built on his earlier results — he had recorded his 250th career tour-level win earlier in the tournament, a milestone he acknowledged after the match. His semifinal was hard-fought against a Mannarino playing well above his ranking in the second half of the draw. Losing the first set in a tiebreak, he came back with back-to-back dominant sets to close out the veteran Frenchman.

Hurkacz's path was perhaps the less publicized one through the draw. He met Harris — the qualifier who had become one of the week's breakout performers by beating top seed Vacherot — in the semifinals and won a tiebreak-decided opener before taking the second set more cleanly. Hurkacz brings Tour pedigree to the final that fits a player capable of handling big occasions.

The Final Matchup

Hurkacz and Shapovalov have met multiple times on Tour. Both are players who compete above their seeds in Asian conditions, and Monday's final on the Chengdu hard courts offers each a title opportunity going into the China Open week. A Hurkacz win would add to a career that already includes a Miami Masters title and a Wimbledon semifinal. For Shapovalov, who has been seeking consistency at the Tour's top level for several years, a Chengdu title would be the biggest of his career.

What's Next

The Chengdu Open final takes place Monday evening, September 29. The winner and runner-up will both receive ranking points and prize money before attention turns to the China Open in Beijing, which begins Wednesday. Shapovalov is not listed in the Beijing field, while Hurkacz's Beijing entry status depends on his Chengdu result. The Asian swing continues through October, with the Shanghai Masters and Wuhan Open to follow.
