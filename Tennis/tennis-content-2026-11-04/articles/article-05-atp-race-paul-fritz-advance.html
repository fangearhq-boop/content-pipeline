ATP Race to Turin: Paul and Fritz Keep Dreams Alive After Paris Day 3 Wins

By Marcus Cole

November 4, 2026

Four spots in the ATP Finals in Turin are taken. Four more remain, and Wednesday's Paris Masters results have sharpened the picture of who might claim them. Tommy Paul and Taylor Fritz both won in Paris on Wednesday, ensuring the Race to Turin will come down to the final days of the season.

Alexander Zverev, Carlos Alcaraz, Ben Shelton, and Daniil Medvedev have all secured their places at the ATP Finals in Turin, which runs November 15 through 22. The remaining four berths are decided by the ATP Race to Turin standings at the conclusion of the Paris Masters — the final event of the 2026 season — this Sunday.

What Wednesday's Wins Mean

Tommy Paul's 7-5, 6-4 defeat of Holger Rune and Taylor Fritz's 3-6, 6-3, 6-4 win over Benjamin Bonzi both push those players further into the Paris draw, where each additional round brings more Race points. Paris is worth 1,000 Race points to the champion, 600 to the finalist, and significant points at the quarterfinal and semifinal stages as well.

Neither Paul nor Fritz was in a confirmed qualifying position coming into the week. Their wins don't lock anything up, but they keep the door open — and right now, that door was the only thing standing between them and a season that ends in Paris rather than Turin.

The Current Bubble

As of Wednesday morning, the standings place Roberto Bautista Agut Jodar in the eighth and final qualifying spot by approximately 24 points over Alex de Minaur. The standings from spots five through nine are separated by margins that a single good or bad result in Paris can easily shift.

The confirmed qualifiers sit clear of the chase: Zverev and Alcaraz locked up their spots early in the season, Shelton clinched in the fall, and Medvedev sealed his place in Vienna. Everyone below that line is playing elimination tennis in Paris.

Djokovic's Situation

Novak Djokovic occupies an unusual position in the Race picture. The 11-time Paris Masters champion is not in the Race's top eight right now, and his only remaining path to Turin requires reaching at minimum the Paris semifinals. Djokovic beat Valentin Vacherot in the first round Monday. He will need to keep winning — probably all the way to the final — to push himself into qualifying contention.

At 39, Djokovic's Paris form has been excellent this week, but the draw between him and a semifinal includes several dangerous opponents. The Race math is straightforward: he needs points, and Paris is the last place to get them.

How Paris Points Work at the Final Event

Paris, as a Masters 1000 event and the season's final tournament, distributes points on the same scale as other Masters events. The key difference this week is that there's no next tournament to make up ground — what happens between now and Sunday is final. Players currently in bubble positions know that a quarterfinal exit might be enough to hold their spot, while a first- or second-round loss could knock them out entirely if others in the standings are still advancing.

What's Next

Paul and Fritz both advance to the third round of the Paris Masters and will face tougher opponents in the quarterfinal bracket. For the Race picture, the clearest dates to watch are Friday and Saturday as the quarterfinals and semifinals take shape. The ATP Finals field will be finalized after Sunday's Paris final. Players and fans tracking Turin qualification should check the live Race standings on the ATP Tour website after each day's results are posted.
