Monfils Wins on 40th Birthday — Historic Record (TIER 1)
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Window: 10:30 AM ET Gael Monfils turned 40 years old on Tuesday — and he celebrated the way only Gael Monfils can. The Frenchman walked out to Arthur Ashe Stadium for his 18th and final US Open appearance and defeated Adolfo Daniel Vallejo 2-6, 6-1, 6-2, 6-0. He fired 37 winners and grew stronger as the night progressed, losing only the opening set before taking complete control. With the victory, Monfils became the oldest man to win a US Open match since Jimmy Connors, who also won on his 40th birthday — September 2, 1992. Connors that year made a legendary run to the quarterfinals as a wild card at age 39 and then returned at 40. Now Monfils joins that exclusive club. The win was also his 34th career US Open victory, the most by any French player in the history of the tournament. What made the night even more special: wife Elina Svitolina, the Ukrainian star, was there to share it. The two have been playing mixed doubles together at this US Open, reaching the semifinals — competing as a couple at a Grand Slam for the first time. Monfils revealed after the match that Svitolina had given him a "special gift" before he took the court. Monfils will retire at the end of the 2026 season. He is playing for the memories now. Tuesday night's birthday win gave him one he will never forget. ### Image Post Caption Window: 10:30 AM ET Gael Monfils wins on his 40th birthday at the 2026 US Open. The oldest man to win a US Open match since Jimmy Connors in 1992. His 34th US Open win — the most by any Frenchman in history. His 18th and final appearance at Flushing Meadows. What a night.

Nothing about her performance Tuesday suggested she is working through any physical limitation. Her serve was sharp, her footwork assured, and she closed the match without drama.
