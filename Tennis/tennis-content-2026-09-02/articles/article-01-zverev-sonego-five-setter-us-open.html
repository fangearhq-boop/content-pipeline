Zverev Survives Five-Set Battle Against Sonego to Open US Open Campaign

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | September 2, 2026

The world No. 1 needed four hours and 53 minutes to outlast Lorenzo Sonego in a five-set opener, raising real questions about his form heading into the second week.

Alexander Zverev is through to the second round of the 2026 US Open — but Tuesday night at Arthur Ashe Stadium offered the clearest reminder yet that the world No. 1 seed remains vulnerable at a tournament already turned upside down by early exits.

Zverev defeated Italy's Lorenzo Sonego 6-4, 3-6, 6-7(7), 7-5, 6-4 in four hours and 53 minutes, surviving one of the most grueling first-round matches in recent US Open memory. It is the longest match of Zverev's career at Flushing Meadows.

A Match That Had Everything

Zverev controlled the opening set, breaking Sonego to claim it 6-4 with relative comfort. But the Italian dug in and leveled the match by taking the second set 6-3, injecting real doubt into the contest.

The third set went deep. With both players trading heavy groundstrokes and showing no signs of fading, the set reached a tiebreak — and Sonego edged it 9-7 to take a two-sets-to-one lead. For a moment, the unthinkable appeared possible: the US Open's No. 1 seed eliminated in the first round for the third straight match of the tournament's opening days, following Novak Djokovic's exit on Day 1 and Arthur Fils' loss to Stefanos Tsitsipas on Day 2.

Zverev steadied in the fourth set, winning 7-5 to level the match. Then, in the fifth, he finally overcame Sonego 6-4 to book his place in round two.

A Draw Already Rewritten

Zverev entered the 2026 US Open as the clear favourite, elevated to No. 1 seed after Jannik Sinner's pre-tournament withdrawal due to a knee injury and Carlos Alcaraz's extended absence through his wrist injury — though Alcaraz is back and won his R1 in straight sets on Day 2.

With Djokovic already gone, Fils eliminated, and now the world No. 1 pushed to the absolute limit in round one, the draw is genuinely open. No top player has moved through without drama. Zverev's path to a potential title now involves recovering from a physically punishing match in a compressed schedule.

What's Next

Zverev advances to the US Open second round and will next face an opponent from the bottom half of his section of the draw. His priority now is recovery. The second round at the US Open will begin Thursday, September 3, with the tournament entering its second week next Monday.

The world No. 1 has survived. He has not yet impressed. In a wide-open draw, that may be enough — for now.
