US Open Day 4: Second Round Begins With Alcaraz, Rybakina, Gauff and Osaka All in Action

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | September 2, 2026

Thursday at Flushing Meadows brings the start of the second round, with defending champion Carlos Alcaraz making his first post-return test and Elena Rybakina continuing her injury comeback.

The 2026 US Open moves into the second round on Thursday, September 3, with a full Day 4 slate on ESPN beginning at 11:30 AM ET. After a first round that produced five-set epics, birthday miracles, and an early exit for the world No. 1 from before Wimbledon, the second round brings its own compelling narratives.

Here is what to watch at Flushing Meadows on Day 4.

Alcaraz Returns: vs. Faria (R2)

Carlos Alcaraz's US Open return has gone smoothly so far. After winning his first-round match against Roman Safiullin 6-4, 6-4, 6-4 on Day 2 — his first competitive match after 139 days away due to a wrist injury — the defending champion now faces Portugal's Jaime Faria in the second round.

Alcaraz's return has captured most of the tournament's early storylines. Can he sustain form across a two-week major while managing a wrist that kept him out from April? Thursday's match against Faria will offer the next piece of that picture. Alcaraz is No. 2 seed and a former champion at Flushing Meadows.

Rybakina: Ankle Watch Continues vs. Bouzas Maneiro

Elena Rybakina showed minimal sign of discomfort in her 6-3, 6-2 first-round win over Thea Frodin. Her second-round test comes against Spain's Jessica Bouzas Maneiro. Rybakina was unable to walk without pain in the days before the tournament, so every match she plays is effectively a health update. A second straight clinical performance on Thursday would represent a significant moment in the WTA's title picture.

Gauff: Defending Her Title

Coco Gauff — the reigning US Open champion, No. 4 seed, and one of the most watched players in New York — cruised past Zeynep Sonmez 6-3, 6-4 in the first round and returns for her second-round match on Thursday. Back-to-back US Open titles would be historic, and Gauff is the clear favourite from her section of the draw.

Osaka vs. Siniakova

No. 13 seed Naomi Osaka faces Czech Katerina Siniakova in what shapes as one of the more intriguing second-round women's matches of Day 4. Osaka has been targeting a Grand Slam run all season, and a deep run in New York would mark another milestone in her ongoing return to elite form.

Men's Draw: Auger-Aliassime vs. Khachanov

In the men's second round, No. 3 seed Felix Auger-Aliassime faces Karen Khachanov. Auger-Aliassime reached his first Masters 1000 final at the Canadian Open earlier this summer and enters New York as one of the most in-form players remaining in a draw already thinned by the exits of Djokovic, Sinner, and Fils.

What's Next

Day 4 coverage begins on ESPN at 11:30 AM ET, with primetime coverage from 7:00 PM ET through 11:00 PM ET. The second round at the US Open will run through Friday, September 4, with the third round starting Saturday.

After three days of first-round drama, the field is starting to take shape. Thursday will sharpen the picture considerably.
