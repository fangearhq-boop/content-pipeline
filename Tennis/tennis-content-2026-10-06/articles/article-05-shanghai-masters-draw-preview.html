Shanghai Masters 2026: Zverev Tops Loaded Draw as Race to Turin Enters Defining Week

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap

October 6, 2026

The Rolex Shanghai Masters opens Wednesday, October 7, and with Jannik Sinner absent due to a knee injury, Alexander Zverev takes center stage as the tournament's top seed. The Masters 1000 event — running October 7-18 at the Qi Zhong Tennis Center — features one of the strongest fields of the autumn swing, including Carlos Alcaraz (fresh from his Japan Open title), Daniil Medvedev, Novak Djokovic, and defending champion Valentin Vacherot. For the Race to Turin standings, this is arguably the most consequential week of the season.

The Draw at a Glance

Zverev's draw places him in the same half as Novak Djokovic, setting up a potential semifinal between two players who have combined for four Grand Slam titles in 2026. On the other side, Carlos Alcaraz and Daniil Medvedev are in the same quarter — a potential quarterfinal match that would be one of the event's headline contests.

Alcaraz opens against Juan Manuel Cerundolo, or a qualifier, in the first round. Medvedev — who arrives from Beijing still processing his disqualification for hitting a spectator with a ball during the China Open semifinals — will be eager to put that incident behind him and rebuild his Race points. His DQ means he left Beijing with zero ranking points and zero prize money.

Defending champion Vacherot qualified for Shanghai on the back of his remarkable 2025 title run. Ben Shelton and Felix Auger-Aliassime are also in the field, both in contention for ATP Finals spots and needing strong results over the next two weeks.

Race to Turin — The Numbers

The Race to the ATP Finals is where Shanghai's stakes become crystal clear. Zverev currently leads the Race standings at approximately 8,740 points, with Sinner at 7,950 — a gap of roughly 800 points. But Sinner, by withdrawing from Shanghai, loses approximately 3,550 Race points he accumulated there last year. Zverev, meanwhile, is projected to lose only around 1,080 points from the Shanghai defense even if he exits early, and stands to gain far more if he advances deep.

If Zverev wins Shanghai, his Year-End No. 1 claim becomes mathematically near-certain barring a dramatic Sinner comeback in Vienna and Paris. This is the week the Race could effectively be decided.

Below the top two, the bubble for the remaining six ATP Finals spots is tight. Ben Shelton leads the pursuit at approximately 4,430 Race points, with Alcaraz around 4,100 (boosted by today's Japan Open title), Medvedev at roughly 2,520, and Felix Auger-Aliassime, Alex de Minaur, and Tomáš Macháč all in contention. A Shanghai semifinal finish could be the decisive push for players on the bubble.

What's Next

Main draw action begins Wednesday, October 7 in Shanghai. First-round matches will determine who advances to face the top seeds in the second round. The quarterfinals are scheduled for October 13-14, the semifinals for October 16-17, and the final on October 18.
