WTA Beijing Quarterfinal Picture Takes Shape as Gauff, Swiatek, Andreeva Navigate Fourth Round

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap

October 6, 2026 | Beijing, China

With top seed Elena Rybakina already eliminated in a third-round upset, the 2026 China Open women's draw has taken on an open look as Tuesday's fourth-round action shapes the quarterfinal bracket. Coco Gauff, Iga Swiatek, Mirra Andreeva, and Linda Noskova are among those competing for the eight remaining spots, with Thursday's quarterfinals as the next major milestone in what is shaping up to be one of the most unpredictable WTA 1000 draws of the autumn.

The Draw After Rybakina's Exit

Rybakina's exit — a stunning third-round loss to qualifier Anastasia Charaeva — sent shockwaves through the Beijing draw. The world No. 1 had entered as the top seed for her first WTA 1000 appearance since reaching No. 1 in September following her US Open victory over Aryna Sabalenka. Her absence reshapes the upper half of the draw and opens the door for multiple players who might otherwise have faced a tougher path.

Third seed Coco Gauff is the defending Beijing champion and has looked the part of a title contender. She eased past 16-year-old Chinese wild card Sun Xinran 7-5, 6-1 in the third round, playing with the confidence of someone comfortable on Beijing's hard courts. Eighth seed Iga Swiatek, a two-time former Beijing champion, has also advanced steadily, most recently defeating Linda Vekic in the third round.

The Gauff-Swiatek Collision Course

The marquee storyline in the women's draw remains the potential Gauff-Swiatek quarterfinal. Both players occupy the same quarter of the draw, meaning they cannot meet before Thursday's last-eight stage. If both win their fourth-round matches, they will face each other for a semifinal berth — a match-up that would be their 17th meeting on the WTA Tour.

Swiatek leads their head-to-head 11-5 and has dominated their meetings on hard courts. But Gauff is the defending champion here, has been playing some of her best tennis of 2026, and Beijing has become one of her favorite venues.

Other Contenders

Fourth seed Mirra Andreeva faces Karolína Muchová in one of the day's feature fourth-round matchups. Andreeva, at 19, has been one of the revelations of the WTA season; Muchová, the 2023 French Open finalist, is known for her ability to produce upsets on big stages. Fifth seed Linda Noskova — who blasted through her third-round match in 55 minutes — is also in the mix for a deep run.

What's Next

Fourth-round matches continue Tuesday and Wednesday (October 6-7), with Thursday's quarterfinals to follow on October 8. The semifinals are scheduled for October 10, and the final on October 11. With Rybakina out and Sabalenka's status unclear, the women's title remains genuinely open. Multiple players have genuine claims to reach Sunday's final, and Thursday's quarterfinals could feature several marquee matchups.
