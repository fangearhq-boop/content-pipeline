Carlos Alcaraz Defends Japan Open Title, Becomes First Back-to-Back Champion Since Sampras

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap

October 6, 2026 | Tokyo, Japan

Carlos Alcaraz etched his name into Japan Open history on Tuesday, defeating Jiří Lehečka 7-6(4), 6-4 in the final at Ariake Colosseum to become the first man since Pete Sampras in 1994 to successfully defend the Tokyo title. The victory extends Alcaraz's perfect record in Japan to 9-0 all-time and delivers his first title since returning from a wrist injury that sidelined him for much of mid-2026.

It was a match that required Alcaraz to be patient. Lehečka, ranked No. 19 in the world, came out swinging and pushed the first set to a tiebreak. But the Spaniard's composure held — he won the breaker 7-4 and then broke Lehečka's serve twice in the second set to close out a 6-4 victory.

A Record Built in Tokyo

No court has been kinder to Alcaraz than the outdoor hard courts of Ariake. He won his first Japan Open title in 2025, and he returned in 2026 having already won his quarterfinal and semifinal matches without dropping a set on the way to the final. In the semifinal, he defeated compatriot Jaume Munar to set up a final rematch against Lehečka — who had eliminated Corentin Moutet, qualifier Hugo Vacherot and others en route to his first ATP 500 final.

The nine-and-zero record in Tokyo is remarkable for any player on any surface. For Alcaraz, who turned 23 this year, the Japan Open has become a reliable source of points, confidence, and trophies in the crucial autumn swing.

First Title Since Wrist Injury Return

The victory carries extra significance beyond the record books. Alcaraz battled a wrist injury in 2026 that forced him off the tour for an extended period, and this title marks his return to the winner's circle since that layoff. The manner of his win — composed under pressure in the tiebreak, dominant in the second set — suggested a player who has fully rediscovered his rhythm.

"It's a great feeling to defend the title," Alcaraz said after the match, according to reports. "This tournament means a lot to me."

What's Next

Alcaraz heads directly into the Rolex Shanghai Masters, which opens Wednesday, October 7. He will be among the top contenders in a field led by No. 1 seed Alexander Zverev. Alcaraz enters Shanghai on the opposite side of the draw from Zverev, with a potential quarterfinal matchup against Daniil Medvedev possible if both advance. Lehečka, despite the defeat, will collect valuable ranking points from his runner-up finish. The autumn hard-court swing continues at full pace.
