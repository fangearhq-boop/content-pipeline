Medvedev Disqualified After Hitting Spectator; Djokovic Eyes Record 7th Beijing Title vs de Minaur

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap

October 6, 2026 | Beijing, China

The China Open semifinals on Monday produced one of the most dramatic finales of the ATP season. Daniil Medvedev was disqualified from his semifinal against Novak Djokovic after striking a ball into the stands in apparent frustration, hitting a spectator near the eye. The incident occurred with Djokovic leading 7-5, 5-3 — a point away from completing a straight-sets victory — and ended Medvedev's tournament abruptly. It also extends Djokovic's unbeaten record in Beijing to 33-0 as he prepares to face Alex de Minaur in Tuesday's final.

The Incident

With Djokovic a game from victory in the second set, Medvedev smashed a backhand into the front rows of the Beijing stands after a point went against him. The ball struck a spectator near the left eye. The front-row victim was helped out of the arena, a hand held over the affected eye, while the chair umpire and tournament supervisor descended onto the court to consult with Medvedev.

The Russian offered little argument. He walked to the net, shook Djokovic's hand, and accepted his disqualification. Per ATP rules, Medvedev will lose all ranking points and prize money earned from the tournament. Major media — including ESPN, CNN and Al Jazeera — confirmed the disqualification within minutes of the incident.

It echoes a now-infamous moment from the 2020 US Open, when Djokovic himself was defaulted for inadvertently striking a line judge with a ball after losing a game. History, with a twist.

De Minaur's Path to the Final

In the bottom half of the draw, Alex de Minaur reached his first China Open final when Hubert Hurkacz retired from their semifinal with the Australian leading 6-4, 3-2. Hurkacz, who defeated Alexander Zverev in the quarterfinals, appeared to struggle physically before withdrawing. For de Minaur, it is a golden opportunity: he arrives in the final fresh, injury-free, and with the chance to claim his first ATP 500 title on Beijing's outdoor hard courts.

Djokovic and the 7th Title

Novak Djokovic has never lost a match in Beijing. His 33-0 record at the China Open is the most dominant site record in the modern ATP era. He has won the title on every one of his six previous visits: 2009, 2010, 2012, 2013, 2014 and 2015. After an 11-year absence, the Serbian returned in 2026 and has simply picked up where he left off.

Djokovic leads the head-to-head against de Minaur 3-1 and enters the final as the heavy favorite. But de Minaur has proven himself capable of upsets against the biggest names in the sport, particularly on hard courts, and playing in a Grand Slam-level atmosphere in Beijing could suit the Australian's aggressive style.

What's Next

The China Open men's final takes place Tuesday, October 6 at the National Tennis Center in Beijing. A Djokovic victory would give the Serbian his 7th Beijing title and extend his all-time record to 34-0 at the event. For de Minaur, it represents the biggest final of his career at this level. Regardless of the result, both players head to Shanghai for the Rolex Masters 1000 starting Wednesday.
