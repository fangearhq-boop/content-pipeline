Zverev and Alcaraz Headline Davis Cup Thursday — Germany and Spain Favored to Advance

By Ryan Calloway | November 25, 2026 | Tennis Fanrecap

Thursday brings the final two Davis Cup quarterfinals in Bologna, and both sessions are anchored by players who dominated 2026. Alexander Zverev leads Germany against Great Britain in the morning. Carlos Alcaraz headlines Spain against Austria in the afternoon. The winners meet in the semifinals on Saturday, November 28.

These are the matches the event has been building toward since the bracket was set. Both favorites enter with question marks, which is what makes them worth watching.

Germany vs. Great Britain — Zverev's Statement Match

Alexander Zverev collected his year-end No. 1 trophy at the Nitto ATP Finals in Turin on November 16. Thursday will be his first competitive match since then — the first time he steps onto a court representing his country after completing the most decorated season of his career.

Zverev won Roland Garros and the US Open in 2026 and finished the year ranked No. 1 in the world, the first German in ATP history to do so. His arrival in Bologna as Germany's No. 1 singles player carries a weight of expectation that did not exist before this year.

Great Britain are appearing in the Davis Cup Finals for the first time since 2023. They qualified via a 3-0 win over Ecuador and have shown the grit required to reach this stage. What they face on Thursday is a Germany squad led by the best player in the world, in peak form, with something to prove. The upset is possible. The odds are steep.

Austria vs. Spain — Alcaraz With a Question Mark

Spain captain David Ferrer named a squad that includes Carlos Alcaraz, Alejandro Davidovich Fokina, and several other capable players. Alcaraz is the reason Spain are considered among the title favorites, and his fitness will be the central story of Thursday's afternoon session.

He carries a left thigh concern from the ATP Finals in Turin. The nature and severity of the injury have not been fully disclosed, but the fact that it has been reported as an ongoing concern means the medical staff will be monitoring him closely. If Alcaraz plays Thursday and plays well, Spain are heavy favorites to advance. If his thigh limits him, Austria have a path.

Austria have not been a Davis Cup power, but they are a team with competitive singles players who know how to win a close rubber. If the door opens against a less-than-fit Alcaraz, they will walk through it.

The Semifinal Picture

If both favorites advance, Saturday's semifinal — Zverev-led Germany against Alcaraz-led Spain — will be one of the most anticipated team tennis matches in years. Two of the three best players in the world, on opposing sides of a Davis Cup semifinal, in Bologna, with a final appearance on the line.

The other semifinal is Czech Republic against the winner of Italy vs. South Korea. That bracket was set before Thursday's matches, and it creates a final-week schedule that rewards the surviving teams with a full weekend of tennis at the highest level.

Thursday is where the last two spots get filled. Both sessions are worth watching from the first ball.

What's Next

Germany vs. Great Britain and Austria vs. Spain take place Thursday, November 26 at BolognaFiere. The winners advance to Saturday's semifinals on November 28. Full match results and analysis will follow.
