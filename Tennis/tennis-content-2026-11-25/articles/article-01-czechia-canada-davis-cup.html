Czech Republic Sweep Canada 2-0 as Lehecka Clinches Davis Cup Quarterfinal

By Ryan Calloway | November 25, 2026 | Tennis Fanrecap

Czech Republic swept Canada 2-0 in Monday's Davis Cup quarterfinal at BolognaFiere, advancing to the semifinals with a performance built on composure and opportunism. Tomas Machac won the opening rubber 6-2, 3-6, 6-2 against Liam Draxl, and Jiri Lehecka closed it out 7-6(4), 6-2 over Félix Auger-Aliassime in a match that told a stark statistical story.

FAA had nine break points against Lehecka. He converted none of them.

Nine Break Points, Zero Converted

That number — nine opportunities, zero taken — encapsulates what happened in the second rubber. Auger-Aliassime generated the chances but could not finish them, and Lehecka, ranked No. 14 in the world, held firm every time. The first set went to a tiebreak, and Lehecka took it 7-4. The second set was more straightforward, with the Czech player winning 6-2 to close out the tie and send Canada home.

It was not the first time this week that the break point conversion rate proved decisive, but it was the number that made the result feel definitive. Canada had the openings. They did not take them.

Machac Sets the Tone

Tomas Machac's win over Draxl in the first rubber gave the Czech Republic the platform they needed. Machac dominated the first set 6-2, was broken back in the second to drop it 3-6, then reasserted control in the third, winning 6-2 again. It was a solid, controlled performance against a Canadian player who had little support behind him from the bench.

Czech Republic never needed the doubles rubber. The tie was settled in straight singles, 2-0, with the efficiency of a team that knew exactly what it needed to do.

Depleted Canada Had No Margin

Denis Shapovalov and Gabriel Diallo were both absent from Canada's squad through injury. Their absences were confirmed before the tie began, and the depth gap was real. Draxl and Auger-Aliassime are capable players, but asking them to win a Davis Cup quarterfinal against a full Czech Republic squad, without their two most experienced team members, was always a significant ask.

The Canadian program now faces a difficult winter. When their full squad returns, they will be a more formidable team. But in Bologna on Monday, they did not have that squad.

Czechia's Semifinal Awaits

Czech Republic advance to the semifinals, where they will face the winner of Tuesday's Italy vs. South Korea quarterfinal. That match takes place at BolognaFiere on Friday, November 27. If Italy win, Czechia will face the three-time defending champions on their home soil. If South Korea pull off the upset, it sets up a semifinal with significantly different stakes.

Either way, Czech Republic have done what they came to do in the quarterfinals. Lehecka delivered when his country needed him most. That is a result worth remembering — and a result Canada will have a long offseason to think about.

What's Next

Czech Republic face the Italy vs. South Korea winner in the Davis Cup semifinals on Friday, November 27 at BolognaFiere. The other semifinal pairings will be decided by Thursday's quarterfinals between Great Britain vs. Germany and Austria vs. Spain.
