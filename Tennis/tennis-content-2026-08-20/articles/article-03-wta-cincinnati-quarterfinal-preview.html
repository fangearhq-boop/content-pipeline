Cincinnati WTA Quarterfinal Preview: Gauff vs. Kostyuk Headlines an Open Draw

By Elena Voss | August 20, 2026 | Tennis Fanrecap

With Aryna Sabalenka stunned by Sara Bejlek in Thursday's Round of 16 and Elina Svitolina having withdrawn with an ankle injury earlier in the week, the Cincinnati WTA draw arrives at the quarterfinals without its two highest-profile contenders. What remains is a genuinely open, compelling field heading into Friday's matches.

The marquee match: Coco Gauff against Marta Kostyuk.

Gauff vs. Kostyuk — The History and the Stakes

Coco Gauff reached the quarterfinals with a 6-3, 6-2 win over Czech player Marie Bouzkova — a performance that showed Gauff at her most dominant, winning in just over 90 minutes. She's been building her game steadily through the Cincinnati draw and arrives at the quarterfinal in strong form.

Marta Kostyuk's path was more dramatic. The Ukrainian beat 17-year-old Mirra Andreeva 4-6, 6-0, 6-2 — turning a lost first set into a dominant two-set run that underlined Kostyuk's ability to shift momentum mid-match. Andreeva, who had been one of the tournament's breakout stories, had no answer after the first set.

The head-to-head between Gauff and Kostyuk stands at 2-0 in Kostyuk's favour heading into Friday. Both previous meetings have been close, competitive affairs, and hard courts tend to suit Gauff's aggressive baseline game. For Gauff, this is an opportunity to change a H2H that has not gone her way — on a surface where she has history and where her serve can be a decisive weapon.

Bejlek vs. Keys — The Cinderella Match

The other featured quarterfinal on Friday is Sara Bejlek against Madison Keys. Bejlek, who delivered the week's defining upset by beating Sabalenka, now faces a compatriot of Sabalenka's American rival — a player who knows pressure tennis and has a powerful serve to match Bejlek's own weapon.

For Keys, this is a solid draw. For Bejlek, it's a chance to add a WTA 1000 semifinal to what has already been a week she'll never forget.

What's Next

The Cincinnati WTA quarterfinals are scheduled for Friday, August 21. Semifinals follow Saturday, with the final on Sunday — just as US Open Fan Week begins. For any player who reaches the final, Sunday is a statement moment: a Masters 1000 final the week the US Open officially opens its doors is a headline that writes itself.

The tournament that started with Sabalenka as the firm favourite now has no clear leader. That's what upsets do — they don't just eliminate a player, they open the draw for everyone else.
