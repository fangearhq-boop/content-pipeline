Four Americans Reach Cincinnati ATP Quarterfinals as Both Top Seeds Fall

By Ryan Calloway | August 20, 2026 | Tennis Fanrecap

It was the kind of day American tennis has been waiting for.

Tommy Paul, Frances Tiafoe, Taylor Fritz, and Brandon Nakashima are all in the Cincinnati Open quarterfinals after Thursday's Round of 16 produced one of the most dramatic collective results for American men at a Masters 1000 event in recent memory. The Americans were not alone in advancing — Arthur Fils and Flavio Cobolli also moved through — but the depth of US representation in Friday's quarterfinals is a genuine story.

It became even more striking because the top two seeds on the ATP side are both out.

Paul Saves a Match Point to Eliminate Zverev

The headline result of the day: Tommy Paul defeated No. 1 seed Alexander Zverev 4-6, 7-6(6), 6-4 in 2 hours and 45 minutes. Paul dropped the first set and found himself facing a match point in the second-set tiebreak before recovering to win it 8-6. He then broke Zverev twice in the third set to close out one of the biggest wins of his career.

Zverev had entered Cincinnati as the overwhelming favourite on the men's side. His exit, combined with the early elimination of Daniil Medvedev and Novak Djokovic in the earlier rounds, leaves the men's draw without any of its traditional hard-court heavyweights.

No. 2 seed Felix Auger-Aliassime also fell on Thursday, going down to Frances Tiafoe 6-3, 6-4 in straight sets. Tiafoe was dominant from the start, breaking early in both sets and never allowing FAA to find a foothold.

The American Quartet

Beyond Paul and Tiafoe, the American story runs deeper. Taylor Fritz beat Australian qualifier Christopher O'Connell 6-4, 6-3 in a comfortable performance that showed Fritz at his hard-court best. And Brandon Nakashima — who arrived in Cincinnati off the back of his runner-up finish at the Canadian Open in Montreal — beat Nuno Borges 6-3, 6-7(5), 6-3 to reach back-to-back Masters 1000 quarterfinals.

Nakashima, who saved three match points against Medvedev in the Round of 16 two days ago, has been the American story of the summer. But on Thursday, he was joined by three compatriots who matched his achievement.

Other quarterfinalists include Arthur Fils (France), who beat Alex de Minaur 6-3, 6-4, and Flavio Cobolli (Italy), who held off Rafael Jodar 4-6, 7-6, 6-3.

What's Next

The Cincinnati ATP quarterfinals take place Friday, August 21. With Djokovic, Medvedev, Zverev, and Auger-Aliassime all out, the title is there to be claimed by a player who might not be considered one of the sport's established elite — but is certainly playing like it this week.

The US Open main draw begins August 30, with Fan Week starting Saturday. For Paul, Tiafoe, Fritz, and Nakashima, Friday is not just a quarterfinal — it's a live audition for US Open seedings and form entering Flushing Meadows.
