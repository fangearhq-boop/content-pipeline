### STORY 1: Bejlek Shocks Sabalenka

#### Long-Form Post — 8:00 AM ET

Sara Bejlek walked onto court against the world No. 1 and played the match of her life.

The 20-year-old Czech, ranked No. 35, beat Aryna Sabalenka 7-6(7), 6-4 in the Cincinnati Open Round of 16 Thursday to reach her first-ever WTA 1000 quarterfinal. The scoreline alone doesn't tell the story.

In the first-set tiebreak, Sabalenka led 5-1 — one mistake from closing out the set. Then something shifted. Bejlek reeled off six consecutive points to steal the tiebreak 9-7. Sabalenka, who hit four double faults and got only 50% of first serves in, couldn't find an answer in the second set either. The world No. 1 is out of Cincinnati.

Bejlek next faces Madison Keys in the quarterfinals on Friday.

The hard-court swing just got a lot more interesting — and a name you'll want to remember before the US Open is Sara Bejlek.

### STORY 2: Four Americans Reach Cincinnati ATP QFs

#### Long-Form Post — 10:30 AM ET

Four Americans are in the Cincinnati Open quarterfinals. Read that again.

Tommy Paul beat top seed Alexander Zverev 4-6, 7-6(6), 6-4 — saving a match point in the second set tiebreak. Frances Tiafoe knocked out No. 2 seed Felix Auger-Aliassime 6-3, 6-4. Taylor Fritz advanced past Christopher O'Connell 6-4, 6-3. And Brandon Nakashima — fresh off his Montreal runner-up finish — beat Nuno Borges 6-3, 6-7(5), 6-3 to make it back-to-back Masters 1000 quarterfinals.

Both top seeds are out. Four Americans are alive. Arthur Fils (France) and Flavio Cobolli (Italy) also advanced.

The quarterfinals are set for Friday, August 21. With Djokovic, Medvedev, Zverev, and Auger-Aliassime all eliminated, the Cincinnati title is more contested than it's been in years.

### STORY 3: WTA QF Preview

#### Long-Form Post — 12:30 PM ET

Friday's WTA quarterfinals in Cincinnati are going to be must-watch tennis.

The feature match: Coco Gauff vs. Marta Kostyuk. Gauff reached the quarterfinals with a commanding 6-3, 6-2 win over Marie Bouzkova. Kostyuk beat 17-year-old Mirra Andreeva 4-6, 6-0, 6-2 in an emphatic turnaround. The two have met twice before — Kostyuk leads the head-to-head 2-0. With Sabalenka gone and Svitolina having withdrawn earlier, the WTA Cincinnati title is genuinely up for grabs. Could we see an American champion in Keys or Gauff? A Kostyuk breakthrough? A Bejlek fairytale?

Friday's quarterfinalists have all beaten someone to get here. That's what makes this week so good.

### STORY 4: US Open Fan Week Starts Saturday

#### Long-Form Post — 6:00 PM ET

The US Open is almost here.

Fan Week starts this Saturday, August 23, at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center in Queens. Qualifying matches, practices, Mixed Doubles, and Arthur Ashe Kids' Day are all part of the week leading up to the main draw — which begins August 30.

But the two biggest questions in tennis remain unanswered.

Jannik Sinner, the world No. 1, has not played since Wimbledon due to a right knee problem. He's believed to be on course to play, but his participation is still day-by-day. Carlos Alcaraz, the defending US Open champion, last played April 14 in Barcelona. He skipped both Montreal and Cincinnati with a wrist injury and is currently training in Spain with Holger Rune — trying to figure out if he's ready to defend.

Nine players have already withdrawn from the main draw. The men's field hasn't been this wide open in memory.

Which men's player do you think wins if Sinner and Alcaraz don't make it?

### STORY 5: Who Is Sara Bejlek?

#### Long-Form Post — 9:00 PM ET

If you didn't know Sara Bejlek's name before today, you do now.

The 20-year-old from the Czech Republic entered the Cincinnati Open ranked No. 35 in the world and went 0-3 against top-10 opponents heading into her Round of 16 match. Her opponent: Aryna Sabalenka, world No. 1, one of the most dominant servers in women's tennis.

What followed was one of the most dramatic matches of the summer.

Bejlek hit three aces against zero double faults. She won 67% of her first serve points. When Sabalenka led 5-1 in the first-set tiebreak, Bejlek simply didn't blink — she won six straight points to steal the set. The second set was Bejlek's from the start.

She's now in her first-ever WTA 1000 quarterfinal, facing Madison Keys on Friday. She's one match from a semifinal at a hard-court Masters event, with the US Open starting Fan Week on Saturday.

Timing is everything in tennis. Bejlek's has never been better.
