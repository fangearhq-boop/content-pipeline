Ben Shelton Reaches First ATP Finals Semifinal — Beats Jodar 7-6(8), 7-5
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Ben Shelton is in the ATP Finals semifinals for the first time, and Thursday night in Turin showed exactly why he belongs there. He beat Rafael Jodar 7-6(8), 7-5 in just over two hours. The first-set tiebreak lasted until 10-8 — tense, prolonged, and the defining moment of the match. Shelton hit 14 aces. Jodar, in his debut year at the event, fought hard but exits at 0-3 without a win. It was the win Shelton needed. He came into the day at 1-1 in the group, knowing a loss would have opened the door for Arthur Fils. He closed it, finished 2-1, and booked his place in the last four alongside Alcaraz and Zverev. Shelton's semifinal opponent is Carlos Alcaraz on Saturday. It is a matchup that will generate a lot of interest — the No. 2 seed vs the American who finally made the ATP Finals last four. Who do you like in Alcaraz vs Shelton on Saturday?

Zverev Goes 3-0 — Beats Fils 6-4, 7-6(6) to Top McEnroe Group Unbeaten
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Alexander Zverev completed a perfect group stage at the ATP Finals, beating Arthur Fils 6-4, 7-6(6) on Thursday evening to finish 3-0 in the McEnroe Group. It was his 21st career win at this event, and the tiebreak was anything but straightforward. Fils dug in and saved five consecutive match points before Zverev finally closed it 8-6. The mental composure to convert under that pressure was notable — a reminder of why Zverev remains the year-end No. 1 and the two-time champion of this tournament. The concern is the ankle. Zverev appeared to hurt his right ankle in the closing stages, and while he completed the match without issue, the question of his fitness for Saturday's semifinal is now the week's biggest subplot. Fils is out at 1-2. His tournament ends without a semifinal place, but his comeback win over Jodar in the group stage earned him a moment in Turin. Do you think Zverev's ankle will affect him in the semifinals?
