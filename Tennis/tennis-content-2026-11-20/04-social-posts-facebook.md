### STORY 1: Shelton Beats Jodar, First ATP Finals Semifinal

**Long-Form Post (8:00 AM ET)**

Ben Shelton is in the ATP Finals semifinals for the first time, and Thursday night in Turin showed exactly why he belongs there.

He beat Rafael Jodar 7-6(8), 7-5 in just over two hours. The first-set tiebreak lasted until 10-8 — tense, prolonged, and the defining moment of the match. Shelton hit 14 aces. Jodar, in his debut year at the event, fought hard but exits at 0-3 without a win.

It was the win Shelton needed. He came into the day at 1-1 in the group, knowing a loss would have opened the door for Arthur Fils. He closed it, finished 2-1, and booked his place in the last four alongside Alcaraz and Zverev.

Shelton's semifinal opponent is Carlos Alcaraz on Saturday. It is a matchup that will generate a lot of interest — the No. 2 seed vs the American who finally made the ATP Finals last four.

### STORY 2: Zverev Beats Fils 6-4, 7-6(6)

**Long-Form Post (10:30 AM ET)**

Alexander Zverev completed a perfect group stage at the ATP Finals, beating Arthur Fils 6-4, 7-6(6) on Thursday evening to finish 3-0 in the McEnroe Group.

It was his 21st career win at this event, and the tiebreak was anything but straightforward. Fils dug in and saved five consecutive match points before Zverev finally closed it 8-6. The mental composure to convert under that pressure was notable — a reminder of why Zverev remains the year-end No. 1 and the two-time champion of this tournament.

The concern is the ankle. Zverev appeared to hurt his right ankle in the closing stages, and while he completed the match without issue, the question of his fitness for Saturday's semifinal is now the week's biggest subplot.

Fils is out at 1-2. His tournament ends without a semifinal place, but his comeback win over Jodar in the group stage earned him a moment in Turin.

### STORY 3: Day 6 — Medvedev vs Tiafoe

The last open question at the ATP Finals gets answered tonight.

Daniil Medvedev (1-1) and Frances Tiafoe (1-1) meet at the Inalpi Arena in Turin — the winner takes the final semifinal spot. The loser goes home.

Medvedev leads their career head-to-head 4-0, all on hard courts. That is a significant number heading into a match like this. But Tiafoe earned his first ATP Finals win earlier this week — beating Cobolli in a tiebreak before bagelling him in the second — and momentum counts for something.

Carlos Alcaraz plays Cobolli in the afternoon session, but that result means nothing for the standings. Alcaraz is already through. Tonight's match is the one that matters.

The semifinal draw means the winner of this match faces Alexander Zverev on Saturday.

### STORY 5: Zverev Ankle Concern

**Long-Form Post (6:00 PM ET)**

A footnote from Thursday night that could become a major story by Saturday.

Alexander Zverev hurt his right ankle late in his win over Arthur Fils, and while he completed the match and finished the group stage 3-0 unbeaten, the footage of him checking the ankle after the final point was noticeable.

Zverev is the top seed, the year-end No. 1, and a two-time ATP Finals champion (2018, 2021). He enters the semifinals with every reason to be confident. But an ankle issue, however minor it appears tonight, adds an element of uncertainty to a tournament he has been the favourite for all week.

His semifinal opponent will be known by tonight — Medvedev or Tiafoe. Whoever it is will now be doing some mental arithmetic about how fit Zverev actually is.
