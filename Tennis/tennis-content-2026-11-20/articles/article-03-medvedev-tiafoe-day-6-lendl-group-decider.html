Medvedev vs Tiafoe: The Last Lendl Group Spot Gets Decided in Turin Tonight

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | November 20, 2026

Three of the four ATP Finals semifinalists are known. The fourth is decided tonight. Daniil Medvedev (1-1) and Frances Tiafoe (1-1) meet in the final Lendl Group match of the week at the Inalpi Arena in Turin — the winner takes the remaining semifinal spot and faces Alexander Zverev on Saturday. The loser is eliminated.

Earlier in the day, Carlos Alcaraz plays Flavio Cobolli in the other Lendl Group match. That result carries no standing implications — Alcaraz is already through as group winner, Cobolli is already eliminated at 0-2 — but it gives Alcaraz the opportunity to manage his fitness ahead of Saturday's semifinal against Ben Shelton.

The Head-to-Head and What It Tells Us

Medvedev leads the career head-to-head against Tiafoe 4-0, all on hard courts. That is the number Tiafoe has to overcome tonight. The two have not met at the ATP Finals before — this is the first time they face each other at this event. Hard-court results favour Medvedev, whose flat, deep baseline game and relentless return of serve make him a difficult opponent in best-of-three formats on fast indoor surfaces.

But Tiafoe is on form. He beat Cobolli earlier in the week — a result that required him to save two set points in a first-set tiebreak before dominating the second 6-0. That kind of swing, from two points away from losing the set to winning the second in a bagel, requires composure and belief. He has both.

What Each Player Needs

For Medvedev, a win here would add another ATP Finals semifinal to his record. He has been a fixture at the year-end championship for years — a finalist on multiple occasions, a consistent presence in the second week. His first match of the week, a loss to Alcaraz, left him needing a result, and he got it by beating Tiafoe earlier in round-robin play this season.

For Tiafoe, the stakes are simpler: a first ATP Finals semifinal would be a significant landmark. He took his first win at this event earlier in the week. Making the last four is the next step, against an opponent who has beaten him every time they have played.

What's Next

The winner of tonight's Medvedev-Tiafoe match faces Alexander Zverev in the ATP Finals semifinals on Saturday. Zverev completed the group stage 3-0 but was seen checking his right ankle after his Thursday win over Fils, adding an injury question to the bracket. The other semifinal — Alcaraz vs Shelton — is already confirmed. The timeline for tonight's Medvedev-Tiafoe match follows the afternoon Alcaraz-Cobolli session at the Inalpi Arena.
