Zverev Goes 3-0 — Beats Fils 6-4, 7-6(6) to Top McEnroe Group Unbeaten

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | November 20, 2026

Alexander Zverev completed the McEnroe Group stage without dropping a set, beating Arthur Fils 6-4, 7-6(6) on Thursday evening at the Inalpi Arena in Turin. It was his 21st career win at the ATP Finals. It was also the most dramatic of his three group-stage victories: Fils saved five consecutive match points in the second-set tiebreak before Zverev closed it out 8-6. The win cements his status as the top seed entering the knockout rounds — but he left the court nursing his right ankle, and that is now the conversation heading into Saturday.

Five Match Points and a Tiebreak That Would Not End

The first set was controlled. Zverev won it 6-4 without excessive drama, breaking when needed and holding his serve throughout. The second set followed a similar pattern until the tiebreak, where the match's tension came to a head.

Zverev reached match point. Then again. Five times, Fils found a way to push the score back to deuce in the tiebreak. Each time, the arena held its breath. Then Zverev stepped in and won two straight points to close at 8-6. The sequence was the kind that defines a tournament for a player — Fils had every reason to believe he could force a third set and fight on, but Zverev had the poise to win when it was most difficult.

Fils exits at 1-2. His win over Jodar in the group stage was a moment — the first French win at the ATP Finals since Jo-Wilfried Tsonga in 2011 — but it was not enough to reach the last four. He will leave Turin with that record and a sense of what the highest level looks like.

The Ankle Concern

Late in the match, Zverev appeared to roll or tweak his right ankle. He completed the final points and the match without retiring, but the footage of him checking the ankle in the closing stages was noticed immediately. After the final point, he was seen testing the joint before leaving the court.

Zverev is the year-end No. 1 and a two-time ATP Finals champion — he won in 2018 and again in 2021. His semifinal takes place on Saturday, with the opponent to be determined by Friday's Medvedev-Tiafoe result. The 48 hours between the group stage and the semifinal will be closely watched. Any official update from the Zverev camp on his fitness will draw significant attention.

What's Next

Zverev faces the winner of Friday's Medvedev-Tiafoe match in the ATP Finals semifinals on Saturday at the Inalpi Arena. His health between now and then is the central storyline of the second half of the tournament. Meanwhile, the McEnroe Group is fully settled: Zverev tops the group at 3-0, and Ben Shelton advances as runner-up at 2-1 after his win over Jodar earlier Thursday. Shelton will face Alcaraz in the other semifinal on Saturday.
