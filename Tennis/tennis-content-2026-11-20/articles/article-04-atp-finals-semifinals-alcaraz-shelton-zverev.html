Alcaraz vs Shelton, Zverev vs TBD: The ATP Finals Semifinals Take Shape

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | November 20, 2026

The ATP Finals semifinals are taking shape. Three of the four participants are confirmed: Carlos Alcaraz, Alexander Zverev, and Ben Shelton will play on Saturday at the Inalpi Arena in Turin. The fourth spot is decided on Friday evening when Daniil Medvedev and Frances Tiafoe play the final Lendl Group match of the week.

Alcaraz, the No. 2 seed, faces Shelton in one semifinal. Zverev, the No. 1 seed, will face whichever player survives Medvedev-Tiafoe. Both matches take place Saturday at the Inalpi Arena.

Alcaraz vs Shelton: The Form Player Against the Newcomer

Carlos Alcaraz enters this semifinal with an 11-match winning streak and a flawless group stage — two wins, zero break points conceded, 72 minutes against Medvedev, 69 minutes against Tiafoe. The figures tell the story of a player operating at a high level. He is the Ivan Lendl Group winner, he has the No. 2 seed, and the bracket has delivered him a semifinal against the McEnroe Group's runner-up.

Ben Shelton is making his first ATP Finals semifinal appearance. He beat Jodar 7-6(8), 7-5 on Thursday, earning his place in the last four after a tiebreak that required composure and nerve. Shelton has beaten Alcaraz before in their career meetings. The head-to-head is not stacked entirely against him. But going up against Alcaraz's current level — with 11 consecutive wins and a left thigh that caused no visible difficulty despite a medical timeout in the group stage — will be the sternest test Shelton has faced this season.

Zverev's Side of the Draw

Zverev finished the McEnroe Group 3-0, winning all three matches without dropping a set. He is the year-end No. 1, a two-time ATP Finals champion (2018, 2021), and by any reading of the form book the tournament favourite. His semifinal opponent will be Medvedev or Tiafoe — both of whom he has met many times before at this level.

The ankle is the caveat. Zverev was seen checking his right ankle after Thursday's win over Fils, following an apparent injury in the closing stages of the match. He completed the match without issue, but 48 hours before a semifinal is not a comfortable timeline for such a development. Any update on his condition before Saturday is expected to be one of the main pre-match stories of the day.

What's Next

The fourth semifinalist is confirmed on Friday evening following the Medvedev-Tiafoe result. The ATP Finals semifinals then take place Saturday at the Inalpi Arena in Turin — Alcaraz vs Shelton, and Zverev vs the Friday winner. The final is Sunday. Zverev's fitness update before Saturday will be closely watched by everyone in the draw.
