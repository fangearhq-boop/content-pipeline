ATP Race to Turin: Zverev Leads by 700 Points as Asian Swing Begins

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | October 1, 2026

The PIF ATP Live Race to Turin has entered its most consequential stretch — and Alexander Zverev is the man in front. The German top seed, who secured his Race lead by winning the 2026 U.S. Open, enters this week's double-500 event at 8,650 points, approximately 700 ahead of Jannik Sinner (7,950). Both players have already qualified for the ATP Finals in Turin, which runs November 15-22. Everything that remains is about the seedings and the six bubble spots beneath them.

The picture below the top two is where the Asian swing matters most. Ben Shelton holds third place at 4,430 points, followed by Carlos Alcaraz at approximately 4,050. Alcaraz is actively competing this week in Tokyo, making his draw the most impactful for Race positioning among the top four.

The Bubble Fight

The next tier — the players competing for spots five through eight at the ATP Finals — is remarkably tight. Daniil Medvedev, fresh off his Hangzhou title, sits at approximately 2,420 points. Arthur Fils, Alex de Minaur, and Jakub Menšík are within 40 points of one another, all clustered around 1,855-1,890.

That tightly packed bubble means every round at the China Open, Japan Open, and Shanghai Masters carries enormous weight. A quarterfinal run in Beijing or Tokyo can jump a player over two or three rivals. A semifinal can effectively clinch qualification. Players in this zone need to treat the next three weeks like a second season finale.

The Sinner Variable

Normally, a player ranked No. 2 in the Race who has already qualified would be a settled element. Sinner is anything but. The Italian, absent since Wimbledon with a knee injury, is projected to drop an additional 3,550 Race points through year-end as he defends 2025 results he will not repeat. That would push him below several of the current bubble players in the final Race standings — though his ATP Finals spot is already secured on points regardless of how the Race ends.

Zverev, meanwhile, defends only around 1,080 points. Even in a worst-case scenario in Beijing and Shanghai, he closes the year ranked No. 1 in the Race. His focus this week is not the Race — it is the title in Beijing, where a potential quarterfinal against Djokovic is already in view.

What's Next

The ATP Race standings will shift significantly over the next two weeks. The Shanghai Masters — beginning October 7 — is a 1000-level event with the biggest points available in the fall calendar. Players on the bubble need results there, not just in Tokyo and Beijing. After Shanghai, only the Vienna Open and Paris Masters remain before the ATP Finals itself. The time to move is now.
