Japan Open Day 1: Fils Wins Top 10 Debut, Tabilo Stuns Tommy Paul 6-1, 6-2

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | October 1, 2026

The Japan Open in Tokyo opened its main draw on Wednesday with two results that will have the tennis world talking. Arthur Fils, having crossed into the Top 10 for the first time in his career this week, marked the milestone with a 6-3, 6-2 victory over compatriot Luca Van Assche. Alejandro Tabilo then followed with a ruthless demolition of seventh seed Tommy Paul: 6-1, 6-2, in a result that spoke more like a drubbing than a first-round match at an ATP 500 event.

The two results put Day 1 of the Japan Open — running simultaneously with the China Open in Beijing — squarely on the map. With defending champion Carlos Alcaraz yet to play as of Thursday, the tournament's opening session was defined not by its headliner but by the players trying to build momentum around him.

Fils in the Top 10

For Arthur Fils, this week represents a milestone he has been building toward for two years. The 20-year-old Frenchman entered the Top 10 of the ATP Rankings this week for the first time, and wasted no time performing like he belonged there. Against Van Assche — a fellow Frenchman who has been climbing the rankings himself — Fils was precise and aggressive throughout, winning in 94 minutes.

Fils had been one of the most promising players on tour in 2025, but his 2026 season required patience. The Tokyo win keeps him in strong form heading into the final stretch of the year, with ATP Finals qualification still within reach for the young Parisian.

Tabilo Dismantles Tommy Paul

If Fils' win was composed, Alejandro Tabilo's was outright dominant. The Chilean — who reached a career-high ranking earlier this year — handled seventh seed Tommy Paul with what can only be described as efficiency taken to its extreme: 6-1, 6-2 in a match that was never competitive. Paul, an American who has been a consistent Top 15 force, found himself unable to build any rhythm against Tabilo's flat, penetrating groundstrokes.

The result adds to a strong record Tabilo has developed this fall as a legitimate spoiler on hard courts. It also shifts the bottom half of the Japan Open draw considerably, opening a path toward the later rounds for a player who has the game to go deep at 500-level events.

The Asia Double-Bill

This week is unusual in that two ATP 500 tournaments — Beijing and Tokyo — are running in parallel, splitting the field across two continents. Players on the ATP Tour had to choose their draw weeks in advance. Beijing drew Djokovic, Zverev, Medvedev, and Rublev. Tokyo attracted Alcaraz, Fils, and much of the emerging generation. The points on offer are identical at both venues, making the combined week enormously significant for Race to Turin positioning.

What's Next

Fils and Tabilo both advance to the second round at the Japan Open, where they will face opponents determined by the rest of the first-round bracket. Alcaraz is expected to open his title defense on Thursday or Friday. With the Shanghai Masters (ATP 1000) beginning October 7, the Tokyo and Beijing winners will have little time to rest before the Asian hard-court swing reaches its biggest event of the season.
