Carlos Alcaraz walked onto the Inalpi Arena on Monday evening and gave his ATP Finals campaign the most emphatic possible start.

Alcaraz now sits at 1-0 in the Ivan Lendl Group. Next up: Daniil Medvedev on Wednesday. If both players keep winning, that match will effectively decide who tops the group.

### STORY 2: ATP Finals Day 3 — Fils vs Jodar, Zverev vs Shelton

**Long-Form Post (10:30 AM ET)**

Day 3 at the ATP Finals serves up elimination tennis in the McEnroe Group.

Arthur Fils (0-1) and Rafael Jodar (0-1) both lost their opening matches. Today's match between them is effectively do-or-die — the loser falls to 0-2 and cannot reach the semifinals. Jodar leads their head-to-head 2-1, with both of his wins this year coming in straight sets on hard courts, the same surface they're playing on in Turin. Fils, the first Frenchman at the ATP Finals since Gaël Monfils in 2016, needs to end that run today.

Then comes the evening: Alexander Zverev (1-0) versus Ben Shelton (1-0). Both players won on Day 1, so this match shapes the top of the McEnroe Group. Zverev leads their head-to-head 6-0 — a run that includes beating Shelton in five sets in the 2026 US Open final. Shelton will be looking to change that number, but Zverev's dominance in this matchup is hard to argue with.

### STORY 3: Medvedev Beats Cobolli 7-6(4), 6-3

**Long-Form Post (12:30 PM ET)**

Daniil Medvedev opened his 2026 ATP Finals account on Monday evening in Turin, and he did it the Medvedev way — methodical, relentless, and almost completely unchallenged.

The result: 7-6(4), 6-3 over Flavio Cobolli in 85 minutes. Medvedev faced zero break points across the match, won 86% of points when Cobolli hit a second serve, and never gave the Italian a path back into the contest. The first set was competitive enough — Cobolli had the Inalpi Arena crowd behind him as a home-country debutant — but the tiebreak went Medvedev's way, and the second set was relatively comfortable from there.

Medvedev is now 1-0 in the Ivan Lendl Group alongside Alcaraz, who was equally dominant earlier in the day. Tiafoe and Cobolli both sit at 0-1 and need to respond in their next matches to stay alive.

### STORY 4: Andreeva Champion Reaction Quotes

**Long-Form Post (3:00 PM ET)**

It has been less than 24 hours since Mirra Andreeva lifted the WTA Finals trophy in Indian Wells, and her post-match comments are already some of the most memorable quotes of the tennis season.

On the moment she felt the most pressure in the final against Svitolina: "It was the second time in my life where I just thought I am going to die on the court but she is not getting the win." That is not a figure of speech — it is a window into how she thinks when things are hardest. She makes a decision, and she executes.

On her celebrations: "I am going to celebrate, get a car." She was not being modest. The WTA Finals prize includes a Mercedes-AMG SL 55, and Andreeva, 19, was going to enjoy it.

Her first words on court after winning: "I want to thank myself." Her one-word summary of her 2026 season: "consistent, happy and work."

There is a clarity to how she talks about herself that matches the clarity of how she plays. She is 19, she is WTA No. 2, and she just became the third teenager in history to win the WTA Finals.
