The result: 7-6(4), 6-3 over Flavio Cobolli in 85 minutes. Medvedev is now 1-0 in the Ivan Lendl Group alongside Alcaraz, who was equally dominant earlier in the day. Tiafoe and Cobolli both sit at 0-1 and need to respond in their next matches to stay alive. ### Long-Form Post It has been less than 24 hours since Mirra Andreeva lifted the WTA Finals trophy in Indian Wells, and her post-match comments are already some of the most memorable quotes of the tennis season. On the moment she felt the most pressure in the final against Svitolina: "It was the second time in my life where I just thought I am going to die on the court but she is not getting the win." That is not a figure of speech — it is a window into how she thinks when things are hardest. She makes a decision, and she executes. On her celebrations: "I am going to celebrate, get a car." She was not being modest. The WTA Finals prize includes a Mercedes-AMG SL 55, and Andreeva, 19, was going to enjoy it. Her first words on court after winning: "I want to thank myself." Her one-word summary of her 2026 season: "consistent, happy and work." There is a clarity to how she talks about herself that matches the clarity of how she plays. She is 19, she is WTA No. 2, and she just became the third teenager in history to win the WTA Finals. What was your favorite part of Andreeva's 2026 WTA Finals run?
