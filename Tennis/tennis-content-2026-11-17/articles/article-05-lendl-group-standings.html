Lendl Group Standings: Alcaraz and Medvedev Both 1-0 as Tiafoe and Cobolli Need a Win

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | November 17, 2026

After the first round of Ivan Lendl Group matches at the ATP Finals in Turin, the standings are clear and the message to two of the four players is equally clear: win or go home. Carlos Alcaraz and Daniil Medvedev are both 1-0 and in strong positions to advance to the semifinals. Frances Tiafoe and Flavio Cobolli are both 0-1 and need to respond.

Alcaraz and Medvedev Set the Standard

Alcaraz set the tone on Monday afternoon with a 6-2, 6-2 demolition of Tiafoe in just 69 minutes. It was his 10th consecutive win, extending a form run that began with back-to-back titles in Vienna and Paris. He broke Tiafoe four of five times and looked in complete control from the opening game.

Medvedev followed in the evening session with a methodical 7-6(4), 6-3 win over Cobolli. He faced zero break points, won 86% of points on Cobolli's second serve, and needed 85 minutes. It was different tennis from Alcaraz's demolition — more patient, more positional — but the result was equally decisive.

Both wins came without real drama. That, in its own way, is a statement about the level of tennis both players are producing right now.

Tiafoe and Cobolli Must Respond

Tiafoe, making his ATP Finals debut, lost to the No. 2 seed. That is not a disgrace — it is a first-round group match against one of the best players in the world on current form. The challenge is what comes next. He faces Cobolli in his second group match, in a contest that amounts to a must-win for both debutants if either wants to stay in the draw.

Cobolli, the Italian who had the home crowd behind him all evening at the Inalpi Arena, ran into Medvedev at his most economical. The first-set tiebreak was competitive; the second set was not. Cobolli will need to produce his best tennis to beat Tiafoe and keep his semifinals hopes alive.

Alcaraz vs Medvedev: The Looming Group Decider

If both Alcaraz and Medvedev win their next group matches — Alcaraz faces Cobolli and Medvedev faces Tiafoe — their direct head-to-head on Wednesday becomes the effective group decider. These two have met in Grand Slam finals before, and their styles produce a compelling tactical contest. Medvedev's wall-like returns and baseline patience against Alcaraz's first-strike variety and athleticism is exactly the kind of match the ATP Finals was built around.

The result of that match, whenever it comes, will determine who tops the Ivan Lendl Group and who they face in the semifinals.

What's Next

The Lendl Group next matches will be scheduled across Day 4 and Day 5 of the ATP Finals. Watch the official ATP draw page for updated scheduling. The final round of group play determines the two Lendl Group semifinalists, and both spots are still very much up for discussion — though Alcaraz and Medvedev hold the advantage.
