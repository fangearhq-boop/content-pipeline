Medvedev Outduels Cobolli 7-6, 6-3 to Open ATP Finals Campaign

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | November 17, 2026

Daniil Medvedev opened his 2026 ATP Finals account Monday evening with a demonstration of the qualities that have made him one of the most reliable performers at this event. He beat Flavio Cobolli 7-6(4), 6-3 in 85 minutes, faced zero break points across the entire match, and won 86% of points when Cobolli hit a second serve. It was not spectacular. It was not meant to be. It was Medvedev tennis — thorough, controlled, and effective.

The Match

Cobolli, the Italian debutant playing in front of a home crowd at the Inalpi Arena, started the match well. The first set was competitive, with Cobolli keeping pace early and feeding off the energy of a crowd that wanted him to win. Medvedev is not easily rattled by crowd involvement. He managed the conditions, picked his spots, and took the tiebreak 7-4.

The second set was more straightforward. Medvedev's returning, his primary weapon on hard courts, returned to form after the tiebreak pressure of the first set. He began winning the majority of his second-serve points on return and found the kind of weight-of-shot consistency that makes it very difficult to hold serve when he is locked in. Cobolli, who served well throughout, was left with no clean looks at a break in the second set. Medvedev closed it out 6-3.

The zero break points conceded is the most telling statistic. Medvedev, who can be undone on days when his first serve percentage drops and his returns fall short, never found himself in a position of having to defend. That kind of match management under pressure in the opening round of a major round-robin event is exactly what a title contender needs.

Cobolli's Position

For Cobolli, the defeat leaves him at 0-1 in the Ivan Lendl Group, now needing a win over Frances Tiafoe in his next match to keep the semifinal door open. Tiafoe is also 0-1 after losing to Alcaraz 6-2, 6-2. The two debutants face each other in what will be a must-win for both — a match that will be watched closely both for its outcome and for what it says about whether either player can produce their best tennis after an opening-round loss to a top-two player.

What's Next

Medvedev faces Frances Tiafoe next in the Lendl Group, while Carlos Alcaraz will take on Cobolli. But the match that matters most for the group standings will be Alcaraz vs Medvedev on Wednesday — a collision between the two unbeaten players that will effectively determine who leads the group heading into the final round. For Medvedev, Monday's win is a solid foundation. The harder test is still to come.
