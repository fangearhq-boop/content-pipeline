2026 US Open Seeds Set: Zverev No. 1 Men, Sabalenka No. 1 Women; Draw Ceremony Thursday

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | August 24, 2026

The 2026 US Open seedings are final, determined by the world rankings as of Monday, August 24 — the day after the Cincinnati Open finals. The draw ceremony is Thursday, August 27. Main draw competition begins Sunday, August 30 at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center in Flushing, Queens.

This year's seedings carry more context than most, because the field has shifted significantly from what it looked like at the start of the summer. Jannik Sinner, the No. 1 player in the world for much of 2026, withdrew before Cincinnati with a right knee injury. That single absence reshaped the entire men's seeding structure.

Men's Seeds: Zverev No. 1 Without Sinner

Alexander Zverev enters the US Open as the No. 1 men's seed — a consequence of Sinner's withdrawal rather than a typical tournament outcome. Zverev is a major champion and one of the most dangerous players in the world on hard courts, so the No. 1 position is not a surprise in terms of quality. It is simply a different kind of assignment than it would have been in a year where Sinner was present.

Carlos Alcaraz is the No. 2 seed, returning from his April wrist injury. Novak Djokovic is seeded No. 4. The projected top 8 also includes Felix Auger-Aliassime (No. 3), Flavio Cobolli (No. 5, fresh from a Cincinnati semifinal run), Alex de Minaur (No. 6), Daniil Medvedev (No. 7), and Ben Shelton (No. 8).

The draw is genuinely wide open. No player in the bracket has played hard-court tennis at the top level this week — Cincinnati's finalists Fils and Tiafoe are unseeded or low-seeded relative to their current form. Alcaraz arrives without match practice. The bracket will not sort itself out cleanly at this US Open.

Women's Seeds: Sabalenka at the Top, Two Americans in Top Four

Aryna Sabalenka is the No. 1 women's seed. Despite losing to Sara Bejlek in Cincinnati's quarterfinals — one of the more stunning upsets of this summer — Sabalenka's season-long ranking holds her at the top of the draw. Iga Swiatek is the No. 2 seed after her Cincinnati semifinal loss to Jessica Pegula.

Coco Gauff, the Cincinnati champion, enters as the No. 3 seed. Jessica Pegula, who reached the Cincinnati final through a spectacular semifinal win over Swiatek, is the No. 4 seed. Two American women in the top four seeds is an uncommon occurrence in this era of international depth, and it sets up the possibility of multiple American deep runs at the home Slam.

Mirra Andreeva is projected as the No. 5 seed. The women's draw will carry significant intrigue around the quarter-bracket assignments — specifically, whether Gauff or Pegula lands in the same quarter as Sabalenka or Swiatek, which could produce a major before the final.

What's Next

The US Open draw ceremony takes place Thursday, August 27. When the draw is released, the bracket matchups for all 128 men's and women's singles players will be confirmed, including first-round opponents and potential quarterfinal paths. Main draw play begins Sunday, August 30. Fan Week is ongoing at Flushing Meadows, with the sold-out Federer, Roddick, McEnroe, and Agassi exhibition scheduled for Arthur Ashe Stadium tomorrow, August 25.
