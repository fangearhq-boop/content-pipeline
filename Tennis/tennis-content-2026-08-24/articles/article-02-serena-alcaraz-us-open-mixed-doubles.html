Serena Williams and Carlos Alcaraz Partner for 2026 US Open Mixed Doubles

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | August 24, 2026

The 2026 US Open mixed doubles draw now features a pairing that nobody predicted but everyone will watch. Serena Williams and Carlos Alcaraz have been granted a wildcard into the mixed doubles event, the United States Tennis Association announced Friday. It is Serena's first appearance at Flushing Meadows since the 2022 US Open, and Alcaraz's first Grand Slam competition since a right wrist injury ended his season in April.

Serena returned to professional tennis earlier in 2026 after a four-year absence. She will not play singles at this year's US Open — ESPN confirmed she is focusing on doubles only — but her presence at the venue where she won six of her Grand Slam singles titles carries its own gravity.

A Star-Studded Mixed Doubles Field

The mixed doubles draw was already notable before the Serena-Alcaraz announcement. Aryna Sabalenka and Novak Djokovic have entered as a pairing. Iga Swiatek and Casper Ruud, who reached the 2025 final, are back to make another run. Defending champions Sara Errani and Andrea Vavassori will attempt to hold their title.

Venus Williams also received a wildcard entry into the event, adding to the Williams sisters' presence at this year's US Open. The two have a long history at Flushing Meadows, and seeing both on the grounds simultaneously — even in separate competition — carries significance beyond the match results.

Alcaraz and Serena Williams are one of three wildcard pairs. They arrive with different preparation windows: Williams has been playing matches in 2026; Alcaraz is returning from months away. Their first-round draws and scheduling will be closely watched when the full order of play emerges.

Alcaraz's Return to the Grand Slams

Beyond the mixed doubles story, Alcaraz's confirmation for singles is significant. The Spaniard has been the most compelling force in men's tennis over the past three years, but the 2026 season was interrupted in April by the wrist injury. He missed the French Open. He missed Wimbledon. He missed the North American hard-court swing entirely.

His last major result before the injury was a runner-up finish at a tournament earlier this spring. The US Open will be his first Grand Slam since then. He enters unseeded in the traditional sense at this late stage, but his ranking was protected during his injury absence. He is confirmed as the No. 2 men's seed — a standing that reflects where he was before the injury rather than any recent hard-court matches.

For the first week of the US Open, the mixed doubles with Serena gives him a way to find court time, rhythm, and match intensity before singles begins on August 30.

What's Next

The US Open main draw begins August 30 at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center in Flushing, Queens. The draw ceremony is Thursday, August 27. Mixed doubles competition typically begins in the first week of the tournament alongside early-round singles. The full schedule and draw for mixed doubles will be released following the main draw ceremony.
