Cincinnati R3 Day: Swiatek vs Sakkari, Rybakina vs Frech, and Zverev Night Session

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | August 17, 2026

Monday is the biggest day of the Cincinnati Open so far. Five high-stakes third-round matches span the full schedule, from the morning session through a late-night ATP contest under the lights. By Tuesday, the quarterfinal picture — on both the men's and women's sides — will be nearly complete. With Novak Djokovic already gone and Ben Shelton eliminated in R2, the draw is as open as anyone can remember.

WTA R3: Rybakina, Swiatek, and Eala All On Court

The defending Cincinnati WTA champion Elena Rybakina (KAZ, No. 2 seed) opens the Monday schedule against Magdalena Frech (POL) at 12:30 PM on Stadium Court. Rybakina leads their head-to-head 3-0 overall, 2-0 on hard courts, but Frech has been consistent this season and will push for a first career win over the big-serving Kazakh.

At 3:00 PM, Iga Swiatek — seven wins in a row and coming off a 6-3, 6-0 demolition of Arango on Sunday — faces Maria Sakkari (GRE) on P&G Stadium Court. Their head-to-head is nearly even at 5-4 to Swiatek, and Sakkari remains one of the most dangerous unseeded threats in the draw. If Swiatek wins, she advances to the quarterfinals of a tournament she won in 2025.

The evening's wildcard match is Alexandra Eala (PHI) vs Amanda Anisimova (USA, approximately No. 10) — their first career meeting, scheduled for 8:30 PM. Eala won the DC Open two weeks ago and has a 22-9 record on hard courts in 2026. Anisimova hits the ball with exceptional power and is dangerous at this stage of a Masters event.

No. 1 seed Aryna Sabalenka does not play Monday — her R3 match against Wang Xinyu is scheduled for Tuesday August 18.

ATP R3: Tirante, Jodar, Zverev

The man everyone is watching is Thiago Agustín Tirante (ARG, No. 50), who stunned Djokovic in the second round on Saturday. His R3 opponent, Martín Landaluce (ESP), is a young Spaniard who beat Jack Draper and Matteo Arnaldi to get here — two serious scalps. Their previous meeting went to Landaluce in Miami 2026 qualifying. Whether Tirante can maintain the form that toppled a former world No. 1 will be Monday's ATP opener.

At 3:00 PM on Stadium Court, Rafael Jodar (ESP, 19) faces Alejandro Tabilo (CHI) in a match that has equal H2H history: Tabilo beat Jodar 6-1, 6-2 at Indian Wells 2026; Jodar beat Tabilo 6-7(6), 6-4, 6-4 at the Washington DC Open semifinal. Jodar is coming off back-to-back weeks of significant results — a Montreal semifinal run and then a dramatic shoelace-interrupted comeback over Shapovalov in R2 here.

The night's main event is No. 1 seed Alexander Zverev (GER) against Terence Atmane (FRA), not before 8:30 PM on P&G Stadium Court. Zverev survived a tight second-round match against Cameron Norrie, dropping the first set before winning 3-6, 6-3, 6-3. With Djokovic gone and Shelton eliminated, Zverev is the last major seed standing in the men's draw. His result tonight will shape the entire QF narrative.

What's Next

Tuesday's order of play will complete the third-round picture. Sabalenka vs Wang Xinyu and any remaining R3 matches will bring the quarterfinal bracket into full view. What is already clear is that Cincinnati 2026 will not be decided by the usual suspects in the usual ways — the upsets and absences have created an unusually open draw, and Monday's results will determine who takes advantage of it.
