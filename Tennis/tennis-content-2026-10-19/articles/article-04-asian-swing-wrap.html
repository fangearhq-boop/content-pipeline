2026 Asian Swing Wrap: Two First-Time Masters Champions and a Fortnight to Remember

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | October 19, 2026

TENNIS — WORLD TOUR

The 2026 Asian swing is over. Two weeks of tennis across Shanghai and Wuhan produced two first-time Masters champions, a handful of historic milestones, and more than a few storylines that will carry into the European indoor season.

Alejandro Davidovich Fokina won the Shanghai Rolex Masters — his first ATP Masters 1000 title and the first Shanghai title for a Spanish player. Maja Chwalinska won the Wuhan Open — her first WTA title, making her only the fourth player to win a WTA 1000 on debut in the category.

The Stories That Defined the Swing

Rafael Jodar announced himself as Spain's next big thing. The 20-year-old reached his first Masters 1000 final at Shanghai, beating Daniil Medvedev in a controversial semifinal before falling to Fokina. His ATP Finals race is alive and his ranking will reflect a significant boost.

Sonay Kartal produced arguably the fortnight's greatest comeback narrative. Ranked No. 170 after six months out injured, the British qualifier reached the Wuhan final — the first British woman to do so in the Open Era — before losing to Chwalinska. She leaves Wuhan in the WTA top 50.

Zheng Qinwen, who won the China Open in Beijing the week before, gave Chinese tennis two major talking points before the swing had even reached its midpoint.

What's Next for the ATP

The ATP heads indoors for the Paris Rolex Masters, October 31–November 8 at the Plenitude Arena. Jannik Sinner is the defending champion. Zverev, Alcaraz, Fokina, and Jodar headline the likely field. Wildcards include French players Cazaux, Rinderknech, Atmane, and Vacherot.

After Paris, the ATP Finals in Turin close out the year for the top eight players.

What's Next for the WTA

The WTA Finals are set for November 8-15 at the Indian Wells Tennis Garden in California, after the event's move from Riyadh. Sabalenka is confirmed in the field. Swiatek, despite her Wuhan exit, remains comfortably in contention. Chwalinska's rise to No. 11 puts her squarely in the race.

What's Next

The tour gets a brief pause before Paris. Watch for entry list and draw announcements for the Rolex Paris Masters this week, and expect the WTA Finals qualifying picture to sharpen significantly by month's end.
