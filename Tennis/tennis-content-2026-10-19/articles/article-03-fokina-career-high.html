Fokina Reaches Career-High No. 13 in the World Following Shanghai Masters Victory

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | October 19, 2026

SHANGHAI, China

Alejandro Davidovich Fokina climbed to a career-high world ranking of No. 13 on Monday, the official result of his maiden ATP Masters 1000 title at the 2026 Shanghai Rolex Masters. The Spaniard entered the tournament ranked No. 26 and leapt 13 spots by defeating compatriot Rafael Jodar 1-6, 7-6(5), 6-4 in Sunday's final.

The win was Fokina's third title of 2026 and made him the first Spanish player to win the Shanghai Masters in the event's history.

The Shanghai Campaign

Fokina's path to the title was not smooth. He received a walkover when French teenager Arthur Gea retired injured in their quarterfinal meeting. In the semifinals, he dispatched top seed Alexander Zverev 5-7, 6-4, 6-3 — a result that eliminated the world No. 1 from title contention and sent Fokina into his second career Masters 1000 final.

The final itself mirrored the semifinal in its volatility. Fokina dropped the first set 1-6 before turning the match around in set two's tiebreak and controlling set three. He came back from a set down to lift the trophy for the first time at Masters level.

Career Context

The No. 13 ranking surpasses Fokina's previous career high, reached in June 2025. He has been one of the ATP's most polarizing players — capable of brilliant tennis on any surface but prone to inconsistency that has frustrated supporters and analysts alike.

The Shanghai title, and the form he showed across the Asian swing, suggests a player who has found more consistency at the highest level. Three titles in a single season at his ranking is a genuine achievement.

What's Next

Fokina is expected to enter the Paris Rolex Masters field (October 31–November 8) as a seeded player for the first time at the Plenitude Arena. Defending champion Jannik Sinner and top seed Carlos Alcaraz headline the draw alongside Zverev, who will be looking to bounce back after his Shanghai semifinal exit.

With his ranking now at No. 13, Fokina is also in strong position for the ATP Finals in Turin in November, where the eight best players of the season compete for the year-end title.
