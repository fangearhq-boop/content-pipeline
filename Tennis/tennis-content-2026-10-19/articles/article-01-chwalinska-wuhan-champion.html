Chwalinska Wins Wuhan Open: First WTA Title Puts Her in the World Top 20

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | October 19, 2026

WUHAN, China

Maja Chwalinska climbed from a set and a break down to defeat Sonay Kartal 2-6, 6-4, 6-2 in the 2026 Wuhan Open final on Sunday, claiming her first career WTA title and entering the world's top 20 at a career-high No. 11.

The 22-year-old Pole, seeded No. 16 at the tournament, needed 2 hours and 43 minutes to complete the comeback. She dropped the first set to the free-swinging Kartal and fell behind early in the second before rattling off a run of games that tilted the match irreversibly in her favor.

Chwalinska won the final six games of the match, closing out the third set 6-2. It was a statement performance from a player who entered 2026 as the Roland-Garros runner-up and now leaves the Asian swing as a WTA 1000 champion.

Historic Company

Chwalinska became just the fourth player to win her first career WTA title at the 1000 level, joining Naomi Osaka (Indian Wells 2018), Bianca Andreescu (Indian Wells 2019), and Victoria Mboko. Winning at the highest tier of non-Grand Slam competition on debut is extraordinarily rare.

The win also capped a week in which she beat world No. 1 Iga Swiatek 7-5, 6-1 in the semifinals. Swiatek, dealing with a leg issue, was largely unable to sustain rallies in the second set.

"She's been one of the best players all season," said one analyst tracking the WTA race. "Beating Swiatek and then coming back to beat Kartal in the final — that's a week to remember."

Ranking Impact

Chwalinska's ranking moves from No. 22 to a career-high No. 11 effective Monday. She is now firmly in contention for the 2026 WTA Finals, scheduled for November 8-15 at the Indian Wells Tennis Garden in California.

The Polish player had already turned heads at Roland-Garros earlier in the year, reaching the final as a sub-20 seed. Sunday's result confirms she is not a one-tournament wonder — she is a genuine top-10 threat.

What's Next

Chwalinska will need to maintain her ranking over the next two weeks to secure a WTA Finals berth. The tour moves indoors with the Paris Rolex Masters (October 31–November 8) on the ATP side, while the WTA Finals begins November 8 at Indian Wells.

For Kartal, Sunday's runner-up finish delivers a guaranteed rise into the WTA top 50 — the result of one of the most extraordinary comeback runs in recent memory. Chwalinska, however, goes home with the trophy and a story that will follow her career for years to come.
