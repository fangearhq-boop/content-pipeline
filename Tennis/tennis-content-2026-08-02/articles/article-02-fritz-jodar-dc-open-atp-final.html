Fritz and Jodar Reach DC Open ATP Final — 19-Year-Old Spaniard Guaranteed Top 20 Debut

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | August 2, 2026

The DC Open ATP 500 final is set: Taylor Fritz (USA) against Rafael Jodar (Spain, age 19). Both players came from a set down to win their semifinals Saturday, and Jodar's run to the final has guaranteed him an ATP Top 20 debut this week — making him one of the youngest players in recent years to reach that landmark.

Fritz Overcomes Nakashima in All-American Semifinal

The day's first semifinal featured two Americans: No. 1 seed Fritz against unseeded Brandon Nakashima. Nakashima, who had stunned defending champion Alex de Minaur in the quarterfinals, came out aggressive and took the first set 6-3. Fritz, a veteran who has been in finals before without always converting, recalibrated. He won the second set 6-3 and then the third 6-3, using his serve and forehand to take control of the baseline exchanges he had lost in the opener.

Fritz's path to the final this week has required grinding through adversity: he saved a match point against Alex Michelsen in the quarterfinals before outlasting him, and then came from a set down against Nakashima Saturday. He arrives in the final having already proven he can win the close ones.

Jodar Does It Again: Another Comeback Win

In the second semifinal, 19-year-old Rafael Jodar added another three-set comeback to his increasingly remarkable week. After losing the first set to Alejandro Tabilo in a tiebreak — 7-5 to the Chilean — Jodar settled, played cleaner tennis, and took the next two sets 6-4, 6-4 to advance.

It was Jodar's second consecutive comeback win from a set down this week. He had also eliminated Alejandro Tabilo to reach the semifinals, having previously knocked out Arthur Fils in the second round and Lorenzo Musetti in the quarterfinals.

The run to the final has mathematical consequences: Jodar will debut inside the ATP Top 20 this week, regardless of whether he wins or loses Sunday. That is a landmark the 19-year-old from Madrid has achieved after winning his first ATP title on clay in Marrakech in April 2026. He now stands at his first ATP 500 final and his first on a hard court.

Final Preview: Fritz vs. Jodar

Taylor Fritz and Rafael Jodar have met once on the ATP Tour: Fritz won 7-6(4), 6-4 at the Delray Beach Open earlier in 2026. Fritz is the more experienced player, the higher seed, and the American playing effectively on home soil in Washington. The crowd will likely favor him.

But Jodar has shown all week that he is not intimidated by favorable odds running against him. He has beaten Fils, Nishikori, Musetti, and Tabilo — all of whom had reasons to expect victory. His movement and ball-striking on hard courts have impressed throughout the tournament.

The ATP final begins no earlier than 2:30 PM ET Sunday at the William H.G. FitzGerald Tennis Center.

What's Next

Sunday's DC Open ATP final — Fritz vs. Jodar — starts no earlier than 2:30 PM ET. Both players are expected to head to the Canadian Open in Montreal following the conclusion of the DC Open. The ATP Montreal main draw (IGA Stadium) opens August 4 with Alexander Zverev as the No. 1 seed. Fritz is also in the Montreal draw. Jodar's new Top 20 ranking will be reflected in the ATP standings published Monday.
