Correction: Sabalenka Is WTA Toronto No. 1 Seed, Not Pegula — Canadian Open Draw Starts Monday

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | August 2, 2026

A correction to the Canadian Open draw information published yesterday: Aryna Sabalenka (Belarus) is the No. 1 seed at the WTA Toronto National Bank Open, not Jessica Pegula. Additionally, the WTA main draw begins Monday, August 3 — not August 2 as previously stated. We regret the errors.

Correct WTA Toronto Top Seeds

The full WTA Toronto seedings at the top of the draw are as follows: Aryna Sabalenka (1), Elena Rybakina (2), Jessica Pegula (3), Coco Gauff (4). Sabalenka is the world No. 1 and one of the tour's most dominant players in 2026.

Pegula, whose correct seeding is No. 3 in Toronto, is currently playing the DC Open WTA final in Washington on Sunday. She will move to Toronto following the conclusion of the DC Open. Top 32 seeds in the WTA draw receive first-round byes and will open play in approximately the second round, beginning August 4-5.

The WTA Toronto main draw covers matches through August 13 at Sobeys Stadium. Prize money and ranking points at this WTA 1000 event are among the highest of the season outside the Grand Slams.

ATP Montreal: Zverev, FAA Lead a Wide-Open Field

At ATP Montreal, the IGA Stadium draw is led by Alexander Zverev (Germany), the No. 1 seed and Roland Garros 2026 champion. Felix Auger-Aliassime is seeded No. 2 at home — the first time the Canadian has been seeded as high as No. 2 at a Masters 1000 event. Taylor Fritz, fresh from the DC Open finals weekend, is also in the Montreal draw.

The ATP Montreal field is notably diminished by major withdrawals. Jannik Sinner (world No. 1) withdrew citing workload management before the US Open and Cincinnati. Carlos Alcaraz (No. 3) continues his recovery from a wrist injury and also skipped Montreal. Novak Djokovic also passed on the event for the second consecutive year. The tournament director publicly criticized ATP scheduling policy in response to the wave of high-profile absences.

Despite those absences, Zverev brings genuine title credentials as the Roland Garros champion, while Auger-Aliassime's home-crowd support and recent form make him a contender. ATP Montreal main draw matches begin Tuesday, August 4.

What's Next

WTA Toronto main draw matches begin Monday, August 3. ATP Montreal main draw matches begin Tuesday, August 4. Both tournaments run through August 13, with finals that weekend. The Canadian Open is one of the last major tuneups before the US Open begins August 24 in New York.
