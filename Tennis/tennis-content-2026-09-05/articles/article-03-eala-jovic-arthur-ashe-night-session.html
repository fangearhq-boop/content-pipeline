Friends and Rivals: Eala vs. Jovic Upgraded to Arthur Ashe as Philippine Tennis Watches

By Marcus Cole | September 5, 2026

No. 17 seed Alex Eala meets No. 14 Iva Jovic in the Arthur Ashe Stadium night session — a third-round match upgraded from Louis Armstrong due to overwhelming ticket demand, with Philippine tennis history on the line.

When the US Open announced its order of play for Saturday, there was one detail that caught many observers by surprise: the Eala-Jovic women's third round match had been moved from Louis Armstrong Stadium to Arthur Ashe.

The reason was ticket demand. Not for a semifinal. Not for a quarterfinal. For a third-round match between two women's seeds — because the two players involved have captured something real at this year's US Open, and the tournament noticed.

Alex Eala of the Philippines, No. 17 seed and the highest-ranked Filipino player in Grand Slam history, meets No. 14 Iva Jovic of the United States at 7 PM ET on the main stage in New York. They are close friends on Tour. They are separated by just a few ranking spots. And one of them is going to reach the US Open round of 16 tonight, with the other going home.

A Rivalry Built Between Friends

Eala and Jovic's friendship is genuine and well-documented — the kind of relationship that develops when two young players rise through the ranks at roughly the same time, crossing paths at academies and junior circuits before both breaking onto the WTA Tour.

On the court, Jovic has the edge. Their head-to-head stands at 2-0 in the American's favor heading into tonight's meeting. Eala will need to reverse that record on the biggest stage of her career to keep her historic US Open run alive.

Eala has been dominant in her two matches here. She dropped just five games over two rounds — 6-1, 6-2 over Stoiana in R1, then 6-1, 6-4 over Oliynykova in R2. She has looked composed, aggressive, and comfortable on the hardcourts of Flushing Meadows.

Jovic, meanwhile, won her R1 over Magdalena Frech 7-5, 6-3 and followed with a 6-4, 6-4 win over Francesca Jones. The 14th seed is in solid form and playing with confidence.

The Stakes for Philippine Tennis

Eala's tournament has already made history. As the No. 17 seed — the highest Grand Slam seeding in Philippine tennis history — she has cleared two rounds convincingly. A win over Jovic tonight would put her in the fourth round of a Grand Slam for the first time in her career.

The Philippines does not produce a lot of world-class tennis players. The country follows Eala with an intensity that few sports moments can match back home. Her matches at this US Open have aired across the archipelago in the early morning hours, with millions watching. Arthur Ashe Stadium tonight gives her the biggest platform she has ever had.

The pressure is real. So is the opportunity.

What's Next

Alex Eala vs. Iva Jovic plays as the night session at Arthur Ashe Stadium on Saturday, September 5, at approximately 7 PM ET. The match is televised on ESPN. The winner advances to the US Open round of 16 on Sunday, September 6 — where a spot in the quarterfinal awaits on the far side of the draw.
