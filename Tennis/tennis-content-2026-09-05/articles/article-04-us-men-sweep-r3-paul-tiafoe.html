Four Americans in US Open R4: Paul's Five-Set Grind and Tiafoe's Dominant Win Complete the Sweep

By Ryan Calloway | September 5, 2026

Tommy Paul survived a five-set war over Alexander Bublik 6-4, 3-6, 6-7(4), 6-1, 6-3 to join Frances Tiafoe — who handled Valentin Vacherot 6-4, 6-2, 6-4 — in the US Open round of 16, alongside Alcaraz and Shelton to give the United States four men in the fourth round at the same major.

By the time the afternoon session was done at the US Open on Saturday, the men's scoreboard told a story about American tennis that has been building for years.

Four men were through to the round of 16. Two of them are American-raised players who came up through the US system. One is Spain's most decorated young star who arrived on the American hardcourt circuit and made it his home. One is a Georgia-born power server who represents American thunder tennis in its rawest form. And together, they represent what might be the deepest US Open men's draw performance for American tennis in a very long time.

Tommy Paul and Frances Tiafoe punched their tickets to join Alcaraz and Shelton in the R4 on Saturday — Paul through grit, Tiafoe through authority.

Paul Survives the Bublik Test

No. 20 seed Tommy Paul needed every one of five sets to put away Kazakhstan's Alexander Bublik, the No. 15 seed, at Louis Armstrong Stadium. The final score was 6-4, 3-6, 6-7(4), 6-1, 6-3 — a match that swung multiple times before Paul's superior fitness and consistency wore Bublik down in the decisive sets.

Bublik is one of the most entertaining and unpredictable players on Tour — a serve-and-volleyer in an era of baseliners, a player who can produce extraordinary tennis and then give a set away with apparent indifference. Paul handled those swings patiently, and when Bublik dropped his focus in the fourth and fifth sets, Paul did not let him recover.

Paul's reward is a round of 16 date with Carlos Alcaraz on Arthur Ashe Stadium. Alcaraz leads the head-to-head 6-2 and has won their last five meetings, which is about as daunting a statistical summary as any player could face. But Paul earned this moment, and the New York crowd will give him every advantage they can.

Tiafoe Puts on a Show, Medvedev Awaits

Frances Tiafoe's match against No. 22 seed Valentin Vacherot was decidedly less dramatic but arguably more impressive. Tiafoe won 6-4, 6-2, 6-4 — clean, controlled, and clinical in a way that Tiafoe at his best can be when all his weapons are clicking together.

The R4 draw handed Tiafoe a matchup with Daniil Medvedev, who defeated Artur Rinderknech to advance. Tiafoe vs. Medvedev is exactly the kind of contrast that makes tennis endlessly interesting: Tiafoe's athleticism and aggression against Medvedev's mechanical baselinecontrol and defensive excellence. Arthur Ashe will be loud for this one.

What's Next

The US Open round of 16 takes place Sunday, September 6, with all four American men in action: Alcaraz vs. Paul and Tiafoe vs. Medvedev are the headliners, with Shelton vs. Tsitsipas also on the schedule. ESPN and ABC carry coverage from 11 AM ET, with the primetime session at 7 PM on ESPN. Sunday is when the tournament's second week truly begins to take shape.
