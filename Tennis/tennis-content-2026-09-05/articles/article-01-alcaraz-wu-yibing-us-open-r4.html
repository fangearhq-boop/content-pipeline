Alcaraz Rolls Into US Open R4 With Best Flushing Meadows Record in Open Era History

By Ryan Calloway | September 5, 2026

Defending champion Carlos Alcaraz defeated Wu Yibing 6-3, 6-4, 6-1 to extend his Grand Slam win streak to 17 and push his career record at Flushing Meadows to 27-3 — a .900 winning percentage that surpasses Pete Sampras, Roger Federer, and Novak Djokovic.

Carlos Alcaraz arrived at Arthur Ashe Stadium on Saturday afternoon and did what he almost always does in New York: he won, and he won convincingly.

The defending US Open champion defeated China's Wu Yibing 6-3, 6-4, 6-1 in straight sets, advancing to the round of 16 in just under 90 minutes. It was crisp, clean, and commanding — the kind of performance that reminds the field why Alcaraz at Flushing Meadows is a different proposition than Alcaraz anywhere else.

The win extended his current Grand Slam win streak to 17 matches. His career record at Flushing Meadows now stands at 27-3 — a .900 winning percentage that is the best of any player at this venue in the Open Era, ahead of Pete Sampras (.888), Roger Federer (.864), and Novak Djokovic (.856).

He is 23 years old.

Return From Injury, Uninterrupted

What makes these numbers more striking is the context. Alcaraz last played competitive tennis on April 14 in Barcelona, when a wrist tenosynovitis diagnosis ended his spring season and cost him Roland Garros and Wimbledon. He returned at the US Open — arguably the biggest stage in the sport — and has gone through his opening three rounds without dropping a set.

There is a word for this kind of return, and it is not "rust."

Alcaraz is now bidding to become the first man to defend his US Open title since Roger Federer won five consecutive titles from 2004 through 2008. Whether he reaches that milestone depends on what the draw puts in front of him — and the draw has produced a compelling round of 16 matchup.

R4: Alcaraz vs. Paul

No. 20 seed Tommy Paul survived a ferocious five-set battle against Alexander Bublik at Louis Armstrong Stadium, winning 6-4, 3-6, 6-7(4), 6-1, 6-3 to earn his place in the round of 16. Paul is a popular New York crowd favorite and a capable hardcourt player at his best.

The head-to-head is not in Paul's favor: Alcaraz leads 6-2 overall and has won their last five meetings. But Paul earned this moment the hard way, and Arthur Ashe at full voice can produce unexpected results.

On paper, Alcaraz is the overwhelming favorite. On a tennis court in New York, nothing is guaranteed — which is exactly what makes this worth watching.

What's Next

Carlos Alcaraz faces No. 20 seed Tommy Paul in the US Open round of 16 on Sunday, September 6, at Arthur Ashe Stadium. Coverage begins on ESPN at 11 AM ET with primetime at 7 PM ET on ESPN. Paul will need to beat an Alcaraz who is rounding into peak form to book a quarterfinal spot. For Alcaraz, a win would be his 18th consecutive Grand Slam match victory and keep the three-peat bid firmly on track.
