### STORY 1: Alcaraz def. Wu Yibing 6-3, 6-4, 6-1 (Tier 1)

**FB Long-Form — 8:00 AM ET**
Carlos Alcaraz showed exactly why he is the defending champion at the US Open on Saturday, cruising past China's Wu Yibing 6-3, 6-4, 6-1 at Arthur Ashe Stadium to reach the round of 16.

The win extended Alcaraz's Grand Slam win streak to 17 matches and pushed his career record at Flushing Meadows to an astonishing 27-3. That .900 winning percentage is the best ever recorded at the venue in the Open Era — better than Pete Sampras, Roger Federer, and Novak Djokovic. He is simply the best Flushing Meadows player of his generation.

What makes this even more remarkable? Alcaraz's last competitive match before this US Open was on April 14 in Barcelona. Four and a half months out with a wrist injury, and he has come back looking entirely untroubled. He's now 3-0 for the week without dropping a set.

Next up is No. 20 seed Tommy Paul, who had to survive a grueling five-set battle over Alexander Bublik (6-4, 3-6, 6-7, 6-1, 6-3) to earn this spot. Alcaraz leads the head-to-head 6-2 and has won their last five meetings. But Paul on a hardcourt in front of a New York crowd is not to be underestimated.
