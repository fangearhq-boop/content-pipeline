ATP Finals Day 2: Alcaraz and Medvedev Begin Group Stage Campaigns in Turin

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | November 16, 2026

After a compelling opening day in the John McEnroe Group, the 2026 Nitto ATP Finals in Turin shifts its spotlight Monday to the Ivan Lendl Group — and the names are unmistakable. Carlos Alcaraz and Daniil Medvedev both begin their title campaigns today at the Inalpi Arena, in a day that promises two distinct stories: a clash of personalities in the afternoon and a home-crowd atmosphere in the evening.

Afternoon: Alcaraz vs. Tiafoe

The day's first singles match puts World No. 2 Carlos Alcaraz against Frances Tiafoe, the American making his first ATP Finals appearance. Alcaraz leads their ATP head-to-head series 2-1, though their meetings have tended to produce high-intensity exchanges rather than straightforward outcomes.

Tiafoe, seeded seventh at this event, is one of four players making their ATP Finals debuts in Turin — the others being Arthur Fils, Rafael Jodar, and Flavio Cobolli. For Tiafoe, reaching this stage caps a strong 2026 season, and the main arena atmosphere at the Inalpi Arena is exactly the kind of stage where he tends to lift his level.

Alcaraz, meanwhile, enters without a title at this event. He is the No. 2 seed behind World No. 1 Alexander Zverev, who won his Day 1 opener Sunday. For Alcaraz, a first ATP Finals crown would mark the last major missing piece from his résumé at the season-ending event.

Evening: Medvedev vs. Cobolli

The evening session brings its own storyline. Daniil Medvedev (No. 4) faces Flavio Cobolli (No. 5) — an Italian making his ATP Finals debut in his home country. Cobolli's presence in Turin has generated substantial local excitement, and the crowd will be decidedly in his corner.

Medvedev leads their head-to-head series 2-1 and brings the experience of a player who has competed at this event multiple times. He has not previously won the ATP Finals singles title, adding another layer of motivation to his campaign. Cobolli, by contrast, has the momentum of a breakout 2026 season and the energy of competing at home — a combination that has undone more experienced opponents before.

Day 1 Context: McEnroe Group Results

Sunday delivered two McEnroe Group wins that set the tone for the week. World No. 1 Alexander Zverev — a two-time ATP Finals champion — opened his title defense with a clinical 6-4, 6-4 win over Spanish debut qualifier Rafael Jodar. The result was controlled from the first game and never in serious doubt.

The evening session was more competitive. Ben Shelton (No. 3) outlasted French debutant Arthur Fils 6-2, 3-6, 6-3 in a three-setter that required the American to close out a service break in the final set. Fils, reaching the ATP Finals for the first time, became the first Frenchman to compete in the season-ending event since Gaël Monfils did so in 2016.

The Format and What's at Stake

The eight players are split across two four-man round-robin groups, with the top two in each advancing to the semifinals. Each player in the Ivan Lendl Group plays the other three opponents once. With four players and three rounds, every match carries direct implications for semifinal qualification — a loss today does not eliminate, but it removes all margin for error.

What's Next

Tuesday brings Day 3, when the John McEnroe Group resumes. Zverev and Shelton — winners on Day 1 — both play their second group matches, with Day 1 losers Jodar and Fils looking to avoid an early elimination. The Lendl Group results from today will also begin to shape the semifinal picture heading into the second half of the round-robin stage.
