ATP Finals Day 1 Recap: Zverev and Shelton Win in McEnroe Group

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | November 16, 2026

The 2026 Nitto ATP Finals opened Sunday at the Inalpi Arena in Turin with two McEnroe Group matches that set up very different kinds of drama. World No. 1 Alexander Zverev was precise and efficient. Ben Shelton needed to grind out a three-setter. Both left the court as winners, and both now carry a full head start into Tuesday's second round of group matches.

Zverev 6-4, 6-4 over Jodar

The opening match of the 2026 tournament was a statement from the top seed. Zverev, a two-time ATP Finals champion and the World No. 1, faced Spain's Rafael Jodar — a debut-year qualifier appearing at the season-ending event for the first time. The result was controlled from start to finish.

Zverev won 6-4, 6-4 in a performance that showed no signs of a player adjusting to conditions or finding his footing. He broke early in each set and defended those breaks without difficulty. For Jodar, who earned his place in the field through his results during the 2026 season, the loss came against a player who has been here before and knows exactly how to manage the occasion.

Zverev's win kept him on track in a tournament he has won twice before. With the top two players advancing from each group to the semifinals, a Day 1 win puts him in a commanding position heading into his next two group matches.

Shelton 6-2, 3-6, 6-3 over Fils

The evening session delivered something different. Ben Shelton (No. 3) and Arthur Fils (No. 6) went the distance, with the American ultimately winning 6-2, 3-6, 6-3 in a match that went through several momentum shifts before settling in Shelton's favor.

Shelton controlled the first set, broke early in the third, and held his nerve when Fils began to push. The Frenchman won the second set convincingly and had the crowd anticipating a tighter match, but Shelton converted his break in the third set and served out the match without being threatened again.

For Fils, the result was a loss on a stage he reached by putting together one of the best seasons of his career. He is the first Frenchman to compete at the ATP Finals since Gaël Monfils participated in 2016. The 10-year gap reflects how few French players have reached the top eight in the world by November's end, and Fils's presence here is worth acknowledging regardless of Monday's scoreline.

McEnroe Group Standings After Day 1

Zverev and Shelton lead the John McEnroe Group with one win each. Jodar and Fils each have one loss. With two rounds of group play remaining, the positions are far from settled — but both Day 1 winners have put themselves in the best possible position ahead of Tuesday.

What's Next

Monday is Ivan Lendl Group day, with Carlos Alcaraz and Daniil Medvedev both opening their campaigns. The McEnroe Group returns Tuesday, when Zverev and Shelton play their second matches — and Jodar and Fils face must-win pressure after their opening losses. Turin is only just getting started.
