### Long-Form Post Elina Svitolina's run to the 2026 WTA Finals final deserves to be remembered on its own terms. Eight years after winning the title in 2018, the 32-year-old Ukrainian made it back to the final, defeating Linda Noskova 6-2, 6-1 in the semifinals. It was a remarkable return for a player who has dealt with serious physical setbacks and carried the weight of representing Ukraine throughout. In the final, she lost to Andreeva 7-5, 6-3. What followed at the trophy ceremony drew more attention than the match itself. Svitolina declined the post-match handshake — consistent with the Ukrainian Tennis Federation's standing policy regarding Russian and Belarusian players. Her runner-up speech thanked the crowd, her team, and Ukraine, but did not mention the champion. Fans have been divided over whether the omission was understandable or inappropriate. Andreeva, to her credit, called Svitolina's season "amazing." Svitolina acknowledged the remark with a nod. There is no clean way to cover this story.
