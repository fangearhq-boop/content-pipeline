### STORY 1: Shelton defends Montreal title
**Sources:** ATP Tour official, Tennis Majors, Sofascore, Perfect Tennis
**Key facts:**
- Ben Shelton def. Brandon Nakashima 6-3, 7-6(4) in Montreal ATP 1000 final
- Shelton did not drop a single set in the entire tournament (5 matches)
- Joins Andy Murray (2009-10), Novak Djokovic (2011-12), Rafael Nadal (2018-19) as only men in Open Era to defend Canadian Masters title
- First man since Nadal (2018-19) to successfully defend the Canadian Masters crown
- Shelton was 1-4 at Masters 1000 events in 2026 entering this tournament
- Quote: "One match can change everything" (ATP Tour)
- Shelton was injured mid-match yesterday (Aug 13 SF) — collided with LCD panel, bleeding elbow/hand — but recovered to win final
- Doubles final also won by Orlando Luz/Rafael Matos (BRA) — first Brazilian pair to win ATP Masters 1000 doubles title
**Confidence:** HIGH (ATP Tour official source + Tennis Majors + Sofascore)
