**Type:** FOLLOW-UP (2026-10-31 S2: Basel SFs → final day)
**Byline:** Ryan Calloway
**Posting window:** 8:00 AM ET (final preview), 3:00 PM ET (result/reaction)

Neither Jiří Lehečká nor Valentin Vacherot was supposed to be in today's Basel final, but here they are. Vacherot, ranked No. 31 and representing Monaco, beat sixth seed Taylor Fritz 7-6(5), 6-3 to become the first player from Monaco to reach the Swiss Indoors final. Lehečká, the No. 8 seed ranked 21st, came back from a set and match point down to beat Jan-Lennard Struff 3-6, 7-5, 6-4. Their head-to-head is 1-1: Lehečká won in Tokyo this season, Vacherot won in Paris last year. Lehečká is the favorite, but this has been an unpredictable week in Basel. There's also a notable subplot: Vacherot is drawn against Djokovic in the Paris Masters first round starting Monday — a challenging turnaround if he wins today.

### STORY 4: ATP Race to Turin — De Minaur Holds Ninth, Fritz Fading, Three Spots Still Open
**Tier:** T2
**Type:** FOLLOW-UP (2026-10-31 S4: Medvedev clinches → Race update with Paris approaching)
**Byline:** Marcus Cole
**Posting window:** 12:30 PM ET

As the final Masters 1000 event of 2026 gets underway in Paris, four spots remain at the Nitto ATP Finals (November 15–22, Turin). Alexander Zverev, Carlos Alcaraz, Ben Shelton, and Daniil Medvedev are the four confirmed qualifiers. By reaching the Vienna final, Alex de Minaur moved to 3,210 Race points — one more than Rafael Jodar in eighth place. A Vienna title today would add 500 points (300 already banked for reaching the final), pushing him to approximately 3,380 and a clearer margin. Taylor Fritz, who lost to Vacherot in the Basel semifinal, entered this week as a legitimate contender but will need a strong Paris run; his hopes were described as "fading" after the loss. Djokovic, Arthur Fils (injured at Basel), and Frances Tiafoe round out the contenders fighting for the remaining spots.

The 2026 WTA Finals are one week away. Play begins Saturday, November 8, at Indian Wells Tennis Garden and runs through Sunday, November 15. Her absence leaves the tournament genuinely open, with at least four players capable of winning.
