### Finalists
- **Arthur Fery** (GBR, 24 years old)
  - Wimbledon 2026 SF — major breakthrough

  - First ATP Tour final
  - Known for serve-and-volley, net game

  - SF result: def. Benjamin Bonzi (FRA) 6-2, 4-6, 6-1

  - Spanish coaching/training background
  - Strong clay background but improving on hard courts

### Final Details
- Venue: Winston-Salem, NC (BB&T Arena / Wake Forest campus)
- Scheduled: Saturday Aug 29 at 4 PM ET
- Broadcast: Tennis TV, ESPN
- Significance: Last ATP Tour event before US Open main draw begins Aug 30
