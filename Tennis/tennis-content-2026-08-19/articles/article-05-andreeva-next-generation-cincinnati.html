Born in 2007, Making Quarterfinals: Andreeva Leads Next Gen's Cincinnati Moment

By Marcus Cole | August 19, 2026 | Tennis Fanrecap

Mirra Andreeva was born in 2007. She has just reached the quarterfinals of the Cincinnati Open, one of the largest tennis events outside the Grand Slams. The numbers require a moment to absorb.

Andreeva (RUS) defeated Janice Tjen 6-1, 7-6(5) on Wednesday in the Round of 16, moving through a draw that includes the WTA's best players and coming out the other side with a quarterfinal appointment against Marta Kostyuk (UKR, No. 10 seed). Kostyuk leads the head-to-head 2-1 — Andreeva's toughest test yet in this event.

A Generation Arriving Early

Andreeva is not the only young player making noise at this Cincinnati event. Rafael Jodar (ESP) is 19 years old and advanced to the Round of 16 after dismissing Alejandro Tabilo 6-2, 6-1, dropping only three games. Jodar had a Masters 1000 semifinal appearance in Montreal earlier this summer. He arrived in Ohio and continued his season without missing a beat.

Brandon Nakashima (USA, 22) — not truly a teenager anymore but very much part of this emerging generation — just dismantled Daniil Medvedev in three sets, saving three match points along the way. He's now in the Cincinnati quarterfinals off the back of a Montreal final appearance.

In the span of one Cincinnati week, players in their teens and early twenties have eliminated multiple former world No. 1s and multiple Grand Slam champions. The next generation of tennis is not waiting its turn.

Andreeva's Road Ahead

Winning this Cincinnati quarterfinal would represent Andreeva's biggest result of the season. Kostyuk has her number in their recent meetings, but the Ukrainian withdrew from her previous Cincinnati match due to the ankle injury that ended Svitolina's run. Andreeva has played fearless tennis all week. The quarterfinal will tell us how ready she is for the very top level.

What's Next

Andreeva faces Kostyuk in the Cincinnati quarterfinals on Thursday, August 20. The US Open draw is announced August 27, and the main draw begins August 30 — another chance for this new generation to make its mark at a Grand Slam.
