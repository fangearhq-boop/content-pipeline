Sabalenka, Gauff, and Andreeva Advance; Svitolina Withdraws From Cincinnati

By Marcus Cole | August 19, 2026 | Tennis Fanrecap

Three WTA heavyweights dominated their Cincinnati Round of 16 matches on Wednesday while Elina Svitolina's tournament — and her pre-US Open preparation — came to an abrupt end.

Aryna Sabalenka, Coco Gauff, and Mirra Andreeva all advanced in clinical fashion. Svitolina withdrew with the right ankle injury she initially sustained during her Canadian Open semifinal run in Toronto, eliminating one of the event's top contenders and sending a ripple of concern through the US Open picture.

Sabalenka Cruises in 54 Minutes

Sabalenka (BLR, No. 1 seed) was barely tested, winning 6-1, 6-3 in just 54 minutes. The WTA No. 1 has been one of the most dominant players at this event, and her efficiency on Wednesday was a reminder of why she is the clear title favorite in Cincinnati. She did not face a break point in the match.

Gauff Dominant Over Ann Li

Coco Gauff (USA) was similarly impressive against Ann Li, winning 6-1, 7-6(3). The American dropped only one game in the opening set before Li pushed her to a tiebreak in the second. Gauff closed it out comfortably. She advances to the Cincinnati quarterfinals with her serve and forehand looking sharp heading into the US Open.

Andreeva Advances; Faces Kostyuk in QF

Mirra Andreeva (RUS) defeated Janice Tjen 6-1, 7-6(5) to book her place in the Cincinnati quarterfinals. The 17-year-old, who was born in 2007, continues her quiet march through the draw. Her reward is a quarterfinal against Marta Kostyuk (UKR, No. 10 seed), who leads the head-to-head 2-1.

Svitolina Out — US Open Concern

The day's most significant development was Svitolina's withdrawal. The Ukrainian, seeded No. 9, had been one of the more consistent players on the WTA tour during the North American swing after her Canadian Open semifinal run. The same right ankle that gave her trouble in Toronto has now ended her Cincinnati campaign. Her draw position passes via walkover, with Wang Xinyu advancing into a quarterfinal against Madison Keys.

Svitolina's ability to recover before the US Open — main draw August 30 — will be closely watched over the next 11 days.

What's Next

The Cincinnati WTA quarterfinals are scheduled for Thursday, August 20. Sabalenka and Gauff are among the favorites to advance to the semifinals. Andreeva faces her toughest test yet against Kostyuk. The US Open draw is announced August 27, with the main draw beginning August 30.
