Cincinnati ATP Round of 16 Preview: Zverev, de Minaur, Jodar All in Action

By Ryan Calloway | August 19, 2026 | Tennis Fanrecap

The Cincinnati ATP draw has been dramatically reshaped. Novak Djokovic fell to Thiago Agustin Tirante in the second round. Daniil Medvedev was stunned by Brandon Nakashima in a three-set thriller. What remains is a Round of 16 with genuine intrigue — and a path to the title that is more open than anyone predicted before the tournament began.

Three matches stand out on Wednesday's schedule.

Zverev vs. Paul — The Marquee Match

Alexander Zverev (GER, No. 1 seed) against Tommy Paul (USA) is the day's most anticipated contest. Zverev has been the tournament's steadiest presence through the early rounds, grinding through a pair of tiebreaks to beat Terence Atmane in the third round and extending his 2026 tour win total to 46. Paul, a natural hard-court player who thrives in the Cincinnati atmosphere, represents a genuine test.

Zverev has been the logical US Open favorite since Jannik Sinner withdrew from Cincinnati with a knee injury. A deep run here would reinforce that status. Paul has the game to push him.

de Minaur vs. Fils — Speed vs. Power

Alex de Minaur (AUS) faces Arthur Fils (FRA) in a matchup that pits two of the tour's fastest players against each other. De Minaur survived a rain-interrupted third round match to advance. Fils, who was part of the all-2000s Montreal quarterfinal just weeks ago, brings aggressive ball-striking and clay-court confidence that transfers well to hard courts.

The winner likely faces the Zverev-Paul winner in the semifinals.

Jodar vs. Cobolli — The Teen Continues

Rafael Jodar (ESP, 19) has been one of the tournament's stories regardless of what happens Wednesday. After dropping just three games against Alejandro Tabilo in the third round, the Spanish teenager now faces Flavio Cobolli (ITA) in what should be a competitive baseline battle. Jodar's 2026 season — which includes a Masters 1000 semifinal in Montreal — marks him as one of the sport's most exciting young players.

What's Next

Winners of Wednesday's Round of 16 matches advance to the Cincinnati quarterfinals, scheduled for Thursday, August 20. The US Open draw is announced August 27, with the main draw beginning August 30. Cincinnati results carry significant weight for seedings and momentum heading into the year's final Grand Slam.
