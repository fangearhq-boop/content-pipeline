### STORY 1: Shanghai Shock — Hurkacz Beats Djokovic for First Time, Ends Four-Time Champion's Run
- **Tier:** T1
- **Type:** FOLLOW-UP (Oct 8 S3: Shanghai Day 2, Hurkacz vs. Djokovic R2 set up → R2 RESULT)
- **Byline:** Elena Voss
- **Summary:** Hubert Hurkacz delivered the biggest upset of the Asian swing on Friday (Oct 9), defeating Novak Djokovic 6-4, 6-3 in the second round of the Rolex Shanghai Masters — the first time Hurkacz has beaten Djokovic in their head-to-head history (was 0-8 all-time).
