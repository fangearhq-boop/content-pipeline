Swiatek Plays for Serena's WTA 1000 Record in WTA Beijing Quarterfinals

By Ryan Calloway | October 9, 2026

Beijing, China

Iga Swiatek arrived at the WTA Beijing quarterfinals on Friday with something specific on the line. Win, and she becomes the player with the most WTA 1000 main-draw victories in the history of the tour — surpassing Serena Williams' 148 all-time. Swiatek tied that record in her Round of 16 win over Iva Jovic two days ago.

Standing between her and No. 149 is Elise Mertens, the Belgian veteran who has been in superb form this week after shocking No. 3 seed Coco Gauff 6-3, 6-3 in their last match — Mertens' first win over Gauff in six meetings. Swiatek has beaten Mertens in both of their previous encounters, both in straight sets. Oddsmakers listed Swiatek as a heavy favorite.

The match was scheduled for Friday, October 9, in Beijing. With the semifinal field set for Saturday and the final on Sunday, the Beijing draw is reaching its conclusion — and Swiatek has navigated it well despite being seeded No. 8, far below where she spent much of her recent career.

What the Record Means

At her peak, Swiatek was untouchable at the WTA 1000 level. She won multiple titles at this tier and spent years accumulating the main-draw victories that have brought her to the brink of Williams' record. The record will not restore her to the top of the rankings — she sits 10th in the WTA Race to Finals at 3,584 points, 650 outside the last qualifying spot — but it will cement her place in the sport's historical narrative regardless of what the rankings say in 2026.

Williams set her 148-win mark over a career that produced 23 Grand Slam singles titles and redefined what professional tennis could look like. Swiatek would pass it at age 25, with potentially years of WTA 1000 play still ahead of her.

The Rest of the Draw

The other quarterfinals Friday give the draw its full flavor. Home favorite Qinwen Zheng faces No. 6 seed Elina Svitolina in their first-ever meeting. Zheng reached the quarterfinals by rallying from a set down to beat Alina Charaeva 6-1, 2-6, 6-4, and the home crowd in Beijing has been fully behind her run. Svitolina, the Ukrainian veteran, defeated Ann Li 6-2, 6-3 to reach her 29th WTA 1000 quarterfinal — a number that reflects just how consistent she has been at the highest level of the tour for years.

The fourth quarterfinal pits No. 4 seed Mirra Andreeva against No. 19 seed Ekaterina Alexandrova — a match with a surprising head-to-head dynamic. Alexandrova leads their H2H 2-0, with wins in Stuttgart and Bad Homburg earlier in the season. For Andreeva, winning this match would erase what has become an uncomfortable data point against a lower-ranked opponent.

What's Next

Semifinals are scheduled for Saturday, October 10, with the final on Sunday, October 11. For Swiatek, the Beijing draw presents a genuine path to a title and the WTA Finals qualification points she needs to break into the top eight. A quarterfinal win alone would not close the gap — she needs 650 more points to reach the last qualifying spot — but a title or a deep run changes the math considerably.

This has been a chaotic WTA Beijing draw. Elena Rybakina, the world No. 1, fell in the first round. Aryna Sabalenka lost in the second round. Gauff, the No. 3 seed and defending champion, fell to Mertens. What remains is a field where the next two days will reward whoever has the most energy and consistency in a packed end-of-season schedule.
