Shanghai Masters Day 3 — Alcaraz and Medvedev Enter an Open Draw

By Elena Voss | October 9, 2026

Shanghai, China

Two days into the Rolex Shanghai Masters, the story is not who is advancing. It is who is already gone.

Novak Djokovic, a four-time Shanghai champion who arrived off his 102nd career title in Beijing, was beaten by Hubert Hurkacz 6-4, 6-3 in the second round Friday morning — his earliest-ever Shanghai Masters exit. Jannik Sinner was never here. No. 7 seed Holger Rune fell in the first round to qualifier Daniel Altmaier.

Into this widened draw, Carlos Alcaraz and Daniil Medvedev step on Day 3. Both had first-round byes. Neither has hit a competitive ball yet in Shanghai. The adjustment from bench to court is something each player handles differently, and the draw they're walking into is not what the seedings suggested when the week began.

Alcaraz Enters

Alcaraz is seeded No. 4 in Shanghai, a reflection of where his season has taken him. He arrives in China off a title defense at the Japan Open in Tokyo, where he defeated Jiri Lehecka 7-6(4), 6-4 — his first to defend the Japan Open title since Pete Sampras in 1994 and his first title since returning from a wrist injury. Alcaraz's form in Shanghai is unknown entering Day 3, but his match sharpness from Tokyo is a legitimate advantage.

He is also one of the players most squarely in the Race to Turin bubble picture, needing a strong Shanghai week to move up the standings. With Djokovic out, one of the larger point hauls available in the bracket has shifted — everyone below Zverev in the Race is now recalculating.

The Altmaier Problem

While the seeded players take their turns, Daniel Altmaier is the story no one planned for. The German qualifier came into the tournament ranked outside the top 100. He beat No. 7 seed Holger Rune 1-6, 7-6(5), 6-4 in the first round to establish himself as a legitimate threat. Now he faces No. 5 seed Ben Shelton — one of the most powerful servers on the ATP Tour.

Altmaier has the results to show he belongs in this draw. Shelton has the firepower to end the story quickly. The match is one of the more intriguing Day 3 matchups on a card that also features the headliners making their first appearances.

What's Next

The third round continues through the weekend, with the fourth round and quarterfinals to follow next week. Alexander Zverev — the top seed, now in the third round after defeating Wu Yibing despite an ankle scare — will be watching closely as his half of the draw fills in. The tournament runs through October 18.

For a draw that was expected to feature Zverev, Sinner, Alcaraz, Medvedev, Djokovic, and more, the Shanghai Masters 2026 is already producing a different narrative. The field is open. The points are available. The players entering on Day 3 know exactly what that means.
