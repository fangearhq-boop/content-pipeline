Jacquet Stuns Etcheverry in Career-Best Win to Reach Hangzhou Open Quarterfinals

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | September 26, 2026

Kyrian Jacquet produced the most significant victory of his ATP career Friday at the Hangzhou Open, defeating No. 3 seed Tomás Martín Etcheverry of Argentina 6-2, 6-3 to advance to the quarterfinals of the ATP 250 event.

The Frenchman was clinical throughout. He broke Etcheverry's serve early in both sets and never allowed the Argentine to settle into the match — a result that speaks to how well Jacquet has played throughout the week and to the wide-open nature of a field missing most of the ATP's top ten, who are competing at Laver Cup in London.

Jacquet will face sixth seed Fabian Marozsan (Hungary) in the quarterfinals. Marozsan advanced with a grind against Taro Daniel on Friday, requiring three sets to get through after dropping the first: 6-7(2), 7-5, 7-6(1). It was the kind of win that takes something out of a player, and Jacquet — coming in on the momentum of his upset — could be in favorable shape heading into that quarterfinal.

Rublev and Gaston Set to Meet

The other confirmed quarterfinal pairing in the draw will be No. 2 seed Andrey Rublev against Hugo Gaston. Rublev advanced past Rinky Hijikata in the Round of 16, while Gaston had to go the distance against Jaime Faria, eventually winning 4-6, 7-6(5), 6-4 in a 2-hour-46-minute contest. The Gaston-Faria match was one of the more extended bouts of the week.

Rublev enters as the clear favorite in that matchup but Gaston is a notoriously awkward opponent — his lefty slice and unconventional game create difficulties for players more comfortable with rhythm from the baseline.

Medvedev's Status Pending

No. 1 seed Daniil Medvedev was scheduled to make his Hangzhou debut in the Round of 16 against Valentin Royer, but the result of that match was not yet confirmed at time of writing. Medvedev, who received a first-round bye as the tournament's top seed, has not competed since the US Open and enters this stretch of the Asian swing looking to build points for the Race to Turin and the ATP Finals.

What's Next

Hangzhou Open quarterfinals are scheduled for Saturday and Sunday, September 26-27. If Medvedev and Rublev both advance, a semifinal between two of the ATP's biggest names looms. Jacquet's run continues to be the tournament's most compelling narrative, and a win over Marozsan would put him within one match of a final. The Hangzhou title carries ATP 250 ranking points and prize money toward the Race to Turin.
