Sabalenka Crushes Noskova 6-4, 6-3, Keeps Three-Peat Dream Alive at US Open

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | September 8, 2026

US Open 2026 | Aryna Sabalenka | Quarterfinals | WTA

Aryna Sabalenka delivered a clinic on Tuesday at the 2026 US Open, dismantling Wimbledon champion Linda Noskova 6-4, 6-3 in just 75 minutes to advance to the semifinals. The world No. 1 never faced a serious threat, breaking Noskova twice in each set and finishing with 14 winners against 14 unforced errors.

The win keeps alive one of the most compelling storylines in women's tennis this season: Sabalenka's bid to become the first player to win three consecutive US Open titles since Serena Williams completed that feat in 2014.

A Performance Built on Dominance

From the first game, Sabalenka dictated terms from the baseline. She landed two aces and committed just two double faults throughout the match — a sign of the serving precision that has become her trademark at Flushing Meadows. Noskova, who stunned the tennis world by winning Wimbledon earlier this year, could find no foothold against the relentless power coming from the opposite side of the net.

The 6-4 first set gave Sabalenka the early momentum, and the 6-3 second set was never genuinely in doubt. The match clock read one hour and 15 minutes when Sabalenka closed it out — businesslike, focused, and fully in control.

The Three-Peat Target

Sabalenka won the US Open in 2024 and again in 2025. A third consecutive title would place her in extraordinarily rare company. Since the Open Era began, only Serena Williams has won three straight US Opens — she accomplished it in 2012, 2013, and 2014.

The Belarusian world No. 1 has made no attempt to downplay the significance of what she is chasing. She is now two wins away from the title. Standing in her way in the semifinals is the winner of Tuesday's evening match between Coco Gauff and Mirra Andreeva.

Noskova's Summer Ends Here

For Noskova, the quarterfinal exit marks the end of a remarkable summer that included her Wimbledon title. The 20-year-old Czech player reached the US Open quarterfinals for the first time in her career, which in itself represents significant progress. Against Sabalenka at her best, however, there was simply no path to victory.

Noskova managed to hold service in most games but could not create the break opportunities needed to stay in either set. Sabalenka's return game was too precise, and her groundstrokes too heavy for Noskova to redirect.

What's Next

Sabalenka will face either Coco Gauff or Mirra Andreeva in the semifinals, scheduled for Thursday at Arthur Ashe Stadium. Gauff, the 2023 US Open champion, and Andreeva, the 2026 French Open winner, meet in the evening session on Tuesday. Their quarterfinal result will shape what promises to be a must-watch semifinal.

Sabalenka has now won 16 consecutive matches at the US Open across her last three appearances. History suggests she is the player to beat — but two more must-win matches stand between her and a third consecutive title.
