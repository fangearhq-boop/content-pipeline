US Open QF Day 2 Preview: Zverev Faces the Dutchman, Pegula Meets Navarro in All-American Clash

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | September 8, 2026

US Open 2026 | Alexander Zverev | Jessica Pegula | Emma Navarro | Quarterfinals

With the first four quarterfinals underway Tuesday, Wednesday brings the other side of the US Open draw into focus. No. 1 seed Alexander Zverev defends his title against Botic van de Zandschulp. And on the women's side, an all-American quarterfinal guarantees that a U.S. woman will be in the semifinals: No. 3 Jessica Pegula against No. 26 Emma Navarro.

Two matches. Four compelling storylines. Here is what to watch for on Wednesday at Flushing Meadows.

Zverev vs. Van de Zandschulp: The Champion's Caution

Defending champion Alexander Zverev is the heavy favorite in his quarterfinal, but the German world No. 1 himself has declined to treat it as a formality. "Botic maybe loves this tournament more than any other," Zverev said this week — an acknowledgment that van de Zandschulp's comfort level at the US Open is genuine, and that upsets at this stage are possible when a player arrives with that kind of confidence in a specific venue.

Van de Zandschulp earned his spot with a gritty win over lucky loser Arthur Gea in the fourth round — a match that tested his endurance before giving him a slot in the last eight. His serve and forehand are legitimate weapons at hardcourt pace. Zverev, who won the title here in 2025, will enter as the commanding favorite, but a Dutchman who feels at home at Ashe is not a player to dismiss.

Pegula vs. Navarro: Revenge or Repeat?

The women's all-American quarterfinal between Jessica Pegula and Emma Navarro carries a direct piece of unfinished business. At the 2024 US Open, Navarro defeated Pegula at this stage of the tournament en route to a semifinal run. Two years later, they meet again — same tournament, same round, same court.

Pegula, the No. 3 seed, has been one of the most consistent performers in women's tennis over the past three seasons. She entered the quarterfinals with straight-set wins throughout and brings the kind of baseline reliability that wears opponents down over the course of a best-of-three match.

Navarro's rise to No. 26 in the world has been built on her ability to disrupt higher-ranked players at Grand Slams specifically. She defeated Coco Gauff at the 2024 US Open — a result that announced her as a threat at the highest level. Her counterattacking game, which exploits pace and redirects it with precision, is well-suited to Flushing Meadows hardcourt.

An American Woman Is Going to the Semis

Regardless of Wednesday's result, the US Open women's draw will send an American player into the semifinals. Sabalenka's side of the draw already features Gauff or Andreeva; Pegula or Navarro will face the winner of Zverev's half on the women's side. For American tennis, a week that began with Coco Gauff already in the quarterfinals has expanded to include multiple women in the final stages.

What's Next

Wednesday's quarterfinals at Flushing Meadows will complete the semifinals draw. By Thursday, both the men's and women's semifinal matchups will be set. Women's semifinals are scheduled for Thursday; men's semifinals on Friday. The US Open final is Sunday, September 13.

Tuesday's results from the night sessions — Gauff or Andreeva, Alcaraz or Shelton — will reshape the picture once more before Wednesday begins. There is still a great deal of tennis left to play.
