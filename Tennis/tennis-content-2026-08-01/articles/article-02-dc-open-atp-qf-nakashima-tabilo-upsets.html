Nakashima Ousts No. 1 De Minaur, Tabilo Upsets Shelton — DC Open ATP Semifinals Set

By Elena Voss | August 1, 2026

Washington, D.C.

The top two seeds are gone. Alex de Minaur, the defending champion and No. 1 seed, and Ben Shelton, seeded No. 2, were both eliminated on Friday afternoon in the DC Open ATP quarterfinals. Brandon Nakashima ended de Minaur's title defense 7-6(5), 6-4. Alejandro Tabilo eliminated Shelton. When the dust settled, Taylor Fritz stood as the only top-four seed still in the draw, and 19-year-old Spaniard Rafael Jodar had secured a spot in the semifinals — along with a guaranteed entry into the ATP Top 20.

Nakashima Ends the Title Defense

De Minaur arrived in Washington as both the defending champion and the world No. 1. He had opened his title run by snapping a 12-1 career head-to-head deficit against Stefanos Tsitsipas, and he appeared to be building the momentum that a defending champion needs. Nakashima had other plans.

The American, ranked well outside the top 20, took de Minaur to a first-set tiebreak and won it 7-5. The second set was more one-sided — Nakashima converted his opportunities and closed it out 6-4. De Minaur, who has now left Washington without the trophy he came to defend, will regroup with the Canadian Open in Montreal on the horizon, though he is also in the bracket there.

Tabilo Accounts for Shelton

The upset on the other side of the draw was equally decisive. Alejandro Tabilo, the Chilean ranked well outside the top seeds, defeated Ben Shelton to advance to the semifinals. Shelton had been among the favorites in the draw given his powerful game on hard courts. Tabilo's victory leaves the bottom half of the bracket open in a way that few predicted entering the quarterfinal session.

Jodar Into the Top 20

Perhaps the week's most compelling story — beyond the upsets of the seeded players — belongs to Rafael Jodar. The 19-year-old Spaniard began the tournament by eliminating No. 8 seed Arthur Fils in the first round, followed that with a second-round win over Kei Nishikori, and on Friday knocked out No. 6 seed Lorenzo Musetti 1-6, 6-1, 6-4 in a match that illustrated exactly why he is considered one of European tennis's next generation of stars. He was bageled in the opening set, then responded by winning the second and third sets combined 7-1.

Jodar will enter the ATP Top 20 for the first time after this tournament, regardless of his result in the semifinals. He faces Alejandro Tabilo on Saturday, an all-unexpected semifinal that few could have forecasted at the start of the week.

Fritz Survives

Taylor Fritz was the only seed from the top four to advance to the semifinals, and his path required him to come back from a match point down against compatriot Alex Michelsen. Fritz won the quarterfinal and will face Brandon Nakashima on Saturday in what is an all-American semifinal matchup. The winner advances to Sunday's final at FitzGerald Tennis Center.

What's Next

The DC Open ATP semifinals take place Saturday, August 1. Nakashima vs. Fritz is scheduled not before 6:30 PM ET. Jodar vs. Tabilo follows. The final is Sunday, August 2. The Canadian Open in Montreal begins its main draw on August 4, with the field led by No. 1 seed Alexander Zverev — whose draw was released Friday. The upper echelon of the men's draw will have opportunities at both events over the coming ten days.
