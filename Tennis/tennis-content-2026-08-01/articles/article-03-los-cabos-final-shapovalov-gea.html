Shapovalov Returns to Los Cabos Final; Gea Makes History at 21 for First ATP Title Shot

By Marcus Cole | August 1, 2026

Los Cabos, Mexico

The Mifel Tennis Open final is set. Denis Shapovalov will face Arthur Gea on Saturday in Los Cabos — a matchup between a past champion returning to the scene of an earlier title and a 21-year-old Frenchman stepping onto an ATP Tour final court for the very first time. Gea will enter the Top 100 regardless of today's result. Shapovalov wants to win the tournament again.

Shapovalov Beats Norrie in Three Sets

Denis Shapovalov defeated Cameron Norrie 6-3, 5-7, 6-4 in Friday's semifinal to book his place in the final. Shapovalov took the first set decisively and appeared to be in control of the match until Norrie found his range in the second set and leveled. The third set became a test of resolve, and Shapovalov produced it — closing out the match and completing his run to the final.

Shapovalov is a previous Los Cabos champion and arrived at this week's tournament with a strong record on these courts. His season has not been without its injury complications, but the Canadian has been fully competitive throughout this week's draw, and a title here would represent a significant milestone in what has been a challenging year.

Gea Makes History

Arthur Gea's semifinal path was different. The 21-year-old from France, ranked No. 127 heading into the week, faced Coleman Wong in a semifinal that required every bit of concentration he had. Wong — who had produced the tournament's biggest upset by beating No. 1 seed Jiri Lehecka earlier in the week — pushed Gea to a tiebreak in the first set before the Frenchman closed it out 7-6(7). The second set was more controlled, with Gea winning 6-3 to complete the win.

This is Gea's first ATP Tour final. He arrived in Los Cabos as an unseeded player in an ATP 250 field; he leaves it as a Top 100 player for the first time. Regardless of Saturday's result, the ranking points from a final appearance are sufficient to carry him inside the world's top 100 — a threshold he has been approaching for much of the 2026 season.

Earlier in the week, Gea had eliminated No. 3 seed Francisco Cerundolo to reach the semifinals. His run through the draw represents a comprehensive performance across four matches against increasingly difficult opponents.

What's Next

The Mifel Tennis Open final takes place Saturday, August 1 in Los Cabos. Shapovalov enters as the experienced finalist with a previous title at the venue; Gea enters as the young player with nothing to lose and everything to gain. The winner claims the ATP 250 title and the ranking points that accompany it. Both players are expected to move on to the North American hard-court swing's Masters 1000 events in the coming weeks — events that will demand a significantly different level of competition. But first, Saturday's final.
