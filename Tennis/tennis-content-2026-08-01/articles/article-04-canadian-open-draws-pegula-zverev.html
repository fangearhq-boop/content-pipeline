Canadian Open Draws Set: Pegula No. 1 in Toronto, Zverev Leads Montreal; Draper Earns Wildcard

By Ryan Calloway | August 1, 2026

Toronto / Montreal

The National Bank Open draws were released Friday, July 31, laying out the field for the Canadian Open's dual-tournament format. Jessica Pegula is the No. 1 seed in a 96-player WTA draw at Sobeys Stadium in Toronto, where main draw play begins Saturday, August 2. In Montreal, Alexander Zverev leads the ATP field at IGA Stadium, with Felix Auger-Aliassime seeded No. 2 — a career-first at a Masters 1000 level — on home soil. ATP main draw play begins August 4.

WTA Toronto: Pegula Leads the Field

Jessica Pegula arriving in Toronto as the No. 1 seed is a reflection of a strong first half of the 2026 season. Her presence at the top of the draw comes while she is also competing in the DC Open semifinals in Washington on Saturday — a level of concurrent activity that underscores the compressed nature of the summer hard-court swing.

The 96-player draw in Toronto is one of the strongest WTA fields of the North American swing. Main draw action begins August 2, with the tournament running through August 13. The WTA 1000 level event carries significant ranking implications across the top 50 of the world rankings.

ATP Montreal: Zverev and FAA at the Top

Alexander Zverev comes to Montreal as the No. 1 seed on the strength of his 2026 results — Roland Garros champion and Wimbledon finalist. The German is one of the favorites at any event he enters at this stage of the season, and Montreal's hard courts suit his game.

Felix Auger-Aliassime's No. 2 seeding deserves its own paragraph. The Canadian is seeded second at a Masters 1000 for the first time in his career, and he is doing it at home in Montreal, where the crowd will be among the most supportive he has played in front of anywhere. The combination of strong results, home venue, and the highest seeding of his career makes FAA one of the most compelling storylines heading into the ATP side of the Canadian Open.

The wildcards for Montreal include Alexis Galarneau, Liam Draxl, and Jack Draper. Draper's wildcard is the one that carries the most narrative weight. The British player held a career-high ranking of No. 4 in 2025, but a left arm bone bruising has caused him to miss every tournament in 2026 — including the Australian Open, Roland Garros, Wimbledon, and the DC Open. The Montreal wildcard is his first opportunity to return to tour-level competition. He arrives ranked No. 147.

Absent Headliners

The notable absences from the ATP Montreal draw — Jannik Sinner (No. 1 who withdrew citing rest), Novak Djokovic (injury), and Carlos Alcaraz (wrist) — have been well-documented since the seedings were confirmed. The field remains strong with Zverev, FAA, and the full contingent of players ranked below the top three. But the gap left by the three highest-ranked players in the world will be a topic of conversation throughout the week.

What's Next

WTA Toronto main draw play begins Saturday, August 2. ATP Montreal main draw play begins Tuesday, August 4. Both tournaments run through August 13. For players competing in Washington this weekend — including Pegula, who plays her DC Open WTA semifinal on Saturday — the Canadian Open represents an immediate next step. The North American hard-court season accelerates toward the US Open, which follows in late August.
