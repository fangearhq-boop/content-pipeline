Ningbo Open Quarterfinals Thursday: Rybakina Leads, Kostyuk in Must-Win Mode After Swiatek Exit

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | October 21, 2026

The BNB Ningbo Open moves into its quarterfinals on Thursday, October 23, with a WTA 500 draw that has been reshaped by withdrawals, early upsets, and the sudden elevation of its WTA Finals stakes. With Iga Swiatek now officially out of the tournament — and out of the 2026 season entirely — the field at the Ningbo Indoor Tennis Center carries more weight than a mid-week WTA 500 usually does.

Top seed Elena Rybakina, who won this title last year, leads the remaining draw. Marta Kostyuk, seeded 4th, has been advancing through the bracket and is set for a quarterfinal appearance on Thursday. Both players have reason to push deep: Rybakina as defending champion, Kostyuk in urgent need of Race to Indian Wells points after Swiatek's withdrawal clarified just how tight the qualification math is.

Kostyuk Active in Singles and Doubles

Kostyuk entered the week knowing she needed tournament points. On Monday, she and Leylah Fernandez provided a first look at that form in doubles, dispatching Gorgodze and Putintseva 6-1, 6-2 in 54 minutes in the first round. The quick, dominant result was exactly the kind of efficiency the doubles circuit rewards.

Her singles campaign has her in the quarterfinals, with the race to the WTA Finals now demanding she not only win Thursday but go as deep as possible in the remaining rounds. She currently sits 434 points behind Linda Noskova in the Race standings (per WTA Race document, Oct 18). Noskova is not competing this week, which means every point Kostyuk earns at Ningbo narrows the gap directly.

Early Round Departures

The main draw at Ningbo has seen several early exits from seeded players. Seeds Frech, Kalinina, and Korneeva have all been eliminated in the rounds preceding the quarterfinals. The early departures have opened the bracket, but the four quarterfinalists still standing represent competitive tennis at the WTA 500 level.

Magdalena Chwalinska, who won the Wuhan Open last week and withdrew from this event with a left thigh injury earlier in the week, will not appear at Ningbo. Yuliya Putintseva, who fell in the doubles alongside Gorgodze on Monday, also did not advance past the early rounds in singles. The field has thinned in ways that, on paper, give Rybakina and Kostyuk cleaner paths to the final.

Tournament Schedule

The BNB Ningbo Open runs through Saturday, October 25. The quarterfinals take place Thursday, the semifinals Friday, and the final Saturday. With the WTA Finals qualification window still technically open for other events, the results from Thursday onward will matter well beyond the trophy.

What's Next

Thursday's Ningbo quarterfinals are the next milestone for the WTA Finals Race and for Rybakina's title defense. Results will update the Race standings ahead of any final qualification decisions. The Japan Women's Open in Osaka (WTA 250) also continues through October 25, though its points allocation is lower and the Race impact more limited. The full WTA Finals field at Indian Wells will be confirmed once all remaining qualifying events conclude.
