ATP Race to Turin: Vienna and Basel Open Oct 26 as Final Qualification Push Begins

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | October 21, 2026

Three tournaments. Five weeks. As many as five open spots — depending on how Jannik Sinner's injury-enforced absence reshuffles the qualifying order. The ATP Race to Turin is entering its decisive phase, and the scoreboard going into the final stretch of the 2026 season is closer than it looks.

Alexander Zverev, Carlos Alcaraz, and Ben Shelton have clinched their places at the ATP Finals (November 15–22, Turin). Sinner sits second in the Race with approximately 7,950 points but is out for the year, which means the field heading to Turin will not include him. His absence effectively brings the qualification cutline down by one position, giving additional players a path into the eight-man field.

The Bubble: Seven Players, Five Spots

Below the three confirmed qualifiers, the Race standings as of October 18–19 show a genuinely tight cluster. Flavio Cobolli sits 5th at approximately 3,540 points. Daniil Medvedev has climbed to 6th with around 3,420 points, buoyed by his deep Shanghai run. Frances Tiafoe (7th, approximately 3,380 points) and Arthur Fils (8th, approximately 3,325 points) are separated by fewer than 60 points — the equivalent of a round-of-32 result at a Masters 1000.

The chasing pack adds further names: Sebastián Báez Jódar rose to 9th following his Shanghai final run, the first ATP 1000 final of his career. Álex de Minaur sits 10th at approximately 2,880 points. Novak Djokovic, who received a wildcard into the Swiss Indoors at Basel, is 11th at approximately 2,840 points. From 5th to 11th, no more than roughly 700 points separate the entire group — a single strong tournament result can move a player from the bubble into confirmation, or out of the top eight entirely.

Three Remaining Scoring Events

The final three tournaments on the Race calendar before the Turin cutoff each carry significant point potential. The Erste Bank Open in Vienna (ATP 500, Wiener Stadthalle, opens October 26) offers up to 500 points for the winner. The Swiss Indoors in Basel (ATP 500, St. Jakobshalle, also opening October 26 with qualifying beginning October 24–25) offers the same maximum. The Paris Rolex Masters (ATP 1000, October 31–November 8) is the biggest remaining event — the winner earns 1,000 Race points, with deep runs carrying commensurate value.

Vienna's seedings include Zverev (No. 1) and Medvedev (No. 3), giving both top seeds and bubble players competitive draws. Basel has drawn Shelton (No. 1), Fils (No. 3), and Jódar (No. 4) as seeds, alongside wildcards Djokovic and Stan Wawrinka. The presence of Djokovic and Wawrinka at Basel — both on wildcards — adds historical narrative to an event that already has significant Race stakes.

What's Next

The Vienna and Basel main draws both begin October 26, with Basel qualifying running October 24–25. Players competing at Almaty this week — including Medvedev and Rublev — will need to move directly to one or both of the ATP 500 events. The overlapping schedules will create schedule choices that could affect the Race standings before Paris even begins.

By the time the Paris Rolex Masters concludes on November 8, the ATP Finals field will be complete. The eight players heading to Turin will be decided across three tournaments, five weekends, and a Race table that currently has no clear safe zone below 4th place.
