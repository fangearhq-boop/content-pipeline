Swiatek Ends 2026 Season With Ningbo Withdrawal, Misses WTA Finals for First Time Since 2019

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | October 21, 2026

Iga Swiatek has withdrawn from the BNB Ningbo Open and announced she is ending her 2026 season, citing a left thigh injury that has been affecting her since the Wuhan semifinal. The withdrawal means she will miss the WTA Finals for the first time since 2019, ending a run of six consecutive appearances at the season-ending championship.

The injury is the same one that hampered her in Wuhan, where she lost to Magdalena Chwalinska in the semifinals. Rather than push through at Ningbo, Swiatek cited an ongoing problem with her left thigh as the reason for her decision to step away from competition entirely for the remainder of the year.

A Streak That Defined an Era

The numbers behind what ends today are worth sitting with. Six consecutive WTA Finals appearances. Six straight years of being among the eight best players in women's tennis when the season concludes. Since 2020, it has been one of the most consistent individual qualifying records in women's tennis — Swiatek appeared in the Finals through injury-interrupted seasons, through the peaks of her dominance and the more competitive years that followed.

The last time she missed the Finals was 2019, when she was 18 years old and still two years away from her first Grand Slam title at Roland Garros. The streak that begins its count from 2020 ends now, in a tournament withdrawal from a WTA 500 event in China in October.

Entering the week at Ningbo, Swiatek sat approximately 545 points behind Karolina Muchova, who holds the 7th qualifying position in the Race to Indian Wells (per WTA Race standings, Oct 18). To qualify, she would have needed to win Ningbo outright and then score additional Race points in Tokyo — a scenario that required near-perfect tennis over two consecutive weeks, with a compromised left thigh. The decision to shut down the season rather than continue chasing that scenario is not surprising given the injury history of the past two weeks.

Impact on the WTA Finals Field

With Swiatek's season over, the 2026 WTA Finals field at Indian Wells (November 8–15) will be contested without her. Seven of the eight qualifying spots are now confirmed: Elena Rybakina (1st, 7,502 points), Aryna Sabalenka (2nd), Mirra Andreeva (3rd), Jessica Pegula (4th), Coco Gauff (5th), Elina Svitolina (6th), and Karolina Muchova (7th). The eighth spot remains in play between Linda Noskova (4,354 points) and Marta Kostyuk (3,920 points), with Kostyuk still competing at Ningbo and a quarterfinal scheduled for Thursday, October 23.

Swiatek's withdrawal also removes any uncertainty about the bottom of the Race standings. Her name will remain on the list at approximately 4,189 points — 9th — but with no remaining events, she cannot add to that total. Kostyuk, currently 434 points behind Noskova, is the only player realistically in range of changing the field.

What's Next

The focus for Swiatek now shifts to an off-season recovery. There is no indication yet of when the thigh issue will fully resolve, but ending the season in October rather than fighting through two more weeks gives her the maximum time to prepare for the 2027 season. She will return to the circuit in January for the Australian Open swing as one of the clear favorites for the year's first Grand Slam title.

For the immediate tennis calendar, all eyes shift to Ningbo. The BNB Ningbo Open quarterfinals on Thursday will carry significant WTA Finals implications as Kostyuk bids to close the gap on Noskova for the final Indian Wells spot. The tournament runs through Saturday, October 25.
