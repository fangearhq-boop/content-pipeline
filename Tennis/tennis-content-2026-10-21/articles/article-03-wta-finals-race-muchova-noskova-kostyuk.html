Swiatek Out, Race Clarified: Noskova Leads Kostyuk by 434 Points for Final WTA Finals Berth

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | October 21, 2026

Iga Swiatek's decision to end her 2026 season had an immediate effect on the WTA Finals Race to Indian Wells: it removed the most high-profile name from the qualification math and left the battle for the final spot much cleaner than it was 24 hours ago. Seven players are now confirmed for the November 8–15 championship at Indian Wells Tennis Garden. The eighth berth is down to two.

Linda Noskova sits at 4,354 points in the Race standings (per WTA Race document, Oct 18). Marta Kostyuk has 3,920. The gap is 434 points — roughly the value of a quarterfinal at a WTA 500 event. Kostyuk is playing at Ningbo, a WTA 500, with a quarterfinal scheduled for Thursday, October 23. Noskova is not competing this week.

The Full WTA Finals Picture

Seven of the eight spots are settled. Elena Rybakina leads the Race with 7,502 points and qualified earlier in the season. Aryna Sabalenka (6,560 points) and Mirra Andreeva (6,306 points) follow. Jessica Pegula (5,835 points) and Coco Gauff (5,774 points) are confirmed. Elina Svitolina (5,229 points) earned her spot during the Asian swing. Karolina Muchova (4,735 points) holds the 7th qualifying position and is not in danger of being overtaken.

Swiatek, who now sits 9th in the Race at approximately 4,189 points with no remaining events, has removed herself from any qualification scenario. That clarity — knowing exactly which players remain in play — is the one silver lining of her withdrawal for fans watching the race.

The Math for Kostyuk

For Kostyuk to seriously threaten Noskova's lead, she needs at minimum a strong Ningbo result. At a WTA 500, a quarterfinal exit earns 100 points. A semifinal earns 185 points. A final earns 305 points. A title earns 470 points. Closing a 434-point gap against a player not competing requires Kostyuk to reach the Ningbo final at minimum — and even a final might not be enough if Noskova holds her total going into the season's final weeks.

The race doesn't technically end at Ningbo — there are other events still on the schedule — but Ningbo is the clearest and most immediate opportunity Kostyuk has to change the standings. The quarterfinal on Thursday is effectively must-win if she wants to keep any pressure on Noskova.

What's Next

Kostyuk takes the court for her Ningbo quarterfinal on Thursday, October 23. The BNB Ningbo Open runs through Saturday, October 25. Also still in progress: the Japan Women's Open in Osaka (WTA 250, through October 25), though that event carries far lower Race points.

The WTA Finals field will be fully confirmed once all remaining events with Race implications conclude. If Kostyuk can win Ningbo, the conversation continues. If she exits early, Linda Noskova will almost certainly complete her qualification to Indian Wells without needing to step back on court.
