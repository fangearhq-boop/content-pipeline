NOT VERIFIEDBRIEF
Korpatsch Wins Hamburg — First German Champion Since Steffi Graf in 1992 - **Tier:** 1 - **Angle:** FOLLOW-UP — Tamara Korpatsch (GER, 31, wildcard) defeated Anna Bondar (HUN) 6-3, 6-3 in the MSC Hamburg Ladies Open (WTA 500) final on Sunday July 26. Korpatsch won in 1 hour and 28 minutes — on home clay in her hometown of Hamburg. She became the first German player to win the Hamburg title since Steffi Graf in 1992, a gap of 34 years. Graf had won six consecutive Hamburg titles from 1987–1992. Korpatsch entered the week ranked approximately No. 95; the title takes her into the WTA top 50 for the first time in her career (previous best: No. 71 in October 2023). This is her second career WTA title (first: 2023 Transylvania Open). Her career record vs. Bondar is now 5-0.

NOT VERIFIEDBRIEF
Tagger Wins Prague — First WTA Title at 18, Youngest in Top 50 - **Tier:** 1 - **Angle:** FOLLOW-UP — Lilli Tagger (AUT, 18) defeated Daria Snigur (UKR, No. 8 seed) 7-6(3), 6-2 in the Livesport Prague Open (WTA 250) final on Sunday July 26 to claim her first WTA Tour singles title. Tagger, born February 17, 2008, is now the youngest player in the WTA top 50 (approximately No. 45). She is only the second teenager — after Mirra Andreeva — to win a WTA singles title in 2026. Tagger trained at the Francesca Schiavone Academy in Varese, Italy, and won the 2025 French Open girls' title before turning professional. She plays with a one-handed backhand, highly unusual on the WTA Tour. In the final, Snigur built a 5-2 lead in the first set before Tagger leveled at 5-5 and won the tiebreak 7-3, then ran out the second set 6-2.
