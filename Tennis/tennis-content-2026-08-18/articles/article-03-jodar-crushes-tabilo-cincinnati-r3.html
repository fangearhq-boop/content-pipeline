Jodar Drops Just 3 Games to Crush Tabilo, Joins Alcaraz and Shelton in Elite Cincinnati Teen Club

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | August 18, 2026

The 19-year-old Spaniard struck 26 winners to demolish Alejandro Tabilo 6-2, 6-1 in 75 minutes and advance to the Cincinnati Round of 16.

Rafael Jodar keeps making the rest of the ATP tour look ordinary.

The 19-year-old Spaniard dropped just three games in a 75-minute demolition of Alejandro Tabilo (6-2, 6-1) on Monday at the Cincinnati Open, reaching the Round of 16 at a Masters 1000 event for what is becoming a regular occurrence in his 2026 season. With the win, Jodar became only the third teenager to reach the Cincinnati Round of 16 this decade, joining Carlos Alcaraz and Ben Shelton in that select company.

His weapon of choice Monday: 26 winners, relentless pressure, and the kind of mental clarity that most players his age never fully develop.

A Statement Performance

Tabilo is no easy out. The Chilean hard-court specialist entered the match with experience at this level and a game built for faster surfaces. But Jodar refused to let him settle into any rhythm. The first set established the tone — clean, aggressive, with barely a wasted shot — and the second set continued the demolition.

Jodar improved his head-to-head record against Tabilo to 2-1 with the win. His 26 winners were a reflection of how well he's striking the ball right now — not just hitting hard, but finding targets with consistent depth and pace.

Part of an Exclusive List

The comparison to Alcaraz and Shelton is not casual. Both have gone on to become major champions. Jodar, who started 2026 ranked No. 168 in the world, has assembled one of the most remarkable breakthrough seasons on the ATP Tour in years — multiple semi-finals, a title on clay, and now a Cincinnati R4 run that has the tennis world paying close attention.

He has played fearlessly throughout this hard-court swing, and in Cincinnati — a Masters 1000 event with one of the deepest draws on the calendar — he looks like he belongs completely.

What's Next

Jodar advances to the Cincinnati Round of 16, where matches begin Wednesday, August 19. A quarter-final run would represent yet another milestone in a season that already has surpassed any reasonable expectations. The US Open follows at the end of the month, where Jodar will enter as one of the most dangerous unseeded threats in the draw.
