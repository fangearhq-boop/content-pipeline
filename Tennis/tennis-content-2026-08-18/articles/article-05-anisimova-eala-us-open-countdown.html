Anisimova Ends Eala's Cincinnati Run; US Open Looms 12 Days Away

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | August 18, 2026

Amanda Anisimova rallied from a set down to beat Alex Eala 4-6, 6-4, 6-2, while Jannik Sinner and Carlos Alcaraz remain the central question marks ahead of the final Grand Slam of 2026.

Alex Eala's 2026 hard-court season was something to behold. It just ran into a roadblock named Amanda Anisimova.

The Filipina took the first set 4-6 against the American No. 9 seed Monday in Cincinnati, and for a time it looked like another upset run might be in the making. But Anisimova regrouped. She won the second set 6-4 and then closed out the match emphatically 6-2 in the third, reaching the Cincinnati Round of 16 for the first time since 2018.

For Eala, who entered the week 22-9 on hard courts in 2026, it was a narrow defeat that still represents one of the best stretches of her career. Anisimova was simply too consistent in the final two sets.

Anisimova's Comeback Story

Anisimova's path back to elite-level tennis has not been straightforward. She has dealt with extended absences from the tour and the kind of mental and physical challenges that derail many careers. To be back in a Cincinnati Round of 16, beating a rising talent in Eala, is a meaningful achievement.

Her ability to flip the switch after losing the first set speaks to a competitive resilience she has shown throughout her career. She now advances to a Round of 16 matchup that will test her again — but on Monday, she had every answer.

US Open: 12 Days and Counting

With the Cincinnati tournament heading into its final stages, the backdrop is unavoidable: the US Open begins August 30 at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center in New York. The draw will be announced August 27.

Two names dominate the conversation heading into Flushing Meadows.

Jannik Sinner, the World No. 1 who won Wimbledon in July, has not played a competitive match since. He withdrew from Cincinnati in early August with a right knee injury and has been rehabbing in Turin. Sinner is on the US Open entry list and considered likely to compete, but he will arrive without any hard-court match play under his belt.

Carlos Alcaraz, the defending US Open champion, last competed in April — a second-round withdrawal at the Barcelona Open with a right wrist injury. He has since missed Roland Garros, Wimbledon, and both North American Masters 1000 events. Reports out of Spain indicate Alcaraz has returned to practice and was training in Murcia, but going from extended inactivity to the intensity of a best-of-five Grand Slam is a significant ask.

Both players are on the entry list. Neither has confirmed full fitness. The field — led by Zverev, with Jodar, Shelton, Swiatek and Rybakina all in form — will be ready if either or both are compromised.

What's Next

Anisimova advances to the Cincinnati Round of 16, with matches beginning Wednesday, August 19. The US Open draw is announced August 27. Main draw play begins Monday, August 30, at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center in New York.
