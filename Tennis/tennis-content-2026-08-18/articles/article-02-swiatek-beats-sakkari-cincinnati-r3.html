Swiatek Drops First Set, Then Dominates Sakkari to Reach Cincinnati Last 16

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | August 18, 2026

Iga Swiatek won 12 of the last 14 games against Maria Sakkari on Monday, extending her winning streak to eight matches as she defends her Cincinnati title.

Iga Swiatek has made an art form of the comeback. On Monday in Cincinnati, she needed it again — and delivered it emphatically.

The Polish No. 7 seed lost the opening set 4-6 to Maria Sakkari in their third-round clash at the Western and Southern Open, then shifted into a gear Sakkari simply couldn't match. Swiatek won 12 of the last 14 games, taking the second and third sets 6-1, 6-1 to advance to the Round of 16 at the Lindner Family Tennis Centre in Mason, Ohio.

The win extends Swiatek's winning streak to eight consecutive matches heading into the final stretch before the US Open.

Sakkari's Strong Start Couldn't Hold

Sakkari played a sharp first set. The Greek veteran moved well, handled Swiatek's heavy groundstrokes effectively, and capitalized on the opportunities she created to win 6-4. There were genuine signs that an upset was brewing at Lindner Family Tennis Centre.

But from the start of the second set, the match turned. Swiatek began finding her range off both wings, and Sakkari's window to compete narrowed rapidly. By the time the match ended, the final two sets had gone to the defending champion by a combined 12-2 scoreline.

That kind of dominance, after conceding the first set, speaks to something Swiatek has always had: the mental fortitude to reset and impose her game regardless of what came before.

A Defending Champion in Form

Swiatek won the Cincinnati title in 2025, making this run a title defence as well as a statement of US Open readiness. With eight straight wins and the form she showed against Sakkari, she enters the Round of 16 as the clear WTA favourite at this tournament.

The timing could not be better. The US Open begins August 30 in New York, 12 days away. With the draw to be announced August 27, Swiatek will enter Flushing Meadows in peak condition — something that cannot be said for several rivals currently navigating injury uncertainty.

What's Next

Swiatek advances to the Cincinnati Round of 16, where she will face a next-round opponent from the evolving draw. The WTA quarter-finals are scheduled for August 20-21, with the semi-finals on August 22 and the final on August 23. A Cincinnati title would give Swiatek significant momentum heading into the US Open, completing her third title in 2026.
