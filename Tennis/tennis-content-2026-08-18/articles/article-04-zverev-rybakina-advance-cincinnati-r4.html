Zverev Grinds Through Double Tiebreak, Rybakina Fights Rain to Set Up Cincinnati R4

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | August 18, 2026

The top seeds did what top seeds must do — Alexander Zverev and Elena Rybakina both survived difficult conditions to advance to the Cincinnati Round of 16.

The clean win was not on the menu Monday in Cincinnati. But the top seeds found a way.

Alexander Zverev (No. 1 seed) edged Terrance Atmane 7-6(4), 7-6(6) to earn his 46th tour victory of the 2026 season, while Elena Rybakina (No. 2 seed) beat Magdalena Frech 6-4, 7-6(2) while navigating two rain stoppages. Both are through to the Cincinnati Round of 16, which begins Wednesday.

Zverev Earns It in Both Tiebreaks

Zverev's match against Atmane was a test of composure from first tiebreak to last. The French hard-court specialist pushed the German top seed through two competitive sets, and on another night the result could have gone differently. But Zverev is a player who knows how to win close matches at this level — this season has been proof of that, with his Roland Garros title and Wimbledon runner-up finish confirming his status as the sport's premier hard-court threat with Sinner and Alcaraz unavailable in Cincinnati.

The 46th win of 2026 adds to a season that has already delivered in the biggest moments. Zverev is the favourite to win the Cincinnati men's title with Sinner (knee) and Alcaraz (wrist) both absent.

Rybakina Undeterred by Delays

Rain stoppages are among the most disruptive elements in outdoor tennis. They break momentum, force players off court mid-match, and test mental resilience as much as physical skill. Rybakina handled two of them against Frech without visible effect.

The Kazakh No. 2 seed took the first set 6-4 and then navigated a competitive second set, winning the tiebreak 7-2 to complete the victory. It was professional, efficient, and exactly what you want from a top seed.

Rybakina enters the Round of 16 as one of the top contenders for the Cincinnati WTA title, alongside defending champion Swiatek and No. 1 seed Aryna Sabalenka, who begins her Cincinnati campaign Tuesday.

What's Next

The Cincinnati Round of 16 runs Wednesday, August 19, with quarter-finals on August 20-21. For Zverev, a potential semi-final clash with a resurgent Jodar or one of the other hard-court challengers looms. For Rybakina, the draw will determine whether a Swiatek semi-final awaits — a match that would be one of the most anticipated at this tournament. The US Open begins August 30 at Flushing Meadows.
