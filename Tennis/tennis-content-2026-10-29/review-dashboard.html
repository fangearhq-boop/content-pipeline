Djokovic Wins Basel Return, Beats Humbert 4-6, 6-3, 6-4
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Novak Djokovic is still very much alive in the Race to Turin. Wednesday night in Basel, Djokovic fell behind Ugo Humbert in the first set and looked vulnerable. Humbert, playing well, took the opener 6-4. Then came a medical timeout for a back issue when Humbert was already trailing 4-1 in the second set — whether that changed the dynamic is up for debate, but Djokovic won the second set 6-3 and closed the third 6-4. This was Djokovic's first Basel win since 2011. Fifteen years. He saved 5 of 6 break points in what was a tight match start to finish. The result puts him back inside the top 10 and keeps his ATP Finals push going. Next up: Jan-Lennard Struff in the second round today. Every match now matters. Who do you think has a better shot at making the ATP Finals — Djokovic or de Minaur?

### Long-Form Post The last big event of the ATP season is almost here. The Rolex Paris Masters draw ceremony is tomorrow (Friday, October 30), and the main draw begins November 2 at the Plenitude Arena in Nanterre. The event runs through November 8 — and it's worth 1,000 Race points to the champion. That number matters enormously right now. With Vienna and Basel each offering 500 points, and Paris offering 1,000, the total available between now and Nov. 8 is more than enough to completely reshape the Race to Turin standings. Jannik Sinner is out for the season, which means a reshuffled seeding and four more confirmed spots at the ATP Finals for the rest of the field to chase. Alcaraz, Zverev, Fritz, and Shelton are all expected to headline the Paris field, along with Cobolli, Medvedev, Fils, Djokovic, and others fighting for the final spots. Who do you think wins Paris this year?
