Vienna Day 3: Cobolli Survives Dimitrov, Musetti Upsets Tiafoe to Shake Race Standings

By Ryan Calloway | October 29, 2026 | Tennis Fanrecap

Thursday's third day of competition at the Erste Bank Open in Vienna produced two results with direct consequences for the Race to Turin. Flavio Cobolli defeated Grigor Dimitrov 6-4, 3-6, 6-3 to hold his ground in the standings, while Lorenzo Musetti continued one of the indoor season's more impressive runs by eliminating Frances Tiafoe — a win that complicates Tiafoe's ATP Finals qualification in a significant way.

Alexander de Minaur also advanced at Vienna, making it a productive day for players outside the top four confirmed qualifiers. The quarterfinal draw is taking shape with Alcaraz and Zverev both through from their second-round victories earlier in the week.

Cobolli Holds His Ground Against Dimitrov

Cobolli's 6-4, 3-6, 6-3 win over Grigor Dimitrov was the kind of match that validates a Race position. Dimitrov is a veteran indoor court performer with a title at the indoor hard court level, and a three-setter against him tests more than just tennis quality — it tests mental consistency under pressure.

Cobolli entered this match sitting 6th in the Race to Turin with approximately 3,640 points. A win in Vienna that includes a deep run could be the difference between a Turin debut and staying home. He is currently separated from Medvedev in 5th (3,660 points) by a margin so thin that one tournament win could leapfrog him.

Musetti Ends Tiafoe's Vienna Week

Lorenzo Musetti's elimination of Frances Tiafoe is the more significant Race development from Thursday at Vienna. Musetti recorded his sixth consecutive victory — a streak that has made him one of the indoor season's form players — while Tiafoe's exit at this stage deals a direct blow to his standing in 8th place.

Tiafoe had approximately 3,380 points entering Vienna, leaving him inside the bubble but without much cushion. A strong result at Vienna could have reinforced that position. Instead, his exit means the points he might have earned go to Musetti, who is improving his own ranking in the process. With Paris Masters — worth 1,000 points to the champion — opening its main draw November 2, Tiafoe still has a path, but the window is narrowing.

A note on Alexander Zverev's season: earlier pipeline reports noted that Vienna results could help Zverev "lock up the year-end No. 1 ranking." To clarify: Zverev clinched year-end No. 1 on October 12, when Alcaraz fell to Bergs in Shanghai and Sinner announced his season was over. His Vienna run is about form, titles, and ATP Finals positioning — the No. 1 milestone is settled.

What's Next

Vienna quarterfinals are scheduled for the coming days, with Zverev and Alcaraz both in the draw and projected to meet opponents who advanced Thursday. De Minaur also remains in the tournament. The Paris Masters draw ceremony takes place Friday evening, and the main draw begins November 2 — the last major event of the ATP season and the final 1,000-point opportunity in the Race to Turin.
