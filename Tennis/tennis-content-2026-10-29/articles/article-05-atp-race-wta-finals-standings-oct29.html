ATP Race Shifts Again as Tiafoe Exits Vienna; WTA Finals Draw Expected Around Nov. 5

By Ryan Calloway | October 29, 2026 | Tennis Fanrecap

Thursday's results at Vienna and Basel moved the Race to Turin standings for the second day in a row, with Flavio Cobolli's win reinforcing his 6th-place position and Frances Tiafoe's loss to Lorenzo Musetti threatening to push him below the qualifying line at a critical moment. Novak Djokovic, meanwhile, advanced in Basel and climbed back into the top 10.

The Race to Turin — Updated Picture

The four confirmed qualifiers remain Zverev, Alcaraz, Shelton, and the reserved spot left by Sinner's season-ending withdrawal. The remaining four spots are contested among a group of seven or eight players within meaningful striking distance.

Current approximate standings outside confirmed qualifiers:

  • Medvedev — 5th (~3,660 pts)
  • Cobolli — 6th (~3,640 pts), held after Vienna win over Dimitrov
  • Fils — 7th (~3,475 pts), still active at Basel
  • Tiafoe — 8th (~3,380 pts), exits Vienna after Musetti loss
  • Jódar — 9th (~3,159 pts)
  • de Minaur — 10th (~2,880 pts), still active at Vienna
  • Djokovic — 11th (~2,840 pts), advances at Basel

Vienna and Basel each offer 500 points to the champion, with both draws still in progress. The Paris Masters — draw ceremony Friday, main draw November 2 through 8 — adds 1,000 points to the champion and significant points to quarterfinalists and semifinalists. Players currently outside the top eight can still qualify; players inside the top eight can still miss.

WTA Finals Draw Expected Around Nov. 5

On the women's side, the 2026 WTA Finals draw is expected approximately November 5, three days before the event opens at Indian Wells Tennis Garden on November 8. The eight-player field is fully confirmed: Rybakina (No. 1, defending champion), Sabalenka, Andreeva, Pegula, Gauff, Svitolina, Noskova, and Muchova.

The format uses two groups of four, with the top two players from each group advancing to the semifinals. This is the first WTA Finals held at Indian Wells Tennis Garden, following the event's move from Riyadh. The outdoor hard court setting at Indian Wells — at elevation, with afternoon conditions very different from an indoor arena — adds an additional variable that players have noted in recent days.

The draw question that has generated the most discussion: whether defending champion Rybakina and No. 2 Sabalenka land in the same or opposite groups. A group-stage meeting between the two would be the season's defining match before the semifinals.

What's Next

The Paris Masters draw drops Friday evening, October 30. Vienna and Basel continue through the weekend. The WTA Finals draw is expected around November 5, with the event itself running November 8 through 15 at Indian Wells. The ATP Finals in Turin begin November 15 — the final week of the 2026 professional tennis season.
