US Open Third Round Preview: Alcaraz, Shelton Headline a Loaded Friday

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | September 4, 2026

The US Open third round gets underway Friday with defending champion Carlos Alcaraz facing Wu Yibing on Arthur Ashe, No. 8 seed Ben Shelton meeting Denis Shapovalov, and multiple title contenders in action on both sides of the draw.

The US Open field reaches its second week inflection point on Friday. With the first two rounds now complete, 16 players will remain after Day 7 action — and the matches on the schedule are worth circling.

ESPN carries coverage from 11:30 AM ET, with primetime action beginning at 7 PM on ESPN2.

Alcaraz vs. Wu Yibing — Arthur Ashe Stadium

Carlos Alcaraz returns to Arthur Ashe Stadium for the most meaningful match of his comeback so far. The defending champion has won his first two rounds convincingly — dominant in R1 against Safiullin, resilient in R2 after dropping the first set to Faria before winning the next three.

Wu Yibing (CHN) is one of the more dangerous hard-court players in the current ATP field. The Chinese No. 1 has a clean, flat game that can trouble top players when he finds his range. Alcaraz will be favored, but this is a match where the scoreline might not reflect the difficulty of the contest.

Shelton vs. Shapovalov

No. 8 seed Ben Shelton faces Denis Shapovalov in one of the most watchable matchups of the round. Both players are explosive and capable of big serving days. Shelton has been one of the Americans thriving this week at Flushing Meadows; Shapovalov brings unpredictability and firepower.

Also in the men's third round: Tommy Paul meets No. 15 seed Alexander Bublik; Frances Tiafoe faces Valentin Vacherot.

Women's Third Round

On the women's side, No. 1 seed Aryna Sabalenka continues her three-peat bid. Sabalenka, who won her first two rounds comfortably, is the clearest favourite on this side of the draw. Iga Swiatek is also in action in R3, having navigated her first two rounds without dropping a set.

Coco Gauff faces Cristina Bucsa in her R3 match following Thursday night's straight-sets win over Badosa. Elena Rybakina, who dispatched Bouzas Maneiro 6-2, 6-4, is also through and looking dangerous.

What's Next

After Friday's third round, the draw narrows to 16 players — the Round of 16. The weekend will bring the fourth-round matches, with quarter-finals to follow early next week. The second week of a Grand Slam is where legacies and trajectories are shaped. Friday is where the competition begins in earnest.
