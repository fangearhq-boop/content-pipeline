Rybakina's Ankle No Issue as She Sweeps Into US Open Third Round

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | September 4, 2026

No. 2 seed Elena Rybakina dismissed Spain's Jessica Bouzas Maneiro 6-2, 6-4 in just over an hour at the 2026 US Open, silencing any remaining questions about her fitness following a Cincinnati ankle injury.

Three weeks ago, Elena Rybakina retired from her Cincinnati quarterfinal with a left ankle injury, unable to continue while trailing 1-4 in the first set against Iga Swiatek. She arrived at the US Open without confirming she could practice at full capacity. Before these championships began, there were genuine questions about whether she could compete.

On Thursday, she played 63 minutes of dominant tennis and left the court looking entirely unfazed.

Heavy Hitting, Minimal Errors

Rybakina's game plan against Jessica Bouzas Maneiro was straightforward and efficiently executed: big serve, heavy groundstrokes through the court, keep the Spaniard deep and on the defensive.

Bouzas Maneiro had no answers for the Kazakh's serve, which is among the most dangerous weapons in women's tennis. On the few occasions when rallies extended into baseline exchanges, Rybakina's depth and pace proved equally difficult to handle. The 6-2, 6-4 scoreline understates how comfortable the No. 2 seed looked.

Context: The Ankle

The Cincinnati injury created genuine doubt. Rybakina had said before the US Open that she wanted to make sure everything was okay with the ankle. That caution was understandable — she had been unable to practice on the courts leading up to the tournament.

Two rounds in New York later, the ankle has not been a visible factor. She won her R1 match against Frodin 6-3, 6-2 in 74 minutes. Thursday's 6-2, 6-4 result continues the pattern of controlled, efficient tennis.

What's Next

Rybakina advances to the US Open third round. If she maintains this form, the Kazakh — a two-time Grand Slam finalist who has never won the US Open — will represent a serious threat in the top half of the women's draw, particularly with Sabalenka as the only other major contender in her section. Their potential path could meet before the final.
