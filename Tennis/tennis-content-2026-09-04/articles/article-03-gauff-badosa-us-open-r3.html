Gauff Downs Badosa in Two Sets to Continue US Open Title Defense

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | September 4, 2026

Defending champion Coco Gauff defeated Paula Badosa 6-4, 7-6(7/5) at Arthur Ashe Stadium on Thursday night, advancing to the US Open third round where she will face compatriot Cristina Bucsa.

Coco Gauff's title defense remains firmly on course.

The No. 4 seed — and 2025 US Open champion — defeated Spain's Paula Badosa 6-4, 7-6(7/5) at Arthur Ashe Stadium in just under two hours Thursday evening, securing her place in the third round at Flushing Meadows. The match required patience and composure in the second set, but Gauff delivered both when it mattered.

A Tighter Evening Than Expected

Gauff controlled the opening set, breaking early and managing her serve efficiently to take it 6-4. Badosa, the No. 7 seed, is one of the most capable players on the WTA tour on any surface, and she brought that quality to the night session at Arthur Ashe.

The second set pushed to a tiebreak, where both players traded points deep into the breaker. Gauff eventually closed it out 7-5, sealing the match and the straight-sets victory. She has yet to drop a set through two rounds at these championships.

Title Defense on Track

Gauff is one of three former US Open champions still in the women's draw, alongside Sabalenka and Rybakina. She has looked precise through two rounds — a first-round win followed by a straight-sets effort against a quality opponent in Badosa.

Her next opponent, Cristina Bucsa, presents a very different challenge. The Spaniard is known for her defensive baseline play and ability to extend rallies. Gauff will need to be aggressive and patient in equal measure.

What's Next

Gauff faces Cristina Bucsa in the US Open third round. The match is expected to take place over the weekend. If Gauff continues at this level, a quarter-final berth is well within reach — and the defending champion clearly has that goal in sight.
