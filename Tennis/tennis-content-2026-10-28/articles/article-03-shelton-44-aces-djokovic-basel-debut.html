Shelton Fires 44 Aces at Basel; Djokovic Opens Wednesday Against Humbert

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | October 28, 2026

Ben Shelton spent two hours and 36 minutes at the Swiss Indoors firing 44 aces past qualifier Kovacevic, and still needed a deciding-set tiebreak to close the match. Meanwhile, Novak Djokovic is scheduled to play his first Basel match since 2011 on Wednesday, facing Ugo Humbert in what will be one of the week's most closely watched first-round appearances.

Shelton: 44 Aces, Three Sets Anyway

Ben Shelton's serve is, by some metrics, the most intimidating weapon in men's tennis right now. Tuesday at Basel, the No. 1 seed served 44 aces against Kovacevic, a qualifier or lucky loser who was not supposed to push the top seed into a third set. He did anyway.

The match went the distance and Shelton needed the tiebreak in the third to close it. Kovacevic, whoever the draw delivered to face Shelton, played well enough to make the ace count irrelevant for long stretches. The final result was a Shelton win, but it was harder than 44 aces might suggest.

That tension — a dominant serve and a match that stays competitive — is something Shelton has navigated throughout his career. His serve opens the court and finishes points before rallies start, but when it doesn't, opponents stay in matches longer than they otherwise would. Tuesday was a version of that story. He advances, and the draw opens up ahead of him.

Djokovic: First Basel Match Since 2011

Novak Djokovic has not played at the Swiss Indoors since 2011 — a 15-year absence from one of the premier indoor 500-level events on the calendar. On Wednesday, he opens against France's Ugo Humbert in a first-round match that carries significant weight for his season.

Djokovic entered Basel as a wildcard. His ranking no longer places him among the eight players who earn automatic entry into ATP 500 events. His position in the Race to Turin is approximately No. 11, which means he needs results to climb into the qualifying spots. The Swiss Indoors offers 500 Race points to its champion — meaningful but not sufficient on its own for Djokovic's position.

The head-to-head between Djokovic and Humbert stands at 1-0 in Djokovic's favor coming into Wednesday's match. Humbert, currently ranked inside the top 30, is a capable clay-court and indoor player who will not make this straightforward. The match is scheduled for approximately 4:30 PM local Basel time.

The Basel Draw at a Glance

With Shelton through from the top of the draw and Djokovic entering Wednesday, the Swiss Indoors is shaping up around familiar names at key positions. Andrey Rublev won his first-round match Monday, and the results from the rest of the week will determine whether the Race picture shifts at all before Paris.

The tournament runs through November 1. Every round from here adds Race points for the players still in contention, making this week — across both Basel and Vienna — among the most consequential of the indoor season.

What's Next

Djokovic vs. Humbert is the marquee Basel match on Wednesday. A Djokovic win would keep him in contention for a deeper run and a meaningful Race points haul. Shelton advances to his second-round match later in the week. The full Basel draw picture will come into focus once Wednesday's remaining first-round matches are completed. For all players still alive at Vienna and Basel, Thursday's second-round matches begin to sort out who has a genuine shot at the week's title — and the Race points that come with it.
