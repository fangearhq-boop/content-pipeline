ATP Race to Turin: Paris Is the Final Decider, Jodar Leads 8th Place by 24 Points

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap

November 2, 2026

The ATP Race to Turin has four open spots and one event left to decide them. Vienna and Basel are done. Paris, worth 1,000 points to the champion, is the last chance anyone has to change the picture.

Four players have already qualified: Alexander Zverev, Carlos Alcaraz, Ben Shelton, and Daniil Medvedev. The remaining four spots are held by Flavio Cobolli, Arthur Fils, Frances Tiafoe, and Rafael Jodar. Between Jodar in 8th and Alex de Minaur in 9th, the gap is 24 points. One match. One result. That is the margin separating the last confirmed qualifier from the first player still waiting.

The Bubble in Detail

Jodar's position is legitimate but fragile. He holds 8th place — the final qualifying spot, with Jannik Sinner out for the season — and enters Paris needing to protect that standing through an entire draw of players who all want what he has. A first-round loss, combined with a deep run from de Minaur, could flip the standings before the week's end.

De Minaur arrives with his own momentum. He reached the Vienna final, which means he has already earned points this week that push his Race total higher. The 24-point gap figure reflects the standings entering Paris, and de Minaur's Vienna runner-up result may already be narrowing it further depending on how Jodar's week went. Both players are in the Paris draw.

Cobolli, Fils, and Tiafoe each hold their respective spots, but the margins between 5th and 8th are not enormous. A semifinal run from any player currently outside the top eight — including Djokovic — reshuffles the order dramatically.

The Math of Paris Points

The Paris Masters pays 1,000 Race points to the champion. A finalist earns 600, a semifinalist 360, a quarterfinalist 180, and a second-round winner 90. The differences between rounds are large enough that a player who enters Paris 200 points outside the cut can exit Paris in the top eight if they reach the final while the player ahead of them loses early.

Djokovic is the most visible name chasing a qualifying spot from the outside. He is ranked around 11th or 12th in the Race, which puts him further back than de Minaur. For Djokovic to qualify, he needs a final at minimum while players ahead of him lose early. The math is possible. It requires a lot of things to go right simultaneously, and it starts with a win over Vacherot on Monday.

What's Next

The Race standings will update daily through the Paris draw. By Thursday, when the quarterfinals approach, the picture will be significantly clearer. By Sunday's final, the full eight-player field for Turin will be known.

The ATP Finals run November 15-22 in Turin. Paris concludes November 8. The week between them is travel and preparation. For the four players still chasing qualification, this is the only week that matters left in their 2026 season.
