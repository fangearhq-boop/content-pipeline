Shanghai Math: Sinner Loses 3,550 Race Points This Week as Zverev's Year-End No. 1 Bid Accelerates

By Marcus Cole October 5, 2026

The Numbers Are About to Move

The Rolex Shanghai Masters begins this Wednesday, October 7. For the ATP Race to Turin — the competition that determines which eight players qualify for the year-end championship in Turin — this week represents the most significant single points shift of the remaining season. And the player who benefits most never steps onto the court.

Jannik Sinner won the Shanghai Masters in 2025. That result is worth approximately 3,550 points in his Race to Turin defense. Beginning Wednesday, as the tournament progresses without him, those points begin to expire from his Race total. By the time Shanghai concludes, Sinner's Race standing could fall from 7,950 points to approximately 4,400 — a reduction of nearly 44 percent from his current position in two weeks.

Zverev's Opportunity

Alexander Zverev enters Shanghai with approximately 8,740 Race points — already leading Sinner by nearly 800 points — and now steps into an event where his defending points are a fraction of Sinner's. Zverev won the Shanghai QF in 2025, leaving him to defend far fewer points this year regardless of how far he advances. A semifinal or final appearance in Shanghai would push his total into the 9,000-range while Sinner's total simultaneously craters.

The arithmetic of the resulting gap — potentially exceeding 4,500 points — means Zverev's Year-End World No. 1 status would become mathematically near-impossible to lose even with four or five weeks of the season remaining. No German player has ever held the Year-End No. 1 ranking. Zverev, who turned the 2026 season into a two-Grand-Slam campaign at Roland Garros and the US Open, would be the first.

The Bubble Below

With Sinner and Zverev both already qualified for Turin, the Race competition matters most for the players in positions three through eight fighting for the remaining spots. Ben Shelton holds No. 3 at approximately 4,430 points. Alcaraz trails slightly at around 4,100, with his Tokyo title challenge this week capable of adding meaningful Race points. Daniil Medvedev (2,520), Alex de Minaur, Arthur Fils, and Jiří Menšík all sit below 2,000 Race points, needing substantial results in Shanghai and beyond to secure qualification.

Alcaraz is the most interesting case among the bubble players: if he wins Tokyo (possible given he reached the final), claims Shanghai points, and continues performing at his current level, he could push his Race total above 5,000 before November, effectively ensuring his Turin appearance.

What's Next

The Rolex Shanghai Masters runs October 7-19. Updated Race to Turin standings will be published after each round. The next milestone to watch: when Sinner's defense begins dropping in the system after each Shanghai round conclusion, and whether Zverev converts his opportunity in the early rounds to push his total above 9,000. The ATP Finals in Turin are scheduled for November; eight players will compete for the year-end championship.
