Laver Cup 2026: Full Squads Confirmed — Alcaraz and Zverev Lead Europe Against Shelton and Fritz

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | September 17, 2026

With the Laver Cup just eight days away, both complete six-player rosters for the 2026 edition have been confirmed. Team Europe and Team World are set to clash at The O2 Arena in London from September 25 through 27, in what promises to be the most competitive edition of the annual event since its debut in 2017.

Team Europe: The Defending Legacy

Captained by French tennis icon Yannick Noah, Team Europe will field a squad anchored by Carlos Alcaraz and US Open champion Alexander Zverev. Joining them are Casper Ruud, the consistent Norwegian who has been a Laver Cup regular, rising Italian Flavio Cobolli, Czech star Jakub Mensik, and Spanish youngster Rafael Jodar.

The headline absence is impossible to miss. World No. 1 Jannik Sinner, who won the 2026 Australian Open and has dominated the ATP tour for most of the year, will not participate due to the right knee injury that cost him the US Open. Sinner is targeting the China Open in Beijing (September 30) as his comeback event, meaning Europe's deepest weapon stays in reserve for the fall hard-court swing.

Even without Sinner, Europe's core is formidable. Alcaraz and Zverev represent arguably the two most dangerous players in the draw outside of the No. 1 himself.

Team World: Looking to Make It Back-to-Back

Team World's captain Andre Agassi — the eight-time Grand Slam champion now running the team bench — has built a squad around the Americans and Australians who broke Europe's 8-year stranglehold on the Laver Cup title in 2025. Ben Shelton, who reached the 2026 US Open final, headlines alongside Taylor Fritz, Alex de Minaur, Alexander Bublik, Learner Tien, and Tommy Paul.

The experience and firepower on Team World's bench is unprecedented. Shelton is coming off one of the best Grand Slam campaigns in recent American tennis history. De Minaur continues his rise as one of the ATP's most consistent performers. Fritz and Paul add depth from a US generation that has finally arrived as a force in men's tennis.

Format and What to Watch For

The Laver Cup uses a unique cumulative scoring format across three days. Friday and Saturday each feature four matches (three singles and one doubles), with point values increasing daily — Day 1 matches are worth one point each, Day 2 matches two points, and Day 3 matches three points. The first team to 13 points wins. If tied at 12–12 heading into Sunday evening, a deciding match goes to a Champions Tiebreak.

The Laver Cup is played on a distinctive black hard court at The O2 Arena. It does not offer ATP ranking points, making it a pure prestige event — which, paradoxically, often raises the intensity of competition.

Key matchups to watch: Alcaraz versus Shelton is the most anticipated singles duel. Zverev versus Fritz pits the US Open champion against America's top-ranked player. And whatever doubles pairing Agassi sends out against Noah's best should be spectacular.

What's Next

The Laver Cup opens Thursday, September 25 with the first day session. Evening sessions on Friday and Saturday will be broadcast globally. Coverage in the United States is available on Tennis Channel and streaming platforms. The complete session schedule and order of play will be announced closer to the event opening.
