Alcaraz's Packed Fall Swing: Laver Cup, Tokyo, Shanghai, Six Kings, Paris — And an ATP Finals Bid

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | September 17, 2026

Carlos Alcaraz doesn't do rest. Not in the fall. Not ever.

With the US Open behind him, the 23-year-old Spanish superstar has confirmed one of the most demanding fall schedules of any player on tour — a non-stop run through London, Tokyo, Shanghai, Saudi Arabia, Paris, and potentially Turin that will define his 2026 season-ending position and the overall shape of the ATP standings heading into 2027.

The Full Fall Calendar

Alcaraz's confirmed schedule runs as follows:

  • September 25–27: Laver Cup — The O2 Arena, London (Team Europe, captain Yannick Noah)
  • September 30–October 6: Japan Open — Tokyo (ATP 500)
  • October 7–18: Shanghai Masters — Shanghai (ATP 1000)
  • October 21–24: Six Kings Slam — Saudi Arabia (exhibition)
  • November 2–8: Rolex Paris Masters — Paris (ATP 1000)
  • November TBD: Nitto ATP Finals — Turin (if qualified)

The calendar is not just busy — it's structured to maximize points at every meaningful stop. Tokyo and Paris are ATP 500 and 1000 events respectively, both offering ranking points that feed directly into the Race to Turin standings. Shanghai, as one of the year's premier ATP 1000 events, carries enormous point stakes.

The Race to Turin Picture

As of September 17, Race to Turin leader Alexander Zverev has all but locked up the top position after his US Open title. Jannik Sinner remains second despite his injury layoff. The third through eighth spots remain genuinely contested.

Alcaraz, currently hovering on the edge of qualification, will need strong performances at Tokyo and Shanghai to secure his Turin spot. The Laver Cup, which does not offer ATP ranking points, is a prestige commitment rather than a strategic one — but the fall's back-to-back ATP events immediately after give him the opportunity to quickly build momentum.

For Alcaraz, the Six Kings Slam is a paid exhibition event in Saudi Arabia that sits between Shanghai and Paris. It has no impact on his ranking but typically features several top players in a high-profile, invitation-only format.

What's Next

Alcaraz kicks off his fall campaign at the Laver Cup on September 25, where he is expected to be one of Team Europe's primary singles players under captain Yannick Noah. His Japan Open first-round match is scheduled from September 30. Watch for results from Tokyo as the first real test of Alcaraz's standing in the Race to Turin before Shanghai's pivotal ATP 1000 week.
