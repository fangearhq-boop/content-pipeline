Kostyuk vs. Swiatek: The Toronto R16 Rematch That Has the US Open Build-Up Buzzing

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | August 8, 2026

When the Round of 16 draw came up at WTA Toronto, one match-up immediately stood above the others: Marta Kostyuk versus Iga Swiatek.

Kostyuk defeated Swiatek at Roland Garros 2026 in what was one of the significant upsets of the clay-court season. Now the two players meet again on hard courts in Toronto, with the US Open beginning in 16 days, and the outcome carries implications that extend well beyond this week in Canada.

Kostyuk earned her place in the Round of 16 with a commanding 6-3, 6-1 win over Madison Keys on Thursday. It was a performance that left little room for uncertainty — she broke Keys twice in the first set and twice again in the second, and the American had no real opening to get back into the match.

Rematch Stakes

Swiatek entered the Toronto tournament as No. 7 seed, a ranking that reflects what has been — by her own elevated standards — an uneven two seasons. Roland Garros was supposed to be the tournament where she reset. Instead, Kostyuk ended her run early in what became one of the talking points of the French Open.

Toronto is the first opportunity for Swiatek to answer that result on a different surface. Hard courts have historically been slightly more difficult territory for her compared to clay, where she has built much of her reputation. But she reached this stage in good form and will enter the match as the player with the stronger ranking and the deeper hard-court history at the top level.

Kostyuk's confidence, however, is very much intact. She has beaten Swiatek recently, she beat Keys convincingly, and she has been one of the more consistent performers on the WTA Tour in 2026.

The Wider Toronto Picture

Saturday's Round of 16 at WTA Toronto also features Aryna Sabalenka against Ekaterina Alexandrova. Sabalenka, the world No. 1, has won 24 of her last 25 hard-court matches in 2026 and leads their head-to-head 5-3. She is bidding for a third consecutive US Open title and her form in Toronto has been the most consistent of any player in the field.

Alexandra Eala, the Filipino star, reached the Round of 16 with a 6-3, 5-7, 6-4 victory over Caty McNally. Eala has been one of the emerging stories of the season, and her performance in Toronto continues a run of impressive results at the top level.

Elena Rybakina, the No. 2 seed, has been equally impressive on serve — she leads the WTA Tour with 306 aces in 2026 and reached the Round of 16 with a 6-2, 7-5 win over Ann Li.

What's Next

Kostyuk and Swiatek are scheduled for Saturday, August 8. A win for Kostyuk would mark back-to-back wins over the same opponent at a Grand Slam and a WTA 1000 in the space of two months — a statement that would carry real weight heading into the US Open. A win for Swiatek would provide exactly the kind of confidence-building victory her season has been missing on hard courts.

Either way, the outcome shapes the US Open narrative for both players. The tournament begins August 24 in New York, and the Round of 16 at Toronto is the last major test before the world's best women's players arrive at Flushing Meadows.
