Van Assche Topples Rublev in Estoril, Sets Up All-French Semifinal

By Ryan Calloway July 25, 2026

The Upset That Defined the Week

Andrey Rublev played what the ATP called his "most dominant win of 2026" earlier this week — a 6-1, 6-1 demolition of Timofey Skatov in 59 minutes. On Friday in Estoril, Luca Van Assche reminded everyone that dominant form has a shelf life. The young Frenchman, ranked No. 78, took down Rublev, the No. 1 seed and No. 14 in the world, in the Millennium Estoril Open quarterfinals. Van Assche called it "the biggest win of my career" afterward. Based on the opponent and the context, that is hard to argue with.

The result was the standout story of a day that produced two other notable quarterfinal outcomes. Hugo Gaston beat Portuguese wildcard Tiago Torres 6-3, 6-4 to set up an all-French semifinal against Van Assche on Saturday. In the top half of the draw, Luciano Darderi continued his remarkable 2026 clay season, recovering from a poor first set to beat his opponent 2-6, 7-5, 6-2. That was Darderi's Tour-leading 22nd clay-court win of the year. He faces Belgian No. 4 seed Alexander Blockx in the other Saturday semifinal.

What Van Assche's Win Means

Beating a top-15 player when they are playing their best tennis of the season is not an accident. Van Assche had to sustain a high level against an opponent who had just crushed a fellow professional without dropping more than two games per set. The fact that Van Assche executed well enough to take the match speaks to something real in his game.

The Frenchman is 23 years old and has been building toward results like this for the better part of two years. His ranking at No. 78 does not capture the talent level on display against Rublev. Opponents will need to recalibrate what they expect from him now that a win like this is on his resume.

Torres's run was also worth noting. The Portuguese wildcard upset No. 3 seed Alejandro Tabilo 6-4, 6-4 earlier in the week to reach his first ATP quarterfinal, giving the home crowd a reason to stay invested before Gaston ended the fairytale on Friday.

Darderi's Clay Season in Context

Twenty-two clay-court wins is a number that deserves its own sentence. Luciano Darderi has become one of the tour's most reliable clay-court performers in 2026, and his comeback from a rough first set on Friday is exactly the kind of match that confirms a player's quality. Winning two sets after losing one 2-6 — particularly in a quarterfinal — requires a specific kind of mental toughness that does not always show up in rankings.

His semifinal against Blockx is the match that will determine whether he reaches a second Estoril final. Darderi was the defending champion here, meaning a run to the final would carry additional meaning.

What's Next

Saturday's Estoril semifinals: Van Assche vs. Gaston and Darderi vs. Blockx. Both matches are scheduled with a 12:30 PM ET start for the first semi. The winner of the Van Assche-Gaston match will be the first Frenchman in an Estoril ATP final in recent memory. Tennis Fanrecap will report results as they are confirmed Saturday afternoon.
