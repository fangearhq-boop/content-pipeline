Hamburg Semifinals Set: Sherif Beats Badosa Again, Oliynykova Survives Three Sets

By Ryan Calloway July 25, 2026

Sherif and Badosa: Round Two Goes the Same Way

Mayar Sherif beat Paula Badosa 6-2, 6-3 in the MSC Hamburg Ladies Open quarterfinals on Friday — the second time in eight days she has gotten the better of the Spaniard. Last weekend in Iasi, Sherif won the final when Badosa retired. This time, Sherif won straight sets on the clay without any assistance. The margin was convincing, and the statement was clear: on this surface, right now, Sherif has Badosa's number.

The Egyptian player will face Anhelina Kalinina (Ukraine) in Saturday's semifinal. Kalinina beat home favorite Tamara Korpatsch 6-1, 6-3 in the quarterfinals — a result that was not close enough to give the home crowd much to hold onto. Kalinina has been efficient all week. The Sherif-Kalinina matchup is Saturday's feature semi, with two players in form meeting on a clay surface that suits both of them.

Oliynykova's Three-Set Battle

Oleksandra Oliynykova, the No. 1 seed, came through the toughest quarterfinal of the day. She beat Elina Avanesyan (Armenia) 6-3, 2-6, 6-2 — a match that went sideways in the second set before Oliynykova reclaimed control. Three-set wins in quarterfinals are both a sign of quality and a warning flag heading into the next round. Oliynykova has the game, but Saturday's semifinal against Anna Bondar will test whether Friday's effort left a mark.

Bondar arrived in the Hamburg semifinals via a 3-6, 7-5, 6-1 win over Alina Charaeva — a match that also went three sets but ended convincingly in the third. Hungary's Bondar is an experienced clay-court player who has been consistent throughout the week. Against a top seed coming off three sets, she has a genuine window.

The Last Clay Stop Before North America

Hamburg's final on Sunday will close out the European clay season. After this weekend, the tour shifts to North American hard courts with the National Bank Open in Toronto and Montreal beginning next week. For whichever player wins the Hamburg title Sunday, it represents a meaningful final data point before the surface changes completely.

A Sherif win would give her back-to-back clay titles and arrive as one of the tour's genuine stories of the summer. Oliynykova winning from the top seed position would be the expected result but no less earned. Either way, the Hamburg final on Sunday closes the European chapter of 2026 with an actual match worth watching.

What's Next
