### STORY 2: Federer Exhibition Recap

**Long-Form Post:**

He came back. He won. And the crowd at Arthur Ashe got exactly the night they paid for.

Roger Federer defeated Andy Roddick 6-3 in their singles set Tuesday night — adding to a head-to-head record that already stood at 21-3 in Federer's favor. Then Federer and John McEnroe combined to beat Roddick and Andre Agassi 7-5 in doubles. Both sets were played with no-ad scoring, which kept the pace tight and gave the crowd regular moments of drama.

The atmosphere was something else. Anna Wintour walked out for the coin toss. Matthew McConaughey narrated a hype reel on the scoreboard. In the stands: Serena Williams, Felix Auger-Aliassime, Casper Ruud, and Alexandra Eala — some of the brightest names in the current game, watching the men who shaped the era before them.

Twelve US Open singles titles sat in that stadium between the four players. It showed.

### STORY 4: US Open Qualifying Day 3 — Jordan Lee

**Long-Form Post:**

Jordan Lee is 16 years old. He won the Wimbledon boys' singles title. And right now, he is two wins away from the US Open main draw.

Lee beat Mark Lajal 4-6, 6-3, 6-3 in the first round of qualifying — a wildcard, unseeded, playing at Arthur Ashe for the first time. Today, in the second round, he faces Daniel Rincon, who upset the fifth seed to get there.

This is what qualifying looks like at its best: a teenager from America, on the biggest stage in American tennis, playing his way toward a main draw spot that could change his career trajectory. Nobody gave him a path. He is making one.

Jacob Fearnley (GBR) is also in the second round, having beaten Roberto Carballes Baena 7-6(3), 6-3. Bianca Andreescu is competing in the women's qualifying as part of her tour return.
