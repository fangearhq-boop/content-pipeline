US Open Qualifying Day 3: Jordan Lee One Win From the Main Draw

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | August 26, 2026

Qualifying at the 2026 US Open is generating its own headlines before the main draw even begins. Sixteen-year-old Jordan Lee — an American wildcard who won the Wimbledon boys' singles title — is into the second round of qualifying and needs one more win to reach the final qualifying round. He defeated Mark Lajal 4-6, 6-3, 6-3 in his R1 match, fighting back from a first-set deficit to advance.

Today, Wednesday August 26, is the third day of qualifying at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center. Day 3 features the second-round matches across both the men's and women's draws. Qualifying runs through Thursday August 27, with players needing to win three rounds to earn a main draw spot.

Jordan Lee: The Story of the Qualifying Field

Lee's route to this point is not typical for a 16-year-old. He won the Wimbledon boys' singles championship earlier this year, which elevated his profile considerably in junior tennis circles. But stepping into the US Open qualifying draw — as a wildcard, unseeded, facing ranked professionals — is a different challenge entirely.

His first-round win over Lajal showed the ability to manage a match that turned against him early. Down a set, Lee refocused and won the next two convincingly, 6-3 and 6-3. In the second round, he faces Daniel Rincon, who earlier in qualifying beat fifth seed Titouan Droguet 2-6, 6-4, 7-6(1) — a result that shows Rincon is a legitimate competitor.

World Tennis Magazine described Lee's first-round win as evidence that he "reaches another level in competitive tennis" at this stage of his development. A second-round win would put him into the final qualifying round, three wins away from the main draw.

Other Second-Round Contenders

Jacob Fearnley of Great Britain advanced from R1 with a 7-6(3), 6-3 win over Roberto Carballes Baena. Fearnley, seeded fourth in the qualifying draw, is experienced at this tournament — he reached the main draw last year and extended Alexander Zverev to two sets in the second round. Getting through qualifying efficiently is the goal; he is widely expected to be in the main draw.

Bianca Andreescu (CAN), the 2019 US Open champion, is competing in the women's qualifying draw as part of her return to professional tennis. Her presence in qualifying underscores the depth of competition — former Grand Slam champions are fighting for spots alongside teenagers seeking their first major appearance.

The Qualifying Field

The 2026 US Open qualifying draw has been described by multiple outlets as one of the most competitive in recent memory, a reflection of how deep professional tennis has become at the lower end of the rankings. Dozens of players ranked between 100 and 250 in the world are competing for sixteen main draw spots across the men's and women's draws.

The 128 combined first-round matches — 64 men's, 64 women's — were split between Monday and Tuesday. Second-round matches run Wednesday and Thursday. The final qualifying round takes place on the same day as the draw ceremony.

What's Next

Jordan Lee faces Rincon today in the second round. If Lee wins, he moves into the final qualifying round, which runs Thursday, the same day as the main draw ceremony at noon ET. The main draw begins Sunday, August 30. A qualifying win does not guarantee anything in the main draw — but it earns a player the right to be there.
