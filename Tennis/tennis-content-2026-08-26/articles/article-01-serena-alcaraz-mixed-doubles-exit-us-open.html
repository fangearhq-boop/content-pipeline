Serena and Alcaraz Exit US Open Mixed Doubles in Quarterfinals

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | August 26, 2026

The run is over. Serena Williams and Carlos Alcaraz were eliminated from the 2026 US Open mixed doubles on Wednesday, falling to Flavio Cobolli and Belinda Bencic 5-4(4), 4-1 in the quarterfinals. Their time in the draw lasted one full day — one win, one loss — but what it represented was larger than the scoreline.

Earlier the same day, the pairing had won their opening match, defeating Erin Routliffe and Lloyd Glasspool 5-3, 4-1. It was a convincing first-round performance that suggested the celebrity partnership could produce something real. But the quarterfinal was a different challenge, and Cobolli and Bencic proved more than capable.

How the Match Played Out

The first set went to a tiebreak, which Cobolli and Bencic won 7-4. Cobolli closed it out with an ace. In the second set, the decisive moment came in the fourth game, when Cobolli's return drew a mishit from Alcaraz on game point, allowing the pair to break. They served out the match from there.

Mixed doubles at the US Open is played using a super tiebreak format — a fast-scoring format that rewards clean hitting and limits the kind of sustained pressure that either Alcaraz or Williams might have imposed in a full three-set match. The quick format suited Cobolli and Bencic, who came in as a more practiced partnership.

What the Entry Meant

This mixed doubles wildcard was always about more than titles. For Serena, it was a return to the stadium where she won six US Open singles titles — a chance to compete at Flushing Meadows once more and feel the crowd's response. She retired from singles competition in 2022, and her appearances since have been rare.

For Alcaraz, the mixed doubles served a different purpose: competitive match time. He has not played a professional tennis tournament since suffering a right wrist injury in April. The US Open singles draw begins August 30, and arriving there with match experience — even in the mixed doubles format — is better than arriving cold. Both his R1 and quarterfinal matches gave him data on how the wrist performs under pressure.

Cobolli and Bencic Advance

Flavio Cobolli (ITA) and Belinda Bencic (SUI) move on to the mixed doubles semifinals. Their win was decisive — they did not appear challenged at any point beyond the first-set tiebreak. Cobolli's serving was a consistent weapon throughout, and the partnership showed the kind of coordination that comes from prior mixed doubles experience at the major level.

What's Next

For Alcaraz, the focus shifts entirely to the singles draw. He is the No. 2 seed at the 2026 US Open, behind Alexander Zverev. The main draw begins August 30. His performance this week — both in mixed doubles and at the Winston-Salem Open — will be studied closely for any signs of how the wrist has responded.

Serena's brief return to Flushing Meadows comes to its end. The draw ceremony for the singles event takes place today, Thursday August 27, at noon ET. The serious business of the 2026 US Open is about to begin.
