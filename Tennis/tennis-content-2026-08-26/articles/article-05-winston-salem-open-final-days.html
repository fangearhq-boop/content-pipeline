Winston-Salem Open: Final Days Before the US Open

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | August 26, 2026

The Winston-Salem Open enters its final stretch this week with quarterfinals and semifinals running Wednesday and Thursday at Wake Forest University. The ATP 250 tournament wraps August 29 — one day before the 2026 US Open main draw begins — making it the last meaningful preparation event for players who chose to compete here.

For most players in the draw, the calculus of playing Winston-Salem versus resting before New York is straightforward: match sharpness versus physical freshness. The players who are here made the calculation that the former matters more. Whether that choice proves correct will become clear in the first week of the US Open.

Alcaraz: The Ongoing Wrist Watch

Carlos Alcaraz entered the Winston-Salem Open on a wildcard — the first time he has played this tournament — as his first competitive action since suffering a right wrist injury in April. He is the No. 2 seed at the US Open and arrives at Flushing Meadows as one of the most scrutinized players in the field, with every match he plays this week serving as a data point on his injury recovery.

He also competed in the US Open mixed doubles with Serena Williams on Wednesday, playing two matches in one day before losing in the quarterfinals to Flavio Cobolli and Belinda Bencic. That combination of mixed doubles and Winston-Salem singles represents the most match time Alcaraz has logged since his injury — the volume of competitive tennis this week was the point.

The Tournament's Role in the Calendar

The Winston-Salem Open is an ATP 250, the lowest rung of the Masters-qualifying event structure. The champion earns 250 ranking points — meaningful for players in the 30–80 range but not transformational for top-10 players. What it offers is competitive matches on hard courts, in the same climate and time zone as New York, in the days immediately before the US Open.

Players who have won here in recent years and subsequently performed well at the US Open understand the specific value. The Winston-Salem courts are fast. The conditions — humidity, heat, afternoon wind patterns — are comparable to what players will face at Flushing Meadows. There is no better simulation.

What the Final Days Reveal

Quarterfinalists and semifinalists at Winston-Salem will enter the US Open with three to four completed hard-court matches in the past week. For a player returning from injury, that match load matters. For a player in form, it adds momentum. For a player who is physically depleted, it can be a problem.

The tournament's finals take place Saturday August 29, giving the champion one full day of rest before the US Open draw begins play on August 30. Given the tight timeline, some players may choose to retire from Winston-Salem rather than risk compounding physical wear before the Grand Slam.

What's Next

Winston-Salem quarterfinals and semifinals run Wednesday and Thursday. The final is Saturday August 29. The same day, the US Open draw — released Thursday at noon ET — becomes the context through which all Winston-Salem results are interpreted. The 2026 US Open main draw begins Sunday, August 30. Alcaraz's performance at Winston-Salem will be the primary pre-tournament story heading into Flushing Meadows.
