2026 US Open Draw Ceremony: What to Watch For at Noon ET Today

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | August 26, 2026

At noon ET today, Thursday August 27, the 2026 US Open singles draw will be released. Every matchup for the August 30 main draw depends on what the ceremony produces — which half Novak Djokovic lands in, whether Alcaraz faces someone dangerous in the first round, how far apart the top seeds are placed from one another.

The seedings are already established. The names are set. What we do not yet know is the bracket. Here is what to focus on when the draw drops.

Men's Seeds to Watch

Alexander Zverev enters as the No. 1 seed — the first time he has held the top spot at a US Open. Jannik Sinner, who held the world No. 1 ranking earlier this season, withdrew with a knee injury, opening the door for Zverev to lead the field. Zverev's draw position determines everything about his projected path.

Carlos Alcaraz is seeded No. 2 and has the most compelling narrative in the field: he is returning from a right wrist injury that sidelined him since April. He has completed mixed doubles and Winston-Salem matches this week, but the Grand Slam singles draw is the real test. The draw could give him a comfortable first-round entry or an early challenge that forces a genuine reading of his recovery.

Novak Djokovic, at No. 4, is hunting his 25th Grand Slam title. He has won the US Open four times. The draw determines whether he and Alcaraz could collide in the semifinals — or whether Zverev and Alcaraz are on a collision course for the other half. At the majors, the bracket shapes the story before a ball is struck.

Women's Seeds to Watch

Aryna Sabalenka holds the No. 1 seed. Iga Swiatek is No. 2. Coco Gauff enters as both the defending champion and the No. 3 seed — a combination that makes her one of the most watched players in the women's draw. Jennifer Pegula is seeded No. 4 after her Cincinnati finalist run.

Alexandra Eala (PHI) is seeded at a Grand Slam for the first time. As the first Filipino player to earn a seeded position at a major, the draw will determine whether she faces a fellow seeded player early or gets a more open path into the second week.

The Withdrawal Factor

More than fourteen players have withdrawn from the original 2026 US Open field. Sinner's absence is the headline, but each withdrawal reshuffles the bracket potential in ways that create genuine opportunities for unseeded players and lower seeds. The draw ceremony will make those open sections visible.

For players ranked just outside the seeded bracket, a favorable quarter of the draw — one depleted by withdrawals — can be the difference between a first-week exit and a deep run.

What's Next

The draw releases at noon ET today. First-round analysis and matchup breakdowns follow quickly after. The US Open main draw begins Sunday, August 30. Night sessions at Arthur Ashe will feature the highest-profile first-round matches — which ones those are depends entirely on how the ceremony unfolds today.
