Basel R16 Chaos: Wild Card Kym Stuns Shelton, Vacherot Topples Jódar

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap

October 30, 2026

The round of 16 at the Swiss Indoors in Basel produced one of the most unsettled days of the indoor autumn swing. Wild card Jérôme Kym — ranked No. 222 in the world — defeated top seed Ben Shelton. Valentin Vacherot eliminated third seed Rafael Jódar. Jan-Lennard Struff beat Novak Djokovic for the first time in their career head-to-head. When the dust settled Thursday, three of the tournament's most prominent names were out.

The quarterfinal field looks nothing like the pre-tournament bracket projected. And for several players in the ATP Finals Race, the results have immediate consequences.

Kym's Biggest Career Win

Jérôme Kym, a 23-year-old Swiss player from Aargau, entered the Swiss Indoors on a wild card. He had beaten Arthur Rinderknech 7-6, 6-4 in his opening match. In the round of 16, he faced Ben Shelton — one of three players who has already clinched a spot at the ATP Finals in Turin and the tournament's top seed.

Kym won. The upset is the biggest of his career by a wide margin. Playing in his home country on a wild card, he knocked out the tournament favorite in front of the Basel crowd. The Swiss faithful at St. Jakobshalle, who spent part of Monday evening saying goodbye to Stan Wawrinka, had another home moment to celebrate by the end of the week.

For Shelton, the defeat has limited consequences beyond Basel. He is already qualified for Turin. His season continues in November with the ATP Finals themselves.

Vacherot Upsets Jódar

Third seed Rafael Jódar was also eliminated in the round of 16, losing to Valentin Vacherot, a qualifier who entered the week with little tournament pedigree at this level. Jódar's loss matters for the Race to Turin: unlike Shelton, the Spaniard was not yet qualified for the ATP Finals and needed a deep run in Basel to strengthen his position. His early exit tightens the race further in the final weeks of the season.

Jódar had entered the week as one of the players fighting for the last ATP Finals spots alongside Djokovic, Medvedev, Cobolli, Tiafoe, and others. His Basel points now max out at the round-of-16 level.

The Basel Quarterfinal Lineup

The remaining quarterfinals are: Kym vs. Lehečká, Struff vs. Borges, Fritz vs. Fils, and Vacherot's match against the surviving bracket winner. Arthur Fils — the No. 2 seed — advanced safely, as did Taylor Fritz, who beat Denis Shapovalov 6-4, 6-3. Nuno Borges also reached the quarters with an upset of his own, beating Casper Ruud 1-6, 6-3, 6-4 in three sets.

The Fritz vs. Fils quarterfinal is the marquee matchup: two players who both entered the week inside the ATP Finals qualification picture, playing against each other to advance. One player's tournament points get cut short. That result will affect the Race standings.

What's Next

The Basel quarterfinals are scheduled for Friday. Meanwhile, the Paris Masters begins on the same day — Friday, October 31 — and will draw several of the players eliminated from Basel this week. Djokovic, Jódar, and others whose ATP Finals bids depend on Paris will turn their attention to the Masters 1000 immediately. Paris runs through November 8, one week before the Finals begin in Turin.

The autumn indoor swing has been unpredictable. Basel's quarterfinals carry that momentum into the final qualifying stretch.
