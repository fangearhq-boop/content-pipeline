Vienna and Basel Open Sunday — Wawrinka Tribute Highlights Loaded Indoor Week

By Ryan Calloway | October 24, 2026 | Tennis Fanrecap

The European indoor season's most consequential week begins Sunday when the Erste Bank Open in Vienna and the Swiss Indoors in Basel both launch their first rounds on the same day. Between the wild cards, the ATP Race implications, and a farewell tribute to one of Switzerland's greatest tennis players, there's a lot happening before a single ball is struck.

In Vienna, Alexander Zverev enters as the top seed in a draw that also includes Carlos Alcaraz on a wild card. Zverev, who has already confirmed his spot at the ATP Finals in Turin, arrives looking to add another major title to a 2026 season that includes the Year-End No. 1 ranking. Alcaraz's wild card appearance — he is also an ATP Finals qualifier — gives the Vienna draw star power it might not have had through the normal seeding process.

The full Vienna seedings: Zverev (1), Alcaraz (WC/2), Medvedev (3), Cobolli (4), de Minaur (5), Tiafoe (6), Paul (7), Mensik (8).

Basel and the Wawrinka Farewell

Basel is framed differently. The Swiss Indoors has historically been one of the most emotionally charged events on the ATP calendar — Roger Federer won it 10 times — and this year the tournament has organized a tribute night for Stan Wawrinka, scheduled for Sunday's opening session. Wawrinka, a three-time Grand Slam champion who has spent much of his career playing in Federer's shadow in Switzerland, is likely making one of his final appearances at a tournament he considers home.

On the competitive side, Ben Shelton leads the Basel draw as the top seed, with Novak Djokovic playing on a wild card. Djokovic's position is notable for two reasons: he is outside the ATP Finals top eight in current standings, and a deep run in Basel could legitimately change that picture before the Paris Masters.

The ATP Race Backdrop

Both Vienna and Basel award 500 ATP Race to Turin points to their champions. Three players have already confirmed their ATP Finals spots — Zverev, Alcaraz, and Shelton — leaving five spots open among a tight group of contenders. The Vienna and Basel winners effectively hand themselves a major advantage entering the Paris Masters, the season's final Masters 1000 event.

Notable Race players in this week's draws: Medvedev (Vienna), Cobolli (Vienna), de Minaur (Vienna), Tiafoe (Vienna), Fils (Basel), Jódar (Basel), Djokovic (Basel). The math gets compressed fast.

What's Next

First-round action begins Sunday, October 26, across both Vienna and Basel. Draws will produce the specific first-round matchups that start shaping the week. The tribute to Wawrinka on Sunday at Basel is expected to be one of the emotional highlights of the indoor season. Watch the Race standings closely — by the end of next weekend, the picture for Turin will look considerably clearer.
