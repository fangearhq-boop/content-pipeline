ATP Race to Turin Enters Final Phase With Three Events Left and Five Spots Open

By Elena Voss | October 24, 2026 | Tennis Fanrecap

The ATP Race to Turin is in its final stretch, and the math is getting critical. With three events remaining — Vienna and Basel (both Oct 26–Nov 1) and the Paris Masters (Oct 31–Nov 8) — only three of the eight ATP Finals spots are confirmed. Five are still up for grabs among a field of at least seven credible contenders.

Alexander Zverev, Carlos Alcaraz, and Ben Shelton have sealed their places in Turin. Jannik Sinner, who was defending ATP Finals champion, was ruled out for the season with a right knee injury, leaving his position effectively open and adding one extra available slot to the mix.

The Contenders and the Points Gap

As of last week's rankings, the group chasing the five remaining spots — in approximate Race order — includes Flavio Cobolli, Daniil Medvedev, Frances Tiafoe, Giovanni Mpetshi Perricard (Fils), Rafael Jódar, Alex de Minaur, and Novak Djokovic. Roughly 700 points separated the group from fifth to eleventh place in the standings, which means a single strong week can scramble the order.

The points available in the final three events: Vienna winner earns 500, Basel winner earns 500, Paris Masters winner earns 1,000. Semifinalists and finalists in each event also earn meaningful points. For a player currently sitting outside the top eight, a title at any of the three remaining events represents a potential leapfrog opportunity.

Medvedev's Almaty Wildcard

One factor not yet priced into this week's standings: Daniil Medvedev is in the Almaty Open semifinal today. The ATP 250 in Kazakhstan offers 250 points to the champion — less than Vienna or Basel, but a meaningful boost before the week begins. A Medvedev title in Almaty before Vienna's first round Sunday would put him in a stronger position entering the tournament where he is the third seed.

Alexander Bublik, Medvedev's potential Almaty final opponent, also has Race considerations. Bublik is currently outside the top eight but a title week — Almaty final plus a deep Vienna run — would transform his standing before Paris.

What's Next

Vienna and Basel both begin Sunday, October 26. The Paris Masters follows Oct 31. Turin spots will likely be sealed or eliminated by the time Paris concludes on November 8. Expect the Race standings to shift dramatically every day next week — the condensed schedule of Vienna, Basel, and Paris overlapping means results pile up fast. Players who go deep in multiple events have the best chance; anyone who exits in the first round at both ATP 500s is probably watching Paris as their last hope.
