Weekend Preview: Two Finals on Sunday, Two Events Opening — Busiest Day of the Indoor Season

By Marcus Cole | October 24, 2026 | Tennis Fanrecap

Sunday, October 25 might be the most content-dense single day of the tennis calendar outside of Grand Slam second-week sessions. Two tournaments reach their finals on the same afternoon, and two more begin their first rounds. For tennis fans, it's a scheduling gift. For players with ATP Race implications, it's the starting gun on the most important week of the fall.

Almaty Final — Medvedev Defends

Saturday's Almaty Open semifinals will determine who faces whom in Sunday's final, but the logical outcome — if the seeds hold — puts Daniil Medvedev against Alexander Bublik in a Kazakh indoor final with real ATP Race context. Medvedev is the defending champion and top seed. Bublik gets to play in front of a home crowd that has treated him like a local hero all week. The head-to-head (7-1 Medvedev) suggests the outcome, but Bublik has a habit of defying expectations.

In the other half, Stefanos Tsitsipas faces qualifier Nikoloz Basilashvili — a matchup that felt routine a few days ago before Basilashvili dismantled Adrian Mannarino 6-3, 6-1 in Thursday's quarterfinal. Tsitsipas is the better player, but qualifiers in ATP 250 semifinals exist precisely because they've already beaten someone they weren't supposed to.

Ningbo Final — Rybakina Looks to Defend, Fernandez Still Running

The Ningbo Open final also takes place Sunday. Elena Rybakina, the defending champion and top seed, is in the semifinal today against Diana Shnaider. On the other side, Leylah Fernandez's surprise run continues — she beat Ekaterina Alexandrova 4-6, 6-2, 6-1 Thursday and faces Marie Bouzková in a semifinal that few predicted either player would reach.

Rybakina won the Ningbo title a year ago and has been among the most consistent players on the women's tour. With WTA Finals coming in November at Indian Wells, this week is a meaningful preparation block. Winning a second consecutive Ningbo title would be a statement.

Vienna and Basel Also Launch Sunday

As if Sunday weren't full enough, the Erste Bank Open in Vienna and the Swiss Indoors in Basel both begin first-round action Oct 26. Zverev, Alcaraz, Shelton, Medvedev, and Djokovic are all in the mix — and a Wawrinka tribute night at Basel adds an emotional layer to the opening day.

The indoor swing has arrived at its peak. There is genuinely too much tennis happening simultaneously to watch all of it.

What's Next

Almaty and Ningbo finals are Sunday, October 25. Vienna and Basel first rounds also begin October 26. By Monday morning, two champions will have been crowned and both 500-point events will be in full swing. The ATP Race picture changes with every result.
