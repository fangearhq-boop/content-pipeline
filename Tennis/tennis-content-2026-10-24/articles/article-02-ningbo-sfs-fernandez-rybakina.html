Fernandez Stuns Alexandrova to Reach Ningbo SF as Rybakina Marches On

By Marcus Cole | October 24, 2026 | Tennis Fanrecap

Leylah Fernandez isn't supposed to be in the Ningbo Open semifinals. Nobody expected this. That's part of what makes her run this week worth paying attention to.

The Canadian defeated Ekaterina Alexandrova 4-6, 6-2, 6-1 Thursday in the BNB Ningbo Open quarterfinals, completing a comeback from a lost opening set to earn a spot in Saturday's semifinal. Fernandez now faces Marie Bouzková for a place in Sunday's final — a matchup that, a week ago, would have been impossible to predict for either player.

What makes the Fernandez win significant beyond the result: Alexandrova was the player who defeated Marta Kostyuk in Wednesday's second round — the match where Kostyuk's retirement handed Karolina Muchova the final WTA Finals spot. Fernandez beating the player who ended Kostyuk's week adds a narrative layer to a quarterfinal that already had plenty of subtext.

Rybakina Defending, Shnaider Pressing

On the other side of the draw, Elena Rybakina handled her quarterfinal assignment with the composed efficiency expected of a defending champion and top seed. She defeated Kawa 6-1, 3-6, 6-1 — dropping only four games across the first and third sets despite the middle-set wobble. Rybakina now faces sixth seed Diana Shnaider in a semifinal with considerably more bite than her quarterfinal matchup.

Rybakina leads her head-to-head with Shnaider 2-0, but Shnaider has been in clean form all week, dispatching fourth seed Marta Jovic's replacement 6-3, 4-6, 6-1 in Thursday's quarterfinal. The Russian is playing her best tennis of the fall swing and will test the defending champion's authority in a way Kawa did not.

Rybakina's week also carries a separate layer of significance. With the WTA Finals field now confirmed — she is one of the eight players bound for Indian Wells in November — her Ningbo defense is a statement-setting final tune-up before the season's most prestigious event.

What's Next

Both Ningbo Open semifinals take place Saturday, October 24. The final is Sunday, October 25. On one side: the defending champion Rybakina against a focused Shnaider. On the other: Fernandez, riding a momentum wave, against Bouzková. WTA Finals preparation is the backdrop for at least one of these players, and Fernandez's unexpected run may be giving the entire field something to think about before Indian Wells.
