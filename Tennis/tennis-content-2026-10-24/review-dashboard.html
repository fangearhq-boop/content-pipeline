### Long-Form Post The Almaty Open quarterfinals produced exactly the drama an ATP 250 event needs to stand out, and now the draw has set two compelling semifinals for Saturday. Daniil Medvedev, the defending champion and top seed, remained in cruise control, defeating Mikhail Atmane 6-4, 6-4. His reward: a semifinal against Alexander Bublik. On paper it looks lopsided — Medvedev leads their head-to-head 7-1 — but Bublik earned his spot the hard way, surviving Karen Khachanov 4-6, 7-6(5), 7-6(5) in a three-set thriller. The week's biggest surprise is in the other half. Qualifier Nikoloz Basilashvili turned in a dominant quarterfinal, dismantling seventh seed Adrian Mannarino 6-3, 6-1. He'll face Stefanos Tsitsipas, who handled James Duckworth 6-3, 6-4. Tsitsipas leads their head-to-head 3-1, but Basilashvili's run is the story of the week. With 500 ATP Race to Turin points available to the champion, there's plenty at stake beyond the trophy. The final is Sunday, October 25. Fernandez has been one of the pleasant surprises of the week in a draw that's already seen multiple upsets. Both semifinals are Saturday. The final is Sunday, October 25. ATP Race to Turin — The Crucial Week Begins
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post The ATP Race to Turin is entering its final stretch, and the math is getting critical. Alexander Zverev, Carlos Alcaraz, and Ben Shelton have already confirmed their spots at the ATP Finals in Turin. That leaves five places open, contested by at least seven players: Cobolli, Medvedev, Tiafoe, Fils, Jódar, de Minaur, and Djokovic — with roughly 700 points separating the group as of last week. Three events remain: Vienna (500 points to the winner, Oct 26–Nov 1), Basel (500, same week), and the Paris Masters (1000, Oct 31–Nov 8). Notably, Jannik Sinner is ruled out for the season due to a right knee injury, leaving his spot effectively open. One wildcard this weekend: Medvedev is in the Almaty semifinal today, where 250 points are available. A title there before Vienna even begins could shift the standings meaningfully. What's your prediction — who grabs the last spot in Turin? --- ### Image Post Caption 3 confirmed for Turin. 5 spots left. Vienna, Basel, and Paris are the final three chances.
