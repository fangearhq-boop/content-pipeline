### STORY 1: Halys Makes History — Maiden ATP Title in Kitzbühel
- **Tier:** 1
- **Angle:** NEW STORY — Quentin Halys (FRA, 29) defeated defending champion Alexander Bublik (KAZ, No. 1 seed) 6-4, 7-6(6) in the Generali Open Kitzbühel final on Saturday July 25 to claim his first career ATP title. The win ended a 14-year professional journey — Halys earned his first ATP ranking point in 2012 at age 15. He became the first Frenchman to win the Kitzbühel title in the Open Era (France was 0-6 in previous Kitzbühel finals). Halys fired 17 aces and never faced a break point in the match (1h33m). He rises 32 places to No. 51 in the ATP Rankings (career high was No. 46). Bublik's bid for the first Kitzbühel title defense since 2013 came up short.
- **Posting Window:** 8:00 AM ET
