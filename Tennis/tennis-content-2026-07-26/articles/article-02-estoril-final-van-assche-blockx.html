Estoril Final: Van Assche vs. Blockx, Two First-Timers Compete for the Title

By Marcus Cole July 26, 2026

An Unprecedented Final

The Millennium Estoril Open final on Sunday features Luca Van Assche of France against Alexander Blockx of Belgium — two players competing in their first ATP 250 final, both unseeded, both without a professional title to their name. It is a matchup built entirely from the week's upsets rather than from the draw's intentions.

Van Assche, ranked No. 78, set the tone on Friday when he defeated top seed Andrey Rublev in the quarterfinals. He described the win over Rublev — who had just dismantled Timofey Skatov 6-1, 6-1 in 59 minutes earlier in the day — as "the biggest win of my career." He followed it by beating fellow Frenchman Hugo Gaston 6-4, 7-5 in the all-French semifinal on Saturday.

The Blockx Comeback That Defined the Week

Blockx's road to the final was more dramatic. In his semifinal against second seed Luciano Darderi — the Tour leader in clay wins in 2026 with 22 — Blockx lost the first set 5-7, then fell 0-5 in the third. The match appeared to be heading to a routine exit.

Instead, Blockx won seven consecutive games. He claimed the third set in a tiebreak, won the second set in a tiebreak that he dominated 7-1, and closed out a 5-7, 7-6(1), 7-6(5) victory that ranked among the week's most remarkable results. "I have no words," Blockx said afterward. The phrase captured something honest about what had just happened: he had pulled off a comeback that most players on the same clay, in the same situation, would not have managed.

Head-to-Head and Context

Van Assche and Blockx met earlier in 2026 on hard courts, with Van Assche winning 6-2, 6-4. That result carries limited weight on clay in Estoril, on a Sunday, with a title at stake. Clay court conditions reward different qualities — patience, heavy topspin, physical resilience — and both players have shown those qualities across this week's matches.

Van Assche is the more established of the two at this level. His win over Rublev this week is the biggest scalp of his career so far. Blockx has not beaten a seed as high as Darderi before this week. The question for Sunday is whether Van Assche's earlier-in-the-week high — toppling Rublev — carries forward into the final, or whether Blockx's extraordinary semifinal momentum gives him a psychological edge that matters more than the rankings difference.

What's Next

Whoever wins the Estoril final today claims their first ATP Tour title and a significant ranking jump heading into the North American hard-court season. The final is scheduled for 3:00 PM local time (10:00 AM ET) at the Clube de Ténis do Estoril in Cascais. It is the last ATP 250 clay final of the 2026 European swing. For Van Assche and Blockx both, it represents an opportunity that neither player was seeded to reach. Tennis Fanrecap will have the result as soon as it is confirmed.
