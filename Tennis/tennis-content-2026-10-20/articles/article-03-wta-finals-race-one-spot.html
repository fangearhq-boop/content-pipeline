One Spot Left: The WTA Finals Race Enters Its Final Chapter

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | October 20, 2026

Seven players have secured their berths at the 2026 WTA Finals at the Indian Wells Tennis Garden (November 8–15). One spot remains — and at least three players have a realistic claim on it.

The confirmed field: Elena Rybakina, Aryna Sabalenka, Mirra Andreeva, Jessica Pegula, Coco Gauff, Elina Svitolina, and Linda Noskova (who qualified as Wimbledon champion under the Grand Slam champion provision). All seven locked in their berths before the Wuhan Open concluded.

Who's Fighting for the Last Ticket

Three players are positioned to claim the final Indian Wells berth. Karolina Muchova holds the strongest position on points after a season that has included consistent deep runs at majors and WTA 1000 events. Marta Kostyuk is close behind. Iga Swiatek, who has fallen from the top of the Race to Indian Wells following a leaner second half of the season — including a semifinal exit at Wuhan — is the third major candidate.

The race will likely be settled at the Kremlin Cup in Moscow or, in some scenarios, on the first day of the Finals themselves. The eighth berth goes to the highest-ranked Grand Slam champion of the year who falls between No. 8 and No. 20 in the race and has not already qualified — a provision that could complicate the standings if a Grand Slam winner enters contention.

Chwalinska on the Periphery

Maja Chwalinska's Wuhan Open title and the resulting jump to world No. 11 have pushed her into the conversation. Her exact position in the Race to Indian Wells points standings was not confirmed as of Monday, but her ranking now puts her in the range where additional points from a tournament appearance could have an impact.

The catch: both Chwalinska and Sonay Kartal have withdrawn from their next events with injuries. If Chwalinska does not play again before the Finals, her race points from Wuhan alone may not be sufficient. But her situation will be worth watching.

What's Next

The WTA Finals field will be complete before the November 8 draw. With Muchova likely to confirm her spot, the drama centers on whether Swiatek or Kostyuk can overtake her — and whether Chwalinska enters late with a surprise result. The 2026 WTA Finals at Indian Wells will feature eight of the best players in the world across a format that has historically produced some of the year's most compelling tennis.
