Paris Rolex Masters Preview: Race to Turin Stakes Arrive at Bercy

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | October 20, 2026

The Paris Rolex Masters opens October 31 at the Plenitude Arena in Bercy, Paris. The penultimate ATP Masters 1000 of the season runs through November 8 — and for the players chasing a spot at the ATP Finals in Turin, every match will matter more than usual.

No Sinner to Defend

Jannik Sinner won the 2025 Paris Masters and will not be defending his title. The Italian's 2026 season ended early, leaving the Bercy field without its reigning champion. That opens the draw in a way that makes Paris 2026 genuinely unpredictable — there is no dominant figure expected to arrive as the prohibitive favourite.

Alexander Zverev is the closest thing to that. Confirmed as the 2026 Year-End No. 1, Zverev arrives in Paris with the relaxed confidence of someone who has already won the year. His two majors — Roland-Garros and the US Open — are behind him. But Bercy is one of the few ATP Masters titles that has eluded him, and he is expected to treat Paris as a genuine title target rather than a warm-up.

The Race to Turin Storyline

Bercy is the last significant points opportunity before the ATP Finals in Turin. The bubble of players competing for the final spots is unsettled after the Asian swing. Daniil Medvedev, who lost in the Shanghai semifinals to Jódar, comes to Paris with something to prove after a turbulent autumn. Alex de Minaur, a consistent performer on hard courts, is in the same bracket of players watching the Turin standings carefully.

Rafael Jódar, 20, may be the most interesting name in the Paris conversation. His Shanghai run raised his profile globally and lifted him to a career-high ranking. His arrival at Bercy will determine how quickly the ATP world adjusts its view of him. A deep Paris run would make his ATP Finals case almost impossible to deny.

Vienna and Basel First

Before Paris, both Vienna and Basel open this coming week. Many of the Paris contenders will play through one of those 500-level events first. The scheduling means some players could be arriving at Bercy mid-week, having just played a match in Vienna or Basel. The compressed calendar is a known feature of the European indoor swing, and one that plays to the advantage of deeper squads and players with stronger recovery.

What's Next

The Paris Rolex Masters draw will be released closer to the October 31 start date. Watch for the seedings once the Vienna and Basel results are factored in. Bercy is not a guaranteed event for all players — some will prioritize rest after Vienna and Basel — but for the ATP Finals bubble, it is non-negotiable. Paris and Turin run back to back. The year ends in 18 days.
