The 2026 Asian Swing Is Over — Here's What It Told Us

By Elena Voss

October 18, 2026

The 2026 Asian swing ended on Sunday with two tournaments producing results nobody had scripted at the start of October. Alejandro Davidovich Fokina is the Shanghai Masters champion. The Wuhan Open title belongs to either Sonay Kartal or Maja Chwalinska — both first-time WTA 1000 finalists, both making their debut at this stage. Both events gave the tennis world something to talk about.

ATP: The Spaniards Take Shanghai

The Shanghai Masters final was a Spaniard vs. Spaniard affair, with Fokina overcoming Jodar 1-6, 7-6(5), 6-4 in a comeback that defined the week. Fokina wins his first Masters 1000 title and a new career high of No. 13. Jodar, 20 years old and in his first major final, proved that his name belongs in these conversations — and he still has time.

The upsets along the way were just as significant. World No. 1 Alexander Zverev was eliminated in the semifinals by Fokina. Daniil Medvedev — who had reached the Shanghai final in six consecutive prior appearances — fell to Jodar in a semifinal marred by a heated umpire dispute. The draw produced real results rather than the favorites trading wins until the end.

WTA: Wuhan Delivers Surprises at Every Turn

The Wuhan Open, the final WTA 1000 event of 2026, turned in the kind of week that tournaments dream about. Sonay Kartal, ranked No. 170 and returning from a six-month injury absence, beat the No. 6 seed Elina Svitolina and then beat Jelena Ostapenko in a semifinal that went to two tiebreaks. Maja Chwalinska defeated Iga Swiatek 7-5, 6-1 in the other semifinal, with Swiatek managing a leg issue throughout.

A No. 170 vs. No. 22 final at a WTA 1000 is not supposed to happen. It did. Both players are in their first WTA 1000 final. Regardless of who wins, the event produced a story worth telling.

What the Asian Swing Told Us

The swing confirmed that Fokina is capable of performing at Masters level when everything clicks — and gave him a title to show for it. It confirmed that Jodar is a genuine threat at the highest level, not just an exciting prospect. It revealed that Medvedev's Shanghai invincibility had limits. And on the women's side, it reminded the tour that rankings are often the least reliable predictor of a tournament's outcome.

Kartal's rise — from No. 170, from six months injured, to a WTA 1000 final — is one of the best stories the WTA has had this year. The fact that she finished it at the final event of the Asian swing makes the timing of the story even better.

What's Next

The ATP heads to the Paris Rolex Masters, beginning October 26, for the final ATP Masters 1000 event of 2026. ATP Finals qualification spots are still up for grabs. On the WTA side, the finals draw in Guadalajara will come into focus once the rankings from Wuhan are confirmed. Several qualification battles remain live heading into November.
