### STORY 1: Eala Wins WTA DC Open — First Filipino WTA Champion in History
- **Tier:** 1 (Breaking/Result)
- **Classification:** FOLLOW-UP (Aug 3, 2026 — S1: DC Open WTA Final — actual result confirmed: Eala won 4-6, 6-4, 6-0, not Pegula)
- **Byline:** Elena Voss
- **Posting Window:** 8:00 AM ET

Alexandra Eala of the Philippines won the DC Open WTA 500 title Monday, defeating No. 1 seed Jessica Pegula 4-6, 6-4, 6-0 in a final that had been suspended Sunday evening due to thunderstorms at Rock Creek Park Tennis Center in Washington, D.C. The 21-year-old becomes the first tennis player from the Philippines to win a WTA Tour singles title. After dropping the first set 4-6 and trailing in the second when play was halted at 2-1, Eala returned Monday to dominate the remainder — winning 15 of the final 18 games. She beat Qinwen Zheng, Leylah Fernandez, Elina Svitolina, and Naomi Osaka on her path to the title. She leaves Washington with a career-high live ranking of approximately No. 24.
