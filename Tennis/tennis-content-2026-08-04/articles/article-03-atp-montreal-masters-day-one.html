ATP Montreal Masters 1000 Opens: Zverev, FAA, Shelton Lead Wide-Open Draw Without Sinner or Alcaraz

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | August 4, 2026

The ATP Masters 1000 in Montreal officially opened its main draw Tuesday at IGA Stadium, and the absence of tennis' three biggest stars has created one of the most genuinely competitive draws of the season. Jannik Sinner, Carlos Alcaraz, and Novak Djokovic are all out. The question of who claims the Montreal title — and the narrative ground that comes with it — is wide open.

The field is led by Alexander Zverev (Germany) as the No. 1 seed. Ben Shelton (USA) is the third seed and the defending champion. Alex de Minaur (Australia) is fourth. Andrey Medvedev (Russia) is fifth. The draw was compressed after rain cancelled Sunday's first-round matches, meaning the early rounds will move quickly.

Auger-Aliassime at Home — and Seeded No. 2 for the First Time

The tournament's most compelling subplot entering Day 1 is Felix Auger-Aliassime's position as the No. 2 seed — a career first at Masters 1000 level, and one that comes in his home city of Montreal. FAA grew up in Quebec and has played National Bank Open his entire career, but never as a seeded player in the top half of the draw. The Montreal crowd will expect something from him this week. The bracket gives him a legitimate shot to deliver it.

The absence of Sinner (elbow injury sustained at Wimbledon), Alcaraz (right wrist, out since April), and Djokovic creates a vacuum at the top of the sport heading into the North American hard court summer. None of them projects to return before Cincinnati (August 13–23). Montreal belongs to the group that remains.

Draper Returns, Rain Compresses Early Schedule

Jack Draper of Great Britain received a wildcard entry into the Montreal draw and plays his first tour match since a 2025 injury sidelined him for months. Draper was inside the top 20 before the injury, and his wildcard return in Montreal is one of the storylines worth tracking as the week progresses.

The rain that cancelled Sunday's first-round matches and pushed both DC Open finals to Monday also affected the Montreal schedule. First-round action that was scheduled for Sunday has been folded into the early days of this week, meaning the main draw and opening rounds will overlap before the bracket settles into its natural rhythm.

What's Next

The Montreal Masters 1000 runs through August 13. Results from this week will carry significant weight heading into the Cincinnati Open (August 13–23) and the US Open, which begins its main draw August 24 in New York. Zverev, Shelton, and FAA are the favorites in Montreal. The player who lifts the trophy at IGA Stadium will enter the rest of the summer with real momentum.
