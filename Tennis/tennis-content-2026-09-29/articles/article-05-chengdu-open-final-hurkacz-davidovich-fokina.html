Chengdu Open Final: Hurkacz Meets Davidovich Fokina After Shapovalov Three-Set Battle

By Marcus Cole | September 29, 2026

ATP | Chengdu Open

The 2026 Chengdu Open final is set: Hubert Hurkacz will face No. 2 seed Alejandro Davidovich Fokina in Monday evening's title match, scheduled no earlier than 7 PM local time.

Hurkacz earned his place the hard way, defeating Denis Shapovalov 6-7(8), 6-3, 6-3 in three sets. Shapovalov — who had already recorded his 250th career ATP Tour win during the tournament — served up 14 aces in a semifinal that refused to follow the script. He took the first set in a tiebreak before Hurkacz steadied and controlled the final two sets to advance.

Shapovalov's Week

Denis Shapovalov will leave Chengdu disappointed but with something real to show for the week. His 250th career ATP win earlier in the draw was a career milestone; his semifinal performance — 14 aces, a first-set tiebreak won, a genuine chance against a quality opponent — reflected a player whose serve and aggressive baseline game remain formidable when firing.

The third set was where Hurkacz's experience told. He is a player who absorbs pressure through the serve-and-backhand combination, and once he took back control in the middle of the second set, Shapovalov's window narrowed. The 6-3, 6-3 finish in sets two and three was efficient from Hurkacz.

Davidovich Fokina's Path

The No. 2 seed reached the final on the other half of the draw, where earlier Mannarino had made news by defeating defending champion Shang Juncheng in the round-of-16. Davidovich Fokina has been consistent throughout 2026 and in particularly strong form on Asian hardcourts during this stretch of the calendar. He arrives in the final as the seeded favourite in terms of recent form, though Hurkacz's serve makes him dangerous on any indoor surface.

What a Title Means for Both

Both players are engaged in the ATP Race to Turin conversation for 2026, with the Asian swing's combined points total capable of shifting the leaderboard significantly. A Chengdu ATP 250 title does not deliver Masters-level points, but it contributes to a swing total that compounds through October's Shanghai event.

For Hurkacz, a title here would mark a return to his best level after a summer stretch that produced inconsistent results. His best tennis — big serve, clean forehand, effective net approach — has been in evidence this week in Chengdu. The final is an opportunity to confirm it with a result.

Davidovich Fokina, seeded and physically fresh, will be confident on the other side of the net. The Spaniard's ability to construct points from the baseline and handle the faster courts in Asia makes him a genuine challenge in any final setting.

What's Next

The Chengdu Open final takes place Monday evening, September 29. Simultaneously, Medvedev and Rublev are contesting the Hangzhou Open final. Following both events, the Asian swing continues with the Japan Open in Tokyo and the Shanghai Masters in October — the most important ATP event of the fall calendar before the year-end ATP Finals in Turin.
