### STORY 2: Ann Li Ends Sabalenka's Wuhan Streak

**Long-Form Post**
Window: 8:00 AM ET

This is the kind of result that will be talked about for years.

Ann Li, an American ranked outside the top 50, has just ended Aryna Sabalenka's legendary unbeaten run in Wuhan — winning 3-6, 6-4, 7-6(5) in one of the most dramatic matches of the 2026 WTA season.

The match had everything. Sabalenka won the first set and looked in control. Li clawed back the second. In the third, Sabalenka served for it, saved nine match points, and pushed the tiebreak to the edge. But on the 10th match point, Li got through.

Sabalenka received a right knee medical timeout and committed 44 unforced errors — unusual for the normally composed Belarusian. Her Wuhan record had been one of tennis' most remarkable statistics: three titles (2018, 2019, 2024), not a single loss at the venue since her first title eight years ago.

It's over now. Ann Li's biggest career win. One of the great upset stories of the 2026 WTA season.
