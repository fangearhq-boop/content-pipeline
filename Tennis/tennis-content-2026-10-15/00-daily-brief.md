### STORY 2: Ann Li Ends Sabalenka's Legendary Wuhan Winning Streak
**Tier:** 1
**Type:** NEW (Massive WTA upset — Sabalenka's unbeaten Wuhan streak ends)
**Byline:** Elena Voss
**Status:** CONFIRMED RESULT

Ann Li (USA) shocked the tennis world, defeating top seed and three-time Wuhan champion Aryna Sabalenka 3-6, 6-4, 7-6(5) in the Wuhan Open Round of 16 on October 14. Li saved nine match points before converting on the 10th. Sabalenka received a right knee medical timeout and committed 44 unforced errors. The loss ends Sabalenka's historic unbeaten streak at the Wuhan Open — which dated back to 2018. WTA posted "THE BIGGEST WIN OF HER CAREER" following Li's victory.
