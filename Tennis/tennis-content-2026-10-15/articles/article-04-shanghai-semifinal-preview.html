Shanghai Masters Semifinal Set: Zverev vs. Davidovich Fokina Headlines as Friday QFs Loom

By Marcus Cole

October 15, 2026 — Shanghai, China

The Shanghai Masters semifinal picture is taking shape. Two spots are locked in after Thursday's quarterfinals. Two more will be decided on Friday in matches that are genuinely difficult to call.

Here is where things stand, and what to watch for in the final quarterfinal day of the 2026 Shanghai Masters.

Confirmed: Zverev vs. Davidovich Fokina

The first semifinal is set. Alexander Zverev, the world No. 1, meets Alejandro Davidovich Fokina after dismantling Alex de Minaur 6-4, 6-4 in the quarterfinals. Fokina advanced when Arthur Gea retired with a right leg injury — a heartbreaking end to a breakout tournament for the young Frenchman, but a semifinal berth for the Spaniard regardless of how it came.

Zverev has been the most dominant player in Shanghai all week. He has not dropped a set. He has absorbed the challenge of opponents who came in with momentum, and he has neutralized each of them efficiently. At the point in the draw when the competition gets hardest, Zverev looks like the player most likely to be holding the trophy on Sunday.

Davidovich Fokina is reaching his fourth career Masters 1000 semifinal. He is a player who can produce elite tennis on a given day — technical, aggressive, and comfortable at this level. Zverev will be the heavy favorite, but Fokina has beaten names like his before.

Friday's Remaining QFs: Medvedev vs. Paul

Daniil Medvedev and Tommy Paul meet in one of Friday's quarterfinals, and it is genuinely one of the more interesting QF matchups left in the draw.

Medvedev arrived in Shanghai after his disqualification in Beijing — struck a spectator with a ball in frustration in the semifinal, lost his points and prize money, and came to Shanghai needing a statement. He beat Tsitsipas 6-4, 7-5 in the Round of 16 and has not dropped a set in the tournament. His serve, his defense, and his ability to neutralize power hitters make him one of the hardest players to beat on a hard court.

Paul, meanwhile, has had one of his best seasons. He reached the quarterfinals at all six ATP Masters 1000 hard-court events in 2026 — a stat that speaks to consistency at the highest level. He beat Zizou Bergs 7-6(5), 6-4 in the Round of 16, ending the Belgian's brilliant run. His serve and groundstrokes are both weapons on this surface.

Medvedev is the slight favorite, but Paul is more than capable of advancing.

Jodar vs. Ruud

The second Friday quarterfinal — Rafael Jodar vs. Casper Ruud — is the hardest to call of the four QF spots.

Jodar has had a breakthrough week in Shanghai, beating Botic van de Zandschulp in three sets and advancing steadily through a strong side of the draw. Ruud advanced when Felix Auger-Aliassime retired mid-match with a knee injury while Ruud was leading 5-2 in the first set.

Both players are capable of reaching a Masters semifinal. Jodar is the slight favorite coming in, but Ruud has experience at this level and will be difficult to break down in a long match.

What's Next

Daniil Medvedev vs. Tommy Paul and Rafael Jodar vs. Casper Ruud are both scheduled for Friday, October 16 at the Shanghai Masters. The semifinals are Saturday, October 17, with the final on Sunday, October 18. Alexander Zverev and Alejandro Davidovich Fokina have confirmed their places in Saturday's first semifinal.
