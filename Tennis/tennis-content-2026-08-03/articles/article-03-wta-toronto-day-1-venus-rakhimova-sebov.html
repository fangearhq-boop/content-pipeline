WTA Toronto Day 1: Rakhimova Defeats Venus, Sebov Advances for Canada

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | August 3, 2026

The WTA National Bank Open opened its 2026 main draw Sunday in Toronto with a full first-round slate — and two results defined the day. Kamilla Rakhimova of Uzbekistan defeated Venus Williams 6-4, 6-1 in a rain-delayed match. Katherine Sebov, 27, of Canada defeated Aoi Ito of Japan 6-3, 6-4 to become the first Canadian to advance to the second round of the 2026 event.

The weather created complications throughout the session at Sobeys Stadium. Matches were delayed and rescheduled, and at least one result — Bianca Andreescu vs. Nikola Bartunkova, scheduled for the Sunday evening session on Centre Court — was not confirmed at the time of writing.

Rakhimova Defeats Venus, 6-4, 6-1

The result was decisive but the match was more competitive for a stretch than the scoreline suggests. Williams, a seven-time Grand Slam champion who turned 46 in June, held with Rakhimova through much of the first set before the Uzbek player found her range. Rakhimova broke through to take the set 6-4, then won nine of the last 11 games to run away with the second 6-1.

Williams has been competing at the WTA 1000 level throughout 2026 as she continues what appears to be the final chapter of a career that began in the late 1990s. She had lost a first-round match at Wimbledon and at each previous tournament she entered this season, but she continues to appear in draws and compete at the highest level. Rakhimova was composed and clinical once she got ahead and did not allow her opponent back into the match.

Sebov First Canadian to Advance

Katherine Sebov grew up watching the National Bank Open and made it count Sunday afternoon. The 27-year-old Canadian defeated Aoi Ito of Japan 6-3, 6-4 in a clean performance to claim the first Canadian advancement in the 2026 NBO main draw. It was Sebov's first WTA 1000 win since the Miami Open in 2023, and she did it in front of a home crowd that has been starved for local results at the tournament in recent years.

Wild card Ariana Arseneault, also Canadian, fell 6-2, 6-4 to Jessica Bouzas Maneiro of Spain. Sloane Stephens earned a main draw berth through qualifying, having defeated Harriet Dart 3-6, 6-0, 6-2 to advance from qualifying rounds.

Seeds Await

Top seeds Aryna Sabalenka and Elena Rybakina both have first-round byes in this year's NBO and will open their tournaments beginning Tuesday. Sabalenka, seeded first, is the world's top-ranked player and enters the tournament as the clear favorite. Rybakina, seeded second, is always dangerous on hard courts and has the serve and backhand to trouble anyone in the draw.

Third seed Jessica Pegula, who won the DC Open WTA 500 final in Washington Monday morning, will also arrive in Toronto for her next first-round match. Pegula's DC Open win gives her added confidence heading into the WTA 1000 event.

What's Next

The WTA National Bank Open continues Monday with the remainder of the first-round schedule, including any matches delayed from Sunday. Top seeds Sabalenka and Rybakina are expected to begin their tournaments Tuesday. The event runs through August 13 at Sobeys Stadium in Toronto. The Cincinnati Open follows, and the US Open begins August 24 in New York.
