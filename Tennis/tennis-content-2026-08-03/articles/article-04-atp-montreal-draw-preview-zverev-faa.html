ATP Montreal Draw Preview: Zverev Tops Depleted Field, FAA Eyes First Masters Title at Home

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | August 3, 2026

The 2026 National Bank Open in Montreal opens its main draw tomorrow, Tuesday August 4, at IGA Stadium, and before the first ball is struck, the tournament has already been defined in part by absence. Jannik Sinner, Carlos Alcaraz, and Novak Djokovic are all out. That leaves Alexander Zverev as the No. 1 seed in a draw that still contains serious talent — but that also gives several players an opportunity they might not have had otherwise.

Montreal's tournament director was public in his frustration, calling the withdrawal wave involving those three stars — and others — an "absolute disaster" for the event. But the players who are present have real motives: a Masters 1000 title is a Masters 1000 title regardless of the field, and for Felix Auger-Aliassime in particular, the draw could not have come at a more meaningful moment.

Seeds and Structure

The top five seeds are Zverev (1), Auger-Aliassime (2), Shelton (3), de Minaur (4), and Medvedev (5). Zverev arrives carrying the momentum of an extraordinary 2026 season: he won Roland-Garros in June for his first Grand Slam title, then reached the Wimbledon final where he lost to Sinner. He is the clear favorite here.

Auger-Aliassime is seeded No. 2 at a Masters 1000 for the first time in his career. That milestone comes in Montreal, his home city, in front of a crowd that will be loudly behind him from the first match. He is 26 years old and has been one of the best players in the game for several years without yet winning a Masters 1000 title. This tournament represents one of his strongest opportunities to date.

Ben Shelton, the defending champion from the 2025 edition held in Toronto, is seeded third and opens against either Jenson Brooksby or a qualifier. Daniil Medvedev, a former world No. 1 who won the Canadian Open in 2021, is seeded fifth. Alex de Minaur is seeded fourth and arrives from the DC Open, where he was the No. 1 seed before falling in the quarterfinals.

Draw Storylines

Zverev and Taylor Fritz — who is competing in today's DC Open ATP final against Rafael Jódar — are in the same quarter. Zverev beat Fritz in straight sets at Wimbledon, snapping a seven-match losing streak against the American. If Fritz reaches Montreal after a DC Open win, a potential quarterfinal meeting would be among the more anticipated matches of the draw.

Shelton and Medvedev are positioned to potentially meet in a quarterfinal of their own. Luca Van Assche of France — who won his first ATP title at Estoril in July — is also in the draw and could test FAA in the early rounds.

Who Is Missing

Sinner sustained an elbow injury during Wimbledon and is targeting Cincinnati (beginning August 13) as his next event. Alcaraz has been out since April with a right wrist injury sustained at the Barcelona Open; he is also targeting Cincinnati and the US Open. Djokovic has skipped the Canadian swing entirely and will prepare directly for New York. The absences change the calculus at the top of the draw significantly.

What's Next

The ATP National Bank Open in Montreal runs from August 2-13 at IGA Stadium, with main draw play beginning Tuesday, August 4. Top seeds receive byes into the second round. After Montreal, the Cincinnati Open runs August 9-17, followed by the US Open, which begins August 24 in New York. Players who perform well in Montreal will enter Cincinnati with significant ranking points and momentum.
