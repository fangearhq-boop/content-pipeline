### STORY 1: Pegula Wins DC Open WTA 500 Final — Eala Makes History
- **Tier:** 1 (Breaking/Result)
- **Classification:** FOLLOW-UP (Aug 2, 2026 — S1: Eala def. Osaka, WTA SF/Final preview)
- **Byline:** Ryan Calloway
- **Posting Window:** 8:00 AM ET

Jessica Pegula won the DC Open WTA 500 title Monday morning, defeating Alexandra Eala 6-4, 6-3 in a match that was suspended Sunday evening due to thunderstorms and resumed at noon ET Monday at Rock Creek Park Tennis Center. Pegula had taken the first set 6-4 before storms halted play with Eala leading the second set 2-1. When play resumed, Pegula closed out 6-3 for her 12th career WTA title and third of 2026 (also won in Dubai and Charleston). Eala, 22, from the Philippines, reached a career-high live ranking of No. 24 after her historic run — becoming the first Filipino player to reach a WTA 500 singles final. She defeated Zheng, Fernandez (defending champion), Svitolina, and Osaka on her way to the final.
