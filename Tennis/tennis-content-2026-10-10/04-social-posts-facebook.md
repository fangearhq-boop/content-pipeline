### STORY 2: Zheng vs. Mertens SF

#### Long-Form Post — 8:00 AM ET

The WTA Beijing semifinal between Qinwen Zheng and Elise Mertens may be the match of the tournament — and it's happening today.

Mertens has put together one of the more remarkable runs you'll see at a WTA 1000. She beat Coco Gauff 6-3, 6-3 in the fourth round. Then she took out Iga Swiatek — the two-time China Open finalist — 7-6(0), 6-3 in the quarterfinals, without facing a single break point. That's wins over Gauff and Swiatek in the same tournament.

Zheng, playing in Beijing as a home wild card, has her own momentum: she defeated Elina Svitolina in a tight two-set quarterfinal, and the local crowd has been electric behind her all week.

The winner faces Andreeva in Sunday's final. This one is going to be a battle.
