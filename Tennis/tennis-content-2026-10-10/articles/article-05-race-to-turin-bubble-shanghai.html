Race to Turin Bubble: Four Spots, Seven Players, a Masters 1000 in the Middle

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | October 10, 2026

ATP | Race to Turin | ATP Finals | Shelton | Medvedev | Djokovic | Shanghai Masters

Two players have already punched their tickets to the ATP Finals in Turin this November. Alexander Zverev, leading the Race to Turin with approximately 8,800 points, is through. Carlos Alcaraz, in third place with 4,560 points, is through. That leaves six spots — though Jannik Sinner occupies second place in the standings with 7,950 points before his season-ending withdrawal, meaning the effective competition is for four remaining spots among a tight cluster of seven.

The Bubble Players

As of the most recent standings, the battle for the remaining ATP Finals berths looks like this:

  • Ben Shelton (USA, No. 4) — 4,440 points. The American has had a strong 2026 and currently sits closest to the safety line.
  • Flavio Cobolli (Italy, No. 5) — 3,540 points. The Italian has been one of the tour's breakout stories this season.
  • Frances Tiafoe (USA, No. 6) — 3,340 points. Tiafoe remains in the mix but needs a strong stretch run.
  • Arthur Fils (France, No. 7). The young Frenchman has been consistent and is within striking distance.
  • Daniil Medvedev (Russia, No. 8). Still in Shanghai and advancing — Medvedev has won at the ATP Finals level before, and a deep Shanghai run could dramatically alter his standing.
  • Novak Djokovic (Serbia, No. 9). Djokovic climbed six places after winning the Beijing title this week, vaulting him back into the qualifying picture.

Why Shanghai Changes Everything

The Rolex Shanghai Masters is an ATP Masters 1000 — the second-highest points category on tour, behind only the four Grand Slams. A champion here earns 1,000 points. A finalist earns 600. A semifinalist earns 360. For players on the bubble, the difference between winning or losing a quarterfinal could be the difference between qualifying for Turin or watching on television.

That calculus applies most sharply to Medvedev and Djokovic, who are both still in the draw this week. Medvedev has already advanced to the third round with a dominant performance over Struff. Djokovic, despite his Beijing success, enters Shanghai as a seed in a form run that has also seen him lose to Hubert Hurkacz in Round 2 here — a result that means he picks up no points from Shanghai if his run ended there.

After Shanghai, the Paris Bercy Masters (another 1000-point event) follows before the November 8 deadline for Turin qualification. Every remaining match counts.

The Wildcard: Zverev's Ankle

A potential wrinkle emerged Friday when Zverev slipped during his second-round win and left the court favoring his left ankle. A Zverev withdrawal would not affect his qualification — it is already settled — but it would redistribute seeded-player points and alter paths through the draw for everyone below him. Players on the bubble would be watching closely.

What's Next

The Race to Turin standings will update as Shanghai progresses through next week. The ATP Finals in Turin are scheduled for November 15-22, 2026. The final qualification window closes after the Paris Masters (November 2-8). Fans looking for the live standings can track them at atptour.com.
