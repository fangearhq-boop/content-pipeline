### STORY 2: Sinner Officially Withdraws From China Open — Knee Not Ready
**Type:** FOLLOW-UP (Sep 24 S2: no WD filed, draw looming → official withdrawal announced today with personal statement)
**Tier:** 1 (World No. 1 misses title defense, Asian swing increasingly in doubt)
**Byline:** Marcus Cole
**Angle:** Jannik Sinner has officially withdrawn from the 2026 China Open (Beijing, Sep 30–Oct 6), releasing a statement on Friday: "I'm very sorry to announce that I won't be able to play in Beijing this year. My knee is not where I would like it to be, and I'm not ready to compete, but I will do everything possible to be there on court as soon as I can." The world No. 1 has not played a competitive match since Wimbledon in July. He was the defending China Open champion, having beaten Learner Tien 6-2, 6-2 in the 2025 final, and now misses his own title defense for the first time. Sinner is targeting a return at either the Shanghai Masters (Oct 5-18) or the Vienna Open (Oct 26), though neither has been confirmed. His ATP Finals place as No. 2 in the Race to Turin (9,630 pts) is secured regardless.
