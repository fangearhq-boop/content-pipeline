Singapore Open Semifinals Set: Andreeva Into Last Four as Wang Xinyu and Sakkari Also Advance

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap

September 25, 2026

The WTA 500 Singapore Open has its semifinal lineup after a quarterfinal Friday that delivered both clinical dominance and a surprise walkover. Top seed Mirra Andreeva, Elise Mertens, Xinyu Wang, and Maria Sakkari will contest the last four spots at the OCBC Arena on Saturday.

Andreeva, the Roland Garros champion and the tournament's No. 1 seed, moved through the quarterfinals with the kind of efficiency her opponents have come to dread. She defeated No. 6 seed Leylah Fernandez 6-4, 6-3, never losing her composure on the indoor hard court. The result was no great surprise — Andreeva had looked sharp all week — but the margin served as a reminder of her quality at the top of her game.

Mertens Into the Semifinals via Walkover

Elise Mertens (No. 4 seed, Belgium) reached the semifinals without striking a ball, advancing by walkover after Maja Chwalińska withdrew from their scheduled quarterfinal. Mertens will need to find match sharpness quickly heading into her semifinal against Andreeva — a significant challenge given the gap in form between the two players this week.

The Belgian veteran, however, has navigated tight matches under pressure before and should not be underestimated simply because of how she arrived in the last four.

Prozorova's Run Ends; Wang Xinyu Advances

On the other side of the draw, Tatiana Prozorova's remarkable week came to an end at the quarterfinal stage. The Russian had been one of the stories of the tournament, upsetting No. 3 seed Alexandra Eala of the Philippines in the round of 16 — a result that drew significant attention given Eala's status as one of the sport's most-watched rising players. But Prozorova could not find the same magic against Xinyu Wang, who ended her run with a 7-5, 6-4 victory. Wang, a Chinese player competing in front of a supportive Asian crowd, controlled the match from the back of the court and advance to the semifinals of a WTA 500 event for one of the best results of her career.

Sakkari Survives Gibson in Tiebreak Drama

Maria Sakkari (No. 7 seed, Greece) required two tiebreaks to get past Australia's Talia Gibson — a match that stretched to 7-6(2), 7-6(1) and required full concentration from the veteran. Gibson, largely unseeded, pushed Sakkari all the way and the winning margin was fine throughout. Sakkari's experience ultimately proved decisive.

The semifinal setup on the other half of the draw is Wang Xinyu vs. Sakkari — a contrasting styles matchup between Wang's baseline control and Sakkari's power game.

What's Next

Semifinals are scheduled for Saturday at the OCBC Arena in Singapore: Andreeva vs. Mertens, and Wang Xinyu vs. Sakkari. The singles final is Sunday. Andreeva is the clear favourite to take the title and give herself a strong start to the Asian swing heading into the Wuhan Open and the China Open, where she will be among the contenders.
