Hangzhou Open R16: Medvedev Makes His Entrance as Wide-Open ATP 250 Heats Up

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap

September 25, 2026

The ATP 250 Hangzhou Open enters its Round of 16 on Friday, and the main attraction is the tournament debut of No. 1 seed Daniil Medvedev. The former world No. 1 has been waiting in the wings since his first-round bye, and now faces Frenchman Valentin Royer for the right to advance to the quarterfinals of the $1,039,090 hard court event in China.

Royer earned his place in the Round of 16 with a composed 6-3, 6-4 win over Australian Adam Walton in the round of 32 on Thursday. The Frenchman serves as one of five French players in the Hangzhou draw — a notable presence that underscores France's growing depth in the men's game. For Royer, facing Medvedev is a steep challenge, but on an indoor hard court in Asia, upsets are not unheard of.

A Tournament Wide Open at the Top

What makes the 2026 Hangzhou Open particularly compelling is the absence of most of the ATP's top 10, who are all in London competing at the Laver Cup. Medvedev (No. 1 seed here) and fellow contenders Rublev (No. 2), Etcheverry (No. 3), and Halys (No. 4) are among the highest-ranked players in the field, but none of them are ranked in the top five globally heading into this week.

That creates the kind of opportunity that players ranked outside the top 30 dream about: a credible path to a title at an ATP 250 with a strong prize fund. The hard court surface in Hangzhou suits aggressive baseliners, and the draw has enough depth to produce a compelling finish.

Medvedev, despite his seeding at this event, has not played a competitive match since the US Open. That rust factor is real — he will likely need a match or two before hitting full stride — which is precisely what makes Royer's assignment less daunting than it looks on paper.

French Contingent Looking Strong

Beyond Royer, the French presence in Hangzhou is a talking point for this week. France has emerged as one of the deeper nations in men's tennis in 2026, and a strong showing at this ATP 250 would add further evidence of that depth at the tour level.

The draw also features Kyrian Jacquet, who has earned wins earlier in the week, providing more Frenchmen with routes to the later rounds.

What's Next

Medvedev's R16 match against Royer is the central storyline Friday in Hangzhou. The quarterfinals will be held over the weekend, and the Hangzhou Open final is scheduled for Monday, September 29 — one day before the China Open begins in nearby Beijing.
