ATP Finals Draw: Zverev and Alcaraz Land in Separate Groups — Turin Begins November 15

By Ryan Calloway | November 12, 2026 | Tennis Fanrecap

The 2026 Nitto ATP Finals groups were drawn Thursday morning in Turin, and the bracket has been set for the season-ending championship that begins at the Inalpi Arena on November 15. Year-end No. 1 Alexander Zverev and second seed Carlos Alcaraz land in different groups — which means the two title favorites will only play each other if both advance to the semifinals.

The John McEnroe Group features Zverev, Ben Shelton, Arthur Fils, and Rafael Jodar. The Ivan Lendl Group pairs Alcaraz with Daniil Medvedev, Flavio Cobolli, and Frances Tiafoe. The top two from each group advance to the semifinals; the full tournament runs through November 22.

The McEnroe Group: Zverev's Title Defense Starts Here

Alexander Zverev enters the ATP Finals carrying the full weight of an extraordinary 2026 season. He won Roland Garros and the US Open, clinched the year-end No. 1 ranking — the first time in his career and the first time for a German player — and arrives in Turin chasing a third ATP Finals title that would make him one of the most decorated players in the event's history.

His group opponents will not be passengers. Ben Shelton reached the Paris Masters final two weeks ago, losing to Alcaraz in a competitive two-set match. Arthur Fils has been a consistent top-10 presence all season and plays explosive, attacking tennis that can unsettle any opponent. Rafael Jodar, the rookie who became the first player in the tournament's history to qualify in his debut year on the ATP Tour, is a genuine wildcard — no one has much footage on how he handles this environment.

The McEnroe Group looks like Zverev's to win. It is not, however, guaranteed.

The Lendl Group: Alcaraz vs. Medvedev Is the Match of the Week

The Ivan Lendl Group is loaded. Carlos Alcaraz arrives on the back of back-to-back Masters 1000 titles in Vienna and Paris, a 10-match winning streak, and five 2026 titles in total. The one prize missing from his resume is the ATP Finals. This tournament is specifically what he has come to Turin for.

Daniil Medvedev knows Alcaraz's game as well as anyone on the tour. The two have met in Grand Slam finals before, and their round-robin match — whenever it falls in the November 15–17 group stage schedule — will likely be the most watched match of the week before the knockouts begin. Medvedev, a former year-end No. 1, has won the ATP Finals once before and is dangerous on the indoor surface in Turin.

Flavio Cobolli and Frances Tiafoe complete the Lendl Group as first-time participants. Both are capable of beating top players on a given day; both will be motivated by the size of the stage.

Four Debutants in the Field

One of the notable features of the 2026 ATP Finals is the number of first-time qualifiers. Cobolli, Tiafoe, Fils, and Jodar are all making their tournament debuts. Jodar's presence is historically significant — no player had ever qualified for the ATP Finals in the same season they first appeared on the ATP Tour, and the Spaniard has ended that streak.

Jannik Sinner, the defending year-end No. 1 who was absent from Turin last year due to suspension, is again unable to compete — this time due to a knee injury that has ended his season early. His absence reshapes the narrative around both groups and removes the one player who might have been considered Zverev and Alcaraz's most credible challenger.

What's Next

The 2026 Nitto ATP Finals begins on Saturday, November 15, at the Inalpi Arena in Turin. Round-robin play runs through Wednesday, November 18, with the semifinals on Friday, November 20, and the final on Sunday, November 22. Ticket information and streaming schedules are available via the official ATP Tour site.
