Zverev Wins 2026 US Open, Defeats Shelton in Four Sets for Second Major of the Year

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | September 14, 2026

Alexander Zverev is the 2026 US Open champion.

The German top seed defeated Ben Shelton 6-3, 7-6(2), 5-7, 6-2 on Sunday at Arthur Ashe Stadium in New York, claiming his first US Open title and his second Grand Slam of the 2026 season. Zverev had also won Roland Garros in June, making him the only man to claim two major titles in 2026.

It was a match that illustrated the gap between the seasoned champion and the first-time finalist. Zverev was in total control for stretches that made the scoreline look even more one-sided than it was. Shelton broke Zverev's serve for the first time in the match — the first time in 66 consecutive service games — in the third set, sparking a brief comeback that earned him that set. But Zverev immediately reasserted dominance in the fourth.

A Dominant Fourth Set

The decisive moment came early in the fourth set. A 24-point rally ended with Shelton's backhand sailing wide, giving Zverev an early break and control of the set. Shelton had shown tremendous fight throughout the two-week tournament, but Zverev's level was simply too high in the final period.

Zverev moves his all-time head-to-head record against Shelton to 6-0. Shelton has now faced Zverev at the Slam level and at Masters events and has yet to find a way through.

The prize money for Zverev: $7.67 million.

American Drought Extends to 23 Years

For American tennis fans, the result means the men's Grand Slam drought stretches on. The last American man to win a major was Andy Roddick, who claimed this very title at the 2003 US Open — 23 years ago. Shelton's run to the final was the closest any American man has come since, and the crowd at Arthur Ashe Stadium was behind him every step of the way.

The drought is not for lack of talent. Shelton reached the final at 23. The future looks promising, even if Sunday belonged to Zverev.

What's Next

Zverev, now the undisputed No. 1 in the world, will turn his attention to the Asian swing — the China Open in Beijing and the Shanghai Masters — as he aims to consolidate his year-end ranking. Shelton will look to regroup, with the Australian Open in January offering the next chance to put his name on a major.

For Zverev, the bigger question is whether a third Grand Slam in 2026 is possible. The year-end Masters and finals circuit follows Asia, but with Roland Garros and the US Open already in hand, 2026 is already the best season of his career — and there are still months to go.
