NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Three hours at Arthur Ashe Stadium on Saturday night, and when it was over, Iva Jovic had done it again. The No. 14 seed defeated No. 17 seed Alex Eala 7-5, 3-6, 7-5 in one of the matches of this US Open. Eala — the first Philippine player ever seeded at this tournament — had fought back beautifully to take the second set, then led 4-2 in the deciding third before Jovic found another gear and won five of the last six games. It was Jovic's third career win over Eala (H2H 3-0), and it sends the American teenager to her first US Open round of 16. She'll face Coco Gauff on Monday — the all-American fourth-round collision everyone wanted to see the moment Eala's score went to 7-5 in the third. For Philippine fans, Saturday night was devastating but also undeniably historic. Alex Eala walked into the 2026 US Open as a seeded player for the first time in Philippine tennis history, became the first Filipino to reach the third round as a seed, and went toe-to-toe with a future US Open champion in a night-session match at Arthur Ashe that the crowd won't forget. That's not a footnote. That's a legacy. Jovic is 21. Eala is 22. The next decade of this sport is going to be something. What did you think of Eala's US Open run — did she exceed your expectations? ### Image Post Caption Jovic battles past Eala in a 3-hour Arthur Ashe marathon. First US Open R4 in her career.
