Eala Gets Her Rematch: No. 17 Seed Takes on Oliynykova in US Open R2

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | September 3, 2026

Alex Eala faces Oleksandra Oliynykova in their second career meeting Thursday at Louis Armstrong Stadium — a rematch of the Strasbourg clash Oliynykova won in May 2026, now on the hard courts of Flushing Meadows.

Alex Eala has been here before. Same opponent. Different stakes. Different surface. And a very different version of herself.

The No. 17 seed and Philippine tennis trailblazer faces Ukraine's Oleksandra Oliynykova in the second round of the 2026 US Open on Thursday at Louis Armstrong Stadium. The match is scheduled as the second on court after Felix Auger-Aliassime's contest against Karen Khachanov, with Eala's start time projected at approximately 12:40 PM ET.

It is a rematch. And for Eala, it carries the weight of unfinished business.

The Strasbourg Defeat

The two players met once before, in the first round at Strasbourg in May 2026 on clay. Oliynykova won that match 3-6, 7-5, 6-3 — a result that followed a familiar pattern. Eala won the first set but couldn't sustain the level through a demanding second and third. The head-to-head stands 1-0 in Oliynykova's favour.

Crucially, they have never played on hard court. The 2026 US Open second round is their first meeting on the surface Eala has made her home in this calendar year, posting a 22-9 hard-court record through Cincinnati.

Eala's US Open Form

Eala has arrived in New York in the best form of her career. She is the No. 17 seed at this US Open — the highest Grand Slam seeding in the history of Philippine tennis — and she backed that ranking up immediately in round one.

Her opening match against American qualifier Mary Stoiana produced a 6-1, 6-2 result in 78 minutes at Louis Armstrong Stadium. There was nothing tentative about it. Eala was dominant from the first game, controlling points with her groundstrokes and never letting Stoiana settle. It was the performance of a player who belongs on this stage.

That dominant win means she enters the Oliynykova rematch with momentum and confidence. Oliynykova, for her part, is no easy draw. The Ukrainian defeated American wild card Reese Brantmeier 6-4, 6-4 in round one and has shown she can handle pressure situations. She leads the career head-to-head.

The Stakes

This match is the most significant of Eala's career on the WTA stage. She is seeded in the second week draw at a Grand Slam, playing at the peak of her form, on a surface that suits her, in front of a New York crowd that has already embraced her. A third-round appearance at the US Open would be the deepest Grand Slam run of her career and would mark another milestone for Philippine tennis on the world stage.

The Strasbourg match is behind her. What matters now is Louis Armstrong on Thursday afternoon.

What's Next

A win over Oliynykova sends Eala into the US Open third round for the first time in her career. She would be among the seeded players remaining in the bottom half of the draw and would enter the second week with genuine momentum. A loss sends her home — but does nothing to diminish one of the most historic appearances by a Philippine tennis player at a Grand Slam.

Either way, Eala has already changed what Philippine tennis looks like at the world's biggest events. On Thursday afternoon, she gets to show the world what she is capable of.
