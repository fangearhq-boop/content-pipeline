Thursday Night at Flushing: Gauff, Zverev and Rybakina Headline US Open Evening Session

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | September 3, 2026

Three of the 2026 US Open's biggest names take centre stage Thursday evening: No. 4 seed Coco Gauff faces Paula Badosa at 7 PM ET on Arthur Ashe, No. 1 seed Alexander Zverev plays Quentin Halys, and No. 2 seed Elena Rybakina continues her comeback against Jessica Bouzas Maneiro.

Thursday afternoon at the US Open has already produced results. Carlos Alcaraz is through. Learner Tien has upset the farewell narrative. The second round is completing its business at Flushing Meadows.

But Thursday evening is when the tournament turns its full attention to its biggest stars. Three massive second-round matches are scheduled for the night session, and all three carry significant storylines heading into the second week.

Gauff vs. Badosa — Arthur Ashe, 7 PM ET

No. 4 seed Coco Gauff, the defending US Open champion, opens the prime-time session against Spain's Paula Badosa. Gauff won her first-round match against Zeynep Sonmez 6-3, 6-4 and has looked sharp through her opening match. She enters Thursday as the favourite to defend her title from last year.

Badosa is a serious test. The Spaniard has shown the ability to compete at the highest level on hard courts and has experience performing on the biggest stages. Gauff holds the head-to-head advantage, but Badosa has the serve and the firepower to push the defending champion in the night session spotlight.

This is the match of the day on the women's side of the draw.

Zverev vs. Halys — The No. 1 Seed's Statement Match

Alexander Zverev stepped into the 2026 US Open as the top seed after Jannik Sinner's knee injury forced a withdrawal. He survived a four-hour, 53-minute five-set battle against Lorenzo Sonego in the first round — a reminder that even the world No. 1 can be pushed hard at Flushing Meadows.

France's Quentin Halys is his second-round opponent. Zverev is expected to be the heavy favourite, and the format for a No. 1 seed in early rounds demands that he handles this match cleanly and without drama. His best tennis has been consistently good all season; Thursday is the night to show it.

Rybakina vs. Bouzas Maneiro — The Ankle Test Continues

Elena Rybakina's ankle injury has been one of the major storylines of the 2026 summer. The No. 2 seed retired from her Cincinnati Open quarterfinal in August and had been unable to walk without pain in the days before the US Open. She passed the first test convincingly, beating Thea Frodin 6-3, 6-2 in round one with minimal sign of discomfort.

Spain's Jessica Bouzas Maneiro provides the second test. Bouzas Maneiro won her opening match and is capable on hard courts. Rybakina's form and serving ability should carry her through — but the key for the No. 2 seed is making sure the ankle holds through a potentially demanding rally-heavy match.

If Rybakina comes through Thursday intact and confident, she will be a genuine title contender heading into the second week.

What's Next

By the end of Thursday's evening session, the US Open draw will have a much clearer picture. Wins for Gauff, Zverev, and Rybakina would put three of the tournament's biggest names firmly in position for the third round. Any slip — especially from the women's defending champion or the men's top seed — would reshape the entire conversation heading into the second week.

Coverage begins at 7 PM ET on ESPN. Arthur Ashe Stadium will be full. This is what the US Open is built for.
