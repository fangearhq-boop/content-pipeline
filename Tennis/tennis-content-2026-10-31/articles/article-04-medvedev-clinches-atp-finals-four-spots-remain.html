Medvedev Clinches ATP Finals Spot — Four of Eight Qualified, Race Enters Final Week

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap

October 31, 2026

Daniil Medvedev confirmed his place at the Nitto ATP Finals in Turin by reaching the Erste Bank Open semifinals on Friday, completing the qualification requirement with his win over Adrian Mannarino in Vienna. He is the fourth player to lock in a Finals berth this season, joining Alexander Zverev, Carlos Alcaraz, and Ben Shelton.

With four spots claimed and four remaining, the Race to Turin enters its final and most decisive stretch: the Rolex Paris Masters, the season's last Masters 1000 event, running November 2–8 at the Plenitude Arena in Nanterre. Every match in Paris carries Race implications for the players still outside the qualified eight.

The Four Confirmed Qualifiers

Zverev, who will hold the year-end No. 1 ranking, was the first to confirm. Alcaraz and Shelton followed. Medvedev, the 2021 US Open champion and a perennial ATP Finals participant, secured his spot in Vienna after a strong late-season run that included his Almaty Open title last week.

Medvedev's path to qualification was not linear. He lost early at several key autumn events and entered the Vienna week in fifth place in the Race standings, needing points to secure his place. A quarterfinal win over Mannarino gave him the mathematical confirmation. He now carries the additional freedom of playing the rest of Vienna and the Paris Masters without qualification pressure — a circumstance that historically has produced some of his best tennis.

The Race Is Still Wide Open

The four remaining ATP Finals spots are among the most contested in years. The players currently in contention include Fabio Cobolli, Arthur Fils (injury uncertain), Frances Tiafoe, Rafael Jódar, Alex de Minaur, Novak Djokovic, Taylor Fritz, and Jan-Lennard Struff. Several of these players are separated by fewer than 500 points, meaning a single deep Paris run — or an early exit — can shift multiple positions in the standings simultaneously.

Djokovic's situation is the most watched. He sits outside the current top eight in the Race and needs a semifinal or better at Paris to qualify with confidence. A first-round loss would almost certainly end his qualification chances. He is seeded 10th at Paris, meaning he plays Monday with no first-round bye.

De Minaur is in Vienna's semifinals, which means he still has 500 Race points available this week before Paris even starts. A Vienna title combined with a Paris run would make him a very difficult player to keep out of Turin.

What's Next

The ATP Finals field will be finalized by the end of Paris Masters week (November 8). The remaining four spots will be determined by where players stand in the Race to Turin after the final in Nanterre. No further adjustments to the standings are possible once Paris concludes.

The Nitto ATP Finals run November 15–22 in Turin, Italy. All eight qualifiers will be confirmed and announced before the draw is made in early November. Medvedev has appeared at the Finals in multiple years and was the 2021 champion. He goes to Turin already knowing his name is in the field.
