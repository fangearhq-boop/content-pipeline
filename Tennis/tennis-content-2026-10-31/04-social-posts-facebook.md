### STORY 2: Basel Semifinals

#### Long-Form Post — 8:00 AM ET
The Basel quarterfinals were supposed to clarify things. Instead, we now have more questions.

Arthur Fils — seeded second, ranked seventh in the Race to Turin — retired Friday when he was trailing Taylor Fritz 6-2, 2-0. Back injury. Three days before the Paris Masters starts. Fils was one of the players who needed a strong Paris run to qualify for the ATP Finals. Now that's uncertain.

Meanwhile, Valentin Vacherot — unseeded, unranked among the tournament's seeds — is in the semifinals after beating both Rafael Jódar (3rd seed) and Holger Rune on back-to-back days. His run is a genuine story in a week full of them.

Friday's semifinals: Fritz vs. Vacherot, and Jiří Lehečká vs. Jan-Lennard Struff. After Kym beat Shelton and Struff beat Djokovic, this is the bracket Basel deserved.

The Paris Masters draw is set.

Novak Djokovic, however, comes in at No. 10. He plays a first-round match. Given that he sits outside the top eight in the ATP Race to Turin, every match in Paris matters enormously. The ATP Finals are the goal. The road runs through whatever he draws on Monday.
