### STORY 1: Cincinnati Finals Day — Historic Double — TIER 1

**Why it matters:** Two simultaneous historic milestones at the same tournament on the same day is exceptionally rare. The ATP final is a genuine landmark — the first Masters 1000 final between two Black players in ATP history. The WTA final is the first all-American women's Cincinnati final in 56 years. Both angles serve different audience segments and both deserve full Tier 1 treatment.

**Best angles:**
- Primary: "History twice over" — frame both finals as a single remarkable day
- Secondary ATP: Tiafoe's career arc — from rising prospect to multiple Masters final appearances, and Fils as the next French talent
- Secondary WTA: Pegula and Gauff as doubles partners and now singles rivals; American women's tennis depth

**Audience hook:** American tennis fans, diversity in sports, Cincinnati locals

**Claim types:** Match results (SF scores), historical superlatives (first-ever, since 1970) — HIGH priority for verification ✓
