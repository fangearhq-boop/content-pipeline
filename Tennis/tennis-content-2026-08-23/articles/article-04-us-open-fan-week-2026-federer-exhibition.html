US Open Fan Week 2026 Opens: Free Admission, Kids' Day, and Federer Returns August 25

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | August 23, 2026

The 2026 US Open is one week away from its main draw, and Flushing Meadows is already open to the public. US Open Fan Week runs from August 23 through August 29 at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center in Queens, New York — seven days of free grounds admission, qualifying match tennis, and player activity before the Grand Slam begins in earnest.

Today, August 23, is Arthur Ashe Kids' Day. And on Monday, August 25, Roger Federer returns to Flushing Meadows for the first time since 2019.

Arthur Ashe Kids' Day

Arthur Ashe Kids' Day opens Fan Week this year, running from 9:30 AM to 6 PM at the National Tennis Center. The event is built around introducing younger fans to the sport — player appearances, tennis clinics, games, and activity stations are all part of the day's programming.

It is the largest grassroots tennis event in the world by attendance, and this year it serves as the official launch of Fan Week rather than occurring later in the schedule. Organizers moved it to the first day to give families the best access to players who are arriving at the tournament grounds for the first time this summer.

How to Get In

Fan Week is free, but not unconditional. Visitors 18 and older are required to hold a Fan Access Pass, available through the US Open mobile app. The pass is free to register and grants entry to the tournament grounds for the duration of Fan Week. Children under 18 can enter with a registered adult and do not need their own pass.

What the Fan Access Pass covers: US Open qualifying matches (the preliminary rounds that determine which players enter the main draw), player practice sessions, the revamped Mixed Doubles Championship, and all Fan Week events including Arthur Ashe Kids' Day. What it does not cover: the Roger Federer exhibition on August 25, which requires a separate paid ticket.

Federer on Arthur Ashe Stadium — August 25

The headline attraction of Fan Week is Roger Federer's return to Flushing Meadows. On Monday, August 25, Federer will partner with Andy Roddick to face John McEnroe and Andre Agassi in a legends doubles exhibition at Arthur Ashe Stadium. The match starts at 7 PM.

Federer retired from professional tennis in 2022. This will be his first appearance at the USTA National Tennis Center since his 2019 US Open run, the last Grand Slam he entered as an active player. The exhibition sold out at face values starting around $238 per ticket; resale is the only remaining option.

The lineup of all four participants carries the weight of four combined Grand Slam titles running into the dozens. Federer holds 20 major titles, Roddick claimed one US Open championship, McEnroe won four Grand Slams and Agassi eight. Even as a casual exhibition, the names ensure the event draws maximum attention during a week that is already generating buzz.

What Else Happens This Week

Beyond the Federer exhibition and Kids' Day, Fan Week offers a full week of qualifying tennis. The US Open qualifying draws began last week and continue through August 29, with players competing for spots in the main draw across multiple courts at the National Tennis Center. Some of those players are touring veterans fighting to stay in draws; others are young qualifiers attempting to break through to their first Grand Slam main draw.

Media Day, when main draw players arrive and are available for press, typically falls mid-week. The US Open draw ceremony is Thursday, August 27, with seedings based on the Monday, August 24 ATP and WTA rankings.

What's Next

Fan Week runs through August 29 at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center. The Roger Federer exhibition at Arthur Ashe Stadium is Monday, August 25 at 7 PM. Draw ceremony is Thursday, August 27. The 2026 US Open main draw begins Sunday, August 30. Women's final: September 12. Men's final: September 13.
