Shanghai Day 5: Zverev, Alcaraz, and Shelton All in Third-Round Action

By Ryan Calloway | October 11, 2026

ATP | Rolex Shanghai Masters

Sunday at the Rolex Shanghai Masters is headlining itself. The world's No. 1 seed, the No. 2 seed, and one of the ATP's most exciting young players are all scheduled for third-round matches on the same day — a lineup that makes Day 5 one of the most anticipated sessions of this year's Shanghai draw.

Alexander Zverev, Carlos Alcaraz, and Ben Shelton all advanced through the second round and face different opponents today as the field of 64 continues to thin toward the quarterfinals.

Zverev: Ankle Watch Continues

Top seed Zverev faces Quentin Halys in the third round, his second match in Shanghai after a difficult win over Wu Yibing in Round 2. That win came by a score of 6-4, 7-6(4), but it was not without concern — Zverev went down after a late fall in the second set and left the court favoring his left ankle. He was expected to continue, and has, but the ankle will be monitored closely by his team throughout the week.

Halys advanced to the third round after a three-set win over Alexander Blockx. Zverev's head-to-head record gives him a significant advantage entering Sunday's match, and his serve has looked sharp in the early rounds.

Alcaraz: The Return Continues

Carlos Alcaraz made his first appearance at the Shanghai Masters in 730 days on Saturday, and the results did not suggest he was affected by the time away. His 6-3, 6-3 win over Juan Manuel Cerundolo was completed in just 66 minutes, with 24 winners, eight unforced errors, and a first-serve winning percentage of 86%.

He arrives at Shanghai on a seven-match winning streak, having defended his title at the Japan Open in Tokyo the week before. Sunday's third-round match represents his first real test of the tournament — and he'll need the level he showed against Cerundolo if he wants to make a run into the second week.

Shelton: Birthday Weekend Continues

Ben Shelton turned 24 on Saturday and celebrated with a 6-3, 7-6(6) win over Daniel Altmaier. Sunday he faces Arthur Gea in the third round, looking to extend the birthday weekend with another win and keep his Race to Turin campaign alive.

Shelton currently sits fourth in the Race to Turin standings with approximately 4,440 points — safely inside the qualifying positions for the ATP Finals in Turin on November 15-22. But the margins are tight enough that a deep Shanghai run would provide critical separation from the players chasing him in fifth through ninth place.

The Broader Picture

Beyond the three headliners, Daniil Medvedev has already reached the third round after a dominant win over Jan-Lennard Struff — a win that featured 21 aces. And Chinese wildcard Zhou Yi, ranked No. 277 in the world entering this week, is into the third round after pulling off a stunning upset over No. 5 seed Lorenzo Musetti in three sets.

The tournament runs through October 18. The quarterfinals are scheduled for Tuesday and Wednesday, with the semifinals and final to follow in the back half of the week. The points available in the later rounds — 180 for quarterfinals, 360 for semifinals, up to 1,000 for the winner — mean Sunday's results are just the beginning of what could be a decisive week for the Race to Turin.

What's Next
