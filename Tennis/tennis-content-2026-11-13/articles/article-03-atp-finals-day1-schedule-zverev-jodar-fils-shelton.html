ATP Finals Day 1 Schedule: Zverev Opens vs. Jodar at 2 PM CET; Fils Takes on Shelton at 8:30 PM

By Marcus Cole | November 13, 2026 | Tennis Fanrecap

The 2026 Nitto ATP Finals opens Sunday at the Inalpi Arena in Turin with two matches from the John McEnroe Group. Year-end No. 1 Alexander Zverev faces Rafael Jodar not before 2:00 PM CET. The evening session pairs Arthur Fils against Ben Shelton at 8:30 PM CET. Carlos Alcaraz does not play until Monday, when he opens the Ivan Lendl Group against Frances Tiafoe also not before 2:00 PM CET.

The tournament runs November 15–22, with the round-robin stage concluding November 20, the semifinals on November 21, and the final on November 22. The top two players from each group advance to the knockouts.

Zverev Opens Against Tournament History

Zverev's first match pits him against one of the most remarkable qualifier stories of the 2026 season. Rafael Jodar became the first player in ATP history to qualify for the year-end Finals in his debut year on the tour — a milestone that reflects the depth and competitiveness of the 2026 ATP season.

For Zverev, the opening match is both routine and loaded. He is the year-end No. 1, a Roland Garros and US Open champion this season, and a two-time ATP Finals winner — 2018 and 2021. He has been in this position before and knows what it takes to run the full week in Turin. The 2:00 PM CET slot is a familiar opener: afternoon light at the Inalpi Arena before the evening session brings the more anticipated atmosphere.

Jodar, for his part, qualified ahead of established names by producing the kind of consistent results across the year that the Race to Turin rewards. Making the Finals in a debut season is rare enough that it has never happened before. What he does once there is a different kind of test.

Fils vs. Shelton: The Night Session That Sets the Tone

The 8:30 PM CET session may be the most anticipated match of Day 1. Arthur Fils and Ben Shelton are two of the most watchable young players in the game — both big servers, both capable of generating crowd energy, both making their ATP Finals debuts this week.

Shelton arrived in Turin straight from the Paris Masters final, where he lost to Carlos Alcaraz. He has had one of the more consistent seasons of any American male in recent years and enters the McEnroe Group as a genuine threat to Zverev's smooth path to the semifinals. Fils has been a top-10 presence through 2026 and is the kind of player who performs best when the pressure is highest.

Their McEnroe Group result carries immediate implications. The loser of this match will need to beat Zverev or the other debutant to advance — not an impossible task, but a significantly harder one.

What's Next

Alcaraz opens the Ivan Lendl Group on Monday, November 16, against Tiafoe at the Inalpi Arena. His round-robin schedule also includes the tournament's marquee match: Alcaraz vs. Medvedev, a rematch of their 2023 US Open final on indoor hard courts in Turin. That match has not yet been scheduled but falls during the November 15–20 round-robin window.

Doubles play also begins November 15. The tournament reaches its knockout stage with the semifinals on November 21 and the final on November 22.
