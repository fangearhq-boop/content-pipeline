**Long-Form Post (8:00 AM ET)**

### STORY 5: ATP Finals Head-to-Head Preview

**Long-Form Post (6:00 PM ET)**

The 2026 Nitto ATP Finals starts Sunday, and the head-to-head records between the eight players reveal a lot about where the drama will come from.

In the John McEnroe Group, the most revealing number is Zverev vs. Shelton: 4-0. The year-end No. 1 has never lost to the American, and their most recent match was the 2026 US Open final — a five-set battle Zverev won in a tiebreak. That is a significant head-to-head advantage going into their round-robin encounter. Arthur Fils and Rafael Jodar are debutants who are talented enough to push Zverev, but the numbers favor the top seed to advance.

The Ivan Lendl Group is tighter. Alcaraz and Medvedev have played each other in Grand Slam finals — including the 2023 US Open — and there is no dominant H2H between the two. Alcaraz arrives with five titles this year and a 10-match winning streak. Medvedev has the indoor hard-court game and the experience of winning at this event before. Cobolli and Tiafoe are both first-timers with the weapons to disrupt the favorites.

Indoor hard courts in Turin favor big servers and aggressive players. That suits Shelton, Fils, and Tiafoe as much as it suits the top seeds.
