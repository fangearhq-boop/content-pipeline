Medvedev Storms Into Shanghai Quarterfinals With Straight-Sets Win Over Tsitsipas

By Marcus Cole

October 14, 2026 — Shanghai, China

Daniil Medvedev sent a clear message at the 2026 Shanghai Masters on Wednesday: the Beijing controversy is behind him, and he is here to win.

The No. 3 seed dispatched Stefanos Tsitsipas 6-4, 7-5 in the Round of 16, booking a quarterfinal berth with a performance that was efficient, focused, and entirely free of the drama that surrounded his departure from the Beijing ATP 500 just days ago.

In Beijing, Medvedev was disqualified from the semifinal after striking a spectator with a ball in frustration — a moment that cost him the match, his ranking points, and his prize money. The fallout dominated the tennis conversation heading into Shanghai. On Wednesday, Medvedev answered in the only language that matters.

A Clean Performance on a Big Stage

Against Tsitsipas, Medvedev was in control from the opening games. He broke early in the first set and never let the Greek player find a rhythm. The 6-4 opening set was his in 36 minutes. In the second, Tsitsipas pushed harder — but Medvedev's serve held firm when it needed to, and the Russian closed out the match at 7-5.

It was the kind of measured, professional win that Medvedev has built his reputation on. No flashpoints. No meltdowns. Just clean, high-level tennis that is difficult to beat when he is in this mode.

The win extends Medvedev's impressive record against Tsitsipas across their career head-to-head. He has consistently proved a difficult matchup for the Greek player, particularly on hard courts in Asia.

QF Awaits: Bergs or Paul

Medvedev now waits on the result of the Zizou Bergs vs. Tommy Paul match, also on Wednesday's schedule. The winner will face him in the quarterfinals.

Paul, seeded in the tournament, beat defending champion Valentin Vacherot 6-4, 6-2 in the previous round. Bergs, ranked around No. 39, arrived on the Shanghai stage after one of the tournament's biggest upsets — dispatching No. 2 seed Carlos Alcaraz 7-5, 7-6(4) in the third round on Monday.

On paper, Paul might be the more expected quarterfinal opponent. But Bergs has shown this week that expected outcomes do not apply to him right now.

The Beijing Shadow

Medvedev's disqualification in Beijing was a significant moment in his 2026 season. He had been playing some of his best tennis in years, and the DQ — while leaving him with no points or prize money — did not represent a form collapse, just a poor decision in a moment of frustration.

He has won three titles in 2026 and his aggressive baseline game remains one of the toughest to combat in the men's game. At Shanghai, a tournament he has won before, he looks like one of the genuine threats to Zverev's reign as the tournament's top seed.

The Shanghai hard courts suit Medvedev's game — fast enough to reward his flat ball-striking, but not so fast that it negates his baseline patience. If he can keep playing at this level, a deeper run looks very possible.

What's Next

Daniil Medvedev faces the winner of the Zizou Bergs vs. Tommy Paul Round of 16 match in the Shanghai Masters quarterfinals on Thursday, October 15. With Alexander Zverev on the other side of the draw, a potential Zverev–Medvedev semifinal is also in view if both advance.

The quarterfinals begin Thursday. Medvedev will be ready.
