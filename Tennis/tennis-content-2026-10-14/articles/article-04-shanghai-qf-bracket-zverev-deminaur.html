Shanghai Masters QF Bracket: Zverev vs. de Minaur Headlines a Wide-Open Last Eight

By Marcus Cole

October 14, 2026 — Shanghai, China

With the Round of 16 nearly complete at the 2026 Shanghai Masters, the quarterfinal bracket is taking shape — and it does not disappoint.

The draw that was supposed to be straightforward, with Jannik Sinner and Carlos Alcaraz providing expected deep runs, has become anything but. Sinner withdrew before the tournament began. Alcaraz was eliminated in the third round by Zizou Bergs. And now the quarterfinals feature a mix of proven champions, dangerous veterans, and at least one player most people had never heard of before this week.

The Headliner: Zverev vs. de Minaur

Alexander Zverev, the No. 1 seed and newly confirmed ATP Year-End No. 1, faces Alex de Minaur in what looks like the marquee quarterfinal.

Zverev reached the last eight with a battle — he defeated Alexander Bublik 7-6(6), 4-6, 7-6(1) in three sets, managing an ankle concern along the way. After the match, he acknowledged the issue directly: "My ankle was acting up unfortunately again, I'm starting to feel my body more, but happy to be through." Given that he is carrying the weight of Year-End No. 1 expectations and this late-season grind, the ankle bears watching.

De Minaur, meanwhile, has been in superb form. He dismissed Karen Khachanov 6-3, 6-4 to reach the QF and has now made consecutive Masters 1000 quarterfinals. The Australian is playing with aggression and timing — and in their recent Laver Cup meetings, he has beaten Zverev twice. This is not a straightforward win for the German.

Gea Continues His Fairy Tale

Arthur Gea may be the most remarkable story of this tournament — and that is saying something in a week that also includes Bergs eliminating Alcaraz.

The 21-year-old Frenchman beat No. 15 seed Jakub Mensik 7-6(4), 6-4 to reach his first Masters 1000 quarterfinal. This follows his third-round win over No. 5 seed Ben Shelton. Gea arrived in Shanghai having never played in a Masters 1000 main draw. He is now in the last eight.

His opponent: Alejandro Davidovich Fokina, who eliminated Hubert Hurkacz in R4. Davidovich Fokina is a dangerous player on hard courts — but Gea has shown this week that danger cuts both ways.

Ruud vs. Jodar — Veterans and Rising Stars

Casper Ruud reaches the quarterfinals after Felix Auger-Aliassime was forced to retire from their Round of 16 match with a knee injury, with Ruud leading 5-2 in the opening set. The walk-over is not the ideal way to advance, but Ruud is in the last eight and will face Rafael Jodar — the Spanish young gun who beat Botic van de Zandschulp 6-7(4), 6-4, 6-2 in another three-set battle.

Jodar has been one of the year's most exciting emerging players. His match against van de Zandschulp showed composure in the face of adversity — the kind of composure that could cause problems for even an experienced player like Ruud.

Medvedev's Quarter — The Wild Card

The final quarterfinal slot comes from the Zizou Bergs vs. Tommy Paul match — the winner faces Daniil Medvedev, who dominated Stefanos Tsitsipas 6-4, 7-5 on Wednesday. Whether it is Bergs (who stunned Alcaraz in R3) or Paul (who is playing clean, efficient tennis), Medvedev gets a genuine challenge in the last eight.

The quarterfinals run Thursday and Friday, October 15-16. This Shanghai draw has been the story of the Asian swing's second week — and the last eight promise more.

What's Next

The 2026 Shanghai Masters quarterfinals begin Thursday, October 15. Zverev vs. de Minaur is the headline. But in a tournament that has already produced Bergs over Alcaraz and Gea over Shelton, the definition of "headline" is subject to change at any moment.
