Winston-Salem Open Semifinals: Duckworth, Fery, Buse and Bonzi Battle for the Last Title Before the US Open

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap

August 28, 2026

One final tune-up remains before the US Open begins Sunday — and four players are competing for it today.

The 2026 Winston-Salem Open semifinals take place Friday at the Wake Forest University Tennis Complex in Winston-Salem, North Carolina. Two matches will determine who meets Saturday at 4 p.m. ET in the final — the last ATP 250 title event before Flushing Meadows opens its gates.

The Semifinal Matchups

In the first semifinal, Australian veteran James Duckworth faces Great Britain's Arthur Fery. Duckworth advanced through the quarterfinals in emphatic fashion and represents the Australian tour contingent that has been active through the US Open hardcourt swing. Fery, 22, pulled off a memorable quarterfinal comeback at the event — a result that generated attention from the tennis media and confirmed he belongs at this level.

The second semifinal pits Peru's Ignacio Buse, the tournament's third seed, against France's Benjamin Bonzi. Buse's run through the Winston-Salem bracket has drawn notice — the Peruvian is part of a growing contingent of South American players establishing themselves on the ATP Tour, and reaching a final in an American hard-court tournament before the US Open would be a meaningful milestone. Bonzi, 29, is an experienced campaigner who has seen deep ATP runs before and brings steadiness to a high-pressure situation.

A Wide-Open Field

With Carlos Alcaraz and most top-10 players heading directly to New York rather than competing in Winston-Salem, this year's draw had an unusually open path for players ranked outside the elite. All four semifinalists represent that dynamic: none is a household name to casual tennis fans, but each has played winning tennis throughout the week and earned the right to compete for a title on Friday.

The champion takes home a trophy Saturday afternoon — and then turns the page to New York, where the US Open main draw begins less than 24 hours later on Sunday.

Context for the US Open

For the Winston-Salem finalists, Saturday's match carries practical stakes beyond the title itself. A tournament victory provides a late confidence surge heading into the year's final Grand Slam, additional ranking points, and — perhaps most importantly — match sharpness on the hard-court surface where the US Open is played.

Whether it's Duckworth, Fery, Buse, or Bonzi who lifts the trophy Saturday, they will arrive at the US Open with fresher legs than players who have been competing deep into tournament draws all summer.

What's Next

The Winston-Salem Open final is Saturday, August 29, at 4:00 p.m. ET at Wake Forest University Tennis Complex. The winner earns ATP 250 ranking points and a title heading into the US Open, which begins Sunday, August 30.
