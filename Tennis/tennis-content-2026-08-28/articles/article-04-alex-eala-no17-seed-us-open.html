Alex Eala Seeded No. 17 at US Open — The Highest Grand Slam Seeding in Philippine Tennis History

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap

August 28, 2026

Alex Eala hasn't played a point yet at the 2026 US Open. She has already made history.

The 21-year-old from the Philippines enters the US Open women's singles draw seeded No. 17 — the highest Grand Slam seeding ever earned by a Filipino tennis player. The record stands before her first-round match begins Sunday.

A Milestone Build

The climb has been swift. Eala arrived at the 2026 Wimbledon Championship seeded No. 29 — itself a historic mark for Philippine tennis at the time. Now, just two Grand Slams later, she has improved that to No. 17 at the US Open.

The jump reflects strong results across the hardcourt summer swing, a surface that suits Eala's aggressive baseline game and powerful serve. She enters Flushing Meadows with momentum, confidence, and the weight of a nation watching.

The Draw

Eala opens against a qualifier in the first round Sunday — a favorable matchup that allows her to settle into the tournament without facing an immediate top-ranked opponent. Qualifying rounds were still ongoing as of Thursday, so her specific first-round opponent will be confirmed before Sunday's first serves.

The bracket beyond that grows progressively more demanding. Eala is drawn in the quarter of the women's draw headed by No. 4 seed Coco Gauff. Should Eala advance deep, she could face multiple past Grand Slam champions in the later rounds — players who have won major titles and know how to perform under the pressure of New York.

That prospect doesn't dim the opportunity. It defines it. A deep run by Eala would confirm that her seeding is a reflection of genuine competitiveness at the highest level, not just a moment to be noted and moved past.

What a No. 17 Seeding Means

Grand Slam seedings provide meaningful structural protection. As No. 17, Eala is guaranteed to avoid the top 16 seeds until the third round at the earliest. That creates a realistic path through two matches before she would face a player seeded within the top tier of the field.

That structure gives players like Eala — talented, ranked well, but not yet at the elite level — a fighting chance to prove themselves in the biggest moments of the season. The seeding is a reward for consistency across the tour. Now it becomes a starting point for the US Open.

What's Next

Alex Eala opens her 2026 US Open campaign in the first round Sunday, August 30. She faces a qualifier — her specific opponent to be confirmed after qualifying concludes. If she advances, she would face either Oleksandra Oliynykova or Reese Brantmeier in the second round.
