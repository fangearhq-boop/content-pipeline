Cincinnati Men's Draw: Zverev and Djokovic in Same Half, Both Chasing US Open Momentum

By Marcus Cole August 12, 2026

The Draw at a Glance

The 2026 Western and Southern Open draw was released Tuesday, and the top line is straightforward: Alexander Zverev is the No. 1 seed after Jannik Sinner (right knee) and Carlos Alcaraz (wrist) both withdrew before the draw. Novak Djokovic is seeded third, returning to the ATP Tour for the first time since Wimbledon.

The full top eight seeds: Zverev (1), Felix Auger-Aliassime (2), Djokovic (3), Daniil Medvedev (4), Alex de Minaur (5), Taylor Fritz (6), Flavio Cobolli (7), Ben Shelton (8). Rafael Jodar, currently playing tonight's Montreal semifinal, is seeded 12th in the Cincinnati draw. The tournament runs August 13-23 at the Lindner Family Tennis Center in Mason, Ohio.

Zverev and Djokovic — A Potential SF Before the US Open

The most significant detail in the draw is structural: Zverev and Djokovic are in the same half. They could potentially meet in the quarterfinals or semifinals rather than the final. Djokovic is a three-time Cincinnati champion (2007, 2012, 2018) and Zverev won the title in 2021. A Zverev-Djokovic match in the top half of the draw, with a US Open place on the line, would be the match of the tournament.

Also in the same half: Jodar at seed 12, and Cobolli at seed 7. Cobolli reached the Roland Garros final in June, losing to Zverev. A rematch in Cincinnati is possible in the quarterfinals. These two have unfinished business from Paris.

Djokovic's return is the news beyond the draw. He has not played since Wimbledon. At 39, he is entering a hard-court swing after a clay and grass season that produced no titles. His first round match against either Tirante or Choinski could tell a lot about his form and fitness heading into the US Open, where he is a three-time champion.

Wide Open Without Sinner and Alcaraz

For only the second time in the last several years, neither Sinner (world No. 1) nor Alcaraz (defending Cincinnati champion, No. 3) will be in the Cincinnati draw. Sinner is managing a right knee issue and is targeting the US Open main draw (August 25). Alcaraz has been sidelined since April with a wrist injury and is also targeting Cincinnati as his return, but his participation remains uncertain as of this writing.

Without those two, the draw is genuinely open. Zverev has not won a Masters 1000 since Montreal 2023. Medvedev has not won a Masters in 2026. Fritz won DC Open two weeks ago. De Minaur has been consistent but has not broken through at Masters level this season. Cincinnati in 2026 is a tournament without a clear favourite beyond the bracket position of Zverev.

What's Next

Cincinnati first-round matches begin August 13. The tournament runs 11 days through August 23, one day before the US Open main draw is released. Watch the Djokovic opener closely for any signs of rust or injury. Zverev's path through the top half, and whether he and Djokovic actually meet before the final, will frame the narrative for the first three days. The US Open begins August 24.
