ATP Finals Final Preview: Two Semifinals, Four Champions, and the Sunday Matchup That Awaits

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | November 21, 2026

By Sunday morning, the 2026 ATP Finals final will be set. Two matches on Saturday determine who plays for the title at the Inalpi Arena in Turin — Alcaraz versus Shelton at 2:30 PM CET, followed by Zverev versus Medvedev at 8:30 PM CET. The final takes place not before 3:00 PM CET on Sunday, November 22. What it looks like depends entirely on what happens on Saturday, and the four players remaining are as strong a group of semifinalists as the event has had in years.

The Four Contenders

Carlos Alcaraz has never won the ATP Finals. He has won five titles in 2026 — the Australian Open, Doha, Tokyo, Vienna, and Paris — and enters the semifinals with an 11-match winning streak and a 3-0 group stage. He is managing a left thigh concern. He is 21 years old and playing at a level that makes him the event favorite despite the injury questions.

Ben Shelton has never reached the ATP Finals final. His first appearance in the semifinals arrived this week after beating Rafael Jodar in a 10-8 first-set tiebreak. His game — a dominant serve, powerful groundstrokes, genuine athleticism — is built for indoor hard courts. He beat Alcaraz at the US Open in September, which is the most significant result of his case.

Alexander Zverev won the ATP Finals in 2018 and 2021. He is the year-end No. 1, the top seed, and went 3-0 in the group stage without dropping a set. He is managing an ankle issue he described as likely permanent. He is seeking a third title at this event, which would put him in rare company.

Daniil Medvedev won the ATP Finals in 2020. He holds a 14-8 career head-to-head over Zverev and has been the week's most clinical performer — 15 aces in his Tiafoe win, efficient ball-striking throughout. He is seeking his second title at the event and his first since his 2020 victory.

The Potential Finals

The marquee final is Alcaraz versus Zverev — a matchup between the season's most decorated player and the year-end No. 1. They are arguably the two best players in the world right now, and a final between them at the season-ending championship would be a fitting conclusion to 2026. Alcaraz's injury is the main variable; if he is healthy, this would be the defining match of the year.

Medvedev versus Shelton would be the final no one predicted before the week began. Medvedev the methodical former champion, Shelton the explosive first-time finalist. High stakes, contrasting styles, and no prior meeting in a major context.

Alcaraz versus Medvedev has been a recurring theme across the last several years — multiple Grand Slam final meetings, deep career knowledge of each other's games. If both advance, this would be the third most anticipated final, with the history between them lending it weight beyond just the title on offer.

Zverev versus Shelton would be a generational contrast: the established No. 1 defending his ATP Finals legacy against the American who has broken through this season. Their only prior meeting this year was the McEnroe Group match, which Zverev won with an ankle concern.

What's Next

The two semifinals play out on Saturday at the Inalpi Arena. Alcaraz versus Shelton begins at 2:30 PM CET. Zverev versus Medvedev follows at 8:30 PM CET. The ATP Finals final is scheduled for Sunday, November 22, not before 3:00 PM CET at the same venue. The 2026 season ends there — in Turin, with one of these four players lifting the trophy.
