Medvedev Beats Tiafoe 6-3, 6-4 to Reach ATP Finals Semifinals — First Time Since 2023

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | November 21, 2026

Daniil Medvedev returned to the ATP Finals semifinals for the first time since 2023, beating Frances Tiafoe 6-3, 6-4 on Friday evening at the Inalpi Arena in Turin. It was a clinical performance from the former champion — 15 aces, a decisive break in each set, and the kind of controlled aggression that makes Medvedev one of the most difficult players to beat on indoor hard courts when he is operating at this level.

The result completed the Day 6 schedule and confirmed the full semifinal draw. Medvedev faces Alexander Zverev in the night session on Saturday. Carlos Alcaraz plays Ben Shelton in the afternoon. The top four seeds have all advanced — a clean outcome that reflects how the week played out.

How the Match Unfolded

Medvedev controlled the match from the outset. He broke Tiafoe once in the first set to open a lead and never gave it back. His serve was exceptional throughout — 15 aces over the course of the match, with consistent placement that prevented Tiafoe from building any early-ball pressure on return.

The second set followed a similar pattern. Medvedev secured another break to take the lead and held comfortably through the closing games. The 6-4, 6-4 combination was a measure of how thoroughly he dominated. Tiafoe is not an easy opponent — he beat Flavio Cobolli earlier in the week in a match that showed his ability to win on the big stage — but he could not find a way through on Friday.

Tiafoe exits the event at 1-2. His win over Cobolli was the high point of his ATP Finals debut, and he gave Italian fans a moment to remember when he bagelled the home favorite in the second set of that match. But the decider against Medvedev showed the gap between the top of the tour and the players still working their way toward it.

What It Means for Medvedev

This is Medvedev's fourth appearance in the ATP Finals semifinals. He won the event in 2020, reaching the final and defeating Dominic Thiem. He has been to the semifinals before and knows what is required. Getting there in 2026 is a reminder that despite a season without a Grand Slam title, he remains one of the most dangerous players at this event.

His semifinal opponent is Zverev — the year-end No. 1 and the two-time champion of this tournament. Medvedev holds a 14-8 career head-to-head advantage over Zverev, a number that reflects years of competitive matches between the two. The main concern entering the match from Zverev's side is an ankle issue that flared up during his win over Arthur Fils on Thursday evening.

Medvedev will carry form into the semifinal. He has been methodical and efficient all week in a way that has not always been his calling card at this event. If his serve continues at the level it reached on Friday, he will be difficult to stop.

What's Next

Medvedev faces Zverev in the ATP Finals semifinals on Saturday evening at 8:30 PM CET at the Inalpi Arena in Turin. The winner advances to Sunday's final. The other semifinal — Alcaraz versus Shelton — takes place earlier in the day at 2:30 PM CET. The ATP Finals final is scheduled for Sunday, November 22, not before 3:00 PM CET. For Tiafoe, the season is now complete. His ATP Finals debut ends at 1-2, but competing at the season-ending championship is an achievement that reflects the form he has shown throughout the year.
