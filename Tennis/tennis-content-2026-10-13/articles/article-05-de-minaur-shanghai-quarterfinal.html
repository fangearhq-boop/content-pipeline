De Minaur Dominates Khachanov to Reach Back-to-Back Masters 1000 QF

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | October 13, 2026

Alex de Minaur has been one of the quiet success stories of the Shanghai Masters, and his quarterfinal berth is anything but quiet at this point. The Australian defeated Karen Khachanov 6-3, 6-4 on Tuesday to book a second consecutive Masters 1000 quarterfinal appearance — a run that reflects a player playing some of the best tennis of his career.

The numbers told the story. De Minaur hit 22 winners and won 80% of his first-serve points in a match that lasted approximately 1 hour 24 minutes. Khachanov, a consistent performer at the top level, never found the rhythm to challenge the Australian's relentless baseline game.

A Consistent Force in Shanghai

De Minaur reached the fourth round with decisive victories through the draw. Against Khachanov, he was at his mobile, efficient best — using his speed to neutralize Khachanov's power and finding the court with his forehand at a high rate throughout. The match was never particularly close, which is a significant statement given the quality of his opponent.

His performance came alongside an injury update — Yahoo Sports reported he addressed his physical condition after the win, though he gave no indication it would affect his quarterfinal availability. Fans will be watching closely to see how he holds up.

The Race to Turin Implications

De Minaur has been climbing the ATP Race to Turin standings throughout the back end of the season. A deep Shanghai run — perhaps a semifinal or final — could make a significant difference in whether he secures one of the eight spots for the ATP Finals in November. He enters the quarterfinal with significant momentum and a favorable draw if his run continues.

What's Next

De Minaur faces the winner of Alexander Zverev and Alexander Bublik in the quarterfinals, scheduled for October 15. A win there would send him to the semifinals. Both potential opponents present a significant challenge, but de Minaur has shown this week that he is not here just to fill a draw slot. Shanghai has revealed an Australian playing with confidence, direction, and the tools to beat the very best.
