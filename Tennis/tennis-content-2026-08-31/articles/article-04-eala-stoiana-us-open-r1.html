Eala Opens US Open Campaign vs. Qualifier Stoiana, Carrying Historic Seeding

By Elena Voss

August 31, 2026

US Open 2026

Before a single ball was struck on Day 2 of the 2026 US Open, Alexandra Eala had already made history. Her No. 17 seeding — the highest ever earned by a Filipino player at a Grand Slam — was announced with the draw last week and immediately sent waves across the Philippines and throughout the women's tennis world.

On Monday at Flushing Meadows, Eala gets to defend that seeding on court. She opens her US Open campaign against American qualifier Mary Stoiana in a first-round match that pits the sport's rising star against one of the tournament's most promising young newcomers.

A Historic Seeding

Eala's No. 17 seeding surpasses her previous best of No. 29, earned at the 2026 Wimbledon Championships earlier this summer. It is the highest Grand Slam seeding ever achieved by a Filipino player, male or female, and reflects the remarkable trajectory of the 20-year-old's career over the past 12 months.

Her section of the draw places her in a quarter headed by No. 4 seed Coco Gauff. A deep run for Eala could include a potential fourth-round meeting with the American. Between now and then: Stoiana, and whoever emerges from the second round.

Who Is Mary Stoiana?

Stoiana, 18, is one of the more intriguing qualifiers in the women's draw. An NCAA All-American who won the collegiate No. 1 ranking before turning professional, she earned her spot in the main draw by winning three consecutive qualifying rounds, defeating Yue Yuan in the final qualifying match 6-3, 3-6, 6-4.

She and Eala have history — from their ITF junior days, where Eala defeated Stoiana 7-5, 6-2. This is their first meeting at the WTA level, and the context is entirely different: a Grand Slam main draw, under prime-time conditions, with Eala the clear favorite.

The Eyes of a Nation

Interest in Eala's US Open run has been enormous in the Philippines, where she has become a national sports figure. Filipino fans tuned in by the millions for her Wimbledon run earlier this year, and the US Open is expected to draw similar attention.

Her coaching team has emphasized discipline and patience on the big points. Eala's serve, which has improved considerably through 2026, will be a key weapon against Stoiana's baseline game.

What's Next

Eala vs. Stoiana is scheduled for the Day 2 slate at the 2026 US Open. The winner advances to the second round, where Eala would likely face a seeded opponent from the Gauff quarter. If Eala progresses, her fourth-round potential against Gauff would be one of the most anticipated matchups of the women's tournament.

The 2026 US Open continues through Sunday, September 13 at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center in New York.
