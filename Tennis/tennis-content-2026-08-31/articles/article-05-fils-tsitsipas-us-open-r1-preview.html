Fils vs. Tsitsipas Headlines Day 2 Men's Action at 2026 US Open

By Marcus Cole

August 31, 2026

US Open 2026

When the ATP published its list of first-round matches to watch at the 2026 US Open, two names near the top were Arthur Fils and Stefanos Tsitsipas. The Cincinnati champion against the former No. 3 in the world, in the opening round of a Grand Slam that has already been blown wide open by Djokovic's shock exit on Day 1.

It is, by any measure, a compelling first-round draw — and one that illustrates exactly the kind of star-studded chaos that the US Open delivers every year.

Fils Arrives on a Wave

Arthur Fils has spent the better part of 2026 announcing himself as one of the ATP's genuine contenders. His victory at the Cincinnati Masters 1000 — where he dismantled American Frances Tiafoe 6-3, 1-6, 6-0 in the final — was historic on multiple levels: his first Masters 1000 title, the first Masters title by a French player since Jo-Wilfried Tsonga won in Canada in 2014, and the first time two Black men had met in a Masters 1000 final.

At 24 years old, Fils is seeded No. 14 at this US Open and carries the weight of enormous expectation from the French tennis community. A title in New York would make him the first French US Open champion since Yannick Noah won Roland Garros in 1983 — though Fils would be the first-ever French male US Open champion.

His head-to-head record against Tsitsipas stands at 5-2 in his favor, including wins on multiple surfaces. He approaches this match as the decided favorite.

Tsitsipas Looks to Reclaim Ground

Stefanos Tsitsipas has never won the US Open in a career that includes a Roland Garros final appearance and multiple deep Grand Slam runs. At 28, the Greek player remains a top-20 force but has found it difficult to string together the kind of momentum that made him one of the tour's dominant players between 2019 and 2022.

Tsitsipas told reporters he is "ready for the challenge" that Fils presents — a reflection of the respect he holds for the younger Frenchman's game. A win here would be a significant statement.

Berrettini-Wawrinka: The Other Day 2 Highlight

The ATP also flagged the meeting between Matteo Berrettini and Stan Wawrinka as a Day 2 match to watch. Both players are former Grand Slam champions — Wawrinka with three major titles, Berrettini with a Wimbledon title — meeting in the first round of the US Open. Encounters between former Slam winners this early in a tournament are genuinely rare and carry an outsized emotional weight.

What's Next

Fils vs. Tsitsipas is scheduled for the Day 2 evening session at the 2026 US Open, with live coverage available on ESPN and Tennis Channel. The winner advances to the second round, where a seeded opponent from the upper bracket awaits.

The 2026 US Open continues through Sunday, September 13 at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center in Flushing, New York.
