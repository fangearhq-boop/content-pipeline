US Open Day 2: Alcaraz Returns, Sabalenka Opens Three-Peat Quest, Fils Faces Tsitsipas

By Ryan Calloway

August 31, 2026

US Open 2026

Day 1 delivered one of the most stunning upsets in US Open history when Novak Djokovic fell to Mariano Navone in five sets while battling illness. Day 2 at Arthur Ashe Stadium promises to be equally riveting — perhaps more so — with three of the tournament's biggest stories set to unfold on Monday at Flushing Meadows.

Alcaraz's Long-Awaited Return

Carlos Alcaraz has not played a singles match since April 2026, when a right wrist injury — diagnosed as tenosynovitis — forced him to withdraw from competition before the French Open and Wimbledon. He has spent months recovering, and the 2026 US Open marks his return to the Grand Slam stage as defending champion.

His opening opponent is Roman Safiullin, the Russian No. 30 seed who is no stranger to upsetting top players. Safiullin reached the fourth round at Wimbledon 2026, defeating No. 24 Andrey Rublev and No. 26 Joao Fonseca before falling to Djokovic in four sets. He beat Alcaraz once before — at the 2023 Paris Masters — before Alcaraz avenged that defeat at the 2024 Paris Olympics.

The head-to-head stands at 1-1. The stakes could not be higher.

Alcaraz is listed as a heavy favorite, but questions about his fitness, match sharpness after four months away, and the psychological pressure of returning as defending champion make this genuinely unpredictable. Former player and analyst Greg Rusedski has publicly said Alcaraz will struggle in his opening match due to the long layoff.

Sabalenka's Three-Peat Pursuit

No. 1 seed Aryna Sabalenka is the two-time defending US Open champion — winner in 2024 and 2025 — and she opens her 2026 campaign against Colombia's Camila Osorio. Sabalenka has been dominant at Grand Slams throughout 2026, having reached the Australian Open final and winning Roland Garros. A third consecutive US Open title would make her the first player in the Open Era to accomplish that feat.

Osorio is a capable baseliner, but Sabalenka's power and court coverage make her a prohibitive favorite in R1. The bigger matchups — and the bigger pressure — come later in the draw.

Fils vs. Tsitsipas: The Day 2 Marquee ATP Match

No. 14 seed Arthur Fils arrives at the 2026 US Open having just captured the Cincinnati Masters 1000 title, defeating Frances Tiafoe in the final. The ATP ranked his first-round encounter against former No. 3 Stefanos Tsitsipas as one of the tournament's most anticipated R1 matchups.

Fils leads the career head-to-head 5-2 against Tsitsipas and enters the match with significant momentum. Tsitsipas, who has never won the US Open, has the experience and quality to push Fils — but the current trajectory favors the Frenchman.

Also on the Day 2 schedule: former Grand Slam champions Matteo Berrettini and Stan Wawrinka meet in R1, a matchup the ATP highlighted as a must-watch for fans of big-serving attacking tennis.

What's Next

Day 2 at the 2026 US Open begins Monday with day sessions at Arthur Ashe Stadium starting at 11:30 AM ET. Alcaraz vs. Safiullin is scheduled for the afternoon session at 1:00 PM ET, with Sabalenka vs. Osorio and Fils vs. Tsitsipas following in the evening block.

With Djokovic now out of the draw, the path through the men's bracket looks dramatically different. Whether Alcaraz can reclaim his form after four months away will be the defining question of the next two weeks at Flushing Meadows.
