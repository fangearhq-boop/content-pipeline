### STORY 1: Djokovic Falls to Navone (Tier 1)

**FB Long-Form — 8:00 AM ET**
The US Open had barely gotten started, and tennis history was already being made.

No. 4 seed Novak Djokovic — four-time US Open champion, 24-time Grand Slam title holder — walked off the court at Flushing Meadows on Sunday night after falling to Mariano Navone 7-6(5), 5-7, 4-6, 6-2, 6-1. A match that lasted approximately four hours and 36 minutes, and one that Djokovic spent visibly battling illness throughout. He vomited courtside multiple times. He faded dramatically in the fourth and fifth sets.

It is, astonishingly, the first time in Djokovic's entire career that he has lost in the first round of the US Open. It ended a streak of 78 consecutive Grand Slam first-round wins. The last time he lost in R1 at any Grand Slam was at the 2006 Australian Open — 20 years ago.

At 39, Djokovic has now gone 12 consecutive Grand Slam events without adding to his record 24 titles. His pursuit of No. 25 — a number that has defined his final chapter — extends at least into 2027.

What do you think — is this the beginning of the end for Djokovic at Grand Slams, or will he find a way back?
