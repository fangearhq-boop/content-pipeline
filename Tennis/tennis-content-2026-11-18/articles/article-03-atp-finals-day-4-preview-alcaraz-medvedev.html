ATP Finals Day 4 Preview: Alcaraz vs Medvedev — Group Decider Time in Turin

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | November 18, 2026

The ATP Finals has been building toward this match since the groups were drawn. Carlos Alcaraz and Daniil Medvedev meet Wednesday afternoon in Turin — not before 2:00 PM CET (8:00 AM ET) — with the winner advancing to the semifinals. Both players opened the Lendl Group with convincing wins. Neither has shown any sign of the kind of inconsistency that might make the other a heavy favourite. This is as closely matched a Day 4 contest as the tournament could have produced.

The Form Guide

Alcaraz is on a 10-match winning streak. He dismantled Frances Tiafoe 6-2, 6-2 in just 69 minutes in his opening match, breaking serve four of five times and extending a run of form that stretches back through Vienna and Paris. Tiafoe called him Superman afterward, and while that might seem like hyperbole, the numbers from that match support the sentiment. Alcaraz has been playing the best indoor tennis of his career this autumn.

Medvedev beat Flavio Cobolli 7-6(4), 6-3 on Day 2. The match was not as one-sided as Alcaraz's but it was equally authoritative in a different way — Medvedev faced zero break points and won 86% of points when Cobolli hit a second serve. That is not the performance of a player in poor form. It is the performance of a player who has arrived in Turin with his game constructed and his plan clear.

Head-to-Head and Surface Context

Alcaraz leads their all-time head-to-head 7-3. That includes wins across all surfaces, but the indoor record is a tighter picture. Alcaraz won both their previous indoor meetings, including their 2023 ATP Finals encounter in Turin itself — this venue — but Medvedev beat him at Indian Wells 2026 and the two players have been closer in result this season than the overall H2H suggests.

Indoor hard courts slightly favour Medvedev's grinding game and deep returns. But Alcaraz's serving and footwork have been exceptional on the indoor boards all autumn, and he has shown an ability to vary his game in ways that make it difficult for Medvedev to settle into a rhythm. The key tactical question on Wednesday will be whether Medvedev can disrupt Alcaraz's first-strike game or whether Alcaraz can dictate the pace before Medvedev's returns lock in.

Evening: Cobolli vs Tiafoe

The evening session brings together Cobolli and Tiafoe — both at 0-1 and in serious need of a win. The match (not before 8:30 PM CET) carries significant stakes for both players. A loss puts either player at 0-2 and effectively ends their tournament. Cobolli plays in front of his home crowd at the Inalpi Arena, which has been an important factor all week. Tiafoe will be motivated to respond after a heavy first-match defeat to Alcaraz.

What's Next

Wednesday's Lendl Group matches will shape the remaining ATP Finals draw. If Alcaraz and Medvedev both win their Day 4 matches, the group seedings are locked in heading into their Day 6 matches. If there is an upset, the entire Lendl Group finale changes shape. Either way, Day 4 is the defining day of the week in this half of the draw.
