McEnroe Group After Day 3: Zverev Qualified, Shelton and Fils Set Up Thursday Showdown

By Elena Voss | Tennis Fanrecap | November 18, 2026

The Alexander McEnroe Group at the ATP Finals has crystallised following Tuesday's two matches in Turin. Alexander Zverev is through to the semifinals at 2-0. Ben Shelton and Arthur Fils both sit at 1-1 and will meet Thursday in a match that will determine who joins the world No. 1 in the last four. Rafael Jodar is 0-2 and has been all but mathematically eliminated.

Zverev's Perfect Group Stage

Zverev's route to qualification has been exactly what his status as the world No. 1 and two-time ATP Finals champion would suggest. He beat Jodar 6-4, 6-4 on Day 1, then followed that with Tuesday's 6-2, 7-6(4) win over Shelton. The 14 aces and zero double faults in the second match underlined both his technical sharpness and his ability to serve his way out of pressure situations. He now has a Day 5 match against Fils with the semifinal place banked.

Shelton and Fils: The Setup for Thursday

Both players share the same record but arrived at it via different paths. Shelton beat Fils in straight sets on Day 1, then lost to Zverev in a competitive 7-6 second set on Day 3. Fils lost to Shelton on Day 1 and then recovered with a three-set comeback win over Jodar on Day 3 — the first French win at the ATP Finals since Tsonga in 2011.

Their Thursday match (Day 5) will be a direct head-to-head for the second semifinal spot. Shelton leads their recent H2H with the Day 1 win, but Fils's comeback against Jodar showed he can respond under pressure and manage big occasions. This is a match between two players who know each other well and have results going each way over the course of the season.

Jodar's Narrow Path

Rafael Jodar, the first player to qualify for the ATP Finals in his debut year on tour, sits at 0-2 after losses to Zverev and Fils. His tournament is not technically over — he would need to beat Shelton on Thursday and hope Fils defeats Zverev in the same session. That combination of results is possible in theory, but it requires Jodar to win a match he has lost twice already and for Fils to beat the already-qualified world No. 1. His coaches will be candid about the probability.

What Jodar has shown across the week, however, is that he can compete at this level. His ability to push experienced players and his first-set win over Fils on Tuesday were genuine positives for a first tournament at this stage of competition. At 22 and in his debut year, there will be more ATP Finals in his future.

What's Next

Thursday's McEnroe Group finale features Zverev vs Fils (with Zverev already qualified) and Shelton vs Jodar (the elimination match). The result of Shelton-Jodar and the Fils-Zverev match together will determine the final group standings. Expect the second semifinal spot from this group to be decided before the day is out.
