**Long-Form Post (10:30 AM ET)**

Arthur Fils was down a set, facing elimination at the ATP Finals, and then he found another gear entirely.

The final score — 4-6, 6-2, 6-2 over Rafael Jodar — doesn't quite capture how the match unfolded. Jodar took the first set and had the momentum. Fils, who'd lost to Shelton in his opening match, looked like he might be heading for a straight-sets exit. Instead, the Frenchman responded with two clinical sets that showed exactly why he qualified for Turin in the first place.

Jodar is now 0-2 and needs significant external help to advance. Fils, meanwhile, faces Alexander Zverev on Thursday — the already-qualified world No. 1. It's a tall order, but after Tuesday's comeback, nothing looks impossible.
