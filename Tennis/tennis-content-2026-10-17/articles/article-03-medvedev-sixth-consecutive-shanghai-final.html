Medvedev Reaches Sixth Consecutive Shanghai Masters Final, Ends Jodar's Historic Run

By Ryan Calloway

October 17, 2026 — Shanghai, China

Daniil Medvedev is in the Shanghai Masters final for the sixth year in a row. The Russian fifth seed defeated Rafael Jodar in the second Shanghai semifinals on Saturday, extending a streak of Shanghai finals appearances that is without precedent in the modern game.

For Jodar, the tournament ends at the semifinal stage — but the 20-year-old Spaniard leaves Shanghai having already made history as the tournament's youngest-ever semifinalist and with his ATP Finals qualification hopes very much alive.

Medvedev's Shanghai Mastery

What Medvedev has built at the Shanghai Masters is one of the most consistent records in tournament history. He won the title in 2019, and has reached the final in every edition since — a run that speaks not just to skill but to something more difficult to quantify: the ability to perform at one specific tournament year after year at the highest possible level.

His 2026 Asian swing has been exceptional. He won the ATP 250 in Hangzhou, reached the semifinals in Beijing, and now finds himself in yet another Shanghai final. His record across the three-tournament run was 11-1 coming into Saturday's match — the kind of form that turns a stretch of weeks into a statement about where a player stands in the game.

Medvedev's semifinal win over Jodar, while confirming his place in Sunday's final, also required him to navigate a genuine test. The 20-year-old had dropped the first set in all four of his previous Shanghai matches and won anyway. He had Medvedev's experience and efficiency to contend with this time, and that proved the difference.

Jodar's Week in Context

Rafael Jodar entered Shanghai as the 12th seed and leaves it having produced one of the most compelling individual runs of the tournament. He beat van de Zandschulp, Lehecka, and Ruud in successive rounds — coming from a set down in each one — before meeting Medvedev in the final four.

At 20 years old, he is the youngest player to reach the Shanghai Masters semifinals. The ATP Finals in Turin was his stated goal coming into the Asian swing, and a semifinal result at a Masters 1000 carries significant points. His qualification hopes remain alive, though he will need strong results to close the gap on the players above him in the race.

Jodar's week is not a consolation story. It is a marker of what is coming from a player who has not yet reached the peak of his career.

Sunday's Final

Medvedev's final opponent is Alejandro Davidovich Fokina, who earlier Saturday defeated World No. 1 Alexander Zverev 5-7, 6-4, 6-3 in one of the tournament's biggest upsets. Fokina has beaten Medvedev twice in six career meetings, and arrives in the final playing some of the best tennis of his year.

Medvedev enters as the more experienced finalist at this venue and carries the weight of six Shanghai finals into the match. Fokina carries the momentum of a semis performance that shocked the world No. 1.

What's Next

Daniil Medvedev plays Alejandro Davidovich Fokina in the Shanghai Masters final on Sunday, October 18. A win would give Medvedev his second Shanghai title and add another entry to one of the more remarkable tournament-specific records in recent tennis history. Rafael Jodar returns to the tour with 360 ranking points from Shanghai and a semifinal run that has changed the trajectory of his season.
