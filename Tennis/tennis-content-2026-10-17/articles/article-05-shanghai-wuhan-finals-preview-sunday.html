Shanghai and Wuhan Finals Preview: Fokina vs. Medvedev, Kartal Awaits

By Marcus Cole

October 17, 2026 — Shanghai/Wuhan, China

Two weeks of tennis in China reach their conclusion on Sunday. In Shanghai, Alejandro Davidovich Fokina and Daniil Medvedev play the Masters 1000 final. In Wuhan, Sonay Kartal awaits her opponent from Saturday's all-Polish semifinal. Both finals are worth following closely.

Shanghai Final: Fokina vs. Medvedev

Alejandro Davidovich Fokina has never won an ATP Masters 1000 title. Daniil Medvedev won Shanghai in 2019 and has reached the final every year since. Those two facts define the dynamic of Sunday's match before a ball is hit.

Fokina earned his place in the final by defeating World No. 1 Alexander Zverev 5-7, 6-4, 6-3 in the semifinal — a performance built on 36 winners and near-perfect serving under pressure in the final set. The quality he showed against Zverev, particularly in the third set, is the kind of level that can beat anyone in a final.

Medvedev, meanwhile, reached his sixth consecutive Shanghai final by defeating Rafael Jodar in the other semifinal. The Russian fifth seed has spent the Asian swing accumulating results with his usual efficiency — 11-1 across Hangzhou, Beijing, and Shanghai. He brings experience at this venue that no other player can match.

The head-to-head between them stands at 2-6 in Medvedev's favor, but two of Fokina's victories have come recently. In a one-match final, history matters less than current form. Fokina's form on Saturday was exceptional.

Wuhan Final: Kartal vs. Swiatek or Chwalinska

Sonay Kartal's week in Wuhan has been improbable enough already. The British qualifier, ranked No. 170 in the world, beat No. 6 Svitolina in the quarterfinals and No. 34 Ostapenko in consecutive tiebreaks in the semifinal. She now waits to find out who she plays in Sunday's final.

If Iga Swiatek advances from Saturday's all-Polish semifinal, Kartal faces the world No. 1 and the tournament's top seed. Swiatek has won five Grand Slams and has been the sport's dominant force for years. She is a difficult final opponent for anyone.

If Maja Chwalinska wins Saturday's semifinal, Kartal faces the 2026 French Open runner-up — herself a player who has already proved capable of competing at the very highest level. Chwalinska would also arrive in the final having just beaten the world No. 1, which would give her considerable momentum.

Either way, the Wuhan final is Kartal's tournament. She has already exceeded every expectation. Sunday is the chance to exceed them one more time.

ATP Finals Implications

Sunday's Shanghai final has ATP Finals consequences. Fokina's run puts him in striking distance of Turin qualification, and a title would essentially confirm his spot. Medvedev is already comfortably positioned in the Race to Turin. For Jodar, a semifinal result moves him meaningfully up the standings but likely not enough — he will need results in the coming weeks.

Zverev has already qualified and leaves Shanghai disappointed. His path to a third major of 2026 was not closed by a loss to a peer but by an opponent who played better. That will sting.

What's Next

The Shanghai Masters final between Alejandro Davidovich Fokina and Daniil Medvedev takes place on Sunday, October 18. The Wuhan Open final between Sonay Kartal and the winner of Swiatek vs. Chwalinska is also on Sunday, October 18. Both finals mark the conclusion of the 2026 Asian swing.
