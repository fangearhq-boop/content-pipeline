Davidovich Fokina Stuns World No. 1 Zverev to Reach Shanghai Masters Final

By Elena Voss

October 17, 2026 — Shanghai, China

Alejandro Davidovich Fokina arrived at the Shanghai Masters as a difficult draw, a player capable of beating anyone on his day. On Saturday, that day arrived emphatically. The Spaniard defeated World No. 1 Alexander Zverev 5-7, 6-4, 6-3 to reach his second career ATP Masters 1000 final.

It was not the result anyone anticipated. Zverev had been the overwhelming tournament favorite, the French Open and US Open champion this year, and a player who had shown no vulnerability all week. Fokina changed that calculation in two confident hours on court.

The Match That Defied Expectations

The first set went as most expected. Zverev controlled it, serving with authority and dictating exchanges from the baseline. He took it 7-5, and the match appeared to be following a predictable script.

Fokina rewrote it from the second set onward. He broke Zverev in the 10th game of the second set to level the match, then produced some of his best tennis in the third. He lost just three points on his serve across the entire final set — a display of serving precision that left Zverev with almost nothing to work with.

The final winner count told the story: 36 for Fokina, 28 for Zverev. The world No. 26 outplayed the world No. 1 when it mattered most.

A Return to the Highest Level

Davidovich Fokina's win over Zverev is the defining result of his season. The 26-year-old from Málaga has spent portions of 2026 hovering outside the Top 20, and this run in Shanghai pulls him back firmly into the conversation at the top of the game.

His last Masters 1000 final came at Monte-Carlo, where he pushed the eventual champion before coming up short. His Shanghai run has been built on controlled aggression and consistent serving under pressure — both of which were on full display in the third set against Zverev.

The win also has ATP Finals implications. Zverev had already qualified for Turin; Fokina's ranking boost puts him closer to a late-season push for one of the remaining spots.

A Formidable Final Opponent

Fokina's final opponent on Sunday is Daniil Medvedev, who defeated Rafael Jodar in the other semifinal to reach his sixth consecutive Shanghai Masters final. Medvedev knows this court and this tournament better than almost anyone alive — he won the title here in 2019 and has spent a week going through opponents with characteristic efficiency.

The head-to-head between Fokina and Medvedev is manageable from Fokina's perspective. He now holds a 2-6 career record against the Russian, but the last two meetings have gone in his favor, and the form he showed against Zverev suggests he enters the final with genuine confidence.

This will be a test of whether Fokina's upward trajectory through the week can be sustained against the most consistent Shanghai performer of the modern era.

What's Next

Alejandro Davidovich Fokina faces Daniil Medvedev in the Shanghai Masters final on Sunday, October 18. A title would be his first ATP Masters 1000 crown and one of the biggest wins of his career. Medvedev enters as a slight favorite, but Fokina's performance against Zverev makes him a credible threat to lift the trophy.
