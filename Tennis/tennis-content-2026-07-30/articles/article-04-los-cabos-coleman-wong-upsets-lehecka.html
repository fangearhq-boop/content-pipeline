Coleman Wong Shocks Top Seed Lehecka in Los Cabos, Closing In on Top-100 Debut

By Elena Voss | July 30, 2026

Los Cabos, Mexico

This week's biggest North American hard court upset did not happen in Washington. It happened at the Mifel Tennis Open by Telcel in Los Cabos, Mexico, where Coleman Wong of Hong Kong defeated No. 1 seed Jiri Lehecka 1-6, 6-3, 6-4 on Wednesday to produce the result the ATP Tour called the biggest career win of his life by ranking. Wong's name is now worth knowing, and the ranking that will reflect this win may push him through a threshold he has been approaching: the top 100.

How the Match Unfolded

The first set was not encouraging for Wong. Lehecka — ranked inside the ATP's top 15, a consistent presence on hard courts who had entered the week as the tournament's prohibitive favorite — dispatched the opening set 6-1 with minimal resistance. This was Lehecka playing to his ranking and making Wong look exactly like what the seedings suggested: a player who had gotten here on merit but was facing a different level of opponent.

Then the match changed. Wong won the second set 6-3, finding something in his game that the first set hadn't revealed — the ability to build points, stay disciplined on the baseline, and force Lehecka into positions where the Czech player's normally reliable groundstrokes went awry. The third set followed the same pattern. At 6-4, Wong had completed the comeback and delivered the most significant win of his career.

The ATP Tour noted the result in its daily coverage and framed the win specifically in terms of ranking significance — this is a win that moves Wong's potential ranking points in the direction of a top-100 position. A player from Hong Kong breaking into the top 100 would itself be a notable milestone for tennis in that part of the world.

Other Los Cabos Results

The rest of the Los Cabos field has been working through its bracket with less drama. Cameron Norrie, the fifth seed, earned his second win of the week on Wednesday, defeating James Duckworth 4-6, 6-3, 6-4 after losing the first set. Norrie needed three sets to advance but showed the kind of recovery tennis that his form earlier this season had been missing — he's now two wins deep in a tournament he entered with real momentum after breaking his own six-match losing streak in Round 1 against Kovacevic.

Francisco Cerundolo, the third seed, was more comfortable — 7-5, 6-2 over Tristan Boyer, a result that puts the Argentine into the quarterfinal bracket without any complications. Denis Shapovalov, the eighth seed and defending champion at this tournament, has now won five consecutive matches at Los Cabos across his career, adding to a record here that suggests something particular about the conditions suits his game.

What's Next

The Los Cabos quarterfinals are taking shape through Thursday July 30. With Lehecka out, the top of the draw has opened considerably. Cerundolo, Norrie, and Shapovalov remain in contention. The tournament runs through August 1, making it one of several events feeding into the North American hard court swing leading to the Canadian Open and the US Open in late August. Coleman Wong's next match will be the biggest of his career regardless of opponent — the quarterfinal of an ATP 250 is not territory he has reached before.
