US Open Day 3: Zverev, Gauff, Fritz, Keys and Eala All in Action as First Round Concludes

By Ryan Calloway

September 1, 2026

US Open 2026

The first round of the 2026 US Open reaches its conclusion on Tuesday, and the final day of opening-round play brings some of the most anticipated names in the draw onto the courts at Flushing Meadows.

It has already been a chaotic opening fortnight: Djokovic fell in five sets on Day 1, Tsitsipas stunned Cincinnati champion Fils on Day 2, and Carlos Alcaraz made his first competitive appearance in 139 days — winning in straight sets. By the time Tuesday's matches are complete, the full second-round picture will be known.

Zverev Opens on Arthur Ashe

No. 1 seed Alexander Zverev (Germany) begins his US Open campaign against Lorenzo Sonego (Italy) on Arthur Ashe Stadium. Zverev arrives at Flushing Meadows as one of the two surviving top seeds — the draw has already shed Djokovic and Fils — and the tournament's opening days have only enhanced the pressure on the German to deliver at a major he has yet to win.

Sonego, ranked just outside the top 40, is a difficult opponent at any stage of a Grand Slam. He can be disruptive off both wings and has the ability to mix up pace and spin in ways that unsettle power hitters. Zverev will be expected to advance, but the first serve and return numbers will be watched closely.

Gauff and Fritz on Home Soil

Coco Gauff, the No. 4 seed and defending US Open champion, opens her title defense against Turkey's Zeynep Sonmez on Arthur Ashe Stadium. The New York crowd will be firmly behind their home favourite, and Gauff — who has been in strong form on the hard-court swing — will be looking to make a statement on the sport's biggest stage.

Taylor Fritz (No. 9 seed) faces a different kind of pressure: an all-American first-round matchup against wildcard Darwin Blanch. Blanch, 20, earned his main-draw place through strong qualifying play and has the kind of game — flat, aggressive, hard to read on serve — that could cause problems on a fast surface. Fritz, who reached the US Open final in 2024, won't take the match lightly.

Australian Open champion Madison Keys also plays Day 3, taking on Alina Korneeva (Russia), before the evening session delivers Zverev's opener under the lights.

Eala Makes History

Alexandra Eala (Philippines, No. 17 seed) faces American qualifier Mary Stoiana on Louis Armstrong Stadium in what is one of the most anticipated match-ups of the final day of first-round play. Eala's No. 17 seeding is the highest-ever Grand Slam seeding by a Filipino player — a record she set simply by reaching this tournament as a seed.

Stoiana, 18, advanced through three qualifying rounds to reach the main draw and has been one of the most promising young American players on the collegiate and ITF circuits. Eala holds a 1-0 lead in their limited prior head-to-head from a 2024 ITF event, but this is their first WTA-level meeting and the occasion will be unlike anything Stoiana has encountered before.

What's Next

Tuesday's matches complete the first round of the 2026 US Open. The second round begins on Wednesday, with Alcaraz facing Faria and Tsitsipas playing Harris among the early marquee matchups.
