Alcaraz Wins on Return, Beats Safiullin in Straight Sets to Begin US Open Title Defense

By Ryan Calloway

September 1, 2026

US Open 2026

Carlos Alcaraz walked into Arthur Ashe Stadium on Monday night having not played a competitive tennis match in 139 days. He walked out of it as the most reassuring presence in the 2026 US Open draw.

The defending champion defeated Roman Safiullin 6-4, 6-4, 6-4 in his first singles match since sustaining a right wrist injury at the Barcelona Open on April 14. In a tournament that has already shed No. 3 seed Novak Djokovic and No. 10 seed Arthur Fils in the first round, Alcaraz's composed, clinical performance sent a clear message: he's here, and he's ready.

"I'm just really happy I was able to compete again," Alcaraz said after the match. "I missed the crowd, the energy, the competition."

No Rust, No Drama

The natural concern heading into the match was physical: could a player returning from four-plus months away sustain his level over a best-of-five set match at a Grand Slam? The answer, across just over two hours on court, was an unambiguous yes.

Alcaraz controlled the baseline exchanges from the opening games, generating his customary combination of heavy topspin from the forehand and precision from the backhand. Safiullin — a capable baseline player who pushed Djokovic close in previous meetings — found few answers. The Russian was broken in the fifth game of each set, and Alcaraz served out all three sets without drama.

Alcaraz had acknowledged before the match that returning to top-level competition after such a long absence was never easy. "It wasn't an easy decision," he had said. Having made it, he appeared at peace with the result from the first game.

A Draw That Has Opened Up

Alcaraz's victory arrives in a context that has changed dramatically since the draw was made. Jannik Sinner, the world No. 1, withdrew before the tournament with knee tendon inflammation. Djokovic — who was seeded third and considered a genuine title contender — fell to qualifier Mariano Navone in five sets while visibly ill on Day 1. And on Day 2, No. 10 seed Fils was eliminated by Tsitsipas.

With No. 1 seed Alexander Zverev yet to open his campaign, Alcaraz has emerged as the clearest current favourite. His section of the draw remains to be confirmed as results continue, but a potential Alcaraz-Zverev semifinal or final is the matchup many in the sport are already anticipating.

For now, Alcaraz's next test comes in the second round: a meeting with Portugal's Jaime Faria. Faria, 23, has had a strong 2026 season on hard courts. It will be a step up in difficulty, but one Alcaraz will expect to handle.

What's Next

Carlos Alcaraz advances to the second round of the 2026 US Open and will face Jaime Faria (POR) on Wednesday. If he progresses, he would be among the leading candidates to reach a third US Open final in his career. The tournament continues through Sunday, September 13, with the men's final scheduled for that date.

Day 3 on Tuesday features No. 1 seed Alexander Zverev making his debut against Lorenzo Sonego, alongside Coco Gauff, Taylor Fritz, and Madison Keys.
