### STORY 1: Djokovic Survives Vacherot in Paris — Shoulder Scare, Turin Hopes Still Alive
**Tier:** 1 | **Pillar:** Match Result | **Byline:** Marcus Cole

Novak Djokovic defeated Valentin Vacherot 6-7(5), 7-5, 6-1 in the first round of the Rolex Paris Masters on Monday, surviving a shoulder scare and saving three break points at 4-4 in the second set before dominating the decider. The match lasted 2 hours and 35 minutes. Djokovic called for a medical timeout at 2-1 down in the second set for treatment on his right shoulder. He then broke back for 2-2 and never looked back, closing the match with a dominant third set. He next faces qualifier Rinky Hijikata. Djokovic sits 11th in the Race to Turin with 2,940 points — 319 behind 9th-placed Rafael Jodar — and needs a semifinal minimum to qualify.
