Pegula Defeats Navarro 3-6, 6-4, 6-3 to Reach US Open Semifinals for Third Straight Year

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | September 10, 2026

US Open 2026 | Jessica Pegula | Emma Navarro | Quarterfinals | WTA

Jessica Pegula has made US Open semifinals a habit. The No. 3 seed defeated Emma Navarro 3-6, 6-4, 6-3 on Wednesday at Arthur Ashe Stadium to advance to the last four for the third consecutive year. The match lasted 2 hours and 20 minutes and required Pegula to come from behind after dropping the first set to the player who beat her at this same tournament two years ago.

In 2024, Navarro knocked Pegula out in the round of 16 on this same court. This time, Pegula went down a set again before finding her way through. The final score did not capture how hard Pegula had to work, but it captured the result: she is through to the semifinals, and Navarro is not.

A Familiar Script, a Different Ending

Navarro took the opening set 6-3 and looked confident doing it. The 22-year-old American has developed into one of the most dangerous players in the women's draw over the past two years, and her movement and defensive positioning gave Pegula early problems. For a set, it looked like the 2024 US Open was about to repeat itself.

But Pegula is a different player than she was two years ago. Her experience at this stage of Grand Slams — and specifically at this venue — showed in the second and third sets. She won the second 6-4 and the third 6-3, growing more authoritative as the match went on. Navarro could not find the same level she had shown in the opener, and Pegula took full advantage.

Three Straight US Open Semifinals

Pegula's run of three consecutive US Open semifinals is one of the most consistent stretches of any player at Flushing Meadows in recent years. She has reached the semifinal stage in 2024, 2025, and now 2026 — each time finding a way to get through a loaded draw. At 30 years old, she is performing at the peak of her career at exactly the right tournament.

The elusive piece for Pegula has always been converting a semifinal into something more. She fell to Sabalenka in the 2024 final. This time, she gets another chance — and it begins on Thursday.

Four Americans in the US Open Semifinals

Pegula's victory means the 2026 US Open will feature an unprecedented American presence in the final four. On the women's side: Pegula (No. 3) and Coco Gauff (No. 4), who defeated Mirra Andreeva earlier Wednesday. On the men's side: Ben Shelton and Frances Tiafoe, who both won their quarterfinals on Tuesday and will meet in an all-American semifinal Friday.

Four Americans in Grand Slam semifinals simultaneously is a rare achievement, and the US Open crowd has every reason to be fully invested in both draws heading into the final stretch of the tournament.

What's Next

Pegula faces Aryna Sabalenka in Thursday's women's semifinal at Arthur Ashe Stadium. Sabalenka is the defending champion, chasing a third consecutive US Open title. The two players met in the 2024 US Open final, where Sabalenka won in straight sets. Sabalenka leads their all-time head-to-head 9-4.

Pegula has never won the match that matters most here. Thursday is her chance to change that.
