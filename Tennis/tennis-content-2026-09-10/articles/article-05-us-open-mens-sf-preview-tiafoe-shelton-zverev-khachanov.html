US Open Men's Semifinals Friday: All-American Tiafoe vs. Shelton, Zverev vs. Khachanov

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | September 10, 2026

US Open 2026 | Men's Semifinals | Tiafoe | Shelton | Zverev | Khachanov | ATP

The US Open men's draw is down to four players, and the semifinals scheduled for Friday at Arthur Ashe Stadium are as compelling as any in recent memory. On one side: Frances Tiafoe and Ben Shelton, two Americans who won back-to-back five-set thrillers on Tuesday night and will now meet each other. On the other: No. 1 seed Alexander Zverev, the defending champion, facing Karen Khachanov in a match that will determine who plays in Sunday's final.

An American man will be in the US Open final for the first time in over two decades. Tiafoe and Shelton have already made that happen. The only remaining question is which one.

Tiafoe vs. Shelton: The All-American Semifinal

Frances Tiafoe's path to the semifinals required one of the most dramatic comebacks of this US Open. He fell two sets down to fellow American Alex Michelsen on Tuesday before rallying to win 5-7, 3-6, 7-5, 6-3, 7-6. It was his second career US Open semifinal, and the manner of victory — grinding through five sets against a qualifier who had nothing to lose — showed everything that makes Tiafoe dangerous at this stage of a tournament.

Ben Shelton's quarterfinal win over Carlos Alcaraz was even more remarkable in its circumstances. The match finished at approximately 3:30 AM ET — the latest finish in US Open history — after Shelton outlasted the defending champion 6-7(5), 6-1, 6-3, 1-6, 7-6(10-6). He sealed it with an ace in the 10-point match tiebreak. Shelton is now in his third career Grand Slam semifinal.

The two Americans are friends on the ATP Tour and have shared practice courts throughout the season. On Friday, they will share a court for very different reasons. Their head-to-head record and individual styles — Tiafoe's relentless groundstrokes and vocal crowd engagement against Shelton's serve-driven aggression — set up a semifinal that New York will be watching closely.

Zverev vs. Khachanov: The Other Half

Alexander Zverev, the No. 1 seed and defending US Open champion, continues his pursuit of a second consecutive title after a commanding 6-2, 7-5, 6-1 victory over Botic van de Zandschulp on Wednesday. It was a methodical performance from Zverev, who conceded relatively few opportunities throughout. He has not been seriously tested in this tournament and arrives at the semifinals in strong form.

Zverev's opponent Friday is Karen Khachanov, who reached the final four after Alexander Blockx retired midway through their quarterfinal with Khachanov leading 6-2, 7-5. The retirement means Khachanov has played less tennis than any other semifinalist at this US Open, and how that affects his momentum — positively or negatively — is one of the match's more interesting variables.

Khachanov reached the US Open final in 2022 and knows what it takes to make a run at Flushing Meadows. But Zverev, the defending champion, is the clear favorite to advance from this half.

What's Next

Both men's semifinals take place Friday at Arthur Ashe Stadium, with coverage on ESPN. Tiafoe vs. Shelton is expected in the afternoon session, with Zverev vs. Khachanov to follow. The men's final is Sunday, September 13. The women's final is Saturday, September 12. New York is four matches away from closing out its Grand Slam. The 2026 US Open is delivering on every storyline it promised.
