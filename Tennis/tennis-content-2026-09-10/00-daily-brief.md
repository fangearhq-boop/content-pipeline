### STORY 1: Gauff Saves Match Points, Rallies Past Andreeva to Reach Women's SF (TIER 1)
**Status:** FOLLOW-UP (2026-09-09 S4: Gauff vs. Andreeva QF preview)
**Angle:** RESULT — Coco Gauff (USA, No. 4) defeated Mirra Andreeva (RUS, No. 5) 2-6, 7-6(7), 6-2 on Wednesday at Arthur Ashe Stadium. Andreeva won 7 of the first 9 points and dominated the first set in 26 minutes. Gauff rallied in the second-set tiebreak, won a 39-shot rally at 5-3, then saved match points to take the tiebreak 9-7. The third set was all Gauff. She advances to meet Elena Rybakina in Thursday's women's semifinal.

### STORY 2: Rybakina Beats Zheng, Claims World No. 1 Ranking for First Time (TIER 1)
**Status:** FOLLOW-UP (Rybakina ankle/fitness thread — new No. 1 angle is new story)
**Angle:** RESULT/MILESTONE — Elena Rybakina (KAZ, No. 2 seed) defeated Zheng Qinwen 3-6, 6-1, 6-4 in Wednesday's quarterfinal, and in doing so secured the world No. 1 WTA ranking for the first time in her career. She is the first Kazakh player — man or woman — to reach No. 1 in the world. She will end Aryna Sabalenka's 99-week reign at the top when new rankings are released Monday. Rybakina faces Gauff in the women's semifinal Thursday.

**Angle:** RESULT — No. 3 seed Jessica Pegula (USA) defeated No. 26 Emma Navarro (USA) 3-6, 6-4, 6-3 in an all-American quarterfinal on Wednesday at Arthur Ashe Stadium.
