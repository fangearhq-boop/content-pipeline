### STORY 2: Rybakina World No. 1

**Long-Form Post:**
Elena Rybakina is the new world No. 1 — and she didn't just earn it with one match. She earned it at the US Open in front of the whole world.

By defeating Zheng Qinwen 3-6, 6-1, 6-4 in the quarterfinals on Wednesday, Rybakina secured enough points to surpass Aryna Sabalenka in the WTA rankings. When the new rankings are released Monday, Sabalenka's 99-week run at the top will officially end. Rybakina will take her place.

This is a historic moment. She becomes the first Kazakh player — in any sport, on the WTA or ATP Tours — to reach world No. 1. And she did it while still in the tournament, still with the possibility of winning her first US Open title.

Her semifinal opponent Thursday is Coco Gauff, the defending 2023 champion. Rybakina beat Gauff in this year's Australian Open final. This is a rematch with massive stakes: a US Open final spot, and the incoming world No. 1 against the player who dethroned Sabalenka at her own house.

What a week. What a tournament.
