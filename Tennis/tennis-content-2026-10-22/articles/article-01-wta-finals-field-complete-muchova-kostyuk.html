WTA Finals Field Is Set: Muchova Claims Last Spot After Kostyuk Retires in Ningbo

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | October 22, 2026

The 2026 WTA Finals field is complete. Marta Kostyuk's retirement from her second-round match at the BNB Ningbo Open on Wednesday — down 6-4 in the first set against Ekaterina Alexandrova — ended her race for the final qualifying spot and handed that berth to Karolina Muchova, who was not even competing this week.

It is a fitting, if anticlimactic, conclusion to a qualifying race that has defined much of the WTA calendar since the Shanghai swing. The drama played out not in a quarterfinal run or a match-deciding tiebreak, but in a retirement, a medical timeout, and the quiet confirmation that the last name on the Indian Wells list belongs to Muchova.

The Complete Field

Eight players will compete at the Indian Wells Tennis Garden from November 8 to 15. They are:

  1. Elena Rybakina
  2. Aryna Sabalenka
  3. Mirra Andreeva
  4. Jessica Pegula
  5. Coco Gauff
  6. Elina Svitolina
  7. Linda Noskova
  8. Karolina Muchova

Muchova's qualification comes on the back of consistent points accumulation across 2026. She entered this week holding the 7th position in the Race standings, but with Iga Swiatek having ended her season the previous week with a thigh injury, the competition for the final spot had narrowed to a race between Kostyuk (3,920 points) and Noskova (4,354 points). Kostyuk needed a deep run at Ningbo to overtake Noskova — a quarterfinal exit would not have been enough. The retirement sealed the result before it could even begin to develop.

Kostyuk's Campaign Falls Short

Kostyuk entered Ningbo as the 4th seed and with genuine momentum from a strong mid-season. She had climbed significantly in the Race rankings through the summer and had been one of the more consistent performers on the WTA tour across 2026. But the gap to Noskova — who had qualified as the 7th player in the Race and was no longer competing this week — was ultimately too large to close with a single tournament's results.

Her retirement against Alexandrova, with a medical timeout taken after the first set, suggests she was dealing with something beyond a tactical situation. The details of the injury were not immediately confirmed, but losing the first set 6-4 and then withdrawing indicates a physical issue rather than a decision to protect her position. There is no position left to protect when a Ningbo title is what you needed.

What's Next

The 2026 WTA Finals at Indian Wells Tennis Garden run from November 8 to 15. With the complete field now confirmed, the draw can begin to take shape. Rybakina enters as the top qualifier; Sabalenka and Andreeva round out the top three. The event will be the final major competition of the WTA season before the circuit closes out 2026.

For Kostyuk and Swiatek, the WTA Finals will be something to watch from the sidelines this year. Both are expected to return in 2027. Jovic, 11th in the Race, is listed as a possible alternate if an injury forces a withdrawal before the event begins.
