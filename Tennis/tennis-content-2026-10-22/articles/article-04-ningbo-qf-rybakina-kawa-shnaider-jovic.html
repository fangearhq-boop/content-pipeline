Ningbo QF Set: Rybakina vs. Kawa, Shnaider vs. Jovic as WTA Finals Race Resolves

By Ryan Calloway | Tennis Fanrecap | October 22, 2026

The BNB Ningbo Open quarterfinals are confirmed for Thursday, and the field that will compete in the final four days of the WTA 500 event takes shape against the backdrop of a race that is now resolved. With Marta Kostyuk's retirement from her second-round match on Wednesday, the WTA Finals qualifying story that has been attached to this tournament all week is over. What remains is a title hunt.

The Quarterfinal Matchups

Top seed Elena Rybakina advanced to the quarterfinals when Polina Kudermetova retired from their round-of-16 match while trailing 5-0 in the second set. Rybakina, the defending champion, has not been genuinely tested this week — which is either a concern about her match readiness or a reflection of her dominance at a tournament where she knows the conditions well. She will face Poland's Katarzyna Kawa in the quarterfinals.

Kawa is unseeded and was not expected to reach the last eight. Her presence in the quarterfinals is a result worth noting; facing Rybakina in this form, on this surface, at this tournament, is a difficult assignment.

The other confirmed quarterfinal has Diana Shnaider against Iva Jovic. Shnaider reached the last eight by defeating Alexandra Eala 7-5, 6-4 in a competitive round-of-16 match. Jovic, the American teenager, enters as one of the more interesting players still competing at Ningbo. Her ranking of 11th in the WTA Race, while no longer relevant to Finals qualifying, makes her a player to watch heading into the off-season.

Rybakina's Defense

Rybakina is the clear favorite to win the tournament. As the top seed and defending champion, she enters the quarterfinals in the best position of any player in the draw — both in terms of seeding and in terms of the physical demands the week has placed on her so far. A title at Ningbo would be a strong way to close out a season that has had its share of challenges.

The semifinal is Friday, and the final is on Saturday, October 25. The schedule gives players limited recovery time between rounds, which can be a factor for those who have played more difficult matches to reach this stage.

What's Next

Thursday's quarterfinals will determine the Ningbo semifinal lineup. Rybakina vs. Kawa and Shnaider vs. Jovic are the confirmed matchups. The BNB Ningbo Open wraps up Saturday with the final, closing out China's fall swing on the WTA calendar. After Ningbo, the WTA schedule begins to wind down toward the WTA Finals at Indian Wells on November 8.
