ATP Race to Turin: Vienna and Basel Open Sunday, Five Spots Still Contested

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | October 22, 2026

The ATP Race to Turin has three events remaining and five open spots. Vienna and Basel both open Sunday, October 26, beginning the final decisive stretch before the ATP Finals in Turin on November 15. The players who have already confirmed their places — Alexander Zverev, Carlos Alcaraz, and Ben Shelton — will be at Vienna and Basel looking to build seedings and momentum. Everyone else will be looking to exist in the top eight when the final qualifying deadline passes after Paris.

The Field and the Standings

Below the three confirmed qualifiers, the Race standings entering this week have Flavio Cobolli in 4th, with Frances Tiafoe, Arthur Fils, Daniil Medvedev, Rafael Jodar, Alex de Minaur, and Novak Djokovic all clustered in positions 5 through 11. The margins between these players are narrow, and a quarterfinal run at either Vienna or Basel — both ATP 500 events — can move a player up or down two or three positions in the standings.

Fils, who enters the week projected in the top eight, is one of the players with the most at stake. He has been consistent in the second half of the season but has not broken through to a title. A Vienna or Basel semifinal or final would significantly strengthen his position before Paris. Jodar, who reached the Shanghai final and has been climbing the rankings all season, enters the European indoor swing with real momentum.

Vienna and Basel Draws

The Erste Bank Open in Vienna opens Sunday with Zverev as the No. 1 seed. The draw also includes Alcaraz (wild card), Medvedev, Cobolli, de Minaur, Tiafoe, Tommy Paul, and Jakub Mensik among the seeds. Basel's Swiss Indoors opens simultaneously with Shelton as the top seed, joined by Félix Auger-Aliassime, Fils, Djokovic (wild card), Jodar, Taylor Fritz, Alejandro Davidovich Fokina, and Jiri Lehecka.

The overlapping schedules mean the Race standings will shift significantly across the same week — players split between the two events will be earning points simultaneously, and the picture heading into Paris will depend heavily on who advances deepest in both draws.

What's Next

Vienna and Basel run from October 26 to November 1. Paris Masters (Rolex Paris Masters, Plenitude Arena) follows immediately, opening October 31 and running through November 8 — the final ATP Masters 1000 of the season and the last major points opportunity before the Turin cutoff. The ATP Finals begin November 15.

For players on the bubble of the top eight, the next three weeks are the season. A title at any of these three events would almost certainly secure a Turin place. A first-round exit at all three would likely not.
