Almaty Open Quarterfinals Set: Tsitsipas Leads After Rublev Exits

By Marcus Cole | Tennis Fanrecap | October 22, 2026

The 2026 Almaty Open quarterfinals are set after a Wednesday of results that included one notable upset and a pair of workmanlike wins from players who have made their careers through longevity and adaptability. No. 7 seed Stefanos Tsitsipas, Australian veteran James Duckworth, French veteran Adrian Mannarino, and Georgian wild card Nikoloz Basilashvili are the four players standing as the draw heads into its final stages.

Mannarino Upsets Rublev

The most significant result of the day came from Mannarino, who defeated No. 2 seed Andrey Rublev 6-4, 3-6, 6-3 to reach the quarterfinals. Rublev had been one of the stronger threats in the draw and entered the match as a significant favorite. Mannarino's win was not a fluke — he won the decisive third set with authority, dropping only three games after leveling the match in the second.

It continues a pattern for Mannarino, who has been one of the more underestimated players on tour in the second half of the season. He is not a power hitter and he does not hold serve at the rate the top seeds do, but on indoor hard courts his ability to construct points and vary pace can neutralize more physically dominant opponents. Rublev's exit opens up that quarter of the draw considerably.

Duckworth and Basilashvili Through

James Duckworth beat Luciano Darderi 7-5, 7-6(7) to reach the quarterfinals — a win that required two tiebreak saves and a level of composure from the Australian that his lower ranking does not always reflect. Duckworth has made his way through tough draws in smaller ATP 250 events before, and Almaty suits his game.

Nikoloz Basilashvili handled Facundo Diaz Acosta more comfortably, winning 6-2, 6-4 to advance. The Georgian, competing on home-region turf in Almaty, was efficient and composed in a match that never became seriously complicated.

Tsitsipas: The Clear Favorite

Tsitsipas has been in sharp form all week. His second-round win over Kukushkin — a 6-1, 6-2 result that lasted just over an hour — was the most decisive performance of any player still in the draw. He enters the quarterfinals as the top remaining seed, with a draw that, on paper, should allow him to reach the final without facing another seeded opponent in the top four of the original draw.

What's Next

The Almaty Open quarterfinals are scheduled for Thursday. Semifinals follow Friday, with the final on Saturday, October 24. Tsitsipas is seeking his first ATP title of 2026; Mannarino, Duckworth, and Basilashvili are each looking for a result that would rank among the better performances of their recent seasons.
