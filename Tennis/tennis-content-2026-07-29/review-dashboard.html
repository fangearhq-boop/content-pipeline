Cruz Hewitt Wins First ATP Match in Washington — 22 Years After Lleyton's DC Title

Cruz Hewitt Wins First ATP Match in Washington — 22 Years After Lleyton's DC Title - **Tier:** 1 - **Angle:** NEW STORY — Cruz Hewitt (AUS, 17, World No. 612) defeated Marcos Giron 6-3, 6-4 in 74 minutes in the first round of the Mubadala Citi DC Open on Tuesday July 28. It was Cruz's first ATP Tour match win. He became the youngest player to win a match in Washington since Kei Nishikori in 2007. His father, former world No. 1 Lleyton Hewitt, won the Washington title in 2004 — exactly 22 years before Cruz's breakthrough. Lleyton was courtside for the match. Cruz recently made the Wimbledon junior singles final (lost to Jordan Lee, USA). He is ranked No. 612 in the world. Note: July 28 TFR pipeline incorrectly placed Cruz Hewitt in Los Cabos (Story 4); his ATP debut and first win occurred at the DC Open in Washington.
