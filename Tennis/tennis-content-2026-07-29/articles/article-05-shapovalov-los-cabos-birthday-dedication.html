Shapovalov Wins Los Cabos Opener for Fifth Straight Year, Dedicates Victory to Wife on Birthday

By Ryan Calloway | July 29, 2026

Los Cabos, Mexico

Denis Shapovalov has not lost at Los Cabos. On Wednesday, in his first-round match at the Mifel Tennis Open by Telcel Oppo, the defending champion defeated Rinky Hijikata 2-6, 6-3, 6-2 to improve his career record at this tournament to 5-0. The win was his second since mid-April, following an injury-disrupted stretch that included a retirement from Wimbledon. After the match, he dedicated the victory to his wife, Mirjam Björklund, whose birthday it was.

A Familiar Recovery

The first set was a rough one for the Canadian. Hijikata held his serve, pressured Shapovalov's return game, and ran out to a convincing 6-2 set. At that scoreline it looked as though the defending champion might have an early exit on his hands.

Then Shapovalov found his game. He won the second set 6-3 and was even more dominant in the third, taking it 6-2 as Hijikata's level dropped and the Canadian's movement improved. The final 2-6, 6-3, 6-2 scoreline is the tournament record of a player who knows how to find his best tennis on these specific courts.

The 5-0 record at Los Cabos is not a coincidence. The conditions — hard court, summer heat, altitude — suit Shapovalov's serve-and-forehand game. He won the title here for the first time in 2025 and has not lost a match here since the tournament began including him in its field.

The Birthday Dedication

Shapovalov dedicated the win to Mirjam Björklund, his wife, on her birthday. The dedication was reported by the ATP Tour's on-site press corps. Small moments like this carry a weight beyond the match itself — they are reminders of what professionals carry with them onto the court, the personal lives that exist alongside the professional competition.

Shapovalov has been open about the difficulty of his 2026 season. A rib fracture at Queen's Club, a retirement from Wimbledon, a winless stretch from mid-April until now — the Los Cabos result offers a reset. A dedicated win is a meaningful one.

What's Next

Shapovalov advances to the second round of Los Cabos, where he will face a more challenging opponent. The tournament runs through August 1 in Los Cabos, Mexico. Top seed Jiri Lehecka leads the field, with Luciano Darderi, Francisco Cerundolo, and Karen Khachanov in the upper seeds. Shapovalov, seeded eighth, has the draw history here that gives him a chance regardless of the bracket.

After Los Cabos, the Canadian Open in Montreal begins August 4 — where Shapovalov's ranking may require additional qualifying consideration. The US Open hard court swing is under way. For now, Los Cabos is delivering exactly what Shapovalov needed: a win, and a reason to carry the momentum forward.
