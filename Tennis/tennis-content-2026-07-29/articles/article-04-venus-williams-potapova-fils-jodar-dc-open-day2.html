Venus Williams Drops 12th Straight Match; Fils Exits Hurt as DC Open Day 2 Takes Its Toll

By Marcus Cole | July 29, 2026

Washington, D.C.

Tuesday at the Mubadala Citi DC Open produced two significant exits alongside the day's breakthrough wins. Anastasia Potapova defeated Venus Williams 6-3, 6-3 in straight sets, and No. 8 seed Arthur Fils lost to Rafael Jodar 7-6(5), 6-3 after stopping for physio treatment on a recurring back and hip injury. Both results carry meaning beyond the scoreline.

Williams Drops to 0-9 in 2026

Venus Williams is 45 years old and still competing, which carries its own weight as a sporting story. But the results in 2026 have been difficult to watch for anyone who has followed her career. With Tuesday's loss to Potapova, Williams stands at 0-9 for the year and has now lost 12 consecutive matches.

Potapova — ranked No. 27 — was efficient and controlled. The 6-3, 6-3 result did not require Potapova to reach her ceiling. Williams struggled to create points and could not execute under pressure. The match was over in under an hour.

Williams has said little publicly about her plans or timeline. She entered DC Open on a wildcard, received a standing ovation from the crowd, and was cheered as if the results were secondary. For the fans in Washington who grew up watching her, that sentiment is understandable. The won-loss record is another matter.

Fils Calls for Physio, Jodar Advances

Arthur Fils entered the DC Open as the No. 8 seed. He was beaten 7-6(5), 6-3 by Rafael Jodar — a first-set tiebreak that went to Jodar followed by a second set that saw Fils call the physio for the recurring back and hip issue that has been a problem since Rome. The same injury limited Fils to one tournament (Wimbledon) after his Rome exit, and it surfaced again on Tuesday.

Jodar took the match without drama once Fils's movement was compromised. The Spanish player advances to face Kei Nishikori in the Round of 16 — a matchup between two players who each knocked off higher-ranked opponents to get there.

The question for Fils now is the US Open swing. Cincinnati and New York are weeks away, and a recurring back and hip issue at the start of the summer hard court season is not a development any player wants. His camp has not commented on the severity or timeline.

The Rest of Day 2

Jakub Mensik, seeded seventh, came back from a set down to beat Trevor Svajda 6-2, 6-7(5), 6-3 — not a clean win but an effective one. Ben Shelton, the No. 2 seed, advanced. The top of the ATP bracket held on Tuesday after Monday's Tiafoe upset created some early disorder.

What's Next

Potapova advances in the WTA draw and will face a higher-seeded player in the Round of 16. Jodar faces Nishikori. Fils's condition will be monitored by the ATP medical staff. The DC Open continues through August 2, with Wednesday's matches — particularly Fernandez vs. Eala and de Minaur vs. Tsitsipas — now shaping the second week of the tournament.
