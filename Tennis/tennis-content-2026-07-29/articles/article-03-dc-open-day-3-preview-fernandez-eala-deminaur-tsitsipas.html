DC Open Day 3: Fernandez vs. Eala Headlines Wednesday; de Minaur Takes On Tsitsipas

By Elena Voss | July 29, 2026

Washington, D.C.

Wednesday at the Mubadala Citi DC Open features two headline clashes that arrived through very different paths — and both are better for the journey. Defending WTA champion Leylah Fernandez faces Alexandra Eala at 11:00 AM ET on Stadium Court, a matchup shaped by the results of the previous two days. In the men's draw, defending ATP champion Alex de Minaur opens his title defense against Stefanos Tsitsipas (wildcard) scheduled Not Before 1:00 PM ET.

If Tuesday proved that the young stars of this tournament were rising — Cruz Hewitt winning his first ATP match, Eala coming back from a set and a break to beat Zheng — then Wednesday is where those threads will be tested against the tournament's established order.

Fernandez vs. Eala

Leylah Fernandez is the defending DC Open champion. She won her first-round match on Monday — 6-1, 6-4 over Magda Linette — looking composed and unhurried. Fernandez knows what it takes to win this tournament and what the pressure of defending looks like.

Eala arrives from a different direction entirely: she came back from 4-6, 3-4 down against Qinwen Zheng on Tuesday, winning 12 of the next 13 games to close 6-4, 6-1. The momentum is entirely on her side. The confidence is high. She is ranked No. 29 in the world — her career best — and she just beat the Olympic champion on Stadium Court.

Fernandez is the better player on paper. Eala has the better story going into Wednesday. Between those two realities, this is the match of the tournament so far.

de Minaur vs. Tsitsipas

Alex de Minaur won the DC Open last year. He arrives as the No. 1 seed in the draw, and the defending champion's pressure applies regardless of seeding. His first opponent is Stefanos Tsitsipas on a wildcard — which means the former world No. 3 entered the draw without ranking points and without the expectations that come with seeding. The wildcards in this field have been generating results throughout the week.

Tsitsipas's recent form has been inconsistent, but he has the game to cause problems on hard courts at any time. De Minaur has the advantage of his serve and his movement, which are two of the most reliable weapons in the draw. The Not Before 1:00 PM slot puts this match in the afternoon heat, and Stadium Court at DC Open can play fast.

The Rest of Wednesday

Taylor Fritz (No. 3 seed) faces Kamil Majchrzak in the day session. Fritz was efficient in his first-round match on Monday and is one of the likelier deep-run candidates remaining in the ATP bracket. Rafael Jodar, who upset No. 8 seed Arthur Fils on Tuesday, faces Kei Nishikori — a veteran who beat Marton Fucsovics in three sets to open his week.

The evening session (Not Before 6:30 PM ET) features Ashlyn Krueger against No. 3 seed Naomi Osaka. Osaka entered the week with limited match play and has been building form across the summer. Krueger is a difficult first opponent for anyone.

What's Next

Wednesday shapes the DC Open's quarterfinal picture. The women's and men's brackets will be significantly clearer by end of day. Both Fernandez-Eala and de Minaur-Tsitsipas are matches that could go three sets, and neither outcome is certain. The DC Open continues through August 2.
