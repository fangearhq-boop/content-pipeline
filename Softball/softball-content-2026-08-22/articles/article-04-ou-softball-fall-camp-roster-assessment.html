Portal Closed: Oklahoma Softball's Fall Camp Roster Is Taking Shape

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | August 22, 2026

The summer transfer portal window has closed for Oklahoma softball, and Patty Gasso heads into fall camp with a rebuilt roster and a clear mission: get back to Oklahoma City. After the program's first Women's College World Series miss since 2015, the off-season work has been deliberate, targeted, and — based on the pieces assembled — genuinely promising.

Here is what the current roster looks like, and what fall camp needs to prove.

The Additions

Macie Harter is the headline transfer. The former Middle Tennessee State outfielder finished the 2026 season as her team's leader in batting average (.346), slugging percentage (.677), on-base percentage (.486), runs, hits, doubles, walks, and stolen bases. She was named Second Team All-Conference USA and has two years of eligibility remaining. At OU, she competes immediately for an everyday outfield spot alongside the incoming class.

Ori Mailo, the No. 4 overall recruit in the 2027 class and the No. 1 infielder, is already on campus for fall 2026. Her arrival gives Gasso a foundational piece at the infield — a position where the Sooners have traditionally built championship-caliber depth. Mailo's presence in fall camp as a true freshman gives her a head start on acclimating to the program's pace.

Payton Westra, rated No. 2 overall in the 2027 class, brings another high-ceiling option to the outfield. Her combination with Harter gives Gasso multiple paths in an outfield that had real question marks entering the off-season.

The Departures

Kasidi Pickering's departure to Texas Tech remains the biggest off-season story for this program. Pickering hit .384 in her career at OU and was one of the most productive hitters in recent Sooner history. She left during the portal window and is now a Red Raider. Tia Milloy also departed.

Those are real losses. Gasso is not pretending otherwise. But Pickering's departure also opened roster space that allowed OU to pursue Harter — a specific type of outfield addition the program needed.

What Fall Camp Needs to Answer

Fall camp is when roster construction on paper becomes roster construction in reality. The questions heading in: Where does Mailo lock in defensively at the infield? Does Harter slot immediately as an everyday starter? Can Westra develop quickly enough to push for significant minutes in her freshman year?

The broader question is whether this group — built through the portal and recruiting in the wake of a WCWS miss — has the on-field cohesion to compete in the Big 12 and make a deep postseason run. Patty Gasso has built that cohesion before. She built it after the 2015 miss. She built it through every roster transition since.

Eight WCWS titles at OU were not built on one off-season. Fall camp is the start of the next chapter.

What's Next

Fall camp begins for Oklahoma softball, with the roster now at its final form following the closure of the summer portal window. Ori Mailo is on campus; Payton Westra joins. Macie Harter brings immediate transfer production. The 2027 regular season is months away — but the work that determines how competitive this team can be starts in fall camp. Watch for position battle updates and scrimmage reports as the program builds toward the spring.
