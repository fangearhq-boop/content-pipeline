21 Days: USA Softball Opens Group Oklahoma Against Mexico on September 12

By Jordan Reeves | I Love Softball | August 22, 2026

Twenty-one days from now, Team USA Softball opens the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Oklahoma at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. The first opponent on September 12, Opening Day, is Mexico — ranked No. 7 in the world.

It is the right measuring stick. Mexico is not a team USA should take lightly, and the group stage results that matter most to USA's path forward are the ones that establish early dominance before the round-robin tightens up. How this team handles Opening Day will set the tone for everything that follows.

The Group Oklahoma Field

Six teams compete in the four-day round-robin at Devon Park from September 12–16. USA (No. 2 in the world) is the favorite, but the field is genuinely competitive. The Netherlands (No. 6) and China (No. 8) are both capable of winning games against any opponent, and Mexico (No. 7) has been a consistent international presence across multiple World Cup cycles.

Rounding out the group are New Zealand (No. 23) and South Africa (No. 36). Every team plays every other team in the round-robin format — 15 games total over four days.

The top two teams at the end of the round-robin advance to the 2027 Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland — held April 5–11, 2027. That Finals is the direct LA28 Olympic qualifier. The third and fourth place teams play off for the second Finals berth. The bottom two teams are eliminated from the qualification path.

For USA, advancing in the top two is the expectation. But expectations have to be earned on the field, and they start with Mexico on September 12.

The Oklahoma Connection

Four players from the University of Oklahoma are on USA's 16-player roster for Group Oklahoma: Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell. Playing in Oklahoma City means playing in front of a home crowd that knows them — and for a state that has long embraced softball at every level, the atmosphere at Devon Park will be unlike anything else in the World Cup schedule.

The 16-player roster was finalized and announced officially, giving the coaching staff time to build rotations and game-plan specifically for each opponent in the group. Mexico's lineup and pitching staff are well-scouted at this level of international competition — and USA's preparation over the final 21 days will reflect that.

What a Strong Opening Day Means

In a four-day round-robin, the tone of the tournament can shift quickly based on early results. A dominant Opening Day win against Mexico — not just a win, but a statement — would send a signal to the Netherlands and China that the No. 2 team in the world is operating at the level its ranking suggests.

For a team competing in its own backyard, with four OU players in the lineup and an Oklahoma City crowd behind them, the energy on September 12 is going to be real. The question is whether USA converts that energy into the kind of performance that makes the rest of Group Oklahoma go according to plan.

Twenty-one days. The path to LA28 starts in OKC.

What's Next

The WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Oklahoma opens September 12 at Devon Park, Oklahoma City. USA's first game is against No. 7 Mexico on Opening Day. The four-day round-robin runs September 12–16. The top two teams advance to the 2027 Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland (April 5–11, 2027), which serves as the LA28 Olympic qualifier. For fans in Oklahoma, this is must-attend softball.
