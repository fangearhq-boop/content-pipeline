#### Long-Form Post
**Window:** 3:00 PM ET

The stakes in Group Oklahoma are straightforward: six teams compete in a four-day round-robin format (September 12–16). The top two advance to the 2027 Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland — the direct LA28 Olympic qualifier. Third and fourth place play off for the second Finals spot.

USA's four OU players on the roster — Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell — bring additional home-crowd energy to a venue where they know the atmosphere well.

21 days. The LA28 path starts in OKC on September 12.

### STORY 5: Texas Softball — Isa Torres

#### Long-Form Post
**Window:** 8:00 PM ET

Texas already won back-to-back national championships. Then they went out and added the 2026 D1Softball National Player of the Year.

Isa Torres spent four years at Florida State building one of the most decorated offensive careers in recent collegiate softball history. In 2026, she was named the D1Softball National Player of the Year — the sport's most individual recognition — before entering the transfer portal. She chose Texas.

The Longhorns' 2027 pitching staff is already anchored by Teagan Kavan, who made history as the first 2x WCWS Most Outstanding Player. Their new additions this off-season include catcher Samantha Swan (Arizona State) and lefty Samantha Lincoln (Texas Tech). Now Torres gives them an offensive centerpiece who is arguably the best hitter available in the transfer portal this cycle.

Only two programs in softball history have won three straight WCWS titles: UCLA (1988–90) and Oklahoma (2021–23). Texas is going for it.

Adding Isa Torres didn't just make them better. It made them more dangerous than they were when they won the last two titles. That is a remarkable thing to be able to say.
