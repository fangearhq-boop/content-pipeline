Four Days Away: The 2028 Contact Period Opens October 17 and the Recruiting Rush Is Coming

By Jordan Reeves | October 13, 2026 | I Love Softball

If you follow college softball recruiting closely, circle October 17 on your calendar. That is when the contact period for the Class of 2028 opens — the first window where coaches can initiate direct, official contact with prospective recruits. And based on how aggressively programs have already been building this class, the next two weeks will be busy.

The contact windows run October 17-18 and October 24-25. That is four days of official access in eight days. Expect a wave of commitment announcements.

Who Is Already Ahead

Oklahoma has been the most aggressive program in the 2028 class. The Sooners entered this week with five commits already, including outfielder Grace Tonga, who committed on September 22 as Oklahoma's fifth pledge from the class. With Love's Field selling out fall exhibitions and a roster featuring Kendall Wells, Jayda Kavan, and Alex Berzon, the pitch to recruits practically writes itself: come to Norman and compete for a national championship every year.

Florida locked in two high-profile pieces early. No. 2 overall 2028 prospect Kylie Holden — a five-star middle infielder — committed to the Gators earlier this fall. Catcher Brooklyn Evans followed. Tim Walton has built a program that returns six of nine everyday starters for 2027, meaning recruits know exactly what they are walking into: a roster with proven experience AND available roster spots.

Alabama, Texas, UCLA, and LSU are expected to be active in the contact window as well. Each program has a different pitch based on conference placement, portal activity, and current roster construction.

Why the Contact Period Matters

Before the contact period opens, coaches can communicate with recruits through questionnaires and electronic communication — but they cannot have official phone calls or in-person conversations that go beyond chance encounters. The contact window changes that completely. Coaches can call. Recruits can take official visits. The relationship moves from digital to direct.

For programs that have been tracking a recruit since her freshman year of high school, the contact period is the moment the recruiting pitch becomes a real conversation. Some commitments that have been quietly brewing for months will go public within days of October 17. Others will happen within hours.

The college softball recruiting landscape for the Class of 2028 has already been shaped by early-period activity, transfer portal commitments trickling into the high school class, and a contact window that has elite programs fully prepared. The 137-plus portal entries tracked this cycle show just how much roster movement is happening at every level. For 2028 high school recruits, the message from programs is clear: commit now before the class fills.

What's Next

The October 17-18 contact window opens four days from now. Watch the Extra Inning Softball recruiting feed, the On3 transfer portal tracker, and program-specific social media accounts for commitment announcements in real time. By October 21, the 2028 class landscape will look noticeably different than it does today.

Oklahoma is ahead. Florida is ahead. The rest of the sport has four days to finalize their pitches before the race accelerates.
