OU Battle Series Game 3: What to Watch Tuesday at Love's Field

By Emily Rawlings | October 13, 2026 | I Love Softball

The wait is almost over. Oklahoma softball's Battle Series returns to Love's Field on Tuesday night, and this time, the anticipation is even higher.

Game 3 of the 2026 fall Battle Series tips off at 6:30 PM CT on October 14. Tickets are $10.88 for general admission, and the game will stream live on 1Oklahoma.com for fans who cannot make it to Norman in person.

After the walk-off opener on September 30 — Kayla Agbayani's two-RBI double sealed a dramatic win in front of a sold-out crowd — Patty Gasso's team has had two weeks to refine, adjust, and sharpen. Tuesday is the next public look at a roster that has Sooner Nation buzzing in October like it is already March.

The Stars to Watch

Kendall Wells enters her second season as the most dangerous bat in college softball. Wells finished 2026 with 39 home runs — breaking the NFCA Freshman of the Year mark and setting a new standard for what first-year power can look like in the sport. She slugged alongside the best in the Big 12 last spring. Now she does it in the SEC.

Jayda Kavan won the Women's College World Series Most Outstanding Player award twice. She is, by any measure, one of the most decorated players to ever wear an Oklahoma uniform — and she arrived at Norman this fall with something still left to prove. The way Kavan and Wells have meshed in the early fall practices has coaches talking.

Jordan Cruz and Rylee Ludlow bring veteran experience to a lineup that already skews elite. Cruz is a prototypical No. 3 hitter — smart, disciplined, dangerous in the middle of the order. Ludlow provides the kind of contact-and-speed combination that makes lineup construction simple.

On the mound, Alex Berzon remains the standard. The 2026 season saw Berzon finish with a 2.02 ERA, 52 wins, and 416 strikeouts. She is the rare pitcher who can carry a program's postseason ambitions on her own, and watching her command in fall workouts is a reminder of just how complete she has become.

Why This Game Matters

Fall ball is often dismissed as exhibition activity — warm-up games that count for nothing in the final standings. But the Battle Series has become something different at Oklahoma. When Love's Field sells out in October, that is not an accident. It reflects the trust Gasso has built with a fan base that understands what this program is building.

Game 3 is the third of at least five fall exhibition opportunities. Each one is a chance for the coaching staff to evaluate the new faces, test lineup configurations, and give prospects a taste of the pressure that will define March through June. For recruits watching from the stands, it is also a real-time advertisement for what the 2027 season could look like.

Gasso was visibly moved after the opener's sold-out crowd. She has said publicly that the fall turnout sends a message — not just to players, but to the rest of the country — about where Oklahoma softball stands.

What's Next

Battle Series Game 4 is scheduled for October 21, also at Love's Field. After the fall schedule closes out, the 2027 regular season begins in February with a full SEC slate that will test this roster against the deepest competition in the sport. The question of whether Oklahoma can win a national championship with Wells and Kavan sharing a lineup is the biggest storyline in college softball heading into next year.

Tuesday night at 6:30 PM CT, it continues to take shape.
