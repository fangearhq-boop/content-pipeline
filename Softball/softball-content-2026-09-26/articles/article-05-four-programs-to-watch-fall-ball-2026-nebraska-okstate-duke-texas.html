4 Programs to Watch This Fall — Nebraska, Oklahoma State, Duke, and Texas Have Stories Worth Following

By Emily Rawlings | September 26, 2026

You already know about Texas. You've heard about Oklahoma's rebuild, Tennessee's returning pitching tandem, and Alabama's 13 returners.

But fall ball in college softball is bigger than the perennial powers. Programs at every level are using September and October to evaluate new rosters, integrate transfers, and give freshmen their first competitive reps. Sports Illustrated highlighted four programs — Nebraska, Oklahoma State, Duke, and Texas — as the fall 2026 storylines worth following beyond the usual headlines. Here's why each one matters.

Nebraska

The Huskers have been one of the most interesting programs to follow in college softball for the past several seasons — a program with consistent Big Ten success that has steadily built toward a moment of national relevance. Nebraska's fall ball slate is an opportunity to see how their recruiting and portal additions integrate with a returning core that has tournament experience.

For a program that has made the NCAA Tournament regularly but has yet to make a deep WCWS run, fall ball is the first look at whether 2027 is the year that changes. The talent is there. The question is execution at the highest level.

Oklahoma State

The Cowgirls have been a consistent contender in the Big 12 — which is saying something in a conference that features Texas and Oklahoma at their peaks. Oklahoma State's fall ball story centers on how their pipeline continues to produce and whether new additions can give the coaching staff the depth it needs to compete when the bracket opens in May.

Fall games against quality competition will give Cowgirls fans their first look at the 2027 roster taking shape. In a conference that will once again feature multiple top-ten programs at the start of the season, fall ball preparation matters.

Duke

The Blue Devils have been one of the ACC's most underrated programs for several years — a team that consistently performs well in conference play but hasn't quite broken through to the national conversation in the way their talent might suggest. That may be changing.

Duke's 2026 fall roster features pieces that could push the program further into the spotlight than it's been in recent memory. ACC softball has grown significantly more competitive in recent seasons, and Duke's fall preparation will be a first look at whether they have the pieces to make a statement in that environment.

Texas (Again — For a Different Reason)

Yes, Texas is the headline program in college softball right now. But Sports Illustrated's spotlight on the Longhorns this fall isn't about their returning core — it's specifically about how they're managing the transfer portal alongside their existing championship infrastructure.

The Torres and Lincoln additions represent Mike White's philosophy in action: be selective, be strategic, and add portal pieces that complement rather than replace what you've built. Watching how those pieces integrate with the existing chemistry of a back-to-back championship program is its own storyline — and one that will shape every program's portal strategy for years to come. If Texas pulls off the three-peat, the question of how they built this roster will be studied.

What's Next

Fall ball 2026 is just getting started. Nebraska, Oklahoma State, Duke, and Texas are all in the early stages of their fall schedules — games that don't count in the standings but absolutely count for program-building. Watch closely. The 2027 regular season is still months away, but the stories are already forming. The teams that use fall well tend to be the ones still playing in May.
