Summer Window Closing: What Patty Gasso's OU Softball Rebuild Looks Like Right Now

By Emily Rawlings | I Love Softball | August 21, 2026

The summer transfer portal window is nearly closed, and Patty Gasso's Oklahoma softball rebuild is taking shape. After a first WCWS miss since 2015, the Sooners entered the offseason with clear priorities: reload the roster, add talent that can compete immediately, and set the foundation for a 2027 program that looks like OU again.

The work done so far suggests that foundation is forming — piece by piece, with genuine talent at key positions.

The Latest Addition: Macie Harter

The most recent portal move is Macie Harter, a transfer from Middle Tennessee State who hit .346 with 7 home runs and 23 RBI in her final season with the Blue Raiders, earning Second Team All-Conference USA honors. She brings two years of eligibility remaining, which means her OU impact could span the next two seasons.

Harter is expected to compete for outfield playing time, potentially alongside Payton Westra — the No. 2 overall recruit in the Class of 2027 who committed to Oklahoma. That kind of competition in fall camp is exactly what a rebuilding program needs: talented players pushing each other for starting spots, rather than a talent-thin roster with no internal pressure.

The 2027 Recruiting Class: Blue-Chip Foundation

Even amid the transfer activity and roster losses, Gasso has kept the recruiting pipeline flowing. The 2027 class features two of the highest-ranked prospects in the country.

Payton Westra, ranked No. 2 overall, is an outfielder from Mesa, Arizona who brings the kind of athleticism and bat that top programs build around. Ori Mailo — ranked No. 4 overall and No. 1 at her position among infielders — is already on campus. The Waianae, Hawaii native enrolled early in 2026 and redshirted, giving her a full fall camp with the program before contributing in 2027.

Both prospects represent exactly the kind of foundational pieces a rebuild requires: high-floor, high-ceiling players who were recruited despite the program's 2026 struggles. That tells you something about the faith in what Gasso is building.

The Losses and What They Mean

The departures have been significant. Kasidi Pickering, a two-time All-American who hit .384 across three seasons at Oklahoma, transferred to Texas Tech — a significant blow. Tia Milloy's departure added to the offseason attrition.

These are not easy losses to absorb. Pickering was a cornerstone of OU's offensive production, and her presence is now strengthening a Texas Tech program that was already formidable. That is the transfer portal working at its most acute: a rebuilt rival getting better at the same time as a program trying to bounce back.

But Oklahoma has been here before. Gasso has rebuilt this program multiple times over her career, and the combination of elite recruiting and targeted portal adds is the same formula that has produced championships.

What's Next

The summer portal window is closing. With Harter added, Westra committed, and Mailo already on campus, Oklahoma's 2027 roster is largely set. Fall camp begins the next chapter — where Gasso evaluates what she has, where the competition falls out, and whether there are any final adjustments to make before the season begins. For a program rebuilding with urgency, every fall practice rep matters.
