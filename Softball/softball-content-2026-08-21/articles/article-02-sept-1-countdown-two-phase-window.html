11 Days to September 1: The Two-Phase Contact Window Class of 2028 Needs to Know

By Jordan Reeves | I Love Softball | August 21, 2026

With 11 days until September 1, most Class of 2028 families are tracking one date — and missing the second one. September 1 is not the only deadline that matters for Division I softball recruiting. There is a two-phase window that opens in the next three weeks, and understanding both phases before they hit could be the difference between a calm, confident recruiting process and a chaotic scramble.

Phase 1: September 1 — Communication Opens

September 1, 2026 is when Division I coaches can contact Class of 2028 recruits directly for the first time. That means calls, texts, and emails. Until September 1, coaches have been limited to sending pre-contact materials — printed or electronic recruiting information — but no direct communication was permitted.

On September 1, all of that changes at once. Coaches who have been watching film and building their call lists for months will be reaching out simultaneously. For recruits at the top of those lists — and with a record 1,226 nominations submitted through Extra Inning Softball's Elite 100 process for the Class of 2028 — the volume of contact on and shortly after September 1 can be overwhelming for families who are not prepared.

The recruits who handle September 1 well are the ones who went into it with a clear sense of their school priorities. They know which programs excite them, which coaches they want to hear from, and what questions they plan to ask during those first conversations. They are not making decisions in the moment — they are gathering information according to a plan they built before the window opened.

Phase 2: September 9 at 7:00 AM ET — In-Person Contact Opens

Eight days after September 1, on September 9, 2026 at 7:00 AM Eastern Time, the in-person contact window opens. This is when Division I coaches can visit recruits at their homes, schools, and competitions. It is also when recruits can take official visits to campus for the first time.

The September 9 window catches many families off guard because the focus on September 1 is so intense. But official visits — the most consequential step in the recruiting process for many recruits — are scheduled starting on September 9. Programs with large pipelines will begin filling their fall official visit calendar almost immediately. Recruits who are not thinking about September 9 logistics ahead of time may find that the fall weekends they wanted are already spoken for by the time they start asking.

Preparation for September 9 means having your fall schedule accessible and knowing which Saturdays or weekends could work for a campus visit. When a coach calls on September 1 and asks whether you are available to visit in October, the recruits who say "let me check my schedule and get back to you" are already behind the recruits who can answer yes on the spot.

What to Do in the Next 11 Days

The families who enter September 1 and September 9 with confidence are the ones who do the preparation now. That means ranking your school list clearly. Identifying your top programs and your reasons why. Talking through visit logistics with your family and your club coach. Keeping your fall academic schedule visible so scheduling conversations do not catch you unprepared.

The 1,226 players in the Class of 2028 who received Elite 100 nominations this year — a record — are all navigating this same process. Most of them will receive contact from multiple Division I programs in the first week of September. The recruits who commit to programs they genuinely love are overwhelmingly the ones who entered the window knowing what they wanted, not the ones who figured it out in the moment.

What's Next

September 1 opens D1 communication. September 9 at 7:00 AM ET opens in-person contact and official visits. Between now and then, Class of 2028 recruits have 11 days to finalize their school rankings and visit availability. The window comes fast. The families who prepared are the ones who come out of September with clarity.
