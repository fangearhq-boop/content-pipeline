22 Days to OKC: USA Softball's Path to Brisbane Runs Through Group Oklahoma

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | August 21, 2026

In 22 days, Team USA takes the field at Devon Park in Oklahoma City for the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup — Group Oklahoma. The stakes could not be higher: the top two teams from the six-nation field advance to the 2027 Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia, where the official LA28 Olympic berths are on the line.

This is the most important tournament USA Softball will play between now and the Los Angeles Olympics — and it is happening at the same venue where the national team won gold at the International Cup earlier this summer.

Understanding Group Oklahoma: Six Teams, Two Berths

The Group Oklahoma event is a round-robin group stage format. Six nations compete: USA, Netherlands, Mexico, China, Brazil, and New Zealand. All six teams will play each other across the September 12–16 window at Devon Park.

At the conclusion of round-robin play, the top two finishers advance directly to the 2027 Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland — approximately 45 minutes north of Brisbane — scheduled for April 5–11, 2027. The Finals is where LA28 Olympic qualification is formally awarded.

The format leaves almost no margin for error. A slip-up in group play against an opponent ranked outside the top five in the world could cost a team its Finals berth. For USA, ranked No. 2 in the world, the expectation is advancement — but the group itself is not without teeth.

The Opponents: Not a Soft Draw

USA's five group opponents represent a genuine test. The Netherlands enter the tournament ranked No. 6 in the world — a program with European championship pedigree and a roster capable of making games uncomfortable. Mexico at No. 7 is a consistent threat in WBSC competition, and China at No. 8 has the pitching depth to keep any game close.

Brazil, returning to the Women's Softball World Cup after replacing South Africa in the group, adds an additional variable. New Zealand rounds out the six-team field.

None of these opponents are automatic wins at the international level. For a team like USA that is expected to advance comfortably, the real challenge is about consistency — executing through a five-game stretch without a lapse that could send shockwaves through the standings.

USA's Roster: Experience and OU Depth

The 16-player roster announced for Group Oklahoma features a blend of Olympic veterans and current collegiate stars. Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola bring direct experience from the 2020 Tokyo Olympics, providing leadership in critical moments.

Oklahoma leads all programs with four players on the roster: Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell. The Sooners' pipeline to Team USA remains among the strongest in the country even as the program rebuilds its collegiate roster following this summer's transfer activity.

USA enters Group Oklahoma ranked No. 2 in the world — behind Japan — and with strong confidence built from the International Cup gold medal performance at this same Devon Park venue earlier this year.

What's Next

Group Oklahoma runs September 12–16, 2026 at Devon Park, Oklahoma City. The top two nations advance to the 2027 Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia (April 5–11), where LA28 Olympic berths are awarded. USA is 22 days away from opening play. The road to Los Angeles 2028 starts in Oklahoma City.
