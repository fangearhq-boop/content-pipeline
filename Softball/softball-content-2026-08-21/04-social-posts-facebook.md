### STORY 2: September 1 Countdown — 11 Days, Two-Phase Window

**Long-Form Post (11:00 AM ET)**
11 days until September 1 — but here's what a lot of Class of 2028 families don't know: September 1 is actually just Phase 1.

Phase 1 — September 1:
D1 coaches can now call, text, and email recruits directly for the first time. The phones start ringing. Group chats light up. Coaches who have been watching film for months finally get to have a real conversation.

Phase 2 — September 9, 2026 at 7:00 AM ET:
The in-person contact window opens. This is when coaches can visit recruits at their homes, schools, or events. And recruits can take official visits to campuses for the first time.

If your family is preparing for September 1 but hasn't mapped out the September 9 timeline, you may be behind on scheduling. Official visit weekends fill fast — especially at top programs. Schools start reaching out about fall unofficial visits the moment coaches can contact you.

Recommendation for the next 11 days: Know your schools. Know your answer to "Can we schedule a visit?" Have your fall schedule accessible. Don't let the second wave catch you unprepared.

The 1,226 players who received EIS nominations this year — a record for the class — are all going through the same process. Preparation is what separates recruits who commit on their own terms from those who feel rushed.

11 days. Get ready for both phases. 🥎

### STORY 3: USA WNT — 22 Days, Group Oklahoma

**Long-Form Post (3:00 PM ET)**
22 days until Team USA opens the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup — Group Oklahoma — at Devon Park in Oklahoma City.

This is the event where USA punched its LA28 Olympic ticket. Or at least where they'll try.

Here's how it works: six teams play round-robin in Oklahoma City (Sept 12–16). The top two advance to the 2027 Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia — just outside Brisbane — on April 5–11, 2027. The Finals is where the official LA28 Olympic berths are awarded.

USA's group opponents: Netherlands (No. 6 in world), Mexico (No. 7), China (No. 8), Brazil, and New Zealand.

This is not a soft draw. The Netherlands and China have upset potential. A slip in group play is still possible.

But USA enters ranked No. 2 in the world, with a 16-player roster that includes four Oklahoma Sooners — Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell. Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola are Olympic veterans anchoring the squad.

The road to LA28 goes through Devon Park this September. 22 days. ⏳ 🇺🇸

### STORY 5: Texas Three-Peat History

**Long-Form Post (8:00 PM ET)**
Here's what makes a Texas three-peat in 2027 so rare — and so compelling.

Only two programs in the history of college softball have won three straight Women's College World Series titles:

UCLA — 1988, 1989, 1990.
Oklahoma — 2021, 2022, 2023.

That's it. In the entire modern era of D1 softball, only two programs have pulled it off. The competitive nature of the sport — the portal, the transfer rules, the talent distribution — makes sustaining dominance across three consecutive seasons extraordinarily difficult.

Texas won in 2025. Texas won again in 2026, going 49-12. Teagan Kavan became the first player in WCWS history to win the Most Outstanding Player award twice.

Now fall camp is underway. The Longhorns have 24 players on roster with 11 new additions, including a former Oklahoma pitcher via the transfer portal. Kavan is back as a senior.

If Texas wins in 2027, they would be the third program in history to accomplish the three-peat — joining UCLA and Oklahoma in a category that barely exists.

Are the Longhorns capable? Fall camp is where you find out. 🤘
