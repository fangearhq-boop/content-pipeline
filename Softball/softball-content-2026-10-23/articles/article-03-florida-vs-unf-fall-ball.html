Florida Hosts UNF Today — The Gators' Fall Ball Case for 2027 Title Contention

By Sarah Mitchell | October 23, 2026

Florida has scored 49 runs in two fall games. Today they play again.

The Gators host UNF this afternoon at Katie Seashole Pressly Stadium in Gainesville. Admission is free and the game is open to the public. It is the third fall appearance for a Florida program that has used this autumn to establish itself as one of the clear favorites for the 2027 Women's College World Series.

The numbers from fall ball have been hard to ignore. A 20-0 victory over Santa Fe on October 16. A 29-1 dismantling of Carson-Newman on October 17. Those were not just comfortable wins — they were demonstrations of a lineup with depth, power, and purpose.

Sophia Edwards Has Made a Statement

Three home runs in two games. That is Sophia Edwards' fall ball line, and it is the kind of production that catches the attention of everyone tracking the 2027 landscape.

Florida's roster already boasted six starters returning from a program that has been among the elite programs in college softball for years. Head coach Tim Walton's teams are built around pitching and defense, but the offensive depth this fall has been a genuine revelation. Edwards' emergence as a power threat adds another dimension to a lineup that already had plenty of weapons.

The full pitching staff also returns for 2027, which gives Florida the combination that tends to define championship teams: a lineup that can score, and a rotation that can shut down opponents. When both sides of that equation are operating at a high level, deep postseason runs become the expectation rather than the aspiration.

What Today's Game Against UNF Means

UNF has faced Florida in prior fall ball settings, including a 16-1 loss in 2024 during a Jacksonville visit. Today's game at Katie Seashole Pressly Stadium is a different venue — the Gators are at home — and a different team. The UNF program provides a quality opponent for a fall game, and Florida's coaching staff will use the opportunity to evaluate depth pieces and continue building chemistry heading into 2027.

For fans who want to watch this Gators team before February, today is a free chance. The stadium is one of the best college softball facilities in the country, the admission is free, and the team on the field is one of the top 2027 title contenders. That combination does not come around every day.

Kylie Holden — rated No. 2 overall in the 2028 class by Softball America — is already committed to Florida, giving the Gators program a pipeline of talent that extends well beyond the current roster. The 2027 team is built to compete now. The 2028 class ensures it stays that way.
