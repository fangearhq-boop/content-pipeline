2028 Extra Elite 100 Top 5 Drops Monday — Contact Window Opens Tomorrow

By Emily Rawlings | October 23, 2026

The most-watched moment in 2028 college softball recruiting is 96 hours away.

Extra Inning Softball's 2028 Extra Elite 100 — the ranking that defines the class, shapes the recruiting landscape, and drives the conversation among coaches and fans alike — releases its Top 5 this Monday, October 27. After weeks of reveals working down from No. 100, the countdown has reached its final chapter.

More than 1,200 players were nominated for this cycle, making the Class of 2028 the largest ever evaluated by Extra Inning Softball. The breadth of the field reflects both the growth of the sport and the sophistication of the programs that have spent the past year identifying and evaluating this group. Now it comes down to five.

What We Know About the 2028 Top 5 Picture

The current landscape entering Monday's reveal has a few names consistently appearing at the top of national discussions.

Michaela Landers, a Texas commit, is the consensus top infielder in the 2028 class across multiple services. She brings elite bat-to-ball skills and plus-plus power projection that put her at or near the top of every evaluator's list. Her commitment to Texas adds significant depth to a Longhorns program that has continued to recruit at the highest level.

Kylie Holden — already committed to Florida — is rated No. 2 overall by Softball America. The five-star infielder from Michigan represents exactly the type of multi-dimensional player that programs build around. With Holden already locked in, the Gators' 2028 class has a foundation that most programs would envy.

Oklahoma commit Schlader sits at No. 3 in current rankings, giving the Sooners another top-tier prospect to complement what is already a deep incoming class. The pattern of OU, Florida, and Texas occupying the top spots in the 2028 class mirrors what those programs have done in recent recruiting cycles — and suggests that the 2027 national title race will be followed immediately by a 2028 class fight that is just as competitive.

Contact Window Opens Tomorrow

Before Monday's reveal, a critical calendar event takes place: the NCAA contact window opens tomorrow, October 24, and runs through October 25. This two-day window allows coaches to make direct contact with 2028 prospects — and it will almost certainly trigger a wave of commitment announcements before, during, and after the Top 5 drops.

Programs that have been watching prospects all fall have their targets identified. The contact window is the moment those conversations move from observation to direct recruitment. Expect a busy 72 hours in softball recruiting, starting tomorrow morning and running through the Top 5 reveal on Monday.

Tennessee, Texas Tech, UCLA, and Arizona have all been active in the 2028 class as well. The full landscape of commitments that emerges over the next week will give a clearer picture of which programs are building toward 2028 the same way they have dominated recent recruiting cycles.

What's Next

The 2028 Extra Elite 100 Top 5 releases Monday, October 27, on Extra Inning Softball's website. The contact window runs October 24-25 — expect commitment announcements throughout. After Monday's reveal, the complete 2028 class picture will be clearer, and the next major recruiting calendar event will be the November signing period for the Class of 2027. The pipeline is always moving in college softball, and this week is one of its most active moments of the year.
