Oklahoma Hosts USAO Tonight at Love's Field — Here's What to Watch

By Emily Rawlings | October 23, 2026

Four Battle Series games. Months of intrasquad work. All of it has been building toward tonight.

Oklahoma hosts the University of Science and Arts of Oklahoma on Thursday night at Love's Field in Norman. First pitch: 6:30 PM CT. Stream free on 1oklahoma.com. It is the Sooners' first fall matchup against an external opponent — a chance to measure everything the 2027 squad has been developing since September against a team wearing a different color.

For fans of college softball, tonight is a must-watch event. This group of Sooners does not require a reason to watch, but having an opponent on the other side of the field adds a layer of clarity that intrasquad formats cannot replicate.

The Players to Watch

Kendall Wells does not need an introduction at this point. After 39 home runs and NFCA Freshman of the Year honors during the 2026 spring season, she has continued to put on a show in fall ball — including another home run in Game 4 of the Battle Series just two days ago. At Love's Field on a fall evening against a live opponent, Wells is exactly the type of player who elevates the moment.

Haley Kavan's return for a graduate season is one of the biggest stories in college softball. A two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player, Kavan has been the standard-bearer for Oklahoma pitching across back-to-back championship runs. Her presence in the circle tonight — and through all of 2027 — is a competitive advantage that no recruiting class can simply manufacture.

Jocelyn Berzon arrived in Norman via transfer from LSU, where she compiled a 52-25 record across 486.1 innings over three seasons. Her sub-2.00 ERA gives head coach Patty Gasso a second elite option in the circle. Tonight is another chance for Berzon to build chemistry with the Oklahoma defense and continue her development in the Sooners' system.

What Tonight Means for the Fall Schedule

Oklahoma's 2026 fall schedule has eight total games. After tonight's contest against USAO, only one game remains on the calendar — October 30 against Seminole State, which closes out the Battle Series. That means tonight is the second-to-last public look at this version of the Sooners before the offseason, and the last time they face an external opponent until February.

USAO is an NAIA program based in Chickasha, Oklahoma. While the talent gap between a Division I powerhouse and an NAIA school is significant, that does not diminish what tonight offers: real competition, real game situations, and a chance for Gasso and her staff to evaluate performance in a non-intrasquad context.

The Battle Series format has done its job this fall. It has put Wells in front of a crowd that set a fall attendance record. It has given Berzon reps in a new program. It has let Kavan ease into a graduate season on her own terms. Tonight, those pieces come together for the first time against a real opponent.

What's Next

Tonight's game streams live and free on 1oklahoma.com — first pitch at 6:30 PM CT. After that, one game remains on the fall schedule: October 30 vs Seminole State at Love's Field. Then the offseason begins in earnest. The next public look at Oklahoma softball after that will be February 2027, when the Sooners open their season and the national title chase begins for real.
