### STORY 1: Jadelyn Allchin — AUSL Championship Series MVP

**Confirmed Facts (HIGH confidence):**
- Utah Talons swept Chicago Bandits 2-0 to win 2026 AUSL Championship
- Game 1 (July 25): Utah 2, Chicago 1 — Georgina Corrick CG
- Game 2 (July 26): Utah 6, Chicago 1 — Montana Fouts CG (7 IP, 8H, 1R, 4K)
- Jadelyn Allchin named Championship Series MVP

- Allchin quote: "The whole series, I think we woke up and we were just ready to go... the big thing that I thought today was like heart. I think we brought a lot of heart to the past two games. Knowing that showing up with heart for ourselves, for our teammates, like showing up for the game in general, is what I think got us through it all."

- Utah Talons = first back-to-back AUSL champions in league history
