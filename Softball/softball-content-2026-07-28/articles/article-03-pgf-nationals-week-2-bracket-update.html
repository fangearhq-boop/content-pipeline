PGF Nationals Week 2: Sweet 16 Set for 14U, Elite 8 for 12U and 10U After Day 2 of Bracket Play

By Jordan Reeves

July 28, 2026

The field is thinning at PGF Nationals Week 2, and the best teams are rising to the top.

After two days of bracket play at the nation's most prestigious travel softball tournament in Orange County, California, the 14U Winner's Bracket has narrowed to its Sweet 16, while both the 12U and 10U Winner's Brackets are now at the Elite 8 stage. The tournament is accelerating toward its championship weekend, with all title games scheduled at Deanna Manning Stadium at Bill Barber Park in Irvine.

Week 2 — which features the 14U, 12U, and 10U age divisions — runs through August 2.

How the Bracket Reached This Point

Pool play ran over the weekend of July 26-27, with hundreds of teams competing across multiple venues in the Irvine and Huntington Beach areas of Orange County. Bracket play kicked off following pool completion, immediately raising the stakes for every team still alive in the Winner's Bracket.

The format at PGF Nationals is bracket-style with a loser's bracket providing a second-chance path. Teams that emerge from pool play with strong records enter the Winner's Bracket; those who struggle find themselves fighting through elimination rounds in the loser's side. By Day 2, the Winner's Bracket had thinned considerably — 16 teams remain alive in 14U, and eight in each of the 12U and 10U divisions.

More Than Just a Tournament

PGF Nationals is the showcase moment of the travel softball calendar, but for older players in the 14U bracket, it carries an additional significance this week: the NCAA contact period ends July 31, meaning college coaches have just a few more days to make in-person contact with prospects before the window closes.

The bleachers at PGF Nationals venues are routinely filled with college coaches from across the country, evaluating the next generation of collegiate talent. For 14U players — many of whom are entering the Class of 2029 or 2030 recruiting cycle — these final days of the contact period represent invaluable visibility in front of the coaches who will shape their futures.

The September 1 recruiting window, which opens the early contact period for the Class of 2028, is 35 days away. Programs are actively building their boards.

Week 1 Champions Set the Standard

Week 1 of PGF Nationals — featuring the 16U and 18U divisions — closed out last week with two champions crowned. In the 16U Premier division, Athletics Mercado won on a walk-off by Teuila Jaelynn Eteaki. AZ Rising claimed the 18U Premier title with a run-rule victory, with Goose Goodwin (a Northwestern commit) and Jordynn Parnell (a Duke commit) among the standouts in the run to the championship.

The Week 2 divisions will now determine their own champions before August 2.

What's Next

Bracket play continues Tuesday and Wednesday, with each round further thinning the field toward the quarterfinals and semifinals. Championship Sunday at Deanna Manning Stadium will determine the Week 2 champions across all three age groups.

All games are available via live streaming on PGF.TV. For families following teams from home, the bracket updates and streaming access make PGF Nationals one of the most well-documented youth softball events in the country.

The young players competing at PGF Nationals this week are the future of the sport. For many of them, these games — in front of college coaches, in championship brackets, under tournament pressure — are the moments that define their development as players. Championships matter. But so does everything that happens on the way to one.
