Isa Torres Transfers to Texas: D1Softball's 2026 National Player of the Year Joins Defending Champions

By Emily Rawlings

July 28, 2026

Texas won the 2026 national championship. And then they went and signed the best player in the country.

Florida State senior shortstop Isa Torres — named D1Softball's 2026 National Player of the Year after one of the most dominant seasons in recent college softball history — signed with the Longhorns on June 18. The move pairs the nation's top individual performer with a program that just conquered the Women's College World Series, creating what figures to be one of the most anticipated rosters in 2027.

A Season That Defied Expectations

The numbers Torres put up in her final spring with Florida State were not just good — they were historically good. She hit .530, which ranked third in the nation, and set FSU single-season records across four major statistical categories: batting average, runs scored (78), slugging percentage (.978), and on-base percentage (.591). She drove in 56 runs and hit 16 home runs while playing shortstop at a .989 fielding percentage.

The awards reflected the performance. Torres earned NFCA 1st-Team All-American honors, the NFCA Gold Glove at shortstop, and swept the ACC's two highest individual honors — Player of the Year and Defensive Player of the Year. The D1Softball National Player of the Year award cemented her standing as the nation's best collegiate player in 2026.

A Homecoming

There is a personal dimension to the decision as well. Torres is a native of Georgetown, Texas — a city less than 30 miles north of Austin and the University of Texas campus. Choosing the Longhorns is, in many ways, a return home for a player who grew up watching the program from close range.

Texas head coach Mike White and his staff have built one of the most attractive destinations in college softball, combining elite player development with national-title pedigree. For a player of Torres's caliber entering her final year of eligibility, the combination of winning and homecoming made the decision straightforward.

Texas Keeps Building

Torres is not the only addition to an already-powerful Texas program this offseason. The Longhorns also landed former Arizona State catcher Samantha Swan and former Texas Tech left-handed pitcher Samantha Lincoln through the transfer portal, addressing depth behind the plate and in the circle alongside a roster that already won a national title.

The breadth of Texas's portal class — topped by the nation's best player — reflects a program at the peak of its power. With Torres anchoring the infield and setting the tone at the top of the lineup, the 2027 Longhorns will be the team to beat from the first day of fall practice.

What's Next

The NCAA contact period ends July 31, three days from now. The September 1 recruiting window — which opens early contact for the Class of 2028 — is 35 days away. The transfer portal for the 2026-27 cycle is largely closed, with programs now finalizing the rosters they will take into fall practice.

For Texas, fall practice begins with a decorated national champion roster now led by the best player in college softball. For the rest of the country, watching Torres play in burnt orange for the first time will be must-see softball. The defending champion is hungry — and more talented than ever.
