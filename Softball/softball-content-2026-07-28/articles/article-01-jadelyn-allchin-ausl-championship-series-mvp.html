Jadelyn Allchin Named AUSL Championship Series MVP After Dominant Sweep Performance

By Sarah Mitchell

July 28, 2026

Jadelyn Allchin did not just contribute to the Utah Talons' second straight AUSL Championship. She carried it.

The Talons outfielder was named the 2026 AUSL Championship Series MVP after a performance that swung the series decisively in Utah's favor. Across the two-game sweep of the Chicago Bandits, Allchin drove in six of the Talons' eight total runs — including three RBI in the clinching 6-1 Game 2 victory on Sunday at Davis Diamond in College Station, Texas.

No player in the series did more. And Allchin knew it meant more than a stat line.

The Moment That Defined the Series

Game 2 began with Allchin in the batter's box in the top of the first inning, and she wasted no time making her presence felt. Her two-run home run to right field put the Talons on the board early and immediately put the Bandits in a position they never recovered from. Utah led from the very first inning and never looked back.

She was not finished. In the fourth inning, with the score 2-0, Allchin delivered an RBI double down the right field line to extend the lead to 4-0. By the time Bri Ellis added a solo home run to cap the scoring, the outcome was never in doubt. The Talons won 6-1, completing the first championship sweep in AUSL history.

"The whole series, I think we woke up and we were just ready to go," Allchin said after the game. "The big thing that I thought today was like heart. I think we brought a lot of heart to the past two games. Knowing that showing up with heart for ourselves, for our teammates, like showing up for the game in general, is what I think got us through it all."

A UCLA Legacy on a Championship Roster

Allchin's championship run with the Talons carries an added dimension that resonated far beyond Utah. She was one of five former UCLA Bruins on the championship roster — a group whose shared background and shared standard proved to be part of the Talons' culture of excellence.

The UCLA connection underscores what professional softball has become: a league where the best programs in college softball now have a direct pipeline into the professional game, and where the values instilled in college carry forward into a championship run.

Back-to-Back Champions

The victory made the Utah Talons the first team in AUSL history to win consecutive championships. Head coach Cindy Ball-Malone becomes the first coach to accomplish that feat as well — a milestone that reflects the organizational stability and talent evaluation that the Talons have built over two seasons.

Montana Fouts provided the pitching backbone in Game 2, throwing a complete game and holding the Bandits to one run on eight hits while striking out four. The combination of elite pitching and timely, powerful offense from Allchin made the Talons nearly impossible to beat on softball's biggest stage.

Game 1, played Saturday, was a 2-1 Utah victory behind a complete-game performance from Georgina Corrick — two dominant pitching performances bookending a championship that never felt in doubt.

What's Next

For Jadelyn Allchin, the offseason begins as a two-time AUSL champion and the league's Championship Series MVP. She has established herself as one of the most clutch performers in professional softball — a player who elevates in the biggest moments when her team needs her most.

For the Utah Talons, the task now turns to defending what has become a dynasty. The AUSL's expansion into its third season in 2027 will bring new competition, new talent, and new challengers. But the Talons have already proven that winning once is not a fluke — and that winning twice makes you a standard.

The rest of the league will spend the offseason figuring out how to stop them.
