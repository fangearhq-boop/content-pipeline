Texas Fall Opener Is 28 Days Away — and the Three-Peat Chase Is About to Begin

By Sarah Mitchell

August 31, 2026

The Texas Longhorns have a fall schedule. They have most of their championship roster returning. They have a head coach who already knows how to win. And they have Teagan Kavan.

In 28 days — September 29 — the 2026 Texas fall schedule opens with Murray State at McCombs Field (6 PM CT, free admission). The six-game fall slate closes November 6 at Texas A&M's Davis Diamond (7 PM CT). Between those two dates, head coach Mike White will begin shaping the roster that will pursue something almost no team in softball history has accomplished: three consecutive national championships.

The Roster Taking Shape

Six of nine starters from the 2026 national championship team return. That is an unusual level of continuity for a team that just won back-to-back titles — a testament to the program's ability to retain talent and develop players who can step into expanded roles.

There are gaps to fill. Reese Atwood and other contributors from the 2026 championship squad have graduated, and White acknowledged the team will address those needs through the portal. "We will look in the transfer portal again this year to bring in some pieces," he confirmed following the fall schedule announcement. The portal remains open, and Texas has the recruiting brand to attract high-level additions.

Everything Runs Through Kavan

Teagan Kavan is back for her senior season. She is the only player in Women's College World Series history to earn Most Outstanding Player honors twice — a distinction she secured in back-to-back seasons in 2025 and 2026. Her presence alone makes Texas the team to beat in any preseason conversation.

Only two programs in softball history have won three consecutive national championships: UCLA (1988-90) and Oklahoma (2021-23). Texas is chasing a mark that almost no program has reached.

What's Next

Texas opens fall ball September 29 at McCombs Field against Murray State (free admission, 6 PM CT). The fall schedule runs through November 6 at Texas A&M. Ahead of that opener, White and his staff will finalize portal additions to round out the 2027 roster alongside the six returning starters. The three-peat bid officially begins the moment the first pitch of fall ball leaves Kavan's hand.
