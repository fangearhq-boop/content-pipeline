September 1 Is Here: The D1 Contact Window Is Open for Class of 2028 Softball Recruits

By Sarah Mitchell

August 31, 2026

The wait is over.

September 1 has arrived, and with it comes the moment that every Class of 2028 softball recruit and their family has been counting down to. Under NCAA Division I rules, today is the first date on which college coaches can officially initiate recruiting contact — calls, texts, emails — with prospective student-athletes in the junior class.

For recruits who spent the summer at showcases, sent emails to coaches, built relationships at camps, and put their film in front of programs: today is when it pays off.

What Happens on September 1

The contact window does not open quietly. For the programs that have done their evaluation work all summer, September 1 is an active day — and the timing of a program's outreach reveals a great deal about where a recruit stands on their board.

The first hour of the contact window — the calls that go out before 8 AM ET — is the most significant. Coaching staffs spend weeks building their call-order lists, and the recruits who hear from a program first are typically the highest-priority targets on that board. A first-hour call is a signal. It is not a guarantee, but it means a program has made your athlete one of their top priorities for the 2028 class.

Calls that come later in the day — mid-morning, afternoon — represent programs that are still deciding between options or targeting secondary priorities. Those calls still matter. Any official contact from a D1 program on September 1 indicates genuine interest.

The In-Person Window: September 9

The phone call window is only the beginning. On September 9 at 7 AM ET, the in-person contact window opens — the first date on which coaches can attend high school practices, visit facilities, and meet with recruits and their families face-to-face.

Programs that made September 1 calls will likely follow up with in-person visits for their highest-priority targets in the days and weeks following September 9. For recruits who have not yet received a call but are still working toward D1 offers, the in-person window creates another point of access.

What's Next

September 1 is the beginning, not the end, of the Class of 2028 recruiting cycle. Coaches will make their most important calls today, and the weeks that follow — including in-person visits starting September 9 — will produce the first wave of verbal commitments from the 2028 class. Recruits who have not yet been contacted by D1 programs should continue reaching out proactively: the window is now fully open in both directions.
