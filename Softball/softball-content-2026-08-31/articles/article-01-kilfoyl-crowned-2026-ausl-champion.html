Kilfoyl Crowned 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup Champion After Last-Game Comeback Over Klingler

By Jordan Reeves

August 31, 2026

Lexi Kilfoyl is the 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup individual champion.

In the Series 4 finale on Sunday night at the Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois, Team Kilfoyl defeated Team Klingler 4-3. When the final out was recorded, the individual points were tallied — and Kilfoyl's name was at the top of the leaderboard. The comeback from 24 points back was complete.

Sami Williams delivered the clutch performance that sealed it. Williams came up in a crucial late-game moment and provided exactly what Team Kilfoyl needed to hold on for the victory and flip the individual standings in the closing hours of the 2026 All-Star Cup season.

How the Leaderboard Flipped

Heading into the Series 4 finale, Baylee Klingler led the individual standings with 890 points. Kilfoyl trailed at 866 — a 24-point gap that would require a near-perfect performance to overcome. Hannah Flippen, who had been eliminated from title contention after Team Kilfoyl defeated Team Flippen 4-1 in Game 2 of the series, was no longer a factor.

In AUSL scoring, Game MVP awards carry 100 or more points of value. A dominant individual performance — one that earned MVP recognition for Kilfoyl's side — combined with a quieter night from Klingler was the only path to the championship. Kilfoyl walked it.

The individual scoring swing in the final game was enough to push Kilfoyl past Klingler and claim the title that Klingler had led for most of the All-Star Cup season.

Three MVPs, One Title

What made Kilfoyl's championship run remarkable was the body of work. She earned three Game MVP awards across the 2026 All-Star Cup — the most of any player in the league. Those performances were what kept her mathematically alive entering the final week and provided the blueprint for how she would need to finish.

Her performance in Series 4 followed a similar pattern. With Sarah Willis throwing five innings of one-run ball in Game 2 and Taylor McQuillin closing out the final two frames in a save, Team Kilfoyl proved that individual success in this league requires more than one player. The team delivered. Then Kilfoyl delivered in the finale.

What's Next

The 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup is complete, and Lexi Kilfoyl is its individual champion. Baylee Klingler, who went 4-0 as a captain across the regular series and led the individual standings for most of the competition, finishes as the runner-up after a championship night that came down to the final out. Attention in professional softball now shifts to the 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage in Oklahoma City, where several AUSL players will represent Team USA beginning September 12.
