Audrey Lowry Is Making Her Case as OU's Fall Battle Series Winds Down

By Jordan Reeves

August 31, 2026

The Oklahoma Sooners entered fall camp with a pitching roster rebuilt almost entirely from scratch. They are leaving it with a name to watch.

Audrey Lowry, a sophomore left-handed pitcher, has emerged as one of the standouts of Oklahoma's fall Battle Series. In a recent outing, Lowry threw 2.2 innings, allowing 1 hit, issuing 0 walks, and striking out 2 batters — a clean line that speaks to both her stuff and her command. She is one of just two returning pitchers on the 2027 Sooners roster, alongside senior Kierston Deal.

For a program that went through a significant roster rebuild following its first WCWS miss since 2015, identifying a reliable returner in the pitching staff is meaningful. Lowry is making the case that she is that player.

The Transfer Integration Continues

Lowry's emergence comes alongside the integration of two high-profile transfers that head coach Patty Gasso added through the portal. Sydney Berzon, a two-time All-American from LSU, brings one of the most accomplished pitching resumes of any transfer in the portal this cycle. Macie Harter, an outfielder from MTSU who hit .346 in 2026, adds punch to a lineup that needed rebuilding depth.

The fall Battle Series has given Gasso the opportunity to evaluate how all of these pieces fit together before the formal fall schedule begins. The picture being developed now will shape the Sooners' 2027 roster decisions heading into the spring season.

Two Calendars Running Simultaneously

Gasso enters September managing two programs at once. In 12 days — September 12 — she departs for Oklahoma City to serve as head coach of the USA Women's National Team at the 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage. She returns to Norman after the group stage concludes September 16.

The fall opener at Love's Field is September 30 — 29 days from today. Outside opponents on the schedule include USAO (October 23), Seminole State (October 30), and East Texas A&M (November 4). Home fall game tickets are $10.88 per game or $70.69 for the all-fall pass.

What's Next

Patty Gasso departs for WNT duty September 12 and returns September 16. The Sooners' formal fall opener is September 30 at Love's Field. The next month of practice — with Lowry, Deal, Berzon, and Harter all competing for roles — will do more to define OU's 2027 identity than anything else in the offseason. The results of these internal scrimmages won't make headlines, but they will shape the rotation that takes the field in February.
