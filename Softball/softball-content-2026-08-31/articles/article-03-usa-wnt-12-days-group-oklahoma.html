12 Days to Group Oklahoma: USA Softball Enters Final Countdown Before World Cup Group Stage

By Emily Rawlings

August 31, 2026

The 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage begins September 12 in Oklahoma City. As of today, USA Softball has 12 days to prepare.

Head coach Patty Gasso leads a 16-player roster into the Group Oklahoma competition at Devon Park — the same venue where the USA Women's National Team went 5-0 to win the gold medal at the 2025 International Cup. The familiarity with the venue is one advantage. The roster's experience at the international level is another.

The Roster Built for This Moment

Gasso assembled a group with significant international experience at the top of the lineup. Seven athletes on the roster were part of the 2025 World Games gold medal squad. Ten players competed in the 2025 USA Down Under Series. The returning veterans provide a backbone of international familiarity that will matter in a tournament where early momentum is everything.

Two members of the 2020 U.S. Olympic Team are on the roster: Valerie Garcia and Aubree Mulipola. Both bring Olympics-level experience to a competition where the ultimate prize — a berth to the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics — is still at stake further down the road.

The USA's four Sooners — Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell — give Oklahoma a familiar presence in the lineup and add a familiar dynamic to a roster that Gasso knows well from her work in Norman.

The Group Stage Path

USA opens against No. 7 Mexico on September 12. The opener is an opportunity to set the tone early and build confidence before facing the toughest test of the group: No. 6 Netherlands. The Netherlands-USA matchup will almost certainly determine who finishes at the top of the group and secures the more favorable path through the bracket.

The top two teams from Group Oklahoma advance to the World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia (April 5-11, 2027). The highest-placed finisher at the Finals earns the USA a direct berth to the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. Every game in Oklahoma City matters.

What's Next

USA Softball opens World Cup Group Stage play September 12 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City against No. 7 Mexico. The group runs through September 16. Patty Gasso will be managing her national team commitment while simultaneously overseeing OU's fall program — she returns to Norman after the group stage concludes on September 16, with the Sooners' formal fall opener at Love's Field set for September 30.
