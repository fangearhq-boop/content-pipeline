### STORY 5: Texas 2027 — Fall Opener 28 Days Away; Portal Search Begins
**Tier 3 — FB Long-Form + FB Image Caption**

**FB Long-Form Post**
Window: 8:00 PM ET

The back-to-back WCWS champions are 28 days from fall ball.

Texas opens its 2026 fall schedule September 29 at McCombs Field against Murray State (6 PM CT, free admission) and closes November 6 at Texas A&M's Davis Diamond (7 PM CT). Head coach Mike White has been clear: the Longhorns will return to the transfer portal to add pieces for 2027.

With six of nine starters back from the national championship squad — and Reese Atwood and others off to graduation — there are spots to fill. White's portal strategy will shape how complete this roster looks heading into the spring season.

At the center of everything: Teagan Kavan.

The pitcher who has won two consecutive WCWS Most Outstanding Player awards — the only player in tournament history to do so — is back for her senior season. Her return alone makes Texas the team to beat entering the 2027 season.

Only UCLA (1988-90) and Oklahoma (2021-23) have ever won three consecutive national championships. Texas is chasing a mark that almost nobody has reached.

Fall ball kicks off September 29. 🤘 #HookEm
