2028 Extra Elite 100 Top-5 Reveal: College Softball's Most Anticipated Recruiting Moment Is Here

By Sarah Mitchell | October 27, 2026 | I Love Softball

Two weeks of countdowns, debates, and predictions culminate today as Extra Inning Softball releases the final five spots in its Extra Elite 100 rankings for the Class of 2028 — the largest recruiting ranking cycle in the history of the list.

More than 1,200 athletes were nominated for this class when the process began, shattering previous cycles. Since October 13, EIS has published rankings in batches, working from No. 100 down through No. 6. Today's reveal of positions 5 through 1 closes the book on what the publication called its most competitive class ever.

The No. 1 player in the Class of 2028 gets named today.

Where We Stand Before the Top 5

Heading into today's reveal, the team rankings tell a compelling story. Texas has long held the top team spot in Class of 2028 commitments, with pitcher Landers holding the No. 1 position in earlier installments. Florida enters with Holden slotted at No. 2 overall, and Oklahoma's Schlader has claimed No. 3. Tennessee has built one of the most complete 2028 classes in the country, adding Kuiper and Eldred among others.

Positions 6 through 100 are already set. The final five names are the last piece of a puzzle that recruiting analysts, coaches, and fans have been assembling all month.

Why This Class Matters

The Class of 2028 represents current high school juniors — players who will arrive on campus in the fall of 2028 and immediately become part of rosters competing for national championships in 2029 through 2031. The recruiting landscape for this class has been unusually intense.

The October 24-25 contact window — when coaches could make direct contact with prospects — has now closed. The next window doesn't open until spring 2027, meaning today's rankings effectively close the public chapter on this recruiting cycle until February. The commit wave following the contact window added dozens of new pledges across the top programs.

A separate Fastpitch Fan Pick Top 5 also publishes this week, offering a crowdsourced counterpoint to the EIS rankings. Both lists will fuel recruiting conversation heading into the offseason.

The Top Programs in the Running

Texas leads the way in team rankings, a testament to the Longhorns' ability to recruit at the highest level under head coach Mike White. Florida and Oklahoma are right behind, with both programs consistently landing the top players at every position. Tennessee has made a significant push in the 2028 class, highlighting how programs like the Lady Vols are building national recruiting pipelines even in off-peak years.

The top five overall players in any EE100 class typically become household names by the time they arrive on campus. Past top-5 players have gone on to earn All-American honors as freshmen and anchor WCWS-bound rosters. This class figures to be no different.

What's Next

The full Class of 2028 Extra Elite 100 rankings are available now at extrainningsoftball.com. The next major recruiting window for the class opens in spring 2027, when coaches and prospects will reconnect heading into the summer travel ball season.

For fans of Texas, Florida, Oklahoma, Tennessee, and every other program with a top-100 commit, today is a day to celebrate — or recalibrate. The class of 2028 rankings are official.
