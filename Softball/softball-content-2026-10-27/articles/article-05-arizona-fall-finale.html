Arizona Softball Closes Fall Ball This Weekend: 10 Letterwinners, a Big 12 Award-Winner, and a New Era

By Emily Rawlings | October 27, 2026 | I Love Softball

Arizona softball wraps up its 2026 fall slate this weekend with back-to-back sessions at Hillenbrand Stadium on Thursday, Oct. 30 and Friday, Oct. 31. It's the final chapter of fall ball for a program that enters 2027 with legitimate momentum and something to prove in the Big 12.

Head coach Caitlin Lowe's Wildcats bring ten letterwinners back into the fold, led by a player who already has one of the most prestigious individual awards in the conference on her résumé.

Devyn Shockey: Big 12 Defensive Player of the Year

Devyn Shockey earned the Big 12 Defensive Player of the Year award in 2026 — one of the conference's most competitive individual honors. Shockey's ability in the field has been a cornerstone of Arizona's defensive identity, and her return for 2027 gives Lowe a reliable presence at a key position.

Shockey isn't just a defensive specialist; she's a leader. Having a player of her caliber anchor the fielding corps gives younger players a model to follow and gives opponents one fewer easy base to take. In close games, defense wins, and Arizona has a defensive anchor.

Ten Letterwinners: More Than a Number

When a program returns ten letterwinners, it means the roster isn't starting over. Arizona isn't rebuilding — it's reloading. Players who have been through Big 12 competition, who have faced the grind of a conference schedule that includes Oklahoma, Texas, and other perennial contenders, bring invaluable experience to a roster that will need it in March.

Caitlin Lowe, who played at Arizona and was a national championship-winning outfielder, has built a culture at Hillenbrand that blends experienced leadership with genuine recruiting upside. The fall has been a chance to integrate the new pieces with the returning core.

Building in the Big 12

Arizona joined the Big 12 in 2024, and the transition to one of the nation's toughest conferences has been an adjustment. But the Wildcats are past the adjustment phase. With two full seasons of Big 12 competition under their belts, Arizona's experienced roster is better equipped to handle the league's demands.

The fall program has emphasized competition, conditioning, and the kind of detail work that separates good programs from great ones. This weekend's finale gives Lowe and her staff one last look at the full roster before the offseason programming period begins in earnest.

What's Next

Arizona's fall ball sessions close Thursday, Oct. 30 and Friday, Oct. 31 at Hillenbrand Stadium in Tucson. After this weekend, the Wildcats enter the offseason preparation period before their 2027 schedule begins. Keep an eye on Arizona — a program with ten letterwinners, a Big 12 award-winner at the defensive position, and a head coach who demands excellence is a dangerous team heading into next spring.

Bear down, Wildcats.
