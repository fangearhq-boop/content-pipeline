Watch the SEC's Best Pitching Staff for Free: Tennessee's Intersquad Is Wednesday

By Sarah Mitchell | October 27, 2026 | I Love Softball

On Wednesday, Oct. 29 at 5 PM ET, Tennessee softball holds its final public event of the fall at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium — and admission is free.

The Lady Vols' public intersquad is your last chance to watch what many consider the deepest pitching staff in college softball before the 2027 season begins. Karlyn Pickens, Kelly Mardjetko, and freshman Bailee Shelton are all expected to throw. Three pitchers. Three completely different skill sets. One afternoon, no charge.

Karlyn Pickens: Dominance Defined

Pickens put up one of the most dominant statistical pitching seasons in Tennessee program history in 2026: 194 strikeouts and a 1.70 ERA. Those numbers don't just lead the SEC — they lead the country. Pickens is a power pitcher with elite spin, the kind of arm that other programs build their rosters hoping to avoid facing in the postseason.

Watching Pickens in a live setting gives fans a sense of just how special she is. High-speed on television doesn't fully translate what hitters face in the box. Seeing the arm action, the movement, and the confidence from the Sherri Parker Lee Stadium bleachers is something different entirely.

Kelly Mardjetko: The Most Underrated Ace in the SEC

Mardjetko went 16-3 with a 1.26 ERA in 2026. If Pickens is the headliner, Mardjetko is the co-ace who doesn't get enough credit. Her ability to change speeds, command the strike zone, and compete in high-leverage situations makes her arguably the most complete pitcher on Tennessee's staff.

A staff with one of these two arms is dangerous. A staff with both — plus a third option in Shelton — is a problem for every team in the SEC.

Bailee Shelton: The Freshman Who Already Looks Like a Future Ace

In her fall finale against Lee University on Oct. 23, freshman Bailee Shelton threw three innings of one-hit softball with seven strikeouts. Seven strikeouts in three innings. That's the kind of performance that makes pitching coaches exhale.

Shelton gives head coach Karen Weekly a legitimate third arm in a rotation that already has two of the best starters in college softball. If Shelton develops the way her fall has suggested, Tennessee could have three legitimate aces for 2027 — a genuinely historic staff depth.

What's Next

Tennessee's public intersquad is Wednesday, Oct. 29 at 5 PM ET at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium in Knoxville. Admission is free. After Wednesday, the Lady Vols shift to offseason conditioning. Head coach Karen Weekly has expressed a desire for tougher fall competition going forward — a signal that the program isn't just satisfied with dominating exhibition opponents, but is actively seeking preparation that matches what they'll face in the postseason.

The 2027 season will be here before you know it. Wednesday is your last chance to see what's coming.
