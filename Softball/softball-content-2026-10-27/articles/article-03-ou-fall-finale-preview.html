Oklahoma Softball's Fall Finale Is Thursday: Last Look at Wells and Kavan Before Spring

By Jordan Reeves | October 27, 2026 | I Love Softball

If you want to see Oklahoma softball before the 2027 season begins for real, Thursday is your last chance.

The Sooners host Seminole State College in their final fall exhibition on Thursday, Oct. 30 at 6:30 PM CT at Love's Field in Norman. Admission is free. The game streams live on 1oklahoma.com for fans who can't make the drive.

Two of the most decorated players in college softball will take the field one last time before spring.

Kendall Wells: The Most Dangerous Freshman Sophomore in America

Kendall Wells arrived at Oklahoma in 2026 as a heralded recruit and promptly exceeded every expectation. She hit 39 home runs as a freshman — one of the most prolific power seasons for a first-year player in recent memory — and was named the NFCA Freshman of the Year. She enters her sophomore season as one of the most feared hitters in the country.

This fall has been about refinement for Wells. The power was never the question. Adding consistency against varied pitching, working on specific plate approach, and continuing to integrate into the Sooners' lineup construction are the priorities. Thursday's game against Seminole State offers one final look at where that work stands.

Rylie Kavan: Two WCWS MOPs and Counting

Rylie Kavan has done something no one else in the sport has done: won the Women's College World Series Most Outstanding Player award twice. She returns to Norman in 2027 with two championship trophies on her résumé and unfinished business on her agenda. Kavan's presence in the Sooners' lineup is a luxury that very few programs in the country can match.

Thursday's fall finale gives Kavan — like Wells — one last tuneup before the offseason workload shifts to full-season preparation. Fans who make it to Love's Field will see one of the most accomplished rosters in collegiate softball.

OU's Battle Series: A Fall Built on Competition

Oklahoma hasn't wasted its fall. The Sooners ran an extended Battle Series of intrasquad games throughout September and October before adding outside opponents. They faced USAO on Oct. 23 in the final Battle Series game, with the Seminole State game on Oct. 30 serving as the standalone fall closer.

For a program that regularly competes for national championships, the fall is about sharpening edges, not proving points. The Sooners already know what they are. Thursday's game is about finishing the work, building momentum, and sending the roster into the offseason with confidence.

What's Next

Oklahoma's final fall exhibition is Thursday, Oct. 30 at 6:30 PM CT at Love's Field. The game is free, with a live stream at 1oklahoma.com. After Thursday, the Sooners shift to winter conditioning and 2027 preparation. Their Big 12 schedule was announced in September, with conference play opening in March.

If Oklahoma is your team — or if you just appreciate elite college softball — Thursday night in Norman is worth your time.
