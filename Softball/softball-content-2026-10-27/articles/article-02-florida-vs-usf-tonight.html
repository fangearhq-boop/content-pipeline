Florida Softball Seeks 4-0 Fall Record in Free Game Against USF Tonight

By Emily Rawlings | October 27, 2026 | I Love Softball

Florida softball has been the most dominant team in fall ball this season — 62 runs scored, 6 allowed, three wins from three games. Tonight, the Gators get a chance to close out October with a fourth straight lopsided victory.

The University of Florida hosts South Florida at Katie Seashole Pressly Stadium in Gainesville at 6 PM ET on Tuesday, October 27. Admission is free. For anyone who wants to see a 2027 national title contender at close range, this is an easy decision.

A Fall Season Unlike Any Other

Florida's three fall games have been performances more than competitions. The Gators opened with a 20-0 throttling of Santa Fe College on October 16, followed by a 29-1 demolition of Carson-Newman the very next day. Their third game was a win over UNF, bringing the cumulative run differential to 62-6 across nine innings of fall action.

Sophia Edwards has been the offensive standout, hitting three home runs across the fall schedule. Edwards, who returns as part of a core group of at least six starters from the 2026 Florida roster, has looked every bit the power threat the Gators expected heading into 2027.

What USF Brings

South Florida isn't a pushover. The Bulls have a history of competitive fall games against in-state opponents, and they make the trip to Gainesville with a chance to test themselves against one of the nation's top programs. For USF players, a game against Florida is an audition as much as it is a game — a chance to measure where they stand against elite competition.

Last year, Florida beat USF 12-1 in a fall exhibition at the same venue. With the Gators rolling even more dominantly in 2026, tonight's margin could be similarly wide.

Six Starters Back, Upside Ahead

What makes Florida's fall production especially exciting is the context of who's playing. The Gators return at least six starters from 2026 — a team that was already among the nation's elite. Adding the performances of newcomers and freshmen to that core suggests the 2027 Gators could be historically deep.

Florida's coaching staff has used the fall to experiment with lineup construction, test pitching options, and integrate new players into game situations. Tonight's game against USF is part of that continued evaluation process, even if the expected result is another comfortable Gators win.

What's Next

Tonight's 6 PM ET game at Katie Seashole Pressly Stadium is free and open to the public. If tonight's result follows the pattern of Florida's fall, the Gators will close October with a 4-0 record and a run differential north of 70.

From here, Florida shifts to winter conditioning and spring preparation. Their 2027 schedule will be released in the coming months, but all signs point to the Gators entering the season as a top-five program nationally — and one of the most dangerous offenses in the country.
