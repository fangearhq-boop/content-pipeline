### STORY 3: 5-for-5 Day-After — De-Commit Wave

**Long-Form Post** | Window: 1:00 PM ET

This is the story that needs to be talked about. 😔

The day after Signing Day, Memphis softball pulled scholarship offers from three committed Class of 2027 athletes: Cassidy Ott, Kinley Patten, and Isabell Gonzales.

All three took to social media to share what happened. Cassidy Ott said she was "involuntarily de-committed from the University of Memphis due to the new 5-year eligibility rule."

This is the 5-for-5 roster squeeze playing out in real life. The Tenth Circuit's November 6 ruling means the rule is in effect. The House v. NCAA settlement caps rosters at 25 players. When veteran players exercise their fifth year, there's no room for the incoming class.

Kentucky's Rachel Lawson warned months ago that there would be "casualties." She wasn't wrong.

Memphis may be first. It almost certainly won't be last. Roughly 200 state-court cases involving individual athletes are still active.

These are real kids who built their college plans around these commitments.

Texas Tech just made program history on Signing Day. 🤠

Hollynn Gentry — the No. 11 overall recruit in the country and a catcher — signed with the Red Raiders, becoming the first five-star freshman to ever sign with Texas Tech softball in program history.
