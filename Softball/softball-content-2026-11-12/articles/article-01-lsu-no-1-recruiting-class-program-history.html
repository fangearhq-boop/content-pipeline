LSU Lands No. 1 Recruiting Class in Program History

By Jordan Reeves | November 12, 2026 | I Love Softball

Beth Torina has built plenty of winning teams at LSU. But she has never done this before. On National Signing Day, Torina's 2027 recruiting class was ranked No. 1 in the nation by Softball America/Rivals — the first time in program history the Tigers have ever claimed the top spot in a signing class ranking.

The class includes six signees: five-star pitcher Sydney Gonglick, five-star infielder Bryli Seifert, catcher Jewel Cooper, pitcher Maci Bryant, utility player Lillie Gaudio, and infielder Jillian Wimmer. Torina also added transfer portal twins Mackenzie and Madison Pickens from Ole Miss and Louisville, respectively, deepening the roster ahead of the 2027 season.

What Makes This Class Historic

LSU softball has been a consistent top-five program under Torina, but climbing to No. 1 represents a recruiting ceiling the program had never before reached. Torina's 2026 class finished No. 3 nationally — itself a program high at the time. The 2027 class is a step beyond that, and the first at LSU to wear the top ranking.

The two five-star recruits anchor the class. Sydney Gonglick is a pitcher who Softball America identified as one of the top circle prospects in the country. Bryli Seifert, ranked as a five-star infielder, adds to an offense that already has significant returning production. The addition of the Pickens twins — Mackenzie from Ole Miss, Madison from Louisville — gives Torina experienced portal pieces on top of the high school foundation.

The SEC Context

LSU's No. 1 ranking sits atop a conference that dominated the 2027 signing class rankings. Softball America's rankings placed six SEC programs in the national top 10. Florida landed at No. 2, and programs throughout the conference reinforced the SEC's position as the dominant force in college softball recruiting.

The concentration of elite recruiting talent in the SEC sets up a compelling 2027 championship race. LSU, Florida, Oklahoma, Tennessee, and other conference powers will all enter the season with roster upgrades from historically strong signing classes. For Torina, the No. 1 ranking represents both a recruiting validation and an increased expectation heading into next season.

Torina's Recruiting Evolution

Beth Torina has coached LSU softball since 2013, and the program has been a consistent SEC contender throughout her tenure. But the recruiting rankings tell a story of a program that has gradually elevated its national footprint. Back-to-back top-three classes, culminating in the first-ever No. 1 ranking, reflect a recruiting infrastructure that is now operating at the highest level in the sport.

The addition of the Pickens twins is worth noting separately — it shows that Torina's program is not only recruiting elite high school talent but also competing for the top names in the transfer portal. In the post-NLI landscape where roster construction requires both pipelines, LSU has now demonstrated proficiency in both.

What's Next

Signing Day marks the beginning of a recruiting window that runs through August 1, 2027. Additional signings can still occur within that window. For LSU, the focus now shifts from recruiting to development — integrating Gonglick, Seifert, the Pickens twins, and the rest of the class into a roster that already has significant returning talent. Softball America will update its team recruiting rankings as more programs finalize their classes. For now, LSU sits at the top for the first time in program history.
