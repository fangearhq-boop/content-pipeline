Florida Does It Again: Gators Land Nation's No. 2 Class for Second Straight Year

By Sarah Mitchell | November 12, 2026 | I Love Softball

Florida softball signed six recruits on National Signing Day and officially earned the No. 2 recruiting class in the country, according to Softball America/Rivals. It is the second consecutive year the Gators have finished in the top two nationally — a level of recruiting consistency that is essentially unmatched in the sport.

The class includes Taylor Jennings (No. 9 overall, No. 2 corner infielder nationally), Taegan Lermann (No. 19 overall), Samantha Nichols (No. 31 overall, No. 7 middle infielder nationally), Addison Allaire, Niayli Calvo, and Josslynn Grimsley. All six will join a program that went 4-0 in the fall, outscoring opponents 73-9, with six starters returning from the 2026 roster.

Taylor Jennings Leads the Class

At No. 9 overall and No. 2 at her position nationally, Jennings is the headliner of a class that could have multiple significant contributors. The No. 2 ranking among corner infielders nationally means Jennings arrives with legitimate expectations of impacting Florida's lineup from the start.

Lermann at No. 19 and Nichols at No. 31 add further depth to what is already a stacked incoming group. When a single recruiting class contains three players ranked in the top 31 nationally, the floor of contributions is unusually high.

What Back-to-Back Top-Two Classes Actually Means

The persistence of elite recruiting is what separates programs that contend annually from those that cycle in and out of the national conversation. Finishing in the top two nationally once is an achievement. Doing it in consecutive years — and doing it on top of an already-talented returning roster — is a signal that the program's recruiting infrastructure is operating at an elite level.

Florida's 2027 roster is not being rebuilt. It is being reinforced. Six starters return from a squad that was already one of the best in the country. The incoming class is not patching holes — it is adding depth and next-level talent behind a core that has already proven itself.

The 2027 Florida Picture

Among programs best positioned for a national title run in 2027, Florida's combination of factors is difficult to match. The 4-0 fall record demonstrated that returning veterans are healthy and productive. Transfer Caroline Edwards contributed four home runs in fall action. The No. 2 incoming class adds proven talent at multiple positions.

Florida's future is also secured. No. 1 overall Class of 2028 recruit Avery Landers is already committed to the Gators, extending the pipeline beyond next season. For a program operating at this level, the question in 2027 is not whether Florida can compete for a national championship. It is whether any team can match them depth-for-depth over the full course of a season.

What's Next

Florida's signing window remains open through August 1, 2027, though the core of the class is now set. The roster will continue to take shape as the program enters winter training. Softball America will refine its final class rankings over the coming weeks as other programs complete their signing classes. The Big 12 and SEC schedules for 2027 have been announced, with conference play providing the high-stakes environment where this recruiting investment will ultimately be tested.
