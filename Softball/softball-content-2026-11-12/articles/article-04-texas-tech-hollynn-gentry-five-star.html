Historic Signing: Texas Tech Lands First Five-Star Freshman with Hollynn Gentry

By Jordan Reeves | November 12, 2026 | I Love Softball

Texas Tech softball added another program-first on National Signing Day. Catcher Hollynn Gentry — ranked No. 11 nationally by Softball America/Rivals — signed with the Red Raiders, becoming the first five-star freshman to ever sign with Texas Tech in program history. Coach Gerry Glasco called her a major addition behind the plate.

Gentry was joined by outfielder Skylar Knight from Trinity, Texas, and infielder Taylor Poland, ranked No. 131 nationally, as part of a four-player signing class. The fourth signee was part of Wednesday's announcement. For Glasco, the high school class completes a 2027 recruiting picture that also includes the No. 1 transfer portal class in the country.

What Gentry Brings

At No. 11 overall and as the first five-star catcher ever to sign with Texas Tech, Gentry represents a new standard for what the Red Raiders can recruit. The position matters: catching is among the most demanding roles on the field, and elite catchers do not come to Lubbock at this level. That Glasco landed Gentry signals that his program now competes for top-five-at-position talent nationally.

Gentry steps into a roster that already features significant offensive talent from the portal class. Kasidi Pickering, who signed in June after three seasons at Oklahoma (.384 BA, 50 HR, 169 RBI), gives Texas Tech one of the most dangerous individual offensive weapons in the Big 12. Bradi Gallaway, Jayden Heavener, and Moriah Polar add further depth to what is now the deepest Texas Tech offensive lineup in program history.

Program Trajectory

Texas Tech has reached the Women's College World Series championship series in back-to-back seasons. For a program that had historically been on the periphery of national title conversations, the rapid ascent under Glasco has been one of the most significant stories in college softball. The Red Raiders are no longer a surprising WCWS presence — they are a program that expects to be there.

The addition of the first five-star freshman in program history is a recruiting inflection point. Programs that land players like Gentry tend to continue landing them. The national reputation that comes with signing a top-11 recruit reinforces Texas Tech's ability to attract the next one.

The 2027 Big 12 Picture

The Big 12 softball season opens March 12, 2027. Texas is the defending national champion. Oklahoma brings back Kendall Wells and Rylie Kavan. Arizona has fallen ball experience under Caitlin Lowe. The conference is loaded, and Texas Tech — with the No. 1 portal class and now the first five-star freshman in program history — enters the year as one of the most dangerous teams in the league.

Glasco has not been building toward a moment. He has been building a standard. Wednesday's signing class is the most visible evidence yet that the standard has changed.

What's Next

Texas Tech's roster for 2027 is now nearly complete. The focus shifts to winter conditioning and the integration of Pickering, Gentry, and the rest of the portal and high school additions into Glasco's system. The Big 12 schedule opens March 12, and the Red Raiders will be one of the most watched teams in the country when it does. For Gentry, the first five-star catcher in program history, the story is just beginning.
