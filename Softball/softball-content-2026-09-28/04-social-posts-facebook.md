#### Facebook Long-Form — 3:00 PM ET

Patty Gasso isn't rattled.

Oklahoma softball lost three players to the transfer portal this offseason — Tia Milloy and the Zache sisters, Riley and Berkley. In another program, three significant portal departures might signal trouble. For Gasso and the Sooners, it's a puzzle to solve.

The first big answer: Sydney Berzon. The LSU pitcher earned NFCA Second Team All-American honors in 2024 and Third Team All-American honors in 2023 — she's a proven, high-level arm with one year of eligibility remaining. Gasso also added Macie Harter from Middle Tennessee, who hit .346 with seven home runs, 23 RBI, and 19 stolen bases in 46 starts last season.

Gasso's public comments were measured but clear: "We definitely are going to take a look-see in the portal to see what we can find for sure. We don't need a lot, don't need much at all."

That kind of confidence from a coach who has won multiple national titles isn't bravado — it's experience. Oklahoma still has the recruiting infrastructure and the program culture that made them a dynasty. They lost in 2025 and 2026 to Texas. That doesn't mean the Sooners are down. It means they're hungry.

Berzon is the kind of addition that changes a pitching staff. Watch Oklahoma this fall.
