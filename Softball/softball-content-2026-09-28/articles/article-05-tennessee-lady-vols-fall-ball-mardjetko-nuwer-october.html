Tennessee Fall Ball Is Rolling — Mardjetko, Nuwer, and Nine Starters Building Toward 2027

By Emily Rawlings | September 28, 2026

Tennessee's fall ball schedule is in motion, and for a program with two of the best returning pitchers in the country, that matters.

The Lady Vols opened fall ball Thursday, September 25 against Walters State at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium. They followed that up with a Sunday home game against Lipscomb on September 27. Next up: Carson-Newman on Thursday, October 2. The schedule is rolling, and Sage Mardjetko and Erin Nuwer are getting competitive reps for the first time since the 2026 postseason ended.

The Arms Are Back

Mardjetko finished the 2026 season at 16-3 with a 1.26 ERA and 171 strikeouts. Nuwer went 15-1 with a 1.01 ERA. Together, they combined for 31 wins and eight shutouts — a pitching duo that is, by any measurable standard, among the best in college softball. Both are returning for 2027. Both are pitching this fall.

For a sport where pitching depth separates good teams from elite ones, Tennessee's situation is exceptional. Most programs would build an entire identity around one ace with those numbers. The Lady Vols have two of them. Fall ball is where they sharpen those edges — competitive reps, game situations, working on sequences against unfamiliar hitters.

The Walters State and Lipscomb games serve that purpose. Carson-Newman on October 2 serves it further. The schedule isn't about results in October. It is about preparing two elite arms for the demands of an SEC season and a postseason run in the spring.

Nine Starters Back

Tennessee is not rebuilding — they are returning nine starters from the 2026 WCWS squad. That continuity is significant. The Lady Vols don't have to integrate a new lineup or teach a new system to a collection of freshmen. They have experienced players who have already competed on the biggest stages in the sport, learning what it takes to perform in May and June.

Fall ball for a team with that kind of roster continuity is different than fall ball for a program in transition. The Lady Vols are fine-tuning, not building from scratch. They are staying sharp, getting game action, and keeping competitive habits fresh through October. That is a luxury Tennessee is fortunate to have.

What's Next

Tennessee's fall schedule continues through October and November with additional exhibitions at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium. Carson-Newman arrives Thursday, October 2. For fans who want an early look at the 2027 Lady Vols, the fall schedule is free to attend and represents the most accessible preview of a team that should be among the national title favorites when the spring season begins. Mardjetko and Nuwer are getting their reps. Nine starters are back. The Lady Vols are building toward something significant.
