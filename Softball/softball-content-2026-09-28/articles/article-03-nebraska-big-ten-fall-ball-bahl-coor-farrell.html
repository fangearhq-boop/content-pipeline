Nebraska Hits the Ground Running in Fall Ball — Bahl, Coor, and Farrell Make a Strong Early Statement

By Jordan Reeves | September 28, 2026

Nebraska has a score to settle.

The Cornhuskers finished the 2026 season at 43-15, made it to the Knoxville Super Regional finals, and lost to Tennessee 1-0 in a game that could have gone the other way on any given pitch. One run, one game, one out away from Omaha. For a program at Nebraska's level, that kind of near-miss doesn't fade quietly. It gets addressed.

Fall ball is where that addressing begins. And so far, it looks like Nebraska is addressing it well.

Bahl Plus Two New Pieces

Jordy Bahl is entering her senior season as one of the best pitchers in college softball. The Cornhuskers built their 2026 run around her, and she is back for one more shot at Omaha. The question heading into fall ball was which supporting pieces would step up around her.

The early answer involves two transfers. Hannah Coor came from Oklahoma — one of the premier programs in the history of the sport — bringing experience at the highest competitive level. Jesse Farrell transferred from UNLV to Nebraska, adding another piece to a lineup that needs depth around Bahl's pitching.

In Nebraska's first fall game against Colorado State, all three showed up. Coor drove in runs. Bahl contributed at the plate. Farrell hit a solo home run. Final score: Nebraska 5, Colorado State 0. It is one fall exhibition against one opponent in September, and nobody is writing dynasty proclamations from it. But the integration looked smooth, and that is the most important early data point.

The Big Ten Picture

Nebraska is not the only Big Ten program with something to prove this fall. The conference landscape is competitive, and fall ball is how programs signal where they stand heading into 2027.

In the Big 12, Oklahoma State is rebuilding with five transfer additions: pitcher Delainey Everett from Mississippi State, catcher Kennedy Proctor from Utah, and utility pieces from Houston, Ole Miss, and Wisconsin. Their fall slate includes a trip to Arkansas — a legitimate measuring stick against a program that sent Oklahoma State packing from the 2026 NCAA Tournament.

Duke finished 43-17 in 2026, losing in the Super Regionals to Arkansas. Five key players graduated, and the program is navigating a transition heading into 2027. Duke's fall action will be the first window into how they replace that production.

The common thread: fall ball 2026 is a crowded, competitive preview of a spring season that should be as compelling as any in recent memory.

What's Next

Nebraska continues its fall schedule through October and November. The Cornhuskers' biggest competitive test may come from within the Big Ten — Michigan and Ohio State both have legitimate rosters heading into 2027. For a team that came within one run of Omaha, every fall rep is preparation for a spring run that Rhonda Revelle's program is clearly building toward. Bahl's senior season starts in earnest this fall. The Cornhuskers are not interested in another near-miss.
