Torres and Kavan Together for the First Time — Texas Opens Fall Ball Tuesday Against Murray State

By Emily Rawlings | September 28, 2026

College softball has seen its share of roster upgrades. It has rarely seen anything like this.

When Texas opens fall ball Tuesday night against Murray State College at McCombs Field in Austin, Isa Torres and Teagan Kavan will take the field together as Longhorns for the first time. Torres was the 2026 D1Softball National Player of the Year at Florida State, where she hit .530 across 55 games. Kavan is the two-time Women's College World Series Most Outstanding Player — the only player in the history of the award to win it twice, and she won them back-to-back in 2025 and 2026. They are both wearing burnt orange now. The game starts at 6 PM CT. Admission is free.

Building a Three-Peat Roster

Texas has won the last two national championships. The 2025 title. The 2026 title. Now they are trying to do something that only two programs in the history of the sport have done: win three consecutive national championships.

UCLA won three straight from 1988 to 1990. Oklahoma won three straight from 2021 to 2023. Both dynasties were built on continuity, elite pitching, and one or two defining players who anchored everything else. Texas has that combination heading into 2027.

Coach Mike White added Torres from Florida State to complement what was already the deepest lineup in college softball. He added Samantha Lincoln from Texas Tech to bolster the pitching rotation behind Kavan. Sophia Bordi, a transfer from Oklahoma, joins the program and will be eligible to compete in 2027. The foundation — Kavan's return, nine-plus starters back from the WCWS squad, a system already proven at the highest level — was already strong before any of those additions.

What Tomorrow's Game Actually Means

No game against Murray State in September counts in the standings. There are no titles on the line Tuesday evening at McCombs Field. That is exactly what makes fall ball worth watching.

Fall exhibition games are the only public glimpse fans get at how rosters are integrating before the spring season begins. How Torres fits into the lineup behind Kavan. How Lincoln's arm looks alongside the returning rotation. How the team's chemistry feels in a competitive setting with a new collection of players sharing the same dugout.

These are questions that won't be answered definitively in one fall game. But they start to get answered Tuesday. And for fans who have watched this program win back-to-back national titles and are now wondering whether a third is possible, seeing Torres and Kavan together — even against Murray State — is a preview of what the spring could look like.

What's Next

Texas has six matchups scheduled in their 2026 fall ball slate. Tuesday's opener against Murray State at 6 PM CT at McCombs Field is the first. The schedule also includes a matchup against Texas A&M and Texas State, giving the Longhorns a chance to see multiple opponents before spring practice begins in earnest. The three-peat bid officially starts Tuesday evening. For those who can make it to Austin, admission is free.
