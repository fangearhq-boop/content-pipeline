### STORY 2: September 1 Countdown — 12 Days

**Long-Form Post (11:00 AM ET)**
12 days until September 1 — and for the Class of 2028, the hardest part isn't the recruiting process. It's knowing what to do when the phones start ringing all at once.

Here's what experienced recruiting families will tell you: the first 48 hours after Sept 1 are unlike anything you've prepared for. Coaches are calling the same recruits. Multiple schools may be offering at the same time. Commitment decisions that felt distant suddenly feel urgent.

The recruits who handle this well are the ones who made their decisions BEFORE the window opened — not during.

That means: rank your schools now. Know which campus felt right. Know what you value most: coaching philosophy, program culture, academics, location. Talk to your family. Have a framework.

You don't have to commit the first week. But the recruits who rush in without a plan often make decisions they later question. The ones who prepared are calm when the phones ring.

12 days. The window is coming. Are you ready? 🥎

### STORY 3: USA WNT — 23 Days

**Long-Form Post (3:00 PM ET)**
23 days until Team USA opens WBSC World Cup group play in Oklahoma City — and Group C is loaded.

The official 16-player roster is locked. Four Oklahoma Sooners (Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, Kelly Maxwell) lead the way. Veterans Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola bring 2020 Olympic experience. Seven players from the 2025 World Games gold medal team round out a deep squad.

Group C opponents:
• Netherlands — No. 6 in the world
• Mexico — No. 7
• China — No. 8

Top two teams advance to the Brisbane Finals (April 5–11, 2027) — the direct path to LA28 Olympic qualification.

USA is No. 1. This should be their tournament to advance — but the group isn't a layup. Netherlands and China are both capable of pulling off an upset.

Games begin Sept 12 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. If you're in the area — this is a must-see. 🇺🇸

### STORY 4: Oklahoma Portal — 11 Days to Deadline

**Long-Form Post (1:00 PM ET)**
The transfer portal closes in 11 days — and Oklahoma softball is making the most of every hour.

Patty Gasso is rebuilding after the Sooners' first WCWS miss since 2015, and the moves she's made this summer paint a picture of a program determined to bounce back fast.

Additions: Macie Harter joins via the transfer portal. The 2027 recruiting class is taking serious shape — Payton Westra (No. 2 overall) and Ori Mailo (No. 4 overall, who was already on campus this past season) headline a class that also includes pitchers, infielders, and outfielders across multiple signing periods.

Losses: Kasidi Pickering departed for Texas Tech. Tia Milloy transferred out. Those are significant names to lose in one cycle.

But Gasso has rebuilt before. The Sooners won three straight WCWS titles (2021–23) before falling short in 2025 and 2026.

Aug 31 is the deadline. Watch for any last-minute additions as programs finalize their rosters for the fall semester.

### STORY 5: Texas Three-Peat Roster

**Long-Form Post (8:00 PM ET)**
Texas softball's fall camp is underway — and the roster they're putting on the field in 2027 is starting to take shape.

24 players. 11 new additions. A former Oklahoma pitcher added through the transfer portal. And Teagan Kavan back as the unquestioned anchor of the staff — the first player ever to win WCWS Most Outstanding Player twice.

The question everyone in the sport is asking: can the Longhorns become only the third program in history to win three straight Women's College World Series titles?

The answer won't come until Oklahoma City in June 2027. But the roster they're assembling suggests Texas isn't treating this like a team just trying to maintain. They're trying to be the best team in the country again — for the third time in a row.

For context: only UCLA (1988–90) and Oklahoma (2021–23) have ever done it. Texas would join that list as just the third program ever.

Fall camp. New roster. Same standard. 🤘
