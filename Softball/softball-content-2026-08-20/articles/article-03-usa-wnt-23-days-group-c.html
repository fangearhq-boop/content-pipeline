USA Softball Faces Netherlands, Mexico, and China in Oklahoma City — 23 Days Out

By Jordan Reeves | I Love Softball | August 20, 2026

With the official 16-player roster locked and the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage just 23 days away, the attention for Team USA now turns to the competition. And Group C is not a group to take lightly.

USA Softball will face the Netherlands (No. 6 in the world), Mexico (No. 7), and China (No. 8) in Oklahoma City beginning September 12 at Devon Park. The top two teams from each group advance to the Brisbane Finals — the tournament that will determine which nations qualify for the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics.

The Roster USA Is Bringing

The 16-player roster announced by USA Softball on July 1 reflects both established veterans and the next generation of American talent. Four Oklahoma Sooners — Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell — anchor the squad with players who know each other and the big stage well.

Veterans Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola bring 2020 Olympic experience, a valuable resource in a tournament where the pressure of must-win games can separate teams regardless of talent. Seven players from the 2025 World Games gold medal squad also return, giving USA a significant cohesion advantage over opponents still building team chemistry.

The roster represents multiple conferences and programs, with SEC players leading the way (10 athletes), followed by Big 12 (three athletes) and Big Ten (three athletes).

What Group C Means

The Netherlands, Mexico, and China are not pushovers. Each has risen to the top tier of international softball, and in a tournament format where only two teams advance per group, an upset loss can have enormous consequences — even for the world's No. 1 team.

Netherlands (No. 6) has been one of Europe's most consistent programs, capable of pitching-and-defense style baseball that slows down powerful offensive lineups. Mexico (No. 7) has improved dramatically in recent years and is one of the more dangerous No. 7 seeds in recent World Cup memory. China (No. 8) is a disciplined, patient offensive team that has historically given USA trouble in tournament settings.

For USA, the goal is not just to advance — it is to win the group outright, carry momentum into the Brisbane Finals, and ultimately secure that LA28 Olympic berth. Every game in Oklahoma City matters in that context.

Oklahoma City as Host

Devon Park in Oklahoma City has a deep connection to USA Softball history. Hosting the Group Stage in OKC brings the team back to a city and a fan base that understands and celebrates the sport. Tickets and attendance information are available through the WBSC and USA Softball websites.

For fans in the region — especially those with connections to any of the four OU players on the roster — the Group Stage represents a rare opportunity to watch Team USA compete on home soil in a meaningful international qualifier.

What's Next

Group Stage games run September 12–16 at Devon Park, Oklahoma City. The top two teams per group advance to the Brisbane Finals, April 5–11, 2027, where LA28 Olympic qualification is at stake. Coverage details and the full WBSC schedule are available at wbsc.org and usasoftball.com.
