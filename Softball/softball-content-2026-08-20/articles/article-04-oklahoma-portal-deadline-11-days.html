Oklahoma's Roster Revolution: Portal Deadline Looms as Gasso Builds for 2027

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | August 20, 2026

The transfer portal deadline is August 31 — 11 days away — and Patty Gasso is using every hour of it. After Oklahoma's first Women's College World Series miss since 2015, the Sooners' offseason has been defined by aggressive roster reshaping through the portal and a recruiting class that suggests the program is betting on a rapid return to the sport's elite.

The roster revolution in Norman is real, and the window to add more pieces is closing fast.

What Oklahoma Has Added

Macie Harter is the most recent portal addition, joining the Sooners and bringing immediate lineup depth. Alongside the transfer additions, Oklahoma's 2027 recruiting class has become one of the more talked-about in the sport.

Payton Westra, rated No. 2 overall in the 2027 class, is committed and projected to factor into the everyday outfield. Ori Mailo, ranked No. 4 overall and the No. 1 infielder in the country, was already on campus during the 2026 season — giving her a developmental head start before her freshman year even officially begins. The class also includes pitchers, infielders, and outfield depth across multiple signing periods.

Oklahoma has secured its fourth verbal commitment to the 2027 class, and with 11 days left in the portal window, more additions remain possible.

The Losses That Triggered the Rebuild

The departures were significant. Kasidi Pickering — one of the most accomplished hitters in OU's recent history, a three-year starter who hit .384 with 50 home runs and 169 RBIs — entered the portal and committed to Texas Tech, where she joined fellow transfer Jaysoni Beachum in what became the nation's No. 1-ranked transfer class. Tia Milloy also departed.

Losing two All-Americans — one of them a player who had been at the center of OU's offensive identity for three seasons — in a single portal cycle would challenge any program. The question Gasso had to answer quickly was whether Oklahoma could attract comparable talent to replace them before the window closed.

The early answer: yes, though through a different mechanism. Rather than replacing proven transfers one-for-one, OU is betting heavily on incoming recruits who could develop into stars under Gasso's coaching staff.

Why the WCWS Miss Matters

Oklahoma's streak of WCWS appearances was among the defining accomplishments of Gasso's tenure. Three straight national championships (2021–23) had established the Sooners as the sport's unquestioned standard. Missing the WCWS in 2025 and 2026 — back-to-back — carries a different weight than a single down year.

The urgency in the portal and recruiting activity reflects that weight. Programs watching OU's roster shake out know that Gasso's rebuild is a high-stakes bet on returning to the top within two or three seasons. Every commitment to the 2027 class, every portal addition, is a piece of that plan.

What's Next

The transfer portal deadline is August 31. Any additional additions to Oklahoma's roster for the 2026-27 academic year must come through before that date. The 2027 recruiting class continues to take shape through the fall, with September 1 — just 12 days away — opening the first-contact window for the Class of 2028. Preseason rankings and fall practice reports will start filtering out as programs open camp over the coming weeks.
