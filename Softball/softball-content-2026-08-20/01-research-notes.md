## STORY 2: September 1 Recruiting Window

**Source:** Multiple (NextCommit, NCSA, 12sportsconsulting.com)
**Confidence:** HIGH for Sept 1 tradition framework; MEDIUM for potential Sept 9 date change

- September 1 is the traditional first-contact window for D-I softball coaches and Class of 2028 recruits
- Before Sept 1: coaches can send printed materials and some electronic materials, cannot call or text
- After Sept 1: calls, texts, official/unofficial visits all permitted
- Class of 2028 = current rising juniors (HS class of 2028)
- Commitment wave typically hits 2–3 weeks after Sept 1
- **NOTE:** One search source (NextCommit) indicated the NCAA Division I Cabinet may have changed the date to September 9, 2026 (permanent change effective August 1, 2026). TREAT AS MEDIUM CONFIDENCE — conflicts with established pipeline framing. Will use Sept 1 language in articles but not state exact countdown number confidently.
