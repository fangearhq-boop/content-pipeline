Series 3 Captains Are Set — Kowalik Leads the 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup Heading Into Aug. 24

By Emily Rawlings

August 14, 2026

The 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup has its Series 3 captains: Kayla Kowalik, Keilani Ricketts, Danieca Coffey, and Devyn Netz. And heading into the penultimate series, Kowalik — the 2025 All-Star Cup champion — holds the No. 1 spot on the individual points leaderboard with two series still to play.

Series 3 opens August 24 at 3:00 PM ET at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois, with a doubleheader: Team Coffey versus Team Ricketts first, followed by Team Netz versus Team Kowalik. All games broadcast on ESPN+, MLB Network, and CBS Sports Network.

How Kowalik Got Here

Kowalik was unavailable for Series 1 (August 8-9), sitting out the competition's opening round while Garcia, Ricketts, and Corrick captained the first set of teams. When she returned for Series 2, she returned as a captain and made up for lost time immediately — leading Team Kowalik to an unbeaten record through the second series and accumulating enough individual points to take over the overall lead.

That trajectory — missing a full series, returning as captain of an unbeaten team, and vaulting to No. 1 on the leaderboard — is a reminder of why Kowalik was the 2025 All-Star Cup champion. She doesn't just compete. She dominates the moments that matter.

The Rookie Movers From Series 2

While Kowalik grabbed the headlines in Series 2, two rookies made the kind of leaderboard moves that signal they intend to be factors in the final two series. Emma Lemley rose 26 spots in the individual standings after Series 2. Korbe Otis moved up 22 spots to reach No. 10 overall — a significant leap for a first-year player in a competition dominated by experienced professionals.

The AUSL format rewards individual performance throughout each series, not just team wins. MVP designations, statistical bonuses, and win points all factor into the standings. That means a single standout series — or a single standout game — can dramatically shift where a player sits on the leaderboard. Lemley and Otis are proving that out.

Sami Williams Is Still in This Race

Sami Williams — the AUSL's 2026 regular-season Hitter of the Year — earned Series 1 MVP honors with a two-run double in the third inning of the opener on August 8 for Team Garcia. That MVP earned her 60 individual points and established her as the early leaderboard leader before Kowalik's return reshuffled the standings.

Williams remains in contention. The gap to Kowalik is real, but with two full series remaining — and each series offering multiple MVP opportunities and win bonuses — the race is far from over.

What's Next

Series 3 opens August 24 at Parkway Bank Sports Complex. The four captains — Kowalik, Ricketts, Coffey, Netz — will draft entirely new teams from the 45-player pool before the series begins. Series 4 and the conclusion of the 2026 All-Star Cup follow the week of August 28-30, with the individual champion crowned when the final standings are tallied.

Kowalik leads. Ten days remain until the competition resumes. Follow all the action at theausl.com and on ESPN+, MLB Network, and CBS Sports Network.
