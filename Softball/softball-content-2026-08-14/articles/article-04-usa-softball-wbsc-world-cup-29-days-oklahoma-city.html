29 Days to Oklahoma City: USA Softball Eyes the World Cup Group Stage at Devon Park

By Emily Rawlings

August 14, 2026

Twenty-nine days. That is how long until the USA Women's National Team opens the 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. The date is September 12. The opponents are Netherlands, Mexico, China, Brazil, and New Zealand. The stakes are a spot in the 2027 World Cup Finals in Brisbane, Australia.

The USA has competed at Devon Park before — very recently. At the 2026 USA Softball International Cup, held July 30 through August 3 at the same venue, the Women's National Team went 8-2 and won gold, defeating Japan 2-0 in the championship game. Montana Fouts pitched a one-hit shutout in that final. Maya Brady was named Tournament MVP. The team knows Devon Park. The question is whether the World Cup competition will be steeper than the IC field — and it will be.

The Roster

The USA Softball 16-player roster for the World Cup Group Stage was announced July 1. It features Olympic veterans Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola alongside International Cup gold medalists Fouts, Brady, Jayda Coleman, and Janae Jefferson. The pitching staff runs deep, with multiple options capable of handling the starting role against top-tier international competition.

The group stage will give fans a first extended look at how the coaching staff sequences its pitching and constructs lineups against opponents who represent the best of their respective international programs. Netherlands, in particular, is one of the strongest European programs in the sport and has reached the World Cup medal rounds in recent cycles.

The Format and What's at Stake

The Group Oklahoma stage runs September 12-16 and features a round-robin format among the six nations. The top finishers from the group advance to the playoff bracket. The ultimate prize — a direct berth in the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals in Brisbane, Australia — goes to the top two finishers from each qualifying group worldwide.

Brisbane 2027 (April 5-11) is the sport's biggest stage at the international level. For the USA, reaching the Finals is the expectation, not the ceiling. The goal is not simply to qualify — it's to arrive in Brisbane as one of the favorites to win the whole thing.

What's Next

The USA Women's National Team opens Group Stage play September 12 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. Game times and opponent matchup schedule will be confirmed by USA Softball and WBSC closer to the event. Follow USA Softball at usasoftball.com and WBSC at wbsc.org.
