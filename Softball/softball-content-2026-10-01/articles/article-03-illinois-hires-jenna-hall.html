Illinois Turns to Its Own: Jenna Hall Named New Softball Head Coach

By Jordan Reeves

October 1, 2026

Illinois didn't have to search far to find its next softball coach. The answer was already part of the Illini family.

The University of Illinois has hired Jenna Hall as its new head softball coach, completing a quick search following the departure of Tyra Perry after 11 seasons. Hall is a former Illini first baseman who built a decorated playing career in Champaign before being inducted into the Illinois Athletics Hall of Fame in 2019.

For a program that has struggled to find its footing in recent years — Illinois went 15-39 overall and 3-20 in Big Ten play during the 2026 season, its third consecutive sub-.500 year — the hire carries a sense of homecoming and renewal.

A Decorated Illinois Legacy

Hall's playing credentials are deep. A four-time All-Big Ten First Team honoree during her time in Champaign, she earned 2006 First Team All-American honors — the kind of individual accolade that reflects a player at the peak of her abilities. Her ties to the program are genuine, not just a title on paper, and that connection has already generated significant support from the Illinois softball community.

After her playing career, Hall developed her coaching credentials before returning to Champaign in this new role. She arrives at a program that faces real challenges — not just in the win-loss column but in rebuilding recruiting pipelines and reestablishing the Illini as a competitive presence in one of college softball's most demanding conferences.

A Program in Need of a Reset

Perry's tenure ended after an 87-125 overall record that included four consecutive seasons of decline in the Big Ten. The 2026 campaign, in which Illinois finished 3-20 in conference play, was the program's lowest point. Perry had been there for every year of the slide, and when the end came, it wasn't a surprise.

What was something of a surprise was the speed of the hire. Illinois moved quickly, identifying Hall as its target and completing the process in short order. That urgency signals that the athletic department understands what's at stake in the next recruiting cycle — the window to recover the 2027 recruiting class is narrow, and every week without a coach matters.

What's Next

Hall will immediately begin the work of reshaping Illinois softball's roster and recruiting class heading into the 2026-27 offseason. With fall practice already underway for programs across the country, her first priority will be getting to know the players already in the program and evaluating what the team has. The 2027 regular season will be Hall's first real test — and with the Big Ten as demanding as ever, her Illinois roots give her a head start in understanding what the job demands. I Love Softball will track the rebuild from day one.
