Lightning Cancels Oklahoma's Fall Opener, But Ella Parker Stole the Show First

By Sarah Mitchell

October 1, 2026

Oklahoma softball's fall Battle Series opener barely got started before the weather had other plans — but in the brief window before the lightning arrived, Ella Parker made sure the 2026-27 season announced itself with a bang.

Parker drilled a home run on the very first at-bat of the game, launching a shot to right-center field off pitcher Audrey Lowry before the clouds rolled in over Norman on September 30. It was the only highlight that needed to happen.

Officials had already moved the game up 2.5 hours — from the original 6:30 PM start to 4 PM — hoping to beat the forecast inclement weather that had been tracking toward Norman all day. The precaution bought the teams some time, but not enough. Lightning moved into the area after roughly 45 minutes of play. The delay stretched another 45 minutes before officials made the call: the game was officially cancelled.

One At-Bat, One Moment

In a game defined by what didn't happen, Parker's home run stands as the defining image of the afternoon. Batting first for Team 1, she drove the first pitch of the fall season over the outfield fence — a statement of intent, even if the game itself never got the chance to deliver more of them.

The Sooners enter 2026-27 as one of the most anticipated teams in the country, with a roster that includes standout players who will get their first real public looks over the fall exhibition slate. This opener was supposed to be the first glimpse of what head coach Patty Gasso has assembled for the upcoming season. Saturday's game provided only a tease.

Next Up: Tarleton State on October 4

The Sooners will have their next opportunity to take the field on Saturday, October 4, when they host Tarleton State at Love's Field. First pitch is scheduled for 1 PM.

Oklahoma's fall slate serves as a preview for one of the most anticipated seasons in recent memory. The program is coming off a 2026 season that ended with a loss to Texas in the postseason — a result that has fueled an offseason of roster reshaping and renewed hunger in Norman.

The cancelled opener is a minor setback in what figures to be a long fall of meaningful development time. Parker's leadoff home run, brief as it was, offered a reminder that when the games do get played, there will be plenty to watch.

What's Next

Oklahoma is back in action on October 4 against Tarleton State at Love's Field (1 PM CT). Future fall dates include additional Battle Series games and non-counting exhibition contests that will give fans their first extended look at the 2026-27 roster.
