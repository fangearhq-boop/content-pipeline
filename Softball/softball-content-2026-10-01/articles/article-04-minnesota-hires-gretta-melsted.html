Minnesota Lands NFCA Hall of Famer Gretta Melsted as New Head Coach

By Sarah Mitchell

October 1, 2026

Minnesota didn't settle when they went looking for a coach. They found exactly what they needed.

The University of Minnesota has hired Gretta Melsted as its new head softball coach, replacing Piper Ritter who was let go in May 2026 after six seasons. Melsted arrives in Minneapolis as one of the most accomplished coaches in the history of college softball, carrying an 813-313 career record, a Division II national championship, and a December 2024 induction into the NFCA Hall of Fame.

For a program that hasn't reached the NCAA Tournament since 2023, the hire is a significant statement of intent.

A Proven Builder

Melsted spent 20 seasons at Augustana University in Sioux Falls, South Dakota, transforming the Vikings into one of the country's elite Division II programs. Her 813-313 record doesn't tell the full story — she guided Augustana to 14 NCAA Tournament appearances and won the 2019 Division II national championship, cementing her legacy as one of the most successful coaches at any level.

The NFCA Hall of Fame induction in December 2024 was the formal acknowledgment of what the softball world already knew: Melsted is an elite coach who turns programs into winners. She has never had a losing season at Augustana. The winning is built into her system.

Taking on a New Challenge

Moving from Division II to the Big Ten is a significant step up in competition, recruiting demands, and institutional pressure. But the logic of the hire is clear. Melsted's ability to recruit, develop players, and build team culture is exactly what Minnesota needs after years of declining results under Ritter, who went 16-36 overall (7-17 in the Big Ten) in her final season before being dismissed in May.

The Gophers' 2027 recruiting class took a hit when standout 2027 recruit Kendal Headley decommitted following Ritter's dismissal — a reminder of just how quickly a coaching change can ripple through a program's pipeline. Melsted's first job in Minneapolis will be to reassure committed players and start rebuilding that pipeline in earnest.

What's Next

Melsted will begin work immediately on the Minnesota roster and 2026-27 fall practice evaluations. Her first full season as a Gophers head coach will come in 2027, when she'll face the full weight of Big Ten competition. The early indication — a Hall of Fame hire who has never had a losing season — suggests Minnesota softball is pointed in the right direction. I Love Softball will follow the Gophers' rebuild throughout the offseason and into the new season.
