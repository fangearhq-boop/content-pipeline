Fall Ball Nation: How Softball Programs Are Preparing for 2026-27

By Emily Rawlings

October 1, 2026

October is here, and that means fall softball is in full swing across the country. From Oregon's free public exhibitions to Louisiana's four-game slate, programs everywhere are giving fans their first glimpse at rosters that have been retooled, rebuilt, and reloaded through the offseason and transfer portal.

Here's a look at what's happening as the 2026-27 season begins to take shape.

Oregon Goes Big with Eight Free Fall Games

One of the most fan-friendly fall schedules in the country belongs to the Oregon Ducks. The Big Ten program is hosting eight free fall exhibition games at Jane Sanders Stadium in Eugene, running through November 1. The schedule includes matchups against Clackamas Community College (October 11), Western Oregon (October 14), Western Washington in a doubleheader (October 18), additional doubleheaders on October 24-25, and a final intrasquad scrimmage on November 1.

For Oregon fans, these games offer a chance to see new faces in the lineup and evaluate how head coach Melyssa Lombardi's program is evolving heading into its second year in the Big Ten.

Louisiana Adds Four Fall Exhibitions

Louisiana (Sun Belt) has unveiled its own fall schedule, including a home game against McNeese on October 9 and a home doubleheader weekend featuring Mississippi Gulf Coast and Pearl River on October 14-15. The exhibitions give the Ragin' Cajuns evaluation time for their 2026-27 roster before the official season begins in the spring.

Transfer Portal: 250+ Movers and Shakers

The transfer portal remains one of the defining forces in college softball, and the 2026 cycle is no different. The ON3 portal tracker is monitoring more than 250 transfers across the country as programs look to plug holes and upgrade rosters.

LSU is making noise with the commitment of Tori Edwards, identified by recruiting analysts as the top-ranked transfer in the 2026 cycle. The Tigers continue to be among the sport's top destinations for portal talent.

Meanwhile, Tennessee's Ella Dodge has committed to Florida State — one of several transfers in the Vols-to-ACC pipeline that has emerged in recent offseasons. Fresno State also added significant pieces through the portal: designated player Kaelia Ricks from Ohio State, pitchers Samantha Lotus and Delaney Wright, and infielder Jayden Ramos from Arkansas.
