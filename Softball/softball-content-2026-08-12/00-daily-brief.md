### STORY 2: LLSWS Champions Head Home — Johnston County NC Celebrates
**Tier:** 2 | **Status:** FOLLOW UP
**Who/What:** Johnston County Little League (Clayton, NC) returned home as 2026 Little League Softball World Series champions after defeating Owensboro, Kentucky 2-0 on August 10 at Stallings Stadium in Greenville, NC. Pitcher Anna-Lynn Clark threw a complete-game shutout (93 pitches, 3 hits, 6 Ks) in the championship — fewer than 24 hours after a 109-pitch, 11-K shutout in the semifinals. Clark finished the tournament with 41 strikeouts, most of any pitcher in the field. Belle Cox drove in the first run with a single in the second inning. It was North Carolina's 4th LLSWS title (2015, 2019, 2024, 2026) and the first time Johnston County has represented NC at the LLSWS.
**Why it matters:** Historic pitching performance, homecoming celebration angle, NC softball legacy story.
