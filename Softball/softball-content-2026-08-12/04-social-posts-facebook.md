### STORY 2: LLSWS Johnston County Homecoming

**Long-Form Post — Window: 9:00 AM ET**
Johnston County is coming home as champions.

The 2026 Little League Softball World Series title belongs to North Carolina — won Sunday in Greenville with a 2-0 shutout over Owensboro, Kentucky. It's North Carolina's fourth LLSWS championship (joining 2015, 2019, and 2024), and the first time Johnston County has represented the state in the tournament.

The story of this tournament is Anna-Lynn Clark. The Johnston County pitcher entered Sunday's championship game having just thrown a 109-pitch, 11-strikeout complete-game shutout in the semifinal — the day before. She came back and did it again: 93 pitches, 3 hits, 6 strikeouts, zero runs. Over the entire LLSWS tournament, Clark struck out 41 batters — more than any other pitcher in the field.

Belle Cox drove in the first run of the championship with a single in the bottom of the second inning. Johnston County added a second run on a Kentucky error in the third. Clark did the rest.

North Carolina heads home. And Johnston County does it for the first time. 🥎

### STORY 3: Texas Tech Softball Portal Update

**Long-Form Post — Window: 3:00 PM ET**
The Texas Tech softball roster for 2027 is still taking shape — and there was more movement this summer.

Infielder Bailey Lindemuth has entered the transfer portal after one season at Texas Tech. The third baseman hit .396 with 50 hits, 8 doubles, and 5 home runs across 68 games and starts with the Red Raiders.

Meanwhile, the Red Raiders added Bradi Gallaway from Louisiana Tech. As a freshman with the Lady Techsters, Gallaway hit .317 with 11 home runs, 12 doubles, and 47 RBI — an impressive first college season.

All of this comes on top of an already-stacked incoming class. NiJaree Canady enters her senior season in Lubbock after being selected No. 2 overall in the 2026 AUSL Draft by the Texas Volts. Kasidi Pickering arrived from Oklahoma (.370 average, 50 career home runs), Natalie Heavener came from LSU (139.1 innings pitched, 2.81 ERA), and Chelsea Beachum brings elite offensive production (.406 avg, .549 OBP, .671 SLG).

Texas Tech's 2026 transfer class was ranked No. 1 nationally by Softball America. Fall camp is just ahead — this roster is going to be something. 🥎 #TexasTech

### STORY 4: USA WNT World Cup Countdown

**Long-Form Post — Window: 5:30 PM ET**
31 days. That's how long until Team USA opens the 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage at Devon Park in Oklahoma City.

The 16-player roster was announced on July 2. Oklahoma leads all programs with four athletes on the World Cup squad: Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell. Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola — both veterans of the 2020 U.S. Olympic Team — anchor the group alongside Megan Grant.

Group Oklahoma features five nations: Netherlands (world No. 6), Mexico (No. 7), China (No. 8), Brazil (No. 17 — Brazil stepped in after South Africa withdrew), and New Zealand (No. 23). The top two teams advance to the 2027 Finals.

The USA has recent momentum: they went 8-2 overall at the 2026 International Cup at this same Devon Park venue from July 30 through August 3, competing against six other World Cup nations.

Group stage runs September 12-16. 🥎 #SoftballUSA

### STORY 5: Aspen Boulware / South Carolina

**Long-Form Post — Window: 1:00 PM ET**
South Carolina just landed one of the most highly-recruited softball players in the country.

Aspen Boulware — who was the No. 1-ranked prospect in the Class of 2027 nationally — reclassified to the Class of 2026 and enrolled early at the University of South Carolina. After reclassifying, she ranked as the No. 1 middle infielder in the 2026 class.

Boulware isn't just a highly-ranked recruit on paper. She proved it on the international stage. At the 2025 U-18 WBSC Women's World Cup, she earned All-World Team honors and led the entire tournament in runs scored as Team USA won gold.

She'll be eligible to compete for the Gamecocks starting in the 2027 spring season. South Carolina's 2026 signing class ranked No. 9 nationally before Boulware's arrival.

This is a major win for the Gamecocks program. 🥎 #SCarolina #Softball
