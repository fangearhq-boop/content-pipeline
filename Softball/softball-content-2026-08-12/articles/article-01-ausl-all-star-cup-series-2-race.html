With Kowalik Out, the 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup Is Anyone's Race

By Sarah Mitchell

August 12, 2026

The 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup has entered Series 2 at the Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois, and for the first time in recent memory, the individual championship race is running without a defending champion in the field. Kayla Kowalik — who won the 2025 title with 1,570 points — was announced unavailable before the competition began and will not compete in the four-week event.

That absence has completely reshaped who's expected to contend for the individual crown this summer. Forty-five athletes entered the 2026 All-Star Cup field across four series running through August 30. In Kowalik's place, a handful of players are positioned to make a run — and the Series 1 results already gave the field something to pay attention to.

Williams Wins Series 1 MVP

Sami Williams delivered the breakout performance of the 2026 opening week. Competing for Team Garcia — captained by Olympic gold medalist Rachel Garcia — Williams drove home two runs with a clutch double in the third inning on August 8, lifting her team to the Series 1 victory. For her performance, Williams earned Series 1 MVP honors, the first of the 2026 All-Star Cup.

It was a statement moment for Williams. With Kowalik's seat empty, every MVP award and team win this August comes with additional individual-race significance. The points leader after four series becomes the 2026 All-Star Cup champion.

The Roster Shuffle Behind the Scenes

Kowalik's absence triggered a cascade of changes before the first game was played. Georgina Corrick — the 2026 AUSL Player of the Year with a 2.04 ERA — was elevated from player to Team 3 captain for Series 1, stepping into the role Kowalik was set to hold. Bubba Nickles-Camarena of the Oklahoma City Spark was added to the player pool to replace Kowalik on the roster. Michaela Edenfield of the Texas Volts also joined as a replacement for another roster spot.

The three Series 1 captains as the competition opened: Rachel Garcia, Keilani Ricketts, and Corrick. The redraft format gives captains significant influence over who plays for them each series, meaning the team compositions will continue to shift week to week through the August 30 finale.

What Series 2 Looks Like

With Series 2 games ongoing this week, the field is still sorting out where the individual points are stacking up. The format plays four complete series — each featuring a new draft, new teams, and new MVP opportunities — before a champion is crowned at the end of August.

The 2026 All-Star Cup is broadcasting on ESPN+, MLB Network, and CBS from the Rosemont complex throughout the month. Fan voting determined several spots in the player pool, adding players like fan-elected picks to the 45-person field alongside the league's top performers.

One thing is clear: no one from this year's field has held this trophy before. For the first time since Kowalik's 2025 win, a new All-Star Cup champion is guaranteed — and the competition to claim that title begins in earnest this week.
