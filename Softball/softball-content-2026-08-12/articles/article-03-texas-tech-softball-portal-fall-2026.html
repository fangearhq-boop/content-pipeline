Texas Tech Softball: Lindemuth Exits, Gallaway Arrives, and the 2027 Roster Takes Shape

By Jordan Reeves

August 12, 2026

The transfer portal never really closes for Texas Tech softball. The Red Raiders — who held the No. 1-ranked transfer class in college softball for the 2026 season per Softball America — have seen more roster movement this summer, with one player heading out and a talented freshman addition coming in ahead of the 2027 campaign.

Infielder Bailey Lindemuth has entered the transfer portal after one season in Lubbock. The third baseman contributed meaningful production in her time at Texas Tech, hitting .396 with 50 hits, 8 doubles, 5 home runs, and 32 RBI across 68 games. She was a reliable piece for the Red Raiders, but with the program's roster continuing to evolve, Lindemuth is looking for a new home.

Gallaway Commits to Fill the Gap

While Lindemuth heads into the portal, Louisiana Tech freshman Bradi Gallaway has committed to Texas Tech. Gallaway's first college season at Louisiana Tech turned heads: she hit .317 with 11 home runs, 12 doubles, and 47 RBI as a freshman. Those are numbers that make a program with national title aspirations take notice.

Gallaway gives the Red Raiders another bat with real power potential at the corner or middle infield spots. At 15 of 23 roster players entering 2026 having not started their careers in Lubbock, Texas Tech has built its identity around finding talent through the transfer portal — and Gallaway fits that mold.

The Core That's Already There

The portal movement is happening around a nucleus that already looks like a contender. NiJaree Canady — widely considered one of the premier pitchers in the country — enters her senior season at Texas Tech after being selected No. 2 overall in the 2026 AUSL Draft by the Texas Volts. Canady's return alone makes the Red Raiders a national championship conversation.

Alongside Canady, Texas Tech has assembled a collection of elite transfers from across the sport. Kasidi Pickering arrived from Oklahoma, bringing a .370 average and 50 career home runs. Natalie Heavener transferred from LSU after posting a 2.81 ERA across 139.1 innings of work. Chelsea Beachum provides one of the more complete offensive profiles in the country at .406 average, .549 on-base percentage, and .671 slugging.

When you look at those names together — Canady, Pickering, Heavener, Beachum — it is easy to understand why Softball America ranked Texas Tech's transfer class No. 1 nationally for 2026. The 2027 roster was always going to be in flux at the edges while that core remained intact.

What's Next

Texas Tech will enter fall camp with one of the most talented rosters in the country. The Red Raiders fell short of a national championship in the WCWS Final, and that experience — combined with this roster composition — will drive expectations heading into 2027. Lindemuth's landing spot will be one to watch in the portal, while Gallaway's arrival gives Texas Tech additional depth at the corners for next spring.
