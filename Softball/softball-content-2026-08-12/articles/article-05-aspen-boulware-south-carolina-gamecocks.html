Aspen Boulware Reclassifies and Enrolls at South Carolina — The Gamecocks Land a Future Star

By Emily Rawlings

August 12, 2026

South Carolina just pulled off one of the biggest recruiting wins in program history. Aspen Boulware — the No. 1-ranked prospect in the Class of 2027 nationally — reclassified to the Class of 2026 and enrolled early at the University of South Carolina. She will be eligible to compete in the 2027 spring season, meaning the Gamecocks add an elite shortstop to their lineup one year ahead of schedule.

Boulware's reclassification dropped her ranking within the 2026 class — where she'd now be evaluated against prospects who have already been committed for a year or longer — but the result is the same: South Carolina has a player who was the top-ranked recruit in her national class, and she's already on campus.

Who Aspen Boulware Is

Before Boulware's name appeared in any college recruiting database, she was proving herself on the biggest international stage available to her age group. At the 2025 U-18 WBSC Women's World Cup, Boulware earned All-World Team honors and led the entire tournament in runs scored as Team USA took home the gold medal.

That kind of international resume isn't common for a shortstop still working toward a high school diploma. Boulware's combination of elite fielding ability, offensive production, and proven performance under international competition pressure put her in a category well above the typical high-profile recruit. The No. 1 national ranking for her class wasn't a projection — it was a verdict on what she had already shown.

What It Means for South Carolina

The Gamecocks' 2026 signing class already ranked No. 9 nationally before Boulware's early enrollment. Adding the top middle infielder in the class — regardless of the reclassification technicality — gives South Carolina a foundational piece at the most demanding defensive position on the field.

South Carolina softball has been building steadily as a program. Landing a player of Boulware's caliber accelerates that trajectory and signals to future recruits that the Gamecocks are a legitimate destination for elite talent. An early enrollee of this profile also gives the coaching staff a full extra spring to integrate her into the system before she plays her first official game in 2027.

The Class of 2026 She Joined

The 2026 recruiting class Boulware joined has significant depth at the top. After her reclassification, the class included No. 1 pitcher prospect Caroline Stanton (committed to Florida) and Payton Westra (committed to Oklahoma), among others. The SEC has dominated 2026 recruiting, with nine of the top ten school recruiting classes nationally belonging to SEC programs, led by Oklahoma.

Boulware's early arrival at South Carolina positions the program to compete for SEC and national relevance earlier than many expected — and gives the Gamecocks a signature name to build around for years to come.

What's Next

Boulware will practice with the Gamecocks through the fall and be eligible to compete starting in the 2027 spring season. South Carolina's full 2027 roster and schedule information will become available closer to the start of the season. Keep an eye on gamecocksonline.com for program updates as fall ball begins.
