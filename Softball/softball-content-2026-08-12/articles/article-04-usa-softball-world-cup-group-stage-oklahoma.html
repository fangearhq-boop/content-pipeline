31 Days to Devon Park: USA Softball's World Cup Group Stage Set for Oklahoma City

By Sarah Mitchell

August 12, 2026

Thirty-one days from now, Team USA opens the 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. The 16-player roster is confirmed, the competition group is finalized, and the USA comes in with fresh momentum from a strong run at the International Cup at this same venue just two weeks ago.

The Group Oklahoma field features five nations — Netherlands, Mexico, China, Brazil, and New Zealand — with the top two finishers from the six-team group stage earning spots in the 2027 Finals. The United States, ranked No. 2 in the world, will be heavy favorites to advance, but the group includes legitimate competition in a format that allows no margin for significant stumbles.

Oklahoma Leads All Schools With Four Spots

The University of Oklahoma leads all programs with four athletes on the 2026 World Cup roster: Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell. That level of representation from a single school reflects both Oklahoma's sustained dominance in college softball and the pipeline the program has built into USA Softball's national team structure.

The roster, announced July 2, also features familiar names from USA Softball's recent international competition history. Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola — both veterans of the 2020 U.S. Olympic Team — return to anchor the group. Megan Grant is also on the 16-player squad. Seven athletes on the World Cup roster competed on the 2025 World Games gold medal team.

The Group Oklahoma Field

The 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup features 18 nations split across three groups of six competing in Oklahoma City, Lima, and Prague. Group Oklahoma's five opponents represent a range of world rankings:

  • Netherlands — world No. 6
  • Mexico — world No. 7
  • China — world No. 8
  • Brazil — world No. 17 (replaced South Africa, who withdrew from the competition)
  • New Zealand — world No. 23

The group plays a full round-robin format September 12-16. Brazil's inclusion came after South Africa withdrew from the competition; the swap adds a team with World Cup experience to the Oklahoma City field.

International Cup Provides the Blueprint

The USA used the 2026 International Cup — held at Devon Park July 30 through August 3 — as a direct warmup for the World Cup Group Stage. Competing against six other World Cup nations, Team USA went 8-2 overall across the week-long tournament. The familiar venue, the competition level, and the roster chemistry built during the International Cup should translate well when the World Cup begins on September 12.

What's Next

Group Oklahoma opens September 12 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City, with play running through September 16. The top two finishers advance to the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals. Fans can follow the competition at wbsc.org and through USA Softball's official channels at usasoftball.com.
