LLSWS Day 2: Johnston County Dominates Canada, Latin America Upsets Asia-Pacific

By Jordan Reeves

August 4, 2026

Monday was the biggest day of the 2026 Little League Softball World Series. Five games played at Stallings Stadium in Greenville, North Carolina, and the bracket is taking shape quickly. The host-state team made their first-ever World Series appearance count. A Puerto Rican pitcher put together a performance that will be talked about long after the tournament ends. Southeast continues to roll. And now, four teams face elimination starting today.

North Carolina 16, Canada 4

Johnston County Little League — representing North Carolina, the host state, in their first-ever LLSWS appearance — saved the tournament's opening statement for Monday. The game had originally been scheduled for Sunday evening at 7 PM ET before rain forced the postponement. Monday morning at 9 AM, they made Canada wait no longer.

Canada (Repentigny, Quebec) led 3-2 after the first inning. It looked like a competitive game unfolding. Then North Carolina erupted. Twelve runs in the bottom of the third inning. The game was functionally over by the time the inning ended. Kinsley Godwin and Abbey Robinson each had multiple hits for the host team. Pitcher Kathryn Utech added four strikeouts across her two innings of work. Final score: 16-4. For a team making their first World Series appearance, that is a debut to remember.

Latin America 7, Asia-Pacific 2

The most impressive pitching performance of Day 2 came from Guayama, Puerto Rico's Latin America squad — and it came against the team that had won their Day 1 game and entered Monday with confidence. Anahia Nieves struck out 12 batters across six innings of work, allowing just two hits. In a 7-2 final, that is the kind of dominant outing that announces a team as more than just a participant. Migdalee Mejia went 2-for-3 with 3 RBI at the plate.

Asia-Pacific, representing Sangō, Japan — which had won its Day 1 game against Mid-Atlantic — now sits in the loser bracket. They will need to win out to stay alive. Latin America moves forward with a genuine ace who proved on Monday she can shut down international-caliber competition.

Southeast 15, Europe-Africa 0

Lake Mary, Florida's Southeast team hasn't slowed down after a 14-0 Day 1 win over Northwest. They run-ruled Bologna, Italy's Europe-Africa squad in just three innings Monday, 15-0. Seven different players got hits. Zoey Brandon and Avery Little each had three hits in the game. If Southeast is supposed to be the dominant team in this bracket, they haven't given anyone a reason to argue against that yet.

Europe-Africa joins Canada in the loser bracket, with both teams now facing elimination in today's Day 3 action.

What Day 3 Looks Like

Tuesday, August 4 marks the first day of loser-bracket elimination play at the 2026 LLSWS. Four games are on the schedule at Stallings Stadium, all on ESPN networks. Canada and Europe-Africa face each other at 7 PM ET — the loser goes home. Two other first-round loser-bracket matchups will also play out during the day.

North Carolina, Southeast, and Latin America appear to be the teams with the most momentum through the first two days. The double-elimination format means a single loss doesn't end a team's World Series, but it does narrow the margin for error significantly. Every run in every inning is now consequential.

What's Next

The 2026 LLSWS continues through August 9 at Stallings Stadium in Greenville, North Carolina, with all 22 games on the ESPN family of networks. Free admission for all games. Today's four games begin the process of eliminating teams from the double-elimination bracket, and by the end of Tuesday's slate, the field will be narrowed. The championship game is August 9. Check the Little League website and ESPN for the full broadcast schedule.
