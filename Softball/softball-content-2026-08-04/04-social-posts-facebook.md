The 2026 USA Softball International Cup is over, and the USA Women's National Team is leaving Devon Park in Oklahoma City with a gold medal.

The WNT and Japan both arrived at the gold medal game unbeaten. The WNT beat the USA Elite Team 6-2 in the semifinal. The Elite Team and Canada played a bronze medal game to close out the day.

**Context:**
The International Cup is a high-level international event featuring some of the world's top softball nations. It's not a direct World Cup qualifier — the USA will compete in WBSC Group Stage competition in September — but it serves as premium international preparation against top opponents in an elimination format.

For this group of players, a 2-0 gold medal win over No. 1 Japan, with Montana Fouts nearly untouchable, is exactly the kind of closing statement they needed heading into the competitive cycle. 🥎🇺🇸

Monday was the biggest day of the 2026 Little League Softball World Series so far. Five games played at Stallings Stadium in Greenville, North Carolina, and here's what happened:

Pitcher Anahia Nieves was the story: 12 strikeouts across 6 innings, allowing just 2 hits. Migdalee Mejia added 3 RBI going 2-for-3 at the plate. This team has a real ace.

### STORY 3: September 1 Recruiting Window — 28 Days Out

**FB Long-Form — Post Time: 1:00 PM ET**

🥎 **SEPTEMBER 1 IS 28 DAYS AWAY — AND THE PHONES ARE ABOUT TO RING**

Every year, September 1 is the most important date on the college softball recruiting calendar. For the Class of 2028, it is coming up in 28 days.

Here's what September 1 means: it's the first date Division I coaches are permitted to initiate direct contact with Class of 2028 recruits. Before September 1, coaches can send printed and electronic materials — but they cannot call, text, or arrange meetings or visits. On September 1, all of that changes.

**Why it matters this year:**
The 2026 summer showcase season is essentially over. PGF Nationals (both weeks), major regional showcases, and the LLSWS are all done or wrapping up. The data is in. The college coaches have watched thousands of hours of film and in-person competition, and their priority lists for the Class of 2028 are finalized.

The programs that call on September 1 (or the first day or two of September) are sending a clear message: you are a top priority for us. Programs that wait until October or November are telling a different story. Early calls reflect genuine organizational interest.

**For families:** If a college coach calls your daughter on September 1, take that seriously. If you don't hear from anyone you wanted to hear from, fall league and junior-year performance in front of new evaluators is still the path.

The clock is ticking. 28 days. 📞🥎

Edwards gives Florida another middle-of-the-order bat in a lineup that didn't need much help — but just got some anyway.

Oklahoma. Tennessee. Oklahoma State. Alabama. These programs aren't done either. But when the portal fully settled, Texas Tech and Florida made the biggest single-player upgrades at the position-player level. Fall practice is weeks away. The 2027 race is very real. 🥎

### STORY 5: 2026 Asian Games Softball — 53 Days Out

**FB Long-Form — Post Time: 5:30 PM ET**

🥎 **ASIAN GAMES SOFTBALL — 53 DAYS OUT. THE PREP WORK IS HAPPENING NOW.**

Women's softball at the 2026 Asian Games runs September 26–October 3 at Anjō Sports Park Softball Ground in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan. Eight teams will compete: Japan, China, Chinese Taipei, Philippines, South Korea, Hong Kong, Singapore, and Thailand.

With 53 days until the opening pitch, the nations in this field are in active preparation.

**Japan:**
The host nation and World No. 1 enters as the overwhelming favorite. Home-field advantage in international softball is real — Japanese teams compete on familiar surfaces, in front of their own crowds, in their native environment. Japan has been the benchmark for women's softball in Asia for decades, and Aichi-Nagoya adds another layer to their advantage.

**Philippines and South Korea:**
These two programs have been the most proactive in their preparation. Earlier this summer, the Philippines and South Korea held a joint training camp in Seoul — a sign that both nations are treating this tournament seriously as a step toward the 2027 World Cup competitive cycle. Beating Japan or China in Aichi-Nagoya would represent a major program milestone for either nation.

**China and Chinese Taipei:**
Both are perennial medalists at the Asian Games. Any realistic conversation about the podium in Aichi-Nagoya involves these four: Japan, China, Chinese Taipei, and one of the rising programs.

53 days. The preparation is underway. 🥎
