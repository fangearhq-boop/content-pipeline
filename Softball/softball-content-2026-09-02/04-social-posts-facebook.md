That's the reality of elite recruiting: programs with top targets on their board make the first calls of the day, because the first call carries weight. It signals that your name was at the top of someone's list when the clock hit midnight.

So what happens now?

**One week from today** — September 9 at 7:00 AM ET — the in-person evaluation window opens. That's when coaches can attend tournaments, showcases, and travel ball events to watch Class of 2028 prospects live for the first time. If September 1 was about phone calls, September 9 is about coaching staffs showing up in person.

A few things to keep in mind as the fall recruiting season unfolds:
• Extra Inning Softball received **1,226 nominations** for their Class of 2028 recognition program — a record high for any class in the organization's history. The talent pool is deep.
• Programs that came up short in the 2026-27 portal cycle are aggressive right now. They need to build through high school recruiting.

If you're a 2028 prospect or you have one in your family, the next 7 days matter.

### STORY 4: OU Fall Camp — Gasso's 10-Day Countdown

#### Long-Form Post — 5:30 PM ET
**10 Days. Then Gasso's Gone — What That Means for OU's Fall Camp 🥎**

Head coach Patty Gasso is 10 days away from leaving Norman.

On September 12, Gasso departs for Oklahoma City to manage Team USA in the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group C Qualifier (September 12-16). Along with her go four Sooners — Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell — who are on the national team roster.

That means OU's fall camp has a deadline built right into it.

For the next 10 days, Gasso has a window to evaluate, set expectations, and install the 2027 foundation at Love's Field. Then the program pivots to her assistants while she coaches the country.

What's the early read on OU's fall?

Sophomore LHP Audrey Lowry has been the story of OU's scrimmages so far: 2.2 innings, 1 hit, 0 walks, 2 strikeouts. She and Brooke Deal are the two returning pitchers on a staff that will also need to integrate Alex Berzon (two-time All-American from LSU) and Hannah Harter (.346 BA from MTSU).

The first public fall game is October 3. The fall opener for OU is September 30 at Love's Field against Tarleton State.

Ten days isn't a lot of time. But in this program, every day in the circle counts. 🥎 #OUSoftball

### STORY 5: Texas Tech Fall Chemistry Test

#### Long-Form Post — 8:00 PM ET
**Texas Tech Has the Talent. Fall Ball Answers the Chemistry Question. 🥎**

On paper, Texas Tech's 2027 roster is loaded. Seven current or former All-Americans — Kaitlyn Terry, Mia Williams, Taylor Pannell, Moriah Polar, Kasidi Pickering, Jasmyn Burns, and Jaysoni Beachum — are on the same team. Pitcher Jayden Heavener arrives from LSU with a 13-9 record and 2.81 ERA. Beachum (.375 career BA, 37 HR, 180 RBI at FSU) and Pickering (.384 career BA, 50 HR, 169 RBI at OU) are two of the best offensive players to transfer to the same program in recent memory.

Most of this group has never practiced together a single day.

That's what fall camp is for. And that's the real storyline in Lubbock.

Head coach Gerry Glasco is building the chemistry that makes this roster into a team. Chemistry doesn't come from talent alone — it comes from shared reps, trust built in the circle and the batter's box, and the kind of competitive environment that fall ball creates. Those pieces have to fit before February.

Texas won two straight national championships. Texas Tech was the runner-up in 2026. The Red Raiders enter 2027 as one of the most anticipated teams in the country.

But fall is where they begin to answer the real question: can all of that talent find a way to play together? 🥎 #SoftballTwitter
