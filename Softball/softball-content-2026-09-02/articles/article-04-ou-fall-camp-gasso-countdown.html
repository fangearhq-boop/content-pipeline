Ten Days Left: OU's Fall Camp Begins Its Countdown Before Gasso Departs for Team USA

By Sarah Mitchell | September 2, 2026 | I Love Softball

Patty Gasso has exactly ten days before she leaves Norman.

On September 12, Gasso departs for Oklahoma City to manage Team USA in the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group C Qualifier (September 12-16 at Devon Park). Along with her go four Sooners — Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell — all of whom made the 16-player national team roster. The qualifier runs through September 16, meaning OU's fall program will operate without its head coach and four key players for at least five days at a critical early moment in fall camp.

What Gasso Has to Do Before He Goes

Ten days is not a lot of time. But it is enough time to set direction, evaluate new faces, and establish the competitive standard for a program preparing for a 2027 season with serious title aspirations.

The early read from OU's first scrimmage was encouraging. Sophomore left-hander Audrey Lowry stood out in her initial appearance: 2.2 innings, 1 hit, 0 walks, 2 strikeouts. Lowry and senior Brooke Deal are the two returning pitchers on the OU staff — meaning the pitching depth will rely on new faces coming through the portal to integrate quickly.

Those new faces include Alex Berzon, a two-time All-American from LSU who brings 486.1 career innings pitched, a 2.02 ERA, and 406 strikeouts to the Sooners' circle. Hannah Harter, a transfer from Middle Tennessee State, adds a .346 batting average, 7 home runs, and 23 RBI to OU's lineup. Both are in the early stages of integrating into a new program — exactly the kind of evaluation that fall camp is designed for.

The Fall Calendar

OU's fall schedule is set. The opener is September 30 at Love's Field against Tarleton State. After the WNT qualifier concludes September 16, Gasso returns and can reassume direction of the program. Subsequent fall games include USAO on October 23, Seminole State on October 30, and East Texas A&M on November 4. The first public scrimmage open to fans will be October 3.

All fall games are general admission, priced at $10.88 per contest, with an all-fall pass option available for the full schedule.

The Bigger Picture

For a program with Oklahoma's resources and recruiting history, the dual role Gasso plays — Sooners head coach and national team manager — is a mark of the program's standing in the sport. But it also creates genuine logistical complexity in September.

The fact that four of OU's key players are also representing the country during that same window means the Sooners' fall integration happens in two phases: before September 12 (with full staff and a full roster), and after September 16 (when Gasso and the four Sooners return with WNT experience in hand).

How both phases unfold will shape OU's approach to the 2027 season. Ten days until the first test.
