USA Softball's World Cup Group Qualifier Roster: 2 Olympians, 7 World Games Golds, 4 Sooners — 10 Days Out

By Sarah Mitchell | September 2, 2026 | I Love Softball

Ten days from now, USA Softball opens the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group C Qualifier in Oklahoma City. The full 16-player roster has been set, and it's one of the most experienced national team assemblies in recent memory.

The tournament runs September 12-16 at OGE Energy Field at Devon Park — the same venue where the Women's National Team won International Cup gold in August. Heading into the qualifier, USA enters ranked No. 2 in the world and as the host nation with the tournament's top billing.

The Roster at a Glance

The 16-player USA roster brings together the best of multiple elite program cycles. Among the key components:

Two 2020 Olympians: Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola are both on the team. Both competed at the Tokyo Olympics, both have extensive international experience, and both are proven performers on the biggest stages in the sport.

Seven 2025 World Games gold medalists are included on the roster — players who competed and won gold at the 2025 World Games in Chengdu, China. That level of recent international championship experience shapes a team's identity in a way that individual talent alone cannot replicate.

Ten Down Under Series veterans also appear on the roster — players who competed in the USA Softball Down Under Series against Australian competition. That series served as a key preparatory environment for this World Cup cycle.

Four Sooners on the Team

The Oklahoma University program is well-represented: Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell will all compete for Team USA in Oklahoma City.

Head coach Patty Gasso — who coaches all four of them at OU — will also manage Team USA in the dugout. That creates an unusual dynamic in OU's fall program: Gasso and four key players depart for WNT duty on September 12, leaving OU's fall camp in the hands of assistant coaches for the duration of the qualifier.

The Competition

Five nations stand between USA and a direct 2027 World Cup Finals berth. The field by world ranking:

  • No. 6 Netherlands
  • No. 7 Mexico
  • No. 8 China
  • No. 17 Brazil
  • No. 23 New Zealand

USA opens the tournament against No. 7 Mexico on September 12. The matchup with No. 6 Netherlands carries particular weight — it figures to be the decisive contest for the top seed in the group, and the higher seed carries an advantage in the September 16 playoff bracket.

The tournament format runs through an Opening Round from September 12-15, with playoffs on September 16. The top two teams from the final standings earn direct berths in the 2027 Women's Softball World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia (April 5-11, 2027). A third spot — a Wild Card — will be decided through a repechage game on the final day.

What's Next

The goal is clear: win the group, take a direct berth, and send the national team program into the 2027 Finals cycle with momentum. After the International Cup gold, the WNT arrives in Oklahoma City as the team the rest of the world is chasing.

Tickets for the September 12-16 event are available through USA Softball. The opening ceremony is scheduled for September 12 at 6:30 PM. First pitch for USA vs. Mexico follows at 7:30 PM. Devon Park, Oklahoma City, is where this World Cup cycle begins in earnest.
