Oklahoma Softball Hosts Final Fall Game Thursday at Love's Field

By Jordan Reeves | October 29, 2026 | I Love Softball

One more night. Oklahoma softball's final fall game is Thursday, October 30 at 6:30 PM CT at Love's Field, and it is free for everyone. The Sooners host Seminole State in what will be the last public look at this team before spring 2027 — a roster that enters the offseason among the top three national title contenders in the country.

The game will also be streamed free at 1oklahoma.com for anyone who can't make it to Norman in person.

The Last Fall Look at Wells and Kavan

If you've been paying attention to Oklahoma's fall, you already know what Kendall Wells and Rylie Kavan are capable of. If you haven't, tomorrow night is your chance to catch up before the calendar flips.

Wells has hit 39 home runs through fall competition — a number that would stand out at any level of softball. She arrived at Oklahoma as one of the most recruited players in her class, and the Sooners' NFCA Freshman of the Year candidacy is not just a preseason projection. It's backed by production that has been consistent throughout the fall Battle Series.

Then there's Rylie Kavan, a two-time Women's College World Series Most Outstanding Player. That distinction belongs to one of the most decorated résumés in active college softball. She is back for another season, and every fall at-bat has reinforced the point: Kavan doesn't have a zone where she is easy to retire.

Pairing those two in the same lineup gives Oklahoma's offense a dimension that very few teams in the country can match. Pitchers planning to face the Sooners in 2027 have spent the fall taking notes. The good news for Oklahoma fans is that Thursday offers one more free look at this combination before the lights go down for the winter.

A Fall Built Around Competition

Oklahoma ran its fall schedule through the Battle Series format, a multi-game stretch against a variety of opponents at Love's Field. The Sooners also faced USAO on October 23. The structure gave the coaching staff what it needed: real game reps for new faces, load management for veterans, and a controlled environment at a venue that draws thousands of fans even for fall exhibitions.

Love's Field has changed the atmosphere around Oklahoma softball. The new facility, with its capacity, sightlines, and energy, has become one of the best venues to watch college softball at any time of year. For a fall game that's free to attend, it's a remarkable value for fans in the region.

The Seminole State matchup is the capstone. It closes the books on a fall that has left Oklahoma's coaching staff confident heading into winter conditioning.

What's Next

Thursday's game against Seminole State at 6:30 PM CT is Oklahoma's last public fall event. After this, the Sooners enter winter conditioning and spring schedule preparations. The 2027 season schedule will be released in the coming months.

For fans who want to see Wells, Kavan, and the rest of Oklahoma's roster before spring, tomorrow is it. Love's Field. 6:30 PM CT. Free admission or free stream at 1oklahoma.com. Don't miss the final fall appearance of one of the best rosters in college softball.
