Tennessee Softball's Final Public Intersquad Is Tonight — Free at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium

By Emily Rawlings | October 29, 2026 | I Love Softball

If you're anywhere near Knoxville, tonight is not an event to miss. Tennessee softball holds its final public intersquad of the fall at 5 PM ET at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium. Admission is free. And if you show up, you'll get to watch what is arguably the deepest pitching staff in the SEC work through live at-bats one last time before winter.

Coach Karen Weekly confirmed earlier this week that Thursday night is "the last official night of fall ball." After tonight, the Lady Vols move into winter conditioning and spring preparation — away from public view until the 2027 season begins.

Three Pitchers, Three Profiles

The reason this event is worth your evening is Tennessee's pitching staff, and tonight you can watch all three of its top arms in one session.

Karlyn Pickens finished the 2026 season with 194 strikeouts and a 1.70 ERA. Those are not numbers that require context — they are elite by any standard in college softball. Pickens is back, healthy, and competing for what would be one of the most dominant back-to-back seasons a Tennessee pitcher has ever assembled.

Kelly Mardjetko went 16-3 with a 1.26 ERA in 2026. She does not miss the zone, she doesn't give up damage in bunches, and she competes in a way that keeps defenses engaged behind her. A 1.26 ERA across a full season is a number that makes opposing coaches uncomfortable from the moment lineup cards are exchanged.

Then there's freshman Bailee Shelton. She has already struck out 7 batters in 3 innings of fall action as a true freshman. The raw stuff is unmistakable, and the poise she has shown in early looks suggests she is not someone who will need a full season to figure out college softball. Weekly has been measured in her public comments about Shelton, but actions speak: she's pitched in every fall opportunity given to her.

A Fall That Said Everything and Nothing

Tennessee went 3-0 in fall competition, capping the outdoor portion of its schedule with a 14-1 win over Lee University in 10 innings on Oct. 23. Elsa Morrison and Emma Clarke both homered in that game. The pitching staff was near-perfect.

And yet, Weekly didn't come away from the fall fully satisfied. She said openly that she wishes the team had scheduled tougher pitching competition this fall. That is a remarkable thing for a coach to say after a 3-0 record that includes a 14-1 finish. It tells you something important: Weekly doesn't believe you build a championship program by dominating teams that aren't equipped to challenge you. She wants her hitters tested because she knows the level they'll face in March and April.

The fact that she's saying this publicly in the fall is a good sign. It means the standard inside the program is being set above whatever the fall competition could provide — and that standard is WCWS or better.

What's Next

Tonight's intersquad at 5 PM ET is the last scheduled public event on Tennessee's fall calendar. After this, the Lady Vols go dark until the 2027 season schedule is released and media availability resumes in the winter.

If you can get to Sherri Parker Lee Stadium tonight, go. The admission is free. The pitching is elite. And this is your final look at one of the most anticipated Tennessee softball rosters in recent memory before it returns for what the program expects to be a run at the national championship.

First pitch is at 5 PM ET. Sherri Parker Lee Stadium. Free.
