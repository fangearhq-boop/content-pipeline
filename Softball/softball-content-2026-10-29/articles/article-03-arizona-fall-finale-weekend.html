Arizona Softball Closes Fall at Hillenbrand With Three-Game Weekend

By Sarah Mitchell | October 29, 2026 | I Love Softball

Arizona softball wraps up its fall schedule this weekend at Rita Hillenbrand Memorial Stadium in Tucson, with three games across two days. It's the final opportunity for coach Caitlin Lowe to evaluate her full roster before winter — and for a program that returned 10 letterwinners with a Big 12 Defensive Player of the Year at the center of it all, that evaluation figures to produce a lot of encouraging answers.

On Friday, October 30, Arizona hosts Chandler-Gilbert Community College at 5:30 PM and Benedictine Mesa at 7:30 PM. On Saturday, October 31, Grand Canyon University visits Hillenbrand at noon. All three games are part of the Wildcats' fall exhibition slate.

Devyn Shockey Anchors the Return

Devyn Shockey was named Big 12 Defensive Player of the Year after the 2026 season, and she is back for the Wildcats in 2027. That distinction alone makes Arizona one of the more defensively stable programs in the conference — but Shockey is more than a defensive anchor. She sets a tone in how the Wildcats compete, and her presence in the lineup changes what opposing offenses can attempt to do.

Arizona returns 10 letterwinners from a program that played in the Big 12 last season. That kind of continuity is not something every program in a power conference can claim, and it is the reason this fall weekend carries genuine meaning beyond the box scores. Lowe isn't evaluating a team built from scratch. She's evaluating an experienced roster that has played together, knows the system, and is figuring out where the ceiling is.

Caitlin Lowe's Second Fall Evaluation

Lowe is in her second year as Arizona's head coach, and this fall represents a meaningful step in her development of the program's identity. Her first year was about learning — players, staff dynamics, what the program needed to build on and what needed to change. This year's fall is about results. What does the core group she inherited look like under competition pressure? Who from the freshman class is ready to contribute immediately in 2027?

The three opponents this weekend — Chandler-Gilbert, Benedictine Mesa, and Grand Canyon — provide different challenges. Junior college programs can be unpredictable; they have motivated players who are fighting for opportunities just like anyone else. Division I programs like GCU are legitimate competition at any time of year. Lowe will get useful reps across all three games.

Hillenbrand Stadium gives Arizona a home-field advantage even in fall exhibitions. The venue has hosted some of the most important games in program history, and there's an energy to playing there that translates even into October scrimmages.

Building an Identity in the Big 12

Arizona's move into the Big 12 created a new competitive landscape for the program. The conference brings regular-season games against Oklahoma, Texas, Texas Tech, Oklahoma State, and others — some of the most competitive softball programs in the country. Shockey's DPOY award shows the program can compete and win individual honors in that environment. The next step is building a roster that can compete for the conference title and advance deep into the NCAA Tournament.

This weekend won't answer all of those questions. But it will give Lowe a final data point before winter, and it will give Arizona fans one more look at a program that is trying to establish itself as a contender in one of the toughest conferences in college softball.

What's Next

After this weekend's three-game finale, Arizona's fall schedule is complete. The Wildcats will move into winter conditioning before the 2027 season schedule is released. Lowe and her staff will use the coming weeks to finalize their evaluations and set goals for the spring.

With Shockey back, 10 letterwinners in the fold, and a coaching staff that is now into its second year learning each other, Arizona enters the offseason with a credible case that 2027 could be a breakthrough year for the program in the Big 12.
