Gasso Returns to Love's Field: Oklahoma Sooners Hold First Full-Staff Fall Practice Today

By Jordan Reeves | I Love Softball | September 17, 2026

After five days coaching Team USA in Oklahoma City, Patty Gasso is back in Norman — and the full Sooners coaching staff convenes for the first time since September 12.

Thursday, September 17 marks a milestone for Oklahoma softball. After coaching Team USA through a dominant run at the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Oklahoma City, head coach Patty Gasso, associate head coach JT Gasso and pitching coach Rocha returned to Norman on Wednesday evening and are back on the field today for the Sooners' first full-staff fall practice of the 2027 campaign.

Five days with associate coaches holding things together. One successful trip to Devon Park for Team USA. And now, 13 days before the fall opener against Tarleton State at Love's Field, the team that Oklahoma fans have been waiting all summer to see is finally intact.

The Gap That Was Filled

Gasso departed for Team USA duties on September 12, leaving associate coaches to run the Norman operation for five days. The Sooners practiced under that arrangement from September 12-16 while Gasso and her staff helped lead USA to a 5-0 run through Group Oklahoma, including a dominant 7-0 win over China in the playoff round that clinched a spot in the 2027 Finals in Redcliffe, QLD.

Those five days were productive for the associate coaching staff and the roster, but there is a different kind of energy that arrives when the head coach walks back through the doors. Today is that day at Love's Field.

The Players Waiting for This Moment

Oklahoma enters fall camp with a roster built around three high-profile additions alongside returning core players. Kendall Wells, who made history as a sophomore last season with 32 home runs — setting the NCAA freshman home run record in her debut season — leads the offensive core with the kind of power that opposing pitchers fear.

Sydney Berzon arrived from LSU with two All-American designations and a pitching resume that reads like a case study in durability: 486.1 innings pitched, a 2.02 ERA and 406 strikeouts across her LSU career. She is the cornerstone of Oklahoma's pitching staff moving forward.

Macie Harter (.346 BA, 7 HR, 23 RBI from Middle Tennessee State) adds contact ability and versatility to a lineup that already has Wells anchoring the middle of the order. Together, these three transfers — alongside the returning pieces from OU's 2026 roster — give Gasso a deep, layered group to work with over the next six weeks of fall competition.

Fall Schedule Ahead

Oklahoma's eight-game fall schedule runs from September 30 through November 4. The Sooners open September 30 against Tarleton State at Love's Field, followed by outside opponents at USAO (Oct. 23), Seminole State (Oct. 30), and East Texas A&M (Nov. 4), with four Battle Series intrasquad games threaded throughout.

All fall games are played at Love's Field, giving Gasso and her staff controlled conditions for the evaluation process. Thirteen players are new to the program this fall, providing plenty of competition and opportunity as the coaching staff begins to shape the rotation, lineup and defensive alignments for 2027.

What's Next

The immediate priority is simple: today's full-staff practice. From there, the next two weeks are about preparation and reps before Tarleton State arrives at Love's Field on September 30 for the first public look at the 2027 Oklahoma Sooners. Gasso will have plenty of answers from these fall sessions, and the intensity of the recruiting class — combined with the transfer additions — gives this program reason for optimism heading into the 2027 regular season. The full staff is back. The real work begins today.
