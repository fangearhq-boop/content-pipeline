Maya Brady Hits Three Home Runs as USA Defeats China 7-0, Books Redcliffe Finals Ticket

By Emily Rawlings | I Love Softball | September 17, 2026

Team USA closes Group Oklahoma with a dominant run-rule, punching their ticket to the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals in Australia.

There were five days of softball at Devon Park in Oklahoma City, and then there was Wednesday.

Team USA defeated China 7-0 in six innings on September 16 to claim the first direct berth to the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia — and they did it in emphatic fashion. Maya Brady hit three home runs and drove in five, pitching ace Gabbie Garcia was untouchable, and the run-ahead rule brought an early end to the most anticipated game in Group Oklahoma's five-day run.

USA finished the tournament 5-0 — five games, five wins, the most dominant team at Devon Park from the first pitch to the last. Head coach Patty Gasso and her staff are back in Norman today, and the road to Redcliffe is confirmed.

Brady's Three-Home-Run Day

Maya Brady was the story of the afternoon. She hit three home runs against China, driving in five runs across the contest. The decisive blow came in the sixth inning, when Brady launched a 276-foot two-run shot to deep right-center field — the third home run of her day — that triggered the run-ahead rule and sent USA to the Finals.

The performance showcased the offensive firepower that USA has carried throughout Group Oklahoma. The Americans ran the table in the group stage with three run-rule victories, and Wednesday's final game was no different — a performance that announced USA's arrival in the global softball conversation heading into the Redcliffe Finals next April.

Garcia's Perfect Outing

While Brady provided the offensive fireworks, Gabbie Garcia handled business on the mound. Garcia retired all nine batters she faced through the sixth inning, striking out six along the way. It was the kind of pitching performance that makes opposing lineups feel helpless — precise, consistent and devastating when it needed to be.

Garcia, a Tokyo 2020 silver medalist who is part of a USA roster with deep international experience, delivered when the stakes were highest. Combined with the offensive support from Brady and the rest of the lineup, the 7-0 final was never in doubt.

The Road to Redcliffe

The 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, QLD are scheduled for April 5-11. USA's place in that eight-team field is now confirmed. The Finals serve as one of the primary qualifiers on the pathway to the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics — meaning Wednesday's win carries stakes that extend well beyond the tournament scoreboard.

The team that Gasso assembled — including four Oklahoma Sooners (Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings and Kelly Maxwell) alongside veterans like Garcia and Sahvanna Mulipola, who carry Tokyo 2020 silver medals — proved to be exactly the unit needed for this moment. They won every game, allowed almost nothing defensively, and left Devon Park as the clear top team in their group.

What's Next

Gasso, JT Gasso and Rocha returned to Norman on Wednesday evening after Group Oklahoma concluded. Today, September 17, marks the first full-staff practice for Oklahoma softball since the coaching trio departed for Team USA duties on September 12. The Sooners' fall opener against Tarleton State at Love's Field is 13 days away.

For USA softball, the next chapter is Redcliffe. For Gasso and the Sooners' coaching staff, the next chapter begins today at Love's Field. Wednesday's 7-0 win was the punctuation mark on a historic week in Oklahoma City. Now comes everything that follows.
