Tennessee Hosts Carson-Newman Thursday as Mardjetko and Nuwer Keep Building Their Fall Case

By Sarah Mitchell | September 29, 2026

Tennessee's fall ball season is in full swing, and Thursday's home game against Carson-Newman at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium is the next step in a carefully constructed set of reps for the Lady Vols' returning pitching staff.

First pitch is 6:00 PM on October 2. Admission is free. And the two pitchers taking the mound — Sage Mardjetko and Erin Nuwer — are among the best returning arms in the country entering the 2027 season.

The Numbers That Make Mardjetko and Nuwer Terrifying

In 2026, Sage Mardjetko went 16-3 with a 1.26 ERA and 171 strikeouts. Those are not statistics you produce by accident. That is the output of a pitcher who can dominate at every level of competition, who generates swings and misses consistently, and who can hold leads when the offense needs time to wake up.

Erin Nuwer went 15-1 with a 1.01 ERA — a number that means opposing hitters were scoring at essentially one run per nine innings against her. Together, Mardjetko and Nuwer combined for 31 wins and eight shutouts in 2026.

Both return to Tennessee for 2027. Both are already throwing in the fall. The Lady Vols have a pitching staff built to compete for a national championship, and Thursday's game against Carson-Newman is part of the maintenance work — keeping those arms sharp, building innings into the fall, and giving each pitcher competitive game situations before the spring schedule begins.

Nine Starters Back, Free Admission, and a Full Fall Schedule

Tennessee isn't just strong in the pitching circle. Nine starters return for the 2027 season, giving the Lady Vols the kind of positional experience that allows a coaching staff to run fall ball as genuine evaluation rather than pure introduction.

The fall schedule runs through October with games against Northeast Alabama (Oct. 9-11), a road trip to Murfreesboro to face Middle Tennessee (Oct. 18), and additional home games through the month. Seven of Tennessee's eight fall games are at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium, and every one of them is free for the public.

Thursday's Carson-Newman game gives head coach Karen Weekly and her staff another live look at a returning roster that enters 2027 with legitimate national championship aspirations. For fans, it is a chance to see elite college softball for no cost at one of the sport's best venues.

What's Next

After Thursday's Carson-Newman game, Tennessee continues its fall slate with games on October 9-11 at home against Northeast Alabama, Northwest Florida State, and Roane State. The lone road trip comes October 18 in Murfreesboro.

The Lady Vols have the pieces to make a deep postseason run in 2027. Mardjetko and Nuwer are the foundation of that case. Come out to Sherri Parker Lee on Thursday, watch them work, and see for yourself why Tennessee belongs in the national championship conversation.
