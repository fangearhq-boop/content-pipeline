Asian Games Softball: China Rolls Through Super Round as Japan Eyes Gold on Home Soil

By Sarah Mitchell | September 29, 2026

The Super Round at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games is proving exactly as competitive as the advanced metrics suggested — and China is making the clearest statement that Japan's path to gold will not be a formality.

China defeated South Korea 8-4 on Monday in Anjō, Japan, a game that featured three home runs and a level of offensive production that no team in this Super Round should overlook. Chinese Taipei controlled the Philippines 10-1 in five innings the same day. Japan, ranked No. 1 in the world by the WBSC, has remained unbeaten throughout the competition. The October 3 Final is four days away. Everything is still to play for.

China's Power Surge Changes the Conversation

Heading into the 2026 Asian Games, most observers expected Japan vs. Chinese Taipei to be the defining rivalry. China, ranked No. 8 globally, was viewed as a credible but secondary threat. Through the first four days of competition, that framing has started to shift.

Three home runs against South Korea is not an accident. It is a signal. China has been hitting the ball out of the park when the moment calls for it, and their 8-4 victory over a South Korean team that beat Thailand 7-0 in the Opening Round was a dominant performance at the right time.

The question is whether China can bring the same power display against Japan's elite pitching — the kind of pitching that held opponents to essentially nothing in the Opening Round. That matchup, somewhere in the Super Round, is the game that will likely decide the gold medal before the October 3 Final is even played.

Chinese Taipei Is Methodical, Not Flashy

Chinese Taipei (No. 5 globally) beat the Philippines 10-1 in five innings, which tells you almost everything you need to know about how they approach a game. They don't need drama. They execute, they score early, and they let their pitching close out what the offense opens.

That style will test the Philippines, who advanced from the Opening Round with one of the tournament's most dramatic moments — a seven-run sixth inning to beat South Korea 7-3 when elimination loomed. The Blu Girls have shown they can win under pressure. They have not yet shown they can go toe-to-toe with a Chinese Taipei squad that has been clinical all week.

Still, no one expected Philippines to be here at all. Every game they play in the Super Round is bonus territory — and bonus territory sometimes produces bonus moments.

The LA28 Dimension

Softball is returning to the Olympic program at the 2028 Los Angeles Games, ending a 20-year absence after the sport was removed from the schedule following Beijing 2008. That context transforms every major international tournament on the calendar — including this one.

WBSC ranking points from the 2026 Asian Games will feed directly into the Olympic qualifying picture. For Japan, gold here reinforces their position as the world's premier program. For China, a strong Super Round performance and a potential silver or gold pushes them closer to an Olympic berth. For Chinese Taipei and the Philippines, every win is a step toward the qualification pathway they need.

The stakes are not abstract. The teams on the field at Anjō Sports Park right now are playing for medals this week and for positions in the global standings that will matter two years from now.

What's Next

The Super Round continues through October 2 in the round-robin format, with all four teams — Japan, China, Chinese Taipei, and Philippines — still competing. The gold medal Final is scheduled for October 3 at Anjō Sports Park.

The key matchup to watch through the Super Round is Japan versus China. Japan is playing at home, the crowd is behind them, and their pitching has been untouchable. China has the power to be the first team that challenges that narrative. Whether they can bring Monday's offensive energy against the No. 1 team in the world is the question that will define this tournament — and begin answering questions about what the road to LA28 will look like for Asian softball.
