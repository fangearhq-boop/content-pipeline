Oklahoma Opens Fall Ball Tomorrow: Sydney Berzon Headlines Patty Gasso's Rebuilt Sooners

By Jordan Reeves | September 29, 2026

The 2026 transfer portal cycle tested Oklahoma softball in ways few expected. The Sooners lost key players from the roster that had defined their championship era. Patty Gasso's answer has been characteristically aggressive: bring in the best available talent and trust the program's culture to produce results.

Oklahoma opens its 2026 fall ball slate on Wednesday at Love's Field — eight games against a mix of Battle Series opponents and outside programs — with 21 players who will begin the work of becoming a team capable of competing for the 2027 national title. The headliner is pitcher Sydney Berzon, and the supporting cast is deep enough to make this one of the more compelling fall rosters in the country.

Sydney Berzon Is the Ace This Roster Needed

Berzon arrives from LSU with two NFCA All-American honors on her resume. She has faced the best lineups in the Southeastern Conference and produced at an elite level. For an Oklahoma staff that needed a proven arm at the top of the rotation, the Berzon transfer addresses the most urgent need on the roster.

Patty Gasso has noted publicly that the pitching staff entering 2027 is larger than previous years, creating depth but also requiring management. Berzon's arrival brings a hierarchy to that staff — a No. 1 pitcher around whom everyone else can be deployed. That kind of clarity matters in March and April when conference schedules demand consistency from the rotation.

She has not pitched in an OU uniform yet. Fall ball is where she starts building that connection with the catching staff, the infield, and a program that expects to be in Oklahoma City every June.

Harter Adds Pop to the Lineup

Alongside Berzon in the headline transfer class is Macie Harter from Middle Tennessee, who hit .346 with seven home runs last season. Harter gives Oklahoma a right-handed bat with proven college production — the kind of middle-of-the-order presence that complements power hitters with contact and the ability to drive runners in.

The 12 returning players bring familiarity with the Gasso system and the standard OU sets for preparation and competition. The nine newcomers — including five-star freshmen Payton Westra and Ki'ele Ho-Ching, part of a recruiting class ranked No. 1 nationally — bring the raw talent that programs like Oklahoma need to stay at the top over multiple years.

Eight fall games will not answer every question about this roster. But they will begin to reveal which newcomers are ready to contribute immediately and which will need the full spring season to develop.

Gasso's Track Record With Roster Turnover

Patty Gasso has navigated more transfer portal cycles than almost any coach in college softball. Oklahoma's three consecutive WCWS titles from 2021-23 were built on a roster that turned over significantly each year, and Gasso has consistently found ways to integrate new talent without sacrificing the program's identity.

The 2027 Sooners are not a finished product in September. They are a project — one with excellent raw materials and a coaching staff that has proven they know how to develop them. The fall schedule is where that project starts taking shape.

What's Next

Oklahoma's fall schedule runs through late October with games against Seminole State, Wichita State, Oklahoma Christian, Langston, Oklahoma Baptist, and additional opponents. Each game is an evaluation opportunity for Gasso and her staff as they determine the 2027 starting lineup and rotation.

The big picture question for OU is straightforward: Can Berzon + returning core + top recruiting class add up to a championship team? The 2026 Sooners didn't reach the title game. The 2027 Sooners — rebuilt and reloaded — want a different outcome. Tomorrow at Love's Field is where that story begins.
