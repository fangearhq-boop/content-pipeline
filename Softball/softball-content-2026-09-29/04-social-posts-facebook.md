### STORY 2: Texas Fall Opener TONIGHT

**FB Long-Form (11:00 AM ET)**
It's game night in Austin. 🤘

Texas softball opens its 2026 fall season tonight against Murray State at McCombs Field — 6 PM CT, free admission. But what makes tonight genuinely historic is who's suiting up.

Isa Torres transferred from Florida State to Texas this offseason. She hit .530 last season and was named the 2026 national Player of the Year — the best hitter in college softball, now wearing burnt orange. Teagan Kavan returns after winning back-to-back WCWS Most Outstanding Player awards — the only player in the history of the Women's College World Series to win it twice. They have never played together. Until tonight.

Texas is hunting a three-peat. Only two programs in history have won three straight WCWS titles: UCLA from 1988-90 and Oklahoma from 2021-23. The two-time defending champion Longhorns believe this is their year to join that conversation.

Tonight is the first public look at what that 2027 Texas team could become. McCombs Field. 6 PM CT. Free.

### STORY 5: Oklahoma State — Historic WCWS Run, Building for 2027

**FB Long-Form (8:00 PM ET)**
Before this year, Oklahoma State had never appeared at the Women's College World Series.

That changed in 2026. The Cowgirls went 47-13, earned the program's first-ever WCWS berth, and showed the country that Stillwater has become a legitimate national contender. It was one of the best stories in college softball all season.

Now, with five transfers added and fall ball underway, head coach Kenny Gajewski's squad is already looking ahead to 2027. The fall schedule is loaded — Seminole State (Tuesday), Wichita State (Oct. 4), Oklahoma Christian (Oct. 8), Langston (Oct. 11), Oklahoma Baptist (Oct. 13), and a marquee test against Arkansas (Oct. 17).

The question for Oklahoma State isn't whether 2026 was a fluke. It wasn't. The question is whether the Cowgirls can build on it and push deeper into the 2027 postseason. Fall ball will start answering that question.
