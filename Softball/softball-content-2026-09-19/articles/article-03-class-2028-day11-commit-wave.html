Class of 2028 Day 11: Commit Wave Rolls On as Top Prospects Still Weigh Options

By Emily Rawlings | I Love Softball | September 19, 2026

Eleven days into the in-person contact window, the Class of 2028 commit list keeps growing — but the biggest names are still undecided, and programs are lining up for their decisions.

The September 9 in-person contact window opened at 7 AM ET and has not paused for a single day since. Day 11 arrives with the Class of 2028 already featuring an impressive list of committed prospects — and an equally impressive list of elite talents still actively weighing their options.

This is the phase of the recruiting window that separates the committed class from the uncommitted stars — and right now, the uncommitted stars are among the most coveted in recent memory.

Where the Committed Class Stands

As of Day 11, the known committed roster includes: Finlee Williams (No. 1 overall, Oklahoma), Peyton Ushery (No. 2 overall, Georgia), Paityn Donalson (FSU), Braylyn Saenz (OU), Star Gutierrez (Texas Tech), Sophia Galindo and Nevaeh Wilson (Tennessee), Kate Losh and Ryann Harris (Florida), Siena Myers (South Carolina), and the Arizona duo of Ridling and Stell.

The geographic and conference breadth of that list is striking. The SEC, ACC, Big 12 and Pac-12 are all represented in a class that is just 11 days old in its in-person contact period. Programs across every major conference are moving to lock up talent — and doing so at a pace that reflects the depth and quality of this cycle.

With 1,226 Extra Innings Softball nominations — a record for any Class of 2028 — there is simply more talent available than programs can immediately absorb. The commit wave continues because the pool of uncommitted prospects remains deep and attractive.

The Uncommitted Stars

The most closely watched names in the recruiting window right now are the prospects who have not yet made their decisions — and several of them are elite.

Michaela Landers, Kylie Holden and Sophia Schlader remain uncommitted and continue to receive attention from the sport's top programs. These are prospects who carry the kind of recruiting profiles that move class rankings significantly when they finally choose. Joley Bennett — a five-star prospect widely targeted by programs including LSU — is another name to watch closely in the coming days and weeks.

The window runs through mid-October. Each day that passes brings more in-person visits, more official offers and more decisions. The programs that land these uncommitted names will significantly strengthen their 2028 class positions.

What the Numbers Say

The 1,226 EIS nominations figure is not just a record — it is a signal about the class's depth. In recent cycles, classes with strong early commit waves and record nomination totals have produced disproportionately high numbers of All-Americans and draft picks once they reach college. The Class of 2028 has the early indicators of a genuinely historic group.

The first official class rankings update from the major recruiting services is expected around mid-October. That update will reflect the full impact of the in-person window commit wave and begin to establish the hierarchy that will shape how fans and programs evaluate this class through the remainder of the recruiting cycle.

What's Next

The in-person contact window continues through mid-October, with additional commit announcements expected across all major conferences in the coming days. The top uncommitted prospects — Landers, Holden, Schlader, Bennett — will continue to receive maximum attention as programs narrow their focus to the names still available. When the first class rankings update drops in mid-October, the Class of 2028 picture will come into sharper focus. Until then, the wave keeps rolling.
