Day 3, 11 Days to Go: Oklahoma Sooners Fall Camp Building Toward Sept. 30 Opener

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | September 19, 2026

Patty Gasso and her full staff are three days into fall camp at Love's Field, with the Sept. 30 opener against Tarleton State now 11 days away.

Three days in. Eleven days to go. The Oklahoma Sooners are exactly where they need to be.

Full-staff fall camp at Love's Field entered Day 3 on Saturday, with Patty Gasso, JT Gasso and associate head coach Rocha — all back in Norman since September 16 after leading Team USA through Group Oklahoma — now fully integrated into the daily rhythm of preparing the 2027 Sooners squad. The Sept. 30 fall opener against Tarleton State is approaching fast, and every rep in these 11 remaining days will shape what kind of team takes that field.

The Roster Taking Shape

Oklahoma returns 12 players from its 2026 roster and adds nine newcomers — a roster composition that makes fall camp as much about identity-building as skill evaluation. The pieces Gasso is working with are exceptional on paper. Translating them into a cohesive unit before live competition begins is the challenge of these early September weeks.

Kendall Wells heads the list of returners. As a sophomore, Wells set the NCAA freshman home run record with 32 — a mark that turned heads nationally and immediately established her as one of the most dangerous power hitters in the country. Her continued development in the fall camp will be among the most watched storylines in Norman.

Sydney Berzon arrived from LSU as the headlining transfer addition. The two-time All-American posted 486.1 innings over her LSU career with a 2.02 ERA and 406 strikeouts — numbers that make her an immediate anchor for the Sooners' pitching staff. Macie Harter, who hit .346 at Middle Tennessee, gives Oklahoma an elite contact presence in the lineup.

The Fall Schedule Blueprint

Oklahoma's 2026 fall schedule features eight games running September 30 through November 4. The format includes four Battle Series intrasquad matchups and four contests against outside opponents: Tarleton State, the University of Science and Arts of Oklahoma, Seminole State College and East Texas A&M.

The structure gives Gasso and her staff a deliberate progression — intrasquad work that controls the evaluation environment, supplemented by live competition against outside programs that tests how the Sooners respond to game-speed situations. It is a format designed to answer specific roster questions before the 2027 regular season arrives.

The Sept. 30 opener against Tarleton State is the first public data point. Eleven days of practice separate the team from that moment.

The National Team Effect

One element unique to this fall camp is the national team pipeline that feeds directly into the roster. Gasso coached Team USA through a perfect 5-0 Group Oklahoma run, with four Sooners — Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings and Kelly Maxwell — in the WNT lineup. That experience does not disappear when the coaches and players return to Norman. It becomes part of the culture.

The lessons from Devon Park — how to perform under international pressure, how to execute in elimination moments, how to compete at the highest level — travel back to Love's Field with the entire coaching staff. Fall camp, for Oklahoma, is always about building something. This year, it is being built by a staff that just competed at the top of the world.

What's Next

The Sooners open fall action on September 30 against Tarleton State at Love's Field. The full schedule runs through November 4, giving Gasso six weeks to evaluate all 21 players on the fall roster. The first class rankings for the 2027 regular season and beyond will begin forming in the weeks that follow — and what happens between now and Nov. 4 will go a long way toward establishing Oklahoma's standing heading into the spring.
