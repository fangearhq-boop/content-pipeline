Redcliffe 2027 Countdown Begins: USA Leads Eight-Nation Field in Race Toward LA28

By Jordan Reeves | I Love Softball | September 19, 2026

Group Oklahoma is over. All eight nations are confirmed. And Team USA — 5-0, dominant, and riding Maya Brady's three-homer performance — now sets its sights on Redcliffe, Queensland.

The qualifying tournament is done. The field is complete. And the countdown to one of the most important softball events in years has officially begun.

With Group Oklahoma in the books — concluded September 16 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City — all eight nations are confirmed for the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia. The Finals run April 5-11, 2027, and they serve as a primary qualifier on the pathway to the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. For every nation in that eight-team field, Redcliffe is not just a world championship — it is the most direct route to LA28.

Team USA: Dominant in Group Oklahoma, Focused on Redcliffe

Team USA entered Group Oklahoma as the top seed and left it as the undisputed standard. Going 5-0 through the tournament — including a 7-0 run-rule victory over China in the Sept. 16 clincher — USA demonstrated the kind of depth and firepower that makes them a heavy favorite heading to Australia.

Maya Brady's three-home run performance in the finale was the signature moment. The young outfielder drove in five, including a decisive two-run shot in the sixth inning that triggered the run-ahead rule. Gabbie Garcia was equally impressive on the mound, retiring all nine batters she faced and striking out six.

USA earned the first direct berth to Redcliffe. Mexico won the second. China, despite finishing third in the bracket, claimed the Wild Card — ensuring all three Group Oklahoma nations travel to Queensland in April.

The Complete Eight-Nation Field

The eight nations heading to Redcliffe QLD for the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals: USA, Mexico, China (from Group Oklahoma), joined by Australia, Canada, Japan, Puerto Rico and Chinese Taipei — five nations who earned pre-qualified berths.

It is a field that covers every major pillar of international softball. Australia, the host nation, will compete at Redcliffe Stadium on home soil. Japan brings decades of Finals experience and the technical excellence that has made them perennially dangerous. Canada and Chinese Taipei are consistent presences at the sport's highest level. Puerto Rico represents the continued growth of the game across the Americas.

USA will enter as the favorite — but the Redcliffe field will test them in ways Group Oklahoma did not.

Gasso's Dual Mission

One of the unique elements of USA's Redcliffe preparation is the dual role of head coach Patty Gasso. After returning to Norman on September 16 — the same day USA clinched their spot — Gasso is now simultaneously preparing Oklahoma for its 2027 collegiate season and laying the groundwork for the national team's April run in Australia.

Four Sooners are on the WNT roster: Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings and Kelly Maxwell. Veterans like Garcia and Janie Reed Mulipola — who carry Tokyo 2020 silver in their résumés — provide the experience layer around the emerging stars like Brady.

That combination of youth and veterans, all under Gasso's direction, is what made Group Oklahoma so convincing. Whether it translates to a Redcliffe title — and the LA28 implications that come with one — is the question that will define the next seven months.

What's Next

The 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, are scheduled for April 5-11, 2027. All eight nations now enter a preparation phase that will run parallel to their respective domestic seasons — for American players, that means the entire 2026-27 collegiate year serves as a bridge to April.

Team USA's next significant moment comes when the national team reconvenes ahead of the Finals. Until then, the Group Oklahoma performance stands as the clearest preview of what USA can deliver on the world stage — and Maya Brady's three-homer night in the clincher stands as the defining image of a squad that punched their Redcliffe ticket with authority.
