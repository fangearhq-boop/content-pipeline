And the stakes have never been higher for American softball. 🥎🌏 With Group Oklahoma officially in the books, all eight nations are confirmed for the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia — April 5-11, 2027. What makes Redcliffe 2027 different from a standard world championship is its role as a primary qualifier for the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. Every result in Queensland next April carries Olympic weight. For Team USA — coached by Oklahoma's Patty Gasso, with four Sooners in the lineup and veterans like Garcia and Mulipola carrying Tokyo 2020 experience — this is the next step in a mission that began decades ago and runs through Los Angeles.
