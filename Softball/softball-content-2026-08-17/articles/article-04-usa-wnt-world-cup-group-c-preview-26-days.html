USA Softball's World Cup Group C: The Real Tests Are the Netherlands, Mexico, and China

By Jordan Reeves | I Love Softball | August 17, 2026

Twenty-six days from now, USA Softball opens the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. The 16-player roster has been set since July 1. The venue is the same one that hosted the International Cup three weeks ago. The only thing left is to play the games.

The path in Group C runs through three countries that will not give ground easily.

The Group C Breakdown

USA's five opponents in Group C, ranked by the WBSC world rankings at the time of the group draw:

  • Netherlands — No. 6 in the world
  • Mexico — No. 7
  • China — No. 8
  • New Zealand — No. 23
  • South Africa — No. 36

Only the top two teams from Group C advance to the 2027 World Championship in Brisbane, Australia. The Netherlands, Mexico, and China are ranked opponents with rosters capable of manufacturing runs against anyone in the field. USA is the No. 1 ranked team in the world and the favorite to advance, but the margin for error is thin when three of the five opponents sit in the world's top eight.

Inside the Roster

The 16-player USA roster carries significant international experience. Two members — Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola — were part of the 2020 U.S. Olympic team that brought home gold from Tokyo. Seven players competed on the 2025 World Games gold medal squad. Ten were part of the 2025 USA Down Under Series.

By conference, the SEC dominates the roster. Ten of the sixteen players earned their college credentials in the Southeastern Conference. The Big 12 contributes three players; the Big Ten adds three more. Oklahoma leads all individual schools with four members of the squad: Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell.

The roster's depth of international experience matters in a format where the margin between advancing and going home runs through a handful of games over five days. Players who have been in those high-stakes environments before — specifically Garcia and Mulipola — bring something into the dugout that statistics don't fully capture.

What Advancing Means

A top-two finish in Group C sends USA to Brisbane for the 2027 World Championship. That is the goal the entire preparation cycle has been building toward. The World Cup Group Stage in OKC is the qualification tournament, not the destination. Every game in Group C is in service of that Brisbane bid.

For context on the stakes: a team that wins the Group Stage and fails to advance gets nothing but experience. The 2027 World Championship in Brisbane is where the global softball crown is decided. Groups in OKC are where that path gets opened or closed.

USA's combination of world ranking, roster experience, and home-field familiarity at Devon Park makes them the Group C favorite. But the Netherlands, Mexico, and China will each present genuine tests over the five-day window. Softball at this level does not produce automatic wins.

What's Next

Group Stage play runs September 12–16 at Devon Park, Oklahoma City. USA's full schedule within Group C will be confirmed through WBSC's official event pages. The top two finishers advance to the 2027 Women's Softball World Championship in Brisbane, Australia.
