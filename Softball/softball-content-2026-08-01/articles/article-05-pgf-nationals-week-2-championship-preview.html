PGF Nationals Week 2 Championship Day Arrives Tomorrow — One Day of Bracket Play Left

By Emily Rawlings

August 1, 2026

After weeks of bracket play in the Huntington Beach and Irvine area, the PGF Nationals Week 2 is one day away from deciding its champions. Tomorrow — August 2 — the 14U, 12U, and 10U divisions will each crown a national champion at Deanna Manning Stadium, Bill Barber Park in Irvine, California. The bracket has narrowed to its final competitors, the stakes couldn't be higher, and — with the September 1 recruiting window now 31 days out — the timing could not be more loaded.

Championship Day: What to Know

Deanna Manning Stadium is one of the premier venues in travel softball. Named for a legendary PGF figure, it has hosted some of the biggest moments in the tournament's history. Tomorrow it will host three championship games across three age divisions — 14U, 12U, and 10U — all live on PGF.TV.

Teams that survive to tomorrow have already navigated a demanding week of pool play and bracket games against the best travel softball programs in the country. The finals are not decided by which team brings the most talent — they're decided by which team performs under the unique pressure of a championship day. That's what makes PGF Nationals different from a regular tournament, and it's what makes the final moments tomorrow must-watch softball.

The September 1 Countdown

Every championship game at Deanna Manning Stadium tomorrow takes place exactly 31 days before the September 1 recruiting window opens. September 1 is the date when Division I college coaches can officially begin calling high school recruits in the Class of 2028. Every player who takes the field in a PGF Nationals championship game is building the resume that college coaches will reference when those calls begin.

College coaches have been on the sidelines all week in Irvine, clipboards in hand, building their scouting reports. They can't make phone calls yet. They can watch. They can evaluate. And what they see tomorrow — at Deanna Manning Stadium, in the championship rounds — will inform recruiting decisions for the next two to three years. For the athletes competing, this is more than a trophy. It's an audition.

A Summer of Travel Softball Comes to a Close

PGF Nationals Week 2 is one of the final major travel softball tournaments of the summer calendar. After the PGF championships, the elite club season begins to wind down as players turn their attention to school-year tryouts, fall practice, and showcase events. For many families, tomorrow represents the last major competition before the schedule resets entirely.

The significance isn't lost on anyone at Deanna Manning Stadium. Travel softball families understand the investment — in time, in travel, in coaching, in development — that it takes to reach the PGF Nationals championship round. Tomorrow is the payoff.

What's Next

Watch tomorrow's championships live on PGF.TV. Championship results will set the stage for the fall season and establish early recruiting narratives heading into the September 1 contact window. The 2026 travel softball summer has been exceptional — top programs from across the country competed at the highest level all season. Tomorrow, we find out who finishes on top.
