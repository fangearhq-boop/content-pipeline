The Little League Softball World Series Opens Tonight in Greenville — Here's How to Be Part of It

By Sarah Mitchell

August 1, 2026

The moment youth softball has been building toward all summer arrives tonight. The 2026 Little League Softball World Series opening ceremony takes place at 6 PM ET at Stallings Stadium at Elm Street Park in Greenville, North Carolina — and it is open to the public, free of charge. Twelve regional and international champions will take the field for the first time, the parade of champions begins at 5:15 PM, and by the end of the evening, the tournament that defines a generation of youth softball players will officially be underway.

Tonight's Opening Ceremony

Gates open before 5:15 PM, when the parade of champions kicks off the festivities. Twelve teams will march in — eight U.S. regional champions plus four international delegations — each representing the best youth softball program from their corner of the world. The parade circles the field before the ceremonial first pitches, which will be thrown by two active Athletes Unlimited Softball League players: Amanda Lorenz of the Texas Volts and Kinzie Hansen-McKinzie of the OKC Spark.

The fan zone features DJ Cutty Styles keeping the energy going before the ceremony, along with friendship bracelet stations, face painting, inflatable air hockey, and food trucks. All of it is free. Parking is also free. If you're within driving distance of eastern North Carolina, there is genuinely no reason to miss this.

The Teams

Eight U.S. regional champions qualified for the 2026 LLSWS, representing programs from across the country. Among the confirmed U.S. participants: Daviess County Little League from Kentucky (Central Region), Toms River Little League from New Jersey (Mid-Atlantic Region), Bristol Girls Softball Little League from Connecticut (New England Region), Johnston County Little League representing the host state of North Carolina, Snohomish Little League from Washington state (Northwest Region), Lake Mary Little League from Florida (Southeast Region), and Greater Helotes Little League from Texas (Southwest Region). Four international teams round out the 12-team field.

Tournament Play Begins August 2

The opening ceremony is the preamble. The games start Saturday, August 2, and run through August 9. For the first time in the tournament's history, all 22 games will be broadcast on ESPN linear platforms — not just streaming, but broadcast television. Every inning of every game will be available to fans nationwide, giving these young players one of the biggest stages in youth sports.

That matters because these athletes are the next generation of the sport. The players competing in Greenville this week are the college coaches' future top recruiting targets, the Athletes Unlimited Softball League's next draft picks, and potentially — years from now — Olympians. They're also kids who love softball, playing the biggest game of their lives in front of a national audience. That combination is exactly what makes the LLSWS one of the most compelling events in softball every summer.

What's Next

The opening ceremony tonight at 6 PM ET is just the start. Check littleleague.org for the full August 2–9 game schedule and ESPN's broadcast lineup. If you can't make it to Greenville in person, tune into ESPN to watch the next wave of softball stars take the field for the first time under the national spotlight. The tournament runs through August 9, and by the end, one program will have etched its name into Little League Softball World Series history.
