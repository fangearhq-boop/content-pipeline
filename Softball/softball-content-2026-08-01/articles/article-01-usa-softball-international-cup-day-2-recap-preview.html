USA Ran Rules Australia in Four Innings. Now Day 2 Brings Canada and American Samoa.

By Sarah Mitchell

August 1, 2026

If Day 1 of the 2026 USA Softball International Cup was a statement, Team USA made it loud. The USA Women's National Team run-ruled Australia 12-0 in just four innings Thursday night at Devon Park in Oklahoma City, hitting four home runs and piling up nine hits before the game was called. The USA Women's Elite Team also won, defeating No. 5 Chinese Taipei 6-4 in a competitive opener that featured a comeback. Both squads are 1-0, and both are back in action tonight.

The Night 1 Recap

The WNT-Australia game was over almost before it began. Maya Brady set the tone with a home run and four RBI. Janae Jefferson, Dejah Mulipola, and Skylar Wallace also cleared the fences, giving USA four long balls in four innings. The final: USA WNT 12, Australia 0, in four innings via the run-rule. The No. 2-ranked team in the world left no doubt.

On the other side of the bracket, the Elite Team's 6-4 win over No. 5 Chinese Taipei was more of a battle. Chinese Taipei, ranked fifth in the world, pushed back — but the Elite Team was the better squad in the end, earning a win that sets up a huge Day 2 matchup against the American Samoa team.

In the other Day 1 matchups, No. 1 Japan defeated American Samoa in Group A, and No. 4 Canada defeated No. 8 China in Group B, setting the complete 1-0 through every top seed through opening night.

Tonight's Schedule

Two games on ESPN+ headline the Day 2 slate:

  • 5 PM ET — USA Elite vs. American Samoa (ESPN+)
  • 8 PM ET — USA WNT vs. No. 4 Canada (ESPN+)

The afternoon slate streams on USA Softball TV: No. 1 Japan vs. No. 5 Chinese Taipei at 11 AM ET and No. 8 China vs. No. 11 Australia at 2 PM ET. On paper, the USA's Day 2 matchups look favorable — American Samoa is No. 42 in the world, and Canada, while ranked fourth, has yet to be truly tested. Don't expect either to roll over, but the USA enters as a heavy favorite in both games.

The WNT-Canada match at 8 PM ET is the one to watch. Canada was the first team to beat the USA at the 2024 Paris Olympics — a loss that stings and one the WNT has had motivation to answer ever since. Tonight is not the Olympics, but it's the same tournament stage, the same two rivals, and a result that matters for 2027 World Cup seeding.

What's at Stake in the Bracket

The 2026 USA Softball International Cup features eight teams split into two groups of four. Each team plays three round-robin games, with the top two from each group advancing to the playoff round on August 2. Medal games are August 3. The six WBSC-ranked nations — Japan, Canada, China, Australia, Chinese Taipei, and the two USA squads — are all competing for points toward the 2027 Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage qualification.

With today's games (Day 2) and tomorrow's Day 3, the group standings will take shape. A USA sweep tonight sets up the two squads to clinch playoff positions before the final round-robin day. If Maya Brady and the WNT handle Canada with anything like the authority they showed against Australia, those conversations will happen fast.

What's Next

Tune in tonight on ESPN+ — 5 PM ET for the Elite Team, 8 PM ET for the WNT. Day 3 is Sunday, August 2, followed by the playoff round. Medal games are Monday, August 3. The full five-day schedule means USA has no days off between now and the final, so roster management becomes a question. But after a 12-0 opening night, momentum is squarely on Team USA's side.
