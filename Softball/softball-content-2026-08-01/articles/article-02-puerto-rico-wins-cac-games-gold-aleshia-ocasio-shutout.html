Puerto Rico Wins CAC Games Gold — Aleshia Ocasio's Shutout Was the Whole Story

By Emily Rawlings

August 1, 2026

Aleshia Ocasio walked out to the circle in Santo Domingo on Thursday with a gold medal on the line, and she made sure nobody touched it. The Puerto Rico pitcher threw a complete-game shutout — seven innings, ten strikeouts, two hits — as Puerto Rico defeated Cuba 2-0 in the women's softball gold medal game at the 2026 Central American and Caribbean Games. Puerto Rico went unbeaten throughout the tournament. Ocasio barely missed a beat.

How Puerto Rico Won

The gold medal game was tight. Cuba sent out left-hander Yilian Tornes, who limited Puerto Rico to two runs — both of which came in the fourth inning on RBIs from Carsyn Gordon and Odalys Cordova. Two runs turned out to be all Ocasio needed.

Ocasio dominated from start to finish. Her ten strikeouts through seven innings gave the Cuban lineup no answer at the plate. Cuba managed just two hits all game — and neither traveled far enough to matter. When the final out was recorded, Puerto Rico had completed one of the cleanest gold medal runs the CAC Games softball tournament had seen.

Puerto Rico's path to gold was not a straightforward march. In the super round before the final, they survived a thriller against the U.S. Virgin Islands — a game that went down to the wire before Puerto Rico prevailed. Then, against Cuba in the final, Ocasio made it look easy. Tournament total: five wins, zero losses.

Who Is Aleshia Ocasio?

Ocasio has been a fixture in Puerto Rico's international softball program for years. She is also a veteran of the Athletes Unlimited Softball League, where she has competed at the professional level and built the kind of big-game experience that showed Thursday in Santo Domingo. Throwing a complete-game shutout with ten strikeouts in a gold medal game — on the road in the Dominican Republic — is exactly the moment Ocasio's career has been building toward.

For the broader Puerto Rico softball program, this gold medal matters beyond the win-loss column. Puerto Rico has been one of the most consistent women's softball nations in the Western Hemisphere for years, and Thursday's performance on the international stage arrives at exactly the right moment: as the sport looks ahead to the 2027 Women's Softball World Cup qualification cycle.

The Bigger Picture

The 2026 CAC Games in Santo Domingo ran from July 26 through July 31 and featured eight nations competing in women's softball: Puerto Rico, Cuba, Mexico, Dominican Republic, Venezuela, Colombia, El Salvador, and the U.S. Virgin Islands. Puerto Rico entered as the defending CAC Games champion and world No. 3 — and played like it from the first game to the last.

The silver went to Cuba. Mexico earned bronze. Puerto Rico had already lifted three gold medals and one bronze at the 2026 CAC Games across all sports before the softball final, making Thursday's result another chapter in a dominant overall performance by Puerto Rican athletics in Santo Domingo.

What's Next

Puerto Rico's next major international challenge comes in the 2027 Women's Softball World Cup cycle, where results in tournaments like the CAC Games and the USA Softball International Cup feed directly into seeding and qualification. After Thursday's performance — particularly Ocasio's masterpiece in the gold medal game — Puerto Rico has made a clear statement about where they stand. They are not a team to be overlooked on the international stage, and they will arrive at the 2027 World Cup with momentum, confidence, and one of the best pitchers on the international circuit.
