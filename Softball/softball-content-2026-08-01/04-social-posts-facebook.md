### STORY 2: Puerto Rico Wins CAC Games Gold

**FB Long-Form — Post Time: 11:00 AM ET**

🏆 **PUERTO RICO IS THE 2026 CAC GAMES SOFTBALL GOLD MEDALIST.**

On July 31 in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, Puerto Rico shut out Cuba 2-0 in the gold medal game — completing an unbeaten run through the entire Central American & Caribbean Games softball tournament.

The story of the game: **Aleshia Ocasio**.

The veteran AUSL pitcher was absolutely masterful on the biggest stage. Complete game. 7 innings. 10 strikeouts. Just 2 hits allowed. She gave the opposition nothing to work with, and Puerto Rico's offense did just enough — scoring twice in the 4th inning on RBIs by Carsyn Gordon and Odalys Cordova.

Puerto Rico's path to gold:
- Pool play: Two run-rule wins (15-0 and 16-1)
- Super round: Survived a thriller against the U.S. Virgin Islands
- Gold medal game: Shut out Cuba 2-0

This is a huge result for Puerto Rico's international softball program. They went 5-0 through the tournament and never trailed. As the sport looks toward the 2027 Women's Softball World Cup, Puerto Rico has served notice: they are a serious threat.

Congratulations to Puerto Rico and to Aleshia Ocasio — what a performance. 🌟

Here's what Texas Tech is getting:

**Jaysoni Beachum, 3B/OF**
- Career at FSU: **.375 batting average. 37 home runs. 180 RBIs.**
- 2024 NFCA Freshman of the Year
- 2024 ACC Freshman of the Year
- 2026 Second Team All-American

Together, Torres and Beachum accounted for 28% of FSU's runs and 35% of their home runs in 2026. Head coach Lonni Alameda now faces a rebuild of historic proportions for a program that has been a perennial national power.
