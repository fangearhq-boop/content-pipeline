Team Watley Wins the AUSL Pro Game 6-2 — and the All-Star Cup Is Saturday

By Emily Rawlings

August 6, 2026

Wednesday night at Stallings Stadium in Greenville, the 2026 AUSL Pro Game delivered what it promised. Team Watley defeated Team Fernandez 6-2 in front of the Little League Softball World Series crowd — 24 of the best softball players in the world, coached by Natasha Watley and Lisa Fernandez, playing a full nine-inning exhibition at the same venue where the LLSWS was unfolding all week.

How the Game Went

Team Watley controlled the game from the outset, finishing with 6 runs, 7 hits, and 1 error. Team Fernandez managed 2 runs on 5 hits but committed 3 errors — costly in a game decided by execution margins. The final scoreboard: Watley 6, Fernandez 2. The players wore their regular-season AUSL uniforms, making it easy to recognize roster affiliations throughout the game.

Both coaching legends — Watley, a multiple World Cup champion and USA softball icon, and Fernandez, the three-time Olympic gold medalist who is the most decorated pitcher in USA softball history — were on the field as more than figureheads. The exhibition was competitive. The crowd had been at the LLSWS all week. They knew who they were watching.

The Week These Players Put In

The Pro Game didn't happen in isolation. The AUSL players arrived at Stallings Stadium on Tuesday — the same day as LLSWS pool play games — doing first pitches and meet-and-greets with the competing teams. Wednesday morning, before the night's game, they ran a youth Tee Ball clinic for two local Little League programs. By the time the Pro Game tipped off at 7 PM ET, the LLSWS kids and their families already knew who these players were. That's the design: professional softball players showing up not as spectators but as participants in the Little League week, making the connection between youth softball and the highest level of the game visible and personal.

Next: The AUSL All-Star Cup Starts Saturday

The Pro Game was a preview. The All-Star Cup is the main event. The 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup begins Saturday, August 8, in Rosemont, Illinois, and runs through August 30. Forty-five players are participating, spread across 12 games broadcast on ESPN, MLB Network, and CBS Sports Network. This is the summer's final showcase of AUSL talent — the players who finished the regular season and championship, now together in one venue for one last extended run before the offseason. If you were watching the Pro Game Wednesday night and want to see more of the same talent, Rosemont is where it continues this weekend.

What's Next

AUSL All-Star Cup coverage begins Saturday, August 8. The LLSWS championship game is Saturday, August 9. Both events close out the same week — and for softball fans, it is one of the most content-rich weekends of the year. Keep an eye on ESPN, MLB Network, and CBS Sports Network for the All-Star Cup schedule. The full game list runs through August 30.
