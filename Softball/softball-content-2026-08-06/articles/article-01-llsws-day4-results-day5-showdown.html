LLSWS Day 4: Washington and Mid-Atlantic Advance, Lake Mary vs. NC Tops Today's Schedule

By Sarah Mitchell

August 6, 2026

Day 4 of the 2026 Little League Softball World Series at Stallings Stadium in Greenville, North Carolina eliminated two more teams Wednesday and set up the tournament's most anticipated game: an undefeated showdown between Lake Mary, Florida, and the NC host team from Clayton, happening today at 1 PM ET on ESPN.

Game 13: Washington 9, Canada 1

Washington (Snohomish, WA) eliminated Canada (Repentigny, Quebec) in the Purple Bracket, winning 9-1. The performance was defined by pitcher Madison Locke, who threw all six innings, allowing 4 hits and 1 run while issuing zero walks and striking out 12 batters. Janessa Malone hit 3-for-3 with 2 RBI; Layla Bailey added 2 RBI. Canada is eliminated. Washington advances and stays alive in bracket play.

Game 14: Mid-Atlantic 11, Asia-Pacific 0

Toms River, New Jersey's Mid-Atlantic squad avenged the tournament's stinging opening. On August 2, Japan's Asia-Pacific team from Sangō had beaten them 4-1. Wednesday, Mid-Atlantic shut them out 11-0. Pitcher Jaelyn Brown held Japan scoreless; third baseman Analeise Curley anchored the defense. New Jersey scored in the first inning on a pair of Japanese errors, then built the lead with sacrifice flies through the next two innings and never let off. Japan is eliminated. Toms River advances.

The Unbeaten Teams

Heading into Day 5, four teams remain undefeated in pool play. Lake Mary, Florida (Southeast) is the most dominant — they have outscored opponents 29-0 across two games that never reached the four-inning mark. Their run-rule wins over Snohomish, WA (14-0) and Bologna, Italy (15-0) have made them the tournament's most feared offense. Clayton, North Carolina (the NC host entry from Johnston County) is also 2-0, with wins of 16-4 over Canada and 2-1 over Texas. They are the crowd favorite at Stallings Stadium, the home team playing before a home crowd in their first-ever LLSWS appearance. Washington (Snohomish, WA) and Mid-Atlantic (Toms River, NJ) both advanced Wednesday and remain alive.

Today: Game 15 at 1 PM ET on ESPN

Lake Mary meets Clayton NC at Stallings Stadium today at 1 PM ET, live on ESPN. Both teams are 2-0. Neither team has been scored on — a combined run differential of 29 to zero for Lake Mary, while Clayton has allowed a total of five runs in two games. This is the first meeting of two undefeated teams in the 2026 tournament. One of these programs will leave with their first loss. Game 16 follows at 5 PM ET on ESPN: Latin America vs. the Game 8 winner.

What's Next

The LLSWS championship game is scheduled for Saturday, August 9. The bracket continues to narrow from here — every game eliminates a team or forces an elimination next round. The Lake Mary–NC matchup is the pivot point of this tournament: whoever wins today is on the short path to Saturday. Today's slate tips off at 1 PM ET on ESPN.
