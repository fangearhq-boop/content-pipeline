USA Softball Won the International Cup. Now the World Cup Qualifier Is 37 Days Away.

By Emily Rawlings

August 6, 2026

On August 3 in Oklahoma City, Montana Fouts carried a no-hitter into the seventh inning and the USA Women's National Team defeated Japan 2-0 to win the International Cup gold medal. The summer showcase event closed with the WNT standing at the top. Three days later, the next checkpoint on the international calendar is already 37 days out.

The Next Stop: WBSC World Cup Group C Qualifier

The WBSC Softball Women's World Cup Group C qualifier is scheduled for September 12-16, 2026 in Oklahoma City. The same venue — Devon Park — that hosted the International Cup tournament. The same USA program that closed August with gold now returns to OKC in September to compete for 2027 World Cup qualification. The qualifier is not an exhibition and it is not a showcase — it is a competition with bids to the 2027 Women's Softball World Cup on the line.

Why This Matters

The International Cup was a competitive summer event that brought elite national teams together and provided meaningful competition context. The World Cup qualifier is a step up in stakes — qualifying automatically for the 2027 World Cup places a program in the sport's highest-profile international tournament. For USA softball, which has won the World Cup multiple times and enters every international event as a medal contender, the Group C qualifier is the pathway to the next major global stage.

Beyond the 2027 World Cup, the broader lens is the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics — softball's return to the Olympic program after its restoration to the Games. Every international result between now and 2028 carries weight in the qualification picture. The World Cup qualifier in September is the next step in that longer arc.

The Momentum Is There

The USA WNT's International Cup run — 6-2 win over their Elite B-team in the semifinals, then Fouts' near-no-hitter over Japan in the final — demonstrated that the national team program is operating at its peak. Montana Fouts was named AUSL Pitcher of the Year for the regular season and pitched the WNT to gold in the same month. Maya Brady was named the International Cup Tournament MVP. The roster showed depth, execution, and the finishing ability that has defined USA softball in high-stakes moments.

Thirty-seven days separate the end of the International Cup and the start of the World Cup qualifier. That turnaround is not unusual for a national team program that operates year-round. The WNT will be ready.

What's Next

The WBSC Softball Women's World Cup Group C qualifier runs September 12-16 in Oklahoma City at Devon Park. Coverage will follow when rosters and the full field are announced. For USA WNT developments through August, follow the AUSL All-Star Cup (August 8-30 in Rosemont, IL) — several WNT players, including Montana Fouts, are part of the All-Star Cup roster. The international picture builds from here.
