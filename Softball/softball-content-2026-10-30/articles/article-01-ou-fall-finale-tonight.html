OU Fall Finale Tonight: Sooners Host Seminole State at Love's Field

By Jordan Reeves | October 30, 2026 | I Love Softball

Oklahoma softball closes out its fall schedule tonight at Love's Field, hosting Seminole State at 6:30 PM CT in a free exhibition streamed live on 1oklahoma.com. It is the final public look at the 2027 Sooners before the calendar turns to the spring season, and it features two of the most compelling names in college softball front and center.

Wells and Kavan Headline the Roster

Kendall Wells enters tonight's game with a fall of momentum behind her. The Sooners' outfielder and power hitter finished the 2026 regular season with 39 home runs, earning NFCA Freshman of the Year honors — one of the most decorated first-year campaigns in recent program history. Tonight gives fans another look at a hitter who projects as one of the most dangerous bats in the country heading into her sophomore year.

Rylie Kavan, a two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player, anchors the other end of the lineup. Kavan's postseason track record speaks for itself. Having claimed MOP honors twice, she is the kind of player who elevates every at-bat and every game-day atmosphere she walks into. For anyone who has not seen her play live, tonight at Love's Field is the opportunity.

Fall Ball as a Preview Window

Oklahoma's fall exhibitions have drawn strong crowds to Love's Field all month. The Battle Series format — which pitted the Sooners against various opponents throughout October — gave head coach Patty Gasso and her staff extended looks at roster construction heading into the spring. Tonight's game against Seminole State is the coda to that process.

Fall ball serves a specific purpose for a program like Oklahoma. With the roster largely set and the spring schedule still months away, these games are about rhythm, evaluation, and building team chemistry. Fans get access to Love's Field at no cost, and the players get game-speed reps in a low-pressure environment. It is a rare alignment of interests, and tonight is the last chance to take advantage of it until February.

How to Watch

Tonight's game is free to attend in person at Love's Field in Norman, Oklahoma. For fans who cannot make it to the stadium, the game streams live and free on 1oklahoma.com. First pitch is scheduled for 6:30 PM CT.

No tickets are required. Admission is open to the public for all remaining fall exhibitions at Love's Field.

What's Next

After tonight, Oklahoma softball goes dark publicly until the spring 2027 schedule begins. The Sooners are expected to open play in February, with the full Big 12 schedule running through May and a postseason run as the program's standard expectation. Tonight at Love's Field is the last preview before that journey starts in earnest. For fans watching from home or filling the seats in Norman, it is a sendoff worth paying attention to.
