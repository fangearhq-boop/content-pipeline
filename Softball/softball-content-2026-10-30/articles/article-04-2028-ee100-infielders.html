2028 EE100 Infielders Rankings Drop Today as the Biggest Class in List History Nears Completion

By Jordan Reeves | October 30, 2026 | I Love Softball

The 2028 Extra Elite 100 recruiting cycle enters its final stretch today as Extra Inning Softball releases the Infielders 201-300 rankings at extrainningsoftball.com. Outfielders 201-259 follow tomorrow, closing out the positional rankings for a class that drew more than 1,200 nominations — the largest in the history of the list. The framework for understanding the 2028 class is now almost fully assembled, and the picture at the top is clear.

The Top-5 Is Set

The moment the softball recruiting world had been building toward all month arrived Monday when Extra Inning Softball revealed the Top-5 of the 2028 Extra Elite 100. No. 1 is Landers, committed to Texas — a player whose standing at the top of the class reflects the Longhorns' continued ability to recruit at the highest national level. No. 2 is Holden, committed to Florida, and No. 3 is Schlader, committed to Oklahoma, whose 70-plus mph riseball has made her one of the most watched pitching prospects in the country.

Texas, Florida, and Oklahoma holding the top three spots reflects where recruiting power has settled in the modern era of college softball. All three programs entered the 2024-2025 academic year as national title contenders, and all three are securing the talent to remain at that level through the 2028-2031 window.

Scale of the 2028 Class

More than 1,200 nominations makes this the largest EE100 class in the history of the ranking. That number reflects the growth of the sport's recruiting pipeline at the high school level, the expansion of travel ball programs nationwide, and the increased visibility that comes with college softball's growing media footprint.

For programs outside the traditional top tier, a larger class means more opportunity to identify and land impact players. Tennessee added commits including Kuiper and Eldred. Oklahoma added Schlader and several other top-100 prospects. Florida's Holden headline a class that gives the Gators a recruiting win in the critical 2028 cycle.

The Contact Window Is Closed

The October 24-25 contact window — when coaches were permitted to make direct contact with 2028 prospects — has now closed. The commit wave that followed the window added dozens of new pledges across the sport's top programs. The next opportunity for direct contact does not open until spring 2027, meaning today's rankings and tomorrow's outfielders release mark the effective end of the 2028 recruiting public conversation until February.

Recruits who remain uncommitted after this window will weigh their decisions through the offseason, with the spring travel ball season serving as the next major evaluation period for coaches and prospects alike.

What's Next

Today's Infielders 201-300 release and tomorrow's Outfielders 201-259 wrap up the positional rankings for the 2028 class. Extra Inning Softball's hitter hot lists follow in November, providing additional context on the batting prospects in the class. For fans tracking the 2028 recruiting landscape, the full picture is now largely in view. Texas leads the class. Florida and Oklahoma are right behind. And more than 1,200 athletes started this process for a reason — the sport is growing, and the talent pipeline is deeper than ever.
