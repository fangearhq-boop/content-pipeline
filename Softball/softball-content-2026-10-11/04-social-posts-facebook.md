### STORY 1: Texas Longhorns Fall Ball

**FB Long-Form — 11:00 AM ET**
The defending national champions are back at work — and they have two of the most dangerous players in college softball doing it together for the first time.

Isa Torres transferred to Texas from Florida State after a historic 2026 season: .530 batting average, 16 home runs, 56 RBI, and the D1Softball Player of the Year award. She wasn't just one of the best players in the country last season — she WAS the best.

Teagan Kavan won her second Women's College World Series Most Outstanding Player award in June, cementing herself as one of the elite pitchers in program history.

Can Texas three-peat? The last programs to win three straight were UCLA (1988–90) and Oklahoma (2021–23). It's a rare feat — but the talent is there.

### STORY 3: Tennessee Coaching Search

**FB Long-Form — 3:00 PM ET**
Two key voices in Tennessee's dugout are walking out the door.

Craig Snider — a former Texas Tech head coach who joined the Lady Vols in June 2024 as hitting coach — and assistant Stephanie Sanders are both departing after the 2026 season. D1Softball's coaching carousel first reported the news, and as of today no replacements have been named.

This matters: Snider was part of a staff that helped Tennessee return to the Women's College World Series in both 2025 and 2026. His background as a former head coach gave the program a veteran presence in a critical offensive role. Sanders' contributions were in player development and on-field coaching.

Karen Weekly has built one of the sport's elite programs. But replacing two experienced coaches before signing day — and before the 2027 preseason begins — is a real challenge. Who she brings in will say a lot about where Tennessee is headed.

No names have been publicly connected to either opening. Expect the coaching carousel to move quickly once candidates emerge.
