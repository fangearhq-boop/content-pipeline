Class of 2027 Recruiting Roundup: Tennessee, OU, Arizona and UCLA All Making Moves

By Emily Rawlings | October 11, 2026 | I Love Softball

October is when college softball's recruiting calendar shifts into high gear for the Class of 2027. The contact period opened September 1 for 2027 prospects, giving coaches their first chance to build direct relationships with the players who will define programs for years to come. Two weeks in, the commitments are already piling up at the top of the recruiting board.

Here is where the landscape stands heading into mid-October, with multiple Power Four programs already landing players who will reshape their rosters for the 2027 season and beyond.

Tennessee Adds a Four-Star Outfielder

Emma Pynes, a four-star outfielder, has committed to Tennessee. Pynes gives Karen Weekly's program another building block for its post-2026 roster rebuild, adding to a recruiting class that carries extra importance given the staff turnover the Lady Vols are navigating. Tennessee reached the Women's College World Series in both 2025 and 2026, and the 2027 recruiting class needs to reinforce the roster as upperclassmen graduate and move on.

Oklahoma Lands the No. 3 Overall Player in the Class

Patty Gasso added Finlee Williams — the No. 3 overall prospect in Softball America's Class of 2027 rankings — as the fourth verbal commitment to the Sooners' class this month. Williams is an outfielder, giving OU another elite piece in a recruiting cycle that is rapidly becoming one of the deeper classes in program history. Oklahoma is also tied with Texas Tech for No. 1 in the 2028 recruiting class, suggesting the Sooners' pipeline is as healthy as it has been in years.

Arizona, UCLA, and Florida Make Early Noise

Arizona head coach Caitlin Lowe — in her first full recruiting cycle leading the Wildcats — signed three top commits from the 2027 class in September and early October. Lowe's reputation as one of the game's best players gave her instant credibility on the recruiting trail, and Arizona's early class is proof that the program is a legitimate destination again.

UCLA secured a pair of top-ranked standouts from the 2027 class, while Florida added a trio of sluggers. The Gators — who landed No. 2 overall 2028 prospect Kylie Holden just last week — are building aggressively across multiple class years, suggesting head coach Tim Walton is in an aggressive growth phase.

The No. 1 overall prospect in the entire class of 2027 is five-star Aspen Boulware, who committed to South Carolina. Boulware's decision to head to Columbia is a statement: the Gamecocks are no longer just a regional power. They are a national one.

What's Next

October and November are historically the most active months for verbal commitments in the 2027 class. As fall ball games provide coaches with additional evaluation time, the pace of commitments will continue to accelerate. Programs that have not yet secured their top targets for 2027 are running out of time — the best players make their decisions early, and the board is filling up fast. Check back weekly as the Class of 2027 takes shape across the country.
