Red Raiders on the Road: Texas Tech Fall Ball Heads to West Texas A&M

By Sarah Mitchell | October 11, 2026 | I Love Softball

Texas Tech softball is hitting the road for fall ball Sunday, traveling to Canyon to face West Texas A&M in the latest installment of the Red Raiders' fall schedule. For a program that finished as the 2026 national runner-up at 54-14, fall ball is less about proving anything and more about installing the pieces that will push them back to Oklahoma City in May.

Head coach Gerry Glasco enters the fall with seven All-Americans returning to his roster — a number that reflects the depth of talent he has assembled at Lubbock. The question is not whether Texas Tech is good. It is whether the new additions are ready to contribute alongside that returning core.

New Faces, Familiar Ceiling

Three names stand out among TTU's fall additions: Lacey Beachum, who came out of Florida State's transfer portal and was previously reported as an All-American in her own right; a highly recruited bat named Pickering; and Heavener, another incoming piece for Glasco's 2027 roster. In a program already loaded with All-Americans, adding talent of this caliber is a sign that Texas Tech is not content with runner-up finishes.

The Red Raiders are also tied with Oklahoma for the No. 1 ranking in the 2028 recruiting class, per Softball America — a pipeline signal that suggests the program's best years may still be ahead of it.

Why West Texas A&M?

The Lady Buffs are a natural fall opponent for the Red Raiders — geographically close, Division II-level talent, and a team that can give Tech's starters quality at-bats and innings without the risk of injury that comes with a traditional scrimmage. Last fall, the two programs played a 10-inning game that finished 29-5 in favor of Tech. Expect a similar dynamic Sunday as Glasco gets his first extended look at how his new roster pieces perform in a game setting.

What's Next

Texas Tech's fall slate continues through the end of October, featuring additional games against New Mexico and Odessa College. The final stretch of fall action will give Glasco a clearer picture of his starting lineup and rotation heading into preseason preparations. For a program with national championship ambitions, every fall rep counts — and this Red Raiders squad has the talent to make 2027 the year they finally finish the job.
