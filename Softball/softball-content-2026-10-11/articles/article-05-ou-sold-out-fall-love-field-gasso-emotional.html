Sold Out in October: Patty Gasso's Emotional Reaction Says Everything About Oklahoma's Hunger

By Emily Rawlings | October 11, 2026 | I Love Softball

Patty Gasso is not easily moved. Thirty-plus years of coaching, seven national championships, and a career that has made her the standard by which every college softball head coach is measured — she has seen it all. But when she looked out at Love's Field in Norman and saw it sold out for a fall intrasquad scrimmage, she got emotional.

That reaction tells you more about where Oklahoma softball is right now than any preseason ranking could. The Sooners missed the Women's College World Series in 2026. In Norman, that is not acceptable.

The Roster That Has This Fan Base Dreaming

Jordy Coleman Wells is back. The slugger tied the all-time single-season NCAA home run record with 39 in 2026 — a feat that would have put any other program in Oklahoma City. Instead, the Sooners fell at the Super Regional stage, ending their season short of the goal. That sting drove the offseason, and it is driving the crowd support this fall.

Sydney Berzon transferred in from LSU and brings a 2.02 ERA, 52 career wins, and more than 400 strikeouts to OU's rotation. She is one of the most accomplished transfer pitchers in recent college softball history. Pair her with Wells in the same dugout, and you have a team that expects to be in Oklahoma City every May.

Battle Series Game 3 Is Tuesday

The third installment of Oklahoma's fall Battle Series intrasquad games takes place this Tuesday, October 14, at 6:30 PM CT at Love's Field. Admission is free. If the first two installments are any indication, the stands will be full again — and Gasso will be watching her team prepare for a 2027 season that the program believes can end only one way.

What's Next

Battle Series Game 3 is Tuesday, October 14, at 6:30 PM CT at Love's Field in Norman — free admission. Game 4 follows on October 21. After the Battle Series concludes, OU plays USAO (Oct. 23), Seminole State (Oct. 30), and East Texas A&M (Nov. 4) to close the fall slate. For a fan base that sold out a scrimmage, this fall is less about fall ball and more about the promise of what is coming in 2027.
