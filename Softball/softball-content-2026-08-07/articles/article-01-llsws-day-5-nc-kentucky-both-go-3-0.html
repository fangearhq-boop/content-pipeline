Anna-Lynn Clark Fans 13 as NC Goes 3-0; Kentucky Blanks Puerto Rico at the LLSWS

By Sarah Mitchell

August 7, 2026

Day 5 of the 2026 Little League Softball World Series at Stallings Stadium in Greenville, North Carolina delivered the clearest statement yet about who belongs in the bracket championship picture — and it came on the end of Anna-Lynn Clark's arm.

Clark, the pitcher for North Carolina's Clayton Little League entry, threw a complete game Saturday, striking out 13 Florida batters in a 5-2 win over Lake Mary that ended the Florida team's unbeaten run and sent NC to 3-0 heading into the weekend. In the Orange Bracket, Kentucky's Daviess County team did its part with a clean 2-0 shutout of Puerto Rico, joining NC as the only teams left with perfect records and Saturday bracket championship byes.

Clark Makes it Back-to-Back Double-Digit Strikeout Outings

The NC pitcher had posted a standout performance earlier in the tournament, and she raised the level on Day 5. Clark struck out 13 across six innings, allowing five hits, two runs — only one earned — and two walks. NC's offense provided the cushion when it needed it most, erupting for five runs in the fifth inning to turn a one-run game into a comfortable lead.

For Lake Mary, the result was a sharp contrast to the tournament's first two days. The Florida team had outscored opponents by 29 combined runs heading into Thursday's game — dominating both contests so thoroughly that the final margins never told the full story. Against Clark, they had no answer. Lake Mary drops to 2-1 and now faces a must-win elimination game today.

Kentucky Makes It Two Perfect Records

In the Orange Bracket, Kentucky's Daviess County team was equally methodical. The 2-0 shutout over Puerto Rico gave Kentucky a 3-0 record and delivered them to Saturday's Orange Bracket Championship without needing to play Friday. Puerto Rico, which entered at 1-1 after splitting its first two games, now faces its own elimination test later today.

With both NC and Kentucky safely into Saturday's bracket championships, the tournament's bracket picture is as clear as it has been all week. Two teams are rested, 3-0, and waiting. Two other bracket slots will be filled by the teams that survive today's elimination games.

Day 6: Elimination Games Today at Stallings Stadium

Today's schedule features two games with high stakes and no margin for error. At 3 PM ET on ESPN, Lake Mary, Florida faces Washington in the Purple Bracket elimination game. The loser goes home. Whoever wins advances to face North Carolina in Saturday's Purple Bracket Championship.

The Orange Bracket elimination game follows at 6 PM ET, with Puerto Rico facing the TBD qualifier from that side of the bracket. North Carolina and Kentucky both have rest days today before their Saturday championship games.

The 2026 LLSWS has delivered consistent offense and multiple dominant pitching performances across five days. Day 6 narrows the field further. For Lake Mary — a team that arrived in Greenville having allowed no runs in 18 innings — Friday is the chance to show what this tournament run really means.

What's Next

Saturday, August 8 brings the bracket championship games — North Carolina in the Purple Bracket, Kentucky in the Orange Bracket, each facing the Day 6 survivor from their side. Championship wins Saturday would advance both teams to Sunday's Grand Final. Full Day 6 results and Saturday's matchup details will be available at LittleLeague.org and on ESPN.
