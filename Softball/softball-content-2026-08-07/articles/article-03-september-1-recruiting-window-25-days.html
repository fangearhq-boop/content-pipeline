September 1 Is 25 Days Away — and the Class of 2028 Is Already Making History

By Jordan Reeves

August 7, 2026

In 25 days, the phone lines open.

September 1 is the date NCAA Division I softball coaches can initiate direct contact with Class of 2028 prospects for the first time. For the thousands of high school juniors beginning their recruiting journeys this fall, it is the single most anticipated date on the softball calendar — the day the coaches who have been watching, evaluating, and quietly building lists can finally make their first call.

What September 1 Actually Means

Before September 1, Division I coaches are prohibited from initiating contact with Class of 2028 athletes. They cannot call them, text them, or email them. Even if a prospect reaches out first, a D1 coach cannot legally respond to initiate a recruiting relationship before the window opens.

When midnight strikes on September 1, all of that changes simultaneously. Phone calls, text messages, emails, official visit invitations, and unofficial visit invitations all become permissible at the same moment. Top programs will have organized their call lists and be ready to reach out before the clock turns. The athletes who are at the top of those lists will hear from multiple programs within hours.

For a prospect who hasn't yet heard much, September 1 is also an opportunity. Any coach who sends a message, makes a call, or offers a visit on that date is telling you exactly how highly they value you.

A Record Class of 2028

The volume of interest in this class is unprecedented. Extra Inning Softball, which runs one of the most widely-followed recruiting recognition programs in the sport, has received more than 1,226 nominations for their Class of 2028 program — the highest nomination count for any class in the company's history.

That number reflects a summer of exceptional exposure. PGF Nationals, major showcase tournaments, and events like the Little League Softball World Series sent thousands of Class of 2028 athletes in front of college coaching staffs. The record nominations suggest that coaches saw an unusual number of players they want to flag for formal recognition — and that the competition for the best players in this class will be intense from Day 1.

How to Be Ready

For Class of 2028 athletes, the 25 days remaining before September 1 matter. Programs that have been watching want to know who is organized, who has film ready, and who has thought through their priorities. A well-edited highlight video, an accurate and updated athletic profile, and a clear list of schools you want to hear from on September 1 are the basics.

The coaches already know who they are calling first. The athletes who are prepared for the conversation from Day 1 will have an advantage over those who are caught off guard.

What's Next

September 1, 2026 falls on a Tuesday. The first wave of Class of 2028 commitments will likely arrive within days — and possibly within hours — of the window opening. Follow d1softball.com and Extra Inning Softball's commit tracker for real-time updates as programs make their first offers and athletes announce their decisions.
