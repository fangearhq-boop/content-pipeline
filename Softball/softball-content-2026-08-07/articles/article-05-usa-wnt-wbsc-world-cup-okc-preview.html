USA Won the International Cup in OKC. Now They Come Back for the World Cup Group Stage.

By Emily Rawlings

August 7, 2026

Montana Fouts retired 18 consecutive Japan batters on August 3 before a leadoff single in the seventh inning ended her bid for perfection. She finished the game with a shutout anyway. USA 2, Japan 0. International Cup gold at Devon Park in Oklahoma City.

Four days later, USA's next challenge is already on the board — and it returns them to the same venue.

The WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage

The WBSC Softball Women's World Cup Group Stage is scheduled for September 12-16, 2026 in Oklahoma City at Devon Park. That is 36 days away. The event serves as a qualifying pathway toward the 2027 Women's Softball World Cup and, on the longer trajectory, the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics.

USA earned an automatic berth as the host nation. Their group is confirmed: Netherlands (No. 6 world ranking), Mexico (No. 7), China (No. 8), New Zealand (No. 23), and South Africa (No. 36).

The group includes three of the world's top eight teams. Netherlands and Mexico have been consistent contenders at the international level. China, ranked No. 8, has historically been among the sport's most technically proficient programs. USA will be heavy favorites, but the group stage is the format where any result can create complications — and the WNT will need to perform with consistency across five games in five days.

The Roster

USA named a 16-player WNT roster for the event. The confirmed players include Rachel Garcia, Dejah Mulipola, Montana Fouts, and Maya Brady — athletes who have been central to USA's recent international success, including the International Cup gold and the 2025 World Games medal. Also named are Kendall Wells (Oklahoma) and Taylor Shumaker (Florida), both making their international debuts with the WNT.

The roster is built around SEC athletes. Ten of the 16 players come from Southeastern Conference programs, with Oklahoma leading the school breakdown with four athletes and UCLA contributing three. The experience distribution spans veterans of the 2020 Olympic squad — Garcia and Mulipola — through athletes appearing in major international competition for the first time.

Devon Park, Again

There is something notable about USA returning to Devon Park in Oklahoma City just over a month after winning gold there. The venue is familiar. The stakes are different. The International Cup was an exhibition format with development objectives. The World Cup Group Stage determines World Cup qualification and establishes international ranking points that matter for seeding in future events.

For USA, the path back to Devon Park is a short one. The road ahead — through the World Cup and toward the 2028 Olympics — is considerably longer.

What's Next

The 2026 WBSC Softball Women's World Cup Group Stage runs September 12-16 at Devon Park, Oklahoma City. USA begins group play September 12. Full schedule and ticket information will be available at wbsc.org and usasoftball.com. The top finishers from the Group Stage advance to the final phase of World Cup qualifying, with World Cup berths to be determined by those results.
