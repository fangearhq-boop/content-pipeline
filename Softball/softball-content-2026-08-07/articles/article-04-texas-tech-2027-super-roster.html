Texas Tech Is Assembling the Most Complete 2027 Roster in College Softball

By Sarah Mitchell

August 7, 2026

Texas Tech went to the Women's College World Series Finals in 2026 under head coach Gerry Glasco. Then they went to work.

The Red Raiders have assembled what is widely regarded as the No. 1 transfer class in college softball heading into 2027 — and the individual résumés in that class make the case for Texas Tech as the preseason favorite before fall camp has even begun.

The Additions

Jaysoni Beachum arrives from Florida State as a 2-time All-American and former NFCA National Freshman of the Year. In 2026, Beachum hit .406 with 10 home runs and 69 RBI — performances that earned her 2nd Team All-America honors and the No. 3 ranking in the transfer portal with a 5-star grade from On3. She has two years of eligibility remaining under the updated five-in-five eligibility rule, making her one of the most valuable pieces in the portal regardless of position. Beachum hit .406 at Florida State — the program she is leaving — meaning she produced at that level despite being at a Power Conference program competing against top pitching every weekend.

Kasidi Pickering is the No. 2 ranked transfer in the portal, rated by Softball America. She comes to Lubbock from Oklahoma, where she was a 2024 national champion and WCWS All-Tournament honoree. Her 2026 numbers: .370 batting average, 20 home runs, 61 RBI. Across 186 career games at OU, she posted a .384 career average with 50 home runs and 169 RBI. She adds elite offensive production at an outfield/designated player role that complements Beachum rather than competing with her in the lineup.

Jayden Heavener fills the pitching need. The left-hander transfers from LSU, where she went 13-9 with a 2.81 ERA across 139.1 innings, striking out 128 batters and earning NFCA All-Region honors. She has two years of eligibility. Heavener gives Glasco a proven starter who has handled SEC competition — something Texas Tech pitchers will face in conference play every series.

The Existing Foundation

These three additions join a roster that was already built for postseason depth. Kaitlyn Terry, the UCLA transfer and 2-time All-American, brings versatility as a two-way player. Mia Williams, who transferred from Florida, earned 2026 NFCA All-American recognition before committing to Texas Tech. Returners Taylor Pannell, Moriah Polar, and Jasmyn Burns provide continuity from a program that was already playing championship softball before the portal class was finalized.

On paper, the argument that this is the deepest roster in college softball is straightforward: the program has multiple All-Americans at multiple positions, a proven pitching addition, and a coaching staff that just built a WCWS Finals team before acquiring any of these players.

What's Next

Texas Tech players are beginning campus arrivals this month, with fall practice and scrimmages to follow. Glasco has noted in past fall cycles that his most important evaluations happen in the fall — how players adjust to a new environment, how new transfer additions integrate into team chemistry, and how the competitive depth shakes out at each position. The 2027 season will not start until the spring, but the process begins now. Watch for fall camp updates as this roster takes the field together for the first time.
