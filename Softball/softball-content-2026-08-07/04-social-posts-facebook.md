### STORY 2: AUSL All-Star Cup Preview

#### Long-Form Post — 11:00 AM ET

The 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup kicks off tomorrow — and the format makes it unlike anything else in softball.

**What it is:** 45 of the best professional softball players, 12 games, August 8 through August 30, at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois.

**The twist:** Teams are completely redrafted before each of four series. That means players will compete with and against different teammates throughout the event. There are no permanent rosters, no team standings, and no team winner. The Athletes Unlimited scoring system tracks individual performance across every game, and the event will crown an individual champion.

**The fan vote** sent eight players directly into the field: Reese Atwood, Rylie Boone, Alyssa Brito, Bri Ellis, Jayda Coleman, Megan Grant, Karlyn Pickens, and Jordan Woolery.

**Team breakdown heading into the draft:**
- Chicago Bandits: 9 players
- Carolina Blaze: 9 players
- Utah Talons (2026 AUSL Champions): 9 players
- Oklahoma City Spark: 8 players
- Portland Cascade: 6 players
- Texas Volts: 6 players

**TV:** All 12 games air nationally on ESPN platforms, MLB Network, and CBS Sports Network.

The defending AUSL champion Utah Talons players bring championship momentum into a format that strips away team records entirely. No one wins this as a team. That's what makes it worth watching.

### STORY 3: September 1 Recruiting Window

#### Long-Form Post — 1:00 PM ET

Twenty-five days from now, the phone lines open.

September 1 is the date NCAA Division I coaches can initiate direct contact with Class of 2028 softball prospects for the first time. Before that date, coaches cannot call, text, or email these athletes — even if the athlete reaches out first, coaches are not permitted to respond. After September 1, everything opens simultaneously: phone calls, text messages, emails, official and unofficial visit invitations.

For the Class of 2028, that's the moment.

Extra Inning Softball has received 1,226 nominations for their Class of 2028 recognition program — the most they have ever processed for any class in their company's history. That number reflects how many players went through major summer exposure events this year and how many coaches are already watching.

The top prospects in this class already know which coaches have been watching. What September 1 changes is the direction of the communication. Coaches who have been silently evaluating film, watching at PGF Nationals, and planning their call order are about to start dialing.

If you're a Class of 2028 player: have your film ready, know your priorities, and be prepared for a busy few weeks starting September 1.

### STORY 4: Texas Tech 2027 Roster Preview

#### Long-Form Post — 3:00 PM ET

On paper, no program in college softball has assembled a 2027 roster like Texas Tech.

Head coach Gerry Glasco took the Red Raiders to the Women's College World Series Finals in 2026. Then, in the transfer portal, he went to work.

**Jaysoni Beachum** (from Florida State) is a 2-time All-American and former NFCA National Freshman of the Year. In 2026 at FSU, she hit .406 with 10 home runs and 69 RBI — earning 2nd Team All-America honors. She is the No. 3 rated transfer in the portal with 5-star grade from On3, and she has two years of eligibility remaining.

**Kasidi Pickering** (from Oklahoma) is the No. 2 rated portal transfer. She hit .370 with 20 home runs and 61 RBI in 2026. Over 186 career games, she carries a .384 average with 50 home runs and 169 RBI. She was a 2024 national champion at Oklahoma and a WCWS All-Tournament honoree.

**Jayden Heavener** (from LSU) is the pitching anchor of the class. She went 13-9 with a 2.81 ERA across 139.1 innings last season, striking out 128 batters and earning NFCA All-Region honors. She has two years of eligibility.

They join a roster that already includes **Kaitlyn Terry** (UCLA, 2-time All-American) and **Mia Williams** (Florida, 2026 NFCA All-American), along with returners Taylor Pannell, Moriah Polar, and Jasmyn Burns.

Campus arrivals begin this month. Fall practice is weeks away. This group hasn't played a single game together yet.

### STORY 5: USA WNT World Cup Group Stage Preview

#### Long-Form Post — 5:30 PM ET

Four days after winning International Cup gold in Oklahoma City, USA Softball's next target is already on the calendar.

Montana Fouts was near-perfect in the gold medal game on August 3 — retiring 18 consecutive Japan batters before a leadoff single in the 7th in a 2-0 shutout at Devon Park. The USA Women's National Team finished the International Cup 5-0.

Now the WNT turns its attention to the next milestone: the WBSC Softball Women's World Cup Group Stage, scheduled for September 12-16 in Oklahoma City. At Devon Park. The same venue.

USA earned an automatic berth as host. Their group opponents are confirmed: **Netherlands** (No. 6 world), **Mexico** (No. 7), **China** (No. 8), **New Zealand** (No. 23), and **South Africa** (No. 36).

The 16-player WNT roster has been named. It includes Rachel Garcia, Dejah Mulipola, Montana Fouts, Maya Brady, Kendall Wells (Oklahoma — international debut), and Taylor Shumaker (Florida — international debut). The roster is SEC-heavy, with 10 athletes from Southeastern Conference programs.

The World Cup Group Stage is the pathway to the 2027 World Cup — and, on the longer timeline, the 2028 Olympics.

36 days away.
