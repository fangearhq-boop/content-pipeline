Three Days Left With Gasso: OU's Final Full Camp Push Before WNT

By Jordan Reeves | September 9, 2026

Oklahoma Softball

The Sooners' fall camp clock has two numbers on it: three days until Patty Gasso departs for Team USA duties, and 21 days until the fall opener against Tarleton State at Love's Field on September 30.

Gasso leaves September 12 with JT Gasso and Jennifer Rocha to coach the United States at the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Oklahoma in Oklahoma City, returning September 17. For Oklahoma, this means the final stretch of fall camp with the complete coaching staff is compressed into three days — and every competitive rep inside that window carries extra weight.

What the Competition Looks Like

The internal competition that matters most right now runs through two players who are pushing each other in every scrimmage scenario: Kendall Wells and Sydney Berzon.

Wells, the sophomore who shattered the NCAA freshman home run record with 32 home runs in her first season, carries that power into every at-bat. She homered twice off Berzon in fall scrimmages — and that detail reveals something significant about both players. Berzon does not give up home runs cheaply. The LSU two-time All-American pitched 486.1 innings with a 2.02 ERA and 406 strikeouts during her career with the Tigers. A pitcher of that caliber getting beaten in a fall scrimmage means the opposing hitter is doing something real.

Wells is doing something real. And Berzon is competing hard enough that being beaten by a Wells home run is a meaningful data point, not a casualty of fall practice complacency.

The Transfer Additions in Context

Berzon's arrival from LSU is the most high-profile of Oklahoma's portal additions, but Macie Harter (.346 BA, 7 HR, 23 RBI from MTSU) is another piece Gasso is evaluating during this final full stretch of supervised camp. With Gasso, JT Gasso, and Rocha all away during the WNT window, the reps these players get under full coaching supervision in the next three days represent the deepest evaluation opportunity before the fall schedule begins.

Oklahoma combines 12 returning players with nine newcomers this fall. The nine newcomers need to learn the system, and the 12 returners need to absorb new competition for roster roles. Three days of complete staff oversight compresses what would normally unfold over several weeks into a concentrated evaluation window.

The Bigger Picture

Gasso's willingness to step away to coach Team USA says something about the depth of the program she has built. An Oklahoma squad that handles 12 days without its head coach and still opens fall competition at full strength is a program operating with systemic strength, not just star-dependent talent.

The Sooners didn't reach the Women's College World Series in 2026 — a miss that still stings in Norman and will drive this group through every fall rep. The players who were there for that miss, combined with the portal additions Gasso recruited to address the gaps, are building toward a program that doesn't let that happen again.

Three days of complete coaching staff. Twenty-one days to the fall opener. Every rep is a brick in that wall.

What's Next

Gasso and the OU assistant coaches depart September 12 for the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Oklahoma in Oklahoma City, returning September 17. Oklahoma's fall schedule opens September 30 against Tarleton State at Love's Field. Subsequent fall games include contests against USAO, Seminole State College, and East Texas A&M, with four Battle Series games also on the schedule.
