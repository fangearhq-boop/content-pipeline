Three Days to Devon Park: USA Softball Enters Final Countdown

By Emily Rawlings | September 9, 2026

USA National Team

Three days remain before USA Softball takes the Devon Park field for the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Oklahoma — and the final preparation window is closing fast.

The tournament opens Thursday, September 12, at OGE Energy Field in Oklahoma City. USA draws No. 7 Mexico in the opening night matchup at 7:30 PM, with the Opening Ceremony preceding play at 6:30 PM. Five days of competition will determine which two of the six nations punch their tickets to the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia — the first formal qualification step on the road to the LA28 Olympic Games.

For USA, this is familiar terrain. They went 5-0 at the International Cup at Devon Park earlier this year, and Head Coach Patty Gasso is returning to the same facility with largely the same personnel to compete for stakes that are exponentially higher.

The Roster Built for This

USA's 16-player Group Oklahoma roster is not an assembly of players meeting for the first time. It is a roster forged through years of international competition together.

Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola hold 2020 Olympic gold medals. Seven members of the squad competed on the 2025 World Games gold-medal team that represents the deepest proof of this group's collective capability. Ten players made the 2025 Down Under Series trip. Gasso runs this roster with the same coaching staff she deploys at Oklahoma: JT Gasso and Jennifer Rocha alongside her, giving USA a unified system from day one of tournament play.

Three days of final preparation bring a group this connected to its sharpest competitive state.

Opening Night and the Netherlands Question

USA's path through the round-robin is straightforward in its structure and demanding in its execution. The opening game against No. 7 Mexico sets the tone. Mexico will arrive in Oklahoma City motivated — playing on U.S. soil with the crowd against them, competing against the host nation for a spot in the World Cup Finals. Treating any opening-night opponent as a formality is how tournaments get complicated early.

The critical seeding matchup will come against No. 6 Netherlands. The Dutch have been a consistent force in international softball, capable of stretching any team's pitching rotation and manufacturing runs through patient at-bats. Finishing above Netherlands in the round-robin standings means entering the September 16 play-off bracket as the top seed — and the top seed in this tournament matters considerably.

The September 16 play-off format: top two teams meet at 1:00 PM, with both advancing directly to the 2027 Finals in Australia. Third and fourth meet at 4:00 PM. The Repechage at 7:00 PM determines the second berth to Australia. USA wants to be in the 1:00 PM match. That means winning the round-robin.

What's at Stake

This is not a tune-up tournament. Two spots in Group Oklahoma advance teams to the first World Cup Finals of the new Olympic cycle — and with LA28 two years away, every international competition result carries weight in the qualification architecture.

USA has built this roster specifically for this moment. Garcia, Mulipola, and the World Games veterans understand the difference between competing in international events and competing for Olympic access. The preparation, the familiarity with Devon Park, the continuity of the coaching staff — all of it converges on Thursday evening.

What's Next

The WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Oklahoma runs September 12–16 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. USA opens Thursday at 7:30 PM against No. 7 Mexico. The round-robin concludes September 15, with play-offs and Finals-berth determination on September 16. The 2027 Finals are scheduled for April 5–11 in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia.

Three days. The countdown is nearly gone.
