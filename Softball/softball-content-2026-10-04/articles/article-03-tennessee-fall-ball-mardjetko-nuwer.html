Tennessee Builds on Perfect Fall Ball Start: Mardjetko, Nuwer and the Vols' 2027 Case

By Jordan Reeves | October 4, 2026 | I Love Softball

Tennessee softball is 2-0 in fall ball, having outscored opponents 25-2 through two games, and nobody should be surprised by that. What matters more than the results — which, against Walters State and Lipscomb, were essentially foregone conclusions — is what's happening inside Sherri Parker Lee Stadium as Paige Mardjetko and Mikayla Nuwer continue sharpening the pitching staff that already ranks among the nation's most feared.

Thursday evening, Tennessee hosted Carson-Newman for their third fall game of the young season. The Vols are deep into a fall schedule that includes nine contests before November and a series of SEC-level tests ahead. The 2027 banner chase starts in games like these.

The Numbers Behind the Hype

There is no manufactured story here — the numbers tell it directly. Paige Mardjetko went 16-3 with a 1.26 ERA and 171 strikeouts in 2026. She was a first-team NFCA All-American and one of the most unhittable starters in the country regardless of conference.

Mikayla Nuwer went 15-1 with a 1.01 ERA and 90 strikeouts in the same season. Together, the two combined for 31 wins and eight shutouts. Combined ERA under 1.15. That's a pitching staff that can shut down anyone in the country on any given day.

Both are back for 2027. Both are healthy. Both are competing in fall ball.

The Unfinished Business

Tennessee has reached the Women's College World Series semifinals four times in the last five years. It's a remarkable run of consistent excellence — and it's also the definition of unfulfilled potential. Getting to Oklahoma City repeatedly and not bringing home a national title creates a chip that doesn't go away between seasons.

The 2027 Vols return nine starters alongside their ace pitching staff, including hitters who know what WCWS pressure feels like. Experience plus elite pitching is the formula that produces championships, and Tennessee has both entering the season.

Fall Schedule Ahead

Tennessee's fall slate is dense with opportunity to keep developing ahead of the spring season. After hosting Carson-Newman on October 2, the Vols play Northeast Alabama on October 9, Northwest Florida State on October 10, and Roane State on October 11. All three games are at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium in Knoxville with free admission.

Later in fall, Tennessee travels to Middle Tennessee on October 18 before hosting Lee University on October 23 to close out the fall schedule. Six games across five weeks of continued development.

What's Next

The weekend of October 9-11 is the first significant multi-game stretch of Tennessee's fall schedule, and it will give coaches and fans a longer look at how the roster depth behind Mardjetko and Nuwer is developing. Offensive consistency, fielding cohesion behind the ace staff, and the development of younger players all come into clearer focus across a full weekend slate.

Tennessee softball has been knocking on the door of a national championship for five years. The 2027 roster has the ingredients to finally kick it open. Fall ball is where that conversation earns its credentials.
