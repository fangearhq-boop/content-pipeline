Texas Tech Opens Fall Ball With Seven All-Americans and a Roster Built to Contend

By Sarah Mitchell | October 4, 2026 | I Love Softball

On a Saturday afternoon at Tracy Sellers Field, Texas Tech softball opened its 2026 fall schedule against West Texas A&M with the most decorated roster in program history already in place. Seven All-Americans suited up for Joey Glasco's Red Raiders — a number that would define most programs' best season, and for Texas Tech is simply their baseline heading into 2027.

The Red Raiders have been ascending for years under Glasco, and the 2027 roster represents the fullest expression of that climb. Portal recruiting, high school recruiting, player development — all three pillars firing at once to produce something Lubbock hasn't seen before in the sport.

The Beachum Addition

Of all the pieces in Texas Tech's new-look roster, Jaysoni Beachum may be the most anticipated. The transfer from Florida State enters Lubbock having hit .406 with 10 home runs and 69 RBI in her final season with the Seminoles, where she was ranked as the No. 1 corner infielder in the transfer portal.

Beachum brings the kind of bat that changes the construction of a lineup around it. Teams must account for her at the plate, which opens up opportunities for the hitters around her — and on a Texas Tech roster that already has six returning All-Americans, there are hitters around her. The combination could make the Red Raiders' offense one of the most difficult to gameplan against in 2027.

No. 1 in the 2028 Class

TTU's excellence isn't limited to the current roster. The Red Raiders are tied for No. 1 in the Class of 2028 recruiting rankings alongside Oklahoma, having landed both Makenzie Thomas and Khloe Williams in recent weeks. Building a program requires sustainable pipeline, and Texas Tech has constructed one.

The combination of immediate impact through the transfer portal — Beachum being the marquee example — and long-term investment through elite high school recruiting positions TTU as a program built not for one run but for sustained contention in the Big 12 and beyond.

Fall Schedule

Saturday's opener against West Texas A&M was the first of four fall games for Texas Tech. The Red Raiders travel to West Texas A&M on October 11 before returning home for a doubleheader against New Mexico on October 18 and a final fall game against Odessa College on October 24. All home games at Tracy Sellers Field offer free admission.

For a roster this loaded, fall games against regional competition are less about the results than about the integration — getting seven All-Americans plus a marquee transfer to play as a cohesive unit before SEC-caliber competition arrives in the spring.

What's Next

Texas Tech's remaining fall schedule will give coaches and fans a fuller picture of how this roster fits together, with particular attention on how Beachum slots into the lineup and how the pitching staff performs in competitive reps. The 2028 recruiting class will continue developing with more commits expected through the fall window.

For a program on the rise, Saturday's fall opener was the first page of a chapter that Red Raider fans have been waiting to read. Seven All-Americans on the field at once in Lubbock. The question isn't whether Texas Tech is good — it's whether they're ready to be great.
