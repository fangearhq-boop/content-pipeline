Big 12 Fall Ball Week 2: Texas Dominates, OU Gets Real Action, TTU Unveils Its Roster

By Emily Rawlings | October 4, 2026 | I Love Softball

Fall softball is not just a warmup — it's a preview of power. Week 2 of the 2026 fall slate across the Big 12 and SEC has delivered a series of early indicators about which programs are positioning themselves to compete for a national championship in 2027. Here's the rundown of what we're watching.

Texas: The Defending Champion Sets the Tone

The numbers from Texas's fall opener on September 29 were not subtle. The Longhorns defeated Murray State 28-1 — a margin that speaks less about Murray State and more about what back-to-back WCWS champions look like when they're playing loose in fall ball.

Lexi Torres, the 2026 D1Softball Player of the Year who hit .530 in her debut season at Texas, and Jordyn Kavan, the two-time Women's College World Series MVP, are both back for 2027. The three-peat narrative — only UCLA (1988-90) and Oklahoma (2021-23) have ever won three consecutive national championships — begins in games like this one. Texas has the roster to chase it.

The Longhorns' fall schedule continues with Texas State on October 13, Temple College on October 20, McLennan Community College on October 27, and UTSA on October 29. All games are at McCombs Field in Austin.

Oklahoma: The Redemption Arc Starts Now

After a season-ending loss to Texas in 2026 and a Battle Series opener cancelled by lightning on September 30, Patty Gasso's Sooners finally got real fall game action Saturday. Kendall Wells — who set the NCAA single-season home run record at 39 in 2026 — and Sydney Berzon — the LSU transfer and two-time All-American pitcher — are the anchors of a rebuilt roster that has unfinished business.

Oklahoma's fall Battle Series continues October 7, 14, and 21 at Love's Field. The Sooners have something to prove, and fall ball is where that proof begins accumulating.

Texas Tech: The Challenger Emerges

Seven All-Americans. The No. 1 recruiting class (tied with OU). Jaysoni Beachum (.406 BA at FSU) in the transfer portal. Saturday's fall opener against West Texas A&M introduced a Red Raiders roster that demands to be taken seriously as a national contender heading into 2027.

TTU's fall schedule runs through October 24. Glasco's program has quietly built the kind of infrastructure — portal savvy, recruiting pipeline, player development — that produces programs capable of winning championships, not just conference games.

Tennessee: The Dark Horse With Elite Pitching

Add Tennessee into the national picture. The Vols are 2-0 in fall ball, the Mardjetko-Nuwer pitching duo (combined 31 wins, 8 shutouts, under 1.15 ERA in 2026) is back and healthy, and the program has made the Women's College World Series semifinals four of the past five years. They haven't won it yet, which makes them motivated in a way that defending champions rarely are.

Tennessee's fall schedule runs through October 23. Northeast Alabama, Northwest Florida State, and Roane State are up next this weekend, all free at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium in Knoxville.

Transfer Portal: The October Window Is Open

More than 250 transfers are currently tracked by ON3 across Division I softball as October's signing window opens. The most high-profile moves already completed — Berzon to OU, Beachum to TTU, Tori Edwards (ranked top-five nationally) to LSU, Ella Dodge from Tennessee to FSU — reshaped rosters across the country.

October will bring more portal activity as coaches finalize their 2027 roster needs following fall ball evaluations. Programs like Oregon (hosting eight free fall exhibitions through November 1 at Jane Sanders Stadium) and Louisiana (playing fall games through mid-October) are doing the same work, building toward a spring season that will determine next year's national champions.

What's Next

The next two weeks of fall ball will clarify the 2027 national picture considerably. Texas, Oklahoma, Tennessee, and Texas Tech all have significant fall games ahead. The transfer portal window will produce more roster moves. And somewhere across the country, a team that isn't being talked about yet is quietly putting together the roster that will surprise everyone in Oklahoma City next June.

Week 2 of fall ball is over. The conversations have started. The 2027 season is taking shape.
