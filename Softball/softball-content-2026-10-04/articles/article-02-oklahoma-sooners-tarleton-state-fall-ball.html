Oklahoma Gets Real Fall Action: Wells and Berzon Finally Together Against Tarleton State

By Emily Rawlings | October 4, 2026 | I Love Softball

After Mother Nature had other plans on September 30 — a lightning strike that cut the Battle Series opener short after barely 45 minutes of play — Oklahoma softball got back to Love's Field on Saturday for their first real outside-opponent game of fall 2026. The opponent was Tarleton State, the time was 1:00 PM, and for the first time in a game setting, Kendall Wells and Sydney Berzon were in uniform together for Patty Gasso's rebuilt Sooners.

That pairing — an outfield bat who rewrote the NCAA record book last spring and a transfer pitcher who spent three seasons making hitters look silly at LSU — is exactly what Sooner faithful have been waiting to see. Fall ball is the first real test of whether the 2027 Oklahoma roster is the answer to the question that's been hanging over Norman since Texas eliminated them in 2026.

What Wells Brings Back

There is no understating what Kendall Wells did in 2026. Her 39 home runs set the NCAA single-season record and earned her the Johnny Bench Award as the nation's top catcher and NFCA Fielder of the Year honors. She hit .358 with 88 RBI on a team that, despite the individual brilliance, couldn't punch through Texas in the postseason.

Now entering her sophomore year, Wells returns to a program with a new-look roster around her and something to prove. Fall ball is where the numbers start becoming a narrative — and hers is already one of the most compelling in college softball.

Berzon Steps Into Norman

Sydney Berzon arrived at Oklahoma carrying the reputation of a closer who became a starter — a three-time NFCA All-Region selection and two-time All-American who spent three dominant seasons at LSU before transferring to Norman. Berzon's career at LSU established her as one of the elite pitchers in the country, and Gasso made landing her a priority in the offseason.

The fit makes sense on paper: Oklahoma needs pitching depth after losing their 2026 staff, and Berzon needs the right environment to reach her ceiling. Fall ball is where that pairing either confirms or complicates the offseason optimism. Saturday against Tarleton State was the first real data point.

A Note on the September 30 Opening

The Battle Series opener that never was still provided a preview of what this offense can do. Before the skies opened up, Ella Parker launched a home run on the first official at-bat of fall — off Audrey Lowry, on the opening pitch sequence of the game. The crowd at Love's Field barely had time to react before the weather forced everyone off the field.

It was a foreshadowing, not a statement. Saturday's game against Tarleton State was the real thing.

What's Next

Oklahoma's fall Battle Series continues with three more games at Love's Field: October 7 at 6:30 PM, October 14 at 6:30 PM, and October 21 at 6:30 PM. All games are at Love's Field in Norman. All games are ticketed at $10.88 per game.

For a program that went to five consecutive Women's College World Series from 2021 through 2025 before stumbling in 2026, fall ball isn't just about player development — it's about reestablishing an identity. Wells, Berzon, and the 2027 Sooners are in the early stages of writing that story. The pages are filling in now.
