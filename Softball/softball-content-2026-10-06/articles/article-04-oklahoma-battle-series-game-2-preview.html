Oklahoma's Battle Series Returns Tonight: Game 2 at Love's Field After a Sold-Out Game 1

By Sarah Mitchell | October 6, 2026 | I Love Softball

Less than a week after a sold-out crowd of 2,351 watched Gabbie Garcia and Kendall Wells go back-to-back at Love's Field, Oklahoma's fall Battle Series is back for Game 2 — tonight at 6:30 PM CT in Norman.

The Sooners are two of the four scheduled Battle Series competitions into their fall, and what the opener showed — beyond the offense, beyond the crowd, beyond Patty Gasso's emotional postgame moment — is that this program has generated the kind of fan investment that makes fall ball feel like something more.

What Game 1 Established

Game 1 of the Battle Series used the standard fall format: six pitchers, three innings each, nine innings total, no official score. Within that structure, the Sooners hit four home runs, including the back-to-back shots from Garcia and Wells in the seventh inning that brought the already-loud Love's Field crowd to its feet.

Kendall Wells set the NCAA single-season home run record with 39 in the spring 2026 season. Her bat has not cooled. Garcia followed her over the fence on the very next pitch. That sequence — in a fall scrimmage, months before a game counts — told every person in those stands exactly what they wanted to know about 2027.

Gasso's postgame reaction — described by those in attendance as genuinely emotional — reflected what a sold-out fall crowd represents for a program that ended 2026 earlier than expected. The fans showed up. The team showed them why that was the right call.

The Roster Taking Shape

Twenty-one players make up the 2026 fall roster: 11 returning letterwinners, 6 true freshmen, and 3 transfers. Among the most-watched names is Sydney Berzon, who arrives from LSU as a two-time NFCA All-American and brings one of the sport's most decorated offensive track records to Norman. First impressions of Berzon in crimson and cream have been a consistent talking point since the fall schedule was announced.

In July, USA Softball named 10 Sooners to 2026 USWNT rosters — a number that underscores not just depth but the national-caliber talent distributed throughout this program's lineup.

Tonight's Format and Remaining Schedule

Game 2 tonight follows the same six-pitcher, three-inning rotation as Game 1. Love's Field will again be the venue, with first pitch set for 6:30 PM CT. Tickets are $10.88 at the gate.

The fall schedule continues with Battle Series games on October 14 and October 21 — all at Love's Field. Fall opponents across the season include Tarleton State, the University of Science and Arts of Oklahoma, Seminole State, and East Texas A&M. Oklahoma is now in the SEC, and their spring conference schedule will include the league's heavyweights when 2027 season play begins.

What's Next

After tonight's Game 2, attention will shift to what the coaches saw across both fall outings — which freshmen are ahead of schedule, how the new transfers are integrating, and what the pitching depth picture looks like heading into winter preparation.

For fans in Norman tonight, the context is simple: a sold-out crowd came last week and got a show. The show continues at 6:30 PM CT.
