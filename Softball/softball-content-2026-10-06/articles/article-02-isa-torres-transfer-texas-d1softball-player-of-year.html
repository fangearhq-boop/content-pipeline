Isa Torres Commits to Texas: The Best Hitter in College Softball Is a Longhorn

By Emily Rawlings | October 6, 2026 | I Love Softball

The transfer portal cycle has a clear winner, and it goes by burnt orange and cream. Isa Torres — the D1Softball National Player of the Year and the most productive hitter in college softball in 2026 — has committed to Texas after entering the transfer portal from Florida State. The move is the headline story of the entire 2026-27 portal season.

Torres' numbers at Florida State this past spring were not just impressive — they were historic. She posted a .530 batting average and hit 16 home runs, a combination that led to unanimous Player of the Year recognition across the sport's major awards platforms. There are very few lines in the history of college softball that compare to .530 with 16 home runs.

What Torres Brings to Texas

Texas made multiple portal moves this cycle, but none remotely approaches the impact of landing a shortstop who won Player of the Year. Torres changes the projected 2027 Texas lineup from dangerous to elite. She is the kind of middle-of-the-order presence that forces opposing pitchers to make a decision before the at-bat even begins.

Per ON3 and SI's transfer portal rankings, Torres was rated No. 2 among all top impact transfers in the 2026-27 portal class. Ranked No. 2 in the entire cycle — across every program — and heading to a Texas program that already has infrastructure, facilities, and a postseason track record. For the Longhorns, this is not an upgrade. It is a reconfiguration of expectations.

The Florida State Departure

The Torres commitment comes as part of a broader Florida State portal departure. Jaysoni Beachum, another high-profile Seminole, committed to Texas Tech in the same cycle. FSU's program, which produced two of the portal's most coveted names, now faces the challenge of rebuilding its roster around the players who remained in Tallahassee.

It is worth noting that programs lose portal talent regularly — even great programs. The 2026-27 movement does not necessarily signal a long-term decline for Florida State. But replacing a .530 hitter is an unsolved problem in any sport.

The Portal Landscape

With 137 players having entered the 2026-27 transfer portal and roughly 88 percent already committed, the window for late-moving programs is closing. Approximately 15 players remain available as of early October. The cycle produced significant movement across every conference, reshaping rosters from the SEC to the Big 12 to the ACC.

Torres' decision to Texas may be the cycle's defining moment. When the 2027 season begins and the cameras find the Longhorns' lineup card, her name will be near the top — and programs across the country will have spent the winter preparing for the at-bat they know is coming.

What's Next

The portal window continues to narrow through October. Programs still hunting for specific position needs must move quickly. For Texas, attention now turns to how this group — Torres included — assembles in fall workouts and what a spring schedule will look like for a Longhorn program now positioned as a legitimate national title contender.

The D1Softball Player of the Year is a Longhorn. The rest of college softball has been notified.
