Coaching Carousel Closes Out: Kentucky Hires Hackbarth, Three More Programs Set New Eras

By Jordan Reeves | October 6, 2026 | I Love Softball

The 2026 coaching carousel is wrapping up, and four programs have finalized their new leadership in the past week. The hires run from Power Four stalwarts to programs looking for their first winning tradition — and each carries its own set of implications for the 2027 season and beyond.

Leading the group chronologically: Kentucky softball hired Maddi Hackbarth on October 1 as its new head coach.

Kentucky Hires Maddi Hackbarth

Hackbarth comes to Lexington from Santa Clara, where she served as an assistant coach before the Wildcats came calling. The hire signals a desire for fresh energy at Kentucky — a program competing in the Southeastern Conference that needs to recruit against SEC powers for both talent and portal signings.

The staff restructuring that accompanied the hire is notable. Kristine Himes — Kentucky's longtime associate head coach — transitions to a new role as Director of Player Analytics & Development, a title that reflects the sport's increasing reliance on data-driven coaching infrastructure. Brittany Cervantes was elevated to Chief of Staff, rounding out a reshuffled leadership group around Hackbarth.

For Himes, the role change preserves institutional knowledge while making space for the new head coach's vision. It is a thoughtful staff architecture — one that does not simply clear the room but finds ways to retain experience.

California Gets Its Ninth Head Coach: Steve Singleton

California named Steve Singleton as the Bears' ninth head coach in program history. Singleton arrives from Texas, bringing Big 12 coaching experience to the Pac-12 successor landscape. Rebuilding Cal into a postseason regular requires the same mix of recruiting acumen and player development that Singleton demonstrated in Austin.

Boston College Bets on Beth Krysiak

The Eagles promoted outside the Power Four, hiring Beth Krysiak from Saint Francis. The move carries logic: Krysiak engineered back-to-back NEC automatic tournament bids at Saint Francis — a difficult achievement at that level of the sport. Taking a proven bid-winner and giving her Power Four resources is a reasonable bet for Boston College as it continues building its ACC softball profile.

Houston Needs a Rebuild — Chrissy Schoonmaker Gets the Job

Houston's numbers in Big 12 play over the past two seasons were difficult: 8-41. That is a program in need of significant reconstruction, and the Cougars turned to Chrissy Schoonmaker — former head coach at George Washington — to begin that work. Schoonmaker brought in seven freshmen in her first recruiting class, signaling a commitment to building through the traditional pipeline alongside portal additions.

What's Next

With fall ball underway and the transfer portal still active, each new hire faces immediate pressure to both shape their current roster and recruit for 2027. Hackbarth at Kentucky has the most visible platform — Big Blue Nation and SEC recruiting territory — and her early months will set the tone for how the program repositions itself in the conference landscape.

The NFCA coaching transactions page continues to track any remaining openings. The carousel is almost done turning. Now the real work begins.
