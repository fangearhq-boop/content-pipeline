Transfer Portal Tracker: Beachum to Texas Tech, Pickering Still Deciding, Window Closing

By Emily Rawlings | October 6, 2026 | I Love Softball

With the Isa Torres commitment to Texas dominating the headlines, it is easy to overlook the significant activity happening across the rest of the 2026-27 softball transfer portal. The window is narrowing — 137 players entered, roughly 121 have committed, and about 15 remain available — and what happens in the next few weeks will shape rosters across the country heading into the spring.

Here is where things stand.

Jaysoni Beachum Commits to Texas Tech

Florida State is not just losing Isa Torres in this portal cycle. Jaysoni Beachum — another high-profile Seminole — has committed to Texas Tech in the same portal window. The double departure from FSU to Big 12 programs is a significant storyline: two of the cycle's most impactful portal names leaving the same program for conference rivals.

Texas Tech, which also has Kasidi Pickering in its orbit, is assembling a formidable portal class for 2027. If Pickering ultimately chooses the Red Raiders as well, it will be one of the stronger portal hauls in recent memory for a program trying to rise in the Big 12 standings.

Kasidi Pickering: Oklahoma or Texas Tech?

Kasidi Pickering is the portal's most closely watched remaining decision. The outfielder is currently tracking Oklahoma and Texas Tech as her top two destinations, per ON3 reporting. Both programs have made significant additions this cycle and have roster needs at the outfield position that Pickering would address immediately.

Oklahoma — which landed Sydney Berzon from LSU earlier in the cycle — has the platform, facilities, and now the SEC affiliation to make a compelling case. Texas Tech has momentum from the Beachum commitment and the broader class they are building. Pickering's decision could tip one program into elite portal territory for the 2026-27 class.

Alabama Adds Pitching: Kailey Plumlee From Tennessee

While the offensive names dominate the headlines, pitching market moves matter just as much. Alabama acquired left-handed pitcher Kailey Plumlee from Tennessee — a high-ceiling arm ranked inside the top 25 of the 2024 recruiting class. In-conference transfers add a layer of familiarity: Plumlee has faced SEC lineups, knows the travel schedule, and understands the level of competition Alabama will see every weekend in 2027.

For Tennessee, losing a top-25 recruit to a conference rival stings — but the Lady Vols enter fall ball with strong pitching depth and returning arms who produced dominant numbers in 2026.

The Portal By the Numbers

Total entries: 137. Committed: approximately 121 (roughly 88 percent). Still available: approximately 15. Withdrawn: 1. For programs still searching for upgrades, the pool has thinned considerably. The names still available include a mix of position players and pitchers — but the blue-chip options have largely found homes.

ON3's Transfer Portal Wire and collegesoftball.app are tracking all remaining moves in real time as the window closes.

What's Next

Pickering's decision is the most immediate storyline to watch. After that, the portal cycle will wind down to final decisions from the 15 or so players still available, most of whom will commit quietly to programs with specific roster needs that match their profiles.

By the time the 2027 season opens, these portal moves will have reshaped the national power structure. Texas has Torres. Texas Tech has Beachum. Alabama has Plumlee. The board is being set — and there are a few moves still to be made before the game begins.
