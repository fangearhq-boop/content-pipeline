Series 3 Opens: Coffey Wins Game 13, Kowalik Crushes Netz 13-5 in Game 14

By Emily Rawlings | I Love Softball | August 24, 2026

The AUSL All-Star Cup Series 3 opened Sunday at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois, and Kayla Kowalik made an immediate statement. In Game 14, Kowalik's squad dismantled Team Netz 13-5 on ESPN+, producing one of the most dominant single-game performances of the summer. Earlier in the afternoon, Team Coffey opened Series 3 with a 4-2 victory over Team Ricketts in Game 13 on ESPNU. Both results set the tone for what may be the final decisive week of the individual standings race.

Six days remain before the 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup individual champion is crowned after Series 4 on August 30.

Where the Individual Race Stands

Kowalik entered Series 3 leading the individual standings with 860 total points — a margin of approximately 256 points over Keilani Ricketts, who sits second at 604. Danieca Coffey is third at 550 points, and Devyn Netz fourth at 530. With Series 3 opening exactly this way — Kowalik winning by eight runs while Ricketts's team fell to Coffey — the gap at the top may be widening rather than closing.

In the AUSL All-Star Cup format, individual statistical production feeds directly into the points standings alongside team results. A 13-5 win generates more individual counting stats — at-bats, runs, hits, RBIs — than a tight 4-2 contest. For Kowalik, Game 14 was not only a team win. It was a points-accumulation performance at exactly the moment she needed one.

Game 13: Coffey 4, Ricketts 2

The afternoon's first game on ESPNU featured a competitive matchup between Team Coffey and Team Ricketts. Coffey's squad came away with a 4-2 victory — a result that, on paper, leaves the individual standings landscape largely unchanged but confirms Danieca Coffey as a competitive force in Series 3. Ricketts entered the series in second place overall. Losing the opener does not eliminate any player from the individual race, but it does mean Ricketts must respond in the Series 3 games that remain.

Series 3 features multiple games spread across the week before Series 4 closes out August 30. The full results from every game in the series will factor into the standings calculation.

Game 14: Kowalik 13, Netz 5

Team Kowalik's Game 14 performance on ESPN+ was the afternoon's signature result. A 13-5 final score — eight-run margin — is by any measure a dominant opening for the individual standings leader. In a format where offensive production generates standings points, a run-heavy win translates directly into statistical accumulation for every player on Kowalik's roster.

Netz entered Series 3 at 530 points overall, the fourth-ranked captain. A lopsided loss in Game 14 makes her path to the individual title nearly unnavigable at this stage of the competition.

What's Next

Series 3 continues through the week at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois, with all games broadcast on ESPN, MLB Network, and CBS Sports Network. Series 4 follows August 30, after which the 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup individual champion will be officially crowned. Kowalik leads. The chasers need multiple dominant performances in the days ahead. Six days remain — and Sunday's results suggest the gap is not closing.
