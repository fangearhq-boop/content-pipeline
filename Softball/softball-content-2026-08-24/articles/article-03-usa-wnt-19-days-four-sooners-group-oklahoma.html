19 Days Out: Four Sooners Headline USA's Group Oklahoma Roster

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | August 24, 2026

Nineteen days from now, USA Softball opens the 2026 WBSC World Cup Group Oklahoma at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. The stakes are direct: the top two of six nations advance to the 2027 WBSC Softball World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland — the direct qualifier for the LA28 Olympics. And for four players on the 16-member USA roster, this tournament will unfold in a city they know as well as anywhere in the world.

Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell — all former Oklahoma Sooners — are part of Team USA's Group Oklahoma mission. OU leads all programs with four players on the roster.

The Group Oklahoma Field

The six-nation group includes USA (No. 2), Netherlands (No. 6), Mexico (No. 7), China (No. 8), New Zealand (No. 23), and South Africa (No. 36). On paper, USA is the clear favorite. But the Netherlands and Mexico are legitimate international programs — both have competed at the podium level in recent World Cup cycles — and underestimating either would be a mistake.

USA opens Group Oklahoma against Mexico (No. 7) in the final game of the day on September 12. That opener will set the tone for the remainder of the four-day competition. A confident start against Mexico sends a message to the rest of the field.

The top-two qualifier format means a single bad game can carry enormous consequences. USA's objective is clear: finish in the top two of six and advance to Redcliffe.

Four Sooners in Oklahoma City

Oklahoma leads all collegiate programs with four athletes on the 16-member USA WNT roster. Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell all built their collegiate careers in Norman, with Devon Park and the broader Oklahoma City softball environment as familiar terrain.

Brito is a catcher who combines elite pitch-framing with a reliable bat in the middle of the lineup. Coleman is one of the most dynamic outfielders in the sport — a speed threat at the top of the order who was central to Oklahoma's back-to-back WCWS titles in 2021 and 2022. Jennings is a two-time All-American shortstop whose defense and run production have made her a cornerstone of both the OU and USA rosters. Maxwell is one of the most accomplished pitchers in college softball history, a key arm in OU's dominant stretch and a power weapon at the top of USA's staff.

Having four players who know each other from years together in Norman is not a small advantage. Chemistry is not automatic in international competition, and familiarity at the highest level of play matters.

The LA28 Stakes

Group Oklahoma is not a standalone event. It is a qualifier for a qualifier. The top two nations from the September 12–16 group stage advance to the 2027 WBSC Softball World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland (near Brisbane), April 5–11, 2027. That Finals event is the direct Olympic qualifier for LA28.

For USA, the objective is not merely to advance — it's to advance as the top seed. Winning Group Oklahoma outright puts USA in the strongest possible position heading into the 2027 Finals and the path to competing for a third Olympic gold.

What's Next

Group Oklahoma begins September 12 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City, with USA opening against Mexico (No. 7). The full competition runs September 12–16. Top two of six advance to the 2027 Finals in Redcliffe, QLD — the LA28 qualifier. Four Sooners are on the roster. Nineteen days from now, the most important international softball competition of 2026 begins.
