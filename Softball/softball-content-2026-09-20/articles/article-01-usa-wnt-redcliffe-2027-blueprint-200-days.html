200 Days to Redcliffe: Team USA Shifts Focus to April After Dominant Group Oklahoma Run

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | September 20, 2026

Four days after going 5-0 through Group Oklahoma, Team USA closes the qualification chapter and opens the preparation one. The countdown to Redcliffe — and the road to LA28 — has officially begun.

The qualifier is over. The field is set. And the most important stretch of the road to the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics has officially begun.

Four days after Team USA closed Group Oklahoma with a dominant 7-0 run-rule victory over China at Devon Park in Oklahoma City, the national team begins the longest preparation window of their Redcliffe campaign. The 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals are set for April 5-11, 2027, in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia — and every day between now and then is a building block toward a tournament that carries Olympic weight.

What USA Built in Oklahoma City

Team USA finished Group Oklahoma at 5-0, outscoring the field by a margin that left little doubt about their readiness. The signature performance came on September 16, when USA met China in a winner-take-direct-berth clincher. Final score: 7-0. Tournament result: dominant.

Maya Brady was the breakout story of Group Oklahoma. The young outfielder hit three home runs in the clincher against China, driving in five runs and becoming the defining image of USA's tournament performance. Her emergence as a power presence alongside the team's veteran core signals the kind of offensive depth that makes USA a multi-dimensional threat at Redcliffe.

On the mound, Gabbie Garcia was untouchable. In the clincher, she retired all nine batters she faced — striking out six — in an outing that showcased exactly why she anchors USA's pitching rotation. Garcia and Janie Reed Mulipola carry Tokyo 2020 silver in their résumés; that veteran presence around the emerging talent is what separates USA from the field on paper.

The Eight-Nation Field

USA and Mexico both earned direct berths from Group Oklahoma — the top two finishers in the bracket advance to Redcliffe automatically. China claimed the Wild Card, ensuring all three Group Oklahoma nations travel to Queensland in April.

The five pre-qualified nations complete the field: Australia, Canada, Japan, Puerto Rico, and Chinese Taipei. Australia enters as the host nation, competing at Talobilla Park in their home state of Queensland — an advantage that has never been easy to neutralize on the international stage. Japan brings decades of Finals experience and the technical excellence that has made them perennially dangerous at this level. Canada, Puerto Rico, and Chinese Taipei each represent the breadth and depth of the international softball landscape.

USA will enter Redcliffe as the favorite. But this is a field that can beat anyone on any day — and the Group Oklahoma performance, as dominant as it was, was against a subset of the full competition. April 2027 will be a different kind of test.

Gasso's Dual Mission: Norman and Redcliffe

One of the defining features of USA's Redcliffe preparation is the role of head coach Patty Gasso. After returning to Norman on September 16 — the same day USA clinched — Gasso resumed full-staff fall camp at Love's Field, beginning the dual mission that will define her professional calendar through April.

Oklahoma's four WNT players — Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell — will spend the 2026-27 collegiate year playing for the Sooners, building the form and fitness that feeds directly into April's Australian run. For Gasso, every fall camp practice, every fall game, every winter preparation block is both a Sooners development session and a national team scouting opportunity.

That dual purpose is not a distraction. For a coach of Gasso's caliber, it is a competitive advantage — and the 200-plus days between now and Redcliffe give her program the longest sustained preparation window in the sport.

What's Next

The next significant milestone on the Redcliffe preparation timeline comes when the WNT reconvenes ahead of the April Finals. Until then, the players return to their respective programs — Brady, Garcia, Mulipola, and the full 16-player roster — to build their form across the collegiate season.

Oklahoma opens its fall schedule on September 30 against Tarleton State at Love's Field. Texas opens September 29. Texas Tech is already deep into fall camp. The domestic 2026-27 season, which runs through June, is simultaneously the national team's preparation platform — and the 2027 WCWS is only weeks before the Redcliffe Finals begin.

The road to LA28 runs through Redcliffe. And with a 5-0 Group Oklahoma performance as the opening statement, Team USA has made clear they intend to arrive in April ready to finish the job.
