Kendall Wells Homers in Four Straight as Oklahoma Counts Down to Sept. 30

By Emily Rawlings | I Love Softball | September 20, 2026

Day 4 of full-staff fall camp at Love's Field, and Kendall Wells is making a statement. With the Sept. 30 opener against Tarleton State 10 days away, Oklahoma's sophomore slugger has homered in every fall competition game.

Kendall Wells picked up right where she left off.

Four fall competition games into Oklahoma's 2026 campaign at Love's Field, the sophomore outfielder has homered in every single one. For a player who set the NCAA freshman home run record with 32 last season, the fall performance is less a surprise than a confirmation — confirmation that the player who announced herself to college softball last year has spent the offseason getting sharper, stronger, and more dangerous.

Day 4 of full-staff fall camp finds the Sooners 10 days from the September 30 opener against Tarleton State, and head coach Patty Gasso has a front-row seat to the most important evaluation stretch of Oklahoma's preseason.

The Transfer Trio Taking Shape

Beyond Wells, the early returns on Oklahoma's three-player transfer class are giving Gasso's staff significant optimism heading into the fall schedule.

Sydney Berzon is evaluating as the Sooners' projected ace. The two-time All-American arrived from LSU carrying 486.1 innings pitched, a 2.02 ERA, and 406 career strikeouts — a résumé that makes her one of the most accomplished pitchers to transfer in recent memory. Early fall camp has given the coaching staff its first extended look at how that experience translates to Norman, and early indications have been encouraging.

Macie Harter provides a complement in the lineup. The transfer from Middle Tennessee batted .346 last season and gives OU an elite contact presence to pair alongside Wells' power. Across the first four days of full-staff camp, the integration of those two offensive profiles — Harter's contact game, Wells' power — is one of the more compelling subplots of fall 2026 in Norman.

Building After Group Oklahoma

Gasso returned to Love's Field on September 16 after leading Team USA to a 5-0 record through Group Oklahoma in Oklahoma City. The Sooners' head coach guided the national team to the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals — with four of her own players in the WNT lineup — and immediately stepped back into the Oklahoma fall camp routine.

JT Gasso and Rocha, who served alongside Patty Gasso on the WNT coaching staff, also returned to Norman for full-staff Day 1 on September 17. The three coaches bring not only their daily program expertise but the sharpened scouting perspective that comes from evaluating 16 of the country's best players over two weeks of international competition.

That perspective now gets applied to a roster of 21 players — nine newcomers integrating with 12 returning players — with 10 days to make the most consequential evaluations before the fall schedule begins.

What's Next

Oklahoma's fall schedule opens on September 30 against Tarleton State at Love's Field. Tickets are $10.88 per game; an All-Fall Pass covering all eight games is available for $70.69. After Tarleton State, the Sooners host the University of Science and Arts of Oklahoma (October 23), Seminole State (October 30), and East Texas A&M (November 4).

Ten days of camp remain before the opener. Wells' four-game home run streak will give way to game-speed evaluation. And the question for Gasso is no longer whether her players can perform in practice — it's whether the pieces Gasso has assembled can perform together when the opponent is wearing a different uniform.

Based on Day 4, the answer looks promising.
