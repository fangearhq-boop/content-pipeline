Blackout Day 4: Contact Resumes Monday, Then the Race Begins

By Sarah Mitchell

November 28, 2026

The phones have been quiet for four days. No calls from coaches. No texts. No DMs, no emails, no casual Instagram replies. For five days spanning Thanksgiving week, the NCAA's Division I softball recruiting calendar goes completely dark — and Saturday marks Day 4 of that silence.

The Thanksgiving recruiting blackout (Nov. 25-29) is an annual pause built into the college softball calendar. It prohibits all direct contact between college coaches and recruits during one of the busiest family weekends of the year. No exceptions. No workarounds. The silence is total.

What it isn't, however, is idle time for the programs in the middle of it.

What Coaches Are Doing Right Now

Behind the scenes, college softball programs are spending this final stretch of the blackout doing exactly what players do during film study: preparing to execute the moment the whistle blows.

Target lists for the Dec. 1 transfer portal are being refined. Scholarship offer structures are being finalized. Calling orders for the morning of Nov. 30 are being set, because when contact resumes Monday, every program in the country will be reaching out to their recruits simultaneously.

The first coach to call, the first to send the right message, the first to make a recruit feel seen on the morning after a long silence — that matters. Programs know it. That's why the final hours of the blackout aren't rest. They're preparation.

Three Deadlines, Five Days

The reason Monday morning matters so much is what follows it. After contact resumes Nov. 30, three separate deadlines arrive in rapid succession:

  • Dec. 1 (Tuesday): The transfer portal opens. Players across the country who have been waiting to officially enter can do so starting at 12:01 a.m. Programs can begin contacting portal entrants immediately.
  • Dec. 2-4 (Wednesday-Friday): The fall signing window. High school recruits in the Class of 2027 can sign official commitments. For programs, this is the moment to convert verbal commits to paper — and potentially flip uncommitted blue-chip prospects.
  • Dec. 8-12 (Wednesday-Sunday): Dead period. All in-person recruiting — visits, campus tours, evaluations — stops again. The window is not long.

That means programs have roughly seven effective recruiting days between when contact resumes Monday and when the dead period shuts things down again. Seven days to close portal targets. Seven days to lock in Signing Day commits. Seven days to execute an entire offseason's worth of roster planning.

What to Watch Monday Morning

When the phones start ringing Nov. 30, the storylines to watch are already in place. Oklahoma still has a gap at pitcher in its No. 1-ranked 2027 class. Arizona is entering the portal window in desperate need of a frontline arm after losing D1Softball Player of the Year Mariah Torres to Texas Tech in June. LSU, Tennessee, and Florida all enter the window with specific roster needs they've been unable to address since the fall evaluation period closed.

The recruits with the most leverage? The uncommitted ones. Pitchers in particular — a position of relentless demand and limited supply — will find themselves fielding calls from multiple programs the moment the blackout ends. For the top unsigned arms in the 2027 class, this is their moment to hear from every program, compare offers, and decide where their future lies.

Four days ago the sport went quiet. In 36 hours, it comes back louder than ever.
