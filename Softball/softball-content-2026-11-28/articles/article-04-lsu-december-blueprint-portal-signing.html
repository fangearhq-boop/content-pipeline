LSU's December Blueprint: How a Blue Blood Programs for the Portal Sprint

By Sarah Mitchell

November 28, 2026

The transfer portal opens Tuesday. The signing window opens Wednesday. A dead period starts the following Wednesday. Somewhere in that seven-day window, every program in college softball will be making decisions that reshape their 2027 rosters — and LSU will be moving faster than almost anyone.

That's not an accident. It's a blueprint.

LSU has built one of the most consistent recruiting programs in college softball. The Tigers have finished in the top five of final recruiting class rankings in multiple consecutive cycles, combining high-school signees with strategic portal additions. When the December window opens, they don't scramble. They execute a plan they've been building since the fall evaluation period closed in October.

What December Looks Like for a Top Program

For programs at LSU's level, the December portal window isn't a reactive scramble — it's the moment a plan reaches its activation stage.

During fall practices and evaluation periods, coaching staffs across the country build detailed target lists: portal players they've watched, made contact with through legal recruiting channels, and identified as genuine fits for their programs. When the blackout ended in past years, the first calls on Monday morning went to those exact players.

The coaching staff knows who they want. The compliance office has already reviewed scholarship availability. The NIL team has an offer structure ready. What looks like a fast-moving offseason from the outside is often months of groundwork reaching its conclusion in a single week.

What LSU Needs This December

After losing pitcher Jayden Heavener — who posted a 2.81 ERA with 128 strikeouts in her final season at LSU — the Tigers have a gap in their rotation heading into 2027. With two years of eligibility remaining when she entered the summer portal, Heavener represented significant production that the program now needs to replace.

December's portal window is LSU's primary mechanism for doing that. The question isn't whether the Tigers have the resources to recruit a top pitcher — they clearly do. The question is whether the right player enters the portal in December, and whether LSU can get there before Arizona, Ole Miss, and Auburn do.

Beyond pitching, LSU will also be evaluating portal options for position players, depending on how the fall practices informed their staffers' views on the existing roster depth. Programs at LSU's level never stop improving their rosters, even when those rosters are already strong.

The Signing Day Dimension

Simultaneously with the portal, LSU's coaching staff will be closing on any uncommitted 2027 high-school prospects they've identified as targets. The fall signing window (Dec. 2-4) runs on top of the portal, requiring coaching staffs to manage two distinct recruiting tracks at once.

Programs that handle both well typically have the deepest support staffs: dedicated recruiting coordinators, graduate assistants tracking portal activity in real time, and clear communication between the head coach, associate head coach, and recruiting staff. LSU has built exactly that infrastructure over years of competing at this level.

The result is a program that can close a portal commitment on Tuesday afternoon, finalize a high-school signing Wednesday morning, and still have bandwidth to monitor who else enters the portal later in the week.

What's Next

LSU's December recruiting activity will begin in earnest Monday, Nov. 30, when contact with recruits resumes after the blackout. The portal opens Dec. 1, and LSU is expected to be active from the start. Signing Day runs Dec. 2-4.
