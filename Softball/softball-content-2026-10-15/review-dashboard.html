5-for-5 in Motion — How Coaches Are Responding to the Eligibility Ruling
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Judge Sweeney's injunction in Wisne v. NCAA changed things last week — and now the softball world is dealing with the reality of what "5-for-5" actually means in practice. Class of 2022 athletes who used their eligibility through 2025-26 can petition to return for a fifth season in 2026-27. The NCAA is appealing to the 10th Circuit Court of Appeals, but the injunction stands during that process. That means it's in effect — right now — and coaches are making decisions accordingly. Here's the picture on the ground: 📋 COACHES ARE CALLING — Programs that lost veterans last spring are reaching out. Not every player who left wants to come back, but some do — and rosters are shifting. 📋 SCHOLARSHIP LIMITS STILL APPLY — 5-for-5 returners still take a scholarship spot. Programs at capacity have to decide: is this veteran worth the roster space we'd otherwise use on a newcomer? 📋 THE APPEAL IS REAL — The NCAA will argue its case before the 10th Circuit. The injunction could be modified, upheld, or overturned. Players returning under it do so with that uncertainty. 📋 THE CLOCK IS TICKING — Fall camp is weeks away. Decisions have to be made now, not after the appeal plays out. The one thing coaches agree on: this isn't the last we've heard of it. 🥎 Would you want your program to bring back a 5-for-5 eligible veteran?
