Texas 2028 Recruiting: Outfielder Zoey Abdullah Commits to Longhorns

By Emily Rawlings | October 15, 2026 | I Love Softball

The defending Women's College World Series champions are not done building.

Texas softball has added outfielder Zoey Abdullah to its 2028 recruiting class, bringing the Longhorns' known commits to four. Abdullah joins a class that already includes top-ranked Michaela Landers, giving Texas one of the stronger early 2028 foundations among programs nationwide.

What Abdullah Brings

Abdullah is an outfielder whose commitment continues a Texas recruiting philosophy that has prioritized athleticism and left-handed power in the corners. Outfield depth and production have been hallmarks of Texas softball's best rosters, and Abdullah's commitment signals that head coach Mike White and his staff are applying that same template to the 2028 class.

She becomes the fourth known member of a class that the Longhorns are assembling with the same methodical approach that has produced back-to-back top-five recruiting classes. With Michaela Landers already committed as the class's top-ranked player, the additions around her give the class substance and depth.

The Dynasty Blueprint

Texas won the 2026 Women's College World Series and enters the 2027 season as the team to beat. Isa Torres — who transferred from Florida State and posted a .530 batting average with 16 home runs, earning D1Softball Player of the Year — anchors the offense alongside Teagan Kavan, a two-time WCWS MOP. That 2027 squad is built to contend for another title.

But program-building doesn't stop with the current roster. The 2028 commits arriving in Austin in two years will be walking into a program with championship infrastructure, championship culture, and the credibility that comes with winning the biggest games. That's a recruiting pitch that sells itself — and Texas is using it.

What's Next

The 2028 evaluation and contact window opens October 17-18, giving Texas coaches the opportunity to push for additional commits as the weekend unfolds. The Longhorns enter that window with four commits and a compelling case to make to the class's remaining uncommitted top prospects.
