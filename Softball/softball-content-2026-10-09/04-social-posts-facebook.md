### STORY 2: Texas Tech 2027 Coaching Staff Overhaul

**Long-Form Post:**
Change is coming to Lubbock — and that's saying something for a program that just went to its second straight Women's College World Series final.

Texas Tech will not renew assistant coaches Hunter Veach and Kayla Kowalik ahead of the 2027 season. In their place, the Red Raiders have hired Jeff Cottrill and Shelby Pendley, who join returning assistant Tara Archibald in supporting head coach Gerry Glasco.

Glasco enters Year 3 at Texas Tech with a jaw-dropping 41-7 record in Big 12 play. He's also brought in the No. 1 transfer portal class in the country this offseason, so the pieces are in place even as the coaching staff shifts. The Red Raiders aren't rebuilding. They're reloading.

Texas already beat them in Stillwater in June. Texas Tech hasn't forgotten.

### STORY 3: Big 12 2027 Softball Schedule

**Long-Form Post:**
The Big 12 has released its 2027 softball schedule, and for Texas Tech fans, it marks the official start of the 3-peat conversation.

The Red Raiders open the 2027 season at the NFCA Leadoff Classic in Clearwater, Florida, kicking things off February 12 against Notre Dame. Tech will play six games at the classic before heading home to prep for Big 12 play, which begins March 12 against Kansas.

The 2027 Big 12 slate features marquee matchups against Arizona, UCF, Iowa State, Baylor, and Houston — plus a regular-season finale against Arizona State, the same team that beat Texas Tech in last year's Big 12 Tournament championship.

And don't forget: defending national champion Texas is also in the Big 12. The rematch is coming.

### STORY 4: NCAA Officiating Director

**Long-Form Post:**
Here's a hire that matters for every softball fan who has ever yelled at an umpire.

On October 5, the NCAA named Marty Abezetian as its Director of Training and Video Review for softball umpires. Abezetian brings 17 seasons of collegiate umpiring experience to the role, including working the 2025 Women's College World Series. He's no stranger to the position — he previously served six years as the softball video services coordinator and was the first-ever director of training and video review before this return.

His focus: consistency, development, and making sure the next generation of collegiate softball umpires is ready. If you've ever wanted better officiating at the highest level of the sport, this appointment is a step in the right direction.

### STORY 5: Texas State $16.5M Facility

**Long-Form Post:**
The future of Texas State softball is being built right now — literally.

Texas State broke ground this month on a $16.5 million renovation and improvement project for its baseball and softball programs. The driving force behind it? An $11 million gift from Eric and Candice Hays, one of the largest private donations in the program's history.

Phase 1 of the project is expected to wrap up by June 2027, just in time for the Bobcats to take the field in a transformed facility. For a Sun Belt Conference program looking to compete at the next level, this kind of infrastructure investment signals serious intent.

College softball is growing everywhere. Texas State is making sure it's not getting left behind.
