Texas Tech Shakes Up Coaching Staff Ahead of 2027 3-Peat Bid

By Sarah Mitchell | October 9, 2026 | I Love Softball

Texas Tech softball is making major changes heading into 2027 — and considering that this is a program coming off back-to-back Women's College World Series final appearances, that is not a sentence most fans expected to read this fall.

The Red Raiders will not renew assistant coaches Hunter Veach and Kayla Kowalik ahead of the 2027 season. In their place, Texas Tech has hired Jeff Cottrill and Shelby Pendley, who join returning assistant Tara Archibald in supporting head coach Gerry Glasco as he begins Year 3 in Lubbock.

The move signals that Glasco is not content to run the same program that fell short in the 2026 WCWS final against Texas. He is actively reshaping his staff around the next phase of the program's ambitions.

Who Glasco Is, and Why This Matters

Glasco arrived at Texas Tech with a reputation as one of the best offensive minds in college softball and has more than lived up to it. Through his first two seasons, the Red Raiders went 41-7 in Big 12 Conference play — a record that speaks for itself in the most competitive conference in the sport.

Texas Tech reached the WCWS final in each of Glasco's first two seasons. The 2025 run ended in a loss to Texas Tech's eventual championship game opponent, and the 2026 run ended in a final against Texas — Mike White's second national title with the Longhorns. Glasco has been at the doorstep twice. The 2027 season is his best chance yet to walk through it.

Portal Class Adds to the Mix

Beyond the coaching staff changes, Texas Tech brought in what multiple outlets have ranked as the No. 1 transfer portal class in the country this offseason. The combination of a coaching overhaul and elite portal additions suggests Glasco is treating 2027 as a full-scale retooling — not a tweak.

The Big 12 Conference released the full 2027 softball schedule, giving fans the first official look at the road ahead. Texas Tech opens the season February 12 against Notre Dame at the NFCA Leadoff Classic in Clearwater, Florida, before heading into a Big 12 slate that ends with a regular-season finale against Arizona State — a rematch of the 2026 conference tournament championship game.

What's Next

Texas Tech's fall slate continues through late October, giving Glasco and his new staff their first extended look at the 2027 roster in live action. For a program that has been knocking on the door for two straight years, the window to win a national championship is wide open — and the Red Raiders are doing everything possible to make sure they're ready when it reopens in February.
