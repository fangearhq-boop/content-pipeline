Oklahoma Softball Veterans Shine in Fall Battle Series Game 2

By Jordan Reeves | October 9, 2026 | I Love Softball

The lights came on at Love's Field in Norman on Wednesday night, and Oklahoma's veterans made sure everyone noticed. In the second installment of the 2026 Fall Battle Series, the returning core of the Sooners gave fans a clear look at the kind of team Patty Gasso is building for the 2027 season.

The Battle Series is an intrasquad competition that splits Oklahoma's roster into three squads and puts them through live game situations in front of fans — free admission, a fall tradition. Game 1 on September 30 had drawn 2,351 fans and featured back-to-back home runs from Gabbi Garcia and Kendall Wells. Game 2 gave the veteran class a chance to build on that momentum.

According to reporting from Sooners On SI, the veterans took center stage — a promising development for a program that loses no time in looking ahead to a 2027 campaign where it will chase another SEC title and a return to Oklahoma City.

Building a Roster in Real Time

The Battle Series is more than a feel-good fall tradition. It is how Gasso evaluates her 21-player fall roster under pressure, and it gives her staff critical data before the spring. The roster includes transfers such as Sydney Berzon, a two-time NFCA All-American from LSU, alongside a class of six freshmen who arrived this fall.

Game 2 was the second of four scheduled Battle Series contests. Games 3 and 4 are set for October 14 and October 21, also at Love's Field, both at 6:30 PM CT. Admission remains free for all four events — one of the more fan-friendly fall traditions in college softball.

Oklahoma's Fall Ball Central hub, launched on soonersports.com on September 29, has been the primary vehicle for tracking progress through the fall. It includes notes, updates, and a running look at how new and returning players are developing.

What's Next

Battle Series Game 3 is Tuesday, October 14, at 6:30 PM CT at Love's Field. Following that, the Sooners will wrap up fall ball with Game 4 on October 21 before turning their full attention to the 2027 season. For a program that has built its identity around staying at the very top of college softball, every fall rep counts.
