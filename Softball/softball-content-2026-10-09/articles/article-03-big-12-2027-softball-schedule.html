Big 12 Releases 2027 Softball Schedule: Texas Tech's 3-Peat Bid Is Official

By Emily Rawlings | October 9, 2026 | I Love Softball

The Big 12 Conference has released its 2027 softball schedule, and with it comes the official start of one of the most compelling story lines in college softball: Can Texas Tech reach its third straight Women's College World Series final?

The Red Raiders open the 2027 season February 12 against Notre Dame at the NFCA Leadoff Classic in Clearwater, Florida. Texas Tech will play six games at the classic before returning home to begin preparations for Big 12 play, which opens March 12 against Kansas at Tracy Sellers Field in Lubbock.

What the Conference Slate Looks Like

The 2027 Big 12 schedule puts Texas Tech against Baylor, UCF, Iowa State, Arizona, Houston, Oklahoma State, and Arizona State over the course of conference play. The regular-season finale against Arizona State carries immediate history — the Mustangs beat the Red Raiders in the 2026 Big 12 Tournament championship game, meaning both programs will show up for that finale with something to prove.

Texas is also in the Big 12, still the defending national champion after Mike White's team beat Texas Tech in the 2026 WCWS final. Any preview of the 2027 conference race that doesn't account for the Longhorns trying to defend is incomplete.

Gerry Glasco is 41-7 in Big 12 Conference play over his first two seasons at Texas Tech. The conference has never been more competitive, and the schedule reflects that — there are no easy weeks on the Red Raiders' calendar.

What the NFCA Leadoff Classic Sets Up

The NFCA Leadoff Classic in Clearwater has become one of the premier early-season tournaments in college softball, drawing Top 25 programs from around the country. Opening February 12 against Notre Dame gives Texas Tech an immediate national-stage look. Six games in Clearwater will provide the first real data points of the 2027 season for the Red Raiders and their fans.

What's Next

With the schedule set and a revamped coaching staff in place, Texas Tech will complete its fall slate through late October before the offseason ramp-up toward February. The pieces are aligned for another deep run. Whether Glasco can finally cut down the nets in Oklahoma City is the question that will define his tenure in Lubbock.
