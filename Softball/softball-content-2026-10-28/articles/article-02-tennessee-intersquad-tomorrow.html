Tennessee Softball's Free Public Intersquad Is Tomorrow — Here's Why You Should Go

By Sarah Mitchell | October 28, 2026 | I Love Softball

There are not many chances to watch a pitching staff like Tennessee's without paying for a ticket. Tomorrow is one of them.

The Lady Vols hold their final public intersquad of the fall on Thursday, October 29, at 5 PM ET at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium in Knoxville. Admission is free. For anyone within driving distance, it is the last opportunity to see Tennessee softball before winter conditioning begins and the team disappears into spring prep.

The Pitching Staff That Changes Everything

College softball has deep pitching rosters throughout the country. Tennessee's is different. The Lady Vols return three pitchers who would be the No. 1 arm on most programs in the SEC — and they have them all in the same rotation.

Karlyn Pickens recorded 194 strikeouts with a 1.70 ERA in 2026. She is one of the most dominant pitchers in the country, with the kind of stuff that makes even good college hitters look lost. Pickens will be in the circle tomorrow.

Kelly Mardjetko went 16-3 with a 1.26 ERA last season. Sixteen wins. A 1.26 ERA. For a pitcher who is not even the clear-cut ace of her own staff, that is a remarkable line. She is back and healthy for 2027.

Then there is Bailee Shelton. The freshman threw three innings in Tennessee's fall finale against Lee University and struck out seven. Seven strikeouts in three innings — in her debut. Shelton already looks like a future ace, and she is only just getting started.

What Fall Has Shown

Tennessee finished its fall schedule 3-0 against outside competition, with the most notable result being a 14-1 win over Lee University in the fall finale on October 23. Head coach Karen Weekly was candid after that game, saying she wants tougher fall competition in coming years. That kind of transparency from one of the best coaches in college softball tells you something: this team is not just surviving fall — it is looking for something harder to do.

When a program of Tennessee's caliber is outpacing what fall opponents can provide, the spring is going to be a different story entirely. The competition from mid-February onward will be worthy of what the Lady Vols have been preparing for.

What's Next

After tomorrow's public intersquad, Tennessee shuts down for the winter. The next time fans see this team in game action will be in 2027, when the regular season begins.

Tomorrow at 5 PM ET is the last free chance to see what might be the best pitching staff in the SEC. Sherri Parker Lee Stadium. No ticket needed.
