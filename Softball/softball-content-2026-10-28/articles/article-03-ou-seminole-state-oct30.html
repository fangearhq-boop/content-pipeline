Oklahoma Softball's Fall Finale Is Thursday: Last Look at Wells, Kavan Before Spring

By Emily Rawlings | October 28, 2026 | I Love Softball

In two days, the curtain comes down on Oklahoma softball's fall season. The Sooners close their 2026 fall slate Thursday, October 30 at 6:30 PM CT, hosting Seminole State at Love's Field in Norman. Admission is free, and the game streams at 1oklahoma.com.

For Sooners fans and college softball observers, it is the last public look at one of the top title contenders in the country before the 2027 season begins in February.

Two Stars, One Last Fall Tuneup

Kendall Wells does not need more to prove. She hit 39 home runs as a freshman last season and won the NFCA Freshman of the Year award in one of the most decorated rookie campaigns in recent memory. Heading into her sophomore year, she is one of the most feared hitters in college softball.

Rylie Kavan has already won the Women's College World Series Most Outstanding Player award twice. She is the kind of player who defines an era for a program, and the Sooners have her entering 2027 with everything still in front of her.

Thursday's game against Seminole State gives both players — and the rest of the OU roster — one more fall exhibition at full speed before the winter break. For the coaching staff, it is one more data point before finalizing the game plans that will carry Oklahoma into the spring.

The Sooners' 2027 Outlook

Oklahoma consistently enters the season as one of the two or three best teams in the country, and 2027 is unlikely to be different. The core is intact, the coaching staff is experienced at this level, and the culture built in Norman produces championship-caliber performances year after year.

The addition of Jordy Berzon to an already loaded lineup rounds out what could be the most dangerous offensive roster Oklahoma has fielded in years. If the pitching holds — and it has every reason to — the Sooners are a legitimate bet to be in Oklahoma City for the WCWS when June arrives.

What's Next

Thursday is the end of the line for fall ball in Norman. After the Seminole State game at 6:30 PM CT, Oklahoma shifts to winter preparation and begins building toward the 2027 season.

The game is free to attend at Love's Field and free to stream at 1oklahoma.com. Two days away. The last chance to see the Sooners before everything counts.
