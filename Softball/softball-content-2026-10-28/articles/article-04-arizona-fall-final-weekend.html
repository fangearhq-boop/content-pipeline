Arizona Softball Closes Fall With Three Games at Hillenbrand This Weekend

By Jordan Reeves | October 28, 2026 | I Love Softball

Arizona softball wraps up its fall season this weekend with three games in two days at Hillenbrand Stadium in Tucson. Thursday's doubleheader features Chandler-Gilbert Community College at 5:30 PM and Benedictine University Mesa at 7:30 PM. Friday's finale is Grand Canyon University at noon.

For head coach Caitlin Lowe, the weekend is the final opportunity to evaluate her full roster before the winter break and begin finalizing the game plans that will carry the Wildcats into 2027.

Shockey Leads a Deep Group of Returners

Arizona returns 10 letterwinners from its 2026 roster, and the headliner is Devyn Shockey. The infielder won the Big 12 Defensive Player of the Year award last season — a recognition that only confirms what anyone who watched her play already knew. Shockey changes the calculus for opposing offenses from the moment she takes the field.

Her defensive presence at the infield corner gives Arizona an anchor that most programs in the Big 12 cannot match. Paired with the returning depth elsewhere on the roster, she is the kind of returning piece that gives a coaching staff confidence heading into a new season.

Lowe's First Full Fall Evaluation

Caitlin Lowe has been building toward something with this Wildcats program. The fall schedule — which has featured multiple opponents across several weeks — has given the new coaching staff a thorough look at what they have and what they need to refine before February.

Hillenbrand Stadium is one of the finest softball venues in the country, and Tucson's climate means conditions will be ideal for this final fall weekend. Three competitive games against GCU and two community college programs will give the coaching staff a final data set while giving fans a last look at the 2027 Wildcats before the long wait until spring.

What's Next

After Sunday's noon game against Grand Canyon, Arizona shifts into winter conditioning and begins the long preparation for the 2027 season. The Big 12 has continued to establish itself as one of the top conferences in college softball, and Arizona will be in that mix from day one.

Both games Thursday and Friday's finale at Hillenbrand Stadium are expected to be open to the public. A program on the rise, a beautiful facility, and three games in two days — the final fall weekend in Tucson is worth a visit.
