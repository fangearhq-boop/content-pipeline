Playoff Day in Oklahoma City: USA Chasing Direct Ticket to 2027 Finals at Devon Park

By Jordan Reeves | I Love Softball | September 16, 2026

Group Oklahoma's bracket runs today — two nations earn direct berths to Redcliffe, QLD, and a spot on the road to LA28.

Five days of softball at Devon Park in Oklahoma City have led to this. On Wednesday, the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Oklahoma City 2026 holds its playoff bracket — three sessions, two tickets to the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia, and one of the final qualifying paths to the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics.

Team USA enters the bracket as the top seed after a dominant 4-0 run through the group stage. The Americans ran the table with authority, opening with an 11-0 run-rule win over Mexico on Day 1 and following it with a 17-0 demolition of Brazil on Day 2 — a run-rule in four innings that announced to the field that USA was not merely participating, but dominating.

The Bracket and What's at Stake

Wednesday's playoff schedule at Devon Park runs three sessions. At 1 PM ET, the No. 1 and No. 2 seeds square off — the winner of that game earns their direct berth to the 2027 Finals in Redcliffe, QLD. At 4 PM ET, the No. 3 and No. 4 seeds meet. And at 7 PM ET, the winners of those two games play a Repechage match, with the winner claiming the second and final direct ticket to Australia.

Only two nations advance directly. The stakes are real: the Redcliffe Finals, scheduled for April 5-11, 2027, serve as one of the primary qualifiers on the road to the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. Every game today carries Olympic implications.

USA's Dominant Group Stage Run

USA enters Wednesday's bracket having collected three run-rule victories in the group stage — a margin of victory that goes beyond the scoreboard and speaks to the depth and firepower of the roster that head coach Patty Gasso assembled with her USA coaching staff.

Gasso, who also serves as head coach at the University of Oklahoma, has been at Devon Park since September 12 alongside JT Gasso and Rocha, coaching a Team USA roster that includes four Sooners: Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings and Kelly Maxwell. The local talent connection gives this run a particular energy in Sooner country.

The roster also features international experience that few nations can match. Gabbie Garcia and Sahvanna Mulipola carry Tokyo 2020 silver medals and seven World Games gold medals between the group. USA went 5-0 at Devon Park during the 2025 USA Softball International Cup, and this week has added to that comfort with the venue.

China and the Competitive Field

China entered the bracket after an impressive group stage run of their own. The two nations were the only undefeated teams through most of the round-robin, and both figure prominently in today's bracket. The Netherlands also made their presence known through the group stage, finishing third or fourth among the six-nation field that also included Brazil, Mexico and New Zealand.

The competitive nature of this field is part of why Group Oklahoma matters — these are among the top-ranked softball programs in the world, and the competition preparing for Redcliffe and LA28 is arriving here on the Oklahoma City diamond.

What's Next

By the time the Repechage concludes Wednesday evening, two nations will have secured their places in the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, QLD. Those Finals will join Group Prague and Group Lima results to complete the eight-team field for Australia next April. For Gasso and her WNT staff, the conclusion of Group Oklahoma also means the drive back to Norman — and a reunion with the full Oklahoma Sooners coaching staff that begins Thursday with the first full-staff fall practice. Devon Park has been the stage. Redcliffe will be the destination. And for USA softball, today is the day to claim it.
