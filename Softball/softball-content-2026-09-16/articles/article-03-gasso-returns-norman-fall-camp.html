Gasso Returns to Norman Today: Full Staff Reunites Thursday as Fall Countdown Hits 14 Days

By Emily Rawlings | I Love Softball | September 16, 2026

After coaching Team USA through Group Oklahoma at Devon Park, Patty Gasso is back — and the real work at Love's Field begins Thursday.

Patty Gasso is coming home. After leading Team USA through the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Oklahoma City at Devon Park — spending five days coaching the national team through a dominant group stage and today's playoff bracket — Gasso, JT Gasso and Rocha are expected to return to Norman on Wednesday afternoon. The Oklahoma Sooners' associate coaching staff has held practice in their absence. But Thursday is when the full Oklahoma coaching team reunites for the first time since September 12.

It is also when fall camp gets real. The fall opener against Tarleton State at Love's Field is 14 days away — September 30 — and a new-look roster full of both returning talent and high-profile additions is waiting to be shaped into a team.

Five Days Without the Full Staff

When Gasso departed Norman on September 12 for Devon Park, she left behind a program in good hands. The associate coaching staff took over daily operations and managed practice through last week and into this week. It was a test of the program's institutional depth — and by all accounts, the Sooners passed it.

But there is a different energy that comes with the full coaching staff together. Gasso knows this roster in ways that matter for fall camp evaluation. Her eye for mechanics, her relationships with the players and her ability to push the right buttons will be crucial in the 14 days between now and Tarleton State.

The Players Waiting to Be Evaluated

The returning anchor is Kendall Wells, who set the NCAA freshman home run record with 32 in her first season and enters her sophomore year with expectations through the roof. Wells's power bat will be the centerpiece of the lineup regardless of what else the fall shows — but how she develops in Year 2 will define what kind of season Oklahoma can chase.

The two marquee additions are at the top of the pitching rotation. Sydney Berzon, a two-time All-American from LSU who threw 486.1 innings with a 2.02 ERA and struck out 406 batters in her career with the Tigers, brings an immediate upgrade to a circle that returns only Lowry and Deal. Berzon's presence alone changes the ceiling of this team. Alongside her in the depth evaluation is Macie Harter, who posted a .346 batting average with 7 home runs and 23 RBI at Middle Tennessee State and offers versatility in the lineup.

OU's fall schedule runs eight games, all at Love's Field: Tarleton State (Sept. 30), USAO (Oct. 23), Seminole State (Oct. 30) and East Texas A&M (Nov. 4), plus four Battle Series intrasquad contests.

What's Next

Thursday's first full-staff practice is the real starting gun for Oklahoma's fall. Gasso will spend the next two weeks evaluating how this roster's combinations work — which lineup configurations click, which pitching matchups look sharp and which young players are ready to contribute. The first public look comes September 30 against Tarleton State. What happens between now and then, behind Love's Field's closed gates, will lay the foundation for everything that follows in 2027.
