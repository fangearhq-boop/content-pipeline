Fall Ball Nation Week 2: Programs Coast to Coast Get Back on the Field

By Emily Rawlings | I Love Softball | October 2, 2026

October is here and softball fans have plenty to follow. Week 2 of the fall ball season has programs from Tennessee to Oregon putting new-look rosters on the field, while the transfer portal continues to generate headlines of its own.

Here is what is happening around the country this week.

Tennessee: Fast Start in Knoxville

The Lady Vols opened their fall schedule with a pair of dominant wins — 15-1 over Walters State and 10-1 over Lipscomb — and carry a 2-0 record and a plus-23 run differential into October. Tonight, Tennessee hosts Carson-Newman at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium at 6 PM ET. Admission is free, making it one of the best early fall ball opportunities in the region for fans who want a live look at the program.

The Lady Vols have seven home fall games total on the schedule, so there are multiple chances to see the roster before the 2027 season begins.

Oklahoma: Battle Series Back on Track

OU's fall schedule got off to an interrupted start when lightning suspended Battle Series Game 1 at Love's Field on Sept. 30. Despite the delay, Ella Parker made the most of her time in the box, blasting a home run on her very first at-bat.

Battle Series Game 2 is Saturday, Oct. 3 at 1 PM at Love's Field against Tarleton State. Additional Battle Series dates run through October — games are also scheduled Oct. 7, 14, and 21. For a program that significantly remade its roster through the portal this offseason, fall ball is the first extended opportunity for Patty Gasso to see what she has.

Oregon: Open Exhibitions All Fall

Oregon is hosting eight free fall exhibitions at Jane Sanders Stadium in Eugene through November 1. The exhibitions are open to the public at no charge, giving fans in the Pacific Northwest multiple opportunities to watch the Ducks ahead of the 2027 season.

Oregon has been one of the more active programs in the recent transfer portal cycles and the fall slate will give their new additions a chance to integrate into the system before conference play resumes in the spring.

Louisiana: Fall Schedule in Place

Louisiana softball has four dates on its October calendar. The Ragin' Cajuns travel to face McNeese on Oct. 9, then return home for a doubleheader on Oct. 14 before adding another home game Oct. 15. A rematch with McNeese rounds out the month on Oct. 27.

Louisiana has been active in regional fall competition in recent seasons and the early October games will give the Sun Belt program a chance to evaluate depth and sort out lineup questions ahead of spring.

Transfer Portal: 250+ Moves and Counting

The 2026-27 transfer portal cycle has logged more than 250 moves across Division I softball, according to ON3's tracking. That number includes high-profile names like Jaysoni Beachum of Florida State, who entered the portal this week and is ranked the No. 1 corner infield prospect available, as well as dozens of role players and emerging contributors changing programs.

The October transfer window is one of the most active recruitment periods on the calendar, and the pace of movement is expected to continue through the end of the month.

What's Next

Week 2 of fall ball is just getting started, with games across the country scheduled through the weekend and beyond. For fans wanting to follow along, check your program's official athletics website for fall ball dates and free admission details — October is one of the best months to catch live softball without any cost at the gate.

Transfer portal activity will keep building over the coming weeks, with commitments expected to start rolling in as programs lock in their 2027 rosters. Stay tuned to I Love Softball for the latest news as fall ball and the portal window continue.
