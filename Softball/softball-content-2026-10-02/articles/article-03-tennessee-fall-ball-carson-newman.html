Tennessee Lady Vols 2-0 in Fall Ball, Host Carson-Newman Tonight

By Jordan Reeves

October 2, 2026

I Love Softball

Lady Vols Off to a Dominant Start

The Tennessee Lady Vols have made a statement in the early going of their fall ball schedule, rolling to a 2-0 record while outscoring opponents 25-2 across two games. The team will look to make it three for three when they host Carson-Newman tonight at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium in a 10-inning exhibition. First pitch is scheduled for 6 PM Eastern.

The convincing margins have given the Tennessee coaching staff a wide range of looks at the roster. Comfortable scores allow for lineup flexibility and extended work for players across the depth chart, which is exactly what fall ball is designed to provide.

Recapping the Early Fall

Tennessee opened fall ball with a 15-1 victory over Walters State. Fourteen runs of difference established the tone for what this offense can look like when everything is working, and holding the opponent to a single run added a two-way dimension to the win.

The second game followed a similar script. Tennessee defeated Lipscomb 10-1, again limiting the opponent to one run while producing double-digit offense. Back-to-back single-run allowances suggest a level of pitching consistency that is encouraging to see this early. Holding two consecutive opponents to one run while scoring 25 total is the kind of start that builds confidence on both sides of the ball.

Tonight's Exhibition: What You Need to Know

Tonight's game against Carson-Newman is scheduled for 10 innings, a departure from the standard seven-inning format. The extra length gives both rosters more opportunities for evaluation and provides the Tennessee staff with additional at-bats and pitching appearances to assess. Ten-inning exhibitions are common in fall ball, where player development and information-gathering matter as much as the final score.

Sherri Parker Lee Stadium will host the game with free admission for all fans. Gates open one hour before first pitch, which means doors will be open at 5 PM Eastern for the 6 PM start. Fans planning to attend should note that concessions will not be available tonight, so planning ahead is advisable.

The combination of no ticket cost and an early evening start makes tonight a straightforward option for fans in the Knoxville area. Fall ball games at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium carry a different energy than spring competition, but the caliber of the players on the field remains unmistakable.

The Fall Schedule and What It Builds

Tennessee has seven home fall games scheduled in total, and tonight's Carson-Newman exhibition is the third. The full slate of home games gives the program a substantial window of in-stadium work before the calendar turns toward winter preparation and eventually the spring season.

Fall ball allows new players to build familiarity with Sherri Parker Lee Stadium, gives returners a chance to refine adjustments made in the offseason, and creates a lower-stakes setting for the coaching staff to experiment with roles and configurations. Seven home games is a meaningful sample, and each one adds to the picture that will define how Tennessee lines up when the games count.

With a 2-0 record and a 25-2 run differential already in hand, the Lady Vols carry genuine momentum into the second half of the fall schedule. The challenge now is sustaining that level of execution and developing the depth that could prove critical come March.

What's Next
