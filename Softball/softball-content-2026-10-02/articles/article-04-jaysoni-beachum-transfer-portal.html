Jaysoni Beachum Leaves FSU, Visits Florida as No. 1 Transfer Portal Target

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | October 2, 2026

Florida State's Jaysoni Beachum — one of the most accomplished third basemen in Seminoles history — has entered the transfer portal, and she is already drawing attention from one of FSU's biggest rivals.

Beachum recently visited the University of Florida, posting a photo of herself in a Gators uniform on social media. No commitment has been announced, but the visit put the college softball world on notice: one of the best available players in the portal has a clear frontrunner in the race for her signature.

What Beachum Brings

The numbers speak for themselves. In 2026, Beachum hit .406 with 10 home runs and 69 RBIs — the fourth-most RBIs in Florida State program history. Those aren't just good numbers; they are elite production at a premium position.

Softball America ranks Beachum No. 3 overall among all available transfers and No. 1 at corner infield. That designation reflects both her offensive output and her defensive value at third base, where her arm, range, and footwork have drawn consistent praise throughout her career.

Beachum had the kind of 2026 season that should have kept her at FSU for a senior campaign. Her decision to leave — she announced on social media that "it's time to move on" — surprised Seminoles fans but is not uncommon in an era when the transfer portal has reshaped roster building at every program.

Why the Florida Visit Matters

Florida and Florida State have one of the most intense rivalries in college softball. The Gators and Seminoles square off multiple times a year, and the fact that Beachum — a homegrown FSU star — is considering crossing to Gainesville adds fuel to an already heated dynamic.

Florida has been an aggressive portal program under its coaching staff, building depth at multiple positions with nationally ranked transfers. Adding the No. 1 corner infield prospect in the portal would represent a significant upgrade in a lineup that is already competing at the SEC level.

That said, no commitment has been confirmed, and Beachum's caliber means she will receive visits from multiple programs. Power Five coaches from around the country are watching this recruitment closely.

Implications for Florida State

Losing Beachum is a significant departure for the Seminoles, who must replace her .406 bat and 69-RBI production in the middle of their lineup. FSU head coach Lonni Alameda has navigated roster transitions before, but a player of Beachum's profile will not be easy to replace through the portal or through recruiting alone.

The Seminoles have other talent on the roster, but the production Beachum provided at third base is the kind of hole that shows up immediately when spring competition begins. How FSU responds in the portal over the next several weeks will define a large portion of their 2027 outlook.

What's Next

Jaysoni Beachum has not announced a commitment, but the Florida visit is a strong signal of her intentions. Programs hoping to land her will need to move quickly — top transfer portal targets rarely stay on the market for long once visits are made public.

The October signing window keeps transfer portal momentum at full speed, and Beachum's destination will be one of the most-watched decisions in college softball this fall. Check back for updates as her recruitment develops.
