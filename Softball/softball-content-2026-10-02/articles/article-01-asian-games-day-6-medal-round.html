Asian Games Day 6: Prelim Round Complete, Medal Round Set for Saturday

By Sarah Mitchell

October 2, 2026

I Love Softball

Preliminary Round Closes With a Bang

The preliminary round of the Asian Games softball tournament wrapped up Friday at Anjo Sports Park in Anjo, Aichi Prefecture, Japan, delivering three decisive results that finalized the field for Saturday's medal games. With an Olympic berth to Los Angeles 2028 on the line, the competition has been fierce from the first pitch, and Day 6 was no exception.

China and Chinese Taipei both closed the prelim round with dominant wins, while a rain-shortened contest between Hong Kong and Singapore still managed to produce a clear-cut result.

Day 6 Results

China defeated Korea 8-4 in a power showcase. Three home runs off Chinese bats powered the offense, and the pitching staff held Korea's lineup in check. The win kept China undefeated through the preliminary round.

Chinese Taipei dispatched the Philippines by a score of 10-1, with the game ending in five innings under the run rule. The mercy-rule finish gave Chinese Taipei's pitching staff a chance to conserve arms ahead of a busy Saturday. Chinese Taipei enters the medal round with a 4-1-plus record.

The final game saw Hong Kong edge Singapore 5-2 in a contest cut short by rain. Both teams logged their final prelim result before the skies closed the game down. Singapore finishes outside the medal picture, while Hong Kong moves forward.

Final Prelim Standings

Japan stands alone at the top of the field, completing the preliminary round without a loss. China joins them as the only other undefeated team remaining. Chinese Taipei holds a 4-1-plus record, placing them firmly in the medal conversation, while the Philippines finished at 3-2-plus. Japan and China have separated themselves through pitching depth, offensive production, and disciplined defense.

Japan's Pitching Has Been the Story

No single performance better captured Japan's dominance than Mio Goto's perfect game against Korea earlier in the week. Goto retired all 18 batters she faced, striking out nine and needing just 64 pitches to complete the gem. It was a masterclass in efficiency and command, and it underscored why Japan's pitching depth is considered the best in the region.

Japan also routed Singapore 10-0, adding to a mounting shutout streak that has opposing lineups searching for answers. Both Japan and China have extended their streaks of not allowing runs through extended stretches of the tournament. Japan's staff has cycled through multiple arms without a meaningful drop-off in quality, positioning them well for a condensed Saturday schedule.

The Olympic Qualifier Dimension

What makes these games more than a regional championship is the direct pathway to Los Angeles 2028. The top finishers earn Olympic berths, meaning every out and every run carries consequences that extend beyond Anjo Sports Park. Japan carries the weight of expectation as the top seed and host nation. China arrives as the clearest challenger, with an offense capable of hitting the ball out of the park and a pitching staff nearly as stingy as Japan's. Chinese Taipei will need a near-perfect performance to claim gold, but their bracket position gives them the chance.

What's Next

Medal games are scheduled for Saturday, October 3, at Anjo Sports Park in Anjo, Aichi Prefecture, Japan. The gold medal game represents more than a regional title: the winner secures a berth in the softball competition at the Los Angeles 2028 Olympic Games. First pitch times will be confirmed by tournament officials, and fans should expect a full day of high-stakes softball with historic implications.
