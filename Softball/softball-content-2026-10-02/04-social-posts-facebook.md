### STORY 1: Asian Games — Medal Round Set

**Long-Form Post** | Window: 9:00 AM ET

The 2026 Asian Games women's softball tournament is headed to its medal round, and the field is set.

After Day 6 of competition at Anjō Sports Park in Aichi, Japan, both Japan and China remain undefeated through the preliminary round. China won a chippy 8-4 game over Korea on Friday, hitting three home runs along the way. Chinese Taipei dominated the Philippines 10-1 in five innings via the run rule, and Hong Kong edged Singapore 5-2 in a rain-shortened game.

The gold medal game on Saturday (Oct. 3) will be the culmination of a round-robin that saw Japan throw a perfect game earlier this week — pitcher Mio Goto retired all 18 Korean batters in an 18-strikeout performance. Japan and China have combined to outscore opponents by triple digits this tournament.

Here's what makes Saturday even bigger: a top finish at the Asian Games earns a direct berth to the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. The Japan-China matchup, if it materializes in the final, would be must-watch softball.

### STORY 2: OU Battle Series Preview

**Long-Form Post** | Window: 11:00 AM ET

Oklahoma's fall Ball Series is back on after lightning cut Game 1 short.

The Sooners got through about three innings of their first Battle Series game on Sept. 30 before the weather intervened. Ella Parker made the most of her at-bats, launching a home run on the very first pitch of the game. That might be the best highlight you see all fall.

The next chapter comes Saturday: Patty Gasso's squad hosts Tarleton State at 1 PM at Love's Field in Norman. It's one of eight fall competitions on OU's schedule, spread through late October.

For a program that loaded up through the portal this offseason, fall ball is the first real live look at a new-look Sooners roster before the spring.

### STORY 3: Tennessee Fall Ball

**Long-Form Post** | Window: 3:00 PM ET

Tennessee's Lady Vols are making a statement early in fall ball.

After opening the fall schedule with a 15-1 blowout of Walters State and a 10-1 win over Lipscomb, the Lady Vols are 2-0 and outscoring opponents 25-2. That's a lot of firepower showing up in October.

Tonight, they get another chance at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium when they host Carson-Newman at 6 PM ET in a 10-inning exhibition. Admission is free — gates open at 5 PM, no concessions — so if you're in Knoxville, it's worth checking out.

Tennessee has seven home fall games on the schedule. Good time to get your first look at the 2027 roster.

### STORY 4: Jaysoni Beachum Transfer Portal

**Long-Form Post** | Window: 5:30 PM ET

The biggest name in the 2026-27 softball transfer portal is officially on the move.

Jaysoni Beachum, a third baseman who just completed her career at Florida State with a .406 batting average, 10 home runs, and 69 RBIs this season — the fourth-most in FSU history — has announced she's entering the transfer portal.

Softball America ranks Beachum No. 3 overall and No. 1 corner infielder among all portal transfers.

And here's the part that will get FSU fans talking: Beachum recently visited the University of Florida, posting a photo of herself in a Gators uniform on social media. No commitment has been announced, but the visit makes her destination crystal clear as a frontrunner.

For any program that lands her, Beachum is an immediate difference-maker.
