Tomorrow at 7 AM ET: What the Class of 2028 In-Person Window Really Means

By Sarah Mitchell | September 8, 2026

Recruiting

Tonight is the last quiet night of the recruiting cycle for the Class of 2028. At 7:00 AM ET tomorrow — Tuesday, September 9 — Division I softball coaches gain a new right: the ability to show up. In person. At a high school. At a practice facility. At a family's front door. After eight days of phone calls, FaceTime sessions, and texts since the September 1 communication window opened, the recruiting conversation moves from screens to face-to-face.

The Class of 2028 represents the largest pool of nominated prospects in Extra Innings Softball history — 1,226 athletes carrying a spot in the EIS database. And a record pool means the competition for the top names is fierce, the phone time since September 1 has been extensive, and tomorrow's first hour of in-person access is the most consequential window coaches have had all summer.

What Changes at 7 AM

Before September 1, Division I coaches could not call, text, or use any electronic communication with Class of 2028 prospects. After September 1, the phones opened. Since then, coaches have been working their call lists, FaceTiming, using social media direct messages, and laying groundwork for everything that happens next.

Tomorrow adds everything physical: off-campus evaluations, in-person meetings at the recruit's home or school, and the ability to bring a prospect onto campus for an official visit. For a program that has been dialing a No. 1 prospect multiple times per day, tomorrow is the chance to sit across the table, look her in the eye, and make the full pitch.

The NCAA calendar designates this window to Labor Day proximity, not a fixed date — so the window falls on September 9 this year. Coaches have had it circled since spring.

Ushery Set the Tone, Now the Wave Arrives

Peyton Ushery became the signature commitment of the early cycle when she pledged to Georgia on September 1 — the same day the phone window opened. Ranked No. 2 in Line Drive Media's HOT 100 for the Class of 2028, Ushery's announcement signaled that the top of this class is ready to move fast.

Tomorrow will accelerate what September 1 started. Coaches who have been in contact by phone for eight days are ready to convert conversations into commitments. Prospects who have been weighing options are ready to make decisions. The first 60 to 90 minutes after 7:00 AM will likely produce a cluster of announcements across high school softball social media — the commit wave that the class has been building toward all summer.

What Prospects Should Know Tonight

If a prospect has a priority school in mind and hasn't finished that conversation, tonight is the night to have it. Coaches who receive a clear "you're my top choice" signal tonight will show up early tomorrow. A commitment announcement before 8:00 AM isn't unusual — it is expected for the top of the class.

For families navigating a first visit from a college coach tomorrow: it is a compliment, not a pressure tactic. Programs that make the in-person investment early are signaling a sincere interest. Ask good questions. Ask about the coaching staff's long-term tenure. Ask how the program develops players who don't start as freshmen. The first visit matters, but the decision doesn't have to happen in it.

What's Next

The in-person contact window opens Tuesday, September 9 at 7:00 AM ET. In-person contact, campus visits, and off-campus evaluations are all now permitted for Division I programs recruiting the Class of 2028. The first commit announcements of the new phase are expected within minutes of the window opening. Watch your recruiting feeds closely — tomorrow morning will be a busy one.
