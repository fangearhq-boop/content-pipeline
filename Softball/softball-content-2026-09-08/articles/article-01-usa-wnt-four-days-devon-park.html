Four Days Out: USA Softball Prepares for the World Cup Stage at Devon Park

By Jordan Reeves | September 8, 2026

USA National Team

Four days from now, the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Oklahoma opens at Devon Park in Oklahoma City — and the United States enter as the most prepared team in the six-nation field. The same players. The same coaching staff. The same venue where USA went 5-0 at the International Cup just months ago. The only difference on September 12 is that the stakes are exponentially higher.

Two spots in this tournament advance directly to the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia — and those two spots are the first official qualification rung for the LA28 Olympic Games. This is not exhibition work. This is the beginning of the Olympic pathway.

A Roster Built for This Moment

Head Coach Patty Gasso leads a 16-player roster that combines international experience with the kind of talent the college game has developed in recent years. Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola bring 2020 Olympic gold back to the national jersey. Seven members of the squad competed on the 2025 World Games gold-medal team, and 10 players made the 2025 Down Under Series trip. This is not a group being assembled — it is a group being activated.

The coaching staff mirrors what Gasso runs at Oklahoma. JT Gasso and Jennifer Rocha are on the bench alongside her, giving the national team a unified vocabulary from the first drill of training camp through the final out of September 16.

Opening Night: USA vs. No. 7 Mexico

The tournament schedule opens Saturday with Brazil vs. China at 1:00 PM and New Zealand vs. Netherlands at 4:00 PM. USA closes opening day against No. 7 Mexico at 7:30 PM, with the Opening Ceremony at 6:30 PM preceding the match.

Mexico will arrive energized. Playing in the United States with home-crowd noise skewing heavily against them only sharpens the team's edge. The Mexicans are ranked No. 7 in the world for a reason — and their roster will play like they have something to prove.

But USA has spent significant time preparing for exactly this environment. Devon Park is familiar ground. The pressure is familiar. And the coaching staff has drilled this squad for the opening-game mentality: handle the moment early, establish rhythm, and protect home.

The Netherlands: The Real Seeding Battle

No. 6 Netherlands is the team that will decide where USA finishes the round-robin. The Dutch have been a consistent force in international softball, capable of stretching any opponent's pitching and executing under pressure. Finishing above them in the standings means entering the September 16 play-offs in the top position.

That seeding distinction matters in the play-off format. The top two teams from the round-robin meet at 1:00 PM on September 16 — and the winner earns a direct berth to the 2027 Finals in Australia. Third and fourth play at 4:00 PM, with the loser then facing the winner of that match in the Repechage at 7:00 PM for the second Australia spot.

USA wants the 1:00 PM match. Getting there requires winning the round-robin, and winning the round-robin requires handling Netherlands.

What's Next

Game action begins Saturday, September 12, at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. USA opens at 7:30 PM against No. 7 Mexico. The five-day tournament runs through September 16, with the play-off bracket deciding which two nations punch their tickets to the 2027 Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland.

The countdown is at four days. Devon Park is ready. Now it falls to the players to make it count.
