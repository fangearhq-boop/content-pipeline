Oklahoma Softball's Final Four Days: What the Last Complete Week Means Before Gasso Departs

By Emily Rawlings | September 8, 2026

Oklahoma Sooners

Four days. That is what Oklahoma softball has left with its complete coaching staff before Patty Gasso, JT Gasso, and Jennifer Rocha depart for Oklahoma City to lead Team USA in the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Oklahoma beginning September 12. And in a fall camp where the Sooners are integrating a rebuilt pitching staff and establishing the tempo for a bounce-back season, four days of fully staffed, intentional practice is a meaningful window.

The fall opener — September 30 at Love's Field against Tarleton State — is 22 days away. What gets installed between now and Saturday determines how much of the season's infrastructure is in place before the head coach steps aside for 12 days of national team duty.

Wells and Berzon: The Headline Competition

The most-discussed competition in Norman this fall has been the matchup between sophomore slugger Kendall Wells and transfer pitcher Sydney Berzon. Wells smashed the NCAA freshman home run record last season with 32 home runs — adding a .358 batting average, 88 RBIs, and a 1.028 slugging percentage to earn the NFCA National Freshman of the Year and Johnny Bench Award.

Berzon is the answer to an obvious question: who challenges her every day in practice? The two-time All-American from LSU brings 486.1 innings of work, a 2.02 ERA, and 406 strikeouts to Norman — a résumé that commands respect from anyone stepping into the batter's box. The early scrimmage results have reinforced what Gasso's staff hoped: Wells has homered twice off Berzon, and Berzon is making adjustments.

That is the best-case scenario for a fall camp. The best hitter and best pitcher pushing each other toward the edges of their capability.

Harter Brings Offensive Depth

Macie Harter (.346 BA, 7 HR, 23 RBI at MTSU) represents the kind of bat that strengthens a lineup without demanding the spotlight. Harter's arrival gives Oklahoma depth in the middle of the order — a proven hitter from a mid-major program who can handle quality pitching and punish mistakes at the plate.

With Wells and now Harter pushing through the early weeks of fall camp, Gasso's lineup is developing multiple genuine threats. The pitching side — Berzon anchoring, with other fall contributors competing for roles — has every incentive to get sharper.

Four Days That Have to Count

Gasso's 12-day departure isn't a disruption — it is a feature of the program's unusual position. The head coach of the Sooners is also the head coach of the United States national team. That duality produces recruiting advantages, roster prestige, and national exposure. It also produces four days in September where every practice minute carries extra weight.

The Sooners have weathered Gasso's national team absences before. The infrastructure is there. The question this fall is whether the newcomers — Wells in her sophomore leap, Berzon in her first season in Norman, Harter in a new offensive context — can build enough of a foundation in the time they have before the first game counts.

What's Next

Oklahoma's fall schedule opens September 30 vs. Tarleton State at Love's Field ($10.88/game; All Fall Pass available). Gasso and the Team USA coaching staff depart September 12 for Group Oklahoma at Devon Park — returning after the tournament concludes September 16. The Sooners' full staff will be back together by the time the first fall game tip-off arrives.
