PGF Nationals Week 2: Championship Day Is Three Days Away

By Sarah Mitchell

July 30, 2026

Week 2 of the 2026 PGF Nationals is in its most critical stretch. With championship games set for August 2 at Deanna Manning Stadium in Irvine, the 14U, 12U, and 10U divisions are playing deep into bracket competition right now in the Huntington Beach and Irvine area of Southern California.

Teams that came through pool play and survived the early bracket rounds are now a few wins away from standing on the biggest stage in youth travel softball. Every out counts.

What's Happening Right Now

The PGF Nationals is the premier travel ball showcase in the country, drawing the top programs in 14U, 12U, and 10U competition. Week 2's opening ceremony was held July 27 at HB Central Park, and bracket play has been running since July 26. All games are being streamed live on PGF.TV for fans who can't make the trip to Southern California.

Championship day arrives August 2, with the championship games taking place at Deanna Manning Stadium and Bill Barber Park at Irvine — two of the finest youth softball venues in the country. For the programs that reach championship day, it's a moment that defines the entire summer.

The Recruiting Stakes

What makes the PGF Nationals uniquely high-stakes is its proximity to the September 1 recruiting window. College coaches across Division I, II, and III are currently prohibited from making direct contact with uncommitted prospects — but they're watching. The September 1 window, which opens in 32 days, is when those coaches can pick up the phone.

A championship run at PGF Week 2 this week doesn't just win hardware. It builds film. It builds a résumé. For the athletes competing here, these games are essentially an extended tryout in front of the coaches who will define their college futures.

What's Next

Championship games arrive on August 2 at Deanna Manning Stadium. Follow along live at PGF.TV. With the September 1 recruiting window less than five weeks away, the players who perform in the championship rounds will be the ones generating the most calls from college programs on September 1 morning. That's the real prize being competed for here.
