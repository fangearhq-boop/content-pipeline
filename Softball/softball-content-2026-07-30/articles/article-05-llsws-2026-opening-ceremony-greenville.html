The Little League Softball World Series Is Two Days Away — Here's How to Experience It

By Emily Rawlings

July 30, 2026

The 2026 Little League Softball World Series is almost here. The opening ceremony is this Saturday, August 1 at 6 PM at Stallings Stadium at Elm Street Park in Greenville, North Carolina, and tournament play begins August 2. Eight days of softball, free admission for every game, and a field full of the best youth softball players in the country.

If you're a softball fan and you're anywhere near eastern North Carolina — or you're looking for a reason to make the trip — this is your guide.

Opening Ceremony: Saturday, August 1

The festivities kick off August 1 from 6 to 8 PM at Stallings Stadium. The opening ceremony features a live DJ, family activities, food trucks, and official recognition of the players, coaches, and officials participating in the tournament. It's the moment the competing teams formally arrive on the LLSWS stage — and the atmosphere inside Stallings Stadium for opening ceremonies is one of the most electric nights in youth softball.

The venue: Stallings Stadium at Elm Street Park, 1055 S Elm Street, Greenville, NC 27858. Free admission.

The Tournament: August 2–9

Tournament play runs August 2 through August 9. Twelve U.S. regional champions have punched their tickets to Greenville, joined by international teams from around the world. The bracket will determine who survives the double-elimination format and eventually claims the 2026 LLSWS championship on August 9.

All games are free to attend, and the full schedule is available at littleleague.org. Little League International's 2026 broadcast package covers 340+ games on ESPN platforms this summer, with significant LLSWS coverage streaming throughout the week.

Why This Tournament Matters

The Little League Softball World Series isn't just a youth tournament — it's a pipeline preview. The athletes competing at Stallings Stadium this week are 11 and 12 years old today. In five to eight years, many of them will be in college softball lineups, and a handful will be aiming for professional careers and international competition. The LLSWS has a history of producing future stars, and watching these games is watching the next generation arrive.

For the families in Greenville this week, it's also a reminder of what makes softball special at every level — the pure love of the game, the teamwork, and the thrill of competing on the biggest stage you've ever seen.

What's Next

Opening ceremony is Saturday, August 1 at 6 PM in Greenville. Tournament games begin Sunday, August 2, and the championship is August 9. Follow along on ESPN+ and the ESPN family of networks, or make the trip to Stallings Stadium — admission is free for every game. This one is worth the drive.
