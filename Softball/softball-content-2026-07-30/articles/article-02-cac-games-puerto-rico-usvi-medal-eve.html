Puerto Rico and USVI Are Unbeaten at the CAC Games — Medal Games Are Tomorrow

By Emily Rawlings

July 30, 2026

The women's softball bracket at the 2026 Central American & Caribbean Games has been a showcase of dominant pitching and run-rule scoring. Through two days of pool play at the Juan Pablo Duarte Olympic Centre in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands have emerged as the clear leaders — and the gold medal will almost certainly run through one of them when the medal games tip off tomorrow, July 31.

Puerto Rico: Defending Champion, On a Tear

Puerto Rico entered the 2026 CAC Games as the defending champion and the world No. 3 ranked team. They've played like it. In their opening game, Puerto Rico beat Guatemala 15-0 in just four innings — a run-rule mercy-rule finish that left no doubt about the talent gap. In Game 2, they went even shorter: a 16-1 run-rule win over the Dominican Republic, finishing in three innings.

Two games played, two run-rule wins, one run allowed total. Puerto Rico's pitching has been suffocating and their offense has been relentless. If they play at this level in the medal games, defending the CAC gold will be a formality.

USVI: The Bracket's Other Perfect Team

The U.S. Virgin Islands has quietly built one of the more impressive two-game résumés in the tournament. USVI beat El Salvador 15-0 in three innings, then followed that up with a 7-0 shutout of Cuba — a result that effectively eliminated Cuba from gold medal contention.

The USVI's win over Cuba is particularly significant. Cuba is historically one of the stronger teams in the CAC region, and a 7-0 shutout signals that USVI is pitching at a level that can contain the region's best. An all-Caribbean final between Puerto Rico and USVI would be one of the best outcomes for the tournament's profile.

The Full Eight-Team Field

Eight nations entered the tournament: Puerto Rico, Cuba, Mexico, Dominican Republic, Venezuela, Colombia, El Salvador, and the U.S. Virgin Islands. Joining Puerto Rico and USVI as unbeaten teams through Day 2 was Mexico. Cuba, Venezuela, and others have losses already, making tomorrow's medal games a battle for the nations that survive today's final pool play round.

What's Next

Medal games are scheduled for July 31 in Santo Domingo. Puerto Rico will enter as the favorite after their historic run-rule sweep of pool play. Watch for USVI as the team most capable of pulling an upset — their win over Cuba proved they're not just making up the numbers. Whether Puerto Rico can claim back-to-back CAC gold is the question the medal round will answer. Tune in tomorrow.
