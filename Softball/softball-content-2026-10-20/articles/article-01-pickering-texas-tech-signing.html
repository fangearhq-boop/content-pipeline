Kasidi Pickering Signs with Texas Tech: Portal's Biggest Bat Lands in Lubbock

By Emily Rawlings | I Love Softball | October 20, 2026

The transfer portal's most coveted infielder has found her next home. Kasidi Pickering, the three-time Oklahoma standout who entered the portal this summer after three seasons in Norman, has officially signed with Texas Tech — choosing the Red Raiders over her former program and a slate of suitors that reportedly included several Power conference programs.

Pickering's credentials speak for themselves. Over three seasons at Oklahoma, she posted a .384 career batting average, 50 home runs, and 169 RBI — numbers that put her among the most productive offensive players in the Big 12. In her final season as a Sooner, she hit .370 with 20 home runs in 59 appearances, earning All-American recognition. Her left-handed swing, speed on the basepaths, and defensive versatility made her the portal's most complete position player heading into the 2026-27 offseason.

A New Staff, A New Direction in Lubbock

Texas Tech enters Pickering's arrival having undergone its own transformation. Head coaches Cottrill and Pendley, who took over the program in 2026 following the departure of the previous staff, immediately identified the transfer portal as a pathway to accelerate a rebuild. Their approach has been aggressive: alongside Pickering, the Red Raiders also added Jaysoni Beachum — a dynamic infielder from Florida State — and Moriah Polar, who led the nation in batting average at Purdue. The result is a portal class widely regarded as one of the nation's deepest.

For Pickering, choosing Texas Tech over Oklahoma — the program she spent three years building a career at — is a statement. It signals confidence in the new staff, in the program's direction, and in her own ability to lead a rebuild rather than rest on an existing powerhouse. The Red Raiders finished 2026 with momentum from a staff overhaul and a No. 1 portal class ranking, and Pickering gives them the marquee name to match the ambition.

Big 12 Implications

In a conference already saturated with elite talent — Oklahoma, Texas, Oklahoma State, and others all fielding national contenders — Pickering's move to Texas Tech reshapes the competitive balance heading into 2027. The Sooners will absorb her departure alongside other portal changes, while the Red Raiders gain an immediate impact bat at a position of premium value.

The timing also carries significance. The 2027 season will be Texas Tech's second full year under its new coaching regime, and Pickering's presence in the lineup signals to future recruits — including the increasingly competitive Class of 2028 — that the program is building something real. For a Big 12 that is growing more top-heavy with talent, every addition at this level shifts the calculus for every other program.

What's Next

Pickering will transition into the Red Raiders' roster ahead of spring 2027 practices. With her arrival, Texas Tech's projected lineup becomes one of the most productive in the conference on paper. The question now is how quickly Cottrill, Pendley, and the coaching staff can build the kind of cohesion that translates portal success into postseason runs. The pieces are in place. The spring will tell the story.

For the rest of the Big 12, the message from Lubbock is clear: Texas Tech is no longer a rebuilding project. It's a destination.
