OU Battle Series Game 4: Tomorrow at Love's Field, Wells and Kavan Lead the New-Look Sooners

By Jordan Reeves | I Love Softball | October 20, 2026

Oklahoma softball fans have one more evening to see this roster before winter arrives. Game 4 of the Battle Series goes tomorrow night — Wednesday, October 21, at 6:30 PM CT at Love's Field in Norman. For a program that has made fall ball a showcase tradition, Game 4 might be the most anticipated preview yet of what 2027 could look like.

The Sooners return a roster headlined by two players who represent very different eras of the program's success. Jordy Kavan, a two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player, brings championship experience and elite offensive production that few college softball players have ever matched. Grace Wells, the 2026 NFCA Freshman of the Year, brought 39 home runs in her debut season — a freshman power display that drew comparisons to the program's all-time greats. Seeing them in the same lineup, together, in front of a sold-out Love's Field, is exactly what head coach Patty Gasso has been building toward.

What to Watch

Sydney Berzon in the circle is the story within the story for Game 4. The left-hander has been one of the Battle Series' most watched pitchers — a strikeout arm with the command to match. How Berzon looks in live competition against a range of opposing hitters gives a preview of the depth Gasso will have at her disposal come Big 12 play.

The Battle Series format pits Sooner against Sooner, which in a season with this roster means any given at-bat features All-Americans on both sides of the pitch. Cruz, Ludlow, and others in the lineup give the competition an intensity that mirrors the regular season atmosphere. Gasso has said publicly that these fall contests challenge her players in ways that intrasquad scrimmages simply cannot replicate.

Gasso's Emotional Fall

Coach Patty Gasso was visibly moved by the crowd that turned out for the Battle Series opener — a sold-out Love's Field that reflected the program's continued hold on its fanbase even after roster turnover. That kind of support, she noted, creates an obligation to deliver. Game 4 is the penultimate chance to repay it before spring. Game 5 follows October 23 against USAO.

What's Next

Game 4 is tomorrow at 6:30 PM CT, streaming live on 1oklahoma.com (free for 1Oklahoma members; $10 pass for non-members). Game 5 is Saturday, October 23, against the University of Science and Arts of Oklahoma. After that, the Sooners head into their winter preparation period — and the pressure of expectations that come with a roster this talented begins in earnest.

Tomorrow night at Love's Field is more than a fall intrasquad. It's a preview of what Oklahoma believes it can become. Don't miss it.
