Recruiting Blackout Day 2: What Programs Do When the Phones Go Silent

By Emily Rawlings | November 26, 2026 | I Love Softball

No calls. No texts. No emails. No contact of any kind.

Day 2 of the NCAA Division I softball Thanksgiving recruiting blackout is underway. The five-day shutdown — running November 25 through November 29 — prohibits coaches from reaching out to any recruit from any class year. It is one of the most absolute restrictions in the NCAA recruiting calendar, and it arrives this year at one of the most consequential moments of the offseason.

Five days from now, the phones turn back on. Six days from now, the December transfer portal opens. Seven days from now, National Signing Day begins.

What the Blackout Prohibits

The Thanksgiving shutdown is a complete communications blackout. Coaches cannot make calls, send texts, send emails, or initiate any electronic contact with any prospect — high school, junior college, or transfer portal — during the November 25-29 window. The prohibition applies to entire coaching staffs, not just head coaches.

What coaches can still do: watch film, hold internal team meetings, evaluate existing roster players, and conduct administrative and recruiting office work that does not involve direct contact with prospects. It is a time for preparation, not action.

Contact officially resumes November 30, the day before the portal opens.

The Loaded Timing

Blackouts are routine. This one's timing is not.

Several programs — including Arizona, Ole Miss, and Auburn — are urgently hunting pitching talent for the December portal window. Oklahoma is hoping to add a frontline pitcher before its No. 1 class is finalized. These programs have exactly five days of silence before they can begin those conversations, and then the December 8-12 dead period follows just one week after the portal opens, compressing the effective recruiting window to roughly seven days.

High school coaches and prospects cannot be contacted during the blackout either, which matters for programs trying to finalize their HS classes before December 2. Any verbal commitment that has not yet been made official must stay unofficial until November 30 at the earliest.

The silence, in other words, is not wasted. It is loaded with intention.

What Happens on November 30

The day contact resumes is one of the busiest single days in the entire recruiting calendar. Every coach who has been holding off on a call makes it. Every prospect who has been waiting for a final offer receives it. Every program that has been monitoring a portal target moves to open communication simultaneously.

Then, the next morning, the portal opens. And the morning after that, Signing Day begins.

Three consecutive days — November 30, December 1, December 2 — will cover more roster-shaping ground than the previous two weeks combined. The blackout does not delay the offseason. It compresses it.

What's Next

The Thanksgiving blackout ends November 29. Contact resumes November 30. The December transfer portal window opens December 1, running through December 15. National Signing Day for the Class of 2027 runs December 2-4. The dead period follows December 8-12. By December 15, the December portal window closes and roster pictures across Division I will be largely set heading into the holiday break.
