National Signing Day Is Coming: Class of 2027 HS Signings Begin December 2

By Sarah Mitchell | November 26, 2026 | I Love Softball

The silence is temporary. The signatures are permanent.

With the NCAA Division I softball Thanksgiving recruiting blackout holding every program in suspended animation through November 29, coaches across the country are doing something unusual: waiting. No phone calls. No texts. No contact of any kind with recruits. Just preparation.

That preparation has a specific target: December 2. That is when the fall National Signing Day window opens for the Class of 2027, giving programs their first official opportunity to accept National Letters of Intent from high school prospects they have spent years recruiting.

What National Signing Day Actually Is

National Signing Day is not a single moment — it is a window. The fall signing period for Division I softball runs December 2-4, 2026. Within those three days, any high school prospect who has verbally committed to a program can make it official by signing an NLI. The letter binds the prospect to the institution and the institution to a scholarship offer.

A second signing period follows on February 3, 2027, for prospects who do not sign in December. But for most programs, December is the target. Getting commitments on paper before the new year provides roster certainty heading into the spring season.

The Class of 2027 has been a heavily recruited cycle. Programs like Oklahoma, Texas, Tennessee, and Florida have made major investments in their HS pipelines, and the December 2 window will convert many of those verbal commitments into binding agreements.

Why This Signing Day Is Different

The timing of this year's fall signing period is unusual in one important way: it falls the day after the December transfer portal window opens (December 1).

That overlap creates a compressed, chaotic week for every coaching staff in Division I. On December 1, the portal opens and programs can begin contacting transfer prospects. On December 2, signing day begins and those same coaches are accepting NLIs from their high school classes. Through it all, they must manage scholarship accounting, official visit invitations, and recruiting visit logistics simultaneously.

The dead period that follows — December 8-12 — further compresses the effective working window. Programs that need to close portal recruits must do so in the first week of December before communication with any recruit is prohibited again.

For programs with unfinished recruiting business, this week is not just important. It is irreplaceable.

The Programs to Watch

Oklahoma enters Signing Day with the No. 1 overall class in the country, per D1Softball. The Sooners have committed position players across multiple positions, with the standout piece being No. 1 overall prospect Julianna "Goose" Hutchens, who is already enrolled in Norman. The question heading into Signing Day is whether OU can add a frontline pitcher through either the HS class or the portal.

Texas, the two-time defending national champion, has already filled its most pressing needs via the summer portal window. The Longhorns' December activity is expected to focus on completing their HS class after adding transfers Samantha Swan, Samantha Lincoln, and Isa Torres earlier this offseason.

Tennessee, Alabama, and Florida are also expected to have significant Signing Day activity as programs in both the SEC and the rest of the country finalize their class standings.

What's Next

The Thanksgiving blackout clears November 29, and contact with recruits resumes November 30. The December transfer portal window opens December 1. National Signing Day runs December 2-4. After the dead period (December 8-12), programs have until December 15 before the portal window closes. The final signing opportunity before spring practice is February 3, 2027. National preseason polls are expected to drop in January, at which point a fully assembled Class of 2027 will factor into program rankings for the first time.
