Arizona's Pitching Crisis: Five Days Before the Portal Opens and No Ace in Sight

By Jordan Reeves | November 26, 2026 | I Love Softball

When the December transfer portal opens on December 1, no program will have more urgency than Arizona.

The Wildcats enter the December window with a rotation hole that cannot be patched with depth pieces. They need an ace — a frontline starter capable of carrying them through a Pac-12 schedule and into the postseason. And they have been without one since June, when their biggest star entered the portal and never looked back.

The Loss That Created the Crisis

Mariah Torres was the best pitcher in college softball in 2026. The D1Softball Player of the Year, Torres led Arizona through the season before entering the transfer portal at its close and committing to Texas Tech. Her departure left a rotation-sized hole that the Wildcats have been unable to fill in the months since.

Torres was not just a rotation piece. She was the rotation. Building around a talent of that magnitude and then losing her in a single offseason portal decision is the kind of set-back programs spend years recovering from — if they respond correctly.

Arizona's response begins December 1.

The Competition Is Fierce

Arizona is not the only program hunting a pitching ace in the December portal. Ole Miss and Auburn have both been identified as programs looking for high-caliber arms in the December window. With the portal limited to a 15-day span that is further disrupted by a December 8-12 dead period, the effective recruiting window is roughly seven days — December 1 through December 7.

That is seven days for multiple high-profile programs to identify, contact, and secure commitments from a small pool of elite transfer pitchers. The bidding will be competitive. The scholarship packages will be significant. The school that makes the right pitch — no pun intended — first may have the edge.

What Arizona Needs

The Wildcats need a starter with proven, high-level experience. A strikeout arm. Someone who has pitched in meaningful games and performed when it mattered. The December portal typically includes players who either did not receive early release from their commitments in June or who took additional time to evaluate their options.

Arizona's ability to offer a prominent stage — a Power Conference program with a strong history — is an asset. The Wildcats can make a compelling case to the right pitcher. But they must make it quickly once December 1 arrives, before Ole Miss or Auburn gets there first.

The Thanksgiving blackout adds one more layer of urgency: Arizona cannot even have preliminary conversations with transfer targets until November 30. That is one day before everything officially opens.

What's Next

The recruiting blackout clears November 29, with contact resuming November 30. Arizona's coaching staff is expected to move aggressively in the portal from the moment it opens December 1. The dead period (December 8-12) means any portal commitments not locked in during the first week of December will need to wait until December 13 to finalize. The portal window closes December 15. If Arizona does not secure a frontline pitcher in this window, their next opportunity is the spring portal, which opens after the final weekend of the regular season in 2027.
