### STORY 1: National Signing Day Dec 2-4

#### Long-Form Post

One week from today, the first official signing period for the Class of 2027 begins.

National Signing Day runs December 2-4 — just four days after the NCAA's Thanksgiving recruiting blackout ends on November 29. It is the first time coaches will be able to officially accept National Letters of Intent from high school prospects in the 2027 class.

The timing this year is especially significant. The December transfer portal window opens December 1 — one day before HS signings begin. The dead period follows December 8-12. Programs have a compressed, overlapping window to simultaneously finalize their HS recruiting classes, work the portal, and manage official visit invitations.

Coaches are using this week's mandated silence to organize paperwork, finalize scholarship offers, and prepare for the sprint that starts Sunday.

The Class of 2027 rankings will shift significantly once these commitments become official. Who do you think makes the biggest splash on signing day?

### STORY 2: Recruiting Blackout Day 2

#### Long-Form Post

Day 2 of the NCAA Division I softball Thanksgiving recruiting blackout is underway.

From November 25-29, coaches at every Division I program in the country are prohibited from making any contact with recruits — no phone calls, no texts, no emails, no DMs. The rule applies to any recruit from any class year, not just the 2027 class currently being signed.

It is the same blackout that happens every Thanksgiving. But the timing this year hits differently. With the December transfer portal just five days away, the December 2-4 National Signing Day one week out, and programs still hunting pitching talent, the five-day freeze comes at the worst possible moment for programs with unfinished business.

Contact resumes November 30. The sprint begins the next day.

### STORY 3: Arizona Pitching Hole

#### Long-Form Post

Arizona softball faces the most urgent pitching crisis in the country heading into December.

In June, the Wildcats lost Mariah Torres — the D1Softball Player of the Year — when she entered the transfer portal and landed at Texas Tech. It was the program's single biggest roster loss in the offseason. Arizona does not have a proven ace to replace her.

The December transfer portal window opens December 1. The Wildcats have until December 7 (before the December 8-12 dead period) to identify and secure a replacement. Ole Miss and Auburn are also searching for high-end portal pitching, which means Arizona will face direct competition for every top arm available.

Five days. One portal window. One critical need.

No program is watching the Dec 1 countdown more closely than Arizona.

### STORY 4: Oklahoma Signing Day

#### Long-Form Post

Oklahoma enters National Signing Day (December 2-4) with the No. 1 recruiting class in the country.

Julianna "Goose" Hutchens — the No. 1 overall prospect in the 2027 class — is already enrolled in Norman. Finlee Williams (top outfielder), Riley Hilliard (No. 2 catcher), and Emma Reynolds (4-star shortstop) are all committed. By most measures, the Sooners are having the best recruiting year of any program in the sport.

There is one problem: no pitching ace.

Oklahoma hosted pitcher prospects Vivian Knott and Aspen Boulware before the Thanksgiving blackout started. Whether either commits in the December 2-4 signing window — or whether the Sooners find their ace in the December portal (also opening Dec 1) — is the top storyline of the week.

A No. 1 class without a frontline pitcher leaves a question mark heading into the SEC. The next 11 days should answer it.

### STORY 5: Perfect Storm Timeline

#### Long-Form Post

The most compressed, consequential stretch of the college softball offseason starts in three days.

Here is the timeline:

📅 November 29: Thanksgiving blackout ends. Contact resumes.
📅 December 1: December transfer portal window opens (15-day window, closes Dec 15).
📅 December 2-4: National Signing Day — coaches accept NLIs from HS Class of 2027.
📅 December 8-12: Dead period — no contact with any recruit. Portal still open.
📅 December 15: Portal window closes.

That is 11 days where everything happens at once. Programs will be on the phone with portal transfers one hour and attending high school official visits the next. Scholarship logistics, NLI paperwork, and recruiting calls all overlap.

For programs with positions of need — Arizona needing a pitcher, Oklahoma chasing an ace, Ole Miss and Auburn in pursuit — these 11 days are season-defining.

The chaos starts Sunday. The roster that comes out the other side will largely determine who contends in June.
