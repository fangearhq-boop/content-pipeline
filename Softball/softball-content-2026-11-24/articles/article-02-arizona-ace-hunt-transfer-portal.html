Arizona's Ace Hunt: Seven Days Until the Portal Changes Everything

By Emily Rawlings

November 24, 2026

Arizona went 15-2 in fall ball. They return Big 12 Defensive Player of the Year Regan Shockey. Coach Caitlin Lowe has the program positioned for a genuine title run.

They just don't have an ace.

When Arizona's pitcher — the D1Softball National Player of the Year — left for Texas Tech in the offseason, the Wildcats didn't just lose an arm. They lost a generational talent who made everything around her look better. And in a postseason format where you face the country's best lineups in elimination games, committee pitching has a ceiling.

Seven days from now, the transfer portal opens. Arizona's December is the most important month in the program's recent history.

The Prize

Oklahoma State's Saylor Davis is expected to enter the portal on December 1 — day one — with three full years of eligibility remaining. Davis is the name at the top of every pitching-needy program's December list. She is a left-handed pitcher with an elite profile, and three years of eligibility means a program that lands her isn't just buying one postseason run. They're buying a foundation.

Arizona is not the only program with the facilities, the competitive timeline, and the reputation to recruit her. Ole Miss, Auburn, and at least ten other programs are searching for elite pitching via the December portal. The demand dramatically outpaces the supply.

The Window Is Narrow

The dead period begins December 9 and runs through December 12. That means portal entrants who make decisions before the dead period give themselves — and programs — the most clarity. For Arizona, this translates to a roughly eight-day sprint: December 1 through December 8.

Programs that built relationships before today's recruiting shutdown — when coaches could still call, text, and email freely — have a structural advantage. The teams that were in contact with Davis and other portal prospects through November are the ones most likely to close in December.

Arizona's coaching staff has had all fall to build those relationships. What happens in the next eight days will reveal whether that preparation translates into a commitment.

What Arizona Can Offer

The pitch Arizona can make is compelling. The program is legitimate. The Big 12 opens March 12 and will be one of the country's most competitive conferences. Shockey and the returning roster give a portal pitcher a defense behind her and a lineup in front of her that can score runs.

What Arizona needs in return is an arm that can go deep into games against elite competition — the kind of pitcher who gets the ball in an elimination game and doesn't give it back. Texas Tech has Kasidi Pickering. Oklahoma has Jordy Bearce behind Caitlin Pryor. Arizona has the roster to win a title. It needs the pitcher to prove it.

What's Next

The transfer portal opens December 1. Expect a wave of pitchers to enter on day one, with Saylor Davis among the most closely watched. Programs have eight days before the December 9-12 dead period. Arizona's December will define whether Caitlin Lowe's first full roster can compete for a national title in 2027. Watch for early portal movement announcements before December 9.
