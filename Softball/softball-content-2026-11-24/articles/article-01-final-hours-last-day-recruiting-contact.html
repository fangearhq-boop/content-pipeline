Final Hours: Today Is the Last Day for Unrestricted Recruiting Contact

By Sarah Mitchell

November 24, 2026

Midnight tonight, the phones go silent.

Monday, November 24 is the final day Division I softball coaches may call, text, or email any recruit without restriction. Starting tomorrow morning — Tuesday, November 25 — the NCAA's five-day recruiting shutdown begins, and with it comes a complete communication blackout between coaches and prospective student-athletes.

For five days, no calls. No texts. No emails. No social media DMs. Nothing.

When the phones finally come back on, the calendar doesn't get any easier. The transfer portal opens December 1 — just seven days away. Then, barely a week after that, the dead period runs December 9 through 12, restricting in-person recruiting visits. The offseason window to build relationships with the class of 2027 is narrowing by the hour.

The Last Rush

Across the country, coaches are spending Monday the way they always spend the last free day before a shutdown — making every contact count. That means prospect calls that have been on the schedule for weeks, urgent texts to players who are still weighing their options, and final in-home visits squeezed into a window that closes at midnight.

Programs that have been in contact with top targets all fall are using today to push those conversations toward commitments. And historically, the final day before a recruiting shutdown produces a flurry of announcement posts on social media as recruits who were ready to decide simply needed one final push.

Watch for commitment announcements throughout the day. When coaches have one last chance to close, the ones on the bubble tend to decide.

What Comes After

The shutdown-to-portal-to-dead-period sequence is unique in its compression. In past years, teams had longer stretches of unrestricted contact that gave coaches time to pursue multiple prospects in sequence. This year's calendar stacks three distinct communication barriers back-to-back-to-back.

The five-day shutdown ends November 30, giving coaches exactly one day to re-engage before the portal formally opens December 1. Then the sprint begins: programs have eight days — December 1 through December 8 — before the dead period restricts activity again.

Everything that happens in those eight days will be shaped by what coaches accomplished in the weeks and months before the shutdown began. The pipeline work — relationships built in September, October, and early November — is what determines who wins the December portal.

The Class of 2027 Is Not Done Yet

National Signing Day on November 11 locked in the initial class of 2027 recruits. But the class is far from complete. High school seniors who chose not to sign early, junior college transfers, and players currently in portal consideration can still commit in the coming weeks.

Programs that hold No. 1 national classes — like Oklahoma — are largely locked in. But mid-tier programs that need one or two more pieces to push their rosters to the next level are using today as a critical last-chance contact moment.

The class of 2027 will ultimately be defined less by what happened on November 11 and more by what happens in December. Today is the last day to set up those December conversations.

What's Next

The five-day recruiting shutdown runs Tuesday, November 25 through Saturday, November 29. Coaches may resume contact on Sunday, November 30. The transfer portal opens Monday, December 1 — watch for a wave of entries from players across the country, including Oklahoma State's Saylor Davis, who is expected to enter on day one with three years of eligibility remaining. The dead period runs December 9 through 12. January brings the first preseason polls of the 2027 season.
