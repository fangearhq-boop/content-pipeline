Medal games today at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. 🇺🇸

### STORY 5: Asian Games Softball — 54 Days Out

**FB Long-Form — Post Time: 5:30 PM ET**

🥎 **2026 ASIAN GAMES SOFTBALL: 54 DAYS OUT. HERE'S WHAT TO WATCH.**

Women's softball at the 2026 Asian Games starts September 26 at Anjō Sports Park Softball Ground in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan. Eight nations compete over eight days. Here's what to understand before the first pitch is thrown.

**The Host and the Favorite:**
Japan enters as host nation and WBSC World No. 1. Home-field advantage in Aichi-Nagoya — their fans, their park, their environment. Japan is the overwhelming favorite and has been the dominant force in Asian softball for decades.

**The Competition:**
The eight-team field: Japan, China, Chinese Taipei, Philippines, South Korea, Hong Kong, Singapore, and Thailand. China and Chinese Taipei have the longest history of competing near Japan's level. Both are legitimate contenders for a podium finish. The Philippines and South Korea held a **joint training camp in Seoul** ahead of this tournament — two nations cooperating on preparation for the same event, a sign that both programs are investing seriously in this competition.

**Why It Matters Beyond the Medal:**
The 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage runs on a qualification system, and Asian Games placement directly affects which Asian nations earn automatic Group Stage entries and which ones have to fight through regional qualification rounds. For the Philippines, South Korea, and Chinese Taipei, the difference between a top-three and a fourth-place finish could mean the difference between direct qualification and an extra qualifying hurdle.

54 days out. The teams are already preparing. The qualification stakes are already real. Mark your calendar for September 26.
