PGF Nationals Week 2 Champions Are Crowned. The September 1 Clock Is Running.

By Jordan Reeves

August 3, 2026

The 2026 PGF Nationals Week 2 championships are finished. Three Premier division title games played out Sunday at Deanna Manning Stadium at Bill Barber Park in Irvine, California, and the 14U, 12U, and 10U champions have been crowned. What comes next for every player who competed this week matters just as much as what happened on the field.

What Week 2 Represents in the Recruiting Calendar

PGF Nationals is not just a tournament — it's the most important recruiting showcase of the summer, and Week 2 is no different. The 14U, 12U, and 10U Premier divisions draw college scouts from programs at every level, and every at-bat, every inning pitched, every defensive play on this field gets evaluated with recruiting implications in mind.

The reason the timing matters is what follows: September 1 is 29 days away. That's the date when Division I college coaches are permitted to begin calling Class of 2028 prospects directly for the first time. PGF Nationals Week 2 is the last major showcase event before those calls start. Players who turned in strong performances this week will be getting those calls. Players who didn't will have the fall season to rebuild their profile before the evaluation period intensifies again.

Week 2 Versus Week 1

PGF Nationals runs in two separate weeks at Deanna Manning Stadium, and the divisions are staggered across them. Week 1, which concluded earlier in the month, covered the 18U and 16U Premier divisions — the age groups where recruiting conversations are already active and transfer portal decisions can follow. Week 2 brought in the younger Premier divisions, where the recruiting horizon is longer but the talent is unmistakably high-level.

Week 2 crowning 14U, 12U, and 10U champions matters because the players who win titles at this age group are the names college coaches will be tracking for the next two, four, or six years. Winning at PGF Nationals at 12U doesn't get you a scholarship offer today — but it puts your name in a coach's notebook, and that notebook gets reviewed every recruiting cycle through your junior and senior seasons.

What Comes Next

The PGF Nationals season wraps with Week 2's conclusion, but the softball calendar doesn't slow down. For players who competed in Irvine this week, the next steps are fall league and fall practice schedules — the period when club programs transition from showcase mode into development mode, and when individual players work on the skills that didn't show up the way they wanted to at nationals.

For coaches watching the recruiting pipeline, the September 1 opening of the calling window is the next major marker. The players who had strong weeks at PGF Nationals — Week 1 and Week 2 both — are the players whose phones will start ringing in a month. Three divisions got their champions Sunday in Irvine. The recruiting race for those champions starts now.
