Fall Ball Roundup: FSU's Unmatched Pitching Return, Penn State Opens Round Robin, UAB Begins a New Era

By Emily Rawlings | October 5, 2026 | I Love Softball

While the attention this fall has been on the sport's biggest programs in the SEC and Big 12, there is meaningful news being written outside those conferences. Florida State is the only program in America returning two All-American pitchers and is dealing with early schedule disruptions. Penn State launched its fall slate with a multi-team round robin. UAB is beginning again under a new head coach. The sport is active everywhere.

Florida State: Two All-American Pitchers, One Postponed Game

Florida State softball enters 2027 with a pitching advantage no other program in the country can claim. Ashtyn Danley and Jazzy Francik are both All-Americans returning to the Seminoles' staff — the only program in the nation to have two returning All-American pitchers heading into next spring.

The fall started on October 2 with a home game against Chipola College. A day later, the program announced that Sunday's scheduled game against Florida A&M had been postponed with no rescheduled date announced. The reason was not specified.

FSU's remaining fall schedule: Troy on October 10, Gulf Coast State College on October 17, Auburn on October 18, and two games against LSU in Orange Beach, Alabama on October 24-25. The final fall date is at Florida on November 13.

The Seminoles will be a national contender in 2027. The pitching alone guarantees attention.

Penn State: Round-Robin Format Kicks Off Big Ten Fall Ball

Penn State opened its fall softball slate this past weekend with a multi-team round robin at home. The Nittany Lions hosted Bucknell on Saturday morning and Seton Hill on Saturday afternoon, then played East Stroudsburg on Sunday. A midweek game against Lock Haven follows on October 7.

The Big Ten's fall ball is underway across the conference, with programs using these weeks to evaluate rosters, develop young talent, and work new transfers into systems. Penn State's round-robin format maximizes game repetitions while keeping individual opponent preparation light — a practical choice for a fall with multiple evaluation priorities.

UAB: Megan Curry and a Clean Slate

In Birmingham, UAB softball is starting over with a new voice. Megan Curry was named the seventh head coach in program history on July 27, 2026, bringing a 61-win record from Samford — where she guided the Bulldogs to a SoCon Championship game appearance — and a reputation as a rising program-builder.

Curry assembled a new staff immediately: Hunter Veach as associate head coach, Ryan Greenwood as assistant coach, and Kiley Goff as assistant coach. The program's fall slate opens October 16 when Lurleen B. Wallace Community College visits Mary Bowers Field for a 6 PM first pitch — the first look at the Curry era in action.

UAB's path is one of building. The program spent part of the summer under scrutiny before Curry arrived to establish direction. Fall 2026 is the first chapter of whatever comes next for the Blazers.

What's Next

LSU's fall ball opens October 15 against Northwestern State at Tiger Park — the SEC's latest entrant to the fall schedule. The Big Ten, ACC, and Conference USA are all active. The national fall calendar is full.

For every program profiled here, these weeks matter. The 2027 season is being assembled now, through these games and these evaluations and these decisions made quietly in October while everyone else watches football. That's how programs build toward May.
