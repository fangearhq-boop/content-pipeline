Tennessee Softball Is 3-0 in Fall Ball and Already Signing the 2028 Class

By Emily Rawlings | October 5, 2026 | I Love Softball

Tennessee softball does not believe in waiting. While most programs are still figuring out their 2027 fall rosters, the Lady Vols went 3-0 in fall ball on Thursday with a dominant showing against Carson-Newman — and have already secured five commitments to the 2028 recruiting class, including the No. 4-ranked prospect in the country.

The program that has reached the Women's College World Series semifinals in four of the past five years is building its next two teams simultaneously, and fall ball is where that work gets done.

Mardjetko and Nuwer Carry Their 2026 Form Into October

The biggest reason Tennessee's fall 2026 outlook is so promising is the same reason it was promising in the spring: Paige Mardjetko and Mikayla Nuwer. Mardjetko went 16-3 with a 1.26 ERA and 171 strikeouts in the 2026 regular and postseason. Nuwer was 15-1 with a 1.01 ERA. Those numbers represent arguably the most dominant ace tandem in the sport last season.

Both pitchers are back, still developing, still pushing each other in practice, and in possession of their numbers from a spring in which Tennessee came within a handful of games of a national title.

The 2028 Class Is Taking Shape

Off the field, Tennessee announced five commitments to the 2028 recruiting class since September. The headliner is Nevaeh Wilson, a shortstop and utility player from Eastvale, California who is ranked No. 32 overall in the 2028 class by Softball America and recently earned a roster spot on the U18 U.S. Women's National Team. The class also includes the No. 4 overall player in the country.

Recruiting two years in advance at this level reflects the program's current standing nationally. When Tennessee calls, players answer. The 2028 commitments signal that the program's pipeline is healthy at every level.

This Weekend's Fall Schedule

Tennessee heads into a busy fall weekend at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium. Northeast Alabama visits for a 6 PM Friday contest on October 9, followed by Northwest Florida State College on Saturday and Roane State on Sunday. All three games are free admission.

For Tennessee fans in the area, this weekend provides a rare extended look at a team building toward a 2027 title run. The pitching will be worth the trip.

What's Next

Tennessee's fall ball slate continues through late October, with the coaching staff using each game to evaluate its 2027 roster in competition settings. The 2028 commitments will continue to roll in as the program holds official visits and deepens its recruiting calendar heading into the winter.

The Lady Vols are 3-0. The 2028 class is 5-0. Rocky Top is doing just fine.
