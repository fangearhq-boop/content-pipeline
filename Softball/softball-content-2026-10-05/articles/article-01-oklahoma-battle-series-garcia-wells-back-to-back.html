Back-to-Back: Garcia and Wells Highlight Sold-Out Oklahoma Battle Series Opener

By Sarah Mitchell | October 5, 2026 | I Love Softball

The 2026 Oklahoma softball fall didn't officially have a scoreboard on Saturday, but it didn't need one. Two home runs hit back to back by Gabbie Garcia and Kendall Wells in the seventh inning of the Battle Series opening scrimmage told every fan inside a sold-out Love's Field everything they needed to know about this team's ceiling.

The crowd numbered 2,351 — a sold-out house for a non-competitive fall exhibition — and by the end of the nine-inning workout, head coach Patty Gasso was in tears. The response from the fanbase, just months removed from a spring that ended earlier than expected, moved her.

What the Battle Series Format Revealed

The Battle Series scrimmage uses a structured format: six pitchers rotating through three-inning stints, no official score kept, nine innings total. It is less a competition than a demonstration — a chance for coaches to evaluate depth and for fans to see the roster up close.

What Saturday demonstrated was that Oklahoma's offense has not lost a step. Four home runs total in nine innings of fall ball against a mix of pitching represents the kind of production that confirms rather than creates expectations. Wells — who set the NCAA single-season home run record with 39 in the 2026 spring season — did not miss. Garcia followed her over the fence on the very next at-bat.

Back to back. The crowd responded accordingly.

Gasso's Emotional Moment

Patty Gasso has seen many fall ball crowds at Love's Field over her tenure in Norman, but the reception for this group caught her off guard. A sold-out crowd showing up to a non-scored, late-afternoon fall scrimmage reflects the hunger that exists in Oklahoma softball right now.

The 2026 season ended at the hands of Texas. The transfer portal brought Sydney Berzon from LSU. The Battle Series opener showed the pieces assembling around that spine — and the fans, apparently, noticed.

What's Next

Battle Series Game 2 is Tuesday, October 7 at 6:30 PM CT at Love's Field. Tickets are $10.88 general admission. The schedule continues with games on October 14 and October 21 at the same venue.

For those who missed Saturday's sold-out opener, Tuesday provides another chance to see this roster take shape. Based on what Garcia, Wells, and the rest of the group showed in Game 1, there will be more moments worth seeing before this fall is over.
