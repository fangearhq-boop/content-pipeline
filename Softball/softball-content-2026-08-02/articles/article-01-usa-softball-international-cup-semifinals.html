USA Came Back From 5-0. Both Squads Are Unbeaten. Semifinals Are Today.

By Sarah Mitchell

August 2, 2026

Pool play is over at the 2026 USA Softball International Cup in Oklahoma City, and the picture couldn't be clearer heading into Sunday's semifinals: both Team USA squads — the Women's National Team and the Women's Elite Team — are unbeaten. What they did to get there is worth examining, because the path was not always clean.

The WNT Comeback Nobody Expected

It would have been easy to write off Team USA Women's National Team after the first inning of their Day 2 matchup against No. 4 Canada. Down 5-0 before the WNT had even settled in, the deficit felt steep — especially against a Canadian squad that had shown throughout this tournament that they could compete at the highest level.

They didn't write it off. Inning by inning, the WNT chipped away. The offense that had rolled Australia 12-0 in four innings on Day 1 rediscovered its edge, and the team that came out of that first inning in crisis eventually won the game 10-7. Final: USA WNT 10, Canada 7.

The result matters beyond the final score. Canada beat the USA in the 2024 Paris Olympics gold medal game — a loss that has been a motivating force for this program ever since. Sunday night in Oklahoma City was not Paris, and this was pool play, not a gold medal game. But finishing a 10-7 comeback win against the same opponent on a night when it mattered for seeding? That is a statement.

Zerkle Puts the Elite Team Away

While the WNT was staging its comeback, the USA Women's Elite Team was handling business in efficient fashion. The Elite Team faced American Samoa in Day 2 action and turned in a five-inning run-rule: USA Elite 10, American Samoa 3.

The story of that game was Morgan Zerkle. She hit a three-run home run to break the game open, then added a walk-off triple in the same contest — finishing with five RBI in a dominant performance. When a player puts together a three-run shot and a walk-off triple in the same game, she's doing everything asked of a leadoff and lineup anchor. Zerkle delivered exactly that.

Combined with the Day 1 win over No. 5 Chinese Taipei (6-4), the Elite Team finished pool play 2-0 and advances to the semifinals.

What the Semifinals Look Like

Both USA squads now join Japan — which also finished pool play unbeaten — in the playoff round. The semifinal round takes place today, August 2, at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. Medal games are scheduled for Monday, August 3.

The tournament format sends the top two teams from each group into the playoffs. Group A (Elite, Japan, Chinese Taipei, American Samoa) and Group B (WNT, Canada, China, Australia) have produced their four semifinalists. Exactly who advances from each group and which semifinal matchup features the USA teams will determine the bracket shape — but with both USA squads unbeaten, the USA has the best possible position going into the final two days.

Japan, ranked No. 1 in the world, remains the clearest test for either USA team that makes a final. But the WNT proved on Saturday night that they can respond to adversity. Down 5-0 and still winning. That is not something a team does by accident.

What's Next

Semifinals are today, Sunday, August 2 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. Medal games — gold and bronze — are scheduled for Monday, August 3. 2027 Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage qualification points are distributed based on final tournament placement, making Tuesday's results consequential beyond the trophy. Both USA squads have earned the right to compete for gold. The work isn't done — but after pool play, they're in the best possible position to finish the job.
