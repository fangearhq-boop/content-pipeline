PGF Nationals Week 2 Championship Day: Titles on the Line in Irvine

By Jordan Reeves

August 2, 2026

Championship day has arrived at PGF Nationals Week 2. After a week of bracket play across Huntington Beach and Irvine, the 14U, 12U, and 10U Premier division champions will be crowned today at Deanna Manning Stadium, Bill Barber Park in Irvine, California.

All championship games stream live on PGF.TV. And while the scoreboard will settle who takes home the title, the implications of today's games extend well past the trophy presentation.

What PGF Nationals Represents

PGF Nationals is the preeminent national tournament for youth softball in the United States. For the Premier division — the top competitive level — it draws the best programs from across the country and serves as a proving ground for players who will go on to compete at the college level.

Week 2 covers the younger age groups: 14U, 12U, and 10U. These are players ranging from the early stages of their recruiting exposure down to players just beginning to develop their games in competitive travel ball environments. All of it matters — just for different reasons at different stages.

The 14U level is where recruiting visibility starts to crystallize for the best players. Programs at this age group are building the class of 2028 and 2029 recruiting lists that college coaches will spend the next two to three years cultivating. The championship game today is the most prominent showcase of the week.

The September 1 Clock

Every high school softball player in the country — and every parent paying attention to recruiting — knows the date: September 1. That is the day when college coaches can begin making direct phone calls to prospects from the Class of 2028, the current crop of 14U and incoming 15U players.

From today, that window is 30 days away. For players performing in the 14U Premier championship game this afternoon in Irvine, today is essentially the last major showcase before the phone lines open. College coaches who have been tracking these players at the PGF Nationals circuit — who attended bracket games this week, who saw semifinal performances last night — will be making their priority contact lists for September 1 based in large part on what they witnessed this week.

That context makes today's championship game more than just a tournament finale. It is, for many of the players competing, a final audition.

PGF Week 1 Already in the Books

Week 1 of PGF Nationals — which covered the 18U and 16U divisions — wrapped on July 27 with order-of-finish results published by Line Drive Media the same day. The older age groups saw their champions crowned and recruiting conversations accelerate immediately after results posted.

Week 2 follows the same trajectory. By tonight, the 14U, 12U, and 10U champions will be official. By tomorrow, the recruiting community will have processed the results and begun the final run to September 1 with updated target lists.
