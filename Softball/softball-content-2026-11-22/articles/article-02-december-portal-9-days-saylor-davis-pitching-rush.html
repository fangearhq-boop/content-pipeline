9 Days to December Portal: Saylor Davis and the Pitching Rush That Could Reshape 2027

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | November 22, 2026

The December transfer portal opens Dec. 1, and the first named pitcher of this window is already on the runway. Oklahoma State's Saylor Davis has signaled she will enter the portal on opening day, bringing three years of eligibility to a market that is already the most competitive in college softball's history.

Davis is the preview of what's coming. She is one of several arms expected to enter the December window, and programs like Arizona, Oklahoma, Ole Miss, and Auburn are preparing to move fast. The window is only 15 days, but the effective negotiating period is even shorter — a four-day dead period runs Dec. 9 through Dec. 12, leaving teams just eight days at the front of the window to find, contact, and close on the pitchers they need.

The Buyers and What They Need

Arizona is the most urgent case. The Wildcats went 15-2 in the fall under first-year head coach Caitlin Lowe, with Regan Shockey (Big 12 Defensive Player of the Year) anchoring the infield and 10 letterwinners returning. But the program lost Isa Torres — D1Softball's National Player of the Year — to Texas Tech in the spring portal. Without a true frontline starter, Arizona's ceiling is limited regardless of how well the offense performs. The December portal is the only remaining fix before the 2027 season begins.

Oklahoma enters the window from a position of relative strength but still wants to add. Patty Gasso already brought in Harter from MTSU and Hansen from CSI. With Jordy Eckholt Wells (39 home runs, NFCA Freshman of the Year in 2026) and Tiare Kavan (two-time WCWS MVP) returning, plus Goose Hutchens already enrolled as the No. 1 player in the 2027 class, the Sooners have the roster to win a national title. But Gasso has shown repeatedly that she wants elite pitching depth, and December offers the chance to add it.

Ole Miss and Auburn are both in the market. Auburn has the No. 4 Softball America offensive class — a legitimate pitching addition behind Bobby Pierce's group could transform the Tigers from a Sweet 16 program into a WCWS contender. Ole Miss has similar needs: a proven SEC-caliber arm that makes the Rebels more than a road win for the elite programs.

The Eight-Day Window

The dead period creates an unusual dynamic in this window. Teams that reach the Dec. 9 deadline without a commitment face a four-day communication blackout before the portal closes on Dec. 15. That means the first eight days — Dec. 1 through Dec. 8 — are when the real market happens. Any team that waits past Dec. 8 to make its move is gambling on being able to close a deal in the final days.

Programs with a clear need and a clear budget will move on Day 1. The teams that hesitate in the name of due diligence will often find the best pitchers already committed to faster-moving rivals.

What's Next

Davis's availability is the clearest early signal about what this window holds, but the full picture won't emerge until Dec. 1. Teams are already running their internal boards, identifying their targets, and preparing their pitches. For Arizona especially, everything rides on what happens in the next nine days. The Wildcats have the fall results and the coaching staff to compete nationally — they just need the arm to prove it.
