NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post The de-commit wave that hit Memphis right after Signing Day may just be the beginning. 😔 Memphis pulled scholarship offers from three 2027 commits — Cassidy Ott, Kinley Patten, and Isabell Gonzales — the day after the November signing period opened. Cassidy Ott described herself as "involuntarily de-committed due to the new 5-year eligibility rule." Her team simply didn't have a roster spot. Here's the squeeze: The 5-for-5 rule collides head-on with the House v. NCAA settlement's 25-player roster cap. If a program is holding 5-for-5 returners AND signed a full class, commits get cut. Kentucky head coach Rachel Lawson warned months ago there would be "casualties." Memphis likely isn't alone. Three former Arizona players — Sydney Stewart, Jalen Adams, and Grace Jenkins — reportedly want to come back under 5-for-5. But there's a catch: while ~200 court cases continue, athletes who return can be rostered but cannot practice or play in games. Recruits who worked for years to earn offers are caught in the middle of a legal battle they had no part in creating. ### Image Post Caption "Involuntarily de-committed." Two words no recruit should ever have to use. The 5-for-5 roster squeeze is here — and Memphis won't be the last. 😔 #CollegeSoftball
