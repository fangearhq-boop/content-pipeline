Oklahoma Claims No. 1 Spot in D1Softball 2027 Signing Class Rankings — But Softball America Sees It Differently

By Emily Rawlings | November 14, 2026 | I Love Softball

D1Softball released its 2027 signing class rankings this week — and the Sooners are sitting at No. 1. But Softball America's list tells a different story, with LSU at the top for the first time in program history. The divergence between the two most-read ranking services has sparked the first real debate of the 2027 season.

D1Softball's top 10 reads: Oklahoma, LSU, Oregon, Auburn, Stanford, Florida, UCLA, Arizona State, Tennessee, and Texas A&M. The service weights its rankings heavily on in-person evaluation and projected roster impact — a methodology that rewards depth and returning talent alongside incoming classes.

Oklahoma checks every box in that framework. The Sooners return NFCA Freshman of the Year Kendall Wells, who slugged 39 home runs in her debut season, and two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player Rylie Kavan. Add five-star outfielder Finlee Williams and five-star catcher Riley Hilliard to that core, and head coach Patty Gasso has constructed arguably the most complete roster in the country heading into the spring.

Softball America Sees LSU on Top

Softball America, which weights individual prospect star ratings more heavily, has LSU at No. 1 — a historic first for the Tigers program. Beth Torina's Signing Day haul included five-star pitcher Sydney Gonglick, five-star infielder Bryli Seifert, and portal additions Mackenzie and Madison Pickens (the Pickens twins out of Ole Miss and Louisville). With six SEC schools in Softball America's top 10, it was a dominant performance for the conference on the recruiting front.

"The SEC placed six programs in the top 10 by Softball America," read one recruiting roundup. "LSU headline the list with the first No. 1 class in program history."

The philosophical divide between the two services is nothing new, but it sharpens the conversation: Is Oklahoma the team to beat because of roster depth, or is LSU the team to beat because of incoming star power?

The Case for Oklahoma

At its core, D1Softball's argument for Oklahoma is a holistic one. Wells and Kavan are established forces in the lineup; Williams and Hilliard give Gasso four elite-level position players before spring training begins. The Sooners' 2026 season planted the seeds — and the class of 2027 harvests them.

One additional factor: Goose Hutchens, who was originally the top-ranked recruit in the 2027 class, reclassified to 2026 and is already enrolled at Oklahoma. That means the Sooners added the No. 1 player in this cohort a year early — a move that doesn't show up in 2027 class rankings but absolutely shows up in the roster.

What's Next

Preseason rankings from D1Softball, USA TODAY, ESPN, and others are expected to begin rolling out in January. At that point, all five preseason top-10 candidates — Oklahoma, LSU, Florida, Tennessee, and Texas Tech — will get a formal look. For now, the debate over who holds the No. 1 class is exactly the kind of conversation that makes the offseason worth watching.

The 2027 Women's College World Series is in Oklahoma City from June 1–9. Five months away. The race starts now.
