Arizona Closes Fall Ball with 15-2 Win Over UNLV — Caitlin Lowe's Debut Fall in Tucson Complete

By Sarah Mitchell | November 14, 2026 | I Love Softball

Caitlin Lowe's first fall as head coach at Arizona is officially done — and it ended exactly the way she would have drawn it up. The Wildcats closed their 2026 fall ball schedule Friday with a 15-2 win over UNLV at Hillenbrand Stadium in Tucson. The convincing result capped a fall campaign that served as proof of concept for a program in transition.

Lowe, the former Arizona outfielder and 2008 Olympic gold medalist who was hired to lead her alma mater's program, inherited a team that lost two of its most decorated players this offseason. Shortstop Isa Torres — D1Softball's 2026 National Player of the Year — and infielder Jaysoni Beachum (now at Texas Tech) transferred out before Lowe even coached her first game. The Wildcats had every reason to stumble. Instead, they finished fall with a statement performance.

What Lowe Is Working With

Arizona returns 10 letterwinners, giving Lowe a substantial foundation to build on. At the top of that list is Regan Shockey, the 2026 Big 12 Defensive Player of the Year. Shockey's presence at the top of the lineup and in the infield provides defensive stability — and a veteran leader — that every new coaching staff needs in its first year.

Reports from Arizona's fall notebook indicate that the offense showed encouraging signs, with players like Kez Lucas making an impression with power at the plate. For a team that lost a National Player of the Year, the ability to find offensive production from multiple sources will be critical in 2027.

Arizona also signed a top-15 class nationally per Softball America and ranks second in the Big 12 Conference in recruiting rankings — a significant vote of confidence in Lowe's program and her ability to recruit at the highest level in her opening weeks on the job.

The Big Picture for 2027

The Big 12 softball landscape is competitive at every level. Texas Tech arrives in 2027 with a loaded portal class headlined by Kasidi Pickering. Oklahoma returns two of the most decorated players in the sport. Arizona's path to a Big 12 title runs through both programs.

What Lowe's debut fall suggests is that Arizona won't be a pushover. A 15-2 final says something about the gap in talent between this Wildcats program and the opponents it closes fall against — but the gap between Arizona and the Big 12 elite is what spring practice and the 2027 season will measure.

What's Next

Arizona transitions to winter training and individual workouts before spring practice begins. The 2027 schedule has not yet been released. Arizona fans should watch for a preseason ranking in January and the first Big 12 schedule announcement in the coming weeks. Lowe's first real measuring stick game against conference opposition will come in March — and the softball world will be watching.
