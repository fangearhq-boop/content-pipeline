FSU Softball Closes Fall in Gainesville — A Resilient Debut Fall Without Torres and Beachum

By Emily Rawlings | November 14, 2026 | I Love Softball

Florida State closed out its 2026 fall ball schedule Thursday with a road game at Florida's Katie Seashole Pressly Stadium in Gainesville — a fitting end to a fall defined by resilience rather than results.

The Seminoles entered this fall in unfamiliar territory. Shortstop Isa Torres, D1Softball's 2026 National Player of the Year, transferred out of Tallahassee this offseason. So did infielder Jaysoni Beachum, now a Texas Tech Red Raider. Two cornerstones, two empty roster spots, one program left to answer the question of what it's built on when the headliners are gone.

The fall's opening answer was emphatic: FSU beat Chipola College 19-5 in its first fall exhibition. The lopsided score signaled a team with real depth — not one playing on fumes while it waits for two stars to come back.

What the Fall Showed

FSU's fall slate ran from September through November, ending at Florida. That's a measured approach — building through exhibition play against lesser competition before testing yourself in a rivalry setting to close the fall. Head coach Lonni Alameda's program has done this before: lost big names and rediscovered its identity through the offseason grind.

The rebuild is real, but so is the roster. FSU consistently ranks near the top of the ACC in pitching depth and has a history of finding the next great pitcher and the next great infielder from within its own program. The 2027 class will add to that pool.

The Road Back

No program stays elite by accident, and no program loses two All-Americans in one offseason without feeling the pinch. FSU's situation heading into 2027 will be measured against what everyone expected — a step backward — and against what the Seminoles demand of themselves, which is a spot in Tallahassee's March standings and a path to the WCWS.

Closing fall on the road at Florida is a signal. It says this team isn't protecting anything, isn't hiding anything, and isn't lowering expectations. If that mentality holds into the spring, FSU's rebuild may be shorter than outsiders expect.

What's Next

FSU transitions to winter training before spring practice resumes. The 2027 ACC schedule will be released in the coming weeks. Watch for FSU's first preseason ranking in January — that number will tell you everything about how the country values this program's depth versus its star power losses. The opening ACC series in March will be the first real test of where the Seminoles stand in the new landscape.
