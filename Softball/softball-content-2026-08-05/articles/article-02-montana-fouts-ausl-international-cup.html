Montana Fouts Won the AUSL and the International Cup. Both in the Same Three Weeks.

By Emily Rawlings

August 5, 2026

On July 26 in College Station, Texas, Montana Fouts pitched the Utah Talons to a 6-1 win over the Chicago Bandits and the 2026 AUSL Championship. The Talons were back-to-back champions. Fouts was named AUSL Pitcher of the Year.

Eight days later, she was in Oklahoma City at Devon Park, pitching the USA Women's National Team to a 2-0 win over No. 1 Japan in the International Cup gold medal game. She took a no-hitter into the seventh inning. Japan's only baserunner all game was a leadoff single to begin the seventh.

This was Montana Fouts' summer. Two titles. One month.

The AUSL Championship

Utah's championship win over Chicago was defined by Jadelyn Allchin, who hit a home run, doubled, drove in three runs, and made a diving headfirst catch that became the defining highlight of the AUSL postseason. But behind every Talons win this season was Fouts on the mound.

Her 2026 AUSL season: 7-4 record, 2.36 ERA, 63 strikeouts in 61.1 innings pitched. Her strikeout-to-walk ratio was 63:10. Opponents hit .200 against her. She set multiple AUSL single-season records across wins, strikeouts, and innings pitched. The Pitcher of the Year award was not a close decision.

This was Utah's second consecutive AUSL title — and both were delivered by the same pitcher.

Then the International Cup

Three weeks after the AUSL Championship, Fouts was at Devon Park for the USA International Cup. The USA Women's National Team had gone undefeated through pool play, defeating Australia, Canada (in a 10-7 comeback from a 5-0 deficit), and China, before winning the semifinal 6-2 over the USA Elite Team. In the gold medal final, they faced Japan — the world's No. 1 team, the defending International Cup champion, and a team that had beaten USA Elite 9-2 in pool play.

What Fouts did in that game is worth remembering. She retired 21 of 22 batters. She threw eight strikeouts. She walked no one. The only baserunner Japan managed was a leadoff single to begin the seventh inning. USA won 2-0. Jayda Coleman drove in the first run. Janae Jefferson added an RBI double to make it 2-0. Fouts did the rest.

Tournament MVP: Maya Brady, who also broke the AUSL single-season home run record during the regular season with the Oklahoma City Spark.

What's Next: The AUSL All-Star Cup

The AUSL All-Star Cup begins August 8 in Rosemont, Illinois at Parkway Bank Sports Complex, running through August 30. Forty-five players, four series, with teams re-drafted by top leaderboard performers after each series in the Athletes Unlimited format. All 12 games air nationally on ESPN platforms, MLB Network, and CBS Sports Network.

Fouts is on the All-Star Cup roster. So are Allchin, Brady, Jayda Coleman, Karlyn Pickens, Reese Atwood, and the rest of the game's best players. After a month that produced two championships, the best softball in the world continues in Rosemont this week.

If you've been meaning to start watching the AUSL, now is an excellent time to begin.

What's Next

The AUSL All-Star Cup runs through August 30. The WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group C qualifier — the actual World Cup qualification event for USA — is September 12-16 in Oklahoma City. The AUSL has put a season of elite softball on tape for the rest of the year to evaluate. Montana Fouts has already given everyone the highlight reel.
