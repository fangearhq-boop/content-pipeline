LLSWS Day 3: Northwest and Mid-Atlantic Advance, AUSL Pro Game at Stallings Stadium Tonight

By Sarah Mitchell

August 5, 2026

Day 3 of the 2026 Little League Softball World Series at Stallings Stadium in Greenville, North Carolina, delivered two clear results and a preview of the elimination bracket taking shape. Tuesday also set up today's marquee event: the 2026 AUSL Pro Game at 7 PM ET on ESPN2 — 24 of the world's best softball players at the same stadium where these kids are competing for a national title.

Game 9: Northwest 7, Southwest 3

Northwest (Medford, Oregon) beat Southwest 7-3 in a loser-bracket elimination game Tuesday morning at 10 AM ET. Madison Locke had the standout performance — 3 hits, 3 runs, and 4 RBI — and kept Northwest alive in the tournament. Southwest is eliminated. Northwest advances to Wednesday's bracket play and will face the winner of Tuesday evening's Canada vs. Europe-Africa matchup.

Game 10: Mid-Atlantic 13, New England 3 — Mercy Rule in 4 Innings

Toms River, New Jersey's Mid-Atlantic squad mercy-ruled Bristol, Connecticut's New England team 13-3 — the game ending after just 3.5 innings. Mid-Atlantic scored nine runs in the first inning and four more in the second, turning it into a statement game before the halfway mark. New England is eliminated. Mid-Atlantic advances to Wednesday to face the winner of the West vs. Asia-Pacific matchup from Tuesday evening.

The Host Team Stays Perfect

North Carolina's Johnston County squad — making their first-ever LLSWS appearance — remains 2-0 in pool play. The host team does not play Wednesday, sitting comfortably in a position where a strong performance in Thursday's bracket games could determine whether they continue their historic run. NC has outscored their opponents 18-7 in two games. The crowd at Stallings Stadium has been behind them from the first pitch.

Tonight: The AUSL Pro Game at 7 PM ET on ESPN2

The 2026 AUSL Pro Game takes place tonight at Stallings Stadium at 7 PM ET on ESPN2 — and the setup is exactly what it sounds like. Twenty-four AUSL players, divided into two teams wearing their regular-season uniforms, coached by two of the sport's all-time legends: Lisa Fernandez and Natasha Watley.

The players have been at Stallings Stadium since Tuesday — attending LLSWS games, doing first pitches and meet-and-greets with the competing teams, and this morning hosting a youth Tee Ball clinic for two local Little League programs before tonight's game. The connection between the LLSWS and the professional game is intentional, and this week makes it visible in a way that no highlight package can replicate. The kids see the pros. The pros played these same fields not long ago.

Watch the AUSL Pro Game tonight at 7 PM ET on ESPN2.

What's Next

Day 4 continues Wednesday at Stallings Stadium. Game 13 (10 AM ET on ESPN2) features Mid-Atlantic against the West or Asia-Pacific winner. Game 14 (1 PM ET on ESPN2) features Northwest against the Canada or Europe-Africa winner. The LLSWS bracket continues to narrow — the championship game is Saturday, August 9. Every game from here eliminates someone. The host team watches, waits, and prepares.
