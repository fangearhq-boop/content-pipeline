Texas Tech's Transfer Class Goes Deeper Than You Knew: Kasidi Pickering Is a 50-Home Run Outfielder

By Jordan Reeves

August 5, 2026

Everyone in softball heard about Jaysoni Beachum committing to Texas Tech. A .406 average at FSU, 10 home runs, 69 RBI, and the 2024 NFCA National Freshman of the Year award will generate that kind of attention. The headline was earned. But the full picture of what Texas Tech built in the 2026 transfer portal goes considerably further than Beachum, and it starts with a name that deserves the same level of attention.

Kasidi Pickering: What She Brings

Kasidi Pickering played three seasons at Oklahoma before committing to Texas Tech on June 19, 2026. Her career line at OU: .384 batting average, 50 home runs, 169 RBI across 186 games. That is not a prospect profile. That is a proven producer at the highest level of college softball.

Her 2026 season alone: .370 average, 20 home runs, 61 RBI in 59 games. She hit .370 in a down year. She was a freshman on Oklahoma's 2024 national championship team and was named to the WCWS All-Tournament Team that year. She has one season of eligibility remaining.

She was an NFCA First Team All-American. She is the kind of player that programs build lineups around — not complement them with.

The Pitching Addition: Jayden Heavener

Texas Tech also added Jayden Heavener from LSU — a pitcher who led the LSU staff with a 13-9 record and 2.81 ERA in 2026 and has two years of eligibility remaining. Pitching depth has been the one area where Texas Tech's 2027 case could be questioned. Heavener's addition removes that question.

The TTU rotation entering 2027 now features Heavener alongside the established returning pieces from a program that reached the Women's College World Series championship series in 2026. The combination of power in the lineup and depth in the circle is what championship contenders look like.

What This Class Adds Up To

Texas Tech's 2026-27 transfer class has been rated the No. 1 transfer class in the country. That ranking was built on Beachum + Kasidi Pickering as the centerpiece offensive additions, with Heavener addressing the pitching staff. The returning core from a team that finished as national runner-up provides the foundation. The portal additions provide the margin.

Programs that add one elite transfer do well. Programs that add Beachum, Kasidi Pickering, and Heavener in the same off-season — while already returning the nucleus of a WCWS finalist — do not just compete for national titles. They enter the season as the team to beat.

A brief clarification for prior coverage: Kasidi Pickering (Oklahoma outfielder) is a different person from Karlyn Pickens, who is an AUSL pitcher. The correct name for the TTU transfer is Kasidi Pickering.
