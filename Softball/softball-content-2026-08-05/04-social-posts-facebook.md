### STORY 2: Montana Fouts — AUSL + International Cup

**Long-Form Post (11:30 AM ET)**

🥎 **MONTANA FOUTS' SUMMER: TWO TITLES, THREE WEEKS**

On July 26 in College Station, Texas, the Utah Talons beat the Chicago Bandits 6-1 to win the 2026 AUSL Championship. Montana Fouts pitched. The Talons won back-to-back AUSL titles. Jadelyn Allchin was the MVP — she homered, doubled, drove in three runs, and made a headfirst diving catch that became the defining highlight of the postseason.

Fouts was also named AUSL Pitcher of the Year: 7-4, 2.36 ERA, 63 strikeouts in 61.1 innings. Her strikeout-to-walk ratio was 63:10. Opponents hit .200 against her. She set multiple AUSL single-season records.

Three weeks later, she was back in Oklahoma City at Devon Park — but this time for the USA Women's National Team. She took a no-hitter into the seventh inning of the International Cup gold medal game and beat No. 1 Japan 2-0.

Back-to-back AUSL titles. International Cup gold. Named to the All-Star Cup roster.

The AUSL All-Star Cup starts August 8 in Rosemont, Illinois. Twelve games on ESPN, MLB Network, and CBS Sports Network through August 30. Don't miss it.

### STORY 5: Philippines "Blu Girls" — Asian Games

**Long-Form Post (6:00 PM ET)**

🥎 **THE PHILIPPINES "BLU GIRLS" ARE GOING TO AICHI-NAGOYA FOR A MEDAL**

The Philippines national softball team — known as the Blu Girls — haven't medaled at the Asian Games since 1998. Three consecutive Games ended in 4th place: 2014, 2018, 2022. Close each time. No medal.

They're ranked No. 13 in the world and 4th in Asia. Japan (host, World No. 1) enters as the overwhelming favorite. But something happened in Lima, Peru last month that changed the conversation.

At the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Lima qualifier, the Philippines faced Puerto Rico — the No. 3 team in the world. Walk-off win for the Blu Girls, 5-4. They stayed alive in the qualifier. They showed up when it counted.

September 26. Aichi-Nagoya. The Asian Games softball competition begins. The Philippines will be there, and this time they're not just hoping to medal.

They're training for it.
