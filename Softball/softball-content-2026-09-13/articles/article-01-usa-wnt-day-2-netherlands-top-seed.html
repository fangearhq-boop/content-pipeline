Day 2 at Devon Park: USA Faces Netherlands Tonight in Top-Seed Showdown

By Emily Rawlings September 13, 2026

A Dominant Start, and Now the Real Test

Team USA made the kind of opening statement at Devon Park that shuts up any pre-tournament skepticism. On Saturday night, the United States offense powered through No. 7 Mexico to close out Day 1 of the 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage with authority. The result was never really in doubt.

Now comes Sunday. USA faces Brazil at 2 PM ET, then lines up for what has always been the marquee matchup of this tournament: No. 6 Netherlands at 8 PM ET. That game decides the top seed. Rachel Garcia, Dejah Mulipola, Tiare Jennings, and the rest of the roster have been building toward this moment all summer.

Why the Netherlands Game Changes Everything

The Netherlands entered this event ranked No. 6 in the world. They are not an opponent that allows big innings and they play clean, disciplined softball. Every run will need to be earned Sunday night.

The tournament format makes the top seed especially valuable. Two teams advance directly from this Group Stage to the 2027 World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland. Third and fourth place go to the Repechage on Sept. 16, where they compete for the remaining wild-card berth. There is a real gap between finishing first or second versus finishing third or lower. The teams that lock up direct Finals berths also lock up their place as LA28 Olympic qualifiers ahead of the rest of the field.

USA came into the week with an unblemished history at Devon Park. In five prior International Cup games at this venue, Team USA was 5-0. Gasso and her staff clearly know how to prepare for this arena.

The Sooner Reunion and the Full Roster

For Oklahoma fans watching tonight, four Sooners are in the lineup: Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell. All four were part of recent OU championship rosters, and watching them in USA uniforms at the same park where they will compete this spring adds a particular dimension to this event.

Garcia and Mulipola bring Olympic experience. Both competed at the Tokyo 2020 Games as members of the silver-medal U.S. team. Seven players on this roster were part of the World Games 2025 gold-medal squad. This is not a developmental group. The WNT arrived in Oklahoma City to win, and after Saturday night, it looks exactly like a team that intends to do that.

What's Next

Tonight's Netherlands match at 8 PM ET at Devon Park is the story. A USA win locks the top seed and simplifies the path through the Sept. 16 play-off bracket. The two teams that advance to Redcliffe in April 2027 will have earned it here, in front of a home crowd, in a building where the Sooners played all spring. Watch tonight's game on AWSN.
