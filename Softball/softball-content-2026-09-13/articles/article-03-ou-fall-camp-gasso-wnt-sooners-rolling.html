Gasso Is in OKC. The Sooners Are in Norman. Fall Camp Is Rolling Either Way.

By Sarah Mitchell September 13, 2026

Two Programs Running at Once

Patty Gasso is 95 miles away coaching the United States Women's National Team at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. JT Gasso and Jennifer Rocha are there too, both listed on the WNT coaching staff through Sept. 16. That means every coach who traveled with the program this summer is currently in OKC for the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage.

Back in Norman, OU's associate coaches are running fall practice. And based on what the Sooners have put on the field through the early fall period, that program does not appear to need supervision. This is what a mature program looks like. The staff runs itself. The players know what the standards are.

The Players Worth Watching

Kendall Wells is the obvious headliner. As a freshman in 2026, she set the NCAA single-season home run record with 32 — passing the previous mark and earning NFCA National Freshman of the Year honors along the way. She's a sophomore now and the expectations are different. The question isn't whether she can hit home runs. It's whether the rest of her game grows around that power.

Sydney Berzon arrived from LSU as a two-time All-American with 486.1 career innings pitched, a 2.02 ERA, and 406 strikeouts. She's the kind of arm that changes a pitching staff's ceiling. Getting a full fall to work through her role in the circle before the spring schedule opens is exactly what OU needs from her. Macie Harter hit .346 at Middle Tennessee State and is quietly one of the more versatile bats on this roster.

Counting Down to Tarleton State

Oklahoma's fall opener against Tarleton State is 17 days away, on Sept. 30 at Love's Field. All eight fall exhibition games are at Love's Field, giving fans multiple chances to see this new-look roster before the spring season begins. The full fall slate runs through November 4.

Gasso returns from OKC after Sept. 16, which means she gets three weeks of practice time with the full staff before the first fall game. That is about the right amount of runway to install what she wants to install and get the answers she needs heading into 2027.

What's Next

Gasso and the WNT staff return from OKC on Sept. 16 after the Group Stage concludes. From there, OU has 14 days of full-staff practice before the Tarleton State opener on Sept. 30. The biggest questions — how the Berzon/Wells battery fits together, whether Harter can hold down the top of the lineup — start getting answered in the next few weeks at Love's Field.
