Day 4 Delivers: The 2028 Commit Wave Hits Power Programs

By Jordan Reeves September 13, 2026

The Window Is Open and No One Is Waiting

The Sept. 9 in-person contact window for the Class of 2028 opened four days ago, and the commitments are already stacking up. Programs that spent weeks building relationships over the phone are now getting on campuses, watching live workouts, and closing on the top-rated players in the cycle. Four days in, the class is moving faster than most predicted.

The headline today belongs to Florida State. The Seminoles landed their first 2028 verbal: Paityn Donalson from Lakeland Christian High School in Lakeland, Florida. Her numbers as a sophomore are not what most coaches see in a player at that grade level. She hit .693 with 11 home runs, 50 RBI, and 35 stolen bases, reaching base at a .748 clip while striking out just once in 27 games. She also contributes in the circle, making her a genuine two-way option for head coach Lonni Alameda's staff.

Texas Tech Gets Its First, and It Won't Be the Last

Gerry Glasco's program landed its first 2028 verbal on Sunday as well. Outfielder Star Gutierrez commits to Texas Tech, giving the Red Raiders the first building block of what Glasco hopes becomes a signature recruiting class in Year 2 of his tenure. Coming off a WCWS runner-up finish in June, TTU's recruiting pitch has real credibility now.

Elsewhere, Tennessee head coach Karen Weekly's staff secured commitments from Sophia Galindo and Nevaeh Wilson, two names that had been trending toward the Lady Vols for several weeks. Florida now has two 2028 verbals after adding pitcher and two-way player Kate Losh to go alongside catcher Ryann Harris, who committed a week ago. The Gators are building early depth at premium positions.

Oklahoma's Busy Week Continues

Oklahoma has been particularly active since the window opened. Middle infielder Braylyn Saenz, rated No. 41 nationally and 13th at her position, committed to the Sooners. OU also hosted Sophia Schlader and Caelyn Campos for official visits, with both among the prospects drawing the most attention from major programs.

This follows the earlier news that No. 1 overall recruit Finlee Williams committed to Oklahoma on Day 2 of the window. For OU, this is a class that is taking shape fast, and the early momentum suggests the Sooners intend to load up given how many players were lost after the 2026 season.

What's Next

The in-person window runs through the fall, and coaches who secured early commits will spend the coming weeks targeting the remaining uncommitted prospects in the top 30. Look for the No. 1 rankings from Softball America and Extra Innings Softball to start shifting by mid-October as official visits produce more announcements. The pace of the Class of 2028 suggests it could end up as one of the deeper and earlier-committing classes in recent memory.
