### STORY 2: 10th Circuit Grants NCAA Stay

#### FB Long-Form Post — 11:00 AM ET

Important update for anyone following the Class of 2022 fifth-year eligibility story.

The 10th Circuit Court of Appeals voted 2-1 on August 21 to grant the NCAA an emergency stay in Wisne v. NCAA. What that means: Judge Charlotte Sweeney's preliminary injunction — which briefly allowed Class of 2022 athletes to return for a fifth year — is now on hold while the full appeal is decided.

The Class of 2022 athletes who had re-enrolled or re-joined rosters under the injunction are, per the NCAA, ineligible again pending the appeal. However, the NCAA has said it will not penalize schools that added those athletes in good faith.

There may be a narrow exception: a Tennessee state court judge denied the NCAA's stay in a separate state-level lawsuit, so some Class of 2022 athletes in Tennessee could still have a separate legal avenue.

For most programs nationwide, the door has closed — for now. The 10th Circuit appeal is ongoing with no ruling date announced.

We'll continue tracking. When this changes, we'll let you know.
