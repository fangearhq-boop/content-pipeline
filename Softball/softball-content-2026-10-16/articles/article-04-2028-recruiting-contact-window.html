The 2028 Recruiting Contact Window Opens Tomorrow — What to Watch This Weekend

By Jordan Reeves | October 16, 2026 | I Love Softball

Tomorrow morning, D1 softball coaches across the country will pick up their phones and start making calls to Class of 2028 prospects. The October 17-18 contact period is here — and for a recruiting class this talented, this weekend could be a watershed moment.

The Class of 2028 became eligible for direct contact from D1 coaches starting September 1, 2026. Since then, programs have been limited in how they could communicate with prospects. This weekend changes that. Coaches can now reach out directly, attend showcase events, and make their pitches face to face.

Where the Top Programs Stand

Three programs have separated themselves in the early 2028 recruiting race.

Tennessee leads the national conversation with five commits, including the No. 4 ranked player in the class. After coaching changes earlier this year, Karen Weekly's staff has demonstrated the program's recruiting pull is undiminished.

Oklahoma also has five commits, including Grace Tonga (OF) and Sophia Schlader. Patty Gasso's staff has been methodical in building this class around positional depth — a philosophy that has defined Oklahoma's roster management for years.

Florida locked in arguably the best commit in the class early: No. 2 overall Kylie Holden, a five-star middle infielder, chose the Gators alongside catcher Brooklyn Evans. Tim Walton has his core pieces secured.

Where to Watch

College Coaches Showcase Camps are scheduled this weekend in Lehi, Utah (Saturday, October 17) and Bellevue, Nebraska (Sunday, October 18), among other locations around the country. These events serve as prime contact-window opportunities: coaches attend, prospects perform, and conversations happen in person that can accelerate the commitment process.

For prospects in the top 50 who remain uncommitted, this weekend represents one of the highest-leverage moments on the recruiting calendar. Expect a wave of announcements on Sunday and Monday as the contact period produces results.

What's Next

Watch D1Softball.com and ExtraInnings Softball's commitment tracker this weekend for announcements as they come in. The programs at the top of the 2028 class are locked in. The battle now is for the uncommitted prospects in the top 25 — and this weekend is when that battle begins in earnest.

The 2028 class is being called one of the most talent-dense in recent memory. Two days from now, we'll know a lot more about where those prospects are going to play.
