LSU Lands Mickey Winchell From Notre Dame: Fall Transfer Portal Roundup

By Sarah Mitchell | October 16, 2026 | I Love Softball

LSU softball's first fall transfer portal addition is a fast, contact-first outfielder who spent the last four years proving she can play at a high level in the ACC.

Notre Dame senior outfielder Mickey Winchell has committed to the Tigers. Winchell hit .335 with 14 stolen bases for the Fighting Irish in 2026, earning a reputation as one of the better contact hitters and baserunners in her conference. She heads to Baton Rouge for her senior season with something to prove in the SEC.

What Winchell Adds to LSU

LSU's offseason began with significant outfield attrition. Jayden Heavener — a sophomore pitcher who had drawn national attention — transferred to Texas Tech. Other pieces shifted. Head coach Beth Torina and her staff needed to restock the outfield with proven, experienced players.

Winchell fits that description. A .335 average in the ACC in 2026 against quality pitching is a meaningful credential. The 14 stolen bases reflect the kind of speed and instincts that translate to the SEC — where every base matters and the games are decided on margins. She profiles as a leadoff-type who can set the table for a Tigers lineup looking to compete for an SEC title in 2027.

This is exactly the kind of addition the fall portal window is designed for: a graduate transfer or senior with eligibility who wants a new environment and brings immediate production with her.

The Rest of the Fall Portal Landscape

Winchell's commitment is LSU's first confirmed fall move, but other programs have been active as well.

Oklahoma has been steadily adding pieces to offset the summer departures. Incoming transfers include sophomore outfielders Macie Harter and Adi Hansen, and redshirt-junior catcher Abbie Gregus — all of whom fill gaps left by a summer portal class that saw the Sooners lose outfielder Kasidi Pickering (50 career home runs) and pitcher Berkley Zache to Texas Tech.

Texas Tech is the portal story of the offseason. Between Pickering, Heavener, and other additions, Gerry Glasco has built what many analysts regard as the No. 1 portal class in the country. Texas Tech's portal success validates what Glasco has built in Lubbock — and they're not done yet.

The fall window is quieter than the June-July undergraduate portal rush, but the moves being made now — graduate transfers, mid-year enrollees — represent the final tuning of rosters before February. Programs that address needs in October are better positioned for the spring than those waiting until the season starts.

What's Next

The fall graduate transfer window continues through November. Watch for additional moves from programs with outfield or pitching depth needs heading into conference play. LSU's addition of Winchell is a sign that the Tigers intend to compete in the SEC's top tier next spring — and that Beth Torina is not waiting around to rebuild.
