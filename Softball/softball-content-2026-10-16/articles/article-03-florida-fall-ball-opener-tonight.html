Florida Softball Opens Fall Ball Tonight — Free Admission at Katie Seashole Pressly Stadium

By Emily Rawlings | October 16, 2026 | I Love Softball

After months of recruiting news, transfer portal moves, and offseason speculation, the 2027 Florida Gators softball season gets its first real look tonight.

Florida opens fall ball at 6 PM at Katie Seashole Pressly Stadium — free admission — against Santa Fe College. It's a 7-inning format, it's October, and the result in the box score won't count for anything. But for Tim Walton's program, tonight matters. It is the first opportunity for this roster, assembled with a 2027 national title in mind, to put on a uniform and play together.

What Florida Is Working With

The numbers on this team are as good as any in the country. Six of nine starters from 2026 return to the Gainesville campus. The pitching staff is intact — no significant arms transferred out, which is significant in a college softball landscape where the portal has made pitching depth harder to maintain. Two high-impact transfers have joined the program, and Florida's 2027 recruiting class is regarded as one of the best in the SEC.

Texas won the 2026 Women's College World Series. Florida was in the chase all season — the Gators have the talent and the coaching to challenge for the title, and Walton has said as much in fall camp previews. Tonight is where that conversation moves from hypothetical to visible.

The Fall Ball Schedule

Florida has seven home fall exhibition games scheduled at Pressly Stadium, all free to the public. Tonight's opener is the only game on the fall calendar using a 7-inning format — all remaining fall games will be played to 10 innings.

The one road game on the fall schedule is November 15 at UCF in Orlando. The full fall slate will give Walton and his staff an extended look at new roster additions, starting assignments, and lineup combinations before the season begins in February.

For fans in Gainesville, the fall schedule represents seven opportunities to see a legitimate national title contender in a low-pressure environment. Free admission, fall weather, and a Gators roster built for 2027 — the value proposition is hard to beat.

What's Next

Tonight's result is the start. The full fall schedule runs through mid-November, giving Florida ample time to develop chemistry and work through lineup questions before the competitive season begins.

Check floridagators.com after tonight's game for the result and a first look at how the new pieces performed. The 2027 title race has been talked about for months. Starting tonight, it gets played out on the field.
