### STORY 1: USA WNT — Eight Days to Group Oklahoma (Tier 1 — FOLLOW UP)
Team USA is eight days from opening the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. The USA draws No. 7 Mexico in the night game on Sept. 12 at 7:30 PM — the same venue where Team USA went 5-0 to win the International Cup. Tickets are on sale through WBSC. The six-nation field (USA, Brazil, China, Mexico, Netherlands, New Zealand) competes Sept. 12-16, with the top two advancing to the 2027 World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, QLD — an LA28 qualifier. Netherlands (No. 6) is the top-seed decider. Patty Gasso coaches; roster includes 7 World Games gold medalists, 2020 Olympians Garcia and Mulipola, and 4 Sooners.

### STORY 3: Oklahoma Fall Camp — Wells Delivers, Berzon Impresses (Tier 2 — FOLLOW UP)
OU's fall camp is producing early results. Kendall Wells — who broke the NCAA single-season freshman home run record in 2026 — has hit four home runs in her first three fall scrimmages per reporting from SI and Heartland Sports. Transfer pitcher Sydney Berzon (2x All-American, LSU) has also turned heads, retiring all six batters faced in the fall exhibition against OCU and allowing just 1 run over 2 IP in the first Battle Series scrimmage. Patty Gasso departs in eight days (Sept. 12) for Team USA coaching duties. Fall opener at Love's Field is Sept. 30.

SI named the Longhorns one of "4 College Softball Programs to Watch This Fall" — alongside Nebraska, Oklahoma State, and Duke. Yahoo Sports profiled Teagan Kavan as "unbeatable," detailing her back-to-back WCWS MOP awards. Coach Mike White returns six of nine starters, including Kavan and Katie Stewart. Only UCLA (1988-90) and Oklahoma (2021-23) have won three straight national titles.
