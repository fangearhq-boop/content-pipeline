Eight Days Out: Team USA Returns to Devon Park for the WBSC World Cup Group Stage

By Sarah Mitchell

September 4, 2026

The countdown is at eight days. On Sept. 12, Team USA returns to Devon Park in Oklahoma City — the same venue where they went 5-0 to win the International Cup earlier this year — for the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage. The stakes couldn't be higher: only two of six nations will advance to the 2027 World Cup Finals and the path toward the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics.

Tickets are on sale through WBSC. The six-team field competing Sept. 12-16 includes USA, Brazil, China, Mexico, Netherlands, and New Zealand. Opening day at Devon Park starts with Brazil vs. China at 1:00 PM, followed by New Zealand vs. Netherlands at 4:00 PM, and the night showcase: USA vs. No. 7 Mexico at 7:30 PM, preceded by an opening ceremony at 6:30 PM.

Home Turf, Proven Venue

Devon Park isn't unfamiliar territory for this USA squad. Weeks ago, Team USA went undefeated through the International Cup at this exact facility, winning the gold medal without a single loss. That institutional knowledge — knowing the field dimensions, the crowd atmosphere, the travel logistics in Oklahoma City — is a real advantage heading into a tournament this important.

USA enters ranked No. 2 in the world and received an automatic berth as tournament host. Patty Gasso, who doubles as Oklahoma's head coach, leads the coaching staff. The 16-player roster is built for international competition: seven World Games gold medalists, 2020 Olympians Valerie Garcia and Dejah Mulipola, and four Sooners — Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell.

The Netherlands Question

While Mexico (No. 7) is USA's opening-night opponent, Netherlands (No. 6) looms as the more consequential matchup. The Dutch represent the most dangerous competition in the field and will likely determine which nation claims the No. 1 seed heading into the tournament's decisive final day on Sept. 16.

Only two of the six nations advance to the 2027 World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia — an event scheduled for April 5-11, 2027, that serves as a critical qualifier on the road to LA28. The remaining four nations will compete in a Wild Card repechage on Sept. 16, with one additional berth available. For USA, there is no margin for error in pool play.

What's Next

Team USA's Group Oklahoma schedule runs Sept. 12-16 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. The USA opens against No. 7 Mexico at 7:30 PM on Sept. 12, with Netherlands (No. 6) to follow later in the round-robin. The top two finishers from the six-team field advance directly to the 2027 World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, QLD — a major step on the LA28 Olympic qualification pathway. Tickets are available through WBSC. Eight days away and counting.
