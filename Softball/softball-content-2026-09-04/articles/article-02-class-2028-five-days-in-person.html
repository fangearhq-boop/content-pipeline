Five Days to the In-Person Window: What Class of 2028 Recruits Should Expect Sept. 9

By Emily Rawlings

September 4, 2026

The phone calls started on Sept. 1. The DMs followed. The Zoom requests are filling up this week. But five days from now, the Class of 2028 recruitment officially moves into a new dimension — one that involves coaches showing up at your school, your home, or your next showcase event.

On Sept. 9 at 7 AM ET, the in-person contact window opens for the Class of 2028. Under NCAA rules, college coaches can now conduct off-campus visits to recruits — a step that transforms a recruiting conversation from something that happens on a screen into something that happens across a kitchen table or behind a dugout fence.

The Week Before In-Person Always Matters

For elite recruits, the five days before an in-person window opens are often the most revealing. Coaches who are truly serious will call more frequently, send more personalized emails, and schedule video sessions to make sure a recruit already feels wanted before they walk through the door. Coaches who are less invested will be quieter.

A record 1,226 Extra Inning Softball nominations signals how deep this class runs. Top prospects Michaela Landers, Kylie Holden, and Sophia Schlader headline a class that evaluators have called one of the strongest in recent years — rich with middle infielders, catchers, and a loaded pitching pool. For coaches, that depth means competition for the top names is fierce.

What Sept. 9 Changes

The Sept. 1 communication window allowed direct phone calls, emails, social media direct messages, and video calls. Those tools matter — but they can only do so much. In-person visits are the next escalation. Coaches can watch a recruit's practice, meet her family, and make the kind of impression that a phone call simply cannot replicate.

Under the NCAA's 5-5 recruiting rules, coaches can be active in a recruit's junior and senior years — meaning the Sept. 9 in-person window launches nearly two years of face-to-face recruiting. For a Class of 2028 prospect receiving her first college visits, it's the start of the most real and high-stakes phase of the process.

What's Next

The Sept. 9 at 7 AM ET in-person contact window opens five days from now for the Class of 2028. Coaches across the country have been preparing their visit plans for weeks, and the most coveted prospects should expect a busy schedule of coaches at their schools, homes, and fall showcase events in the days and weeks that follow. This is when recruiting gets personal — and when the commitments start accelerating.
