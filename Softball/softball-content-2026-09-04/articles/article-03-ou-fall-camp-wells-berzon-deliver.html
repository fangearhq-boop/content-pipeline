Oklahoma's Fall Camp Is Delivering Early: Wells Already Hitting, Berzon Impressing

By Jordan Reeves

September 4, 2026

The early evidence from OU's fall camp is giving Sooner fans something to feel good about — even as the clock ticks down to Patty Gasso's eight-day departure for Team USA duties.

Kendall Wells, who broke the NCAA single-season freshman home run record in 2026 and earned NFCA Freshman of the Year honors, has already launched four home runs in her first three fall scrimmages. In the fall exhibition against OCU, she went 2-for-4 with two home runs and three RBI. Gasso told SI that Wells's September form has been impressive, continuing the momentum from a historic freshman campaign.

Berzon Making Her Case

The other big story from fall camp is Sydney Berzon. The two-time All-American from LSU — who pitched 486.1 career innings with a 2.02 ERA and 406 strikeouts — arrived as the headliner of OU's pitching staff rebuild, and her early looks are backing that billing.

Berzon retired all six batters she faced in the fall exhibition against OCU. In the first Battle Series scrimmage against the OU lineup, she allowed just one run over two innings. Gasso told reporters she believes Berzon will fulfill her potential at Oklahoma. For a pitching staff that returns only two arms from 2026 — sophomore Audrey Lowry and Deal — those early Berzon numbers are significant.

The Eight-Day Ticking Clock

Gasso departs in eight days to serve as head coach for Team USA at the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage (Sept. 12-16, Devon Park, OKC). That means the coaching staff has less than two weeks to get Wells, Berzon, transfer Macie Harter (.346 BA, MTSU), and the rest of a 21-player roster into a working rhythm before Gasso heads to Oklahoma City.

The fall opener is Sept. 30 at Love's Field against USAO. The full fall schedule runs through early November, giving the coaching staff and players time to build the chemistry and rotations that will define the 2027 season. With 12 returning players and nine newcomers, this is the most significant evaluation period in OU softball in years.

What's Next

Oklahoma's fall opener is Sept. 30 at Love's Field against USAO. Patty Gasso departs Sept. 12 for Team USA coaching duties at the WBSC Group Stage in OKC. Before then, the coaching staff has eight days to maximize the evaluation of Wells, Berzon, Harter, Lowry, and the full roster. Early returns from fall camp suggest the rebuild is further along than expected — which is good news for a program looking to return to the WCWS in 2027.
