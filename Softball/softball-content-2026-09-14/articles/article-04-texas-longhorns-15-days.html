Fifteen Days to the Opener: Texas Longhorns Fall Camp is Building Toward Something Special

By Sarah Mitchell | September 14, 2026

AUSTIN, Texas — Defending Women's College World Series champion Texas has been at work all fall, and with the September 29 opener against Murray State now just 15 days away, the first public look at this roster is getting close.

The Longhorns went 53-12 in 2026. They swept Texas Tech in the WCWS finals. Their coaching staff earned NFCA Staff of the Year honors for the second consecutive year. The returning core is deep. And they added the best transfer in the country.

Reese Torres arrived in Austin this summer from FSU, where she won the 2026 D1Softball Player of the Year award (.530 BA, 16 HR, 56 RBI). She's from Georgetown, Texas — a small town 30 miles north of Austin — which makes this feel less like a transfer and more like a homecoming. Torres is now suiting up for the team that just won a national title, in the state she grew up in, under a coaching staff that has done nothing but win.

The Three-Peat Question

Jayda Kavan enters her senior season as the most accomplished active player in college softball. The two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player — a distinction no one in history has achieved except her — is returning for one more attempt at something only two programs have ever done: three consecutive national championships.

UCLA did it from 1988 to 1990. Oklahoma did it from 2021 to 2023. No program has done it since, and many have tried. Texas won it in 2025 and 2026. One more and they join that exclusive company.

Kavan knows what it takes. She has been through two championship runs, stood in the circle at OGE Energy Field in both title games, and delivered each time. She's the anchor of this team's identity heading into 2027.

Fall Camp Setting the Tone

With Torres settling into the Austin atmosphere and Kavan leading by example, the Longhorns fall camp has had a purposeful energy. The coaching staff earned NFCA recognition for a reason — they know how to build culture in the fall and translate it into March performance.

Katie Stewart, who holds the Texas program record with 31 career home runs, is also back for 2027, giving the Longhorns a lineup capable of sustaining the offensive production that made them WCWS champions.

The fall opener on September 29 is free and open to the public at McCombs Field (6PM CT). It's not preseason rankings or media narratives — it's the first actual look at what Texas has been building since the trophy was handed over in June.

What's Next

Texas opens fall play September 29 at McCombs Field against Murray State (6PM CT, free admission). The fall schedule runs through November 6 with a road trip to Texas A&M. For fans wanting to see Torres, Kavan, and this roster in action for the first time, September 29 is the date to circle. The three-peat bid officially begins then.
