USA Softball Enters Day 3 Unbeaten, Playoffs Two Days Away at Devon Park

By Sarah Mitchell | September 14, 2026

OKLAHOMA CITY — Team USA enters Day 3 of the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage on Monday having yet to lose a game at Devon Park, with the six-nation tournament's playoff round just two days away.

USA rolled through the first two days of competition at the Oklahoma City venue, defeating Mexico in a dominant showing on Day 1 before dismantling Brazil 17-0 in just four innings on Day 2 — a mercy-rule victory that demonstrated exactly how deep and dangerous the USA roster is heading into the final stretch of pool play.

The tournament runs through Wednesday, with the round-robin phase concluding on Tuesday before playoffs take center stage on September 16. The stakes couldn't be higher: the top two finishers earn direct berths to the 2027 Women's Softball World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland — the qualifying tournament for the LA28 Olympics.

Stakes at Devon Park

USA and China entered Day 3 as the only unbeaten teams in the field. Both nations are on track to secure the two direct Finals berths, though the path through the remaining round-robin games and the September 16 playoff format will determine seeding and matchups.

The playoff structure: 1st vs. 2nd play at 1:00 PM ET on September 16, with the winner earning a direct trip to the 2027 Finals in Redcliffe. Third and fourth place teams meet at 4:00 PM ET, followed by a Repechage bracket at 7:00 PM ET.

USA enters with history on its side. The program is 5-0 in international competition at Devon Park, the Sooner-owned venue that has become one of the premier softball facilities in the country.

A Coaching Staff Built for This Moment

Head coach Patty Gasso, who leads Oklahoma to WCWS contention year after year, is running the bench for Team USA alongside assistant coaches JT Gasso and Amanda Rocha. The trio has been in Oklahoma City for the duration of the tournament and will return to Norman following the September 16 playoff round.

Four Oklahoma Sooners — Brito, Coleman, Jennings, and Maxwell — are on the USA roster, giving the Group Oklahoma tournament a distinctly Sooner feel.

Key veterans Ana Paula Garcia and Megan Mulipola, both Tokyo 2020 silver medalists, provide experienced leadership in a lineup built for international competition. USA has won seven World Games gold medals, a testament to the program's sustained dominance.

The Competition

The six-team field — USA, China, Netherlands, Mexico, New Zealand, and Brazil — is competing in a 15-game round-robin format (September 12-15) before Monday and Tuesday's remaining games set the playoff bracket. China's track record makes the USA-China matchup the central storyline of the group stage.

Netherlands (No. 6 in the WBSC rankings) was the pre-tournament pick to push hardest for one of the two direct Finals spots, while Mexico (No. 7) demonstrated competitive fight in Day 1 before falling to USA.

What's Next

Day 3 games at Devon Park begin Monday, with the round-robin phase wrapping Tuesday. All eyes then shift to Wednesday, September 16, for the playoff round that will determine which two nations head directly to Redcliffe, QLD and the LA28 Olympic qualifying pathway. For USA Softball, two days of work stand between them and another step toward the Paris 2028 Games.

Streaming is available through AWSN, and the WBSC's official Gametime platform carries live coverage throughout the tournament.
