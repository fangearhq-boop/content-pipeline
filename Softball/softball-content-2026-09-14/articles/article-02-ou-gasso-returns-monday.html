Gasso Returns Monday — Oklahoma Fall Camp Enters Its Final Phase With 16 Days to Opener

By Emily Rawlings | September 14, 2026

NORMAN, Okla. — Patty Gasso has been 20 miles away all week, coaching Team USA at Devon Park. On Monday, after the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage playoffs conclude, she comes home.

Gasso, along with assistant coaches JT Gasso and Amanda Rocha, will return to Norman following the September 16 playoff round — meaning the Oklahoma Sooners' complete coaching staff will be reunited for the final stretch of fall camp before the September 30 opener against Tarleton State.

The reunion can't come soon enough. Not because things have fallen apart in Gasso's absence — they haven't — but because what's waiting for Gasso in Norman is one of the most talented fall rosters the program has assembled in years.

Wells, Berzon, Harter: The Fall Camp Core

Kendall Wells has been the centerpiece of fall camp since Day 1. The sophomore set the NCAA freshman home run record (32) in 2026, adding .358 BA, 88 RBI, a 1.028 slugging percentage, and honors that included NFCA Freshman of the Year, SEC Freshman of the Year, and the Johnny Bench Award. Wells is back, healthy, and apparently picking up where she left off.

Sydney Berzon, the LSU transfer who arrived this summer with a 2.02 ERA and 406 career strikeouts across 486.1 innings (52-25 record, 2x All-American), has given OU a true ace. She and Wells have been competing against each other in fall scrimmages — a matchup that has already produced memorable moments, including Wells homering off Berzon twice in one session.

Macie Harter, the MTSU transfer who hit .346, completes an upgraded pitching staff that gives Gasso real depth heading into the 2027 season.

Associate Coaches Holding the Fort

With Gasso in Oklahoma City for the WNT commitment, the Sooners' associate coaches have been running fall camp in Norman — a responsibility they've handled well. The structure Gasso built at OU is designed to function even when its head coach is wearing a different jersey.

That's not unusual for a program of this caliber. Gasso has balanced the dual obligations of OU and USA Softball throughout her career, and the Sooners have never let the WNT commitment become a distraction.

16 Days to Tarleton State

The fall opener is September 30, when Tarleton State visits Norman. With Gasso back in the building by Monday, Oklahoma has 14 practice days with its full coaching staff before that game — more than enough time to sharpen what's already a polished roster.

The 2027 season officially begins in the spring, but October's fall schedule gives Gasso the first real look at how Torres, Berzon, Harter, and the rest of this group function together. The expectations in Norman are always high. This year, there are reasons to think they're warranted.

What's Next

Gasso returns to Norman on Monday, September 16, following the WBSC playoff round. The fall opener against Tarleton State is September 30, and the first public exhibition is set for October 3. For Sooner fans, the wait is almost over — and what they'll see when fall camp opens up should be worth it.
