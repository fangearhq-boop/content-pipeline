Class of 2028 Commit Wave Rolls On Five Days Into the In-Person Contact Window

By Jordan Reeves | September 14, 2026

Five days after the September 9 in-person contact window opened for the Class of 2028, the softball recruiting commit wave is already rolling — and it shows no signs of slowing down.

Oklahoma State landed its first 2028 pledge this week: Peyton Cleary, a top-100 infielder from Liberty North High School in Missouri. LSU signed five-star Joley Bennett. Oklahoma hosted five-star prospects Sophia Schlader and Caelyn Campos on campus visits. Tennessee, South Carolina, and Texas A&M each pulled in Top 30 talent.

And this is still early. The biggest names in the class — many of whom began fielding calls, texts, and FaceTime conversations when communication opened on September 1 — remain uncommitted. The pace of new pledges will only accelerate as programs compete for the top unsigned prospects.

What the Window Means

The September 9 date marked a meaningful escalation in the recruiting calendar under the NCAA's permanent calendar structure. Before September 9, coaches could communicate with 2028 prospects by phone, text, and video call (permitted beginning September 1). Once the 9th arrived, the window expanded to include in-person contact: campus visits with coaches, off-campus evaluations, and facility tours with current team members.

For elite high school players and their families, the in-person experience often accelerates commitment timelines. Campus visits create emotional connections that calls can't replicate — and programs know it. The result is a flurry of official and unofficial visits in the weeks immediately following the window opening.

Programs Making Early Moves

Oklahoma State's Peyton Cleary commitment is notable because it represents the program's first formal 2028 pledge. Cleary, an infielder from Liberty North High School in Missouri, ranks among the top 100 players in her class — a strong early return for an OSU program that is actively rebuilding its recruiting pipeline after its 2026 Super Regional exit.

LSU's signing of five-star Joley Bennett continues the Tigers' pattern of landing elite pitching and positional talent early in the recruitment cycle. Bennett joins a program that already made significant moves in the 2027 class.

Oklahoma's campus visit hosting of Sophia Schlader and Caelyn Campos — both five-star prospects — signals that the Sooners are aggressively pursuing the top names available. Hosting visits this early in the window is a deliberate strategy by programs that believe their facilities and atmosphere close recruits.

A Record-Setting Class in the Making

The 2026 recruiting cycle set an EIS (Elite Impact Softball) nominations record with 1,226 players nominated. The Class of 2028 cycle is tracking similarly, with early evaluations suggesting comparable depth at the top of the class.

The biggest uncommitted prospects in the class are watching how the first few weeks of the in-person window play out before making decisions. For many, the goal is to see multiple programs in person before committing — a process that typically plays out through October.

What's Next

Expect the commit wave to continue building through September and into October. The top-10 uncommitted players in the Class of 2028 — still unsigned after the communication and in-person windows opened — represent the biggest prizes left on the board. Programs that land them in the next 30 days will have defined their 2028 class in a meaningful way. Check back weekly as the wave builds.
