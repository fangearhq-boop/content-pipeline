Team USA Goes 5-0 at WBSC World Cup in OKC, Books Trip to Brisbane Finals

By Sarah Mitchell | September 23, 2026

The message was delivered loud and clear at Devon Park in Oklahoma City: Team USA is coming for a world title, and they are not interested in close games.

The USA Women's Softball National Team wrapped up the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage — Group C event on September 16 with a perfect 5-0 record, dominating every opponent in sight and securing one of two Finals berths from the Oklahoma City bracket. Mexico earned the second ticket, while China went 4-1 and claimed the Wild Card spot to also advance to Brisbane.

A Historic Margin of Dominance

The numbers from OKC were staggering. Team USA didn't just win — they run-ruled multiple opponents, outscoring the field by absurd margins over five games. On Day 2 alone, the U.S. outscored its opponents 25-0 across two games, beating Brazil 17-0 in just four innings and the Netherlands 8-0 in five.

The Opening Round results told the same story every game:

  • USA def. Mexico, 11-0
  • USA def. Brazil, 17-0 (4 innings, run rule)
  • USA def. Netherlands, 8-0 (5 innings, run rule)
  • USA def. New Zealand, 9-0 (run rule)

USA finished Group C undefeated and ranked No. 1 coming out of Oklahoma City.

Oklahoma Paved the Way

Oklahoma led all collegiate programs with four players on the Group C roster: Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell. The Sooners were well represented in a tournament held just miles from their own campus at Love's Field, giving the Oklahoma City crowd plenty of reason to cheer.

The 16-player Women's National Team roster blended Olympic veterans with standout recent college players from programs including Oklahoma, UCLA, Tennessee, Texas, and Alabama — a depth chart that makes Team USA the clear favorite heading into the Finals.

Next Stop: Brisbane, April 2027

The 2026 Group Stage was only the first chapter. The 2027 Women's Softball World Cup Finals are set for April 5–11 in Redcliffe (Brisbane), Australia — and Team USA will be there alongside Mexico, China (Wild Card), and the Group Stage qualifiers from the Prague bracket.

The two-stage format is part of the WBSC's renewed emphasis on building toward the Los Angeles 2028 Olympics, where softball returns to the Olympic stage. For Team USA, every game from OKC to Brisbane to LA28 is part of the same mission.

Based on what they showed at Devon Park, that mission looks well within reach.

What's Next

Team USA's attention now shifts to preparation for the April 2027 Finals in Brisbane. College players on the national team roster will return to their programs for fall ball, with several — including the Oklahoma quartet — suiting up for fall games beginning as early as September 30. On the international stage, the path to LA28 runs directly through Brisbane, and Team USA left Oklahoma City looking like the team everyone will be chasing.
