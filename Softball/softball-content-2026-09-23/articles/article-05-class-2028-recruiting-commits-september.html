Class of 2028 Softball Recruiting Heats Up — Multiple Power-Five Commits Announced in September

By Emily Rawlings | September 23, 2026

September 1 marked the official opening of the contact period for current high school juniors, and the Class of 2028 wasted no time making its presence felt. In just the first three weeks of the month, a wave of notable commits have been announced across Power Five programs — signaling that one of the most anticipated recruiting classes in recent memory is already taking shape.

Notable September 2028 Commits

Several names drew attention as programs began locking in their 2028 targets:

  • Michaela Landers — Committed to Texas. Landers is among the top-rated names in the 2028 class, highlighted by On3 as one of the elite prospects at her position.
  • Peyton Ushery — Five-star outfielder committed to Georgia. A high-ceiling prospect at one of softball's premier SEC programs.
  • Kate McCartney — Infielder committed to Oklahoma. One of two 2028 infielders the Sooners have landed this month.
  • Gia Ryan — Infielder also committed to Oklahoma, giving Patty Gasso's program two Class of 2028 commits in quick succession.
  • Lainie Keefer — Four-star power hitter committed to Texas Tech, continuing the Red Raiders' push to build through elite recruiting.
  • Siena Myers — Four-star catcher committed to South Carolina, giving the Gamecocks a top-rated backstop for the class.

On3 has identified Landers, Kylie Holden, and Sophia Schlader as among the most highly regarded names in the 2028 class overall.

The 2027 Class Is Already One of the Best

The 2028 recruiting buzz comes on top of an already elite 2027 class taking shape. LSU currently leads the 2027 team recruiting rankings with a composite score of 96.2, followed by Oklahoma and Oregon. These programs are not just winning now — they are stacking rosters for years to come.

For context, September 1 represents a pivotal moment in the recruiting calendar. NCAA rules allow Division I coaches to make direct contact with high school juniors for the first time, which typically triggers a surge of public commitment announcements from prospects who have been in contact with programs for months.

What This Means for the Programs

Texas, which just won back-to-back national championships, adding Landers to their 2028 class is a signal that the pipeline continues. Oklahoma, still stinging from missing the 2026 WCWS, landing two infielders in McCartney and Ryan shows Gasso's recruiting hasn't skipped a beat. Georgia's addition of a five-star outfielder in Ushery adds to what should be a formidable future roster in Athens.

Every September commitment reshapes the competitive landscape of college softball — not for this season, but for 2028, 2029, and beyond.

What's Next

The 2028 recruiting class will continue to take shape through the fall and into 2027. September is typically the loudest month for junior-class commitments, so more major announcements are expected in the coming weeks. Keep an eye on On3, D1Softball, and 247Sports for updated rankings as programs continue to lock in their 2028 targets. The future of college softball is being decided right now.
