Tennessee Opens Fall Ball Thursday With One of Softball's Scariest Pitching Staffs

By Sarah Mitchell | September 23, 2026

If you're looking for a program built to compete for a national title in 2027, start with Tennessee — and start with the pitching.

The Lady Vols open their 2026 fall schedule this Thursday, September 25, hosting Walters State at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium. All home fall games are free, making this week the perfect opportunity to get your first look at a Tennessee team coming off yet another deep postseason run.

The Mardjetko-Nuwer Combination

In 2026, Tennessee made its fourth Women's College World Series semifinal appearance in five seasons. The engine of that run was the pitching circle.

Sage Mardjetko finished the year 16-3 with a 1.26 ERA and 171 strikeouts, earning NFCA First Team All-America honors. Paired with Erin Nuwer, the two combined for 31 wins and eight shutouts across the season. That is not just a good pitching tandem — that is the kind of one-two punch that wins championships.

Both are back for 2027.

For opposing programs that spent the summer strategizing about how to beat Tennessee, the answer hasn't changed: you have to solve Mardjetko first. And through two-plus seasons, very few teams have done it consistently.

Fall Schedule at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium

Tennessee has eight fall games on the schedule, seven at home and one road trip to Murfreesboro:

  • Sept. 25 — Walters State
  • Sept. 27 — Lipscomb
  • Oct. 2 — Carson-Newman
  • Oct. 9 — NE Alabama
  • Oct. 10 — NW Florida State College
  • Oct. 11 — Roane State
  • Oct. 18 — at Middle Tennessee (road)
  • Oct. 23 — Lee University

Every home game at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium is free and open to the public. Fall ball in Knoxville has become one of the better-kept secrets in college softball — the atmosphere is genuine and the talent on the field is real.

A Program in a Consistent Window

What Tennessee has built over the past five seasons is impressive from any angle. Four WCWS semifinal appearances. An elite pitching staff. A recruiting pipeline that has kept the Lady Vols among the SEC's elite year after year.

Fall 2026 represents another opportunity to see where this group lands on the national title pecking order — and right now, with Mardjetko and Nuwer healthy and motivated, Tennessee belongs in any serious conversation about who wins it all in 2027.

What's Next

Tennessee opens fall play Thursday, September 25 at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium — free admission. The Lady Vols will use the fall schedule to evaluate their depth beyond Mardjetko and Nuwer while continuing to develop offensive contributors who can support one of softball's elite pitching staffs.
