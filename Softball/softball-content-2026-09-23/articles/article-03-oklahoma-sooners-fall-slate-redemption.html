Oklahoma Softball Opens Fall Slate Sept. 30 — Gasso's Sooners Eye 2027 Redemption

By Jordan Reeves | September 23, 2026

Motivation is not something Oklahoma softball will be lacking this fall.

After a rare absence from the Women's College World Series in 2026, Patty Gasso and the Sooners have spent the offseason rebuilding, recruiting, and refocusing. That process gets its first public showcase on Tuesday, September 30, when Oklahoma opens its 2026 fall schedule at Love's Field against Tarleton State at 6:30 p.m. CT.

All eight fall competitions are free and open to fans — a chance for the OU faithful to get their first look at the new-look 2027 roster that Gasso has assembled.

A Program With Something to Prove

Let's be direct: missing the WCWS is not what Oklahoma does. The Sooners have been one of the most consistent programs in college softball history under Gasso, and the 2026 absence stung. But programs of Oklahoma's caliber don't wallow — they reload.

The 2027 roster is being built with that chip firmly in place. New additions alongside returning talent give Gasso options she'll spend the fall evaluating. The Sept. 30 opener against Tarleton State is the first concrete evidence of where this group stands.

Full Fall Schedule at Love's Field

Oklahoma's fall slate runs from September 30 through November 4, with all eight games played at Love's Field:

  • Sept. 30 — Tarleton State (6:30 p.m. CT, intrasquad Battle Series also included)
  • Additional games through Nov. 4 vs. University of Science and Arts of Oklahoma (USAO), Seminole State College, and East Texas A&M

Admission is free for all fall games. The intimate setting of Love's Field gives fans a direct look at how Gasso is deploying her pieces heading into the 2027 regular season.

Oklahoma Represented at the World Cup

The Sooners haven't been idle since the regular season ended. Oklahoma led all collegiate programs with four players on the USA Softball Women's National Team roster for the WBSC World Cup Group Stage event held September 12–16 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City: Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell.

Team USA went 5-0 in OKC and earned a berth in the 2027 World Cup Finals in Brisbane. Having four Sooners participate — at a tournament played in their own backyard — provided a confidence-building moment for the program heading into fall practice.

What's Next

Oklahoma opens fall ball September 30 at Love's Field — free, live, and no shortage of story lines to watch. How Gasso deploys her returning players against new roster additions will give the first real clues about what the 2027 Sooners are built to do. For a program with national title expectations in its DNA, this fall matters.
