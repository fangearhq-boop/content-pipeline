Day 6 With Gasso Back: OU Softball Counts Down 8 Days to First Fall Game

By Sarah Mitchell September 22, 2026

The Full Staff Is Together

Patty Gasso returned to Norman on Sept. 16 following the conclusion of Group Oklahoma, where she served as head coach of the USA Women's National Team. Since then, the Sooners have been operating with their full coaching staff for the first time this fall. JT Gasso and Rocha round out the staff on the field at Love's Field daily.

Today marks Day 6 of that full-staff setup. Eight days from now, the 2027 Oklahoma Sooners play their first live game. For a program that missed the Women's College World Series in 2026 and is now rebuilding with purpose, every practice day matters. The coaching staff is watching carefully.

The Players Everyone Is Watching

Kendall Wells enters fall camp as the reigning NCAA freshman home run record holder. Her 32 home runs in 2026 set the mark for any first-year player in Division I history. She is a sophomore now. The expectations have shifted from "impressive freshman" to "this program's next star." Every swing this fall will be measured against that standard.

Sydney Berzon is the transfer anchor. The two-time All-American threw 486.1 innings at LSU with a 2.02 ERA and 406 strikeouts. She is now in crimson and cream, integrating with a new battery, new fielders behind her, and a Gasso staff that has its own way of doing things. Abby Harter, who hit .346 at Middle Tennessee State, joins as the second key transfer bat. The integration between those three players and the 12 Sooner returners is the central story of this fall camp.

What Schlader Adds to the Equation

The news from the recruiting calendar dovetails with what is happening at Love's Field. Sophia Schlader, a top-ranked pitching prospect in the 2028 class, committed to Oklahoma this week. She joins Finlee Williams (No. 1 overall outfielder) and Valeria Saenz as pillars of Gasso's 2028 class. The pipeline is building even as the present is being assembled.

For the coaches watching fall ball, the knowledge that help is coming in 2028 does not change the urgency of 2027. But for the fan base, it signals that the rebuild has a plan. Gasso has been through WCWS misses before and responded. The 2027 roster has the talent to respond.

What's Next

Sept. 30 marks the Sooners' first fall game at Love's Field. All eight fall contests are at general admission pricing. The first real look at how Wells, Berzon, and Harter perform together in live action will tell coaches and fans a great deal about how this roster fits. After the first game, the Sooners face outside opponents through Nov. 4 at Love's Field.
