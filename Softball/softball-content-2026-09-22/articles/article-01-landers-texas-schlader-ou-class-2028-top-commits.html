Landers to Texas, Schlader to OU: The 2028 Class Just Lost Its Two Biggest Names

By Jordan Reeves September 22, 2026

The Week That Changed the 2028 Class

In the span of four days, the two most-watched uncommitted players in the 2028 softball recruiting class made their decisions. Michaela Landers, Softball America's No. 1 overall prospect, committed to Texas on Sept. 18. Sophia Schlader, a top-rated pitching prospect who had been on every blue-blood's radar, chose Oklahoma.

Day 13 of the Sept. 9 in-person window has produced the biggest one-week haul since the window opened. And the ripple effects will be felt for years. Texas adds a future cornerstone to a roster that just won back-to-back titles. Oklahoma gives Patty Gasso a top 2028 pitcher to anchor a class already featuring multiple elite commits.

Who Is Michaela Landers?

Landers is a shortstop and middle infielder from Waxahachie, Texas. In 2026, she hit .639 with 25 home runs and 52 extra-base hits in 41 high school games. Those 25 home runs broke the Dallas-area high school record. She has already represented the USA at the U15 level, helping the team win the Pan American Championship in Mexico.

Scouts describe her as a five-tool player: elite arm strength, lightning-quick reactions, plus-plus range defensively, real raw power, and above-average speed. She visited Texas, Florida, and Oklahoma before picking the Longhorns. The hometown pull was real. Georgetown, Texas is 30 miles north of Austin.

Texas already has Torres (.530 BA, 16 HR at FSU in 2026) committed for fall 2027. Pairing Torres's present with Landers's future may be the most seamless transition a program has ever assembled.

What Schlader to OU Means for Gasso's Rebuild

Oklahoma missed the Women's College World Series in 2026 for the first time in years. Gasso has been building the 2027 and 2028 classes with urgency. The 2028 class already includes Finlee Williams (No. 1 overall outfielder by multiple outlets) and Valeria Saenz. Now Schlader joins as a top-ranked pitching arm.

Pitching has been the question mark hanging over OU's rebuild. Sydney Berzon (2x All-American, 2.02 ERA at LSU) arrives this fall as the stopgap. Schlader gives Gasso a pipeline answer for 2029 and beyond. The 2028 OU class is shaping up as one of the top in the country.

For softball recruiting watchers, it is hard to overstate how rare it is to land a top-ranked pitcher and a top-ranked position player in the same in-person window. Gasso has done exactly that.

Who Is Still Out There?

The 2028 class still has uncommitted names at the top. Kylie Holden and a handful of other five-star prospects remain available. Programs that missed out on Landers and Schlader are regrouping. The window stays open, and mid-October should bring a new wave of rankings updates and commitments.

The 1,226 nominations to Extra Innings Softball's class ranking set a record for the 2028 cycle. This class is deep. The top tier may be gone, but the second and third tier is loaded.

What's Next

Watch for Kylie Holden's decision in the coming weeks. Mid-October rankings updates from Softball America and Line Drive Media will reshuffle the leaderboard. Texas and Oklahoma both appear positioned for top-five class rankings when the cycle closes. And for fans of either program, the 2028 season is already worth circling on the calendar.
