195 Days to Redcliffe: Eight Nations Set for the 2027 WBSC Finals

By Emily Rawlings September 22, 2026

The Field Is Complete

Group Oklahoma delivered its results, and the bracket for the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals is now complete. Eight nations will compete at Talobilla Park in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia from April 5-11, 2027. Redcliffe is 45 minutes north of Brisbane. And this tournament matters beyond just a world title: it is an LA28 Olympic qualifier.

USA and Mexico won direct berths out of Group Oklahoma. China secured the Wild Card. Australia, Canada, Japan, Puerto Rico, and Chinese Taipei had already pre-qualified. Eight nations. One crown. And 195 days to figure out who wants it most.

USA's Case for the Title

The Americans went 5-0 at Group Oklahoma, capping the group stage with a 7-0 run-rule win over China. Maya Brady was the story of that final match: three home runs, five RBI. Gabby Garcia was dominant out of the circle, retiring all nine batters she faced with six strikeouts. The combination of an elite offense and a dominant arm gave USA a tournament that felt more like a statement than a qualification.

Garcia and Valerie Mulipola bring Tokyo 2020 silver-medal experience to the roster. Four Oklahoma Sooners (Brito, Coleman, Jennings, Maxwell) give the team a core of players who work together daily under Patty Gasso, who also serves as head coach of the WNT. That dual role creates a continuity that few national teams can replicate.

The Threats on the Other Side of the Bracket

Japan has won more World Cup titles than any nation in history. Australia is hosting on home soil at Talobilla Park, which will create an atmosphere that no visiting team will be prepared for on day one. China proved they belong in the top tier with a Wild Card berth. Canada and Chinese Taipei both have players who compete at the college and professional level in the United States.

This is not a tournament the USA should pencil in as a formality. Every game at Redcliffe will be contested. The margin between USA and the field at a World Cup is thinner than it looks from the Group Oklahoma results.

What's Next

USA will not reconvene as a full national team until closer to April. In the meantime, Gasso returns to building the 2027 Oklahoma program while the WNT's other college players continue fall ball on their respective campuses. The next milestone on the road to Redcliffe is announcing the 2027 national team coaching staff and spring training schedule, expected in early 2027. April 5 at Talobilla Park is the destination. The preparation starts now.
