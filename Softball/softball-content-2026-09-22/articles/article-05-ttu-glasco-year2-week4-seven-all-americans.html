Glasco's Avengers Hit Week 4: TTU's Seven All-Americans Enter First Live Action

By Sarah Mitchell September 22, 2026

From Practice to Games: Week 4 Changes Everything

Three weeks of fall camp have been practice, film, repetitions, and culture-building. Week 4 is different. This is game week for Texas Tech, and the seven All-Americans Gerry Glasco assembled are about to find out what they look like when the other team is actually trying to get them out.

The Red Raiders were the 2026 Women's College World Series runner-up. They lost to Texas in the final. Glasco took over a program in transition and got it to the national title game in Year 1. Year 2 has a different pressure attached to it: contenders do not get to show up and be satisfied with second place twice.

The All-American Trinity

The three headliners are from three different programs, and each brings a profile unlike the others. Olivia Beachum transferred from Florida State, where she hit .406 with 10 home runs, 69 RBI, and earned All-ACC First Team. She is the most consistent bat on the roster. Morgan Pickering came from Oklahoma, ranked as the No. 2 transfer in the portal. In her career she hit .384 with 50 home runs and 169 RBI. Her power sets the tone in the lineup.

Kat Heavener is the left-handed starting pitcher from LSU. She went 13-9 with a 2.81 ERA and 128 strikeouts. TTU's pitching staff was always good enough to win games in 2026 but not dominant enough to close out the title game. Heavener is the answer. Kassidy Terry, Williams, Pannell, and Polar round out the seven All-Americans. This is not one or two standouts carrying a team. This is depth at every level of the roster.

What Week 4 Reveals

Three weeks of fall camp tell coaches how players move, how they communicate, and how they handle correction. Live games tell them something else: who competes. Some players look different the moment there is a score on the board. That is information Glasco cannot get from practice. This week, the Red Raiders will begin to show which players raise their level when it counts.

Star Gutierrez, the first TTU commit in the 2028 class, is watching from a distance. The future of the program is being built even as the present is being tested. Glasco's Year 2 has a clear goal. The work of getting there starts this week.

What's Next

Texas Tech's fall schedule continues through late October with several exhibition games. The full regular-season schedule gets underway in February 2027. Between now and then, Glasco wants the All-American chemistry to solidify into something greater than the sum of its parts. Week 4 is the first real test of whether that is happening on time.
