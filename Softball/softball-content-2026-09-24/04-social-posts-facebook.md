### STORY 2: Texas Longhorns — 5 Days to McCombs

**FB Long-Form** | 11:00 AM ET

Five days from now, two of the most decorated players in college softball history will take the field together in burnt orange for the very first time.

Isa Torres is the 2026 national Player of the Year — .530 batting average, 16 home runs, 56 RBI. She transferred to Texas from Florida State this offseason and is the most coveted hitting transfer in the history of the portal. Teagan Kavan is the only player ever to win the WCWS Most Outstanding Player award twice. She's back for one more run.

Together, they headline a Texas team that went 53-12 last year, captured back-to-back WCWS titles, and is now chasing something only three programs have ever done: three consecutive national championships. Only UCLA (1988-90) and Oklahoma (2021-23) have pulled off the three-peat.

Tuesday, September 29, 6 PM CT at McCombs Field, free admission. Come see the two-time defending champs start their bid for history.

### STORY 3: Oklahoma Sooners — 6 Days to Love's Field

**FB Long-Form** | 3:00 PM ET

The Oklahoma Sooners open fall ball in six days — and if their roster is any indication, Gasso's program hasn't lost a step.

Wells, Berzon, and Harter headline nine newcomers integrating with a dozen returning Sooners. Jordyn Wells set the NCAA freshman home run record with 32 homers last year at a different school. Jordan Berzon racked up 406 strikeouts and a 2.02 ERA at LSU. Taylor Harter hit .346 at Middle Tennessee State. Now they're all in Norman, learning Gasso's system.

The Sept. 30 opener at Love's Field vs. Tarleton State (6:30 PM, $10.88) will be the first public look at this new Sooners roster. Four Battle Series intrasquad games plus matchups against USAO, Seminole State, and East Texas A&M round out the fall schedule through November 4.

Oklahoma didn't make the WCWS last year. That absence has driven this offseason's rebuilding effort. The fall opener is the first step toward proving they belong back at the top.

The NFCA released the 2027 Leadoff Classic schedule this week — and Texas Tech is in it.

That means Gerry Glasco's seven All-Americans will get one of the most coveted early-season showcase spots in college softball. Jaysoni Beachum, Kasidi Pickering, Jayden Heavener, and four more decorated players will take the field in front of a national audience earlier in 2027 than almost any other program.

The stage matters. Texas Tech was the 2026 WCWS runner-up — one game away from a national title. Glasco is entering Year 2, and the expectation is that the transfer-rich culture built in Year 1 translates into a championship in Year 2. The Leadoff Classic will be one of the first real data points.

Seven All-Americans on one roster is genuinely unprecedented in recent program history. The Leadoff Classic will give everyone a first look at whether they play like one team — or seven individual stars. If it's the former, Texas Tech could be the team to beat in 2027.
