Joley Bennett to LSU, Kylie Holden Remains the 2028 Class's Last Big Uncommitted

By Sarah Mitchell | September 24, 2026

The 2028 softball recruiting class is moving fast — and the latest update has Joley Bennett committed to LSU.

Bennett, a 2028 infielder described as the top prospect from Louisiana in her class, chose the Tigers as her home program. It's another elite signing for a recruiting class that has been defined by its depth and pace since the September 9 in-person contact window opened.

A Class Taking Shape

The list of committed prospects since the window opened three weeks ago reads like a who's who of future college softball stars. Michaela Landers — the class's No. 1 overall prospect — committed to Texas. Sophia Schlader committed to Oklahoma. The schools accumulating talent include Florida State, Georgia, Tennessee, Florida, South Carolina, Texas Tech, Arizona, and now LSU.

Programs like Texas and Oklahoma — both of which landed elite top-tier prospects in Landers and Schlader respectively — have used this window to secure cornerstones for their 2028 classes. For LSU, Bennett represents a homegrown building block at a position where depth and defensive range define championship rosters.

The class itself has drawn extraordinary attention from evaluators. A record 1,226 players received Extra Inning Softball nominations in the 2028 cycle — a sign of just how deep the talent pool runs at the high school level this year.

Who's Left: Kylie Holden

As the major wave of elite commitments settles, one name stands out as the remaining prize: Kylie Holden. National recruiting observers have identified Holden as the top uncommitted prospect in the 2028 class — a player whose decision, whenever it comes, will generate significant coverage.

These situations often play out quietly. A player at Holden's level receives attention from virtually every major program, and the decision-making process is deliberate. The in-person contact window runs through mid-October, and her announcement — if it comes before it closes — will land with weight in the recruiting world.

What's Next

Mid-October brings the first official class rankings update for 2028, which will give the college softball world its first formal look at who committed where and how programs rank against each other in the recruiting cycle. Where Holden lands will be one of the defining storylines of that update. The class of 2028 is shaping up to be one of the deepest in recent memory — and it's still not finished. Watch for more commits in the next three weeks as programs race to close their classes before the window ends.
