Six Days to Love's Field: Oklahoma's Reloaded Roster Gets Its First Public Test

By Emily Rawlings | September 24, 2026

For the Oklahoma Sooners, six days is a short countdown to a very important moment.

When Patty Gasso's team takes the field on September 30 at Love's Field against Tarleton State (6:30 PM), it will be the first public look at a roster that has been completely rebuilt since the 2026 season. After an offseason defined by significant transfer portal activity, Oklahoma enters fall ball with nine new faces alongside twelve returners — and plenty of questions about how it all fits together.

The New Additions Who Have to Deliver

Three names dominate the conversation around this year's newcomers.

Jordyn Wells arrives from her previous program after setting the NCAA record for home runs by a freshman with 32. That production at the collegiate level, at the youngest age possible, signals exactly the kind of power hitter that Oklahoma's lineup needed. Wells will be expected to hit near the top of the order and protect the middle of the lineup from her first day in crimson and cream.

Jordan Berzon brings pitching depth from LSU — and not just depth, but star power. Berzon posted 406 strikeouts and a 2.02 ERA at LSU, earning multiple All-America honors in the process. At Oklahoma, she joins a rotation that now needs her to be a true ace-level contributor from day one.

Taylor Harter hit .346 at Middle Tennessee State and provides versatility and contact ability that rounds out the offense. Three major pieces, all of them arriving with credentials that demand immediate production.

Gasso's System and the WCWS Absence

Oklahoma didn't make the Women's College World Series last year. In a program that had gone to Oklahoma City for 10 consecutive seasons, that absence stung — and it has driven this offseason's recruitment and retooling effort more than anything else.

Gasso spent part of her summer with the USA Women's National Team at the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage in Oklahoma City. That experience returned her to Norman energized and focused. Full-staff practice began September 17, and the past week has been about installing systems, building chemistry, and getting Wells, Berzon, and Harter to understand the Sooner way of doing things.

Tuesday's game won't reveal everything. But it will reveal something. Watching nine newcomers respond to their first competitive environment in Oklahoma uniforms tells a coaching staff things that practice simply cannot.

What's Next

After the Tarleton State opener, Oklahoma's fall schedule includes intrasquad Battle Series games and matchups against USAO (Oct. 23), Seminole State (Oct. 30), and East Texas A&M (Nov. 4). Individual tickets are $10.88 per game; the All Fall Pass covering all eight events is $70.69. The countdown ends Tuesday night at Love's Field — and the 2027 season's first real data points begin.
