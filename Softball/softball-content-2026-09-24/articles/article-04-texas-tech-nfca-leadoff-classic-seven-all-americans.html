Texas Tech Lands NFCA Leadoff Classic Spot — Seven All-Americans Get Marquee Stage

By Jordan Reeves | September 24, 2026

The NFCA released the 2027 Leadoff Classic schedule this week — and Texas Tech is in it.

For Gerry Glasco's Red Raiders, an invitation to one of the sport's most prestigious early-season showcases is both a reward for 2026 and a challenge for 2027. Texas Tech was the WCWS runner-up last year. Now the program is entering Glasco's second season with the most decorated roster in its history and the full weight of national championship expectations riding on every decision.

Seven All-Americans, One Roster

The collection of talent assembled in Lubbock this offseason is extraordinary. Seven All-Americans are practicing together under one coaching staff — a number that simply doesn't happen in college softball.

The headliners are clear. Jaysoni Beachum brought a .406 batting average, 10 home runs, and 69 RBI from Florida State. Kasidi Pickering is one of the most productive hitters in recent college softball history — .384 batting average and 50 home runs from Oklahoma, paired with plate discipline that saw her draw at least 40 walks in each of her last three seasons. Jayden Heavener is the left-handed pitcher who went 13-9 with a 2.81 ERA and 128 strikeouts at LSU.

That's three first-year Red Raiders who were All-Americans at programs that finished ahead of Texas Tech in 2025. Now they're all in red and black together. The Leadoff Classic will be one of the first times a national audience sees them compete as a unit.

Why Year Two Is When It Gets Serious

The first year under a new coaching staff is always about installation. Culture, language, system, trust — all of it has to be built from scratch when a new group of talented players arrives from different programs. Glasco spent Year 1 doing exactly that, and the results were impressive: WCWS runner-up, one game from a national championship.

Year 2 is different. The vocabulary is shared. The expectations are established. The question is whether all that talent — built from Beachum, Pickering, and Heavener plus returners like Kaitlyn Terry, Mia Williams, and Taylor Pannell — translates into a championship when the competition gets its hardest.

The Leadoff Classic won't answer that. But it will give observers their first real look at how Texas Tech plays when the setting is elevated and the opponent is prepared. That's valuable, and Glasco knows it.

What's Next

Texas Tech's fall schedule is currently underway, with the Leadoff Classic slotted for early in the 2027 spring season. Fall games this month and in October will give Glasco's staff a chance to develop chemistry and evaluate depth before the spring ramp-up begins. The expectation in Lubbock is clear: seven All-Americans should translate into a national championship. The Leadoff Classic will be one of the first real tests of whether this roster can back that expectation up.
