Tennessee Opens Fall Ball Tomorrow — Mardjetko-Nuwer Duo Gets First 2027 Test

By Jordan Reeves | September 24, 2026

Tomorrow night, one of the most anticipated pitching partnerships in college softball returns to action.

Tennessee opens its 2026 fall ball slate Thursday at 6 PM CT against Walters State at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium. It's the first time Sage Mardjetko and Erin Nuwer will pitch in a game setting since the 2026 Women's College World Series — and for Lady Vols fans who watched what they did together last spring, that's a reason to pay attention.

A Pitching Duo That Earned Every Accolade

Mardjetko's 2026 season was remarkable by any standard. She finished 16-3 with a 1.26 ERA and 171 strikeouts, earning NFCA First Team All-America honors. She wasn't a supporting act — she was the engine of a Tennessee rotation that gave the Lady Vols legitimate national title contention.

Nuwer complemented her perfectly. Together the two combined for 31 wins and 8 shutouts, a total that puts them among the most productive one-two pitching combinations in the country in 2026. Both are returning for 2027, and both will be counted on to carry Tennessee's rotational workload again.

Why Fall Ball Matters

Fall games aren't officially counted in a program's record, and the competition level is lighter than the spring schedule. But that misses the point. For a coaching staff trying to integrate new players, experiment with lineups, and test pitchers on stamina and command after a summer away from the game, fall ball is valuable information.

For Mardjetko and Nuwer specifically, tomorrow's game is about shaking rust. It's about getting the mechanics clicking and the strike zone dialed in before October games arrive and coaches start seeing clearer pictures of how the rotation will look come February.

Tennessee's fall schedule stretches through early November and includes a handful of home games at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium — all of which are chances to evaluate depth, rotate arms, and set the tone for 2027.

What's Next

Tomorrow's opener is the beginning of a month-plus evaluation window for Tennessee's coaching staff. If Mardjetko and Nuwer look like themselves early in the fall, the Lady Vols enter 2027 with legitimate claims to a top-five national ranking. The programs watching them most closely — Texas, Oklahoma, UCLA, and Florida State — will all be doing the same thing this fall: taking stock of who looks sharp and who still needs work. For Tennessee, the expectation heading into tomorrow is clear. Let's see if the pitching is as good as advertised.
