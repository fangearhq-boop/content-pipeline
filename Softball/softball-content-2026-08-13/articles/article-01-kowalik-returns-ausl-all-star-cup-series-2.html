Kowalik Is Back — and She's Leading the 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup After Series 2

By Sarah Mitchell

August 13, 2026

Reports suggesting that Kayla Kowalik — the 2025 AUSL All-Star Cup champion — was absent from the entire 2026 competition were wrong. Kowalik was unavailable for Series 1 (August 8-10), but she returned for Series 2 as a team captain at the Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois. Team Kowalik went unbeaten through the second series, and Kowalik is now the individual points leader in the 2026 championship race.

After a week that had the softball world wondering who would fill the void left by the defending champion, Kowalik answered the question herself: she did. The 2025 All-Star Cup winner has reclaimed her place in the competition and, based on Series 2, she intends to win it again.

How Series 2 Captains Were Set

The AUSL All-Star Cup format determines captains by individual performance. After each series concludes, the top three players in the overall point standings are named captains and draft entirely new teams for the following series. For Series 2, that meant Kowalik earned a captain's spot by accumulating enough points to lead the field — a remarkable position given she entered the competition a series late.

Kowalik was joined in captaining Series 2 teams by Jailey Willis and Jazmyn Falby. The redraft format meant all 45 players in the All-Star Cup pool entered a new competition under new team configurations, with each captain selecting their squad from scratch. That's part of what makes the individual race so compelling — team loyalty is temporary, but points are permanent.

Where Sami Williams Stands

Sami Williams — the AUSL's 2026 regular-season Hitter of the Year — won Series 1 MVP honors after delivering a clutch two-RBI double in the third inning of the Series 1 opener on August 8 for Team Garcia. That MVP1 performance earned her 60 individual points, positioning her as one of the early leaders before Kowalik returned for Series 2.

With Kowalik now in the competition and accumulating points at a rapid rate, Williams faces a real deficit heading into the final two series. But the race is far from over — two full series remain, each offering multiple MVP opportunities and win bonuses that can shift the standings significantly.

What's Ahead

Series 3 is scheduled to begin August 24 at the Parkway Bank Sports Complex, with a new captain trio determined by the Series 2 final standings. Series 4 and the conclusion of the 2026 All-Star Cup follow in the final week of August, with the event wrapping up August 30. All games broadcast on ESPN+, MLB Network, and CBS.

The full individual championship picture will become clear as the final two series play out. What is already clear: Kayla Kowalik is in this race, she is leading it, and the competition for the 2026 title just got significantly more interesting.
