30 Days to Oklahoma City: USA Softball's World Cup Group Stage Is Almost Here

By Sarah Mitchell

August 13, 2026

Thirty days from today, the USA Women's National Team opens the 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. September 12 through 16, the same venue where Team USA went 8-2 at the International Cup just two weeks ago, becomes the site of the first meaningful step toward the 2027 World Cup Finals in Brisbane.

The 16-player final roster has been set since July 1, 2026. With a month remaining, the preparation phase is giving way to the competition mindset. This is a group that has played together at high levels recently — and they're returning to familiar ground.

The Roster Leading the Charge

Two of the most decorated players in USA Softball history anchor the squad: Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola, both members of the 2020 U.S. Olympic Team. Garcia brings pitching depth alongside Karlyn Pickens and Kelly Maxwell. Mulipola's presence behind the plate provides the kind of veteran leadership that matters in tournament settings where every inning is high-stakes.

The roster also introduces two players making their international debuts: Kendall Wells from Oklahoma and Taylor Shumaker from Florida. Seven athletes from the 2025 World Games gold medal squad are on the final roster, giving this group a blend of tested international experience and fresh energy heading into the competition.

Group Oklahoma: The Road to Brisbane

The United States competes in Group Oklahoma alongside the Netherlands (No. 6 in the world), Mexico (No. 7), China (No. 8), Brazil, and New Zealand. Brazil replaced South Africa in the group. The format sends the top two finishers from Group Oklahoma to the 2027 World Cup Finals in Brisbane, Australia, scheduled for April 5-11, 2027.

The USA is the No. 2 team in the world and enters Group Oklahoma as the heavy favorite to advance. But the competition is not routine — the Netherlands, Mexico, and China have all been competitive at the international level in recent years, and every game in a World Cup group stage carries higher stakes than a friendly or exhibition.

Devon Park as a Proving Ground

The International Cup result — 8-2, at the same facility, against an overlapping set of competition — is exactly the kind of preparation window the coaching staff wanted heading into September. The venue is familiar. The travel logistics are known. The players have played in front of Oklahoma City crowds before.

For USA Softball, the Group Stage is not just about qualifying for Brisbane — it's about establishing the form and rotation depth that will carry the program through the 2027 Finals. How Garcia, Pickens, and Maxwell are deployed in Group Oklahoma will shape how the team is built heading into the World Cup.

What's Next

The 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage runs September 12-16 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma. The top two finishers from Group Oklahoma advance to the 2027 World Cup Finals in Brisbane, Australia (April 5-11, 2027). For schedules, rosters, and live results, visit usasoftball.com and wbsc.org.
