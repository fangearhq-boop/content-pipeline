### STORY 4: USA WNT — 30 Days to OKC

#### Long-Form Post
**Window:** 5:30 PM ET

One month from today, the USA Women's National Team opens the 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage in Oklahoma City.

September 12, Devon Park. The same venue where the USA went 8-2 at the International Cup just two weeks ago.

The 16-player roster is set and it's deep. Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola bring Olympic experience. Kelly Maxwell and Karlyn Pickens anchor the pitching. Kendall Wells from Oklahoma and Taylor Shumaker from Florida make their international debuts.

The competition in Group Oklahoma: Netherlands, Mexico, China, Brazil, and New Zealand. The top two finishers from the group advance to the 2027 World Cup Finals in Brisbane, Australia next April.

For the USA, this isn't just a group-stage tune-up — it's the first major international competition of the two-year World Cup cycle. A dominant group-stage run sets the tone heading into Brisbane.
