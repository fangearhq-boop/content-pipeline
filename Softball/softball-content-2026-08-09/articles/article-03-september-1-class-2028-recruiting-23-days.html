September 1 Is 23 Days Away: What Happens When the D1 Recruiting Window Opens

By Jordan Reeves

August 9, 2026

The countdown is almost over. On September 1, at midnight, NCAA Division I softball coaches will be permitted to initiate direct contact with Class of 2028 recruits for the first time. Twenty-three days separate today's Class of 2028 prospects from that moment.

This is the threshold every elite softball recruit and every D1 program has been working toward all summer. Before September 1, coaches can only respond if an athlete contacts them first. After September 1, everything changes.

What Opens at Midnight

When the clock hits September 1, D1 softball coaches are permitted to call recruits directly, send texts and emails, and extend invitations for both unofficial and official campus visits. The restriction on coach-initiated contact lifts entirely and simultaneously for all programs.

That's the key phrase: simultaneously. Every program in the country — from the perennial national contenders to rebuilding mid-majors — becomes active at the same moment. A prospect at the top of multiple programs' lists will receive contact from all of them within hours of midnight.

It's an intense 48 hours for anyone ranked highly. Programs know the best recruits will have options, and no one wants to be the program that called second.

A Record-Setting Class

Extra Inning Softball has received 1,226 nominations for their Class of 2028 recognition program — the most nominations they have ever processed for any recruiting class in the company's history. That number reflects a generation of young softball players who have invested heavily in the recruiting process, traveled to showcases, submitted film, and put themselves in front of coaches well before any of those coaches could make a direct call.

The volume of nominations signals how deep this class is and how seriously the sport takes the early identification of talent. By the time September 1 arrives, programs won't be discovering Class of 2028 prospects for the first time. They'll be formalizing relationships they've been building for months.

What Recruits and Families Should Know

If you have a Class of 2028 athlete preparing for September 1, the work before the window opens matters as much as what happens on the day. Coaches have already built their call lists. The athletes at the top of those lists earned that position through their performance at summer showcases, their academics, and the game film programs have been collecting and reviewing.

September 1 doesn't end the process — it starts it. The initial wave of contact will produce early offers, but the process of finding the right fit, making visits, and signing a National Letter of Intent extends well into the following year. Families should prepare for an intense first week and then a longer, more deliberate process of evaluation on both sides.

What's Next

September 1, 2026 is 23 days away. For Class of 2028 softball recruits, the final weeks before the window opens are a critical opportunity to wrap up showcase appearances, update film, and stay visible to programs on their target list. The window opens at midnight on September 1 — and it opens for every program in D1 softball at the same time.
