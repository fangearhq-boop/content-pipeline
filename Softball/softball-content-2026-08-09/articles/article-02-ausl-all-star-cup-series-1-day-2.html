AUSL All-Star Cup Series 1: Day 2 Brings Corrick vs. Ricketts Tonight at 8 PM ET

By Emily Rawlings

August 9, 2026

The 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup is live. After Day 1 opened the competition at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois, tonight's Day 2 matchup brings another three-team round-robin game: Team Corrick faces Team Ricketts at 8 PM ET. Coverage continues on ESPN platforms, MLB Network, and CBS Sports Network.

This is professional softball in a format built entirely around the individual. No team champion is crowned at the end of August 30. Only one player walks away with the title.

The Three Captains

Series 1 is captained by three of the sport's most recognizable names. Rachel Garcia, Team 1's captain, is a two-time Olympic gold medalist and one of the most decorated pitchers in USA Softball history. Team 2 captain Keilani Ricketts is a former Oklahoma standout who has been a force in professional softball since her collegiate days. Team 3 captain Georgina Corrick brings international experience and a track record in the AU/AUSL circuit.

Each captain conducted her team's draft from the 45-player field before Series 1 began. Those rosters will be completely redrafted before Series 2 starts — the All-Star Cup resets the teams before each of the four series, ensuring that individual performance, not team loyalty, drives the leaderboard.

Kowalik's Absence Opens the Race

Any conversation about the 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup starts with who isn't here: Kayla Kowalik. The defending individual champion posted a batting average of .486 and accumulated 1,590 individual points in 2025 to claim last year's title. Kowalik is unavailable for Series 1, reshaping the early competition entirely.

Her absence doesn't make the competition weaker — it makes it more open. Without a clear defending champion on the leaderboard through Series 1, Garcia, Ricketts, and Corrick have the opportunity to set the tone that the rest of the field will have to chase through August 30.

The Format

Each of the four series features three captains, a full roster draft, and a round-robin of games at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont. Points accumulate throughout all four series into a single individual leaderboard. Series 1 runs through August 10, with the Day 3 finale between Team Ricketts and Team Garcia on Monday at 7 PM ET. Series 2, 3, and 4 follow through the end of August.

Today's schedule: Team Corrick vs. Team Ricketts, 8 PM ET. Tomorrow: Team Ricketts vs. Team Garcia, 7 PM ET.

What's Next
