34 Days to OKC: USA Softball Opens WBSC World Cup Group Stage on September 12

By Emily Rawlings

August 9, 2026

The USA Women's National Team just won the International Cup gold medal at Devon Park in Oklahoma City on August 3. In 34 days, they'll be back at the same venue for something with higher stakes: the WBSC Softball Women's World Cup Group Stage, September 12-16.

Head coach Patty Gasso leads a team that arrives at Devon Park knowing the field, knowing the mound, and knowing what it takes to win there. That familiarity is not an accident — it's an advantage.

Group C Opponents

USA (world No. 2) was drawn into Group C alongside Netherlands (No. 6), Mexico (No. 7), China (No. 8), New Zealand (No. 23), and South Africa (No. 36). The round-robin runs through September 15, with play-offs on September 16.

USA's opening game on September 12 is against Mexico, scheduled as the final game of opening day. The Netherlands and China represent the group's most credentialed opposition. New Zealand and South Africa fill the remainder of the round-robin.

For context, the WBSC World Cup Group Stage spans three cities. Prague hosted the first group phase in June; Lima hosted the second in July. Oklahoma City is the final group stage before the competition moves to the championship rounds.

The Roster

Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola — both 2020 U.S. Olympic gold medalists — headline the 16-player roster. Montana Fouts, who pitched the USA to International Cup gold with a near-perfect performance against Japan on August 3, and Maya Brady round out the core. The roster includes seven athletes from the 2025 World Games gold medal squad and 10 players who competed in the 2025 USA Down Under Series.

Gasso on the team: "This is an outstanding group of athletes, and the depth of talent in our program continues to grow. I'm excited to see what we can accomplish together as we chase gold this fall."

What's at Stake

The WBSC World Cup Group Stage isn't just a competition in isolation. Results at Oklahoma City feed directly into the 2027 Women's Softball World Cup qualification process and the pathway to LA28 Olympic competition. The top nations from the group stages advance toward the championship rounds, and standings determine seeding that affects the entire road to 2027 and 2028.

USA enters as the world's second-ranked program and the host nation, carrying momentum from International Cup gold and the energy of a home venue they've already won at this summer. Japan remains No. 1 in the world rankings.

What's Next

The WBSC Softball Women's World Cup Group Stage opens at Devon Park in Oklahoma City on September 12. USA's first game is against Mexico. The round-robin concludes September 15 and play-offs follow on September 16. Tickets and broadcast information will be available through WBSC and USA Softball official channels.
