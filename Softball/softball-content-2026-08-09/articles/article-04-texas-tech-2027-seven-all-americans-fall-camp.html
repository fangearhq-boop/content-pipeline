Texas Tech 2027: Seven All-Americans, One Roster, Fall Camp Weeks Away

By Sarah Mitchell

August 9, 2026

Texas Tech's 2027 softball roster is something the Big 12 hasn't seen before. Seven current or former All-Americans are now on the same team in Lubbock — and they've never practiced together. Fall camp is weeks away.

Head coach Gerry Glasco oversaw back-to-back WCWS Final appearances in 2025 and 2026. The team he lost those games with was talented. The team he's about to put on the field for 2027 is something different entirely.

The New Arrivals

Jaysoni Beachum arrived from Florida State with a résumé that few transfers in the history of the sport can match. In 2026, she batted .406 with 10 home runs and 69 RBI, walking 54 times while striking out just 8. She is a two-time All-American and the 2024 National Freshman of the Year. Softball America ranked her the No. 3 player in the transfer portal. FSU's program loses a generational talent in the infield.

Kasidi Pickering came from Oklahoma, where she won a national championship. In 2026, she hit .370 with 20 home runs and 61 RBI. She was ranked No. 2 in the transfer portal. Pickering gives Texas Tech an outfield presence that would start anywhere in the country.

Jayden Heavener made the move from LSU. She finished 2026 with a 13-9 record, a 2.81 ERA, and 128 strikeouts in 139.1 innings. An NFCA All-Region selection, Heavener adds frontline pitching depth to a staff that already had options.

They join returning Red Raiders Kaitlyn Terry, Mia Williams, Taylor Pannell, and Jasmyn Burns — four players who have already competed at the highest level in college softball. That's seven All-Americans on one roster.

The Question Lubbock Is Asking

This group, on paper, is extraordinary. But paper doesn't play games. Beachum has never taken a ground ball next to Williams. Pickering has never stood in the same outfield as Pannell. Heavener has never warmed up alongside a pitching staff she's just met. Fall camp is where these relationships begin.

Texas Tech has been to two WCWS Finals. They know what it takes to get there. What they haven't been able to do is win it. The 2027 roster was built specifically to solve that problem — but every championship roster requires more than talent. It requires a team that trusts itself under pressure, communicates without hesitation, and knows exactly what to do when the game is on the line.

Glasco has built the ingredients. Fall camp will start the process of turning them into something cohesive.

What's Next

Texas Tech's fall camp begins when the fall semester opens in Lubbock — a matter of weeks from now. The 2026-27 college softball season will kick off in February 2027. Between now and then, this roster has to learn to play together. Seven All-Americans sharing a dugout is unprecedented. Whether it becomes a dynasty is a question only the season can answer.
