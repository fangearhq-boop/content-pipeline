### STORY 2: AUSL All-Star Cup Series 1 Day 2

#### Long-Form Post
**Window:** 11:00 AM ET

The 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup is underway in Rosemont, Illinois, and tonight's Day 2 matchup is must-watch softball.

Team Corrick takes on Team Ricketts at 8 PM ET. Yesterday's Day 1 clash between Team Garcia and Team Corrick opened Series 1 at Parkway Bank Sports Complex. The format is unlike anything else in professional softball: three teams, 45 total players, competing in a purely individual points race. Teams will be fully redrafted before each of the four series running through August 30 — so there's no team champion, only an individual winner.

What makes Series 1 especially interesting is the absence of defending champion Kayla Kowalik. Last year, Kowalik ran away with the competition — batting .486 and accumulating 1,590 individual points to claim the title. She's sitting out Series 1. That leaves Series 1 captains Rachel Garcia, Keilani Ricketts, and Georgina Corrick to step up in the individual race. All three are legitimate contenders.

Tonight's game: Team Corrick vs. Team Ricketts, 8 PM ET. Coverage on ESPN platforms, MLB Network, and CBS Sports Network.

### STORY 3: September 1 — 23 Days Out

#### Long-Form Post
**Window:** 1:00 PM ET

Twenty-three days.

That's what stands between Class of 2028 softball recruits and the moment they've been working toward: September 1, when NCAA Division I coaches are permitted to initiate direct contact for the first time. Calls, texts, emails, unofficial and official visit invitations — all of it opens at the same moment at midnight.

Before that date, D1 coaches can only respond to athlete-initiated contact. They've been watching. They've been evaluating. They've built their lists. And when the clock strikes midnight on September 1, the phones start ringing.

This year's class has generated record-setting interest. Extra Inning Softball has received 1,226 nominations for their Class of 2028 recognition program — the most nominations they've ever processed for any recruiting class in company history.

If you're the parent of a Class of 2028 prospect, the next 23 days are about staying visible, finishing strong at any late-summer showcases, and making sure your film is current. September 1 doesn't end recruiting — it starts it. The first wave of calls will come fast.

### STORY 4: Texas Tech 2027

#### Long-Form Post
**Window:** 3:00 PM ET

Texas Tech's 2027 softball roster is unlike anything the Big 12 has seen.

Seven current or former All-Americans on a single roster. Jaysoni Beachum arrived from Florida State (.406 average, 10 home runs, 69 RBI in 2026; two-time All-American and 2024 National Freshman of the Year). Kasidi Pickering came from Oklahoma (.370 average, 20 HR, 61 RBI; All-American and national champion). Jayden Heavener made the trip from LSU (13-9, 2.81 ERA, 128 strikeouts). They join returning Red Raiders Kaitlyn Terry, Mia Williams, Taylor Pannell, and Jasmyn Burns — each with their own credential.

None of them have shared a practice field together.

Fall camp starts in the coming weeks. Head coach Gerry Glasco built this team to chase what the 2025 and 2026 teams couldn't quite finish. Texas Tech lost in the WCWS Finals both years. This roster was assembled to change that.

The talent is there. Now comes the part nobody can manufacture — chemistry, trust, and the kind of cohesion that only comes from doing the work together.

The 2027 season hasn't started yet. But Lubbock is watching.

### STORY 5: USA WNT — 34 Days to OKC

#### Long-Form Post
**Window:** 5:30 PM ET

The calendar says August 9. September 12 is 34 days away.

That's when the USA Women's National Team opens the WBSC Softball Women's World Cup Group Stage at Devon Park in Oklahoma City — the same venue where they just won International Cup gold six days ago.

USA (world No. 2) will face Mexico first on September 12, then work through a round-robin that includes Netherlands (No. 6), China (No. 8), New Zealand (No. 23), and South Africa (No. 36) before play-offs on September 16.

Head coach Patty Gasso leads a roster built around proven international performers: Rachel Garcia, Dejah Mulipola, Montana Fouts, and Maya Brady headline a group that includes seven athletes from the 2025 World Games gold medal squad.

The stakes go beyond this event. Results from the WBSC World Cup Group Stages feed directly into 2027 World Cup qualification and the pipeline for LA28 Olympic competition. These games matter.

34 days. Devon Park. USA softball.
