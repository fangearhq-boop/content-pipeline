### STORY 3: Texas Longhorns — New Coaching Staff

#### Long-Form Post
**Window:** 1:00 PM ET

The best program in college softball is not standing still.

Texas — back-to-back Women's College World Series champions — has finalized the coaching staff that will guide the Longhorns' three-peat chase in 2027. Head coach Mike White isn't just bringing in any assistants. He's bringing in coaches with head-coaching pedigree.

Rick Fremin spent 10 seasons as head coach at Southeastern Louisiana (2016-2026) before joining Texas as Associate Head Coach. That's 10 years of building a program from the top — the kind of experience that translates into recruiting relationships, staff development, and game management at the highest level.

Amber Freeman joins as hitting coach from Arizona, where the Wildcats went 85-31 in two seasons. Pitching coach Pattie Ruth Taylor returns.

And at the center of all of it: Teagan Kavan, entering her senior season as the first player in Women's College World Series history to win consecutive Most Outstanding Player awards. The 2025 MOP. The 2026 MOP. And now one more year.

Texas beat Texas Tech in the WCWS final in 2025. They beat Texas Tech in the WCWS final again in 2026. The three-peat has to start with someone stopping Kavan, and nobody has figured out how to do that yet.

The 2027 season doesn't start until February. But the staff is set. The ace is back. The chase is on. 🥎 #HookEm #WCWS

### STORY 4: USA WNT — 28 Days to World Cup

#### Long-Form Post
**Window:** 5:30 PM ET

28 days to September 12.

The USA Women's National Team opens the 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage in Oklahoma City — Devon Park — the same venue where they went 8-2 at the International Cup just two weeks ago.

This is what group play looks like: Netherlands (world No. 6), Mexico (No. 7), China (No. 8), Brazil, and New Zealand (No. 23). Six teams, top two advance. The top two finishers go to Brisbane, Australia for the 2027 World Cup Finals (April 5-11).

The USA roster — 16 athletes — is locked. Oklahoma leads all schools with four players: Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell. Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola anchor the veteran core. Kendall Wells and Taylor Shumaker make their international debuts.

Netherlands, Mexico, and China are the games to watch. None of those are guaranteed. That's what makes this group stage matter.

Sept. 12-16. Devon Park, OKC. 🥎 #SoftballUSA
