USA Softball Is 28 Days From the World Cup — Here's Who Stands in Their Way

By Sarah Mitchell

August 15, 2026

Twenty-eight days from today, the USA Women's National Team opens the 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. September 12 through September 16. The same venue where the USA went 8-2 at the International Cup just two weeks ago.

The group stage field is set, and the USA (world No. 2) faces five opponents across the preliminary round: Netherlands (No. 6), Mexico (No. 7), China (No. 8), Brazil, and New Zealand (No. 23). The top two finishers in group play advance to the 2027 World Cup Finals in Brisbane, Australia, scheduled for April 5-11, 2027.

The Threats in the Group

Netherlands, Mexico, and China represent the three toughest tests in the group. The Netherlands at No. 6 in the world have been one of the most consistent programs in international softball over the past decade, with strong pitching and disciplined at-bats that can frustrate even the best lineups. Mexico at No. 7 brings power and athleticism. China at No. 8 is capable of game-to-game surges that can disrupt a tournament bracket.

None of those three games are automatic for the USA. The group stage format — where every loss has standings implications — means the USA cannot afford to sleep through any matchup in the preliminary round, regardless of the opponent's ranking.

The 16-Player Roster

The USA carries 16 athletes for the World Cup group stage, a finalized roster led by veterans Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola alongside Kelly Maxwell and Karlyn Pickens in the pitching rotation. Kendall Wells and Taylor Shumaker both make their international debuts in the group stage — the first USA caps of their careers.

Oklahoma leads all schools with four players on the roster: Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell. UCLA has three; Kentucky and Tennessee each have two. The roster breadth reflects the depth of Power Four talent feeding USA Softball's pipeline across the country.

What's at Stake

The World Cup group stage is not just about winning games. The top two finishers earn berths to the 2027 World Cup Finals in Brisbane — the global championship event that precedes the 2028 Olympics qualification cycle. For USA Softball, the goal at Devon Park is straightforward: finish in the top two, punch the Brisbane ticket, and carry momentum into the next phase of the international calendar.

What's Next

The WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage runs September 12-16 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. The full schedule and broadcast details will be confirmed closer to the event. After the group stage, the top teams advance; for the USA, anything short of a top-two finish would be a significant disappointment. Twenty-eight days to find out.
