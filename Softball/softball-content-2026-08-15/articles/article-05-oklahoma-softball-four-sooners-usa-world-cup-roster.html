Four Sooners, One Roster — Oklahoma Leads All Schools on the USA World Cup Team

By Emily Rawlings

August 15, 2026

The transfer portal took Kasidi Pickering to Texas Tech. The 2026 season ended, players graduated, and the offseason churn began. And when the 16-athlete USA Softball roster for the 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage was announced, Oklahoma still led all schools in the country.

Alyssa Brito. Jayda Coleman. Tiare Jennings. Kelly Maxwell. Four Sooners on a 16-player national team roster — 25 percent of the squad representing the United States in Oklahoma City this September. UCLA has three players on the team. Kentucky and Tennessee each have two. Everyone else has one or none.

What Four Out of Sixteen Means

The USA Softball national program is built from the best players across the entire country. This roster includes athletes from Oklahoma, UCLA, Kentucky, Tennessee, Florida, and other programs from coast to coast. It is selected by USA Softball's coaching staff based purely on performance at the elite level.

For four players from one program to represent 25 percent of that roster is not an accident. It is the result of Patty Gasso's consistent approach to recruiting, player development, and building softball players who can compete at the international level. Oklahoma has been a USA Softball pipeline for years, and the 2026 World Cup roster confirms that status continues even through an offseason of significant roster turnover.

The Four Sooners

Kelly Maxwell is the pitching anchor of the group — an Oklahoma arm who has developed into one of the most reliable starters in USA Softball's current rotation. Jayda Coleman and Tiare Jennings represent the Sooner outfield's presence at the international level, two players who have been central to Oklahoma's offensive production in recent seasons. Alyssa Brito adds depth in the infield, giving USA Softball another versatile Sooner contributor.

Together, the four bring the kind of big-game experience that comes from competing in Norman. Oklahoma has been to the Women's College World Series in seemingly every recent season. The players who survive and thrive in that environment are equipped for the international stage.

The Oklahoma Pipeline Continues

Gasso's program is in a transitional offseason — Pickering departed for Texas Tech, the 2026 roster turned over, and the 2027 team is being rebuilt. But the World Cup roster tells a different story about Oklahoma's national standing. You can redistribute talent through the transfer portal. You cannot easily replicate what Gasso has built as a pipeline to USA Softball.

The World Cup Group Stage runs September 12-16 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City — essentially a home game for the four Sooners on the roster. If that's not home-field advantage by another name, it's the closest thing to it.

What's Next

The WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage opens September 12 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. Brito, Coleman, Jennings, and Maxwell will represent both Oklahoma and the United States in front of what figures to be a significant Sooner fanbase in the stands. The top two teams from the group stage advance to the 2027 World Cup Finals in Brisbane, Australia (April 5-11, 2027). For the USA, that is the only acceptable outcome.
