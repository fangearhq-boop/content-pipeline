Series 3 Goes to Four Teams — The 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup Expands Nine Days Out

By Sarah Mitchell

August 15, 2026

The 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup returns in nine days with its most expansive format yet. For the first time in this season's competition, Series 3 will feature four teams — captains Kayla Kowalik, Keilani Ricketts, Danieca Coffey, and Devyn Netz — when the doubleheader opens August 24 at 3:00 PM ET at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois.

Series 1 and Series 2 each ran with three teams. The jump to four changes the math on the individual race in significant ways: more games, more MVP opportunities, and more individual points available across the series. With the championship determined by the cumulative leaderboard, the expanded format could swing the standings in ways two-team-per-series competition could not.

Kowalik at No. 1 Heading In

Kayla Kowalik enters Series 3 as the individual points leader. The 2025 All-Star Cup champion was unavailable for Series 1 at the start of August — sitting out while Garcia, Ricketts, and Corrick captained the first round of teams. When she returned as a Series 2 captain, she led Team Kowalik to an unbeaten run through the second series and accumulated enough points to vault to the top of the overall leaderboard.

That kind of trajectory — missing a full series and still reaching No. 1 — is exactly what makes Kowalik the player to beat. She doesn't just compete; she compresses her production into the moments when she's active. Two series remain, and she has the individual lead and a captain's slot.

The Challengers Still in It

Sami Williams earned Series 1 MVP honors and remains in contention despite the gap that has opened. Two series' worth of points remain on the board, and Williams has shown she can rack them up when the spotlight is on her.

The Series 2 standout movers were both rookies. Emma Lemley rose 26 spots in the individual standings after the second series. Korbe Otis moved up 22 spots to reach No. 10 overall — a significant jump for a first-year player in a competition where the veteran professionals usually dominate the top of the board.

Both Lemley and Otis will enter Series 3 with momentum. In an expanded four-team format that creates more playing opportunities for every athlete in the 45-player pool, that momentum could carry forward quickly.

The Series 3 Matchups

The doubleheader on August 24 opens with Team Coffey versus Team Ricketts at 3:00 PM ET, followed by Team Netz versus Team Kowalik. Each captain will select her roster from the full player pool before the series begins, creating fresh team compositions with different chemistry and competitive dynamics than Series 1 or 2.

Keilani Ricketts, captaining for the second consecutive series, brings continuity at the captain level. Coffey and Netz step into the captain role for the first time in the 2026 Cup, while Kowalik returns to defend her individual lead. All four are established professionals whose presence at the captain level signals how competitive the Series 3 teams will be from game one.

What's Next

Series 3 opens August 24 at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois, with all games broadcast on ESPN+, MLB Network, and CBS Sports Network. The full All-Star Cup runs through August 30, with the Series 4 finale and individual championship to follow. For every player still within striking distance of Kowalik's lead, August 24 is when the last realistic chance to close the gap begins.
