Texas Finalizes 2027 Coaching Staff — Fremin, Freeman Join the Three-Peat Machine

By Emily Rawlings

August 15, 2026

The Texas Longhorns are not coasting on back-to-back Women's College World Series titles. Head coach Mike White has finalized his 2027 coaching staff with three key additions, building a supporting cast around ace Teagan Kavan's senior season and a legitimate three-peat chase.

Rick Fremin — who spent 10 seasons as head coach at Southeastern Louisiana from 2016 to 2026 — joins Texas as Associate Head Coach. Amber Freeman, who helped Arizona go 85-31 over two seasons, comes aboard as hitting coach. Pitching coach Pattie Ruth Taylor returns for continuity in the position most responsible for the Longhorns' championship pitching staff.

Head-Coaching Experience at Every Level

The Fremin hire reflects how White is approaching the three-peat attempt: with seriousness. Fremin doesn't arrive as a developmental coach learning the Power Four level. He arrives with 10 years of head-coaching experience, program-building instincts, and the recruiting relationships that come from leading a program for a decade.

Freeman's track record at Arizona adds another dimension. The Wildcats went 85-31 in her two seasons on staff — a stretch that represents genuine postseason success. Bringing that expertise into an offense that already won two consecutive championships raises the ceiling on what Texas can do in the batter's box in 2027.

The Kavan Anchor

No coaching staff announcement changes the fundamental reality at the center of Texas's 2027 season: Teagan Kavan is coming back. The right-hander is entering her senior year as the first player in the history of the Women's College World Series to win consecutive Most Outstanding Player awards — 2025 and 2026, back to back, a distinction that had never happened before in more than 70 years of the event.

Texas went 49-12 in 2026 and beat Texas Tech in the WCWS final for the second consecutive year. The Longhorns' path to a three-peat runs through Kavan's arm and through a supporting cast — both players and coaches — that White has now assembled around her final college season.

The Historical Context

Only two programs in college softball history have won three consecutive national titles: UCLA (1988-1990) and Oklahoma (2021-2023). Texas is chasing that list. The 2027 season doesn't open until February, but the infrastructure for a run at history is now in place.

The staff is set. The ace is back. For every program that finished behind Texas in 2025 and 2026, the message from Austin is clear: we are not satisfied with two.

What's Next

The 2027 Texas Longhorns softball season opens in February. Preseason polls will begin circulating in the fall, and Texas is the presumptive No. 1 pick in every ranking that has been discussed. The official roster — including portal additions Samantha Swan (catcher from Arizona State) and incoming recruits — will be confirmed when fall camp opens. Watch for early preseason honors and media day coverage to establish Texas's national narrative heading into their three-peat campaign.
