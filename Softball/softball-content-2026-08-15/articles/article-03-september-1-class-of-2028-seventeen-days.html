Seventeen Days to September 1 — The Class of 2028 Is Almost There

By Jordan Reeves

August 15, 2026

Seventeen days from now, the softball recruiting calendar will change for the Class of 2028. On September 1, Division I coaches can make direct contact — phone calls, texts, emails — with high school juniors for the first time. Until then, the class of athletes representing the most competitive recruiting cycle in recent memory waits.

The wait has been productive. A record 1,226 athletes were nominated for the Class of 2028 through Extra Inning Softball, the highest total any class has ever generated. The Extra Elite 100 position rankings are published and in the hands of every coaching staff that matters. Programs spent the summer at showcases, on the road, and on the phone with parents — every conversation stopping just short of the direct contact the September 1 window will unlock.

What the September 1 Window Actually Opens

Before September 1, Division I coaches are limited in what they can send directly to Class of 2028 prospects. Some electronic communications have been permitted since June 15. But direct calls, texts, and off-campus contacts — the recruiting methods that build real relationships and accelerate commitment timelines — are not permitted before September 1.

When that date arrives, it opens all of it at once. The coaches who have watched a prospect's film, evaluated their summer schedule, and built a mental offer list can now call directly and say what they've been thinking for months.

For top-ranked prospects, September 1 can bring a flood of calls within the first hours of the morning. For programs serious about a specific recruit, September 1 is not a soft deadline — it's the starting gun. Programs that delay risk ceding early momentum to rivals who called first.

The Final Prep Window

The next 17 days represent the last quiet stretch before the Class of 2028's recruiting season accelerates dramatically. For recruits, this is the time to finalize campus visit priorities, ensure film is updated and accessible, and verify academic eligibility requirements. The coaches calling on September 1 will arrive with information. Recruits benefit from arriving equally prepared.

For programs, the work of the summer — the evaluations, the discussions with families, the scholarship budget planning — converts into real outreach starting in 17 days. The programs that have done that work thoroughly will make their calls with confidence. The ones that haven't will find themselves behind.

What's Next

September 1 arrives in 17 days. The first wave of Class of 2028 commitments — many from prospects who have already received offers and been waiting for the contact window to build relationships before deciding — will follow shortly after the window opens. Watch for commitment announcements to spike in the first two weeks of September and track the early signing period in November 2027, when the Class of 2028's official decisions become binding.
