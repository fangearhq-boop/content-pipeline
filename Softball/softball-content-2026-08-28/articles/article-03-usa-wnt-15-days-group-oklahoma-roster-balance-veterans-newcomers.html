15 Days Out: The Roster Balance That Could Win Group Oklahoma for USA Softball

By Jordan Reeves

August 28, 2026

The WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage is 15 days away. USA Softball will take the field at Devon Park in Oklahoma City on September 12, and the team heading into Group Oklahoma City carries a specific combination of experience and depth that the coaching staff has been building toward all summer.

At the center of it: a core of proven international performers alongside newer contributors getting their biggest stage yet.

The Olympic Core

Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola are the two members of this roster who have competed in an Olympic Games. Both were part of the 2020 U.S. Olympic team that brought home the silver medal from Tokyo. That experience — of competing in the biggest moments international softball produces — is something no number of World Cup appearances fully replicates.

Seven players on this roster were also part of the 2025 World Games gold medal squad. That group provides a layer of recent international success that ties the roster together across the gap between Olympic and non-Olympic experienced players.

The Group Oklahoma Stakes

USA enters the event ranked No. 2 in the world. The six-team group includes Netherlands (No. 6), Mexico (No. 7), China (No. 8), New Zealand (No. 23), and South Africa (No. 36).

The format is unforgiving: only the top two finishers after round-robin play advance to the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland — a milestone stop on the LA28 Olympic qualification pathway. Four teams go home.

USA opens against Mexico (No. 7) on September 12. The Netherlands game — likely the most competitive matchup in the group — could determine the top seed and carry implications for how the bracket plays out in Redcliffe. Entering that game having already closed out Mexico would put USA in a position to control the tournament from the front.

The Oklahoma Connection

Four Sooners — Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell — will represent OU on the USA Softball roster. Oklahoma City will feel like home in more ways than one when the World Cup Group Stage begins at Devon Park, the same venue where USA won the 2025 International Cup gold medal.

What's Next

The WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage runs September 12-16 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. USA opens against Mexico (No. 7) on September 12. Only the top two of six advance to Redcliffe, QLD for the 2027 Finals, part of the LA28 qualification pathway. Fifteen days remain.
