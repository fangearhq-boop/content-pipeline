Three Game MVPs, One Chance: Kilfoyl Enters the AUSL Finale as the Challenger to Beat

By Jordan Reeves

August 28, 2026

When the 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup individual standings are examined at season's end, one number will stand out alongside Lexi Kilfoyl's name: three. That's how many Game MVP awards the Chicago Bandits pitcher has earned this season — more than any other player in the field.

Until Series 4, every one of those MVPs went into someone else's win column. Tonight, that changes.

The Point Architecture

The AUSL All-Star Cup scoring system rewards both statistical performance and individual excellence. Kilfoyl's 866 points break down into 136 stat points — accumulated through pitching and hitting contributions across the series — and 100 points from her three Game MVP award bonuses.

That MVP bonus accumulation is the clearest indicator of what Kilfoyl brings to a competition. Three awards across the full All-Star Cup means she has been named the best individual performer in her game, at the highest level, three times. No one in the field has done it more.

The challenge is that most of those performances came while Kilfoyl was earning points for someone else's team. In Series 3, she was on Team Klingler — who went on to extend her unbeaten captaining record. The points Kilfoyl accumulated helped Klingler build toward the 890-point lead she carries into the finale.

The Teammate-Turned-Rival Dynamic

There is something particular about competing in the finale against the player whose team you just helped win. Kilfoyl enters Series 4 as a captain for the first time this season after being on the winning side as a non-captain in Series 3.

Her all-time captaining record of 8-6 suggests she knows how to lead a squad when the opportunity comes. What she hasn't been able to do until now in 2026 is accumulate the cap points that come with leading a winning team yourself. That opportunity opens tonight against Klingler (890 points) and Hannah Flippen (750 points).

The gap to first is 24 points. Achievable, given what Kilfoyl has shown she can do when the competition is at its highest.

What's Next

Series 4 of the AUSL All-Star Cup runs August 28-30. Game 1 — Team Klingler vs. Team Flippen — tips tonight at 7 PM ET on CBS Sports Network. The individual champion is crowned August 30. Kilfoyl's team competes in the finale she's been building toward. Tonight, she's finally doing it for herself.
