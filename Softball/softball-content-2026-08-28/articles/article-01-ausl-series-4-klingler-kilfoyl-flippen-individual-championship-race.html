Series 4 Is Here: Klingler vs. Kilfoyl in the AUSL All-Star Cup Finale

By Sarah Mitchell

August 28, 2026

The moment the 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup has been building toward arrives tonight. Series 4 — the fourth and final series of the summer — opens at 7 PM ET on CBS Sports Network at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois. The individual champion will be crowned on August 30.

The standings entering the finale: Baylee Klingler leads with 890 points, Lexi Kilfoyl sits second at 866, and Hannah Flippen holds third at 750. That 24-point gap between Klingler and Kilfoyl is the number that defines Series 4.

Klingler's Unbeaten Run

Baylee Klingler enters Series 4 with a record no other captain in All-Star Cup history can match this season: 4-0. She has won every team competition she has captained in 2026. In Series 3, her team closed with a shutout of Team Palacios. She has been the most consistent competitor at the top of the standings for weeks.

The corner infielder's 24-point lead over Kilfoyl is real but not unassailable. A strong individual series — measured by stat points, MVP awards, and win bonuses — can shift the race. That's the math Klingler has to protect starting tonight.

Kilfoyl's Arc: Teammate to Rival

Lexi Kilfoyl's road to Series 4 as a captain runs directly through Series 3 as a member of Team Klingler. The Chicago Bandits pitcher spent the previous series helping Klingler win — and she did it better than almost anyone in the field.

Kilfoyl leads the 2026 All-Star Cup with three Game MVP awards, more than any other player. Her individual point total of 866 includes 136 stat points and 100 points from MVP award bonuses. She carries an 8-6 all-time captaining record into her first turn as captain this season.

Tonight, she leads her own squad against the player she was helping win two weeks ago. That dynamic — earned through performance, not circumstance — is the story of this finale.

Flippen and the Field

Hannah Flippen rounds out the three-captain Series 4 field at 750 points. The middle infielder climbed to third place on the strength of her Series 3 results and enters the finale with something to prove against both competitors above her.

Series 4 is a two-day event. Whatever combination of game results, stat accumulation, and MVP award points emerges from Rosemont over the next 48 hours will determine who claims the 2026 AUSL individual title.

What's Next

Game 1 of Series 4 is tonight: Team Klingler vs. Team Flippen at 7 PM ET on CBS Sports Network. The 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup individual champion is crowned on August 30. This is the finale — and it starts right now.
