Gasso Is Coaching Both Teams: What Oklahoma's Unprecedented Fall Looks Like

By Sarah Mitchell

August 28, 2026

Oklahoma softball has navigated plenty of unusual fall camps over Patty Gasso's tenure. But the fall of 2026 is different. When the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage opens September 12 in Oklahoma City, four of OU's core players — Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell — will be competing for Team USA. And for the first time, so will the coaches.

Patty Gasso, JT Gasso, and Jennifer Rocha are all serving on the USA Softball coaching staff for the Group Stage. That means Oklahoma's entire top coaching infrastructure will be in OKC through September 16 alongside its four players.

Building the Roster in Two Parts

Back in Norman, fall camp is already underway — and it's being built around a core that doesn't include Brito, Coleman, Jennings, or Maxwell until mid-September.

Sydney Berzon arrived from LSU carrying one of the more impressive pitching resumes in the country. The two-time All-American posted 486.1 innings pitched, a 2.02 ERA, and 406 career strikeouts for the Tigers. Softball America rated her the top available player in the transfer portal at the time of her commitment to OU. She's getting her first look at the Sooner system this fall.

Macie Harter comes from Middle Tennessee State, where she hit .346 with seven home runs, 15 doubles, and 23 RBI. The two-time All-Conference USA honoree brings a different profile to the OU lineup — a high-contact bat with real power that the program needed after losing key contributors from the 2026 squad.

Both are integrating with the returning Sooners who stayed home for the fall — and Gasso will have a clear picture of how those pieces fit before Brito, Coleman, Jennings, and Maxwell return from OKC.

What the Battle Series Is Showing

The first Battle Series intrasquad scrimmage of the fall drew 2,351 fans to Love's Field — a crowd that signals the level of investment Norman still carries for Sooner softball despite the program's first WCWS miss since 2015 last season. Public Battle Series events begin October 3.

The scrimmages are giving the coaching staff data on how Berzon and Harter fit with the players who've been in the system. By the time the WNT four return from OKC, the pieces of the 2027 OU roster will have been tested for the first time without them — and Gasso will know exactly what they're walking back into.

What's Next

The four Sooners competing at the World Cup Group Stage in OKC return after September 16. Public Battle Series events begin October 3 at Love's Field. The first look at what the 2027 Oklahoma Sooners become in fall competition is coming — and the coaching staff will have logged more hours building it this fall than any predecessor had to manage.
