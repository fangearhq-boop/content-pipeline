4 Days to September 1: The Final Prep Window for Class of 2028

By Emily Rawlings

August 28, 2026

September 1 is four days away. For Class of 2028 softball recruits, that date marks the first time NCAA Division I coaches are allowed to call, text, or email them directly. It is the opening of the most significant contact window in the recruiting process — and the preparation for it is happening right now, whether recruits are aware of it or not.

The coaches are finalizing their lists. The question is where any given recruit lands on them.

What Coaches Are Doing This Week

In the final days before September 1, program staffs are locking in their priority call-order lists. These are typically 8 to 12 names per program — the athletes the coaching staff wants to reach in the first hour of September 1 morning, before the day gets away from them.

How recruits end up on those lists, and how high, is shaped by what coaches saw over the summer. Tournament performances, updated film, camp evaluations, and direct communication with coaches during legal contact periods all factor in. The last-minute film update you post this week, or the message you send to a coach who reached out in July, can still move you up or down that list before the phones start ringing.

What September 1 Morning Actually Looks Like

When September 1 arrives, the contact is immediate. First calls from top-priority programs often come before 8 AM. In program offices with multiple staff members, one coach may be dialing recruits while another handles email or social media outreach simultaneously.

The first hour is dense. A program that gets through its first-hour list by 9 AM still has hours of contact to do, but the early calls carry a specific message: you are a priority.

Recruits who are not expecting the calls, or who are not reachable, miss that signal. The coach moves to the next name. Calls come again later, but the sequence has already communicated something.

September 9 and the In-Person Window

September 1 opens the phone, text, and email contact window. The in-person contact window — official campus visits and in-person meetings with coaches — opens separately on September 9 at 7:00 AM ET. That timing allows programs to identify and prioritize in-person visit targets after the September 1 conversations.

For recruits who receive significant interest on September 1, the September 9 window often moves quickly. Programs that were in consistent contact over the summer tend to have in-person visit planning already underway by the time September 9 arrives.

What's Next

The Class of 2028 first-contact window opens September 1. Four days remain to update film, respond to summer coach messages, and prepare to be reachable on that morning. September 9 at 7:00 AM ET opens the in-person window. The timeline is compressed — and it moves fast once it starts.
