Oklahoma Softball Portal Rebuild: Harter Joins as Gasso Reloads for 2027

By Emily Rawlings

August 19, 2026

The roster turnover that followed Oklahoma softball's first Women's College World Series absence since 2015 has been significant. The rebuild, however, is already well underway.

Macie Harter is the latest addition to the Sooners through the transfer portal, bringing veteran experience to a program that lost several key contributors over the summer. Oklahoma also added a fourth verbal commitment to its 2027 recruiting class this week, signaling that Patty Gasso's long-game approach to program building remains intact even as the immediate roster reshapes itself.

The Departures

The portal exit list from Norman was notable. Kasidi Pickering, a first-team All-American and one of the most productive outfielders in Oklahoma's recent history, transferred to Texas Tech. Tia Milloy, a sophomore utility player with two years of eligibility remaining, also left. The Zache sisters — pitcher Berkley and catcher Riley — departed as well, leaving a meaningful gap in both the rotation and behind the plate.

Taken individually, each move was manageable. Taken together, they represented the most significant roster reshuffling at OU in several years. Missing the WCWS for the first time since 2015 changed the program's offseason calculus in ways that are still playing out.

The Additions

Gasso's response has been methodical. Harter joins through the transfer portal as a veteran piece. The fourth 2027 verbal commitment adds to a class that is being built to compete for a national title in two years — not just to fill roster spots this fall.

Ori Mailo, the No. 4 overall recruit in the Class of 2027, is already on campus. Her combination of athleticism and hitting potential gives OU a foundational piece at the infield. Payton Westra projects as an everyday outfielder, bringing the kind of depth that programs need when multiple contributors depart in the same offseason.

What Fall Camp Reveals

Oklahoma's fall schedule offers five home Battle Series events at Love's Field, beginning October 3 and running through November 6. These are not soft matchups or tune-up games — they are the first competitive look at how this roster actually functions together.

With admission set at $10 for general admission seating, Sooner fans will have early access to the fall preview. Gasso will use these events to identify the lineup and rotation that gives this group the best chance heading into 2027 spring practice.

The Context

Oklahoma softball has reached the WCWS in 27 of the last 28 seasons. One miss does not rewrite the program's identity. But the portal era has made it easier for top talent to move quickly, and the Sooners are now on the other side of that equation after spending years as one of the primary beneficiaries of portal additions.

Gasso has rebuilt before. The program returned to national contention after every previous rough patch, and the current class of additions, combined with the incoming 2027 recruits, gives Oklahoma a legitimate path back to Oklahoma City.

What's Next

Oklahoma's first Battle Series event at Love's Field is October 3. Between now and then, fall camp will begin to sort out the rotation and lineup puzzle. Watch for additional portal movement through the August 31 transfer deadline, and monitor the 2027 recruiting class for a fifth verbal commitment as fall season approaches.
