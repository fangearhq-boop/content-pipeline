USA Softball Announces Official Roster for OKC World Cup Qualifier — 24 Days Out

By Sarah Mitchell

August 19, 2026

The roster is set. USA Softball has officially announced its 16-player squad for the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage in Oklahoma City, with competition beginning September 12 at Devon Park — the same venue where USA went 8-2 and captured gold at the International Cup earlier this month.

The stakes for this event are as high as they get in international softball. The top two finishers from Group C advance to the Brisbane, Australia Finals in April 2027 — which serve as a direct qualification path for the Los Angeles 2028 Olympics. This group stage is not a tune-up. It is the beginning of the Olympic qualification process.

The Roster

USA Softball confirmed the full 16-player roster through its official channels. Teagan Kavan, who became the first two-time Most Outstanding Player in Women's College World Series history after leading Texas to back-to-back national titles, is among those named to the squad. The inclusion of Kavan — entering her senior season at Texas — reflects how the college-to-national team pipeline continues to fuel USA's depth.

Four players with Oklahoma ties are also part of the squad: Alyssa Brito, Tiare Coleman, Rylie Jennings, and Jaylen Maxwell. The OU connection represents a quarter of the roster and illustrates the degree to which Patty Gasso's program has contributed to USA Softball's national team pipeline over the past decade.

Group C Competition

USA's group includes the Netherlands (No. 6 in the world rankings), Mexico (No. 7), and China (No. 8), along with additional group opponents. The Netherlands opener is a true test. The Dutch program has been one of international softball's most consistent contenders, and facing them in the first group game leaves no room to ease into the tournament.

Mexico and China round out the group. Both are capable of an upset. USA cannot afford to treat any group game as a guaranteed win when two advancement spots are on the line.

What Advances to Brisbane

The WBSC Women's Softball World Cup is structured in three six-team group stages. Oklahoma City hosts the final group. The top two finishers from each group — six nations total — advance to the Brisbane Finals from April 5-11, 2027. Those Finals determine which nations qualify for the softball competition at the Los Angeles 2028 Games.

Softball returns to the Olympics at LA28 after missing the Tokyo and Paris Games. The qualification path runs directly through events like this one. For USA Softball, finishing in the top two in Oklahoma City is not aspirational — it is expected. But expectations still have to be earned on the field.

Devon Park's Recent History

Devon Park has become one of the most significant venues in international softball in a short period of time. The International Cup, held at the same location July 30 through August 3, gave USA's roster a chance to compete in the venue environment before the World Cup qualifier arrives. A gold medal result and an 8-2 record at the International Cup established the kind of comfort and familiarity with the site that can matter in a September tournament.

What's Next

The WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage runs September 12-16 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. USA's official schedule within Group C will confirm the Netherlands opener date within the week. Watch USA Softball's official channels for roster updates, practice reports, and pre-tournament information. Brisbane is the destination — Devon Park is where the journey begins.
