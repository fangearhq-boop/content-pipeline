AUSL All-Star Cup Series 3 Draft Is Three Days Away

By Sarah Mitchell

August 19, 2026

Three days. That is all that separates professional softball from the next chapter of the 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup individual championship race.

The Series 3 draft takes place Thursday, August 22, at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois. Three captains — Keilani Ricketts, Sydney Coffey, and Brianna Netz — will each build a roster from the 45-player pool. Once teams are set, play begins Sunday, August 24, in what has already become the most compelling individual competition the sport has seen.

The Race Entering Series 3

Kayla Kowalik enters Series 3 as the No. 1 player in the individual standings. She has been the player to beat throughout the 2026 All-Star Cup, and nothing about Series 2 changed that. But the gap at the top is not comfortable.

Romero climbed 42 points in Series 2 to reach No. 9 in the standings. Lemley added 26 points in the same stretch, continuing an upward climb that has made her one of the tournament's breakout storylines. With two full series remaining after this week's draft, anyone in the top 10 of the individual standings has a realistic path to the championship.

The individual champion is crowned after Series 4 concludes on August 30. That means Thursday's draft effectively begins a 12-day sprint to the title.

What the Draft Decides

The All-Star Cup format is what separates this event from everything else in professional softball. Teams are redrafted before every series. Players do not accumulate team loyalty or familiarity. Every series is a fresh start — which means every captain's draft strategy is a live decision with individual-race consequences.

Do captains draft the best players to maximize their team's chance of winning? Or do they draft with an eye toward limiting the points that Kowalik — or whoever their closest rival is — can accumulate? The All-Star Cup creates a genuine tension between team results and individual ambitions, and that tension tends to play out visibly at the draft table.

Ricketts, Coffey, and Netz bring different strengths as captains. Each will be watching the individual standings as they make their selections. Who ends up on which team on Thursday will define much of what happens on the field over the following week.

The Format

Each series in the All-Star Cup features 12 games among the three teams. Individual points are awarded throughout gameplay based on performance metrics. Team wins contribute to individual totals as well, creating alignment between team competition and the individual race — but not perfect alignment. A player can accumulate strong individual numbers on a losing team, and a winning team's captain may finish the series with fewer individual points than an opponent.

The 45-player roster for the 2026 All-Star Cup was built with fan input. Fan votes determined a portion of the selections, and the pool includes players from across all six AUSL franchises. All games air nationally.

What's Next

The Series 3 draft is Thursday, August 22. Once rosters are announced, teams will have two days before Series 3 play opens Sunday, August 24, at Parkway Bank Sports Complex. The individual standings update after each game day. With the championship gap potentially changing on a single performance, check the standings daily through August 30.
