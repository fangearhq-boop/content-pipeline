### STORY 2: Oklahoma Softball Portal Rebuild

#### Long-Form Post
Patty Gasso is doing what Patty Gasso does — rebuilding.

Oklahoma softball came up short at the 2026 WCWS, ending a remarkable era with a loss to Mississippi State in the Super Regional and missing the Women's College World Series for the first time since 2015. The portal reshuffling that followed was real: Kasidi Pickering headed to Texas Tech, Tia Milloy transferred out, and both Zache sisters moved on.

But the rebuild is already moving.

Macie Harter is the latest addition through the transfer portal, bringing veteran depth to a roster that still has real talent. Oklahoma also added its fourth verbal commitment to the 2027 recruiting class this week, continuing to build a long-term foundation. Ori Mailo — the No. 4 overall recruit in the Class of 2027 — is already on campus. Payton Westra projects as an everyday outfielder.

The fall schedule (five home Battle Series events at Love's Field starting October 3, all $10 general admission) gives Gasso her first real look at this new group in competitive settings.

Missing the WCWS once doesn't change what OU is. The program has been there 27 of the last 28 years. Gasso rebuilds. The Sooners are not done.

### STORY 3: September 1 — 13 Days, Class of 2028 Prep Checklist

#### Long-Form Post
Thirteen days. That's how long Class of 2028 softball recruits have before their phones start ringing in ways they haven't rung before.

September 1, 2026 is the first day Division I coaches can make off-campus visits and direct phone calls to 2028 recruits. With 1,226 nominees in the Extra Inning Softball class — a record — the recruiting landscape is about to get loud.

Here's what the best-prepared recruits will have ready before that first call:

**1. Game film that represents your best.** Coaches are looking for specific things on Day 1. Make sure your most recent, most complete film is easily accessible — not a highlight reel from two seasons ago.

**2. A ranked list of your top schools.** When a coach calls, they'll ask about your interest level. Knowing your own answer ahead of time prevents decisions made in the excitement of the moment. You can be interested without committing.

**3. A family decision framework.** How long will you take to evaluate? What are your non-negotiables? What questions do you need answered before you're ready to commit? Have these conversations before September 1, not during it.

**4. Realistic expectations about the timeline.** History says most 2028 commitments happen within two to three weeks of first-contact day. That doesn't mean you have to move that fast — but know that many of your peers will be committing quickly.

The programs that call on September 1 are sending a message: you're a priority target. The ones that call in October are sending a different one. The silence is a message too.

Know your value. Know your options. Pick up the phone ready.

### STORY 4: USA Softball WNT — Official Roster Set for OKC, 24 Days Out

#### Long-Form Post
The wait is over. USA Softball has officially announced its 16-player roster for the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage in Oklahoma City.

The event runs September 12-16 at Devon Park, the same venue where USA went 8-2 and won gold at the International Cup just weeks ago. Group C features some of the world's best competition: the Netherlands (No. 6 in the world), Mexico (No. 7), and China (No. 8), plus additional group opponents.

Here's what's at stake: the top two finishers from each group advance to the Brisbane, Australia Finals (April 5-11, 2027). Those Brisbane Finals are a direct path toward LA28 Olympic qualification — the first softball qualification event for the Los Angeles Games.

Teagan Kavan — who became the first two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player in history when she led Texas to back-to-back national titles this spring — is confirmed on the USA roster. Four players with Oklahoma ties (Brito, Coleman, Jennings, Maxwell) are also part of the squad.

USA enters as one of the clear favorites in Group C. The Netherlands opener will set the tone.

24 days. The world is watching Devon Park again.

### STORY 5: Texas Softball — Lands Former OU Pitcher, Building Toward Three-Peat

#### Long-Form Post
The Texas-Oklahoma rivalry doesn't take the offseason off.

Texas softball has added a former Oklahoma pitcher through the transfer portal, continuing a portal exchange that has defined the offseason between the two programs. While Oklahoma absorbed the loss of Kasidi Pickering to Texas Tech, among other departures, Texas has been on the other side of those portal equations — gaining pieces that reinforce their already deep roster.

With fall camp underway at UT, the Longhorns are building what would be an unprecedented roster if successful.

The numbers are already staggering: 11 new additions to a 24-player squad. Teagan Kavan returning for her senior year as the only two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player in the history of the event. A complete coaching staff with Fremin (now associate head coach after a decade as head coach at SLU) and Freeman handling hitting. A 49-12 record in 2026 that wasn't even close to their ceiling.

But the target is simple and the history is daunting. Three straight Women's College World Series titles have been won twice in history: UCLA (1988-90) and Oklahoma (2021-23). No program has ever won four straight. Texas is attempting to become just the third program to win three in a row.

The portal build is part of that chase. Every addition is a statement of intent.

Fall camp tells you who's ready. The results will start to matter in spring.
