Oklahoma's Rebuilt Roster: Sydney Berzon and the Case for a Sooners Resurgence

By Jordan Reeves | I Love Softball | August 23, 2026

The off-season story of Oklahoma Softball is one of deliberate reconstruction — and the final pieces of that rebuild are now in place. Fall camp is underway in Norman, and Patty Gasso heads into the 2027 season with a roster that has changed substantially from the team that missed the WCWS for the first time since 2015.

The addition most deserving of attention: Sydney Berzon. The two-time All-American pitcher from LSU is now a Sooner — and her presence fundamentally changes the conversation about what Oklahoma's pitching staff can be in 2027.

Sydney Berzon: The Key Addition

Berzon's credentials speak for themselves. A two-time All-American at LSU, she is the type of experienced, decorated arm that Oklahoma's pitching staff needed after losing key contributors. The ability to acquire a pitcher of that caliber through the transfer portal — and to do it in a cycle where pitching talent at the top end was scarce — reflects well on Gasso's off-season work.

Beyond the credential, Berzon brings postseason experience and the competitive mindset that comes from pitching at a high-pressure SEC program. That context is directly relevant at Oklahoma, where every fall practice is preparation for a WCWS run.

The Full Roster Picture

Berzon joins a roster that was already being rebuilt in the lineup. Macie Harter — transferred from Middle Tennessee State — posted a .346 batting average with 7 home runs and 23 RBI in 2026 and was named Second Team All-Conference USA. She brings two years of eligibility and a consistent production profile that fits immediately in a lineup needing an everyday contributor.

On the horizon, the 2027 recruiting class adds two blue-chip prospects. Ori Mailo (No. 4 overall), an infielder, is already on campus for fall 2026 — ahead of schedule and available for the full camp process. Payton Westra (No. 2 overall), an outfielder, is also committed and will complete the incoming class.

There is one complicating factor in the early fall calendar: four Sooners — Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell — are competing on the USA WNT roster at the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage through September 16. That means fall camp in August and early September runs without some of Oklahoma's most experienced players. They return after September 16, and the fall exhibition schedule — which opens October 4 — accounts for the timeline.

The Historical Context

The last time Oklahoma missed the Women's College World Series was 2015. What followed was the most dominant stretch in college softball history — a run that included three consecutive national championships from 2021 through 2023. The Sooners have built a program that knows how to respond to adversity.

Gasso's track record includes 8 WCWS titles at Oklahoma. The program's infrastructure — recruiting relationships, facilities, culture — remained intact even after the 2026 miss. The off-season roster rebuild, headlined by Berzon and Harter, represents a targeted correction rather than a complete teardown.

What's Next

Oklahoma's fall exhibition schedule opens October 4 and runs through November 9. The calendar also includes the "Battle Series," six intrasquad scrimmages with aggregate scoring. Four Sooners return from WNT duty after September 16. When Brito, Coleman, Jennings, and Maxwell rejoin a roster that already includes Berzon, Harter, Mailo, and Westra, the full scope of Gasso's rebuild will be on the field for the first time.
