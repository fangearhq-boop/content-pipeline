20 Days Out: A Full Preview of Group Oklahoma and the Road to LA28

By Emily Rawlings | I Love Softball | August 23, 2026

Twenty days from now, USA Softball opens Group Oklahoma at Devon Park in Oklahoma City — and the stakes are as high as they get outside of an Olympic Games. The WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage (September 12–16) is the qualifying event for the 2027 World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia, which serves as the direct LA28 Olympic qualifier. The path to Los Angeles 2028 runs through Oklahoma City.

Six nations. Four days of round-robin competition. The top two advance. Here is what that field looks like — and what USA must accomplish to make the 2027 Finals.

The Six-Team Field

Group Oklahoma features: USA (No. 2 in the world), Netherlands (No. 6), Mexico (No. 7), China (No. 8), New Zealand (No. 23), and South Africa (No. 36). The United States enters as the clear favorite — ranked second in the world behind Japan, which is not in this group — but the competition is not a formality.

Netherlands and Mexico are the two nations that pose the most realistic challenge to USA. The Dutch have historically been among the most consistent programs in international softball, appearing in World Cup finals rounds and producing elite pitching. Mexico has been a consistent international competitor, earning respect at every major World Cup cycle and finishing on the podium at regional events.

China (No. 8) is also capable of competing against anyone on a given day. Collectively, the top three challengers in the group — Netherlands, Mexico, and China — mean that USA cannot afford to take any game lightly in a four-day round-robin format.

Opening Day: USA vs Mexico

USA's first game in Group Oklahoma is against Mexico on September 12 — Opening Day at Devon Park. According to the WBSC schedule, USA faces Mexico in the final game of the day on September 12. A strong showing in that opener sets the tone for everything that follows.

There is competitive and symbolic value in winning Opening Day convincingly. It establishes USA's standard early, it applies psychological pressure to the other teams tracking the results, and it ensures USA enters the second day of competition with no margin anxiety.

The Oklahoma Connection

Four members of the 16-player USA roster are current Sooners: Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell. Competing at Devon Park in Oklahoma City — a venue all four know from their collegiate careers — adds a unique home-crowd dimension to USA's preparation.

Veteran international players Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola also bring championship experience to a roster that includes players from the 2025 World Games gold medal squad.

What's Next

Group Oklahoma runs September 12–16 at Devon Park, Oklahoma City. USA (No. 2) opens against Mexico (No. 7) in the final game of the day on September 12. The top two of six teams advance to the 2027 WBSC Softball World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland (April 5–11, 2027) — the LA28 Olympic qualifier. Twenty days away. The road to Los Angeles starts in OKC.
