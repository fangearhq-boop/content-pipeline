Series 3 Starts Tomorrow: AUSL's Final Week of Individual Racing Is Here

By Jordan Reeves | I Love Softball | August 23, 2026

The AUSL All-Star Cup Series 3 begins Sunday, August 24 — tomorrow — and with it, the individual title race enters the most decisive stretch of the summer. Game 13 tips off at 3:00 PM ET on ESPNU, followed by Game 14 at 5:30 PM ET on ESPN+. Teams are set. The standings are what they are. And there are exactly seven days left until the individual champion is crowned after Series 4 on August 30.

Kayla Kowalik enters Series 3 in first place. Behind her, Sierra Romero and Emma Lemley are the two names most capable of disrupting that order — if the next seven days go exactly right.

The Individual Standings Race Entering Series 3

Kowalik's No. 1 position heading into Series 3 was built across eight weeks of professional softball. She holds the lead for a reason. But the AUSL All-Star Cup format creates legitimate closing opportunities: teams are fully redrafted before each series, individual statistical performance feeds directly into standings points, and a single dominant series by a challenger can change the picture dramatically.

Romero demonstrated exactly that in Series 2, climbing +42 points to reach No. 9 overall. That kind of surge — 42 points in a single series — is the type of performance that keeps individual races alive. Emma Lemley was not far behind, adding +26 points to her total. Both players proved in Series 2 that they are capable of big production weekends.

The challenge for Romero and Lemley heading into Series 3 is clear: they need big individual statistical performances in a compressed timeline, while simultaneously hoping Kowalik has a quieter-than-usual series. Both conditions need to be true at the same time. That is the nature of catching a leader in a points-based race.

What the Series 3 Draft Means

Captains Kayla Kowalik, Keilani Ricketts, Danieca Coffey, and Devyn Netz assembled their Series 3 rosters at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois on Friday, August 22. Those rosters are now the stage on which the individual race will play out this weekend.

In the AUSL format, team wins generate shared points that compound with individual statistical production. A player on a competitive, winning team has more runway to accumulate points than one on a team that struggles to take games. For Romero and Lemley — both trying to close a gap on Kowalik — landing on teams capable of winning games is not just helpful. It is necessary.

The redraft format means former teammates now line up as rivals. Alliances shift. Every game carries standings implications that cut across team and individual interests simultaneously.

How to Watch Series 3

All AUSL All-Star Cup games broadcast nationally on ESPN, MLB Network, and CBS Sports Network. Tomorrow's Series 3 opener (Game 13) airs at 3:00 PM ET on ESPNU. Game 14 follows at 5:30 PM ET on ESPN+. Games continue through the week as Series 3 runs its full slate before Series 4 closes out August 30.

The venue throughout is Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois, which has hosted the entire 2026 All-Star Cup across all four series.

What's Next

Series 3 opens Sunday, August 24 with Game 13 at 3:00 PM ET on ESPNU. Seven days remain before Series 4 concludes August 30 and the 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup individual champion is crowned. Kowalik leads. Romero and Lemley are chasing. Every game from here forward matters — for individual standings, for team outcomes, and for the final shape of the summer's most competitive professional softball showcase.
