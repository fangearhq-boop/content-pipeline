LSU Signs the No. 1 Recruiting Class in Softball — A Historic First for the Tigers Program

By Sarah Mitchell | November 15, 2026 | I Love Softball

Beth Torina has done something no LSU softball coach has done before her. The Tigers' 2026 National Signing Day class — announced November 11 — now ranks No. 1 nationally by Softball America and Rivals, marking the highest recruiting class ranking in LSU softball program history. It is also the program's second consecutive top-three class.

The six-player haul is built around pitching depth: five-star utility/pitcher Sydney Gonglik, four-star pitcher Macie Bryant (ranked No. 22 overall, No. 5 in Texas), four-star catcher Jewel Cooper (No. 29 overall), four-star infielder Bryli Seifert (No. 32 overall), four-star utility player Lillie Gaudio, and four-star infielder Jillian Wimmer (No. 39 overall). Two of the six signees hold five-star designations.

What This Class Does for LSU

Gonglik and Bryant address a rotation that has cycled through significant departures in recent seasons. With two elite pitching prospects arriving in Baton Rouge simultaneously, Torina has an opportunity to build the kind of rotation depth that contending programs need across a 60-plus game regular season and into the postseason.

Cooper fills what has historically been one of LSU's thinner positions. At No. 29 overall in the 2027 class, she arrives with legitimate expectations of contributing immediately. Seifert (No. 32) adds another infield option in a class that balances positional needs with ceiling.

"This class speaks to where LSU softball is headed," the program's official release read. The release also confirmed the No. 1 ranking is a first in program history.

The Bigger Picture: SEC Dominance in 2027 Recruiting

LSU's No. 1 class is part of a broader story: the Southeastern Conference placed multiple programs in Softball America's top 10 on Signing Day. The SEC's recruiting footprint — talent-rich states, elite facilities, NIL infrastructure, and postseason visibility — continues to make it the most productive recruiting ground in the sport.

The only counterpoint to LSU's Softball America No. 1 ranking is D1Softball's separate rankings, which place Oklahoma at the top. The two services use different evaluation methodologies — D1Softball weights roster depth and in-person evaluation, while Softball America relies more heavily on individual star ratings. Both services have credible track records, and the disagreement is not unusual in recruiting rankings. It simply reflects that two exceptionally talented programs have assembled two exceptional classes.

What's Next

LSU opens spring 2027 play against a schedule Torina has not yet released publicly. The standard expectation is that the Tigers will compete for the SEC West in a division that now includes both Alabama and Arkansas as perennial challengers. The WCWS is in Oklahoma City June 1-9, 2027.

Preseason rankings from D1Softball, USA TODAY, and ESPN are expected in January — the first major indicator of where the media consensus places LSU relative to Oklahoma, Florida, Tennessee, and Texas Tech. With the No. 1 recruiting class by one of the two major ranking services, LSU will arrive in those rankings as one of the favorites, not a sleeper.

The 2027 Women's College World Series race began on November 11. The Tigers just made their opening statement.
