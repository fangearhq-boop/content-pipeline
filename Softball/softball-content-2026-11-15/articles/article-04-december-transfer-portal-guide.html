The December Softball Transfer Portal Opens in 16 Days — Everything You Need to Know

By Sarah Mitchell | November 15, 2026 | I Love Softball

If you follow college softball closely, circle December 1 on your calendar. That is when the Division I transfer portal window is expected to open for softball, beginning a 15-day window that will reshape rosters across the country. For fans who have been waiting to find out where players land, this is the window.

The timeline: the window opens December 1 and runs through December 15. After that date, players who have entered the portal but have not committed must wait until the next window (June 2027) to sign with a new school.

What Makes This Window Different

Two simultaneous legal and policy changes make the December 2026 portal window unlike any before it.

The first is the House v. NCAA settlement, which established a 25-player roster cap for Division I softball programs. Programs that previously carried 28, 30, or more scholarship players must now comply with that limit. For programs at the cap, adding a portal transfer means making a roster decision on someone who is currently enrolled. That is a painful choice — and it will suppress demand from cap-maxed programs.

The second is the 5-for-5 eligibility rule, currently under appeal in the Tenth Circuit Court of Appeals. Under the rule as written, athletes from the class of 2022 who are eligible for a fifth year may return to their programs. Under the current injunction, those athletes can be rostered — but they cannot practice or play in games while the litigation continues. For athletes considering the December portal, that creates an unusual consideration: entering the portal as a potential fifth-year athlete means landing somewhere that may carry them on the roster without being able to use them competitively.

Programs Most Likely to Be Active

The programs with the most visible portal needs heading into December include Oklahoma (pitching depth after Berkley Zache's departure), Arizona (new staff, roster rebuilding under Caitlin Lowe), Florida State (significant production losses to transfer this fall), and Ole Miss (heavy portal outflow this summer).

Programs sitting above or near the 25-player cap are in a different position: they can be active only if they are willing to open a spot first. Expect some programs to announce scholarship reductions or roster cuts in late November to position themselves for December activity.

What's Next

The portal opens December 1 — 16 days from today. The movement will be rapid once it begins. Programs that have done their recruiting homework in November will be ready to move; those that have not will be chasing in a compressed market.

In a sport where a single elite transfer can change a program's postseason ceiling, the December 2026 window may turn out to be one of the most consequential in softball history. Watch for announcements beginning December 1.
