### STORY 1: Tenth Circuit Rules for NCAA in Wisne — Fifth Year Closed for Class of 2022

**Long-Form Post (9:00 AM ET)**

The legal fight over college softball's fifth-year eligibility just reached a major turning point.

The Tenth Circuit Court of Appeals reversed and vacated the nationwide preliminary injunction in *Wisne v. NCAA*, handing the NCAA a significant victory in the 5-for-5 eligibility litigation. The court found that the plaintiffs — class-of-2022 athletes who sought a fifth year of competition under COVID-era eligibility rules — failed to demonstrate a likelihood of success on their antitrust argument. Specifically, the panel rejected the plaintiffs' definition of the relevant labor market.

For softball players who had been counting on a fifth season, this ruling closes what many had considered the likeliest path to eligibility.

But the story isn't over. Approximately 200 concurrent lawsuits filed in state courts still involve up to 850 athletes across all sports, and a new 46-athlete group has already filed a fresh suit seeking an immediate preliminary injunction. Plaintiffs in the Wisne case can also petition for en banc rehearing from the full Tenth Circuit.

What does this mean for softball programs? The December transfer portal window opens in just 13 days (December 1–15). Programs that had budgeted roster spots for returning class-of-2022 players now need to reassess — and fast. The portal has become the primary relief valve.

We'll continue tracking this. The legal calendar is moving quickly.

### STORY 4: LSU Signs Portal Twins Pickens + Winchell to Complete No. 1 Class

**Long-Form Post (3:00 PM ET)**

LSU's No. 1-ranked signing class is only part of the story.

After announcing the top-rated 2027 recruiting class in program history on November 11, coach Beth Torina went back to work. She signed portal twins Mackenzie and Madison Pickens — a rare simultaneous acquisition of sibling pitchers — and Notre Dame outfielder Michey Winchell, who will be a senior for the Tigers in 2027.

Winchell brings immediate starting experience from the ACC to a lineup that will need veteran leadership to surround its younger signees. The Pickens twins add pitching depth and rotation options.

What's emerging at LSU is a blueprint: sign the top high school class, add targeted portal pieces, manage outgoing transfers selectively rather than chasing every available name. The result is a 2027 roster that balances elite youth with proven experience.

The Women's College World Series in June 2027 is still far away. But if the Tigers can stay healthy and gel during the spring, Beth Torina is building a genuine championship contender.

The Lady Vols are legitimate SEC title contenders alongside Alabama, LSU, and OU. Their challenge in 2027 will be doing what Tennessee-caliber talent demands: reaching Tallahassee and competing for a national title.
