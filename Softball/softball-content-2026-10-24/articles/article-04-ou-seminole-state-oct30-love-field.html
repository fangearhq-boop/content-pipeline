Oklahoma's Final Fall Exhibition Is Oct 30 — One Last Look at Wells, Kavan, and the 2027 Sooners

By Sarah Mitchell | October 24, 2026 | I Love Softball

Oklahoma Sooner fans have one more fall date to circle before the long winter wait begins. The Sooners will host Seminole State College in their final fall exhibition on Thursday, October 30 at 6:30 PM CT at Love's Field in Norman. The game is free and streams live on 1oklahoma.com — no ticket required.

For a team that spent the fall showcasing some of the most electric individual talent in college softball, this is a meaningful final act. After a Battle Series that drew packed crowds to Love's Field and concluded with a matchup against USAO on October 23, the Sooners have one more chance to put the 2027 roster in front of fans before the calendar turns.

The Players to Watch

If you have not yet seen Kendall Wells in person, October 30 is your opportunity. The NFCA Freshman of the Year earned that honor the hard way — by hitting 39 home runs in her first college season, a mark that made her one of the most talked-about freshmen in the history of the sport. Wells is now a sophomore and, if anything, even more dangerous with a full year of college experience behind her.

Jordy Kavan is the other headliner. A two-time Women's College World Series Most Outstanding Player, Kavan has already etched her name into the upper tier of all-time great college pitchers. Watching her work through a lineup — the movement, the command, the compete level even in fall exhibition settings — is something softball fans do not take for granted.

Pitcher Alyssa Berzon provides the third dimension of what has been one of the nation's most formidable rotations. Together, Kavan and Berzon give head coach Patty Gasso a 1-2 combination that most programs would not be able to match with their entire staff.

A Strong Fall for the Sooners

Oklahoma's fall schedule included a multi-game Battle Series at Love's Field that drew thousands of fans and featured highlight-reel moments from all corners of the roster. The Battle Series opener on September 30 drew 2,351 fans. Garcia and Wells hit back-to-back home runs in one of those games, the kind of moment that becomes a highlight clip fans share all winter.

The Sooners are routinely projected as a top-3 national title contender for 2027. With Wells, Kavan, and Berzon as the centerpieces, and a supporting cast that showed genuine depth throughout the fall, that projection feels grounded in reality rather than hope.

What's Next

Thursday October 30 at 6:30 PM CT is the last time Sooner fans will see this team in action until the 2027 season begins. The game is free, it streams on 1oklahoma.com, and it features the most decorated individual talent in college softball right now.

After October 30, Oklahoma heads into winter training. The next time you see Kendall Wells stepping into the box at Love's Field, it will count for real. Between now and then, there is one more fall game to enjoy.
