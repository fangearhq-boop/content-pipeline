LSU Meets Florida State in Orange Beach — The Fall Showcase Worth Watching This Weekend

By Emily Rawlings | October 24, 2026 | I Love Softball

If you are anywhere near the Gulf Coast this weekend, there is a fall softball event you should not miss. LSU and Florida State are facing off in a two-game showcase at Orange Beach Softball Stadium in Orange Beach, Alabama — and the matchup on paper is as compelling as anything you will see outside of conference play.

Game 1 tips off today, Saturday October 24, at 4 PM CT. Game 2 follows on Sunday October 25 at noon CT. Admission is $10 cash at the gate. The venue, the Orange Beach Sportsplex, has become one of the Gulf South's premier youth and college softball facilities, and this weekend it hosts its highest-profile matchup of the season.

Two Programs at Very Different Crossroads

Florida State enters this fall with the most dominant regular-season record in recent college softball history. The Seminoles went 52-1 in the 2026 regular season — a number that barely seems real until you look it up twice. FSU has been a perennial national title contender, and this fall is as much about maintaining roster continuity and development as it is about competition.

LSU finished 40-19 in 2026, a season with real highs and some frustrating lows. The Tigers are using this fall to answer questions about their 2027 roster construction, player development, and pitching depth. This weekend against Florida State is the most rigorous competitive environment they will see until SEC play resumes.

The contrast makes this showcase more interesting than most. One program is protecting a dynasty; the other is chasing one. The players know the stakes even if the games don't count.

Why Orange Beach

The Orange Beach Sportsplex has hosted fall tournaments and showcases for years, and its proximity to both LSU (roughly six hours) and FSU (about five hours) makes it a natural neutral-site venue. The stadium features quality playing surfaces, ample seating, and the kind of deep-autumn Gulf Coast weather that makes outdoor softball genuinely enjoyable in late October.

Games like this — open to the public, accessible, affordable — are exactly what the sport needs to build casual fans into die-hard ones. For $10, you get to watch two of the top programs in the country. That is remarkable value at any level of sports.

What to Watch For

Even though these are fall exhibitions, there is plenty to track for fans of both programs. For FSU, watch which pitchers get extended outings and how the offensive lineup sequences. With a 52-1 record to protect as context, the Seminoles will want to show that the standard carries into 2027.

For LSU, the story is about competitive fire and roster identity. Which hitters look ready to carry the offense next spring? Which pitchers look like they belong in a top-25 rotation? Fall ball answers those questions, even if it does not fill the box scores.

Games like this one also serve as informal scouting events for 2028 recruiting targets who may be watching. Both programs are active in the 2028 class, and performances at fall showcases have a way of showing up in recruiting conversations for months afterward.

What's Next

If you cannot make it to Orange Beach, check both programs' social channels for any live or recap coverage. Game 2 is Sunday at noon CT — same admission, same venue.

After this weekend, both programs will enter the quiet period before winter workouts and the ramp toward preseason. Florida State and LSU next face the public eye when their 2027 seasons begin, likely in February. Until then, Orange Beach is the place to be.
