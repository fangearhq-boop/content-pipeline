Texas Tech's New Era Closes Out Fall Today — Cottrill, Pendley, and the No. 1 Portal Class Take the Field

By Jordan Reeves | October 24, 2026 | I Love Softball

Texas Tech closes its 2026 fall season today when it hosts Odessa College at Rocky Johnson Field in Lubbock, with first pitch scheduled for 1 PM CT. It is the final fall exhibition of the Kelsey Cottrill era — and after the most transformative off-season in program history, the Red Raiders head into winter with significant expectations for 2027.

New head coach Kelsey Cottrill and assistant Traci Pendley inherited a program that was already a WCWS finalist in 2026, and immediately doubled down by assembling what recruiting databases have ranked as the No. 1 portal class in college softball. The fall has been their first extended look at this roster in practice and competition. Today is the culmination of that evaluation period.

The Roster That Has Everyone Talking

The headliner of the portal class is Kasidi Pickering, who arrives from Oklahoma after three seasons that produced a .384 batting average, 50 home runs, and 169 RBI. Those are numbers that belong in a Hall of Fame conversation. Pickering could slot in at any position in the infield and immediately becomes one of the five best hitters in the Big 12.

She is not alone. Paige Beachum arrives from Florida State, bringing experience from one of the most successful programs in the country. Heavener, Polar, and Gallaway round out a portal class with depth at multiple positions. And those are just the newcomers.

The returners are just as impressive. Kailey Williams came back to Lubbock after setting a program home run record with 27 in a single season. First-team All-American Caylan Terry also returned, giving Cottrill continuity at the top of the lineup. The combination of proven returners and elite transfer talent makes this one of the most intriguing rosters in the country heading into 2027.

What This Game Means for the Staff

Cottrill and Pendley have spent the fall installing their systems — offensive philosophy, pitching rotations, defensive alignments, culture-setting. Today is less about the final score and more about completing that process. Last fall, Texas Tech handled Odessa College 15-0 in 10 innings. The expectation for this year's team, with far more talent, is higher.

The bigger benchmark is the Big 12 conference schedule, which opens March 12. Texas Tech's path to the Big 12 title — and a deep NCAA tournament run — runs through a conference that includes Oklahoma, Oklahoma State, and Baylor. With this roster, the Red Raiders are capable of winning it all.

What's Next

After today's finale, Texas Tech goes into the winter training phase with its 2027 roster established. The Big 12 2027 season kicks off in February with the NFCA Leadoff Classic on February 12, where TTU opens against Notre Dame, before conference play begins March 12.

If Cottrill and Pendley can translate this talent into a cohesive unit by February, the Red Raiders may be the most dangerous team in the Big 12 that nobody is fully talking about yet. Today's game at Rocky Johnson Field is the last public look before the transformation goes behind closed doors.
