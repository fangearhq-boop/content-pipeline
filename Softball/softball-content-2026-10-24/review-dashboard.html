Tennessee Closes Fall With 14-1 Win Over Lee University
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Tennessee wrapped up its 2026 fall schedule with a dominant 14-1 win over Lee University on Thursday, closing out what has been an impressive exhibition run for the Volunteers. The headline of the night belonged to freshman Bailee Shelton, who carved through the Lee lineup with 7 strikeouts across 3.0 innings, allowing just one hit. Shelton's ability to work different zones was praised by the coaching staff — and she had the team's best hard-hit ball rate allowed of any pitcher this fall. Head coach Karen Weekly was candid after the game, saying the competition didn't challenge the Vols the way she would have preferred late in the fall. That honest assessment isn't a concern — it's a compliment to how far this roster has come. When you have Karlyn Pickens (194 strikeouts, 1.70 ERA) and Sage Mardjetko (16-3, 1.26 ERA) anchoring a rotation that also now features Shelton, the standard for competition has to rise. For fans who want one more look before the long winter wait, the Vols have one final public intersquad: **Thursday, October 29 at 5 PM ET at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium.** It's free and open to the public. Tennessee enters 2027 with arguably the deepest pitching staff in the SEC. The 2027 national title conversation starts here. ### Image Post Caption Tennessee 14, Lee University 1.
