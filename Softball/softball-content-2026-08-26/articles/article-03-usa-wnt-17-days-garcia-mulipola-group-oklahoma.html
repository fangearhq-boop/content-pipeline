17 Days Out: Garcia and Mulipola Are USA's Veterans in Group Oklahoma

By Jordan Reeves | I Love Softball | August 26, 2026

Seventeen days remain before USA Softball opens Group Oklahoma at Devon Park in Oklahoma City — and the roster selected for the 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage carries experience that matters beyond raw talent. Among the 16 players named to the roster, two were there in 2020 when USA competed for Olympic gold: Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola. That background does not guarantee anything. But it provides something that youth and college accolades cannot replicate — the knowledge of what tournament pressure actually feels like at the international level.

The Group Oklahoma Field

The 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage runs September 12 through September 16 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. Six nations compete in a round-robin format. The top two advance to the 2027 Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia — the pathway to LA28 Olympic qualification.

The six nations in Group Oklahoma: USA (ranked No. 2 in the world), Netherlands (No. 6), Mexico (No. 7), China (No. 8), New Zealand (No. 23), and South Africa (No. 36). USA opens the group stage against Mexico on September 12.

On paper, two of the six teams — USA and one other — advance. In practice, determining which nation finishes second behind USA is not straightforward. The Netherlands has been a consistent force in international softball for over a decade. Mexico is athletic, pitch-first, and capable of forcing extended games that tax USA's rotation. Both nations are realistic threats to finish second in the group, and both are realistic upset risks if USA has a poor performance in a single game.

Why Garcia and Mulipola Matter

Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola were part of the 2020 U.S. Olympic Team in Tokyo. Garcia pitched and played in the field at the highest level of international competition. Mulipola caught behind the plate in a tournament where every opponent had studied USA's tendencies for years. They are not on this roster as honorary selections — they are on the roster because they can play, and because their experience in high-pressure international settings is a resource the younger players on the team can draw from.

Seven additional players on the 2026 roster were part of USA's gold medal performance at the 2025 World Games. The roster also includes four University of Oklahoma players — Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell — who are currently with the team and have been part of USA's preparation block. Together, the 16-player group represents a blend of Olympic-level experience, recent international gold medal experience, and some of the top college talent in the country.

The Stakes of Finishing First or Second

Advancing from Group Oklahoma as the top seed versus the second seed carries meaningful implications for the 2027 Finals bracket. A USA team that finishes first in the group stage arrives in Redcliffe, Queensland in April 2027 with a more favorable initial draw. Finishing second does not eliminate the path to the Finals or to LA28 qualification — it changes the bracket and potentially the road to the medal round.

USA's preparation for Group Oklahoma has included the 2025 World Games (gold) and the 2026 International Cup at Devon Park, the same venue, in July. The team went 5-0 in the International Cup pool stage at Devon Park. Familiarity with the venue is an advantage. Experience under pressure — which Garcia and Mulipola carry — is another.

What's Next

The 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage runs September 12-16 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. USA opens against Mexico (No. 7) on September 12. The top two nations advance to the 2027 Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland — the direct LA28 qualifying pathway. Information on tickets and broadcast schedule is available at wbsc.org.
