Series 3 Is Over. Klingler Leads 890–866 Over Kilfoyl. Series 4 Starts Friday.

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | August 26, 2026

The 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup individual race has a new leader — and it is not the player who held first place all summer. Baylee Klingler closed Series 3 on Tuesday night with a 1-0 shutout victory over Team Palacios at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois, capping an unbeaten run as an All-Star Cup captain and vaulting her to the top of the individual standings with 890 points. Lexi Kilfoyl is second at 866. Hannah Flippen is third at 750. Kayla Kowalik, who entered Series 3 in first place, is no longer at the top.

Four days remain. Series 4 begins Friday, August 28. The 2026 individual champion will be crowned on August 30.

How Tuesday Night Ended Series 3

The final game of Series 3 was a pitchers' duel. Amber Fiser and Lexi Kilfoyl combined on a two-hit shutout against Team Palacios, allowing just one walk while striking out five across seven innings. The lone run came in the top of the first inning: Sydney Romero reached base on a fielding error, advanced to third, and scored on a Baylee Klingler sacrifice fly. The run was unearned. It was also sufficient.

For Klingler, Tuesday's victory completed a perfect Series 3 and extended her all-time record as a captain in All-Star Cup play to 4-0. She is the only player in the 2026 event to go undefeated as a team captain across multiple series. That consistency — winning games, producing statistical points, anchoring her roster through tight situations — is reflected in the standings.

The Standings: Klingler Overtakes Kowalik

The biggest story out of Series 3 is not what Klingler did — it is what the standings now show. Kayla Kowalik entered Series 3 in first place with 860 points, having led the individual race through the first two series of the All-Star Cup. After Series 3, Klingler sits at 890. Kowalik is no longer first.

This is meaningful because the format rewards sustained production across all four series. Klingler has now produced enough across her games — wins, statistical contributions from her roster, and the points generated by her captaincy — to push past the previous leader. The math entering Series 4 is straightforward: Klingler leads, but Kilfoyl at 866 is only 24 points back. In a single series where both players are captaining teams, that gap can move in either direction.

Elsewhere in the standings, Amber Fiser made one of the biggest moves during Series 3, climbing 13 places. Reese Atwood jumped 14 places. Both are now inside the top 15 and will bring that momentum into Series 4 under whichever captain drafts them.

Series 4 Captains and the Road to August 30

The top three players in the individual standings after Series 3 become the Series 4 captains. That means Klingler, Kilfoyl, and Flippen will each draft teams and compete in the final series of the 2026 All-Star Cup.

Series 4 opens Friday, August 28 at 7 PM ET, with Team Klingler facing Team Flippen on CBS Sports Network. All remaining games are available across ESPN, MLB Network, and CBS Sports Network. The full schedule is at theausl.com/all-star-cup/.

The format gives every player still in the field an opportunity to produce statistical points and influence the standings. For Klingler, the goal is to protect a lead. For Kilfoyl, it is to close 24 points. For Flippen, it is to make up ground on both. None of these scenarios are decided before Friday's first pitch.

What's Next

Series 4 of the 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup begins Friday, August 28 at 7 PM ET. Team Klingler and Team Flippen open the final week. The individual champion will be crowned after Series 4 concludes on August 30. All games are broadcast on ESPN, MLB Network, and CBS Sports Network. Track standings at theausl.com/all-star-cup/.
