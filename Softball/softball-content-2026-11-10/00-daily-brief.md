## Today's Top Stories

### STORY 1: Tenth Circuit Overturns 5-for-5 Injunction — NCAA Wins Key Ruling
**Tier:** 1 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP
On Friday, November 6, the Tenth Circuit Court of Appeals reversed and vacated the nationwide preliminary injunction in *Wisne v. NCAA* that had allowed Class of 2022 Division I athletes to seek a fifth season. The panel, led by Judge Carolyn McHugh, found the district court's all-inclusive labor-market definition unsupported by the evidence. The ruling comes five days before Signing Day and ends the nationwide injunction, though state-court cases involving roughly 200 additional lawsuits continue. NCAA chief legal officer Scott Bearby responded: "Injunctions like the one entered by the district court have created chaos for the 2026-27 season."

### STORY 2: Signing Day Tomorrow — Class of 2027 Signs on Nov. 11
**Tier:** 1 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP
November 11 is National Signing Day, the first day Class of 2027 softball recruits can sign athletics aid agreements. Five programs have the most at stake: Florida (elite incoming class), Tennessee (post-Kelsey Pickens rotation depth), Oklahoma (Kendall Wells and Rylie Kavan anchors), Texas (defending champion standard), and Texas Tech (No. 1 portal class headlined by Kasidi Pickering, .384 BA, 50 HR, 169 RBI). The 5-for-5 ruling from Friday adds clarity: programs now sign knowing the eligibility rule is in effect.

### STORY 3: EE100 Class of 2028 Rankings Rollout Complete — National Top 25 Lands
**Tier:** 2 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP
Extra Inning Softball's Class of 2028 Extra Elite 100 rankings rollout concluded this past weekend with the national top 25 lists on November 7-8 and the Pitcher-Hitter Duo Top 25 on November 9. The rollout ran eight regional hitters lists from November 1-6 and closes with the national reveals. No. 1 overall Avery Landers (Texas), No. 2 Ellie Holden (Florida), and No. 3 Mia Schlader (Oklahoma) top the class. With 1,200-plus nominations, the 2028 class is the largest in Extra Inning Softball history.

### STORY 4: OU Fall Wrap — ETAM Canceled, Rose State Steps In
**Tier:** 2 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP
Oklahoma's planned November 4 exhibition against East Texas A&M was canceled after ETAM withdrew, citing unforeseen circumstances — the day after their men's basketball team beat OU 68-64 at Lloyd Noble Center. Rose State College stepped in as the replacement opponent. The Sooners close their fall with that game in the rearview, heading into winter workouts with Kendall Wells (39 HR, NFCA Freshman of the Year) and Rylie Kavan (2x WCWS MOP) healthy and ready for 2027.

### STORY 5: Texas Tech's Portal Revolution — Pickering, Glasco, and the WCWS Dream
**Tier:** 3 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP
Texas Tech signs Kasidi Pickering tomorrow as part of a historic portal class assembled by head coach Gerry Glasco. The Red Raiders enter the Nov. 11 signing window as the most fascinating program in fall softball. With Pickering (.384 BA, 50 HR, 169 RBI at OU) anchoring the offense and a supporting cast from the No. 1 portal class nationally, Texas Tech is making a credible bid to reach Oklahoma City in 2027.
