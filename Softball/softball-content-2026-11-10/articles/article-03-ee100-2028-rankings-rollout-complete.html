EE100 Class of 2028 Rankings Rollout Is Complete — Landers, Holden, Schlader Lead the Largest Class Ever

By Sarah Mitchell | November 10, 2026 | I Love Softball

Extra Inning Softball's Class of 2028 Extra Elite 100 rankings rollout is complete. The sequence that began November 1 with the Northeast regional hitters hotlist wrapped up over the weekend with the national top 25 lists on November 7-8 and the Pitcher-Hitter Duo Top 25 on November 9. For the first time in the cycle's history, 1,200-plus athletes received nominations, making the 2028 class the largest ever evaluated by the platform.

The Top Three

No. 1 overall in the Class of 2028 is Avery Landers, an infielder committed to Texas. Landers topped every regional and national ranking throughout the rollout — a consistent result across the eight regional lists and the national reveal. Texas lands a generational infielder who, if she develops on her current trajectory, will be the face of the program's 2031 postseason run.

No. 2 is Ellie Holden, committed to Florida. The Gators secured Holden during the spring contact window and have been able to plan around her development since. No. 3 is Mia Schlader, an infielder committed to Oklahoma with a 70-plus mph riseball that evaluators have already flagged as an elite pitching tool at the high school level. For OU, that combination — elite bat at the plate, elite arm in the circle — is a projection that extends the program's tradition of two-way impact players.

What 1,200-Plus Nominations Means

The sheer volume of this class reflects how early elite recruiting has become in college softball. Athletes nominated in the Class of 2028 are current eighth-graders and freshmen — some as young as 13 and 14. That programs are seriously evaluating players this young has drawn both enthusiasm from fans who want to track the pipeline and concern from those who believe the pressure of national rankings lands too early on developing athletes.

The regional rollout model serves a purpose in that context. By spreading the reveal across eight regions over six days before the national lists, Extra Inning Softball gives regional talent a moment before the national conversation absorbs everything. Programs in smaller markets — the Mountain Region, the Midwest — benefit from regional visibility that a single national list would flatten.

The Programs That Won the Cycle

Texas, Florida, and Oklahoma top the commit list by sheer ranking of their current 2028 commitments. Tennessee secured multiple top-100 commits during the fall contact window that closed in late October. Texas Tech, in the middle of its portal revolution, also added 2028 commits this fall — a signal that Gerry Glasco is building on both the immediate and long-term timeline simultaneously.

The next phase of the 2028 cycle begins with the December contact period. Programs will use the Winter Meetings and early 2027 official visits to close on uncommitted prospects. For now, though, the rankings are set. The Class of 2028's pecking order has been established.

What's Next

With the 2028 rankings complete, the focus shifts immediately to November 11 Signing Day and the Class of 2027 that signs tomorrow. The 2028 class will return to the conversation in December and throughout the winter contact windows, with individual rankings potentially updating as players develop. For the top three — Landers, Holden, and Schlader — tomorrow's Signing Day is a reminder of what they are preparing for: signing their own letters two years from now, in a college uniform that is already waiting for them.
