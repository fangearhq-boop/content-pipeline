Signing Day Is Tomorrow — Five Programs With the Most at Stake on November 11

By Jordan Reeves | November 10, 2026 | I Love Softball

Tomorrow, the offseason officially begins. November 11, 2026 is National Signing Day — the first date on which Class of 2027 softball recruits can sign athletics aid agreements with their college programs. The National Letter of Intent was eliminated in October 2024, but the tradition and significance of Signing Day has not changed. Five programs enter Tuesday with more on the line than anyone else.

Florida — Loaded and Adding

Florida closed out its fall schedule at 4-0 with a staggering run differential, outscoring opponents 73-9. First-year transfer Caroline Edwards contributed four home runs in the fall. The Gators enter Signing Day with six starters returning from their 2026 squad and an incoming class expected to rank among the top 10 nationally. No. 2 overall Class of 2028 recruit Ellie Holden is committed to Florida, locking in the program's future even as this year's class finalizes.

Florida's Signing Day objective is straightforward: add depth behind a strong returning core. Any top-10 final ranking on the high school side would be consistent with expectations.

Tennessee — Rebuilding the Rotation

Kelsey Pickens, who posted a 1.70 ERA and 194 strikeouts in 2026, has exhausted her eligibility. Tennessee's 2027 rotation needs new pieces, and Signing Day is where Karen Weekly adds them. The Volunteers finished their fall at 3-0, with Madison Mardjetko (16-3, 1.26 ERA in 2026) and freshman Hannah Shelton showing they can hold the fort. But Weekly has been targeting incoming pitchers who can contribute immediately.

Tennessee also has four freshmen who made strong impressions in fall ball. Signing Day is about completing the picture. A rotation-first approach is expected.

Oklahoma — Signing Around Wells and Kavan

Kendall Wells, who hit 39 home runs and earned NFCA Freshman of the Year honors in 2026, is back for her sophomore season. Rylie Kavan, the two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player, anchors the pitching staff. OU's Signing Day objective is to add quality around that core: a middle-of-the-order bat, speed at the top of the lineup, and depth in the circle.

The fall schedule ended with the ETAM cancellation-turned-Rose State game, but OU's performance this fall was solid. Coach Patty Gasso heads into Signing Day with a clear set of needs and the program's prestige to attract players who fill them.

Texas — Defending Champion Standard

Texas won the 2026 national title and has an obligation to match it. The Longhorns enter Signing Day with No. 1 overall Class of 2028 recruit Avery Landers already committed for next year, but tomorrow's signing class is about maintaining the present standard. Texas typically ranks in the top five nationally on Signing Day, and 2026 is not expected to be different.

Texas Tech — The Biggest Story of the Fall

Texas Tech has dominated the fall recruiting conversation in a way no program has in recent memory. Head coach Gerry Glasco assembled the No. 1 portal class in the country, headlined by Kasidi Pickering, who transfers from Oklahoma after three seasons in which she hit .384 with 50 home runs and 169 RBI. Tomorrow, Pickering signs. What Glasco adds from the high school side will determine whether the Red Raiders have a top-five Signing Day nationally.

For a program that has historically been on the periphery of national title conversations, this Signing Day is a statement. The Red Raiders have never been here before.

What's Next

Signing Day announcements will roll out throughout the morning and afternoon on November 11. Programs will post on social media as agreements are signed, and recruiting services will update their national class rankings throughout the day. By evening, the initial picture of the Class of 2027 landscape will be clear. The 5-for-5 ruling from Friday reduces — but does not eliminate — roster uncertainty, so programs will be signing with one eye on the state-court cases still unresolved. Tomorrow, however, belongs to the recruits.
