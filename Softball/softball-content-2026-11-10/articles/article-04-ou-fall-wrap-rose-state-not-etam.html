OU's Fall Finale Got a Surprise Guest — ETAM Canceled, Rose State Stepped In

By Emily Rawlings | November 10, 2026 | I Love Softball

Oklahoma's November 4 exhibition against East Texas A&M at Love's Field did not go as planned — because East Texas A&M did not show up. The Lions withdrew, citing unforeseen circumstances, and Rose State College stepped in as a replacement. It was an unusual ending to what had been a smoothly run fall schedule for the Sooners, but it did not change what matters: Oklahoma heads into winter workouts with Kendall Wells and Rylie Kavan healthy and ready for 2027.

The Cancellation

East Texas A&M informed Oklahoma that it would not travel to Norman for the scheduled 6 PM exhibition. The timing drew immediate attention: the night before, ETAM's men's basketball team had defeated Oklahoma 68-64 at Lloyd Noble Center. Oklahoma did not publicly cite the basketball result as a factor in ETAM's softball withdrawal, and no official connection has been confirmed. But the optics were impossible to ignore.

Rose State, a community college located about 20 miles from Norman, agreed to fill the slot. The first pitch moved from 6 PM to 6:30 PM. Fans who already had tickets were told they remained valid for the updated time and opponent.

What Oklahoma's Fall Looked Like

The substitution with Rose State was a footnote to what had been an encouraging fall for Oklahoma softball. The Sooners ran through the Battle Series, their annual fall exhibition schedule, with Kendall Wells contributing multiple home runs — including a back-to-back shot with another Sooner in one game — and Rylie Kavan working in the circle with her usual precision.

Wells, who hit 39 home runs and earned NFCA Freshman of the Year honors in 2026, is entering her sophomore season as one of the most dangerous power hitters in college softball. Kavan, the two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player, remains the anchor of a pitching staff that is deeper than it was a year ago.

Coach Patty Gasso used the fall to evaluate depth pieces, test lineup combinations, and get new additions comfortable in the program's system. By every visible measure, those evaluations went well.

Winter Workouts Begin

With the fall slate now complete, Oklahoma moves into its winter conditioning and skill development phase. The 2027 spring season opener is approximately 10 weeks away. The program's standard is unambiguous: Oklahoma has been to the Women's College World Series in every recent season, and the 2027 spring calendar gives the Sooners another opportunity to extend that run.

Tomorrow's Signing Day adds the final piece of the offseason picture. Oklahoma will formalize its 2027 incoming class, adding around the Wells-Kavan core. The program's No. 3 overall Class of 2028 commit, Mia Schlader, is a reminder that the pipeline does not stop at next year.

What's Next

Signing Day is November 11. Oklahoma's spring schedule is expected to drop in the coming weeks. For fans waiting to see the 2027 Sooners on the field, the wait is now measured in months, not seasons. The fall is done. The work has started. Love's Field will be loud again come February.
