5-for-5 at the December Crossroads
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Here's where things stand with the NCAA's 5-for-5 eligibility rule — and why Dec. 1 is a critical milestone. The Tenth Circuit Court of Appeals reversed the federal injunction in Wisne v. NCAA earlier this month. That was a win for the NCAA — the federal shield that had protected affected athletes is gone. But 850 athletes are still in limbo. That's because 200+ state court cases are still alive and active across the country. State courts operate independently of the Tenth Circuit, and those cases haven't been dismissed. The December portal opening on Dec. 1 adds a layer of urgency. Athletes who have been waiting for legal clarity now face the question: enter the portal and try to find a new situation, or stay and keep fighting? For programs, the message is mixed — some athletes may become available who weren't before. But the 25-player House settlement cap still limits how many any program can add. There's no clean resolution coming before January. But Dec. 1 through Dec. 15 will be revealing. ### Image Post Caption 200+ state court cases. 850 athletes. The federal shield is gone — but state courts are still fighting. Dec. 1 brings the portal AND the next chapter of the 5-for-5 battle. #5for5 #NCCASoftball

### Long-Form Post Quietly, while everyone debated the SEC, the Big 12 is building something historically rare in college softball. Three teams. Three different paths to the top. The Wildcats are rebuilding and winning simultaneously. Big 12 conference play opens March 12. For the first time, the conference could realistically send three programs to the WCWS quarterfinals.
