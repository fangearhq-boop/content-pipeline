10 Days: The December Transfer Portal Is Coming and These Teams Need Pitchers

By Emily Rawlings | I Love Softball | November 21, 2026

The December transfer portal opens Dec. 1. That's 10 days from now. And unlike the summer window, which reshuffled rosters at every level, the December window is specific: programs need pitchers, and not just any pitchers. They need aces.

Several top-10 programs enter this window with gaping holes on their pitching staffs. The teams that move fastest and land the right arm will have the best shot at competing in June. Here's who needs what — and why the next two weeks will define the 2027 title race before it officially starts.

Arizona: The Most Urgent Need

No program needs this window more than Arizona. The Wildcats went 15-2 in the fall under first-year head coach Caitlin Lowe — a remarkable performance that proved the roster is talented, coachable, and motivated. But Regan Shockey (Big 12 Defensive Player of the Year) cannot carry this team alone.

When Mariah Torres — the D1Softball National Player of the Year — transferred to Texas Tech, Arizona lost its identity in the circle. The Wildcats went from contender to question mark in a single decision. The December portal is Lowe's chance to answer that question directly. Arizona has the program, the NIL infrastructure, and the roster to attract an elite transfer arm. If they land one before Dec. 15, they're back in the conversation.

Oklahoma: Depth Behind Wells

Oklahoma already holds the No. 1 class by D1Softball, and Patty Gasso added Harter from MTSU and Hansen from CSI earlier in the portal cycle. But the Sooners' rotation needs an elite arm at the top — the kind of pitcher who can win a game in Tucson or Tallahassee in May.

With Fale Aviu Wells (39 HR, NFCA Freshman of the Year) and Nicole Kavan (2x WCWS MOP) driving the offense, OU is capable of outscoring opponents all spring. But a deep WCWS run requires dominant pitching. Gasso has 10 days to find that pitcher before the window closes Dec. 15.

Ole Miss and Auburn

Both Ole Miss and Auburn enter the December window with similar needs: SEC-caliber pitching to elevate classes that are strong offensively but thin in the circle. Auburn holds the No. 4 class nationally per Softball America, and Bobby Pierce's offensive core is legitimate. One elite transfer arm is all that separates the Tigers from being a genuine dark horse.

Ole Miss is in a similar position — a roster with offensive pop but uncertainty in the circle. The Rebels know their window is narrowing as the December portal fills quickly.

What's Next

The dead period runs Dec. 9-12, which means no recruiting contact for four days right in the middle of the window. That makes Dec. 1-8 the most valuable stretch of the entire offseason. Programs that have done their homework — made contact, built relationships, run film — will be positioned to close quickly.

Watch for announcements in the first 72 hours after the portal opens. That's when the elite arms will move. The team that lands one before the dead period sets up a rotation that can win in June. The one that waits until the final days of the window gets the leftovers — and in December's market, there are very few pitchers who would change a program's ceiling.
