What January Polls Will Tell Us That December Can't

By Emily Rawlings | I Love Softball | November 21, 2026

December is about transactions. January is about verdicts. The first preseason polls of the 2027 season — which drop in January — will be the sport's first real attempt to process everything that happened over the fall and early winter: the recruiting classes, the portal moves, the 5-for-5 legal battle, and the offseason shakeup that reshuffled rosters from Tucson to Baton Rouge.

These polls will be the most contested in the sport's history, and here's why they matter more this year than any year before them.

Two No. 1 Teams — And No Consensus

Heading into the January polls, the two major services have never agreed less. D1Softball ranks Oklahoma No. 1. Softball America has LSU at the top — the first time in program history the Tigers have held that position. Both services have defensible cases, and neither is going to back down without data.

The first preseason poll will be a verdict on that debate. If Oklahoma lands an elite pitcher from the December portal and closes with a dominant class, the services may converge. If LSU adds through the portal and stays intact, Softball America's ranking looks more prescient. Either way, January settles the argument — temporarily.

What December's Portal Will Feed Into January

The December portal window (Dec. 1-15) closes several weeks before the first January polls. That means every move made in that window will be factored in. Arizona lands a pitcher? They move up. OU adds an elite arm? Their No. 1 ranking looks safer. Auburn gets the transfer they need? Suddenly a team ranked No. 4 becomes a legitimate top-two conversation.

The dead period (Dec. 9-12) means the most significant announcements come in two bursts: Dec. 1-8 and Dec. 13-15. Watch those windows carefully. The January poll will reflect what happens in them.

5-for-5 and Roster Clarity

If state courts produce significant eligibility rulings before January, rosters could shift. Some programs may gain athletes they didn't expect. Others might lose players if rulings go against them. The January polls will incorporate whatever roster clarity has emerged from the 200+ ongoing state court cases — which makes this January's rankings uniquely dependent on events outside the sport's control.

What's Next

December is 10 days away. January is about six weeks out. In the span of time between those two moments, the 2027 season will take shape. The teams that emerge from December with the rosters they needed — elite pitching, cleared eligibility, stable coaching staffs — will walk into January's preseason polls as favorites. The teams that couldn't get what they needed will spend the spring trying to prove those polls wrong.

Watch December. It tells you who January will reward.
