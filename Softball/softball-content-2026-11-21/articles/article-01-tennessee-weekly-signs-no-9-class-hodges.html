Tennessee's No. 9 Recruiting Class Features No. 1 Pitcher Kaylee Hodges

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | November 21, 2026

Karen Weekly has been building something in Knoxville, and the 2027 signing class is the latest proof. Tennessee signed six players ranked in the top 80 by Softball America, landing the No. 9 class nationally. The headliner is Kaylee Hodges, the top-ranked pitcher in the entire Class of 2027.

Hodges joins a rotation that already returns Sage Mardjetko, who posted a 16-3 record and 1.26 ERA in 2026 while earning NFCA First Team All-American honors. Add a true freshman in Shelton who showed seven strikeouts in three innings in fall scrimmages, and the Tennessee pitching staff may be the deepest in the SEC entering 2027.

What Hodges Brings

The No. 1-ranked pitcher in the Class of 2027 is not just a recruiting number. Hodges represents the kind of frontline arm that programs spend entire offseasons chasing through the transfer portal. Tennessee did it through recruiting — and that distinction matters in an era when continuity is rare.

All six of Tennessee's 2027 signees rank in the top 80 nationally by Softball America. That depth of class — not just one elite player — is what separates a good signing day from a great one. Weekly didn't just add a star. She added a class with no weak spots.

The Foundation Is Already Stacked

Nine starters return from a 49-12 Tennessee team that competed deep into the 2026 postseason. Weekly signed a contract extension through 2030 this fall, signaling long-term stability at a time when coaching changes have disrupted programs across the SEC.

Sophia Knight (.394 average) anchors the offense. The 2026 pitching staff was the best in the conference by ERA. And now, with Hodges arriving and Mardjetko returning, Tennessee enters 2027 with the kind of rotation depth that wins WCWS championships in June.

Tennessee has not won a national title, but this program has never been better positioned. Nine returning starters, the No. 1 incoming pitcher in the country, and a head coach whose contract says she isn't going anywhere for years.

What's Next

Tennessee will not make a major move in the December transfer portal — the foundation is already built. The focus shifts to the January preseason polls, where the Vols figure to land firmly in the top five. For fans watching the 2027 title race, Tennessee is not a sleeper. The Vols are a frontrunner, and the signing of Kaylee Hodges is the latest reminder of why.
