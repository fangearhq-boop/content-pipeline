The Big 12 Is Building a Three-Team WCWS Threat — Here's Why 2027 Is Different

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | November 21, 2026

The SEC has dominated college softball for most of the sport's modern era. But heading into 2027, something genuinely new is happening in the Big 12. Three programs in the same conference — Oklahoma, Texas Tech, and Arizona — are all legitimate WCWS quarterfinalists. The conference opens play March 12, and the internal battles between these three teams may be the most important games played all season.

Oklahoma: The Powerhouse With a Point to Prove

Patty Gasso's Sooners hold the No. 1 class by D1Softball, with Julianna "Goose" Hutchens already enrolled on campus. Fale Aviu Wells (39 HR, NFCA Freshman of the Year) and Nicole Kavan (two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player) return to the lineup. The Sooners lost Kasidi Pickering to Texas Tech and need an elite arm from the December portal, but the offensive firepower is unquestioned.

Oklahoma is the standard. Every other team in the conference is chasing them. But the gap has narrowed dramatically.

Texas Tech: The Portal Engine

Kasidi Pickering's arrival changes everything. The former OU All-American batted .384 with 50 home runs and 169 RBI over three seasons in Norman before transferring to Lubbock. Texas Tech's No. 1 transfer class nationally — per Softball America — adds multiple supporting pieces around Pickering, including Jocelyn Gentry, the first five-star freshman in program history.

Second-year head coach Dana Glasco has built Texas Tech into a legitimate power in a single offseason cycle. The Red Raiders are not a conference curiosity. They're a contender.

Arizona: Rebuilding and Winning at the Same Time

Caitlin Lowe won 15 of 17 games in her first fall as Arizona's head coach. Regan Shockey, the Big 12 Defensive Player of the Year, anchors a defense that held opponents to minimal runs throughout the fall schedule. Arizona still needs a pitcher from the December portal — that remains the program's ceiling variable — but the structure Lowe has built in her first months on campus is impressive.

Arizona is the program most capable of surprising everyone. If Lowe lands an ace before Dec. 15, the Wildcats are a legitimate national semifinalist.

What's Next

The Big 12 conference opener is March 12 — and by that point, we'll know what all three programs look like after the December portal closes and January preseason polls reset the rankings. For fans who've watched the SEC dominate for a decade, 2027 offers something new: a conference that might send three teams to Oklahoma City in June. That's not a projection. It's a reasonable expectation, and the first time anyone could say that about a conference not named the SEC.
