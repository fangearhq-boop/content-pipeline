USA Softball Returns to OKC in 33 Days — Coming Off International Cup Gold at the Same Venue

By Jordan Reeves

August 10, 2026

USA softball already knows what winning looks like at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. They just did it there a little over a week ago.

From July 30 to August 3, the USA Women's National Team went 5-0 at the 2026 USA Softball International Cup at Devon Park, outscoring opponents 32-8 and claiming gold in the tournament that served as the program's final major preparation event before the WBSC Softball Women's World Cup Group Stage. On September 12 — 33 days from today — USA returns to the same complex for something larger.

The International Cup as a Preview

The International Cup wasn't a casual exhibition. USA WNT beat USA Elite 6-2 in the semifinal — meaning the program's second-tier roster pushed the national team enough that it required a real competitive performance to advance. The WNT outscored all opposition by a combined 24 runs across five games. Whatever adjustments head coach Patty Gasso and her staff wanted to evaluate, the International Cup gave them competitive data to work from.

The roster heading into September is led by two players who have been here before. Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola are both 2020 Olympians who understand what it means to carry a national team through a tournament with real stakes at every game. They're joined by seven athletes from the 2025 World Games gold medal squad and 10 players from the 2025 Down Under Series — a roster built on tournament experience rather than raw potential.

The Group Stage Opponents

USA (No. 2 in the world) faces its opponents in a round-robin Group Stage September 12-16 at Devon Park. The schedule opens against No. 7 Mexico on September 12. The full group: No. 6 Netherlands, No. 7 Mexico, No. 8 China, No. 23 New Zealand, and No. 36 South Africa. USA earned automatic entry into the host group.

The challenge of any group stage format is maintaining competitive focus through round-robin games that don't all carry equal pressure. Against opponents ranked significantly below the USA, the risk is mental — keeping the team sharp for the games that matter while handling the earlier matchups professionally. Games against Netherlands and China will test whether this roster is ready for the level of competition they'll face in the World Cup Finals.

What's at Stake Beyond the Group Stage

The WBSC World Cup now operates in a two-stage format: Group Stage results in 2026 feed into the World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Australia on April 5-11, 2027. Those Finals results, in turn, are part of the qualifying pathway toward LA28 Olympic competition. Every game in Oklahoma City this September has downstream implications for the next two years of international softball.

For USA softball, the mission is familiar: go to OKC, play well, advance the program toward another Olympics appearance. The venue is the same. The goals are the same. Thirty-three days from now, the games begin.

What's Next

The WBSC Softball Women's World Cup Group Stage opens September 12 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. USA plays Mexico in the Group Stage opener. Play continues through September 16 before the playoff rounds. Results determine USA's seeding heading into the 2027 World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Australia.
