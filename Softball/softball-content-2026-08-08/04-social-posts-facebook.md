### STORY 5: USA WNT World Cup Group Stage

#### Long-Form Post — 5:30 PM ET

International Cup gold: won.

Next mission: 35 days away.

The USA Women's National Team won the International Cup on August 3 in Oklahoma City — Montana Fouts delivered a near-perfect complete game in a 2-0 shutout of Japan at Devon Park. Five days later, the same venue is already on the calendar for the next challenge.

**WBSC Softball Women's World Cup Group Stage**
**September 12-16 | Devon Park | Oklahoma City, Oklahoma**

USA draws an automatic berth as host. Their group is set:
- Netherlands (World No. 6)
- Mexico (World No. 7)
- China (World No. 8)
- New Zealand (World No. 23)
- South Africa (World No. 36)

The 16-player roster that heads to OKC includes Rachel Garcia, Dejah Mulipola, Montana Fouts, and Maya Brady, along with newcomers Valerie Wells (OU) and Taylor Shumaker (Florida) making their WNT debuts. Ten of the 16 players compete in the Southeastern Conference.

This is the path to the 2027 World Cup — and beyond that, the 2028 Olympics. Thirty-five days.
