The Texas Tech 2027 Class Is Arriving — Fall Camp Is Around the Corner

By Sarah Mitchell

August 8, 2026

The announcements are over. The portal entries are closed. The transfers are official. Texas Tech's historic 2027 class is arriving on campus in Lubbock, and within weeks, Jaysoni Beachum, Kasidi Pickering, Jayden Heavener, Kaitlyn Terry, and Mia Williams will share a practice field for the first time.

None of them have ever been a Texas Tech Red Raider together. They have been competing against each other, in some cases directly, for the past two years. Now head coach Gerry Glasco — the coach who took this program to the 2026 WCWS Finals — gets to see what his offseason vision looks like in red and black.

The Newcomers, One More Time

Jaysoni Beachum comes from Florida State, where she was the 2024 NFCA Freshman of the Year and earned 2026 Second Team All-American honors. Her 2026 numbers: .406 batting average, 10 home runs, and 69 RBI. She ranked among the top three players in the entire transfer portal in most evaluations.

Kasidi Pickering made the move from Oklahoma, where she was a key contributor to the 2024 national championship team. She hit .370 with 20 home runs and 61 RBI in 2026, and her combination of power and contact production at the Big 12 level made her one of the most coveted position players available. The Pickering addition at the deadline of the portal cycle was considered a program-altering move.

Jayden Heavener pitched at LSU, where she went 13-9 with a 2.81 ERA and 128 strikeouts across her final season as a Tiger. She comes to Lubbock as a legitimate front-of-rotation option for Glasco, who needed pitching depth behind the returners already in place.

Kaitlyn Terry arrives from UCLA as a two-time All-American pitcher with postseason experience. Mia Williams, a 2026 NFCA All-American from Florida, rounds out the class at the position level. When the full depth chart is laid out, this is the broadest concentration of All-American-level talent that has ever entered a fall camp for a single softball program.

What Glasco Gets to Work With

The 2026 WCWS Finals appearance gave this program a standard. Glasco took the Red Raiders further than any team in program history, and then went to work on building a roster that could finish what the 2026 team started. The transfer portal class has been ranked No. 1 nationally by multiple independent services.

Fall camp is the first real test. Depth charts will be sorted. Rotations will take shape. The team that competes in this fall's scrimmages and exhibition games will look very different from the team that took the field at the 2026 WCWS. That team had a ceiling. This one is still determining where the ceiling is.

What's Next

Texas Tech's fall practice schedule will be released through the program's official athletic department channels. First-year eligibility and any transfer waiver updates will be confirmed through the athletic department and NFCA tracking. The 2027 regular season is expected to begin in February, with full conference schedules and early-season tournament brackets released in the fall. Official Texas Tech athletics information is available at texastech.com/sports/softball.
