USA Softball Is Back in OKC in 35 Days — World Cup Group Stage Opponents Are Set

By Emily Rawlings

August 8, 2026

Montana Fouts threw a near-perfect complete game against Japan on August 3 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City, and the USA Women's National Team walked away with International Cup gold. Five days later, the calendar to the next mission is already running: 35 days until the WBSC Softball Women's World Cup Group Stage returns to the same venue.

Devon Park will host the Group Stage September 12-16. The USA Women's National Team earned automatic entry as the host nation. The group is set. The roster is named. The path to the 2027 World Cup — and beyond it, the 2028 Olympics — runs through the next five weeks.

Who USA Will Face in the Group Stage

The Group Stage field is a legitimate test against top-ranked international competition. USA's confirmed group opponents:

Netherlands enters as the No. 6 world-ranked team and one of the most consistent programs in international softball. Mexico (No. 7) and China (No. 8) round out the top eight of the world rankings within USA's group. New Zealand (No. 23) and South Africa (No. 36) complete the six-team field. This is not a group that will be navigated without preparation — the Netherlands, Mexico, and China are all programs with World Cup and Olympic pedigrees.

USA enters as the world's No. 2 ranked team behind Japan, coming off back-to-back tournament gold medals. The International Cup gold in OKC was the most recent confirmation that the program remains among the standard-setters by which every other national team measures itself. The Group Stage is an opportunity to demonstrate that the depth of this 16-player roster goes beyond Montana Fouts and the names fans know best.

The Roster Heading to OKC

The 16-player WNT named for the World Cup Group Stage includes Rachel Garcia, Dejah Mulipola, Montana Fouts, and Maya Brady — four of the most recognizable names in USA Softball. The roster also introduces two players making their WNT debut: Valerie Wells from Oklahoma and Taylor Shumaker from Florida. Ten of the 16 athletes compete in the Southeastern Conference, reflecting how dominant SEC programs have been in developing national-team-caliber talent.

The group has competition experience at every level. For the two debutants, September 12-16 will be their first time wearing USA colors in official WBSC play. For veterans like Garcia and Mulipola, it adds to a career built on international performance at the highest stages. The coaching staff will integrate the full roster through the next five weeks of preparation.

What This Group Stage Means

The WBSC Softball Women's World Cup Group Stage is not just a tournament — it is a direct qualification pathway. Performance in the Group Stage, and in the subsequent knockout rounds that follow, determines seeding and advancement toward the 2027 World Cup and, through that, the 2028 Olympics qualification process. For USA, first place in the group is the goal. Every result in Oklahoma City matters.

What's Next

The WBSC Softball Women's World Cup Group Stage begins September 12 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. Game schedule, broadcast information, and bracket details will be available through usasoftball.com and wbsc.org. The full 16-player USA WNT roster is available on the USA Softball official website.
