AUSL All-Star Cup Opens in Rosemont: Garcia, Ricketts, and Corrick Take the Captain's Chair

By Emily Rawlings

August 8, 2026

Everything that was announced as a preview is now a competition. The 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup officially opened Saturday at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois — and before the first pitch was thrown, the 45-player field had already split into teams, picked captains, and begun figuring out how to win an event that rewards individual performance above everything else.

Rachel Garcia, Keilani Ricketts, and Georgina Corrick were named Series 1 captains. Each had up to two minutes per pick in the draft, selecting rosters from the full pool of players who qualified for the All-Star Cup. The rosters they assembled today are the rosters they'll compete with for the duration of Series 1. After this series ends, those rosters dissolve. Everyone goes back into the pool. A new set of captains picks again. That process repeats four times through August 30.

What the All-Star Cup Is — and Isn't

Understanding the competition requires separating it from every other softball event. There is no permanent team. There are no team standings at the end of August. The Chicago Bandits, Carolina Blaze, Utah Talons, Oklahoma City Spark, Portland Cascade, and Texas Volts provided the player pool — their rosters made up the 45 participants — but those organizational identities don't carry over into the All-Star Cup.

The Athletes Unlimited scoring system tracks individual performance across every game in every series. At-bats, runs, wins, strikeouts, defensive plays — all of it accumulates in a single leaderboard. Whoever has the most points at the end of the final series is the All-Star Cup individual champion. It is a format designed specifically to produce the best answer to a question professional softball has always wanted to ask: of these 45 players, right now, who is the best?

Garcia brings the pedigree of a national team ace and multiple Athletes Unlimited seasons. Ricketts is a veteran of the format and one of the most recognized names in the game. Corrick adds international experience as a pitcher with Australian national team background. The Series 1 captains each bring different strengths to the draft table — and different strategic philosophies about how to build a winning team in a format where winning a team game and accumulating individual points are not always the same goal.

How to Watch

All 12 games of the 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup will air nationally. ESPN platforms, MLB Network, and CBS Sports Network have the broadcast rights. The schedule runs four series through August 30, with each series taking place at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont. The stadium sits just outside Chicago, and the venue has hosted professional softball consistently since the AUSL's founding season.

Eight players arrived at the All-Star Cup through the fan vote: Reese Atwood, Rylie Boone, Alyssa Brito, Bri Ellis, Jayda Coleman, Megan Grant, Karlyn Pickens, and Jordan Woolery. All eight entered the draft pool along with the 37 players selected by the AUSL's other process. Whether they were fan-elected or organization-qualified, every player is drafting from the same pool today. Status means nothing in the Athletes Unlimited format. Points mean everything.

What's Next

Series 1 runs through the weekend at Parkway Bank Sports Complex. After Series 1 concludes, the player pool resets and new captains will be announced for Series 2. The All-Star Cup runs through August 30, at which point the individual leaderboard produces the 2026 champion. Full schedule, scores, and standings are available at theausl.com and on ESPN platforms.
