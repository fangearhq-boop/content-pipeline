Florida Survives; NC and Kentucky Wait — Bracket Championship Day Arrives at the LLSWS

By Sarah Mitchell

August 8, 2026

The Little League Softball World Series arrives at its most consequential day of the tournament on Saturday, and the bracket is exactly what the first week of play set up: two unbeaten teams waiting on one side each, facing opponents who had to fight their way back in through the elimination round.

Friday's games answered the final bracket questions. Florida (Lake Mary Little League) held Washington to zero runs in the Purple bracket elimination, winning 5-0 to keep their 2026 LLSWS run alive. Across the bracket, New Jersey advanced from the Orange elimination by defeating Puerto Rico. The results mean Saturday belongs to two championship games — and one grand final berth on the line in each.

Florida Shuts Out Washington to Force a Rematch

Florida's Day 6 game against Washington was not the same squad that struggled to generate offense against North Carolina on Day 5. Lake Mary held Washington scoreless across the full game, winning 5-0, and in doing so earned their way back to face North Carolina for the Purple Bracket Championship.

The rematch angle is the story that frames everything about the Purple Championship today. On Day 5, North Carolina's Anna-Lynn Clark went six innings and struck out 13 Florida batters in a 5-2 win that sent NC to 3-0. Clark's performance was one of the best individual pitching lines of the entire 2026 LLSWS. Florida was held scoreless until late in that game and allowed five runs in the fifth inning before the outcome was decided.

Friday changed the narrative. A different Florida team showed up — one that dominated Washington on the mound and found enough offense to make the score comfortable. They earned the right to face NC again. Today, they get it.

Kentucky and New Jersey in the Orange Championship

The Orange bracket tells a different story. Kentucky (Daviess County Little League) went 3-0 through the tournament's first week without facing an elimination scenario. They beat Puerto Rico 2-0 on Day 5 and have had a full rest day before Saturday's Championship game. New Jersey won the Orange elimination Friday to advance into the Championship.

Kentucky arrives today as the Orange bracket's dominant team by record and by margin. The Daviess County squad has allowed minimal runs across their three games and enters Saturday with the rotation advantage that comes from not needing to play on Friday. New Jersey, meanwhile, has earned their spot by surviving two consecutive high-pressure games. Fresh legs versus battle-tested experience — both carry advantages today.

The Stakes: One Grand Final Berth Per Bracket

Saturday's outcomes are direct: win the Orange Championship and you're in Sunday's Grand Final. Win the Purple Championship and you meet the Orange winner on Sunday. There is no more bracket flexibility. No more second chances after today.

North Carolina arrived in Greenville as the host representative and has done everything a host team could ask: three wins, a near-perfect tournament, and now a bracket championship at home. Kentucky has shown why a 3-0 record through pool play means something at this level. Florida showed Friday that their Day 5 loss to NC was a result of one exceptional pitcher — not a team-wide collapse.

For Anna-Lynn Clark, the Purple Championship is the third start of an extraordinary tournament. She has not allowed an earned run in multiple consecutive outings and will carry the No. 1 seed's momentum into a rematch against the opponent she last faced four days ago. The 2026 LLSWS has been defined by her performance. Saturday gives it a final test.

What's Next

Saturday's bracket championship winners advance to Sunday's Grand Final at Stallings Stadium in Greenville, North Carolina. The Grand Final will determine the 2026 LLSWS Champion. Both Saturday championship games are available to watch on ESPN. Full schedule, results, and bracket updates are available at LittleLeague.org.
