AUSL Series 4: Championship Eve — Kilfoyl vs. Klingler With the Title on the Line

By Sarah Mitchell

August 29, 2026

The 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup individual championship is one night away from being decided — and the race at the top of the leaderboard has never been tighter.

Tonight's Game 2 of Series 4 features Team Flippen against Team Kilfoyl at 8 PM ET at the Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois. It could be the most consequential game of the entire All-Star Cup season.

Entering Series 4, Baylee Klingler leads the overall leaderboard with 890 points. Lexi Kilfoyl sits just 24 points back at 866. Hannah Flippen is third at 750. The individual champion is announced August 30 — tomorrow — meaning tonight's performance carries enormous weight for both Klingler and Kilfoyl.

24 Points. One Night Left.

In AUSL scoring, a single Game MVP award is worth 100 or more points. That means a strong performance by Kilfoyl tonight — combined with a quiet night from Klingler in a separate game — could flip the standings entirely before the champion is crowned.

Kilfoyl has made this case all season. She has earned 3 Game MVP awards in 2026 — the most of any player in the league. Her ability to control games and rack up individual scoring moments is precisely the trait that makes her capable of a dramatic comeback in these final hours.

Klingler, meanwhile, has been the model of consistency. The corner infielder leads the league at 890 points because she has produced week after week across all four series, and her 4-0 all-time record as a captain entering the season established her as the top player in the league entering this final stretch.

Teammate Turned Rival

There is an added layer to tonight's matchup. Kilfoyl spent Series 3 as a member of Team Klingler — a teammate and collaborator in the push toward the individual standings. In Series 4, she drafted her own roster, became a captain, and is now facing Klingler as a direct rival.

That dynamic is central to what makes the All-Star Cup format so compelling. Players compete for the same individual title while cycling through different team configurations, creating alliances that shift week to week. Tonight, the two players who have spent the most time at the top of the leaderboard are on opposite sidelines.

Series 4 Structure

Series 4 opened Thursday night, August 28, with Team Klingler facing Team Flippen on CBS Sports Network. Tonight's matchup — Team Flippen vs. Team Kilfoyl — completes the round-robin schedule. The individual champion is crowned tomorrow, August 30, based on cumulative leaderboard points across all four series.

Kilfoyl carries an 8-6 all-time record as a captain into Series 4, making her one of the most experienced captains in league history at this stage of her career.

What's Next

The 2026 AUSL individual champion will be announced on August 30, closing out the All-Star Cup season. Following the All-Star Cup, attention turns to the AUSL regular season and the upcoming WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage in Oklahoma City (September 12-16), where several AUSL players will represent Team USA. Watch tonight's game at 8 PM ET for the final chapter of one of the most competitive leaderboard races in All-Star Cup history.
