14 Days to Group Oklahoma: USA Softball Countdown Begins in Earnest

By Jordan Reeves

August 29, 2026

Two weeks from today, USA Softball throws the first pitch of the 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage — and every game in Oklahoma City carries the weight of an Olympic dream.

The Group Oklahoma competition runs September 12-16 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. Six nations compete in round-robin play, with the top two finishers advancing to the Finals in Brisbane, Australia in April 2027. At the Finals, the highest-placed team earns a direct berth to the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics — making every inning in OKC a step on the road to LA28.

Gasso Leads a Battle-Tested Staff

Head coach Patty Gasso, who also leads the Oklahoma Sooners, takes charge of the USA WNT for the Group Stage. She is joined on the coaching staff by JT Gasso (assistant) and Jennifer Rocha (pitching coach) — a trio that adds continuity to a program navigating a new World Cup format.

The coaching staff's preparation has been intensive, with the roster finalized weeks ago and key tactical decisions already locked in. Seven athletes from the 2025 World Games gold medal squad are on the roster, giving the team a foundation of international tournament experience heading into one of the most competitive group fields in recent memory.

Veteran Roster, Young Stakes

The USA roster is headlined by 2020 Tokyo Olympians Valerie Garcia and Aubree Mulipola, both of whom bring irreplaceable big-game experience to a roster that blends established stars with AUSL standouts. The combination of Olympic veterans and active AUSL players gives the WNT a dynamic that sets it apart from most of its group opponents.

USA enters as the No. 2-ranked team in the world. The group field includes No. 6 Netherlands, No. 7 Mexico, No. 8 China, No. 23 New Zealand, and No. 36 South Africa. Mexico opens the tournament against USA on September 12, and the Netherlands matchup will likely determine which team tops the group.

The Stakes in Full

The new two-stage format for the WBSC World Cup — Group Stage in 2026, Finals in Brisbane in April 2027 — means that missing the top two in Group Oklahoma ends the USA's World Cup cycle before it reaches the Final. That structure puts real pressure on the Group Stage in a way that was not present under prior formats.

USA is the overwhelming favorite to advance, but the Netherlands has consistently challenged American dominance in international competition and ranks just two spots below USA worldwide. The Gasso-led staff will need to execute.

What's Next

Group Oklahoma runs September 12-16, 2026, in Oklahoma City. USA opens against No. 7 Mexico on September 12, with the Netherlands matchup serving as the likely top-seed decider. The two group winners advance to the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals in Brisbane, Australia (April 5-11, 2027), where the path to the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics continues. With 14 days until the first pitch, USA Softball is ready.
