OU Fall Camp: Gasso Builds Two Rosters as Battle Series Heats Up

By Sarah Mitchell

August 29, 2026

Patty Gasso is watching two rosters at once — and she would not have it any other way.

Oklahoma's fall Battle Series is rolling through Love's Field in Norman as the Sooners evaluate new additions alongside returners. The first intrasquad scrimmage drew 2,351 fans — a testament to the appetite for Sooners softball even in the off-season. And two weeks from now, Gasso will step away from Norman to lead the USA Women's National Team at the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage in Oklahoma City.

The dual responsibility — fall camp evaluation and national team coaching — is unusual even by Gasso's standards. But it reflects her standing as the foremost coach in the sport.

The New Additions Under the Microscope

Two transfer additions are drawing the most scrutiny in fall camp. Pitcher Sydney Berzon arrives from LSU with one of the most impressive résumés in the transfer portal: a two-time All-American with 486.1 career innings pitched, a 2.02 ERA, and 406 strikeouts. She gives Oklahoma a legitimate front-of-rotation arm to pair with the returning staff.

Outfielder Macie Harter comes from MTSU with a .346 batting average, 7 home runs, and 23 RBIs in the 2026 season. Harter's bat has drawn consistent praise from evaluators, and her ability to fit into Oklahoma's lineup will be a central storyline through the fall.

Gasso has spoken about the pleasure of watching this roster self-organize. "They take their own infield and I'm watching it like, 'What do you need me for?'" she said this summer. "I'm just enjoying watching this." That confidence in the group's chemistry bodes well for early cohesion.

The WNT September Window

From September 12 to 16, Gasso shifts to Devon Park in Oklahoma City to coach the USA WNT at Group Oklahoma. The Sooners will continue their fall Battle Series in her absence. It is a logistical challenge but not a new one — Gasso has balanced national team and program responsibilities throughout her career.

The upside for OU recruits watching is that Gasso will have freshly sharpened international coaching experience when she returns to Norman, and the WNT experience often accelerates the development of program tactics used at the collegiate level.

The Larger Picture

Oklahoma missed the WCWS for the first time since 2015 in the 2026 season, finishing short of where the program expects to be. Gasso's response has been aggressive portal work and the kind of fall structure that leaves nothing to evaluation until January. Battle Series games in October and November, plus exhibitions against East Texas A&M and Oklahoma Christian, give the staff meaningful looks before the 2027 signing class closes and spring arrives.

What's Next

The Battle Series continues through the fall with public exhibitions beginning October 3. Gasso departs for Group Oklahoma coaching duties September 12-16, returning to Norman as the Sooners' fall schedule intensifies. The 2027 OU campaign will be shaped by what happens in these evaluation weeks — and based on the talent assembled, expectations around Norman are quietly climbing again.
