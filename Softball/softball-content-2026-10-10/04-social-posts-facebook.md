### STORY 4: Tennessee Assistants Snider and Sanders Departing

**Long-Form Post:**
Tennessee softball is entering a transition period on its coaching staff.

Per D1Softball coaching carousel coverage, assistants Craig Snider and Stephanie Sanders are not returning to the Lady Vols for the 2027 season. Both joined head coach Karen Weekly's staff in June 2024 and were part of two consecutive Women's College World Series appearances.

Snider arrived with significant credentials — he had been the head coach at Texas Tech before making the move to Knoxville, bringing a rare perspective from someone who had run his own Power Five program. Their departure does not diminish what Tennessee accomplished, but it does mean Weekly will reload the coaching staff heading into a 2027 season where expectations are as high as they've been in years. No replacement hires have been announced yet.
