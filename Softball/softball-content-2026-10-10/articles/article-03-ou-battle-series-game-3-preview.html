OU Softball Battle Series Game 3 Preview: October 14 at Love's Field

By Sarah Mitchell | October 10, 2026 | I Love Softball

Oklahoma softball's fall program rolls into its third installment on Tuesday, October 14, when the Sooners hold Battle Series Game 3 at Love's Field in Norman. First pitch is 6:30 PM CT, and admission is free. It is the most accessible look fans will get at the team Patty Gasso is building for 2027, and through two games this fall, there are reasons to pay close attention.

Game 1 on September 30 was shortened by lightning after about three innings, but drew 2,351 fans and featured back-to-back home runs from Gabbi Garcia and Kendall Wells. Game 2 on October 7 brought more extended action, with Oklahoma's veterans stepping forward and drawing strong reviews from the coaching staff.

What Makes This Roster Different

Oklahoma missed the Women's College World Series in 2026 — a program first in years, and one that Gasso addressed directly during the offseason. The response was aggressive. The Sooners entered the transfer portal and emerged with several high-impact additions, led by Sydney Berzon, the two-time NFCA All-American pitcher from LSU who posted a 2.02 ERA across 52 collegiate wins before heading to Norman.

Berzon's presence alongside Kendall Wells, who set the NCAA single-season home run record as a freshman with 39, gives Oklahoma a combination of offensive and pitching threats that few programs can match. Wells hit .358 with 88 RBI in 2026, won the NFCA Freshman of the Year award, and earned the Johnny Bench Award as the nation's top catcher. She returns as a sophomore with even more on her plate.

The Battle Series is where those two learn to play together in game situations. Three games in, the staff is gathering the data it needs to set lineups and rotations heading into spring.

Battle Series Structure

Oklahoma runs its fall Battle Series as a three-squad intrasquad competition, splitting its 21-player fall roster into groups that rotate opponents across four games. The format gives pitchers live reps against their own teammates, gives hitters a chance to face unfamiliar pitching tendencies, and gives coaches a look at how players respond under pressure.

Beyond the Battle Series, OU has additional fall games against outside competition: Tarleton State was a confirmed opponent in early October, and matchups against the University of Science and Arts Oklahoma, Seminole State, and East Texas A&M are also on the fall schedule. The Battle Series is the marquee format — the games with the most competitive intensity — and Game 3 on October 14 is the next chapter.

What's Next

Following Game 3, Battle Series Game 4 is set for October 21, also at Love's Field with free admission. The full fall schedule wraps in early November, at which point Gasso's staff will finalize its evaluations ahead of spring practices and the 2027 season. For a program hungry to return to the WCWS after missing last spring, every fall rep matters.
