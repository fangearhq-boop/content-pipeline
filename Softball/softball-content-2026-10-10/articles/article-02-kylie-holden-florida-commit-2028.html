No. 2 Prospect Kylie Holden Commits to Florida Softball

By Jordan Reeves | October 10, 2026 | I Love Softball

Florida softball has its eyes on the 2028 class, and one of the class's biggest prizes is already committed to Gainesville. Kylie Holden, the No. 2 overall prospect in the Class of 2028 according to national recruiting rankings, has committed to the Gators. The five-star middle infielder is one of the most coveted prospects in the country, and Florida landed her.

In a recruiting cycle that will define programs for years to come, commitments like Holden's carry enormous weight. The contact period for the Class of 2028 opened on September 1, and Florida moved quickly.

Why Florida

The Gators have built a reputation as a developer of offensive talent. Middle infielders who commit to Florida do so with an expectation of growth — not just in physical tools, but in game intelligence, decision-making, and the kind of technical refinement that comes from playing for a program with deep coaching continuity.

Holden is the kind of prospect programs spend years recruiting. Ranked No. 2 overall in her class and rated as a five-star recruit, she has the tools to be an impact player from her first at-bat in college. The Gators' 2028 class is now positioned as one of the nation's strongest, and it is only October of the commitment cycle's opening fall.

A Trend Worth Watching

The Class of 2028 recruiting landscape has been active since the September 1 contact period opened. Other notable early 2028 commitments include prospects to Georgia, Oklahoma, Texas Tech, and South Carolina — all programs investing early in the next wave of talent.

Florida is no stranger to recruiting this high. The Gators have consistently pulled from the top tiers of national recruiting classes, and Holden's commitment signals that Florida remains a top destination for elite middle infield talent.

At the five-star level, the expectation is not just that a prospect will become a starter — it is that she will be a difference-maker. Holden has a year and a half of high school ahead of her before she arrives in Gainesville. If the trajectory continues, the Gators may have their next cornerstone infielder already signed up.

What's Next

Holden will sign a National Letter of Intent when the early signing period opens in November 2027. Until then, she continues her high school career as the No. 2 overall prospect in her class — with Florida's orange and blue already in her future. For the Gators' coaching staff, the 2028 class is just getting started.
