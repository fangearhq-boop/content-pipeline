Tennessee Assistants Craig Snider and Stephanie Sanders Departing After 2026 Season

By Emily Rawlings | October 10, 2026 | I Love Softball

Tennessee softball will enter the 2027 season with a reshuffled coaching staff. According to D1Softball coaching carousel coverage, assistants Craig Snider and Stephanie Sanders are not returning to the Lady Vols for next season. Both coaches joined head coach Karen Weekly's staff in June 2024 and were part of two consecutive Women's College World Series appearances. No replacement hires have been announced.

The departures come as Tennessee finishes a fall ball schedule that has featured strong individual performances, including sophomore Emma Clarke hitting for the cycle in a recent win. The program's talent base is deep. The question now is which coaching additions Weekly makes to support it.

Snider's Background

Craig Snider arrived at Tennessee with significant credentials. He had served as the head coach at Texas Tech before making the move to Knoxville — a rare step for someone who had run his own program at a Power Five school. His presence on Weekly's staff brought a different perspective to the Lady Vols' coaching room: someone who had managed roster construction, recruiting, and in-season decisions at the highest level of the sport independently.

Under the staff that included Snider and Sanders, Tennessee returned to the Women's College World Series in both 2025 and 2026. That is the standard by which WCWS-caliber programs are evaluated, and by that measure, the pairing worked.

What Comes Next

Tennessee is a destination for coaching talent as much as it is for recruits. Weekly has built one of the most respected programs in the sport, and her track record attracts coaches who want to be part of a contender. The coaching carousel in college softball moves quickly in the fall, and Weekly will likely move quickly to replace two experienced staff members before recruiting season intensifies.

For the Lady Vols' 2027 roster — which returns Peyton Mardjetko, Madison Nuwer, Emma Clarke, Alannah Leach, Gabby Leach, and Briley Knight — the coaching transition is a backdrop to a season that has significant potential. The roster was built for this. The task now is finding the staff to maximize it.

What's Next

Tennessee will complete its fall schedule over the coming weeks. Replacement coaching hires are expected to be announced before the end of fall, allowing new staff members to participate in recruiting and spring preparation. Watch D1Softball's coaching carousel page and the official UTsports.com athletics site for hire announcements as Weekly reloads for 2027.
