The Draft Is Done. Series 4 Opens Tomorrow. Three Days to the 2026 Champion.

By Jordan Reeves | I Love Softball | August 27, 2026

Series 4 teams are finalized. Tomorrow night at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois, the 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup finale begins — and after three series and weeks of individual standings movement, the race comes down to three players. Baylee Klingler leads with 890 points. Lexi Kilfoyl is second at 866. Hannah Flippen is third at 750. The individual champion is crowned August 30.

Friday's opener: Team Klingler vs. Team Flippen, 7 PM ET on CBS Sports Network.

What Separates Klingler From the Field

Baylee Klingler enters Series 4 as the individual leader for one reason: sustained production across every series. She is 4-0 all-time as an All-Star Cup captain. She closed Series 3 with a shutout win over Team Palacios — the lone run coming on an unearned score that held up across seven innings. Through three series, no captain has been more consistent in the results column than Klingler.

Her 24-point lead over Kilfoyl is real, but it is the kind of margin that a strong series can erase. The All-Star Cup format rewards statistical production across the full roster — wins matter, but so do individual player stats from every player on each captain's drafted team. Klingler will need to draft well, manage her roster effectively, and keep winning to protect what she built in Series 3.

Kilfoyl jumped two spots during Series 3 to claim second place overall. She also combined on the two-hit shutout in the final game of Series 3, demonstrating her value on the mound. Entering Series 4 as a captain, she controls her own destiny. A dominant series from Kilfoyl's roster — and a quieter one from Klingler's — could flip the standings entirely.

Flippen and the Wild Card Captains

Hannah Flippen at 750 points sits 140 back of Klingler. In a format that rewards cumulative production, that gap will be difficult to close — but Flippen did not reach third place by running out the clock. She earned her captaincy by producing consistently across the first three series, and she is entitled to compete for the title in Series 4 the same way any captain does.

The draft also matters. In a short final series, the players each captain selects will influence outcomes significantly. Amber Fiser and Reese Atwood both made major moves during Series 3 — Fiser climbed 13 places in the individual standings, Atwood moved up 14. Both are now inside the top 15 and will be attractive draft selections for all three captains. Where those athletes land affects the individual race beyond just the captain's record.

Kendra Falby (4th), Michaela Edenfield (5th), and Jadelyn Allchin (6th) round out the top six heading into the finale — each with an opportunity to finish the series with a strong enough performance to move up the final standings. The individual race is not just about captains.

The Format That Decides Everything

All games in Series 4 will be broadcast across ESPN, MLB Network, and CBS Sports Network. The full schedule is available at theausl.com/all-star-cup/. Friday's 7 PM ET opener on CBS Sports Network — Team Klingler vs. Team Flippen — sets the tone for the final stretch. All games through August 30 count toward the individual standings.

After four series, the player with the most accumulated points is the 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup individual champion. There is no tiebreaker round. There is no playoff. The standings at the end of August 30 are final.

Klingler leads. Kilfoyl is 24 points back. The race is not over.
