Four Days to In-Person Contact: What the Class of 2028 Recruiting Rush Looks Like Right Now

By Sarah Mitchell September 5, 2026

The First Big Commit Is Already In

Sept. 1 opened the phone/email/DM contact window for the Class of 2028, and D1 coaches didn't wait to see who would blink first. Within hours of the window opening, Peyton Ushery — ranked No. 2 in the Line Drive Media HOT 100 — committed to Georgia. It was the first major D1 verbal commit of the 2028 cycle, and it sent a clear message: the best prospects in this class were not waiting around.

That was four days ago. Since then, coaches across the country have been flooding phones, inboxes, and DMs. The contact period for 2028 prospects doesn't cap out on volume — it's an all-hands sprint from every major program, and the conversations are happening around the clock. Four days from now, Sept. 9 at 7 AM ET, the in-person window opens for the first time. Coaches can visit schools and homes. The pace is about to shift into another gear.

The Class That Set the Nominations Record

Extra Inning Softball received 1,226 nominations for the Class of 2028 — a record. That depth shows up in the rankings. There are 16 five-star athletes in the latest On3 and Softball America rankings, headlined by Michaela Landers (Texas Bombers), Kylie Holden (Unity), and Sophia Schlader (Beverly Bandits, two-way player). Also in the mix: Caelyn Campos and Sydney Root from Batbusters, Ryann Harris and Joley Bennett from Texas Bombers, Kate McCartney and Emma Johnson from Lady Magic.

Middle infielders and catchers are the headline strength of this class. But the pitching pool is deeper than most recent cycles, and the two-way players — like Schlader — add another dimension. Any program hoping to land multiple top-50 prospects from 2028 needed to be working the phone hard before Sept. 1. By the time Sept. 9 arrives, a program's standing with the best prospects will already be established.

What Sept. 9 Actually Means

The in-person window is different from the phone/DM contact that started Sept. 1. When that window opens at 7 AM ET next Tuesday, coaches can show up at school campuses and at a recruit's home. They can watch the prospect in person during evaluations or unofficial visits. For a lot of families, that's when recruiting becomes real in a way that a phone call doesn't capture.

Prospects who are already verbally committed — like Ushery — give their programs a concrete early-cycle win. But the real action over the next week will be among the unsigned top-50. Programs that nail the in-person visit and show up with a clear plan for how a recruit fits their system will have a massive advantage. For the Class of 2028, the recruiting race just entered its most consequential week so far.

What's Next

The Sept. 9 in-person contact window opens at 7 AM ET. Watch for more major commits to follow Ushery's lead in the days before and after that date. Top prospects like Landers, Holden, and Schlader are the ones to follow most closely as programs make their pushes. More commits are coming soon.
