The Clock Is Ticking: Oklahoma Has One Week to Maximize Fall Camp With Gasso

By Emily Rawlings September 5, 2026

Seven Days Until Gasso Leaves

Patty Gasso doubles as head coach at Oklahoma and head coach of Team USA. Both jobs are about to pull in opposite directions. On Sept. 12, Gasso heads to Devon Park in Oklahoma City to lead the WNT through Group Oklahoma — an event that runs through Sept. 16. That means this week — right now — is the last stretch of fall camp with the entire OU coaching staff in Norman.

For a program rebuilding after a WCWS miss, that matters. Oklahoma fell short of the Women's College World Series in 2026 for the first time since 2015. This fall is about reset: new faces, new pitching, and the weight of expectation that comes with being Oklahoma. Every rep this week is one more data point that Gasso files away before leaving for five days.

Wells and Berzon Setting the Tone

Kendall Wells broke the NCAA freshman single-season home run record with 32 HRs during the 2026 spring season. She earned the NFCA Freshman of the Year award and is now working through fall camp as a sophomore with the full weight of those expectations behind her. Gasso has spoken publicly about Wells in recent weeks, and the early fall scrimmage numbers have been encouraging. Four home runs in the first three fall scrimmages suggest the swing is right where it needs to be.

Sydney Berzon is the other name driving optimism in Norman. She posted a 2.02 ERA over 486.1 innings at LSU — a two-time All-American coming to a program that desperately needs a proven ace. In her first Battle Series work, Berzon retired all six batters she faced in a fall exhibition against Oklahoma Christian and pitched two innings with one run in Battle Series play. Macie Harter, who hit .346 at Middle Tennessee State, rounds out a pitching group that looks deeper than last year's.

What the Next Seven Days Are For

Fall camp isn't just about individual performance. It's about building the communication patterns that define how a team plays together in March. Who calls off a fly ball. Who covers second on a bunt. Who takes charge when a pitcher gets in trouble. That work is Gasso's work, and it happens in the small moments of practice that don't end up on social media.

Oklahoma's fall opener is Sept. 30 at Love's Field — a Battle Series game at 6:30 PM. By then, Gasso will be back from Group Oklahoma and the coaching staff will be fully reassembled. But the foundation of what the 2026-27 Sooners will be gets built this week. Seven days is enough time to make it count.

What's Next

Oklahoma's first fall game is Sept. 30 at Love's Field, followed by a matchup against Tarleton State on Oct. 3. Gasso returns from WNT duty around Sept. 17, with five full weeks of fall work remaining before the final fall date on Nov. 4. Watch for Battle Series results and any public comments from Gasso on the progress of Wells, Berzon, and the new roster this week.
