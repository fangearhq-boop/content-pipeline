Seven Days Out: USA Returns to Devon Park for the WBSC World Cup Group Stage

By Jordan Reeves September 5, 2026

One Week Away

Seven days from now, Team USA walks back onto the field at OGE Energy Field at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. Same venue where they went 5-0 at the International Cup. Higher stakes. The 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage runs Sept. 12-16 and sends only two of six nations to the 2027 World Cup Finals. The other four go home.

Opening night is Sept. 12. USA faces No. 7 Mexico at 7:30 PM, preceded by a 6:30 PM opening ceremony. The six-team field — USA, Brazil, China, Mexico, Netherlands, and New Zealand — plays a full round-robin before the Sept. 16 playoff format decides which two programs advance to Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia for the April 5-11, 2027 Finals. Those Finals are a critical qualifier on the road to the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. Tickets are on sale through WBSC.

Kilfoyl Comes Back to Devon Park

On Aug. 30, Lexi Kilfoyl won the 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup. She went 6-0 for the Chicago Bandits during the regular season, won three Game MVP awards in the All-Star Cup, and carried Team Kilfoyl to the championship. Less than two weeks later, she's heading back to Devon Park as part of the 16-player USA WNT roster.

That's the remarkable thing about this particular edition of Team USA. Several of these players competed in the AUSL All-Star Cup just days ago. The international calendar doesn't give much room to breathe. Kilfoyl and her teammates aren't just in good form — they've been competing at a high level all summer. Coming into Group Oklahoma with that momentum matters.

Why Netherlands Is the One to Watch

USA is ranked No. 2 in the world and enters as the host nation. Opening-night opponent Mexico (No. 7) is a genuine challenge, but the matchup that could define the round-robin is Netherlands (No. 6). The Dutch are the most dangerous team in the field behind USA and will likely determine which nation claims the top seed heading into the decisive final day.

The 16-player USA roster includes 2020 Olympians Valerie Garcia and Dejah Mulipola, plus seven athletes from the 2025 World Games gold medal squad. Patty Gasso, who is also Oklahoma's head coach, leads the coaching staff. USA went 5-0 at the International Cup at Devon Park earlier this year. That institutional familiarity — the field, the crowd, the travel logistics in Oklahoma City — is a genuine edge in a short tournament where every game counts.

What's Next

USA opens against No. 7 Mexico at 7:30 PM on Sept. 12 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. The round-robin runs through Sept. 15, with playoffs on Sept. 16: the top two nations advance directly to the 2027 World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, QLD. Third and fourth place enter a repechage for one additional berth. Tickets are available through WBSC. Seven days away.
