Game Day at Devon Park: USA Opens Group Oklahoma Against Mexico

By Jordan Reeves | September 12, 2026

Team USA

The week of anticipation ends tonight. The United States Women's National Team takes the Devon Park field at 7:30 PM ET to open the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Oklahoma against No. 7 Mexico. After days of travel, preparation, and previews, the round-robin begins.

Head coach Patty Gasso arrived in Oklahoma City from Norman today. She brings the full coaching staff — JT Gasso and Jennifer Rocha — along with a 16-player roster that has spent the summer building toward this week. The experience in that dugout is extraordinary: Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola earned gold at the Tokyo 2020 Games. Seven players on the Group Oklahoma roster won gold at the 2025 World Games. This is not a young group learning the national team stage — this is a team that has been there before.

Six Nations, Five Days, Two Tickets

Group Oklahoma runs September 12–16 at Devon Park with six nations competing in a round-robin format: USA (No. 2), Netherlands (No. 6), Mexico (No. 7), China (No. 8), Brazil (No. 17), and New Zealand (No. 23). The prize for the top two teams is a direct berth to the 2027 World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland — April 5–11 — and, by extension, a direct pathway toward the Los Angeles 2028 Olympic qualification process.

Tonight's opening card shows Brazil vs. China at 1 PM, New Zealand vs. Netherlands at 4 PM, and the headliner — Mexico vs. USA — at 7:30 PM. Opening night is a statement game, but the eyes of the bracket are already on the USA-Netherlands matchup. Beat the Dutch, and USA controls the top-seed race. Finish second in the round-robin, and the September 16 play-off bracket becomes more complicated.

The bracket breakdown on September 16: No. 1 vs. No. 2 at 1 PM (direct path to the 2027 Finals), No. 3 vs. No. 4 at 4 PM, and a Repechage at 7 PM. Top seed is not a formality — it is the difference between the direct path and the long road.

Devon Park Is Home Court

If familiarity is an advantage, USA has it. Earlier this summer, the national team went 5-0 at this same venue during the International Cup — same field, same dimensions, same Oklahoma City crowd. Gasso has referenced that experience throughout the summer as a real competitive edge. The team knows the lighting, the sight lines, and the feel of Devon Park in a way no visiting nation does.

That advantage doesn't win games on its own, but it matters in tight moments. Opening the tournament at a venue where you're already comfortable — and where the crowd will be loudly in your corner — is not nothing.

What's Next

Group Oklahoma continues through September 16. Tonight's USA-Mexico opener at 7:30 PM ET is the first look at this team in competitive action. Monitor the USA-Netherlands round-robin result closely — that game will tell you everything about where USA stands in the top-seed race. Live coverage is available through USA Softball's official channels and AWSN. The September 16 play-off bracket will determine which two nations earn their tickets to Redcliffe and the road to LA28.
