Texas Tech Week 5: Seven All-Americans and the Identity of a Contender

By Sarah Mitchell | September 12, 2026

Texas Tech Red Raiders

Five weeks into fall camp at Texas Tech, Gerry Glasco's Year 2 is starting to look like the program everyone expected when the nation's No. 1-ranked transfer class arrived in Lubbock this summer. The pieces are not just present — they are beginning to fit.

Three headline transfers define the ceiling of this roster. Jaysoni Beachum hit .406 with 10 home runs and 69 RBI at Florida State. Kasidi Pickering — the No. 2-ranked transfer in the country — brought a career .384 batting average and 50 home runs from Oklahoma. Jayden Heavener arrived from LSU with a 13-9 record, 2.81 ERA, and 128 strikeouts, establishing herself immediately as one of the premier left-handed arms in the Big 12.

Seven All-Americans

Add those three names to the four All-Americans already on the TTU roster, and Glasco has assembled a group with seven total All-Americans heading into the 2027 season. That number is not just a headline — it represents the kind of collective individual excellence that produces deep tournament runs.

Glasco knows how to build championship teams. He won two national titles at Louisiana in 2019 and 2023. His first year at Texas Tech, despite the roster being mid-transition, ended with the program reaching the 2026 WCWS championship series — a performance that validated his vision for what the program could become. Year 2 is about turning that runner-up finish into something more.

The Hunger of a Runner-Up

Texas Tech lost the 2026 national championship series to Texas in two games. The Longhorns swept it, 7-3 and 4-1. That outcome is not ancient history — it is the motivation that follows every player into the weight room, every practice rep, every fall scrimmage. The 2026 WCWS runner-up is not a comfortable identity. It is a reminder of how close they came and what it would take to finish the job in 2027.

Week 5 chemistry is turning into the kind of cohesion that takes a collection of All-Americans and turns them into a team. Watch the Raiders this fall.

What's Next

Texas Tech will open its fall schedule in the coming weeks. The first public look at Beachum, Pickering, and Heavener in Red Raider uniforms will tell the full story of how well this transfer class has meshed with the returning players. Keep an eye on the Heavener-Pickering duo as the pitching-catching connection continues to develop. For a program that was one series away from a national title in 2026, the 2027 season begins with legitimate championship expectations.
