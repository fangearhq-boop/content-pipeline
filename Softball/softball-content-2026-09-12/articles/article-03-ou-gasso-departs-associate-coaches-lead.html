Gasso Heads to Devon Park: Associate Coaches Lead OU Through Critical Camp Window

By Emily Rawlings | September 12, 2026

Oklahoma Sooners

Patty Gasso departed Norman today for Devon Park. For the next four days — September 12 through 16 — the Oklahoma Sooners' fall practice is in the hands of the associate coaching staff while Gasso leads Team USA through the WBSC World Cup Group Oklahoma. It is a transition the program planned for all summer, and the players who remain in Norman are ready to hold their own.

Gasso takes JT Gasso and Jennifer Rocha with her to Oklahoma City. That means the coaching configuration in Norman shifts, but the practice calendar does not. The Sooners are 18 days from the September 30 fall opener against Tarleton State, and these are not throwaway days. Every rep matters.

Wells vs. Berzon: The Competition Deepens

The scrimmage sessions this week produced the most compelling subplot in OU fall camp. Kinzie Wells — who set the NCAA freshman home run record in her first season with the Sooners — went deep twice off Sydney Berzon this week. The hits were not a fluke, and they were not softballs from a fatigued arm.

Berzon is a two-time All-American who pitched 486.1 innings at LSU with a career 2.02 ERA and 406 strikeouts. She arrived at Oklahoma as a transfer this summer and brings the most decorated pitching résumé of anyone in the OU circle. When Wells hits Berzon, that means something. When Berzon gets Wells out, that also means something. The competition between these two is the best kind of problem a program can have.

Macie Harter (.346 career BA at MTSU) is pushing for playing time in a lineup that has legitimate depth at multiple positions. The Sooners entering fall games with this many questions answered — in a good way — is encouraging.

The Bigger Picture

Gasso's departure for Team USA duties is not a complication — it's a feature. Oklahoma built this roster knowing that its head coach would be spending part of September in Oklahoma City leading the national team. Brito, Coleman, Jennings, and Maxwell are all on the Group Oklahoma roster. The program has representation in both places simultaneously.

When Gasso returns after September 16, she walks back into a fall camp with 14 days left before games and a team that has been running itself through the most critical competitive stretch of the pre-season. That is trust in the associate staff, and it's trust in the players to respond to it.

What's Next

Gasso and the WNT coaching staff return to Norman after September 16, with the full staff reunited for the final two weeks before the September 30 fall opener against Tarleton State. Watch for updates on the Wells-Berzon competition as the Sooners approach game conditions. The fall schedule gives a first public look at how this roster has developed under the new staff configuration heading into the 2027 season.
