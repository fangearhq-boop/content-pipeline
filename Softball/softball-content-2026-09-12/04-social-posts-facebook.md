🇺🇸 GAME DAY AT DEVON PARK 🥎

After a week of buildup, the moment is here. Tonight at 7:30 PM ET, the United States Women's National Team opens the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Oklahoma against No. 7 Mexico at Devon Park in Oklahoma City.

Patty Gasso has departed Norman and is on-site as Team USA's head coach. She brings a roster loaded with experience — Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola earned gold at Tokyo 2020, and seven players on this squad captured gold at the 2025 World Games. This is the most decorated group USA has assembled for a Group Stage event.

Here's what's at stake: The top two teams from Group Oklahoma earn direct berths to the 2027 World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland — a direct LA28 Olympic qualifier pathway. Netherlands (No. 6) is the primary test in the round-robin. USA needs to beat the Dutch to lock up the top seed and avoid the repechage bracket on September 16.

The venue is an advantage. USA went 5-0 at Devon Park during the International Cup earlier this year. Same field, same dimensions, same partisan OKC crowd.

Tonight starts the real work. Let's go, USA! 🇺🇸

### STORY 3: Oklahoma — Gasso in OKC (Tier 2)

**FB Long-Form**
Window: 1:00 PM ET

🏟️ GASSO IS IN OKC — SOONER CAMP ENTERS NEW PHASE

Today marks a transition every OU player knew was coming. With Patty Gasso, JT Gasso, and Jennifer Rocha at Devon Park for Team USA through September 16, Oklahoma's associate coaches have taken over fall practice.

It's a four-day window — but an important one. The Sooners are 18 days from their September 30 fall opener against Tarleton State, and these are critical reps.

The competition on the mound is getting real. Kinzie Wells — who set the NCAA freshman home run record in her first season — hit two home runs off Sydney Berzon this week in scrimmage. Berzon, a two-time All-American LSU transfer, brings 486.1 innings pitched, a 2.02 ERA, and 406 strikeouts in her college career. The Wells-Berzon dynamic is one of the most compelling storylines heading into fall games.

Macie Harter (.346 career BA at MTSU) is pushing for at-bats in a deep lineup. This Sooner roster has the depth to handle a week without the full staff — and when Gasso returns after September 16, she'll find a team that held its own.

### STORY 4: Texas — Week 5 (Tier 3)

**FB Long-Form**
Window: 3:00 PM ET

🤘 TEXAS FALL CAMP WEEK 5 — 17 DAYS TO THE OPENER

The back-to-back Women's College World Series champions are in Week 5 of fall camp, and the tone has shifted from adjustment to execution.

Isa Torres — the 2026 D1Softball Player of the Year who hit .530 with 16 home runs and 56 RBI at Florida State — is now a Texas Longhorn. Pair her with Teagan Kavan, a two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player entering her senior season, and you understand why this team is different.

Texas opens its fall schedule September 29 against Murray State at McCombs Field — 6 PM CT, free admission. It's the first look at what a three-peat chase looks like in person.

Only two programs have ever three-peated in the modern era: UCLA (1988–90) and Oklahoma (2021–23). Texas won in 2025 and 2026. September 29 is the first step toward 2027.

🔴⚫ TEXAS TECH FALL CAMP WEEK 5 — GLASCO YEAR 2

Five weeks in, and Gerry Glasco's second year at Texas Tech is starting to look like something.

The transfer class that arrived this fall is the nation's No. 1-ranked group: Jaysoni Beachum (.406 BA, 10 HR, 69 RBI from Florida State), Kasidi Pickering (the No. 2-ranked transfer nationally, .384 career BA, 50 career HR from Oklahoma), and Jayden Heavener (LHP, 13-9 record, 2.81 ERA, 128 strikeouts from LSU). Combined with four more All-Americans already in the program, the Raiders have seven total All-Americans heading into the 2027 season.

This is the 2026 WCWS runner-up with a point to prove. They took Texas to two games in the national championship series. With this roster upgrade, the question isn't whether TTU will be dangerous — it's how dangerous.

Week 5 chemistry is turning into Week 6 execution. Watch this team.
