### STORY 4: Texas Longhorns — 14 Days to Fall Opener

**Long-Form Post — 3:00 PM ET**
🤘 14 DAYS UNTIL HISTORY WALKS ONTO MCCOMBS FIELD 🤘

On September 29, at 6 PM CT at McCombs Field, Texas softball opens its fall schedule against Murray State. Admission is free. And it will likely be the most-watched fall game in Texas softball history.

Why? Because September 29 is the first public chance to see Isa Torres and Hannah Kavan sharing a dugout in burnt orange.

Isa Torres needs no introduction. The 2026 D1Softball National Player of the Year. .530 batting average. 16 home runs. 56 RBI. All-ACC. All-American. And a kid from Georgetown, Texas who always wanted to come home. She transferred from Florida State and is now a Longhorn.

Hannah Kavan doesn't need an introduction either. The ONLY player in history to win the WCWS Most Outstanding Player award twice. She did it back-to-back. In 2025 and 2026. Never been done before. She's a senior now and she's chasing something no one has chased in decades.

A three-peat.

Only two programs in NCAA softball history have won three consecutive national championships: UCLA (1988, 1989, 1990) and Oklahoma (2021, 2022, 2023). Texas won it in 2025 and 2026. One more, and the Longhorns join the most exclusive list in college softball history.

Head coach Mike White and his staff just earned their second consecutive NFCA Coaching Staff of the Year award. The engine is fully operational.

Fall schedule:
📅 Sept. 29 — Murray State (free, McCombs Field, 6 PM CT)
📅 Oct. 13 — Texas State
📅 Oct. 20 — Temple College

Two weeks. The most anticipated fall in Austin. 🤘
