USA vs. China Tonight at Devon Park: Top Seed and 2027 Finals Berth on the Line

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | September 15, 2026

Both teams enter Day 4 undefeated. Tonight's 8 PM ET showdown settles who controls the path to Redcliffe and LA28.

There have been four days of softball at Devon Park in Oklahoma City, and it has all been building to this moment. On Tuesday night at 8 PM ET, Team USA and China — the only two undefeated teams remaining in the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Oklahoma City — will meet on the diamond with the top seed and a direct berth to the 2027 Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, on the line.

USA enters the night at 3-0, having run-ruled Mexico on Day 1 and steamrolled Brazil 17-0 on Day 2 to advance on Day 3 as well. China is equally unbeaten through its portion of the round-robin. Neither team has been tested with a loss, and the stakes tonight could not be higher.

What's at Stake

The format of Group Oklahoma is a 15-game single round-robin among six nations — USA, Brazil, China, Mexico, Netherlands and New Zealand — running from September 12 to 15. The top two finishers advance directly to the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, QLD, which also serves as a critical qualifier on the road to the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. A third-place finish earns a Wild Card spot.

With USA and China both at 3-0 heading into Tuesday's slate, tonight's game essentially functions as the group's championship game. The winner secures the No. 1 seed and a favorable playoff position for Wednesday's bracket. The loser still has a path to Redcliffe, but loses control of their seeding and must navigate the bracket accordingly.

Team USA: A Roster Built for Moments Like This

Coaching Team USA through the tournament is a trio familiar to any college softball fan: Patty Gasso (head coach at Oklahoma), JT Gasso and Rocha. The presence of four Sooners — Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings and Kelly Maxwell — adds a hometown flavor at Devon Park, which sits in the heart of Sooner country.

The roster also includes Tokyo 2020 silver medalists Gabbie Garcia and Sahvanna Mulipola, who bring international championship experience to a group that has won seven World Games gold medals collectively. USA went 5-0 at Devon Park during the 2025 International Cup at the same venue, and they are clearly comfortable in these surroundings.

The run-rule margins through three days signal that USA is not just winning — they are dominating. But China has been equally formidable, and this matchup figures to be the most competitive game of the entire Group Oklahoma event.

Tomorrow: The Playoff Bracket

Once tonight's game settles the standings, Wednesday's play-off bracket at Devon Park runs three sessions: 1 PM ET (1st vs. 2nd), 4 PM ET (3rd vs. 4th), and 7 PM ET (Repechage). The top two finishers in the bracket earn their direct tickets to Redcliffe QLD for the 2027 Finals. Gasso and her WNT coaching staff are expected to return to Norman, Oklahoma, on Wednesday afternoon after the bracket concludes.

What's Next

Tonight's 8 PM ET game at Devon Park is the final and most consequential round-robin contest of Group Oklahoma. Fans can watch on AWSN and WBSC streaming platforms. After tonight, the attention shifts fully to Wednesday's playoff bracket, where the path to Redcliffe and LA28 becomes official. For Team USA, the goal is simple: leave Oklahoma City with that No. 1 seed, win the bracket, and punch their ticket to Australia.
