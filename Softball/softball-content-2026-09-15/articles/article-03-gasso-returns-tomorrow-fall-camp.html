The General Is Coming Home: Gasso Returns to Norman Tomorrow With 14 Days to Fall Opener

By Jordan Reeves | I Love Softball | September 15, 2026

After five days coaching Team USA at Devon Park, Patty Gasso reunites with her Oklahoma squad on Wednesday. First full-staff practice is Thursday. The clock is ticking.

The Sooners have been working without their head coach since September 12. On Wednesday, that changes. Patty Gasso returns to Norman after the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Oklahoma City play-off bracket concludes at Devon Park, rejoining a fall camp that has been running smoothly in her absence — but will undeniably shift into a different gear with her presence on the field.

The timeline: Gasso, along with WNT coaching staff members JT Gasso and Rocha, is expected back in Norman on Wednesday. First full-staff practice resumes Thursday. And the fall opener versus Tarleton State at Love's Field is exactly 14 days away.

Five Days Under the Associate Coaching Staff

Oklahoma's associate coaches ran the show while the WNT trio was in Oklahoma City. In any other fall, that might raise eyebrows. But the Sooners have a veteran locker room and experienced returners who know what's expected, and by all accounts, the practices never missed a beat.

What the five-day stretch did do, however, is compress the timeline. With 14 days remaining before the Sept. 30 fall opener, the Sooners now have a finite window to sort out their roster evaluation — specifically around the new additions who need to establish chemistry with a returning core that already knows each other well.

The Players to Watch

Kendall Wells returns as the headliner. The sophomore set the NCAA freshman home run record with 32 last season and is now one of the most feared bats in the sport. She showed flashes of that power in fall scrimmages, and her continued development will be a key storyline this fall.

The new additions getting evaluated are pitchers Sydney Berzon and Macie Harter. Berzon arrives from LSU as a two-time All-American who posted a 2.02 ERA and 406 strikeouts across 486.1 innings with the Tigers. Harter hit .346 at Middle Tennessee State. Both have a lot to prove in an OU uniform, and Gasso's return means the evaluations are about to get much more rigorous.

Returning pitchers Audrey Lowry and Kierston Deal round out the pitching picture. This is a group with significant potential and a lot of questions — exactly the kind of roster that a fall camp is designed to answer.

The Fall Schedule

Oklahoma's eight-game fall schedule runs entirely at Love's Field. The Sooners open September 30 against Tarleton State, then face USAO on October 23, Seminole State on October 30 and East Texas A&M on November 4. With all games at home, the Sooners can focus entirely on player development rather than travel logistics.

Admission is $10 per game, making this an accessible first look for Sooner Nation at what the 2027 team will look like.

What's Next

Thursday's full-staff practice is the reset button. From that point, Gasso has 13 days to finalize fall camp evaluations before the first official game action of the 2026-27 cycle. The questions surrounding Berzon's fit, Wells' evolution and the depth of this pitching staff will begin to get answered then. For now, the most important thing is that the general is coming home — and in softball, that changes everything.
