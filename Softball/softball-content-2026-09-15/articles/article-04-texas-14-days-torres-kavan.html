Two Weeks Until History: Torres and Kavan Debut Together at McCombs Field on Sept. 29

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | September 15, 2026

On September 29, the defending national champions take the field for fall ball — and the sport finally gets to see its most celebrated 1-2 punch share a dugout for the first time.

Fourteen days. That is all that separates the softball world from its most anticipated fall opener in recent memory. On September 29 at 6 PM CT, Texas hosts Murray State College at McCombs Field in what promises to be one of the most-watched free admission games in Longhorn history. The reason is simple: for the first time ever, Isa Torres and Hannah Kavan will wear the same uniform.

The combination of the 2026 D1Softball National Player of the Year and the sport's only two-time Women's College World Series Most Outstanding Player is unlike anything college softball has seen before. And on September 29, fans get the first look at what it actually looks like in action.

Isa Torres: The Player of the Year Comes Home

Isa Torres had one of the most decorated individual seasons in 2026 college softball history. As a member of the Florida State Seminoles, she hit .530 with 16 home runs and 56 RBI, earning the D1Softball National Player of the Year award, All-American first-team honors and ACC Player of the Year recognition. She is a complete offensive force — contact, power, plate discipline — and one of the best hitters in the sport regardless of class or experience.

She is also from Georgetown, Texas, a city just north of Austin. When Torres entered the transfer portal and chose Texas, she called it coming home. For a program that just won back-to-back national championships, landing Torres was not a need so much as an emphatic statement that the Longhorns intend to keep winning.

Hannah Kavan: Two-Time MOP, One More Goal

Hannah Kavan is the only player in NCAA softball history to win the Women's College World Series Most Outstanding Player award twice. She did it in 2025 and again in 2026, both times leading Texas to the national title. She enters her senior season with a singular focus: a three-peat.

If Texas wins a third consecutive national championship in 2027, the Longhorns will join the most exclusive company in the sport's history. Only two programs have ever won three straight: UCLA, which did it in 1988, 1989 and 1990, and Oklahoma, which did it in 2021, 2022 and 2023. No active dynasty. No modern program. Just history waiting to be written.

The Coaching Staff That Has Done It Before

Head coach Mike White and his staff just earned their second consecutive NFCA Coaching Staff of the Year award — a recognition that underscores the continuity and program management that has made Texas the gold standard in the sport right now. With Torres integrating into a roster that already has Kavan and six of nine starters returning from back-to-back champions, White has arguably his strongest team yet heading into the 2027 season.

The Fall Schedule

After the September 29 opener versus Murray State, Texas continues its fall slate with Texas State on October 13 and Temple College on October 20, both at McCombs Field at 6 PM CT. The schedule is intentionally manageable — designed for evaluation, not exhibition-style pressure — giving the coaching staff a clear look at how Torres fits in the lineup alongside the returning core.

What's Next

In 14 days, the questions shift from hypothetical to observable. How does Torres look in autumn workouts? Can she and Kavan develop the partnership that Texas fans are dreaming about? The answers begin September 29 at McCombs Field. Admission is free, and there will not be an empty seat in sight.
