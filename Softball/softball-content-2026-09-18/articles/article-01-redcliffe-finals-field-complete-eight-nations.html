2027 Redcliffe Finals Field Complete: USA, Mexico, China Headed to Australia for LA28 Qualifier

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | September 18, 2026

With Group Oklahoma concluded, all eight nations are confirmed for the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals — a critical qualifier on the road to the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics.

The bracket is set. The field is complete. Eight nations, one destination.

With the conclusion of Group Oklahoma at Devon Park in Oklahoma City on September 16, the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals field is fully confirmed. Team USA and Mexico each earned direct berths from the Group Oklahoma stage, while China secured the Wild Card. They join Australia, Canada, Japan, Puerto Rico and Chinese Taipei — five nations that had pre-qualified — to form the complete eight-team field competing in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia from April 5-11, 2027.

The significance extends well beyond 2027. The Redcliffe Finals serve as a primary qualifier on the pathway to the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. Every game in Redcliffe next April will carry Olympic weight.

How the Group Oklahoma Bracket Played Out

The five-day Group Oklahoma tournament ran September 12-16 at Devon Park. USA entered the bracket as the top seed after going unbeaten in the group stage, and delivered on that status in dominant fashion. In the No. 1 vs. No. 2 play-off on September 16, Maya Brady hit three home runs and drove in five as USA defeated China 7-0 in six innings — the run-ahead rule ending the game early and sealing the first direct ticket to Redcliffe.

Gabbie Garcia and Karlyn Pickens combined for the shutout, with Garcia retiring all nine batters she faced through the sixth inning while striking out six. USA finished Group Oklahoma with a perfect 5-0 record, run-ruling their way through multiple opponents. No other team at Devon Park came close to matching their dominance.

Mexico claimed the second direct berth, while China — despite finishing third in the bracket — earned the Wild Card spot, keeping their Redcliffe 2027 hopes alive. The Netherlands, Brazil and New Zealand were eliminated.

The Complete 2027 Finals Field

The eight nations heading to Redcliffe, QLD for the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals are: USA, Mexico, China (Group Oklahoma representatives), joined by Australia, Canada, Japan, Puerto Rico and Chinese Taipei.

It is a field that represents the full breadth of the global softball landscape. Australia, the host nation, will compete on home soil at Redcliffe Stadium. Japan, perennially among the world's elite, brings the depth of experience that has made them a consistent presence in global finals. Canada and Chinese Taipei have demonstrated sustained excellence on the international stage.

USA, coached by Oklahoma's Patty Gasso with JT Gasso and Rocha on staff, arrives as the team that ran the table at Group Oklahoma. Their 5-0 record and run-rule performances signal a team operating at peak level heading into what is arguably the most important tournament before Los Angeles 2028.

The LA28 Pathway

The 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals in Redcliffe are not just a standalone championship — they are one of the primary events determining which nations will compete in softball at the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. After the sport's return to the Olympic program in Tokyo 2020 (where USA won silver with Garcia and Mulipola on the roster) and its continued evolution at the Paris 2024 and 2028 Games, every Redcliffe result matters.

For Team USA — which has four Oklahoma Sooners in Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings and Kelly Maxwell, plus veterans like Garcia and Mulipola — this is the next step in a mission that began with the Group Oklahoma championship. The ticket to Redcliffe has been punched. The road to LA28 runs through Australia.

What's Next

The 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, QLD are scheduled for April 5-11, 2027. Preparation for all eight nations now begins in earnest. For USA and the American players competing at the collegiate level this fall, the 2026-27 season will serve as the bridge between Group Oklahoma's conclusion and the journey to Redcliffe.

Patty Gasso, who returned to Norman on September 16 after coaching Team USA through Group Oklahoma, is already back at Love's Field with the Oklahoma Sooners — preparing the 2027 collegiate squad while carrying the knowledge of what her USA roster accomplished at Devon Park. The full picture is in view. Eight nations. One road. The world is watching.
