Oklahoma Softball Full-Staff Fall Camp Day 2: 12 Days to the Sept. 30 Opener

By Emily Rawlings | I Love Softball | September 18, 2026

With Gasso, JT Gasso and Rocha back at Love's Field for a second straight full-staff session, the Sooners are building toward the Sept. 30 fall opener against Tarleton State.

Yesterday was the reunion. Today, it is the routine.

Oklahoma softball is in Day 2 of full-staff fall camp at Love's Field, with head coach Patty Gasso, associate head coach JT Gasso and assistant coach Rocha back in Norman after spending September 12-16 coaching Team USA at Group Oklahoma in Oklahoma City. The trio returned the evening of September 16 after USA clinched a direct berth to the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals. September 17 was the first full-staff practice. Today — September 18 — the work deepens.

Twelve days remain until the fall opener against Tarleton State at Love's Field on September 30.

The Roster Taking Shape

The transition from associate-coach-led practice to full-staff sessions is significant. While Oklahoma's associate coaches ran camp competently during the coaching trio's five-day absence, the presence of Gasso and her primary staff brings a different level of game-planning and competitive evaluation.

The names at the center of that evaluation are well established. Kendall Wells, who set the NCAA freshman home run record with 32 as a sophomore, returns for her junior year as Oklahoma's offensive centerpiece. Sydney Berzon, the two-time All-American who came to Norman from LSU with 486.1 career innings pitched, a 2.02 ERA and 406 strikeouts, is integrating into Gasso's pitching structure. Macie Harter, who hit .346 with 7 home runs and 23 RBIs at Middle Tennessee, provides a veteran bat with proven college production.

This is the core Gasso is building around in what will be the 2027 season — a roster assembled with specific championship intent after Oklahoma's first Women's College World Series absence since 2015 in 2026.

The Fall Schedule

The fall program gives Gasso and her staff a structured environment to evaluate depth, test lineups and build competitive chemistry before the spring season arrives. The schedule consists of eight total competitions, all at Love's Field: the Tarleton State opener on September 30, followed by the University of Science and Arts of Oklahoma (USAO) on October 23, Seminole State on October 30, and East Texas A&M on November 4. The Battle Series intrasquad games provide additional live-action reps throughout the fall.

General admission tickets are $10.88 per game. An All Fall Pass for all eight competitions is available for $70.69.

Each game is a data point. Gasso has been evaluating since the first Battle Series scrimmage — and now, with the full coaching staff on the field, every session carries more weight.

The Context: Redemption Season

Oklahoma's absence from the Women's College World Series in 2026 — the program's first since 2015 — was the defining event that shaped this offseason. Gasso has been methodical in her response: constructing a transfer class headlined by Berzon and Harter, trusting Wells to build on her record-setting freshman year, and engaging the WNT opportunity with the same competitive standard she brings to the collegiate program.

The first full-staff practice on September 17 represented the true beginning of the 2027 Oklahoma Sooners season. Every session after it — including today — adds a layer to what Gasso is building. Twelve days to Tarleton State. The lab is fully open.

What's Next

The Sooners open the fall schedule September 30 against Tarleton State at Love's Field. The full slate runs through November 4, with four outside opponents and four Battle Series intrasquad games giving Oklahoma a comprehensive evaluation window before the 2027 season begins. Gasso and her full staff are now on-site daily, which means the preparation level — and the intensity of every fall rep — has officially been elevated.

12 days to go. Love's Field is ready.
