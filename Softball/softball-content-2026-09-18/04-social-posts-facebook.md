### STORY 1: 2027 Redcliffe Finals Field Complete

**Long-Form Post**
The 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals field is complete. Eight nations. One destination. And the stakes could not be higher. 🥎🌏

When Group Oklahoma concluded at Devon Park in Oklahoma City on September 16, three more nations clinched their spots in the 2027 Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia (April 5-11). Team USA went 5-0 through the tournament, capping it with a dominant 7-0 run-rule victory over China in which Maya Brady hit three home runs and drove in five. USA claimed the first direct berth to Redcliffe.

Mexico earned the second direct berth, winning through the playoff bracket. China, despite finishing third, earned the Wild Card spot — giving Group Oklahoma three total representatives in the 2027 Finals.

Those three join Australia, Canada, Japan, Puerto Rico and Chinese Taipei — who all pre-qualified — to form the complete eight-nation field.

This isn't just about 2027. The Redcliffe Finals are a critical qualifier on the road to the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. Every result in Redcliffe next April will have LA28 implications. Team USA, coached by Oklahoma's Patty Gasso, has earned their place at that table.

The bracket is set. The mission is clear. Redcliffe 2027 → LA28.

### STORY 2: Oklahoma Sooners — Full-Staff Fall Camp Day 2

**Long-Form Post**
Yesterday was the reunion. Today, it's about the work. 🌪️

Day 2 of full-staff fall camp at Love's Field, and Patty Gasso, JT Gasso and Rocha are right back in the routine. The coaching trio returned to Norman on September 16 after guiding Team USA to a perfect 5-0 record through Group Oklahoma in OKC, and September 17 marked the first full-staff practice of the 2027 fall campaign. Today, the preparation deepens.

There are 12 days until the Sept. 30 fall opener against Tarleton State at Love's Field. That's 12 days for Wells, Berzon, and Harter to build chemistry under the full coaching staff — 12 days to sharpen what a season of expectations will demand.

Kendall Wells set the NCAA freshman home run record with 32 as a sophomore. Sydney Berzon arrived from LSU as a two-time All-American (486.1 IP, 2.02 ERA, 406 K). Macie Harter hit .346 at Middle Tennessee. These are the pieces Gasso is assembling.

The Battle Series intrasquad format — plus games against Tarleton State, USAO, Seminole State, and East Texas A&M — gives OU a full fall laboratory before the 2027 regular season begins. And that laboratory is fully staffed starting now.

12 days. Love's Field. Let's go. #Sooners

### STORY 4: Texas Longhorns — 11 Days to Sept. 29

**Long-Form Post**
Eleven days. That's the distance between today and the moment college softball fans have been talking about all summer. 🤘

Texas (53-12, back-to-back WCWS champions) hosts Murray State at McCombs Field on September 29 — 6 PM CT, free admission to the public. It's the fall opener, but it's also the first chance to see Isa Torres wearing burnt orange in a game setting.

Torres, who won the 2026 D1Softball National Player of the Year award at Florida State (.530 BA, 16 HR, 56 RBI) and grew up in Georgetown, Texas — 30 miles south of Austin — arrived in Austin as the most coveted transfer in the portal. Paired with Hannah Kavan, the only two-time Women's College World Series Most Outstanding Player in history, Texas has a senior duo that the entire college softball world will be watching.

The three-peat is on the line. Only two programs in history have ever won three consecutive national championships: the UCLA Bruins (1988-90) and the Oklahoma Sooners (2021-23). Texas is attempting to join that club.

Full fall schedule: Murray State (Sept. 29), Texas State (Oct. 13), Temple (Oct. 20), McLennan (Oct. 27), UTSA (Oct. 29), Texas A&M at Davis Diamond (Nov. 6). Head coach Mike White, who won the NFCA Coaching Staff of Year award for the second consecutive season, has built a fall program as carefully as a regular season.

11 days. Free admission. September 29. McCombs Field. #HookEm

### STORY 5: Texas Tech Red Raiders — Glasco Year 2

**Long-Form Post**
Seven All-Americans. One fall camp. This is what a reload looks like at Texas Tech. 🔴

Tim Glasco is in Year 2 of his tenure in Lubbock, and for the first time in Red Raiders history, all seven of Tech's All-American-caliber players are practicing together. The talent pool that Glasco assembled through the transfer portal is extraordinary:

Nicole Beachum (.406 BA, 10 HR, 69 RBI, All-ACC 1st Team at FSU) brings elite contact and power. Kasidi Pickering (No. 2 ranked transfer, .384 BA, 50 HR at Oklahoma) is one of the most complete hitters in the country. Haley Heavener (13-9, 2.81 ERA, 128 K at LSU) is a left-handed pitching anchor. They join Kaitlyn Terry, Mia Williams, Taylor Pannell and Moriah Polar — a collective of credentials that is genuinely unprecedented in Lubbock.

Texas Tech was the WCWS runner-up in 2026 — they fell to Texas in the championship series. With a full offseason to absorb Glasco's culture, and a roster that combines three distinct program identities from FSU, Oklahoma and LSU, Week 2 of fall camp is about building the cohesion that turns talent into titles.

The chem lab is open. The pressure is high. And in Lubbock, they wouldn't have it any other way. #WreckEm
