2028 Recruiting: After the Contact Window Closes, the Class Is Taking Shape

By Emily Rawlings | October 26, 2026 | I Love Softball

The October 24-25 contact window has closed, and the Class of 2028 is starting to look like the most competitive recruiting class softball has ever seen. With more than 1,200 nominations — the largest pool in the sport's recruiting history — the top of the class is beginning to crystallize around a familiar group of powerhouse programs.

Where the Top Commits Have Landed

Texas holds the No. 1 overall recruit in Michaela Landers, a five-tool middle infielder with elite range and raw power. Landers chose Texas over Oklahoma and Florida in a recruitment that drew national attention. For the Longhorns, landing the top prospect in the biggest class ever is a statement about where the program stands.

Florida isn't far behind. Kylie Holden, ranked No. 2 overall by Rivals, committed to the Gators on October 2 at a ceremony before an Ocoee High School football game. The five-star middle infielder gives Florida a cornerstone around which to build a loaded 2028 class that already includes five-star catcher Ryann Harris (No. 8), five-star two-way player Kate Losh (No. 11), and four-star catcher Brooklyn Evans (No. 21).

Oklahoma secured the top-ranked pitcher in the class: Sophia Schlader, No. 3 overall, whose riseball tops out above 70 miles per hour with exceptional spin. Schlader gives the Sooners the kind of ace-quality arm that will make Oklahoma a title contender from day one of the 2028 season.

Tennessee has gone a different direction, loading up on pitching depth. Megan Kuiper, a four-star pitcher ranked No. 40 by Softball America, has committed to the Lady Vols, joining fellow pitcher Reagan Eldred — a five-star prospect — in what is already a strong class for the Knoxville program.

What's Coming This Week

The Extra Elite 100 pitchers rankings for positions 201-300 are expected to drop Monday, October 27. And the moment the community has been waiting for — the Top-5 overall reveal — is anticipated later this week.

That top-five list will answer a question every recruiting follower has been asking: in a class with Landers, Holden, Schlader, and a handful of other elite athletes, who does Extra Inning Softball consider the single best prospect in the game's biggest-ever class?

What's Next

With the contact window closed and the Top-5 reveal coming, the next phase of 2028 recruiting will be the post-contact commit wave — players who made visits during October and are now in the decision process. Expect a steady stream of commitment announcements in the coming weeks as programs that made impressions during the contact period begin to see the results.

Follow extrainningsoftball.com and d1softball.com for the latest rankings and commitment news as the Class of 2028 continues to take shape.
