3 Days: Tennessee's Free Intersquad Is the Best Softball Event of the Fall

By Jordan Reeves | October 26, 2026 | I Love Softball

Thursday evening in Knoxville, the best pitching staff in college softball will take the field — and it won't cost you a dollar to watch.

Tennessee's fall intersquad is this Thursday, October 29 at 5 PM ET at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium. Admission is free. Parking is available. All you need to do is show up.

The Numbers That Make This a Don't-Miss

The Lady Vols' 2026 fall pitching trio has posted numbers that would be remarkable in a full spring season — let alone a limited fall exhibition schedule.

Karlyn Pickens has recorded 194 strikeouts with a 1.70 ERA in fall play. Sage Mardjetko has gone 16-3 with a 1.26 ERA. And freshman Bailee Shelton struck out 7 batters in just 3 innings in her fall debut against Lee University — a first look at what could be a fourth elite arm for a staff that was already among the best in the sport.

Thursday's intersquad is the only time you'll see all three pitch under competitive conditions before Tennessee goes dark for winter workouts.

Why Weekly Called This Out

After Tennessee wrapped up its fall schedule with the Lee University finale on October 23, head coach Karen Weekly was direct about what she saw. She said she wished the fall schedule had featured tougher competition — that the Lady Vols needed more challenging at-bats to fully evaluate where the hitters stand.

The intersquad on Thursday serves that exact purpose. With the fall schedule in the rearview, Weekly gets to see how her lineup responds when the pitching quality goes from fall exhibition opponents to Pickens, Mardjetko, and Shelton.

What to Watch For

Beyond the returning stars, Thursday is a chance to see where Tennessee's position players stand heading into 2027. The pitching is established. The question mark — for Weekly and for outside observers — is whether the lineup can handle elite pitching consistently enough to make a deep WCWS run.

Watch how the hitters adjust from the fall schedule's softer competition to facing their own staff. That evaluation is exactly what the coaching staff will be running in real time.

What's Next

After Thursday's intersquad, Tennessee shifts into off-season mode: winter conditioning, indoor work, and preparation for the 2027 season opener. The next public appearance for the Lady Vols will be the spring schedule, which hasn't been announced yet.

Thursday night at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium is free, and it may be the last time you see Pickens and Mardjetko compete in a Tennessee uniform until February. Make the trip.
