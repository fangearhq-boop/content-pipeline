Oklahoma's Fall Finale: Seminole State at Love's Field Thursday Night

By Sarah Mitchell | October 26, 2026 | I Love Softball

The Sooners close out their fall exhibition schedule this Thursday, and if you haven't made it to Love's Field yet this fall, this is your last chance.

Oklahoma hosts Seminole State on October 30 at 6:30 PM CT at Love's Field in Norman. The game is free to watch on 1oklahoma.com for those who can't make it to the ballpark in person.

What You'll See Thursday

The Sooners enter their fall finale with a roster that has drawn consistent attention from national analysts as one of the three best programs heading into 2027. Cydney Wells, who capped off her freshman season with 39 home runs and NFCA Freshman of the Year honors, is back. Nicole Kavan, a two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player, anchors the lineup alongside her.

Oklahoma has run its fall Battle Series at Love's Field all October, and Thursday is the final installment. After Thursday, the Sooners are done with public fall events, and the next time this roster is on display will be in spring competition.

The Fall in Context

Oklahoma's fall schedule has been an exercise in evaluating depth behind the established stars. With Wells and Kavan back, the coaching staff knows what it has at the core. The real questions this fall have been about the pitching rotation, the infield alignment, and whether new contributors can push the returning starters in practice.

Thursday's exhibition against Seminole State is the final live evaluation before winter workouts begin. It's a low-stakes game in the standings, but it matters for internal competition and for the coaches watching film.

The Bigger Picture for 2027

Oklahoma softball doesn't rebuild — it reloads. With Wells and Kavan among the best players in the country and a program that has competed in multiple Women's College World Series in recent years, the Sooners enter 2027 as genuine title favorites.

Thursday night at Love's Field is the last public proof of concept before the spring. Come out if you can. If you can't, 1oklahoma.com has you covered.

What's Next

After the October 30 Seminole State game, Oklahoma's next listed fall exhibition is East Texas A&M on November 4. Beyond that, the Sooners shift to winter preparation ahead of the 2027 season opener.
