Florida Softball Is 3-0 This Fall — USF Comes to Gainesville Tuesday Night

By Emily Rawlings | October 26, 2026 | I Love Softball

Florida softball is running through the fall undefeated — and tomorrow night, there's a free game at Katie Seashole Pressly Stadium.

The Gators are 3-0 in fall exhibition play, outscoring opponents 62-6 in three blowout wins: a 20-0 shutout of Santa Fe College on October 16, a 29-1 rout of Carson-Newman on October 17, and a 13-5 win over UNF on October 23. Tuesday's home game against USF tips off at 6 PM, and like all of Florida's fall home games, it's free and open to the public.

Edwards Making Her Case

Delaney Edwards has been one of the standout performers of Florida's fall slate, launching three home runs across the team's first three exhibitions. Edwards, who returned to the program this season, has brought veteran pop to the middle of a lineup that already features considerable depth.

The offense as a whole has been relentless. Sixty-two runs in three games against fall competition means the Gators aren't just winning — they're winning by a statement.

What to Watch Against USF

Tomorrow's matchup against USF is Florida's fourth fall game and the first opponent that figures to offer a more competitive look. The Gators have six starters returning from their 2026 Women's College World Series run, so Tuesday night is really about evaluating how the pieces fit together at game speed.

Head coach Tim Walton's programs have a history of using fall ball to develop younger players, and with a roster this experienced, the Gators can afford to experiment. Expect to see multiple pitchers and a full lineup rotation.

The Bigger Picture

Florida remains one of the programs most firmly in the national title conversation for 2027. A 3-0 fall with dominant margins is exactly the kind of confidence-builder a returning WCWS program needs. The pitching staff, the defense, and an offense that has produced 62 runs in three games — none of those things are accidents.

If you're in the Gainesville area Tuesday, there's no reason not to be at the ballpark.

What's Next

After USF on Tuesday (October 27), Florida's fall schedule continues through November. Upcoming home games include Saint Leo on November 6 and Florida State on November 13 before a road trip to UCF on November 15. The Gators wrap their fall slate on November 20 against Jacksonville.

For the latest schedule and game-day information, check floridagators.com.
