Arizona Softball Is Closing Out Fall Ball — and Building Something for 2027

By Jordan Reeves | October 26, 2026 | I Love Softball

While Oklahoma, Texas, Tennessee, and Texas Tech dominate the fall headlines, Arizona softball is quietly wrapping up its own fall schedule — and they deserve more than a footnote in the 2027 national title conversation.

The Wildcats close out their fall exhibitions this Thursday and Friday (October 30-31) with back-to-back home games at Hillenbrand Stadium in Tucson. It's the final installment of a nine-game fall home slate that has given head coach Caitlin Lowe's staff a full look at a roster built around veteran experience.

Shockey Returns as the Anchor

The most important name on Arizona's fall roster is Regan Shockey, who earned Big 12 Defensive Player of the Year honors last season. Shockey's return gives the Wildcats an elite defensive anchor and a player who knows what it takes to compete at the top of the Big 12 — arguably the toughest conference in college softball.

Arizona brings back 10 letterwinners from last season's team, which means this is a program with continuity and experience — two things that matter enormously when navigating a conference schedule that includes Oklahoma, Texas, Texas Tech, and Oklahoma State.

The Big 12 Landscape

The Big 12 is going to be a war in 2027. Oklahoma has Wells and Kavan. Texas has Michaela Landers committed and defending WCWS finalist credentials. Texas Tech has rebuilt around Kasidi Pickering and the No. 1 portal class in program history.

But Arizona isn't simply hoping to survive in that environment — it's a program that has competed at the highest level for years and has the personnel to make a run. A veteran-laden roster anchored by a defensive player of the year caliber performer is a foundation worth watching.

Fall Ball at Hillenbrand

Thursday's games at Hillenbrand Stadium feature Chandler-Gilbert and Benedictine Mesa, with Grand Canyon on the schedule for Friday. These final games give the coaching staff one last extended look at lineup combinations and pitching depth before winter work begins.

Arizona's fall has included matchups against UTEP (doubleheader, October 17), Phoenix College and Pima (October 23), and now the Thursday-Friday close. Nine home games is a meaningful fall workload — the Wildcats have gotten plenty of live reps.

What's Next

After the October 30-31 games, Arizona moves into off-season preparation ahead of the 2027 season. The Wildcats' spring schedule has not yet been announced. For schedule updates and fall game information, visit arizonawildcats.com.

Don't sleep on this program when the 2027 preseason polls come out. Arizona has the veterans, the defense, and the experience to disrupt some predictions in a Big 12 that looks as strong as ever.
