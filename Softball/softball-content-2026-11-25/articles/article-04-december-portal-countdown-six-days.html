6 Days to the December Portal: The Transfer Market Teams Can't Afford to Miss

By Jordan Reeves | November 25, 2026 | I Love Softball

Six days. That is all that separates college softball's biggest offseason roster moves from becoming official.

The NCAA's December transfer portal window opens December 1 and runs through December 15. For programs with roster holes — particularly in the pitching circle — this two-week period could be the difference between a 2027 WCWS run and an early elimination.

The Name Everyone Is Watching: Saylor Davis

Oklahoma State pitcher Saylor Davis has three years of eligibility remaining and is widely expected to enter the portal on Day 1. Davis is a left-handed arm who brings immediate impact to any pitching staff. With the shortage of elite pitching on the transfer market, she will not last long once her name appears in the portal.

The programs best positioned to land her are the ones with the NIL resources and roster clarity to move fast. In this market, that means making an offer before the first weekend of December.

Arizona: The Most Urgent Need in the Country

Arizona went 15-2 in fall ball — one of the best fall records in the country under head coach Caitlin Lowe. But the Wildcats carry a wound into the winter: the departure of Mariah Torres, the 2026 D1Softball Player of the Year, to Texas Tech via the summer portal.

Torres was not just Arizona's best pitcher — she was one of the best in college softball. Finding even a comparable arm in the December pool would be a significant undertaking. Finding a true ace to replace her borders on impossible without a Davis-level transfer.

Arizona is expected to pursue every available pitching option aggressively starting December 1.

The SEC Competition: Ole Miss and Auburn

Arizona is not alone in its pitching desperation. Ole Miss and Auburn have both been identified as programs prioritizing pitching upgrades through the December portal. Both SEC schools have the NIL infrastructure to compete for top names, and both coaching staffs have demonstrated willingness to reshape rosters via the portal.

The same pool of pitching arms is being targeted by multiple Top-25 programs. This window will be competitive.

The Window Is Smaller Than It Looks

The December portal window is 15 days on paper, but a dead period from December 8-12 restricts official visits, compressing the actual recruiting window into the first week. Programs that move slowly will find the best players already committed by the time official visits resume.

The strategic calendar: enter portal (Dec 1), immediate contact begins, official visits Dec 1-7, dead period Dec 8-12 (phone/video allowed, no in-person visits), last-call push Dec 13-15.

What's Next

The recruiting blackout (Nov 25-29) is in effect today, lifting November 30. Coaches can re-engage with targets on November 30 before the portal formally opens December 1. Programs planning to pursue transfer pitching should have their target lists finalized before the blackout ends. With six days until the market opens, the preparation phase is now — the execution phase begins at midnight on December 1.
