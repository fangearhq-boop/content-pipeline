### STORY 1: Texas 3-Peat Chase

#### Long-Form Post
Texas softball is in rarified air.

Back-to-back national champions in 2025 and 2026 — and the Longhorns aren't done building.

Coach Mike White has been busy this offseason, adding catcher Samantha Swan from Arizona State, pitcher Samantha Lincoln from Texas Tech, and infielder Isa Torres from Florida State in the transfer portal. The 2027 signing class brings in catcher Lolo Gamble, outfielder Emree Massaad, pitcher Brooklynn Hagerty, and pitching phenom Madi Doty, described as one of the top pitching prospects in the nation.

No program in modern Division I softball has won three straight national championships. Texas is building the roster to try.

Official 2027 preseason polls won't drop until January, but expect the Longhorns to open near the top.

### STORY 2: Recruiting Blackout

#### Long-Form Post
If you've been following the recruiting scene for your favorite college softball program, today marks a full stop.

The NCAA Division I softball recruiting blackout takes effect today, November 25, running through November 29. For five days, coaches cannot call, text, email, or make any contact with recruits of any class year. No visits. No correspondence. Nothing.

It's the quiet before the storm.

Yesterday was the final day of unrestricted contact — the last chance for coaches to call uncommitted prospects, follow up on visits, and make a final push before the freeze. After the blackout lifts on November 30, programs get one day before the December 1 transfer portal window opens (Dec 1-15).

For programs still chasing high school commits or mapping out their portal strategy, the next 24-72 hours are going to feel very long.

### STORY 3: Oklahoma Pitching Search

#### Long-Form Post
Oklahoma's 2027 recruiting class is the best in the country. Everyone agrees on that.

Julianna "Goose" Hutchens — the No. 1 overall recruit — is already enrolled. Finlee Williams (top outfielder), Riley Hilliard (No. 2 catcher), and Emma Reynolds (4-star shortstop) are committed.

It's an elite class that can fill a lineup. What it doesn't yet have is an ace.

The Sooners have been hosting pitching prospects — including visits from Vivian Knott and national No. 1 Aspen Boulware before today's recruiting blackout went into effect. With the freeze now on through November 29, any pitching commitments will have to wait.

The December portal (Dec 1-15) offers another avenue, with Saylor Davis (OSU, 3 years eligibility) among the top arms expected to enter. Oklahoma finished this past season needing more from the circle to match its explosive offense.

The No. 1 class in the country can hit. Can Patty Gasso find the pitcher to get them over the top?

### STORY 4: December Portal Countdown

#### Long-Form Post
Six days.

That's how long college softball programs have to wait before the December transfer portal window opens (Dec 1-15) — and the floodgates open on player movement.

Here's what to watch when it does:

**OSU's Saylor Davis** — The Oklahoma State pitcher has three years of eligibility remaining and is expected to enter the portal on Day 1. She's the most coveted free agent in this window.

**Arizona's ace hunt** — The Wildcats went 15-2 in fall ball but lost D1Softball Player of the Year Mariah Torres to Texas Tech last summer. Head coach Caitlin Lowe needs a No. 1 pitcher before spring.

**Ole Miss and Auburn** — Both SEC programs have been identified as pitching-desperate heading into 2027. They'll be competing for the same names as Arizona.

The window is only 15 days, closing December 15 before a dead period runs December 8-12 limiting official visits. Programs that wait will be shut out.
