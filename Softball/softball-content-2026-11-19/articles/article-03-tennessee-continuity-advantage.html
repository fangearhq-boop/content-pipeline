Tennessee's Continuity Advantage — 9 Starters Back Is Rare in the Portal Era

By Emily Rawlings

November 19, 2026

The November signing period is over. The portal windows are coming. And the entire college softball landscape is in flux — except at one program in Knoxville, Tennessee, which is essentially returning the same team that went 49-12 in 2026.

Tennessee will bring back nine starters. In the portal era, that number is not just impressive. It is nearly unprecedented at the elite level of the sport.

The Pitcher Who Anchors Everything

Sage Mardjetko went 16-3 with a 1.26 ERA and 171 strikeouts in 2026, earning NFCA First Team All-American honors. She is not just a returning starter. She is one of the best pitchers in college softball, and she will be on the rubber for Tennessee when the Lady Vols open 2027.

Programs spend entire offseasons in the portal trying to find a pitcher with half of Mardjetko's résumé. Tennessee already has her.

The Offensive Core

Sophia Knight hit .394 in 2026. The infield returns its core contributors. The lineup that scored runs in bunches last season — the one that helped produce a 49-win record — will largely be intact.

That is not an accident. It is the result of Karen Weekly's recruiting vision, which has historically emphasized multi-year development over short-term portal patching. Weekly's contract was extended through 2030 — a signal from the Tennessee athletic department that this approach is working and that it wants stability at the top.

What Continuity Actually Means

In the portal era, rosters churn constantly. Programs that win with freshman classes lose those freshmen to the portal after two years if the portal offers them more money or more playing time. Programs that build through the portal bring in players who know different systems, different teammates, different coaching expectations.

Tennessee does not have that problem. These nine returning starters have been in Weekly's system for years. They know the signs. They know each other. They know how to perform in SEC play when the pressure is highest.

That chemistry takes time to build, and it cannot be purchased in a December portal window. Oklahoma is integrating its historic recruiting class — talented, yes, but still new faces learning a new environment. LSU is blending portal additions with signed recruits for the first time under this structure. Tennessee is walking back into spring camp on day one ahead of almost every other top-10 program in the country.

What's Next

Tennessee opens spring practice in January. The Lady Vols' schedule for 2027 will be announced in the coming weeks. Watch for their NFCA preseason ranking in January — a program returning nine starters from a 49-12 team should attract serious top-5 consideration. The national title conversation in 2027 starts with OU and LSU in most projections. Tennessee's quiet continuity may make them the most dangerous program in the field.
