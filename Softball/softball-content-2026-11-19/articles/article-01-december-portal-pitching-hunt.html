December Portal Is 12 Days Away — Which Programs Are Hunting Pitching?

By Jordan Reeves

November 19, 2026

The December transfer portal opens in 12 days. For most college softball programs, the window that runs from December 1 through December 15 is a secondary roster adjustment. For a handful of programs, it is the most important personnel decision of the entire offseason.

The 2026-27 roster landscape has been reshaped by two forces operating simultaneously: the Tenth Circuit's reversal of the Wisne v. NCAA preliminary injunction on November 6, which ended federal protection for 5-for-5 athletes, and the House settlement's 25-player roster cap, which makes every roster slot a resource that cannot be wasted. Coaches who built their December strategy in advance are positioned to strike. Those who didn't are scrambling.

Arizona: The Most Urgent Case

No program enters the December window with a clearer mission than Arizona. Head coach Caitlin Lowe's program went 15-2 in fall ball — a remarkable debut for a first-year head coach. The offense has teeth. Big 12 Defensive Player of the Year Regan Shockey returns. The pieces are in place for a genuine conference title run.

The hole is at pitcher. Arizona lost Mariah Torres — the D1Softball National Player of the Year — to Texas Tech in the summer portal. Replacing a pitcher of that caliber mid-offseason, through the December window, is an enormous ask. But it is the ask Lowe must answer. Without elite pitching, Arizona's ceiling drops from WCWS title contender to bubble team.

Ole Miss and Auburn: The Programs Watching the Board

Ole Miss needs pitching. The Rebels have been consistent, mid-tier SEC performers for several seasons, with enough talent to make the postseason and not enough depth to advance deep. The December window could change that if the right arm becomes available. The 5-for-5 roster pressure means some athletes at programs that cannot guarantee them playing time may choose the portal — and Ole Miss has the resources to recruit that market.

Auburn enters December with the No. 6 D1Softball signing class, which skews offensive. Adding a rotation arm through the portal would balance the roster and elevate the Tigers' 2027 outlook significantly.

The Programs That Don't Need December

Not everyone is hunting. Oklahoma enters December with the No. 1 D1Softball signing class and all five core contributors from last season's core intact. LSU, ranked No. 1 by Softball America — the first time in program history — has a complete class and portal additions already in place. Tennessee returns nine starters from its 49-12 squad and doesn't need a single portal player to compete for a national title.

The separation between prepared and unprepared programs starts December 1. The programs that spent October and November building relationships with potential portal entrants will have options. Those that waited will be left with what everyone else passed on.

What's Next

The portal opens December 1 at midnight and closes December 15. The dead period (no in-person contact or visits) runs December 9-12, which creates urgency to visit and secure commitments in the first eight days of the window. Programs that move fastest will win the December portal. For Arizona, Ole Miss, and Auburn, that sprint starts now.
