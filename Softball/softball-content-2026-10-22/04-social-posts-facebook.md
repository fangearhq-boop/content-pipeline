### STORY 4: Texas Tech 2027 Roster Takes Shape

**Long-Form Post (3:00 PM ET)**

Texas Tech's 2027 softball roster is assembled. And it looks like a WCWS contender.

The Red Raiders entered the offseason as WCWS finalists. Head coach Jeff Cottrill — who arrived from Houston — made immediate moves in the transfer portal to rebuild around a veteran core. The result is a roster that looks ready to compete from day one of the 2027 campaign.

**The Portal Class:**
Kasidi Pickering headlines the group. In three seasons at Oklahoma, she hit .384 with 50 home runs and 169 RBI — numbers that put her in elite company among the best hitters in modern college softball. She signed with the Red Raiders on June 19.

She's joined by Heavener (a top portal bat), Polar, and Gallaway — giving Cottrill four experienced additions alongside a strong returning core. Alex Williams hit 27 home runs last season (program record) and returns along with first-team All-American Terry.

**The Coaching Staff:**
Shelby Pendley brings a resume that speaks for itself. As a shortstop at Oklahoma, she put up 84 home runs and 269 RBI in her career — among the best ever by a Sooner. She transitioned to coaching at New Mexico, Jackson State, and Belhaven before joining Cottrill's staff.

Big 12 play begins March 12. Circle it. The Red Raiders are for real.
