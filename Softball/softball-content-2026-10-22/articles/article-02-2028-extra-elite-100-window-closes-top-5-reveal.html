2028 Extra Elite 100 Window Closes Today — Top-5 Reveal Set for Monday, Oct 27

By Sarah Mitchell | October 22, 2026

The countdown is over. Thursday, October 22 marks the close of the nomination window for Extra Inning Softball's Class of 2028 Extra Elite 100 rankings — and after months of evaluation, one question remains unanswered: who is the No. 1 overall prospect in the class?

The answer arrives Monday, October 27, when Extra Inning Softball releases its highly anticipated Top-5 reveal. It is the most-watched moment in the 2028 recruiting cycle — a moment that will immediately reshape the conversation around every top college program's class.

The Largest Class Ever Evaluated

More than 1,200 players were nominated for consideration in the Class of 2028 — making this the biggest field Extra Inning Softball has ever ranked. The evaluation process factors in statistics, strength of schedule, tournament attendance, performance against elite opponents, willingness to play up age groups, and coach recommendations. It is a comprehensive look at every notable 8th-grade and early high school softball talent in the country.

The rankings have been releasing in tiers for weeks, working from No. 100 down through the teens. Each reveal has sparked conversation on social media, in travel ball circles, and in college coaches' offices. The final tier — the top five — will be the loudest of them all.

The Programs Already Positioned at the Top

Even before the Top-5 is announced, the outlines of the 2028 recruiting landscape are clear.

Florida has already secured a commitment from Kylie Holden, widely regarded as the No. 2 overall prospect in the class by Softball America. Holden is a multi-tool player who fits precisely what Tim Walton has built at Gainesville. She joins a Gators 2028 class that already includes another five-star catcher and a two-way prospect, putting Florida among the national leaders for the cycle.

Texas commit Michaela Landers is the consensus No. 1 infielder by Rivals and On3, and she plays alongside future teammates on TX Bombers Gold — the same travel organization that has produced a remarkable number of Texas commits in recent cycles. Head coach Mike White's program has built a pipeline from that program that would make any recruiting coordinator envious.

Oklahoma's class is anchored by Schlader, who appears in the top three of multiple ranking services. The Sooners have built their brand around recruiting elite players and developing them into WCWS contributors, and the 2028 class appears to continue that standard.

What the Top-5 Reveal Means

The No. 1 overall prospect designation carries weight far beyond a single player. It tells a story about where college softball is headed, which travel ball programs are producing elite talent, and which recruiting pipelines are the strongest in the country.

For the players themselves — eighth and ninth graders who made recruiting commitments at an age when most kids are still picking their first varsity sport — it is a validation of years of development work. For the programs fortunate enough to hold their commitments, it is a significant recruiting edge heading into 2027 and 2028 fall showcases.

The next contact window opens October 24-25. Expect another wave of commitments to follow closely behind the Top-5 announcement.

What's Next

The Top-5 reveal drops Monday, October 27, on Extra Inning Softball. The Oct 24-25 contact window will bring a new wave of 2028 commits. Watch for commitment announcements from uncommitted top-tier prospects in the days surrounding both events. The 2028 recruiting story is far from over — it is just arriving at its most dramatic chapter.
