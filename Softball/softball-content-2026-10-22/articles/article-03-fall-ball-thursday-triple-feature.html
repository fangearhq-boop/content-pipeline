Fall Ball Thursday: Oklahoma, Florida, and Tennessee All Play Oct 23

By Emily Rawlings | October 22, 2026

It does not happen often. Three of the top college softball programs in the country do not typically share a fall ball calendar date. But on Thursday, October 23, Oklahoma, Florida, and Tennessee are all scheduled to host games — and every single one of them is free or freely streamable.

If you follow college softball and you are not watching at least one game on Thursday, you are missing the best day of the fall season.

Oklahoma vs USAO — Love's Field, 6:30 PM CT

Battle Series Game 5 is the headliner for the evening. Oklahoma hosts the University of Science and Arts of Oklahoma in what is the Sooners' final public fall showcase before October 30's closing game against Seminole State.

The lineup is everything fans have come to expect from this program. Haley Kavan — two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player — returns at graduate level, giving Oklahoma a headliner that no other program can match. Kendall Wells (39 HR as a freshman, NFCA Freshman of Year) has been the hottest hitter of fall ball. Jocelyn Berzon anchors a pitching staff that has championship-level depth.

Stream live and free on 1oklahoma.com starting at 6:30 PM CT. Love's Field is also open for in-person attendance.

Florida vs UNF — Katie Seashole Pressly Stadium

Florida returns to Katie Seashole Pressly Stadium for another fall exhibition, this one against North Florida from the ASUN Conference. Admission is free.

The Gators enter this game having already turned heads with their fall ball results. They beat Santa Fe 20-0 on October 16 and followed that with a 29-1 demolition of Carson-Newman on October 17 — 49 combined runs in two days. Six starters are back from the 2026 squad, Tim Walton has the full pitching staff available, and a loaded portal class and recruiting class have been integrated into the program.

Florida looks like one of the favorites to win the 2027 national title. Thursday is another chance to watch that machine work.

Tennessee vs Lee University — Sherri Parker Lee Stadium (Fall Finale)

Tennessee closes its 2026 fall schedule at home against Lee University — and the Lady Vols head coach Karen Weekly has said this is free for all fans, with gates open ahead of first pitch. It is the program's last public game until February.

The Vols have had a remarkable fall. Karlyn Pickens — the No. 1 pick in the 2026 AUSL Draft who returned for a fifth year — has been dominant in her limited fall appearances, posting a 1.70 ERA to go with her 194 strikeouts from last spring. Sage Mardjetko (16-3, 1.26 ERA, NFCA All-American) gives Tennessee two certified aces, a combination Weekly has called the deepest pitching staff in program history.

Add Ehren Earleywine (associate head coach and hitting coach, from the 2026 champion Texas staff) and the Lady Vols' upside for 2027 is enormous.

Why Thursday Matters

Fall ball is different from spring competition — scores do not count, rosters are expanded, and the focus is on development over results. But watching three national title contenders on the same day provides something that preseason rankings never can: actual looks at real players in real competitive situations.

For coaches, scouts, and recruiting followers, Thursday is a gold mine. For fans, it is simply a great day of softball at no cost.

What's Next

Results from all three games will be available Thursday evening. Tennessee's fall schedule will be complete after Thursday. Florida's next scheduled fall game has not been announced. Oklahoma's final fall contest is October 30 vs Seminole State at Love's Field.
