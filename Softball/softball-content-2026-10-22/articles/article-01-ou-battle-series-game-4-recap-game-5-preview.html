Kendall Wells Homers Again in Battle Series Game 4 — Game 5 vs USAO Is Tomorrow

By Jordan Reeves | October 22, 2026

Some stories keep writing themselves. Kendall Wells added another chapter to hers on Wednesday night at Love's Field, sending another home run into the fall sky during Oklahoma's Battle Series Game 4.

It was entirely on brand. Wells spent her freshman spring hitting 39 home runs and earning NFCA Freshman of the Year honors. She has carried that momentum into fall ball, where the Battle Series format gives the Sooners a chance to develop, experiment, and remind everyone exactly what they bring back for 2027.

Wednesday's game — the fourth in Oklahoma's five-game fall series — featured the usual cast of headliners: Haley Kavan, the two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player who returned for graduate eligibility; Nicole Jocelyn Berzon, the sub-2.00 ERA reliever who has emerged as one of the best backend arms in the country; and a lineup anchored by returners like Payton Westra and Goose Hutchens. The Sooners know who they are, and fall ball is the chance to show it.

Tomorrow: Game 5 vs USAO at Love's Field

There is one more chance to watch this group in an official fall setting — and it comes Thursday.

Oklahoma hosts the University of Science and Arts of Oklahoma on October 23 at Love's Field in Norman. First pitch is scheduled for 6:30 PM CT. The game will stream live and free on 1oklahoma.com. No tickets are required to watch online.

For fans who want to be in the stands, Love's Field is one of the best settings in college softball. Oklahoma's series opener on September 30 drew a crowd of more than 2,300 — a strong number for an October fall game. Game 5 represents the last scheduled public look at the 2027 Sooners before the offseason begins in earnest. The final Battle Series contest is set for October 30 against Seminole State.

Head coach Patty Gasso has used these games for development purposes — rotating pitching assignments, getting underclassmen experience, and evaluating incoming players ahead of the spring. By all accounts, that process has gone well this fall.

Wells Is the Headliner, Kavan Is the Foundation

It is impossible to discuss Oklahoma softball without starting with these two players.

Kavan won back-to-back WCWS MOP honors in 2025 and 2026, cementing herself as one of the greatest players in the history of the program. Her decision to return for a graduate season means Oklahoma has one of the most decorated players in college softball lining up for 2027 — a competitive advantage that no portal class can fully replicate.

Wells is the reason scouts are paying attention to this fall series. A record-breaking freshman season that featured 39 home runs — one of the best single-season totals in recent memory — put her on a national stage immediately. She answered with NFCA Freshman of the Year and a confirmed place in Oklahoma's lineup plans for the next two-plus years.

Together with Berzon in the circle, the Sooners look like a legitimate national title contender before spring workouts even begin.

What's Next

Game 5 against USAO is tomorrow, October 23, at 6:30 PM CT. Stream it live on 1oklahoma.com. The final Battle Series game of the fall — October 30 against Seminole State — will close out Oklahoma's public fall schedule before the offseason begins. For fans wanting to see a national title contender taking shape in real time, Love's Field is the place to be.
