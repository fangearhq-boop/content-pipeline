Texas Tech's 2027 Roster Is Set — Pickering Headlines a Complete Portal Class

By Jordan Reeves | October 22, 2026

When Texas Tech made the 2026 WCWS final, it announced something to the rest of college softball: the Red Raiders are no longer just a Big 12 contender. They are a national program. And now, with their full 2027 roster assembled, Jeff Cottrill's team looks ready to back that statement up.

The headline addition was confirmed in June: Kasidi Pickering signed with Texas Tech on June 19, bringing one of the most prolific offensive careers in recent Oklahoma history to Lubbock. In three seasons with the Sooners, Pickering hit .384 with 50 home runs and 169 RBI — numbers that would make her a cornerstone player at any program in the country.

A Complete Portal Class

Pickering did not arrive alone. Texas Tech built a complete portal class around her, adding Heavener, Polar, and Gallaway alongside the Pickering signing to give Cottrill experienced players at multiple positions. The combination of portal additions and a strong returning core — led by Alex Williams (27 HR in 2026, a program record) and first-team All-American Terry — gives the Red Raiders genuine depth up and down their roster.

For a program that reached the WCWS final in its first season under this new staff, the message is clear: Texas Tech is not rebuilding. It is reinforcing.

The Coaching Staff

Head coach Jeff Cottrill came to Texas Tech from Houston, where he built a program capable of competing nationally. His first season proved the hire was correct — WCWS finalists rarely happen by accident, and getting there in year one suggests Cottrill knew exactly how to plug into the talent already in Lubbock.

Associate head coach Shelby Pendley is one of the most accomplished former players to enter the coaching profession in recent years. As a shortstop at Oklahoma, she hit 84 home runs and drove in 269 RBI — career numbers that place her among the most productive players in Sooners history. She has since coached at New Mexico, Jackson State, and Belhaven, and arrives at Texas Tech with extensive experience developing infielders and hitters.

The staff's ties to Oklahoma's talent pipeline — Cottrill's reputation in Texas, Pendley's OU connections — suggest this program will continue to recruit at an elite level for years to come.

Big 12 Opens March 12

Texas Tech's Big 12 schedule opens on March 12, 2027. The conference will be among the most competitive in the country, with Oklahoma still loaded, Oklahoma State building a strong class, and Iowa State and others continuing to develop their programs. For a Red Raiders team that was already a WCWS finalist, the regular season will be the proving ground that determines whether this group can take the next step.

On paper, they absolutely have the roster to do it.

What's Next

Texas Tech's spring schedule will be released in the coming weeks. Big 12 play opens March 12. Watch for the Red Raiders in preseason Top-25 polls — they have the roster to justify a top-five ranking coming out of the WCWS final.
