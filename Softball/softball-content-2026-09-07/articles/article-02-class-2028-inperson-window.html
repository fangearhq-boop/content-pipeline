Class of 2028: In-Person Contact Opens Tomorrow at 7 AM — What It Means

By Emily Rawlings | September 7, 2026

Recruiting

Tomorrow morning at 7:00 AM Eastern Time, the Class of 2028 recruiting calendar takes its biggest single step forward since the contact window opened September 1. The in-person contact period begins, and with it comes everything that phone calls, FaceTimes, and text messages cannot replace: the handshake, the campus tour, and the face-to-face conversation.

For the past week, D1 coaches have been burning up phones. Prospects have been fielding calls from programs across the country, taking Zoom sessions with coaching staffs, and hearing pitches for the first time. But tomorrow, coaches can show up. They can come to a high school practice. They can bring a junior onto campus for a real visit. They can evaluate players in person at fall tournaments and showcases.

What the In-Person Window Unlocks

Under the current NCAA recruiting calendar, September 9 at 7:00 AM ET marks the transition from communication-only contact to full in-person access. That means D1 coaches can now:

  • Conduct off-campus in-person recruiting visits with prospective student-athletes
  • Invite juniors for official or unofficial campus visits
  • Attend high school or travel ball events to watch and meet with recruits

The shift is significant. Hearing a coach's passion over FaceTime is one thing. Standing in a facility and looking a coaching staff in the eye tells you something no video call ever can.

A Class Already Moving Fast

Peyton Ushery didn't wait for September 9. The No. 2-ranked prospect in the Class of 2028 per Line Drive Media's HOT 100 committed to Georgia on September 1, the very first day communication was permitted. The commit wave that Ushery's announcement started is about to go to the next level.

This is a record-setting class by nominations: 1,226 prospects registered through the Extra Innings Softball system, the highest total in the program's history. The depth is real. Programs competing for top-end talent in this class need to move quickly — and they will, starting at 7:01 tomorrow morning.

For Prospects: Be Ready

The recruits who thrive in tomorrow's rush are those who have spent September 1 through 8 doing their homework. Know your top programs. Know the questions you want to ask — about playing time, academics, scholarship structure, and coaching philosophy. Have a list of your top five priorities going into every conversation.

In-person contact windows have a way of accelerating decisions faster than anyone expects. Programs that can get a prospect on campus in the first 48 hours often win the commitment before competitors even schedule a visit.

What's Next

The Class of 2028 in-person contact window opens September 9 at 7:00 AM ET. Campus visit and evaluation activity is expected to spike immediately. Monitoring recruiting services — D1Softball, Line Drive Media, Extra Innings Softball — in the coming week will give the clearest picture of which programs are landing the class's top talent. The commit announcements are coming fast.
