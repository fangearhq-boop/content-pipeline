Oklahoma Softball Enters Final Full Week With Complete Coaching Staff Before WNT Departure

By Jordan Reeves | September 7, 2026

Oklahoma Sooners

Patty Gasso departs for Oklahoma City — as a USA Softball coach — in five days. The clock on this week's practices at Love's Field is ticking louder than any other in the fall camp calendar.

Gasso, along with assistant coaches JT Gasso and Jennifer Rocha, will step away from their OU duties on September 12 to guide the United States Women's National Team at the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Oklahoma. They return after the event concludes September 16. During that window, Oklahoma's fall camp continues under interim direction with a roster that has already shown glimpses of what could be a dangerous 2027 squad.

This week, then, is the last sustained block of time the full staff gets before the departure. Every practice is accounted for. Every evaluation carries weight.

Wells: From Record-Breaker to Established Force

The most-watched player in fall camp is easy to identify: Kendall Wells, the catcher who shattered the NCAA freshman home run record last season with 32. Her sophomore emergence began quietly in the Battle Series scrimmages, where she homered twice off transfer pitcher Sydney Berzon. The dynamic is rare — and telling. Two players competing for excellence while simultaneously making each other better.

Wells finished her freshman campaign with a .358 batting average, 88 RBI, and a 1.028 slugging percentage. The NFCA National Freshman of the Year award, the SEC Freshman of the Year honor, and the Johnny Bench Award all came with it. As a sophomore, the question shifts from discovery to consistency — and her early fall work suggests the maturation is already underway.

Berzon: The Transfer Who Elevates Everything

Sydney Berzon arrived from LSU with a three-year resume that commands attention: 486.1 innings pitched, a 2.02 ERA, 406 career strikeouts, and a 52-25 overall record across her time with the Tigers. She is a two-time All-American.

The fall scrimmages have offered Berzon a chance to integrate with a new catcher, a new coaching staff, and a roster built around an offense that can punish mistakes. The early results from her work with Wells suggest a pairing that could anchor Oklahoma's pitching through 2027.

Macie Harter (.346 BA, 7 HR, 23 RBI from Middle Tennessee State) adds another dimension to the lineup. The Sooners enter fall having absorbed nine newcomers alongside 12 returning players — a retooling that the current practice sessions are designed to accelerate.

What's Next

Oklahoma's fall opener arrives September 30 at Love's Field — the first of four fall games that include matchups with the University of Science and Arts of Oklahoma, Seminole State College, and East Texas A&M. The full staff has five practices remaining before Gasso and company head to Devon Park. Every rep in Norman this week is an investment in what happens when the coaching staff is 20 miles away in Oklahoma City wearing red, white, and blue.
