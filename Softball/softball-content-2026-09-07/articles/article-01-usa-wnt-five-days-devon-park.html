USA Softball's World Cup Countdown: Five Days Until Devon Park Opens

By Sarah Mitchell | September 7, 2026

USA National Team

Five days from now, Devon Park in Oklahoma City becomes the center of the softball world. The WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Oklahoma opens Saturday, September 12, and the United States enter as the two-time hosts of this same competition, riding a 5-0 record at the International Cup played on this very field.

This year's stakes are different. This year, the pressure is real — and the prize is larger than any regular-season title or exhibition gold.

The top two teams from the six-nation group advance directly to the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia. Those two spots in Australia serve as the first qualification rung for the LA28 Olympic Games. Nothing small is on the line.

The Opening Night Draw

USA opens against No. 7 Mexico at 7:30 PM on Saturday night. It is the final game of opening day, after Brazil and China meet at 1:00 PM and New Zealand faces the Netherlands at 4:00 PM. The USA-Mexico match lands at the prime evening slot — with the Opening Ceremony set for 6:30 PM preceding it.

Mexico is ranked No. 7 in the world, and the Mexicans will be energized playing in front of their North American neighbors in an Oklahoma crowd that skews heavily toward their opponents. But USA Head Coach Patty Gasso has prepared this group for moments exactly like this.

Gasso — who also coaches at Oklahoma — leads a national team staff that includes son JT Gasso and assistant Jennifer Rocha. Together they guide a 16-player roster that combines experienced Olympians with rising stars making their international competitive debuts.

The Real Test: Netherlands at No. 6

Mexico is the opener, but No. 6 Netherlands will determine seeding. The Dutch have proven themselves a consistent challenger to USA supremacy in international softball, and finishing ahead of them in the round-robin determines who enters the September 16 play-off bracket in the top position.

Current roster members Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell bring OU experience into the national jersey. Gabbie Garcia and freshly minted international player Kendall Wells — who smashed the NCAA freshman home run record with 32 last season — round out Oklahoma's contribution. Keilani Ricketts, the veteran AUSL standout, also suits up for USA.

September 16: The Play-Off Format

After five days of round-robin play, September 16 brings the decisive matches. The top two finishers from the group meet at 1:00 PM — with the winner earning a direct berth to the 2027 Finals in Australia. Third and fourth place collide at 4:00 PM. Their loser then faces the winner of that game in the Repechage at 7:00 PM, with the second Australia spot going to whoever survives.

USA's road to that 1:00 PM match — and beyond — depends on what they build in the next five days of competition.

What's Next

Game action begins Saturday, September 12, at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. USA takes on No. 7 Mexico at 7:30 PM, with the bracket running through September 16. Tickets are on sale through USA Softball and the WBSC. Two nations leave with tickets to Australia. Everything else goes home empty-handed.

The countdown is at five days. The roster is ready. Devon Park is waiting.
