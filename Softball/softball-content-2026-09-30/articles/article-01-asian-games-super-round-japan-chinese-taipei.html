Japan and Chinese Taipei Take Control of 2026 Asian Games Super Round

By Sarah Mitchell | September 30, 2026

Both nations won Super Round games Tuesday, setting up a collision course toward the October 3 Finals at Anjō Sports Park — with LA28 Olympic qualifying points hanging over every at-bat.

The 2026 Asian Games softball tournament is hitting its climax, and two teams are separating themselves from the field. Japan and Chinese Taipei both won Super Round games on Tuesday, according to the World Baseball Softball Confederation, building momentum ahead of the October 3 gold-medal Finals in Anjō, Japan.

Japan has been the most dominant force in the tournament since it opened September 26. The national team rolled through the preliminary round without a loss, and the Super Round has brought more of the same. Their combination of pitching depth, disciplined hitting, and experienced tournament management has made them the prohibitive favorite for gold.

Chinese Taipei, though, is not playing the role of a secondary contender. Their Super Round victory was a statement win, and the program has shown throughout this tournament that they belong in any conversation about Asian softball's elite.

The Super Round Stakes

The Super Round features four teams: Japan, China, Chinese Taipei, and the Philippines — all of whom advanced from the preliminary round. South Korea, despite their regional reputation, was eliminated after going 1-1 in preliminary play, losing to China 8-4 in a game that featured three Chinese home runs.

The Philippines earned their spot with a remarkable comeback win in preliminary play — a 7-run sixth-inning rally that shocked South Korea and kept their Olympic qualifying hopes alive. They are the underdog story of the tournament, and their continued presence in the Super Round is no accident.

China, which also swept the preliminary round undefeated, entered the Super Round as the other force capable of challenging Japan. How they perform against Japan and Chinese Taipei in the Super Round will likely determine whether the gold-medal game features the two programs it appears to be heading toward.

LA28 Is Always in the Room

The 2026 Asian Games softball competition is not simply about medals. Every result carries Olympic qualifying implications for the 2028 Los Angeles Games. Softball's return to the Olympic program at LA28 — after missing Tokyo 2020 (held 2021) and Paris 2024 — has elevated the stakes for every major international tournament leading up to the games.

For Japan, winning gold here would reinforce their position at the top of international softball. For Chinese Taipei, a strong finish validates their program's growth and secures crucial qualifying points. For China, continued success at the Asian Games level sends a message about their readiness on the global stage.

Even the Philippines, by making it this far, is writing a new chapter in their program's history and earning points toward a berth in Los Angeles.

What's Next

The Super Round continues through October 2 before the gold-medal Finals on October 3 at Anjō Sports Park. Based on the current trajectory, a Japan vs. Chinese Taipei gold-medal game appears increasingly likely — though China has the firepower to disrupt that narrative.
