Tennessee's Fall Ball is Free, and the Lady Vols Look Like a National Title Contender

By Emily Rawlings | September 30, 2026

Sophia Knight hit .394 last year, Mardjetko and Nuwer combined for 31 wins and 8 shutouts, and nine starters are coming back. Tennessee hosts Carson-Newman on Thursday, October 2 — free admission at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium.

If you are a softball fan in Knoxville — or within reasonable driving distance — you have no excuse to miss Thursday night. Tennessee's Lady Vols host Carson-Newman on October 2 at 6:00 PM at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium. Admission is free. And the team you will see is one of the better-positioned programs in the country heading into the 2027 season.

Tennessee has already played twice this fall — a September 25 opener against Walters State and a September 27 home game against Lipscomb. The Lady Vols used those games to work through rotations and give fall reps to their veteran-heavy roster. Thursday's Carson-Newman matchup continues that process, and for fans who haven't seen this team yet, it is the first real look at what Tennessee is building.

Sophia Knight and the Offensive Core

The most compelling name in Tennessee's returning lineup is Sophia Knight. She hit .394 in 2026 — the team's best batting average — and she is back for another season. Knight's combination of contact and power makes her one of the most dangerous returning hitters in the SEC and nationally. When an offense has a .394 hitter returning with experience in a high-pressure conference environment, that is a significant asset.

Nine starters are returning across the lineup for Tennessee. That level of continuity is rare and valuable — experienced players who know each other, know the system, and know what it feels like to compete at the SEC level entering fall camp is an enormous advantage over programs breaking in new contributors at multiple positions.

Mardjetko and Nuwer: One of the Nation's Best Pitching Duos

The pitching staff is where Tennessee may have the most legitimate national championship credential. Sage Mardjetko went 16-3 with a 1.26 ERA and 171 strikeouts in 2026. Erin Nuwer went 15-1 with a 1.01 ERA — one of the single-season ERA marks that stands as a program benchmark.

Combined: 31 wins and 8 shutouts in the 2026 regular season. That is not a number most programs reach with their entire pitching staff. Having two arms of that caliber, both returning with a full season of experience together, gives Tennessee a ceiling that only a handful of programs in the country can claim.

The question for fall is less about whether these two can still pitch at an elite level and more about how Tennessee develops depth behind them. Every program needs depth — and fall ball is where that development begins.

The 2027 Outlook

Tennessee swept through September 25 (Walters State) and September 27 (Lipscomb) and is building toward an October schedule that continues to ramp up. The Lady Vols are not using fall as a maintenance exercise — they are using it to get sharp, evaluate newcomers, and prepare for a 2027 SEC slate that will challenge them from opening weekend through conference tournament.

Texas won the 2026 national championship. The Lady Vols know that. The combination of nine returning starters, an elite pitching duo, and a .394 bat at the top of the order is not a team content to watch someone else hold the trophy next May.

What's Next

Tennessee hosts Carson-Newman on Thursday, October 2 at 6:00 PM at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium. Admission is free. The Lady Vols will continue fall competition through October before turning the page to spring preparation. For fans who want to see one of the nation's most complete teams in person before the pressure of the regular season arrives — Thursday night is the moment.
