PGF Nationals Week 2 Is Underway: 14U, 12U, and 10U Take the Stage in Orange County

By Emily Rawlings

July 27, 2026

Week 1 of the PGF Nationals crowned its champions. Now it is time for the younger divisions to have their moment.

Week 2 of the PGF Nationals opened July 26 in Orange County, California, with opening ceremonies held at Huntington Beach Central Park. The 14U, 12U, and 10U divisions now take center stage through August 2, with games spread across multiple venues in the Irvine and Huntington Beach area and all championship games scheduled for Deanna Manning Stadium at Bill Barber Park in Irvine.

All of the action is streaming live on PGF.TV.

The Biggest Stage in Youth Softball

The PGF Nationals is the premier youth softball tournament in the country. Hundreds of teams compete across age divisions and competitive levels each summer, representing programs from across the United States. For many players, it is the tournament of the year — the one that college coaches travel to watch, and the one that can define a player's recruiting exposure at a critical moment in their development.

Week 2 is no different. The 14U, 12U, and 10U divisions feature elite programs from across the country competing for national bragging rights at a venue that has hosted some of youth softball's most memorable moments.

Week 1 Champions Set the Bar

Week 1 of the PGF Nationals concluded with two memorable finishes in the premier divisions. Athletics Mercado won the 16U Premier title on a walk-off hit by Teuila Jaelynn Eteaki, while AZ Rising claimed the 18U Premier crown by run rule.

Both champions demonstrated the kind of clutch performance and program depth that defines elite youth softball at this level. The Week 2 teams will be looking to produce moments of their own.

The September 1 Connection

For families of players in the 14U division in particular, the PGF Nationals carries recruiting significance that extends beyond the tournament itself. September 1 — the opening of the Class of 2028 recruiting contact window — is just 36 days away, meaning college coaches are watching with genuine interest as these players compete on one of youth softball's biggest stages.

A strong performance at the PGF Nationals in the weeks leading up to September 1 can accelerate a player's recruiting timeline significantly.

What's Next

Week 2 runs through August 2, with championship games bringing everything to a close at Deanna Manning Stadium. Follow the tournament live on PGF.TV and track bracket results at pgfnationals.com.

For the families, coaches, and players competing this week in Orange County — this is the moment you have prepared for. Go play your game. 🥎
