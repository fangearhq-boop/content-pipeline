Utah Talons Win Back-to-Back AUSL Championships With Dominant Sweep

By Sarah Mitchell

July 27, 2026

The Utah Talons are the first back-to-back champions in AUSL history.

In front of a packed crowd at Davis Diamond on the campus of Texas A&M in College Station, Texas, the Talons swept the Chicago Bandits with a 6-1 victory in Game 2 on Sunday, completing the first championship sweep in league history and claiming their second straight title.

No team had ever defended an AUSL Championship before. The Talons have now done it — and done it decisively.

Fouts Delivers When It Matters Most

Montana Fouts, the 2026 AUSL Pitcher of the Year, made sure there was no doubt in Game 2. The Utah ace threw a complete game through seven innings, allowing just one run on eight hits while striking out four. It was her second career AUSL postseason complete game and a masterful performance on the sport's biggest stage.

Fouts finished the 2026 regular season with a 7-4 record, a 2.36 ERA, 63 strikeouts, and seven complete games across 61.1 innings — breaking four AUSL single-season records in the process. On Sunday, she validated every bit of that recognition.

Allchin Claims Series MVP

If Fouts provided the backbone, Jadelyn Allchin supplied the power. The Talons outfielder was named Championship Series MVP after a defining Game 2 performance: a two-run home run to put Utah in command, followed by an RBI double that extended the lead. She drove in three of Utah's six runs.

Bri Ellis — the 2026 Comeback Player of the Year — added a solo home run to help seal the win, capping what had been a breakout season for the outfielder who batted .353 with five home runs and a .450 on-base percentage during the regular year.

Game 1 Was No Less Impressive

Saturday's Game 1 was a tighter affair — a 2-1 Talons victory that set the tone for the series. Georgina Corrick was brilliant in the circle, throwing a complete game and allowing just one earned run on four hits while striking out five. Her performance in Game 1 retired 15 of the final 16 batters she faced.

The Talons' go-ahead run came on a sacrifice fly by Haley Jaquish in the sixth inning. Chicago starter Lexi Kilfoyl, who had gone 6-0 during the regular season, took the loss.

Ball-Malone Makes History

Head coach Cindy Ball-Malone becomes the first coach in AUSL history to win back-to-back championships. She has built the Talons into the standard by which all professional softball programs will now be measured — elite pitching, timely offense, and players who consistently perform when the pressure is highest.

What's Next

For the Talons, the offseason begins with a championship banner to hang and a legacy to defend. For the Chicago Bandits, Lexi Kilfoyl, Sami Williams, and the rest of the roster have proven they can compete at this level — and will be hungry to get back to College Station next summer.

The AUSL has now completed its second full season, and the growth of the league — on ESPN, with a historic ABC broadcast in Game 1 of last year's championship — has put professional softball on a trajectory that would have been hard to imagine just a few years ago.

The Talons are champions. And professional softball has never looked better.
