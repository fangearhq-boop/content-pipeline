2026 AUSL Year-End Awards: Williams, Atwood, Ellis and More Honored at Inaugural Reception

By Jordan Reeves

July 27, 2026

The 2026 AUSL season produced some of the best professional softball this country has ever seen — and the league's inaugural Awards Reception, held July 25 at Davis Diamond in College Station, Texas, gave those performances the recognition they deserve.

Here is a look at every award winner from the first-ever AUSL Awards Reception, along with the numbers behind their remarkable seasons.

Pitcher of the Year: Montana Fouts, Utah Talons

Fouts had a season for the history books: 7-4, 2.36 ERA, 63 strikeouts, seven complete games, and 61.1 innings pitched. She broke four AUSL single-season records — innings pitched, strikeouts, wins, and complete games — and set the single-game K record with 12 strikeouts against the Texas Volts on June 18. Opposing batters hit just .200 against her all season. She was named to the All-AUSL First Team and validated the award by throwing a complete game in the championship-clinching Game 2 the day after the reception.

Hitter of the Year: Sami Williams, Chicago Bandits

Williams put together one of the most complete offensive seasons in AUSL history. She batted .427 on the season, tied for the league lead with 32 hits, and led the entire league with 34 RBI. The Bandits infielder was a consistent force at the plate all season and a primary reason Chicago advanced to the Championship Series.

Rookie of the Year: Reese Atwood, Carolina Blaze

Atwood was the No. 7 overall pick in the 2026 AUSL College Draft and made a strong case that she was worth every bit of that selection. She batted .309 with eight home runs and 25 RBI in her first professional season, leading the Blaze in home runs and establishing herself as one of the most promising young players in the league. A bright future awaits.

Comeback Player of the Year: Bri Ellis, Utah Talons

After a difficult start to her professional career, Ellis made 2026 the year she announced herself. She batted .353 with five home runs and a .450 on-base percentage, ranking fifth in the league in batting average. The outfielder also hit a solo home run in the championship-clinching Game 2 of the AUSL Championship Series, adding an exclamation point to a season that will define her career going forward.

AUSL Impact Award: Odicci Alexander-Bennett, Chicago Bandits

The Impact Award is voted on by AUSL players themselves — making it one of the most meaningful honors in the league. Alexander-Bennett was recognized for her outstanding leadership and service off the field, earning the respect of her peers throughout the 2026 season.

All-AUSL Team and All-Defensive Team

The AUSL also announced its first-ever All-AUSL Team and inaugural All-Defensive Team at the reception, with Jadelyn Allchin among those honored on the All-AUSL squad. The Defensive Team recognizes players who set the standard in the field, continuing the league's emphasis on recognizing complete players.

What's Next

With the 2026 season now complete and the Utah Talons crowned as back-to-back champions, the AUSL turns its attention toward the offseason and the 2027 campaign. The awards announced this week set a high bar — and every player in the league will be working this winter to raise their game and stake a claim to recognition of their own next summer.

Professional softball has never had a season quite like 2026. And the women honored at this week's reception were the reason why.
