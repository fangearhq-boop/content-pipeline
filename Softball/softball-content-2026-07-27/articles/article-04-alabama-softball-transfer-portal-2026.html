Alabama Softball's Real Transfer Portal Class: Ford, Plumlee, and Livingstone

By Sarah Mitchell

July 27, 2026

Several high-profile names have been incorrectly linked to Alabama softball's transfer class for 2026-27, so let's set the record straight.

Two players who have appeared in reports connected to the Crimson Tide — Jaysoni Beachum from Florida State and Tori Edwards from LSU — will not be playing in Tuscaloosa next season. Beachum, a two-time All-American who batted .406 with 10 home runs and 69 RBI in 2026, committed to Texas Tech. Edwards, the 2025 SEC Freshman of the Year who hit .284 with 12 home runs and 45 RBI in her sophomore season at LSU, committed to the Florida Gators.

Alabama head coach Patrick Murphy did add three transfers to the Crimson Tide roster — and each brings something the program genuinely needed.

Karlee Ford, Catcher (Iowa State)

Ford is the marquee addition of Alabama's portal class. The catcher from Iowa State batted .345 with 10 home runs and 49 RBI in 2026, earning All-Big 12 Second Team recognition. She was also named to the All-Big 12 Freshman Team in 2025, establishing herself as one of the better all-around catchers in the country over two college seasons.

Behind the plate, Ford brings a .983 fielding percentage and a 42% caught stealing rate — numbers that will immediately raise the standard for Alabama defensively. She steps into a competition for the starting catcher job vacated by outgoing senior captain Marlie Giles.

Kailey Plumlee, Pitcher (Tennessee)

Plumlee is a left-handed pitcher who crosses conference lines from SEC rival Tennessee. She was Alabama's first portal addition of the cycle and arrives with the pedigree of someone who has competed in the toughest conference in college softball throughout her career. Details on her Tennessee stats were not immediately available, but left-handed pitching depth is a consistent priority for competitive SEC programs.

Noelani Livingstone, Outfielder (JUCO)

Livingstone is Alabama's lone junior college pickup and perhaps the most intriguing addition of the three. She won the 2026 NJCAA Player of the Year award, the highest individual honor in junior college softball, and arrives in Tuscaloosa with proven production and immediate-impact potential. Junior college transfers who earn national recognition at the NJCAA level are often capable of making a meaningful contribution right away at the Division I level.

What's Next

Alabama graduated seven seniors following the 2026 season — Abby Duchscherer, Marlie Giles, Alea Johnson, Kinley Pate, Larissa Preuitt, Alexis Pupillo, and Kristen White. That is a significant amount of experience to replace, and Murphy's portal class addresses specific needs in the catcher's box, the circle, and the outfield.

The Class of 2028 recruiting contact window opens September 1 — just 36 days from now. By then, the Crimson Tide will have a clearer picture of the full 2027 roster as they build toward a program that annually competes for SEC titles and postseason runs.
