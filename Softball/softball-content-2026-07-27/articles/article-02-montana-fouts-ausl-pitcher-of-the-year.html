Montana Fouts Named 2026 AUSL Pitcher of the Year After Record-Breaking Season

By Emily Rawlings

July 27, 2026

Montana Fouts did not just have a good professional softball season in 2026. She rewrote the record book.

The Utah Talons ace was named the 2026 AUSL Pitcher of the Year at the league's inaugural Awards Reception on July 25 in College Station, Texas — and then went out and threw a complete game in the championship-clinching Game 2 on Sunday to confirm every vote she received.

The Numbers Tell the Story

Fouts finished the 2026 regular season 7-4 with a 2.36 ERA, 63 strikeouts, seven complete games, and 61.1 innings pitched. In doing so, she broke four AUSL single-season records: innings pitched, strikeouts, wins, and complete games.

All four. In one season. For a league that prides itself on elite-level pitching, that is a statement that does not need much elaboration.

She also set the AUSL single-game strikeout record on June 18, fanning 12 Texas Volts hitters in a dominant performance. She followed that with a complete-game shutout against the Carolina Blaze on June 26, winning 3-0. For much of the season, opposing batters were simply overwhelmed — they batted a collective .200 against her.

Leading the League in What Matters

Fouts led the AUSL in complete games with seven — a category that reflects not just talent, but durability, command, and the kind of competitive presence that allows a pitcher to finish what she starts. She was named to the All-AUSL First Team alongside some of the league's best performers.

In a league where the talent level is rising rapidly, Fouts managed to separate herself through consistency. She won more games, threw more innings, and struck out more batters than any pitcher in AUSL history over the course of a single season.

A Championship Performance to Seal It

The Pitcher of the Year award was announced the night before Game 1, but Fouts made sure her performance on the field matched the recognition. In Game 2 of the AUSL Championship Series — a 6-1 Utah victory — she went the distance, allowing just one earned run on eight hits while striking out four and guiding the Talons to their second consecutive title.

It was a fitting end to a season that will be remembered for a long time in the history of the sport.

What's Next

Fouts is a two-time AUSL champion and the undisputed ace of the best team in professional softball. As the league continues to grow and the talent pool deepens, the bar she has set in 2026 — four records, a championship, and the Pitcher of the Year award — will be the standard that future pitchers chase.

She is 26 years old and, by every available measure, still getting better.
