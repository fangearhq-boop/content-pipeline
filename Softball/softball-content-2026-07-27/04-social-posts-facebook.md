### STORY 1: Utah Talons Win 2026 AUSL Championship Sweep

**POST S1-FB-LONGFORM** | 9:00 AM ET
The Utah Talons are back-to-back AUSL Champions.

In what may go down as one of the defining moments in professional softball history, the Talons swept the Chicago Bandits on Sunday at Davis Diamond in College Station, TX — winning Game 2 by a score of 6-1 to close out the series in just two games.

No team had ever won back-to-back AUSL Championships before. The Talons have now done it.

Montana Fouts was dominant in Game 2, throwing a complete game through seven innings and allowing just one run on eight hits while striking out four. It was her second career AUSL postseason complete game.

Jadelyn Allchin was named Championship Series MVP after a performance for the ages in Game 2. She launched a two-run home run and added an RBI double, driving in three of Utah's six runs. Bri Ellis added a solo home run to put the game away.

Game 1, played on Saturday, was a tighter 2-1 victory for Utah. Georgina Corrick threw a complete game, allowing just one earned run on four hits while striking out five. It was gritty, efficient, and exactly what a championship pitcher looks like.

Head coach Cindy Ball-Malone becomes the first coach in AUSL history to win back-to-back titles. And this team did it with a roster built around elite pitching and clutch performers who stepped up when the lights were brightest.

This is the moment the Utah Talons were built for. 🏆 #ILoveSoftball #AUSL #UtahTalons

### STORY 2: Montana Fouts — 2026 AUSL Pitcher of the Year

**POST S2-FB-LONGFORM** | 11:00 AM ET
Montana Fouts put together one of the most dominant pitching seasons in professional softball history in 2026.

The Utah Talons ace finished 7-4 with a 2.36 ERA, 63 strikeouts, and 7 complete games across 61.1 innings pitched — and broke FOUR AUSL single-season records along the way. That's innings pitched, strikeouts, wins, and complete games — all records, all in one season.

She was named the 2026 AUSL Pitcher of the Year at the league's inaugural Awards Reception on July 25 in College Station, TX. And then, as if to put an exclamation point on the honor, she went out and threw a complete game in the clinching Game 2 of the AUSL Championship Series.

Some highlights from her 2026 regular season:
• Set the AUSL single-game strikeout record: 12 Ks vs. Texas Volts on June 18
• Threw a complete-game shutout vs. Carolina Blaze on June 26 (3-0 win)
• Opponents batted just .200 against her all season
• Led the league in complete games with 7
• Was named to the All-AUSL First Team

She's a two-time champion now. And she's still getting better.

### STORY 4: Alabama Softball Transfer Portal

**POST S4-FB-LONGFORM** | 3:00 PM ET
There has been some confusion about Alabama softball's transfer portal class for 2026-27, and we want to set the record straight.

A couple of names have been incorrectly linked to the Crimson Tide:
• Jaysoni Beachum (FSU) — she committed to Texas Tech, not Alabama. Two-time All-American with a .406 batting average, 10 HR, and 69 RBI in 2026.
• Tori Edwards (LSU) — she committed to the Florida Gators, not Alabama. The 2025 SEC Freshman of the Year hit .284 with 12 HR and 45 RBI in 2026.

Here are Alabama's ACTUAL three transfer additions:

✅ Karlee Ford (Iowa State) — Catcher. .345 BA, 10 HR, 49 RBI. All-Big 12 Second Team in 2026, All-Big 12 Freshman in 2025. Brings a .983 fielding percentage and a 42% caught stealing rate to Tuscaloosa.
✅ Kailey Plumlee (Tennessee) — Left-handed pitcher. Alabama's first portal addition, crossing conference lines from an SEC rival.
✅ Noelani Livingstone — Outfielder from the JUCO ranks. Won the 2026 NJCAA Player of the Year award and brings immediate impact potential from the junior college level.

Alabama lost seven graduating seniors this offseason, and head coach Patrick Murphy has put together a portal class that addresses real needs — especially behind the plate and in the circle.

September 1, the opening of the Class of 2028 recruiting contact window, is 36 days away.

### STORY 5: PGF Nationals Week 2
