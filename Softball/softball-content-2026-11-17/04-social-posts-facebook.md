### STORY 1: January Preseason Poll Race

**Long-Form Post (9:00 AM ET)**

Signing Day answered one question and created another.

D1Softball said Oklahoma. Softball America said LSU. Two No. 1 rankings, two different methodologies, two different stories about what makes a great recruiting class.

The next debate starts in January, when the NFCA, D1Softball, USA TODAY, and ESPN release their preseason Top 25 polls — the ones that actually set the tone for the season.

Oklahoma's case is built on experience and elite talent. Julianna "Goose" Hutchens — the No. 1 overall recruit — is already in Norman. Kelsi Wells (39 home runs, NFCA Freshman of the Year) and Jocelyn Kavan (two-time WCWS MVP) return to anchor the lineup. Five-star outfielder Finlee Williams and five-star catcher Riley Hilliard are the incoming additions. Patty Gasso has built for now and for later simultaneously.

LSU's case is built on historic achievement. Beth Torina's class claimed the No. 1 spot in Softball America for the first time in program history. Five-star pitcher/utility Sydney Gonglik (No. 1 overall, SA) and four-star pitcher Macie Bryant arrive to give LSU an elite pitching corps. The Tigers went into Signing Day with the program's highest recruiting ceiling ever — and cleared it.

Tennessee is the dark horse in this conversation. The Vols return the deepest pitching staff in the SEC — possibly in the country — and their class ranked No. 5-6 nationally. Senior Kelsey Mardjetko (16-3, 1.26 ERA) and junior Nuwer (4 shutouts) return to a staff that head coach Karen Weekly has built into an elite unit with a 2030 contract extension.

Who gets your No. 1 vote? Drop your preseason pick in the comments.

### STORY 2: 5-for-5 Clock Ticking

**Long-Form Post (11:00 AM ET)**

Fourteen days. That is how long athletes blocked by the NCAA's 5-for-5 rule have to decide what comes next.

The Tenth Circuit reversed the preliminary injunction in Wisne v. NCAA on November 6. The federal class action protection that had been shielding Class of 2022 athletes from the age-based eligibility rule is gone. Athletes who had been practicing, competing, and planning their fifth year on the belief that federal courts would protect them are now subject to the rule as written — and the December portal opens in 14 days.

The situation is complicated by the House v. NCAA settlement's 25-player roster cap. Programs carrying athletes who can be rostered under 5-for-5 but cannot practice or play in games are holding roster spots that could otherwise go to contributors. That math is forcing conversations between coaches and athletes that nobody wants to have.

Kentucky head coach Rachel Lawson called the unfolding situation one with "casualties." She was right. These are real athletes with real scholarships and real college softball journeys caught in a legal dispute that may not be resolved before they age out of eligibility entirely.

December 1 is 14 days away. The clock is running.

### STORY 3: Arizona in December

**Long-Form Post (1:00 PM ET)**

Caitlin Lowe's first fall at Arizona was exceptional. The Wildcats went 15-2. Big 12 DPOY Regan Shockey returns. Ten letterwinners are back. The program has real talent.

But there is one hole that fall ball did not answer: who pitches?

Marissa Torres — the D1Softball National Player of the Year — transferred to Texas Tech in the summer portal. She was Arizona's ace, its front-line pitcher, the reason Wildcat opponents had to build a game plan. She is gone. And her departure left a vacancy that four fall transfer additions did not fully address.

December 1 is when the portal opens. For Caitlin Lowe — a former national champion player who understands exactly what it takes to win at the highest level — this window is the first real test of her program-building instincts.

She can compete at the highest level with what she has. But to reach the WCWS from the new, more competitive Big 12, she needs an ace. What she finds — or does not find — in the December portal sets Arizona's 2027 ceiling.

### STORY 4: Texas Tech 2027

**Long-Form Post (3:00 PM ET)**

Here is a number worth memorizing: 50.

That is how many home runs Kasidi Pickering hit across three seasons at Oklahoma. She also batted .384, drove in 169 runs, and earned NFCA First-Team All-American recognition. She was arguably the most feared bat in college softball.

She signed with Texas Tech in June.

Eric Glasco's program already had the No. 1 transfer class nationally per Softball America, adding Bradi Gallaway, Jayden Heavener, and Moriah Polar to complement returning starters. He signed Jocelyn Gentry — the first five-star freshman in program history. And then Kasidi Pickering.

The Big 12 opens March 12. When Texas Tech steps onto the field for conference play, they will do so as a program that reached the WCWS finals for the first time in recent memory and then immediately upgraded its roster.

The question for the Red Raiders entering 2027 is not whether they are good enough to compete. They clearly are. The question is whether Lubbock is ready to be a national championship destination.

Based on everything we can see right now, the answer appears to be yes.

### STORY 5: 2027 Season Calendar

**Long-Form Post (8:00 PM ET)**

The offseason is not quiet. It never is — but especially not this year.

Here is the calendar every college softball fan should be tracking for the next 90 days.

December 1-15: Transfer Portal Opens.
This year's window is unlike any previous one. 5-for-5 enforcement after the Tenth Circuit's November 6 reversal is pushing affected athletes toward the portal. Arizona, FSU, and Ole Miss are all actively seeking pitching. The window closes December 15.

January: Preseason Polls and Schedule Releases.
The NFCA, D1Softball, USA TODAY, and ESPN will release their preseason Top 25 rankings. Oklahoma vs. LSU for No. 1 is the storyline. Conference schedules also drop, giving fans the first look at when the marquee Big 12 matchups occur.

Mid-January to Early February: Spring Practices Begin.
Full roster preparation begins. New transfers meet returning cores. Spring lineups take shape.

March 12: Big 12 Conference Play Opens.
Texas Tech vs. the Big 12 field — with Kasidi Pickering in the lineup for the first time. OU, Arizona, Texas. The most competitive conference in college softball history opens for business.

Stay tuned. This offseason has a long way to go.
