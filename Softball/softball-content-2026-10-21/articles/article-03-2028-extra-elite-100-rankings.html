Class of 2028 Extra Elite 100 Rankings Drop This Week — What to Know About College Softball's Most Anticipated List

By Jordan Reeves | October 21, 2026

College softball recruiting is about to get a major jolt. Extra Inning Softball is in the final stage of releasing its Class of 2028 Extra Elite 100 national player rankings, with the window closing tomorrow and the all-important top-5 reveal coming on October 27.

This is the most prestigious individual recruiting ranking in the sport — a single list that every elite prospect, their families, and every major program tracks closely. And this year's version may be the most significant one yet.

A Historic Class

Extra Inning Softball evaluated more than 1,200 players for the Class of 2028 rankings — the largest and most competitive pool in the organization's history. The breadth of the class reflects how dramatically the recruiting landscape has shifted over the past two years, with contact windows opening up opportunities for programs to identify talent as early as the eighth grade.

The rankings have been released in tiers throughout October, covering players ranked No. 100 through No. 6. The final tier — the top five — drops October 27. The Extra Select lists covering players ranked No. 101 through No. 200 will follow on October 23–26, and Extra Prospect lists (No. 201–300) are scheduled for October 27–31.

The Names at the Top

The national rankings conversation has centered on three names in recent weeks. Michaela Landers, an infielder committed to Texas, holds the No. 1 spot in the Rivals and On3 player rankings as of early October. Kylie Holden, a five-star infielder from Michigan who committed to Florida, sits at No. 2 in that same system. Sophia Schlader, committed to Oklahoma, ranks third by Rivals and is listed No. 1 by Prep Softball's methodology.

Different ranking systems use different evaluation criteria, which means the final Extra Inning Softball list may not mirror Rivals or On3 exactly. That tension — and the potential for an unexpected No. 1 — is part of what makes October 27 such an appointment for recruiting followers.

The Team Race

On the team side, the Rivals and On3 Class of 2028 recruiting rankings as of mid-October show Florida holding the top spot, followed by Oklahoma at No. 2 and Texas Tech at No. 3. Texas and Tennessee round out the top five in various tabulations.

Those rankings are a snapshot in motion. The Class of 2028 contact window closed October 18, but commitments have continued rolling in. More than 1,000 players in this class committed since September 1 alone. The team rankings will be updated as verbal commitments are made official and more prospects announce their decisions.

What's Next

Mark October 27 on your calendar: that is when Extra Inning Softball reveals the top-5 of the Class of 2028 Extra Elite 100. Between now and then, the Extra Select position-by-position rankings (#101–200) drop daily from October 23–26. For a real-time look at where prospects stand, follow extrainningsoftball.com and the Rivals softball recruiting tracker for updates through the end of the month.
