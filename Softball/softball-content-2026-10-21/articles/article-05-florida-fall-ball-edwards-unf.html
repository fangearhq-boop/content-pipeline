Tori Edwards Is Blazing in Fall Ball — Florida Sets Up UNF on Thursday

By Emily Rawlings | October 21, 2026

Tori Edwards has wasted no time making an impression in Gainesville. The Florida transfer slugger hit 3 home runs in the Gators' first two fall ball games, putting her offensive tools on immediate display as the program builds toward 2027.

Florida opened fall ball with a 20-0 rout of Santa Fe College on October 16, with Edwards connecting for 2 home runs in back-to-back at-bats in her first fall game as a Gator. Two days later, in a 29-1 win over Carson-Newman, she added a third home run in the third inning. The Gators are 2-0 heading into their next exhibition, scheduled for Thursday, October 23, against UNF at Katie Seashole Pressly Stadium. Admission is free.

More Than One New Face

Edwards has been the most talked-about newcomer in these early fall outings, but she is not the only fresh face generating buzz. Freshman pitcher Caroline Stanton made her Florida debut in the Santa Fe game and delivered five hitless innings — a debut that raised eyebrows among those watching the program's 2027 pitching picture.

The Gators return six starters from the 2026 roster and bring back their full pitching staff, making this one of the more experienced and deep lineups in the country heading into next spring. The early fall performances have added two new pieces to what was already a loaded foundation.

Building Toward March

The scorelines in fall ball — 20-0 and 29-1 — tell only part of the story. Fall exhibitions against smaller programs are about evaluation, repetition, and role definition, not scoreboard results. Florida's coaching staff has used these games to see how the returning group meshes with incoming players and to push new additions into real game situations.

Thursday's game against UNF will test the Gators against slightly more competitive opposition and provide another data point before the fall schedule wraps up. Florida's full eight-game fall slate continues through the month, with each game free and open to the public at Katie Seashole Pressly Stadium.

The Recruiting Picture

Florida enters the 2027 season holding the No. 1-ranked Class of 2028 recruiting class, according to Rivals and On3 team rankings. Five-star infielder Kylie Holden, ranked No. 2 nationally in the class, is already committed to the program. Combine that with the Edwards arrival, Stanton's debut, and a six-starter returning core, and the Gators are clearly positioned to compete for a national title the moment the spring season opens.

What's Next

Florida hosts UNF this Thursday, October 23, at Katie Seashole Pressly Stadium. First pitch is free and open to the public. The Gators will continue their eight-game fall schedule through the rest of October. After fall ball wraps, attention turns to offseason preparation and the 2027 season, where Florida will be one of the teams to beat in every conversation about national title contenders.
