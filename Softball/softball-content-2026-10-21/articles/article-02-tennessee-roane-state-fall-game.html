Tennessee Hosts Roane State Today in Rescheduled Fall Game — Lee University Finale Follows Thursday

By Emily Rawlings | October 21, 2026

The Tennessee Lady Vols are back at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium today for a rescheduled fall ball game against Roane State. First pitch is set for 6 PM ET, and admission is free for all fans. Gates open at 5 PM ET.

The game was originally scheduled for October 10 but was canceled due to weather. The rescheduled Wednesday contest gives the Lady Vols one more competitive repetition before the fall schedule closes out with the Lee University finale on Thursday, October 23, also at 6 PM ET and also free to the public.

3-0 and Dominant

Tennessee enters Wednesday's game at 3-0 in fall ball, with their most recent outing — a 19-1 rout of Carson-Newman — serving as the latest evidence of just how deep this program is right now. Head coach Karen Weekly has used the phrase "deepest staff in program history" to describe the 2027 Lady Vols, and the statistics give that claim real substance.

Karlyn Pickens, the No. 1 overall pick in the 2026 Athletes Unlimited Softball League draft, has racked up 194 strikeouts with a 1.70 ERA. Kelly Mardjetko went 16-3 with a 1.26 ERA this past season and earned NFCA All-American recognition. Having both healthy and engaged heading into spring 2027 is one of the more significant competitive advantages any program in the country can claim.

Clarke's Cycle, and What Comes Next

The signature moment of Tennessee's fall has been freshman Emma Clarke hitting for the cycle — a rare feat that showed the kind of all-around offensive upside this freshman class is bringing to Rocky Top. Clarke's cycle added a memorable chapter to a fall that has also produced strong results across the lineup.

The focus now shifts to consistency. Fall games against programs like Roane State and Lee University serve a specific purpose: everyone gets reps, depth players get evaluated, and the staff works through mechanical adjustments without the pressure of the regular season. Wednesday and Thursday provide the final two data points of Tennessee's fall evaluation window.

Free Fall Ball Details

Both remaining games are free of charge at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium in Knoxville. Restrooms will be open, but concessions will not be available. Fans should plan accordingly — and arrive ready to see one of the most talented rosters in college softball in an unusually intimate setting.

What's Next

Tennessee's fall schedule concludes Thursday, October 23, against Lee University at 6 PM ET at Sherri Parker Lee Stadium. After that, the Lady Vols shift into full offseason preparation mode, with eyes squarely on the 2027 season and a program that believes it has everything it needs to win a national championship. For Tennessee fans, this week represents the last free public look at this roster until spring.
