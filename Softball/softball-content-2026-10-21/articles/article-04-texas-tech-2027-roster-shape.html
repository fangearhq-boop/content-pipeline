Texas Tech's 2027 Roster Is Taking Shape — And It Looks Like a Championship Contender

By Sarah Mitchell | October 21, 2026

Now that Kasidi Pickering has officially signed with Texas Tech, the full picture of the Red Raiders' 2027 roster is coming into view — and Gerry Glasco's program appears set to be one of the most dangerous teams in college softball when March arrives.

The portal class alone would make headlines at any program. Pickering, who hit .384 with 50 home runs and 169 RBI over three seasons at Oklahoma, is the headliner. Jaysoni Beachum, a Florida State infielder, joins the class alongside Moriah Polar, Jayden Heavener, Hailey Maestretti, Zoie Bernard, and Bradi Gallaway. Multiple analysts have ranked this the No. 1 transfer portal class in the country.

The Returning Core

What makes the portal haul even more impactful is the group it is joining. Mia Williams, who set the Texas Tech program record with 27 home runs in 2026, returns as the team's primary offensive anchor. Kaitlyn Terry — the program's lone first-team All-American in 2026 — provides another anchor in a lineup that is quickly becoming one of the deepest in the sport.

Texas Tech also has pitching continuity heading into 2027. The one notable departure is Samantha Lincoln, a left-handed pitcher who went 7-0 with a 3.47 ERA before transferring to Texas after the season. That is a real loss, but the Red Raiders have the pieces to absorb it.

A New Staff with Proven Credentials

The coaching changes have been just as significant as the roster moves. Head coach Glasco brought in Jeff Cottrill and Shelby Pendley in July 2026. Cottrill comes with a hitting coach background at Houston, Missouri, and Oklahoma State, while Pendley carries the kind of playing resume that commands immediate respect in any Big 12 locker room: a two-time Big 12 Player of the Year and a member of Oklahoma's 2013 national championship team.

Associate head coach Tara Archibald rounds out a staff that is positioned to not just recruit at an elite level — which Glasco has proven he can do — but also to develop the talent once it arrives.

The Context

Texas Tech reached the WCWS championship series in 2026, falling short of a national title but establishing a clear identity as a program ready to compete at the highest level. The decisions made this offseason — from the coaching hires to the portal class — suggest Glasco is not interested in moral victories. The Red Raiders open the 2027 Big 12 season on March 12.

What's Next

The next major roster development to watch: how the portal class integrates with the returning players during the preseason. Pickering, Beachum, and the rest of the new additions will need to find their footing in a new system quickly. If they do, Texas Tech enters 2027 as a legitimate national championship contender. Watch for the Red Raiders in early-season tournaments starting in February, where the full picture of this roster will come into sharper focus.
