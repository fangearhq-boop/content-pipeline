OU Battle Series Game 4 Preview: Sooners Back at Love's Field Tonight

By Sarah Mitchell | October 21, 2026

Oklahoma softball returns to Love's Field on Wednesday evening for Battle Series Game 4, the fourth of five fall exhibitions in Norman. First pitch is set for 6:30 PM CT, with the game streaming live on 1oklahoma.com — no cable subscription required.

The 2026 fall series has already delivered on its promise. More than 2,300 fans turned out for Game 1 in late September, and the Sooners gave them plenty to talk about, including back-to-back home runs from Gabbie Garcia and Kendall Wells in the seventh inning. That energy has carried through each outing since.

The Names to Watch

Sophia Kavan remains the headliner. The two-time Women's College World Series Most Outstanding Player is putting the final touches on a fall that has showcased her versatility both at the plate and behind it. For fans who want to see the player OU has built its championship identity around, tonight is the night.

Kendall Wells has arguably been the most electrifying story of the fall. The freshman power hitter blasted 39 home runs last spring — a number that earned her NFCA Freshman of the Year honors — and she has continued hitting for power through every fall rep. When Wells steps in, the atmosphere changes.

Nicole Berzon anchors the pitching circle for the Sooners. The right-hander has been consistent throughout fall ball, and head coach Patty Gasso will look to see her build on that momentum heading into spring preparation.

The Final Push

Game 4 is the penultimate fall outing before the Battle Series wraps Thursday, October 23, against the University of Science and Arts of Oklahoma. With just two games left on the fall schedule, the coaching staff is prioritizing meaningful reps and roster evaluation over everything else.

Fall ball is not the regular season — there are no standings, no rankings implications, no conference points on the line. What it does provide is a live look at how the pieces fit together, who has developed over the offseason, and what questions remain heading into February. Tonight's game is another data point in that process.

The Sooners return a core that went deep into the 2026 postseason, and the transfer portal additions have raised expectations in Norman even further. Gasso rarely lets the outside noise change the program's internal standards, but it is hard to look at this roster and not feel the weight of the moment.

How to Watch

Battle Series Game 4 begins at 6:30 PM CT Wednesday at Love's Field in Norman, Oklahoma. Live stream access is available on 1oklahoma.com. There is no traditional television or radio broadcast for the fall exhibition.

What's Next

Battle Series Game 5 — the final fall exhibition — takes place Thursday, October 23, at Love's Field against USAO. After that, the Sooners turn their full attention to offseason preparation and the spring 2027 season. The next time fans can see Oklahoma in a competitive setting will be in February, when the season opens.
