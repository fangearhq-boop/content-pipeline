Eight Days to Change Your Rotation: The December Portal Pitching Race Is On

By Jordan Reeves | I Love Softball | November 23, 2026

Eight days. That is the operative number heading into softball's most consequential recruiting window of the year. The December transfer portal opens Dec. 1, closes Dec. 15, and contains a four-day dead period (Dec. 9-12) in the middle. For programs that need elite pitching — and there are several — the window to find, contact, and lock up an arm runs from Dec. 1 through Dec. 8. Eight days.

No position is more valuable in this window. No commodity is rarer. The programs that identify their targets now, build relationships before the portal opens, and move decisively in that first week of December are the ones that emerge with rotation-changing additions. The teams that wait for the market to develop lose.

Who Needs an Arm

Arizona is the most urgent case. The Wildcats went 15-2 in fall scrimmages under first-year coach Caitlin Lowe — but they did it without pitcher Isa Torres, who transferred to Texas Tech. Torres was one of the top arms in college softball before departing. Arizona has the defense, the experience, and the system. What they don't have is a true front-of-rotation starter to build around. December is their moment to fix that.

Ole Miss and Auburn are right behind them. Both programs want to push into the top tier of the SEC in 2027, and both have identified pitching as the ceiling variable for their rosters. LSU's No. 1 recruiting class and Oklahoma's depth mean the SEC's elite is well-stocked. The teams trying to close the gap need to come out of December with an arm that changes their trajectory.

Saylor Davis and the Portal Market

Oklahoma State pitcher Saylor Davis is reportedly entering the portal on Dec. 1 with three years of eligibility remaining. That availability — three full seasons, not a graduate transfer's one final year — is the kind of rare find that generates serious competition. Programs across the country are aware of her timeline, and multiple programs figure to be in contact immediately when the portal opens.

Davis is exactly what every pitching-needy program is looking for: young enough to represent a long-term building block, experienced enough to contribute immediately, and entering a window where top-tier pitching is in short supply. The program that lands her is not just solving a 2027 problem. It is making a 2028 and 2029 investment as well.

The Structure of the Window

The dead period creates a hard psychological deadline that many coaches and recruits underestimate. When the Dec. 9-12 blackout hits, a prospect who has not received an offer and committed is suddenly in a four-day communication vacuum. Some handle that well. Others don't. Programs that have built the relationship, made the offer, and gotten to yes before Dec. 9 aren't affected by the blackout. Everyone else is.

This means the most important conversations aren't happening Dec. 1. They're happening right now. Teams that reach out today and Monday — the last two days before the NCAA recruiting shutdown closes communication entirely for five days starting Tuesday — are building the foundation for Dec. 1 urgency. The portal window rewards preparation made weeks in advance.

What's Next

The recruiting shutdown begins Tuesday, Nov. 25, and runs through Nov. 29. The portal opens Dec. 1. Arizona, Ole Miss, Auburn, and several other programs figure to be aggressive from the opening bell. The first week of December will produce the portal's most significant commitments, and the landscape of the 2027 SEC and Big 12 pitching races will largely be settled by Dec. 8. Programs watching from the outside have six days left to get ready.
