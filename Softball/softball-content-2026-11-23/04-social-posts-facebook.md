### STORY 1: Florida No. 2 Class — Allaire, Calvo

**Long-Form Post (11:00 AM ET)**

Florida fans have been waiting a long time. This class is the best evidence yet that the wait might not be much longer.

### STORY 2: December Portal Countdown

**Long-Form Post (9:00 AM ET)**

The December transfer portal opens in eight days. And the race for elite pitching is already heating up.

Arizona had one of the best fall records in the country — 15-2 — but they lost pitcher Isa Torres to Texas Tech. Without a true rotation anchor, they're the most motivated buyer in December's market. Ole Miss and Auburn are right behind them.

Oklahoma State's Saylor Davis is reportedly planning to enter the portal on Dec. 1 with three years of eligibility remaining. That kind of availability — a pitcher with three full years ahead — is rare in December windows that typically feature graduate transfers or one-year players.

Here's the structural challenge: the portal opens Dec. 1, but a dead period runs Dec. 9-12. That means coaches have exactly eight days to identify, contact, and lock up their targets before a four-day communication blackout hits. Miss that window and you're waiting for the summer portal.

The teams that win December are the ones who've done the prep work now. Relationships built in November become commitments in the first week of December.

### STORY 3: Recruiting Shutdown Countdown

**Long-Form Post (1:00 PM ET)**

Coaches, check your calendars.

The NCAA softball recruiting shutdown begins Tuesday, November 25 — and it runs through Saturday, November 29. During those five days, coaches cannot call, text, email, or otherwise contact any recruit. This is stricter than a dead period: even written correspondence is prohibited.

Today and Monday are the last unrestricted contact days before that blackout.

What does that mean practically? If a coach has an uncommitted 2027 recruit she's been building a relationship with, now is the moment to make the final push. If a staff has a portal target they've been in contact with ahead of the Dec. 1 opening, the conversations happening in the next 48 hours may be the most important ones of the offseason.

The sequence that follows is unforgiving: shutdown through Nov. 29, then Dec. 1 portal opens, then dead period Dec. 9-12. There is no clean stretch of uninterrupted recruiting time between now and December. Every contact matters.

For recruits still weighing options: expect your phone to be more active today and tomorrow than it's been in weeks. And then very quiet for five days after that.

### STORY 5: Bre Warren Ends Eligibility Fight

**Long-Form Post (5:30 PM ET)**

Bre Warren's fight for a sixth year of softball is over.

The Virginia Tech outfielder and South Carolina graduate transfer filed for a preliminary injunction in Montgomery County Circuit Court after the NCAA denied her eligibility waiver. The court denied the injunction. Now, according to Ground News, Warren will not seek a permanent injunction.

She was on VT's roster this season. She just never got to play.

Warren's case was one of the more visible individual eligibility fights in a year defined by them. The Tenth Circuit reversed the federal five-for-five injunction in November — which closed the federal door for the class of 2022. State courts became the primary avenue, and Warren pursued one of those paths.

She didn't get what she was fighting for. But 200+ cases involving 850 athletes are still active in state courts. The NCAA's eligibility rules remain contested, and the fight for a legislative fix in Congress is ongoing. Warren's chapter is closing. The larger story isn't.
