### STORY 3: Recruiting Shutdown Nov. 25-29

**Sources:**
- NCAA 2026-27 D1 Women's Softball Recruiting Calendar: https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/compliance/recruiting/calendar/2026-27/2026-27D1Rec_WSBRecruitingCalendar.pdf
- NCSA Sports: https://www.ncsasports.org/ncaa-eligibility-center/recruiting-rules/ncaa-recruiting-shutdown

**Verified Facts (HIGH confidence):**
- Softball recruiting shutdown: Nov. 25-29, 2026
- During a shutdown, coaches cannot call, text, or email any recruit
- A shutdown is stricter than a dead period
- During a dead period, written communication is allowed; during a shutdown it is not
- The shutdown period is a restriction on institutions, not on players

**Verified Facts (MEDIUM confidence):**
- After the Nov. 25-29 shutdown ends (Nov. 30), the Dec. 1 portal window opens
- Dec. 9-12 dead period follows the opening of the portal window
