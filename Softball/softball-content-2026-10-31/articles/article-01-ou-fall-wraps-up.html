Oklahoma Softball Wraps Fall Schedule: What the Battle Series Told Us About the 2027 Sooners

By Emily Rawlings | October 31, 2026 | I Love Softball

Oklahoma softball has closed its 2026 fall schedule, ending a month of Battle Series exhibitions at Love's Field that gave fans a sustained look at a roster built around two of the best players in the country. With Kendall Wells and Rylie Kavan headlining a Sooners squad that now pivots toward spring, the question is no longer what Oklahoma has — it is what they will do with it.

A Fall That Answered the Right Questions

The Battle Series format worked exactly as it was designed to. Head coach Patty Gasso and her staff ran a series of Wednesday-night exhibitions at Love's Field throughout October, drawing strong crowds to Norman for a roster evaluation process that doubled as a preview for Sooners fans. The goal was rhythm, game-speed reps, and a look at how the returning core blended with the new pieces.

The answer, by all indications, was well. Wells appeared healthy and sharp throughout fall ball, and the lineup showed the kind of depth that contending programs require. With the Battle Series now complete, the roster evaluation is done and the focus shifts to the February opener.

Kendall Wells: From Freshman to Standard-Setter

When Wells arrived at Oklahoma last year, the expectation was that she would contribute. What she did instead was set a new standard. The outfielder finished the 2026 regular season with 39 home runs and 88 RBI, earning NFCA Freshman of the Year honors — one of the most complete debut seasons in recent program history. She enters her sophomore fall having already established herself as a preseason candidate for every national award.

The fall gave Wells an opportunity to build on that foundation without the pressure of a ranked schedule. That combination — proven output with a low-stakes development environment — is exactly what makes fall ball valuable for a player at her level.

Kavan Remains the Standard

Rylie Kavan's résumé does not require an update, but it does require respect. Two WCWS Most Outstanding Player awards put her in a category occupied by very few names in the sport's history. For Oklahoma, she remains the player every opponent builds its game plan around and every teammate points to as the example of what excellence looks like. Having another fall of work together with Wells adds another layer of chemistry to a lineup that was already difficult to pitch to.

What's Next

Oklahoma softball will not play in public again until the spring 2027 schedule opens, likely in February. The full Big 12 slate will follow, with the conference and national postseason as the program's standard destination. Fall ball provided the preview. Spring will determine whether this version of the Sooners can translate what everyone saw in October into another run toward Oklahoma City.
