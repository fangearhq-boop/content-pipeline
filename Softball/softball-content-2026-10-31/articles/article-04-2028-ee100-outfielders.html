2028 EE100 Outfielders 201-258 Drop Today: What the Final Tier Means for the Class Picture

By Emily Rawlings | October 31, 2026 | I Love Softball

The 2028 recruiting class is nearly fully mapped. Extra Inning Softball drops its Extra Prospect Rankings for Class of 2028 Outfielders — numbers 201 through 258 — today, completing the final stretch of outfielder coverage for a class that has been building toward this moment since the nominations window opened. Combined with the EE100 (1-100) and Extra Select (101-200) tiers already released, the full outfielder picture for the 2028 class is now visible.

What We Know About the Top of the Class

The overall Top 5 across all positions was revealed earlier this week and the program commitments at the top are no surprise to anyone who has followed the class closely. Kaylee Landers, committed to Texas, sits at No. 1. She has drawn comparisons to some of the most complete offensive players to come out of the state of Texas in recent recruiting cycles. No. 2 Holden is committed to Florida, and No. 3 Schlader — an OU commit — has been one of the more talked-about pitching prospects in the class, with a riseball that reportedly sits above 70 mph, a number that is rare at any level and extraordinary at the prospect stage.

Texas, Florida, and Oklahoma holding the top three spots reflects where the strongest program-level recruitment pipelines are. The battle to stay ahead in the 2028 class is a preview of where the power balance in college softball may sit for the 2029-2031 WCWS windows.

The Scale of the 2028 Class

More than 1,200 players were nominated for the 2028 Extra Elite 100 evaluation — the largest class Extra Inning Softball has ever evaluated. That number reflects the continued growth of the sport at the youth and club levels, but it also reflects the platform the EE100 has built as the defining recruiting ranking in college softball. When a class draws 1,200-plus nominations, every tier of the release carries value. The 201-258 outfielders today represent players who have been identified, evaluated, and ranked against the largest comparison pool in the history of the list.

What the 2028 Class Means for Programs Now

The recruiting contact window for the 2028 class closed October 25. With the full ranking picture now visible, programs are in the follow-up phase — building relationships, making offers, and competing for the players who have not yet committed. The players ranked 201-258 in today's outfielder release may not anchor a recruiting class, but the ones who find the right fit with a mid-major program or surprise with late development could define those programs' futures just as meaningfully as the top-5 names define the blue bloods'.

For fans who track recruiting, today's release is the last major piece before the full 2028 class picture settles into its final form.

What's Next

With the outfielder tiers complete and the overall Top 5 locked in, Extra Inning Softball's attention will shift toward hitter rankings and the broader class summary. The early signing period, when many 2028 commits will make their decisions official, remains on the horizon. For the coaches, players, and fans tracking the 2028 class, today's release is one of the final checkpoints before the class begins to crystallize into its finished form.
