### STORY 5: 5-for-5 Eligibility — Court Stay Holds, Tenth Circuit Still Deliberating
**Tier:** 3 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP
The NCAA's 5-for-5 eligibility rule (five years of eligibility must be completed within five calendar years) remains in effect after the Tenth Circuit issued a 2-1 stay on August 21, pausing the district court's July 31 injunction. Oral arguments were heard October 13. No merits ruling has been issued. College softball programs continue planning 2027 rosters under uncertainty.
