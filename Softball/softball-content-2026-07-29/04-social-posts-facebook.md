### STORY 3: Texas Longhorns 2027 Softball

**Long-Form Post:**
One transfer portal window just transformed Texas softball — and it starts with the best player in the country.

Isa Torres, the 2026 D1Softball National Player of the Year, is coming home to Texas. The shortstop from Georgetown, TX hit .530 (an FSU record), scored 78 runs (another FSU record), and won the ACC Player of the Year AND Defensive Player of the Year in her final season with Florida State. She also holds the NCAA record for consecutive hits at 16.

Coach Mike White didn't stop there. Texas also added:
🥎 Samantha Swan — catcher from Arizona State
🥎 Samantha Lincoln — pitcher from Texas Tech

Meanwhile, FSU now faces one of the toughest rebuilds in program history. Torres and Jaysoni Beachum — who transferred to Texas Tech — accounted for 28% of FSU's runs, 35% of home runs, and 29% of hits in 2026. Coach Lonni Alameda lost four key starters to the portal after FSU lost at home in regionals to Stetson and UCF.

Texas is loading up. FSU has work to do.

And with the September 1 portal window just 33 days away — another wave of moves is coming.
