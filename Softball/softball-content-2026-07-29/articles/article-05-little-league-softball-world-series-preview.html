Little League Softball World Series 2026 Heading to Greenville, NC Starting August 2

By Emily Rawlings | July 29, 2026

Regional tournaments are complete and qualifying teams are set — the biggest stage in youth softball arrives in North Carolina next week.

The biggest moment in youth softball is almost here. The 2026 Little League Softball World Series comes to Greenville, North Carolina beginning August 2 — and with regional qualifying complete, the field is set.

Eight U.S. regional champions will join international representatives at the tournament, competing for the title of Little League Softball World Series champion. For many of these players, this will be the most significant competitive experience of their young softball careers.

Greenville, NC: The Heart of Youth Softball

The Little League Softball World Series has a long connection with Greenville, North Carolina. The Eastern Regional Complex provides a world-class venue for youth athletes — complete fields, amenable weather in late summer, and a community that embraces the tradition of Little League.

For families making the trip to Greenville, the tournament atmosphere is one of the most family-friendly in all of sports. Little League events are built around the players and their families, and the World Series brings that mission to its highest level.

From Regionals to the World Stage

Little League softball regional tournaments concluded in recent weeks, with teams competing in regions across the United States and internationally for the chance to represent their area in Greenville. The journey to the World Series is itself a remarkable achievement — these teams have already defeated hundreds of others to earn their spot.

International teams will also compete, bringing a global dimension to the tournament and reflecting the worldwide growth of softball as a sport.

Why It Matters for the Sport

The Little League Softball World Series is more than a tournament — it's a pipeline for the sport's future. Alumni of the LLSWS have gone on to play at the highest levels of college softball, and for many coaches watching from afar, these games provide an early look at the talent that will eventually be competing for NCAA titles in five or six years.

The event is also covered on ESPN, bringing youth softball into living rooms across the country and growing the fan base for the next generation.

What's Next

The 2026 Little League Softball World Series runs August 2-9 in Greenville, North Carolina. Coverage will be available on ESPN platforms. Check Little League's official website at littleleague.org for bracket updates, game schedules, and live scores once the tournament begins. The next generation of softball stars takes the stage in eight days — make sure you're watching.
