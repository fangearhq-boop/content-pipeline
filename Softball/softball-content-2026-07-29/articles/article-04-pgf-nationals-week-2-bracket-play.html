PGF Nationals Week 2: Bracket Play in Full Swing, Championship Games August 2

By Sarah Mitchell | July 29, 2026

The 14U, 12U, and 10U divisions are competing for spots in the August 2 championship games at Deanna Manning Stadium in Irvine, California.

The second week of PGF Nationals is well underway in Orange County, California — and the path to a national title is getting narrower with every game played.

Week 2 runs July 26 through August 2, featuring the 14U, 12U, and 10U divisions at fields across the Huntington Beach and Irvine area. Opening ceremonies were held Sunday at Huntington Beach Central Park, and bracket play began shortly after. Championship games for all three divisions are scheduled for August 2 at Deanna Manning Stadium, Bill Barber Park, Irvine — one of the most iconic youth softball venues in the country.

Live coverage is available throughout the week on PGF.TV.

Why These Games Matter

PGF Nationals is the premier showcase tournament for youth softball in the United States. College coaches from programs across the country attend to evaluate talent, and the players competing this week are among the best of their age groups in the nation.

The timing adds extra significance: the next NCAA recruiting portal window opens September 1 — just 33 days away. For high school players with college aspirations, a strong performance at PGF Nationals in the days leading up to that window can be the difference between a scholarship offer and being overlooked. Coaches are watching, evaluating, and building their rosters right now.

Week 1 Championship Recap

Week 1 of PGF Nationals concluded last week with the 16U and 18U Premier divisions crowning champions. Athletics Mercado captured the 16U Premier national title with a dramatic walk-off victory — Teuila Jaelynn Eteaki delivered the game-winning hit in the final. AZ Rising won the 18U Premier title by run rule. Both championships showcased the elite level of talent on display across the PGF Nationals field.

Championship Weekend Preview

As bracket play continues through Thursday, the field for the August 2 championship games will take shape. Deanna Manning Stadium at Bill Barber Park in Irvine has hosted some of the most memorable moments in youth softball history, and this year's field promises to deliver more of the same.

For families traveling to Orange County for the tournament, the HB Central Park and Bill Barber Park venues are accessible and fan-friendly. PGF.TV provides streaming for those unable to attend in person.

What's Next

Bracket play continues through Saturday, with championship games August 2 at Deanna Manning Stadium. Follow live bracket updates at pgfnationals.com and stream all the action on PGF.TV. The best young softball players in the country are competing for national titles this week — don't miss it.
