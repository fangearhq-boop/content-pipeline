Puerto Rico Dominates at 2026 CAC Games: Two Run-Rule Wins Through Day 2

By Emily Rawlings | July 29, 2026

In Santo Domingo, Puerto Rico's softball team is on a different level — outscoring opponents 31-1 through two complete games of play.

The 2026 Central American and Caribbean Games women's softball tournament is underway in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic — and Puerto Rico has made one thing perfectly clear: this gold medal is theirs to lose.

Through two games at the Juan Pablo Duarte Olympic Centre, Puerto Rico has been untouchable. A 15-0 run-rule victory in four innings on Day 1 was followed by a 16-1 run-rule win in just three innings on Day 2 against the host Dominican Republic. Combined: 31 runs scored, one run allowed, in seven innings of play.

Run-Rule Dominance

Run-rule wins in softball require mercy — and Puerto Rico has shown none to their early opponents. The 15-0 win means Puerto Rico scored roughly 3.75 runs per inning against one opponent, and the 16-1 win saw the game end in the third inning alone. That kind of offensive firepower, paired with shutdown pitching, is what champions look like.

The host nation Dominican Republic was a particularly symbolic victim in the Day 2 result. Games played at home in front of your own fans — and getting run-ruled in three innings — is a difficult way to open the tournament. Puerto Rico gave them no margin for error.

The Tournament Field

Eight nations are competing in the women's softball bracket at the 2026 CAC Games: Puerto Rico, Mexico, the U.S. Virgin Islands, Colombia, Cuba, Venezuela, El Salvador, and the Dominican Republic. Through Day 2, Puerto Rico, Mexico, and the U.S. Virgin Islands are all unbeaten. Venezuela rebounded from its opening loss with a win on Day 2.

The CAC Games are the premier multi-sport event for nations in the Caribbean basin and Central America — the region's equivalent of a continental championship. A gold medal in women's softball carries significant prestige for any national program.

Competition Continues Through July 31

The softball competition runs through July 31 at the Juan Pablo Duarte Olympic Centre. If Puerto Rico maintains this trajectory, a medal-round showdown against Mexico or the USVI would be the marquee game of the tournament.

For softball fans in the United States, the USVI's unbeaten start is another notable storyline. American Samoa is not listed as a competitor in the CAC field — that nation competes in the Oceania region — but the USVI, Colombia, and Cuba all add depth to a bracket that will produce a champion by the end of the month.

What's Next

Day 3 is underway today, July 29. Medal games are scheduled for July 31. Keep an eye on the WBSC (wbsc.org) for live brackets and results from Santo Domingo. If Puerto Rico keeps this up, they will be celebrating gold before the calendar turns to August.
