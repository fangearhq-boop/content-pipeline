Oklahoma Softball — Five Days Until Love's Field, Wells and Berzon Finally Play Together

By Jordan Reeves | September 25, 2026

Five days from now, a battery that could be the most dominant in college softball finally steps onto the field together.

Kendall Wells and Sydney Berzon have both been practicing at Love's Field all fall. They've been in the same practices, the same workouts, the same film sessions. But as of today, they haven't played a real game together yet. That changes September 30 when Oklahoma opens its fall ball slate at Love's Field with an intrasquad Battle Series game at 6:30 PM. The first game against an outside opponent is October 3 against Tarleton State.

The Record-Breaking Freshman

Wells' 2026 season was unlike anything Oklahoma — or college softball — had seen from a first-year player. She hit .358 with 39 home runs and 88 RBI. She broke the NCAA freshman single-season home run record in April when she hit her 31st homer against Texas. She kept going. By the end of the season, her 39 total had broken the all-time NCAA single-season record, previously 37 by Laura Espinoza in 1995.

The awards followed. NFCA Freshman of the Year. SEC Freshman of the Year. Diamond Sports/NFCA Catcher of the Year. And the Johnny Bench Award — the most prestigious honor for a collegiate catcher — which she won as the first freshman in the history of the award. She is 20 years old and entering her sophomore season.

The Transfer Ace

When Softball America evaluated the transfer portal last winter, they named Sydney Berzon the top player available. The LSU right-hander spent four years in Baton Rouge building a career line: 2.02 ERA, 52 wins, 416 strikeouts in 486.1 innings. Two-time All-American. A pitcher who has faced the best lineups in the SEC every season and delivered at an elite level throughout.

Now she's at Oklahoma. The combination of Wells behind the plate and Berzon on the mound gives Patty Gasso something she can build an entire rotation strategy around.

The Redemption Angle

Texas knocked Oklahoma out of the 2026 WCWS. The Sooners have been building toward their 9th national title for years, and 2026 wasn't it. This fall is the beginning of the answer — a rebuilt roster, an elite new-look battery, and a program motivated by exactly what happened last June.

Tickets for Love's Field fall games are $10.88 per game, with an All Fall Pass available for $70.69 covering all eight fall contests through November 4. The full fall schedule includes Tarleton State, the University of Science and Arts of Oklahoma, Seminole State College, and East Texas A&M as opponents.

What's Next

The September 30 intrasquad game is the first public look at what Gasso has assembled. The October 3 game against Tarleton State is when outside evaluators start drawing real conclusions. Between now and February, every fall game is another piece of evidence about whether this Oklahoma roster is genuinely ready to compete for a national title. The building blocks are in place. The battery is elite. Five more days.
