Texas Tech Fall Schedule Is Out — Glasco's Seven All-Americans Debut October 3

By Sarah Mitchell | September 25, 2026

The 2026 WCWS runner-up finally has a date on the calendar.

Texas Tech announced its fall softball schedule earlier this week, and the Red Raiders open on October 3 against West Texas A&M at Tracy Sellers Field in Lubbock. First pitch at 2 PM. Free admission. It's the first time the most decorated roster in Texas Tech softball history will play a game together — and that alone makes it worth circling.

Seven All-Americans Who've Never All Played Together

Gerry Glasco's 2027 Texas Tech roster features seven All-Americans. The headliners: Jaysoni Beachum (.406 batting average, 10 home runs, 69 RBI in her final year at Florida State, where she was a First Team All-ACC honoree), Kasidi Pickering (.384 BA, 50 home runs across her career at Oklahoma), and Jayden Heavener (13-9 with a 2.81 ERA from LSU). There are four additional All-Americans woven into the roster around those three.

These players have been in the same fall camp for weeks now — working together, building chemistry, learning each other's tendencies. But none of them have played an actual game together. October 3 changes that.

The Full Fall Schedule

Texas Tech has five fall games scheduled. After the West Texas A&M home opener on October 3, the Red Raiders travel to Canyon for a rematch on October 11. They host New Mexico in a doubleheader on October 18 (noon start) and close the fall schedule on October 24 against Odessa College — all at Tracy Sellers Field, all free for fans. The schedule is tight and purposeful: enough games to evaluate everyone without overloading players in the fall.

No. 1 in the 2028 Class

The fall ball momentum isn't the only thing building at Lubbock right now. After landing five-star commits Makenzie Thomas and Khloe Williams, Texas Tech has surged to a tie for No. 1 in the national 2028 recruiting class rankings alongside Oklahoma. Ninety-four of the top 150 prospects in the class have now committed nationally, and the Red Raiders are positioned at the top of the list alongside the program that knocked them out of the 2026 championship.

This is what a dynasty looks like in the building. Glasco isn't just trying to win in 2027 — he's stacking the next class at the same time.

What's Next

October 3 at Tracy Sellers Field is the first real answer to the question Red Raiders fans have been asking all summer: what does this roster actually look like in a game? The fall schedule runs through October 24, giving Glasco and his staff six weeks to evaluate lineup combinations, test pitching rotations, and prepare for a 2027 spring in which Texas Tech enters as one of the clear national title contenders. Free admission. First pitch at 2 PM. Mark the calendar.
