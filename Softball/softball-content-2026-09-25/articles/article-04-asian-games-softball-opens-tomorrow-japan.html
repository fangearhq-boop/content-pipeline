Asian Games Softball Opens Tomorrow — Japan Gunning for LA28 Qualification on Home Soil

By Emily Rawlings | September 25, 2026

While fall ball dominates the college softball conversation in the United States, international softball's next major event opens tomorrow in Japan.

The 2026 Asian Games softball competition begins September 26 at Anjō Sports Park Softball Ground in Anjō, Aichi Prefecture — part of the broader Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games. Eight nations compete in a preliminary round running through October 2, with the gold medal game on October 3. For teams with an eye on Los Angeles 2028, every game counts.

Japan: Favorite on Home Soil

Japan enters as the world's No. 1 ranked softball nation and the host country for these Games. The combination of home field advantage, top-tier talent, and the weight of expectation makes them the overwhelming favorite. Japan won the Olympic gold medal at Tokyo 2020 — playing in front of their home fans in a largely empty stadium during the pandemic Games. The 2026 Asian Games, with full crowds expected in Anjō, gives the Japanese program another major stage on home soil.

Chinese Taipei (ranked No. 5 globally) and China (No. 8) are the most credible challengers in the field. Both programs have the depth to push Japan in a tournament format, and any given game could change the medal picture.

Eight Nations, One Gold Medal

The full field: Japan (No. 1), Chinese Taipei (No. 5), China (No. 8), Philippines (No. 13), Singapore (No. 22), South Korea (No. 31), Hong Kong China (No. 32), and Thailand (No. 44). The Philippines represent one of the most compelling storylines — ranked 13th in the world, they're the highest-ranked Southeast Asian program in the competition, and their presence reflects the real growth of softball across the region.

Korea and Singapore both bring programs with serious national investment in the sport. Thailand at No. 44 rounds out the field as the clear underdog, but Asian Games competition often produces surprises.

LA28 Implications

The Asian Games aren't just a regional championship — they sit within a broader Olympic qualification pathway for Los Angeles 2028. Softball is back on the Olympic program for LA28 after returning at Tokyo 2020, and continental championships like these carry qualification implications that give every team in the bracket additional motivation beyond gold.

For Japan specifically, dominating the Asian Games on home soil would be a statement of intent heading into the LA28 qualification cycle that intensifies over the next two years.

What's Next

Preliminary round play begins tomorrow and runs through October 2. The gold medal game is October 3 at Anjō Sports Park Softball Ground. Results from the Philippines, Chinese Taipei, and South Korea matches will be the key storylines to track as the tournament unfolds. Japan is expected to advance to the final — but in a tournament format, nothing is guaranteed. Watch the bracket closely from tomorrow onward.
