NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Oklahoma State softball made its first move in the Class of 2028 on Sunday night. Head coach Kenny Gajewski and the Cowgirls secured a commitment from Peyton Cleary, a top-100 infielder from Liberty North High School in Missouri — and the timing was no accident. The announcement came as the NCAA's two-day contact window for the 2028 class (Oct. 17-18) was closing. OSU used the access, and Cleary is the result. She's the Cowgirls' first 2028 pledge, and she arrives with a profile that earned national attention. Liberty North is a school with strong softball history, and Cleary fits the mold of the high-IQ infielder that Gajewski has consistently prioritized in building OSU's roster. The 2028 class nationally is already loaded at the top. Texas leads with No. 1-ranked infielder Michaela Landers, Ainsley Morvan (22 HR as a high schooler), and two others — all from the TX Bombers Gold program. Oklahoma has five commitments. Florida has No. 2-ranked Kylie Holden and catcher Evans. Florida State just landed twins Keira and Kelsey Luderer over the same contact window weekend. OSU's move with Cleary is exactly the kind of decisive recruiting that separates programs in the modern landscape. The Class of 2028 won't step on campus for two more years, but the decisions being made right now will define where programs stand in the late 2020s. ### Image Post Caption Oklahoma State has its first commitment in the Class of 2028. Transfer Portal Week 3 — Kasidi Pickering Still Undecided
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post The fall transfer portal is winding down — but one major name is still out there. Kasidi Pickering, the Texas Tech infielder and one of the fall portal's most coveted bats, remains uncommitted. Oklahoma and Texas Tech are the reported options, with no announcement made as of Monday. Her decision matters. Pickering is the kind of infielder whose defensive range and offensive production would immediately strengthen whichever program lands her in the Big 12. For Oklahoma — already loaded with Wells, Kavan, and a deep returning core — adding Pickering would create a positional depth few rosters can match. For Texas Tech under new coaches Cottrill and Pendley — rebuilding from last season's transition — landing Pickering would signal that the new staff's momentum is real. The broader portal picture shows how much the landscape has shifted. LSU assembled one of the cycle's top hauls: Mickey Winchell (.335 BA, 14 stolen bases from Notre Dame), plus twins Madison Pickens (Louisville) and Mackenzie Pickens (Ole Miss). OU added Harter, Hansen, and Gregus. The 137-plus total entries reflect how active the fall portal has been league-wide. Most of the major decisions have been made. Pickering is the last significant story. A commitment is expected before the end of October. ### Image Post Caption Kasidi Pickering — Texas Tech infielder, one of the top portal bats available — is still uncommitted.
