OU Battle Series Game 4: Kavan, Wells, and the Best Roster in Fall Softball Play Wednesday

By Emily Rawlings

October 19, 2026

Two days. That's all that stands between fall softball fans and Game 4 of Oklahoma's Battle Series at Love's Field. Wednesday, October 21, first pitch 6:30 p.m. Central. Stream it free at 1oklahoma.com.

Patty Gasso's Sooners have built one of the most decorated fall rosters in the program's history, and this week's game is another chance to see what 2027's national title contender looks like before February.

The Headlines Playing in the Same Lineup

Oklahoma's roster this fall reads like an All-American selection sheet. Jordy Kavan returns as a two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player — the kind of accolade that only a handful of players in the sport's history have earned once, let alone twice. Kavan's combination of plate discipline, power, and competitive instinct makes her one of college softball's most complete hitters.

Playing alongside her: Grace Wells, the sophomore who became a household name last spring by hitting 39 home runs and winning the NFCA Freshman of the Year award. At 39 HR, Wells set a new standard for what freshman power production can look like at the Division I level. Now in her second year, she arrives in fall ball with unfinished business and a new level of experience.

Kavan. Wells. Together. In the same fall lineup. For anyone who follows college softball, that combination is the story of OU's fall schedule.

A Pitching Staff to Match

The Sooners don't need their offense to carry the load — Sydney Berzon gives Oklahoma an ace-level arm in the circle. Berzon and the supporting pitching staff have posted dominant fall numbers, and Gasso has given her staff chances to work through competitive game situations.

The combination of elite offense and a reliable pitching core makes Oklahoma the kind of team that can win a national championship through multiple paths — by dominating with pitching in close games or by scoring runs in bunches when opponents can't keep up. That kind of roster flexibility is what Gasso has built over decades in Norman.

The Battle Series Context

Oklahoma's fall Battle Series has become one of the sport's most anticipated fall showcases. The opener (Sept. 30) drew 2,351 fans to Love's Field — remarkable attendance for a fall exhibition that doesn't count in the record books. Game 4 on Wednesday continues the tradition of giving Oklahoma fans and recruiting prospects an extended look at what the 2027 Sooners will bring.

Game 5 is set for Thursday, October 23 against USAO, rounding out the fall Battle Series schedule.

What's Next

Game 4 at Love's Field (Oklahoma City): Wednesday, October 21, 6:30 p.m. CT. Stream on 1oklahoma.com. The Sooners play again Oct. 23 vs. USAO in Game 5 of the series. After the fall schedule wraps, Oklahoma will shift to 2027 spring preparations — entering the season as one of the nation's top programs with Kavan and Wells headlining a roster built to make a deep WCWS run.
