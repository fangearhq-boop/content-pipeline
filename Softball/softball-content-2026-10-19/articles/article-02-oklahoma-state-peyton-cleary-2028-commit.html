Oklahoma State Lands Top-100 Infielder Peyton Cleary in Class of 2028

By Sarah Mitchell

October 19, 2026

Oklahoma State softball didn't wait for the dust to settle from the first contact window. Sunday night, head coach Kenny Gajewski and the Cowgirls secured a commitment from Peyton Cleary, a top-100 infielder from Liberty North High School in Missouri — the program's first pledge in the Class of 2028.

The timing was deliberate. The NCAA's two-day contact window for the Class of 2028 (Oct. 17-18) had just closed, giving D1 coaches their first opportunity to meet face-to-face with junior prospects. OSU made the most of the access.

Who Is Peyton Cleary?

Cleary is a Missouri-based infielder with the athleticism and skill set that drew Oklahoma State's recruiting staff to her immediately. At Liberty North High School, she has built a profile that put her inside the national top-100 rankings for her class — a significant evaluation given how early the 2028 class is in its development cycle.

The commitment gives OSU a foothold in the Class of 2028 recruiting race as programs across the country scramble to build classes for the 2028-29 season. For Gajewski, landing a top-100 prospect in the first contact window of the cycle sends a message about the Cowgirls' recruiting momentum.

The 2028 Class Is Taking Shape

The national picture for the Class of 2028 is already competitive. Texas has assembled an early leader's position, with No. 1-ranked infielder Michaela Landers and Ainsley Morvan (22 home runs) both already committed, alongside Abdullah and Schellhammer — all from the TX Bombers Gold program.

Oklahoma has five commitments. Florida secured No. 2-ranked Kylie Holden (five-star, Michigan) and catcher Evans. Florida State added twins Keira and Kelsey Luderer from the same contact window weekend. With the contact period now closed, commit announcements are expected to continue through the week as recruits finalize their decisions.

OSU's move with Cleary puts the Cowgirls on the board at a moment when the national class is still highly fluid. Gajewski has shown a consistent ability to identify top Midwest talent, and adding a player of Cleary's profile to the 2028 class early creates a recruiting foundation to build on.

Oklahoma State's Program Trajectory

The Cowgirls enter this recruiting cycle with programs of established national credibility. Gajewski has turned Oklahoma State into a Big 12 program capable of making postseason runs and competing with the sport's blue bloods for top talent. Landing a top-100 national prospect in the first contact window of a new class reflects where OSU stands in the national recruiting conversation.

The Class of 2028 will set the table for OSU's roster in the 2028-29 WCWS cycle. Getting Cleary on board in October 2026 gives the Cowgirls a head start in a race that won't conclude until signing day more than a year from now.

What's Next

The post-contact window period (Oct. 19-21) is expected to produce additional commitment announcements as recruits process their evaluations and make decisions. Oklahoma State will continue building its 2028 class over the coming months. The national picture — led by Texas, OU, and Florida — will clarify further once the full commit wave from this week's window settles. Cleary's commitment is the first piece of OSU's puzzle for 2028.
