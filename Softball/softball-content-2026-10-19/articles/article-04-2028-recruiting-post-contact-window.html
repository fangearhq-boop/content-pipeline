2028 Softball Recruiting: What Happened After the Contact Window Closed

By Jordan Reeves

October 19, 2026

The first contact window for the Class of 2028 closed Saturday (Oct. 18). For 48 hours, Division I coaches had their first opportunity to meet face-to-face with junior prospects — in person, off campus, for the first time. The window is now shut until the next designated contact period.

Now comes the wave. Commit announcements are expected through Oct. 21 as recruits process what they experienced and finalize decisions. Here's where the class stands as of Monday morning.

Texas Is Winning the Class So Far

No program has assembled a stronger early foundation in the Class of 2028 than Texas. The Longhorns lead nationally with No. 1-ranked infielder Michaela Landers already committed, along with Ainsley Morvan — a pitcher/utility player who hit 22 home runs in travel ball last season and committed on October 10. Abdullah and Schellhammer round out the early Texas class, all four of them from the TX Bombers Gold program.

If Texas adds to this group over the coming weeks, the Longhorns could be on track for a No. 1 national class in 2028 — which would represent back-to-back elite recruiting cycles for a program that already landed Isa Torres (D1Softball Player of the Year) from the transfer portal for 2026.

Oklahoma, Florida, and the Chase Pack

Oklahoma enters the post-window period with five commitments — a strong foundation for a program that expects to compete for WCWS championships every spring. Head coach Patty Gasso's recruiting staff has consistently built rosters around multi-year stars, and the 2028 class appears to follow that model.

Florida secured two of the cycle's top names: No. 2-ranked Kylie Holden (five-star infielder from Michigan) and Evans, a catcher who gives the Gators versatility behind the plate. With six starters returning from 2026 and a full pitching staff intact, Tim Walton's program is building for sustained dominance.

Florida State added twins Keira and Kelsey Luderer over the contact weekend — a rare double commitment that immediately vaults FSU's 2028 class into the conversation. Oklahoma State made its first move Sunday night with top-100 infielder Peyton Cleary from Liberty North, Missouri.

What the Commit Wave Looks Like

The Oct. 17-18 contact window is only the first structured face-to-face opportunity of the recruiting cycle. Recruits who received visits are expected to announce over the next few days — the window triggers a predictable wave. Programs that made strong impressions in their 48-hour window should see commitments reflect that by mid-week.

Uncommitted top-50 prospects are now the most watched names in the sport. Programs like LSU (No. 3 class entering fall), Tennessee, Arizona, and UCLA all have the roster and NIL infrastructure to compete for late-breaking commitments. The contact window opened conversations — the next two weeks will tell us how those conversations ended.

What's Next

Commit announcements are expected Oct. 19-21 as the post-window wave builds. The next structured contact period is still several months away. The early national signing period for softball opens November 11, 2026 — giving programs with fully committed classes a chance to lock in their 2028 recruits. The Class of 2028 won't walk on campus until fall 2028, but the decisions made this week will define programs for years.
