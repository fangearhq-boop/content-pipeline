Fall Transfer Portal Watch: Kasidi Pickering Remains the Biggest Name Available

By Sarah Mitchell

October 19, 2026

Fall exhibitions are well underway. Most programs have done their portal work. The 2027 rosters are largely set. But one significant name remains uncommitted: Kasidi Pickering.

The Texas Tech infielder has been one of the fall's most-watched portal stories, with Oklahoma and Texas Tech both reported as primary destinations. As of Monday, no commitment has been announced. Her decision could come any day — and whichever program lands her gets a meaningful boost heading into 2027 spring preparations.

Why Pickering Matters

Portal bats of Pickering's caliber don't stay available for long in the modern landscape. She's the kind of infielder whose defensive range and offensive production at the college level translates directly to Big 12 impact. The programs listed as frontrunners — OU and Texas Tech — both have roster fits and program cultures that would make the transition natural.

For Oklahoma: adding Pickering would deepen an already loaded infield corps alongside Wells, Kavan, and the returning core. For Texas Tech: landing Pickering after the coaching transition to Cottrill and Pendley would signal that the new staff's rebuild is attracting exactly the caliber of talent it needs.

How the Fall Portal Has Shaped 2027 Rosters

The broader fall portal picture shows how much the landscape has shifted. With 137-plus entries, this was one of the more active fall portal cycles in recent memory. Programs across the Big 12, SEC, and Pac-12 used the fall window to address weaknesses and add proven talent.

LSU assembled one of the cycle's most impressive hauls: center fielder Mickey Winchell (Notre Dame, .335 batting average, 14 stolen bases) slots into CF immediately, while twins Madison Pickens (Louisville outfield) and Mackenzie Pickens (Ole Miss infield) add depth to Beth Torina's roster. Jayden Heavener's departure to Texas Tech is the key loss for LSU — but the additions suggest the Tigers addressed it.

Oklahoma added Harter, Hansen, and Gregus — three pieces that build on the returning core's foundation. Texas Tech's transition under new coaches Cottrill and Pendley has been one of the fall's most closely watched programs, and landing Beachum (from Florida State) was already a significant piece of that rebuild.

The Clock Is Ticking

Fall exhibitions continue through late October. The longer portal decisions remain unresolved, the tighter the timeline becomes for programs to integrate new players before spring. Pickering's commitment — whenever it comes — will be one of the final major portal moves of the fall cycle.

The portal's influence on college softball has only grown with each passing year. Programs that navigate it most effectively — both in adding talent and retaining their own — are the ones that consistently appear in Knoxville each June. The fall 2026 portal cycle, with all its activity, is another data point in that ongoing evolution.

What's Next

Kasidi Pickering's commitment is expected soon as fall exhibitions continue. With OU and Texas Tech as the reported frontrunners, expect her announcement before the end of October. The broader transfer portal for the 2027 season will continue to see occasional movement, but the peak of fall activity is winding down. LSU, OU, and TTU's portal hauls will be among the most impactful of the cycle when spring 2027 arrives.
