### Long-Form Post Oklahoma has 11 days to solve a problem: the December transfer portal opens December 1st 🌪️ The good news for Sooner fans: Patty Gasso enters the December window in a position of strength. The December portal is 11 days away. Who Gasso targets — and who answers — will define Oklahoma's 2027 ceiling. **Caption Post** 11 days until the December transfer portal opens.
