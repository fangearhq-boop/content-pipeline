Oklahoma's December Portal Math: What Patty Gasso Needs Before Dec. 1

By Emily Rawlings

November 20, 2026

The December transfer portal opens in 11 days. Oklahoma enters that window in a position most programs would envy: the No. 1 D1Softball signing class, an intact returning core led by Jilian Wells and Grace Kavan, and Goose Hutchens already enrolled and reclassified. By nearly every roster measurement, the Sooners are the most talented program in the country heading into 2027.

The word "nearly" is doing a lot of work in that sentence. Oklahoma still has math to do — and December 1 is when the calculator comes out.

The Losses That Define the Need

Two departures define Oklahoma's offseason calculus. Mariah Torres — the D1Softball National Player of the Year — transferred to Texas Tech. Torres was not just a stat-sheet producer; she was the kind of anchor arm that determines how a program is perceived heading into the postseason. Teams seed you differently when you have that kind of pitcher. Without her, the Sooners' rotation question is legitimate.

Kasidi Pickering's move to Texas Tech compounds the problem. Pickering's departure (.384 batting average, 50 home runs, 169 RBI, NFCA All-American) reshapes the Oklahoma lineup in ways that the portal additions of Macie Harter (MTSU) and Adi Hansen (CSI) only partially address. Both are solid picks — but neither profile as a starter in a WCWS Final Four lineup.

Four-star infielder Emma Reynolds committed in October. The top-ranked recruit from Arizona adds another quality piece to a class already loaded with talent. But Reynolds profiles as a contributor, not the difference-maker the Sooners need at pitcher.

The Returning Core Is Historically Good

To be clear about what Oklahoma is working with: Jilian Wells hit 39 home runs last season and was named NFCA Freshman of the Year. Grace Kavan has won two WCWS MVP awards. Goose Hutchens is on campus now. The incoming class adds Finlee Williams (No. 1 outfielder nationally per D1Softball) and Riley Hilliard (No. 11 overall, No. 2 catcher).

That is an offensive lineup capable of scoring against any pitcher in the country. The question is not whether Oklahoma can hit — it is whether the Sooners can hold leads when the opponent has a frontline arm. The teams that beat Oklahoma in a seven-game WCWS series will be the teams with pitching deep enough to neutralize Wells and Kavan.

What Gasso Needs in December

The priority is simple: a pitcher who can start and win in the postseason. The December portal typically features three types of entrants: players losing roster spots due to 5-for-5 pressure, players at programs that failed to meet expectations, and players simply ready for a larger stage. All three categories will include at least one pitcher capable of starting in the SEC or Big 12.

Oklahoma's combination of name brand, proven postseason track record, and a roster full of returning stars makes the Sooners one of the most attractive destinations for a pitcher who wants to compete for a national title. The challenge is competition: Arizona, Auburn, Ole Miss, and potentially Texas are hunting the same pool of available arms.

Programs that have done the relationship-building work in October and November will have an advantage. The first 48 hours after December 1 tend to be decisive. Pitchers who know where they're going — based on weeks of conversations — commit within hours of the portal opening. Those who haven't built those relationships before December 1 often end up with second choices.

What's Next

Eleven days. That is the window between today and the moment the December portal opens. For Oklahoma, the most important work is happening right now — in phone calls, unofficial visits, and conversations between coaches and players who haven't yet committed but are being courted. Watch for Oklahoma's first December portal commitment within hours of December 1 — the Sooners have enough assets to close quickly when they've identified their target. The arm Gasso adds in December is the difference between a team with WCWS potential and a team with WCWS certainty.
