Series 3, Day 2: Kowalik Wins Again, Ricketts Bounces Back — Five Days Left

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | August 25, 2026

Two days into AUSL All-Star Cup Series 3, and the race looks like this: Kayla Kowalik has won both of her games, Keilani Ricketts has won one and lost one, and five days remain before Series 4 on August 30 crowns the 2026 individual champion. Tuesday's games at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois produced a result that keeps the race alive — just barely — for the chasers.

Kowalik held on for a win in another tight game. Ricketts held on as well, bouncing back after Monday's 4-2 loss to Coffey's squad. Two captains, two wins — but Kowalik's point total keeps growing with each result, while Ricketts is still clawing back what she lost on Day 1.

What Day 2 Means for the Race

After Day 1, the narrative was straightforward: Kowalik dominated, Ricketts stumbled, and the gap at the top widened. Day 2 is more complicated. Ricketts needed to win — and she did. But Kowalik also won, which means the standings math has not changed in Ricketts's favor. The defending leader won a close game in the day's tighter affair, adding to her individual statistical production in a format where every run, hit, and at-bat feeds into the points standings.

Entering Series 3, Kowalik led with approximately 860 points. Ricketts sat at 604 — a gap of roughly 256 points. For context, that gap has to close entirely and then some through a combination of Ricketts outproducing Kowalik in the remaining Series 3 games and across all of Series 4. That is not impossible. But it requires a sustained offensive performance from Ricketts's roster in multiple games while Kowalik's team underperforms relative to the overall field.

Day 2 did not produce that swing. Both captains won. Both added to their totals.

Coffey and Netz — The Other Side of the Race

Danieca Coffey entered Series 3 in third place with approximately 550 points. She won Monday's game against Ricketts, which was a positive result for her standings position. Devyn Netz, who fell to Kowalik's squad 13-5 on Monday, faces the steepest climb with approximately 530 points entering the series.

Series 3 continues through the week at Rosemont. All games broadcast on ESPN, MLB Network, and CBS Sports Network. The schedule and standings — updated live throughout each game — are available at theausl.com/all-star-cup/.

Why the August 30 Finale Matters More Than Ever

The AUSL All-Star Cup individual race has always been designed to come down to the final series. Series 4 on August 30 is the last opportunity for any captain to produce the statistical burst needed to either extend a lead or close one. For Kowalik, the formula is simple: keep winning, keep accumulating. For Ricketts, Coffey, and Netz, the formula requires something different — a dominant Series 4 combined with a quiet one from the leader.

Kowalik has shown no sign of quieting down. Her team's 13-5 performance in Monday's Game 14 was not a fluke — it was a demonstration of why she leads the standings. A tight win on Tuesday shows she can close games too, not just blow them open.

Five days remain. The individual champion is crowned August 30.

What's Next

Series 3 continues Wednesday and Thursday at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois. Each result will shift the individual standings and set the stakes heading into Series 4 on August 30. Watch all games on ESPN, MLB Network, and CBS Sports Network. The 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup individual champion is five days away from being crowned.
