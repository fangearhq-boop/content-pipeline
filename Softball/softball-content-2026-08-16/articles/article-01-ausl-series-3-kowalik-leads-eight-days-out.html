Kowalik Leads AUSL All-Star Cup Into Series 3 — The First Four-Team Run of 2026

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | August 16, 2026

Eight days from now, the AUSL All-Star Cup changes shape. Series 3 — opening August 24 at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois — is the first four-team series in the 2026 run. Kayla Kowalik heads into it leading the individual points race and wearing the captain's armband for the second time this cycle.

Series 1 had three teams. Series 2 had three teams. Series 3 expands the field, and with it, the math gets more complicated. More matchups mean more points available — and more chances to gain or lose ground on the leaderboard. Kowalik enters holding the advantage she built through Series 2, and the question is whether she can protect it and extend it with two series remaining.

The Four-Captain Lineup

Joining Kowalik in the captains' circle for Series 3 are Keilani Ricketts, Danieca Coffey, and Devyn Netz. Ricketts is a Series 1 captain returning for another run at the top. Coffey and Netz round out the four-team format, giving fans four distinct rosters to follow as the competition enters its most complex stage yet.

The expanded format also changes how each game lands in the standings. In a three-team series, a loss to one opponent carries a certain weight; in a four-team series, there are more combinations and a broader set of outcomes to navigate. Kowalik and her staff will need to handle the additional variables while keeping the pressure in the right place.

Rookies in the Mix

The Series 2 individual standings produced one of the more notable movement stories of the season. Lemley jumped 26 spots and Otis climbed 22 spots, reaching No. 10 in the overall rankings. Rookies making that kind of leap in Series 2 is exactly the kind of development that complicates things for the Series 3 frontrunner — fresher legs, rising momentum, and nothing to lose.

For Kowalik, that means the lead she holds now needs active defense. In a four-team format with high-stakes final series, every performance matters more than it did at the start of the cycle.

The Road to August 30

The AUSL All-Star Cup concludes with Series 4, and the individual champion is crowned August 30. That means the next 14 days — from Series 3's opening day on August 24 through the close of Series 4 — are the window in which everything is decided.

Kowalik has been the dominant individual performer through the first two series. The shift to a four-team format tests whether her edge is structural — built into how she plays regardless of the roster around her — or whether it was a product of three-team matchup advantages that the expanded bracket will neutralize.

Ricketts, Coffey, and Netz each have their own reasons to believe August 24 is where the story changes. For now, though, the lead belongs to Kowalik.

What's Next

Series 3 opens August 24 at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois. Series 4 follows immediately, with the overall individual champion crowned August 30. Keep an eye on the leaderboard as Series 3 rosters are finalized and the draft takes shape in the days ahead.
