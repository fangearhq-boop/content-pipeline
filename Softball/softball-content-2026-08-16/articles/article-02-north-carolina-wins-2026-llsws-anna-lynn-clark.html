North Carolina Wins 2026 Little League Softball World Series — Clark's Back-to-Back Shutouts Close the Deal

By Emily Rawlings | I Love Softball | August 16, 2026

Johnston County Little League out of Clayton, North Carolina is the 2026 Little League Softball World Series champion. In a 2-0 win over Kentucky at Stallings Stadium in Greenville, North Carolina wrapped up a dominant tournament run with a performance that will be talked about by everyone who witnessed it.

The story of this championship is Anna-Lynn Clark. On Friday, she threw a shutout in the semifinal against Florida — 109 pitches, 11 strikeouts, no runs. On Saturday, in the championship game, she walked back to the mound and did it again: 93 pitches, 6 strikeouts, 3 hits, and another shutout. Back-to-back shutouts on back-to-back days to close the Little League Softball World Series.

The Championship Game

The decisive moment came in the bottom of the second inning, when Belle Cox drove in the only run of the game with an RBI single. It was the same spot in the order, the same inning, and the same role Cox had played in the game before — opening the scoring from that precise location in the lineup. Routines have a way of showing up in the biggest moments for the best teams.

Kentucky could not break through against Clark. The 3 hits she allowed were scattered and disconnected from scoring opportunities. With Clark dealing, the Kentucky offense never found the runway it needed, and the final score of 2-0 reflected exactly how controlled the game felt from the North Carolina side.

What Clark's Performance Means

Back-to-back shutouts are extraordinary in any context. In the final two games of a national championship tournament, on consecutive days, they are an exceptional display of physical endurance and competitive composure. The cumulative pitch count — 202 pitches over the two starts — tells one part of the story. The results tell the other: no runs allowed, the tournament closed.

For a pitcher that age to absorb the pressure of a semifinal and a final and respond with elite performances in both is the kind of thing that defines a tournament's legacy. This was North Carolina's championship run, and Anna-Lynn Clark made it happen on the mound.

Greenville's Tournament

Stallings Stadium in Greenville, North Carolina hosted the 2026 Little League Softball World Series, and the championship crowd witnessed a clean, decisive conclusion to the tournament. Johnston County LL's path to the title reflects a program that was built to win when it counted — and in the final two rounds, they were as good as anyone in the country at the Little League level.

What's Next

The 2026 Little League Softball World Series champion is North Carolina. Johnston County Little League returns to Clayton with a national title. Anna-Lynn Clark finishes as the defining pitching performance of the entire tournament. The 2027 Little League Softball World Series will give another group of teams the chance to follow that standard.
