27 Days to OKC: Four Sooner Alums Headline USA Softball's World Cup Roster

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | August 16, 2026

Twenty-seven days from now, USA Softball opens the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. The group runs September 12-16. And when the USA takes the field, a quarter of the roster will be made up of players who know exactly what it feels like to play in that city — because they spent their college careers there.

Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell all played softball at the University of Oklahoma under Patty Gasso. Four members of a 16-athlete national team roster from the same program. That is not a coincidence — it is the downstream result of Gasso building the best talent pipeline in college softball over the past decade.

The OU Footprint on USA Softball

No school has more athletes on the 2026 USA World Cup roster than Oklahoma. UCLA is next with three. Kentucky and Tennessee each have two. But it is OU's four that gets the attention — both for the volume and for the specific players involved.

Brito, Coleman, Jennings, and Maxwell are not depth pieces. They are core contributors to the national program. Their presence in Oklahoma City adds a layer of familiarity that most international tournaments don't offer — these players know the stadium, the environment, and the kind of crowd that shows up to watch softball in that city.

For Gasso, seeing four of her former players represent the country at the World Cup is the clearest possible validation of what she has built at OU. Even with Mackenzie Pickering now at Texas Tech, the Sooners' national team pipeline remains the most productive in the sport.

The Group Stage Setup

USA is in a demanding group. The opponents are Netherlands (No. 6 in the world), Mexico (No. 7), China (No. 8), Brazil, and New Zealand (No. 23). The top two teams in the group advance to Brisbane, Australia for the final rounds in April 2027.

That is the stakes. The Group Stage is not a warmup — every result matters for seeding and qualification into the knockout round on the other side of the planet. USA enters as the heavy favorite to advance from the group, but the quality of the field means clean baseball is required. Slip-up against Mexico or China and the path to Brisbane gets complicated.

27 Days

The countdown continues. Between now and September 12, the roster will finalize its preparation, schedules will be confirmed, and the softball world will start paying attention to Devon Park. For fans in Oklahoma City, the home-field advantage comes with the added storyline of watching four former Sooners lead the charge for the United States.

That is the story within the story of the 2026 World Cup Group Stage. The biggest softball stage in the world, in the city where four of the athletes spent their college careers, with the Gasso pipeline on full display.
