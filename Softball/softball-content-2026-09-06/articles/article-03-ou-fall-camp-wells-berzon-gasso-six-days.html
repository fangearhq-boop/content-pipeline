Wells Goes Back-to-Back Off Berzon — Six Days Before Gasso Heads to Devon Park

By Jordan Reeves September 6, 2026

The Scrimmage Detail That Tells the Story

Kendall Wells hit back-to-back home runs in a recent Oklahoma Battle Series fall scrimmage. One of them came off Sydney Berzon. That one sequence captures exactly what makes this OU fall camp different from any other in recent memory: the player brought in to anchor the pitching staff is facing the hitter who broke the NCAA freshman home run record. They're on the same team. And Wells is still hitting bombs off her.

That's not a problem. That's chemistry building in real time. Wells (32 HR as a freshman, an NCAA record) and Berzon (two-time All-American, 52-25, 2.02 ERA, 416 strikeouts at LSU) are the two biggest names on this roster. Their ability to challenge each other in practice — and learn from it — will define what Oklahoma looks like on the mound and at the plate when the spring season begins.

Six Days Until Gasso Is Gone

Patty Gasso leaves for WNT duty on Sept. 12 — the day Group Oklahoma opens at Devon Park in OKC, where she'll serve as Team USA's head coach. That makes this week, starting right now, the last full week of fall camp with the complete coaching staff in place. Every rep, every scrimmage, every film session this week carries extra weight.

The Sooners' fall schedule doesn't start until Sept. 30 at Love's Field. There's time to recover and adjust after Gasso returns from OKC around Sept. 17. But the work done in these six days — while Gasso is watching, coaching, and making decisions — sets the trajectory for the entire fall. The communication Berzon and Harter develop with their catcher. The timing Wells builds in the batter's box. The lineup sequencing the staff starts to envision for 2027.

The Rotation Is Taking Shape

Berzon is the top arm — no debate there. Her résumé at LSU is the most decorated of any transfer pitcher to come to Norman in program history. But Macie Harter (.346 BA, Middle Tennessee State) is also in the mix, and the configuration of that pitching staff for 2027 will be one of the defining storylines of this fall. Gasso has a short window to evaluate both in live competition before she leaves.

Oklahoma missed the Women's College World Series in 2026 for the first time since 2015. The hunger in this building is real. Wells is a sophomore who already has one of the most impressive individual seasons in college softball history on her résumé. Berzon is a senior who won at LSU and came to Norman specifically for a chance at a national title she hasn't won yet. Six days of full-staff practice is the runway to get them pointed in the same direction.

What's Next

Gasso departs for Devon Park on Sept. 12 for Group Oklahoma, where she coaches Team USA through Sept. 16. The Sooners' fall opener is Sept. 30 at Love's Field. This week — the final six days with full coaching supervision — is when the foundation for 2027 gets laid. Watch for Berzon and Harter to both see significant work in scrimmages before the calendar flips.
