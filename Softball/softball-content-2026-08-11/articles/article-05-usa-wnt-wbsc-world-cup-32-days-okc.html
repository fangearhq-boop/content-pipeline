32 Days: USA Softball Returns to Oklahoma City for the WBSC World Cup Group Stage

By Emily Rawlings

August 11, 2026

Thirty-two days.

The USA Women's National Team plays its first WBSC Softball Women's World Cup Group Stage game on September 12 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. That's the same venue where they went 5-0 at the International Cup from July 30 to August 3 — the same park, the same dimensions, the same right-field wall. They know what winning there feels like. Now they go back for something bigger.

The Group and the Stakes

Head coach Patty Gasso leads a 16-player roster to Oklahoma City that includes two 2020 Olympians — Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola — along with seven athletes from the 2025 World Games gold medal squad. The program's depth was on full display at the International Cup, where USA WNT outscored opponents across a 5-0 run before claiming gold.

The Group Stage opponents are set: Mexico (No. 7 world ranking) in the September 12 group opener, followed by Netherlands (No. 6), China (No. 8), New Zealand (No. 23), and South Africa (No. 36) through round-robin play before playoffs on September 16.

These are competitive games against legitimate opponents — but they are also qualifiers. Group Stage results feed directly into the 2027 World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Australia (April 5-11, 2027), which in turn feeds the LA28 Olympic qualifying pipeline. Every game at Devon Park is a step in a sequence that ends at the Los Angeles Olympics. The Group Stage is not a tuneup. It's a qualification event.

Coming off International Cup Gold

Less than two weeks ago, this same roster was in Oklahoma City winning gold. USA WNT went 5-0 through the International Cup at Devon Park — a multi-team preparation event against elite competition that included USA Elite in the semifinal (a 6-2 USA WNT win). The program came away from the event both validated and ready.

There is something meaningful about returning to the same venue that just produced a gold-medal performance. Teams carry momentum differently when they're going back to a place where they've already succeeded. Devon Park is familiar. The conditions are known. What it takes to win there is understood.

That's the environment USA softball re-enters on September 12. Thirty-two days away.

What's Next

The WBSC Softball Women's World Cup Group Stage runs September 12-16 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. USA opens against Mexico in the group opener before finishing out round-robin play ahead of playoff games on September 16.

Following the Group Stage, the bracket moves toward the World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Australia in April 2027. The road to LA28 runs through Oklahoma City this September — and USA softball already knows this park well.

Thirty-two days. The team that won gold here two weeks ago goes back for what's next.
