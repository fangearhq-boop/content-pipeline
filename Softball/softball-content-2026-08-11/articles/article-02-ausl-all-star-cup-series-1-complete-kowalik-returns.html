AUSL All-Star Cup Series 1 Is Done. Now Kayla Kowalik Shows Up.

By Emily Rawlings

August 11, 2026

Three games. Three team captains. One completed opening series. The 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup's first chapter has closed, and the individual points race is already taking shape — just in time for the competition to get considerably more complicated.

Kayla Kowalik enters for Series 2.

The 2025 All-Star Cup individual champion — who batted .486 and accumulated 1,590 individual points last year to claim the title — sat out Series 1 entirely. She was watching from the sideline while Rachel Garcia, Keilani Ricketts, and Georgina Corrick built their early positions in the points race. That's over now. Whoever leads the individual leaderboard heading into Series 2 knows what's walking through the door.

How Series 1 Played Out

The three-team round-robin produced three games across three days at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois, with each game broadcast on ESPN, ESPNU, MLB Network, or CBS Sports Network.

Game 1 (Saturday, August 8): Team Garcia won. Sami Williams delivered a 2-RBI double in the third inning as the decisive blow — and earned the first All-Star Cup MVP of her career. Williams claimed MVP-1 honors (+60 individual points, putting her at 190 total points after one game). Karlyn Pickens earned MVP-2 honors (+40 points), and Sharlize Palacios earned MVP-3 (+20 points).

Game 2 (Sunday, August 9): Team Corrick won 4-3. Megan Grant hit a grand slam as the decisive play in a tight three-team battle that went down to the final outs.

Game 3, the Series 1 finale (Monday, August 10, 7:00 PM ET, ESPNU): Team Ricketts won 2-0 over Team Garcia to close the round-robin and finish the series.

What Happens Next

The AUSL All-Star Cup format is built around individual performance, not team records. Individual points accumulated from hits, pitching performances, and MVP votes carry across all four series. At the end of Series 1, the top three individual point earners become the Series 2 captains and draft entirely new teams from the 45-player field. Teams reset. Individual momentum does not.

That means whoever built the strongest individual Series 1 performance — Williams with her Game 1 MVP haul is an early reference point — starts Series 2 with an advantage. And whoever enters as a Series 2 captain drafts their team knowing Kowalik will be available for the first time.

The 2025 champion didn't sit out Series 1 because of injury or commitment issues. She sat it out strategically — or by design of the event structure. Either way, she enters Series 2 rested, ready, and watching her competition warm up first. That is not a small advantage.

What's Next

Series 2 of the 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup begins this week at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont. New captains, new rosters, same individual stakes. The tournament runs through August 30 — four total series, one individual champion at the end.

The Series 1 results set the opening order. Series 2 is where the real race begins — and for the first time in this year's All-Star Cup, Kayla Kowalik is in the field.
