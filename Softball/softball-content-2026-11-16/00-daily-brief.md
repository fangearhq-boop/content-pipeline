### STORY 2: 5-for-5 After the Federal Reversal — 200+ State Cases Are the Last Hope
**Tier:** 1 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP (5-for-5 covered continuously Oct 29–Nov 15)
The Tenth Circuit reversed the preliminary injunction in Wisne v. NCAA on November 6, handing the NCAA a major legal victory. The Class of 2022 athletes who relied on a fifth year of eligibility are now subject to the age-based rule as written. Athletes who can be rostered but not practice or play under 5-for-5 — the "rostered-not-playing" category — face a forced choice as programs approach the December 1 transfer portal window. With the federal class action injunction gone, approximately 200 state-court cases nationwide remain the last active legal avenue. No ruling is expected before December. Kentucky head coach Rachel Lawson called the unfolding situation one with "casualties." The double squeeze from House v. NCAA's 25-player cap makes every roster slot that goes to an unplayable athlete a strategic liability.

### STORY 3: December 1 Is 15 Days Away — The Most Consequential Softball Transfer Portal Window Ever
**Tier:** 2 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP (December portal previewed Nov 15)
The Division I softball transfer portal opens December 1 and closes December 15. With the House settlement 25-player cap in force and 5-for-5 now enforced after the Tenth Circuit reversal, programs face decisions unlike any previous window. Programs that most need pitching entering December: Arizona (lost Marissa Torres, D1Softball National POY, to Texas Tech), FSU (lost Torres and Jaysoni Beachum), Ole Miss, and programs that relied on portal transfers now aged out under 5-for-5. December is 15 days away. Programs that have identified targets are already in contact within permissible recruiting rules.

### STORY 5: The Big 12 Is About to Become the Toughest Softball Conference in America
**Tier:** 3 | **Classification:** NEW STORY
The SEC has dominated college softball for a decade. The Big 12 is about to change that conversation. Oklahoma (D1Softball No. 1 class), Texas Tech (No. 1 transfer class, NFCA All-American Pickering), Arizona (new HC Caitlin Lowe, 10 letterwinners, Big 12 DPOY Regan Shockey), and Texas (perennial contenders) all enter 2027 loaded.
