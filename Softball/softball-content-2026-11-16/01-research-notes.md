### STORY 2: 5-for-5 After Federal Reversal

**Sources searched:** WebSearch, Mondaq, SI, Read The Field, Time News, D3 Playbook

**Key Facts (HIGH confidence):**
- Tenth Circuit reversed the preliminary injunction in Wisne v. NCAA on November 6, 2026
- Earlier: District court injunction July 31; Tenth Circuit stay August 21; oral arguments October 13
- Court finding: district court erred in how it defined the market
- Result: NCAA's 5-for-5 (age-based) eligibility rule now applies as written to Class of 2022 athletes
- NCAA Athletic Director Bearby described the situation as "chaos" following the reversal
- Rachel Lawson (Kentucky) warned of "casualties" from the dual squeeze
- Approximately 200 state-court cases nationwide remain active — these are the last legal avenue
- House v. NCAA settlement: 25-player roster cap in force
- Athletes eligible under 5-for-5 can be rostered but cannot practice or play during litigation
- December 1 transfer portal opens — many 5-for-5 athletes expected to enter

**Confidence:** HIGH for reversal/dates (multiple legal sources); MEDIUM for exact number of state cases (200+ cited in multiple places but not independently audited)
