The Big 12 Is About to Become the Toughest Softball Conference in America

By Sarah Mitchell | November 16, 2026 | I Love Softball

The Southeastern Conference has owned college softball for the better part of a decade. It sends multiple teams to the Women's College World Series most years, and its pitching talent, facilities, and recruiting infrastructure have made it the standard against which every other conference is measured. That may be about to change.

After Signing Day and the close of fall ball, the Big 12 has assembled a collection of programs that — for the first time — can plausibly claim to rival the SEC's depth at the top of the sport. This is not a coincidence. It is the product of deliberate roster construction, coaching hires, and conference realignment.

Oklahoma: The Standard That Sets the Bar

Patty Gasso's program is the foundation of the Big 12's argument. The Sooners hold the No. 1 recruiting class in the nation per D1Softball, with five-star outfielder Finlee Williams, five-star catcher Riley Hilliard, and already-enrolled No. 1 overall recruit Goose Hutchens. Returners include multiple NFCA All-Americans and a two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player. Oklahoma does not need to prove it belongs in the conversation — it has been the conversation for years. What's different in 2027 is what surrounds it.

Texas Tech: The Most Aggressive Roster Build in Big 12 History

No program made more noise in the offseason than Texas Tech. NFCA First-Team All-American Kasidi Pickering — who posted a .384 batting average, 50 home runs, and 169 RBI across three seasons at Oklahoma — transferred to Lubbock. Jocelyn Gentry became the first five-star freshman in program history. The Red Raiders hold the No. 1 transfer class nationally per Softball America. Big 12 play opens March 12 for Eric Glasco's Year 2 program. Texas Tech arrived on the national stage in 2026. In 2027, it intends to stay.

Arizona: Caitlin Lowe Begins Her Era

Arizona's first season in the Big 12 ended before the program hit its full stride. Under new head coach Caitlin Lowe — a former national champion player who took over the program this offseason — the Wildcats closed fall ball with a 15-2 win over UNLV and return ten letterwinners. Big 12 Defensive Player of the Year Regan Shockey returns at a position that anchors the infield. The pitching situation, with Marissa Torres departed for Texas Tech, is the central question. But the roster depth and defensive infrastructure Lowe inherited give Arizona a legitimate foundation for a top-25 season.

Texas: The Fourth Leg of the Table

Texas enters 2027 as a consistent top-25 program in a conference that now has three others with similar expectations. The Longhorns' recruiting profile — including the No. 1 overall commit in the 2028 class — demonstrates a pipeline that will not run dry. In a Big 12 with this much firepower, Texas's floor may be a conference tournament appearance. Its ceiling, with the right development from key returners and new additions, is a WCWS bid.

What's Next

Big 12 play opens in mid-March across the conference. The first head-to-head matchups among Oklahoma, Texas Tech, Arizona, and Texas will tell us more in a weekend series than any ranking or analysis can predict. But the argument for the Big 12 is already built from real evidence: the No. 1 recruiting class, the No. 1 transfer class, a DPOY-caliber defender at multiple programs, and four coaches with top-25 rosters and legitimate WCWS ambitions.

The SEC should be paying attention. The country's toughest softball conference may be changing addresses.
