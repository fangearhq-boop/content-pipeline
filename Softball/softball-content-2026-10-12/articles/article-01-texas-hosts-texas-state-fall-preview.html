Texas Hosts Texas State Monday — Free Admission, 6 PM CT at McCombs Field

By Jordan Reeves

October 12, 2026

The Texas Longhorns are gearing up for their second fall home game of the season, hosting Texas State on Monday, October 13 at McCombs Field in Austin. First pitch is set for 6 PM CT, and admission is free to the public.

It will be the most competitive test yet for the back-to-back national champions. Texas opened their fall slate on September 29 with a dominant 28-1 rout of Murray State — 16 hits, a statement blowout that barely broke a sweat. Texas State, a program out of the Sun Belt Conference with its own postseason ambitions, represents something closer to a real challenge.

Torres and Kavan: The Duo to Watch

The eyes of the college softball world will be on Isa Torres and Taryn Kavan — two of the game's biggest names — playing competitive fall ball together at McCombs for the first time.

Torres, who transferred from Florida State after winning the 2026 D1Softball Player of the Year award (.530 batting average, 16 home runs, 56 RBIs), brings a proven championship bat. Kavan, a two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player, anchors the lineup from behind the plate. Together, they form arguably the most formidable duo in college softball heading into the 2027 season.

Monday's game gives head coach Mike White an opportunity to evaluate how this combination performs against a live opponent.

The Road Ahead

Texas has a full fall schedule still ahead. After Monday, the Longhorns host Temple College (Oct. 20), McLennan Community College (Oct. 27), and UTSA (Oct. 29), all at McCombs Field. The fall slate closes November 6 with a road game at Texas A&M at 7 PM CT — the most anticipated matchup of the fall.

That November 6 showdown will offer the clearest preview of how Texas stacks up against a Texas A&M program that is also building toward 2027. The Aggies opened their fall with an 18-3 win over Murray State at Davis Diamond, with Mya Perez going 3-for-4 with a home run and five RBIs.

For now, Monday sets the tone. A sharp performance against Texas State would be another signal that the three-peat campaign is on track.

What's Next

Monday's game (Oct. 13, 6 PM CT, McCombs Field) is free to attend. The marquee date to circle is November 6, when Texas travels to Davis Diamond for the season finale against Texas A&M at 7 PM CT. Both games serve as crucial preparation for a 2027 season in which the Longhorns will carry the weight of defending back-to-back national championships.
