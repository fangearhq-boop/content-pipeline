Battle Series Game 3 Is Tuesday — Oklahoma Puts 2027 Roster on Display at Love's Field

By Emily Rawlings

October 12, 2026

Oklahoma softball's Battle Series returns to Love's Field on Tuesday, October 14, and Game 3 may be the most anticipated of the fall intrasquad series yet. First pitch is set for 6:30 PM CT. Tickets are $10.88 for general admission, and the game will be streamed live on 1Oklahoma.com.

Head coach Patty Gasso's 2027 Sooners have been previewing what they can do since the September 30 series opener — a walk-off two-RBI double by Ailana Agbayani that turned Love's Field electric. Now, with Game 3 on Tuesday and Game 4 scheduled for October 21, the full picture of this roster is coming into view.

The Stars to Watch

Tuesday's game gives fans another chance to watch Kendall Wells, who set the NCAA Division I freshman record with 39 home runs last season. Wells was named the 2026 NFCA Division I Freshman of the Year by unanimous vote — the fifth Sooner to win the award since 2014, and only the second catcher in the award's history to receive it. She also earned Diamond Sports/NFCA Catcher of the Year honors.

Alongside Wells is Jayda Kavan, the two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player who transferred to Oklahoma for 2027. Together, they form the most dangerous offensive tandem in the country — and Tuesday's public game is one of the only ways to watch them compete before the 2027 season begins.

Jaylen Cruz and Natalie Ludlow round out a lineup that has championship expectations already baked in.

External Fall Schedule

Beyond the Battle Series intrasquads, Oklahoma will also face four external opponents this fall: Tarleton State, University of Science and Arts of Oklahoma, Seminole State College, and East Texas A&M, all scheduled through November 4. The complete fall slate gives Gasso multiple opportunities to evaluate roster depth beyond the marquee names.

What's Next

Game 3 of the Battle Series is Tuesday, October 14, at Love's Field — 6:30 PM CT, $10.88 GA, streamed on 1Oklahoma.com. Game 4 follows on October 21, also at Love's Field. Oklahoma missed the WCWS in 2026, and everything about this fall points to a team determined to get back to Oklahoma City in 2027.
