LSU Opens Fall Wednesday, Then Heads to Orange Beach for Marquee FSU Showdown

By Sarah Mitchell

October 12, 2026

LSU softball opens its fall 2026 schedule this Wednesday, October 15, hosting Northwestern State at Tiger Park in Baton Rouge. The game is free to attend. It is the first of a packed fall slate that includes one of the most intriguing off-season matchups in college softball: a two-game series at the Orange Beach Sportsplex against Florida State on October 24-25.

LSU vs. Florida State. SEC vs. ACC. Two programs with deep postseason résumés facing off in the Alabama Gulf Coast — no stakes, no rankings pressure, just competitive softball before the 2027 season begins.

Why Orange Beach Matters

The Orange Beach Sportsplex in Orange Beach, Alabama has become a fall ball hub in recent years, drawing programs from across the country to compete in a neutral-site environment away from their home facilities. For LSU, the October 24-25 trip to Orange Beach represents the most competitive back-to-back of their fall schedule.

Florida State has its own strong fall slate. The Seminoles opened their fall on October 2 against Chipola College and hosted Florida A&M on October 4. They close out fall ball on November 13 at Florida, capping a two-month preseason stretch with an instate road trip.

Two games in 48 hours between LSU and FSU will tell coaches — and recruiting observers — a great deal about where both programs stand heading into 2027.

LSU's Full Fall Schedule

Beyond the Northwestern State opener and Orange Beach trip, LSU's fall schedule includes home dates against McNeese, Belhaven, NW Florida State, Southern, and Co-Lin. The fall season caps with the Purple/Gold World Series on November 17-19 at Tiger Park — an intrasquad event that gives Tiger fans the closest look of the preseason at the full 2027 roster competing against itself.

All fall home games are free and open to the public.

What's Next

LSU hosts Northwestern State this Wednesday (Oct. 15) at Tiger Park — free admission. The Orange Beach showdown against Florida State follows October 24-25. Circle the Purple/Gold World Series (Nov. 17-19, Tiger Park) as the final major public event before the 2027 regular season begins. For Florida State fans, watch for the FSU-Florida fall finale on November 13 to close out the preseason in Gainesville.
