Class of 2028 Recruiting Heats Up — OU Lands Fifth Commit, Florida Adds Top Catcher

By Jordan Reeves

October 12, 2026

The 2028 recruiting class is still two years away from playing a college game, but the top programs in softball are not waiting around. Oklahoma has already secured five commitments in the class — including outfielder Grace Tonga on September 22 — and Florida added top catcher Brooklyn Evans, continuing a surge that is building toward the contact period opening next weekend.

The NCAA fall contact period for 2028 prospects runs October 17-18 and October 24-25. Expect a wave of new commitment announcements in the coming days as coaches finalize in-person visits and prospects lock in their choices.

Oklahoma's Early Class-Building

With Grace Tonga's commitment on September 22, Oklahoma now has five 2028 pledges — an unusually deep early commitment list for a class this far out. Patty Gasso's staff has historically recruited well ahead of schedule, and this 2028 class is shaping up as no exception.

Earlier this month, the Sooners landed Finlee Williams, the No. 3 overall recruit in the 2028 class, as their fourth commit. Williams brings another blue-chip addition to a class that now features five prospects across multiple positions. Oklahoma is playing the long game, and they are winning it early.

Florida Stacks Up in 2028

Florida is building arguably the best overall package in the 2028 class. Brooklyn Evans, a top-tier catcher, is the latest commitment for head coach Tim Walton. The Gators also secured Kylie Holden — the No. 2 overall recruit in the 2028 class by most national rankings — approximately two weeks ago. A middle infielder and five-star talent, Holden's commitment was one of the most significant landing spots for any 2028 prospect to date.

With Evans and Holden both committed, Florida is securing the top of its infield/catcher pipeline before most programs have even gotten started.

The Bigger Picture

The No. 1 overall recruit in the 2028 class, Aspen Boulware, committed to South Carolina. South Carolina, Oklahoma, Tennessee, and Arizona have all been notably active in the class. Tennessee added Emma Pynes (4-star outfielder) last week. Arizona is building a class under their rebuilt staff. The race for the top 2028 class is genuinely competitive at the national level.

What's Next

The NCAA fall contact period opens October 17-18, giving coaches and 2028 recruits their first formal in-person access window of the fall. A second contact window runs October 24-25. The dead period follows November 9-12. Watch for another wave of high-profile commitments across the sport in the next two weeks.
