**Long-Form Post (11:00 AM ET)**
The Texas Longhorns are hosting Texas State TOMORROW — Monday, October 13 — at McCombs Field in Austin, and it's completely free to attend.

### STORY 4: Class of 2028 Recruiting

**Long-Form Post (1:00 PM ET)**
We are still two full years away from the 2028 season — but the top programs in college softball are already building rosters, and the class is taking shape fast.

Oklahoma secured their fifth 2028 commitment on September 22, when outfielder Grace Tonga pledged to the Sooners. Patty Gasso's staff now has five commits locked in — an unusually deep early list for a class this far out.

Florida is stacking up talent as well. The Gators landed catcher Brooklyn Evans as their latest commitment. And don't forget: Florida also secured Kylie Holden — the No. 2 overall recruit in the 2028 class — just two weeks ago. With Holden and Evans both committed, Florida is locking down the top of its 2028 pipeline early.

The timing matters: the NCAA fall contact period for 2028 prospects opens this weekend — October 17-18. Expect a flurry of new commitments in the next few days.

South Carolina (Aspen Boulware, No. 1 overall), Oklahoma, Tennessee, and Arizona have all been active. Who lands the top 2028 class? Let us know below! ⬇️

### STORY 5: Florida 2027 Roster Preview

**Long-Form Post (5:30 PM ET)**
Florida softball opens fall ball this Thursday, October 16, hosting Santa Fe College at Katie Seashole Pressly Stadium in Gainesville. Admission is free.

And when fans walk through those gates Thursday, they'll be watching what might be the most loaded Florida roster in years.

The Gators return six of their nine everyday starters from 2026, plus their entire pitching staff. That's rare. In the modern transfer portal era, most programs lose key arms between seasons. Florida held theirs. Add two new transfers and a top-recruiting class, and head coach Tim Walton has assembled something very real.

When the 2027 national title conversation happens, it centers on Texas (defending back-to-back), Oklahoma (hungry to return), Tennessee (WCWS finalist). Florida doesn't always get the same attention — but the numbers say they should.

Six returning starters. Full pitching staff. Two transfers. A top class coming in. Fall ball starts Thursday. Get acquainted with the 2027 Gators now — before everyone else does.
