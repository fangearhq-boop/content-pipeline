### STORY 2: Oklahoma Softball — Fall Schedule Announced, Battle Series Returns
**Tier:** 2 | **Type:** FOLLOW UP
**Priority:** DEVELOPING

Oklahoma softball released its fall 2026 schedule, anchored by five Battle Series competitions at Love's Field. The schedule runs from October through early November, giving the rebuilt Sooners a first competitive look before conference play. Battle Series dates: Oct. 3, Oct. 17, Oct. 23, Oct. 31, and Nov. 6. **Key facts (HIGH confidence):** Fall schedule dates confirmed via on3.com and thefranchiseok.com; Love's Field venue confirmed; $10 admission confirmed.

### STORY 4: USA WNT — 25 Days to OKC, Opening Match Against Netherlands
**Tier:** 3 | **Type:** FOLLOW UP
**Priority:** EVERGREEN

Twenty-five days until the USA Softball Women's National Team opens the 2026 WBSC World Cup Group Stage in Oklahoma City (September 12-16, Devon Park). USA draws the Netherlands (No. 6 world ranking) in Group C alongside Mexico (No. 7), China (No. 8), New Zealand (No. 23), and South Africa (No. 36). Top two teams from each group advance to the Finals in Brisbane, Australia (April 5-11, 2027), which also serves as an LA28 Olympic qualifier. Oklahoma paces all schools with four players on the roster: Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell. Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola are both 2020 Olympic gold medalists on the squad.

**Key facts (HIGH confidence):** Sept 12-16 OKC Devon Park; Group C opponents; Brisbane Finals April 5-11 2027; OU 4 players; Garcia/Mulipola Olympic gold; LA28 qualifier. Sources: usasoftball.com, wbsc.org, soonersports.com.
