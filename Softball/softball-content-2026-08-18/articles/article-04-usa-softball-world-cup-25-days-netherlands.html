25 Days to OKC: USA Softball Opens World Cup Against Netherlands With an Olympic Berth on the Line

By Emily Rawlings | August 18, 2026

I Love Softball / FanGear HQ

Twenty-five days from now, USA Softball opens the most consequential tournament of the 2026 season.

The 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage runs September 12-16 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City — the same venue where the Women's National Team won International Cup gold two weeks ago. The Group Stage is the first leg of a new two-stage World Cup format, with the top two finishers from each group advancing to the Finals in Brisbane, Australia, April 5-11, 2027. The Brisbane Finals serve as the LA28 Olympic qualifier.

Every game at Devon Park in September carries postseason weight. Finishing third in Group C means going home without an Olympic qualifying path.

Opening Against the Netherlands

USA's first opponent in Group C is the Netherlands, ranked No. 6 in the world. The Dutch are the toughest draw in a group that also includes Mexico (No. 7), China (No. 8), New Zealand (No. 23), and South Africa (No. 36).

The Netherlands has been one of the most consistent programs in women's softball outside of the traditional power nations. Getting a result in the opener sets the tone for the entire group stage — and USA's roster is built for exactly this kind of matchup.

The Roster

USA Softball announced a 16-player World Cup roster on July 1, 2026. The squad blends Olympic experience with recent college standouts.

Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola are both members of the 2020 U.S. Olympic gold medal team, giving the WNT veteran leadership at two of the most critical positions. Seven athletes competed at the 2025 World Games gold medal event, and 10 players participated in the 2025 USA Down Under Series.

Oklahoma leads all schools with four players on the World Cup Group Stage roster: Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell. The roster also draws from powerhouse programs including UCLA, Tennessee, Texas, and Alabama.

Why Devon Park Is a Good Sign

The WNT is playing its home group games at the same venue where it went 5-0 at the International Cup just a few weeks ago. Familiarity with Devon Park is a small but real advantage — the team knows the sightlines, the turf, and the crowd atmosphere in OKC.

The Oklahoma City softball environment has been electric this summer, and the group stage figures to draw a significant crowd for a competition with Olympic stakes on the line.

What's Next

The full Group C schedule for September 12-16 has been set by WBSC. USA's opening match against the Netherlands sets the tone for a group stage where top-two matters above all else. A strong start in the opener could put USA in control of its Brisbane qualifying path by day one.
