Texas Softball Fall Camp Is Underway — The Three-Peat Road Starts Now

By Jordan Reeves | August 18, 2026

I Love Softball / FanGear HQ

The road to three begins in August.

Texas softball fall camp is kicking off this week, and the back-to-back national champions are officially beginning the preparations that could lead to one of the rarest achievements in college softball history. Only UCLA (1988-90) and Oklahoma (2021-23) have ever won three consecutive national championships. The 2027 Texas Longhorns will have a chance to join that list — but first, they have to build the team that can do it.

Head coach Mike White has 24 players on the roster. Eleven of them are new.

A Roster Built for Competition

White addressed the roster this summer with candor: "Of the 24 players on our roster, 11 are new additions, so that means a lot of competition within the team for starting positions this year." Nearly half the team is new, which means fall camp is not just preparation — it is evaluation. Starting spots are genuinely open across multiple positions, and the players who earn those spots in fall will likely hold them when the regular season opens in February.

The new-player influx reflects the transfer portal era of college softball, where even championship programs refresh their rosters significantly from year to year. Texas used the portal to bring in Taylor Anderson from Oklahoma State, a utility player who hit .250 as a true freshman before transferring. The complete list of new additions includes a mix of transfers and incoming freshmen, all of whom will compete immediately.

Kavan's Senior Season

One face that is definitely not new is the one the entire college softball world knows best heading into 2027.

Mia Kavan returns for her senior season as the only player in Women's College World Series history to win back-to-back Most Outstanding Player awards. She was the defining player of the Texas championship runs in 2025 and 2026, and she arrives at fall camp with something to play for beyond another title — a legacy that would stand alone in the sport's history if Texas wins a third straight.

Kavan's return anchors everything. It tells recruits, opponents, and the broader softball world that the Longhorns are not rebuilding — they are reloading around one of the best players the game has ever seen.

A Completed Coaching Staff

White enters 2027 with his full staff in place for the first time. Associate head coach Aja Fremin, who spent 10 years as head coach at Saint Louis University before joining the Texas staff, brings a wealth of head-coaching experience to the program. Hitting coach Shaleah Freeman completes the staff and gives White the full support structure he needs to manage both a championship-caliber program and an 11-player integration challenge.

Texas went 49-12 in 2026 and won the SEC Tournament before capturing the national title. The margin of excellence has been consistent. This fall camp is not about fixing what broke — it is about raising a new floor under what remains an elite program.

What's Next

Fall camp runs through the fall, with a conference schedule to be released closer to the 2027 season. Watch for Texas to be among the top five programs in preseason polls when they are released in January. The Longhorns have the talent, the coaching, and the motivation that comes with an unprecedented chase. For now, the three-peat road starts in August. It ends in Oklahoma City — if everything goes right.
