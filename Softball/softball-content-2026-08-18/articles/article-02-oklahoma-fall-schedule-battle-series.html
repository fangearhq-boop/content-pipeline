Oklahoma Softball Releases Fall Schedule — Battle Series Returns to Love's Field

By Jordan Reeves | August 18, 2026

I Love Softball / FanGear HQ

Oklahoma softball is ready to get back on the field.

The Sooners released their fall 2026 schedule this week, built around five Battle Series competitions at Love's Field and two additional exhibition games that will give head coach Patty Gasso her first extended look at a roster in transition. After ending a remarkable 11-year run at the Women's College World Series — Mississippi State won the Super Regional in 2026, denying Oklahoma a return to Oklahoma City for the first time since 2015 — the program enters fall camp with something to prove.

The Battle Series format, which features aggregate scoring and a competitive scrimmage structure unique to the Oklahoma program, returns October 3 as the centerpiece of the fall slate.

The Full Fall Schedule

Here is Oklahoma's complete fall 2026 schedule:

  • Battle Series 1: Oct. 3 at Love's Field, 6:30 PM
  • At East Texas A&M: Oct. 7 at 6:00 PM
  • vs. Oklahoma Christian (home): Oct. 15 at Love's Field, 6:30 PM
  • Battle Series 2: Oct. 17 at Love's Field, 6:30 PM
  • Battle Series 3: Oct. 23 at Love's Field, 6:30 PM
  • Battle Series 4: Oct. 31 at Love's Field, 6:30 PM
  • Battle Series 5: Nov. 6 at Love's Field, 6:30 PM

All six home events are open to the public at Love's Field, with general admission tickets priced at $10.

A Roster in Transition

The fall schedule provides the first public competition opportunity for a Sooner squad that looks significantly different from recent years. Departures include Berkley Zache, Riley Zache, and Tia Milloy. Gasso has been active in the transfer portal, adding pitcher Miali Guachino from Ole Miss and enrolling No. 2 overall recruit Payton Westra from the Class of 2026. Outfielders Kai Minor and Ella Parker return as key pieces of continuity.

Gasso said publicly following the WCWS miss that the program would look to the portal for additional reinforcements through December. The fall schedule gives her an opportunity to evaluate what she has before that final recruiting window opens.

The Battle Series Format

Oklahoma's Battle Series competitions are a signature element of the program's fall calendar. The format uses aggregate scoring across multiple mini-games within each event, creating an intense competitive environment that approximates regular-season conditions. For coaching staffs evaluating depth, position battles, and lineup options, the Battle Series is a critical development tool.

The public events at Love's Field also give the Norman fan base a first look at the new-look Sooners. With OU fans hungry to return to Oklahoma City after the 2026 miss, October 3 is circled on calendars.

What's Next

The fall schedule runs through November 6, giving Gasso and her staff nearly two months of competitive evaluation before the winter signing period. Watch the Battle Series results for early indications of who claims starting roles and which newcomers make the quickest adjustments. The program's 2027 conference schedule and non-conference draw will be released closer to the regular season start.
