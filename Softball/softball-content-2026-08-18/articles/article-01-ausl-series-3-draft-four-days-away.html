AUSL All-Star Cup Series 3 Draft Is Four Days Away — Here's What to Watch

By Emily Rawlings | August 18, 2026

I Love Softball / FanGear HQ

Four days from now, the 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup gets dramatically more interesting.

The Series 3 draft takes place August 22 at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois, and the games tip off August 24 — the first four-team series of this year's event. For the first time in the 2026 All-Star Cup, there are four captains building four separate rosters, making the competition the most complex of the summer.

Heading into the draft, Kayla Kowalik sits at No. 1 in the individual standings. She got there the hard way — without captain-level advantages, relying entirely on on-field production through a dominant Series 2 that featured a four-game Most Outstanding Player sweep. Her 370 points lead the field entering Series 3.

The Captains for Series 3

Three players will be building their teams on August 22: Keilani Ricketts, Danieca Coffey, and Devyn Netz. All three enter Series 3 having built strong point totals through the first two series, and each has a different roster philosophy heading into the draft.

Ricketts brings the pedigree of a Series 1 captain experience. Coffey made consistent noise throughout the early cup games. Netz has been among the most improved performers entering Series 3, generating momentum heading into what promises to be the most competitive stretch of the All-Star Cup.

The AUSL confirmed this week on its official site that the four-team format changes the draft calculus: captains will have deeper pools to select from, which puts a premium on reading the room and building balanced rosters.

What Makes Series 3 Different

Series 1 had three teams. Series 2 had three teams. Series 3 is the first time four teams take the field simultaneously in the 2026 All-Star Cup. That means additional roster spots, additional games, and an expanded opportunity for players in the middle of the individual standings to make a move.

For players like Romero — who gained 42 points in Series 2 to reach No. 9 — Series 3 is the chance to crack the top five. For players like Lemley, who gained 26 points as a rookie, it's a chance to prove the early momentum was not a fluke.

The individual standings become the most important number at the end of August 30, when the Series 4 finale wraps and the 2026 individual champion is crowned. Every draft pick, every lineup decision, and every at-bat this Thursday will be made with that number in mind.

Broadcast and Venue

Series 3 games are scheduled for broadcast on ESPN platforms, MLB Network, and CBS Sports Network — continuing the television coverage that has made the 2026 AUSL All-Star Cup the most visible summer pro softball event in the country. All games are played at Parkway Bank Sports Complex in Rosemont, Illinois.

What's Next

The Series 3 draft on August 22 will reveal the four rosters. After the opener on August 24, standings will shift and the individual race will take shape for the final stretch. Series 4 — and the individual championship — arrives on August 30. For softball fans following the entire arc of the 2026 All-Star Cup, the next two weeks are the most important of the summer.
