### STORY 3: September 1 — 14 Days

#### Long-Form Post
**Window:** 1:00 PM ET

Two weeks from today, the phones start ringing.

September 1 marks the first day Division I softball coaches can call, text, or meet off-campus with Class of 2028 recruits — current high school juniors. Since June 15, coaches have been able to send printed materials and some digital communications, but the real conversation starts September 1.

Here's what families should know heading into that day:

📞 **Call timing matters.** Programs that call in the first few days of September are signaling that you're a priority. A call in October or November typically means a coach is still evaluating your junior season.

📋 **Coaches have had lists built for months.** The late-summer showcase circuit, summer tournaments, and even film submitted during August has been studied. September 1 is when coaches act on what they already know.

🎥 **Your film is in their hands now.** If you competed at a major showcase in July or August, coaches have footage. If not, the calls may still come — but the evaluation window is already shaping up.

For families of Class of 2028 players: 14 days to get mentally and logistically ready. Know which programs matter to you. Have your schedule handy. And understand that the call — or the absence of one — tells you something important.

The countdown is almost done. 📅

### STORY 4: USA WNT World Cup

#### Long-Form Post
**Window:** 3:00 PM ET

Twenty-five days until USA Softball opens the 2026 WBSC World Cup Group Stage.

The venue is Devon Park in Oklahoma City — the same facility where the Women's National Team won International Cup gold just two weeks ago. September 12-16, the WNT will face Group C opponents Netherlands (No. 6), Mexico (No. 7), China (No. 8), New Zealand (No. 23), and South Africa (No. 36), with the top two teams advancing to the Finals in Brisbane, Australia (April 5-11, 2027) — which also serve as the LA28 Olympic qualifier.

USA opens against the Netherlands, the toughest draw in the group.

Oklahoma leads all programs with four players on the roster: Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell. Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola, both members of the 2020 Olympic gold medal team, anchor the veteran core.

The World Cup format is new in 2026 — a two-stage tournament replacing the previous single-tournament model. With a Brisbane berth and an LA28 qualifying path on the line, every group game carries postseason weight.

### STORY 5: Texas Longhorns Fall Camp

#### Long-Form Post
**Window:** 5:30 PM ET

The three-peat chase is officially underway.

Texas softball fall camp is kicking off this week, and the back-to-back national champions are getting to work on what would be one of the most historic runs in college softball history. Only UCLA (1988-90) and Oklahoma (2021-23) have ever won three straight national championships.

Head coach Mike White has 11 new additions among his 24-player roster — nearly half the team is new. That means intense competition for starting spots from day one of fall camp. The coaching staff is complete: associate head coach Aja Fremin (who spent 10 years as head coach at SLU) and hitting coach Shaleah Freeman now round out White's staff.

And then there's Kavan — the only player in college softball history to win back-to-back Women's College World Series Most Outstanding Player awards. She's back for her senior season, carrying the weight of history and the ambition of a program that went 49-12 in 2026 and isn't done yet.

Fall is when the foundation gets built. This fall has extra meaning. 🤘
