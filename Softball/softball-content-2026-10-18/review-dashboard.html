### Long-Form Post Beth Torina has built champions in Baton Rouge before. She's building again. LSU softball's 2027 portal class has its final shape. At the center of it: Mickey Winchell, a graduate transfer from Notre Dame who batted .335, led the Irish with 14 stolen bases, and recorded 59 hits in 2026. Winchell — a 5-foot-9 outfielder from Livermore, California — takes over in center field from Jalia Lassiter, who graduated. She's fast, she can hit, and she brings experience from a major program. And then there's the story that caught everyone's attention: twin sisters Madison Pickens (outfielder from Louisville) and Mackenzie Pickens (infielder from Ole Miss) both chose LSU as their next destination. Add seven incoming high school signees to a portal class of three, and Torina has built a roster that can compete in the SEC from the first week of the season.
