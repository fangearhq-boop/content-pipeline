OU Battle Series Game 4: Wells, Kavan, and Gasso's Best Roster in Years Return Wednesday

By Jordan Reeves

October 18, 2026

Love's Field is ready. The Sooners are ready. Battle Series Game 4 arrives Wednesday night.

Oklahoma softball will play the fourth installment of its fall Battle Series on Wednesday, October 21, at 6:30 p.m. Central at Love's Field in Norman. The game will be streamed on 1oklahoma.com, with free access for members and a $10 pass for non-members.

After a fall that opened with a sold-out, emotionally charged game that brought head coach Patty Gasso to tears, Wednesday's matchup is another opportunity to watch one of the most talented rosters the program has ever put together.

The Roster That Has Norman Buzzing

Grace Wells, the 2026 NFCA Freshman of the Year who set the OU single-season home run record with 39, headlines a lineup that returns nearly intact for 2027. Wells' power — a combination of extraordinary bat speed and elite pitch recognition — is the kind of offensive talent that defines eras in college softball.

Running alongside her: Cydney Kavan, a two-time Women's College World Series Most Outstanding Player whose resume stands among the most decorated in program history. Add pitcher Berzon (who put together a remarkable fall), Cruz, Ludlow, and a supporting cast that would start at most Power Five programs, and Gasso's offensive lineup is imposing top to bottom.

On the mound, OU returns depth that matches its offense. The pitching staff that carried the Sooners through the 2026 season is back in the circle, with Berzon leading a group that has impressed coaches throughout fall workouts.

Battle Series Background

The Battle Series is an intra-squad fall format in which Oklahoma splits its roster into competing teams and plays full games. It's become a fixture of OU's fall schedule — a way to build competitive intensity while keeping the format controlled. Game 4 on Wednesday is the fourth contest of the current series.

Game 5 of the Battle Series is scheduled for October 23, when OU will host the University of Science and Arts of Oklahoma (USAO) at Love's Field.

Gasso's Program, Built for Another Run

Oklahoma has won six national championships, and Patty Gasso has been at the helm for every one of them. After a 2026 season that saw the Sooners fall short of another title, this roster — led by Wells and Kavan — has the talent to make another deep run in 2027.

The energy at Love's Field all fall has reflected that expectation. The sold-out opener drew a crowd that left Gasso visibly emotional. Wednesday night will be another chapter in what looks like a program poised to challenge for the Women's College World Series.

What's Next

Wednesday's Battle Series Game 4 at Love's Field (6:30 PM CT, 1oklahoma.com) is your next chance to watch this roster in action. Battle Series Game 5 follows on October 23 against USAO. The fall schedule wraps up in late October and early November before the Sooners shift their focus to 2027 preseason preparations. With this roster, there's a lot to look forward to.
