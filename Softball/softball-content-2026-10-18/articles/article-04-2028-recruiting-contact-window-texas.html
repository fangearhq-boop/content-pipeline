2028 Recruiting Contact Window Opens — Texas Builds What Could Be the No. 1 Class in the Nation

By Sarah Mitchell

October 18, 2026

The 2028 NCAA softball contact period opened yesterday, and the recruiting landscape is already shifting.

October 17-18 marks the first window when D1 coaches can make in-person contact with high school juniors. For recruits and programs alike, it's a pivotal moment — the first chance to move from phone calls and messages to face-to-face conversations at showcases and events. A second contact period follows on October 24-25.

And while the wave of commitment announcements is still coming, one program has already positioned itself at the top of the 2028 class before the contact window even opened.

Texas: Already the Class to Beat

The Texas Longhorns have four commitments in the 2028 class, and the list reads like a scouting director's dream.

Leading the way is Michaela Landers, ranked the No. 1 infielder in the entire 2028 class by Rivals. She's already a Longhorn. Alongside her: Ainsley Morvan, a 5-foot-3 left-handed hitter from Logansport, Louisiana who slugged 22 home runs as a high school junior — the kind of raw power that catches attention at every level. Morvan committed on October 10, becoming Texas's fourth 2028 pledge.

Zoey Abdullah and pitcher Lauren Schellhammer round out the class. The connection between Landers, Morvan, and Abdullah runs even deeper than their Texas commitments: all three play for Texas Bombers Gold, the same travel program. Their assistant coach? Cat Osterman — the greatest pitcher in Longhorn softball history and a Hall of Fame candidate. The pipeline is as direct as it gets.

The Rest of the 2028 Landscape

Texas is out in front, but the 2028 class is being built across every major program. Oklahoma has locked up five commitments, including outfielder Grace Tonga. Florida secured No. 2 overall recruit Kylie Holden (infielder) and catcher Brooklyn Evans. Tennessee added outfielder Caelyn Campos on September 22.

Over 1,000 players in the 2028 class have committed to some school since September 1, according to Line Drive Media's weekly recruiting tracker. Many of the highest-ranked uncommitted players in the class attended showcases in Lehi, Utah, and Bellevue, Nebraska during the contact window.

What the Contact Window Means

For families and recruits navigating this process, the October contact periods are a milestone. These face-to-face interactions — at showcase events, on campus visits, or in pre-arranged meetings — often accelerate the decision-making process. Programs that have been building relationships all summer now get the in-person moments that can turn interest into commitments.

Expect a wave of 2028 commitments over the next several days, with the second contact window on October 24-25 likely to produce another round of announcements.

What's Next

The October 17-18 contact window closes today. The second window opens October 24-25, with more commitment announcements expected to follow. For Texas, the early positioning — a potential No. 1 class highlighted by Landers, Morvan, and a pipeline through Cat Osterman — is as strong as it gets. Follow D1Softball's 2028 Class Commitment Master List and Line Drive Media for the latest updates as the class continues to take shape.
