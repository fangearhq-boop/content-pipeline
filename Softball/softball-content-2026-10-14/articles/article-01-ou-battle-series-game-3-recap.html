OU Battle Series Game 3: Weather Shortens Scrimmage, Team Maxwell Claims Victory

By Sarah Mitchell | October 14, 2026 | I Love Softball

Oklahoma softball's third fall Battle Series scrimmage at Love's Field came to an early end Wednesday night — but not before Team Maxwell put up 11 runs and reminded everyone watching why this roster is already generating championship conversation in October.

Weather moved in and shortened the proceedings before a winner could emerge in a full nine innings, but the numbers on the board told the story: Team Maxwell was dominant in its offensive time, and the evening provided another meaningful block of fall evaluation time for Patty Gasso's staff heading into the 2027 season.

What Wednesday Showed

The Battle Series format splits the Sooners into three squads named for program legends. The competition is intrasquad — Oklahoma against Oklahoma — but the stakes are real. Every at-bat, every pitch, every fielding rep is data for a coaching staff building its depth chart for a brutal first year in the SEC.

Team Maxwell's 11-run performance, even in a shortened game, is a meaningful number. It speaks to an offense that can generate runs in volume — something Oklahoma will need when it steps into conference play against programs like Georgia, Alabama, and Tennessee in 2027.

The names powering this team are well-known. Kendall Wells, the reigning NFCA Freshman of the Year, finished 2026 with 39 home runs. Jayda Kavan, the two-time Women's College World Series Most Outstanding Player, transferred to Norman after her second WCWS title. Jordan Cruz and Rylee Ludlow are veterans who provide lineup balance and experience. On the mound, Alex Berzon finished 2026 with a 2.02 ERA and 52 wins.

These are the pieces. Fall ball is where Gasso figures out how they fit together.

The Fall Calendar

Wednesday was Game 3 of at least five fall schedule opportunities for Oklahoma. The Battle Series — the intrasquad component — has now seen three of its four scheduled scrimmages. Game 4 is October 21 at Love's Field.

After the intrasquad portion closes out, Oklahoma has three outside opponent games on the fall schedule: USAO on October 23, Seminole State on October 30, and East Texas A&M on November 4. Those matchups against external competition will give the program a different kind of measurement tool — not Oklahoma against itself, but Oklahoma against opponents who want to win just as badly.

The fall schedule also serves another purpose. When Love's Field sold out for the September 30 opener — Kayla Agbayani's walk-off double sealed a dramatic win in front of 2,351 fans — Gasso was visibly moved. She has spoken publicly about what that turnout means for the program's culture and trajectory.

What's Next

Battle Series Game 4 is one week away: October 21, Love's Field. If the pattern from the first three games holds, there will be fans in the stands, highlights on social media, and another chapter in what is becoming a fall season worth watching closely.

The bigger picture: Oklahoma makes its SEC debut in 2027. The talent is there. The chemistry is being forged right now, on a field in Norman, in October, a full semester before the games matter. Every game — even a weather-shortened one — is part of the foundation.
