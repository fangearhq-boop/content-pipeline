### STORY 2: 5-for-5 Eligibility Ruling — Class of 2022 Athletes Can Return for a 5th Season
- **Tier:** 1
- **Classification:** NEW STORY
- **Angle:** A federal injunction issued by Judge Charlotte Sweeney (D. Colorado) in Wisne v. NCAA means Class of 2022 athletes who exhausted eligibility in 2025-26 are eligible to return for a 5th year in 2026-27. The ruling covers softball players directly — any Class of 2022 player can petition their school to return. The NCAA is appealing to the 10th Circuit, but the injunction stands while litigation continues. This is the biggest eligibility shake-up for softball since COVID waivers.
