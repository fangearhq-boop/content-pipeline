### STORY 2: 5-for-5 Eligibility Ruling

**Sources:**
- SI: "How the Court's NCAA 5-for-5 Ruling Impacts College Softball"
- Steptoe law firm analysis: "Court Enjoins NCAA's Five-for-Five Eligibility Rule as Applied to the Class of 2022"
- ESPN: Preliminary injunction coverage
- Swimswam: "Judge Clarifies NCAA Eligibility Order, Limits Injunction to Fifth-Year Waiver"

Key facts (HIGH confidence):
- Case: Wisne et al. v. NCAA
- Judge: Charlotte Sweeney, U.S. District Court (District of Colorado)
- Ruling: Preliminary injunction granted July 31, 2026
- Scope: Class of 2022 athletes who exhausted 4th season in spring 2026 are eligible for 5th season in 2026-27
- Basis: Alleged violation of Section 1 of the Sherman Act
- NCAA response: Appealing to 10th Circuit; requested administrative stay (denied by Sweeney)
- Impact on softball: Class of 2022 players can petition to return IF already enrolled at their school; transfer eligibility question still unresolved
- Status: Injunction stands while appeal proceeds; other injunctions (different courts) were stayed by appellate courts in some states
- NCAA 5-for-5 model adopted June 23, 2026 — excluded Class of 2022; this injunction overrides that exclusion for Colo. District

**Confidence:** HIGH (multiple independent legal and sports news sources confirm injunction)
