### STORY 2: 5-for-5 Eligibility Ruling

#### FB Long-Form Post — 11:00 AM ET

This is the biggest eligibility news in college softball since the COVID waiver years — and it's happening right now.

A federal judge in Colorado has issued a preliminary injunction blocking the NCAA's 5-for-5 eligibility rule as applied to the Class of 2022. What that means: college athletes who played their fourth year of eligibility in the 2025-26 season — including softball players — are eligible to return for a fifth season in 2026-27.

The case is Wisne v. NCAA. Judge Charlotte Sweeney ruled the NCAA's exclusion of the Class of 2022 from the 5-for-5 model may violate antitrust law. The injunction stands while the NCAA appeals to the 10th Circuit.

For softball programs, this is enormous. Coaches who thought their roster was finalized may suddenly have veteran options again. Players who said goodbye last spring may be getting unexpected calls.

The catch: Class of 2022 players likely need to still be enrolled at their school to qualify. Transfer eligibility under this scenario is still unsettled. But the door is open — and some programs will sprint through it.
