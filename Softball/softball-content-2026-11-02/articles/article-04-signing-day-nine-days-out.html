Nine Days to Signing Day: The Five Programs With the Most at Stake on November 11

By Jordan Reeves | November 2, 2026 | I Love Softball

National Signing Day for college softball falls on November 11, 2026 — nine days from today. Across the country, coaches are finalizing scholarship offers and recruits are making the decisions that will shape their collegiate careers. This is the moment when all the recruiting work of the past 12 months becomes official. For five programs in particular, November 11 carries enormous stakes for the 2027 season and beyond.

Florida — Building on the Best Fall Ball in the Country

The Gators enter Signing Day as the hottest team in college softball based on fall performance. Florida is 4-0 in fall play with a run differential that has been the most dominant of any program nationally. Head coach Tim Walton has Tori Edwards — a junior transfer from LSU — producing at an elite rate, and six starting position players are returning for 2027. No. 2 overall prospect Ellie Holden is already committed to Florida. Signing Day is the moment to add depth to a roster that is already stacked at the top.

Tennessee — Filling the Depth Behind Mardjetko and Nuwer

Tennessee closed fall play with a 14-1 win over Lee University and a clear pitching hierarchy: Sage Mardjetko (16-3, 1.26 ERA) is the ace, and Erin Nuwer returns with four shutouts from her freshman season. What Karen Weekly needs on November 11 is pitching depth — arms that can eat innings when the top two are unavailable — and position player versatility. Tennessee's recruiting class will tell the story of whether the Lady Vols have properly replaced what departed after 2026.

Oklahoma — Depth Around Two All-Americans

With Kendall Wells and Rylie Kavan locked in as the foundation of the 2027 Sooners, Oklahoma's Signing Day needs are about the players around them. The Sooners will look to add speed, pitching depth behind their returning rotation, and position versatility that allows the lineup to be creative. Oklahoma enters November 11 in a comfortable position — the top of the roster is set — so the class is about maximizing the margin.

Texas — Defending Champion's Standard

The 2026 WCWS champion returns to Signing Day with the pressure that comes with a national title. Texas must replace departed pieces while adding recruits who understand that the standard is a championship, not merely a tournament appearance. Head coach Mike White has navigated this challenge before, but every cycle presents new variables, and the 5-for-5 eligibility litigation adds roster math uncertainty to an already complex process.

Texas Tech — Completing the Transformation

Texas Tech enters November 11 as the most fascinating program in the sport. The No. 1 portal class, Kasidi Pickering's arrival, and Gerry Glasco's recruiting momentum have generated a level of national attention the Red Raiders have not previously experienced. A strong Signing Day class would signal that high school recruits are now choosing TTU on the merits — not just as a destination for transfers, but as a first-choice program for the sport's top prospects.

The 5-for-5 Wildcard

Every program's Signing Day planning is complicated by the NCAA's 5-for-5 eligibility litigation. The Tenth Circuit's stay keeps the rule in effect, but the court has not ruled on the merits since October 13 oral arguments. Programs cannot fully finalize scholarship distribution without knowing whether additional fifth-year players will become eligible under a potential adverse ruling. November 11 proceeds regardless, but the uncertainty is real.

What's Next

Nine days from today, the 2026 National Signing Day class will be announced across the country. Programs will post their signees on social media, coaches will appear in videos with incoming freshmen, and the 2027 rosters will begin to take their final shape. For the five programs above, and dozens of others, November 11 is one of the most important days on the softball calendar. The countdown is on.
