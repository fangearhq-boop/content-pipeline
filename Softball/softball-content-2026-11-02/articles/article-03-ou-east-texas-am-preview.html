OU vs. East Texas A&M: Two Days Out, Here's What to Watch Wednesday Night

By Emily Rawlings | November 2, 2026 | I Love Softball

In 48 hours, Oklahoma softball will take the field at Love's Field in Norman for what may be the most low-key high-stakes game on the fall calendar. The Sooners host East Texas A&M on Wednesday, November 4, at 6 PM CT in a free admission game that streams live on 1oklahoma.com. It is the final outside competition before Oklahoma transitions into winter workouts — the last public look at a roster that enters the 2027 season as one of the three or four best teams in the country.

What This Game Means for Oklahoma

Fall ball does not count in the standings, but momentum does. Oklahoma has spent October running through the Battle Series — a series of fall scrimmages and exhibition games that gave the staff extended looks at every player on the roster. The East Texas A&M game is the closing chapter of that process.

For a program with national title expectations, the goal of every fall game is clarity. Which freshmen are ready to contribute immediately? Which pieces of the rotation have cemented their roles? Where do the lineups need work before February? The answers to those questions will inform the winter development period that follows.

Wells: The Nation's Most Dangerous Freshman Is Now a Sophomore

Kendall Wells was the 2026 NFCA Freshman of the Year. She hit 39 home runs and drove in 88 runs in her first collegiate season, announcing herself as one of the most explosive power hitters in the sport. As a sophomore in 2027, the expectations only grow.

Fall ball has been the first extended look at how Wells is developing her approach at the plate. Power was never the question. The question is whether she can become the kind of hitter who gets walked intentionally and still finds ways to impact the game. Wednesday's game against East Texas A&M is one more data point in that development arc.

Kavan: The Standard-Bearer for 2027

Rylie Kavan has been the Most Outstanding Player at the Women's College World Series in both 2025 and 2026. She is the face of the Oklahoma program and the standard against which the 2027 roster will be measured. Every fall game is a tuneup, but for Kavan, it is also a reminder of what the Sooners are capable of when she is operating at full strength.

What to watch on Wednesday: Kavan's at-bat quality. In fall ball, it is less about results and more about process — how she works counts, how she adjusts mid-at-bat, how she resets after a strikeout. Those are the details that will not show up in a final line score but will matter enormously when Oklahoma plays Florida or Tennessee in February.

The Opponent

East Texas A&M is a Division I program competing in the Southland Conference. The Lions are not a national powerhouse, but they are a legitimate opponent capable of providing useful game situations for the Sooners. Fall games against regional programs offer OU the chance to work through situations — middle-relief appearances, lineup construction, baserunning decisions — that are harder to manufacture in practice.

What's Next

After Wednesday's game, Oklahoma transitions into the winter. No more outside games until February 2027, when the spring schedule opens and the real work begins. For fans, Wednesday is the last chance to see Wells, Kavan, and the full 2027 roster before the season that matters. Admission is free. The game streams on 1oklahoma.com. If you have been paying attention all fall, you do not want to miss the final chapter.
