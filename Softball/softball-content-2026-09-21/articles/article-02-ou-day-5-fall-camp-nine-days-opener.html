Nine Days Out: Oklahoma Softball's New Roster Takes Shape in Fall Camp

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | September 21, 2026

The Oklahoma Sooners have nine days.

Day 5 of full-staff fall camp is underway at Love's Field, with head coach Patty Gasso, associate head coach JT Gasso and pitching coach Jennifer Rocha back in Norman after the conclusion of the WBSC Group Oklahoma tournament. The Sept. 30 opener against Tarleton State is now firmly in range, and the question the softball world is watching closely is the same one it has asked since the portal closed: can this roster — rebuilt from the inside out — become something special?

Early signs suggest yes.

The Three Names Everyone Is Watching

No fall camp in the Big 12 generates more attention than Oklahoma's, and the 2026-27 Sooners give fans three reasons to watch every practice update closely.

Kendall Wells arrives as a known quantity and an unknown ceiling. The sophomore catcher shattered the NCAA single-season freshman home run record with 32 long balls in 2026, earning NFCA National Freshman of Year honors and making herself one of the most dangerous hitters in the country. The question heading into Year 2 is simple: was 32 the floor, or the peak? Everything seen from Wells in fall camp points to a player who spent the offseason refining, not resting.

Sydney Berzon is the other half of the Sooners' pitching equation. The two-time All-American who spent four seasons at LSU — compiling a 486.1-inning career with a 2.02 ERA and 406 strikeouts — enters Norman with the resume of an ace and the challenge of proving she can replicate her SEC production against different hitters in a different system. Fall ball gives Berzon and her new coaching staff time to build the relationship that matters in a 56-game season.

Macie Harter brings a different kind of value. The outfielder who hit .346 at Middle Tennessee State isn't a highlight-reel name, but she fills a legitimate need — contact from the left side, gap-to-gap approach, and a veteran presence that a roster transitioning through heavy portal activity often lacks.

Twelve Returners, Nine Newcomers

Oklahoma's 2026-27 roster mixes 12 returners with 9 newcomers, a split that creates natural hierarchy questions during fall ball. Who plays where? Who bats where? Which newcomers integrate quickly enough to compete for starting roles by opening day?

Gasso has navigated roster transitions before — the Sooners have been among the sport's elite programs for more than two decades — but this iteration requires more adjustment than most. With the coaching staff managing WNT responsibilities through September, the first full weeks of fall camp together carry extra weight.

The fall schedule provides eight competitive opportunities to answer those questions: Tarleton State (Sept. 30), USAO (Oct. 23), Seminole State (Oct. 30), East Texas A&M (Nov. 4), plus four Battle Series intrasquad games at Love's Field. Tickets are available — general admission at $10.88 per game or an All Fall Pass for $70.69.

What's Next

Nine days from now, the talk becomes tape. The Sept. 30 opener against Tarleton State will be the first public look at how Oklahoma's new roster actually performs under game conditions. Wells' bat, Berzon's command, Harter's approach at the plate — the hypotheticals end and the film begins. Follow Love's Field closely over the next nine days for practice updates, and mark your calendar for September 30.
