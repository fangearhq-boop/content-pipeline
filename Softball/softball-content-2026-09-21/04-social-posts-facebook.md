### STORY 3: Class of 2028 — Landers to Texas, Schlader to OU; Big Week Reshapes the Class

**Long-Form Post**
The biggest week of the 2028 recruiting class just happened — and two rival programs are walking away with franchise-level commitments.

Michaela Landers → Texas: The No. 1 overall prospect in the 2028 class (Softball America) committed to the Longhorns on Sept. 18. The Waxahachie, TX native is a five-tool shortstop/MIF who hit .639 with 25 HR (Dallas-area record), 73 runs and 52 extra-base hits in 41 games. She chose Texas over Oklahoma and Florida.

Sophia Schlader → Oklahoma: Days later, OU landed the No. 1-ranked pitcher and No. 3 overall player in the class: Sophia Schlader from Waukee Northwest HS in Iowa. The 2025-26 Iowa Gatorade Player of Year is a two-time state champion who went 30-1 with 296 strikeouts in 187 innings as a sophomore. She chose OU over Texas.
