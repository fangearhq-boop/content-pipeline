Athletics Mercado and AZ Rising Win 2026 PGF Nationals Championships

By Jordan Reeves | I Love Softball | July 25, 2026

Two national champions were crowned Saturday at Deanna Manning Stadium in Irvine, California. Athletics Mercado won the 16U Premier title in walk-off fashion. AZ Rising won the 18U Premier championship via run rule. A week of competition — more than 300 programs from 34 states — came down to two decisive final games, and both produced winners who earned every bit of the hardware.

16U Premier: Athletics Mercado Walks Off

Athletics Mercado defeated Hotshots National-Galloway 3-2 in the 16U Premier final, which aired live on ESPN+ at 11:30 AM ET. The game went to the final at-bat, and Teuila Jaelynn Eteaki delivered it — a walk-off hit that scored the winning run and gave Athletics Mercado the championship.

Athletics Mercado went undefeated through the tournament. They navigated an elite field that included programs from across the country and came out on the other side without a loss. A walk-off finish on championship day is the kind of result that closes a perfect run with exactly the right ending.

18U Premier: AZ Rising by Run Rule

In the 18U Premier final on ESPNU at 3 PM ET, AZ Rising defeated Lady Dukes Lamar by run rule. The pitching matchup was a preview of what college softball fans will be watching in the next few years: Goose Goodwin, committed to Northwestern, on the mound for AZ Rising against Jordynn Parnell, committed to Duke, for Lady Dukes.

AZ Rising controlled the game from the start. A run rule finish in a national championship game signals the kind of complete-team performance that doesn't leave doubt about who the better team was on the day. Goodwin and the AZ Rising offense made sure of that.

What These Championships Mean

PGF Nationals is not just about the trophy. Every coach at every program evaluating players for the next recruiting class was watching this week. More than 300 programs competed. The ones who made it to championship day were in front of coaches with clipboards, film crews, and contact lists being built for one specific date.

That date is September 1. Thirty-seven days from today, Division I coaches can directly contact the Class of 2028 — the current high school sophomore class — for the first time by phone, text, or email. Before September 1, coaches can evaluate. They can watch tournament film. They can attend games. But they cannot reach out.

The players who competed this week at Deanna Manning Stadium built their cases for that call. Athletics Mercado and AZ Rising won the championships. Everyone who competed gave coaches a reason to dial their number on September 1.

What's Next

PGF Nationals Week 2 continues with the 14U divisions. September 1 — when D1 coaches can make first direct contact with Class of 2028 recruits — is 37 days away. Next year's PGF Nationals will be the next major evaluation window for Class of 2027 recruits. Club softball's fall showcase season begins in August.
