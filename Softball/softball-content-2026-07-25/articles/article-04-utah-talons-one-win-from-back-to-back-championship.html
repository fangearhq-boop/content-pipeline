Utah Talons Are One Win From AUSL History — What a Back-to-Back Would Mean

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | July 25, 2026

No team has ever won back-to-back AUSL championships. The Utah Talons are one win away from doing it.

After defeating the Chicago Bandits 3-1 in Game 1 on Saturday, the Talons lead the best-of-3 AUSL Championship Series 1-0. Game 2 is Sunday, July 26, at 2 PM ET on ESPN at Davis Diamond. A Utah win closes the series and places the Talons in their own category as a franchise in a league that is still only in its second year.

What Made This Team

The Talons were the best team in the AUSL during the 2026 regular season. They finished 16-9 and won the No. 1 seed outright — the direct bye to the Championship Series that gave them five days of rest before Game 1. They won four of their five regular-season matchups against the Bandits. They handled Portland Cascade, the league's Cinderella expansion team, without significant difficulty outside of one outlier game in July.

Hannah Flippen — the 2025 AUSL Defensive Player of the Year — has been the defensive anchor of a team built around making pitchers like Montana Fouts as effective as possible. Fouts had the strikeout tool and the arsenal to dominate. The Talons' defense gave her the ground ball outs and the sequence support she needed to be efficient.

The result was a pitching staff ERA and a team defense that were the best in the league this year, not by accident, but by design.

The Bandits Are Not Done

The Bandits are not out of this series. Chicago has Lexi Kilfoyl, who went 6-0 this regular season and entered Game 1 as one of the most dominant pitchers in the AUSL. She saved the play-in game against Portland Cascade from the bullpen — four strikeouts, two hits allowed in eleven innings — and she will start or pitch a major role in Game 2.

If Chicago wins Sunday, this goes to a Game 3 on Monday at 7 PM ET on ESPN2. A winner-take-all third game against an opponent with everything to lose is exactly the kind of setup that erases series leads quickly. The Talons know this.

What History Requires

Winning back-to-back championships in any professional sports league is difficult because the conditions that produced a first championship — roster health, pitching alignment, opponent matchups — are unlikely to repeat exactly. In the AUSL, where roster construction, scheduling, and competitive balance are still being established, sustaining excellence from one season to the next is even harder.

The Talons have done it so far. They won in 2025. They dominated in 2026. Now they need one more win to make it permanent — to be the standard, not the first.

Sunday at 2 PM ET on ESPN. Game 2. One win from history.

What's Next

Game 2 of the 2026 AUSL Championship Series is Sunday, July 26, at 2 PM ET on ESPN at Davis Diamond, College Station, Texas. The Utah Talons lead 1-0. A Talons win gives Utah back-to-back AUSL championships and makes them the first team in league history to accomplish that. A Bandits win forces a decisive Game 3 on Monday, July 27, at 7 PM ET on ESPN2.
