Utah Talons 3, Chicago Bandits 1: Utah Wins Game 1 of the AUSL Championship

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | July 25, 2026

The Utah Talons defeated the Chicago Bandits 3-1 on Saturday afternoon at Davis Diamond, College Station, Texas, to take a 1-0 lead in the best-of-3 AUSL Championship Series. Montana Fouts started and led the Talons to the win — the first professional softball game ever broadcast on ABC.

The Talons entered Saturday as the No. 1 seed and defending champions. They won the 2025 AUSL title by sweeping the Bandits in two games. They finished this regular season at 16-9, held the top seed, and earned a direct bye to the Championship while Chicago survived a grueling eleven-inning play-in game Thursday against Portland Cascade. They came in rested. They came in ready. And on Saturday, it showed.

Fouts Was the Difference

Montana Fouts had not pitched since July 20. Five days of rest before the biggest start of her professional career. The right-hander entered Game 1 with a 7-4 record, a 2.36 ERA, and 63 strikeouts — the most of any pitcher in the AUSL this season. She holds the league's single-game strikeout record at 12. In two regular-season starts against Chicago this year, she was 2-0 with a 1.19 ERA.

Saturday was more of the same. The Talons held the Bandits to one run. Chicago's lineup, which had battled through eleven innings to get here, could not solve Fouts in the biggest moment of the season. Utah built a lead and protected it.

The Chicago Bandits — Still Standing, But Down 1-0

The Bandits fought. They came into this game having grinded through the play-in game on less rest, with a bullpen that was taxed by Thursday's extra-inning drama. Lexi Kilfoyl went 6-0 during the regular season and saved the play-in game from the bullpen, but Game 1 belonged to the Talons.

Chicago is not out of it. The best-of-3 format means one win on Sunday extends the series to Game 3 on Monday. Kilfoyl is still 6-0. The Bandits have been here before — they played in the 2025 Championship — and they know what the stakes look like.

But they need to win Sunday to stay alive. Game 2 is at 2 PM ET on ESPN at Davis Diamond.

The Series Ahead

The Talons have won five of their six meetings with the Bandits dating back to the start of the 2026 regular season. Their defense — anchored by Hannah Flippen, the 2025 AUSL Defensive Player of the Year — has been the backbone of every big win this season. Their pitching staff, with Fouts at the front, is the deepest in the league.

If Utah wins Game 2 on Sunday, they become the first team in AUSL history to win back-to-back championships. No team has done it. The Talons have one hand on history heading into Sunday afternoon.

What's Next

Game 2 of the 2026 AUSL Championship Series is Sunday, July 26, at 2 PM ET on ESPN at Davis Diamond, College Station, Texas. The Utah Talons lead the best-of-3 series 1-0. A Talons win closes the series and gives Utah back-to-back AUSL championships. A Bandits win sends the series to Game 3 on Monday, July 27, at 7 PM ET on ESPN2. Davis Diamond is sold out for all three potential games.
