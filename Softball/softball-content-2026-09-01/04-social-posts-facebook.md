### STORY 1: September 1 — D1 Contact Window NOW OPEN

**Long-Form Post**
Window: 8:00 AM ET

Today is September 1 — and the D1 contact window for the Class of 2028 is officially open.

Starting at midnight ET, Division I coaches could call, text, and email prospective student-athletes in the Class of 2028 for the very first time. That first hour of the window matters more than people realize. A coach who calls in the opening window is telling you exactly where your athlete ranks on their board.

Here's what today means:

The September 1 contact window is the first day D1 coaches can initiate recruiting conversations with Class of 2028 athletes — rising juniors this fall. Before today, coaches could only respond to athlete-initiated contact. Now, for the first time, they can reach out.

The in-person contact window opens September 9 at 7 AM ET. That's when coaches can make an official campus visit offer or visit a player's home.

If your phone rang this morning — pay close attention to who called and when. If it hasn't rung yet, that's information too. September 1 is one of the most transparent days in the recruiting calendar.

Good luck to every Class of 2028 player out there today. 🥎

### STORY 3: USA WNT — 11 Days to Group Oklahoma

**Long-Form Post**
Window: 1:00 PM ET

USA Softball is 11 days away from opening the 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage.

Here's your Group Oklahoma preview:

📍 Venue: Devon Park, Oklahoma City
📅 Dates: September 12-16, 2026
🇺🇸 Coach: Patty Gasso (who is also coaching Oklahoma's fall camp right up until departure)

The 16-player USA roster includes seven athletes from the 2025 World Games gold medal squad, two members of the 2020 U.S. Olympic Team — Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola — and 10 veterans who competed in the 2025 Down Under Series.

USA opens against No. 7 Mexico on September 12. The Netherlands (No. 6) matchup is the group's top-seed decider. USA is ranked No. 2 in the world — behind Japan — and will be the heavy favorite in the group.

But here's what makes this more than a tune-up: the top two teams from Group Oklahoma advance to the World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia (April 5-11, 2027). And the Finals are a direct LA28 Olympic qualifier.

The stakes are real. The countdown is on. 🇺🇸

### STORY 4: OU Fall Camp — Gasso Departs in 11 Days

**Long-Form Post**
Window: 5:30 PM ET

Oklahoma's fall camp is entering its most important stretch — and it's doing so without a guarantee that head coach Patty Gasso will be around for much of it.

Gasso leaves in 11 days for WNT duty in Oklahoma City (Sept. 12-16 with Team USA). The Sooners' formal fall opener at Love's Field is 29 days away (September 30).

So who's standing out in the window Gasso has left?

Sophomore LHP Audrey Lowry has been one of the most consistent arms in fall workouts — posting a line of 2.2 IP, 1H, 0BB, 2K in a recent outing. She and senior Kierston Deal are the only two returning pitchers on the roster. Everything else in the circle is new.

That makes the transfer additions critical. Sydney Berzon — a two-time All-American from LSU — is the headliner in the circle. Macie Harter, who hit .346 at Middle Tennessee State, brings real production to the lineup. Both are integrating in a fall camp that has a lot to figure out in a short window.

Outside opponents coming to Love's Field: USAO (Oct. 23), Seminole State (Oct. 30), East Texas A&M (Nov. 4).

The Sooners have pieces. The next 29 days are about putting them together. 🥎 #OUSoftball

### STORY 5: Texas 2027 — Fall Opener 28 Days Away

**Long-Form Post**
Window: 8:00 PM ET

Texas is 28 days away from opening fall ball — and the three-peat chase is officially on.

The Longhorns open September 29 against Murray State at McCombs Field (6 PM CT, free admission). The fall schedule closes November 6 at Texas A&M's Davis Diamond.

This is the first fall practice window for a Texas team that has won back-to-back national championships and is chasing something only two programs have ever done: three consecutive titles. UCLA did it from 1988 to 1990. Oklahoma did it from 2021 to 2023. Texas would be the third.

The anchor of this pursuit is Teagan Kavan — the only two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player in history. Six of the nine starters from the 2026 national championship team are back. Head coach Mike White has confirmed he will re-enter the transfer portal to fill the gaps left by graduation.

The recruiting class is building around that returning core. September 1 marks the first day coaches can call Class of 2028 recruits — and Texas is positioned to be in those early conversations.

The fall will tell us a lot about what 2027 looks like in Austin. 🤘 #HookEm #Softball
