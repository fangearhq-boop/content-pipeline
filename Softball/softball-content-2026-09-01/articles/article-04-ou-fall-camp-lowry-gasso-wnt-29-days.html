OU Fall Camp: Lowry Emerges, Gasso Departs in 11 Days, Opener in 29

By Sarah Mitchell

September 1, 2026

Oklahoma's fall Battle Series is operating under a ticking clock.

Head coach Patty Gasso has 11 days left before she departs for Oklahoma City to coach Team USA at the 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage (September 12-16). The Sooners' formal fall opener at Love's Field is 29 days away — September 30. That window between now and the opener, with a significant chunk of it without Gasso on the sideline, will go a long way toward defining what the 2027 Oklahoma team looks like.

The early returns from fall camp have been encouraging.

Lowry Steps Up

The most notable development in OU's fall workouts has been sophomore left-hander Audrey Lowry. In a recent outing during the Battle Series, Lowry turned in a line of 2.2 innings pitched, one hit allowed, zero walks, and two strikeouts — a clean, efficient showing from a pitcher who is quietly becoming one of Gasso's most trusted options in the circle.

The context around Lowry makes that emergence especially important. She and senior Kierston Deal are the only two returning pitchers on the Oklahoma roster. Every other arm in the circle is new to the program — the product of one of OU's most significant roster overhauls in years. Lowry being as ready as she appears is a genuine relief for a pitching staff with a lot of unknowns entering the fall.

The Transfer Additions

The two most prominent additions to the OU roster are Sydney Berzon and Macie Harter. Berzon — a two-time All-American from LSU — is the anchor of the pitching staff rebuild. In four seasons at LSU, she compiled 486.1 career innings pitched with a 2.02 ERA and 406 strikeouts. Getting a pitcher of that caliber to integrate into a new system mid-fall is one of the key evaluation tasks for the Oklahoma staff.

Harter brings hitting production. She batted .346 at Middle Tennessee State and provides a middle-of-the-lineup threat in a lineup that needed depth after losses from the 2026 season. Her integration alongside Berzon is one of the central storylines to follow as the fall continues.

What the Fall Schedule Looks Like

Oklahoma opens the formal fall schedule September 30 at Love's Field — 29 days from today. The Battle Series will continue before then, but the games against outside opponents begin in October. USAO visits Love's Field on October 23, followed by Seminole State on October 30 and East Texas A&M on November 4. All games are general admission at $10.88 per game, with an All Fall Pass available.

Between now and the opener, Gasso will be present for 11 more days before handing off to her assistant staff during the WNT's Group Oklahoma run. Those 11 days — with Gasso having full access to her two rosters simultaneously — are the most valuable evaluation window of the fall.
