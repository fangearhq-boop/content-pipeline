11 Days: USA Softball's Group Oklahoma Preview — Mexico, Netherlands, and the Road to Redcliffe

By Jordan Reeves

September 1, 2026

Eleven days separate USA Softball from its opening pitch at the 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage.

The event runs September 12-16 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City — the same venue where Team USA went 8-2 to win gold at the International Cup earlier this summer. Head coach Patty Gasso's 16-player roster will enter Group Oklahoma as the No. 2-ranked team in the world, behind Japan, and will be the clear group favorite.

But "group favorite" doesn't mean the road is easy. Two opponents in particular make Group Oklahoma worth paying attention to from start to finish.

The Opponents

USA opens September 12 against No. 7 Mexico. The USA-Mexico rivalry in international softball is one of the sport's most consistent and competitive matchups, and while Mexico enters ranked below the United States, the opener on the final day of the first round will not be taken lightly.

The more significant matchup from a seeding perspective is USA vs. No. 6 Netherlands. That game will effectively determine the group's top seed, with the first seed earning a favorable bracket position heading into the Finals. The Netherlands is ranked ahead of Mexico and China and represents the primary group-stage test for the American side.

The full group includes No. 8 China, No. 23 New Zealand, and No. 36 South Africa. The top two teams from the six-team group advance to the World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia (April 5-11, 2027).

The Roster

The 16-player USA WNT roster for Group Oklahoma is built around proven international experience. Seven athletes competed in the 2025 World Games and returned with gold medals. Two members of the 2020 U.S. Olympic Team — pitcher Valerie Garcia and catcher Dejah Mulipola — are among the roster's most experienced contributors. Ten players competed in the 2025 Down Under Series.

Oklahoma leads all schools with four athletes on the roster, a reflection of the Sooners' sustained pipeline to the national team under Gasso's watch. UCLA contributes three players, while Kentucky and Tennessee each have two.

The Stakes

Group Oklahoma is not just a tune-up event. The two teams that advance from this group join the other advancing teams at the World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia on April 5-11, 2027. Those Finals will serve as an official qualifier for the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics.

That makes the seeding earned in Group Oklahoma more than a matter of competitive pride. A first-place finish earns a more favorable Finals bracket. USA has won gold at the WBSC World Cup in every prior edition, and the expectation is to advance — but advancing with the best possible position heading into the 2027 Finals matters.

What's Next

Team USA opens Group Oklahoma on September 12 against No. 7 Mexico at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. The group stage runs through September 16, with the top two teams advancing to the Finals in Redcliffe, Australia in April 2027. Head coach Patty Gasso will be managing dual responsibilities — she is also midway through Oklahoma's fall Battle Series and will leave for OKC on September 12, leaving the Sooners' fall camp in the hands of her assistant staff for the duration of the World Cup group stage. The countdown is at 11 days.
