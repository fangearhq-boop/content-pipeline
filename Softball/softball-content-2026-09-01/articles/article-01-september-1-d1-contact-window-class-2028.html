September 1 Is Here: What the D1 Contact Window Opening Means for Class of 2028 Softball Recruits

By Sarah Mitchell

September 1, 2026

Today is September 1, 2026 — and for Class of 2028 softball recruits and their families, everything just changed.

Starting at midnight ET, NCAA Division I coaches gained the ability to call, text, and email Class of 2028 prospective student-athletes for the first time. This is the official start of the D1 contact window, and for rising juniors who have spent the summer on the tournament circuit building their recruiting profiles, today is when that work starts to pay off — or doesn't.

The first hour of September 1 is the highest-information moment in the recruiting calendar. A program that picked up the phone in the opening window is telling you, in the clearest possible terms, where your athlete ranks on their priority board.

What September 1 Actually Means

Before today, Division I coaches were limited in how they could initiate contact with Class of 2028 recruits. They could respond to athlete-initiated outreach — emails, questionnaires, unofficial campus visit conversations — but they could not pick up the phone and call first.

That changed at midnight ET on September 1. Now, coaches can initiate calls, texts, emails, and direct messages on approved platforms. Official and unofficial campus visits can be offered and arranged. The full weight of a Division I recruiting machine can turn toward a recruit and make its case directly.

The in-person contact window — when coaches can visit recruits at their homes, schools, or club events — opens September 9, 2026 at 7 AM ET. That date moves year to year because it is tied to Labor Day rather than a fixed calendar date.

Reading the Signal

One of the most valuable things September 1 teaches recruits and families is how to read program interest. Not all contact is created equal, and the timing of outreach is a meaningful signal.

Programs that call in the first hour — before noon, before the coaching staff has a chance to get through other obligations — are treating that recruit as a top priority. A coach who calls at 6 AM is not doing it casually. A call at 8 AM means your athlete is likely near the top of a short list.

Programs that call later in the day are still interested but may be working down a priority order. Programs that don't call on September 1 itself might be planning for the in-person window, focusing on a different class, or simply managing a large board — but for recruits who expected to hear from a specific program, the silence on September 1 is itself informative.

The calendar doesn't lie. September 1 is one of the most transparent days in the recruiting process.

What Recruits Should Do Today

For Class of 2028 athletes and families navigating the contact window, a few things matter most on September 1.

Keep your phone accessible and charged. When a D1 coach calls, pick up — even if you don't recognize the number. Log every call with the time, school, and coach's name. That pattern of outreach will tell you a lot about your market over the next few weeks.

Don't make rushed decisions based on September 1 contact alone. The window is just opening. Programs will be making their lists more concrete as the fall progresses, and the in-person window beginning September 9 will bring additional conversations. September 1 is the start, not the deadline.

For recruits who haven't been contacted yet — and won't be by end of day — that's a useful data point too. It might mean recalibrating your target list, continuing to build your film, or focusing more intentionally on the fall tournament circuit.

What's Next

The September 1 contact window marks the formal beginning of D1 recruiting for the Class of 2028, but the process is a marathon, not a sprint. The in-person contact window opens September 9 at 7 AM ET, allowing coaches to see recruits in person for the first time under official NCAA rules. Fall club tournaments and showcases will carry elevated importance through October, as coaches evaluate prospects they've now spoken with on the phone. The Class of 2028 recruiting cycle will accelerate through the fall and into the early months of 2027 — but it all starts today.
