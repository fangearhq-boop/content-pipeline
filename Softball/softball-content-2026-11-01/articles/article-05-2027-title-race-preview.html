2027 Title Race: What Three Months of Fall Ball Tells Us About the National Picture

By Emily Rawlings | November 1, 2026 | I Love Softball

Fall ball exists for development, not evaluation. Everyone in college softball says that. And then everyone in college softball spends October watching every result and drawing exactly the conclusions fall ball is not supposed to produce. That contradiction exists because fall ball tells you something, even if it does not tell you everything. Three months of exhibitions, intersquads, and scrimmages have produced a first draft of the 2027 national title picture. Here is what the evidence says.

Florida: The Most Complete Fall Resume

No program had a more dominant fall than Florida. The Gators went 4-0 with wins of 20-0, 29-1, 13-5, and 11-3, and their run differential of better than 60 runs over four games is the kind of number that does not happen by accident, even against fall-level competition. Tori Edwards, the junior transfer from LSU, has been everything the Gators needed her to be: 4 home runs and 12 RBI in fall play, with power in the lineup and the ability to change the game with one swing.

Six starting position players return for 2027. Head coach Tim Walton has built a roster with proven veterans and explosive additions. Florida hosting the 2026 DII national champion Saint Leo on November 6 is the cherry on top of a fall that has made Gators fans very, very optimistic.

Tennessee: The Pitching Standard

Tennessee's fall ended with a 14-1 win over Lee University, and coach Karen Weekly has been measured but clear in her assessments: the pitching staff is the best on paper in the SEC, even after Karlyn Pickens' departure. Sage Mardjetko (16-3, 1.26 ERA as a junior, NFCA First Team All-America) is the returning ace. Erin Nuwer (4 shutouts as a 2026 freshman) is the No. 2. Bailee Shelton and a top-six national recruiting class round out the staff.

Weekly called that combination "the biggest question mark of the fall — but one I feel good about." In Karen Weekly terms, that is a ringing endorsement. Tennessee without Pickens is still Tennessee: deep, well-coached, and built to win the SEC and advance deep in the postseason.

Oklahoma: Built Around Two of the Best Players in the Country

The foundation of Oklahoma's 2027 case is straightforward. Kendall Wells hit 39 home runs as a freshman. Rylie Kavan has two WCWS Most Outstanding Player awards. Those two players exist on the same roster. Head coach Patty Gasso has used fall ball to build depth around that core, and the Battle Series gave Norman fans a sustained look at a team that plays with purpose.

Oklahoma's next fall game is November 4 against East Texas A&M. After that, it is winter workouts and February's opening weekend. The Sooners are, as they almost always are, among the first names in the national title conversation.

Texas Tech: The Portal Class That Could Change Everything

Texas Tech had the No. 1 portal class in the country this offseason, and its centerpiece is Kasidi Pickering, who transferred from Oklahoma after posting a .384 career batting average with 50 home runs and 169 RBI across three seasons with the Sooners. That is an extraordinary addition. Add that to returner Jocelyn Williams (27 home run program record in 2026), a new coaching staff under Gerry Glasco and Megan Pendley, and a program that reached the WCWS last spring, and the Red Raiders are a legitimate title contender.

The Big 12 opens March 12. Texas Tech's conference path runs directly through Oklahoma and Florida State, among others. This will be a challenging environment to win. The portal class gives them the talent. The spring will tell us if it translates.

Texas: Defending Champions Don't Disappear

The 2026 NCAA championship belonged to Texas, and that matters. Defending champions carry momentum, coaching continuity, and roster retention at levels that matter in preseason evaluation. The Longhorns are not the loudest story in November 2026, but they will be when the D1Softball and Softball America preseason polls publish in December and January. They will be ranked No. 1 or very close to it, and they will have earned that position.

What's Next

The official 2027 preseason polls from Softball America, D1Softball, and the NFCA/GoRout coaches committee will publish between December and January, providing the first consensus national rankings. Until then, the fall picture suggests a 2027 season that will be decided between four or five programs with legitimate national title resumes. The early signing period opens November 13, which will add roster context that shapes those early rankings. Spring is still three months away. The picture is still forming. But the early evidence says 2027 is going to be very, very good.
