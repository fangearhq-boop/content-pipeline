Florida Softball Hosts DII Champion Saint Leo on Nov. 6 — Gators' Dominant Fall Continues

By Jordan Reeves | November 1, 2026 | I Love Softball

Florida softball's fall hasn't just been good. By run differential, it has been the most dominant fall campaign in the country. The Gators enter November at 4-0 with a collective score of 73-9 across their four fall exhibition games, and their next opponent is not some overmatched JUCO squad — it is the 2026 NCAA Division II national champion. Saint Leo University visits Katie Seashole Pressly Stadium on Friday, November 6, for what may be the most intriguing fall matchup of the weekend.

The Saint Leo Factor

Saint Leo's presence on Florida's fall schedule adds a dimension that matters. The Lions won the 2026 DII championship in June, defeating McKendree 5-2 in the title game at Warner Park in Chattanooga. Bringing a DII national champion to Gainesville as a fall exhibition partner is the kind of scheduling decision that provides meaningful competition without the stakes of a regular-season game.

For Florida's hitters, facing a DII title-winner's pitching staff is more of a test than a typical fall opponent would offer. For Gator fans attending the November 6 game, it is a more compelling draw than a scrimmage against a local club team. Head coach Tim Walton does not fill his fall calendar by accident.

Tori Edwards Has Been Everything

The headline from Florida's fall is Tori Edwards, the junior transfer from LSU who arrived in Gainesville as one of the most anticipated additions in the SEC this offseason. Through four fall games, Edwards has validated every bit of that anticipation: 4 home runs and 12 RBI, including a back-to-back home run performance in the Santa Fe opener and a grand slam against North Florida.

At LSU, Edwards developed into one of the SEC's most dangerous bats. At Florida, she joins a returning core of six starting position players and gives the Gators a power presence that upgrades an already capable lineup. Her production rate — 4 HR in 4 games — is not a sample size to project forward, but it is a sample size that says she arrived ready to contribute at the highest level.

Run Differential Tells the Story

Florida's 2026 fall scores: Santa Fe (20-0), Carson-Newman (29-1), North Florida (13-5), USF (11-3 in game one, 2-2 tie in game two). The Gators are outscoring opponents by a margin that makes even the closest game a comfortable win. That kind of run production against fall-level competition suggests the 2027 lineup has significant upside — particularly as Edwards continues her integration and the returning veterans build rhythm together.

For context, the run differential in Florida's fall is not just about power. It reflects a lineup with contact, speed, and situational hitting ability that goes beyond any single player.

What's Next

The November 6 game against Saint Leo at Katie Seashole Pressly Stadium will give Florida fans a final look at the 2027 Gators before the winter shutdown. The spring schedule opens in February with the SEC portion defining the program's postseason path. Florida enters that spring with a defense-optional offense, a returning core, and a transfer addition who has already proven herself in the toughest conference in the country. The Gators are not a dark horse for 2027. They are one of the first names on the short list.
