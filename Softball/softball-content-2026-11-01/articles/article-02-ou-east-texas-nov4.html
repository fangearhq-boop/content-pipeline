Oklahoma Softball Hosts East Texas A&M on Nov. 4 — One More Fall Look at the 2027 Sooners

By Emily Rawlings | November 1, 2026 | I Love Softball

Oklahoma softball fans who missed the Battle Series have one more opportunity before winter. The Sooners host East Texas A&M at Love's Field on Wednesday, November 4, at 6 PM CT, in what will be the program's final fall exhibition. The game is free to attend, with a live stream available on 1oklahoma.com — the same platform that carried Battle Series games throughout October.

The Battle Series in Review

Oklahoma's fall schedule was built around the Battle Series, a series of public exhibitions at Love's Field that gave Norman fans a monthly look at the roster taking shape for 2027. The series drew strong crowds and served its primary purpose: game-speed development for a group of returning players and new additions who need integration time before the spring schedule demands results.

Head coach Patty Gasso uses fall ball as an evaluation period, a chemistry-building opportunity, and a venue for younger players to earn roles against live pitching. The Battle Series is as much about organizational confidence as it is about final scores, and by that standard, Oklahoma's fall was productive.

Wells and Kavan — The Foundation

The conversation about Oklahoma softball always begins with Kendall Wells and Rylie Kavan, and November is no exception.

Wells finished her 2026 regular season with 39 home runs and 88 RBI, earning NFCA Freshman of the Year honors in what was one of the most complete debut campaigns in program history. She enters her sophomore fall as a returning impact player — the kind of player whose second year often produces a statistical leap as the game slows down and pitchers stop testing her with the same sequencing they used when she was unknown.

Kavan occupies a different category entirely. Two WCWS Most Outstanding Player awards make her the standard against which Oklahoma's roster is evaluated. She has been the defining player in OU's recent postseason runs, and her continued presence gives the Sooners a margin for error that few programs can claim at the top of their batting order.

Their fall work together has continued the development of what is already a formidable 1-2 combination.

What to Watch on Nov. 4

The East Texas A&M game is a chance for Gasso's staff to get final evaluations on depth pieces before winter workouts begin. That means younger players in the lineup, pitchers getting competitive innings, and defensive combinations being finalized. For fans attending Love's Field, the draw is simple: a last look at the 2027 Sooners before they next appear in February.

East Texas A&M plays in the Southland Conference and is not a power-conference opponent, which keeps the pressure low while still providing real at-bats against live pitching. These are the conditions fall ball is designed for.

What's Next

After the November 4 game, Oklahoma shifts fully into the offseason. Winter workouts run through December and January before the program opens its 2027 spring schedule in February. The Big 12 conference slate and, ultimately, the postseason are the destinations. The Battle Series told Sooner fans what kind of team is being built. The Nov. 4 game is the final page of that preview. After that, the wait begins for February.
