Oklahoma Signing Day 2026 — Gasso Builds the Core Around Wells and Kavan

By Jordan Reeves | November 11, 2026 | I Love Softball

Patty Gasso enters Signing Day 2026 with a problem most coaches would envy: she is building around talent already in place, not trying to fix something broken. Oklahoma's national championship window is open, the roster foundation is set, and today's class adds the supporting pieces around it.

The Core Is Already in Place

Kendall Wells hit 39 home runs in her freshman season at Oklahoma in 2026 — a single-season total that set a standard for her class nationally — and earned NFCA Freshman of the Year. She is back for her sophomore season. Rylie Kavan, the two-time Women's College World Series Most Outstanding Player, anchors the pitching staff and is returning for another season.

That combination alone would make Oklahoma a preseason top-five team. What Gasso has built on top of it is the reason the Sooners are discussed in the same breath as national title favorites before a single game has been played.

Hutchens Already in Norman

Before today's traditional signing day, Oklahoma had already secured its most significant incoming talent. Juliana "Goose" Hutchens, ranked No. 1 in the Class of 2027 by Softball America, reclassified and joined the Sooners program for the 2026-27 season. Hutchens bypassed the conventional high school signing day timeline entirely, arriving in Norman before most of her recruiting class peers had even finalized their commitments.

Reclassification at this level is rare, and Gasso's ability to accelerate Hutchens' enrollment speaks to both the recruit's readiness and the program's attractiveness. Having the top-ranked recruit in the country already practicing with the team before Signing Day opens is an unusual position.

What Today's Class Needs to Add

With Wells, Kavan, and Hutchens secured, Gasso's focus today is on complementary pieces. Oklahoma needs depth in the circle behind Kavan — a second arm capable of absorbing innings in conference play and postseason runs. The lineup has power, but the Sooners also need speed at the top of the order to put pressure on defenses before Wells and Hutchens can drive in runs.

Gasso has been known for identifying recruits who fit specific roles and develop quickly. The Class of 2027 from the high school side does not need to be a banner class by ranking. It needs to be the right class.

Roster Math Clarified

The Tenth Circuit's November 6 ruling removed the fifth-year uncertainty from Oklahoma's roster planning. Gasso now knows exactly who is eligible, who is returning, and how many spots exist for incoming recruits. That clarity makes today's signing class easier to structure than it would have been under the prolonged injunction period.

Oklahoma is not among the programs that faced de-commit situations from the 5-for-5 ruling. The program's roster management and the specific profile of its returning players avoided the crunch that some other programs encountered.

What's Next

Class of 2027 rankings from recruiting services will update throughout today and over the next several days as programs complete their signing classes. For Oklahoma specifically, the next milestones are the winter workout period and the spring schedule release. The 2027 Sooners are building toward a national title defense. Today's signing class is one piece of that project.
