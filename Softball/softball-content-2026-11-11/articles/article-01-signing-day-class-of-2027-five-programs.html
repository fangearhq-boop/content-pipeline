Signing Day Is Here — Five Programs With the Most at Stake on November 11

By Sarah Mitchell | November 11, 2026 | I Love Softball

The offseason officially begins today. November 11, 2026 is National Signing Day, the first date on which Class of 2027 softball recruits can sign athletics aid agreements with their college programs. The National Letter of Intent was eliminated in October 2024, but the tradition of Signing Day is unchanged. Five programs enter Wednesday with more at stake than anyone else.

The New Rules of Signing Day

Athletes signing today are not signing a National Letter of Intent. They are signing a written offer of athletics aid — a direct agreement between the athlete and the institution. The functional result is the same: a confirmed scholarship commitment. But the landscape has shifted in one important way.

The dead period running November 9 through 12 means coaches cannot have in-person contact with recruits during signing week. Commitments were locked in before this week. Today, the paperwork catches up with what was already decided months ago.

The Tenth Circuit's November 6 reversal of the 5-for-5 nationwide injunction means programs are signing today knowing exactly who is eligible. The fifth-year uncertainty that clouded roster planning for much of this fall is resolved at the federal level, though roughly 200 state-court cases involving individual athletes continue.

Florida — Loaded and Adding More

Florida closed out its fall at 4-0, outscoring opponents 73-9. First-year transfer Caroline Edwards contributed four home runs in fall action. The Gators enter Signing Day with six starters returning from their 2026 squad and an incoming class expected to rank among the top 10 nationally. No. 2 overall Class of 2028 recruit Ellie Holden is already committed to Florida, locking in the program's future even as today's class finalizes.

Florida does not have a glaring need. Its Signing Day objective is depth behind a strong returning core.

Tennessee — Rebuilding the Circle

Kelsey Pickens posted a 1.70 ERA and 194 strikeouts in 2026 and has exhausted her eligibility. Tennessee's 2027 rotation needs new pieces, and today is where Karen Weekly adds them. Madison Mardjetko (16-3, 1.26 ERA) returns as the ace, and freshman Hannah Shelton demonstrated genuine promise in fall ball. But a third arm — or a true second arm behind Mardjetko — is the priority in today's class.

Oklahoma — Building Around the Core

Kendall Wells, who hit 39 home runs and earned NFCA Freshman of the Year in 2026, is back for her sophomore season. Rylie Kavan, the two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player, anchors the pitching staff. Before today's signing began, Oklahoma also secured Juliana "Goose" Hutchens — ranked No. 1 in the Class of 2027 by Softball America — through reclassification. Hutchens joined the program for the 2026-27 season without waiting for Signing Day.

Patty Gasso's objective today is filling the supporting cast around that core: lineup depth, circle depth, and speed at the top of the order.

Texas — Defending Champion Standard

Texas won the 2026 national championship and enters Signing Day with No. 1 overall Class of 2028 recruit Avery Landers already committed for the future. Today's class is about maintaining the present standard. Texas typically ranks in the top five nationally on Signing Day, and 2026 is not expected to be different. The Longhorns do not rebuild. They reload.

Texas Tech — The Biggest Story of the Fall and Now

Texas Tech has dominated the fall recruiting conversation in a way no program has in recent memory. Head coach Gerry Glasco assembled the No. 1 transfer portal class in the country, headlined by Kasidi Pickering, who signed with the Red Raiders in June after three seasons at Oklahoma in which she hit .384 with 50 home runs and 169 RBI. The portal class also includes Bradi Gallaway, Jayden Heavener, and Moriah Polar.

What Glasco adds from the high school side today will determine whether Texas Tech enters 2027 as a top-five national Signing Day class overall. For a program that has historically been on the periphery of national title conversations, this is a defining moment.

What's Next

Signing Day announcements will roll out throughout the morning and afternoon. Programs will post on social media as agreements are signed, and recruiting services will update national class rankings throughout the day. By evening, the initial Class of 2027 landscape will be clear. The December contact window is the next scheduled recruiting milestone for the Class of 2028, keeping the pipeline moving even as today's class finalizes.
