### STORY 1: Signing Day Is Here — Class of 2027 Signs on November 11
**Tier:** 1 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP
Today is National Signing Day. November 11, 2026 marks the first date Class of 2027 softball recruits can sign athletics aid agreements with their college programs. The National Letter of Intent was eliminated in October 2024; athletes now sign direct offers of athletics aid. The signing window runs through August 1, 2027. A dead period (November 9–12) prevents in-person contact during signing week. Five programs have the most at stake: Florida (elite incoming class, 4-0 fall), Tennessee (Karen Weekly rebuilds the rotation after Kelsey Pickens exhausted her eligibility), Oklahoma (Kendall Wells and Rylie Kavan anchors, adding depth), Texas (defending national champion setting the standard), and Texas Tech (Gerry Glasco's No. 1 portal class now paired with today's high school additions). The Tenth Circuit's November 6 reversal of the 5-for-5 injunction means programs sign knowing the eligibility rules are settled nationwide.

### STORY 2: 5-for-5 Creates Signing Day Casualties — Roster Squeeze Forces De-Commits
**Tier:** 1 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP
The Tenth Circuit's November 6 ruling that vacated the 5-for-5 injunction has arrived on Signing Day with consequences. Programs face harder roster math today: retaining fifth-year players under the now-defunct injunction has pushed some into pulling scholarship offers from incoming Class of 2027 recruits. Memphis already pulled offers before today, with committed athlete Cassidy Ott publicly announcing she was "involuntarily de-committed" from the University of Memphis because of the new eligibility rule. Kentucky head coach Rachel Lawson warned months ago that the 5-for-5 rule would create "casualties" — today those warnings are becoming reality. Approximately 200 state-court lawsuits continue nationwide, meaning the roster uncertainty is not entirely resolved.
