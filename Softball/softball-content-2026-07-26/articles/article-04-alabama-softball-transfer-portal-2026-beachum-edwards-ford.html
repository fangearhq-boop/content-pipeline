Alabama Softball Added Three All-Conference Hitters Through the Transfer Portal

By Sarah Mitchell | I Love Softball | July 26, 2026

Alabama softball did not wait for the recruiting class to arrive. The Crimson Tide went into the transfer portal and came out with three proven offensive contributors from three different Power conferences.

Jaysoni Beachum. Tori Edwards. Karlee Ford. Three names. Three conferences. Three All-conference honors. September 1 — the date when Division I coaches can first contact Class of 2028 recruits — is 36 days away. Alabama used the summer to reshape its roster before the next recruiting cycle even begins.

The Additions

Jaysoni Beachum arrives from Florida State, where she was a 2026 First Team All-ACC third baseman. She hit .406 with 10 home runs and 69 RBI in her final season with the Seminoles. At third base, she brings both offensive production and a position the Crimson Tide needed to address.

Tori Edwards comes to Alabama from LSU, where she played first base and utility. She hit 12 home runs, drove in 45 runs, and posted a 1.019 OPS in 2026. Edwards provides a left-handed bat with serious power — the kind of middle-of-the-lineup presence that forces opposing pitchers to respect the entire lineup.

Karlee Ford is a catcher who transferred from Iowa State, where she earned Second Team All-Big 12 honors. Ford hit .345 with 14 doubles, 10 home runs, and 49 RBI. A catcher with that combination of offensive production and a conference-level defensive reputation is a significant addition to any roster.

What the Portal Is Doing to College Softball

The 2026-27 transfer portal window opened June 8 for a 15-day window. Alabama made its decisions quickly. Oklahoma, meanwhile, saw outfielder Kasidi Pickering — who hit .370 with 20 home runs and 61 RBI in 2026 — enter the portal. Oklahoma State, LSU, and other programs across the country are also in the process of rebuilding their rosters through the portal.

The result is a college softball landscape where proven performers move between programs at a rate the sport has never seen. For a recruiter, portal additions send a message: this program is serious about winning now, not just developing talent over time. Alabama's three additions say exactly that.

The September 1 Context

September 1 — the first day Division I coaches can directly contact Class of 2028 prospects — is 36 days away. For programs like Alabama, portal activity and recruiting activity are happening on parallel tracks. A recruit who sees a program aggressively acquire proven talent through the portal understands something about the culture and competitive intent of that staff.

Alabama's coaching staff is not rebuilding. They are upgrading. Three first-year players with All-conference credentials entering a program with the infrastructure and expectations of Alabama softball is a statement about where that program is headed in 2026-27.

What's Next

Alabama's 2026-27 roster, with Beachum, Edwards, and Ford added through the portal, will be among the most closely watched in the country when fall practice begins. September 1 opens the Class of 2028 recruiting window — when coaches can make their first direct contact with the next generation of prospects. Alabama will enter those conversations having just shown the softball world what their offseason approach looks like.
