Montana Fouts Is the 2026 AUSL Pitcher of the Year — and the Record Books Prove It

By Jordan Reeves | I Love Softball | July 26, 2026

Four AUSL single-season records. One Pitcher of the Year award. One championship start on Sunday. Montana Fouts had the kind of 2026 season that redefines what is possible for a pitcher in professional softball.

The Utah Talons right-hander was named the 2026 AUSL Pitcher of the Year, an honor that reflected her dominance across every metric the league tracks. In a six-team, second-year league that features some of the best collegiate pitchers in the country, Fouts was in a category of her own.

The Numbers

Fouts finished the 2026 regular season with a 7-4 record and a 2.36 ERA. Those are the headline numbers. The records behind them tell the deeper story.

She pitched 61.1 innings — a new AUSL single-season record. She struck out 63 batters — a new record. She won 7 games — a new record. She completed 7 games — a new record. She also holds the AUSL's single-game strikeout record, set earlier this season with 12 strikeouts in a 4-3 win over the Texas Volts.

No pitcher in the AUSL's two-year history has dominated a single season across that many categories simultaneously. Innings, strikeouts, wins, complete games — she rewrote all of them in one year.

What It Meant for the Talons

The Utah Talons led the league in team ERA (2.67), strikeouts (143), and complete games (16). They allowed the fewest home runs (17) and fewest earned runs (66) in the AUSL. That is the context in which Fouts operated — but it is also the context she created. A pitching staff is built around its front-of-rotation ace, and Fouts set the standard that everyone else was working to match.

She went 2-0 with a 1.19 ERA against the Chicago Bandits in two regular-season starts. That preparation paid off when the championship arrived. Corrick pitched Game 1. Fouts took the ball for Game 2 on Sunday, carrying both her season-long dominance and the weight of a franchise moment.

The Former Alabama Pitcher Who Redefined a League

Fouts came to the AUSL after a decorated career at the University of Alabama, where she was one of the most accomplished pitchers in Crimson Tide history. In her second AUSL season, she proved that her college success was not a ceiling but a foundation.

She is the most accomplished pitcher in AUSL history after two seasons. At 24, she is in the middle of her athletic prime. Professional softball, as a product, has grown on the strength of what she and her teammates have produced on the field. Fouts is a significant part of why the sport's first network television broadcast — Game 1 of the 2026 Championship on ABC — happened at all.

What's Next

Montana Fouts pitched Game 2 of the 2026 AUSL Championship Series on Sunday for the Utah Talons, who won the series to claim back-to-back championships. Her 2026 Pitcher of the Year award and four AUSL records will remain on the books heading into the league's third season. She enters 2027 as the defending Pitcher of the Year, the defending champion, and the best pitcher in professional softball.
