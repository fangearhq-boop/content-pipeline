PGF Nationals Week 2 Begins: 14U, 12U, and 10U Divisions Take the Field

By Emily Rawlings | I Love Softball | July 26, 2026

PGF Nationals continues. Week 2 begins today in Southern California, with the 14U, 12U, and 10U divisions taking the field through August 2.

Week 1 produced two Premier national champions and sent coaches from every Division I program home with new names on their boards. Week 2 turns the spotlight to the younger age groups — and in youth softball, the players who compete here at 12 and 14 are exactly the ones who will define recruiting boards in the years ahead.

Week 1 Recap: Two Champions Crowned

The Premier championship games at Deanna Manning Stadium in Irvine, California produced two title-clinching performances worth noting.

In the 18U Premier final on ESPNU, AZ Rising Appeal defeated Lady Dukes Lamar via run rule. The pitching matchup featured future collegiate talent on both sides — AZ Rising's dominance from the first inning was complete enough that the game never had the chance to become competitive. AZ Rising enters the offseason as national champions.

In the 16U Premier final on ESPN+, Athletics Mercado Hovermale/Tidd went undefeated through the tournament and finished it in fitting fashion. With the championship on the line against Hotshots National-Galloway, a walk-off hit by Teuila Jaelynn Eteaki gave Athletics Mercado a 3-2 win. Undefeated. National champions. That is as clean a run through a tournament as the sport produces.

More than 300 programs from 34 states competed in Week 1 across all divisions. The field that shows up to PGF Nationals each year is the definition of nationwide reach for a sport that is growing in every corner of the country.

What Week 2 Means

The 14U, 12U, and 10U players who compete at PGF Nationals this week are the recruits coaches will chase in 2028 and beyond. September 1 — when Division I coaches can make direct contact with the Class of 2028 for the first time — is 36 days away. Every player who puts together a performance at nationals this week is building their profile at exactly the moment that matters most.

The players at 10U are competing for their first national championship experience. The players at 14U are on the edge of the recruiting window — some of them will have coaches calling their phones on September 1.

The Little League Softball World Series Is Next

PGF Nationals Week 2 runs through August 2. One week later, the Little League Softball World Series begins in Greenville, North Carolina, running August 2-9. The summer calendar for youth softball has not slowed down, and it will not until mid-August.
