Georgina Corrick's Complete Game Set the Table for Utah's Championship Sweep

By Emily Rawlings | I Love Softball | July 26, 2026

Everyone knew Montana Fouts would be important to the Utah Talons' championship run. Fouts is the 2026 AUSL Pitcher of the Year. She holds four single-season records. She has been the defining pitcher of the league's two-year existence.

But Fouts did not pitch Game 1 of the 2026 AUSL Championship Series. Georgina Corrick did. And Corrick was extraordinary.

The Performance

Corrick took the ball Saturday afternoon at Davis Diamond in front of a sellout crowd, with ABC broadcasting the first professional softball game ever on network television. She answered with a complete-game effort: 4 hits allowed, 1 earned run, 5 strikeouts, 0 walks. She retired 15 of the final 16 batters she faced. By the time the final out was recorded, the Bandits had nothing left.

Sahvanna Jaquish provided the margin. Her go-ahead sacrifice fly in the bottom of the 6th inning gave Utah the 2-1 lead that held. Corrick protected it from there, methodical and sharp through the final innings.

Lexi Kilfoyl started for Chicago and took the loss. Kilfoyl went 6-0 during the 2026 regular season and saved the Bandits' play-in game from the bullpen Thursday night — throwing four scoreless innings to preserve a 6-5 extra-inning win over Portland Cascade. She had been the Bandits' best argument heading into this series. On Saturday, it was not enough.

What the Complete Game Did for Utah

Championships are not won in a single moment. They are built across multiple games, and the decisions that matter most are sometimes the ones nobody talks about afterward.

Corrick's complete game preserved the Talons' bullpen entirely heading into Game 2. When Fouts took the mound Sunday, every reliever on Utah's roster was at full rest. The Talons had a rested ace, a rested bullpen, and a series lead. That combination — built entirely by Corrick's Saturday performance — is as good a position as any team can be in entering a Game 2 with the series on the line.

The Bandits, by contrast, had taxed their pitching on Thursday in eleven innings against Portland. Kilfoyl had thrown relief. The bullpen was tested. By the time Sunday arrived, Chicago was fighting the rest deficit that had been building since the play-in game.

The Unsung Role in a Dynasty

Fouts gets the award. Corrick delivered the win that made the sweep possible. That is not a criticism of Fouts — her season was historic — it is recognition that championships require depth, and Corrick provided exactly what the Talons needed in the exact moment they needed it.

She retired 15 of 16 batters. She walked no one. In a sold-out stadium, on national television, against the best team in the Eastern division of the AUSL, Georgina Corrick was in complete control.

What's Next

The Utah Talons won Game 2 on Sunday to complete the sweep and claim their second consecutive AUSL championship. Georgina Corrick's Game 1 performance will stand as one of the best pitching performances in the league's two-year history — complete game, near-perfect outing, sold-out stadium, network television. The foundation of the sweep was laid Saturday afternoon by a pitcher who did her job with zero margin for error and delivered everything the moment asked of her.
