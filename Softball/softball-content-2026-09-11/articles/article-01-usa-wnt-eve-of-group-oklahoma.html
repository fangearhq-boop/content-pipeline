USA Softball Arrives: One Day Until Group Oklahoma

By Sarah Mitchell | September 11, 2026

Team USA

Tomorrow night, Devon Park lights up for the biggest softball event to hit Oklahoma City in years. The United States Women's National Team opens the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Oklahoma on September 12 against No. 7 Mexico at 7:30 PM ET — and a trip to the 2027 Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland is the prize for the top two teams when the dust settles on September 16.

Head coach Patty Gasso departed Norman today to lead Team USA through the six-nation round-robin. She brings assistants JT Gasso and Jennifer Rocha and a roster packed with Olympic and World Games pedigree. Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola won gold at the Tokyo 2020 Games. Seven players on the current roster captured gold at the 2025 World Games. The experience in that dugout is as deep as any USA team has assembled for a Group Stage event.

The Road Through Group Oklahoma

Group Oklahoma includes six nations: USA (No. 2), Netherlands (No. 6), Mexico (No. 7), China (No. 8), Brazil (No. 17), and New Zealand (No. 23). The round-robin format means USA will face all five opponents before the September 16 play-off bracket sorts out the field.

Opening night against Mexico is a statement game — but the real test comes against Netherlands. The Dutch rank No. 6 in the world, and a win over them in the round-robin sets USA up as the top seed. That matters on September 16, when the bracket breaks down into three windows: No. 1 vs. No. 2 at 1 PM (direct path to the 2027 Finals), No. 3 vs. No. 4 at 4 PM, and the Repechage at 7 PM. Finishing first avoids the repechage bracket entirely.

The venue is familiar. USA went 5-0 at the International Cup at Devon Park earlier this year — the same field, the same dimensions, the same Oklahoma City crowd. That familiarity is a real advantage, and Gasso has emphasized it throughout the summer.

Sooners in USA Jerseys

Four members of Oklahoma's 2027 roster will suit up for Team USA this week. Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell are all on the Group Oklahoma roster — meaning Gasso will be coaching both her national team and, indirectly, a quarter of next season's Sooners. The dynamic is not lost on anyone in Norman.

While Gasso is in OKC, OU's associate coaching staff runs fall camp. The separation lasts through September 16. By September 17, everyone is back in Norman — with the Sooners' fall opener on September 30 exactly two weeks away.

What's at Stake

The stakes extend well beyond the next six days. The WBSC World Cup Group Oklahoma is a direct qualifier for the 2027 Finals in Redcliffe, QLD, April 5–11 — and those Finals feed directly into the Los Angeles 2028 Olympic selection process. For USA softball, a clean run through Group Oklahoma sets the table for everything that follows.

Garcia, Mulipola, and the rest of the roster have been building toward this week since the Olympic cycle began. Gasso, in her role as head coach of the national team since February 2025, has had the summer to prepare.

Tomorrow night at 7:30 PM ET, the work gets measured.

What's Next

Group Oklahoma runs September 12–16 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. Tomorrow's opener — USA vs. Mexico at 7:30 PM ET — sets the tone for the entire tournament. Watch for the USA-Netherlands round-robin result as the clearest indicator of where USA stands in the top-seed race. The September 16 play-off bracket will determine which two nations advance to the 2027 Redcliffe Finals. Live coverage is available via USA Softball's official channels.
