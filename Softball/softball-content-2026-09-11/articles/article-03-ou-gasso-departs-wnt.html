Gasso Heads to OKC: What Happens to Oklahoma Softball Now

By Jordan Reeves | September 11, 2026

Oklahoma Sooners

Patty Gasso left Norman today.

The Oklahoma head coach — who also serves as head coach of the United States Women's National Team — departed for Oklahoma City to lead Team USA through Group Oklahoma at Devon Park (September 12–16). She brings JT Gasso and Jennifer Rocha as assistants, and four of her own players — Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell — will suit up in USA jerseys alongside her.

For the Sooners, it is the moment the fall has been building toward. Associate coaches take over the program through September 16. And the team they've been entrusted with is ready.

A Fall Camp That Answered Questions

Going into fall camp, Oklahoma had real questions to answer. The pitching staff lost both starting pitchers from the 2026 roster, replaced by two transfers who had never worn crimson and cream.

Sydney Berzon is the bigger name. The LSU transfer spent four seasons with the Tigers and became a two-time All-American over 486.1 career innings with a 2.02 ERA and 406 strikeouts. Integrating a pitcher of that pedigree takes time, and fall camp has been exactly that process — reps, scrimmages, and evaluation.

Macie Harter is the other new arm. The Middle Tennessee transfer hit .346 with 7 home runs and 23 RBIs in 2026, suggesting she's not just a pitcher. Her versatility gives the coaching staff options as the fall schedule approaches.

And then there is Kendall Wells. The sophomore outfielder set the NCAA freshman home run record last season with 32 — but what she did in fall scrimmages may be the most encouraging sign of all. Wells hit two home runs off Berzon in live action. Against one of the best pitchers in the country. In fall camp. That bodes well for what comes next.

19 Days to the Opener

Oklahoma's fall schedule opens September 30 against Tarleton State at Love's Field — 19 days from now. The full slate runs through November 4, with games against USAO (October 23), Seminole State (October 30), and East Texas A&M (November 4), all at Love's Field.

The first public game is October 3. Fans will get their first look at Berzon and Harter in an OU uniform, Wells in her sophomore season, and a rebuilt Sooners roster that lost several key contributors from last year's squad.

By the time Gasso returns from OKC on September 17, the countdown to September 30 will be 13 days. Every practice between now and then carries weight.

The Dual Role Challenge

Gasso has operated this dual role since taking over as USA WNT head coach in February 2025. The balance is not easy — Sooners fall camp and Team USA preparation overlap directly in September — but the structure has been in place long enough that both programs know how it works.

The Sooners trust the staff. The staff trusts the players. And the players have given every indication this fall that the trust is warranted.

What's Next

Group Oklahoma runs September 12–16 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. Gasso and her USA staff, including JT Gasso and Rocha, coach Team USA through the round-robin and play-off bracket. The Sooners' associate coaches run fall camp in Norman until September 17. Oklahoma's fall opener is September 30 vs. Tarleton State at Love's Field — first public game is October 3. Follow soonersports.com for fall camp updates and USA Softball's channels for Group Oklahoma coverage.
