Texas Hits Week 5: Torres and Kavan Are 18 Days From Real Games

By Sarah Mitchell | September 11, 2026

Texas Longhorns

Week 5 of Texas Longhorns fall camp has a different energy than the weeks that came before it.

In August, the work was about introductions. A new face in the batting cage. A new pitcher learning the fielders behind her. A new system, a new staff adjustment, a new dynamic. Five weeks in, those introductions are over. Texas is now in execution mode — 18 days from September 29 and an opener against Murray State at McCombs Field.

The question of whether Isa Torres and Teagan Kavan would work together has been answered in practice. Now the real work begins.

What This Duo Brings

Torres arrived from Florida State as the 2026 D1Softball Player of the Year — a .530 batting average, 16 home runs, 56 RBIs, and a National Player of the Year award that made her the most decorated transfer in the country. She grew up in Georgetown, Texas, and returning home to play for the Longhorns carried meaning that goes beyond the stat line.

Kavan is in her senior season and arrives with something Torres has not yet won: back-to-back Women's College World Series MVP awards. No one in the history of the WCWS has won that distinction twice — let alone in consecutive seasons. She is the standard against which future WCWS performances will be measured, and she is spending her final year in burnt orange chasing something that only two programs have ever accomplished.

Only UCLA (1988–90) and Oklahoma (2021–23) have won three consecutive Women's College World Series championships. Texas won in 2025 and 2026. The three-peat attempt begins this fall.

The Fall Schedule

Texas released its 2027 schedule earlier this week — a signal that the program is already thinking about what comes after this fall. But the immediate calendar is McCombs Field, September 29, 6:00 PM CT, free admission. Murray State is the opener. The fall schedule closes November 6 with a road trip to Texas A&M.

Each home fall game at McCombs is free for the public — an opportunity to see the defending national champions in a live setting before the spring season begins. Head coach Mike White has continued building the program's depth around a core of returners and key additions, including Torres.

Katie Stewart holds the Texas program record with 31 home runs. That benchmark provides internal context as Torres gets her first extended run in Longhorns gear.

What's Next

Texas opens its fall schedule September 29 against Murray State at McCombs Field, 6:00 PM CT, free admission. The fall slate runs through November 6 at Texas A&M.
