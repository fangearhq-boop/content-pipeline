Two Days to Devon Park: USA Softball Enters Final Countdown to Group Oklahoma

By Sarah Mitchell | September 10, 2026

USA Softball / International

Thursday is two days away, and everything USA Softball has built since February now points to one place: Devon Park, Oklahoma City.

The WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Oklahoma opens Thursday, September 12, with USA vs. No. 7 Mexico at 7:30 PM CDT — and the tournament that runs through September 16 will determine which two of the six competing nations earn direct berths to the 2027 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia. That Finals is the first formal qualification step on the road to the LA28 Olympic Games.

In other words: Thursday's opener matters more than a single game usually does.

The Field

Six nations compete in the round-robin portion: No. 1 USA, No. 6 Netherlands, No. 7 Mexico, No. 8 China, No. 17 Brazil, and No. 23 New Zealand. The top two finishers advance directly to Redcliffe. Third and fourth place enter the Repechage bracket.

The Netherlands matchup is the one that will determine seeding — No. 6 Netherlands is the only team in the field that can seriously contest USA for the top position. That game, whenever it falls in the round-robin, functions as the tournament's most consequential moment for long-term standing. USA's objective heading in is not simply to advance — it is to advance as the No. 1 seed, carrying momentum into the 2027 Finals.

Home Field Advantage

USA has played at Devon Park before. Earlier in 2026, the USA Softball International Cup took place at the same OGE Energy Field complex — and USA went 5-0. The venue is familiar. The crowd in Oklahoma is familiar. Head Coach Patty Gasso coaches at the University of Oklahoma and brings back a roster that includes four Sooners: Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell.

That connection runs deeper than geography. The players who train in Norman all year know what Oklahoma softball crowds sound like. Devon Park on Thursday will feel like home for a significant portion of this roster.

The Roster's Depth

Rachel Garcia and Dejah Mulipola both competed on the 2020 U.S. Olympic team that won gold. Seven players on this 16-person roster won gold medals at the 2025 World Games. Ten made the 2025 Down Under Series trip. USA has won 11 of 14 World Cup editions in tournament history.

Gasso, who was named USA WNT head coach in February 2025, brings assistants JT Gasso and Jennifer Rocha with her to Oklahoma City — the same staff that has been building toward this tournament for eighteen months.

What's Next

The round-robin runs September 12–15, with play-offs on September 16. The play-off format: first vs. second at 1:00 PM (both advance to Finals 2027), third vs. fourth at 4:00 PM, and the Repechage at 7:00 PM. Two teams leave Oklahoma City with their 2027 Finals berths secured. The others enter the longer road to Australia.

USA steps onto that field in two days. Everything that has happened since February has been preparation for a four-day tournament that decides the next two years of American softball's Olympic path.
