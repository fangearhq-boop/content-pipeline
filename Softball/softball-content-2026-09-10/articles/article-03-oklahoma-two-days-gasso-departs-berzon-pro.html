Two Days Left With Gasso: OU Softball's Final Camp Push With Full Staff

By Jordan Reeves | September 10, 2026

Oklahoma Sooners Softball

Patty Gasso leaves for Team USA in two days. When she gets on that plane to Oklahoma City Thursday morning, she takes JT Gasso and Jennifer Rocha with her — the three coaches who form the core of Oklahoma's staff will be at Devon Park through September 16, coaching the USA Women's National Team in the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Oklahoma.

That leaves Love's Field to operate without them for five days. And it means these next 48 hours are the last full window before the fall camp dynamic changes.

The Pitching Picture

The most significant development at Love's Field this fall isn't a practice result or a scrimmage box score. It's an absence.

Sydney Berzon — who transferred from LSU as one of the most decorated arms in college softball, bringing a 486.1-inning career, 2.02 ERA, 406 strikeouts, and 2x All-American status with her — signed professionally with the Texas Volts in the AUSL in June 2026. She is not in fall camp. The pitching staff role she was expected to fill entering this cycle is now an open competition.

That competition is exactly what fall camp is for. Berzon going pro is not a crisis — it is a clarifying moment. OU now knows which arms are available, which ones can carry a postseason workload, and which ones are ready to grow into a championship pitching staff without a pre-established answer at the top of the rotation. Gasso has two days to set the evaluation framework before departing.

Wells as the Anchor

While the pitching staff sorts itself out, Kendall Wells remains the offense's fixed point. Entering her junior year, Wells carries two seasons' worth of records into fall camp: 32 home runs in her freshman season — the NCAA freshman HR record — followed by 39 home runs in her sophomore year, which set both the OU and SEC single-season records.

There is no comparable hitter in fall camp, and perhaps not in college softball as a whole right now. Whatever gets answered about the pitching staff in the coming weeks, the offense starts with Wells and builds outward from there.

The Fall Schedule

OU's fall schedule opens September 30 with a Battle Series intrasquad game at Love's Field. The first game against outside competition is October 3 versus Tarleton State at 1:00 PM. The full slate runs through a November 4 matchup against East Texas A&M. Twenty days from today, the public gets its first look at how this roster is taking shape.

Two days with Gasso is not a lot of time. But in fall camp, focused time is exactly what matters.

What's Next

Gasso returns from WNT duties following the Group Oklahoma conclusion on September 16. That gives OU approximately two weeks before the September 30 opener to reassemble the full coaching staff and finalize the picture. The pitching staff competition that began in August will have its first public test at Love's Field — with the full coaching staff back to evaluate it.

The next 48 hours are the last complete window before that split. OU is using every minute of them.
