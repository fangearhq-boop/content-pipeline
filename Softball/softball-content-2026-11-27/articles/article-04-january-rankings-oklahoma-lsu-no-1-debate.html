The January Rankings Debate: Can Oklahoma Lock Down the No. 1 Spot Before the Portal Changes Everything?

By Jordan Reeves | November 27, 2026 | I Love Softball

The two most important rankings in college softball do not agree on who is No. 1.

D1Softball has Oklahoma at the top of the 2027 preseason class standings. Softball America has LSU at No. 1 — the first time in program history that the Tigers have held that ranking. Both assessments are defensible. Neither is final. January is when the first official preseason polls drop, and by that point, the December portal window will have already reshuffled rosters across the country.

The team that wins the No. 1 debate may not be the team that is ranked No. 1 right now.

The Case for Oklahoma

Oklahoma's argument for No. 1 starts with the two players already on the roster.

Paige Kavan is a two-time WCWS Most Outstanding Player, returning for another season in Norman. Jayda Coleman Wells hit 39 home runs in 2026 and was named the NFCA Freshman of the Year. Those two players, side by side in a lineup, represent perhaps the most dangerous offensive one-two combination in Division I softball.

The recruiting class adds to it. Julianna "Goose" Hutchens — the No. 1 overall recruit in the Class of 2027 — has already reclassified and enrolled at Oklahoma, skipping her senior year of high school to begin her college career early. Five-star outfielder Finlee Williams and five-star catcher Riley Hilliard have both signed. Emma Reynolds is committed. D1Softball ranks the Sooners' class No. 1 nationally by a significant margin.

The one gap: Oklahoma does not yet have a proven frontline pitcher. The program lost Kasidi Pickering — who hit .384 with 50 home runs and 169 RBI over three seasons — to Texas Tech. It is a roster that can win a national title, but only if the December portal window fills the rotation need.

The Case for LSU

Beth Torina's Tigers reached the No. 1 ranking in Softball America for the first time in program history, and the class that got them there is built for the long game.

Five-star utility player Sydney Gonglik is one of the most versatile incoming prospects in the country. Pitcher Macie Bryant, ranked No. 22 nationally, adds arm depth to a program that has historically been built on offense. The portal additions of Mackenzie and Madison Pickens, the twins who entered the portal from their previous program, give LSU the kind of experience and production that high school signees cannot replicate.

LSU's class represents the culmination of Torina's sustained recruiting investment in an era where the SEC has become the dominant force in college softball — with six programs in most national top-10 lists.

The Dark Horses

Tennessee is not generating the same headlines, but the numbers are stark. The Vols return nine starters from a 49-win season. Sage Mardjetko, who posted a 16-3 record with a 1.26 ERA in 2026, is back for her senior season alongside junior pitcher Marissa Nuwer. Karen Weekly, whose contract has been extended through 2030, also added the No. 1-ranked pitcher in the Class of 2027 — Kaylee Hodges — on National Signing Day. Tennessee may enter January without the No. 1 class ranking, but it may enter the spring with the most complete roster in college softball.

Florida is No. 2 in Softball America's class rankings and added Tori Edwards in the transfer portal from LSU. The Gators have been in the top five of preseason polls in recent years but have never broken through to win a national championship — a distinction that will follow the program until it doesn't.

What Changes in December

Preseason rankings are not settled in November. The December portal window runs December 1-15, and every major program is expected to make additions. Arizona is hunting a frontline pitcher. Oklahoma may add to its rotation. Tennessee and LSU could target specific position upgrades.

Whatever the rankings look like today, January's official polls will reflect a substantially different set of rosters. The program that uses the next two weeks most effectively may enter the new year with a legitimate claim to the No. 1 spot — regardless of where the current class standings put them.

What's Next

The December transfer portal window opens December 1 and closes December 15. The dead period (December 8-12) means programs have roughly seven effective days to close portal commitments. National preseason polls are expected in January — the first official measurement of where every program stands with its fully assembled 2027 roster. Oklahoma, LSU, Tennessee, and Florida are all legitimate candidates for the top spot, with Tennessee's nine returning starters possibly the least appreciated piece of the preseason puzzle.
