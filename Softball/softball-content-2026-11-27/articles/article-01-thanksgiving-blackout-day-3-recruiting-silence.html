Thanksgiving Blackout Day 3: College Softball's Recruiting Silence Hits Its Midpoint

By Jordan Reeves | November 27, 2026 | I Love Softball

Today is Day 3. The silence continues.

For the third consecutive day, every Division I softball coach in the country is prohibited from contacting any recruit — not by phone, not by text, not by email, not by direct message on social media. The NCAA's annual Thanksgiving recruiting blackout, which runs from November 25 through November 29, is at its midpoint, and the sport is in a forced pause at one of the most consequential moments of the offseason calendar.

Contact resumes Saturday, November 29. Twenty-four hours after that, the December transfer portal window opens.

What the Blackout Actually Prohibits

The Thanksgiving blackout applies to all forms of recruiting communication — not just phone calls or in-person visits. Under NCAA Division I rules, coaches cannot initiate or respond to any contact with prospective student-athletes during this period. That includes text messages, emails, group chats, and direct messages on platforms like Twitter, Instagram, or TikTok.

The prohibition is not limited to the Class of 2027. It covers recruits from every class year — a 2028 commit, a 2029 eighth-grader who has already received a scholarship offer, an unsigned 2027 prospect still weighing her options. All communication stops for five days.

The blackout has existed on the softball calendar for years. What is different in 2026 is the timing. The five-day freeze comes immediately before the December transfer portal window, which opens December 1. For programs with critical needs to address — an ace pitcher, a cleanup hitter, a catcher — the blackout extends the wait by nearly a week.

What Programs Are Doing Instead

The silence does not mean inactivity. Coaches cannot contact recruits, but they can prepare for the conversations that start Saturday.

That preparation typically includes reviewing scholarship offer commitments, finalizing paperwork for expected portal entries, coordinating official visit invitations for the December window, and mapping out roster scenarios. Programs that enter the portal window without clarity on their scholarship situation, their available spots, and their priority targets lose ground on the first day.

At Arizona, the preparation is particularly urgent. The Wildcats lost D1Softball Player of the Year Mariah Torres to Texas Tech in June. They have been monitoring potential portal pitchers for months. When the December 1 window opens, Arizona will need to move immediately — the dead period begins December 8, giving the program only seven effective days to identify, contact, and secure a replacement.

At Oklahoma, the preparation is about paperwork. The Sooners hold the No. 1 class in the country per D1Softball, and much of that class is already committed. The focus heading into December is converting verbal commitments into formal agreements and potentially adding a final piece through the portal.

The Clock Behind the Silence

The Thanksgiving blackout ends Saturday, November 29. Contact with recruits resumes that day, giving coaches 24 hours to reconnect before the portal window opens.

December 1 is when the transfer portal window opens for the 2026-27 cycle. Players who have been waiting to enter can submit their portal paperwork, and programs can begin contacting them. The window runs through December 15.

But the dead period — December 8-12 — cuts the effective working window to just seven days. Programs that have not closed their most important portal deals by December 7 must wait until after the dead period ends before resuming communication with those recruits.

Five days of silence now. Seven days of urgency starting Sunday. College softball's most compressed and consequential stretch of the offseason begins the moment the blackout lifts.

What's Next

The Thanksgiving recruiting blackout ends November 29. Coaches can resume contact with all recruits that day. The December transfer portal window opens December 1 and runs through December 15. The dead period runs December 8-12, limiting effective portal activity to the first seven days of the window. National preseason polls are expected in January, with the full picture of the Class of 2027 and December portal moves factoring into early rankings for the first time.
