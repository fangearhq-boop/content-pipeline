### STORY 3: USA WNT 13 Days
**Tier 2 — FB Long-Form + FB Image Caption**

**FB Long-Form Post**
Window: 1:00 PM ET

USA Softball is 13 days away from opening pitch in Oklahoma City.

The 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage runs September 12-16 at Devon Park in OKC — and with an LA28 Olympic berth ultimately on the line, this is one of the biggest events in international softball this decade.

Head coach Patty Gasso leads the coaching staff, joined by JT Gasso (assistant) and Jennifer Rocha (pitching coach). The roster that Gasso assembled includes:

🥇 7 athletes from the 2025 World Games gold medal squad
🏅 2020 Tokyo Olympians Valerie Garcia and Aubree Mulipola
🇺🇸 10 players who competed in the 2025 USA Down Under Series

USA opens against No. 7 Mexico on September 12 — then faces the tougher test: No. 6 Netherlands, which will likely decide who finishes on top of the group.

The top 2 teams from Group Oklahoma advance to the World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Australia (April 5-11, 2027). The highest-placed finisher at the Finals earns a berth to the LA28 Olympics.

Thirteen days. 🇺🇸
