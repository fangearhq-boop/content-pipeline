September 1 Is Two Days Away: What Class of 2028 Recruits Need to Know Right Now

By Emily Rawlings

August 30, 2026

The countdown is real. September 1 — the single most important date in the Division I softball recruiting calendar for Class of 2028 prospects — is two days away.

Under NCAA rules, Division I coaches cannot initiate contact with prospects until September 1 of the student-athlete's junior year. For the Class of 2028, that means Saturday is the first morning coaches can officially pick up the phone, send a text, or write an email. And for families who have spent months — sometimes years — preparing for this moment, two days is not much time.

Why the First Hour Matters

When the contact window opens at midnight on September 1, Division I programs do not wait until business hours. Coaches finalize their call lists in advance, and the order of contact is not random — it reflects the internal priority board each staff has built through film review, camp evaluations, and recruiting services.

A call before 8 AM on September 1 is a signal. It tells a recruit and her family that she is at or near the top of that program's board for the Class of 2028. A call later in the day, or later in the week, is also information.

This is not a system designed to cause anxiety. It is simply how recruiting works at the highest level of college softball. Knowing the mechanics gives families a clear framework for interpreting what happens — and what doesn't happen — in the first 48 hours after the window opens.

The September 9 In-Person Window

The initial contact window — calls, texts, and emails — opens September 1. But the next major milestone follows eight days later: September 9 at 7 AM ET marks the opening of the in-person contact window, when coaches can make official visits to watch a recruit compete or connect face-to-face at a recruiting event.

For recruits who have been attending camps and showcases through the summer, September 9 is when that visibility translates into authorized, direct coach interaction in person. Both dates are significant. Both require preparation.

Final Prep Steps

There is no preparation that can be completed in two days that wasn't started months ago. But there are a few things recruits and families can do right now: make sure contact information is current and accessible, ensure highlight film is updated and easy to share, confirm any coaches who have expressed interest know how to reach you, and be available and responsive on September 1 and the days that follow.

This is the moment the recruiting process pivots from prospect to prospect-being-recruited. Two more days.
