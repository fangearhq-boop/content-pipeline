13 Days Out: USA Softball Prepares to Open WBSC World Cup Group Stage in OKC

By Jordan Reeves

August 30, 2026

Thirteen days separate USA Softball from the opening pitch of one of the most consequential events in the sport's international calendar.

The 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage runs September 12-16 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City — the same venue where USA Softball went 5-0 to capture International Cup gold one month ago. Now the stakes are significantly higher: the path from Group Oklahoma leads, ultimately, to a berth at the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics.

The Roster and Coaching Staff

Head coach Patty Gasso — who simultaneously manages the Oklahoma Sooners fall program — leads the USA coaching staff, joined by assistant JT Gasso and pitching coach Jennifer Rocha.

The 15-player roster reflects the program's depth. Seven athletes from the 2025 World Games gold medal squad are on the team, providing championship experience at the international level. Ten players competed in the 2025 USA Down Under Series. Among them are 2020 Tokyo Olympians Valerie Garcia and Aubree Mulipola — two of the most experienced players in USA Softball history still active in the program.

The Group Oklahoma Field

USA enters as one of six teams competing in Group Oklahoma. The full field:

  • USA — World No. 2
  • Netherlands — No. 6
  • Mexico — No. 7 (USA's first opponent, September 12)
  • China — No. 8
  • New Zealand — No. 23
  • South Africa — No. 36

USA's opening game against No. 7 Mexico on September 12 should be manageable. The more significant test is the matchup against No. 6 Netherlands, which carries top-of-group implications. The top two teams from Group Oklahoma advance to the World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, Australia (April 5-11, 2027).

The LA28 Stakes

Group-stage advancement leads to the Finals — and the Finals determine the next qualifier in the pathway to the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. The highest-placed team from each group at the Finals earns a berth to LA28.

USA has already qualified via the International Cup, which gives the program a runway to manage roster decisions and game plans carefully at the Group Stage. But finishing as high as possible in Group Oklahoma still matters for seeding in Redcliffe. No team in the USA's group should be overlooked, but the Netherlands matchup will be the one that draws the most attention.

What's Next

USA Softball opens the 2026 WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage on September 12 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. Group play runs through September 16. The top two teams from Group Oklahoma advance to the Finals in Redcliffe, Australia (April 5-11, 2027). For updates on roster preparation and the group-stage schedule, follow usasoftball.com and wbsc.org.
