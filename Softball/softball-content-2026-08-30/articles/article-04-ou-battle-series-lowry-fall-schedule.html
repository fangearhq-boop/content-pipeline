Audrey Lowry Emerges, Fall Schedule Set as OU Softball Closes Out Battle Series

By Sarah Mitchell

August 30, 2026

Oklahoma softball's fall Battle Series is wrapping up — and a name worth remembering is rising to the top of the early evaluations.

Audrey Lowry, a sophomore left-handed pitcher, has been one of the standouts of the 2026 fall program. The OU Daily profiled her emergence with a piece centered on the connection she's building within the returning core — and her performance in the Battle Series backs it up. In one notable outing: 2.2 innings, one hit, zero walks, two strikeouts. Clean, efficient, and composed.

Lowry is one of only two returning pitchers on Oklahoma's roster entering 2027. Senior Kierston Deal is the other. That context makes her Battle Series contribution more significant — she is not just competing for a rotation spot, she is establishing herself as foundational to how this staff is built.

Integrating the Transfers

Alongside Lowry and Deal, head coach Patty Gasso is evaluating two high-profile transfer additions. Sydney Berzon — a senior right-hander and two-time All-American who posted 486.1 innings pitched, a 2.02 ERA, and 406 strikeouts at LSU — is working her way into the fall rotation. Outfielder Macie Harter, who hit .346 with seven home runs and 23 RBI at Middle Tennessee State in 2026, is integrating on the offensive side.

The Battle Series has served as the primary evaluation vehicle for Gasso as she determines how these pieces fit around Oklahoma's returning core. The first Battle Series scrimmage drew 2,351 fans to Love's Field — a demonstration of the program's pull even in the offseason.

Fall Schedule: What to Watch

Oklahoma's formal fall schedule opens September 30 at Love's Field and includes four Battle Series games plus matchups against three outside opponents:

  • Oct. 23 — USAO
  • Oct. 30 — Seminole State
  • Nov. 4 — East Texas A&M

Home fall games are available at $10.88 per game or $70.69 for an all-fall pass. The games against outside opponents provide the first competitive, non-scrimmage tests for what Gasso is assembling.

Gasso's Dual Calendar

Starting September 12, Gasso leaves Norman to coach the USA Women's National Team at the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage in Oklahoma City. She will manage USA Softball through the September 16 conclusion of the group stage before returning to Sooners fall duties.

The dual responsibility is unusual and demands precision — but it also reflects the program's standing. Gasso was selected to lead the national team because she is one of the best coaches in the sport. Oklahoma's fall evaluation will continue in her absence, and what she and her staff have built through the Battle Series will guide the process either way.
