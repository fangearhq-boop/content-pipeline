Texas Softball Drops 2026 Fall Schedule: Six Games, Kavan Back, and Portal Activity Ahead

By Emily Rawlings

August 30, 2026

Texas softball has a fall schedule. And based on what head coach Mike White said alongside it, 2027 is already in full preparation mode at Red and Charline McCombs Field.

The Longhorns officially released their 2026 fall slate on August 28 — a six-game schedule that opens September 29 at home against Murray State (6 PM CT, free admission) and closes November 6 with a road game at Texas A&M's Davis Diamond (7 PM CT). All five home dates are free to the public.

The Full Fall Slate

  • Sept. 29 — Murray State (home, McCombs Field, 6 PM CT)
  • Oct. 13 — Texas State (home, 6 PM CT)
  • Oct. 20 — Temple College (home, 6 PM CT)
  • Oct. 27 — McLennan CC (home, 6 PM CT)
  • Oct. 29 — UTSA (home, 6 PM CT)
  • Nov. 6 — at Texas A&M, Davis Diamond, College Station (7 PM CT)

The November 6 road game at Texas A&M will attract particular attention — it is the only game on the schedule against a program with comparable resources and ambition, and the in-state rivalry dynamic adds a layer that the home games won't replicate.

White Signals More Portal Activity

White's comments alongside the schedule announcement carried as much news as the dates themselves. He confirmed that Texas will "look in the transfer portal again this year to bring in some pieces" for the 2027 roster.

That is not surprising given the graduation losses. Reese Atwood and several starters from the back-to-back championship squad have completed their eligibility. Texas needs to add externally to maintain the depth that won two titles, and White has shown a willingness to use the portal strategically. He used it to bring in impactful pieces for both the 2025 and 2026 championship runs.

Kavan: The Returning Constant

Amid the roster turnover, one constant returns. Teagan Kavan — the only player in Women's College World Series history to earn Most Outstanding Player honors in two separate tournaments — is coming back for her senior season. She won it in 2025. She won it again in 2026.

Six of nine starters from the 2026 championship team return alongside Kavan. For any other program, that level of continuity would represent a near-certain title contender. For Texas, it represents the foundation of a three-peat attempt — something only UCLA and Oklahoma have accomplished in softball history.

What's Next

Texas opens fall ball September 29 at McCombs Field against Murray State (free, 6 PM CT). All five home dates are free to the public. The season closes at Texas A&M on November 6. Transfer portal activity is expected between now and the winter signing period. For fans, the fall games represent the first chance to see a team — led by Teagan Kavan — that will enter 2027 as the defending champion and the early favorite to do it again.
