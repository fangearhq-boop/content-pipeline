Nine Days Out: USA Softball Opens Group Oklahoma Against Mexico on Sept. 12

By Sarah Mitchell

September 3, 2026

The countdown has entered single digits. Nine days from now, Team USA takes the field at Devon Park in Oklahoma City to open the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage — and the opponent for that opening night is No. 7 Mexico.

The Sept. 12 schedule has USA and Mexico squaring off at 7:30 PM local time, the final game of opening day at Devon Park. It follows Brazil vs. China (1:00 PM) and New Zealand vs. Netherlands (4:00 PM), with the opening ceremony at 6:30 PM before the USA's debut.

For Team USA, the stakes couldn't be higher. Only two of the six nations competing in Group Oklahoma City will advance to the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup 2027 Finals in Redcliffe, Queensland, Australia — an event taking place April 5-11, 2027, that serves as a critical qualifier on the road to the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics.

The Roster: Gold, Experience, and Familiar Faces

USA Softball's 16-player roster for Group Oklahoma is stacked with international pedigree. Seven players earned World Games gold medals. Two more — catcher Dejah Mulipola and infielder Valerie Garcia — are 2020 Olympians who know the weight of international competition at the highest level.

Oklahoma provides the largest single-school contingent: Alyssa Brito, Jayda Coleman, Tiare Jennings, and Kelly Maxwell all represent the Sooners on the national stage. Head coach Patty Gasso — who doubles as OU's head coach — leads the squad, giving the Sooners' contingent a familiar coaching voice.

The six-team field is strong. The Netherlands (ranked No. 6 in the world) is USA's most dangerous competitor and will likely determine which nation claims the top seed heading into the playoff bracket. Mexico (No. 7) opens the tournament against the USA, while China (No. 8), New Zealand (No. 23), and Brazil round out the field.

Home Turf, Familiar Venue

Devon Park isn't new territory for USA Softball. Just weeks ago, the USA Women's National Team went 5-0 on the same surface during the International Cup, winning the gold medal and defeating Japan in the finale. That confidence on familiar turf could prove to be a difference-maker when the stakes escalate.

Oklahoma City knows how to show up for softball. The venue will host five days of world-class international competition — Sept. 12 through Sept. 16 — with playoff games and a wild-card repechage on the final day to determine which teams punch their tickets to Australia.

What's Next

Team USA's Group Oklahoma schedule runs Sept. 12-16 at Devon Park in Oklahoma City. The USA opens against No. 7 Mexico at 7:30 PM on Sept. 12 and will face the Netherlands (No. 6) in what many expect to be the top-seed decider. The top two finishers advance to the 2027 World Cup Finals in Redcliffe, QLD — a key milestone on the LA28 Olympic qualification pathway. Follow along as the countdown reaches zero and Team USA defends its home turf.
