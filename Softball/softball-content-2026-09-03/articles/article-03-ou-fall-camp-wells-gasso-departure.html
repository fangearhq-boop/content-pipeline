Kendall Wells and the Next Chapter: Oklahoma's Fall Camp Takes Shape Nine Days Before Gasso Leaves

By Jordan Reeves

September 3, 2026

The most dominant freshman in college softball history is back in Norman. And this time, she's not a freshman anymore.

Kendall Wells, the catcher who obliterated the NCAA single-season freshman home run record with 39 home runs in 2026, is back at Oklahoma for fall camp — and she headlines a new-look Sooners roster that head coach Patty Gasso is scrambling to evaluate before a significant clock runs out.

Nine days from today, Gasso leaves for Oklahoma City to serve as head coach of Team USA at the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage (Sept. 12-16). The window to shape this team before her departure is closing fast.

Wells: The Record-Breaker Returns

Wells's 2026 season was historic by any measure. She hit .358 with 70 runs scored, 39 home runs, 88 RBIs, and a 1.028 slugging percentage — numbers that don't look like they belong in a freshman campaign. She broke the NCAA freshman single-season home run record, set the OU program single-season record, and collected the NFCA Freshman of the Year award, SEC Freshman of the Year, and the Johnny Bench Award.

Now she's a sophomore. The question isn't whether Wells can hit — everyone in college softball already knows the answer. The question is what she looks like as a veteran leader on a roster that was rebuilt around her.

Oklahoma returns 12 players from last season and adds nine newcomers. Wells is the anchor, the returning All-American who gives Gasso's program its identity heading into 2027.

The Pitching Puzzle

The pitching situation is the most pressing issue for Gasso to address before she boards a plane for Oklahoma City. Only two pitchers return from Oklahoma's 2026 staff: sophomore lefty Audrey Lowry and Deal. Lowry has been the standout early in fall camp — she threw 2.2 innings in a recent fall scrimmage, allowing just one hit, walking nobody, and striking out two. Encouraging signs from a young arm carrying significant responsibility.

The transfers are expected to carry much of the load. Sydney Berzon arrives from LSU as a two-time All-American with 486.1 career innings pitched, a 2.02 ERA, and 406 strikeouts. She's one of the most experienced and decorated pitching transfers in the country. Macie Harter (.346 BA, 7 HR, 23 RBI at Middle Tennessee State, 2nd Team All-CUSA) rounds out the new additions with her versatility and contact hitting.

Berzon, in particular, gives Oklahoma a proven arm at the top of the staff. Integrating her mechanics and preparation habits with a new program in six weeks of fall camp is the task at hand.

What's Next

Oklahoma opens its fall schedule Sept. 30 at Love's Field against USAO, with additional home games against Seminole State (Oct. 30) and East Texas A&M (Nov. 4). Patty Gasso will be in Oklahoma City from Sept. 12-16 for Team USA's Group Stage competition, leaving the Sooners' day-to-day fall operations in the hands of her staff. The evaluation of Berzon, Lowry, and the 2027 roster will continue through November. The answers Gasso finds before she leaves next week could define Oklahoma's season.
