### STORY 1: USA WNT — Nine Days to Group Oklahoma (Tier 1 — FOLLOW UP)
USA Softball's Women's National Team is 9 days from its opening game at the WBSC Women's Softball World Cup Group Stage in Oklahoma City. The USA opens Sept. 12 against No. 7 Mexico at 7:30 PM at Devon Park — the same venue where Team USA went 5-0 at the International Cup. The 16-player roster is set, headlined by seven World Games gold medalists and 2020 Olympians Valerie Garcia and Dejah Mulipola. Patty Gasso coaches the squad, alongside four Sooners (Brito, Coleman, Jennings, Maxwell). Top two of six teams advance to the WBSC World Cup 2027 Finals in Redcliffe, QLD (April 5-11), an LA28 qualifier. Netherlands (No. 6) is the top-seed decider.

### STORY 2: Class of 2028 — Six Days to In-Person Window (Tier 2 — FOLLOW UP)

OU's fall camp is 9 days from losing head coach Patty Gasso (who departs Sept. 12 for Team USA coaching duties). But the Sooners have a new star to evaluate: returning catcher Kendall Wells, who broke the NCAA freshman single-season home run record with 39 HRs in 2026 and earned NFCA Freshman of the Year honors. The pitching staff remains thin — only Audrey Lowry (2.2 IP, 1H, 0BB, 2K in scrimmage) and Deal return from 2026 — but transfer Sydney Berzon (2x All-American, LSU, 486.1 IP, 2.02 ERA) and Macie Harter (.346 BA, MTSU) are integrating. Fall opener at Love's Field is Sept. 30.

### STORY 4: Texas Longhorns — 26 Days to Fall Opener, Three-Peat Eyes Open (Tier 3 — FOLLOW UP)
Texas opens its fall slate Sept. 29 against Murray State at McCombs Field (6 PM CT, free admission). The Longhorns are chasing what only UCLA (1988-90) and Oklahoma (2021-23) have accomplished: three straight national championships.
