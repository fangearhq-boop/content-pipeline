2028 Softball Recruiting: EE100 Pitchers Drop Monday, Top-5 Reveal Coming This Week

By Jordan Reeves | October 25, 2026 | I Love Softball

The Class of 2028 is about to get a lot more concrete.

Extra Inning Softball is expected to release the Pitchers 201-300 rankings for the Class of 2028 on Monday, October 27. The overall Top-5 reveal is also expected this week — meaning by the end of October, we'll have our first official answer to one of the most-debated questions in recruiting circles: who is the best recruit in the biggest class in history?

The Scale of This Class

The Class of 2028 has received more than 1,200 nominations — the largest nomination pool in Extra Elite 100 history. That's not a rounding error. It reflects the continued growth of youth softball, the deepening talent base across the country, and the sustained investment programs have made in identifying and recruiting underclassmen earlier than ever.

For perspective: this class is being evaluated in 2026, two full years before these players will set foot on a college campus as freshmen. The players receiving Monday's rankings are high school juniors right now. The fact that their careers are generating this level of national attention is a testament to how much the recruiting pipeline has accelerated.

The Rankings Landscape Right Now

Texas IF Michaela Landers has held the No. 1 overall spot in the Class of 2028 rankings. Landers plays infield for Texas Bombers Gold — the same travel team organization that has historically produced a significant number of Texas Longhorns signees. She brings a combination of offensive production and positional value that makes her the consensus top recruit in this class.

Florida's Kylie Holden sits at No. 2 overall. Holden is one of multiple strong Florida commitments heading to Gainesville, joining an already-established recruiting class under head coach Tim Walton.

At the program level, Oklahoma and Tennessee lead the way with five commits each in this class. Florida has Holden and Evans committed. Texas has Landers plus at least three more. FSU has secured commitments from twins Keira and Kelsey Luderer — a notable headline given the challenge of landing two highly recruited players from the same family.

The Contact Window Just Closed

The second contact period of this recruiting cycle ran October 24-25. That means coaches were calling and texting Class of 2028 prospects this weekend, and the normal post-contact-window commit wave is likely already beginning. Watch the recruiting wire closely over the next 48-72 hours — programs that made strong impressions in the contact window tend to see quick follow-through from recruits who made decisions and were simply waiting for permission to announce.

The next contact window for Class of 2028 recruiting has not been announced.

What the Monday Rankings Mean

The Pitchers 201-300 release on Monday completes the pitcher ranking spectrum. Along with the Top-5 overall reveal, softball fans will have a clearer picture of the best pitching prospects in this class and how the overall class stacks up at the top.

For programs still pursuing uncommitted pitchers in this class, Monday's release also functions as a public negotiation: prospects who see their ranking and believe it undervalues their development may open dialogue with new programs. Rankings drops consistently produce unexpected movement in the recruiting market.

What's Next

Monday, October 27 is the date for the EE100 Pitchers 201-300 release. The Top-5 overall reveal is expected this week as well. With the contact window just closed and a wave of commits expected, the 2028 recruiting cycle is entering its most active stretch since the summer. Stay tuned.
