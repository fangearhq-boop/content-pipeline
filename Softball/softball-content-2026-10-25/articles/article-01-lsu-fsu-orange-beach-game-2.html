FSU vs. LSU Game 2 — The Orange Beach Showcase Closes With an SEC Heavyweight Rematch

By Jordan Reeves | October 25, 2026 | I Love Softball

The most compelling fall showcase of October closes today. Florida State and LSU meet for Game 2 of their two-game series at Orange Beach Softball Stadium in Orange Beach, Alabama, with first pitch set for noon CT (1 PM ET). Admission is $10 cash at the gate.

Game 1 was played yesterday, Saturday October 24. Today's rematch is the final chapter of a showcase that has drawn significant attention from the softball community — and for good reason. These are not fall ball exhibitions featuring split rosters or underclassmen getting extra reps. Both programs brought their full 2027 squads. Both programs are legitimately in the national title conversation. This is about as close to a meaningful mid-fall contest as the sport offers.

What These Records Mean

Florida State entered this series with a 52-1 fall record. Louisiana State came in at 40-19. Those numbers are worth pausing on — not because they translate directly to what will happen in March, but because they reflect the kind of competitive standard each program held themselves to through the entire fall schedule.

FSU's 52-1 record is the kind of fall total that raises eyebrows. Even accounting for the mixed quality of fall competition, winning 52 games in the fall requires showing up, executing, and not giving away games against inferior competition. The Seminoles under head coach Lonni Alameda have built a program that takes fall ball seriously as evaluation and preparation time — not as a vacation from spring standards.

LSU's 40-19 includes a more balanced diet of competition. The Tigers, under head coach Beth Torina, have traditionally used fall ball to push their roster in ways that create losses as part of the process. A 40-19 record with high-level competition mixed in is not the same as 52-1 against light opposition — both programs know this, and that's part of what makes today's matchup genuinely informative.

What to Watch For

On the pitching side, both programs enter 2027 with questions that fall ball has started to answer — and some that only spring competition can resolve. FSU's staff has been dominant enough to compile that 52-1 record; who leads the rotation and who serves as the stopper in high-leverage situations will matter enormously when the bracket games arrive.

For LSU, fall ball has been about integrating new roster pieces. The Tigers added Haley Winn (Notre Dame grad transfer, .335 BA, 14 stolen bases) and twin sisters Madison Pickens (Louisville OF) and Mackenzie Pickens (Ole Miss INF) through the portal. Getting that group playing together with a cohesive identity is the project of fall, and Game 2 against a 52-1 opponent is a genuine stress test.

Offensively, these are two programs capable of putting up crooked numbers in a hurry. When two top-10 rosters with full pitching staffs face off in a neutral site setting, the quality of execution — not just talent — tends to determine the outcome. That's what makes this worth watching.

Why Orange Beach Matters

The Orange Beach Sportsplex has become one of the fall softball circuit's premier venues. Located on Alabama's Gulf Coast, the complex draws programs from across the SEC and beyond for fall tournaments and exhibitions precisely because its facility quality, neutral-site atmosphere, and geographic centrality make it a logical destination for two programs like FSU and LSU who don't share a natural geographic bracket.

For fans willing to make the trip, today's game offers a rare opportunity: see two 2027 national title contenders, at a $10 admission price, in a venue that can actually hold a crowd and create atmosphere. Fall softball is often a quiet affair. Today is not that.

What's Next

After today, both programs head into their winter preparation phases. FSU and LSU will not meet again until the 2027 regular season or postseason, making this two-game series at Orange Beach the last live look at both programs before the calendar flips.

For softball fans tracking the 2027 national title picture, the Orange Beach series has been this week's most important data point. Game 2 tips off today at noon CT (1 PM ET) at Orange Beach Softball Stadium. Admission is $10 cash.
