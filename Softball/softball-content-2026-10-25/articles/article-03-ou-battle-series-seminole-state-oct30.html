Oklahoma's Fall Finale Is Thursday — Seminole State at Love's Field, Free on 1oklahoma.com

By Emily Rawlings | October 25, 2026 | I Love Softball

Oklahoma's fall schedule has one game remaining, and it's free for anyone who wants to watch.

The Sooners host Seminole State at Love's Field in Norman on Thursday, October 30 at 6:30 PM CT. The game streams live and free on 1oklahoma.com, which means fans anywhere can tune in for what amounts to the last OU softball sighting of 2026.

What This Season Has Shown

The Battle Series — Oklahoma's fall exhibition format against local and regional opponents — has given fans a consistent look at a roster that enters 2027 as one of the three best in the country. The format works: home games at Love's Field, familiar opponents, and full competitive effort from a program that treats fall ball as genuine preparation for spring.

The headliners are not a surprise if you've been paying attention. Cydney Wells hit 39 home runs last season and was named NFCA Freshman of the Year. That stat line alone would make OU dangerous. But Wells is not the entirety of this offense — she's the centerpiece of a lineup with multiple threats at every position. When a team's freshman of the year slugger returns as a sophomore with a full season of opposing scouting reports in her head, the usual response is adjustment. The early indication from fall ball is that Wells has already begun making those adjustments.

Nicole Kavan won the WCWS Most Outstanding Player award — twice. Two consecutive trips to Oklahoma City, two consecutive MOP trophies. There is nothing left in college softball to prove, which in an odd way makes Kavan more dangerous than ever: she plays with zero pressure and maximum experience.

The Broader Picture

Oklahoma is one of the programs that defines what the 2027 national title race will look like. That picture also includes Tennessee (with their three-pitcher staff), Florida (returning six starters and a rebuilt roster), and Texas Tech (now with Kasidi Pickering and the No. 1 portal class in program history). But OU sits comfortably in the top tier of that group based on everything the fall schedule has shown.

The Sooners also return veteran starters around the Wells-Kavan core, with depth in the rotation and a coaching staff led by Patty Gasso that has demonstrated season after season the ability to peak when it matters. OU doesn't just make the WCWS — they've proven they know how to win there.

Seminole State visits Love's Field Thursday as the final audition opponent for a team that doesn't need an audition. But that's precisely the point of fall ball: you keep getting reps, you keep playing games, and you show up ready in February when it counts again.

What's Next

After Thursday's game against Seminole State, Oklahoma closes the fall and turns toward winter preparations. The Sooners' next competitive action will come in the 2027 preseason schedule before conference play begins.

For fans who want to see the 2027 version of this roster before the offseason, Thursday is the date: October 30, 6:30 PM CT, Love's Field in Norman. Free on 1oklahoma.com.
