### STORY 3: Bermuda Championship One Week Out

**Long-Form Post (12:00 PM ET)**
One of the best FedExCup opportunities left on the schedule is six days away.

The Butterfield Bermuda Championship tees off October 22-25 at Port Royal Golf Course in Southampton Parish, Bermuda — a $6 million FedExCup Fall event offering 500 points to the winner. For players sitting on the fringe of the top 60, Bermuda might be the most important week left on the schedule.

Port Royal is a public course with a Robert Trent Jones pedigree. In calm conditions it plays as a scoring opportunity. But Bermuda sits in the Atlantic, and wind is never a guarantee to stay away. Past champions include Adam Schenk (-12 in 2025), Rafael Campos in 2024, and Camilo Villegas and Seamus Power in previous years. The event tends to crown players who manage their games quietly across four rounds — consistency wins at Port Royal more than fireworks.

With five FedExCup Fall events remaining, every point matters. #PGATour #Golf #BermudaChampionship

### STORY 4: Scheffler 179 Weeks at No. 1

**Long-Form Post (2:00 PM ET)**
Scottie Scheffler enters his 179th consecutive week at world No. 1, continuing one of the most dominant ranking runs in the history of professional golf.

The streak has been unbroken since approximately May 2023. Tiger Woods's all-time record for consecutive weeks at No. 1 stands at 281, set between June 2005 and October 2010. Scheffler needs roughly 100 more consecutive weeks — holding the top spot through approximately mid-October 2028 — to break it.

To put that in perspective: when Tiger ran his streak, no player in the world was close enough to challenge him for the ranking. Scheffler faces a different but real set of challengers in Rory McIlroy, Jon Rahm's return to a major tour, and the depth of the modern PGA Tour. Every week the streak continues is earned.

In 2026 alone, Scheffler won the Tour Championship at East Lake to claim his second FedExCup title. His career earnings on the PGA Tour have also now officially surpassed Tiger Woods on the money list — a milestone that reflects both his dominance and the increased prize purses of the modern era.

The question isn't whether Scheffler can play at this level. The question is whether he can stay healthy and consistent for two more years. The record is within reach. #Golf #ScottieScheffler #OWGR
