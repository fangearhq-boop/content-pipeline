Baycurrent Classic Round 1: Coody and Bridgeman Share the Lead at -6 in Japan

By Marcus Cole | October 8, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

The Baycurrent Classic at Yokohama Country Club in Kanagawa, Japan, opened with Parker Coody and Jonathan Bridgeman sharing the first-round lead at 6-under par. The $8 million purse event, part of the PGA Tour's international fall swing, runs October 8-11 with defending champion Xander Schauffele entering the week as the field's betting favourite.

Coody and Bridgeman's -6 opening rounds put them at the top of a leaderboard that will shift considerably over the next three days. Yokohama Country Club presents a demanding test that tends to compress scoring as the week progresses, rewarding players who build early leads rather than those who chase late.

Schauffele Defending

Xander Schauffele enters as the defending champion and the player with the most experience at this venue on tour. His familiarity with Yokohama Country Club, combined with his form throughout the 2026 season, makes him the clear favourite despite Coody and Bridgeman's early advantage.

Schauffele has demonstrated throughout his career an ability to close tournaments from within striking distance rather than leading wire-to-wire. A position three or four strokes back after 36 holes has historically not prevented him from winning — a fact that the rest of the field will keep in mind as the leaderboard takes shape over the next two days.

The FedExCup Fall Context

The Baycurrent Classic, with its $8 million purse, is among the richer FedExCup Fall events on the calendar. The FedExCup Fall serves as a bridge season between the main FedExCup competition and the following year's tour schedule, with players competing for card security, priority ranking improvement, and the points that can reshape their 2027 positioning.

The field in Japan represents a mix of established tour veterans, players fighting to secure their status, and international competitors for whom the Japan setting provides a meaningful home-market advantage. Japan's golf fanbase is among the most devoted in the world, and the Baycurrent Classic benefits from that enthusiasm.

With LIV Golf's bankruptcy proceedings continuing to reshape the professional game, PGA Tour events this fall have seen more depth than in recent seasons as some players who previously committed to LIV now compete on established tours.

What's Next

Round 2 of the Baycurrent Classic takes place Friday, with Round 3 on Saturday and the final round on Sunday, October 11. After Japan, the PGA Tour moves to the Butterfield Bermuda Championship at Port Royal Golf Course in Bermuda (October 22-25, $6 million purse) before the fall schedule continues through November.
