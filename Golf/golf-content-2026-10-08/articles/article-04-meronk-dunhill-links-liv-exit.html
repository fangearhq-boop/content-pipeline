Meronk Wins Alfred Dunhill Links, Secures DP World Tour Card — and Sets Up His LIV Exit

By Ryan Calloway | October 8, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

Adrian Meronk won the Alfred Dunhill Links Championship at St Andrews this past weekend, rallying past overnight leader Casey Jarvis of South Africa in the final round to claim victory. The win secured Meronk a full DP World Tour card for 2027 and, in practical terms, cleared the path for his expected exit from LIV Golf.

The Alfred Dunhill Links is one of European golf's most historic events, played across three of Scotland's most celebrated courses: St Andrews, Carnoustie, and Kingsbarns. The setting adds weight to any victory. For Meronk, a Polish player and five-time DP World Tour champion, winning at St Andrews while his future on the tour hung in the balance was precisely the kind of moment that defines careers.

The Final Round

Jarvis, the young South African who led entering Sunday, was unable to hold off Meronk's charge in the final round. Meronk played steady, disciplined golf across the back nine at St Andrews to overtake him, converting the lead he needed to close out the championship.

The margin of victory was ultimately less important than what the win delivered: confirmation that Meronk can still compete at the highest level of European professional golf after spending the 2026 season on LIV Golf. For players who made the jump to LIV, one of the persistent questions has been whether their games would translate back when the opportunity arose. Meronk answered that question on Sunday.

The LIV Context

Meronk's departure from the DP World Tour for LIV Golf was driven by the same forces that pulled other European players toward the Saudi-backed league: guaranteed money and a different competitive structure. He was one of the most accomplished European players to make the move, and his presence on LIV was a signal of the league's ability to attract talent from across the global game.

With LIV now in bankruptcy proceedings, Meronk is among the players expected to exit his contract and return to the established tours. His Alfred Dunhill Links victory arrives at exactly the right moment — he did not need to wait for LIV's situation to resolve to begin securing his 2027 position. The DP World Tour card is his. The path back is already open.

Jon Rahm's departure from LIV, confirmed this week by his attorney in a bankruptcy proceeding, has accelerated the conversation about which players will follow. Meronk's exit, when formalized, fits that pattern: the circuit's talent base continues to shrink at the moment it most needs player commitment to close its BC Partners rescue financing.

What's Next

Meronk's separation from LIV Golf is expected to be formalized in the coming weeks, in parallel with Jon Rahm's targeted October 15 finalization date. With his DP World Tour card secured, Meronk has the foundation to compete across the European calendar in 2027. The DP World Tour's fall and winter events provide an immediate set of opportunities, with the tour's final-series events determining season rankings before the calendar closes.

For the Alfred Dunhill Links itself, the championship continues its place as one of the calendar's most beloved events. St Andrews remains the sport's most resonant venue. Meronk's win there, under the circumstances, will be remembered as one of the more meaningful results of the 2026 European golf season.
