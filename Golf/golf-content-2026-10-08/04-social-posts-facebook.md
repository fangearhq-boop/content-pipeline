**Facebook Post 1 — Jon Rahm Leaves LIV Golf**

Jon Rahm is officially leaving LIV Golf.

His attorney confirmed to a bankruptcy judge that he is no longer participating in the circuit — the same circuit where Rahm spent the 2026 season as the individual points and money list leader. A separation agreement is targeted for finalization by October 15.

The news arrives alongside a critical development in LIV's bankruptcy proceedings: the player commitment deadline has been pushed from October 13 to October 25. That extension might suggest more time to save the deal. The reality is more complicated. BC Partners announced a $300 million investment to rescue LIV on October 6. But court documents reveal that only $4 million of that $300 million has actually been committed so far.

Sergio Garcia has already been released from his LIV contract by a New Jersey bankruptcy court. Adrian Meronk, who won the Alfred Dunhill Links Championship this weekend, is also expected to exit. With its biggest names walking away and the investment reality far smaller than the headline suggests, LIV Golf's future has never looked more uncertain.

**Facebook Post 2 — Smotherman Bank of Utah Win**

Austin Smotherman won the Bank of Utah Championship at Black Desert Golf Course in Utah last weekend. It was his 100th PGA Tour start.

Smotherman shot 26-under par and defeated Doug Ghim by two strokes to earn his first career victory on the PGA Tour, taking home $1,080,000 and 500 FedExCup points in the process.

What makes the story compelling is the number: 100. Most professional golfers never get 100 starts on the PGA Tour. The attrition rate is brutal, the competition is relentless, and maintaining status for that long without a win requires years of consistency and competitive nerve. Smotherman had all of that, and he finally added a win.

It's the kind of result that reminds you why professional golf is different from other sports. The window stays open for a long time — if you stay ready when the moment comes.

**Facebook Post 4 — Meronk Dunhill Links / LIV Exit**

Adrian Meronk is a five-time DP World Tour champion who spent the 2026 season competing on LIV Golf. This past weekend, he won the Alfred Dunhill Links Championship — played across St Andrews, Carnoustie, and Kingsbarns in Scotland — after rallying past overnight leader Casey Jarvis of South Africa in the final round.

More importantly, the win secured him a full DP World Tour card for 2027.

Meronk is expected to formally exit his LIV Golf contract as the circuit continues its bankruptcy proceedings. His story is a microcosm of what is happening across professional golf right now: players who left for LIV's guaranteed money are finding their way back to the established tours, using strong performances to secure their futures, precisely as LIV's future becomes less certain.

The Alfred Dunhill Links at St Andrews is one of golf's most historic and beautiful settings. Meronk chose the right stage to announce his next chapter.
