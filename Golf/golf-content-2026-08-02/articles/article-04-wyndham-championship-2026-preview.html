Wyndham Championship 2026 Preview: Greensboro Hosts the PGA Tour's Final Regular-Season Stand

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | August 2, 2026

The Wyndham Championship has arrived as the last event standing between the PGA Tour's regular season and the FedExCup Playoffs. It runs August 5-9 at Sedgefield Country Club in Greensboro, North Carolina, with competitive rounds beginning August 6. The purse is $8.5 million. The FedExCup points for the winner: 500 — exactly what Davis Riley just collected for winning the Rocket Classic in Detroit.

At the conclusion of play on August 9, the top 70 in the FedExCup standings will advance to the FedEx St. Jude Championship at TPC Southwind in Memphis on August 13. For many players in the field this week, that outcome — in or out of the playoffs — is the only result that matters.

The Stakes Are Explicit

The Wyndham has served as the regular-season finale since 2008. In that role, it has consistently attracted a loaded field of players chasing points, rankings, and playoff berths. This year is no different — and the bubble entering Greensboro is, if anything, more crowded and more consequential than in most recent editions.

Brooks Koepka — five-time major champion, 84th in the FedExCup standings after withdrawing from the Rocket Classic with an ulnar nerve injury — has confirmed his entry. This is his last opportunity to qualify. Tony Finau, sitting at 87th, faces the same reality. Jordan Spieth, Justin Thomas, Hideki Matsuyama, and Daniel Berger are also in the field — each of them experienced enough to produce a week that changes their standing.

What a Win Means

Beyond the playoff picture, the Wyndham carries rewards that extend well past August. The winner receives a two-year PGA Tour exemption plus guaranteed invitations to some of golf's most prestigious events: The Sentry, THE PLAYERS Championship, The Masters, and the PGA Championship. For a player who might otherwise be fighting for conditional status or limited exemptions, a Wyndham win is career-altering.

Greensboro has been a consistent venue for players looking to end their seasons on a high note or secure their card for the following year. The event has a genuine community of support and a fanbase that appreciates what the Wyndham represents in the broader PGA Tour structure.

Sedgefield Country Club

Sedgefield Country Club is a classic American parkland course — tree-lined, demanding of accuracy off the tee, and rewarding to the player who manages their game intelligently over 72 holes. It does not typically produce the lowest-of-low scoring weeks, which historically favours the players who are operating with discipline under pressure rather than simply firing at every flag.

For a bubble player like Koepka or Finau, who needs a strong finish rather than necessarily a win, the course characteristics may actually work in their favour — a patient, position-based approach can yield the kind of consistent scoring that climbs a leaderboard without requiring anything heroic.

Looking to Memphis

When Greensboro concludes its final round on Sunday, August 9, the FedExCup regular season will officially be over. The top 70 will have earned their places at TPC Southwind. Everyone else goes home.

For those inside the line looking to protect their position, or those outside it trying to force their way in, the next five days are the entirety of the season. The Wyndham Championship, often understated in the broader narrative of professional golf, carries more than its share of the year's real drama.
