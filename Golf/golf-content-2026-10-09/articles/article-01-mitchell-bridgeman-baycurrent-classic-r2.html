Mitchell and Bridgeman Share the Lead at the Baycurrent Classic After Two Rounds

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | October 9, 2026

Two rounds into the 2026 Baycurrent Classic at Yokohama Country Club in Japan, two players stand alone at the top of the leaderboard. Keith Mitchell and Jacob Bridgeman are both 11 under par, sharing the lead heading into Round 3 with two days still to play at this no-cut PGA Tour event.

The pairing at the top wasn't built the same way. Bridgeman blazed out of the gates, while Mitchell steadily climbed to match him. The result is a genuine co-lead with a quality field of 72 players — all of whom will compete through Sunday — breathing down their necks.

Bridgeman's Hot Start

Jacob Bridgeman, 24, set the early standard with an 8-under 63 in Round 1 that announced his presence to the field. The highlight: he birdied four of his final five holes to cap the round in style. That kind of closing surge separates a good round from a statement round, and Bridgeman made a statement at Yokohama.

Bridgeman has been one of the more promising young players on the PGA Tour in recent seasons — a powerful ball-striker who has shown the kind of scoring ability that makes his peers take notice. This is the position in a tournament he needed to put himself in, and he's done it on one of the more demanding layouts the fall schedule offers.

Mitchell Matches the Pace

Keith Mitchell matched Bridgeman's total with his own strong Round 2, pulling even at 11 under. Mitchell is a veteran presence in the PGA Tour's ranks, known for his prodigious driving distance and the kind of course management that makes him dangerous at any type of venue. Yokohama Country Club suits players who can shape shots and manage a tight layout, and Mitchell has shown he belongs at this number.

The fact that two different players arrived at -11 through different paths is one of the things that makes the weekend unpredictable. They may be at the same score, but they are not necessarily playing the same game.

The Defending Champion's Challenge

Xander Schauffele won this event in 2025 at 19 under par — a score that set the standard for what winning at Yokohama Country Club requires. Schauffele is in the chasing pack entering the weekend, meaning the lead of two rounds is not insurmountable. A Schauffele run over the final 36 holes would not surprise anyone who watched him dominate this event a year ago.

The field also includes Collin Morikawa, Hideki Matsuyama, Jordan Spieth, Justin Thomas, and Wyndham Clark. This is not a field that will simply allow the leaders to run away. Any of these players is capable of posting a 63 or better on this course.

Why This Tournament Matters

The Baycurrent Classic is one of the cornerstone events of the PGA Tour's FedEx Cup Fall schedule. It runs without a cut, meaning all 72 players in the field compete for all four rounds — a format that creates unusual dynamics and prevents any easy exits from contention. Points earned here feed into the FedEx Cup Fall standings, which are finalized at the RSM Classic in November.

For Mitchell and Bridgeman, holding the lead after two rounds puts them in a position to earn not just a title but meaningful standing in the fall race. A win in Japan is a statement event on the PGA Tour calendar.

What's Next

Round 3 begins Thursday evening Eastern Time — which is Friday morning at Yokohama Country Club. The leaders tee off in the final grouping, and with 36 holes left in a no-cut event, the scoreboard can shift dramatically in either direction. Mitchell and Bridgeman each hold a one-shot lead on whoever is in third, which means the margin at the top is real but not safe. The Baycurrent Classic title will not be decided until Sunday afternoon in Japan.
