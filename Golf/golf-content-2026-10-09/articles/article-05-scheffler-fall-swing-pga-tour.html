Scheffler and the PGA Tour Fall Swing — Five Events Left to Define the 2026 Season

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | October 9, 2026

Scottie Scheffler enters the back half of the PGA Tour's FedEx Cup Fall schedule exactly where you'd expect to find the world's No. 1-ranked golfer: well-positioned, patient, and with more than enough game to make the next five events count. After the Baycurrent Classic in Japan wraps up this weekend, the fall schedule runs five more stops through November, culminating at the RSM Classic where the FedExCup Fall standings close out for the year.

Scheffler sits second in those standings heading in, with 1 win and 6 top-10 finishes in 2026 — a quiet-by-his-standards season that has nonetheless kept him ahead of the field in the world rankings and in contention for another strong year-end finish. He carries 19 career PGA Tour wins into the fall.

The Fall Schedule Ahead

Five events remain on the 2026 FedEx Cup Fall calendar after the Baycurrent Classic concludes on Sunday:

The Butterfield Bermuda Championship (October 22-25) at Port Royal Golf Course in Bermuda opens the next window, offering a low-key field by PGA Tour standards but a picturesque setting and legitimate title opportunity for whoever shows up ready. The VidantaWorld Mexico Open (October 29 through November 1) at Vidanta Vallarta is one of two brand-new events on this year's fall schedule — a new international stop that expands the tour's presence in Mexico.

The World Wide Technology Championship at El Cardonal at Diamante in Los Cabos (November 5-8) is a returning favorite on the fall slate, a low-altitude course in Baja California that has produced exciting finishes. The Good Good Championship (November 12-15) at Omni Barton Creek Resort in Austin, Texas, is the second of the two new fall events, bringing a fresh venue and an interesting setting to the schedule. The RSM Classic (November 19-22) at Sea Island, Georgia, closes out the fall — and with it, the FedExCup Fall standings for 2026.

What Scheffler Is Chasing

The FedExCup Fall standings are a separate competition from the main season standings, and finishing first there carries its own reward. Scheffler at No. 2 heading into the final five events is the position a serious contender occupies — close enough to move up with a pair of strong finishes, far enough back that doing nothing is not an option if the goal is finishing on top.

Scheffler's 2026 season has been measured, not explosive. One win and six top-10s is a productive year for most tour players. For Scheffler, who won multiple majors in 2025 and dominated the schedule in ways that are hard to replicate, 2026 has been a step down in headline terms. The fall is a chance to close the year with a run that resets that narrative.

The New Events

Two new events on the fall schedule make this year's stretch different from what came before. The VidantaWorld Mexico Open and the Good Good Championship in Austin represent the PGA Tour's push to expand its international footprint and bring new event formats to the calendar. Both events will be worth watching for how they shape up as competitive tests and whether the new venues attract the level of field participation that makes a fall event feel like a meaningful stop.

For Scheffler, new events are simply two more chances to win and accumulate points. His ability to perform at any venue on any course type has been one of the defining characteristics of his rise to No. 1. The novelty of Mexico City or Austin does not change the competitive equation for him the way it might for players who rely on familiarity with specific course designs.

What's Next

The fall schedule resumes October 22 in Bermuda. Between now and the RSM Classic in November, five titles will be awarded and the FedExCup Fall standings will be decided. Scheffler at No. 2 heading into the home stretch is a story worth watching every week. If he plays the same golf he produced during the first half of 2026 — consistent, clean, occasionally brilliant — the fall could end with him climbing to No. 1 in those standings too. The schedule is set. The work starts next week.
