### STORY 1: Blades Brown Wins WWT Championship at Age 19

**FB Long-Form Post 1**
**Window:** 9:00 AM ET
---
History was made in Los Cabos on Sunday, and a teenager was at the center of it.

Blades Brown, 19 years, 5 months, and 18 days old, won the 2026 World Wide Technology Championship at El Cardonal at Diamante — his first PGA Tour title and reportedly the youngest Tour winner in at least 88 years. Brown and Ben James both finished regulation at 23-under par 265 and headed to sudden death. On the second playoff hole, James missed a birdie putt inside eight feet. Brown made his birdie. Championship sealed.

Brown's winner's share was $1,080,000 from a $6,000,000 purse. It is a life-changing win for a teenager who is now officially one of the most exciting young talents in professional golf. Michael Kim finished third at 22-under, one shot out of the playoff.

Watch this name. Blades Brown is just getting started.

**FB Long-Form Post 2**
**Window:** 11:00 AM ET

Sunday in Los Cabos came down to one putt — and Ben James was on the wrong end of it.

James and Blades Brown had matched each other shot for shot over 72 holes, finishing tied at 23-under 265 and forcing a sudden-death playoff. When they reached the second playoff hole, James had a birdie putt inside eight feet — the kind of look that most Tour players convert under normal circumstances. He missed. Brown made his birdie on the same hole and walked away with the title.

James earned a runner-up finish in what was one of the most closely contested finishes of the 2026 PGA Tour season. Michael Kim took third at 22-under, one stroke back.

Playoff losses like this one sting, but they also remind us of how thin the margin between winning and losing on the Tour really is.
