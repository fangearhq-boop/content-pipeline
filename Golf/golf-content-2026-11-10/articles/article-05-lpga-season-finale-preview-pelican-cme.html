LPGA Season Finale: ANNIKA at Pelican and CME Group Tour Championship Close Out 2026

By Jake Torres | November 10, 2026

Two events remain in the 2026 LPGA Tour season, and they both take place in Florida over the next two weeks. For players chasing titles, rankings positions, and season-ending statements, the time is now.

ANNIKA Driven by Gainbridge at Pelican — November 12-15

The penultimate event of the season is the ANNIKA driven by Gainbridge at Pelican, held at Pelican Golf Club in Belleair, Florida from November 12-15. Named for Annika Sörenstam, the tournament carries significant prestige on the LPGA calendar and traditionally attracts a world-class field looking to position themselves ahead of the season's final week.

Belleair provides a challenging parkland test, and the conditions in mid-November in Florida are typically ideal for scoring — warm temperatures, manageable wind, and firm greens that reward precision approach play. Players who handle the pressure of a late-season, high-stakes event tend to have the best results here.

CME Group Tour Championship — November 19-22

The season closes at Tiburón Golf Club in Naples, Florida with the CME Group Tour Championship from November 19-22. The CME is the LPGA's season-ending spectacular — one of the tour's richest purses and the event that settles end-of-season Player of the Year conversations and rankings positions.

For players who finish the season in Naples, the CME is a chance to put an exclamation point on 2026 or, for those who have underperformed, to rescue a year that did not meet expectations. The stakes are real, and the field is typically the strongest of any event on the schedule.

The Season's Final Chapter

The 2026 LPGA Tour season runs from January 29 through November 22 — the CME Group Tour Championship's final day. It has been a season of compelling stories, international champions, and performances that have reinforced the global depth of the women's game.

Shuri Sakuma, fresh from her first LPGA title at the TOTO Japan Classic, will be among the players heading to Florida with momentum. The CME field will be watching what she does in her first event as a Tour champion.

What's Next

The ANNIKA driven by Gainbridge at Pelican begins November 12. If you have been following the 2026 LPGA season, these final two weeks are the ones that will define its legacy. Mark the calendar — Belleair and Naples are the venues, and women's professional golf at its finest will be on display.
