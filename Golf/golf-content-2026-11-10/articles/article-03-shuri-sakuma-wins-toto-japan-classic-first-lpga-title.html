Shuri Sakuma Wins TOTO Japan Classic for First LPGA Title, Beats Field by 4 Strokes

By Marcus Cole | November 10, 2026

Shuri Sakuma saved her best golf for home soil, and the result was a first LPGA Tour title that she will not soon forget.

Sakuma won the 2026 TOTO Japan Classic at Seta Golf Course in Japan, capping the November 5-8 event with a dominant final-round 64 that gave her a four-stroke margin of victory. It was the kind of performance that leaves no doubt — the champion was clearly the best player in the field that week, and she closed it out in style.

A Dominant Final Round

Sakuma's 64 in the final round was the low score of the week and the defining statement of her championship. While other players in contention navigated the pressure of a final-day leaderboard, Sakuma applied it — building her lead with one birdie after another and leaving Aki Iwai, who finished in solo second, unable to close the gap.

Four strokes is not a narrow win. It is a dominant one. For a first-time LPGA champion, the manner of Sakuma's victory — leading from the front, answering the moment with her best golf — speaks to a mental composure that bodes well for a long career at the top of the women's game.

The Supporting Cast

Aki Iwai's runner-up finish was a strong result in a quality field, while Erika Hara's third-place showing — a career best for the rookie — added an encouraging note to the week. Finding young players posting career-best results in LPGA events is always a sign of the tour's depth and development.

Winning on home soil carries particular resonance for Japanese players, and the atmosphere at Seta Golf Course will have amplified the significance of Sakuma's achievement. The home crowd, the familiar culture, the weight of expectation on the course — she handled all of it and won going away.

What's Next

The LPGA Tour's season enters its final two events: the ANNIKA driven by Gainbridge at Pelican in Belleair, Florida (November 12-15), followed by the CME Group Tour Championship in Naples (November 19-22). Sakuma will travel to Florida as a champion, with the confidence of a first title behind her and the season-ending stretch ahead.

The CME Group Tour Championship carries one of the tour's richest purses and determines final-season rankings. The last two weeks of the 2026 LPGA season will be compelling, and Sakuma is now a name that belongs in every conversation about the tour's current elite.
