Scheffler Is the No. 1 Seed, the FedExCup Leader, and Has Not Won Since January. The Playoffs Start Thursday.

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | August 10, 2026

PGA Tour / Scottie Scheffler / FedExCup Playoffs

Scottie Scheffler's 2026 PGA Tour regular season is over. The ledger reads: World No. 1, FedExCup points leader at 4,123, five runner-up finishes, zero wins since January. That combination does not exist anywhere else in recent golf history. It is strange, singular, and entirely Scheffler's own.

The FedExCup Playoffs begin Thursday in Memphis. A reset is available. The question is whether Scheffler takes it.

The Runner-Up Season

Scheffler's last win was The American Express in January 2026 — his 20th career PGA Tour title. In the months that followed, he finished second at the Masters, second at the RBC Heritage, second at the Cadillac Championship, second at the Travelers Championship, and second at the 3M Open. Five events. Five runner-ups. Five times the most talented player in the world walked away without the trophy.

His 35-consecutive top-25 streak, which had stretched through much of the past two years, snapped at the Genesis Scottish Open when he missed the cut. That ended one remarkable statistical run. But Scheffler's overall standing never wavered. He led the FedExCup points list entering the playoffs by a comfortable margin. The results are there. The W column is not.

What the Playoffs Offer

The FedExCup Playoffs are a separate competition from the regular season. The accumulated points determine seeding and, at the Tour Championship, starting stroke advantages — but they do not determine the champion of each event. Scheffler could win the St. Jude Championship Thursday through Sunday on the strength of four rounds of golf, without any regular-season baggage attached.

He has done it before. Scheffler won the Tour Championship in 2023 and has a history of elevating his game in the postseason. The version of Scheffler who could not convert runner-ups in the regular season does not have to be the version that shows up in Memphis.

What's Next

Scheffler tees off at TPC Southwind in Memphis on Thursday, August 13, as the No. 1 seed in a 70-player field. The top 50 advance to the BMW Championship, and the top 30 from there reach the Tour Championship at East Lake in Atlanta, where the FedExCup champion is crowned. Scheffler is the favorite at every stop. He was the favorite at five regular-season events where he finished second. Memphis is where the question gets asked again, in a format where the answer matters most.
