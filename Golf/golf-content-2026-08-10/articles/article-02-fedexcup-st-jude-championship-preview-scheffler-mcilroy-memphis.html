FedExCup Playoffs Begin Thursday: Scheffler, McIlroy, and 68 Others Converge on Memphis

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | August 10, 2026

PGA Tour / FedExCup Playoffs / FedEx St. Jude Championship

The PGA Tour regular season is over. Michael Brennan's win at the Wyndham Championship on Sunday locked the 70-player field for the FedExCup Playoffs, and now the sport's most consequential three-week stretch begins on Thursday at TPC Southwind in Memphis, Tennessee.

The FedEx St. Jude Championship carries a $20 million purse and 750 FedExCup points — the largest single-event points allocation of the season. By the time the final round concludes August 16, only 50 players will remain in the playoff field. The others are done.

Scheffler: The No. 1 Seed Who Hasn't Won Since January

Scottie Scheffler arrives in Memphis as the No. 1 seed at 4,123 FedExCup points. He is the highest-ranked player in the world, the most dominant presence in professional golf for the better part of three years, and the owner of five runner-up finishes in 2026 without a single win since The American Express in January.

The FedExCup Playoffs reset the narrative. What Scheffler did or didn't do in the regular season carries no weight in Memphis, except in the standings that set his starting position at the Tour Championship. He arrives as the prohibitive favorite and as the player with the most to prove — a combination that makes him the central figure of the entire playoff run.

McIlroy Chasing History

Rory McIlroy arrives with a specific historical target. He has won the FedExCup three times — the most any player has won it. A fourth title would extend a record that already belongs to no one else in the sport. McIlroy's FedExCup pursuit has been the defining thread of his career on the PGA Tour's postseason stage, and Memphis is where that chase resumes.

The St. Jude Championship specifically suits McIlroy. TPC Southwind rewards accurate ball-striking, and McIlroy's driving has been among his strengths throughout 2026.

Fleetwood, Brennan, and the Rest

Tommy Fleetwood, the defending FedExCup champion, is in the field and will be motivated to defend what he won last September in Atlanta. Defending event champion Justin Rose is also competing in Memphis.

Michael Brennan, who locked his playoff berth Sunday by winning the Wyndham, joins as one of the more emotionally charged entries. He earned his spot the hard way and arrives in Memphis with nothing to lose.

What's Next

The FedEx St. Jude Championship begins Thursday, August 13 at TPC Southwind. The top 50 from Memphis advance to the BMW Championship at Bellerive Country Club in St. Louis, August 20-23. From there, the top 30 reach the Tour Championship at East Lake Golf Club in Atlanta, August 27-30, where the FedExCup champion is determined. Three weeks, three cuts, one champion.
