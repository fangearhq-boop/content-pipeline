Michael Brennan Wins the Wyndham Championship at 22-Under, Punches His FedExCup Playoff Ticket

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | August 10, 2026

PGA Tour / Wyndham Championship / FedExCup

Michael Brennan came to Sedgefield Country Club in Greensboro, North Carolina needing a strong week to stay alive in the FedExCup race. He left with the trophy.

Brennan won the 2026 Wyndham Championship at 22-under-par 258, claiming the $1.53 million winner's check and 500 FedExCup points. The victory moved him from 105th in the FedExCup standings — comfortably outside the top 70 — into the playoff field with room to spare. He didn't need to calculate. He won.

A Week That Defined a Season

The Wyndham Championship is the final event of the PGA Tour regular season, and its drama comes almost entirely from the FedExCup bubble it creates. Players who need a result to survive play with a specific weight. Every birdie means something. Every bogey costs more than it should.

Brennan carried that weight through 72 holes and converted it into his best professional result. He was steady throughout the week at Sedgefield, posting rounds consistent enough to hold off the rest of a field that included players in similar situations — men who needed this week to matter.

The final-round co-lead entering Sunday belonged to Brennan and Beau Hossler, who was ranked 122nd and needed an outright win to advance. Hossler fell short of the win, which means his season ends in Greensboro. Brennan's doesn't.

FedExCup Fallout: Fisk Goes Home

Brennan's 500 FedExCup points had immediate consequences for Steven Fisk, who had been sitting just inside the top 70 entering the final round. Brennan's points haul pushed Fisk out of the field, eliminating him from playoff contention. The FedExCup bubble is the defining cruelty of the regular season's last event: winning doesn't just help you, it often hurts someone else.

Fisk's exit and Brennan's advance are two sides of the same result. The 70-player field for the FedEx St. Jude Championship in Memphis is now officially locked, with Brennan inside it and Fisk watching from home.

What's Next

Brennan heads to TPC Southwind in Memphis for the FedEx St. Jude Championship, which begins Thursday, August 13. He arrives as one of the most compelling stories in the 70-player field — a player who earned his spot not by surviving the bubble but by winning the week outright. Of the 70 players competing in Memphis, the top 50 will advance to the BMW Championship at Bellerive Country Club in St. Louis (August 20-23). The Wyndham winner will be among the most motivated players at TPC Southwind.
