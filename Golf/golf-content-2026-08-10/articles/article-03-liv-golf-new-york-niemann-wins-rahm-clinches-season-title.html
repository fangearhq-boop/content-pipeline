Niemann Wins LIV Golf New York, Rahm Clinches Third Consecutive Individual Season Title at Bedminster

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | August 10, 2026

LIV Golf / LIV Golf New York / Joaquin Niemann / Jon Rahm

Two of the biggest storylines in LIV Golf resolved simultaneously at Trump National Golf Club in Bedminster, New Jersey on Sunday. Joaquin Niemann won LIV Golf New York for his 9th career LIV title, and Jon Rahm clinched his third consecutive individual season championship without needing to win the week's event.

The two stories represent different expressions of dominance — one event-by-event brilliance, the other a season-long accumulation of points that no competitor could match.

Niemann's Wire-to-Wire Victory

Niemann finished at 16-under 268, three shots clear of Harold Varner III. He was in control throughout the weekend — Varner III's Saturday charge brought him within two entering the final round, but Niemann closed with a 66 to make it a comfortable margin at the end.

The $4 million individual winner's payout goes to Niemann, who remains the most prolific event winner in LIV Golf history with nine titles. Torque GC, his team, claimed the team competition as well — a clean sweep of both individual and team honors at Bedminster.

Varner III's runner-up is his best result of the 2026 LIV season. His Saturday 65 created genuine final-round tension before Niemann shut the door.

Rahm Makes History With Third Straight Season Title

Jon Rahm finished T41 at Bedminster, but his position in the individual season standings had already been locked in his favor. No competitor entered the final event with enough points to overtake him, making the clinch a mathematical formality even before the first tee shot landed.

Three consecutive LIV individual season titles is a record no one else in the league's history has achieved. Rahm won in 2024, 2025, and now 2026. His consistency across a full season — not just in tournament wins, but in sustained point accumulation across every event — distinguishes him from players who peak at individual events.

Rahm and Niemann together define what success looks like at the top of LIV Golf. Rahm wins the season. Niemann wins the tournaments. In 2026, both have done their version of winning better than anyone else on the circuit.

What's Next

The LIV Golf season continues with its remaining 2026 events before the restructured LIV 2.0 model — backed by BC Partners' $250-300M investment — takes shape for the 2027 schedule. Niemann and Rahm have established themselves as the faces of LIV Golf heading into that transition. Niemann has nine titles and counting. Rahm has three consecutive season championships. Both will be central figures in whatever the league becomes next year.
