RSM Classic at Sea Island Is the PGA Tour's Season Finale — Cards, Points, and Pride on the Line

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | November 16, 2026

The RSM Classic at Sea Island Golf Club, Georgia runs November 19-22 with $7.4 million in prize money, 500 FedExCup Fall points, and the last opportunity for bubble players to secure their 2027 PGA Tour cards.

When the Austin Championship wraps up on Monday morning, the PGA Tour's fall schedule will have one event left before the 2027 season preparation begins in earnest. The RSM Classic at Sea Island Golf Club in Georgia — running November 19 through 22 — is that final act, and the stakes attached to it are real for a significant segment of the field.

At $7.4 million in prize money and 500 FedExCup Fall points on offer, the RSM Classic is among the more valuable fall events on the schedule. More importantly, it is the last chance for players on the edge of the PGA Tour's card threshold to move their FedExCup Fall standing to a secure position ahead of the 2027 season.

The Courses and the Challenge

The RSM Classic is one of the few PGA Tour events that uses two courses in alternating rounds before cutting to a single layout for the weekend. The Seaside Course (7,005 yards, par 70) and the Plantation Course (7,060 yards, par 72) each present different demands, and the transition between them through the Thursday and Friday rounds is a characteristic feature of the week.

Seaside, the weekend course, is the more celebrated of the two — a coastal layout with wind exposure and tighter landing zones that tends to separate the field when scoring conditions tighten. The combination of the two courses in the opening rounds and the focused challenge of Seaside on the weekend makes Sea Island a unique test even among the tour's varied fall venues.

Why the Field Is Strong

Sea Island has an unusual characteristic for a fall tournament: a significant number of PGA Tour players live in the Sea Island community and use the club as their practice base. That proximity creates a field quality that exceeds what would be expected from a mid-fall event without a significant sponsor exemption profile. Players who have history on the course, who know its quirks and its wind patterns, show up at Sea Island in a way they might not at a comparable event held elsewhere.

Sami Valimaki, who shot 23-under to win the 2025 RSM Classic and who arrives at this week's Austin Championship just one shot off the lead, could potentially be defending at Sea Island next week having also won in Austin. That kind of back-to-back form would make him a compelling storyline regardless of the RSM's other particulars.

The Card Stakes

For players who finished outside the top 125 in the FedExCup regular season, the fall schedule represents a second chance at securing 2027 card status. The FedExCup Fall points are distributed across fall events including the RSM Classic, and players who have accumulated enough through the fall schedule to this point may need one more strong finish to lock up their status.

The RSM Classic, as the final fall event, is where those stories resolve. A player who finishes in the top five at Sea Island and vaults into a secure card position has made the most of a final opportunity. A player who misses the cut and falls short of the threshold faces an offseason of qualifying schools and alternate routes back to the main tour. The tournament means different things depending on where a player sits heading into Thursday's first round.

What's Next

The RSM Classic begins Thursday, November 19 at Sea Island Golf Club in Georgia. Golf Channel will carry coverage throughout the week, with NBC handling weekend rounds. After the final putt drops on Sunday, the PGA Tour's 2026 season will be officially complete, and attention will shift to the 2027 schedule beginning in January.

Last year Valimaki set a tournament record at -23. The year before, Maverick McNealy added the RSM to his winners' list. The 2026 champion will be the first name in the event's new chapter. Cards and final standings will be settled by Sunday evening.
