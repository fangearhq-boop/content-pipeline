LIV Golf Lays Off Majority of Staff as Season Ends, Future Uncertain
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post LIV Golf's 2026 season is officially over — and so, for now, are the jobs of the majority of its 300-plus employees. Three days after the season finale at Indianapolis, LIV Golf informed the bulk of its workforce that their employment would end, with U.S. and UK staff warned back in July that redundancies were possible. The Saudi Public Investment Fund, which poured more than $5 billion into the league since 2022, is stepping away after this season. The new investor on the horizon is BC Partners, a London-based private equity firm with ties to GSE Worldwide, an agency representing much of LIV's player roster. A term sheet worth between $250 million and $350 million was signed and received board approval. But as of today, a final transaction has not been completed. LIV Golf CEO Scott O'Neil said the deal was "signed and approved," but the devil remains in the details — whether the financing is debt or equity, whether additional investors need to join, and whether enough prominent players will recommit before the deal becomes real. LIV had an internal deadline around September 1. That day has passed. The proposed LIV 2.0 would run 10 events in 2027 — five international, five in the United States — with reduced purses and a model that would see players become "majority equity holders" rather than simply collecting appearance fees. Commissioner Greg Norman will not guarantee there will even be a 2027 season. Ian Poulter has said "time might run out." Jon Rahm won his third consecutive LIV individual title. The Indianapolis finale was won by La Sasso. More than 50 contracted players are now waiting to see if there is a league to return to. ### Image Post Caption LIV Golf enters September with no Saudi money, a signed-but-not-finalized deal with BC Partners, and the majority of its staff now laid off. The future of the circuit's sixth year has never been less certain.

Solheim Cup Preview — USA and Europe Set for Netherlands Showdown Starting Thursday
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post The Solheim Cup is four days away, and for the first time in the tournament's history, it heads to the Netherlands. Bernardus Golf in Cromvoirt, North Brabant, will host the 2026 match from September 7-13, with Team USA captain Angela Stanford looking to become the first American captain to win back-to-back since 2015 and 2017. Standing in her way is European captain Anna Nordqvist, leading a home-continent squad in front of what is expected to be a sold-out Dutch gallery. Team USA is headlined by Nelly Korda, the world No. 1 player who won four times and captured two major titles in 2026. Korda arrives at the Solheim as arguably the best women's golfer on the planet. Her supporting cast includes Angel Yin, Jennifer Kupcho, Yealimi Noh, Auston Kim, Lauren Coughlin, Allisen Corpuz, Andrea Lee, and Alison Lee. For Europe, Charley Hull — ranked No. 7 in the world — is the marquee name in Nordqvist's lineup. The 2026 European squad includes four players making their Solheim Cup debuts, three of them — Julia López Ramírez, Nastasia Nadaud, and Mimi Rhodes — chosen as captain's picks by Nordqvist. The United States won the most recent Solheim Cup, beating Europe 15.5-12.5 in Virginia in 2024. Before that, Europe had won or tied three consecutive events. Back-to-back American wins would be a genuine statement of depth — and a sign that the gap between the programs has narrowed for good. ### Image Post Caption The 2026 Solheim Cup tips off Thursday at Bernardus Golf in the Netherlands. Nelly Korda leads Team USA. Charley Hull headlines Europe. First-ever Dutch host. Sept 7-13.

Presidents Cup Rosters Set — Scheffler Leads USA at Medinah Sept 24-27
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post The 2026 Presidents Cup rosters are complete after both captains named their six picks Monday. Brandt Snedeker's United States team for Medinah (September 24-27) is a blend of proven veterans and rising talent. Six automatic qualifiers — Scottie Scheffler, Cameron Young, Wyndham Clark, Russell Henley, Sam Burns, and Collin Morikawa — are joined by Snedeker's captain's picks: Chris Gotterup, Xander Schauffele, Justin Thomas, Jacob Bridgeman, Patrick Cantlay, and Jackson Koivun. At 21, Koivun is the youngest player named to a Presidents Cup team, earning his spot just months after leaving Auburn and turning professional. Geoff Ogilvy leads the International side. Automatic qualifiers Si Woo Kim, Hideki Matsuyama, Ryan Fox, Tom Kim, Min Woo Lee, and Adam Scott are joined by picks Corey Conners, Nico Echavarria, Sungjae Im, Ryo Hisatsune, Nick Taylor, and Christiaan Bezuidenhout. Medinah Country Club's Course No. 3 is an iconic venue — it hosted the 2012 Ryder Cup, one of the most dramatic matches in golf history. The 30-match format covers nine foursomes, nine fourball, and twelve singles sessions. Practice rounds begin September 22. For USA, the motivation is clear: Europe beat them at the 2025 Ryder Cup, and this is the first major team event since. ### Image Post Caption Full Presidents Cup rosters confirmed. Scheffler Officially Passes Tiger Woods on PGA Tour Career Earnings List
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Scottie Scheffler has done what seemed unthinkable even five years ago: he has officially passed Tiger Woods as the leading career earner in PGA Tour history. Scheffler's $10 million Tour Championship victory pushed his career official earnings to $130,390,661 — surpassing Tiger's official PGA Tour mark of $120,999,166. The record is now Scheffler's. What makes the number more striking is the speed of it. Scheffler turned 30 just two months ago. His 22nd PGA Tour win came in the same season he claimed his second FedExCup title and his fifth consecutive season as the world's No. 1 ranked player — an unprecedented streak. He won three times in 2026 officially: the American Express ($1,656,000), the FedEx St. Jude Championship ($3,600,000), and the Tour Championship ($10,000,000). Add the separate $23 million FedExCup bonus and his combined 2026 earnings reach $53,937,525 — a figure that would have been inconceivable in any prior era. Tiger's official earnings number predates today's inflated purses, and international money is excluded from official PGA Tour totals on both sides. Still, Scheffler's mark stands as a statement of sustained excellence: 22 wins, two Masters titles, two FedExCup championships, and no real sign of slowing down. ### Image Post Caption Scottie Scheffler: $130,390,661 in career PGA Tour earnings. Tiger Woods held the record at $120,999,166. At 30 years old, Scheffler has 22 wins and two Masters titles — and he's just getting started.

PGA Tour Fall Begins — Biltmore Championship Opens New Season Phase Sept 17
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post The PGA Tour's 2026-27 FedExCup Fall season is two weeks away, and it opens with a new event at one of America's most iconic estates. The Biltmore Championship, set for September 17-20 at The Cliffs at Walnut Cove in Asheville, North Carolina, returns professional golf to the Blue Ridge Mountains for the first time in more than 80 years. Ben Hogan won the final three Asheville Opens at this region from 1940 to 1942 — the next PGA Tour winner here will be answering to a completely different era. The Biltmore Championship serves as the only tuneup opportunity before the Presidents Cup begins on September 24 at Medinah. Expect to see a healthy portion of the U.S. and International rosters use Asheville as a shakedown before team match play. After the Presidents Cup, the fall schedule continues: Bank of Utah Championship (Oct. 1-4, Ivins UT), Baycurrent Classic (Oct. 8-11, Japan), Butterfield Bermuda Championship (Oct. 22-25), and the new Good Good Championship (Nov. 9-15) at Omni Barton Creek Resort & Spa in Austin, Texas. Eight events. Two debuts. The 2026-27 season is underway. ### Image Post Caption The PGA Tour Fall begins Sept 17 with the inaugural Biltmore Championship in Asheville, NC — the first pro tour stop in the Blue Ridge Mountains since Ben Hogan's era. Eight-event fall slate runs through November.
