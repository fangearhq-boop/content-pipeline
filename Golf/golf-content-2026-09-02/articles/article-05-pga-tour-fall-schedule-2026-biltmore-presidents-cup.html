PGA Tour Fall Schedule 2026: Biltmore Championship, Presidents Cup, and Two New Events on the 2026-27 Slate

By Marcus Cole

September 2, 2026

The 2026-27 PGA Tour season launches in two weeks with an eight-tournament FedExCup Fall slate that features two debut events, a Presidents Cup anchor, and a trip to Japan. The first stop — the inaugural Biltmore Championship in Asheville, North Carolina — begins September 17 and returns professional golf to the Blue Ridge Mountains for the first time in more than 80 years.

Biltmore Championship — Sept. 17-20, Asheville, NC

The Biltmore Championship plays at The Cliffs at Walnut Cove in Asheville, North Carolina, a new PGA Tour event hosted on the grounds adjacent to the Biltmore Estate. This is the PGA Tour's first stop in western North Carolina since Ben Hogan won three consecutive Asheville Opens there from 1940 to 1942. The inaugural event carries particular weight as the final competitive tune-up before the Presidents Cup — expect several members of both squads to use the week as preparation before traveling to Medinah.

Presidents Cup — Sept. 24-27, Medinah Country Club, Medinah, IL

The Presidents Cup at Medinah Country Club anchors the fall slate and stands as the marquee event of the opening stretch. Full rosters were confirmed September 1, with Brandt Snedeker's United States side led by Scottie Scheffler and Geoff Ogilvy's International team anchored by Hideki Matsuyama. Thirty matches across four days of competition begin September 24. The format covers nine foursomes, nine fourball, and twelve singles sessions.

The Rest of the Fall Schedule

The Bank of Utah Championship follows October 1-4 in Ivins, Utah. The tour then travels internationally for the Baycurrent Classic in Japan, October 8-11. After a gap, the Butterfield Bermuda Championship runs October 22-25. The fall concludes with the brand-new Good Good Championship, November 9-15 at Omni Barton Creek Resort and Spa in Austin, Texas — the second new event on the fall slate and the season's final event before the holiday break.

The Good Good Championship is named in partnership with the popular golf media brand and represents another effort by the PGA Tour to connect the professional game with the content-creation side of golf that has grown dramatically in audience over the past several years.

What's Next

The Biltmore Championship field and tee times will be announced later this week. Players pursuing FedExCup points in the fall can move significantly up standings in the eight-event slate, which runs parallel to the Presidents Cup's off-limits period for participating players. The full schedule continues into late November before the holiday break preceding the 2027 swing events.
