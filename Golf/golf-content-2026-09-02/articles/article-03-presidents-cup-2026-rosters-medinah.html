Presidents Cup 2026 Rosters Complete: Scheffler Leads USA, Matsuyama Anchors International at Medinah

By Ryan Calloway

September 2, 2026

Both captains announced their six picks Monday, completing the 12-man rosters for both sides ahead of the 2026 Presidents Cup at Medinah Country Club in Medinah, Illinois. The matches run September 24-27, with practice rounds scheduled for September 22-23. For the United States, captain Brandt Snedeker added a 21-year-old first-timer to a team otherwise composed of proven performers. For the International side, captain Geoff Ogilvy rounded out a squad anchored by Hideki Matsuyama.

Team USA: Veterans and a Prodigy

Six players qualified automatically for Snedeker's team: Scottie Scheffler, Cameron Young, Wyndham Clark, Russell Henley, Sam Burns, and Collin Morikawa. Snedeker's six captain's picks were Chris Gotterup, Xander Schauffele, Justin Thomas, Jacob Bridgeman, Patrick Cantlay, and Jackson Koivun.

Koivun, 21, is the story within the story. He left Auburn and turned professional just months ago, and has made a fast enough impact to earn Snedeker's confidence for the biggest team stage in American golf outside of the Ryder Cup. He becomes the youngest player named to a Presidents Cup team. Schauffele and Thomas bring Ryder Cup experience; Cantlay's inclusion gives Snedeker a reliable anchor for the fourball sessions where his steady game shines.

The United States arrives at Medinah with a point to prove. Europe defeated the Americans at the 2025 Ryder Cup, and this Presidents Cup is the first major team event since that loss. Snedeker's squad is stacked — Scheffler alone won the Tour Championship and FedExCup — and will be motivated.

International Team: Matsuyama Leads a Deep Roster

Ogilvy's automatic qualifiers are Si Woo Kim, Hideki Matsuyama, Ryan Fox, Tom Kim, Min Woo Lee, and Adam Scott. His picks are Corey Conners, Nico Echavarria, Sungjae Im, Ryo Hisatsune, Nick Taylor, and Christiaan Bezuidenhout.

Matsuyama remains the International team's defining presence — a former major champion and one of the most dangerous ball-strikers in the world. Adam Scott's inclusion adds veteran leadership. Fox, Tom Kim, and Min Woo Lee represent the sort of electric mid-range talent that Ogilvy can deploy across formats without reservation. The International team has struggled in recent Presidents Cups against American depth, but this roster is competitive enough to make it close.

Medinah and the Format

Medinah Country Club's Course No. 3 is one of the most storied venues in golf, famously hosting the 2012 Ryder Cup where Europe completed one of sport's great comebacks on Sunday singles. The Presidents Cup format runs 30 matches: nine foursomes, nine fourball, and twelve singles. Both captains will be eager to set the tone in the foursome sessions, where pairings strategy can dictate the week's momentum.

What's Next

The Biltmore Championship in Asheville (September 17-20) offers several Presidents Cup participants a tuneup before Medinah. Players will travel to Illinois the following week, with practice rounds September 22-23 before competition begins September 24. Coverage will be available on Golf Channel and Peacock throughout the week.
