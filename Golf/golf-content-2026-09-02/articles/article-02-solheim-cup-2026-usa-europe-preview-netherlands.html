Solheim Cup 2026 Preview: Nelly Korda Leads USA Into First-Ever Netherlands Showdown Against Europe

By Marcus Cole

September 2, 2026

The 2026 Solheim Cup begins Thursday, and for the first time in the biennial competition's history, Europe is hosting on Dutch soil. Bernardus Golf in Cromvoirt, North Brabant, Netherlands, serves as the first-ever Dutch venue for the event, running September 7-13. Team USA captain Angela Stanford arrives looking to make history of her own — a back-to-back American victory would be the first since 2015 and 2017.

Team USA: Korda at the Center

Nelly Korda enters this week as the world No. 1 player and the undisputed headliner of Stanford's squad. Korda won four times in 2026, including two major titles, and is arguably the most dominant women's golfer alive heading into a team format that suits her consistency and competitive temperament.

Stanford's team also includes Angel Yin, Jennifer Kupcho, Yealimi Noh, Auston Kim, Lauren Coughlin, Allisen Corpuz, Andrea Lee, and Alison Lee. The United States won the most recent Solheim Cup, beating Europe 15.5-12.5 in Virginia in 2024, and arrives in the Netherlands with the confidence of a defending champion and the top-ranked individual player in the field.

Team Europe: Nordqvist's Home-Continent Lineup

European captain Anna Nordqvist is rallying a squad that carries four Solheim Cup debutantes, three of them — Julia López Ramírez, Nastasia Nadaud, and Mimi Rhodes — named as captain's picks. Charley Hull, ranked No. 7 in the world, anchors the European side and provides the marquee rivalry matchup against Korda that team-event fans anticipate all year.

Before the 2024 defeat, Europe had dominated the Solheim Cup, winning or tying three consecutive editions. Nordqvist's task is to restore that European advantage on home-continent ground, with a Dutch gallery expected to provide the kind of raucous support that historically lifts the host side.

Format and Key Matches to Watch

The Solheim Cup uses a match play format across three days of competition following two practice days. Both squads field 14 players. The pairings and session formats will be announced each day, with captains choosing their lineups ahead of foursomes and fourball sessions before 12 singles matches close the competition. Any captain who can put Korda out early and often will likely do so — and Nordqvist's response to that calculus may define Europe's strategic approach.

What's Next

Practice rounds at Bernardus Golf begin Wednesday. Competition opens Thursday, September 7, and runs through the closing singles on Sunday, September 13. Live coverage is available via Golf Channel and Peacock in the United States. The winner will be determined by who reaches 14.5 points first in the 28-point format.
