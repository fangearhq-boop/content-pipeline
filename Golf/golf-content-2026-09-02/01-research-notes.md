## Story 2: Solheim Cup Preview

- Dates: September 7-13, 2026 at Bernardus Golf in Cromvoirt, North Brabant, Netherlands
- First-ever Solheim Cup in the Netherlands
- USA captain: Angela Stanford
- Europe captain: Anna Nordqvist
- USA lost most recent Solheim Cup to Europe before winning 2024 15.5-12.5 in Virginia
- Europe had dominated, winning 3 consecutive events (2 wins + 1 tie) before 2024
- USA hasn't won back-to-back Solheim Cups since 2015 and 2017

**Team USA (14 players):** Nelly Korda (world No. 1, 4 LPGA wins + 2 majors in 2026), Angel Yin, Jennifer Kupcho, Yealimi Noh, Auston Kim, Lauren Coughlin, Allisen Corpuz, Andrea Lee, Alison Lee + captain's picks (not yet named in search results)

- Sources: CBS Sports, Sky Sports, Wikipedia, Golf Magic, The Fried Egg
