Walmart NW Arkansas Championship Marks 20th Anniversary Sept. 25-27 With $3M LPGA Purse

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap

September 4, 2026

Twenty years in, the Walmart NW Arkansas Championship is still one of the LPGA Tour's premier events — and the 2026 edition promises to be particularly memorable.

The Walmart NW Arkansas Championship presented by P&G marks its 20th anniversary September 25-27 at Pinnacle Country Club in Rogers, Arkansas, featuring a $3 million purse and a field of 144 of the world's best female golfers. For two decades, the tournament has been a cornerstone of the LPGA calendar and a showcase for the sport in one of America's most passionate golf markets.

A Marquee Autumn Setting

Pinnacle Country Club has hosted the event since 2007, giving the field a familiar track that rewards precision and patience. The course demands accurate iron play and sharp decision-making, consistently producing competitive leaderboards that go down to the wire.

The 20th anniversary edition adds historical resonance to what is already one of the most well-attended and commercially successful events on the LPGA Tour calendar. Arkansas' growing reputation as a golf destination — driven in part by two decades of Walmart championship investment — makes Rogers a genuine home for the sport.

Post-Solheim Cup Timing

The tournament's placement on the calendar is particularly interesting in 2026. Coming just 12 days after the conclusion of the Solheim Cup (September 7-13), the NW Arkansas Championship will draw heavily from the women's team that just competed in the Netherlands — and those players will arrive at Pinnacle Country Club with very different emotional states.

For the winning team's players, confidence and momentum are typically strong in the events that follow the Solheim Cup. For those on the losing side, the event often serves as an opportunity for personal affirmation and a chance to reset. The result is a field where the psychological dynamics are more complex than a standard stroke-play event.

Nelly Korda — the world No. 1 who leads Team USA into the Solheim Cup — is likely to be in the field, as are many of the Solheim Cup participants from both sides.

The $3M Purse and Field Depth

The $3 million purse represents a substantial investment in women's professional golf and places the NW Arkansas Championship among the higher-paying events on the LPGA calendar. With 144 players competing, the field encompasses the full range of world-ranked players, from established major champions to emerging young talent fighting for their cards.

For players seeking to improve their position in the Race to the CME Globe standings heading into the final stretch of the LPGA season, NW Arkansas is a critical counting event.

What's Next

Field announcements and tee times will be released as the tournament approaches. With the Solheim Cup starting Sunday and the Presidents Cup at Medinah beginning September 22, the NW Arkansas Championship caps a genuinely extraordinary stretch of elite team and individual golf.

Twenty years. Still delivering. Rogers, Arkansas knows how to put on a show.
