Presidents Cup 2026: Tiger Woods Captains USA at Medinah, Three Weeks Away

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap

September 4, 2026

The Solheim Cup opens Sunday. Less than three weeks later, it's the men's turn.

The 2026 Presidents Cup tees off September 22-27 at Medinah Country Club in Medinah, Illinois — one of American golf's most historic venues, forever etched into the sport's memory from the 2012 Ryder Cup. This time, the battle is USA versus the International Team, and the home side arrives with considerable firepower.

Team USA: Star Power at Every Position

Tiger Woods makes his debut as USA captain, a role that has generated as much interest as the competition itself. The 15-time major champion brings an unmatched competitive resume and a presence that commands respect from players on both sides of the draw.

His team headlines two of the season's defining players. Scottie Scheffler, who claimed the FedExCup championship at East Lake, arrives as the undisputed top-ranked American and one of the best ball-strikers in the world. Rory McIlroy — who completed his career Grand Slam by winning the 2026 Masters — adds a player of extraordinary championship experience to a team that did not need more star power but has it anyway.

The United States has historically dominated the Presidents Cup, winning all but two editions in the competition's history.

The International Team and Adam Scott

Adam Scott captains the International side — a team that has struggled to win the Presidents Cup since 1998 but has consistently produced competitive golf in recent editions. The International Team draws players from across Australia, Asia, Africa, South America and other non-European nations, giving it a uniquely global identity.

Scott has been one of the International Team's finest competitors and brings captaincy experience, tactical intelligence and a deep understanding of what it takes to compete against an American side that nearly always has more recognized names.

Medinah's Historic Stage

Medinah Country Club's Course No. 3 is steeped in golf history. The club has hosted four major championships, and the 2012 Ryder Cup — where Europe staged one of the greatest comebacks in the event's history, overturning a 10-6 USA lead to win 14.5-13.5 — gave Medinah a dramatic resonance that adds texture to any major competition held on its grounds.

The course will demand precise iron play, premium performance around the greens and the ability to navigate a challenging layout that has tested the world's best for decades.

What's Next

Three weeks is not long. Team practices will begin in the week before the event, and captain's pairings and session formats will be announced as competition approaches. The Presidents Cup runs Thursday through Sunday, September 22-27.

With Tiger Woods on the team bench, Scheffler and McIlroy swinging clubs, and Medinah providing the stage, September's final week promises to be one of the most compelling in golf's year.
