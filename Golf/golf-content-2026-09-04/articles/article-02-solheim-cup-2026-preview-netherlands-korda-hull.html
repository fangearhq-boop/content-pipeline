Solheim Cup 2026 Preview: USA and Europe Set for Historic Netherlands Showdown

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap

September 4, 2026

Three days from now, women's golf takes the stage in a way it has not done in the Netherlands before.

The 2026 Solheim Cup opens September 7 at Bernardus Golf in Cromvoirt, North Brabant — the first time in the competition's 37-year history that it has been held on Dutch soil. Team USA and Team Europe have finalized their 12-player rosters, captains Angela Stanford and Anna Nordqvist have mapped their strategies, and the world's best women golfers are ready.

Team USA: Korda-Led and Deep

The Americans arrived as defending champions, having won the 2024 Solheim Cup 15.5-12.5 in Virginia — the fourth time in the last five editions that USA has won. Stanford's team is built around depth and experience.

Nelly Korda leads the charge. The world No. 1 has been in magnificent form throughout 2026, claiming four victories including two major titles. Her Solheim Cup record stands at 10-5-1 across four prior appearances. Among the returning players, Rose Zhang was 4-0 at the 2024 edition, and Megan Khang brings a 7-3-3 career record. Stanford selected Khang, Zhang and Lindy Duncan as captain's picks to complete the 12-player squad alongside nine automatic qualifiers.

Team Europe: Hungry for Revenge

Anna Nordqvist has assembled a team with eight past Solheim Cup participants and four rookies — including world No. 5 Lottie Woad, who represents Europe's next generation of talent.

Charley Hull is Europe's emotional centerpiece. At world No. 7, she is one of two top-10 players on the European roster, and she is making her eighth consecutive Solheim Cup appearance — the longest active streak on either team. Hull's presence, experience and match-play intensity will be central to Europe's hopes of reversing a 15.5-12.5 defeat and claiming back the trophy.

Europe has a historical edge to draw on: the team won three consecutive Solheim Cups before the 2024 loss, and the motivation to restore that dominance is palpable.

The Course

Bernardus Golf is a private club that opened in 2010 and has quickly established a reputation as one of continental Europe's finest golf experiences. The parkland course in North Brabant will provide a distinctive and unfamiliar setting for both teams, removing any home-course advantage — though Europe's players are more comfortable navigating European conditions.

Key Matchups to Watch

Nelly Korda vs. Charley Hull is the marquee individual rivalry. When these two play in the same session, the atmosphere is electric. Hull's defiant, aggressive style versus Korda's precision and composure makes for compelling golf.

Lottie Woad vs. Rose Zhang — two of the most talented young players in the women's game, both making early-career Solheim Cup appearances. Their performances could define the next decade of this competition.

What's Next

The opening ceremony takes place before competition begins. Foursomes and fourballs sessions run through Saturday, with singles matches on Sunday September 13. The team that reaches 14.5 points wins the cup; 14 points retains it for the holder.

The Solheim Cup is the biggest team event in women's golf. September 7-13 in the Netherlands is going to be exceptional.
