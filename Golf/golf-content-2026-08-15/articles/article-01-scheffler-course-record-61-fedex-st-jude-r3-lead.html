Scheffler Ties Course Record With 61 — Leads FedEx St. Jude by 3 Heading Into Saturday

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | August 15, 2026

Scottie Scheffler birdied his first five holes at TPC Southwind on Friday, kept birdieing, and by the time his round was finished he had tied the course record with a 9-under 61 and held a commanding lead in the FedEx St. Jude Championship.

Scheffler stands at 11-under par heading into Round 3 on Saturday — three clear of Sungjae Im and Viktor Hovland. He made six birdies in his first seven holes Friday and drained 54 feet of birdie putts total. CBS Sports called his Round 2 "the best round of the week by three shots."

It was also a course record. The 61 ties the lowest score ever recorded at TPC Southwind — matching a mark that the Memphis venue has rarely seen challenged.

How the Round Unfolded

Scheffler arrived at TPC Southwind as the FedExCup No. 1 seed — having earned the top regular-season ranking for the fourth time in five years along with a $10 million bonus — but his first round had not matched his billing. Friday was different from the jump.

The opening five holes brought five consecutive birdies. By the sixth hole, Scheffler had birdied six of his first seven. Whatever was working on Friday — the ball-striking, the putting, the course management — it was working in combination, and TPC Southwind had no answer.

The 61 launched him from relative obscurity up the leaderboard and into the outright lead, three shots clear of Im and Hovland and four ahead of Ludvig Aberg. Tommy Fleetwood and Jordan Spieth — who had led or shared the lead after Round 1 — both sit at 6-under, five back.

The Round 3 Picture

Scheffler tees off at 1:45 PM ET Saturday alongside Im. Television coverage runs on Golf Channel from 1 to 3 PM ET, then moves to CBS from 3 to 6 PM ET. ESPN+ provides early streaming coverage starting at 7:45 AM ET.

A three-shot lead entering Moving Day is not decisive — courses have been made up, and TPC Southwind can reward aggressive scoring. Im, the R2 co-runner-up, is a player capable of a charge. Hovland has experience competing in playoff situations.

But Scheffler is the FedExCup No. 1 seed, the world's best player, and the man who just tied the course record. The burden of proof runs in one direction on Saturday afternoon.

Sources for this article include Golf Channel, CBS Sports, Central Oregon Daily, and Heavy.com, all reporting August 14-15, 2026.

What's Next

Round 3 is underway Saturday afternoon at TPC Southwind. Round 4 — the final round — is Sunday. The FedEx St. Jude Championship concludes August 16. The top 70 in FedExCup standings after this event advance to the BMW Championship next week, with the Tour Championship (and the $23M FedExCup prize) waiting at East Lake for the top 30 after BMW.
