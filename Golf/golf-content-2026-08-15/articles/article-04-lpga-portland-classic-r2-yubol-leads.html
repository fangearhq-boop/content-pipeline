Yubol Leads Portland Classic at 12-Under With Two Rounds to Play — Anannarukarn One Back

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | August 15, 2026

Arpichaya Yubol leads the Standard Portland Classic at 12-under par after two rounds at Columbia Edgewater Country Club in Portland, Oregon — with Patty Anannarukarn and Somi Lee Schubert one shot back and a full field still in contention heading into Saturday's Round 3.

The tournament, running August 13-16, carries a $2 million purse and is the 20th event of the 2026 LPGA season. With rounds 3 and 4 ahead, the leaderboard is tight enough that a Sunday charge from almost any player in the top five could flip the outcome.

R2 Leaderboard

Yubol's 12-under total gives her a one-shot lead over a tied group at 11-under: Anannarukarn (Thailand) and Somi Lee Schubert (USA). Hye-Jin Choi (South Korea) is also at 11-under. The group at 9-under — three back — includes Jeeno Thitikul, Eri Hara, Ruoning Zhang, and Haeran Ryu, all of them former winners or players with records of mounting weekend charges.

Thitikul, in particular, has been one of the most aggressive second-half performers on the LPGA Tour throughout her career. Three shots off the lead with 36 holes remaining is not a comfortable buffer against a player of her caliber.

Megha Ganne's LPGA Debut

One of the side stories worth following this week: Megha Ganne is making her professional LPGA debut at the Portland Classic. She received a field exemption through the Inkster Award, which recognizes the highest-ranked college golfer in their final year of eligibility.

Ganne was a four-time All-American at Stanford, where she was part of two NCAA championship-winning teams. Last summer she won the U.S. Women's Amateur at Bandon Dunes. This week marks the transition from amateur to professional — a moment that comes once in any player's career.

Looking Ahead

Round 3 is Saturday August 15, Round 4 is Sunday August 16. The Portland Classic winner will carry momentum directly into the following week's CPKC Women's Open, running August 20-23 at Royal Mayfair Golf Club in Edmonton, Alberta, with a $2.75 million purse. Nelly Korda is expected to lead the field in Edmonton.

Sources for this article include LPGA, Golf Channel, ESPN, and Flashscore, all reporting August 14-15, 2026.

What's Next

Round 3 of the Portland Classic is Saturday at Columbia Edgewater CC. A tight leaderboard means every shot over the weekend carries weight. Watch for Thitikul to make a move on Saturday, and for the battle between Yubol, Anannarukarn, and Schubert to define the final-round setup on Sunday.
