50 Tickets to the BMW Championship: Here's Who's In, Who's Out Heading Into the St. Jude Weekend

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | August 15, 2026

Seventy players entered the FedEx St. Jude Championship at TPC Southwind. Fifty will leave Memphis with BMW Championship tee times. Twenty will be done for the year.

That's the math of the FedEx Cup Playoffs — clean, public, and unforgiving. With two rounds remaining at TPC Southwind, the weekend is sorting the 2026 playoff field in real time.

Spieth: Moving In

Jordan Spieth was the headline bubble story entering the week. He arrived ranked 54th in the FedExCup standings — four places outside the top 50 cutoff that determines who plays at the BMW Championship next week. He had zero top-10 finishes in the entire 2026 regular season. He was out in every sense of the word.

Then he opened with a bogey-free 65 to share the Round 1 lead at TPC Southwind. That performance projected him to move inside the top 50 in the FedExCup standings, which means a BMW Championship tee time is now within reach. He sits at 6-under after two rounds — five back of Scheffler — and needs to keep making birdies through Saturday and Sunday to cement the projection into a result.

Max Homa entered at 52nd, also on the bubble. His week will be equally consequential for BMW field construction.

McIlroy: Under Pressure

Rory McIlroy opened with a 4-over round Thursday, landing 68th out of 69 players. He is 12th in the FedExCup standings entering the week — comfortably inside the top 50 by rank — which means his BMW Championship berth is not in immediate danger from a single bad tournament. But the seeding implications matter.

The FedExCup standings after St. Jude determine the starting positions entering the BMW Championship, which in turn shapes the Tour Championship seeding at East Lake — where the top seed begins at 10-under and every other player starts at a deficit. McIlroy, chasing a record fourth FedExCup title, cannot afford to let a bad St. Jude week create unnecessary obstacles at the later events.

Scheffler: Already Through

Scottie Scheffler, who leads the St. Jude at 11-under after a course-record 61 in Round 2, has no meaningful concern about the BMW cutoff. He is the FedExCup No. 1 seed, the $10M regular-season bonus recipient, and by most calculations an overwhelming favorite to win the FedExCup title if he reaches East Lake. His weekend at TPC Southwind is about winning the event and building momentum, not survival.

What's Next

Rounds 3 and 4 at TPC Southwind are Saturday and Sunday. The FedEx St. Jude Championship concludes August 16. The BMW Championship follows immediately. For the players on the bubble, every birdie this weekend is a playoff lifeline — and every bogey is a step closer to the exit. The full FedExCup standings after Sunday determine the final 50-player BMW field.
