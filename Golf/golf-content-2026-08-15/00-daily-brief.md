### STORY 1: Scheffler's Course-Record 61 Gives Him 3-Shot Lead at FedEx St. Jude Heading Into R3
- **Tier:** 1
- **Category:** PGA Tour / FedEx St. Jude Championship / Scottie Scheffler
- **Fresh angle:** FOLLOW UP to St. Jude coverage. Scheffler fired a 9-under 61 in Round 2 Friday, tying the TPC Southwind course record. He birdied his first five holes and six of his first seven, drained 54 feet of birdie putts total. That launched him from relative obscurity into the lead at 11-under par — a 3-shot advantage over Sungjae Im and Viktor Hovland heading into Round 3 on Saturday. Scheffler tees off at 1:45 PM ET paired with Im. Round 3 broadcast: Golf Channel 1-3 PM, then CBS 3-6 PM ET.
- **Sources:** Golf Channel, CBS Sports, Central Oregon Daily, Heavy.com (Aug 14-15)

### STORY 2: Spieth's Bogey-Free 65 Gives BMW Championship Hope — 54th to Projected Top 50
- **Tier:** 1
- **Category:** PGA Tour / FedEx Cup Playoffs / Jordan Spieth / BMW Championship
- **Fresh angle:** FOLLOW UP to Spieth bubble story. Spieth opened the FedEx St. Jude Championship with a bogey-free 5-under 65 Thursday, sharing the first-round lead as part of a 5-way tie. He arrived ranked 54th in the FedExCup — outside the top 50 that advance to the BMW Championship. After his strong Round 1, he is projected to move inside the top 50. Spieth quote: "In a very different place" than recent seasons. This is his 14th consecutive year in the FedEx Cup Playoffs despite zero top-10 finishes in the 2026 regular season. Also notable: Max Homa entered at 52nd in FedExCup standings, also on the bubble.
- **Sources:** ESPN, PGA Tour, AthlonSports, Yahoo Sports, Heavy.com, golf.com (Aug 13-14)

- **Tier:** 2
- **Category:** LPGA / Standard Portland Classic / Arpichaya Yubol

### STORY 5: BMW Bubble Watch — Who Makes It From St. Jude? Top 50 Advance to Next Leg
- **Tier:** 2
- **Category:** PGA Tour / FedEx Cup Playoffs / BMW Championship
- **Fresh angle:** St. Jude Championship is the first of three FedEx Cup Playoff events. Of the 70 players in the field, the top 50 in the FedExCup standings after St. Jude advance to the BMW Championship next week. McIlroy, who started 4-over at 68th of 69, risks slipping further down. Scheffler's dominant position reinforces his FedEx Cup title contention. Heading into the weekend, Saturday-Sunday R3-R4 at TPC Southwind will determine who plays on.
