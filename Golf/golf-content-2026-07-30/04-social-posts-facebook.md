### STORY 2: The Final Rocket Classic Begins — On a Completely Renovated Detroit Golf Club

#### Long-Form Post — 10:30 AM ET

The final Rocket Classic starts today at Detroit Golf Club — and golfers are arriving at a course they've never played before.

Detroit Golf Club completed a $16.1 million restoration project this year. The scope went well beyond renovation: the course changed from a par 72 to a par 70. A total of 300 yards were added to the scorecard. Holes 7 and 17 were converted from par 5s into long par 4s — one plays 505 yards, the other 537. The number of bunkers increased from 84 to 91. Every single green was rebuilt to match Donald Ross's original 1914 design. The pond near the 14th hole was removed entirely.

The result is a fundamentally different test. Distance is still the primary factor — but the greens are harder, the bunkering is more punishing, and two holes that used to offer birdie opportunities on the back end now demand everything a player has just to make par.

Into this changed landscape comes Cameron Young, the tournament favorite. Young has already won twice in 2026 (The Players Championship, the Cadillac Championship) and enters as the top-ranked player in a field that notably does not include Scottie Scheffler or Rory McIlroy, both resting before the FedExCup Playoffs. Aldrich Potgieter defends after winning the 2025 edition at age 20. Jackson Koivun — who won the 3M Open last week in just his third career start — is in the field for the second consecutive week.
