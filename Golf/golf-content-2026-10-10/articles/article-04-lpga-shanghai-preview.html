LPGA Heads to Shanghai — Preview of the October 15-18 Tournament

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | October 10, 2026

The LPGA Tour continues its fall international swing with a trip to Shanghai, where a tournament is scheduled from October 15 to 18. The event brings the world's best female golfers to China for one of the season's final significant international stops, maintaining the tour's commitment to global competition in the closing weeks of the 2026 season.

Shanghai has become a familiar name on the LPGA calendar. The city's combination of passionate local fans, strong sponsorship backing, and tournament-quality courses has made it a reliable destination for the tour. The October timing gives the event a meaningful place in the season's final stretch, with players using the result to strengthen their year-end rankings ahead of any remaining events.

What to Watch

The tournament begins Thursday October 15 and wraps up Sunday October 18. The field will feature the tour's top-ranked players competing on a course that typically rewards accuracy and short-game precision over raw power. The LPGA's fall international events have historically seen competitive, tight finishes — Shanghai's layout tends to compress the field rather than allow runaway performances.

Watch the players near the top of the world rankings using this event to consolidate their end-of-season positions. For players slightly off the top spots, Shanghai is also a chance to make a move in the season standings with a strong week. The points available make it a meaningful event for anyone with ranking ambitions going into the off-season.

The LPGA's International Commitment

The LPGA Tour's international schedule reflects the tour's global reach. Events in Asia consistently draw strong fields and competitive golf, and the Shanghai stop fits that pattern. The local market's enthusiasm for golf — and for the LPGA specifically — has grown steadily, and the tour's continued return to the city is a signal of the event's health and importance to both the tour and its sponsors.

What's Next

The LPGA Shanghai tournament gets underway Thursday October 15. Full coverage of the field, course setup, and round-by-round scoring will be available through the LPGA Tour's official channels and broadcast partners. Golf Fanrecap will have previews and daily updates when action begins.
