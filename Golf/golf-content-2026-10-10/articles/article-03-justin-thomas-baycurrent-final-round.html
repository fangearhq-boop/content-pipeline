Justin Thomas One Back at Baycurrent Classic — Can He End His Win Drought in the Final Round?

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | October 10, 2026

If Sunday at the Baycurrent Classic ends with Justin Thomas holding the trophy, the story will write itself: the former world No. 1 ending a lengthy win drought against one of the strongest final-round leaderboards of the fall season. That opportunity exists. He enters the day at -12, one shot behind three co-leaders, in a five-man group loaded with players who know how to compete on Sundays.

Thomas brings credentials few on the leaderboard can match. His two major championships — the 2017 and 2022 PGA Championships — confirm his ability to perform when the stakes are highest. His world ranking, quality of ball-striking, and short-game consistency have remained at an elite level even during stretches without wins. The talent is undeniable. The recent results, however, have not reflected it.

The Win Drought Context

Thomas has not won on the PGA Tour in over a year. For a player of his caliber, that gap invites scrutiny — not because the form has been absent, but because closing-round performance has not quite produced the results his 54-hole positions have earned him. He has been in contention. He has not converted.

Sunday at Baycurrent gives him another chance. The co-leaders at -13 are not unbeatable — and against a field with five players one shot back, the leaderboard will compress and expand throughout the final 18 holes. A birdie-birdie start from Thomas at the top of the leaderboard, combined with any stumble from the players ahead of him, puts him in the lead by the time the gallery has warmed up.

The Competition

Thomas is not alone at -12. Adam Scott, a Masters champion, brings the same pedigree and experience. Keith Mitchell has won on Tour and proven his closing ability. Sebastian Jaeger and J. Smith complete a group that will not be intimidated. The five players at -12 are chasing, not waiting — which means the pressure flows both ways on the leaders and the chasers.

What's Next

The Baycurrent Classic final round takes place today. Thomas tees off in one of the featured groups and will be closely watched by the Tour media looking for signs that his win drought is about to end. Whether he delivers — or whether one of the co-leaders or a fellow -12 player edges him out — will be one of the defining storylines of Sunday's coverage.
