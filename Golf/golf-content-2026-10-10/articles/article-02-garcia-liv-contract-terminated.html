Sergio Garcia's LIV Contract Terminated by Bankruptcy Court — PGA Tour Return Now Possible

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | October 10, 2026

Sergio Garcia's ties to LIV Golf have been severed by a U.S. bankruptcy court, marking one of the most significant legal developments in the ongoing restructuring of the breakaway league. The court's termination of Garcia's contract, as part of insolvency proceedings surrounding LIV Golf, opens a potential pathway back to the PGA Tour for the 2017 Masters champion.

A hearing is scheduled for October 14. Players affected by the proceedings have until October 25 to respond. BC Partners is reportedly involved in a $300 million restructuring of LIV's assets, suggesting the league's future — in whatever form it takes — will look significantly different from its current structure.

What It Means for Garcia

Garcia was among the highest-profile players to leave the PGA Tour for LIV when the Saudi-backed league launched. His departure came amid controversy, and his relationship with the European Tour and Ryder Cup selection was affected by his move. The bankruptcy court's termination of his contract effectively removes one of the key legal bindings that kept him outside the Tour's ecosystem.

Whether the PGA Tour would grant Garcia reinstatement, and on what terms, has not been confirmed. The Tour has historically applied reinstatement policies to LIV players, but the circumstances of a court-ordered contract termination — rather than a voluntary departure from LIV — could influence how commissioners approach his specific situation. Garcia has not publicly commented on his intentions following the ruling.

Rahm Also in the Picture

Garcia is not alone. Reports indicate that Jon Rahm's exit from LIV Golf is also being finalized. Rahm, who joined LIV in a deal reported to exceed $500 million, became one of the league's most recognizable assets. His potential departure alongside Garcia would represent a significant shift in LIV's player composition and public profile.

The October 14 hearing and the October 25 player deadline will clarify the legal landscape for all affected players. For now, the court's ruling on Garcia is the clearest signal yet that LIV's bankruptcy proceedings will reshape professional golf's player distribution in ways that were difficult to predict when the league launched.

What's Next

October 14 is the next key date: the bankruptcy hearing will provide further clarity on the proceedings and what they mean for Garcia, Rahm, and any other affected LIV players. The PGA Tour has not issued a formal statement on reinstatement. This story is developing rapidly and will shape the off-season conversation in professional golf heading into 2027.
