Fleetwood at 13-Under: The Englishman Is Running Away from the Field at TPC Southwind

By Marcus Cole | August 14, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

Tommy Fleetwood is playing some of the best golf of his life at the worst possible time for every other player in the FedEx Cup Playoffs field. Through two rounds of the FedEx St. Jude Championship at TPC Southwind in Memphis, the Englishman is 13 under par — a 63 in Round 1 followed by a 64 in Round 2 — and the closest competitor is six shots back.

Scottie Scheffler, the No. 1 seed, carded a 4-under 66 in Round 2 to reach 7-under for the tournament. He extended his score while Fleetwood extended his lead. The gap is the same as it was at the end of Round 1, and the weekend is two rounds away.

Back-to-Back Elite Rounds

A 63 at TPC Southwind is impressive. Backing it up with a 64 under playoff conditions against the strongest field in professional golf is remarkable. Back-to-back rounds in the low-to-mid 60s require not just ball-striking, but putting, course management, and the mental composure to stay aggressive when momentum is already on your side.

Fleetwood arrived in Memphis ranked No. 9 in the FedExCup regular-season standings. That position means a top-10 seed entering the Tour Championship in Atlanta if nothing changes — but winning the St. Jude outright would give him a massive boost in the points standings and stamp this as one of the defining weeks of his career. He is currently on track for exactly that.

Scheffler's Round 2 included four bogeys, three of them on the back nine — the kind of scorecard that suggests a player not quite comfortable with all parts of his game. Fleetwood has had no such issues this week. He is striking it clean, making putts, and avoiding mistakes on a course that punishes both.

What the Gap Actually Means

Six shots is a significant deficit with two rounds remaining, but it is not insurmountable — particularly when the chaser is Scottie Scheffler, who is capable of a 62 on any given day. The question is whether Fleetwood's consistency will continue to limit those opportunities.

The FedEx St. Jude Championship carries a $20 million purse. The top 70 finishers advance to the BMW Championship, which means that while the leaderboard race at the top is between Fleetwood and whoever can run him down, there are secondary battles at the cutline — including players like Jordan Spieth (54th in FedExCup standings) and Max Homa (52nd) fighting for their playoff lives.

Adam Scott, one of the veteran presences in the field, fired a 66 in Round 1 and remains in contention. The weekend promises to sort out the top of the leaderboard quickly once Round 3 tee times get underway Saturday.

What's Next

Rounds 3 and 4 at TPC Southwind take place Saturday and Sunday. Fleetwood will play in one of the final pairings Saturday, and whoever challenges him over the next 36 holes will need to be near-flawless. The playoff stakes amplify every shot: for Fleetwood, this is a chance to win a marquee FedEx Cup Playoff event for the first time; for Scheffler, it is a chance to remind everyone why he was the dominant player of the regular season. The top 70 finishers this week advance to the BMW Championship, where the field narrows to 50 and the pressure compounds further.
