Grace Kim Fires 66 in Round 1 of the Portland Classic, One Back of the Leader at Columbia Edgewater

By Ryan Calloway | August 14, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

The LPGA Tour's 20th event of the 2026 season is off to a sharp start in Oregon. Grace Kim, the Australian professional who has established herself as one of the LPGA's rising talents, opened The Standard Portland Classic with a 6-under 66 at Columbia Edgewater Country Club — leaving her just one shot off the pace after Round 1.

The $2 million purse at Columbia Edgewater has attracted a field that includes some of the best players in women's golf, and early scores suggest the course is yielding birdies without giving them away easily. Arpichaya Yubol turned in a bogey-free round, the kind of mistake-free performance that historically wins on the LPGA Tour when it runs through four rounds.

A Crowded and Talented Field

Kim will need to fend off a field that includes Jeeno Thitikul, one of the world's best players and a consistent presence near the top of any LPGA leaderboard she enters. Lottie Woad, Haeran Ryu, and Hannah Green are also in the field at Columbia Edgewater — a group that ensures Kim's one-shot deficit on the lead is not a comfortable margin heading into Round 2.

Kim's 66 in Round 1 reflects the kind of ball-striking and putting combination that wins tournaments. Being one back of the leader is a position that has historically produced champions in LPGA events — close enough to take the lead with a single strong front nine, far enough that a rough start in Round 2 could be damaging. The next 54 holes will show whether she can sustain the level of play she demonstrated on Thursday.

The Bigger Picture for the LPGA

The Portland Classic is the 20th event of the 2026 LPGA Tour season, which means the schedule is entering its final stretch before the playoffs. Standings, rankings, and tournament exemptions are all heading toward their end-of-season settlements, which means the results in Portland carry weight beyond the $2 million purse — they shape the competitive landscape entering the fall.

After Portland, the tour heads north to Edmonton for the CPKC Women's Open, scheduled for August 20-23 at Royal Mayfair Golf Club in Alberta, Canada. That event carries a $2.75 million purse — one of the richest on the calendar — and represents a major showcase opportunity for whoever emerges from Portland with momentum. For Grace Kim, a strong finish this week in Oregon could set up a consequential stretch run.

What's Next

Rounds 2 through 4 at Columbia Edgewater Country Club complete the Portland Classic through Sunday, August 16. Golf Channel carries Thursday and Friday coverage from 6-9 PM ET, with CNBC airing Saturday (3-6 PM ET) and Sunday (2-5 PM ET) rounds. The CPKC Women's Open in Edmonton begins August 20, giving the field barely four days between events. Players finishing near the top of the leaderboard in Portland will arrive in Edmonton riding momentum; players who slip over the weekend will have little time to reset before one of the season's most prestigious events begins.
