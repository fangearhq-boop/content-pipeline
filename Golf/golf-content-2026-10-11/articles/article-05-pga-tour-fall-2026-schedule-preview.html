PGA Tour Fall 2026: A Complete Guide to the Five Remaining Events

By Marcus Cole | October 11, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

The 2026 Baycurrent Classic is done. Michael Brennan has his trophy, the FedExCup Fall standings have shifted, and the PGA Tour is ready to move on to the final five events of the 2026 competitive golf season.

From the beaches of Bermuda to the fairways of coastal Georgia, the next six weeks offer a varied and genuinely compelling finish to the golf year. Here is everything you need to know about what comes next.

Butterfield Bermuda Championship — October 22-25

Port Royal Golf Course in Southampton, Bermuda, hosts the first stop after Japan. Port Royal is one of the most visually striking venues on the PGA Tour calendar — a public course carved through limestone cliffs with ocean views on multiple holes. The event has historically provided field opportunities for players looking to move their standings significantly, and the relaxed island setting belies the genuine competitive pressure at the top of the leaderboard.

VidantaWorld Mexico Open — October 29-November 1

The Mexico Open moved from the spring calendar to the fall for 2026, landing in Vallarta with an enthusiastic local crowd and a course that rewards longer hitters. The event in Mexico has a history of producing compelling final-round drama and has grown its profile significantly over recent seasons. Expect a strong field drawn by the fall points opportunity and the appeal of the venue.

World Wide Technology Championship — November 5-8

El Cardonal at Diamante in Los Cabos, Mexico, hosts the second consecutive week in Mexico. The course sits along the Pacific coast and features ocean breezes that add an unpredictable element to scoring. Back-to-back weeks in Mexico gives the fall schedule a distinct international feel — and gives players who perform well in Vallarta a chance to build momentum immediately into another event in a similar climate.

Good Good Championship (Inaugural) — November 12-15

The Good Good Championship arrives as one of the most anticipated new events added to the PGA Tour calendar in 2026. Played at Omni Barton Creek Resort and Spa in Austin, Texas, the event is backed by the Good Good media brand — one of golf's most popular digital content creators — and is expected to attract significant fan interest alongside its competitive field.

The inaugural edition brings genuine curiosity: how will a content-brand-backed tournament translate to the on-course product? The venue is regarded as one of the premium resort golf experiences in Texas, and Austin's growing identity as a sports and entertainment city provides an ideal backdrop for a new event trying to establish itself quickly.

RSM Classic — November 19-22

Sea Island Golf Club in St. Simons Island, Georgia, closes the 2026 FedExCup Fall season. The RSM Classic is the traditional season-ender and has a history of producing dramatic final-round finishes as players arrive knowing precisely where they stand in the standings and exactly what they need. The Sea Island resort courses are beloved on Tour, and the setting on the Georgia coast provides one of the most pleasant final-round atmospheres in the sport.

After the RSM Classic, the FedExCup Fall standings are final. The top 60 earn their Signature Event exemptions for 2027. The season is done. And the golf world begins its brief offseason before the 2027 cycle kicks off in the new year.

What's Next

Five events. Six weeks. Three countries. One new tournament. And a FedExCup Fall standings race that will tighten with every result.

The stretch run of the 2026 PGA Tour season begins October 22 in Bermuda. Between now and November 22 at Sea Island, a lot of professional golf will be played and a lot of careers will be shaped by what happens in these final events. That is the nature of the fall — and what makes it one of the most compelling parts of the golf calendar to follow closely.
