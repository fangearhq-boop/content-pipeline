FedExCup Fall Bubble Watch: Five Events Remain, The Race for the Top 60 Is On

By Marcus Cole | October 11, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

With the Baycurrent Classic complete, the 2026 FedExCup Fall season enters its final stretch. Five events remain. The standings will shift at every stop. And for the players hovering around positions 51 through 60, every round of golf between now and November will feel like it matters — because it does.

The top 60 players in the FedExCup Fall standings earn exemptions into the first two Signature Events of the 2027 PGA Tour season. Those spots represent guaranteed tee times in elevated-field events with premium prize purses. For a player outside the top 40 or 50 in world ranking, securing a Signature Event exemption changes the trajectory of an entire season before it even begins.

The Bubble Picture After Baycurrent

Going into the Baycurrent Classic, Jordan Spieth sat around No. 55 in the fall standings — inside the Top 60, but with little margin. Christiaan Bezuidenhout and Max Greyserman were in more precarious territory near the cutline. The Yokohama results will have shifted those positions, with some players rising, others falling, and several likely swapping places near that critical 60th spot.

The winner of any fall event earns a substantial points haul that can move a player from the bubble to the middle of the pack in a single week. But for players hoping to secure their spots through consistent finishes rather than a headline win, the math requires top-15 or top-10 results across multiple remaining events.

The Five Remaining Events

The fall schedule offers five more opportunities after Yokohama:

The Butterfield Bermuda Championship runs October 22-25 at Port Royal Golf Course in Southampton, Bermuda — one of the most scenic venues on the tour calendar, and a place where lower-ranked players have historically broken through with signature performances.

The VidantaWorld Mexico Open in Vallarta follows October 29 through November 1. The Mexico Open moved from the spring calendar to fall in 2026, bringing a passionate host country crowd and a venue that rewards ball-striking.

The World Wide Technology Championship at El Cardonal at Diamante in Los Cabos, Mexico, runs November 5-8, making two consecutive weeks in Mexico that demand players bring their best form to back-to-back events.

November 12-15 brings the inaugural Good Good Championship in Austin, Texas, at Omni Barton Creek Resort and Spa. The event, backed by the Good Good media brand, is one of the most anticipated new additions to the fall calendar and is expected to attract significant attention.

The RSM Classic at Sea Island Golf Club in St. Simons Island, Georgia, closes the fall season November 19-22. As the traditional season-ender, the RSM Classic has a history of dramatic finishes in the standings, with players often arriving knowing exactly what they need from the final event.

What's Next

The FedExCup Fall standings will be updated following each event and published on the PGA Tour's official website. For fans following the bubble closely, the weeks ahead offer compelling storylines: veterans trying to secure status, young players trying to break through, and contenders managing their position over five events played across three countries.

The fall race ends November 22 at Sea Island. Between now and then, a lot of golf remains to be played.
