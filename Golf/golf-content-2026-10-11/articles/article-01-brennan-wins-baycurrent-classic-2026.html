Michael Brennan Wins 2026 Baycurrent Classic: Third PGA Tour Title in 33 Starts

By Ryan Calloway | October 11, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

Michael Brennan made it three.

The 24-year-old from Leesburg, Virginia, closed with a final-round 68 at Yokohama Country Club in Japan on Sunday to win the 2026 Baycurrent Classic at 16-under par (268), finishing one stroke ahead of Jacob Bridgeman and Keith Mitchell, who tied for second at 15-under. It is Brennan's third PGA Tour title and his second win of 2026, following the Wyndham Championship in August.

Brian Harman, Xander Schauffele, and Stephan Jaeger tied for fourth at 13-under. Adam Scott, who had co-led the tournament entering the final round, could not sustain his challenge as Brennan pulled clear.

How the Final Round Unfolded

Brennan's 68 on Sunday included four birdies and was measured and efficient throughout the final round. He held off a field that included multiple established PGA Tour winners and a defending champion in Schauffele, who could not repeat his 2025 Yokohama performance.

Jacob Bridgeman and Keith Mitchell made their own charges, both finishing at 15-under, but could not catch Brennan, who played the closing holes with composure unusual for a player still in his first few years on Tour.

The winner's cheque was $1.44 million from the event's $8 million total purse.

A Win Rate That Stands Alone

To understand what Brennan has done, consider the context. He turned professional in 2024 and won his first PGA Tour event at the Bank of Utah Championship on a sponsor's exemption. He then won the Wyndham Championship in August 2026, and now the Baycurrent Classic.

Three wins in approximately 33 career starts is an efficiency rate that places Brennan in the company of the most productive tournament winners in modern PGA Tour history. The vast majority of professional golfers never win a single tour event. To win three before the age of 25 requires a combination of talent, competitive temperament, and a willingness to perform when the result matters most.

Brennan has shown all three qualities consistently.

What's Next

The PGA Tour's FedExCup Fall season continues with five remaining events: the Butterfield Bermuda Championship (October 22-25), the VidantaWorld Mexico Open (October 29-November 1), the World Wide Technology Championship in Los Cabos (November 5-8), the inaugural Good Good Championship in Austin (November 12-15), and the RSM Classic at Sea Island (November 19-22).

For Brennan, the Baycurrent Classic result is another significant step in a career that is already attracting attention from across the golf world. Whatever comes next in his still-young professional journey, the foundation being laid in 2026 is remarkable.
