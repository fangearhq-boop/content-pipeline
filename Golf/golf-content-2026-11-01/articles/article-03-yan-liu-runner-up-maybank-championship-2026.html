Yan Liu Falls Two Short at Maybank — Runner-Up in a Career-Defining Performance

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | November 1, 2026

Yan Liu came within two shots of making LPGA Tour history. Instead, she finishes the 2026 Maybank Championship with a runner-up and a performance that marks her as one of the most dangerous players in women's professional golf heading into the season's final stretch.

Liu closed at 20-under par (268), two strokes behind champion Miyu Yamashita. Her week peaked in round three, when she fired a stunning 9-under 63 to surge from well back into the championship conversation. That 63 gave the tournament genuine drama and brought Sunday's final round to life with a real contest between two elite players.

The Saturday Surge

Liu's third-round 63 was the round of the tournament from a purist standpoint — a sustained assault on Vidanta's scoring opportunities that cut through a large chunk of Yamashita's lead and turned a comfortable defense into a genuine two-horse final-round race. She made nine birdies, limited her bogeys, and played with the aggressive rhythm of a player who had nothing to lose and everything to gain.

Arriving at Sunday within two strokes, Liu had every reason to believe she could close the gap. Yamashita is an experienced champion, but even experienced champions can be affected by pressure, and two shots entering the final round is genuinely catchable.

It was not to be. Yamashita played steadily and held her advantage, and Liu's best effort on Sunday still came up two short. That is not a failure — that is a performance against the best player in the field, on their home championship turf, and losing respectably.

A Milestone for Chinese Women's Golf

Liu's runner-up finish at a prestigious LPGA Tour event in Asia carries significance beyond the scoreboard. Chinese women's golf has been growing in depth and quality, and a near-miss at a major LPGA title by a Chinese player signals that the talent pipeline is producing players capable of competing at the highest levels. Liu will return to future tournaments with the experience of a Sunday championship battle under her belt — experience that tends to matter enormously the next time around.

What's Next

Yan Liu finishes the 2026 LPGA season with her best result to date and a clear sense of what it takes to contend at the top of the women's game. The closing stretch of the tour schedule gives her the opportunity to turn this near-miss into momentum. As for Yamashita, whose historic back-to-back victory is the story of the day — Liu gave her a genuine challenge, and Yamashita was simply the better player across four rounds. That is the most honest summary of the 2026 Maybank Championship.
