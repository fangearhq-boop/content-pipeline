### STORY 1: Sam Burns Wins FedEx St. Jude — Scheffler Falters with Fill-in Caddie
- **Tier:** 1
- **Category:** PGA Tour / FedEx Cup Playoffs / Sam Burns / Scottie Scheffler
- **Fresh angle:** Scheffler confirmed T-3 (NBC Sports) despite entering R4 with a 2-shot lead. Sam Burns (Shreveport, Louisiana; T-2 entering R4 after a 62 in R3) wins the $20M tournament. The heartbreak story: Scheffler's regular caddie Ted Scott left for a family emergency Saturday night; PGA Tour chaplain Brad Payne (who flew in around 2am) served as fill-in caddie for the final round. Scheffler: "just ball wasn't going in." JJ Spaun also charged, fell short.
- **Sources:** NBC Sports (Scheffler T-3 confirmed), Yahoo News ($40M Shreveport headline), CBS Sports caddie report, Yardbarker
