Three Major Golf Events Next Week: BMW Championship, LIV Indianapolis, and the CPKC Women's Open

By Ryan Calloway | August 16, 2026

The week of August 20–23 features the second FedEx Cup Playoff event at a revived Bellerive, LIV Golf's Indiana stop, and the LPGA's flagship Canadian event with a $2.75 million purse. Something for every golf fan.

Next week delivers three major professional golf events running simultaneously across three continents of the sport. The BMW Championship headlines the week on the PGA Tour, but LIV Golf's Indianapolis event and the CPKC Women's Open in Edmonton mean that golf fans have options regardless of which circuit they follow. Here is what to know about each.

BMW Championship — Bellerive Country Club, St. Louis

The BMW Championship is the second FedEx Cup Playoff event, and it carries higher stakes than the St. Jude that preceded it. The top 50 players from this week's standings at the St. Jude advance to BMW; the top 30 from BMW advance to the Tour Championship at East Lake. Every stroke counts.

Bellerive Country Club in St. Louis is making a long-anticipated return to the PGA Tour schedule. The club last hosted a major professional event in 2018, when Brooks Koepka won the PGA Championship there. The course is long, firm, and demands accurate driving — fairways bordered by deep rough, greens that reward precision approach play, and a layout that has historically separated the field between ball-strikers and those who can mask deficiencies with short-game wizardry.

The field will include Sam Burns riding momentum from his St. Jude victory, Scottie Scheffler looking to reset from his Sunday collapse, and Rory McIlroy searching for the form that went missing in Memphis. Jordan Spieth will be there too, needing a strong week to extend his season beyond Bellerive.

Dates: August 20–23. Location: Bellerive Country Club, St. Louis, Missouri.

LIV Golf Indianapolis — Chatham Hills Golf Club

LIV Golf continues its 2026 schedule with its Indiana stop at Chatham Hills Golf Club in Westfield, Indiana, just north of Indianapolis. The LIV format — individual and team competition, shotgun starts, no cut — runs August 20–23.

Chatham Hills is a private course that has hosted the LIV event in prior seasons. The course layout and competition format are well established for LIV's regular field. With several big names competing in the PGA Tour's Playoff events simultaneously, the LIV Indianapolis field will be anchored by its core roster of contracted players.

Dates: August 20–23. Location: Chatham Hills Golf Club, Westfield, Indiana.

CPKC Women's Open — Edmonton, Alberta

The CPKC Women's Open is the LPGA Tour's flagship event in Canada and one of the most well-attended stops on the schedule. The 2026 edition is played in Edmonton, Alberta, and carries a $2.75 million purse — one of the larger prize pools on the LPGA calendar.

The event follows directly from this week's Portland Classic, meaning the LPGA field heads north without a break. Akie Iwai will arrive in Edmonton as the Portland Classic champion, fresh off a 4-shot win at -24. Her twin sister Chisato, who finished T-3 in Portland, is expected to be in the field as well. The storyline writes itself: can one of the Iwai twins win back-to-back events in consecutive weeks on different continents of the schedule?

Edmonton's climate in late August — cool, variable winds, firm turf conditions — tends to reward players who can manage their ball in the wind and make birdies on the par-5s. The Canadian crowd will be loud and enthusiastic regardless of the leaderboard.

Dates: August 20–23. Location: Edmonton, Alberta, Canada.
