### STORY 1: Sam Burns Wins FedEx St. Jude Championship

#### Long-Form Post — 8:00 AM ET

Sam Burns wins the FedEx St. Jude Championship.

The headline is Burns — a 29-year-old from Shreveport, Louisiana, winning the first event of the FedEx Cup Playoffs in a $20 million tournament. He entered Sunday's final round at T-2 after shooting a stunning 62 in Round 3 (15 of 18 greens hit). He played the kind of Sunday that major champions play.

But the story that will be told for years is what happened Saturday night.

Scottie Scheffler entered the final round with a 2-shot lead. He was the favorite. He was the No. 1 player in the world. And then his caddie, Ted Scott — his longtime partner, his trusted read on every green — had to leave for a family emergency on Saturday night.

In his place: Brad Payne, the PGA Tour's chaplain. Payne flew in at approximately 2am Sunday morning and was on the bag when Scheffler teed it up in the final round of the most important FedEx event of the year. Payne is a man of deep integrity and sincere care for the players on Tour — but he is not a professional caddie, and he had never looped for Scheffler in competition.

Scheffler finished T-3. His own explanation afterward was quiet: "just ball wasn't going in."

That's golf. Sometimes everything breaks your way, and sometimes the universe conspires against you in the most human way possible — and you still have to hit the shot.

Burns capitalized. He played like a champion. He won his biggest title, carried a $3.6 million winner's check out of Memphis, and will be seeded comfortably for the Tour Championship push.

Congratulations to Sam Burns. And sincere thoughts to Ted Scott and his family.

### STORY 2: Akie Iwai Wins LPGA Portland Classic

The number that makes this win remarkable: both Akie and her twin sister Chisato Iwai have now won on the LPGA Tour in their rookie season.

Chisato won at Mayakoba in May. Today, it was Akie's turn. And when Akie holed her final putt on the 18th green, her twin sister — who had just finished T-3 at the same tournament — was right there to celebrate.

Chisato sprayed her with champagne on the 18th green.

That image is the story. Two 23-year-old Japanese rookies, competing at the same event, celebrating together in the most joyful way possible. They arrived in the United States as unknowns to most American golf fans. They are not unknowns anymore.

Akie birdied her final two holes to finish at 67-67-64-66. Gurleen Kaur finished second at -20. Chisato and Linn Grant shared T-3 at -19. Grace Kim was fifth at -18.

The leaderboard was packed. It was Akie who closed cleanest.

Congratulations to Akie Iwai, and what a moment for both sisters and their family. This is the story of the LPGA season.
