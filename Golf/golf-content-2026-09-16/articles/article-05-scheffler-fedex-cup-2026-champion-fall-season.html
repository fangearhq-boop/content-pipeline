Scheffler Wins Second FedEx Cup — Now the Fall Season Begins and the History Conversation Does Too

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | September 16, 2026

Scottie Scheffler came from behind at East Lake Golf Club to win the 2026 Tour Championship by three strokes over Viktor Hovland, finishing at 16-under par and taking his second FedEx Cup title in three years. The victory puts him in extraordinary company: he joins Tiger Woods (2007, 2009) and Rory McIlroy (2016, 2019, 2022) as the only players in history to win the FedExCup more than once.

The win also moved Scheffler past Tiger Woods on the PGA Tour's all-time career earnings list. His $10 million Tour Championship prize brought his career earnings over $130 million, a number that continues to climb with every tournament result.

The Season That Built To This

Scheffler's 2026 was not his typical dominant campaign — he won three times compared to nine in 2024 and six in 2025 — but the quality of those wins and the consistency surrounding them remained elite. The American Express, the FedEx St. Jude Championship, and the Tour Championship. Only two finishes outside the top 15 in 20 starts. No major title this season, which is the one area where his 2026 résumé falls short of his two previous years.

Still, the Player of the Year argument remains his. Consistency at that level over a full season, culminating in East Lake, is what the award recognizes.

The Fall Season Opens Thursday

The FedExCup Fall kicks off with the Biltmore Championship in Asheville, North Carolina on September 17-20. Scheffler isn't expected to be in the Biltmore field — the Presidents Cup at Medinah follows the next weekend, and the defending FedEx Cup champion will focus on team golf ahead of the fall individual swing. But the fall season sets the structure for 2027, and the players who perform across fall events will arrive at next year's major season with important ranking and exemption advantages.

The conversation about Scheffler's place among the game's all-time best will continue to build. Two FedEx Cups in three years. Multiple majors. Career earnings history. The fall season is underway, and it starts with someone else's name on the trophy — but the standard Scheffler set will be what everyone is measured against.

What's Next

The Biltmore Championship tees off Thursday in Asheville, followed by the Presidents Cup at Medinah (September 24-27) and the remainder of the FedExCup Fall. Watch for how Scheffler approaches his Presidents Cup captaincy role under Brandt Snedeker — as the world's No. 1 player, his performance in team golf carries its own narrative weight.
