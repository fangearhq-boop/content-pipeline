Eight Days to Medinah — Full Presidents Cup Rosters Set as Jackson Koivun Eyes History

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | September 16, 2026

The 2026 Presidents Cup at Medinah Country Club is eight days away, and both rosters are locked. U.S. Captain Brandt Snedeker and International Captain Geoff Ogilvy made their final captain's picks on September 1, completing two 12-player teams that will compete September 24-27 in Medinah, Illinois.

The storyline drawing the most attention on the American side is Jackson Koivun, a 21-year-old who won his first PGA Tour event this July — just weeks after shedding his amateur status — and was selected by Snedeker as one of his six captain's picks. Koivun is the second-youngest American ever to play in the Presidents Cup, behind only Jordan Spieth, who appeared in 2013 at 20 years old.

The Full U.S. Roster

Automatic qualifiers: Scottie Scheffler, Cameron Young, Wyndham Clark, Sam Burns, Russell Henley, Collin Morikawa. Captain's picks: Chris Gotterup, Xander Schauffele, Jackson Koivun, Justin Thomas, Patrick Cantlay, Jacob Bridgeman.

The team combines the FedEx Cup champion Scheffler — the world's No. 1 player — with proven Presidents Cup veterans like Thomas and Cantlay alongside newer faces in Koivun and Bridgeman. Snedeker is drawing from a deep pool of American talent at a moment when U.S. golf's depth is arguably stronger than at any point in the modern era.

The International Team

Automatic qualifiers: Si Woo Kim, Hideki Matsuyama, Ryan Fox, Tom Kim, Min Woo Lee, Adam Scott. Captain's picks: Corey Conners, Nicolas Echavarria, Nick Taylor, Sungjae Im, Ryo Hisatsune, Christiaan Bezuidenhout.

Ogilvy's squad is built around Asia-Pacific depth, with Matsuyama, the two-time major champion, as the marquee name. Adam Scott returns as a veteran presence, and the Canadians Conners and Taylor add North American flavor to what is otherwise a roster heavy with talent from South Korea, Australia, and the European tour.

What's Next

Several players from both teams are teeing up at the Biltmore Championship in Asheville this week as a final warm-up. The Presidents Cup opening round is September 24 with five four-ball matches. Match play format means anything can happen — the pairings strategy from both captains will be one of the most closely analyzed elements of the week. Watch for how Snedeker deploys Koivun in his first major team event.
