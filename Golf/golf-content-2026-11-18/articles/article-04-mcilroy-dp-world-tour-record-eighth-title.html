Rory McIlroy Ties All-Time Record With Eighth DP World Tour Race to Dubai Title

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | November 18, 2026

Rory McIlroy has won the DP World Tour's Race to Dubai season-long title for a record-tying eighth time, claiming a milestone that speaks to a level of dominance that European golf has rarely seen across any era.

Tyrrell Hatton won the final event on the DP World Tour calendar — the Dubai tournament that closes out the Race to Dubai — but McIlroy had accumulated the points necessary to secure the season crown regardless of that result. His eighth title ties the all-time record for most Race to Dubai victories in the competition's history.

A Record Built Across a Decade

McIlroy's eight Race to Dubai titles span more than a decade of elite European Tour golf. His first came early in his career as he was establishing himself as one of the world's best players. The subsequent titles — scattered across years when he also racked up four major championships and numerous PGA Tour victories — paint the picture of a player who never neglected his European roots even as global competition called.

What makes the record remarkable is the consistency it requires. The Race to Dubai is a points-based season-long competition where every event matters. Winning it once demands sustained excellence across a full season. Winning it eight times means delivering that sustained excellence in eight different years — across changing equipment, different peers, shifting form, and all of golf's inherent unpredictability.

What Eight Means for His Legacy

McIlroy enters conversations about the greatest European Tour players in history with the strongest statistical claim. His combination of major championships, PGA Tour wins, and now a record-tying eight Race to Dubai titles creates a résumé that few can match.

At 37, McIlroy also does not appear finished. He continues to compete at the sport's highest level on both sides of the Atlantic. The question now is whether he can go outright No. 1 in Race to Dubai history with a ninth title — a feat that would belong entirely to him.

For now, eight stands. And eight is extraordinary.

What's Next

McIlroy's 2027 schedule will begin with the PGA Tour's Hawaii swing in January, with early-season events setting the tone for a campaign that could include another major championship charge. On the DP World Tour, he will inevitably be a contender for a ninth Race to Dubai title whenever the season begins.

The RSM Classic at Sea Island — the PGA Tour's final event of 2026 — provides an optional closing note to the year. Whether McIlroy competes this week or steps away for the offseason, he heads into the break as one of golf's undeniable figures. Eight Race to Dubai crowns says everything.
