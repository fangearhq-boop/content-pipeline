RSM Classic 2026: Contenders, Storylines and What to Watch at Sea Island

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | November 18, 2026

The RSM Classic tees off Thursday at Sea Island Golf Club in Georgia, and without Scottie Scheffler in the field, the PGA Tour's traditional fall closing event is as wide open as it's been in years. The $7.4 million purse is on the table. So is a career-defining moment for whoever steps up over four days on the Georgia coast.

Sea Island is not a course that forgives complacency. The Seaside Course — the primary venue — plays along the Atlantic coast with wind that makes every shot more complicated than it looks on the scorecard. Players who handle its quirks best tend to be those with patience, precision off the tee, and the kind of short game that holds up when the breeze picks up in the afternoon.

The Scheffler Void

Scottie Scheffler's absence changes the narrative entirely. The world's No. 1 player — who has dominated 2026 with historic scoring averages and a FedEx Cup title — is not in the field. That means every other contender arrives knowing the best player in the world isn't between them and the trophy.

These are the moments that elevate careers. Wins at events where the field is competitive but Scheffler-free carry extra weight because they can't be explained away. No "Scheffler wasn't there." Just a champion emerging from a quality field.

The RSM Classic has a history of producing memorable champions. Players who've broken through here have often gone on to multi-win careers. The conditions reward grinding, and the result typically belongs to whoever commits most fully to the week.

What to Watch

Sea Island's coastal setup means Thursday and Friday tee times matter. Early morning starters often get calmer conditions; afternoon wave players battle the breeze. The splits on Day 1 can create a gap before the weekend field is even set.

Watch for players with strong links-style games — those comfortable controlling trajectory and working the ball low in the wind. Wedge play into elevated greens is punished when the breeze is sideways; bump-and-run approaches from outside are rewarded.

Also watch for momentum from the fall swing. Players coming off recent top-10 finishes at events like the Austin Championship and the Bermuda Championship often carry form into Sea Island. The fall schedule rewards consistency, and Sea Island is where that consistency gets rewarded with hardware.

What's Next

The RSM Classic runs November 19–22. It is the final full-field PGA Tour event of the 2026 season. After Sunday's final round, the Tour enters its off-season break before returning in January 2027 with the Hawaii swing.

For whoever wins at Sea Island, the offseason arrives with a title, a paycheck from a $7.4 million purse, and a résumé that just got stronger. The RSM Classic may not be Augusta, but its champions get to spend the winter knowing they've won on Tour — and that's everything.
