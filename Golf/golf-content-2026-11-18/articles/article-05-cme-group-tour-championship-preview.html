CME Group Tour Championship 2026: The LPGA's $11M Season Finale Begins Thursday

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | November 18, 2026

The LPGA Tour's season comes down to four days at Tiburón Golf Club in Naples, Florida, where the CME Group Tour Championship begins Thursday with an $11 million purse — the richest prize pool in women's golf history.

Atthaya Thitikul arrives as the defending champion, having claimed last year's title at an astonishing -26 to par. Nelly Korda, the world's No. 1 ranked player, leads a field assembled to decide the season's ultimate winner. For anyone in the field, this week represents the LPGA Tour at its most dramatic.

The Stakes

The CME Group Tour Championship carries weight that extends beyond the trophy. The winner's share of an $11 million purse is historic. The visibility of a season finale — with the full attention of golf's global media focused on Naples — makes every birdie feel amplified.

For Thitikul, defending at a course where she was nearly flawless last year brings both expectation and opportunity. She won at -26, a number that reflects a player who was simply not missing. Repeating that kind of performance is rare, but Thitikul has shown throughout her career that sustained excellence is her standard.

Korda's presence adds the season-long narrative arc. As the world's No. 1, her performance at the season finale will put a final stamp on her year. A win here would be her defining moment of 2026.

Tiburón Golf Club

The Tiburón Gold Course in Naples has hosted the CME Group Tour Championship for years, and its South Florida setting provides the warm, calm conditions that often produce low scoring. When the wind is mild, players can attack pins and go low fast. When afternoon breezes kick up off the Gulf, the second-hole par three and the closing stretch demand precision over aggression.

The greens at Tiburón are slick and fast — tournament conditions amplify a surface that already penalizes tentative putting. Players who can lag putt effectively and convert from short range will stay in contention deep into Sunday. Missed short putts at this level, on these greens, in this field, are unrecoverable.

What's Next

The CME Group Tour Championship runs November 19–22. It is the final event of the 2026 LPGA Tour season. After Sunday's final round, the winner takes home a historic payday and the title of LPGA's last champion of the year.

Coverage begins Thursday morning, with featured groups and leaderboard updates throughout all four rounds. The LPGA season finale is not just the end of a year — it is the sport's final statement before the calendar turns. And with Thitikul defending and Korda hunting, that statement figures to be memorable.
