### STORY 3: LIV Player Futures

**Long-Form** | Window: 6:00 PM ET
The meeting happened. The answer didn't change.

The PGA Tour board convened Monday, November 16 to discuss what happens to former LIV Golf players now that the Saudi-backed league is winding down in bankruptcy. Commissioner Brian Rolapp emerged with a clear message: there are no current plans for a reinstatement pathway.

That means Jon Rahm, Bryson DeChambeau, and Dustin Johnson — among others — remain in professional limbo. Their LIV contracts are expiring, their PGA Tour status is gone, and the DP World Tour offers only limited alternatives for a roster of players used to guaranteed paydays.

Brooks Koepka is the exception, the rare LIV defector who negotiated a return: a $5 million charitable contribution and a five-year ban on equity stakes in competing ventures. That's a steep price — and the Tour has apparently shown no appetite for making similar arrangements at scale.

LIV Golf disrupted the sport, spent an estimated $5 billion, and now the players who bet their careers on it face a complicated future. #PGATour #LIVGolf #GolfFanrecap
