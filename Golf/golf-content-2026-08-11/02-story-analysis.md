### Story 2 — Scheffler POTY

- **Key tension:** Scheffler needs 2 more wins in the playoffs to be a credible POTY candidate
- **Data story:** 1 win, 5 runner-ups, 9 top-5s, 0 majors, 0 missed cuts (except Scottish Open)

### Story 3 — McIlroy FedExCup

- **Key facts:** Back-to-back Masters, 6 career majors, 3 FedExCup titles; record 4th would be historic
- **Narrative:** He skipped regular season but he's here for the prize

### Story 4 — Rahm LIV Three-Peat

- **Key stat:** 8 top-10s in first 9 starts; 218-point final margin over DeChambeau
- **Angle:** What this means for LIV's legacy as it transitions to BC Partners deal

### Story 5 — LPGA Kuwaki + Portland
