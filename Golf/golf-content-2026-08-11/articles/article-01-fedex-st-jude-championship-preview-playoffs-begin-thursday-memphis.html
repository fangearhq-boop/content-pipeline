FedEx St. Jude Championship Preview: The Playoffs Begin Thursday in Memphis

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | August 11, 2026

Two days from now, the PGA Tour's regular season gives way to the event that matters most. On Thursday, August 13, the FedEx Cup Playoffs begin at TPC Southwind in Memphis, Tennessee, and the stakes have never been clearer: 70 players will tee it up, and only 50 will survive to play another week.

The FedEx St. Jude Championship is the first of three playoff events. Survive here and you advance to the BMW Championship at Bellerive Country Club in St. Louis (August 20-23). Make it through that field cut to the top 30 and you earn a spot at the Tour Championship at East Lake Golf Club in Atlanta (August 27-30), where the FedExCup champion is crowned and a $25 million bonus awaits.

The Favorites

Scottie Scheffler enters as the No. 1 seed and the heavy favorite at +500. He has led the FedExCup standings for most of 2026 and arrives in Memphis as the most consistent player in the world, even if his win total — just one, at The American Express in January — doesn't fully capture his dominance. He has nine top-5 finishes and five runner-up results this season. The playoffs are where he needs to close.

Rory McIlroy (+1,300) is the headliner returning from a selective summer schedule. The back-to-back Masters champion skipped several regular-season events but has confirmed his Memphis start. A three-time FedExCup champion (2016, 2019, 2022), McIlroy has been the defining force of this postseason format in the modern era. He'll be dangerous from the jump.

Xander Schauffele (+1,800) is also in contention. Collin Morikawa has been the tour's best ball-striker by strokes gained on approach and ranks third in adjusted scoring average — the kind of statistical profile that plays well at TPC Southwind.

The Course

TPC Southwind is a par 70 measuring 7,233 yards, and it demands precision above all else. Narrow fairways punish drives that miss their targets. Greens are small and firm, requiring exact approach play. This is not a course that rewards power alone — Morikawa's approach game advantage is real here.

Conditions this week add another layer. Highs near 93°F are expected, along with winds of 10 to 20 mph throughout the tournament. Players managing energy, hydration, and decision-making under heat stress will have an edge in the closing rounds.

Sam Burns has two historical top-5 finishes at this venue and enters with three top-5s in his last five starts overall. He is a name to track.

How the Field Got Here

The 70-player field was finalized Sunday when Michael Brennan won the Wyndham Championship at Sedgefield Country Club in Greensboro, jumping from No. 105 in the FedExCup standings all the way to No. 47. He was the only player to enter the top 70 at Wyndham. Jackson Koivun held on to claim the No. 70 spot and his playoff berth.

Beau Hossler finished runner-up at Wyndham but narrowly missed making the field. It was a brutal way to end a season.

What's Next

The FedEx St. Jude Championship runs August 13-16 at TPC Southwind in Memphis. Golf Channel and NBC will carry the broadcast. The top 50 finishers advance to the BMW Championship (August 20-23, Bellerive CC, St. Louis), with the top 30 from that event moving on to the Tour Championship (August 27-30, East Lake, Atlanta). The FedExCup champion takes home a $25M bonus.
