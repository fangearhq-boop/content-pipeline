#### Long-Form Post — 12:00 PM ET

McIlroy has been selective this year, sitting out several regular-season events to prioritize family and the majors. He skipped the RBC Heritage, the Cadillac Championship, and the Travelers Championship. He finished 32nd at the US Open and 40th at The Open Championship. He'll need to find his game quickly. Scheffler is the +500 favorite and the form horse. But McIlroy (+1,300) has history on his side, and a player with six majors doesn't need warm-up time.

### STORY 5: LPGA — Shiho Kuwaki Wins AIG Women's Open

#### Long-Form Post — 5:00 PM ET

When the 2026 AIG Women's Open ended at Royal Lytham & St Annes on August 2, it ended with a moment that will stay with Shiho Kuwaki for the rest of her career.

The 50th edition of the championship came down to a sudden-death playoff between Kuwaki of Japan and Esther Henseleit of Germany — both finishing regulation at -5 after Henseleit holed a stunning 40-foot birdie on the 72nd hole to tie. On the second playoff hole, Henseleit's tee shot struck a marshal and finished in a bush, forcing a bogey. Kuwaki two-putted for par and won her first LPGA major title in just her 7th major championship start.

The heartbreak story belonged to Yealimi Noh. The American entered Sunday's final round with a 3-shot lead and the momentum of a player who had waited years for this moment. She shot 74. She finished T3 at -4, one shot behind the playoff pair.

The final leaderboard: Kuwaki -5 (winner), Henseleit -5, Noh -4, Korda/Thitikul T4 at -2.

The tournament was the final women's major of 2026. Kuwaki collected $1.5M from a record $10M purse and became the 2nd consecutive Japanese winner of the AIG Women's Open.

The LPGA resumes this Thursday with The Standard Portland Classic at Columbia Edgewater Country Club in Portland, Oregon — $2M purse, the first event of the post-major stretch.
