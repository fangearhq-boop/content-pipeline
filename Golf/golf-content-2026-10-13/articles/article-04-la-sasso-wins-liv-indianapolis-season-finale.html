La Sasso Steals the LIV Golf Season Finale — 22-Year-Old Edges Rahm at Indianapolis

By Ryan Calloway | October 13, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

Jon Rahm shot a final-round 63 at the LIV Golf Indianapolis season finale and still lost.

The man who beat him: Michael La Sasso, 22 years old, finishing at 18-under par to take the championship by one shot. It is the youngest winning performance of LIV Golf's 2026 season and, given the quality of the player he edged at the end, one of the more memorable individual results the league has produced.

How It Happened

La Sasso built his lead over the course of the event and held enough of a cushion heading into the final round that Rahm's late charge — seven under through his final 18 holes — came one stroke short. La Sasso closed cleanly at 18-under and waited to see whether it would hold. It did.

Rahm, whose individual season title was already secured before Indianapolis began, was not playing for the standings. He was playing to win. A 63 on a Sunday in any professional golf event represents elite-level performance. That La Sasso matched it, and then one better, is the measure of what the 22-year-old produced.

Who Is Michael La Sasso

La Sasso joined the LIV Golf roster as part of the league's commitment to developing younger talent alongside its established stars. At 22, he is part of a generation of players who grew up watching LIV Golf operate alongside the traditional tours — his frame of reference for professional golf includes the team format, the guaranteed money, and the event structure the league introduced. Winning on that stage, on the final weekend of the season, against a field that included the three-time defending individual champion, is the kind of result that defines a young career.

LIV's format — compressed 54-hole events over three days — rewards players who can sustain elite play over shorter intervals. La Sasso's ability to close at Indianapolis against Rahm's pressure suggests a player ready for extended competition at the top level, whatever that competition looks like in 2027.

What's Next

LIV Golf's 2026 season is now complete. The future of the league itself remains uncertain following Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund's announced exit from its financial role. For La Sasso and his fellow LIV players, the offseason is likely to be defined less by preparation for the next event and more by waiting for clarity about whether there is one. His Indianapolis win, however the next chapter unfolds, is already on the record.
