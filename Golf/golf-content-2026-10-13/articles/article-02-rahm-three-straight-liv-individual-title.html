Rahm Completes LIV Hat Trick — Third Straight Individual Title as Indianapolis Wraps the Season

By Jake Torres | October 13, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

Jon Rahm had already won the 2026 LIV Golf individual season title before he teed it up in Indianapolis. He had clinched the points championship mathematically in New York, and the numbers at that point left no room for anyone to catch him. He could have played conservatively, managed his way around the course, and saved himself for whatever comes next in his career.

He shot a final-round 63 instead. It wasn't enough to win — 22-year-old Michael La Sasso closed at 18-under to edge him by one — but it was entirely in character. Three consecutive LIV Golf individual titles, a second team championship for Legion XIII, and a final round that reminded everyone what makes Rahm different.

Three Straight: The Numbers

The LIV Golf format distributes points across individual events and team competitions over the course of a season, with the player accumulating the most individual points crowned the season's champion. Rahm won that title in 2024 and 2025. He has now won it in 2026 as well.

Over those three seasons, he won multiple individual events per year, led Legion XIII to two consecutive team championships, and maintained a standard of play that no other LIV player has come close to matching. His nearest rivals finished well behind in the points standing each year. The gap between Rahm and the rest of the LIV field in these seasons is not a matter of fine margins — it is a reflection of sustained excellence at the highest level the league could offer.

At Indianapolis, his 63 in the final round would have won on most days of the year. La Sasso played better. But the championship was already secure, and the story of Rahm's 2026 was already written.

What He Built at LIV

Rahm joined LIV Golf ahead of the 2024 season in a move that generated significant attention. He brought a major championship resume — the 2021 U.S. Open and 2023 Masters, among others — and his departure from the PGA Tour was among the most prominent the league had attracted at that point. In three seasons, he delivered on everything the league hoped his name would represent. He won. He won again. He won again.

Legion XIII, the team he captains, won the team championship in 2025 and again in 2026. For a league that runs a team format alongside individual competition, the combination of individual and team success Rahm brought to his franchise is the best possible outcome a player could produce.

What's Next

LIV Golf's future beyond 2026 is uncertain. Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund has signaled it will end its financial backing after this season, leaving the league's long-term prospects unresolved. Rahm's own situation — he was reportedly offered a substantial separation agreement to leave LIV — adds another layer to a story that is far from finished. What is finished is his 2026 season: three titles, two team crowns, and a runner-up finish in the final event of the year after shooting 63 in a round no one needed him to play. That's the standard.
