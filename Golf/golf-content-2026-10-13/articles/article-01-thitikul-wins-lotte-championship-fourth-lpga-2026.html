Thitikul Survives the Water Hazard and Wins the Lotte Championship — Her Fourth LPGA Title of 2026

By Ryan Calloway | October 13, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

The 16th hole at Hoakalei Country Club had Atthaya Thitikul's number. A two-shot leader with three holes to play, she found the water off the tee on the par-4, surrendering the lead and setting up what looked like a pivotal momentum shift. Instead, she birdied 17. She birdied 18. And she walked away from the Lotte Championship in Ewa Beach, Hawaii, as the winner at 21-under par — three shots clear of South Korea's Ina Yoon.

It was her fourth LPGA Tour title of the 2026 season, her 11th career victory, and arguably the most revealing performance of a year that has marked her out as the best player on tour.

How She Won

Thitikul had entered the final round with a share of the lead and turned in a steady 68 over the back nine. When she drove into the water on 16, the championship appeared to be opening up. Yoon, playing in the group behind, had the momentum and the leaderboard in her favor.

What happened next was a study in composure. Thitikul approached the 17th hole and converted a 20-foot birdie putt, immediately restoring her advantage. Then on the par-5 18th, she hit her approach close enough to birdie and seal the championship. Her final score of 21-under 267 put her three clear of Yoon, who shot a closing 68 to finish at 18-under. Japan's Miyu Yamashita took third at 17-under.

The $450,000 winner's share came from a $3 million purse — fitting for a performance that was equal parts drama and cold-blooded finish.

What This Season Is Becoming

Four wins from an LPGA season is an exceptional number. The last time a player reached that mark this consistently, the conversation shifted from "is she good?" to "how good?" That conversation has already started for Thitikul.

Her win at Hoakalei ties her with Nelly Korda for the most LPGA victories in 2026. It also puts her one win away from surpassing Ariya Jutanugarn's record for the most career LPGA wins by a Thai golfer. Jutanugarn's record stood for years and represented one of the LPGA's most celebrated individual legacies. Thitikul is one swing from writing over it.

The deeper context is her career trajectory. She joined the LPGA Tour in 2022 and now has 11 victories in fewer than five seasons. The pace of wins, the quality of the courses she has won on, and the way she performs under pressure — as she demonstrated on 17 and 18 at Hoakalei — suggest a player still ascending.

What's Next

Thitikul moves directly into the LPGA's Asian swing. The Buick LPGA Shanghai begins October 15 in China, followed by the BMW Ladies Championship (October 22-25) and the Maybank Championship (October 29 to November 1). With Korda matching her win total, the remaining LPGA events carry extra significance in both the individual race and the CME Globe standings. A fifth win would mark one of the great individual seasons in recent LPGA history — and the stage is already set for it to happen.
