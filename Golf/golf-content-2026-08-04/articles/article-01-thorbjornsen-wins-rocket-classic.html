Michael Thorbjornsen Wins the Last Rocket Classic — First PGA Tour Victory in 65 Starts

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | August 4, 2026

Michael Thorbjornsen won the Rocket Classic on Sunday, closing with a 7-under 63 at Detroit Golf Club to finish 18-under 262 and claim his first PGA Tour victory in his 65th start. He beat Xander Schauffele by two strokes.

The win came in the final staging of the Rocket Classic — a tournament that will not return to the PGA Tour schedule after this year. The last name on the winner's board belongs to a player who had never won before Sunday.

The Final Round

Thorbjornsen's 63 was the round that separated him from the field. He came into Sunday needing a performance, and he delivered one — consistent and clean throughout the back nine, building a lead that Schauffele was unable to close.

Schauffele, one of the most accomplished players in professional golf over the past several seasons, finished second. The two-stroke margin reflected Thorbjornsen's control of the day rather than a collapse from the runner-up.

The 18-under total of 262 was built across four days at a course that rewards players willing to be aggressive from the tee. Thorbjornsen played within a consistent framework throughout the week, the kind of week a player remembers regardless of what comes next.

Sixty-Five Starts

Sixty-five PGA Tour starts without a victory is not a number that gets assigned a simple meaning. Some of those starts produce near-misses. Others are weeks where a player was never genuinely in contention. What matters in the end is a single moment: the week where everything comes together.

For Thorbjornsen, that week was Detroit. He entered the Rocket Classic as a professional golfer who had demonstrated he could compete on Tour without yet having the result to prove it on the record. He leaves as a PGA Tour winner.

The difference in what follows — sponsorship conversations, exemption status, confidence going into every subsequent tournament — is not small. A first PGA Tour win is a permanent credential. It cannot be taken back.

The Numbers That Follow Him Home

Thorbjornsen takes home $1,800,000 in prize money. The more immediately consequential number is 500 FedEx Cup points, which secured his place in the FedEx Cup Playoffs before the final standings were calculated.

The playoff spot ensures Thorbjornsen's season continues beyond the regular-season finale. It also ensures that the momentum from a first win gets carried into a competitive context rather than ending with the close of the regular schedule.

The Tournament That Won't Return

The Rocket Classic was held at Detroit Golf Club for the final time this week. The event leaves the PGA Tour schedule after this year, and the players who competed in the final edition will be the last to have that result on their record.

That Thorbjornsen's first win lands in the tournament's final edition is the kind of coincidence that becomes part of a career story. The Rocket Classic ends. A career begins. Whatever comes next for either Thorbjornsen or the venue, Sunday's result is permanent.
