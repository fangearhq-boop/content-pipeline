Bank of Utah Championship Heads Into Moving Day With the Leaderboard Packed at the Top

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | October 1, 2026

Round 3 of the Bank of Utah Championship begins Saturday at Black Desert Resort in St. George, Utah, and the leaderboard is as tight as any this season. After 36 holes, six players are separated by two shots at the top, with more than half the field still realistically in contention for a Sunday finish.

Rory Ostrander holds the 54-hole lead at -15 after rounds of 65-66. He has been the steadiest player on the course through the first two days, posting back-to-back rounds in the mid-60s without a significant stumble.

The Leaderboard After 36 Holes

At -14 and one shot back, Davis Holton and Cam Murray share second place. Holton opened with a 64 on Thursday and followed with a 68 on Friday, while Murray went the opposite direction — a 67 in Round 1 and a 65 in Round 2. Both players arrive at Moving Day with something to prove.

Three players sit at -13 and share third place: Shane Ostrander, Emilio Guzman, and Lucas Mow. Shane Ostrander opened with a 67 and added a 65 on Friday. Guzman carded back-to-back 66s. Mow shot 65-68 and will need to get things moving in the right direction to stay relevant on Saturday.

Scottie Scheffler sits at -11 and T-7 heading into Round 3. Four shots off the pace may seem like a gap, but on a course like Black Desert Resort — where birdies are gettable and the back nine is particularly scoreable — a low Saturday round could make Sunday genuinely interesting for the world's best player.

Brothers at the Top

The storyline that will define the weekend at Black Desert is already written: Rory Ostrander and Shane Ostrander are two shots apart heading into Round 3, and one of them is the defending champion of this event.

Shane Ostrander won the inaugural edition of the Bank of Utah Championship at Black Desert Resort, so he knows this course as well as anyone in the field. He is two shots behind his brother with two rounds to play. That dynamic — a defending champion chasing a sibling who leads the tournament — is rare in professional golf and will be the dominant narrative of Moving Day and Sunday alike.

For Rory, the lead is real but not comfortable. Every player from Holton and Murray down to Scheffler will be making a run. The question for Round 3 is whether Rory can extend his advantage or whether the field — starting with his own brother — will tighten things up further.

About Black Desert Resort

Black Desert Resort is a Pete Dye design that plays as a desert-links hybrid — dramatic volcanic rock formations, wide fairways with selective trouble, and greens that reward precision approach play. At par 72 and approximately 7,500 yards, the course demands both length and accuracy. The desert floor creates firm, fast conditions that punish errant shots while rewarding players who can control their ball flight in the Utah heat.

This is a FedExCup Fall event with a $6 million purse. A win here carries real weight in the fall points standings heading toward the end of the season.

What to Watch Saturday

Round 3 tee times begin Saturday morning, with the leaders going off in the afternoon local time. The pairing of Rory and Shane Ostrander — if the tournament pairs them together given their proximity on the leaderboard — would be one of the more compelling Moving Day pairings of the season.

Scheffler is four back, which is not a comfortable deficit but is far from insurmountable. A 64 or better on Saturday would put him squarely in the conversation heading into Sunday.

The cut has been made. The field is set. Moving Day at Black Desert begins Saturday, and the Ostrander brothers are at the center of everything.
