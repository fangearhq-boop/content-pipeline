The LIV Golf October 13 Deadline Is 12 Days Away — And Several Key Players Still Haven't Decided

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | October 1, 2026

Today is October 1, 2026. October 13 is 12 days away. That date is the publicly announced deadline for players to commit to LIV Golf's merger framework — and as of this morning, several of the most prominent players involved still have not publicly declared their position.

The deadline is not considered soft. Sources familiar with the negotiations have described this as the moment the process concludes, and the October 13 date was set and communicated publicly with that intent. The deal has been months in the making, and the final phase has arrived.

What Players Are Being Asked to Sign

Players who commit to the framework under the merger deal would receive 52.5% equity in the combined entity. That figure is the headline number — and it is a significant one. A 52.5% equity stake would make committed players among the most financially empowered athletes in professional sports history, holding a majority ownership position in one of golf's largest competitive organizations.

The structure represents a direct attempt to align player interests with the long-term commercial health of the combined product. For players who have spent years on the LIV circuit, it is the payoff for their bet. For players still weighing the decision, it is a significant financial and professional commitment with consequences that extend well beyond a single season.

DeChambeau Has Not Committed

Bryson DeChambeau is LIV Golf's most prominent active player. He is the face of the circuit in competitive terms, a two-time major champion with a platform that extends well beyond golf's core audience. He has not publicly committed to the framework as of October 1.

DeChambeau's silence on the matter has been one of the more closely watched elements of the negotiations. Whether that silence reflects ongoing deliberation, a negotiating posture, or something else is not publicly known. What is known is that 12 days remain and the deadline is approaching fast.

At least six marquee players remain undecided as of today. That number is significant — it suggests the final phase of negotiations involves more than one or two holdouts, and that the October 13 date will arrive with real consequence regardless of which direction those players go.

What Happens After October 13

What happens if players do not sign by the deadline is not publicly clear. The framework has not specified what consequences, if any, apply to players who decline or miss the deadline. That uncertainty is itself a pressure point — players are being asked to make a major financial and career decision without a fully visible picture of what the alternative looks like.

Golf media and players across both tours have been in daily watch mode as the countdown has shortened. The negotiations have entered their most compressed phase, and the decisions made in the next 12 days will shape the structure of professional golf for years to come.

The Bigger Picture

The merger framework has been in process long enough that the broad outlines are familiar. But the October 13 deadline has concentrated attention on the specifics — who signs, who hesitates, and what the combined entity actually looks like once the player base is confirmed.

The 52.5% equity figure is the kind of number that does not appear often in professional sports. It is a statement about what LIV Golf believes the combined product is worth and what it is willing to give to secure the players who will define that product. Whether enough of those players agree before October 13 is the question that will be answered in under two weeks.
