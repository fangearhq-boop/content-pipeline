Jon Rahm's LIV Exit: Separation Agreement Filed, November 5 Hearing Is the Final Step

The paperwork is filed. The court date is set. Jon Rahm's path back to professional golf runs through a New Jersey courtroom on November 5.

By Jake Torres | October 19, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

Where Things Stand

Jon Rahm's separation agreement with LIV Golf was filed in New Jersey bankruptcy court around October 15. The hearing for a judge's approval is scheduled for November 5. Until that hearing concludes and the separation is approved, Rahm remains legally bound under his existing contract — and the PGA Tour has stated plainly that it will not hold conversations with any LIV player still under contract.

Rahm's attorney, John Beck, told the court earlier this month that his client had independently reviewed the proposed terms of LIV's restructured league and found them unacceptable. Rahm will not participate in the new version of the league going forward.

What the Agreement Contains

The terms of the separation agreement give Rahm a clean break. Under the deal, he is released from all remaining performance obligations — he owes LIV no further services or appearances. He is free to join or contract with any tour, league, or sponsor without providing notice to LIV or seeking its consent. His name, image, and likeness rights are returned to him. Rahm also waives his financial claims against the series under the agreement.

The financial dimensions of this exit were extensively reported when Rahm first moved to LIV in 2023. Pre-filing estimates put his remaining contract guarantees at more than $100 million. Court documents at the time of LIV's bankruptcy filing listed $7.5 million owed to Rahm specifically. The separation agreement does not mention monetary compensation.

What Comes Next

Rahm is reportedly targeting the Abu Dhabi Championship, scheduled for November 5-8, as his first event following the separation. The timing creates an interesting overlap: the court hearing is on November 5, and the tournament begins the same day. A clean approval on the 5th would make him eligible for that field.

The PGA Tour has said it is prepared to discuss reinstatement with players who have cleared their legal obligations to LIV. Whether that happens before the Abu Dhabi Championship or takes additional time is unclear. Several LIV players before Rahm returned to the PGA Tour through a Returning Member Program launched in early 2026, which required a charitable donation and specific playing conditions. If Rahm follows a similar path, the outlines would include his own set of terms.

The Bigger Picture

Rahm's departure represents the most significant individual exit from LIV since the league launched in 2022. He signed with the series following the 2023 US Open, which he won. He spent two-plus seasons competing on the LIV schedule, making occasional appearances at major championships. In that time, his world ranking declined from inside the top five to outside the top thirty.

Whatever route Rahm takes back to the global mainstream of professional golf, the process moves to its next formal step on November 5. The court will decide. Then the world will see what comes next.
