### STORY 2: Nishimura Wins NW Arkansas Championship for First LPGA Title
- **Tier:** 1
- **Category:** Golf / LPGA / Yuna Nishimura / NW Arkansas Championship / Walmart
- **Summary:** Yuna Nishimura of Japan won the Walmart NW Arkansas Championship at Pinnacle Country Club in Rogers, Arkansas, shooting a final-round 66 to finish at 21-under par and win by five shots. It is her first LPGA Tour victory. She led wire-to-wire after back-to-back opening 63s. She survived an early Sunday scare — even par through nine — before Aki Iwai threatened with six birdies in seven back-nine holes. Nishimura responded with an eagle at the par-5 14th, followed by two more birdies, to pull clear. Aki Iwai (bogey-free 62), Chanettee Wannasaen, and In Gee Chun finished tied for second at 16-under. Winner's purse: $450,000 from a $3 million total purse.

### STORY 3: LIV Golf Bankruptcy — The October 13 Player Deadline Is 15 Days Away
- **Tier:** 1
- **Category:** Golf / LIV Golf / Bankruptcy / BC Partners / Player Contracts
- **Summary:** With LIV Golf's Chapter 11 bankruptcy filing now three weeks old, the critical player deadline is approaching: players holding at least two-thirds in amount and one-half in number of eligible claims must commit to new, lower-cost contracts by October 13 — 15 days away. Original guaranteed contracts for players like Jon Rahm and Bryson DeChambeau are among the claims being restructured. LIV's 2026 season is over; the planned relaunch targets early 2027 with a 10-event schedule.
