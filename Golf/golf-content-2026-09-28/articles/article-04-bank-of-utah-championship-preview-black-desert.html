The Bank of Utah Championship Starts Thursday at Black Desert Resort -- What to Know

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | September 28, 2026

The PGA Tour's fall season returns this week with the Bank of Utah Championship, a full-field event running October 1-4 at Black Desert Resort in Ivins, Utah.

Round 1 tees off Thursday. It is one of the most visually distinctive settings in professional golf.

The Venue

Black Desert Resort opened in 2023 in Ivins, Utah, a small community near St. George set against the red rock terrain that borders Zion National Park. The course was designed with that landscape as the primary visual feature, and the combination of the fairways cutting through the red rock formations has drawn strong reviews since the resort opened.

The Bank of Utah Championship is one of the first Tour events at the venue. The contrast of green fairways against the red-and-orange landscape makes it a course that photographs unlike anything else on the schedule, and it has drawn comparisons to destinations like TPC Scottsdale for its visual identity.

What the Fall Series Means

The PGA Tour's fall schedule, officially called the FedExCup Fall, features full-field events that offer FedExCup points toward the following season's standings. Players competing in the fall swing are largely those who did not qualify for the FedExCup Playoffs in August. The fall gives them an opportunity to build standing heading into 2027, qualify for major championship exemptions, and secure their playing status for the following season.

That structure means the Bank of Utah Championship field will include a mix of younger players trying to establish themselves, veterans working to maintain their Tour cards, and players whose regular-season results did not meet their expectations. The competition is real and the stakes matter for the players on the course.

What's Next

Round 1 begins Thursday, October 1. The final round is Sunday, October 4, which runs concurrent with the Malaysian Grand Prix start in Formula 1 for motorsport fans tracking multiple schedules. Golf Channel and ESPN+ will have coverage of the Bank of Utah Championship across the week. After Ivins, the PGA Tour Fall continues with additional events through the end of October before the Tour's regular season resumes in January.
