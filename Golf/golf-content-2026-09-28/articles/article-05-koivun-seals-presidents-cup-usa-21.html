Jackson Koivun Sealed the Presidents Cup for Team USA. He's 21 Years Old.

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | September 28, 2026

Jim Furyk made the pick. He said it was about composure. He was right.

Jackson Koivun, 21 years old, one of the youngest players ever selected for a United States Presidents Cup team, closed his Sunday singles match against Si Woo Kim 2-up at Medinah Country Club. That point sealed Team USA's 17-13 victory over the International Team and completed the greatest Sunday comeback in Presidents Cup history.

The Match

Koivun and Si Woo Kim were close through much of the round. Kim is a four-time PGA Tour winner with significant pressure-match experience, and the match reflected that. Going into the back nine at Medinah -- a course that has decided events at the highest level for decades -- nothing was decided.

Then Koivun made his move. He won four of five holes on the back nine, building a 2-up lead that proved too large for Kim to overcome. Koivun closed the match. His teammates converged on the green and the scoreboard made it official: USA 17, International Team 13.

Why Furyk Picked Him

When Furyk named his Presidents Cup roster, Koivun was one of the selections that generated discussion. At 21, without the established record that most Team USA members carry into the event, the choice required Furyk to make a judgment about something harder to quantify than statistics.

Furyk said he picked Koivun for his composure. Match play at the Presidents Cup is not solely about ball-striking. It is about handling pressure over five days of team competition, responding to the crowd, managing momentum, and winning holes that matter. Furyk believed Koivun could do that.

Sunday at Medinah confirmed it. When the match was still undecided on the back nine against an experienced opponent, Koivun won the stretch of holes that mattered most.

The Bigger Picture

USA's Presidents Cup record now stands at 14-1-1 across 16 editions. The lone International win was in 1998. The International Team came to Medinah with what should have been a winning position -- the biggest singles-session cushion in the event's history. USA erased it in a day.

Koivun's performance will be remembered in the context of that comeback. The youngest player on the team delivered the clinching point in one of the event's most dramatic finals.

What's Next

For Koivun, a Presidents Cup winner's medal at age 21 is a significant marker at the start of what should be a long professional career. The PGA Tour's fall schedule continues this week with the Bank of Utah Championship at Black Desert Resort in Ivins, Utah, October 1-4. The next major team event on the horizon for American players is the Ryder Cup in 2027.
