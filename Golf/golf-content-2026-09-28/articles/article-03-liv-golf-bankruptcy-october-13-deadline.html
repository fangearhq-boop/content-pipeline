LIV Golf Bankruptcy: The October 13 Player Deadline Is 15 Days Away

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | September 28, 2026

LIV Golf's Chapter 11 bankruptcy filing in New Jersey on September 8 set a countdown in motion. The most important deadline in the restructuring is now 15 days away: October 13.

By that date, players holding LIV Golf contracts must decide whether to accept new, lower-cost deals or walk away. The math required to keep the restructuring alive is specific, and the players at the top of the contract hierarchy face the steepest decisions.

What the Deadline Requires

For the BC Partners rescue deal to proceed, players holding at least two-thirds in dollar amount of eligible player claims AND at least one-half in number of eligible players must agree to the new contract terms by October 13. Both thresholds must be met.

That structure makes the players with the largest contracts the most consequential votes. Jon Rahm and Bryson DeChambeau are among the players owed millions under the original guaranteed contracts LIV issued to attract players away from the PGA Tour and DP World Tour. If the largest creditors refuse new terms, the dollar-threshold may fail even if a numerical majority of players agree.

What BC Partners Is Offering

BC Partners, a London-based private equity firm, committed a $300 million rescue package structured in three parts: a $127.5 million term loan with warrants for 5% of the new LIV parent entity; $147.5 million in preferred equity with warrants for 10%; and $25 million in convertible notes for 30%. If everything closes, BC Partners holds 45% of the new LIV entity. Players would collectively own 52.5%, with management holding 2.5%.

The trade-off for players is accepting lower guaranteed amounts in exchange for equity in the restructured organization. Whether that equity becomes worth anything depends on whether LIV can actually relaunch and compete for audiences and sponsors after bankruptcy.

What Happens Next

A bankruptcy court hearing is scheduled for October 7. That is the next public milestone before the October 13 deadline, and it will give an indication of how the proceedings are tracking. LIV's 2026 season has already ended. The planned relaunch is early 2027 with a 10-event schedule, contingent on the restructuring succeeding.

What's Next

The October 7 court hearing is nine days away. The October 13 player deadline follows six days after that. The golf world will be watching both dates to understand whether LIV Golf's restructuring has the player support it needs to survive. If the deal closes, LIV plans to begin signing sponsors and tournament hosts for its 2027 schedule in the weeks that follow.
