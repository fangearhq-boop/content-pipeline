USA Wins Presidents Cup 17-13 at Medinah -- Greatest Sunday Comeback in Event History

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | September 28, 2026

Team USA won the 2026 Presidents Cup 17-13 at Medinah Country Club, erasing a 3-point singles deficit to complete the greatest Sunday comeback in the event's history.

The International Team entered Sunday's 12 singles matches holding a 10.5-7.5 lead. It was the largest cushion the Internationals had ever carried into the final day in the Presidents Cup's 30-year history. To win, USA needed to take nine of the 12 matches available. They did.

How the Comeback Happened

Scottie Scheffler opened the morning with a statement. The world No. 1 beat Nico Echavarria 2&1, giving USA an early point and setting the tone for a session that only went one way.

Collin Morikawa was trailing his match through the front nine before flipping it entirely on the back nine. Patrick Cantlay put the moment of the week on the board when he drained a 57-foot putt on the 17th green to go 2-up against Adam Scott. Justin Thomas was clinical against Corey Conners, winning 5&3.

The Internationals' best answer came from Tom Kim, who defeated Wyndham Clark 3&2. It was the International side's clearest point of the day and a reminder of how talented their roster is. It was not enough to hold off the American wave.

The Clinching Point

The 2026 Presidents Cup ended when Jackson Koivun closed his match. The 21-year-old rookie, one of the youngest players captain Jim Furyk ever brought to a Presidents Cup, defeated Si Woo Kim 2-up in Sunday singles after winning four of five back-nine holes. Koivun's point sealed the American comeback.

Furyk said at the outset of the week that he selected Koivun for his composure under pressure. Sunday at Medinah validated the call.

USA's Remarkable Record

This is USA's 11th consecutive Presidents Cup title and improves their all-time record to 14-1-1 across 16 editions of the event. The lone International victory came in 1998 at Royal Melbourne. The tie was in 2003 at Fancourt, also in South Africa.

The Internationals have come close in recent years. Going into Sunday 3 points clear was the most promising position they had held in decades. The American singles depth remains the defining structural advantage in this event, and Sunday at Medinah showed why.

What's Next

The PGA Tour's fall season continues with the Bank of Utah Championship at Black Desert Resort in Ivins, Utah, running October 1-4. The Presidents Cup players will have different schedules heading into the fall and toward the 2027 season. The next major team event on the international calendar is the Ryder Cup in 2027.
