Wyndham Clark Enters East Lake With His Most Dangerous Weapon — Momentum

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | August 26, 2026

When Wyndham Clark birdied the 16th hole at Bellerive Country Club last Sunday, it was unexpected. When he birdied the 17th, it was impressive. When he walked up to the 18th tee and did it again — finishing birdie-birdie-birdie to close out the BMW Championship with a three-shot victory — it became the most emphatic closing statement of the 2026 PGA Tour season.

That image — Clark attacking when he could have protected, making birdie after birdie in the final stretch when a par-par-par would have sufficed — is exactly the mentality the Tour Championship demands. East Lake is not a course that rewards survival. It rewards conviction, course management, and the willingness to take on flagsticks when the moment asks for it. Clark has shown, repeatedly this season, that he can do exactly that.

A 2026 Season Worth Noticing

Clark's BMW Championship win was his second on the PGA Tour in 2026. The first was the U.S. Open at Shinnecock Hills — one of the most demanding courses in major championship rotation. Two wins across a season that included a national championship and a FedEx playoff victory gives Clark a résumé that demands respect from any field he enters.

He enters the Tour Championship as the No. 3 FedExCup seed, trailing only Scheffler and Fitzpatrick in the standings. In a year where the no-staggered-strokes format means everything begins at level par on Thursday, that seeding is context rather than advantage. Clark doesn't need a head start. He needs what he already has: a golf game that peaks at the right moments and a mindset built for late-week pressure.

Scheffler is the favorite, and the gap between them in the standings is genuine. But Clark has been the second-most consistent closer in professional golf this season, and the Tour Championship is, ultimately, a week of golf. The player who makes birdies on 16, 17, and 18 on Sunday at East Lake wins the FedExCup. Clark has proven he knows how to do that.

What's Next

Clark tees off Thursday in Round 1 at East Lake. Watch for him to bring the same aggressive approach that won at Bellerive — attacking pins, using momentum, refusing to let leads become burdens. If he stays near the top of the board through the weekend, Sunday at East Lake could become another statement about what this version of Wyndham Clark is capable of.
