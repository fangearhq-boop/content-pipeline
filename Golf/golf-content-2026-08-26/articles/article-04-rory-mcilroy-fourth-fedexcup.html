Nobody Has Won 4 FedExCups. Rory McIlroy Is Trying to Change That.

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | August 26, 2026

The record exists. Nobody in the history of the FedExCup has won the season-long competition four times. Rory McIlroy has won it three times — in 2016, 2019, and 2022 — and he is here at East Lake this week chasing something no player has accomplished before.

McIlroy finished solo third at the BMW Championship at Bellerive Country Club, trailing Wyndham Clark and Patrick Cantlay but showing enough to keep his FedExCup ambitions intact entering the 30-player finale. He is in the field. He is in form. And he has won this tournament before, which counts for something on a course as specific and demanding as East Lake Golf Club.

The Road Runs Through Scheffler

The honest assessment of McIlroy's chances at East Lake is complicated by one name: Scottie Scheffler. The No. 1 seed, the betting favorite, the No. 1-ranked player in the world, and the 2024 FedExCup champion enters this week as the standard the entire field must measure itself against. Scheffler has been better than everyone else for five consecutive years at this stage of the season, and there is no evidence in 2026 that the gap has closed.

What McIlroy has, that others don't, is precedent. He has stood at the top of a leaderboard at East Lake before, with the pressure of the FedExCup finale pressing down, and delivered. The 2022 campaign that ended with his third title came after a season of close calls and significant expectation. He managed it. He knows how to navigate this week's particular emotional landscape in a way that first-timers to the finale cannot replicate.

The no-staggered-strokes format that defines the 2026 Tour Championship actually benefits McIlroy. In a format where the standings advantage is neutralized and everyone starts at even par, the player who shoots the lowest score over four rounds wins. On his best days — which have happened multiple times this season — McIlroy can put up numbers that challenge anyone.

What's Next

Round 1 at East Lake begins Thursday at 11:00 AM ET. McIlroy tees off as part of the 30-player field with the same starting position as everyone else in the field. No head start, no deficit — just 72 holes to determine whether the sport's most experienced FedExCup champion can claim history where no one else has gone. The fourth title is the one that separates him entirely.
