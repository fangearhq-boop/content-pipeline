Tour Championship 2026: East Lake Opens for Business Thursday With $40M on the Line

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | August 26, 2026

East Lake Golf Club has hosted the Tour Championship for years now, and the venue carries a weight that few golf courses in the United States can match. It is where the FedExCup is decided, where seasons are defined, and where 30 of the best players in professional golf will gather starting Thursday, August 27, to compete for a $40 million purse and the sport's most prestigious season-ending trophy.

The 2026 format is clean and decisive. No staggered starting strokes based on FedExCup position — a system that had defined Tour Championships in previous years and rewarded regular-season performance with a head start. This year, all 30 players begin at even par. The lowest 72-hole score wins the Tour Championship and the FedExCup simultaneously. The best player this week wins. Nothing more complicated than that.

First tee shots go off at 11:00 AM ET Thursday with television coverage on ESPN through 1 PM and Golf Channel from 1 to 6 PM. The marquee first-round pairing is Scottie Scheffler — the No. 1 FedExCup seed and betting favorite — alongside No. 2 seed Matt Fitzpatrick at 2:00 PM ET. Justin Rose and Adam Scott tee off at 11:12 AM.

The Storylines Entering Thursday

Scottie Scheffler arrives as the clear favorite, entering the Tour Championship as the No. 1 seed for the fifth consecutive year. He has already secured a $23 million FedExCup bonus for leading the season standings, and a tournament victory at East Lake would add $10 million more and give him the fourth FedExCup title of his career — a number no one has reached before.

Wyndham Clark carries the momentum of a player who doesn't know how to finish quietly. He birdied holes 16, 17, and 18 in the final round of the BMW Championship to win by three shots at Bellerive — the kind of closing kick that suggests confidence and nerve rather than inherited lead management. Two wins in 2026 (the BMW and the U.S. Open at Shinnecock Hills) make him a genuine threat.

Rory McIlroy seeks history. Three FedExCup titles already — 2016, 2019, 2022 — and a chance to become the first player to four. His solo third at the BMW kept him in the field and in contention. On his best days, McIlroy is the one player capable of producing the scoring run that could overwhelm even Scheffler at East Lake.

What's Next

Round 1 at East Lake begins Thursday morning. The leaderboard after 18 holes will tell us which narrative is taking hold: Scheffler dominant again, or a field crowding the board at the top after a clean, stagger-free start. Four rounds of golf at one of the game's great courses, with $40 million in prize money and the FedExCup awaiting the winner. It doesn't get bigger than this.
