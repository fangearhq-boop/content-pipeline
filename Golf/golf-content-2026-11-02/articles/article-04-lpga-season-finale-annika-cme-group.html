LPGA Season Finale Ahead: The ANNIKA (Nov. 12) and CME Group Tour Championship (Nov. 22)

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | November 2, 2026

The LPGA Tour's 2026 season is entering its final stretch. With the Maybank Championship behind us — Yamashita claiming the title last week — two events remain on the calendar, and both carry enormous significance heading into the off-season.

The ANNIKA at Pelican — November 12-15

The season's penultimate event is The ANNIKA driven by Gainbridge at Pelican, scheduled for November 12-15 at Pelican Golf Club in Belleair, Florida. The tournament, named for Annika Sorenstam — one of the greatest players in the history of women's professional golf — is a prestigious event in the LPGA calendar and draws a strong field in advance of the season finale.

Pelican Golf Club provides a challenging test that rewards precision approach play, and the Florida fall conditions typically produce low scoring among the tour's elite. Players entering with something to prove heading into the CME will treat this event as both a competition and a final tune-up.

CME Group Tour Championship — November 19-22

The season ends at Tiburón Golf Club in Naples, Florida, where the CME Group Tour Championship runs November 19-22. The CME is the defining event at the close of the LPGA season, featuring the largest prize purse in women's professional golf.

The field at the CME is restricted to the top players on the tour, making it a season-culminating showdown between the year's best performers. The combination of high stakes, a strong field, and a closing-week atmosphere makes it one of the most watched events the LPGA produces.

For players chasing season-ending honors or looking to cement their place in the rankings, the CME is the final opportunity. Two weeks of play remain in 2026, and the finish line is in sight.

What to Watch

With the season-long narrative heading into its final chapter, the storylines heading into Belleair and Naples will develop through the week. Players who have been close to their first major win, those competing for year-end honors, and veterans making final pushes on a strong season all have something meaningful at stake.

The ANNIKA opens November 12. The CME Group Tour Championship closes November 22. Two events, enormous prize money, and the end of another LPGA season.
