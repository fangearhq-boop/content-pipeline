### STORY 1: Andrew Novak Wins Mexico Open — Tournament-Record 25-Under, First Solo PGA Title

**FB Post 1** — Full recap
**Window:** 9:30 AM ET

Andrew Novak is a PGA Tour champion — and he did it in historic fashion.

The 31-year-old Wofford College graduate closed out the VidantaWorld Mexico Open at Vidanta on Sunday with a five-shot win at 25-under-par 259, a new tournament record at Vidanta Vallarta. Steven Fisk and Kristoffer Ventura shared the runner-up position.
