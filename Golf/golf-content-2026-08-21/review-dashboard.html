### Long-Form Post Window: 8:00 AM ET The most memorable first round of the BMW Championship this week didn't belong to a player who was supposed to be leading. It belonged to Rory McIlroy. McIlroy shot a 6-under 64 at Bellerive Country Club on Thursday, joining Gary Woodland, Wyndham Clark, J.J. Spaun, and Chris Gotterup in a five-way tie for the lead at -6. For context: one week ago, McIlroy finished 66th at the St. Jude Championship. He was 8-over for the tournament. He said afterward that he was "far away from where I need to be." He paired well with the course at Bellerive — the par-70 Robert Trent Jones Sr. design is playing at 7,448 yards this week, asking for precision off the tee and sharp iron play into small targets. McIlroy gave both. Meanwhile, defending FedExCup points leader Scottie Scheffler — who entered the week having won St. Jude by 8 shots and holding a lead of roughly 1,400 FedExCup points — opened at +2 and finds himself in a tie for 44th, eight shots back of the leaders. Scheffler's position at East Lake is not in jeopardy, but a BMW title will require a dramatic Round 2 charge. Round 2 is underway Friday at Bellerive. McIlroy was paired with Xander Schauffele and teed off at 10:47 AM ET. Can McIlroy carry this form through the weekend? Drop your prediction below ⬇️ ---

### Long-Form Post Window: 1:00 PM ET Jon Rahm's 2026 LIV Golf season has been a statement from start to finish — and this week at The Club at Chatham Hills in Westfield, Indiana, it draws to a close. Rahm had already clinched his third consecutive LIV Golf Individual Season Championship before arriving in Indianapolis, having locked up the title at LIV Golf New York with 944.54 season points. The finale then gave him one more opportunity to add to his legacy — and reports indicate Rahm captured the Indianapolis event title as well, capping a dominant campaign at the circuit's final tournament of the year. The Indianapolis event carries a purse of $10.1 million — reduced from the original $20 million following the well-publicized reductions to the LIV Golf prize fund as the Saudi PIF announced it would end its direct backing of the tour after 2026. The finale at Chatham Hills would decide which team claims the 2026 LIV team title. Chatham Hills holds the distinction of being the final course designed by Pete Dye before his death — a fitting stage for the final chapter of a dramatic LIV Golf season. ### Long-Form Post Window: 3:30 PM ET When the BMW Championship got underway Thursday at Bellerive, Gary Woodland was sitting in 31st place in the FedExCup standings — one spot outside the top 30 that earns a trip to the Tour Championship at East Lake. Then he made a hole-out eagle, five birdies, and one bogey for a first-round 64 (-6), tying for the lead. The math shifted instantly. Woodland didn't just put himself in position to win the BMW Championship — he dramatically improved his East Lake chances, because FedExCup points are awarded relative to tournament finish, and the leader after Round 1 is going to accumulate points. Meanwhile, Rickie Fowler entered the week at exactly the 30th and final qualifying spot — the last safe position. The gap between Fowler and Bud Cauley in 29th is described as fractional, measured in decimal points. The gap between Fowler and Woodland below him has now narrowed dramatically after one round. By the time Friday's Round 2 concludes, the entire bubble picture will have shifted again. The BMW Championship is not just a golf tournament this week — it's a qualification event, and the drama at positions 28 through 32 is as compelling as the leaderboard battle at the top. East Lake hosts the Tour Championship from August 27-30. Only the top 30 get in. Who do you think makes East Lake and who misses out? 👇 ---

His BMW Championship title prospects, however, looked uncertain after a first-round 2-over par 72 at Bellerive landed him in a tie for 44th — eight shots behind the co-leaders, including a resurgent Rory McIlroy. Golf's ability to produce unexpected narratives in the span of a single week is part of what makes it endlessly compelling. Round 2 at Bellerive continues through Friday.
