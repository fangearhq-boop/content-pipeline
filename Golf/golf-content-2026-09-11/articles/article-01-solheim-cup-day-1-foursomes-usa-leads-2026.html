USA Leads 2-1 After Solheim Cup Morning Foursomes — Korda and Corpuz Set Pairing Record at Bernardus Golf

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | September 11, 2026

Team USA holds a 2-1 lead over Europe after Friday morning foursomes at the 2026 Solheim Cup, with Nelly Korda and Allisen Corpuz winning their opener to go 5-0 in foursomes together — a new record for most consecutive wins by any pairing in Solheim Cup history.

The 2026 Solheim Cup is underway at Bernardus Golf in Cromvoirt, Netherlands, and Team USA has drawn first blood on the opening day of competition.

After four foursomes matches played Friday morning, USA leads Europe 2-1, with the fourth match still completing at time of writing. The session set the scoreline for what will be a three-day competition across 28 total matches — foursomes and fourballs on Friday and Saturday, followed by 12 singles on Sunday.

The headline from the morning: Nelly Korda and Allisen Corpuz are now 5-0 as a foursomes pairing, establishing a new record for most consecutive wins by any partnership in Solheim Cup history. In the opening match of Day 1, they defeated Charley Hull and Lottie Woad — Europe's most formidable pairing on paper — to put USA ahead from the very first point of the week.

The Korda-Corpuz Partnership

The pairing of Korda and Corpuz in foursomes has become one of the most reliably discussed aspects of Solheim Cup strategy. Going into this week, they stood at 4-0 in the format. Now, after Friday's win over Hull and Woad, they have extended that record to 5-0 — and done it against as formidable an opponent as Europe could offer.

Hull, making her eighth consecutive Solheim Cup appearance, is one of the most experienced match-play performers in the history of the competition. Woad, the world No. 5, is among Europe's most promising young players. That Korda and Corpuz defeated them to open the competition matters beyond the one point — it establishes psychological momentum for USA in the early going.

Korda herself entered this week as the world No. 1 with a 10-5-1 Solheim Cup record, among the most accomplished individual records of any active player. The foursomes format suits her: in alternate-shot golf, her ball-striking sets up her partner, and Corpuz has shown the ability to convert from the positions Korda creates.

Europe's Response

Europe did not fold. One foursomes point went to the home side, keeping the morning session competitive rather than a rout. Angela Stanford's USA team leads, but Anna Nordqvist's European roster — which includes three rookies in Maria Fassi Lopez Ramirez, Perrine Nadaud, and Mimi Rhodes — still has four afternoon fourballs to respond.

The format shift from foursomes to fourballs changes the tactical picture. Fourballs (best-ball format) reward individual brilliance and reduce the risk associated with one poor shot. For Europe's rookies, that's a more forgiving environment to contribute. For Europe's experienced players like Hull and Woad, it's the chance to recover from this morning's result without dragging a partner's round along.

Europe captain Nordqvist will need to manage her selections carefully for the afternoon to close the gap. A 2-1 deficit after the morning session is recoverable — the 2024 Solheim Cup saw USA eventually win 15.5-12.5, but the margins through the first session that year looked similar. Nordqvist has been through Solheim Cups before. She knows the scoreboard is still in its first chapter.
