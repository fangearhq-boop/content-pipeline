Presidents Cup 2026 Rosters Finalized — All 24 Players Confirmed for Medinah, 11 Days Away

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | September 11, 2026

Both the USA and International rosters for the 2026 Presidents Cup at Medinah Country Club are now set, with Brandt Snedeker's 12-man USA team headlined by world No. 1 Scottie Scheffler and featuring 21-year-old Jackson Koivun as its most talked-about pick, while Geoff Ogilvy's International side includes Hideki Matsuyama, Adam Scott, and a multinational group built to challenge on home soil.

The 2026 Presidents Cup is 11 days away. Both teams are confirmed. Medinah Country Club will host the biennial match-play competition from September 22 through September 27, and for the first time in 28 years, a meaningful number of golf observers are watching with genuine interest in both sides.

Captain Brandt Snedeker and his USA team are heavy favorites on historical grounds — the Americans have won 13 of 15 Presidents Cup editions, and the International team has not won since the 1998 edition in Melbourne. But the roster Geoff Ogilvy assembled has depth that recent International sides lacked, and Medinah's layout offers no particular advantage to either team.

Team USA — The Roster

USA's six automatic qualifiers — Scottie Scheffler, Cameron Young, Wyndham Clark, Sam Burns, Russell Henley, and Collin Morikawa — gave Snedeker a formidable base to work with. The automatic list is, essentially, the top of the PGA Tour's competitive hierarchy through the FedExCup season. Scheffler, who won the 2026 FedExCup championship and holds the world No. 1 ranking, anchors the team.

Snedeker's captain's picks completed the 12-man roster: Xander Schauffele, Justin Thomas, Patrick Cantlay, Chris Gotterup, Caleb Bridgeman, and Jackson Koivun.

The Koivun pick has generated the most attention. At 21 years old, a graduate of Auburn University, Koivun won the 3M Open in July in just his third professional start — setting a tournament record in the process. Snedeker selected him as the second-youngest USA Presidents Cup pick in history, behind only Jordan Spieth (age 20 in 2013). For a team that already has its stars, Koivun represents a different value: the unknown, and the upside that comes with a player who has already demonstrated he can win at the highest level without precedent holding him back.

Snedeker also announced a format decision: fourballs — best-ball, where each player plays their own ball and the lower score counts — will be played in the opening round at Medinah. That choice rewards individual brilliance and puts Scheffler, Koivun, and the rest of USA's deepest bench in the format best suited to displaying peak performance without being constrained by a partner's difficulties.

International Team — The Roster

Geoff Ogilvy's International side has automatic qualifiers Si Woo Kim, Ryan Fox, Hideki Matsuyama, Tom Kim, Min Woo Lee, and Adam Scott. Captain's picks: Corey Conners, Nick Taylor, Nico Echavarria, Sungjae Im, Ryo Hisatsune, and Christiaan Bezuidenhout.

Matsuyama is the International team's most accomplished player on the current roster — a major champion, multiple PGA Tour winner, and one of the most consistent performers in world golf over the past decade. Scott, at 46, brings a veteran presence and a career body of work that includes the 2013 Masters. Tom Kim and Min Woo Lee give the team its most dynamic young players.

Bezuidenhout's inclusion is notable — the South African has had a strong 2026 season on the DP World Tour and brings a different profile than many of the American-tour-based players on the International roster.
