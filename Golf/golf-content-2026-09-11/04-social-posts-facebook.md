### STORY 2: LIV Golf Bankruptcy Day 3 — DIP Financing Approved

**Facebook Post 2**
Window: 12:00 PM ET

**Long Form:**
LIV Golf's bankruptcy proceedings reached their first major procedural milestone on Day 3: Judge Kaplan granted the league complex-case designation, approved joint administration, and signed off on the initial $49.6 million in debtor-in-possession financing from the Saudi Public Investment Fund.

What that means in plain terms: the bankruptcy case now has formal structure and the money needed to keep operating through the restructuring process. The PIF loan — which must be repaid before any unsecured creditors can be paid — keeps the lights on while BC Partners and the court work through what comes next.

BC Partners, the UK-based private equity firm that has emerged as LIV Golf's restructuring partner, is now the formal exit financing source under the confirmed plan. Their vision: a smaller, 10-event LIV 2.0 circuit launching in 2027, with venues in Saudi Arabia, Australia, South Africa, and Hong Kong.

For the 48 players whose contracts were automatically terminated when the Chapter 11 petition was filed, the DIP approval doesn't change their immediate situation. Jon Rahm holds an approximately $7.4 million unsecured claim. Bryson DeChambeau has approximately $5.7 million contingent. Neither number guarantees recovery — unsecured creditors typically receive cents on the dollar in proceedings with this much secured debt in front of them.

USA has won 13 of 15 editions of the Presidents Cup. International's last win came in 1998.

### STORY 4: Biltmore Championship — 6 Days Out

**Facebook Post 4**
Window: 6:00 PM ET

**Long Form:**
In six days, Asheville gets its first PGA Tour event in more than 80 years.

The Biltmore Championship tees off September 17 at The Cliffs at Walnut Cove, a Jack Nicklaus-designed course in Arden, North Carolina, set in the mountains just outside Asheville. More than 100 professional golfers will play 7,167 yards of Nicklaus design across a fall landscape that has become one of the region's signature attributes — and a setting that carries real weight for a community still rebuilding from the devastation of Tropical Storm Helene in 2024.

The event is part of the PGA Tour's FedExCup Fall swing, which operates outside the regular-season points race. For players in the field, that means low stakes from a tour perspective — but in a new market, in a unique venue, it's the kind of event where a win creates immediate visibility.

It broadcasts on Golf Channel, PGA Tour Live on ESPN+, Sirius XM, and the World Feed.
