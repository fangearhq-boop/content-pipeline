Trace Crowe Enters Austin Championship Final Round at -14, One Shot From First PGA Tour Title

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | November 15, 2026

Trace Crowe holds a one-shot lead at the Austin Championship entering Sunday's final round, with three players at -13 waiting to pounce. A wire-to-wire victory and first PGA Tour title are on the line at Fazio Canyons Course.

Crowe Stands One Round Away

AUSTIN, Texas — Trace Crowe began this week at the Austin Championship by shooting a 62. He has spent every round since protecting the lead that round gave him. On Sunday, he will attempt to finish what he started.

The 26-year-old enters the final round at Fazio Canyons Course at 14-under-par, one shot clear of Chad Ramey, Sami Valimaki, and Erik van Rooyen, all sitting at -13. Ben Kohles and Max Greyserman are two back at -12 and very much in the picture as the field tees off in moving final-round conditions — tee times were shifted earlier Sunday morning due to weather.

Crowe's path to this moment has been neither clean nor simple. His third round — a 71 — could have unraveled the week entirely. It did not, and two late birdies on Saturday saved his lead when it mattered most. The result was a scorecard that left something to be desired but a position atop the leaderboard that his opening 62 had earned.

What's at Stake

The Austin Championship carries a $6 million purse. For Crowe, the financial component is secondary to what a win represents structurally: a first PGA Tour victory brings a two-year tour exemption, a 2028 Championship Series spot, and secured playing privileges for 2027. The card-and-exemption package is as meaningful as any check the tour offers.

Wire-to-wire victories are relatively rare on the PGA Tour, and a Crowe win today would represent exactly that — control of the event from the opening tee shot to the last putt. His first round 62 set the tone. His middle rounds maintained position. The final round is the exam.

The field behind him is experienced and capable. Van Rooyen has shown elite ball-striking this fall. Ramey is a consistent Sunday performer. Valimaki has the type of game that can go low when the conditions allow. All three are one birdie from sharing the lead.

The Fazio Canyons Course at Omni Barton Creek Resort in Austin has played as a scoring venue all week, and the weather adjustment adds an element of urgency that benefits early starters. How Crowe handles that pressure — the irons, the putting surface reads, the moment itself — will determine whether the story ends with a trophy or a near-miss.

What's Next

Final-round coverage of the Austin Championship is underway today with tee times shifted earlier due to weather. If Crowe converts, he joins a small group of players who have won wire-to-wire on the PGA Tour this season. The leaderboard will be updated throughout the day as the chase at -13 plays out behind him.
