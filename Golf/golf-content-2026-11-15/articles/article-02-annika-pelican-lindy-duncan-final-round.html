Lindy Duncan Leads The ANNIKA at -13, Seeks First LPGA Win in Her 222nd Career Start

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | November 15, 2026

Lindy Duncan holds a one-shot lead entering Sunday's final round of The ANNIKA driven by Gainbridge at Pelican. The 35-year-old veteran, in her 222nd LPGA Tour start without a victory, has the lead she needs. Now she has to hold it.

Duncan Takes the Lead Into Sunday

BELLEAIR, Fla. — Lindy Duncan has been in this sport long enough to know exactly what today means. The 35-year-old enters the final round of The ANNIKA driven by Gainbridge at Pelican in Belleair, Florida, holding a one-shot lead at 13-under-par — and carrying the weight of 222 LPGA Tour starts that have never produced a victory.

That changes today, or it doesn't. There is no middle ground in a final round when the lead is yours.

Duncan shot rounds of 62, 66, and a bogey-free 69 on a cold, blustery Saturday to reach 54 holes at the top of the leaderboard. The 69 on Saturday — clean golf in conditions that pushed several competitors above par — may have been the most revealing round of the week. It showed that Duncan was not going to give this away.

Hye-Jin Choi is at -12, one back after a bogey at the 18th hole on Saturday that dropped her from a share of the lead. Yuri Yoshida is at -11, two shots back. Nelly Korda, the world's No. 1 player, is further back but cannot be counted out of anything on a Sunday with a $3.25 million purse in play.

The Event, the Purse, the Points

The ANNIKA driven by Gainbridge at Pelican is named for Annika Sorenstam, the Hall of Fame player who is widely considered the greatest female golfer in history. It carries $3.25 million in total prize money and 500 CME Global points — a significant allocation in the season-long points race. Final round coverage begins at 1:30 PM ET on Golf Channel and CNBC.

Pelican Golf Club in Belleair is a mature, tree-lined course that places a premium on accuracy. It has played as a scoring venue this week — Duncan's opening 62 made that clear — but Saturdays cold front tightened conditions considerably. Sunday's forecast is expected to be more benign, which typically favors the player chasing records rather than protecting a lead.

That is the tension Duncan will navigate over the final 18 holes. She has the lead. The field has the conditions and the motivation. The first LPGA Tour win of a 12-year professional career is one clean round away.

What's Next

The ANNIKA final round begins at 1:30 PM ET on Golf Channel and CNBC. Duncan tees off in the final pairing alongside Choi, the closest pursuer. Korda's group will also draw attention from fans watching to see if the world's top player can close a gap in the final round.
