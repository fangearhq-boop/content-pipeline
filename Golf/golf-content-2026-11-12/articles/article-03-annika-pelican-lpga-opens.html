ANNIKA at Pelican Opens: LPGA's Penultimate Event Sets the Stage for CME Qualification

By Marcus Cole | November 12, 2026

The ANNIKA driven by Gainbridge at Pelican Golf Club in Belleair, Florida opened Thursday, kicking off the second-to-last event of the 2026 LPGA season. The tournament carries significant stakes as the top 60 players in the season-long points standings after this week earn entry into the CME Group Tour Championship in Naples.

The Penultimate Push Begins

Pelican Golf Club in Belleair, Florida, has become one of the LPGA Tour's favorite autumn venues — a classic Florida layout with well-defined test of ball-striking and a finishing stretch that has produced memorable moments in the tournament's short history. On Thursday morning, the 2026 edition of the ANNIKA driven by Gainbridge opened its first round, and with it began one of the most high-stakes final stretches of the LPGA calendar.

The tournament runs November 12-15, following which the LPGA will pack up and move south to Tiburón Golf Club in Naples for the CME Group Tour Championship, the season's finale and richest event. What makes this week particularly intense is the entry criterion for Naples: only the top 60 players in the season-long LPGA points standings after Pelican qualify for the CME. For anyone on the bubble of that cutoff, every shot this week is a potential career-shaping moment.

What to Watch at Pelican

Pelican Golf Club rewards precision over power — a track where positioning off the tee matters more than distance, and where the ability to hold greens with an iron from tricky angles determines scoring. The course's design creates opportunities for the technically skilled player to separate from the field, and the LPGA's deeper, more technically diverse talent pool tends to produce fascinating leaderboards here.

The CME qualification bubble adds a layer of drama beneath the leaderboard fight. Players who already know they will be in Naples next week can take a more aggressive approach. Those on the bubble must balance risk and reward with their season-long standing in mind. Those already well-clear of the cutoff line may use Pelican to sharpen competitive form heading into the season finale.

These competing motivations create a tournament with unusual internal texture — different players essentially competing in different contests simultaneously, all on the same leaderboard.

The ANNIKA's Growing Legacy

The ANNIKA driven by Gainbridge has built its identity around Annika Sorenstam's connection to the Pelican club and the Florida golf community. Sorenstam's involvement gives the event a distinctive personality in the LPGA calendar, and the week has consistently delivered compelling storylines. The event has featured multiple past champions who used Pelican as a launching pad for strong season-ending performances.

For 2026, the tournament arrives at a moment when the LPGA season's final picture is coming into focus. Big names, CME qualification battles, and the last chance to make a statement before Naples — all of that is in play this week.

What's Next

Four rounds of golf at Pelican will determine who goes to Naples and who does not. The CME Group Tour Championship follows on November 19-22, bringing together the top 60 for the LPGA's most lucrative and prestigious conclusion. For the players teeing it up at Pelican this week, the season's final chapter starts now.
