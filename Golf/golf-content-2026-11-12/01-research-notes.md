### STORY 1: LIV Golf Wind-Down and Liquidation

**Key Facts (HIGH confidence — multiple sources confirm):**
- LIV Golf filed Chapter 11 bankruptcy on September 8, 2026
- At a November 5 bankruptcy hearing, the league's attorney stated LIV would "wind down operations" rather than proceed with LIV 2.0 restructuring
- LIV Golf filed a formal liquidation plan with a New Jersey court on November 10, 2026
- Disclosure statement hearing set for November 24
- Liquidation hearing scheduled for January 7, 2027
- PIF (Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund) withdrew funding in April 2026 — trigger for the collapse
- LIV Golf listed assets of $100M–$500M and liabilities of $500M–$1B in its September filing
- Jon Rahm is the top unsecured creditor (~$7.4-7.5M); DeChambeau, Dustin Johnson, Cameron Smith also major creditors
- Five-year run (league founded 2021/2022)

### STORY 2: PGA Tour on LIV Players

**Key Facts (HIGH confidence):**
- PGA Tour CEO stated there are no plans to welcome back LIV players
- PGA Tour has not developed a returning-member program
- Tour said it would wait for LIV bankruptcy proceedings to conclude before deciding
- DP World Tour pathway exists: top 10 earn PGA Tour cards for 2027
- Patrick Reed is reportedly No. 2 on the DP World Tour points list
- Brooks Koepka returned under a January program with a five-year forfeiture of Player Equity Program potential
