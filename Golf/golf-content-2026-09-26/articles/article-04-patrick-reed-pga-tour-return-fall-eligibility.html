Patrick Reed Cleared for PGA Tour Fall Eligibility After LIV Golf Departure

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | September 26, 2026

Patrick Reed, the 2018 Masters champion, is eligible to play PGA Tour events in the fall season after leaving LIV Golf and completing the required suspension period. Reed spent 2026 competing on the DP World Tour in Europe as part of his planned path back to the PGA Tour, and the one-year window from his last LIV appearance has now expired.

The Road Back

Reed left LIV Golf and chose a return path different from the one taken by Brooks Koepka. Koepka's 2025 reinstatement to the PGA Tour required a $5 million charitable contribution and forfeiture of five years of tour equity — terms that reflected the PGA Tour's stance on what returning LIV players must concede.

Reed's route ran through Europe. By competing on the DP World Tour and building his Race to Dubai ranking, Reed worked toward securing a European Tour card while serving out the PGA Tour's one-year suspension period from his last LIV appearance. That suspension expiration — timed to approximately August 25 — opened the door to PGA Tour fall events.

What Fall Eligibility Means

PGA Tour fall events typically serve as a proving ground for players rebuilding or establishing their standing on the tour. For Reed, competing in fall events gives him tournament rounds, ranking points, and the practical reintegration into PGA Tour competition that will make his full 2027 season arrival smoother.

The 2018 Masters champion is one of the more recognizable names in the sport, and his return carries genuine interest. Reed was a polarizing figure on and off the course throughout his LIV tenure, but results are the only currency that matters in professional golf, and he is a major winner with a demonstrated ability to perform on the biggest stages.

The LIV Landscape He Left

Reed departed LIV Golf before the tour's Chapter 11 bankruptcy filing on September 8 — a filing that revealed LIV owes its top players tens of millions of dollars in outstanding compensation. Players who remained with LIV through the collapse are now creditors in a bankruptcy proceeding. Reed avoided that situation by leaving and choosing the European Tour route.

What's Next

Reed's full PGA Tour return is expected for the 2027 season. Fall events in 2026 will provide a preview of where his game stands after the time away from the tour. Golf Fanrecap will have coverage of Reed's fall schedule as details emerge.
