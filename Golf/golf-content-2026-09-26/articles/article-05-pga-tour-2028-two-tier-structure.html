PGA Tour Announces Two-Tier Structure for 2028: Championship Series and Challenger Series

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | September 26, 2026

The PGA Tour will operate under a two-tier membership structure beginning in 2028. The Championship Series will contain the top 90 players by ranking, and the Challenger Series will house the broader tour membership. Twenty players per year will earn promotion from the Challenger tier into the Championship tier. The announcement represents the most significant structural change to PGA Tour membership in the modern era.

The Two Tiers Explained

The Championship Series is the top tier — the 90 players who will compete in the highest-profile events with the largest purses. Membership in the Championship Series is defined by ranking, which means it is not fixed and not guaranteed. Players who fall outside the top 90 drop into the Challenger Series. Players in the Challenger Series who rise into the top 90 earn their spot in the Championship tier.

Beyond that automatic ranking-based movement, 20 players per year earn promotion specifically from the Challenger Series to the Championship Series, creating a defined pathway for players who perform well in the lower tier to earn their place at the top level regardless of whether they break the top 90 on ranking alone.

Why This Structure Makes Sense

The model is familiar to sports fans in other contexts. English soccer's Premier League and Championship operate on a promotion and relegation framework. The NFL has historically used a roster cut system. Baseball uses minor leagues. Golf, until now, has operated largely on a membership system where once players earned their cards, the route to the best events ran through exemption categories and priority rankings rather than a clearly tiered structure.

The two-tier system creates sharper stakes at every level. For Challenger Series players, every week matters toward that pool of 20 promotion spots. For Championship Series players near the bubble of the top 90, maintaining ranking is a season-long priority. For the tour, the structure provides a cleaner product narrative: the Championship Series events feature the best players, full stop.

Timing and Context

The announcement comes as professional golf's broader landscape is in transition. LIV Golf's Chapter 11 bankruptcy filing in September 2026 and the uncertainty around a BC Partners restructuring deal has sharpened the PGA Tour's position as the dominant circuit in the sport. Announcing a clear, modern structure for 2028 is partly a product story — we know what our future looks like — at a moment when LIV's future is genuinely uncertain.

For players currently on the PGA Tour bubble, the 2026 and 2027 seasons now carry additional weight: they are effectively the audition period that will determine who enters the 2028 Championship Series as an established member and who starts in the Challenger tier and has to earn their way up.
