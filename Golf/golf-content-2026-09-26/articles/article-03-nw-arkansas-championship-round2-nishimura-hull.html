NW Arkansas Championship: Nishimura's -8 Opener Sets the Stage as Hull Shoots 65 in Round 2

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | September 26, 2026

Yuna Nishimura of Japan put together one of the more impressive first rounds of the LPGA Tour season this week at Pinnacle Country Club in Rogers, Arkansas. The 63 she posted — eight under par — gave her the Round 1 lead at the Walmart NW Arkansas Championship and set up what promises to be an exciting final 36 holes.

Nishimura's Round 1

The Japanese player made her move late. Nishimura's Round 1 at Pinnacle Country Club included a birdie followed by an eagle on her final holes — a sequence that turned a solid round into an exceptional one. The 63 is a low number on any course, and at a no-cut stroke play event with a $3 million purse, the Round 1 leader tends to carry target-on-back pressure into the weekend.

Charley Hull's Round 2

England's Charley Hull moved into contention with a second-round 65 on Saturday. Hull has been one of the more consistent performers on the LPGA Tour over the past several seasons, and a 65 in Round 2 puts her in a position to chase down Nishimura or anyone else ahead of her on the leaderboard.

With the final round scheduled for Sunday, the leaderboard is set to tighten. The players who post low numbers on Saturday position themselves for a Sunday chase.

Korda's Presence

The biggest name in women's golf is in the field at Rogers. Nelly Korda, the world No. 1 ranked player and the winner of both the 2026 Chevron Championship and the 2026 U.S. Women's Open, entered the NW Arkansas Championship as the player everyone will be watching on Sunday regardless of her position on the leaderboard.

Korda has been the dominant force in women's golf in 2026. A field that includes 17 Solheim Cup players and 16 major champions is strong on paper, but Korda's name generates the interest that translates into viewership.

The Course

Pinnacle Country Club in Rogers, Arkansas hosts the LPGA Tour annually for what is typically one of the fall season's most attended events. The Walmart NW Arkansas Championship draws crowds that are unusual for late-season LPGA events, and the course sets up as a low-scoring track when the weather cooperates. Eight under par led after Round 1 — a number that tells you conditions are scoreable and the winner's total is likely to be deep in double digits under par.

What's Next

Final round of the Walmart NW Arkansas Championship takes place Sunday, September 27 at Pinnacle Country Club. Golf Fanrecap will have leaderboard updates and post-round analysis from Rogers, Arkansas.
