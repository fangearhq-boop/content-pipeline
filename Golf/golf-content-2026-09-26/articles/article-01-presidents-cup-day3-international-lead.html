International Team Leads Presidents Cup 7-3 After Historic Day 2 Sweep at Medinah

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | September 26, 2026

History is being made at Medinah Country Club. The International team swept all five Day 2 foursomes matches to take a commanding 7-3 lead over the United States at the 2026 Presidents Cup — and now Saturday's dual session presents a genuine path to the team's first Presidents Cup title since 1998.

The Day 2 Sweep

Going five-for-five in foursomes is a statement. The United States entered Day 2 with momentum, but the International team answered with a comprehensive performance across all five foursomes matches. The result: a 7-3 lead that puts the Internationals in commanding position heading into the final two sessions of points play.

The last time the International team led 7-3 after two sessions of Presidents Cup play was 1998 — and that edition ended with the Internationals lifting the trophy. The parallel is not lost on anyone inside the ropes at Medinah.

What Saturday's Dual Session Means

Saturday's schedule is a dual session day: a morning four-ball round followed by afternoon foursomes, four matches per session for eight total points available. The International team needs 8.5 more points from the remaining 20 available — today's eight plus Sunday's 12 singles — to win the Presidents Cup outright.

That means the Internationals can afford a poor Saturday and still enter Sunday's singles with a chance to win. The United States, by contrast, needs to win at least six of Saturday's eight matches just to pull even. An even Saturday still leaves USA needing to dominate Sunday's 12 singles from a deficit.

The USA's Path Back

Scheffler and Burns, Thomas and Koivun, Clark and Morikawa, and Young and Gotterup headline the USA's morning four-ball pairings as the Americans attempt to arrest the slide. In the afternoon, Tom Kim and Si Woo Kim square off against Cantlay and Schauffele in one of the marquee foursomes matchups.

The USA roster is not short of talent. Scottie Scheffler remains the world's best player. But Presidents Cup history — USA leads the all-time series 13-2, winning 12 consecutive editions — has never seen a hole this deep.

The 1998 Echo

Those who remember the 1998 Presidents Cup know what is at stake. That edition, played in Melbourne, saw the International team — led by Greg Norman as captain — beat the USA on their home territory. It stands as the only clear International team victory, with a tie in 2003 the only other time the USA did not win outright.

Twenty-eight years is a long time. The players in this International team were children in 1998. But the record books are the record books, and right now the 2026 International team is following the exact same scoreboard blueprint that produced the trophy in Melbourne.

What's Next

Morning four-ball begins Saturday at Medinah No. 3. Watch the early matches to see if USA can build momentum before the afternoon foursomes.
