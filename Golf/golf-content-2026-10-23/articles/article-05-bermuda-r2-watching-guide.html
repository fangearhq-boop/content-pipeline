Bermuda Championship Round 2 Watching Guide — TV Times, Key Groups, and What Friday Tells Us

By Marcus Cole | October 23, 2026

The Butterfield Bermuda Championship's second round is underway at Port Royal Golf Course in Southampton, Bermuda. Chandler Blanchet leads at 9-under after a career-best 62 on Thursday. Four players are one shot back at 8-under. By Friday evening, the cut will be set and the weekend leaderboard will have taken its early shape.

Here is everything you need to follow Round 2.

TV and Streaming

Golf Channel: 1–4 PM ET, Friday, October 23

Golf Channel carries the live afternoon coverage of Round 2. Both morning groups will have cleared most of the course by the time coverage begins, but the featured afternoon players — including the leaders — will be well into their rounds when the broadcast starts.

Key Tee Times

Chandler Blanchet (leader, -9): 8:03 AM ET, alongside Pontus Nyholm and Jay Leng, from the No. 1 tee. Blanchet will be well into his round before Golf Channel's cameras pick up, so check the PGA Tour's live scoring app for updates through the morning.

Ben Kohles (T2, -8): 11:38 AM ET, alongside Camilo Villegas and John Parry. Kohles, who made six straight birdies in his opening-round 63, starts late enough that his round will fall within the Golf Channel afternoon window.

The Questions Round 2 Answers

Can Blanchet extend? One shot is a narrow lead. A second consecutive low round would change the conversation significantly. If Blanchet goes 67 or better again, the field faces a steep climb heading into the weekend.

Which of the four at -8 makes the first move? Kohles, Conners, Walker, and Silverman are all legitimate contenders. The one who separates from the group — even by two or three shots — becomes the weekend's chief Blanchet challenger.

Does anyone outside the top 5 go low? Joel Dahmen sits at -6, just three back. Port Royal in mild conditions can produce runs of birdies. A 63 or 64 from somewhere down the leaderboard could reshape the weekend entirely.

What does the cut line look like? Based on Round 1's scoring average of 68.5, the cut likely falls around -2 or -3 by Friday evening. Players who are currently on the bubble have everything to play for in the morning groups.

What's Next

Round 2 concludes Friday. Round 3 begins Saturday, with Round 4 (the final round) on Sunday. The champion receives their share of the $6 million purse at Port Royal on Sunday afternoon.
