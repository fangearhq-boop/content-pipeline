LIV Golf 2.0 Deadline Is Sunday — What the Roster Looks Like With 48 Hours to Decide

By Marcus Cole | October 23, 2026

The LIV Golf 2.0 player commitment deadline arrives Sunday, October 25 — 48 hours from now. The revised league, backed by a proposed $300 million investment from BC Partners, needs a viable roster by that date. What it currently has is a list of high-profile departures that makes the task look significantly harder than it did when the month started.

Who Is Leaving

The confirmed departures from LIV include some of the league's most recognizable names. Jon Rahm, who signed with LIV in 2023 in one of the sport's most publicized moves, has filed a separation agreement and intends to leave. Brooks Koepka and Patrick Reed have both announced exits. Cameron Smith, Bubba Watson, Sergio Garcia, David Puig, Byeong Hun An, and Harold Varner III have also announced their departures from the league.

That is a significant portion of the roster that built LIV's brand over the past four years. Whatever LIV 2.0 looks like, it will look different from LIV as it existed through 2026.

The Investment That Depends on the Roster

BC Partners has proposed a $300 million investment in the revised league. The money is not unconditional. The firm has indicated that the investment depends on the league assembling a roster it finds viable — and the identity of specific players, not just the raw number, forms part of that calculation.

LIV's attorneys told a bankruptcy court that a decision on whether LIV 2.0 proceeds is expected by the end of October. The October 25 deadline is the clearest external marker on that timeline.

DeChambeau: The Key Name

Bryson DeChambeau has not committed. BC Partners has identified him as the league's headline player — his popularity on YouTube and his competitive profile make him the closest thing LIV has to a marquee retention target after the wave of departures. Whether DeChambeau stays, leaves, or remains undecided past Sunday will likely be the single most important data point in the league's near-term fate.

The DP World Tour Wrinkle

The DP World Tour has issued an ultimatum: players cannot hold membership on both LIV and the DP World Tour in 2027. That constraint makes the decision harder for any player who still values access to the European Tour ecosystem. Some departing LIV players are almost certainly weighing a return to DP World Tour membership as part of their exit calculations.

What's Next

Sunday's deadline may or may not prove final — the original October 13 date was already extended once. But LIV's attorneys have framed the end of October as the decision window, and the commitment deadline is the clearest piece of that. By Sunday evening, the shape of LIV 2.0's roster — and with it, the shape of its financial future — should be considerably clearer.
