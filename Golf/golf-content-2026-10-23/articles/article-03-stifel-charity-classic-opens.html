Stifel Charity Classic Opens Today at Norwood Hills — Charles Schwab Cup Playoffs Underway

By Jake Torres | October 23, 2026

The PGA Tour Champions Charles Schwab Cup playoffs begin Friday at Norwood Hills Country Club in St. Louis, Missouri. The Stifel Charity Classic, with a $2.3 million purse and a field of 72 players who qualified through the season standings, opens what should be an intensely competitive playoff stretch.

The Field

Stewart Cink enters as the No. 1 player in Charles Schwab Cup standings. Steve Alker, who has been among the most consistent performers on the Champions Tour in recent seasons, is a significant threat. Zach Johnson, a former major champion who has brought that competitive edge to the senior circuit, is also in the field.

The 72-player field is drawn from the top of the season-long standings, meaning every player here has earned their place through consistent performance over the 2026 Champions Tour season. The playoff structure means every position matters — not just this week's result, but the cumulative points picture heading into the final events.

The Course: Norwood Hills

Norwood Hills Country Club in St. Louis, Missouri, is the host venue for the Stifel Charity Classic. The club's West Course is a classic parkland design that rewards precision off the tee and sharp iron play. For Champions Tour players who have seen every kind of layout over long careers, Norwood Hills presents a familiar style of test — but the playoff pressure makes everything feel different.

What the Last Champion Did

Thomas Bjorn won the 2025 Stifel Charity Classic in a playoff over Cameron Percy. Both players reached 12-under par before Bjorn prevailed in extra holes. The winning score of 12-under is a reasonable reference point for what will be required this year, though course conditions and weather will play their usual role.

What's Next

Round 1 of the Stifel Charity Classic is underway Friday. The early leaderboard will begin to identify who has the form to contend in St. Louis this week. Stewart Cink's standings position makes him the player to watch — a strong week here would consolidate his Schwab Cup position heading into the final playoff events.
