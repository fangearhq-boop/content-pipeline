Blanchet's 62 Headlines Bermuda Championship R1 — Four Players Chase at 8-Under as Round 2 Begins

By Ryan Calloway | October 23, 2026

Chandler Blanchet walked off Port Royal Golf Course on Thursday evening having done something he had never done in a PGA Tour round before: shoot 62. Nine under par, powered by two eagles, and backed by some of the mildest scoring conditions the Bermuda Championship has seen in recent memory.

Blanchet leads the Butterfield Bermuda Championship after Round 1 by one shot. Behind him sits a four-way tie at 8-under 63 that includes Ben Kohles, Corey Conners, Danny Walker, and Ben Silverman. Round 2 is underway Friday.

A Career-Best Round Built on Eagles

Blanchet's 62 was not a grinding accumulation of birdies. It was punctuated by two eagles that shifted the entire round. The more dramatic of the two came on the 17th hole, where Blanchet holed a long putt to give himself a cushion heading into the closing stretch. The round added up to a score that represents a career best for the tour veteran and one of the low rounds of the 2026 PGA Tour season.

The conditions helped. The tournament's scoring average through 18 holes was 68.5 — reflecting the calm winds that Bermuda does not always provide. When Port Royal's wind arrives, it is a different test entirely. For now, Blanchet made the most of a favorable opening day.

The Chasing Pack

Four players sit one shot off the lead at 8-under 63. Ben Kohles posted his round with a stretch of six consecutive birdies that drew significant attention during Golf Channel's first-round coverage. Corey Conners, the steady Canadian who has made Port Royal a consistent stop, is in the group. Danny Walker and Ben Silverman round out the quartet.

The margins are thin. The difference between the leader and the 10th or 12th player on the leaderboard is likely no more than three or four shots. Port Royal, even in calm conditions, does not allow large separations to hold for long.

What Round 2 Looks Like

Blanchet tees off in Round 2 at 8:03 AM ET from the No. 1 tee, paired with Pontus Nyholm and Jay Leng. Ben Kohles starts at 11:38 AM ET alongside Camilo Villegas and John Parry. Golf Channel carries live coverage from 1 to 4 PM ET.

The central question Friday is whether Blanchet can build on his lead or whether the tight chasing pack — all four players at 8-under — can erase the one-shot deficit in a single round. With calm conditions potentially holding, low scoring is very much still possible.

What's Next

Round 2 concludes Friday afternoon. If Blanchet holds or extends, he enters the weekend as the clear favorite. If the pack closes, the Bermuda Championship will set up as a genuinely open contest with a wide field of contenders headed into Saturday. Golf Channel's afternoon coverage will tell the story.
