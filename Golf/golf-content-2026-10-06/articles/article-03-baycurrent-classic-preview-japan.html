Baycurrent Classic Preview: PGA Tour Heads to Japan for $8 Million Asian Stop

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | October 6, 2026

The PGA Tour's annual trip to Asia begins Wednesday, and it arrives at one of the most pivotal moments of the FedExCup Fall stretch run.

The 2026 Baycurrent Classic runs October 8-11 at Yokohama Country Club (West Course) in Yokohama, Japan — the PGA Tour's only official event played in Asia. With an $8 million purse and 500 FedExCup Fall points at stake, the tournament carries real weight in the standings race that's been reshaping Tour cards all fall.

The Format and Field

The Baycurrent Classic features a 72-player field with no cut, meaning every player in the field completes all four rounds. The format rewards consistent performance throughout the week — there's no opportunity to make up ground with a hot weekend after surviving the cut.

The field includes 14 members of the JGTO (Japan Golf Tour Organization), giving the event a genuine local flavor. The co-sanctioning agreement between the PGA Tour and JGTO makes Yokohama one of the more internationally diverse fields the Tour puts together all season.

Defending Champion Returns

Xander Schauffele is back to defend the title he won last year with one of the best closing rounds of the 2025 Baycurrent Classic — a 64 that earned him a one-shot victory over Max Greyserman. That win was Schauffele's 10th PGA Tour title, adding to a career that already includes major championship hardware.

Schauffele's closing 64 was a display of controlled aggression that suits Yokohama Country Club's layout. Whether he can recreate it in a field that's gotten sharper — and with a full week of play that will test everyone's legs and concentration — is the primary question entering the week.

The FedExCup Stakes

The Baycurrent Classic is the third of six FedExCup Fall events. After Austin Smotherman's Bank of Utah Championship victory reshaped the standings on Sunday, the Tour now heads to Japan with only two events after this week — Bermuda Championship (Oct. 22) and Mexico Open (Oct. 29).

Players chasing Tour exemptions, status upgrades, or the points needed to qualify for the Championship Series have three chances left. Every position on the leaderboard at Yokohama carries consequences for the final standings.

A Unique Stop on the Calendar

The Baycurrent Classic occupies a genuinely unique position on the PGA Tour schedule. There is no other official Tour event in Asia — this is it. The relationship between the PGA Tour and Japanese golf goes back decades, and the Baycurrent sponsorship reflects the sport's continued commercial importance in Japan.

For the players, Japan offers a different kind of week: the travel logistics are significant, the time zone adjustment is real, and the atmosphere at Yokohama Country Club is unlike anything they encounter at U.S. Tour venues. Players who have competed there before know the adjustment; first-timers are learning on the fly.

What's Next

The Baycurrent Classic begins Wednesday with the first round at Yokohama Country Club. First-round coverage will be available through the PGA Tour's broadcast partners. Golf Fanrecap will track the leaderboard throughout the week.

After Japan, the FedExCup Fall moves to Bermuda (Oct. 22) and Mexico (Oct. 29) for the final two events of the calendar year.
