LIV Golf's October 13 Deadline: One Week for Players to Shape the Future

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | October 6, 2026

Professional golf has never had a week quite like the one that begins today.

Seven days from now, on October 13, the deadline arrives for LIV Golf's restructuring agreement — and with it, either the beginning of a new chapter for the breakaway tour or the collapse of its latest attempt to survive.

What the Deadline Requires

LIV Golf filed for bankruptcy protection on September 8, 2026. Under the terms agreed with lead investor BC Partners — whose involvement is led by Ted Goldthorpe — the new entity, informally referred to as LIV 2.0, would be 52.5% owned by the players who sign on.

But the deal isn't automatic. It requires "a requisite number of players" — roughly half of those with active claims against LIV — to commit by October 13. If enough players don't sign, the restructuring cannot proceed as currently structured.

That number has not been officially confirmed, and the current rate of player sign-ups is unclear. The October 13 date falls exactly 35 days after the September 8 bankruptcy filing.

The Big Names

The players at the center of this situation are the same names that defined LIV's original launch: Jon Rahm, Bryson DeChambeau, and Cam Smith lead the list of players whose decisions will carry the most weight — both practically and symbolically.

Reports indicate that during the bankruptcy proceedings, neither Rahm nor DeChambeau could be guaranteed by LIV, meaning the league could not promise their continued participation. Those are the two highest-profile names in the LIV roster. Their decisions will shape how the rest of the field responds.

The Context: A Deal That Once Fell Apart

This is not the first time professional golf has approached a crossroads of this magnitude. PGA Tour-LIV merger talks began in approximately September 2025 but collapsed before the December 31 deadline of that year.

The BC Partners restructuring is a separate path — one that would give players direct ownership stakes in the entity they compete for. The 52.5% ownership figure represents a significant departure from the original LIV model, in which players were contracted performers rather than stakeholders.

LIV's CEO stated in August 2026 that "funding secured to play beyond 2026." The bankruptcy filing six weeks later suggested that confidence was premature, or that the funding landscape shifted rapidly. Either way, the October 13 deadline is now the defining moment.

What Happens If the Deadline Passes Without Enough Signatures

If a requisite number of players do not sign by October 13, the restructuring deal as currently structured would likely not proceed. That could mean further legal proceedings, renegotiation, or the dissolution of LIV Golf as an ongoing competitive entity.

The implications for the broader professional golf landscape would be significant. Players who left PGA Tour status to join LIV gave up their Tour cards and exemptions. If LIV ceases to operate, their professional options would narrow considerably — at least in the short term.

What's Next

The October 13 deadline will produce one of the most consequential news cycles in professional golf since LIV's original announcement in 2022. Either enough players commit and restructuring proceeds, or they don't and the situation enters uncharted territory.
