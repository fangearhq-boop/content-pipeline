## Story 2: Presidents Cup

**Post (Afternoon)**

The Presidents Cup is four days away. This Thursday, September 24, competition begins at Medinah Country Club in Illinois as USA captain Brandt Snedeker faces International captain Geoff Ogilvy in the 16th edition of golf's global team match.

The USA side is loaded. Automatic qualifiers include Scottie Scheffler, Wyndham Clark, Collin Morikawa, Sam Burns, Russell Henley, and a young star from this week's PGA Tour field. Captain's picks round out a 12-player roster.

30 matches over 4 days. Five four-ball matches open on Thursday, foursomes on Friday, mixed Saturday, and 12 singles on Sunday. USA enters with a 13-1-1 all-time record.

Practice rounds at Medinah begin Tuesday, September 22. Gates open at 9 AM CDT.

## Story 3: LIV Golf Countdown

**Post (Evening)**

The LIV Golf clock is ticking. October 13 — 23 days from today — is the deadline set in the Chapter 11 restructuring agreement filed September 8 in New Jersey bankruptcy court.

Under the BC Partners deal, players holding at least two-thirds of total outstanding claim dollars AND at least half of players by headcount must sign the restructuring support agreement. If the threshold isn't reached, the deal doesn't close.

The structure: BC Partners invests $300M. Players collectively own 52.5%. PIF exits entirely. The Saudi sovereign wealth fund's involvement in professional golf — which began when LIV launched in 2022 — ends completely if this goes through.

The wildcard: Jon Rahm. He's the largest individual creditor at $7.47 million. He said on September 15 he's noncommittal and sees "opportunity" on the DP World Tour. His single decision carries more dollar weight than any other individual player.

## Story 4: Rahm DP World Tour

**Post (Evening)**

Jon Rahm's return path to European golf is now formally documented. He has reached an agreement with the DP World Tour: outstanding fines paid, a commitment to play five DP World Tour events (excluding major championships and co-sanctioned events), and his Ryder Cup eligibility has been restored.

That last point matters. The 2027 Ryder Cup will be played at Adare Manor in Ireland. Rahm is Spanish, has represented Europe his entire career, and his Ryder Cup record is part of his legacy. The DP World Tour agreement puts that path back on the table.

He still has an October 13 decision to make about LIV 2.0. The DP World Tour deal doesn't decide that question — but it means LIV 2.0 is no longer the only option that makes professional sense.

## Story 5: Koivun Sprint

**Post (Evening)**

Jackson Koivun has a week unlike almost any other in professional golf this year.

He made the cut at the Biltmore Championship and enters Round 4 today at -7, sitting eight shots behind leader Neal Shipley. He plays his final round this morning. Then, before Tuesday, he heads to Medinah Country Club in Illinois for Presidents Cup practice rounds starting September 22.

At 21 years old, Koivun is the youngest member of Brandt Snedeker's USA team — a captain's pick making his Presidents Cup debut. The jump from PGA Tour stroke play to Presidents Cup match play against an International squad happens in under 48 hours.

Snedeker picked him for a reason. Sunday and Tuesday will show whether the rookie can carry two responsibilities at once.
