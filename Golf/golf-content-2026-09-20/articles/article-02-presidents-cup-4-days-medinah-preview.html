Presidents Cup 4 Days Out — Snedeker's USA Team Heads to Medinah This Week

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | September 20, 2026

Presidents Cup / Medinah Country Club

The 2026 Presidents Cup opens in four days at Medinah Country Club in Medinah, Illinois. Competition runs September 24–27 across 30 matches — five four-ball matches on Thursday, five foursomes on Friday, four of each format on Saturday, and 12 singles on Sunday. USA captain Brandt Snedeker faces International captain Geoff Ogilvy in the 16th edition of the biennial competition.

Practice rounds begin Tuesday, September 22, with gates opening at 9 AM CDT at Medinah. The first competitive match is scheduled for 11:35 AM CDT on Thursday. This is the 16th edition of the Presidents Cup, and the USA enters with a 13-1-1 all-time record.

The USA Roster

Snedeker's 12-player side includes six automatic qualifiers and six captain's picks. The auto qualifiers — determined by the Presidents Cup points list through earlier in the summer — are Scottie Scheffler, Wyndham Young, Wyndham Clark, Russell Henley, Sam Burns, and Collin Morikawa. All six are proven Tour winners with recent form. Scheffler has been the No. 1 player in the world for extended stretches of 2026.

The captain's picks are Chris Gotterup, Xander Schauffele, Justin Thomas, Patrick Cantlay, Maverick Bridgeman, and Jackson Koivun. Schauffele, Thomas, and Cantlay bring significant Presidents Cup experience to the roster. Gotterup is one of the strongest mid-season additions Snedeker could have made. Bridgeman and Koivun are the youngest members of the side, with Koivun — who plays his final round at the Biltmore Championship today — being the youngest player on either team at 21.

Medinah Country Club

Medinah Country Club has hosted major championship golf before — most notably the 2012 Ryder Cup, one of the most dramatic in the competition's history. Europe trailed 10-6 going into Sunday singles and came back to win 14.5–13.5. The course is well-understood by American players through years of Tour presence, but the match play format under Presidents Cup conditions makes familiarity with the course only part of the preparation equation.

The layout is a test of ball-striking more than anything else — long, tree-lined, with greens that reward precision approach play. For both USA and International players, Thursday's four-ball format (where both partners play their own ball and the team uses the lower score per hole) typically produces aggressive early-week golf and high scoring. The foursomes format on Friday — alternating shots with the same ball — tests partnerships and communication in ways that four-ball does not.

The International Side

Geoff Ogilvy, the 2006 US Open champion, leads an International team that includes Adam Scott, Hideki Matsuyama, and Ryan Fox among its 12 members. The International team has won the Presidents Cup only once — in 1998 at Royal Melbourne — and has been competitive in several editions since, including the 2019 tie at Royal Melbourne, which was decided when play was abandoned in darkness and called a draw. The 13-1-1 USA record reflects a structural imbalance, but Presidents Cups have been close in recent editions.

What's Next

Practice rounds at Medinah begin Tuesday, September 22, and run through Wednesday September 23. First competitive match: Thursday, September 24, at 11:35 AM CDT. The competition runs through Sunday, September 27. Coverage on NBC, Golf Channel, and Peacock throughout the week. The pairings for Thursday's five four-ball matches will be announced before the practice rounds begin.
