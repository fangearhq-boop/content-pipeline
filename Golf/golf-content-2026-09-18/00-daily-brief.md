**Category:** FOLLOW UP
**Beat:** LIV Golf / Bankruptcy / BC Partners / Jon Rahm / Bryson DeChambeau
**Headline:** LIV's Most Important Date Is 25 Days Away — The Oct. 13 Player Commitment Deadline Explained

The Oct. 13 deadline is LIV Golf's existential test. Under the BC Partners restructuring plan, a "requisite number" of contracted players — at least two-thirds in dollar amount and half in headcount — must formally commit to LIV 2.0 for the restart to proceed. With Jon Rahm ($7.5M owed), Bryson DeChambeau, and Dustin Johnson together representing $18.7 million in player claims, their decisions will heavily influence whether that threshold is reached. Twenty-five days remain.

**Why now:** Countdown content on a hard deadline. Oct. 13 is the most significant single date in LIV Golf's future, and it's now under four weeks away.
