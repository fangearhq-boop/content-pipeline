Greyserman and Cole Share the Lead at Biltmore Championship

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | September 18, 2026

Tournament Coverage

Round 2 is underway at the Biltmore Championship in Asheville, North Carolina, with Max Greyserman and Eric Cole sharing the 36-hole lead after both fired 64s in Thursday's opening round. The co-leaders sit at 7-under par, one clear of a six-player logjam that includes Ben Kohles, Chris Kirk, Peter Malnati, Neal Shipley, Mackenzie Hughes, and Jackson Suber.

The setting alone makes this tournament unlike most events on the PGA Tour fall schedule. The Cliffs at Walnut Cove sits in the Blue Ridge Mountains with the Biltmore Estate serving as a backdrop. It is the first PGA Tour event held in western North Carolina in 84 years, and the golf world has taken notice.

Preferred Lies and Conditions

The PGA Tour has approved preferred lies for all four rounds following weather and heat conditions that affected the course prior to the tournament. That accommodation does not diminish what the leaders have accomplished — both Greyserman and Cole navigated a demanding layout and found consistent scoring — but it is a factor the field will carry through the weekend.

Greyserman has been one of the steadier players on tour in 2026. Cole brings experience and a record of performing when tournaments are close. With six players at -6, this leaderboard is not consolidating early. Friday's round will tighten the cut line and establish who has genuine weekend momentum.

An Inaugural Moment for Western NC

The Biltmore Championship is the PGA Tour's fall series opener, and its arrival in Asheville carries historical weight. Western North Carolina has not hosted professional golf at this level since the 1940s. The Cliffs at Walnut Cove is a private course with dramatic elevation changes and mountain views that separate it visually and strategically from anything else on the current tour rotation.

Tournament organizers have pointed to the region's golf culture as a reason the event belongs in this market. Whether the Biltmore Championship earns a long-term spot on the calendar will depend in part on what the next three days produce — but Round 1 has delivered exactly the kind of tight, compelling leaderboard the event needed.

What's Next

Round 2 finishes Friday, setting the 36-hole cut position. With six players separated by a single stroke at the top of the board, the cut will likely fall at -3 or better. The leaders — Greyserman and Cole — will need to separate themselves from the pack Saturday to hold control heading into Sunday at the Biltmore Estate.
