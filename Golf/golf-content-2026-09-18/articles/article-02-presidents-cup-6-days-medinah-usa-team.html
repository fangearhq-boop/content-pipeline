Presidents Cup 6 Days Away — Snedeker's USA Roster and What Medinah Means

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | September 18, 2026

Presidents Cup

Six days from today, the 2026 Presidents Cup begins at Medinah Country Club in Medinah, Illinois. USA captain Brandt Snedeker has constructed a 12-player roster that reflects how dominant American golf has been through this season, and the venue favors a team that can grind holes rather than overpower them.

Scottie Scheffler leads the USA side. He enters as the world No. 1 and the defending FedExCup champion, and his presence gives Snedeker a clear anchor for the four-ball and foursomes pairings. Cameron Young, Wyndham Clark, Russell Henley, Sam Burns, and Collin Morikawa round out the automatic qualifiers — a six-man group that could be competitive at any team event on its own.

The Captain's Picks

Snedeker added six captain's picks that supply depth and versatility. Chris Gotterup, Xander Schauffele, Justin Thomas, Patrick Cantlay, and Jacob Bridgeman bring experience in both match play and high-pressure team formats. The sixth pick, Jackson Koivun, is the youngest player on the USA roster at 21 years old. He is completing the Biltmore Championship this week and will report to Medinah for his international team debut.

The roster construction gives Snedeker options he can deploy across all four days. Four-ball, foursomes, and singles each require different pairing logic, and the USA has enough proven match play performers to handle every format without exposure.

The International Side

International captain Geoff Ogilvy brings a team that includes three major champions: Adam Scott, Hideki Matsuyama, and Ryan Fox. The 2026 Presidents Cup is the International side's chance to end a drought that has stretched since their lone victory in 1998 in Australia. The USA leads the all-time series 13-1-1.

Ogilvy's team has talent. Scott remains one of the more respected figures in professional golf globally. Matsuyama is a proven performer under pressure. The depth behind the top names will determine whether the International side can manufacture enough points to make this competitive deep into Sunday singles.

Format and TV

Competition begins Thursday, September 24, with five four-ball matches. Foursomes and four-ball alternate across Friday and Saturday before Sunday's 12 singles matches close out the event. NBC and Golf Channel handle broadcast; Peacock streams throughout. The Presidents Cup runs through Sunday, September 27.

What's Next

The week ahead at the Biltmore Championship will be one of the last competitive tune-ups for several USA team members before Medinah. Koivun is the most direct example — he plays out his tournament this weekend, then transitions to team competition within days. The Presidents Cup officially begins in six days.
