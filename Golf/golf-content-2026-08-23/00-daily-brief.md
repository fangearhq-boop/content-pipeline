### STORY 1: Clark Leads BMW Championship Final Round at -17 — Five-Shot Lead Heading Into Sunday — Tier 1
Wyndham Clark shot a 5-under 65 in Saturday's third round to build a five-shot lead at the BMW Championship at Bellerive Country Club in St. Louis. Clark stands at 17 under par heading into Sunday's final round. After a double bogey on the first hole, Clark made five birdies and an eagle in an eight-hole stretch before adding four birdies in his first five holes on the back nine. Rory McIlroy and Patrick Cantlay are tied for second at 12 under par, five shots back.

**Status:** FOLLOW UP
**Key angle:** Clark's five-shot lead heads into the final round — and this is the second time this year he has carried a 5-shot-plus lead into a final round (also had six shots at the U.S. Open, won by one)
