### Long-Form Post Window: 8:00 AM ET Wyndham Clark heads into Sunday's final round of the BMW Championship at Bellerive Country Club with a five-shot lead — and a story that tells you everything you need to know about why he might be the most dangerous closer in professional golf. 🏆 Clark shot a 5-under 65 on Saturday to reach 17 under par, five clear of Rory McIlroy and Patrick Cantlay, who are tied at 12 under. The remarkable thing about that 65 is how it started. Clark made a double bogey on the very first hole and then simply refused to let it define his round. He responded with five birdies and an eagle across an eight-hole stretch and then added four more birdies in his first five holes on the back nine. A round that began in disaster became one of the best of his week. This is not the first time Clark has carried a commanding lead into a final round in 2026. He had a six-shot advantage heading into the final round of the U.S. Open at Shinnecock Hills — and won by one. That experience of being hunted and still closing the door matters. McIlroy and Cantlay are five back. Both are capable of a Sunday charge. With the top 30 advancing to next week's Tour Championship at East Lake, the stakes extend beyond the BMW trophy — for every player on the bubble, this final round carries playoff significance. Will Clark close it out? His track record this year says yes.

### Long-Form Post Window: 3:30 PM ET Jon Rahm did not need a great week to win the LIV Golf individual season title for the third consecutive year. He needed the lead he built earlier in 2026 — and that was more than enough. Rahm clinched the 2026 LIV Golf Individual Championship at Trump National Golf Club Bedminster despite finishing tied for 41st at the LIV New York event, posting an 8-over-par total. In any other season, a performance like that would mean nothing beyond a forgotten leaderboard line. In 2026, Rahm's early-season dominance had built such an insurmountable points advantage that no result at Bedminster could knock him out. He won LIV Golf Hong Kong and LIV Golf Mexico City this season. In his first six starts of the year, he had two wins, three runner-up finishes, and a fifth — building the kind of cushion that made the eventual clinch almost inevitable. The $6 million individual championship bonus is the financial reward. The three-peat is the legacy statement. Rahm joined LIV Golf in 2024 and has not let anyone else claim the individual crown since. He was candid about the bittersweet nature of the Bedminster clinch: "I'll try to enjoy the title once I get over how bad I played this week." Three consecutive individual championships. Three $6 million bonuses. One of the most dominant stretches of consistent excellence in professional golf, whatever tour you follow.

Tour Championship Preview — East Lake Awaits the Top 30 Starting Thursday — Tier 2
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Window: 6:00 PM ET The FedExCup Playoffs reach their conclusion next week. After the dust settles at Bellerive today, 30 players will pack their bags for Atlanta and East Lake Golf Club — home of the Tour Championship, August 27-30. 🏆 The Tour Championship is unlike any other event on the PGA Tour schedule. The 30-player field is the smallest in professional golf's major championship circuit. The $40 million in total prize money makes it one of the richest. And the staggered starting-score format means that the player who arrives in the best FedExCup position starts the tournament closest to winning — creating a unique situation where every shot you hit at the BMW this week is also a shot at a head start at East Lake. Scottie Scheffler arrives as the defending Tour Championship champion, having won the 2025 event. The World No. 1 and FedExCup points leader enters East Lake as the player to beat in every sense of the phrase. Rory McIlroy is chasing a record fourth FedExCup title — having won in 2016, 2019, and 2022, no player has claimed four. A win at East Lake would put him alone at the top of that list. Past Tour Championship champions at East Lake have included Viktor Hovland (2024), Rory McIlroy (2023), and Patrick Cantlay (2022). The field that forms today will make the 2026 edition one more chapter in the history of one of American golf's most prestigious events. East Lake. The best 30 players in professional golf. See you Thursday.
