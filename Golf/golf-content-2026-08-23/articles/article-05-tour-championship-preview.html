Tour Championship Preview: East Lake Awaits the Top 30, Scheffler Defends, McIlroy Hunts History

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | August 23, 2026

The FedExCup Playoffs culminate August 27-30 at East Lake Golf Club in Atlanta, Georgia. Once the BMW Championship final round concludes at Bellerive on Sunday, 30 players will advance to the Tour Championship — the season's last event, its biggest prize, and the stage for stories that the sport will spend all winter discussing.

The Tour Championship is different from anything else on the PGA Tour schedule. The 30-player field is the smallest in major championship golf. The $40 million in total prize money is among the largest. And the staggered starting-score format means that the player who arrives in Atlanta with the best FedExCup position begins the tournament closest to the winning target — a direct conversion of regular-season excellence into competitive advantage.

Scheffler Defending, McIlroy Hunting History

Scottie Scheffler arrives at East Lake as the defending Tour Championship champion, having won the 2025 event. The World No. 1, who leads the FedExCup standings heading into Atlanta, starts the tournament in the most favorable position the staggered format offers. His 2026 season has been one of consistent excellence — strong finishes across nearly every event he has entered — even if his win total has not matched his 2024 or 2025 peaks.

Rory McIlroy carries a different motivation. He has won the FedExCup three times — in 2016, 2019, and 2022 — and a fourth would be a record. No player has won four FedExCup championships since the format was introduced. East Lake is a stage McIlroy has thrived on before, and arriving with that record in reach gives him a motivation that extends beyond the individual event.

The Historic Venue

East Lake Golf Club has served as the Tour Championship's home since the event was established and carries the kind of history that most modern golf venues do not. The par-72 course, with its tree-lined fairways, strategic hazards, and demanding Bermuda greens, presents a genuine examination of ball-striking and course management — exactly what separates the 30 best players from the rest of the field in a given season.

Past Tour Championship winners at East Lake include Viktor Hovland (2024), McIlroy (2023), and Patrick Cantlay (2022). Each of those results was shaped by the unique pressure of a small field, a massive prize, and the accumulated context of an entire season reaching its conclusion.

What's Next

The Tour Championship field locks on Sunday when the BMW Championship concludes at Bellerive. Practice rounds are scheduled for Wednesday, August 26. Competition begins Thursday, August 27 and concludes Sunday, August 30 at East Lake Golf Club in Atlanta, Georgia.
