Clark Carries Five-Shot Lead Into BMW Championship Final Round

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | August 23, 2026

Wyndham Clark made a double bogey on the first hole of Saturday's third round at Bellerive Country Club and then proceeded to shoot one of the best rounds of the week. His 5-under 65 built a five-shot lead at the BMW Championship and left Rory McIlroy and Patrick Cantlay staring at a task that looks increasingly formidable heading into Sunday's final round.

Clark stands at 17 under par, five clear of McIlroy and Cantlay, who are tied at 12 under. The third-round storyline was the recovery: after the opening double bogey, Clark made five birdies and an eagle in a stretch of eight consecutive holes, then added four birdies in his first five holes on the back nine. A round that began in disaster finished as a statement of intent from the tournament leader.

A Closer Who Has Done This Before

There is precedent for believing Clark can close this out. Earlier in the 2026 season, he carried a six-shot lead into the final round of the U.S. Open at Shinnecock Hills — and won by one. The experience of being chased, and still walking away with the trophy, is not something every player has. Clark does. That history matters in a final round where anything can happen.

McIlroy, who briefly had a share of the lead earlier in the week before Clark pulled away, is playing well enough to threaten. Cantlay, positioned alongside McIlroy at 12 under, has the game and the composure to produce a low Sunday number. Five shots is not an insurmountable margin in professional golf — but it is a genuine test for anyone chasing it.

The Tour Championship Stakes

The BMW Championship carries significance beyond the trophy. The top 30 players in FedExCup points after the final round advance to next week's Tour Championship at East Lake Golf Club in Atlanta. For bubble players deeper in the field, every shot on Sunday is worth points that determine whether their 2026 season continues or ends at Bellerive.

Clark, safely in the Tour Championship field by his points position, is focused only on closing out the BMW title. For him, Sunday is about completing the job — and his track record in 2026 suggests he is capable of doing exactly that.

What's Next

The BMW Championship final round tees off Sunday, August 23 at Bellerive Country Club in St. Louis. After the tournament concludes, the top 30 FedExCup points holders travel to East Lake Golf Club in Atlanta for the Tour Championship, August 27-30. Scottie Scheffler is the defending Tour Championship champion.
