Jon Rahm Clinches Third Consecutive LIV Golf Individual Season Title

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | August 23, 2026

Jon Rahm has done something no player in LIV Golf's history had done before: win three consecutive individual season championships. Rahm clinched the 2026 title at Trump National Golf Club Bedminster during LIV New York, locking up the crown despite finishing tied for 41st at the event — 8-over par. That result was his worst of the season. His points lead was so large it did not matter.

Rahm joined LIV Golf in 2024 and immediately claimed the individual season title. He repeated in 2025. In 2026, he made the outcome practically inevitable by the time the season reached its final stages, winning in Hong Kong and Mexico City and finishing in the top five at multiple other events. By the time the field arrived at Bedminster, the math had closed around everyone else.

Built on Early-Season Dominance

The 2026 individual title was not built in a single week or a single event. It was constructed across Rahm's first several starts of the year, when he played some of the best and most consistent golf of his LIV career. In his first six outings, he won twice and finished runner-up three more times. That combination of wins and points accumulation put him in a position where only a catastrophic late-season collapse could have cost him the title — and Rahm does not do catastrophic collapses.

He was candid about the Bedminster result: "I'll try to enjoy the title once I get over how bad I played this week." His fuller reflection on the season captured what the three-peat represented: "This year to play as good as I did early on and have such a big lead that I can even enjoy not playing good this week and next week, it feels really good."

The $6 Million Bonus and What Comes Next

The individual season championship comes with a $6 million bonus — Rahm's third time collecting it. LIV Golf has been navigating changes in its financial structure in 2026, with Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund announcing it would end its direct backing after this season and BC Partners stepping in as a new investment partner. Regardless of those institutional shifts, Rahm's on-course dominance has been the constant across all three of his seasons on the tour.

Since joining LIV Golf, Rahm has finished outside the top ten just once in 25 starts. That kind of sustained consistency across an entire schedule, against a field that includes many of the best players in the world, is the foundation of three straight individual championships.

What's Next

LIV Golf's 2026 season continues with its final event at Chatham Hills in Westfield, Indiana. Rahm, with his individual title secured, will play for the team title with his Legion XIII squad. On the PGA Tour, the FedExCup Playoffs conclude this week at the BMW Championship before the Tour Championship at East Lake August 27-30.
