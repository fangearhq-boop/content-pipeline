RSM Classic R2: Neergaard-Petersen, Thorbjornsen, Penge Share Lead at 9-Under

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | November 21, 2026

The 2026 RSM Classic is headed into the weekend as a wide-open tournament. After two rounds at Sea Island Golf Club in Georgia, three players share the 36-hole lead at 9-under par: Rasmus Neergaard-Petersen, Michael Thorbjornsen, and Marco Penge. Nine players are within two shots of the top. With 36 holes remaining in the PGA Tour's season finale, virtually any player in the top 20 can win this tournament.

Neergaard-Petersen's Bogey-Free Run

Rasmus Neergaard-Petersen has been the most consistent player through two rounds. The young European — still seeking his first PGA Tour title — has not made a bogey in 36 holes of competition, shooting 68-65 to reach the top of the leaderboard. His bogey-free play on the demanding Seaside Course is the kind of controlled performance that tournament champions tend to deliver.

At Sea Island, where the coastal conditions and fast greens can expose any weakness in a player's game, navigating two full rounds without dropping a shot is genuinely impressive. Neergaard-Petersen has combined accuracy with patience, avoiding the kind of aggressive decision-making that often unravels players on a course that punishes mistakes.

Thorbjornsen and Penge Right There

Michael Thorbjornsen drew attention with his first-round 64 on the Seaside Course — one of the year's best opening rounds at an RSM Classic — but followed it with a 69 that felt like a slight pull-back from his Thursday pace. He remains at 9-under, which is as good as anyone else in the field, and his ball-striking ability makes him dangerous heading into Saturday.

Marco Penge's 68-65 is the story of a player finding his best form at exactly the right moment. His back-nine move in Round 2 brought him level with the leaders and positions him as a genuine dark horse. Penge's swing has been sharp through two days, and his composure under pressure will be tested considerably more over the weekend.

The Pack Behind

At 8-under, a group that includes Ricky Castillo, Adrien Dumont de Chassart, Lee Hodges, and Rasmus Højgaard gives the tournament multiple additional candidates for the title. The 7-under group, featuring veterans like Harris English and Davis Riley, adds further depth to a leaderboard that refuses to separate.

This kind of compressed leaderboard at the RSM Classic is common — the dual-course format and Sea Island's specific challenges tend to keep scoring clustered — but it makes predicting a winner essentially impossible heading into Saturday.

What's Next

Third-round play at the RSM Classic begins Saturday, with the three co-leaders carrying a one-shot cushion over a large group that could make a move at any time. For Neergaard-Petersen, sustaining the bogey-free ball-striking that has defined his first two rounds will be the key. For Thorbjornsen, recapturing Thursday's precision. For Penge, maintaining the momentum that brought him to the top. Any of the three could win this — and so could a handful of players currently sitting just outside the lead.
