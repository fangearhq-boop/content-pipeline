Golf's Dual Season Finale: RSM Classic and CME Championship Both Closing This Weekend

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | November 21, 2026

Golf fans rarely get a weekend like this one. Beginning Saturday and running through Sunday, both the PGA Tour's RSM Classic at Sea Island in Georgia and the LPGA Tour's CME Group Tour Championship at Tiburón Golf Club in Naples, Florida will be in their final rounds simultaneously. Two tour seasons, two championships, two sets of careers defined — all in a 48-hour window.

The Men's Stage: Sea Island's Final Chapter

The RSM Classic enters the weekend with three players tied at the top — Rasmus Neergaard-Petersen, Michael Thorbjornsen, and Marco Penge all at 9-under par — and nine players within two shots of the lead. The compressed leaderboard means Saturday will likely shuffle the order significantly before Sunday's final round separates a champion.

Beyond the trophy, the RSM Classic is the final event in the PGA Tour's FedExCup Fall schedule. Players on the edge of the top 100 in the standings are still fighting to secure full status for 2027. The parallel tournament for Tour cards adds human stakes throughout the entire field — not just the leading group — that elevate the tension of every round at Sea Island.

The Women's Stage: Naples' Big Finish

At Tiburón Golf Club in Naples, the richest event in women's golf history is heading into its decisive phase. A Lim Kim holds a three-shot lead at 15-under par, buoyed by a Friday hole-in-one on the par-3 16th and a consistent 65-64 start. Defending champion Jeeno Thitikul trails by three. World No. 1 Nelly Korda is six shots back and looking to charge.

The $11 million purse — with $4 million going to the champion — means the financial stakes of the CME Group Tour Championship exceed anything else on the LPGA Tour's calendar. The player who wins Sunday in Naples will close the year as the richest and arguably the most celebrated figure in women's golf for 2026.

A Weekend for All Golf Fans

The simultaneous scheduling of the PGA Tour and LPGA season finales creates an unusual opportunity for golf fans who follow both circuits. The parallel narratives — a wide-open men's field chasing a final title and a women's leader defending a three-shot advantage against a world-class pursuer — give the weekend a layered richness that single-tour weekends rarely provide.

Both tournaments have compelling stories at multiple levels: trophy races, career milestones, Tour status implications, defending champions, and world No. 1 players chasing history. This is as good as it gets on the golf calendar.

What's Next

Third-round play begins Saturday at both venues. By Sunday evening, two new champions will be crowned — or existing ones will defend. The RSM Classic and CME Group Tour Championship finish 2026's professional golf calendar on a high, with stories worthy of the curtain-call moment they represent. Tune in to both. The weekend will reward the attention.
