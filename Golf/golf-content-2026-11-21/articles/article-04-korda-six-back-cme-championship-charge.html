Korda Six Back With 36 Holes Left — Can the World No. 1 Make a CME Championship Run?

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | November 21, 2026

Nelly Korda stands at 9-under par and T-11 after two rounds of the CME Group Tour Championship. She is six shots behind leader A Lim Kim heading into the weekend. The world No. 1 is behind, but this is Nelly Korda — a multiple major champion whose ballstriking, competitive temperament, and experience in the sport's biggest moments make her impossible to dismiss, even from a position that would end most other players' realistic title hopes.

The Math of a Comeback

Six shots in 36 holes is substantial, but it is not impossible. Golf tournaments have been won by players who trailed by more entering the final two rounds, particularly at Tiburón Golf Club in Naples, Florida, where the warm Florida conditions and receptive greens can create scoring opportunities for a player in form.

What Korda would need is a third-round charge — something in the low-to-mid 60s that brings her within two or three shots of Kim heading into Sunday. If that happens, the dynamics of the final round change entirely. A player of Korda's quality, within striking distance, with a major championship pedigree and the highest world ranking in the sport, represents an entirely different pressure on the leader than a mid-field challenger.

Korda's 2026 Season in Context

Korda has had a consistent and productive 2026 season. Multiple wins and steady Tour performance have maintained her world No. 1 status entering Naples. This CME event represents the final opportunity of the year to add another statement result to her 2026 resume — not just for the $4 million winner's check, but for the competitive satisfaction of closing a strong season with a championship.

She didn't play her best golf through two rounds, sitting T-11 rather than near the top. But Korda's ability to find another gear when the moment demands it has been one of the defining features of her career. If the competitive instinct fires over Saturday and Sunday, she has the tools to make something extraordinary happen at Tiburón.

The Realistic Scenario

The honest assessment is that Korda's deficit is significant enough that Kim would need to have a difficult third round for the gap to close within range. But golf is played on courses, not spreadsheets, and anything can happen on 36 holes. A Korda top-five finish is the most probable outcome — which would still represent a strong close to 2026. A Korda title would be the week's greatest narrative achievement.

For now, the story at the top of the CME Group Tour Championship belongs to A Lim Kim. Korda's role is as the pursuer — the world's best player chasing the leader down in the richest event of the season. That, too, is a story worth watching.

What's Next

Korda will need to shoot 65 or better on Saturday to stay in meaningful contention at the CME Group Tour Championship. If she can do that and Kim remains flat or struggles, Sunday could be a finale worth the full attention of the golf world. The world No. 1 entering the final round within striking distance of a major title is exactly the kind of setup that LPGA season finales are made for.
