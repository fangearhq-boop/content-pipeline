Jon Rahm's LIV Exit Path — Nov. 5 Hearing, 12-Month Suspension Ahead
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Jon Rahm is leaving LIV Golf — but getting to wherever he goes next is going to take time. Rahm's lawyer confirmed at a U.S. bankruptcy court hearing on October 7 that he will not participate in LIV 2.0. The two sides are now working toward a consensual separation agreement, with a court hearing scheduled for November 5 to approve it. After that, the math gets complicated. The PGA Tour currently requires former LIV players to serve a 12-month suspension before returning to compete. If that clock starts after the November court hearing, Rahm's earliest realistic return would be in late 2027 — and a full 2027 PGA Tour season would be off the table entirely. The DP World Tour adds another layer. With its November 16 membership deadline looming, Rahm's path back to European events — including Ryder Cup eligibility — is also uncertain. For a former world No. 1 who won the Masters and US Open in 2023, the road back to the tour he left is proving longer than many expected.
