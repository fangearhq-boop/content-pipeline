Butterfield Bermuda Championship Preview: Port Royal, Wind, and a $6 Million Bubble Test

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | October 17, 2026

PGA Tour | Butterfield Bermuda Championship | FedExCup | Port Royal

Five days from now, the PGA Tour will arrive at Port Royal Golf Course in Southampton Parish, Bermuda, for the Butterfield Bermuda Championship — one of the final opportunities in the FedExCup Fall for players fighting to secure their status heading into 2027.

What Port Royal Demands

Port Royal is the shortest course on the PGA Tour at 6,828 yards, playing to a par of 71. On paper, that reads as a birdie parade waiting to happen. In practice, what happens depends almost entirely on the wind.

Bermuda's ocean winds can turn a straightforward-looking layout into one of the most demanding tests on Tour. Past champions have posted scores in the range of 19 to 20 under in calm conditions; 12 under can win when the wind picks up. Adam Schenk won the 2025 edition at 12 under. Rafael Campos took the 2024 title.

The course rewards accuracy over length, and players who can manufacture birdies around the greens tend to thrive here. Scrambling ability matters. So does comfort with awkward stances and uneven lies — Port Royal has some elevation changes that look mild on a TV broadcast but play differently underfoot.

The FedExCup Stakes

The Bermuda Championship offers $6 million in prize money and 500 FedExCup points — a significant total for a Fall event. With the top 60 players in FedExCup points at the end of the Fall earning full exemptions for the 2027 season, the bubble math is straightforward: a strong week at Bermuda can move a player from the edge of the cut line to comfort, or from comfort to somewhere near the top.

For players farther down the list, a Bermuda win changes everything. It can vault a player from a conditional exemption into full status almost overnight. That creates the kind of motivated, competitive field that makes bubble-chasing events compelling to watch even when the leaderboard doesn't feature the biggest names in golf.

What's Next

The Butterfield Bermuda Championship runs October 22–25 at Port Royal Golf Course. The field and tee times will be finalized in the coming days. For fans of PGA Tour golf who want to see players competing with real stakes on a unique and beautiful setting, this is one of the easier decisions on the fall calendar.
