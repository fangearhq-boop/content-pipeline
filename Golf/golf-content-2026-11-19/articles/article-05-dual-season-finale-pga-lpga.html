Two Tours, One Weekend: The PGA and LPGA Close Out 2026 at the Same Time

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | November 19, 2026

The golf calendar is delivering something unusual this week. From Thursday to Sunday, November 19 to 22, both the PGA Tour and the LPGA Tour are playing the final events of their respective 2026 seasons simultaneously. The RSM Classic is finishing the men's tour year at Sea Island in Georgia. The CME Group Tour Championship is finishing the women's tour year at Tiburón Golf Club in Naples, Florida. Two seasons. Two champions. One weekend.

It does not happen often that both tours schedule their season finales on the same dates, and when it does, the result is a rare stretch of days when professional golf fans can toggle between two compelling finales rather than waiting for one to finish before the next begins.

What Each Tour Has at Stake

At Sea Island, the RSM Classic offers a $7.4 million purse and the closing chapter of the PGA Tour's FedEx Cup Fall schedule. With Scottie Scheffler already the season's dominant figure, the final event carries meaning for the players chasing it — card seekers, emerging contenders, and a defending champion in Sami Välimäki who wants to show his 2025 win was not a one-off.

At Tiburón, the CME Group Tour Championship offers $11 million and a $4 million winner's check — the richest prize in women's golf. Atthaya Thitikul defends a title she won at -26. Nelly Korda, the world's No. 1 player, is in pursuit. The standard of competition is as high as it gets anywhere in the game.

The Geography of the Week

Georgia and Florida are geographically close — close enough that the two tournaments share broadcast windows without the kind of scheduling conflict that leaves one invisible. Golf fans on the East Coast can follow both. The coastal Georgia setting of Sea Island and the Florida subtropical setup at Tiburón offer two distinct visual and atmospheric environments for the same week of sport.

After Sunday, the Quiet Begins

When the final putt drops at each venue on Sunday, the professional golf offseason begins for both tours. Players from Sea Island and Naples will depart for whatever comes next — family, preparation, rest — until the 2027 season kicks off in January. For now, through four rounds at two different courses in two different states, 2026 gets its full send-off.
