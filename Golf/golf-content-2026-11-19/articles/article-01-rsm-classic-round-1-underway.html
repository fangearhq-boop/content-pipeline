RSM Classic Round 1 Is Live: Who Steps Up as the Season Finale Opens at Sea Island

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | November 19, 2026

The PGA Tour's final event of the 2026 season is officially underway. The RSM Classic opened Round 1 on Thursday at Sea Island Golf Club in St. Simons Island, Georgia, spreading players across the Plantation and Seaside courses for the opening two rounds. What follows is a $7.4 million weekend without an obvious favorite, and that is precisely what makes it worth watching.

Scottie Scheffler is not here. He clinched the FedEx Cup title weeks ago and has nothing remaining to gain from participating. Without the No. 1 player in the world, the RSM Classic becomes something rarer: a wide-open competition where any of several dozen players has a genuine path to the trophy.

Välimäki Returns to Defend

Sami Välimäki arrives as the defending champion after winning the 2025 edition at -23 to par. The Finnish player's victory last year earned him a place in this week's field on merit, and he will be eager to demonstrate that the title was no fluke. Defending successfully at a PGA Tour event is one of the sport's more difficult tasks — different conditions, elevated expectations, and a field that has had a full year to study what won.

Whether Välimäki can replicate the form that carried him to -23 in 2025 will be one of the story threads running through the week. His comfort with the Georgia coastal conditions will be an asset.

The Favorites Board

Ben James enters as the early leader of the futures market at +1950, followed closely by Michael Brennan (+2050) and Michael Thorbjornsen (+2000). None of those names carries the marquee recognition of a Scheffler or a Rory McIlroy, but that is part of the RSM Classic's consistent appeal — it surfaces emerging talent and determined veterans in equal measure.

Also worth monitoring: Blades Brown, who carried significant momentum into Sea Island after winning the World Wide Technology Championship earlier this month. Brown's recent form suggests a player in a confident groove, and that matters on a course where steady driving and iron precision are rewarded.

Field Depth and Tournament Stakes

Thirty-five players ranked in the world's top 100 have made the trip to Georgia. For some, the RSM Classic is a final opportunity to secure a PGA Tour card for the 2027 season. The FedEx Cup title is gone — Scheffler claimed it earlier this fall — but card-securing finishes remain available, and that pressure often produces committed, aggressive golf from players on the fringe.

The cut format involves both the Plantation and Seaside courses for Rounds 1 and 2, with the top 65 plus ties advancing to the weekend on the Seaside Course exclusively. For those who make it, Sunday afternoon at Sea Island carries real weight.

What's Next

Round 1 continues through Thursday. Early leaderboard action will begin establishing which players have acclimated to the coastal Georgia conditions.
