LIV Golf's $300M Lifeline: BC Partners Deal Would Make Players Majority Owners — With a Mid-October Deadline

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | September 24, 2026

LIV Golf filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy on September 8 with liabilities estimated between $500 million and $1 billion. Now the outline of a potential rescue is in view — and it depends on the players.

London-based private equity firm BC Partners has proposed a $300 million investment to bring LIV Golf through restructuring and re-emerge as a reorganized entity in early 2027. The centerpiece of the deal: under the BC Partners plan, players would own the majority of the restructured tour.

The Deadline That Could Decide Everything

The BC Partners deal is conditional on player commitment. Under the terms, a requisite number of players — specifically, holders representing at least two-thirds of the total eligible player claims by dollar amount and at least one-half by number of players — must formally commit to the restructured tour within 35 days of the September 8 bankruptcy filing.

That deadline falls in mid-October. Approximately 19 days remain. If enough players commit, the $300 million deal moves forward. If the threshold isn't met, the deal dissolves and LIV Golf faces a very different outcome.

What Players Would Be Joining

The PGA Tour has explicitly closed the door on returning LIV members. CEO Brian Rolapp confirmed in mid-September that a returning member programme is "not currently contemplated." The January 2026 window — limited to major and Players Championship winners from 2022-2025 — produced exactly one taker: Brooks Koepka, who paid $5 million to charity and forfeited five years of equity to secure his return.

Jon Rahm, Bryson DeChambeau, and Cameron Smith each declined. Now the LIV players most likely to weigh the BC Partners commitment must evaluate what the restructured tour actually represents: a player-owned organization with institutional backing, potential 2027 season continuity, and a cleaner financial foundation than the Saudi-funded model that collapsed.

What's Next

The restructuring hearing date falls around October 13. The mid-October player commitment deadline overlaps with that process. What LIV Golf looks like in 2027 — if it exists at all — will be determined by what happens in the next few weeks. Watch for public statements from top LIV players as the deadline approaches.
