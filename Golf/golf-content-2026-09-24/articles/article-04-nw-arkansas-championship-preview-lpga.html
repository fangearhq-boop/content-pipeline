NW Arkansas Championship Preview: LPGA's Post-Solheim Reset Begins Tomorrow in Rogers

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | September 24, 2026

The LPGA Tour's first event since the Solheim Cup begins Friday at Pinnacle Country Club in Rogers, Arkansas — and everything about the 2026 Walmart NW Arkansas Championship will carry the weight of what happened two weeks ago.

The United States lost the Solheim Cup 13-15 to Europe. It was a defeat that stung, particularly at home, and particularly for a team that included multiple world-ranked players. Starting tomorrow, the LPGA Tour gets back to individual competition — and for the American Solheim players, that means the first chance to respond.

The Field

The tournament features 16 major champions and a strong international presence. European Solheim Cup standouts including Charley Hull, Linn Grant, Leona Maguire, Celine Boutier, Carlota Ciganda, Nanna Koerstz Madsen, Julia Lopez Ramirez, Nastasia Nadaud, and Lottie Woad are all competing. They arrive in Rogers with Solheim momentum behind them.

Eight American Solheim Cup players are also in the field: Lauren Coughlin, Megan Khang, Auston Kim, Jennifer Kupcho, Andrea Lee, Yealimi Noh, Angel Yin, and Rose Zhang. For all of them, this week is about returning to individual focus after the team competition.

The field also carries a strong Arkansas connection, with several current and former Razorbacks in the lineup including Brooke Matthews, Gaby Lopez, Maria José Marin, Maria Fassi, and Reagan Zibilski.

The Format and Stakes

The Walmart NW Arkansas Championship is a 54-hole stroke play event with a $3 million purse. Three rounds over three days at a course the LPGA Tour knows well after years of competing here. The shorter format means there is no room for a slow start — every round matters immediately.

What's Next

Round 1 begins Friday morning at Pinnacle Country Club. The combination of Solheim returnees — motivated on both sides — and a $3 million prize fund makes this one of the more charged early-round atmospheres of the LPGA calendar year. Watch for which Solheim players carry their form forward and which ones use the week to reset.
