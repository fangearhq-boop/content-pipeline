Presidents Cup 2026 Full Rosters Revealed: Three USA Debutants, Medinah Set for Sept. 24-27

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | September 8, 2026

USA captain Brandt Snedeker and International captain Geoff Ogilvy announced their six captain's picks each on September 1st. Here are both complete 12-player rosters and what to watch at Medinah Country Club.

The 2026 Presidents Cup is 16 days away, and both rosters are set.

Brandt Snedeker's Team USA and Geoff Ogilvy's International Team are locked in for Medinah Country Club in Medinah, Illinois — one of the most celebrated venues in American golf history, having hosted two PGA Championships and a Ryder Cup. The event runs September 24-27, the 16th edition of a competition the United States has dominated throughout its history.

Team USA — Full Roster

The six automatic qualifiers, determined by cumulative FedExCup points through the end of the 2026 season, are Scottie Scheffler (world No. 1 and FedExCup champion), Cameron Young, Wyndham Clark, Russell Henley, Sam Burns, and Collin Morikawa. That group represents one of the deepest collections of automatic qualifiers the United States has assembled in the competition's history — every player has won at least once on tour in recent seasons, and Scheffler's profile elevates the entire lineup.

Snedeker's six captain's picks are Patrick Cantlay, Xander Schauffele, and Justin Thomas — three experienced team competition veterans with Presidents Cup and Ryder Cup pedigree — alongside three first-time Presidents Cup players: Jacob Bridgeman, Chris Gotterup, and 21-year-old Jackson Koivun.

Koivun is the name to watch among the debutants. He turned professional earlier this year after leaving Auburn University, and Snedeker's decision to bring him to Medinah just months into his professional career signals genuine confidence in the young Floridian's ability to handle the pressure of international team competition. Bridgeman and Gotterup bring different profiles — Bridgeman with the steady ball-striking that plays well in alternate-shot formats, Gotterup with the aggressive, attacking style that can elevate a team's energy.

International Team — Full Roster

Geoff Ogilvy's International side features six automatic qualifiers: Si Woo Kim, Hideki Matsuyama, Ryan Fox, Tom Kim, Min Woo Lee, and Adam Scott. The group carries the International team's best recent competition results on its back — Matsuyama and the Kim duo have been among the most consistent performers on the International side in recent Presidents Cups.

Ogilvy's six picks: Corey Conners (Canada's steadiest presence in international competitions), Sungjae Im, Ryo Hisatsune, Nico Echavarria, Nick Taylor, and Christiaan Bezuidenhout of South Africa. The group represents a genuinely global lineup — players from South Korea, Japan, Canada, Colombia, South Africa, and New Zealand giving the International team a breadth of competitive backgrounds.

Matsuyama remains the anchor and the player the USA will plan around. His foursomes record in Presidents Cup competition is the best on the International side historically, and Ogilvy will almost certainly deploy him as an anchor in the draw when it matters most.

History and Expectations

The Presidents Cup's competitive history is notably lopsided: the United States has won 11 of the 15 editions played to date. The International team's sole victory came in 1998 at Royal Melbourne, and their best modern-era challenge has generally come when the competition is hosted outside the United States.

Medinah is not a neutral venue for the International side. The Illinois club has a deep history as a USA stronghold, and the home crowd will add to the familiar advantage the American team typically enjoys when playing on domestic soil. But Ogilvy's roster is among the more cohesive International teams in recent years, and the depth in both the Korean and Australian contingents gives the side legitimate chances across all three match formats.
