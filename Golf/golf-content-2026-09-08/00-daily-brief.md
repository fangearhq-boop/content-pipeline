### STORY 2: LIV Golf Bankruptcy Imminent
Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (PIF) is pulling its multi-billion-dollar investment from LIV Golf after the 2026 season. Reports indicate LIV is preparing a US bankruptcy filing that could come as early as next week (late September). The league has already sent settlement offers to players owed guaranteed money — at pennies on the dollar. Staff layoffs have hit the majority of LIV's 300+ employees. The PIF reportedly invested $5-8 billion over four years. Jon Rahm, Bryson DeChambeau, Cameron Smith, and others face significant financial exposure.

### STORY 4: LIV 2.0 — Last-Ditch Survival Plan
LIV Golf CEO Scott O'Neil is working on a deal worth $250-350 million to create "LIV 2.0" — a scaled-down, restructured version of the league funded by private equity rather than the Saudi sovereign wealth fund. The plan involves converting some player contracts into equity stakes and reducing the playing schedule. O'Neil reportedly close to completing the deal. If successful, LIV 2.0 would support the league through at least 2027. BC Partners cited as a potential investor.
