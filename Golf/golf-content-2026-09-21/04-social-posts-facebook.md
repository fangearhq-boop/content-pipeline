**FB Long-Form** [10:30 AM ET]

Medinah has hosted three Ryder Cups (1991, 1999, and 2012), and every time the world's best players arrive, the course delivers drama. Its tree-lined fairways, immaculate greens, and tournament-hardened gallery infrastructure are built for exactly this kind of event.

The U.S. team, captained by Brandt Snedeker, enters as the heavy favourite. Koivun is a captain's pick — Snedeker saw something worth betting on in the young talent's composure and recent form. How he performs in his first major team event could define his next chapter.

The International team, led by captain Geoff Ogilvy, features Si Woo Kim, Hideki Matsuyama, Adam Scott, and Ryan Fox — a collection of players who are motivated to snap America's dominance in this event.

Competition begins Thursday. NBC and Golf Channel have the coverage. This one's going to be worth watching.

### STORY 3: LIV Golf Deadline

**FB Long-Form** [1:00 PM ET]
LIV Golf's survival is in the hands of its own players.

On September 8, 2026, the tour filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection — a move that shocked but perhaps did not surprise the golf world after years of financial uncertainty and pressure from the PGA Tour. LIV's assets were estimated at between $100 million and $500 million, its liabilities between $500 million and $1 billion. The math was not sustainable.

BC Partners, a London-based private equity firm, stepped in with a proposed $300 million investment to serve as the restructuring lifeline. But there's a condition: players have to commit.

Under the terms of the Restructuring Support Agreement, "a requisite number of players" — specifically, two-thirds of eligible player claims by dollar amount and one-half of players by number — must agree to commit to the restructured tour within 35 days of the September 8 petition date. That deadline is approximately October 13.

If the players commit, the new LIV emerges as a majority player-owned entity, with BC Partners receiving a 2% revenue share for seven years after profitability and the right to purchase an expansion team for $1 after two more teams are added. The tour is expected to re-emerge in early 2027 with four confirmed events across Saudi Arabia, Australia, South Africa, and Hong Kong.

If the players don't commit? The restructuring fails, and LIV Golf's future becomes deeply uncertain.

Twenty-two days remain. The players who took LIV's money now hold its fate.
