### STORY 3: LIV Golf Restructuring

**Verified Facts (HIGH):**
- LIV Golf filed Chapter 11 bankruptcy: September 8, 2026
- Assets: $100M-$500M; liabilities: $500M-$1B
- BC Partners (London PE firm) investing $300M in new financing
- 35-day player commitment deadline from petition date = approximately October 13, 2026
- Requirement: 2/3 in dollar amount of eligible player claims + 1/2 in number of players must commit
- Restructured entity: majority owned by players
- PIF providing $49.6M DIP financing (court approval pending)
- CEO: Scott O'Neil
- Four 2027 events announced: Saudi Arabia, Australia, South Africa, Hong Kong
- Jon Rahm owed $100M+; declined PGA Tour return offer
- PIF estimated total investment in LIV: ~$6B
- BC Partners receive: right to buy expansion team for $1 after 2 more teams added; 2% of all revenue for 7 years after profitability
- Expected re-emergence: early 2027

**Sources:** Sportico (2 articles), LIV Golf official, Wikipedia LIV Golf
