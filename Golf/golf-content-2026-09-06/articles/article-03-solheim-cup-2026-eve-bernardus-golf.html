The Solheim Cup Begins Tomorrow — What to Know Before Practice Rounds Open at Bernardus Golf

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | September 6, 2026

Tomorrow morning, two teams of 12 golfers will begin the week that women's golf has circled on its calendar for two years.

The 2026 Solheim Cup opens its practice week Monday at Bernardus Golf in Cromvoirt, Netherlands — the first Solheim Cup ever played on Dutch soil. The PING Junior Solheim Cup also begins Monday, with young players from across Europe and the United States competing on the same grounds. For the main event, match play competition runs September 11 through September 13, with foursomes and fourballs on Friday and Saturday, and singles on Sunday.

Before the matches begin, here is everything you need to know.

The Teams

Team USA is captained by Angela Stanford, who has assembled a squad built around world No. 1 Nelly Korda. The automatic qualifiers include Yin, Kupcho, Noh, Auston Kim, Coughlin, Corpuz, Andrea Lee, and Alison Lee. Stanford's three captain's picks: Megan Khang, Rose Zhang, and Lindy Duncan.

Team Europe is captained by Anna Nordqvist, who has spoken about her pairings decisions as a challenging "puzzle." Europe's roster includes proven match play veterans such as Charley Hull and Leona Maguire alongside some of the tour's rising names.

The United States are the defending champions, having won the 2024 Solheim Cup 15.5-12.5 in Virginia. Europe had won three consecutive Solheim Cups before that. The momentum belongs to the Americans entering this week — but Nordqvist's squad will be competing on a continent that feels like home.

The Venue

Bernardus Golf in Cromvoirt, North Brabant, is a relatively young course by European standards — opened in 2007 — but it has been developed specifically to host elite competition. The course features tree-lined fairways, precise approach demands, and greens that reward the kind of technical short-game precision that both teams possess in abundance.

A Dutch Solheim Cup is a first in the event's history. Crowd support will likely skew toward Europe, with Dutch fans joining fans from across the continent to cheer on Nordqvist's team. The Americans will need to handle the atmosphere as part of the challenge.

The Format

The Solheim Cup uses a match play format with 28 total points available. A team needs 14.5 points to win outright, or 14 points to retain the cup as the defending champion. Friday features four foursomes matches, followed by four fourball matches. Saturday repeats the same structure. Sunday's singles consist of 12 matches — one for each player on both teams — with every match counting.

Captain's pairings decisions on Thursday will set the tone for the weekend. Stanford and Nordqvist will each reveal their foursomes and fourballs lineups in the days ahead — and those choices may be as consequential as any individual round.

What's Next

Practice rounds begin Monday morning. Teams will spend the next four days learning Bernardus Golf, preparing their pairings, and building the chemistry that match play demands. Competition starts Friday, September 11. The Solheim Cup trophy will have a new home by Sunday evening — either staying with the United States or returning to European hands for the first time since 2022. After months of buildup, the week has finally arrived.
