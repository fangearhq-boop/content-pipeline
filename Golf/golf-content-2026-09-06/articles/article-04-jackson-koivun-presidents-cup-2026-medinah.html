Jackson Koivun: Three Months from Auburn to the Presidents Cup at Medinah

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | September 6, 2026

In June 2026, Jackson Koivun was a college golfer at Auburn University.

On September 24, he will tee it up for the United States at the Presidents Cup at Medinah Country Club — one of the most storied venues in American golf — representing his country in a biennial team event that has featured the best players on the planet for more than three decades.

He is 21 years old. He has played three professional events.

The 3M Open Changed Everything

Koivun turned professional in early summer and made his mark before most players his age had even earned a tour card. In July, in just his third professional start, he entered the 3M Open and dismantled one of the best fields of the summer.

His final score: 25-under 259 — a new tournament record. He defeated world No. 1 Scottie Scheffler by three strokes. The professional golf world, which had barely had time to learn his name, suddenly knew exactly who he was.

USA Presidents Cup captain Brandt Snedeker was watching. When Snedeker announced his six captain's picks on September 1, Koivun's name was on the list. Snedeker had one explanation.

"I just have a gut feeling," Snedeker said.

The History He's About to Make

Koivun is the second-youngest American ever selected for a Presidents Cup. Only Jordan Spieth, who was 20 years old when he made his debut in 2013, was younger. Spieth went on to win three major championships and become one of the defining players of his generation. Koivun is not Spieth — he is his own player, with his own game — but the comparison is not unfair in terms of the moment each arrived at.

At 21, with fewer than five professional starts to his name, Koivun will walk into Medinah Country Club No. 3 representing the United States alongside players like Scottie Scheffler, Collin Morikawa, and Xander Schauffele — golfers who have won major championships and spent years at the top of the world rankings. The gap in experience is vast. The gap in ability may be narrower than anyone expected.

What's Next

The 2026 Presidents Cup runs September 24-27 at Medinah Country Club in Medinah, Illinois. International captain Geoff Ogilvy will lead his squad against Snedeker's Americans. For most of the team, it is a familiar stage. For Jackson Koivun, it is an introduction — to Presidents Cup golf, to team competition at the elite level, and to the expectations that come with winning a major professional title at age 21 in just your third start. Three months ago, he was in college. Now comes Medinah.
