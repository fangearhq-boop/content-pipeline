Jon Rahm Is a Free Agent: Where Does He Play in 2027?

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap

October 15, 2026

Jon Rahm is done with LIV Golf.

The former world No. 1 declared in court proceedings on October 7 that he will not participate in LIV 2.0. His attorneys left no ambiguity: they called the restructuring terms "unacceptable." After three seasons with the breakaway league, Rahm is walking away before the bankruptcy plays out — and now every tour in professional golf is watching where he lands.

The DP World Tour Commitment

The one confirmed piece of Rahm's 2026-27 schedule is the Abu Dhabi Championship, a DP World Tour event scheduled for this fall. It's a marquee event on the European circuit and a logical first stop for a player re-establishing his credentials outside of LIV.

The DP World Tour made its position on LIV 2.0 clear: players who sign with the restructured tour will not be permitted to compete in DP World Tour events in 2027. Rahm's refusal to join LIV 2.0 keeps that door open for him.

The PGA Tour Question

Whether Rahm returns to the PGA Tour in 2027 is genuinely contested. Two credible outlets have published contradictory reports.

Golfmagic — citing sources familiar with the negotiations — reported that Rahm has agreed terms for a PGA Tour return in 2027, with a condition: a roughly $25 million payment to charity as part of his reinstatement agreement. That report has not been independently confirmed and should be treated as unverified.

Golf.com, meanwhile, reported that an immediate PGA Tour return is "impossible" under the tour's current suspension rules governing players who left for LIV Golf. Their analysis suggests the DP World Tour is Rahm's realistic 2027 home, with PGA Tour reinstatement a longer-term project — likely 2028 at the earliest.

The truth is likely somewhere between the two. Rahm has the leverage of being one of the sport's elite players; the PGA Tour has the interest in recapturing a marquee name. Whether the $25 million figure is real, negotiated, or inflated by a leaky negotiating room is unknown.

The Ripple Effect

Rahm's exit from LIV Golf matters beyond his own career. He is one of the highest-profile names to publicly reject LIV 2.0, and his decision may influence how other players on the fence evaluate their options before the October 25 deadline.

At the same time, his free agency reintroduces a genuine draw to the DP World Tour schedule. Events that can secure a tee time from a former world No. 1 in the prime of his career benefit enormously — and the Abu Dhabi Championship is already the beneficiary.

What's Next

Rahm's next confirmed competitive appearance is the Abu Dhabi Championship on the DP World Tour this fall. Between now and then, the October 25 LIV Golf player deadline will either produce a restructured tour or accelerate the bankruptcy's conclusion — and either outcome shapes Rahm's negotiating position for 2027. Watch for confirmed reporting on the PGA Tour reinstatement terms as the bankruptcy proceedings conclude. What's verifiable right now: Rahm is available, he's motivated, and he's one of the best players in the world. That's a compelling free agent regardless of which tour gets him.
