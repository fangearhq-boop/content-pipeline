Buick LPGA Shanghai Opens Today: Thitikul Defends With Four 2026 Wins Behind Her

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap

October 15, 2026

The Buick LPGA Shanghai tees off today — and the defending champion arrives in the best form of her career.

Atthaya "Jeeno" Thitikul enters the tournament at No. 1 in the world and coming off one of the most dramatic closing stretches of the 2026 LPGA season. Last week at the LOTTE Championship in Hawaii, she hit the water on the par-4 16th hole — and then birdied 17 and 18 back-to-back under maximum pressure to seal a three-stroke victory over Ina Yoon of South Korea. The final score: 21-under 267.

It was her fourth LPGA Tour title of 2026, drawing her level with Nelly Korda for the season lead.

A Season for the Record Books

Four LPGA wins in a single season is a benchmark that separates elite from exceptional. Thitikul's 2026 haul places her among the tour's most dominant performers — and it does so with a historical footnote. She is the first Thai woman in a decade to win four or more LPGA events in a single season.

Her 11th career LPGA title came at Hoakalei Country Club in Hawaii with a $450,000 winner's share of the $3 million LOTTE Championship purse. Miyu Yamashita of Japan finished third at 17-under, five strokes back.

The form she's carrying into Shanghai is the kind that defenders dream of — recent, emphatic, and hard-won.

The Thitikul-Korda Season Race

With both Thitikul and Nelly Korda locked at four wins apiece for the 2026 LPGA season, the Shanghai event takes on additional stakes. A fifth win by either player would decisively tip the season dominance narrative.

Korda, who has been one of the game's most consistent performers over the past several seasons, will be watching this week's result closely. The rivalry between the two — both young, both capable of hot streaks that leave the field behind — has defined the 2026 LPGA season as much as any individual result.

Defending in Shanghai

Thitikul won the Buick LPGA Shanghai in 2025 and is now attempting to become the back-to-back champion at the same event — an achievement that would further cement her status as the tour's most complete player. The tournament runs through October 18, giving the field four rounds to challenge the defending title.

Whether anyone can slow her this week is the question Shanghai will answer. Based on the evidence from Hawaii, the burden of proof is on the rest of the field.

The Buick LPGA Shanghai runs through October 18. First-round results will set the leaderboard and give the first indication of whether Thitikul's form from Hawaii carried to China.
