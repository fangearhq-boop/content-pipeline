The DP World Tour is widely seen as Rahm's realistic 2027 home, with PGA Tour reinstatement a longer-term goal.

### STORY 4: Butterfield Bermuda Championship Preview

**Long-Form Post:**

Golf heads to Bermuda in one week. ⛳

The Butterfield Bermuda Championship runs October 22-25 at Port Royal Golf Course — one of the most distinctive venues on the PGA Tour calendar. The course is a Robert Trent Jones design from 1970 that plays as a par-71 at 6,828 yards, perched on Bermuda's South Shore with ocean wind as the dominant factor in every round.

A $6 million purse and 500 FedExCup Fall points are on the line. Defending champion Adam Schenk finished -12 (272 total) in 2025, holding off Chandler Phillips by one stroke.

The signature hole is the par-3 16th — roughly 235 yards over water, with trade winds capable of turning a wedge into a 6-iron and a makeable par into double bogey. It's a birdie opportunity and a disaster waiting to happen in the same breath.

This is one of golf's best hidden gems. A public course on a small island, with a real championship test. Mark your calendar.

### STORY 5: Scheffler ~178 Weeks at No. 1

**Long-Form Post:**

Scottie Scheffler keeps adding to his record books. 📊

As of this week, Scheffler is approaching approximately 178 consecutive weeks as the world's No. 1 ranked golfer — the longest uninterrupted run of the post-Tiger era. His OWGR lead over No. 2 Rory McIlroy stands at roughly 400 points. That kind of gap doesn't shrink easily.

Tiger Woods' record is 281 consecutive weeks at No. 1, set between June 2005 and October 2010. To break it, Scheffler would need to hold the top spot until approximately October 2028 — about two full years from now.

In total weeks at No. 1 (including non-consecutive stretches), Scheffler is approaching 200 — placing him in historical company alongside Tiger and Greg Norman.

This is a storyline that deserves more attention. Week by week, quietly, one of the greatest stretches of sustained world-ranking dominance in the game's history is being written.
