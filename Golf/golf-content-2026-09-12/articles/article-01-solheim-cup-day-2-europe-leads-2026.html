Europe Leads 7-5 After Saturday Foursomes. Sunday Singles Will Decide the 2026 Solheim Cup.

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | September 12, 2026

Maja Stark and Linn Grant beat Nelly Korda and Allisen Corpuz 3&2 as Europe won the Saturday morning foursomes session 3-1 at Bernardus Golf. Europe leads 7-5 with 12 singles points on the line Sunday.

Europe and the United States have played 16 points across two days at the 20th Solheim Cup and Europe holds a 7-5 lead. Saturday morning brought three points for the home side and one for USA in the foursomes session — extending what had been a 4-4 tie into a two-point European advantage that sets up a decisive Sunday.

The match that defined the morning: Maja Stark and Linn Grant defeated Nelly Korda and Allisen Corpuz 3&2. Korda, the World No. 1, and Corpuz had arrived at Bernardus Golf as one of USA's most reliable pairings — they held a 5-0 record together in Solheim Cup foursomes coming into this week. Saturday morning ended that streak. Stark controlled the match with her shotmaking and Grant provided complementary accuracy throughout; the result moved Europe's lead to 7-5 ahead of four afternoon fourball matches.

The Saturday Afternoon Fourballs

Saturday afternoon's four fourball matches brought additional movement on the scoreboard. Europe's Carlota Ciganda delivered a clutch eagle putt in her match. Beatriz Recari and Celine Boutier's pairing of Manon Guyot and Albane Valenzuela contributed, while hull and Lottie Woad — both rested by USA captain Angela Stanford in the morning — played in the afternoon. Their match was halved against Jeongeun Lee6 and Xiyu Lin. The Solheim Cup heading into Sunday singles is set with Europe in front, requiring 14 total points to retain or win the Cup.

USA needs to close the gap. The Americans have 12 points still available in Sunday's singles matches. If the USA wins 8.5 of the 12 singles matches, they retain the Solheim Cup. Europe needs 7 to win or retain.

The Sunday Singles Picture

Twelve singles matches — each worth one point — begin Sunday morning at Bernardus Golf. The captain's draw will be released Saturday evening. Both Angela Stanford (USA) and Suzann Pettersen (Europe) will need to make decisions about ordering and lineup that reflect both the scoreline and the form their players have shown across the week.

For Europe, the calculus is straightforward: maintain the lead, win or halve the majority of singles matches, close out the Cup. For the USA, the task is more complex — a comeback in a single day of 12 individual matches is achievable but requires consistent performance top to bottom in the order.

Alison Lee has been the most consistent American player of the week, going 3-for-3 across her first three appearances. Nelly Korda, rested for the Saturday afternoon session, is expected to feature prominently in the USA's singles lineup. The singles session begins the closing chapter of a Solheim Cup that has been tightly contested from the opening holes on Friday morning.

What's Next

Sunday singles at Bernardus Golf, Netherlands. Either the USA mounts a comeback to retain the Solheim Cup, or Europe wins on home soil. Coverage of every match, result by result, at Golf Fanrecap throughout the day Sunday.
