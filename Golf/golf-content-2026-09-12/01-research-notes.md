## Story 4: LIV Players and PGA Tour Reinstatement

**Confidence: HIGH**

- Brooks Koepka was first LIV player reinstated via PGA Tour "Returning Member Program"
  - Terms: $5 million donation to charity, no equity grants for five years, no access to bonus pool money in return year
- PGA Tour CEO Brian Rolapp offered the same terms to Rahm, DeChambeau, and Cameron Smith with tight deadline
- None of the three accepted
- Rolapp stated the Tour was "not inclined to discuss any paths back while players are under contract"
- LIV contracts are now terminated (all 48 players)
- No announced new reinstatement path as of September 12
- Rolapp quote: described PGA Tour's position as unchanged despite LIV filing
- Akshay Bhatia reportedly turned down a LIV offer earlier in 2026, stayed PGA Tour

**Sources:** CBS Sports, Sportico
