## STORY 5: LIV Golf Financial Crisis — $400M Needed

**Sources:** Wall Street Journal, Financial Times, BBC Sport, Associated Press, The Athletic
**Confidence:** HIGH

**Verified Facts:**
- LIV Golf needs $400 million in additional funding to complete its remaining 2026 schedule
- Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (PIF) has withdrawn ongoing financial support
- PIF has spent more than $5.3 billion on LIV Golf since 2022
- Restructuring specialists Gene Davis (of PIRINATE Consulting Group) and Jon Zinman have been installed on LIV Golf's board
- Their mandate: seek bridge capital and explore restructuring options
- Multiple remaining 2026 LIV events are described internally as "on the fence"
- Bankruptcy is being considered as one possible outcome
- The PGA Tour is monitoring the situation closely
- LIV Golf had 14 events planned for its 2026 season; several of the remaining events are at risk
- Commissioner Greg Norman has not publicly addressed the funding gap

**Context:** LIV Golf launched in 2022 backed entirely by PIF. The Saudi wealth fund's withdrawal marks a fundamental shift in the tour's financial structure.
