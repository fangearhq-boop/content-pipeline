Lucas Herbert Sets All-Time LIV Golf Scoring Record at -30 in Wire-to-Wire UK Win

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | July 27, 2026

Lucas Herbert made history at The Belfry. His wire-to-wire win at LIV Golf UK Presented by JCB finished at 30-under par — an all-time LIV Golf scoring record, shattering the previous mark by four shots. In three days at one of England's most storied golf venues, Herbert played some of the finest tournament golf in LIV's brief history.

Tyrrell Hatton finished second at 24-under, six strokes behind. Bryson DeChambeau was third at 21-under. On a week where -24 and -21 would normally command the headline, Herbert's performance made those numbers feel almost ordinary.

A Record-Setting Week

LIV Golf launched in 2022 and has produced some memorable scoring weeks in its short history. The previous all-time record was 26-under par. Herbert finished four shots better than that — a margin that speaks to the sustained brilliance of his performance across all 54 holes.

Herbert held at least a share of the lead after every round. He was never seriously threatened. His game at The Belfry — a Ryder Cup-storied layout in Sutton Coldfield, England — was as complete as it gets: distance control, precision iron play, and a putter that stayed hot from Thursday through Sunday.

Ripper GC Sweeps the Team Title

Herbert competes for Ripper GC on the LIV circuit, and his dominant individual showing translated into team success as well. Ripper GC won the team title, completing a full sweep of the honors available at LIV Golf UK.

The team format is one of LIV Golf's distinguishing features, and Herbert's week gave his teammates the kind of anchor performance that makes team titles feel inevitable. Ripper GC is a team built around Australian players, and Herbert's record-setting win gives the franchise one of its finest results.

Herbert's Place in LIV Golf History

Herbert arrived on LIV Golf as a recognizable name — an Australian tour regular with European Tour victories — but not as one of the circuit's marquee stars. That changes after this week. The all-time scoring record is the kind of achievement that permanently alters how a player is discussed.

In a season where LIV Golf has faced significant financial questions about its future, Herbert's performance at The Belfry provides the tour with a genuine historical moment to point to. Records matter in golf. Herbert now holds the most significant one in LIV's short history.

What's Next

LIV Golf's 2026 season continues with several events still on the calendar, though the tour's financial situation has created uncertainty around which events will proceed as planned. For Herbert, the record and the win carry him into the remainder of the season as one of its undisputed standout performers — regardless of what happens around him.
