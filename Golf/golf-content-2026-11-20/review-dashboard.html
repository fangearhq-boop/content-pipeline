### Long-Form Post LIV Golf is over. At a bankruptcy hearing in New Jersey on November 5, LIV's lawyers declared the league is "toggling to a value-maximizing wind-down." The restructuring plan — sometimes called LIV 2.0 — has been scrapped. The BC Partners Credit rescue investment of approximately $300 million was withdrawn. LIV filed for Chapter 11 in September 2026, months after Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund pulled its backing in April. The league's last competitive event was held in Indianapolis in August 2026. There will be no 2027 LIV season. Jon Rahm, one of the league's marquee signings, reportedly forfeited approximately $100 million in guaranteed contract claims in order to exit. Players returning to the PGA Tour will face a one-year ban under existing policy. Three years, billions of dollars, and a fractured sport — and now it comes to a close at a bankruptcy courthouse. ### Image Post Caption LIV Golf officially announced a wind-down at a New Jersey bankruptcy hearing on November 5, ending the tour's three-year run. #LIVGolf #Golf

### Long-Form Post For the players outside the top 100 in the FedEx Cup Fall standings, this week at Sea Island is everything. The RSM Classic is the PGA Tour's final fall event, and the top 100 players in the FedEx Cup Fall standings after the final round earn guaranteed status for the 2027 season.
