Jesper Svensson Shoots Back-Nine 29 to Lead RSM Classic After Round 1

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | November 20, 2026

Jesper Svensson made the RSM Classic scoreboard sit up and take notice on Thursday. The Swede posted a back-nine 29 at Sea Island Golf Club, completing a 64 on the Plantation Course to take the solo lead at the PGA Tour's fall finale.

At eight under par, Svensson sits two shots clear of a tie for second between Max Greyserman and Garrick Higgo, who both carded 65s on the Plantation Course to share second at minus-7.

The Round That Put Svensson in Front

Svensson's round was defined by his play coming home. A 29 on the back nine — nine holes of pure momentum — transformed what could have been a solid opening effort into something genuinely exceptional. The number grabbed attention across the leaderboard and left him with a comfortable cushion heading into Round 2.

Michael Thorbjornsen also had an impressive day, shooting 64 on the Seaside Course. His round serves as a reminder that the two-course format at Sea Island can produce low scoring from either venue — the Plantation and Seaside layouts play differently, but both are reachable in ideal conditions.

The Leaderboard After Round 1

Five players share fifth place at minus-6: Lee Hodges, Emiliano Grillo, Hank Lebioda, Maverick McNealy, and Davis Riley. The scoring is tight, and the leaderboard entering Round 2 reflects a week in which many players are within range of the lead.

World No. 1 Scottie Scheffler is not in the field this week, having chosen to rest ahead of the 2027 season.

What's at Stake at Sea Island

The RSM Classic carries more weight than most fall events. This is the final tournament in the FedEx Cup Fall — the last opportunity for players to crack the top 100 in the fall standings and secure full status for the 2027 PGA Tour season.

For players hovering around that 100th-place line, every shot this week carries implications beyond the $7.4 million purse. Some careers hinge on how the week finishes.

Round 2 continues Friday at Sea Island. Svensson tees off as the leader, but the field is packed tightly behind him — and the Seaside Course awaits.
