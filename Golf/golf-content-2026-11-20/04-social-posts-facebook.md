### STORY 3: LIV Golf Is Officially Winding Down

**Long-Form Post (1:00 PM ET)**
LIV Golf is over.

At a bankruptcy hearing in New Jersey on November 5, LIV's lawyers declared the league is "toggling to a value-maximizing wind-down." The restructuring plan — sometimes called LIV 2.0 — has been scrapped. The BC Partners Credit rescue investment of approximately $300 million was withdrawn.

LIV filed for Chapter 11 in September 2026, months after Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund pulled its backing in April. The league's last competitive event was held in Indianapolis in August 2026. There will be no 2027 LIV season.

Jon Rahm, one of the league's marquee signings, reportedly forfeited approximately $100 million in guaranteed contract claims in order to exit.

Players returning to the PGA Tour will face a one-year ban under existing policy.

Three years, billions of dollars, and a fractured sport — and now it comes to a close at a bankruptcy courthouse.

### STORY 4: McIlroy's Record-Tying Eighth Race to Dubai Title

**Long-Form Post (3:30 PM ET)**
Rory McIlroy doesn't need a trophy to win the title that matters most.

McIlroy secured his eighth Race to Dubai crown for the 2026 DP World Tour season — a record-tying achievement — without winning the final tournament. Tyrrell Hatton claimed victory in the season's closing event in Dubai, but McIlroy had accumulated enough points over the course of the year to claim the season-long title on points alone.

Eight Race to Dubai titles. It's the kind of number that defines a generation's dominance over a circuit. McIlroy has bridged the gap between the PGA Tour and the DP World Tour for years, and this latest milestone adds another chapter to a career that keeps finding new records to match.

### STORY 5: RSM Classic Bubble Watch

**Long-Form Post (6:00 PM ET)**
For the players outside the top 100 in the FedEx Cup Fall standings, this week at Sea Island is everything.

The RSM Classic is the PGA Tour's final fall event, and the top 100 players in the FedEx Cup Fall standings after the final round earn guaranteed status for the 2027 season. That means the difference between Sunday's last putt and a full PGA Tour card — or a trip back through Korn Ferry qualifying — could come down to a single shot.

Billy Horschel is among the notable names fighting for position on the bubble. The two-course format at Sea Island — players alternate between the Plantation Course and the Seaside Course in Rounds 1 and 2 before the cut, with Rounds 3 and 4 played exclusively on Seaside — adds a strategic wrinkle to an already pressure-filled week.

There's no second chance after Sunday. Watch the leaderboard carefully.
