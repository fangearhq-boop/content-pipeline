Austin Smotherman Wins Butterfield Bermuda Championship in Playoff Over Seamus Power

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | October 27, 2026

Smotherman birdied the third playoff hole at Port Royal to claim his second PGA Tour title in less than a month, continuing one of the most remarkable hot streaks of the fall swing.

Austin Smotherman is the hottest player on the PGA Tour right now. On Monday at Port Royal Golf Course in Bermuda, he defeated Seamus Power on the third hole of sudden death to win the Butterfield Bermuda Championship, completing a stunning run that has produced two PGA Tour titles in less than 30 days.

Both Smotherman and Power finished regulation at 16-under par, a total of 197 across the 54-hole event, forcing a playoff that extended into Monday afternoon. Smotherman ended it on the third extra hole, earning $1,080,000 from the $6 million purse along with 500 FedExCup points.

Two Wins in One Month

The Bermuda title is Smotherman's second of the 2026 PGA Tour fall swing. He won the Bank of Utah Championship on October 4, then returned to the field in Bermuda and did it again. Two PGA Tour wins within a single calendar month represents an extraordinary stretch in the modern era, the kind of sustained excellence that earns a player significant attention heading into the next events on the schedule.

Power, the Irish veteran, had his chances. He matched Smotherman stroke for stroke through regulation and pushed the playoff deep before Smotherman closed it out on the third hole. The result gives Smotherman the momentum and the FedExCup points to substantially improve his season-long standing.

Port Royal and the Fall Swing

Port Royal Golf Course, the historic layout on the south shore of Bermuda, made for a demanding but fair test. At 16-under, both finalists showed the kind of consistent ball-striking required to win on a course that demands accuracy off the tee and precision approach play. The Butterfield Bermuda Championship has become a staple of the PGA Tour's October schedule, and Smotherman leaves the island as its latest champion.

The win also demonstrates the depth of opportunity in the fall swing, where PGA Tour players who missed the postseason look to build momentum and banking points for the following year. Smotherman has turned the fall into a statement.

What's Next

The PGA Tour moves immediately to Mexico, where the VidantaWorld Mexico Open begins Wednesday, October 29, at Vidanta in Nuevo Vallarta. The question now circling the fall swing is simple: can Smotherman make it three wins in a row? Brian Campbell defends his 2025 title against a field that includes Wyndham Clark, Tony Finau, Akshay Bhatia, and Mexican favourite Álvaro Ortiz.
