Yuri Yoshida Claims First LPGA Title at BMW Ladies Championship in South Korea

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | October 27, 2026

The Japanese golfer outlasted Haeran Ryu and Hye-Jin Choi in a tense final round at Pine Beach Golf Links to claim the $2.35 million event and her first career victory on the LPGA Tour.

Yuri Yoshida has her first LPGA Tour title, and she earned it in one of the most demanding environments in women's golf — a final round in South Korea, at a course that rewards precision, against a field that included several of the game's best players.

Yoshida, the Japanese golfer, won the BMW Ladies Championship at Pine Beach Golf Links on Sunday, October 25, completing a final round that held off Haeran Ryu and Hye-Jin Choi to secure the $2,350,000 event. The win is the signature achievement of Yoshida's LPGA career.

A Final Round to Remember

Ryu and Choi, both South Korean players competing in front of their home galleries, were formidable opponents. The South Korean contingent is always formidable in domestic events — the knowledge of the conditions, the crowd support, and the pride of performing at home create a unique pressure that visiting players must absorb. Yoshida absorbed it and responded with the kind of golf that earns a first career title.

The BMW Ladies Championship has a history of producing close final rounds, and the 2026 edition delivered. Yoshida's ability to hold her nerve in the late holes of a close competition will be remembered as the defining quality of this win.

Japanese Golf on the World Stage

Yoshida's win adds to a recent tradition of Japanese women's golf competing at the highest level globally. The LPGA Tour has seen a wave of Japanese talent reach the top tier over the past decade, and Yoshida's first victory continues that legacy. For Japanese golf fans, a win in South Korea — against Korean opposition, in a Korean-backed event — carries particular meaning.

The $2,350,000 purse and the LPGA Tour points from the BMW Ladies Championship give Yoshida a significant boost in both earnings and her standing on the tour. Her career trajectory is pointed upward.

What's Next

The LPGA Tour's Asia swing concludes with the Maybank Championship in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, running October 29 through November 1. Yoshida will carry her momentum into Malaysia looking to prove the BMW Ladies win was no fluke. Golf Fanrecap will have full coverage of the Maybank Championship from Kuala Lumpur.
