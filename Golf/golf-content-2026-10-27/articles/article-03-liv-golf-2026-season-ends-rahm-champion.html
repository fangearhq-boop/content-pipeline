LIV Golf 2026 Season Ends: Jon Rahm Individual Champion, Team Title Disputed, Future Uncertain

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | October 27, 2026

The 12-event LIV Golf season wrapped in August, with Jon Rahm claiming the individual championship — but questions about the team title and the league's financial future are defining the offseason.

The 2026 LIV Golf season is over, and the questions it leaves behind may matter more than the results it produced.

The league ran 12 events between February 5 and August 30, culminating at LIV Indianapolis in late August. Jon Rahm topped the individual points standings across the full season to claim the individual championship, adding another major achievement to a career that has made him one of golf's premier names regardless of which circuit he plays.

The Disputed Team Title

On the team side, clarity is harder to find. LIV Golf's official records credit Legion XIII as the 2026 team champions — making them repeat winners after their 2025 title. But external sources, including Wikipedia's event records, attribute the team championship to 4Aces GC. The discrepancy has not been publicly resolved by LIV Golf's communications team, leaving the historical record of the 2026 season in an unusual state of ambiguity.

LIV Indianapolis, the season finale held August 20–23, was won by Michael La Sasso of HyFlyers GC. La Sasso, at 22 years old, became the youngest individual event winner in LIV Golf history, a bright note for the league's talent development even as the broader institutional questions gathered.

The Financial Question

The more significant story coming out of the 2026 season involves money and the league's long-term structure. The Public Investment Fund of Saudi Arabia (PIF), which has bankrolled LIV since its launch, committed funding only through the 2026 season. Reports of a $300 million restructuring effort have circulated, but as of August 2026, no public confirmation of 2027 funding or a definitive season schedule has been made.

For the players who left PGA Tour eligibility to join LIV, the uncertainty is concrete. The league's continued existence in 2027 is not guaranteed in its current form, and that reality shapes every contract negotiation and career decision for the players in the field.

What's Next

LIV Golf has not announced a 2027 season schedule or confirmed its funding structure. The coming weeks and months will determine whether the 2026 season was a chapter in an ongoing story or an ending. Golf Fanrecap will continue to monitor developments in the LIV offseason as the restructuring process unfolds.
