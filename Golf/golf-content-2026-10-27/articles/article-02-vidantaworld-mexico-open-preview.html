VidantaWorld Mexico Open Preview: Can Smotherman Three-Peat? Brian Campbell Defends

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | October 27, 2026

The PGA Tour heads to Nuevo Vallarta as Austin Smotherman arrives in Mexico as the hottest player in the world — but defending champion Brian Campbell and a strong field are ready to stop the streak.

The VidantaWorld Mexico Open at Vidanta gets underway Wednesday, October 29, in Nuevo Vallarta, Mexico, and the backdrop to the event has been set by a man who wasn't even the favourite 48 hours ago. Austin Smotherman won the Butterfield Bermuda Championship on Monday, his second PGA Tour title in less than a month, and he now arrives in Mexico as the player everyone is watching.

Three consecutive PGA Tour wins would be extraordinary in any era of the game. In 2026, with the depth of the tour's fall swing, it would be historic.

The Defending Champion

Brian Campbell won the 2025 VidantaWorld Mexico Open and returns to Vidanta with the knowledge of the course and the motivation of a champion who knows the feel of winning here. Campbell is the name the rest of the field will need to beat — his game at Vidanta last year showed the kind of precision iron play the course demands, and returning champions with recent form carry a significant advantage.

He will not be alone at the top of the betting market. Wyndham Clark, one of the most consistent ball-strikers on tour, has the length to attack the Vidanta layout. Tony Finau brings proven closing ability and experience in big moments. Akshay Bhatia, who has been one of the tour's most exciting young players in recent seasons, will be looking to make a statement in the fall swing.

The Home-Country Hope

Álvaro Ortiz provides the tournament with its Mexican story. The native of Guadalajara is one of the most accomplished Mexican golfers on the PGA Tour, and the galleries at Vidanta Vallarta respond to his presence on the course. Ortiz has been a consistent presence in the Mexico Open field, and a home-country win at this event would be a defining moment in his career.

What's Next

The VidantaWorld Mexico Open runs October 29 through November 1 at Vidanta in Nuevo Vallarta. The $6 million purse and 500 FedExCup points make it a meaningful event in the fall swing standings. First-round tee times begin Wednesday morning, with coverage across Golf Channel and the PGA Tour's digital platforms. Golf Fanrecap will have daily leaderboard updates, news, and analysis throughout the week.
