DP World Tour Championship Preview: Race to Dubai Finale Set for November 12–15 in Dubai

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | November 3, 2026

The DP World Tour season is coming to a close — and the finale is one of golf's most dramatic settings.

The DP World Tour Championship runs November 12–15 at Jumeirah Golf Estates in Dubai, UAE. The event carries a $10 million purse and determines the Race to Dubai champion, making it the most consequential week of the European tour's season. Before Dubai, the Abu Dhabi Championship (November 5–8) serves as the penultimate event, adding one final opportunity for players to move on the Race to Dubai standings before the season closes.

The Course

Jumeirah Golf Estates has hosted the DP World Tour Championship since 2009, establishing itself as one of the tour's permanent, iconic venues. The Earth course — the primary venue for the championship — winds through the lush landscape of the Dubai development, featuring dramatic water hazards and undulating greens that consistently produce compelling finishes.

The course rewards precision over power, though in the modern game the best players combine both. Approach play to the greens is critical, and putting on the Earth course's surfaces can be the difference between a winning run and a Sunday stumble.

The Race to Dubai

The Race to Dubai is the DP World Tour's season-long points competition, equivalent to the FedEx Cup on the PGA Tour. The player leading the Race to Dubai after the championship is declared the tour's No. 1 player for the season — one of the sport's most prestigious end-of-year titles.

The championship format adds an extra layer of competition: only the top 50 players on the Race to Dubai standings qualify for the event, meaning every player in the field is already one of Europe's best. The week is never short of quality or drama.

Extra Significance in 2026

This edition of the DP World Tour Championship arrives at a moment of unusual significance. LIV Golf's Chapter 11 bankruptcy and the player contract voidances it triggered have already opened the door for some players to reassert themselves on the DP World Tour in 2026. Looking ahead to 2027, the tour is widely expected to benefit from returning players — most notably Jon Rahm, who announced his departure from LIV Golf in October 2026 after finding the terms of LIV's restructured format "unacceptable."

The prospect of Rahm and other returning LIV players rejoining the DP World Tour's ranks adds narrative weight to a championship that was already one of the season's most anticipated weeks.

What to Watch

The Abu Dhabi Championship (November 5–8) will set the final Race to Dubai standings going into the season finale. Players on the cusp of qualifying for the Dubai field will be fighting for their place, while those already secure will be fine-tuning form ahead of the championship week.

In Dubai, the storylines will be familiar: who can handle the pressure of a season finale, who peaks at exactly the right moment, and who stumbles when the Race to Dubai title is within reach.

What's Next

The Abu Dhabi Championship runs November 5–8 as the final qualifier for the Dubai field. The DP World Tour Championship then begins November 12 at Jumeirah Golf Estates, with the final round scheduled for November 15. The Race to Dubai winner will be confirmed at the conclusion of that round.
