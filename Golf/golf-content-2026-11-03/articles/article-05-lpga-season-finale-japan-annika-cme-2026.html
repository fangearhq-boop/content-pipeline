LPGA Season Finale Stretch: Japan Classic, The ANNIKA, and the $11M CME Tour Championship

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | November 3, 2026

The LPGA Tour's 2026 season is entering its final three weeks — and the events that remain will determine the year's biggest honors in women's golf.

The tour's season-ending stretch begins this week with the TOTO Japan Classic and concludes in late November at the CME Group Tour Championship in Naples, Florida. Between now and then, the Player of the Year race, the Vare Trophy for low scoring average, and the season's richest purse will all be decided.

TOTO Japan Classic — November 5–8

The LPGA Tour heads to Taiheiyo Club in Japan this week for the TOTO Japan Classic. Japan has long been one of the sport's most passionate golf markets, and the TOTO Japan Classic consistently draws some of the tour's strongest fields and most devoted crowds. Japanese fans are famously devoted to the sport, and the atmosphere at Taiheiyo Club during tournament week reflects that passion.

For players chasing year-end honors, the TOTO Japan Classic represents one of the final opportunities to accumulate points and prize money ahead of the season finale. Form heading into Japan — and form coming out of it — will matter enormously when the tour reaches Florida.

The ANNIKA driven by Gainbridge at Pelican — November 12–15

Named after the great Annika Sörenstam, widely considered the greatest women's golfer of all time, the ANNIKA driven by Gainbridge at Pelican takes place at Pelican Golf Club in Belleair, Florida. The event is one of the most prestigious on the LPGA calendar, carrying the name and standards of a player who won 72 LPGA Tour events and 10 major championships during her career.

Pelican Golf Club presents a course where local and regional knowledge can provide an edge. Expect players who have competed well in Florida to feature prominently on the leaderboard. With the CME Group Tour Championship just one week away, the ANNIKA is also a last chance for players to sharpen form ahead of the season's biggest moment.

CME Group Tour Championship — November 19–22

The season ends at Tiburón Golf Club in Naples, Florida, with the CME Group Tour Championship — the richest event in women's golf. The 2026 purse stands at $11 million, a figure that reflects the extraordinary growth of prize money on the LPGA Tour in recent years. The 2026 LPGA season features a total of 33 events and a record $132 million prize fund — numbers that signal just how far the women's game has come.

At the CME Group Tour Championship, the Player of the Year and Vare Trophy races converge with the season's biggest paycheck. The event draws the tour's top 60 players based on the season-long points list, ensuring an elite field. Whoever lifts the CME trophy in Naples will do so as one of the year's definitive champions in women's golf.

What's on the Line

The Player of the Year award, voted on by LPGA Tour members, reflects the player who made the biggest overall impact during the season. It's the sport's most respected peer honor, and the final three weeks of the season can swing the vote dramatically. A player who closes strong — winning in Japan, making the leaderboard at the ANNIKA, and contending at the CME — is positioned for every honor the tour has to give.

The Vare Trophy, awarded to the player with the lowest adjusted scoring average for the season, is decided by mathematics. Every stroke in every round counts, and the final three events will determine whether the frontrunner holds on or is overtaken.

What's Next

The TOTO Japan Classic begins Thursday, November 5, at Taiheiyo Club. The ANNIKA driven by Gainbridge at Pelican follows November 12–15 in Belleair, Florida. The season concludes with the CME Group Tour Championship from November 19–22 at Tiburón Golf Club in Naples. The Player of the Year and Vare Trophy winners will be announced at the conclusion of the season.
