LIV Golf New York at Bedminster: Rahm vs. DeChambeau in a $20M Showdown

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | August 5, 2026

Same weekend. Different tour. Forty miles apart.

While the PGA Tour opens its final regular-season event at Sedgefield Country Club in Greensboro, North Carolina, LIV Golf is building toward what could be one of its most consequential events of the 2026 calendar. LIV Golf New York runs August 6-9 at Trump National Golf Club Bedminster in New Jersey — a $20 million individual purse with an additional $10 million on the team side. The winner takes home $4 million. The winning team collects $3 million.

The direct scheduling conflict is not accidental, and the two tours running simultaneously this week makes the choice unavoidable for fans, broadcasters, and the sport itself: two visions of professional golf's future, playing out in real time, 40 miles apart in the same media market.

Rahm Enters as Betting Favorite — and Season Leader

Jon Rahm leads the 2026 LIV Golf individual season standings by 219 points over Bryson DeChambeau. That margin, substantial but not insurmountable over a full season, makes this week particularly significant. A strong Rahm performance — especially a win — could effectively clinch the LIV individual season title before the calendar reaches its conclusion.

The oddsmakers agree. Rahm enters LIV Golf New York at 9/2 as the betting favorite. DeChambeau is next at 6/1, with Tyrrell Hatton at 13/2 and Joaquin Niemann at 9/1 rounding out the top of the market. Lucas Herbert is available at 14/1 for those who want a longer shot.

For Rahm, this week carries the weight of a potential clincher. A victory at Bedminster would deepen his lead to a potentially insurmountable margin and begin the conversation about what it means to be LIV's individual champion in the tour's fourth full season of operation.

A Course Built for Distance

Bedminster has changed significantly since LIV last visited in 2023. The course has been stretched by more than 125 yards and now plays at 7,651 yards — making it the second-longest venue on the 2026 LIV calendar. That yardage favors length off the tee, and DeChambeau — golf's premier power hitter — will feel at home on a layout that punishes players who cannot generate distance.

The combination of a long course and DeChambeau's game creates a natural counter-narrative to Rahm's favorite status. DeChambeau at 6/1 reflects a real chance of a performance that would bring the season standings back into legitimate contention. He has the tools that Bedminster's layout rewards.

Team Competition Adds Another Layer

LIV Golf's team format runs alongside the individual competition at Bedminster. The $10 million team purse, with $3 million going to the winning team, gives every player in the field a second objective beyond their individual scoring. Cameron Smith's Rippers came into the New York event with momentum after winning both the individual and team titles at LIV UK.

The team element is one of the aspects that distinguishes LIV's format from traditional stroke-play events, and Bedminster's concentrated field — 54 players — means every shot has implications for both the individual leaderboard and the team standings simultaneously.

The Bigger Picture

The scheduling conflict this week is a microcosm of the larger dynamic that has defined professional golf since LIV launched. Both tours have commitments, both have stars, and both are playing for meaningful prize money. The $20 million individual purse at Bedminster exceeds the $8.5 million at the Wyndham Championship by a substantial margin, though the FedExCup implications at Greensboro carry weight that pure prize money cannot capture.

Fans who follow both circuits face a genuine choice. Rahm's chase for what could be an early LIV title clinch is a compelling storyline. So is the FedExCup bubble at Sedgefield, where careers can shift in four days. The two narratives run in parallel, unresolved, until Sunday.

What's Next

LIV Golf New York begins Thursday at Trump National Golf Club Bedminster. Rahm enters with the lead and the favorite tag. DeChambeau enters with the course that suits his game and a deficit to erase. Hatton and Niemann are positioned to capitalize if the top two falter.

By Sunday evening, the 2026 LIV Golf individual season picture will be significantly clearer. If Rahm wins, the conversation about a champion may begin early. If DeChambeau wins and cuts the gap, the final events of the LIV calendar become must-watch material.

Either way, Bedminster delivers a $20 million reason to pay attention.
