### STORY 1: Grant Forrest Wins Open de España

**Post 1 (8:00 AM ET)**
Grant Forrest won the 2026 Open de España — and with it, earned himself a seat at two of the most iconic venues in golf.

The Scottish golfer birdied the 18th hole at Real Club de Campo Villa de Madrid to finish 14-under and beat Eugenio Chacarra and Victor Perez by a single shot. Chacarra had held the clubhouse lead, but Forrest's birdie at the last changed everything.

It is Forrest's third DP World Tour title and his first victory outside Scotland. The two players who finished second and third between them represent some of the best young European talent in the game.

The result earns Forrest invitations to both the 2027 Masters at Augusta National and The Open Championship at St Andrews — two of the four majors, in one birdie putt.

### STORY 2: Jon Rahm Leaving LIV Golf

**Post 2 (8:30 AM ET)**
Jon Rahm is walking away from LIV Golf — and the story of how it ended is as extraordinary as how it began.

Rahm's attorney told a New Jersey bankruptcy court that the proposed LIV 2.0 relaunch terms are unacceptable to his client, and that both sides are working toward a separation agreement with an October 15 deadline. If no deal is reached, the matter could return to court on November 5.

Rahm is reported to be owed between $100 million and $150 million by the bankrupt league.

LIV filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in September after Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund withdrew its backing. The league's CEO says it aims to return in 2027 under a player-first ownership model, but Rahm — who was LIV's highest-profile recruit when he signed in December 2023 — won't be part of it.

The PGA Tour question is more complicated. Rahm previously declined the Tour's Returning Member Program offer. There is no current formal reinstatement path available for him.

Matsuyama at a course in Japan — his home country — is appointment viewing on its own. Combine him with a Masters champion, a player of Tiger's stature, and Jason Day, and this is one of those events that reminds you how much star power the game still carries at its best.
