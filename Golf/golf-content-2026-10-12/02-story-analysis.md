- Tertiary: The Spanish Open stage — Chacarra as home favourite, Forrest as underdog

**Headlines:**

- "Forrest Birdies 18 to Win in Spain, Secures Masters and Open Championship Spots"

**Key Narrative:** Chacarra had the clubhouse lead at 13-under. Forrest was in the final group. At the 18th, he made birdie to reach 14-under and win by one. ### STORY 2: Jon Rahm Leaving LIV Golf

**Hook:** Jon Rahm is leaving LIV Golf. His attorney told a bankruptcy court that Rahm considers the proposed LIV 2.0 terms unacceptable, and a separation agreement has an October 15 deadline. He is owed between $100 million and $150 million. His path back to the PGA Tour is unclear.

**Angles:**
- Primary: Rahm's departure — what it means for LIV and for him personally
- Secondary: The money — $100-150M owed, bankruptcy proceedings
- Tertiary: The PGA Tour question — Rahm declined the Returning Member Program, no current path

**Headlines:**
- "Jon Rahm Is Leaving LIV Golf — And He's Owed Up to $150 Million"
- "Rahm Out: The Biggest Name in LIV Golf Is Walking Away From the Bankrupt League"
- "Jon Rahm Rejects LIV 2.0, Separation Deal Due October 15 — PGA Tour Return Uncertain"

**Key Narrative:** Rahm was the deal that changed LIV's credibility when he signed in December 2023. A two-time major champion, a former world No. 1, a player who looked like the future of the game. His departure from a league that cannot pay him what it owes is both a personal story and a structural story about the end of LIV Golf as we knew it.
