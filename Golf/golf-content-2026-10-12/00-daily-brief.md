### STORY 1: Grant Forrest Wins Open de España — Earns 2027 Masters and Open Championship Invitations
- **Tier:** 1
- **Category:** DP World Tour / Open de España / Grant Forrest / European Golf
- **Type:** FOLLOW UP
- **Byline:** Ryan Calloway
- **Summary:** Scotland's Grant Forrest won the 2026 Open de España at Real Club de Campo Villa de Madrid, finishing 14-under (270) to beat Eugenio Chacarra and Victor Perez by one shot. ### STORY 2: Jon Rahm Leaving LIV Golf — Separation Agreement Deadline October 15
- **Tier:** 1
- **Category:** Jon Rahm / LIV Golf / PGA Tour / Player Contracts
- **Type:** NEW
- **Byline:** Jake Torres
- **Summary:** Jon Rahm's attorney told a New Jersey bankruptcy court that Rahm will not participate in LIV Golf's proposed 2027 relaunch (LIV 2.0), and both sides are working toward a separation agreement with an October 15 deadline. Rahm is reportedly owed between $100 million and $150 million by the bankrupt league. A PGA Tour return is not straightforward — Rahm previously declined the Tour's Returning Member Program offer and no formal reinstatement path is currently available.
- **Why it matters:** Rahm was LIV's most prominent recruit after signing in December 2023. His departure is the clearest sign yet that LIV's bankruptcy has permanently fractured its roster. What happens next — for Rahm, for the other owed players, and for the PGA Tour — is one of golf's biggest stories.
- **Posting windows:** 8:00 AM (news break), 10:30 AM (analysis)
