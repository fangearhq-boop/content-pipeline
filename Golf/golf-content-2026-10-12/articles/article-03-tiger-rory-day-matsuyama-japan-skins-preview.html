Tiger, Rory, Day and Matsuyama Set for Japan Skins Match — October 21 at Narashino

By Marcus Cole

Four of the biggest names in world golf will meet at Accordia Golf Narashino Country Club in Japan on October 21 for a PGA Tour-sanctioned skins match. The field: Tiger Woods, Rory McIlroy, Jason Day and Hideki Matsuyama.

Early betting has McIlroy as the favourite at +150, with Woods right behind at +175.

Why This Event Matters

Skins golf is not stroke play. The format rewards boldness over management — a birdie or eagle on the right hole can win more than a dozen pars. It is a format that brings personality to the surface in a way that conventional tournament golf doesn't, and it creates moments that fans remember long after the number on the 18th is posted.

The four players selected for this event are not chosen at random. McIlroy won the 2026 Masters — he arrives as the reigning Augusta champion, carrying the confidence that major title produces. Woods is Tiger Woods, which is a sentence that still means something in Japan. Day has been one of the game's most consistent performers for more than a decade. Matsuyama, at Narashino, will be playing in front of a crowd that considers him a national hero.

The Japan Factor

Japan has embraced professional golf at the highest level, and the combination of Matsuyama's popularity and Woods's global stature has made the country one of the most enthusiastic hosts on the global golf calendar. Accordia Golf Narashino Country Club, located just outside Tokyo, provides a venue that Japanese fans know well and international fans will be watching closely.

McIlroy comes as favourite, which makes sense — he is playing the best golf of his generation right now. But skins matches have a way of equalizing things. One good hole from Woods or a birdie on a contested skin from Day can shift the result entirely.

What to Watch

The October 21 event sits one day before the Butterfield Bermuda Championship begins — a reminder that the PGA Tour's Fall season continues even as exhibition events capture attention. For the players involved, the Japan skins match is a chance to connect with fans in one of the sport's most passionate markets and deliver the kind of compelling golf that only this specific combination of personalities can produce.

October 21. Narashino. Four champions. This one should be worth watching.
