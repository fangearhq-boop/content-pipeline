LPGA Heads to Shanghai — Three Weeks, Three Countries, the Race to CME Globe Heats Up

By Jake Torres

The LPGA Tour is heading into one of the most demanding stretches on its 2026 calendar — a three-tournament Asian swing spanning three countries across three consecutive weeks. It begins with the Buick LPGA Shanghai in China from October 15–18.

The Asian Swing: Three Events, Three Countries

Shanghai opens the sequence. The BMW Ladies Championship follows at Pine Beach Golf Links in South Korea (October 22–25). The stretch concludes with the Maybank Championship at Kuala Lumpur Golf and Country Club in Malaysia (October 29–November 1).

The players who navigate these three events well will arrive at the season's closing stretch with real momentum in the Race to CME Globe standings. Those who falter — whether from travel fatigue, course unfamiliarity, or the pressure of playing three marquee international events back-to-back-to-back — will find themselves fighting from behind when it matters most.

Shanghai as a Stage

The Buick LPGA Shanghai has established itself as one of the LPGA's premier international events. The combination of the sponsorship profile, the venue quality, and the Chinese fan engagement has made it a consistent highlight of the tour's overseas schedule. Players arrive knowing the galleries will be substantial and the coverage broad.

The 2026 LPGA season spans 33 events across 13 countries and offers a total prize fund exceeding $132 million. The Asian swing accounts for three of those 33 events and a meaningful share of the season's available points. In a season where the Race to CME Globe will determine seedings and tour status for 2027, the timing of this stretch — late in the calendar, before the year-end final events — makes it decisive.

The Race to CME Globe Context

The CME Group Tour Championship, the LPGA's season-ending event, carries the sport's largest single prize. Players who arrive at that event with strong CME Globe standings have the best chance at the biggest cheque in women's golf. The Asian swing is the last major cluster of events before that finale — making Shanghai, South Korea and Malaysia not just individual tournaments, but chapters in a larger story about who finishes 2026 on top.

Tee times for the Buick LPGA Shanghai begin Thursday, October 15. The first drives of the LPGA's Asian swing are three days away.
