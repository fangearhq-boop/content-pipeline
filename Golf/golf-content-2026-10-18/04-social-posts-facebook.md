**Long-Form Post:**

The terms of the deal: Rahm releases his $7.5 million unsecured creditor claim against LIV Golf — money he was owed as part of his original arrangement — and in exchange gets back full rights to his name, image, and likeness.

There are still questions: PGA Tour re-entry procedures, world ranking implications after time away, what schedule he could realistically start. But the legal piece is moving. The path forward exists.
