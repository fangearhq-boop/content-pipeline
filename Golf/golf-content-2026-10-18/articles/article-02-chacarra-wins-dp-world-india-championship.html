Chacarra Wins DP World India Championship, Jumps to No. 1 in Race to Dubai

Third DP World Tour win of the year. McIlroy drops to third. Three events left and a Spaniard is in the lead.

By Jake Torres | October 18, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

How It Happened

Eugenio Chacarra went low at Delhi Golf Club all week. His best round was a third-round 63, nine under par — the kind of score that defines a tournament. He carried the momentum into Sunday and finished at -21 total, winning the DP World India Championship by two strokes.

It was his third DP World Tour victory of 2026, following wins at the KLM Open and Italian Open earlier in the season. He has won three times in one calendar year on a tour where doing it once is considered a strong season.

Race to Dubai Shakeup

Before this week, Patrick Reed held the top spot in the Race to Dubai standings. Chacarra's win changed that. He now leads at 4,092.89 points.

Rory McIlroy finished joint-42nd in the same tournament. McIlroy, who won this event in 2024, had a week to forget and drops to third in the standings. There are three events left in the Race to Dubai season. The gap between Chacarra at the top and McIlroy in third is real — and it will take more than one good week to close.

Chacarra's Breakout Year

Three wins in a season is rare anywhere on tour. Chacarra is 24 years old and this is the year he announced himself as a player who belongs at the top of any European leaderboard.

Spain has produced some of golf's most iconic names. Seve Ballesteros built a legend across two decades. Sergio Garcia won the Masters. Jon Rahm became a world No. 1 and a major champion. Now Chacarra is writing the next chapter. He is not a future name to know — he is the name right now, in October 2026, with the Race to Dubai lead and three events to protect it.

Can McIlroy Recover?

McIlroy has won the Race to Dubai multiple times in his career. He knows how to close out a season. But joint-42nd in India was not a closing performance, and the math with three events remaining is not comfortable.

He will need to play well and hope Chacarra slips. Neither is guaranteed. The Race to Dubai has three events that will decide it — and the name at the top right now is not McIlroy.

What's Next

Three events remain in the Race to Dubai season. Chacarra has the lead and the form. McIlroy has the experience and the need. The Race to Dubai won't be settled this week — but Sunday's result at Delhi Golf Club may be the moment people look back on as the day it tilted toward Spain.
