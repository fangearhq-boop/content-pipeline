LIV Golf Indianapolis Round 1: Rahm Already Crowned, Team Title Battle Is the Main Event

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | August 20, 2026

Jon Rahm arrived at The Club at Chatham Hills in Westfield, Indiana, this week already holding the 2026 LIV Golf Individual Season Championship — his third consecutive title. The math was resolved before the first ball was struck in Indianapolis. What remains to be settled this week is the LIV Team Championship, and those standings are genuinely close.

Round 1 is underway at Chatham Hills, a course with particular significance in golf history. It was the final design completed by Pete Dye, the legendary architect whose death in January 2020 marked the end of one of the most influential careers in golf course design. To host a major professional event at this layout — Dye's last — gives Indianapolis a weight that the standings alone cannot convey.

Rahm's Victory Lap

Three consecutive LIV Individual Season Championships is a dominance of individual competition that mirrors, in its own context, what Scottie Scheffler has done on the PGA Tour. Rahm has won more than anyone else on the circuit across 2024, 2025, and now 2026. His Legion XIII squad has been a consistent presence at the top of the team standings as well.

Rahm can still win this week's individual stroke play event — and he will be trying. But the pressure of chasing a season title is gone. He can play freely, attack pins, and enjoy the round in a way that is different from a stretch run with something at stake. For Rahm, Indianapolis is a celebration of what 2026 has already produced.

The Team Title Race

4Aces GC — the squad of Dustin Johnson, Thomas Pieters, Thomas Detry, and Patrick Kim — enter Indianapolis as the season leader in team standings. Ripper GC, captained by Cameron Smith and featuring Marc Leishman, Lucas Herbert, and Smylie Kaufman, trail by 2.25 points. Legion XIII is in third place.

In LIV's team format, individual strokes in each round contribute to a cumulative team score. With one event remaining in the season, 2.25 points is a margin that can close or expand quickly. A strong round from any Ripper GC member creates pressure. A stumble from the 4Aces squad opens a door. This is where the team format creates drama that pure individual stroke play does not — every shot on Thursday carries team implications.

The bonus money tied to the team championship is substantial. For players on Ripper GC, this week is not a formality — it is the last chance to claim the title that has so far eluded them in 2026.

Chatham Hills: Pete Dye's Final Statement

Pete Dye designed more than 100 golf courses over a career spanning six decades. His work at Harbour Town, TPC Sawgrass, the Ocean Course at Kiawah, and Whistling Straits defined entire eras of professional golf. Chatham Hills, completed shortly before his death at age 94, carries the hallmarks of his late-career philosophy: demanding without being punitive, visually striking, with risk-reward corridors that reward boldness and penalize recklessness.

LIV's decision to bring Indianapolis to this course adds meaning to a week that might otherwise have felt like an epilogue. Playing at Dye's final design is something these players will remember regardless of where they finish.

Entertainment and Format

LIV continues to integrate live entertainment with its competitive rounds. Thomas Rhett performs Friday evening at Chatham Hills. Disco Lines performs Saturday. The hybrid model — professional golf paired with concert-level entertainment — remains LIV's core differentiator from the PGA Tour's traditional model, and Indianapolis is where the 2026 season signs off on that experiment.

The 54-hole strokeplay format concludes Saturday evening. No Sunday final round — the event wraps before the weekend's end, which LIV positions as a feature for fans who want a contained, festival-style experience. Whether that model sustains itself financially is a separate question being answered in New York courtrooms and Riyadh boardrooms. On the course this week, the competition is real.

What's Next

Round 1 scores will be posted throughout Thursday. The team standings update in real time through LIV Golf's app and broadcast on CW Network. Round 2 follows Friday, with the final round Saturday. For 4Aces and Ripper GC, the next 54 holes determine which team closes the 2026 LIV season as champions.
