### Long-Form Post Posting window: 3:30 PM ET 💰 LIV Golf's Financial Future Is in Serious Jeopardy New reporting confirms what the rumors had been suggesting: Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund — which has invested approximately $5 billion in LIV Golf since the circuit launched — will end its financial backing after 2026. The impact on this week's Indianapolis event is visible and stark: 📉 Prize money slashed: $20M → $10.1M (winner gets $2.02M instead of $4M) ❌ Michigan Team Championship: cancelled entirely 💸 ~$60M in promised prize money: gone ⚖️ Lawsuit: Fresh Tape Media filed suit July 21 in New York Supreme Court for $1.23M+ in unpaid fees for LIV Golf Week (January 12-14, West Palm Beach) ⚠️ Players reportedly awaiting payments This is not a small development. LIV Golf was built on the promise of extraordinary prize money — it was the primary recruitment tool for attracting top PGA Tour talent. The financial model that made that possible is ending. What comes next is unclear. LIV Golf has been in various stages of merger talks with the PGA Tour, and the financial pressure could accelerate or collapse those discussions. For the players who signed long-term LIV contracts based on financial promises, the uncertainty is real. The circuit is still operational. The event is happening this week. But the era of unlimited Saudi backing appears to be ending — and nobody knows exactly what happens after 2026. ---
