The Annika Preview: LPGA's Marquee Event Tees Off Tomorrow at Pelican Golf Club

By Ryan Calloway | November 11, 2026

While professional golf's attention has been focused on the LIV Golf collapse, the LPGA Tour is preparing for one of its signature events. The Annika begins November 12 at Pelican Golf Club in Belleair, Florida, with a $3.25 million purse and enormous stakes as the season enters its final two weeks.

An Event That Honours the Greatest

The Annika carries one of the most distinctive names in professional golf. Named for Annika Sörenstam — widely considered the greatest female golfer of all time — the event at Pelican Golf Club draws the LPGA Tour's best players to a venue designed for elite competition.

Pelican Golf Club in Belleair, Florida, provides the kind of championship-caliber test that separates the tour's elite from the field. With its combination of tight fairways, demanding approach shots, and undulating greens, the course rewards ball-striking precision and short-game excellence in equal measure.

For the players competing this week, The Annika is more than a prestige event. With only one more tournament after it — the CME Group Tour Championship, the LPGA's season-ending event with an $11 million purse — the standings entering the final stretch matter enormously for year-end honors and Race to the CME Globe positioning.

The Stakes: Final Two Events of the Season

The 2026 LPGA Tour season ends November 22, with The Annika (November 12-15) and the CME Group Tour Championship (November 19-22 at Tiburón Golf Club in Naples, Florida) the final two tournaments on the schedule. That means every shot hit at Pelican this week echoes into the season finale.

Players who perform well at The Annika will carry both points and momentum into the CME. Those who struggle face a compressed window to recover — just one tournament separates their Pelican result from the season's final chapter. In a year-end race where points separating the top players can be tight, this week's performance carries maximum weight.

What to Watch

The conditions at Pelican Golf Club in November tend to favor players who can manage their emotions and execute under pressure. Florida's coastal weather can produce tricky winds, and the course's design demands course management as much as raw power. Players who can keep the ball in play off the tee and attack pins from reliable positions tend to have an advantage here.

Watch for the Race to the CME Globe standings to shift significantly across the four days of competition. Players near the top of the season-long standings will be protecting their positions while challengers look for opportunities to close the gap before the finale at Tiburón.

What's Next

The Annika runs November 12-15 at Pelican Golf Club in Belleair, Florida. After four days of competition, the LPGA Tour packs up and heads south to Naples for the CME Group Tour Championship — the sport's most lucrative women's event and the perfect stage for a season-closing drama.

Amid all the news surrounding LIV Golf's collapse, it is worth remembering that the best women's golf in the world is happening right now, in Florida, with millions of dollars and season-long glory on the line. The Annika begins tomorrow. Do not miss it.
