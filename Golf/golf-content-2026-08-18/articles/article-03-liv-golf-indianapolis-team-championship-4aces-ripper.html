LIV Golf Indianapolis: Team Championship on the Line as 4Aces Lead Ripper GC by 2.25 Points

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap

Jon Rahm walked into LIV Golf Indianapolis already wearing the 2026 individual championship. His 944.54 season points and 218-point margin over the field meant the title was clinched before a single shot was struck at The Club at Chatham Hills in Westfield, Indiana. What the individual standings couldn't settle — and what makes this weekend's tournament genuinely consequential — is the team race.

The Course

LIV Golf Indianapolis is played at The Club at Chatham Hills, a course with a distinctive place in golf history: it was Pete Dye's final design. Dye, who died in 2020, was responsible for some of the most iconic and demanding courses in the game — TPC Sawgrass, Whistling Straits, Kiawah Island — and Chatham Hills represents the final expression of his design philosophy. The course provides a meaningful stage for what is shaping up to be a championship weekend.

The Team Race

LIV Golf's team format is the circuit's most distinctive competitive element, and the 2026 season has produced an extraordinarily tight finish. 4Aces GC — captained by Dustin Johnson and featuring Thomas Pieters, Thomas Detry, and Anthony Kim — enters Indianapolis leading the team standings. Ripper GC, the team captained by Cameron Smith and including Marc Leishman, Lucas Herbert, and Elvis Smylie, trails by exactly 2.25 points.

Two and a quarter points. Three rounds of stroke play. That margin is tighter than the variance on any given hole at Chatham Hills, which means the team championship is genuinely live for every shot of every round. Johnson's team can clinch with a strong weekend. Smith's team can overtake with one as well. Nothing is settled.

Individual Subplot

While Rahm's individual title is decided, the fight for runner-up has its own intrigue. Joaquin Niemann has accumulated nine individual LIV Golf titles in 2026 — a circuit record for titles in a single season — and is battling Bryson DeChambeau for the individual runner-up position in the season standings. The difference between second and third in the final standings carries meaningful financial implications, giving Indianapolis competitive stakes that extend well beyond the team race.

Niemann's nine-title season represents a remarkable run of individual excellence that deserves recognition on its own terms, separate from the overall points race that Rahm has dominated.

Entertainment

LIV Golf Indianapolis runs August 21-23 with an entertainment package that reflects the circuit's event-focused model. Thomas Rhett performs Friday night, and Disco Lines takes the stage Saturday. The combination of competitive stakes and off-course entertainment is central to LIV Golf's proposition for the events it stages.
