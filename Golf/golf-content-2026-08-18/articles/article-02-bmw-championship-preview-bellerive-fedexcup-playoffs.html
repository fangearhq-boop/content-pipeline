BMW Championship Preview: Scheffler Enters Bellerive as Dominant FedExCup Leader

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap

The FedExCup Playoffs arrive at their penultimate stop Thursday, and for 20 players in the 50-man field, the BMW Championship at Bellerive Country Club in St. Louis, Missouri will mark the end of their 2026 PGA Tour season. Only 30 advance to the Tour Championship at East Lake in Atlanta (August 27-30). Everyone else watches from home.

The Course and the Stakes

Bellerive Country Club carries serious golf history. Brooks Koepka shot 264 at this course to win the 2018 PGA Championship — a record-low score for that major at the time. The layout that produced Koepka's dominant performance now hosts the second FedExCup Playoff event, with a $20 million purse on offer and postseason survival on the line for the bubble players who enter this week knowing that two or three bad rounds could end everything.

The tournament runs August 20-23. The top 50 in FedExCup standings qualified. The top 30 finishers advance to East Lake. Twenty players will not.

Scheffler's Position

The dominant storyline heading into Bellerive involves a player who is not worrying about the bubble. Scottie Scheffler won the FedEx St. Jude Championship last week by 8 strokes, finishing 17-under at TPC Southwind in a performance that demonstrated how far removed he is from the rest of the field on his best week. He enters the BMW Championship as the No. 1 FedExCup seed — over 1,000 points clear of the second position in the standings.

The FedExCup bonus structure rewards players based on their position entering the Tour Championship. Scheffler has already locked in $23 million in bonus money for holding the top position through the BMW. Winning at Bellerive would add further to those earnings while cementing his status as the 2026 season's defining player.

He is the overwhelming betting favorite. That is both accurate and insufficient — Scheffler doesn't just enter as the favorite on the board, he enters as a player who won his last start by the length of a closing stretch that left the field in a different time zone.

The Field Around Him

Forty-nine other players at Bellerive see this week differently. Rory McIlroy, Xander Schauffele, Cameron Young, Collin Morikawa, and Hideki Matsuyama are all in the field — all major champions or major contenders, all capable of winning any week they play their best golf. But none of them arrive carrying what Scheffler brings: an 8-stroke margin from seven days ago and a FedExCup points total that makes the standings look like a different sport.

For the players near the bubble — those near positions 28, 29, 30 in the standings — Bellerive is existential. Every shot matters. Every position gained or lost in the field changes the calculus. The top-30 cutline at the end of Sunday will produce a specific kind of tension that the leaderboard leaders won't experience in the same way.

What's Next

The BMW Championship begins Thursday, August 20, at Bellerive Country Club in St. Louis, Missouri. The tournament runs four rounds through Sunday, August 23. Follow Golf Fanrecap for round-by-round coverage, FedExCup standings updates, and analysis of who advances to East Lake and who goes home.
