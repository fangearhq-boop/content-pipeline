The rest of the field features virtually every top-10 player in women's golf: Hyo Joo Kim, Miyu Yamashita, Ruoning Yin, Minjee Lee, and Hannah Green are all expected to factor into the final leaderboard.

He has already locked in $23 million in bonus money for holding the top position. Scheffler enters as the overwhelming betting favorite.

Around him: a $20 million purse and 49 other players who know that one or two bad rounds could end their playoff run. Rory McIlroy, Xander Schauffele, Cameron Young, Collin Morikawa, and Hideki Matsuyama are all in the field — contenders who need strong Bellerive performances to make the Tour Championship worth talking about.

Which 30 advance? That's the week's central question.
