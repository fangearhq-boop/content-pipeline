Solheim Cup Foursomes Begin Friday — What to Know Before the First Ball Is Hit at Bernardus Golf

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | September 10, 2026

The 2026 Solheim Cup competition opens Friday at Bernardus Golf in the Netherlands. USA captain Angela Stanford defends with Nelly Korda as the anchor. Europe captain Anna Nordqvist is sending out her most prepared team in years. Here is what to watch when the foursomes begin.

After a week of practice rounds, ceremonies, and captain's strategy sessions, the 2026 Solheim Cup gets real on Friday.

Foursomes begin at Bernardus Golf in Cromvoirt, Netherlands — the first Dutch Solheim Cup in the competition's history — with 12 Americans and 12 Europeans competing in alternating-shot format. The three-day structure runs foursomes on Friday, fourballs on Saturday, and 12 individual singles matches on Sunday. Twenty-eight points in total, 14.5 needed to win, and for Europe, 14 to retain the trophy they lost in 2024.

USA is the defending champion. That matters in match play competitions — not because defending champions automatically win, but because the confidence and institutional knowledge of a team that knows what winning here looks like provides a genuine psychological edge in the early sessions.

Angela Stanford's USA Strategy

Angela Stanford's 12-player American roster is built around a specific priority: Nelly Korda as the ace at the top of the lineup, and depth capable of winning at every position below her.

Korda is world No. 1 and has had one of the most decorated seasons in women's golf in 2026 — four tournament wins, two major championships, and a Solheim Cup career record of 10-5-1 that makes her the most productive American player in recent editions. Stanford will pair her with a partner who complements her aggressive, birdie-seeking style in foursomes, where the alternating-shot format rewards chemistry and controlled aggression over individual heroics.

The captain's picks — Megan Khang, Rose Zhang, and Lindy Duncan — add specific dimensions to the lineup. Zhang was 4-0 in her Solheim debut at the 2024 edition, one of the most productive rookie performances in recent memory. Khang carries a 7-3-3 career Solheim record, a mark that shows she handles match play pressure consistently. Duncan's experience and precise iron play give Stanford options in the back end of the lineup.

The question Stanford faces before Friday's pairings announcement is whether to open with Korda in the top spot, anchoring the lineup for maximum expected value, or to hold her for a specific opponent matchup that the captain's intelligence suggests she can dominate.

Anna Nordqvist's European Puzzle

Europe captain Nordqvist — a former major champion who brings competitive insight and tactical intelligence to the role — has described her pairings decisions as a "puzzle." The Europe team has a clear veteran core: Charley Hull (eighth consecutive Solheim appearance), Lottie Woad (world No. 5), Celine Boutier, Carlota Ciganda, and Linn Grant.

Hull's experience as Europe's most senior player creates both opportunity and responsibility. Her match record over eight Solheim appearances is meaningful, and her position as a de facto team leader carries weight in the locker room. Nordqvist is expected to lean on Hull in the foursomes sessions where experience matters most.

The rookies — Maria Fassi Lopez Ramirez, Charlotte Nadaud, and Mimi Rhodes — represent the element of European uncertainty that makes the opening sessions most unpredictable. All three have been described by Nordqvist's team as among the most prepared Solheim Cup debutants Europe has sent. But debut experience in match play at this level carries inherent variables that won't be fully visible until Friday morning.

The Matchup Everyone Wants

Nelly Korda against Charley Hull is the Solheim Cup's marquee narrative — the world No. 1 defending the title she helped win two years ago, against Europe's most experienced and charismatic competitor who is entering what she has described as her "best" Solheim Cup heading.

In foursomes, they may not face each other directly — alternating-shot format pairs teammates, not opponents, in the most direct sense. But in Saturday's fourball, where each player plays their own ball, and particularly in Sunday's singles, Korda-Hull represents the individual match that both captains and both players are aware of. Former European captain Laura Davies publicly challenged Hull before the event to "step up and lead" — an indication of the expectations placed on Hull as the team's defining figure heading into a competition where she has never been on the winning side.

USA hasn't won back-to-back Solheim Cups since 2015 and 2017. Doing so with Korda as the headliner and against an experienced European team playing at home in the Netherlands would represent a definitive statement about the current balance of women's professional golf.

What's Next

Foursomes pairings will be announced by the captains Thursday night or Friday morning ahead of the first tee times. Foursomes play is Friday, September 11. Fourballs on Saturday, September 12. The 12 individual singles matches that decide the 2026 Solheim Cup play out Sunday, September 13.
