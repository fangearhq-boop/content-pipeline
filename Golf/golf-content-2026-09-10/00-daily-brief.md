## Story Summaries

### STORY 1: Solheim Cup Opens Tomorrow
The 2026 Solheim Cup competition begins Friday, September 11 at Bernardus Golf in Cromvoirt, Netherlands — the first Dutch Solheim Cup. USA captain Angela Stanford leads the American team; Europe captain Anna Nordqvist leads Europe. Day 1 features foursomes play; Day 2 is fourball; Day 3 (Sunday, September 13) is the 12 singles matches that will decide the winner. USA needs 14.5 points to win; Europe needs 14.5 to retain (they lost the 2024 edition 15.5-12.5 to USA). Nelly Korda (world No. 1, 10-5-1 Solheim career record) leads USA. Charley Hull (8 consecutive Solheim appearances) is Europe's most experienced player. The first pairings of foursomes and fourball are expected today.

### STORY 2: LIV Golf Bankruptcy Day 2
LIV Golf filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection on September 8, 2026 in a New Jersey federal court. The Saudi Public Investment Fund (PIF) is providing $49.6 million in debtor-in-possession (DIP) financing to keep operations running during the restructuring. All player contracts were automatically terminated upon the bankruptcy filing. Jon Rahm is listed as approximately $7.4 million unsecured creditor; Bryson DeChambeau approximately $5.7 million contingent; Cameron Smith and Dustin Johnson also among leading creditors. BC Partners (London private equity) is expected to fund LIV 2.0 — a 10-event alternative tour with player equity stakes. Players now face three paths: accept LIV 2.0 equity, pursue claims in court, or seek reinstatement on the PGA Tour.

### STORY 3: Rahm and DeChambeau's Three Options
With LIV Golf bankrupt, Jon Rahm and Bryson DeChambeau — the two highest-profile American and Spanish players in the organization — face concrete decisions. Option 1: Accept LIV 2.0 equity stakes (converted from outstanding guaranteed money), compete in the 10-event 2027 schedule. Option 2: Pursue full claims in bankruptcy court (potentially months or years of litigation with uncertain recovery). Option 3: Seek PGA Tour reinstatement. PGA Tour CEO Brian Rolapp has said there is no current standard reinstatement path for LIV players — though individual applications could theoretically be considered. For Rahm, a DP World Tour return as a European player is also an option.

### STORY 4: Presidents Cup 2 Weeks Out
The 2026 Presidents Cup is September 22-27 at Medinah Country Club, Illinois. USA captain Brandt Snedeker and International captain Geoff Ogilvy lead their respective 12-man teams. USA is anchored by Scottie Scheffler (FedExCup champion, world No. 1), supported by Wyndham Clark, Sam Burns, Collin Morikawa, Keegan Bradley, Max Homa, and others including 21-year-old Jackson Koivun as the youngest captain's pick. The International team includes Hideki Matsuyama, Si Woo Kim, Tom Kim, and Adam Scott. USA has won 13 of 15 Presidents Cup editions; the International team last won in 1998.

### STORY 5: Biltmore Championship Preview
The PGA Tour's new Biltmore Championship runs September 17-20 at The Cliffs at Walnut Cove in Arden, North Carolina — the first PGA Tour event in western North Carolina. The inaugural edition is part of the fall non-points season swing. The Cliffs at Walnut Cove is a Jack Nicklaus signature design. The event is positioned as a showcase for the Asheville region, which is still rebuilding its profile following Tropical Storm Helene flooding in 2024. The field has not been announced at time of publication.
