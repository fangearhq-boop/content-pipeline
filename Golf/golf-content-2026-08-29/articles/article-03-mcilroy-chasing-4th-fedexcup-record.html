McIlroy's Record Chase: Can a 4th FedExCup Still Happen at East Lake?

By Jake Torres | August 29, 2026

Rory McIlroy holds a share of the most coveted record in FedExCup history — three titles, tied with Tiger Woods. No player has ever won four. That is the milestone McIlroy is chasing at East Lake this week, and as Round 3 begins on Saturday, the path to it runs through a six-shot deficit and a field that includes Scottie Scheffler and Min Woo Lee.

The Record in Context

McIlroy won the FedExCup in 2016, 2019, and 2022 — three championships spread across six years. Tiger Woods won the FedExCup in 2007, 2009, and 2013. The two players are tied at three titles apiece, and that number has stood as the all-time record since McIlroy matched it in 2022.

A fourth title would give McIlroy sole possession of the most coveted postseason record in the history of this competition. That is the carrot at the end of the week — but first, he needs to get back in the tournament.

The Mathematical Challenge

McIlroy sits at -2 (T20) after Round 1, six shots behind leader Min Woo Lee. He made a T3 finish at the BMW Championship to qualify for the Tour Championship field, but his East Lake opener left him deep in the pack.

In a 30-player field at a par-70 course, six shots is not an impossible gap — it just requires everything to go right. McIlroy would need to post a low number in Round 3, have Lee struggle, and still have something left for Sunday. The combination of factors needed is significant, but McIlroy is precisely the type of player who has authored big weekend charges in high-pressure situations before.

History at East Lake

McIlroy knows East Lake. He has competed here in multiple FedExCup Playoffs, and the course's demands — precision off the tee, sharp iron play, and nerves on the greens — suit his game when it's working.

His 2022 FedExCup win came with moments of brilliance under pressure. If there is a player in this field capable of piecing together back-to-back exceptional rounds to climb from T20 into contention, McIlroy is the name that fits. But he'll need to start delivering that on Saturday.

What's Next

Round 3 is underway at East Lake on Saturday, August 29. The final round plays Sunday, August 30. For McIlroy to keep his record-chasing bid alive, he needs a significant Saturday — something in the range of 6-under or better to start closing the gap on Lee and the players between them.

The record is real. The gap is real. Saturday at East Lake tells us which one matters more this week.
