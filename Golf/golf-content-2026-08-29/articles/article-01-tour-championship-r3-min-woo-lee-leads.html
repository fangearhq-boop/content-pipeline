Tour Championship Round 3: Min Woo Lee Holds the Lead at East Lake, Scheffler and McIlroy Give Chase

By Marcus Cole | August 29, 2026

Min Woo Lee enters Round 3 of the 2026 Tour Championship at East Lake Golf Club with the lead — and a story worth following. The Australian fired a career-matching 8-under 62 in Round 1, going bogey-free over the par-70 East Lake layout to build a three-shot advantage over a group that includes some of the biggest names in professional golf. Round 3 is underway on Saturday, and the $40 million purse is fully in play.

Lee's Career-Best Round

Lee's opening 62 was bogey-free — an eagle of sorts at a course that demands constant precision. East Lake is not a birdie-fest. The par-70 layout has humbled plenty of elite players in the FedExCup Playoffs era, and a round like Lee's — clean, aggressive, and dominant — immediately sets the tone for the week.

Sitting at -8, Lee leads by three shots over a T2 group of Viktor Hovland, Collin Morikawa, and Chris Gotterup — all of whom are legitimate contenders for a Tour Championship victory. Any of the three is capable of posting a low round on Saturday and applying real pressure on Lee down the stretch.

Scheffler Within Range

Scottie Scheffler sits T5 at -5 alongside Ryan Gerard, Akshay Bhatia, and Ludvig Åberg. Three shots back of the leader with two rounds remaining at a 30-player field is a very makeable gap for a player of Scheffler's caliber.

Scheffler is the five-time consecutive FedExCup No. 1 seed — an unprecedented achievement — and won this very tournament in 2024. He has proven repeatedly that he knows how to close at East Lake. A low Saturday round could put him right back in the conversation before the final round even begins.

McIlroy's Long Odds

Rory McIlroy opened at -2 and sits T20, six shots back of Lee. The three-time FedExCup champion — who shares the all-time record with Tiger Woods — needs a historically improbable charge just to stay relevant entering the final round.

McIlroy has produced dramatic weekends at East Lake before. But six shots over two rounds in a 30-player event, with Lee and Scheffler still ahead of him, represents a significant challenge. He'll need to get very low very quickly on Saturday to keep his record-chasing bid alive.

What's Next

Round 3 is underway at East Lake on Saturday, August 29. The final round concludes the 2026 PGA Tour season on Sunday, August 30. The winner claims $10 million from the $40 million total purse and takes home the FedExCup trophy.

Lee's lead is real. So is the talent behind him. Saturday afternoon at East Lake will narrow this down considerably before the championship is decided on Sunday.
