Sei Young Kim Leads LPGA FM Championship After Bogey-Free 64 — Rain Delay Adds Drama at TPC Boston

By Marcus Cole | August 29, 2026

Sei Young Kim entered the LPGA FM Championship at TPC Boston and immediately put the field on notice. The South Korean star fired a bogey-free 8-under 64 in Round 1 — powered by an eagle and six birdies — to grab the solo lead at the $4.4 million event. Round 2 was suspended by heavy rain on Friday, meaning Saturday brings compressed action as the tournament scrambles to catch up.

A Dominant Opening Round

Kim's 64 at TPC Boston in Norton, Massachusetts, was clean and clinical. Going bogey-free over a competitive LPGA layout is difficult at the best of times — doing it while mixing in an eagle and six birdies puts Kim in a commanding position going into the weekend.

The FM Championship, running August 27-30, is one of the LPGA Tour's marquee regular-season events. The $4.4 million purse attracts a strong field, and Kim's Round 1 performance sets a high early bar that the rest of the field now has to chase.

Rain Disrupts the Schedule

Heavy rain suspended play in Round 2 on Friday, leaving the tournament behind schedule. Saturday is now a compressed double-round day for portions of the field, adding a fatigue element and compressing the timetable for players trying to chase Kim down.

The disruption is a wildcard. Kim's early lead means she has some buffer — but condensed scoring windows in multi-round days can produce rapid leaderboard movement. Any player who catches fire in the resumed Round 2 could quickly close the gap before the Saturday round even begins.

The Field Around Her

The FM Championship field includes Nelly Korda, Brooke Henderson, and Lottie Woad — a strong collection of talent capable of producing tournament-winning weekends. Korda is one of the LPGA's most accomplished players and a consistent leaderboard threat. Henderson has multiple major wins. Woad has shown the kind of ball-striking that can produce quick low scores.

Miranda Wang won this event in 2025 at -20, edging Atthaya Thitikul by one shot. That sets a high scoring target for this year's field and shows the kind of score needed to win at TPC Boston when conditions are favorable.

What's Next

The LPGA FM Championship continues Saturday, August 29, with compressed rounds as the tournament catches up from Friday's rain suspension. The event concludes Sunday, August 30.

Kim enters the weekend with the lead and momentum from a strong opening round. Whether the compressed Saturday schedule — and the pressure that comes with defending an early lead — affects her game will be the key storyline as the tournament heads toward its conclusion.
