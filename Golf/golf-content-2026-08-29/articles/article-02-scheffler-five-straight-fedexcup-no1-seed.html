Scheffler's Historic FedExCup No. 1 Streak: Five Straight Years, $23 Million Locked, and Still Chasing More

By Ryan Calloway | August 29, 2026

There is no modern benchmark for what Scottie Scheffler has done in the FedExCup standings. No player in the history of the PGA Tour's postseason points system has entered the Tour Championship as the No. 1 seed five consecutive times. Scheffler has. And at East Lake this week, he is three shots back of the leader with two rounds remaining and very much in the hunt for a second FedExCup title to go with his unprecedented seeding streak.

Five Straight — The Numbers

The FedExCup standings reward consistency over an entire PGA Tour season. To finish No. 1 once requires a season of exceptional play. To do it five consecutive times requires something close to an unbroken run of elite golf across multiple calendar years.

Scheffler won the FedEx St. Jude Championship earlier in the 2026 season by eight shots — his 21st career PGA Tour victory. That win, combined with his season-long consistency, locked him into the top position for the fifth straight year. His $23 million FedEx bonus is already banked regardless of how this week ends.

A Previous FedExCup Champion

Scheffler won the FedExCup in 2024, converting his No. 1 seed into the actual championship. Tommy Fleetwood won the title in 2025 as the defending champion this week. Scheffler's 2024 victory proved that his dominance in the standings translates to results on the course when the stakes are highest.

That history matters at East Lake. Scheffler knows how to win here. He knows the layout, the pressure, and the format. Three shots back of Min Woo Lee in a 30-player field is not an obstacle — it's a starting position for someone who has already shown he can close at this venue.

The Second Title Question

Winning a second FedExCup alongside five consecutive No. 1 seeds would put Scheffler in a category of his own — not just historically strong, but categorically dominant in a way the Tour has never seen.

He is at -5 (T5) after Round 1, sharing the position with Ryan Gerard, Akshay Bhatia, and Ludvig Åberg. A move on Saturday would set up a final-round duel with Lee, who leads at -8. The field at East Lake is tight at the top, and Scheffler's experience gives him a meaningful edge over most of his co-contenders when Sunday pressure arrives.

What's Next

Round 3 at East Lake is underway on Saturday, August 29. The final round plays Sunday, August 30. Scheffler's most direct path to a second FedExCup title runs through a big Saturday — catching Lee before the final round would put him in familiar winning territory.

The streak is already history. The question now is whether the hardware can match it.
