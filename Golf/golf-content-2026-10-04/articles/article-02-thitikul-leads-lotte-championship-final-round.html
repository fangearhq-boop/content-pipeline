Jeeno Thitikul Leads LOTTE Championship by Three Shots Entering Final Round — Fourth Win of 2026 Within Reach

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | October 4, 2026

Jeeno Thitikul has done the hard part. Now comes Sunday.

The Thai golfer enters the final round of the LOTTE Championship at Hoakalei Country Club in Ewa Beach, Hawaii, with a three-shot lead over Ina Yoon of South Korea and Polly Mack of Germany. Thitikul's 54-hole total of 17-under par was built on a third round that began quietly and ended decisively — a 67 that featured four birdies across the back nine, including birdie-birdie on Nos. 17 and 18 to put the field firmly in her rearview.

If she closes out on Sunday, it will be Thitikul's fourth LPGA Tour victory of the 2026 season — and a statement in the Player of the Year race that no competitor has yet been able to match.

How She Got Here

Thitikul shared the 36-hole lead with Polly Mack before separating from the field in the third round. She went out in even par on Saturday's front nine — one birdie, one bogey — before the back nine provided the separation she needed.

Birdies on the first three holes of the back nine gave her momentum. Four straight pars steadied the ship. And her closes on 17 and 18 — two final birdies as the round ended — stretched what might have been a one-shot lead into three.

Yoon and Mack, both legitimate contenders, are close enough to make Sunday a real test. Three shots is not an unassailable margin in professional golf, particularly on a course where birdies are available and conditions can shift. But Thitikul has shown, across four weeks of winning golf in 2026, that she knows how to play with a lead.

The Stakes on Sunday

Four wins in a single LPGA Tour season would represent a remarkable level of consistency for any player in the world. Thitikul is currently the front-runner for LPGA Player of the Year, and a fourth title would likely seal that honor.

Hoakalei Country Club in Hawaii — a course that sits between the Pacific Ocean and the Ko Olina resort area on Oahu — provides one of the most picturesque settings in professional golf. The combination of oceanside scenery and tropical conditions makes the LOTTE Championship one of the tour's premium events, and Sunday's final round will draw significant attention.

Yoon, at 14-under, has been in contention throughout. Mack, also at 14-under, is playing in one of her best weeks of the year. Either player is capable of going low enough to apply pressure. But Thitikul starts as the clear favourite.

What's Next

The LOTTE Championship concludes Sunday, October 4, at Hoakalei Country Club. The LPGA Tour will then move on to its fall schedule as the 2026 season enters its final stretch.

For Thitikul, a win would mark the latest chapter in a season that has already placed her among the elite names in women's professional golf. The final round is the last step.
