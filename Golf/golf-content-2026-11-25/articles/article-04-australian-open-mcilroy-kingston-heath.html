Australian Open Dec 3-6 at Kingston Heath — McIlroy Headlines Elevated Field

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | November 25, 2026

The Australian golf summer has a marquee name. Rory McIlroy has confirmed he will play the 2026 Australian Open at Kingston Heath Golf Club in Melbourne, December 3-6 — and his presence elevates an event that is already enjoying a new era of prestige.

An Event Reborn

In June 2026, the PGA Tour committed to elevating the Australian Open as a co-sanctioned DP World Tour event, a move that dramatically raised the event's profile and prize fund. For Australian golf, it marked a turning point — a signal that the country's most prestigious national championship would once again attract the sport's biggest names on a consistent basis.

McIlroy's confirmation is the first major test of that commitment. His entry means the Australian Open will feature one of the game's most recognizable players on one of its most storied courses, before a crowd that has rarely had the chance to watch the world's best compete at home.

Kingston Heath

Kingston Heath Golf Club, opened in 1925, is widely regarded as one of the finest golf courses in the world. Located in the Sandbelt district southeast of Melbourne, it is a heathland course known for its strategic bunkering and demanding greens — the kind of test that rewards precision over power.

The course has hosted the Australian Open previously and is beloved by the game's elite. It is a course where preparation and course management are as important as raw ability, and where the gallery is as passionate and knowledgeable as any in the world.

Smith and Scott Expected

McIlroy will not be alone at the top of the field. Cameron Smith, currently competing at the BMW Australian PGA Championship at The Lakes in Sydney, is expected to continue his Australian summer at Kingston Heath. The Australian Open represents a second opportunity for Smith to earn an exemption through a co-sanctioned event — adding urgency to his appearance beyond national pride.

Adam Scott is also expected in the field. At 46, Scott is chasing the one title that has eluded him throughout one of the most decorated careers Australian golf has produced. His presence would complete an Australian triumvirate that gives the event the kind of generational narrative that tournament organizers dream about.

The Double-Header Takes Shape

The BMW Australian PGA Championship at The Lakes (November 27-30) and the Australian Open at Kingston Heath (December 3-6) form a prestige double-header unlike anything Australian golf has seen in recent years. Both events are co-sanctioned, both carry world ranking points, and both are drawing world-class fields.

For fans in Sydney and Melbourne, the next two weeks represent a rare opportunity to watch elite professional golf in their home country. The significance of McIlroy's entry for Kingston Heath cannot be overstated.

What's Next

The BMW Australian PGA Championship begins Thursday, November 27, at The Lakes in Sydney. The Australian Open follows December 3-6 at Kingston Heath Golf Club in Melbourne, with Rory McIlroy, Cameron Smith, and Adam Scott all expected in the field.
