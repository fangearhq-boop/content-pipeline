DP World Tour Gives Former LIV Players Invites to Season Opener — 8 in Field at Australian PGA

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | November 25, 2026

The BMW Australian PGA Championship at The Lakes in Sydney is shaping up to be more than a tournament. It is a visible symbol of a golf world actively reassembling itself.

Eight former LIV Golf players are in the field this week, and three of them — players who do not currently hold DP World Tour cards — received special invitations from the tour to participate. It is an unusual gesture, and an intentional one.

The Invitation Signals Something Larger

When a tour extends special invitations to players outside its membership structure, it rarely does so by accident. The DP World Tour's decision to bring three cardless former LIV players into its season-opening event in Australia reflects the broader transition currently underway in professional golf.

LIV Golf has effectively ceased operations following a reconciliation framework between the PGA Tour and the DP World Tour. That framework has opened the door for returning players — but the return is a process, not a switch. Some players have secured DP World Tour cards for 2027. Others are still working through the pathway. The invitations this week give a small group an earlier entry point.

DeChambeau Leads the 2027 Wave

The larger news comes from the confirmed list of players joining the DP World Tour as full members in 2027. Bryson DeChambeau leads a group of at least 10 former LIV players who have secured that status. Dustin Johnson, Jon Rahm, Bubba Watson, Patrick Reed, and Louis Oosthuizen are among those confirmed in the wave.

DeChambeau in particular has been public about his desire to compete at the highest level again. His return to a world-ranking-eligible tour is a headline moment for a player who won the U.S. Open and was among LIV's most prominent signings.

Rahm's inclusion is similarly significant. After winning the Masters in 2023 before his LIV departure, Rahm has remained one of the game's elite ball-strikers. His DP World Tour membership in 2027 will make him eligible to build toward PGA Tour status again, subject to the Tour's existing ban period.

The PGA Tour Ban Remains

It is worth being clear about one thing: the DP World Tour pathway does not immediately restore PGA Tour eligibility. The PGA Tour's policy remains that players are suspended for one year from the date of their last LIV Golf appearance. CEO Brian Rolapp has indicated that an update to this policy may come in early 2027, but as of now, the ban applies.

The DP World Tour serves as the re-entry circuit — the place where returning players rebuild ranking points, regain competitive sharpness, and demonstrate commitment to the mainstream tour structure. For DeChambeau, Rahm, and others, 2027 on the DP World Tour is a stepping stone toward PGA Tour reinstatement.

A Busy Field in Sydney

Back at The Lakes this week, the eight former LIV players in the field include Cameron Smith, who is openly pursuing a PGA Tour return via this very tournament. Their collective presence signals that the Australian summer has become a meaningful gathering point for golf's returning stars — and that the DP World Tour is actively managing that transition on a global stage.

What's Next

First-round play at the BMW Australian PGA Championship begins Thursday at The Lakes in Sydney. The Australian Open follows December 3-6 at Kingston Heath in Melbourne. The larger roster of returning LIV players, including DeChambeau, Rahm, and others, will begin their DP World Tour seasons in January 2027.
