LIV Bedminster Is One Week Away. Its CEO Still Won't Guarantee It Happens.

By Jake Torres | August 1, 2026

The LIV Golf New York tournament at Trump National Golf Club Bedminster is scheduled to begin August 6. Thirteen major champions are in the field. The course is ready. And LIV Golf CEO Scott O'Neil will not commit to the event running as planned.

That is the state of professional golf's alternative tour with one week to go before its marquee American event of the 2026 season.

What's On the Line at Bedminster

The Bedminster field is, on paper, one of the most decorated in any tour event this year. It includes Bryson DeChambeau, Jon Rahm, Tyrrell Hatton, Cameron Smith, Dustin Johnson, Phil Mickelson, Sergio Garcia, Patrick Reed, Bubba Watson, and Joaquin Niemann, among others — a combined 23 major championships across 13 major champions.

The event runs August 6-9 in Bedminster, New Jersey. It is supposed to be one of LIV Golf's signature events on the 2026 calendar.

The Financial Picture

The problem is LIV Golf's financial foundation has cracked significantly in 2026. The Saudi Public Investment Fund — the sovereign wealth fund that created LIV Golf and bankrolled its operation since launch — announced it would stop funding the league after 2026. LIV has been seeking between $250 million and $350 million in outside investment to replace that funding.

That investment has not materialized at the scale required. LIV already cancelled two events in 2026 — the New Orleans event and the Michigan event — citing the league's financial situation. Two cancellations in a single season is a significant disruption for a league that was still trying to establish legitimacy after years of PIF money keeping it afloat.

O'Neil's Non-Commitment

When pressed on whether the remaining 2026 schedule would run as planned, LIV CEO Scott O'Neil declined to guarantee it. That answer, at any professional sports league, would be remarkable. In LIV Golf's current situation, it signals that the Bedminster event's status is genuinely uncertain even as players prepare to tee it up in one week.

The players have not gone public with concerns. The course is prepared. But no one inside LIV's leadership has provided a public, unambiguous commitment that Bedminster happens on schedule.

What Comes After

If Bedminster runs, LIV has one more event on the 2026 calendar: an Indianapolis stop scheduled for August 20-23. After that, the league faces an offseason in which its entire financial future needs to be resolved before 2027 can begin.

The PIF's exit from funding creates a deadline. Without replacement capital or a structural overhaul, LIV's ability to operate beyond 2026 is in question. The players — many of whom signed guaranteed contracts worth hundreds of millions of dollars — are watching the financial situation closely, even if publicly most have chosen not to comment.

What's Next

LIV Golf New York at Trump National Golf Club Bedminster is currently scheduled to begin August 6 and run through August 9. Whether it proceeds as planned will depend on what LIV's leadership communicates in the coming days. The league's final 2026 event — Indianapolis — follows August 20-23. LIV's long-term financial situation is expected to come into sharper focus by the end of the year as the PIF's 2026 funding commitment runs out.
