## STORY 1: Solheim Cup — Two Days Out

The Solheim Cup starts in two days — and professional golf's greatest team competition is headed to the Netherlands.

Bernardus Golf in Cromvoirt hosts the 2026 edition, marking the first time the event has been played at this venue. USA captain Angela Stanford brings a defending championship squad. Europe captain Anna Nordqvist is ready with a roster she believes can end the Americans' reign.

Practice rounds and Pro-Am events begin September 7. The competition itself — foursomes and fourballs — kicks off September 11, with singles on September 13.

Nordqvist has said her pairings decisions feel like a "puzzle" because Europe has so many strong combinations available. That's a captain's dream scenario. Stanford, for her part, has a team that has answered that challenge before.

USA vs. Europe. Defending champions vs. a hungry home continent. Match play. September 11 through 13. #SolheimCup

## STORY 2: LIV Golf Bankruptcy

The league that changed professional golf is about to file for bankruptcy.

LIV Golf's Chapter 11 filing is expected as early as the week of September 7, according to multiple reports. The case will be heard in federal bankruptcy court in New Jersey. The Saudi Public Investment Fund, which bankrolled the league's launch and provided a recent emergency loan of less than $100 million, will be a primary creditor.

For the players — 48 of them spread across 12 teams with multi-year guaranteed contracts — the bankruptcy filing raises urgent questions. Chapter 11 reorganization or liquidation could leave those contracts worth a fraction of their face value. Players who received upfront signing payments may retain them; those waiting on future contract installments face significant uncertainty.

Greg Norman, LIV's founding CEO, helped recruit players by promising a global golf revolution backed by unlimited Saudi investment. The league delivered 14 events per season and reached a broadcast deal with The CW Network in the United States, but never generated revenue close to matching its guaranteed player costs.

The filing, when it comes, will mark the end of LIV Golf as it currently exists. What happens next — whether that means liquidation, acquisition, or some form of restructuring — remains to be seen. #LIVGolf

## STORY 3: Presidents Cup Rosters Confirmed

The Presidents Cup rosters are official — and the headline is who is NOT the USA captain.

Tiger Woods was long speculated as the leading candidate to captain the United States side. Instead, Brandt Snedeker has been named USA captain for the 2026 Presidents Cup. Snedeker, a popular PGA Tour veteran with a career-defining FedEx Cup title, brings experience and credibility to the role.

Geoff Ogilvy — the Australian who won the 2006 U.S. Open — leads the International side. The Presidents Cup pits USA against the rest of the world (excluding Europe, who participate in the Ryder Cup), and Ogilvy's team includes players from Australia, South Africa, Japan, South Korea, and across Latin America.

The story on the USA side is Jackson Koivun, the 21-year-old selected as a captain's pick. Koivun is the youngest player on either team and earns his first Presidents Cup appearance based on a strong 2026 season.

Full 12-man rosters are confirmed for both sides. #PresidentsCup

## STORY 4: Biltmore Championship Asheville

The PGA Tour has a brand-new stop — and it's in one of America's most scenic cities.

The Biltmore Championship Asheville makes its debut September 17-20 at The Cliffs at Walnut Cove in Arden, North Carolina. The venue sits in the mountains of western North Carolina near Asheville, a city known for its arts scene, outdoor culture, and proximity to the Blue Ridge Parkway.

The Cliffs at Walnut Cove is a private residential golf community designed for elite-level play. For one week, it opens to the PGA Tour and will host the first competitive round in its history as a tour venue.

Asheville takes its name from the Biltmore Estate nearby — the historic George Washington Vanderbilt II estate that remains the largest privately owned house in the United States. The tournament branding ties directly to that legacy.

This is the inaugural edition. Mark the calendar: western North Carolina is officially on the PGA Tour map. #BiltmoreChampionship #PGATour

## STORY 5: Nordqvist's Pairings Puzzle

Anna Nordqvist has a good problem.

As Europe's Solheim Cup captain, she's spent weeks building her 12-player roster and now faces the challenge of figuring out who plays with whom. In foursomes — alternate shot — pairing two players whose games complement each other is critical. In fourballs — both players play their own ball — you want aggression and confidence.

Nordqvist described the pairings decisions as a "puzzle" because Europe has so many viable combinations. That's the language of a captain who believes in her team.

The Solheim Cup is a match play event. Two points are available in each foursomes and fourballs session; one point per match in singles. A match not played is a point conceded. Every pairing decision carries weight.

Nordqvist is a three-time major champion who has played in multiple Solheim Cups herself. She knows exactly what these pairings mean. Match play starts September 11 at Bernardus Golf. The puzzle gets solved then. #SolheimCup
