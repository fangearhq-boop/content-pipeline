Solheim Cup 2026 Preview — USA and Europe Ready for Match Play Showdown at Bernardus Golf

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | September 5, 2026

In two days, the best women's golfers in the United States and Europe descend on Bernardus Golf in Cromvoirt, Netherlands, for the 2026 Solheim Cup. The competition begins September 7 with practice rounds and Pro-Am events. When match play starts September 11, one of professional golf's most intense rivalries resumes.

USA captain Angela Stanford carries the weight of defending champions. Europe captain Anna Nordqvist — herself a three-time major winner — is preparing to end that reign on home-continent soil.

The Format and Schedule

The Solheim Cup is a match play team competition played across three days of competition: foursomes and fourballs on September 11 and 12, followed by 12 singles matches on September 13. Each session carries points, and each match is either won, lost, or halved. The team that first reaches 14 points wins the cup.

Bernardus Golf is hosting the Solheim Cup for the first time. The course's parkland layout in the Dutch countryside provides a different test from the links-adjacent venues that often host the European leg — more manicured, more enclosed, with premium on ball-striking consistency across 18 holes.

Angela Stanford's USA Side

Stanford took over the USA captaincy with a clear mandate: keep winning. The American team has been dominant in recent editions of the Solheim Cup, and Stanford's roster reflects that continued depth. The United States has historically relied on strong major champions and world-ranked players to impose their quality in match play.

Defending the cup carries psychological weight. USA players know what it feels like to win the Solheim Cup. Stanford's task is channeling that confidence while preparing for an opponent playing with home-continent energy.

Anna Nordqvist's Europe Puzzle

Europe's Nordqvist described her pairings decisions as a "puzzle" with many options — the kind of problem a captain wants to have. The Europe roster includes a blend of experienced Solheim Cup campaigners and talented players making their debuts at this level.

In foursomes — alternate shot — pairing chemistry is everything. Veterans who know how to manage match play pressure are typically paired with younger players who bring raw energy. Nordqvist herself has lived those moments as a player. Now she brings that experience to the captain's room.

Europe has not won the Solheim Cup since 2019. Home-continent advantage at Bernardus Golf could be a factor. Dutch fans, European supporters, and the broader LPGA community will be watching to see whether Nordqvist can restore Europe to the top of the standings.

What's Next

Practice rounds begin Saturday, September 7. Pairings for the first session — foursomes on Thursday, September 11 — will be announced in the days before play begins. Both captains have full roster flexibility: they choose who plays and who sits for each session, and the order of singles is submitted simultaneously on the morning of September 13.

The Solheim Cup is professional golf's most compelling head-to-head team competition. Two days from now, Bernardus Golf finds out exactly what that looks like.
