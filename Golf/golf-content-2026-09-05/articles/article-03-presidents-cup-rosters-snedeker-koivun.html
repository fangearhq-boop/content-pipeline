Presidents Cup Rosters Set — Snedeker Leads USA, Ogilvy Captains International, Koivun Is the Surprise Pick

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | September 5, 2026

The 2026 Presidents Cup has its captains, its rosters, and its storylines. Full 12-man teams for both the United States and the International side were confirmed September 1, setting the stage for a biennial competition that pits USA against the rest of the world's best outside of Europe.

The headline from the USA announcement: it's Brandt Snedeker, not Tiger Woods.

Snedeker Gets the Nod

Tiger Woods had been the widely discussed candidate for the USA captaincy. His name carried the weight of a 15-time major champion, a cultural figure who transformed the sport, and a man who has gradually increased his involvement in golf administration and competition. The speculation was understandable.

But the PGA Tour's Presidents Cup committee selected Brandt Snedeker. The selection makes its own kind of sense. Snedeker, a popular figure in the locker room and a player who won the FedEx Cup in 2012, brings genuine credibility as a captain. He played in Presidents Cup teams himself, understands the match play dynamic, and has the trust of players who have competed alongside him at the tour level.

The USA roster reflects a team built around current world ranking and 2026 season performance. Snedeker's captain's picks completed the 12-player group, and among those picks is the most discussed selection on either side.

Jackson Koivun at 21

Jackson Koivun is 21 years old and is on the USA Presidents Cup team. Snedeker used a captain's pick on the youngest American to make either side's roster — a statement of belief in a player who has turned heads in 2026 with performances that justified the selection.

Koivun's presence reflects a calculation that match play can suit players who bring aggressive, committed golf. At 21, with no Presidents Cup history to weigh him down, Koivun arrives as a wild card — the kind of player who could make a session-winning difference or contribute meaningfully to the overall result. Snedeker's willingness to make that pick signals confidence both in the player and in his team's overall depth.

Geoff Ogilvy's International Side

Geoff Ogilvy — the Australian who won the 2006 U.S. Open at Winged Foot — leads the International team. The Presidents Cup International side draws from a broad pool: Australia, South Africa, Japan, South Korea, Colombia, and beyond. Ogilvy's own career gave him experience at the highest levels of major championship golf, and his captaincy brings a respected name and a tactician's approach.

The International side has historically been the underdog in Presidents Cup history, with USA holding a significant all-time record in the competition. Ogilvy's task is to build a team culture that can overcome that history and close the gap against a strong American squad.

What's Next

With rosters set, focus turns to the event itself, scheduled for later in the 2026 season. Presidents Cup week brings together a format — best-ball, foursomes, and singles — that rewards team cohesion as much as individual excellence. Both captains now have months to prepare their pairings strategies and set their teams' mindset. Snedeker and Ogilvy have their rosters. The competition begins when both teams tee it up.
