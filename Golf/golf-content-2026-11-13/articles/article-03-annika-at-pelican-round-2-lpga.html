ANNIKA at Pelican Enters Round 2 — LPGA's Penultimate Event Heats Up in Belleair

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | November 13, 2026

The ANNIKA driven by Gainbridge at Pelican is in Round 2 at Pelican Golf Club in Belleair, Florida, as the LPGA Tour's penultimate event of 2026 gets underway with a $3.25 million purse and player-of-the-year implications on the line.

With the 2026 LPGA Tour season down to its final two weeks, every stroke at the ANNIKA driven by Gainbridge at Pelican carries extra weight.

Round 2 is in progress at Pelican Golf Club in Belleair, Florida, as the field works through the second day of a $3.25 million tournament that runs November 12-15. For players with a shot at player-of-the-year honors, season-ending bonuses, or a spot in the Tour's elite categories, this week — and next — could be defining.

A Prestigious Venue for a High-Stakes Week

Pelican Golf Club has become one of the LPGA's most popular and challenging venues on the fall calendar. The course's layout demands precision off the tee, accuracy into the greens, and the kind of putting touch that separates the best from the very best under late-season pressure.

The tournament's title sponsor and namesake, ANNIKA Sörenstam, has long been associated with excellence in women's golf, and the event named in her honor consistently attracts the full strength of the LPGA field. This year is no different, with many of the tour's top players competing in Belleair as the season enters its final chapter.

The LPGA Finish Line Is in Sight

With the 2026 LPGA Tour season scheduled to conclude on November 22, the ANNIKA at Pelican is the second-to-last event before the curtain falls. Players with unfinished business — whether chasing titles, rankings milestones, or season-long honors — have only two tournaments left to accomplish their goals.

The player-of-the-year race, which has featured multiple contenders throughout the 2026 season, is expected to come down to the wire. The ANNIKA and next week's finale could be decisive in determining the year's ultimate award.
