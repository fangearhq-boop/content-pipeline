Austin Championship Round 2: Van Rooyen and Greyserman Co-Lead at the Fazio Canyons Course

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | November 13, 2026

Erik van Rooyen and Max Greyserman share the lead at 8-under after opening 64s at the Austin Championship presented by Compliance Solutions, with Round 2 underway at Omni Barton Creek Resort in Austin, Texas.

The PGA Tour's FedEx Cup Fall schedule has arrived in Austin, Texas, and Round 2 of the Austin Championship presented by Compliance Solutions is in full swing at the Fazio Canyons Course at Omni Barton Creek Resort & Spa.

Erik van Rooyen and Max Greyserman co-lead the $6 million event after both carded opening rounds of 8-under 64 on Thursday. The two players will be among the groups to watch as the tournament moves into its second day of competition, with the cut line expected to be drawn at the halfway point Friday evening.

Van Rooyen in Form — and in the Headlines

Van Rooyen has been one of the more visible figures in professional golf this week — not just on the scorecard, but in the media conversation. The South African has been vocal in his stance that former LIV Golf players should be required to regain PGA Tour status through qualifying school rather than receiving any preferential reinstatement following LIV's dissolution. His comments generated widespread reaction and have kept him in the spotlight heading into the weekend.

On the course, however, van Rooyen has been all business. His 64 placed him at the top of the leaderboard alongside Greyserman, and his combination of off-course visibility and on-course performance makes him the central figure of an Austin Championship week that has been unusually eventful before the halfway stage.

Greyserman Presses for His First Win

Max Greyserman has been among the PGA Tour's more consistent performers in recent seasons, and his opening 64 represents a strong bid for a breakthrough victory. Co-leading after Round 1 on a final-stretch tournament with six-figure FedEx Cup points implications gives Greyserman a genuine opportunity to announce himself on the sport's biggest stage.

What's Next

Rounds 3 and 4 are scheduled for the weekend, with the Austin Championship concluding on Sunday, November 15. The $6 million purse and the FedEx Cup points on offer make this a critical event for Tour members looking to maximize their standing in the season's closing weeks.
