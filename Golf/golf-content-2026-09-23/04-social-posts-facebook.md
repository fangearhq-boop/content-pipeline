### STORY 2: PGA Tour Slams the Door — LIV Players Have No Easy Path Back

**FB Long-Form**
The door is closed. PGA Tour CEO Brian Rolapp confirmed it on September 17: the Tour is "not currently contemplating a returning member programme" for LIV Golf players.

In January 2026, the PGA Tour opened a narrow window. Any LIV player who had won a Major or The Players Championship between 2022 and 2025 could apply for reinstatement — at a cost. The returning player had to donate $5 million to charity and forfeit five years of Tour equity. Brooks Koepka was the only player to accept those terms.

Jon Rahm said no. Bryson DeChambeau said no. Cameron Smith said no.

Now the LIV Golf bankruptcy restructuring deadline is October 13 — 20 days away. The Tour filed for Chapter 11 protection with up to $1 billion in listed liabilities. The players who passed on Koepka's deal in January are now facing a deadline without a safety net.

What comes next for Rahm, DeChambeau, and Smith is genuinely unknown. The PGA Tour says it is not planning another programme. The options are narrowing.
