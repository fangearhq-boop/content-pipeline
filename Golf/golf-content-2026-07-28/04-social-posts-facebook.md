### STORY 2: LIV Michigan in Jeopardy

#### Long-Form Post — 10:30 AM ET

```
The LIV Golf season-ending team championship is scheduled for August 27-30 at The Cardinal at Saint John's Resort in Plymouth Township, Michigan. That is four weeks away.

According to reporting from the Detroit News and Front Office Sports dated July 23, there is no infrastructure on site. Zero. No hospitality suites. No concert stage. No player walking bridge over the signature pond. No signage. Nothing has been built. The event is a month away and the site looks like a golf course — not a tournament venue.

Martin Kaymer, the captain of Cleeks GC and a former major champion, was asked about it. He gave the event a 5% chance of happening. "Highly unlikely," he said.

The context behind that assessment is financial. Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, which spent approximately $5.3 billion backing LIV Golf since 2022, has cut off financial support. LIV is seeking at least $300 million in replacement funding. Contractors working on the Michigan event have had their travel and accommodation canceled. Some are waiting for payment.

LIV Golf already canceled the New Orleans event earlier in 2026. The Michigan team championship would be the season finale — the capstone of the year for a league that is now struggling to finish it.

The event remains on the official schedule. Based on everything being reported, that may be the last place it exists.
```
