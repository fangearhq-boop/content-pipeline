Nelly Korda Arrives at Royal Lytham Hunting Her Third Major of 2026 — The AIG Women's Open Is Here

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | July 28, 2026

Two majors already in 2026. Four career. A record $10 million purse. The final women's major of the year starts Thursday in Lancashire.

The final women's major championship of 2026 begins Thursday at Royal Lytham & St Annes in Lancashire, England. For Nelly Korda, the world No. 1, that is one more opportunity to extend what is already one of the most dominant seasons a women's golfer has ever produced.

Korda has won two major championships in 2026. She won the Chevron Championship in April. She won the US Women's Open in June. Those two titles gave her four career major championships. A win at Royal Lytham & St Annes would give her five career majors and three in a single season — a standard reached by very few players in the history of the sport.

CBS Sports has described Korda as "eyeing a career Grand Slam."

The Championship Details

The AIG Women's Open is the 50th Women's British Open, the 26th edition as an LPGA major championship, and the 7th under AIG sponsorship. Round 1 begins Thursday, July 30. The championship runs through Sunday, August 2. The field is 144 players.

The prize fund is $10 million — a record for this event. For a major championship that has historically lagged the men's game in prize money, the $10 million mark represents a significant benchmark for the women's game.

Royal Lytham & St Annes is one of the most historic links in England. The course has hosted The Open Championship eleven times and has produced some of the sport's most memorable moments. It is a demanding, traditional test — the kind of course that separates the best from the rest across four rounds.

What Korda Is Chasing

Korda's four career majors are the 2021 KPMG Women's PGA Championship, the 2024 Chevron Championship, the 2026 Chevron Championship, and the 2026 US Women's Open. Adding the AIG Women's Open to that list would complete a career collection that includes all five women's major championships — the career Grand Slam.

Players who have won three majors in a single season are a rare group. The names that belong on that list are names that define eras. Korda is being discussed in that context not because of speculation, but because she has already won two of the five this year and has two days until she competes for the third.

The field of 144 players will include the best women's golfers in the world. They will all have a week to beat Korda at Royal Lytham. Based on what she has done in 2026, that has proven to be a difficult assignment.
