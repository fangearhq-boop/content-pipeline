The Numbers Behind Lucas Herbert's -30 at LIV Golf UK — Three Records, One Historic Week

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | July 28, 2026

The winning score is the headline. The records underneath it explain why what happened at JCB Golf & Country Club was the greatest individual week of scoring in LIV Golf history.

Lucas Herbert won LIV Golf United Kingdom at JCB Golf & Country Club in Staffordshire at -30. That is the number that tells you what happened. The numbers underneath it tell you why it was historic.

-30 is the lowest winning total in LIV Golf League history. The previous record was -26. Herbert did not edge it — he shattered it by four shots. In four rounds at a golf course in Rocester, the Australian produced the greatest individual scoring performance the league has ever seen.

The Full Record Count

By the time Herbert completed his final round, three records had been set. The -30 winning total was one. The second was 30 birdies in the week — the most birdies ever recorded in a single LIV event. The third was 32 combined birdies and eagles across the four rounds — again, the most ever in a single LIV event.

Those three records together describe a player who did not simply play good golf. He played a kind of golf that the format had not previously produced — aggressive, relentless, and sustained across all 72 holes.

How He Built It

Herbert's round-by-round scores were 61, 66, 69, and 62. He opened with a bogey-free 9-under 61 — a course record at JCB Golf & Country Club — and he closed with a bogey-free 10-under 62. His worst round of the week was a 69, which would have contended in most tournaments. In this one, it was his floor.

He led wire-to-wire. His closest pursuer at the end was Tyrrell Hatton, who finished at -24 — six shots back. Hatton's total, in a normal week, would be a commanding winning score. This was not a normal week.

Ripper GC Completes the Sweep

Herbert's team, Ripper GC, also won the team title at LIV Golf UK, sweeping both individual and team honors from the same event. The team title added a collective dimension to what was already a dominant individual performance.

Ripper GC is the Australian-themed team on the LIV circuit. The JCB Golf & Country Club result puts them in the conversation for the team championship — the very event in Michigan whose survival is now in question due to LIV's financial situation.

What the Record Means

LIV Golf's format — 54 holes in most regular events, 72 in team championships — does not always produce the kind of sustained scoring assault that 72-hole stroke play can. At JCB Golf & Country Club over July 23-26, Herbert turned the 72-hole format into a showcase. -30. 30 birdies. 32 birds and eagles. Six shots clear. Wire-to-wire.

The records at -26 had stood until last week. Herbert's -30 will stand until someone plays better than the greatest individual week in LIV Golf history. Given the margin, that will take time.
