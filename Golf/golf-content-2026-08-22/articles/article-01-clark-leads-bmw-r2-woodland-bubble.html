Clark Shoots Back-to-Back 64s to Lead BMW Championship; Woodland Storms Off FedExCup Bubble

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap

August 22, 2026

Wyndham Clark has turned the BMW Championship at Bellerive Country Club into a showcase.

Clark shot a second consecutive 64 on Friday to reach -12 through 36 holes, taking the solo lead heading into the weekend of this FedExCup Playoffs event. Back-to-back rounds of 64 on a par-70 layout that has tested the field represent some of the finest sustained ball-striking seen at this tournament in years.

He leads Gary Woodland by one shot. Woodland is running his own compelling story this week — and the FedExCup implications make it all the more significant.

Clark's Dominant Start

Clark's 64 on Thursday set the tone. His 64 on Friday locked in his position at the top of the leaderboard. Where most players see the second round of a major stroke-play event as a day to protect, Clark treated it as an opportunity to extend.

The result is a number that gives him genuine cushion heading into the weekend: -12 through 36 holes, with Woodland at -11, J.J. Spaun at -10, and Chris Gotterup at -9 rounding out the top four.

Clark has been one of the more complete players on tour in 2026. His tee-to-green numbers at Bellerive have been among the best in the field, and his putting has matched his iron play. Going wire-to-wire at a FedExCup Playoff event from this position is not a given — but Clark is giving himself every opportunity.

Woodland and the Bubble

The secondary story at Bellerive this week has been Gary Woodland.

Woodland entered the BMW Championship at 31st in the FedExCup standings — one spot outside the top 30 that advance to the Tour Championship at East Lake. His opening 64 on Thursday moved him into serious contention. His 65 on Friday moved him inside the bubble.

Rickie Fowler, who entered the week at exactly 30th — the last qualifying spot for East Lake — is now on the wrong side of that line as Woodland climbs. Steve Cauley entered at 29th and is also feeling the shift.

FedExCup standings recalculate after each round. Saturday's Round 3 will reset the picture again. But for Woodland, a 64-65 start to a week when his FedExCup future was in serious doubt is exactly the kind of performance that the playoffs are designed to produce.

Scheffler's Back-Nine Surge

The other development worth tracking Friday afternoon was Scottie Scheffler's round.

The World No. 1 opened at +2 on Thursday and found himself well outside the top 10 heading into Round 2. On Friday, something clicked. Scheffler's back nine was 30 strokes — the kind of number that can shift a tournament's narrative even when you're not close to the lead.

Scheffler finished at -5 through 36 holes. He's seven back of Clark. A title charge would require extraordinary golf — but the back-nine 30 suggests his game is arriving at Bellerive, and seven shots is not an impossible deficit over two rounds if the course conditions cooperate.

What's Next

Round 3 is Saturday at Bellerive Country Club. Clark holds the lead by one shot over Woodland. The top 30 players in the FedExCup standings after the BMW Championship advance to the Tour Championship at East Lake Golf Club in Atlanta (August 27-30).

The bubble picture will come into sharper focus after Saturday's round. Clark's lead is real, but this field — Scheffler, Woodland, Spaun, and the rest — is too talented to assume the result is already decided.
