## STORY 2: LIV Golf Deadline Extended

**Long-Form Post:**
The October 13 deadline for LIV Golf players to commit to the LIV 2.0 restructuring plan has been pushed back — and the deal itself has become clearer.

BC Partners, the London-based private-equity firm leading the investment, confirmed that an initial $300 million is in place. Players who sign must convert at least 50% of their unpaid contract value into a 52.5% equity stake in the reorganized entity — meaning they become majority owners but sacrifice the money they were originally owed.

The new commitment deadline is October 25. Reports indicate that enough players have signed on to satisfy the threshold required for the deal to proceed (50% of players with claims, representing at least two-thirds of the total dollar claims outstanding).

Jon Rahm and Bryson DeChambeau are among the notable names whose futures remain uncertain.

What does the new LIV look like? Likely around 10 principal events in 2027 with reduced prize purses — a significant step down from the original, Saudi-PIF-funded model that spent more than $5 billion over five years.

## STORY 3: Jeeno Thitikul Wins LOTTE Championship

**Long-Form Post:**
Jeeno Thitikul's fourth LPGA Tour win of 2026 almost wasn't. Standing over her ball on the par-4 16th at Hoakalei Country Club, holding a two-shot lead with three holes to play, she drove into the water.

Two-shot lead: gone in seconds.

What happened next is the reason she is one of the most compelling competitors in women's golf. On the par-4 17th, she drained a 20-foot birdie putt — a two-shot swing to reclaim the lead. On the par-5 18th, she finished with another birdie to close with a 4-under 68 and win the 2026 LOTTE Championship by three shots over Ina Yoon at 21-under.

Four wins in 2026. That matches Nelly Korda's total on the season. The LPGA Player of the Year race heads into the Asian swing — Shanghai, South Korea, Malaysia — with these two players neck and neck.
