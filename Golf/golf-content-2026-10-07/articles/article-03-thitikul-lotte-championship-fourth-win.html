Thitikul's LOTTE Championship Win — Four Wins, One Dramatic Finish

By Marcus Cole | October 7, 2026

Jeeno Thitikul did not make it look easy. But she made it look like a winner. In a final round at Hoakalei Country Club in Hawaii that came within seconds of going sideways, Thitikul drove into the water on the par-4 16th, surrendered a two-shot lead, and then responded with the two best swings of her week.

A 20-foot birdie putt on the par-4 17th. A closing birdie on the par-5 18th. A three-shot victory over Ina Yoon. Her fourth LPGA Tour win of the 2026 season.

The Moment on 16

Leading by two shots with three holes remaining, Thitikul's tee shot on the par-4 16th found the water. The penalty, the drop, and the subsequent bogey erased the cushion she had built over three and a half rounds at Hoakalei. Playing partners and leaderboard watchers felt the shift immediately.

What separates the best players from the field is the response to that moment. Thitikul walked to the 17th tee with the same composure she had carried all week and proceeded to produce the defining shot of the tournament. A 20-foot birdie putt that broke back the two-shot swing and re-established her lead. By the time she birdied the 18th — a 4-under 68 to close — the margin was three shots.

Four Wins in 2026

With the LOTTE Championship in hand, Thitikul now has four LPGA Tour wins in the 2026 season — matching Nelly Korda's total. The LPGA Player of the Year race, which had been hovering between these two players for months, heads into the Asian swing effectively tied.

Thitikul's four wins have been spread across different course types and conditions, which speaks to the versatility of her game. She has not dominated through one particular strength but through a combination of driving accuracy, short-game precision, and a competitive composure that has made her one of the most reliable finishers in the world when she has the lead entering Sunday.

What the Win Means for the Player of the Year Race

Player of the Year on the LPGA Tour is decided not only by win totals but by the quality and significance of those wins. Thitikul's 2026 résumé now includes four titles. Korda's four wins have included strong results at major events. How the counting criteria ultimately weigh the two résumés will be one of the most closely watched questions over the final weeks of the season.

The Asian swing — Shanghai, South Korea, Kuala Lumpur — runs three consecutive weeks starting October 15. Any separation between Thitikul and Korda during that stretch could settle the award before the season closes.

What's Next

The LPGA Tour has a week off before resuming at the Buick LPGA Shanghai on October 15 at Sheshan Golf Club. Thitikul will carry her LOTTE Championship momentum directly into China, where she has competed well in the past. The Player of the Year race is wide open, and every tee shot over the next three weeks could determine how it ends.
