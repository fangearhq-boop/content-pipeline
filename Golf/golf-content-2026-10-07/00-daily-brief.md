## Today's Top Stories

### STORY 1: Baycurrent Classic Starts Thursday — Yokohama's Biggest Week
The PGA Tour's only annual stop in Japan kicks off Thursday at Yokohama Country Club. Defending champion Xander Schauffele leads a 72-player, no-cut field that also features world No. 5 Wyndham Clark (the highest-ranked entrant), Hideki Matsuyama on home soil, Jordan Spieth, Rickie Fowler, and Colin Morikawa. The $8 million purse carries 500 FedExCup Fall points — the third of eight Fall events. Tom Kim withdrew after winning the Asian Games.

### STORY 2: LIV Golf Pushes Its Deadline to October 25
The deadline for LIV players to commit to the LIV 2.0 restructuring plan has been extended from October 13 to October 25, according to reports. BC Partners confirmed an initial $300 million investment is in place. Players who sign must convert at least 50% of their unpaid contract value into a 52.5% equity stake in the reorganized entity. Enough players have reportedly committed to satisfy the threshold, though Jon Rahm and Bryson DeChambeau remain undecided about their futures.

### STORY 3: Jeeno Thitikul Wins LOTTE Championship — Fourth Win of 2026
Jeeno Thitikul won the LPGA Tour's LOTTE Championship at Hoakalei Country Club in Hawaii on Sunday, closing with a 4-under 68 for a 21-under total and a three-shot victory over Ina Yoon. It was Thitikul's fourth LPGA Tour win of the 2026 season — matching Nelly Korda's tally — and came with a dramatic finish: a water ball on 16 was erased by a 20-foot birdie putt on 17 and a closing birdie on 18.

### STORY 4: Wyndham Clark Arrives at Yokohama as the Season's Standout
Clark is the highest-ranked player in the Baycurrent field at world No. 5, having risen from No. 75 at the start of the year after winning the CJ Cup Byron Nelson and the U.S. Open at Shinnecock Hills. His wire-to-wire US Open win led a run of form that has made him a genuine Player of the Year candidate. At Yokohama this week he and Schauffele represent the two pillars of a loaded, competitive field.

### STORY 5: LPGA Asian Swing Heads to Shanghai After Hawaii
With the LOTTE Championship settled, the LPGA Tour moves into its Asian stretch. Next up is the Buick LPGA Shanghai at Sheshan Golf Club in Shanghai, China, October 15-18, with a $3.2 million purse. The BMW Ladies Championship in South Korea follows October 22-25, then the Maybank Championship in Kuala Lumpur October 29 through November 1. For Thitikul and the race for LPGA Player of the Year, every result over this stretch carries added weight.
