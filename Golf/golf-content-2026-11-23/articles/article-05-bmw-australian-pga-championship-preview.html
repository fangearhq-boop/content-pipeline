BMW Australian PGA Championship Preview: The Lakes Golf Club, Sydney, November 26-29

By Jake Torres | November 23, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

The BMW Australian PGA Championship returns to The Lakes Golf Club in Sydney this Thursday for one of the final tournaments on the global golf calendar, with a four-day event running November 26-29 that brings together an international field on one of Australia's most historic layouts.

The Lakes has been a fixture in Australian golf for generations, and the PGA Championship's return to the Sydney venue provides a compelling setting for what will be one of the year's final competitive rounds before the professional game shifts into its offseason preparations and planning mode.

Post-LIV Context

This year's Australian PGA arrives at a moment of genuine transition for professional golf in Australia. The collapse of LIV Golf — which included an Australian Ripper GC franchise that employed several of the country's most prominent players — has affected the competitive plans of a number of Australians who had built their schedules around LIV's calendar.

Some former Ripper GC players will be working through their post-LIV options at events like the BMW Australian PGA as they assess their paths for 2027. The DP World Tour's decision to open routes to some 19 former LIV players creates opportunities, but Australian players will also be evaluating PGA Tour pathways, Asian Tour options and domestic events as they rebuild their schedules.

The Lakes Golf Club

The Lakes Golf Club in Sydney is a parkland course with significant history in Australian professional golf. The venue has hosted major championships and elite events across multiple decades, and its design presents the kind of strategic variety that separates the best ball-strikers from the field on firm, fast surfaces typical of late November in New South Wales.

What's Next

The BMW Australian PGA Championship runs November 26-29. Following this event, the global professional golf calendar moves toward its end-of-year break, with most tours resuming in January 2027. The Australian golf season typically offers strong international fields in the late November to January window, and this year's lineup reflects the sport's post-LIV transition as players from multiple circuits converge for one of the season's final competitive events.
