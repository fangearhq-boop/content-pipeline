DeChambeau Leads LIV Exodus to DP World Tour as Former Stars Find Post-LIV Homes for 2027

By Marcus Cole | November 23, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

As LIV Golf heads toward its January 2027 liquidation, the sport's biggest displaced names are finding new homes — and the DP World Tour is absorbing much of the fallout. Bryson DeChambeau leads a wave of former LIV players confirmed for the European circuit in 2027, joining as a Category 1 full member via the exemption he earned by winning the 2024 U.S. Open.

Jon Rahm and Louis Oosthuizen are also back on the DP World Tour through career exemptions, alongside Tyrrell Hatton. Dustin Johnson, Cameron Smith and Bubba Watson have signed agreements for specific DP World Tour tournaments. In total, some 19 former LIV players have found routes into the European circuit's ecosystem, in what amounts to a wholesale migration of talent from a collapsed league to the tour that offers their clearest path to major championship qualification.

DeChambeau's Position

DeChambeau's route to the DP World Tour is the most high-profile of the group. His Category 1 membership — the highest classification — via his 2024 U.S. Open title gives him access to any tournament he chooses on the European circuit. That flexibility matters for a player who has had a complicated few years, shifting from the PGA Tour to LIV and now navigating a path back to relevance in golf's major championship ecosystem.

His PGA Tour situation remains unresolved. DeChambeau faces what reports describe as at least a 12-month ineligibility period from the American circuit, counting from his last LIV appearance. That timeline means his 2027 season runs primarily through the DP World Tour, with major championship access through that circuit's world ranking qualification pathways.

Rahm, Oosthuizen and the Others

Jon Rahm's return completes a cycle that began when he left for LIV in December 2023 for a reported $300 million-plus deal. The two-time major champion has been among the most prominent figures in the LIV experiment, and his return to the DP World Tour — which includes his eligibility for the Race to Dubai and world ranking points — restores his path to the full range of professional golf's opportunities.

Oosthuizen, a beloved figure in the game and a former Open Championship winner, returns to the European circuit having spent his LIV years with the Stinger GC team. He has a wealth of major championship experience and world ranking implications that come with DP World Tour membership. Garcia and Westwood, veterans of the European circuit, are also returning after their LIV stints — a full restoration of a generation of talent that briefly stepped away.

What's Next

The DP World Tour's 2027 schedule begins in January. For DeChambeau, Rahm and the others, the season will represent a fresh start — building world ranking points, re-establishing relationships with sponsors and fans in the established circuits, and demonstrating that the talent that made them compelling enough for LIV to pay record figures is still there. The PGA Tour's January 2027 decision timeline on reinstatement will be the next major milestone for how this group ultimately reintegrates into professional golf's full landscape.
