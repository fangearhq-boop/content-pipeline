### STORY 1: Thitikul CME Three-Peat

**Facts (HIGH confidence):**
- Tournament: 2026 CME Group Tour Championship
- Venue: Tiburón Golf Club, Naples, Florida
- Winner: Atthaya "Jeeno" Thitikul
- Final round: 7-under 65
- Winning margin: 2 shots over Aki Iwai
- Nelly Korda finished 3rd, 4 shots back
- First player in CME event history to win three consecutive times
- First LPGA player since Inbee Park (2013-15, Women's PGA Championship) to win same tournament 3 straight years
- Prize: $4 million (largest in women's golf)
- Fifth LPGA title of 2026
- Career earnings after win: $24,292,858 — surpasses Annika Sorenstam's LPGA career earnings record
- Sources: NBC Sports, Yahoo Sports, Sky Sports, Golf Magic, Irish Golf Desk (all confirmed)

**Facts (HIGH confidence):**

- Date: November 8, 2026
- Final round: 9-under 63 (rallied from 7-shot deficit)
