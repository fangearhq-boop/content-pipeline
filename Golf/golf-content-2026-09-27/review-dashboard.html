LIV Golf — BC Partners $300M Deal, Oct. 13 Player Commitment Deadline
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post LIV Golf's future is now in the hands of its players — and there are 16 days to decide. Here's the full picture of where things stand: 📌 September 8: LIV filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in New Jersey federal court. Total liabilities: $500 million to $1 billion. The trigger: Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund pulled its backing. 💰 The rescue deal: London-based private equity firm BC Partners is offering $300 million to fund a restructured "LIV 2.0" where players would become majority owners. The Saudi PIF agreed to provide $49.6 million in temporary financing to keep the lights on during the process. ⏰ The deadline: October 13. Players representing at least two-thirds of total claim value AND at least half of total claim count must commit to the restructuring — or the deal collapses. 💵 What players are owed: Jon Rahm is the largest creditor at approximately $7.5 million. Bryson DeChambeau is owed approximately $5.8 million. Dustin Johnson and Cameron Smith are also listed as unsecured creditors. Total owed to players: approximately $45 million. 🚪 The PGA Tour: Closed the return window. No pathway currently for LIV players. The question is whether Rahm, DeChambeau, Smith, and the rest will bet on themselves by committing to LIV 2.0 — or walk away from the money they're owed and try to find another path forward. October 13 is 16 days away. Comment below — do you think LIV 2.0 will actually happen? 👇 ### Image Post Caption LIV Golf Chapter 11: $300M BC Partners rescue. Oct. 13 player commitment deadline.
