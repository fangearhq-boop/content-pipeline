FM Championship Final Round: Corpuz, Kim, and Yamashita Tied at the Top as Drama at TPC Boston Reaches Its Peak

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | August 30, 2026

LPGA · FM Championship · TPC Boston · Women's Golf

After a week of rain delays, suspended rounds, and compressed scheduling, the 2026 LPGA FM Championship arrives at its conclusion today at TPC Boston in Norton, Massachusetts. A. Corpuz, Hyo Joo Kim, and Miyu Yamashita share the 54-hole lead at 9-under par. One of them — or someone directly behind them — will claim the $4.4 million purse and the FM Championship title before Sunday's final round is complete.

The Three Co-Leaders

Three players share the lead, and each brings a different story into Sunday's round.

A. Corpuz has been one of the steadiest players in the field all week, navigating the weather disruptions and compressed schedule without dropping out of contention. Her -9 total represents consistent scoring across three rounds that were anything but easy to manage logistically or mentally.

Hyo Joo Kim is one of the most decorated ball-strikers in women's golf, and her presence at the top of a final-round leaderboard is never a surprise. Kim has the kind of game — long, precise, and composed — that tends to hold up when 18 holes separate a player from a championship.

Miyu Yamashita enters the final round with momentum. The Japanese standout has been building toward performances like this all season, and her -9 score at TPC Boston represents the highest-stakes Sunday of her LPGA career.

The Weather Factor and Compressed Schedule

This championship has been defined as much by the weather as by the golf. Rain suspended Round 2 on Friday, forcing officials to compress the schedule on Saturday — meaning players completed both their second and third rounds in the same day. That kind of workload, both physical and mental, affects players differently. Some find momentum in the compressed rounds; others carry fatigue into Sunday.

By the time the final round tees off today, every player in the field will have managed an unusual week. Course management and energy management will be factors as much as shotmaking.

The Chasers

One shot back at -8 are Ruoning Yin, M. Metraux, and J. Bae. Any of the three is capable of a final round that reaches -13 or better, and a fast start could put any of them in the lead within the first three holes.

Sei Young Kim, who opened the tournament with a spectacular 8-under 64 that included an eagle and six birdies, is also in the field and capable of a charge. R1 leaders who fall back before the final round have a habit of finding something on Sunday.

Defending champion Miranda Wang won the 2025 FM Championship at -20 and knows TPC Boston as well as anyone in the field. Nelly Korda and Brooke Henderson round out a field that has genuine depth at the top of the leaderboard entering Sunday.

TPC Boston as the Test

TPC Boston is a traditional northeastern parkland track with the length and strategic demands that reward ball-strikers. The course plays differently in the damp conditions that often follow rain, and this week's weather has kept the turf softer than usual. That means more stopping power on approach shots — but also less roll on drives, which changes the calculus on several par-4 and par-5 setups.

The final holes at TPC Boston have historically produced drama in LPGA finales. This Sunday should be no different, with three co-leaders and a half-dozen legitimate contenders all converging on the same stretch of closing holes.

What's Next

The final round begins this morning at TPC Boston. By early afternoon, the 2026 LPGA FM Championship will have its champion. A three-way tie going into the final round of a major women's golf event is among the most compelling setups the LPGA calendar can produce — today delivers on that promise.
