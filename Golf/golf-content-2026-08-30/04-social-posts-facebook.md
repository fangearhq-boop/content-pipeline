Here is where things stand for LIV Golf as the 2026 season draws to a close:

🇸🇦 THE PIF EXIT
Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (PIF), which bankrolled LIV Golf from its 2022 launch, is withdrawing as the primary funder after this season. Over the course of the LIV experiment, PIF spent an estimated $6+ billion on operations, player contracts, and prize money. That era is over.

💰 THE NEW DEAL
According to Forbes, LIV Golf is "well on its way" to a new deal worth between $250 million and $350 million that would support the league through at least 2027 — funding the creation of "LIV 2.0." A new independent board of directors has been formed, and LIV's leadership is pursuing a "multi-partner investment model" rather than a single state-backed funder.

📅 THE 2027 SCHEDULE — TENTATIVE
LIV has already announced four tournament dates for 2027: Saudi Arabia, Australia, South Africa, and Hong Kong. But those commitments are contingent on the new funding deal closing.
