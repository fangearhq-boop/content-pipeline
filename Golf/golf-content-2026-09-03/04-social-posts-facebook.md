### STORY 1: LIV Golf Bankruptcy Expected by September 7

**Long-Form Post | 8:30 AM**

LIV Golf is about to change forever — and the filing could come as soon as this Sunday.

According to the Financial Times and multiple outlets, LIV Golf is preparing to file for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection as soon as September 7, 2026. The league, which launched in 2022 with Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund backing and more than $5 billion in investment, is now facing the end of that funding — and no replacement has been secured.

The immediate picture is stark. LIV has already laid off the majority of its 300+ employees. BC Partners Credit, the private equity firm whose credit deal was supposed to provide a bridge to LIV 2.0, has not signed a finalized agreement. Player settlements are being offered at pennies on the dollar for contracted players who were guaranteed millions through the life of their deals.

For the 50+ players still under LIV contract, the decision tree is hard: settle for cents and join the player-owned LIV 2.0 (10 events, reduced purses), settle and return to the PGA Tour, or refuse and pursue their full payment claims through the bankruptcy process. Jon Rahm — who won the LIV individual title for the third consecutive time at Indianapolis — is among the highest-profile names facing that choice.

Chapter 11 is a restructuring mechanism, not a death sentence. LIV could emerge from bankruptcy with a leaner structure and player equity at the core. But the gap between LIV's ambitions in 2022 and its reality in September 2026 is one of the most dramatic collapses in professional sports history.

Golf's landscape is about to get very complicated, very fast.

### STORY 2: Solheim Cup 2026 — Complete Rosters, Four Days Out

**Long-Form Post | 10:30 AM**

Four days from now, the 2026 Solheim Cup begins. And for the first time in the event's history, it happens in the Netherlands.

Bernardus Golf in Cromvoirt opens its gates September 7 for the opening ceremonies and practice rounds, with the competition proper running September 11-13. Team USA arrives as defending champions, having beaten Europe 15.5-12.5 in Virginia in 2024. Team Europe arrives with a point to prove — they won three consecutive Solheim Cups before that defeat and consider the 2024 result unfinished business.

Both 12-player squads are now complete.

**Team USA** (captain Angela Stanford): Nelly Korda, Angel Yin, Jennifer Kupcho, Yealimi Noh, Auston Kim, Lauren Coughlin, Allisen Corpuz, Andrea Lee, Alison Lee (nine auto qualifiers), plus captain's picks Megan Khang, Rose Zhang, and Lindy Duncan.

The format produces 28 total points across foursomes, four-ball, and singles. USA needs 14.5 to win the Cup. They need 14 to retain it as defending champions.

For USA, the pick of Rose Zhang — who went a perfect 4-0 at the 2024 match — and Megan Khang (fifth Solheim, 7-3-3 career record) speaks to Stanford's belief in experience and proven performers. For Europe, Nordqvist's four captain's picks include Mimi Rhodes and Nastasia Nadaud, adding unpredictability to a squad anchored by Hull and Ciganda's veteran presence.

Sunday, September 13 will decide it. But the talking starts now.

### STORY 3: Nelly Korda vs Charley Hull — Solheim Cup's Marquee Duel

**Long-Form Post | 1:30 PM**

In four days, the 2026 Solheim Cup begins. And while it's a team event, there's one individual rivalry at its center that will define how the week feels: Nelly Korda against Charley Hull.

Korda is the most dominant women's golfer in the world right now. World No. 1. Four LPGA wins in 2026, including two major championships. A 10-5-1 lifetime Solheim Cup record going into her fifth appearance. She enters Bernardus Golf in the form of her career and with a team around her — Rose Zhang (4-0 at the 2024 match), Megan Khang (fifth Solheim, 7-3-3 career), Jennifer Kupcho — that is deep and proven.

Hull is everything Europe needs her to be: experienced, fierce, and hungry. Eight consecutive Solheim Cup appearances is a record in the modern era. She topped the 2026 Ladies European Tour points list to lock in her automatic qualification. Dame Laura Davies — who knows what it means to lead at a Solheim Cup — publicly called on Hull to "step up and lead" this European squad in the Netherlands.

The thing about Charley Hull at a Solheim Cup is this: she doesn't play like a supporting character. She plays every match as if the whole thing depends on her. That mentality is exactly what Europe needs when Korda is on the other side of the fairway.

USA's depth advantage is real. But team events are decided by peak performances, not averages — and Hull, when she's right, is capable of the peak that changes a match.

Will Korda and Hull end up paired against each other in the singles finale? If they do, it'll be the most-watched match of the week.

### STORY 4: Presidents Cup 2026 — Three Weeks to Medinah

**Long-Form Post | 4:00 PM**

Three weeks from today, the 2026 Presidents Cup begins at Medinah Country Club — one of the most storied venues in the history of team golf. And the storylines are already stacking up.

Team USA enters under captain Brandt Snedeker and world No. 1 Scottie Scheffler with a record that is almost hard to believe: the United States has won ten consecutive Presidents Cups. The International team, captained by Geoff Ogilvy, has not won this event since 1998 — 28 years ago.

Both teams are set.

**Team USA**: Scottie Scheffler, Cameron Young, Wyndham Clark, Russell Henley, Sam Burns, Collin Morikawa (auto qualifiers) + Chris Gotterup, Xander Schauffele, Justin Thomas, Harrison Bridgeman, Patrick Cantlay, and Jackson Koivun (captain's picks).

The story that transcends the roster: Jackson Koivun. At 21 years old, he is the youngest captain's pick in Presidents Cup history. He walks into Medinah under enormous pressure — and the expectation that he will eventually become one of the faces of this American team for years to come.

Medinah No. 3 carries its own weight. This is the course of the 2012 Ryder Cup "Miracle at Medinah," where Europe came back from 10-6 down to win. The stadium atmosphere and gallery size at Medinah is unlike anything else in team golf. USA will have the crowd. But the International team knows what this course can produce.

Presidents Cup week starts September 22 with practice rounds. Competition runs September 24-27.

### STORY 5: Biltmore Championship — The Fall Swing's Most Intriguing New Event

**Long-Form Post | 6:30 PM**

Two weeks from Thursday, the PGA Tour does something it hasn't done in more than 80 years: it plays a tournament in the Blue Ridge Mountains.

The inaugural Biltmore Championship arrives September 17-20 at The Cliffs at Walnut Cove in Arden, North Carolina — a Jack Nicklaus-designed course that sits in a mountain valley bordering the Pisgah National Forest and the Blue Ridge Parkway. The last time the PGA Tour competed in this stretch of western North Carolina was the early 1940s, when Ben Hogan was among those who played there.

The event carries a co-title sponsorship from the Biltmore Estate — the Vanderbilt family's 8,000-acre estate in Asheville, the largest privately owned home in America — and Explore Asheville, the region's tourism authority. It is a four-year agreement, establishing the Biltmore Championship as a fixture in the FedExCup Fall for the foreseeable future.

But there's a dimension to this tournament that goes beyond golf. Asheville was devastated by Hurricane Helene, and its recovery has been extraordinary. The Biltmore Championship is, in part, a civic statement about the resilience of a community that loves its mountains and refused to be defined by the storm that struck them.

The field has not yet been officially released, but the event falls in the FedExCup Fall — meaning players from the full Tour card looking to build early points for the 2026-27 season will compete. The scenic backdrop alone should attract players who want their caddie loops to look like something from a travel magazine.

This is the kind of event the PGA Tour needs more of: distinctive venue, rich context, deep regional identity. The Biltmore Championship earns attention before a single shot is hit.
