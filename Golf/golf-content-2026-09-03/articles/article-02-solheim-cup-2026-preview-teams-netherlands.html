Solheim Cup 2026: Complete Team Rosters, Format, and Everything to Know Four Days Out

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | September 3, 2026

Four days from now, the 2026 Solheim Cup begins at Bernardus Golf in Cromvoirt, North Brabant, Netherlands — and for the first time in the event's 35-year history, it does so on Dutch soil. Both 12-player squads are finalized. The defending champions are set. The underdogs are ready. One of women's team golf's most compelling showdowns is about to begin.

The Setting

Bernardus Golf is a private club in the North Brabant region of the southern Netherlands, approximately 90 minutes south of Amsterdam. The course is surrounded by woodland and heath, with a distinctly European pastoral feel that differs sharply from the manicured American resort venues that have hosted previous Solheim Cups. This is the first time the Netherlands has hosted the event, reflecting the growth of women's professional golf across continental Europe and the expanding reach of the Ladies European Tour.

The competition itself runs September 11-13, with the opening ceremony and practice rounds beginning September 7. A total of 28 points are available across the three competition days: foursomes and four-ball sessions on Friday and Saturday, with 12 singles matches filling Sunday. Team USA needs 14.5 points to claim the Cup; as defending champions, they need 14 points to retain it in the event of a tie.

Team USA

Captain Angela Stanford leads a roster built on proven Solheim performers. The nine automatic qualifiers — determined by a cumulative points list from designated LPGA events — are: Nelly Korda (world No. 1, four LPGA wins and two majors in 2026), Angel Yin, Jennifer Kupcho, Yealimi Noh, Auston Kim, Lauren Coughlin, Allisen Corpuz, Andrea Lee, and Alison Lee.

Stanford's three captain's picks tell a clear story about what she values: experience and track record. Megan Khang brings a 7-3-3 career Solheim record into her fifth Cup appearance. Rose Zhang, who went a perfect 4-0 at the 2024 match in Virginia, makes her third Solheim appearance. Lindy Duncan is the one debutant among the picks — she joins Auston Kim as one of two USA rookies in 2026.

Team Europe

Captain Anna Nordqvist — a three-time major winner and former world No. 1 — has built her squad around an experienced core and four new faces. The eight automatic qualifiers are Charley Hull, Lottie Woad, Maja Stark, Celine Boutier, Carlota Ciganda, Linn Grant, Nanna Koerstz Madsen, and Leona Maguire.

Nordqvist's four captain's picks added Julia López Ramírez, Nastasia Nadaud, Mimi Rhodes, and confirmed Leona Maguire from the automatic list. Europe's four new faces bring unpredictability — rookies with nothing to lose can be dangerous in match play — while the experience of Hull and Ciganda anchors the veteran core.

The Context

USA won the 2024 Solheim Cup 15.5 to 12.5 on home soil in Virginia. Before that, Europe had won three consecutive editions — 2019, 2021, and 2023. The back-and-forth nature of the competition's recent history makes a clean prediction difficult. USA's depth advantage is genuine; Europe's motivation to reclaim the Cup on home (or near-home) European soil is equally real.

Team USA has not won consecutive Solheim Cups since 2015 and 2017. If Stanford's squad wins in the Netherlands, that streak ends — and a new chapter of American dominance in women's team golf may be beginning.
