Solheim Cup 2026 Begins in 10 Days — Team USA Faces Europe in the Netherlands

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | September 1, 2026

Ten days from now, the best women's golfers in the world will tee it up at one of golf's most emotionally charged events — and the venue is a first-time host for the competition.

The 2026 Solheim Cup takes place September 11-13 at 's-Hertogenbosch (also known as Den Bosch), in the Netherlands. Team USA faces Team Europe in the biennial match-play competition that is, for women's golf, what the Ryder Cup is for the men's game: the most prestigious team event in the sport, and the one where talent alone is never enough.

The Format and the Stakes

The Solheim Cup brings together 14 players per team — drawn from the LPGA Tour for the Americans and the Ladies European Tour for Europe — across four days of match-play competition. Individual world rankings and prize money totals do not apply here. What matters is the collective, the team room, the captain's strategy, and the ability to perform in front of crowds that care about the outcome as much as the players themselves.

The Netherlands, hosting for the first time, brings European passion to a competition that has historically drawn some of its loudest support when played on the continent. The 's-Hertogenbosch venue adds a new chapter to the Solheim Cup's history.

The American Side

Team USA enters the 2026 Solheim Cup with a strong LPGA season behind them. Ruoning Yin won the FM Championship at TPC Boston on August 30, closing the summer schedule with a dominant performance. The depth of the American team, which includes some of the world's highest-ranked players, gives the USA a strong foundation for match play success.

The LPGA's 2026 season — the tour's 77th edition, running from January 29 to November 22 across 31 events — has produced consistent excellence from American players, and the Solheim Cup is where that consistency gets tested in the most competitive format the sport has.

Europe's Response

The European team, drawing from the Ladies European Tour and LPGA members who hold European eligibility, will bring home-continent energy to the competition. Team Europe has been competitive in recent Solheim Cup editions, and the Netherlands setting — with passionate local galleries — gives them an environment that favors European confidence.

What's Next

Ten days. September 11 arrives quickly. The Solheim Cup is the most important event in women's team golf, and 2026's edition brings it to a country that has never hosted the competition before. Following the Solheim Cup, the LPGA Tour continues with the Walmart NW Arkansas Championship (September 25-27, Rogers, Arkansas) before wrapping its fall swing. But first: the Netherlands, the Solheim Cup, and 28 of the best women's golfers in the world competing for something that cannot be bought, measured in birdies, or reduced to a single statistic.
