PGA Tour Fall Schedule Preview: Biltmore Championship Next, Then the Presidents Cup and Beyond

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | September 1, 2026

The Tour Championship is done. The FedEx Cup has a champion. Scottie Scheffler has his second title and a $10 million check. And now, the PGA Tour turns the page to its fall season — a stretch of events that carries a different energy than the playoff pressure that defined August.

Here is what professional golf's biggest stage has in store for September and October 2026.

Biltmore Championship — September 17-20, Asheville, North Carolina

The fall schedule opens at The Cliffs at Walnut Cove in Asheville, North Carolina — a scenic destination event that gives players a chance to shake off the post-playoff transition before team competition arrives the following week. The Biltmore Championship draws a strong field for an early fall event, with players motivated to build momentum heading into the Presidents Cup and beyond.

Asheville in September is among the more appealing settings on the PGA Tour's calendar. The Blue Ridge Mountain backdrop combined with The Cliffs' championship layout makes for a visually striking and strategically demanding test.

Presidents Cup — September 24-27, Medinah Country Club, Illinois

The marquee fall event is team golf, not stroke play. The 2026 Presidents Cup arrives at Medinah Country Club — one of America's most storied venues — with Team USA captain Brandt Snedeker leading Scottie Scheffler, Collin Morikawa, Wyndham Clark, and the rest of the American contingent against the International Team captained by Geoff Ogilvy.

The Presidents Cup is the PGA Tour calendar's most significant non-major team competition. While it does not award FedEx Cup points, its stakes for national pride and player legacy are real. Thirty matches across four days determine the winner, with singles matches on the final day often serving as the decisive round.

For Team USA, September 24 at Medinah represents both an opportunity and a statement. The 2025 Ryder Cup did not go as planned. The Presidents Cup is the chance to reset.

Bank of Utah Championship — October 1-4, Ivins, Utah

The fall schedule continues with the Bank of Utah Championship in Ivins, Utah — a western destination event that offers fall players a striking canyon and red rock backdrop. The event draws players looking to accumulate FedEx Cup points and wrap up the fall season with a strong finish before the off-season begins in earnest.

What's Next

After the FedEx Cup intensity, fall golf operates at a different pace — but it offers meaningful opportunities for players to reestablish momentum, finish strong in the FedEx Cup standings, and, in the case of the Presidents Cup, compete for something that transcends individual statistics. September and October on the PGA Tour are worth watching. The champions change; the drama does not.
