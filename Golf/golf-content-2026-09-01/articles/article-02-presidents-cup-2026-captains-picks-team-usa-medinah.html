Presidents Cup Captain's Picks Revealed — Team USA Heads to Medinah With Scheffler Leading the Way

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | September 1, 2026

The 2026 Presidents Cup rosters are set. The captain's picks were announced today, completing the 12-man rosters that will compete September 24-27 at Medinah Country Club in Medinah, Illinois — and Team USA, anchored by freshly crowned FedEx Cup champion Scottie Scheffler, enters as the overwhelming favorite to take the Wanamaker Trophy.

Team USA's Roster

Captain Brandt Snedeker's American team features a core of the PGA Tour's premier players. Among those already confirmed heading into today's captain's picks announcement: Scottie Scheffler, Cameron Young, Wyndham Clark, Sam Burns, Russell Henley, and Collin Morikawa. The six captain's picks announced today complete one of the deepest American rosters in Presidents Cup history.

Scheffler's presence as the world's No. 1 player and the man who just won the Tour Championship gives the American side an anchor that no opponent can comfortably match. The Presidents Cup field is drawn from players outside Europe — differentiating it from the Ryder Cup — and the International side, captained by Geoff Ogilvy, will need exceptional team play to compete with this American roster.

Medinah: A Marquee Venue

Medinah Country Club's Course No. 3 is among the most iconic venues in American golf. Originally designed by Tom Bendelow and redesigned by OCM in 2024, the par-72 layout stretches 7,657 yards and rewards precision, patience, and course management. Medinah has hosted multiple major championships and Ryder Cups over the decades — it is a course that brings out the best and punishes the worst in any golfer.

For American fans, the association with Ryder Cup history gives Medinah particular resonance. For Team USA in 2026, competing at this venue while seeking redemption from the 2025 Ryder Cup disappointment adds an extra dimension of motivation.

The Competition Format

The Presidents Cup runs across four days, September 24-27, with practice rounds available to spectators on September 22-23. The competition consists of 30 total matches: nine foursomes sessions, nine fourball sessions, and 12 singles matches on the final day. It is a format that rewards depth and composure under pressure — two qualities the American team has in abundance.

What's Next

With the rosters set, attention turns to team preparation and captain's strategy. Both Snedeker and Ogilvy will finalize their pairings and singles lineups in the coming weeks. Practice rounds at Medinah begin September 22, with competitive play starting September 24. After the Tour Championship drama and the FedEx Cup's conclusion, the Presidents Cup is the golf event that closes out September — and for Team USA, it is an opportunity to prove that 2025's Ryder Cup result does not define this generation of American golfers.
