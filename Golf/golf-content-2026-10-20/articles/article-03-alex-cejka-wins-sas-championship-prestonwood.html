Alex Cejka Wins the 2026 SAS Championship at Prestonwood

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | October 20, 2026

Alex Cejka claimed the 2026 SAS Championship title at Prestonwood Country Club in Cary, North Carolina last weekend, finishing at 9 under par to take the win and $315,000 in prize money. The Czech-German veteran beat Ernie Els into second place — the two-time major champion closing at -6 — while Steven Alker took third at -4.

A Quality Performance at a Showcase Event

The SAS Championship is one of the PGA Tour Champions' most prestigious fall events, held annually at Prestonwood Country Club in the Research Triangle of North Carolina. The course's challenging parkland layout and strong local gallery make it one of the more competitive weeks on the senior circuit, and Cejka's -9 total held up through a final round that saw Ernie Els make a late charge before finishing three shots back.

Cejka has been one of the more consistent names on the Champions Tour through 2026, and the SAS Championship win marks another quality result in a season that has seen him compete at the top of leaderboards regularly.

Els and Alker Close Behind

Ernie Els, the two-time US Open and two-time Open Championship champion, was the runner-up at -6. At 56, Els remains one of the most popular and competitive players on the Champions circuit, and his presence near the top of the Prestonwood leaderboard was no surprise. Steven Alker — who won the Jefferson Lehigh Valley Classic just two weeks earlier — came in third at -4, continuing his strong fall form.

Champions Tour October Form

October has produced a string of strong Champions Tour performances. Vaughn Taylor won the Constellation FURYK & FRIENDS at -17 (October 9-11). Alker took the Jefferson Lehigh Valley Classic at -14 (October 2-4). Now Cejka adds the SAS title at -9. The momentum on the senior circuit is building ahead of the Stifel Charity Classic in St. Louis (October 23-25) and the Simmons Bank Championship in Little Rock the following week.

What's Next

The Champions Tour moves to Norwood Hills Country Club in St. Louis for the Stifel Charity Classic, October 23-25, with a $2.3 million purse. Cejka will look to carry his Prestonwood form into St. Louis, where a second victory in consecutive weeks would make him the story of the senior circuit's fall stretch.
