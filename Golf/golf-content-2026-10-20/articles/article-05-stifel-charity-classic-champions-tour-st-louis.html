Stifel Charity Classic: The Champions Tour Heads to St. Louis This Week

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | October 20, 2026

While the PGA Tour plays its island event in Bermuda, the PGA Tour Champions is heading inland for one of the fall circuit's most anticipated stops: the Stifel Charity Classic at Norwood Hills Country Club in St. Louis, Missouri. The event runs October 23-25 with a $2.3 million purse, and it arrives at the tail end of a strong October run for the senior circuit.

Norwood Hills: A Classic Midwestern Test

Norwood Hills Country Club is a parkland-style layout in north St. Louis County that has hosted significant amateur and professional events across its long history. The course rewards accuracy from the tee over brute distance, and its tree-lined fairways and firm, moderately paced greens favor players who have spent a career developing shot-shaping ability.

For the Champions Tour field — where the average competitor has been playing Tour golf for decades — Norwood Hills is exactly the kind of course that separates the grinders from the bombers. Expect a competitive leaderboard through the weekend with multiple players in contention heading into Sunday's final round.

October Momentum on the Senior Circuit

The Champions Tour has had a strong October. Alex Cejka won the SAS Championship at Prestonwood last weekend at -9, beating Ernie Els (-6) and Steven Alker (-4). Before that, Vaughn Taylor took the Constellation FURYK & FRIENDS at -17, and Alker won the Jefferson Lehigh Valley Classic at -14 in early October. The form across the circuit has been competitive and the scoring has been low, which bodes well for a compelling week at Norwood Hills.

The Charity Component

The Stifel Charity Classic, as its name suggests, has a charitable component woven into its identity — a feature that has become a differentiator for Champions Tour events that want to build community ties in their host cities. St. Louis has a long relationship with professional golf, and the annual presence of Tour Champions players in the metro area draws consistent gallery support.

What's Next

The Stifel Charity Classic tees off Thursday, October 23 and concludes Sunday, October 25 — running simultaneously with the PGA Tour's Bermuda Championship final weekend. The following week, the Champions Tour moves to Little Rock for the Simmons Bank Championship (October 30 – November 1).
