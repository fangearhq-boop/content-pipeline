The logical next step — returning to the PGA Tour — is not as simple as walking through the door. Under current Tour regulations, Rahm remains ineligible for PGA Tour events until late August 2027. Earlier in 2026, the PGA Tour offered him a special reinstatement opportunity. He turned it down.

Earlier reports of a quick PGA Tour return via a $25 million fine and "apology tour" were characterized by Golf Digest as inaccurate. The November 5 date that circulated in legal filings relates to his formal separation proceedings, not a Tour reinstatement.

The bottom line: Rahm won't be playing PGA Tour events in 2026 or in the early part of 2027. The most prestigious venues on the Tour calendar — Augusta, St. Andrews, Pebble Beach — will have to wait at least another year.
