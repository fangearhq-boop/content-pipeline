NW Arkansas Championship Round 1: LPGA Returns to Rogers With Solheim Ghosts in Tow

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | September 25, 2026

The Walmart NW Arkansas Championship at Pinnacle Country Club in Rogers, Arkansas opened Round 1 on Friday, and the stakes reach beyond the $3 million purse. For 18 players — 8 Americans and 10 Europeans — this is the first tournament since the Solheim Cup finished two weeks ago with a 13-15 USA defeat to Europe.

The psychological contrast couldn't be more pronounced. The European contingent — led by names like Charley Hull, Leona Maguire, and Céline Boutier — arrives with the confidence of a winning team, fresh momentum, and the kind of collective energy that winning team competitions generates. The Americans arrive processing a loss in a competition where home-course advantage and crowd support factored prominently.

Why the 54-Hole Format Matters

Pinnacle Country Club's 54-hole format leaves no margin for a slow start. In a 72-hole event, a player can absorb a poor first round and still contend. In three rounds, every shot from Day 1 counts. The course tends to reward precise iron play and consistent birdie looks — and with low scoring typically available on this layout, players who find their game early can separate themselves quickly.

The Solheim Cup dynamic adds another layer. European players who have been feeding off collective success may find it easier to carry that energy individually. American players have faced this scenario before — rebuilding personal momentum after a team loss — and the history is mixed.

The Field and the Stakes

The eight USA Solheim Cup players in the field — including Allisen Corpuz, Moriya Jutanugarn, Danielle Kang, Jennifer Kupcho, Minjee Lee, Sei Young Kim, Angela Yin, and Albane Valenzuela — represent a cross-section of the American team that took the loss. The 10 Europeans have every reason to press their advantage before the season's final stretch.

What's Next

Round 1 results are accumulating throughout Friday afternoon. Round 2 follows Saturday, with Round 3 and the champion crowned Sunday. The NW Arkansas Championship has a loyal Arkansas fanbase known for its enthusiasm and proximity to the course — the atmosphere typically plays like a home game for whoever captures the crowd early. Watch the leaderboard for which Solheim Cup narrative carries into the weekend.
