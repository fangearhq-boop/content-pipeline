Ciganda, Duncan, Choi in Three-Way Tie at ANNIKA as Korda Lurks Four Back

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | November 14, 2026

Three players share the lead at 12-under through 36 holes at the ANNIKA driven by Gainbridge at Pelican, with Carlota Ciganda, Lindy Duncan, and Hye-Jin Choi separated by zero strokes entering Round 3 — and world No. 1 Nelly Korda just four behind.

When three players arrive at the halfway point of a tournament completely tied, the round ahead becomes simultaneously simpler and more complicated. Simpler because the standings are clean — no one enters the day ahead, no one has a hidden advantage. More complicated because every shot either breaks the tie or falls further behind players of equivalent form.

Carlota Ciganda, Lindy Duncan, and Hye-Jin Choi face exactly that scenario Saturday morning at Pelican Golf Club in Belleair, Florida. All three stand at 12-under par through 36 holes of the ANNIKA driven by Gainbridge at Pelican — the LPGA Tour's penultimate event of the 2026 season — separated by nothing, with everything to play for.

Three Different Paths to the Same Number

What makes the three-way tie compelling is how differently each leader got there. Hye-Jin Choi's 61 in Round 1 was the shot-of-the-day, an extraordinary round that announced her intentions loudly. Her 67 in Round 2 — still comfortably under par — showed she could follow up without imploding. Lindy Duncan took the opposite path, with a 62 in Round 1 and a 66 on Friday, maintaining her charge with similar efficiency. Ciganda's 65-63 represents the most consistent run of the three, two rounds in the low-to-mid 60s that speak to precise, sustained play.

Three styles, three different scoring paths, the same destination entering Round 3. One of them will need to separate first, and doing so at Pelican requires both scoring and nerve.

Korda's Shadow

No one on that leaderboard will have more eyes on them in Round 3 than Nelly Korda, who starts the day at 8-under — four strokes behind the leaders. For any other player, four behind with 36 holes remaining is a manageable gap. For the world No. 1, in an event that offers FedExCup implications and LPGA season momentum, four strokes is a scenario she has overcome before.

Korda's ability to string together birdies in extended runs can evaporate a multi-stroke deficit before a leaderboard has time to update. The three leaders know she is there, and that knowledge — that the sport's best player is coming — creates its own form of pressure on a leaderboard that is already tightly packed.

What's Next

Round 3 is Saturday. By Saturday evening, the leaderboard will have a new shape — either one of the co-leaders has broken ahead, Korda has charged into contention, or some combination of the above has reshuffled everything entirely. The ANNIKA driven by Gainbridge at Pelican is in its penultimate round, and the best golf of the week is ahead.

Sunday's Round 4 will close out the 2026 edition of this event, the second-to-last LPGA tournament of the season. Whoever hoists the trophy at Pelican Golf Club will have won a tournament with real stakes — in the standings, in the year-end race, and in the sport's growing conversation about who defines LPGA excellence right now.
