### STORY 1: LIV Golf Financial Crisis — Saudi PIF Ends Funding — NEW

The biggest golf business story of 2026: Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund announced in April it is ending LIV Golf funding after this season. Consequences: the Michigan Team Championship (season closer) was cancelled; total 2026 prize money cut from $70M to $40.1M; Indianapolis individual purse halved from $20M to $10.1M; winning purse drops from $4M to $2.02M; team championship at Indianapolis: $30M total (winner bonus cut from $11.2M to $8.4M). Fresh Tape Media filed a $1.23M lawsuit (NY Supreme Court, July 21) against LIV for unpaid production services for the January West Palm Beach preseason event. Players are also reportedly awaiting owed payments. Total prize money lost vs. promised: ~$60.1M.

**Tier 1** — Major breaking business story with wide golf implications.

### STORY 2: Sepp Straka Withdraws From BMW Championship — NEW

Sepp Straka, No. 5 in the FedExCup standings, has withdrawn from the BMW Championship at Bellerive citing a private family matter. His manager Butler Melnyk issued a statement asking for privacy. Straka is still expected to compete at the Tour Championship (East Lake, Aug 27-30). The withdrawal drops the BMW field from 50 to 49 players. Critical consequence: the top-30 cutoff bubble math shifts — players on the edge of qualification for East Lake will see their position affected by Straka's missing points.

**Tier 1** — Breaking news with direct playoff implications.

### STORY 3: McIlroy at BMW — Two-Time Masters Champion Seeks Playoff Run — FOLLOW UP

Rory McIlroy arrives at Bellerive Country Club as the reigning back-to-back Masters champion — completing his career Grand Slam at Augusta in 2025, then defending in 2026 — yet he enters the BMW Championship outside the top 12 in FedExCup (currently No. 13). After finishing 66th (8-over) at the FedEx St. Jude Championship, McIlroy flew home to South Florida to work with coach Michael Bannon on his swing. He described himself as "far away from where I need to be." Golf Channel counterframed: he "finds encouragement." Sportsbooks have him second at +1600. He has won three FedExCup titles.

**Tier 2** — Compelling redemption narrative for the biggest name in the field.
