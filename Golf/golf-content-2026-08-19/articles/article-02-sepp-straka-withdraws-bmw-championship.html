Sepp Straka Withdraws From BMW Championship: No. 5 FedExCup Stepping Aside for Family

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | August 19, 2026

Sepp Straka will not compete in the BMW Championship at Bellerive Country Club this week. The Austrian golfer, who enters the FedExCup Playoffs ranked fifth overall, announced his withdrawal Tuesday citing a private family matter. His manager, Butler Melnyk, issued a statement asking for the family's privacy to be respected.

Straka is still expected to compete at the Tour Championship at East Lake in Atlanta, which begins August 27. He is comfortably positioned to advance regardless of his BMW absence — the top-30 finishers at Bellerive move on to East Lake, and Straka's standing at No. 5 provides a substantial cushion even without scoring additional points this week.

The Field Effect

Straka's withdrawal drops the BMW Championship field from 50 players to 49. The PGA Tour does not fill the vacated spot, meaning one of the 50 invitations to Bellerive goes unfilled. The practical consequence that will dominate leaderboard conversation is the shift in the top-30 bubble.

With Straka removed from competition, his substantial FedExCup point total is no longer a factor in this week's final standings. Players positioned in the 28th through 31st positions in the cumulative standings — the area where East Lake qualification is genuinely uncertain — will be watching their positions carefully now that one of the most significant points producers in the field is not competing.

The net effect may be modest depending on how the week unfolds, but in a format where FedExCup points determine who gets to compete for the full $25 million at East Lake, every variable in the calculation matters. Players on the bubble typically know their numbers precisely. Straka's name coming off the leaderboard is information they will immediately incorporate into their week's strategy.

Straka's 2026 Profile

Straka has been one of the more quietly consistent performers on the PGA Tour through the 2026 season — his No. 5 FedExCup ranking reflecting cumulative excellence rather than any single dominant week. He represents the kind of golfer the playoffs were designed to reward: players who bring results across an entire season rather than relying on one or two spectacular events to vault them into contention.

The withdrawal decision, in that context, speaks to character as clearly as any score. A player ranked fifth on the FedExCup standings with East Lake position entirely secure is walking away from a $20 million event. Whatever the family matter, the calculus was simple and the conclusion was obvious.

What's Next

The BMW Championship begins Thursday, August 20 at Bellerive Country Club in St. Louis. The top 30 finishers advance to the Tour Championship at East Lake in Atlanta (August 27-30). With Straka out, watch the players in positions 28 through 31 in the FedExCup standings — their tournament will be shaped as much by each other's relative performance as by their individual scores. The week's most interesting golf may unfold on the bubble rather than at the top of the leaderboard.
