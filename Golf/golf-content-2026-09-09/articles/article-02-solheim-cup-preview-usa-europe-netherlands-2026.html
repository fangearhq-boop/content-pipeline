Solheim Cup 2026 Preview: USA Defends, Korda Leads, Netherlands Hosts

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | September 9, 2026

Women's golf's greatest team event arrives Friday. The 2026 Solheim Cup begins September 11 at Bernardus Golf in Cromvoirt, Netherlands — and Team USA comes in as defending champions with what could be their strongest roster ever.

The format is familiar: 14.5 points to win, spread across three days of foursomes, four-balls, and 12 singles matches. The stage is new — the Netherlands hosting its first Solheim Cup, with European galleries ready to roar for Nordqvist's squad.

Team USA: Built to Win Again

Angela Stanford captains the Americans, and she has extraordinary talent to work with. Nelly Korda, the world No. 1, is the clear headliner — she arrives at Bernardus Golf in peak form and eager to lead. Rose Zhang, Megan Khang, Allisen Corpuz, and Jennifer Kupcho round out a core that has no obvious weak spots.

Stanford's nine automatic qualifiers — Korda, Angel Yin, Kupcho, Yealimi Noh, Auston Kim, Lauren Coughlin, Corpuz, Andrea Lee, and Alison Lee — are joined by three captain's picks: Khang, Zhang, and Lindy Duncan.

In 2024, Team USA beat Europe 15.5-12.5. The margin flattered the Americans somewhat — Europe pushed them hard. But USA won, and the 2026 roster is arguably stronger.

Team Europe: Experience, Home Crowds, and Hungry Rookies

Anna Nordqvist captains Europe, and she has a unique advantage: the home crowd. Netherlands golf fans will be loud and passionate, and that energy matters in team competition.

Charley Hull brings a record to the event that no other player in this field can match: eight consecutive Solheim Cup appearances. She is Europe's anchor, emotionally and competitively. Linn Grant adds another world-class talent to the European core.

Nordqvist's captain's picks include three rookies — Nadine Lopez Ramirez, Albane Valenzuela Nadaud, and Mimi Rhodes — who bring an unpredictability that experienced Solheim rosters sometimes lack. Rookies have swung Solheim Cups before.

Format and What to Watch

The Solheim Cup runs Friday through Sunday, September 11-13. The opening ceremony is Wednesday, September 10. Each day includes morning and afternoon sessions of team matches, with 12 singles matches filling Sunday's final day.

Korda against whichever European pairing Nordqvist throws at her is the obvious marquee matchup to watch. But in Solheim Cups, the matches that decide the result often come from the middle of the order — players in the seven through twelve singles slots who grind out half-points that swing the tally.

Europe needs to win at home. USA wants to prove 2024 wasn't luck. The atmosphere in the Netherlands will be extraordinary.

What's Next

The Solheim Cup runs through Sunday, September 13. Coverage begins Friday morning at Bernardus Golf. After the event concludes, the focus of women's golf shifts back to individual competition — but the Solheim Cup always leaves its mark on the players who compete.

For Team USA, a second consecutive win would cement this generation of American women's golfers as one of the best Solheim squads in the event's history. For Europe, a home win in the Netherlands would be among the most celebrated results in the event's 37-year run.
