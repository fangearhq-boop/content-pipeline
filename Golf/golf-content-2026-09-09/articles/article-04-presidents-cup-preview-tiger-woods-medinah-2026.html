Presidents Cup Preview: Tiger Woods Captains USA at Medinah, September 22-27

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | September 9, 2026

The LIV Golf bankruptcy has dominated golf's news cycle this week. But 13 days from now, professional golf's next major team event begins at Medinah Country Club — and it deserves attention.

The 2026 Presidents Cup, September 22-27, features Tiger Woods as Team USA captain, Scottie Scheffler and Rory McIlroy as the squad's headliners, and a Medinah course that has witnessed some of team golf's most dramatic moments.

Tiger Woods: Captain for the Ages

Tiger Woods captaining Team USA elevates every dimension of this event. His preparation for the role has been meticulous — selecting a roster built around the Tour's elite, identifying pairings, and lending the event a weight of expectation that no other captain in the event's history could match.

Scottie Scheffler, the FedExCup champion, and Rory McIlroy, who won the 2026 Masters, anchor the American squad. These are two of the best golfers in the world at the peak of their abilities, and both are hungry for a strong team result.

Adam Scott captains the Internationals — a team that has won just once in 15 Presidents Cup events, back in 2019 in Melbourne. Scott knows what it takes to lead this side; he played in that victory and has been a cornerstone of International teams for two decades.

Medinah: A Course That Knows Big Moments

Medinah Country Club last hosted a major team event in 2012 — the Ryder Cup, where Europe staged one of sport's greatest comeback victories, erasing a 10-6 deficit entering Sunday singles to win 14.5-13.5. The "Miracle at Medinah" remains one of the most remarkable results in golf history.

The Presidents Cup does not typically deliver Ryder Cup-level drama — Team USA's 13-2 historical record tells that story. But the setting, the course, and the format create the conditions for compelling golf regardless of the predicted result.

Context: The LIV Absences

The LIV bankruptcy means several players who would have been Presidents Cup-caliber — Jon Rahm, Bryson DeChambeau, Cameron Smith, Dustin Johnson — are absent. Their contracts terminated Tuesday, but no reinstatement path to PGA Tour membership exists in time to participate.

The impact falls more heavily on the Internationals, who lose Smith and Johnson from consideration, than on Team USA. Scheffler and McIlroy are PGA Tour members in good standing. The American roster Woods has assembled is formidable.

What's Next

The Presidents Cup begins its opening ceremony at Medinah on September 21, with competition from September 22-27. Team pairings and session formats will be announced by both captains in the days leading up to the event.

For Tiger Woods, a Presidents Cup captain's victory would be the latest chapter in a career that refuses to stop generating headlines. For the Internationals, an upset at Medinah would be the greatest result in the event's history outside their 2019 win.

Don't let the LIV news make you miss this.
