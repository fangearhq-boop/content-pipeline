Bermuda Championship Preview: Ben James Favored at Windswept Port Royal

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | October 21, 2026

The Bermuda Championship tees off Wednesday at Port Royal Golf Course in Southampton, Bermuda — one of the more distinctive venues on the PGA Tour calendar. The Atlantic wind, the par 71 layout at 6,828 yards, and a $6 million purse create a tournament that rewards patience and creativity over raw power.

Ben James enters as the betting favorite at +1175 to +1200. Corey Conners, Eric Cole, Austin Smotherman, and defending champion Adam Schenk are among the notable names in the 72-player field.

Port Royal: A Different Kind of Test

Port Royal Golf Course doesn't look like most PGA Tour venues from overhead. It sits on Bermuda's southern coast, where the Atlantic makes itself felt in ways that don't show up on the scorecard until a player is mid-round and the wind has shifted twice. The course plays to a par 71, and two of its par 3s — the 13th and 16th holes — measure 235 yards each, requiring controlled long-iron play under conditions that can change rapidly.

In a typical week on Tour, the premium is on distance and then precision. At Port Royal, the order reverses. Precision under wind pressure comes first. Players who bomb drives and rely on wedges from short range find that the wind erases yardage expectations. Players who shape shots, commit to trajectories, and manage misses tend to appear on the leaderboard.

Ben James and the Field

Ben James as the early betting favorite reflects a combination of current form and course-type fit. Port Royal's demands align well with the kind of accurate ball-striking James brings — a player whose game is built around precision rather than length.

Corey Conners represents one of the best iron players on Tour in any given season, which makes him a natural Bermuda watch. Eric Cole and Austin Smotherman add depth to the field, and Adam Schenk returns to defend a title he won here last season.

Schenk's defense adds the narrative layer that every event benefits from: a player who has already solved this course trying to prove it wasn't a one-time result.

What to Watch

Wind conditions through the week will define the scoring picture. When Port Royal is calm, low scores are achievable and the field compresses. When the Atlantic gusts, scorecards suffer across the board and survival becomes the skill.

The two long par 3s (Nos. 13 and 16) will be key tests in every round. Players who can take on the challenge of 235 yards under wind pressure — and find the green consistently enough to avoid bogey momentum — will have a significant scoring advantage over the field.

First round tees off Thursday. The leaderboard will tell the first part of the story by afternoon.
