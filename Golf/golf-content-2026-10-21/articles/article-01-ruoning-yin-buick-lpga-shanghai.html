Ruoning Yin Repeats at Buick LPGA Shanghai, Edges Choi Hye-jin by One

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | October 21, 2026

Ruoning Yin has done something the modern LPGA Tour rarely allows: she defended. The Chinese star closed out the 2026 Buick LPGA Shanghai on Saturday with a final-round birdie surge to finish 11-under 277 for the week, holding off South Korea's Choi Hye-jin by a single stroke. Amy Yang finished third.

The win is Yin's second of the 2026 LPGA season and earns her $480,000 along with 500 CME Globe points — a significant addition with the year-end championship race entering its final stages.

A Title Defense That Held

Title defenses in professional golf are notoriously difficult. The players who beat you last year have adjusted. The field arrives with a target on one number. Courses change, conditions change, and the pressure of protecting something is uniquely different from the hunger of winning it the first time.

Yin handled it. Through four rounds at Shanghai, she remained consistent when others in the field wobbled, and when Choi Hye-jin pressed late to make a genuine contest of it, Yin had the game to respond. The one-shot margin at the end was tight — but it never felt like Yin was going to let it slip.

The final score of 11-under 277 was enough. Choi finished at 10-under 278. Amy Yang was further back in third.

CME Globe Implications

The 500 CME Globe points that come with a victory like this matter in the back half of the LPGA calendar. The CME Globe Race to the CME Group Tour Championship determines who competes for a $2 million winner's prize at the season finale in Naples, Florida. A second win in 2026 moves Yin into strong position heading into the final tournaments.

At 22, Yin is still early in what projects to be a long and decorated career. Two wins in a single season, including a title defense on an international stage, represents a performance level that matches the highest expectations placed on her when she first joined the Tour.

What's Next

The LPGA Tour continues in the coming weeks with events that will further shape the CME Globe standings before the season finale. Yin will carry momentum; Choi Hye-jin will look to convert the runner-up result into something more.

For Shanghai, the final result reflects the week's best: Yin at 11-under, Choi a shot back, a title defense completed.
