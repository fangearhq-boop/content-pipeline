### STORY 3: Bermuda Championship Preview

**Post 3A**
Window: 11:00 AM ET

The Bermuda Championship is one of golf's more unusual venues — and that's entirely the point.

Port Royal Golf Course in Southampton, Bermuda sits on the southern edge of the island, where Atlantic wind punishes anything close to flat or straight. At 6,828 yards and par 71, with two 235-yard par 3s tucked into the rotation, Port Royal demands patience, creativity, and acceptance that the wind wins some battles.

This year's field features:
• Ben James (favorite, +1175-+1200)
• Corey Conners
• Eric Cole
• Austin Smotherman
• Adam Schenk (defending champion)

The $6 million purse tees off Wednesday. If you want to watch a PGA Tour event where the ocean plays as a factor and the leaderboard reshuffles daily based on who handled the gusts — this is your week.

First round Thursday. Watch this one.

### STORY 4: Vijay Singh Wins SAS Championship

**Post 4A**
Window: 1:00 PM ET

Vijay Singh was 63 years old when he won the SAS Championship last week.

Read that again.

Singh shot 9-under 207 at Prestonwood Country Club in Cary, North Carolina over 54 holes, finishing two clear of Ernie Els. Alex Cejka and Tag Ridings tied for third.

The win moved Singh to No. 6 on the Schwab Cup standings — important timing with the Champions Tour playoffs beginning this Thursday in St. Louis.

There's a version of this sport where Singh is a historical figure, someone you watch on highlight reels from the early 2000s. Instead he's playing competitive golf in 2026 and winning against the best the over-50 game has to offer.

63. And winning. 🏆
