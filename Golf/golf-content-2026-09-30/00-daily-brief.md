### STORY 4: Lauren Coughlin Makes History — 9 Consecutive Birdies at NW Arkansas
- **Tier:** 1
- **Category:** LPGA Tour / Lauren Coughlin / Walmart NW Arkansas Championship / Historic Round
- **Status:** HISTORIC
- **Summary:** On Saturday September 26 at Pinnacle Country Club, Lauren Coughlin teed off on hole 10 and made nine consecutive birdies — the most ever to open any half of a round in recorded LPGA and PGA Tour history.
