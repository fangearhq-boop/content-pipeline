## STORY 2: LIV Golf Bankruptcy and LIV 2.0

**Source:** Yahoo Sports, CNN, CBS Sports, LIV Golf official, Sportico, EssentiallySports

- LIV Golf filed Chapter 11 bankruptcy in the United States on September 8, 2026
- Reason: Lost Saudi Arabia Public Investment Fund (PIF) backing after 2026 season
- Announced Restructuring Support Agreement with BC Partners Advisors L.P.
- CEO Scott O'Neil is calling future plan "LIV 2.0"
- Majority of LIV workforce informed employment ends in first week of September
- New structure details (LIV 2.0):
  - Majority ownership shifts to players
  - 10 events in 2027 (5 US, 5 international), down from 14 events
  - Field expands from 57 to 75 players
  - 54-hole cut introduced for the first time
  - Team concept rebuilt around nationalities
  - Current $30 million purses will drop to lower figure
- 2026 LIV Golf League season: ran February 5 to August 30
  - Team winner: 4Aces GC
  - Individual points leader: Jon Rahm
