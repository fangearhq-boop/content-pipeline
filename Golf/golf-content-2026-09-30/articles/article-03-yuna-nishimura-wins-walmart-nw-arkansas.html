Yuna Nishimura Wins Walmart NW Arkansas Championship for First LPGA Title

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | September 30, 2026

Yuna Nishimura captured her first LPGA Tour title on Sunday at the Walmart NW Arkansas Championship presented by P&G, closing with a 5-under 66 at Pinnacle Country Club in Rogers, Arkansas, to finish at 21-under par and win by five shots.

The 26-year-old Japanese player led from the opening round and never relinquished that lead — a wire-to-wire performance that turned what could have been a nervy finish into a clear-cut victory. Aki Iwai, Chanettee Wannasaen, and In Gee Chun tied for second at 16-under 197.

A Breakthrough a Long Time Coming

Nishimura arrived at Pinnacle Country Club as one of the most accomplished players on the Japan LPGA Tour to have yet claimed a win on the global circuit. She had six victories in Japan and consistent results on the LPGA Tour, but the breakthrough win had remained elusive.

Arkansas changed that. Nishimura shot rounds of 64-62-66 over three days, building her lead methodically and closing without drama. Her final round was clean and composed — the kind of performance that signals a player who was not just winning but was in control throughout.

LPGA's Third First-Time Winner of 2026

With the victory, Nishimura becomes the LPGA Tour's third Rolex First-Time Winner of 2026, joining Yana Wilson and Gina Kim, who won the Dow Championship earlier in the season. The trend of new winners emerging across the 2026 LPGA Tour schedule reflects the depth and competitiveness of the current field.

Japanese players have been a consistent presence at the top of the LPGA leaderboard for years, and Nishimura's win adds to that tradition. Her background on the Japan LPGA Tour provided a strong foundation, but converting that into a global title is a step that not every successful regional player manages to take.

What's Next

With her first LPGA Tour win secured, Nishimura enters the fall stretch of the 2026 LPGA season with full status and renewed momentum. The win also ensures her position in future major championships and other exempted events through at least the next two seasons.

The Walmart NW Arkansas Championship, played at Pinnacle Country Club since 2007, has a history of crowning first-time winners and breakout performances. In 2026, it delivered both — Nishimura's dominant run and Lauren Coughlin's historic Saturday round together making this one of the more memorable editions of the event.
