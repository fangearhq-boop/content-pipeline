LIV Golf Bankruptcy — Oct. 13 Deadline Approaching
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Window: 10:30 AM ET LIV Golf's bankruptcy clock is ticking. The circuit filed Chapter 11 on September 8 in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of New Jersey. Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund — which invested an estimated $6 billion in LIV since its 2022 launch — has since provided $49.6 million in debtor-in-possession financing to keep the organization operational during restructuring. London-based BC Partners is leading the effort to build what insiders are calling "LIV 2.0." The Oct. 13 court deadline forces the next round of legal decisions. The restructuring plan envisions a return to competition in 2027 with four events — Saudi Arabia, Australia, South Africa, and Hong Kong — and a player-majority ownership structure. That structure, if it materializes, would mark a fundamental shift in how professional golf's challenger circuit operates. The creditor list reveals where the money went. Jon Rahm is owed $7.5 million — the largest unsecured player claim. Bryson DeChambeau is owed approximately $5.8 million. Dustin Johnson, $5.5 million. These are just the unsecured claims; the total player obligations run far higher. Rahm has publicly said he is "noncommittal" about LIV 2.0. That phrase carries enormous weight for a circuit trying to convince its biggest names to stay. #LIVGolf #Golf | Follow Golf Fanrecap for updates as the restructuring develops. ---

Asheville's Golf Comeback — First PGA Tour Event Since 1942
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Window: 8:00 AM ET The last time a PGA Tour event came to Asheville, North Carolina, the year was 1942. The world was at war. The American golf circuit was months away from suspending its schedule entirely. Eighty-four years passed. The city grew. It became a mountain destination, a cultural hub, a place known as much for its arts scene as its Blue Ridge views. In 2024, Hurricane Helene devastated the region — a flood that claimed lives and tested the city's resilience in ways it is still working through today. And now, on September 17, 2026, the PGA Tour returns. The Biltmore Championship takes its name from the Biltmore Estate — George Vanderbilt's 8,000-acre estate and 178,000-square-foot chateau that opened in 1895 and remains the largest privately owned home in the United States. The estate defined Asheville's identity more than any other single structure, drawing visitors from around the world and anchoring the region's economy for more than a century. The Cliffs at Walnut Cove is the host venue. The purse is $5 million. The best golfers in the world are here. For the golf community and for Asheville, this week is more than a tournament. It's a homecoming — 84 years in the making. #BiltmoreChampionship #PGATour | Follow Golf Fanrecap for coverage all week. ---
