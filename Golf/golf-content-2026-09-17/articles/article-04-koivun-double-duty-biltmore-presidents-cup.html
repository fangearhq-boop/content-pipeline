Jackson Koivun's Remarkable 10 Days: Biltmore Favorite, Then Presidents Cup Debut

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | September 17, 2026

Jackson Koivun has a very specific 10-day window ahead of him — and the entire golf world is paying attention.

This week: he tees off in Round 1 of the Biltmore Championship in Asheville, North Carolina as the tournament's favorite at +1200. It's the opening event of the FedExCup Fall, and Koivun enters fresh off one of the more impressive debut seasons in PGA Tour history.

Next week: he heads to Medinah Country Club for the 2026 Presidents Cup, representing the United States as the youngest member of Brandt Snedeker's team. His first-ever international match play begins September 24.

What He's Already Done

Koivun turned professional from Auburn University earlier this year. Within months, he had won the 3M Open — posting a closing performance that drew comparisons to the best debut seasons in recent Tour memory. His ball-striking metrics put him in elite company. His putting, always considered his most mature skill coming out of college, has held up under Tour-level pressure.

Snedeker named him a captain's pick for the Presidents Cup shortly after the win, making Koivun the youngest player on a USA team that includes Scottie Scheffler, Xander Schauffele, and Collin Morikawa. At 21, he is the youngest American at a Presidents Cup since Jordan Spieth in 2013.

The Mental Test

The logistical challenge of back-to-back major commitments is manageable — players travel all the time. The mental challenge is something different.

Koivun is trying to win a PGA Tour event while simultaneously preparing for a team format he has never played at the professional level. Match play requires a distinct mindset from stroke play. The team dynamic at a Presidents Cup — practice rounds with teammates, captain's meetings, the weight of representing your country — doesn't resemble anything in a normal Tour week. And he is doing all of this, for the first time, at 21 years old.

The players who handle this kind of compressed timeline best are the ones who stay present. Worry about Thursday's round on Thursday. Medinah comes after. Koivun's ability to compartmentalize over the next 10 days will be one of the quiet storylines worth tracking at both events.

What's Next

Koivun tees off in Round 1 today at The Cliffs at Walnut Cove. If he plays through the weekend, he finishes Sunday, September 20, and travels to Medinah for a Tuesday or Wednesday practice round ahead of the Presidents Cup's September 24 opening.
