7 Days to Medinah — The 2026 Presidents Cup USA Team Is Set

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | September 17, 2026

Seven days from now, the 2026 Presidents Cup begins at Medinah Country Club in Medinah, Illinois. Both rosters are finalized. The American side is loaded. And the International team, under captain Geoff Ogilvy, is more dangerous than the historical scoreline suggests.

The USA Roster

Captain Brandt Snedeker has a deep twelve-man squad. His six automatic qualifiers — the top performers from the FedExCup standings — are Scottie Scheffler, Cameron Young, Wyndham Clark, Russell Henley, Sam Burns, and Collin Morikawa. Snedeker added six captain's picks: Xander Schauffele, Justin Thomas, Patrick Cantlay, Chris Gotterup, Jacob Bridgeman, and Jackson Koivun.

Koivun is the name to know. At 21, he is the youngest member of the USA team. He turned professional this year from Auburn, won the 3M Open in July, and has played himself into one of the sport's most compelling young narratives. He is this week's favorite at the Biltmore Championship in Asheville — and next week he heads to Medinah for his Presidents Cup debut.

The International Side

Captain Geoff Ogilvy — the 2006 U.S. Open champion — leads a roster that includes automatic qualifiers and captain's picks from across the globe outside Europe. Hideki Matsuyama, Adam Scott, and Ryan Fox anchor a squad built on experience and course management. Scott, a former Masters champion, is appearing in his record-extending number of Presidents Cups. Matsuyama's precision off the tee and with the irons makes him one of the most dangerous players in any format.

The International team has won just one of fifteen Presidents Cups — the 1998 edition in Melbourne. Every iteration since has featured the Americans as overwhelming favorites. But Ogilvy's side has the firepower to challenge if it finds early momentum.

The Format and the Stakes

The Presidents Cup runs September 24–27 at Medinah. The format: four-ball and foursomes sessions over the first three days, followed by 12 singles matches on Sunday. The team that reaches 15.5 points first wins. If the score is tied after all matches are complete, the USA retains the Cup as the defending champion.

USA has won 13 of 15 editions. The dominance is historic. But the format rewards momentum, and a fast International start could change the complexion of the week early.

What's Next

Several USA team members — including Koivun and Bridgeman — are playing this week's Biltmore Championship in Asheville as a final tune-up. Opening ceremonies at Medinah are September 23, with four-ball matches beginning September 24.
