**Headline:** LIV Bankruptcy Clock Ticking — Oct. 13 Court Deadline, Rahm Owed $7.5M

LIV Golf's Chapter 11 bankruptcy (filed September 8) has a key court deadline approaching: October 13, when the restructuring plan requires further legal clarity. Jon Rahm is the largest unsecured player creditor at $7.5 million owed. Bryson DeChambeau ($5.8M) and Dustin Johnson ($5.5M) are next. London-based BC Partners is leading the restructuring effort aimed at relaunching as "LIV 2.0" in 2027 — with four events planned (Saudi Arabia, Australia, South Africa, Hong Kong) and a player-majority ownership structure. Rahm remains noncommittal on joining LIV 2.0.

**Why now:** October 13 is 26 days away. The clock is running on a story that reshapes professional golf's landscape.
