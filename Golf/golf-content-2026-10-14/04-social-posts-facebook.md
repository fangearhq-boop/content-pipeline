## STORY 3: Presidents Cup Comeback

**Facebook Post:**
At the end of Saturday at Medinah Country Club, the United States was down three points with 12 singles matches left to play.

What followed was, according to Golf Channel's Brandel Chamblee, "the greatest" Presidents Cup Sunday performance in the event's history.

The USA won 9.5 of 12 available singles points. The clinching moment came from Jacob Bridgeman — a Presidents Cup rookie — who defeated Min Woo Lee 1-up to secure the winning point. The final score: USA 17, International 13.

It was America's 11th consecutive Presidents Cup title. The overall record now stands at 14–1–1.

The comeback erased a stunning Friday Foursomes session in which the International team swept the USA 5–0. Captain Brandt Snedeker's team went from crisis to champion inside 24 hours.

## STORY 4: Scheffler Rankings Streak

**Facebook Post:**
Scottie Scheffler entered this week in his 178th consecutive week as the world's No. 1 ranked golfer.

That number is hard to contextualize — so let's try: Tiger Woods holds the all-time record for consecutive weeks at No. 1 with 281. Scheffler is 103 weeks behind that mark. That's approximately two more years at the top of the rankings.

The gap between Scheffler at No. 1 and Rory McIlroy at No. 2 is larger than McIlroy's gap over virtually any other player. There's no obvious challenger in the immediate field of vision.

The full rankings for the week of October 11–12: No. 1 Scottie Scheffler, No. 2 Rory McIlroy, No. 3 Matt Fitzpatrick, No. 4 Cameron Young, No. 5 Wyndham Clark.

Tiger's record will require Scheffler to stay healthy, stay dominant, and stay motivated through at least the middle of 2028. Nothing in his game says that's impossible.

## STORY 5: Ryder Cup 2027

**Facebook Post:**
The 2027 Ryder Cup is less than a year away — and both teams are already building their rosters.

The venue is The Golf Course at Adare Manor in County Limerick, Ireland. The competition dates are September 17–19, 2027. The course plays to 7,509 yards, par 72, and the Irish galleries are going to make it one of the loudest weeks in golf.

On the USA side: Jim Furyk is the captain, with Stewart Cink and Justin Leonard as vice captains. Furyk promised a "significant overhaul" of the American qualification system after captaining the 2018 USA team to a 17.5–10.5 loss in Paris.

On the European side: Luke Donald returns for a third consecutive captaincy. He won in 2023. He won in 2025. If he wins in 2027, he becomes the first captain in Ryder Cup history to win three consecutive editions — and the first to captain three straight editions since Bernard Gallacher.

Qualification points began accumulating on August 27. The window closes August 22, 2027, after the BMW Championship. Six players earn automatic spots for each team; the captains pick six more.

Eleven months away. Already worth watching.
