Less Than a Year to Limerick: The Road to the 2027 Ryder Cup at Adare Manor

By Jake Torres | October 14, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

The 2027 Ryder Cup is less than a year away. Qualification points have been accumulating since August 27, 2026. Jim Furyk is building the United States team. Luke Donald is building the European team. In September 2027, they meet at The Golf Course at Adare Manor in County Limerick, Ireland, for the 46th edition of the sport's greatest team competition — and Donald will be trying to do something no Ryder Cup captain has ever done.

The Captains

Luke Donald has captained Europe to consecutive Ryder Cup victories. He won in 2023. He won in 2025. If he wins at Adare Manor, he becomes the first captain in the history of the Ryder Cup to win three consecutive editions. He would also be the first captain to serve three straight Ryder Cups since Bernard Gallacher captained Europe in 1991, 1993, and 1995. The historical weight of what Donald is chasing is significant — and it creates a compelling storyline for the European team before a ball is struck.

Jim Furyk is captaining the United States for the second time. His first captaincy was at Le Golf National in Paris in 2018, where the USA lost 17.5–10.5 in one of the more lopsided outcomes in recent Ryder Cup history. Furyk has promised a "significant overhaul" of the American qualification system. His vice captains are Stewart Cink and Justin Leonard, both former Ryder Cup players with substantial team competition experience. Europe's vice captain is Edoardo Molinari.

The Venue

Adare Manor opened its renovated golf course in 2018 and has hosted significant European Tour events since then. The championship setup plays to 7,509 yards at par 72. The County Limerick location in the Irish countryside will produce one of the most partisan atmospheres in Ryder Cup history — European home galleries at Irish venues are an experience that American players do not typically face in the normal PGA Tour schedule.

Public access to the course is limited through 2027 due to Ryder Cup preparations. The competition dates are September 17–19, with the full Ryder Cup week running September 13–19.

How Teams Are Built

Both the USA and Europe use a combination of automatic qualification and captain's picks. Qualification points are accumulated at designated events across the tour schedule — higher-ranked tournaments produce more points. The window closes August 22, 2027, after the BMW Championship. Six players qualify automatically for each team based on points accumulated. Each captain then selects six additional players.

Furyk's promised overhaul of the qualification system means the specific criteria for automatic USA qualification may look different from the 2025 edition. That announcement — whenever it arrives — will generate significant discussion in the American golf community about which players benefit from the new system and which are disadvantaged.

What's Next

The qualification window for the 2027 Ryder Cup closes after the BMW Championship on August 22, 2027. The competition itself runs September 17–19 at Adare Manor in County Limerick, Ireland. The Ryder Cup 2027 is approximately 11 months away.
