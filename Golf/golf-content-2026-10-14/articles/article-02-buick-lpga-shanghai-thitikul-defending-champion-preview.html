Thitikul Arrives in Shanghai as Defending Champion — and Golf's Most In-Form Player Right Now

By Jake Torres | October 14, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

The Buick LPGA Shanghai begins Thursday at Sheshan International Golf Club in Shanghai, and the question of who the favorite is requires almost no analysis. Jeeno Thitikul is the defending champion. She has four LPGA Tour wins in 2026. She is ranked No. 2 in the world. She is the betting favorite at +440 to +450. If you were building a bracket of the most dangerous golfer in women's golf heading into this week, you would start and likely stop with the Thai 23-year-old who already owns a trophy from this venue.

How Thitikul Won Here Last Year

Thitikul won the 2025 Buick LPGA Shanghai in a playoff against Minami Katsu, claiming victory on the fifth extra hole. Playoff wins at a venue tend to stick in a golfer's memory — the pressure, the course details, the specific shots that held up under maximum scrutiny. Returning as defending champion to a course she closed out in extra holes gives Thitikul a psychological familiarity that other players in the field simply do not have.

The 2026 edition plays to 6,735 yards at par 72 at Sheshan International Golf Club in Songjiang District. The purse is $3.2 million, a record for this event. The CME Globe points allocation is 500 for the winner — meaningful across a points race that is still alive heading into the fall.

The CME Globe Stakes

Nelly Korda leads the CME Globe points race heading into Shanghai. Thitikul is second, approximately 900 points behind. The Buick LPGA Shanghai is worth 500 points to the winner, which means a Thitikul victory this week — combined with a difficult Korda result — could significantly alter the race toward the CME Globe finale.

The pressure is real in both directions: Thitikul needs results to close the points gap, and the tournament carries real weight beyond just its own title. Her form — four wins in a single LPGA season, her 11th career title — suggests she is playing some of the best golf of her career at exactly the right time of year.

The Competition

The 81-player field includes Ruoning Yin, the Chinese golfer who won the Buick LPGA Shanghai in 2024 and will carry the enthusiasm of the home crowd at Sheshan. Minjee Lee, Sei Young Kim, and Miyu Yamashita are also notable in the field. But the betting lines reflect the competitive reality: Thitikul, as both defending champion and the most consistent player of the LPGA's 2026 calendar year, enters as the clear number to beat.

What's Next

Round 1 of the Buick LPGA Shanghai begins Thursday, October 15, at Sheshan International Golf Club in Shanghai. The final round is scheduled for Sunday, October 18.
