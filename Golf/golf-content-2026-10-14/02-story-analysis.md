## STORY 1: LIV Golf Chapter 11 — Oct. 25 Deadline, Rahm Says "Unacceptable"

**Headline:** LIV Golf's Oct. 25 Ultimatum: Rahm Walks, Garcia Is Free, and the League Faces Its Reckoning
**Alternate headline:** The Oct. 25 Deadline: LIV Golf Gives Its Players 11 Days to Decide Their Future

**Angle:** The bankruptcy is no longer hypothetical — it's formalized, with a hard deadline. The story is about what happens in the next 11 days: who stays, who leaves, and whether LIV 2.0 can reach the "critical mass" of player commitments it needs to survive as a league. Rahm's "unacceptable" comment is the most newsworthy single element — he's not just leaving, he's openly criticizing the restructuring terms.

**Key narrative beats:**
- Hard deadline creates urgency that wasn't there before
- Rahm's rejection is symbolic — he was the face of LIV's biggest free-agent signing
- Garcia's release via bankruptcy court is also notable — it's now legal, not just contractual
- DP World Tour vs. LIV 2.0: players being forced to pick a side in a very literal way
- PGA Tour staying out of it — "no plan to offer a route back" is a cold response

**Tone:** Analytical, factual, no rooting for either side. This is the biggest story in professional golf right now.

## STORY 4: Scottie Scheffler's 178 Consecutive Weeks at No. 1

**Headline:** 178 Weeks and Counting: Scottie Scheffler and the Chase for Tiger's Record
**Alternate headline:** Scottie Scheffler Has Been the World's Best Golfer for Over Three Years. Tiger's Record Is Still 103 Weeks Away.

**Angle:** Milestone countdown story. Scheffler is at 178 consecutive weeks. Tiger's record is 281. That's 103 more weeks — roughly two more years at No. 1. The story asks: is it plausible? Frames current rankings context. Light and engaging rather than heavy analysis.

**Key narrative beats:**
- 178 consecutive weeks = not a hot streak, it's generational consistency
- 281 is the mountain — Tiger set it for a reason
- The gap between Scheffler and McIlroy (No. 2) is massive, suggesting it won't end soon
- Not just consecutive weeks: career total already ~212 (means he's been No. 1 for most of his career)

**Tone:** Feature/celebration. Fans enjoy ranking milestones.
