LIV Golf's October 13 Deadline Is 14 Days Away — Players Own 52.5% If They Sign

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | September 29, 2026

The restructuring deal that determines whether LIV Golf continues in any form has a hard deadline: October 13. Fourteen days from today, a requisite number of players must have signed on to the BC Partners transaction for the deal to proceed. If that threshold is not met, the league's Chapter 11 reorganization takes a different path.

The threshold requires two-thirds of eligible player claimants — by amount of their claims — and one-half by count to agree to the new structure. Those are not casual preferences. They are legal commitments made in a bankruptcy proceeding, and the players who have the largest claims are, by definition, the ones whose votes carry the most weight in reaching the two-thirds threshold.

What Players Are Signing

The BC Partners deal injects $300 million into the reorganized league. In exchange, players receive significant structural concessions. They take a 52.5% majority ownership stake in the league itself — a claim that, in the prior version of LIV, belonged entirely to Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund. Each player also receives roughly 30% ownership in the team they play for, signing bonuses to reflect the revised terms, and the return of NIL rights that their original contracts with LIV required them to surrender.

The Saudi PIF has stepped back from the new ownership structure. LIV 2.0, if it launches in 2027, will be a private equity operation with player-majority ownership rather than a sovereign wealth fund project. That is a structurally different proposition from what players originally signed up for — and, for many of them, a better one.

The Creditor Calculus

LIV owes more than $64 million to its top 27 creditors. Jon Rahm is owed $7.5 million. Bryson DeChambeau is owed $5.8 million. These are the players whose signatures matter most in reaching the two-thirds threshold by claim amount — and they are also the players with the most direct financial stake in whether the new structure creates more value than the cash they are currently owed.

A player who signs the BC Partners deal is accepting equity in an uncertain future venture in partial exchange for debt owed to them. A player who does not sign is walking away from their contract and joining a creditor queue in a bankruptcy proceeding. Neither option is clean. Both have consequences that extend beyond October 13.

What's Next

The October 13 deadline was set 35 days from the date of the bankruptcy petition. Any player who has not signed by that date is outside the deal's core group. Whether the threshold is met — and whether BC Partners moves forward — will be known within two weeks. The 2027 LIV Golf season, if it exists, depends on what happens in the next 14 days.
