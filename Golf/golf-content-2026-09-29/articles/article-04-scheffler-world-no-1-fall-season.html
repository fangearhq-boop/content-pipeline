Scheffler Won the FedEx Cup. Now He Plays October Golf — and He's Still World No. 1.

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | September 29, 2026

Scottie Scheffler finished 2026's FedEx Cup Playoffs at East Lake in Atlanta with a final-round 66, erasing a three-shot deficit to win the Tour Championship and claim his second FedEx Cup title in three years. He received $10 million in playoff earnings. He surpassed Tiger Woods as the all-time PGA Tour career earnings leader, his total reaching $130,390,661.

That was August 30. Now October is here, the FedExCup Fall has started, and Scheffler is in the field at Black Desert.

The Season That Built This

Scheffler won three times in 2026, tying Chris Gotterup, Matt Fitzpatrick, and Wyndham Clark for the most wins on tour during the season. His third win came two weeks before the Tour Championship, and he carried momentum from the late season into a final-round performance at East Lake that was essentially perfect when he needed it most.

The FedEx Cup win placed him alongside Tiger Woods and Rory McIlroy as the only players to win multiple FedEx Cup titles. He joined McIlroy as the only player to do it twice in three years. These are not incidental statistical achievements. They represent a level of sustained performance that defines how the game is currently measured.

What Motivates a Player Who Has Everything

The honest answer, based on how Scheffler has spoken in previous years, is that he does not operate from a motivation of accumulation. He has spoken consistently about competing for the love of competition — about the practice range, the process, the challenge of the next shot rather than the record it contributes to.

For a player at his level, the fall season is where consistency is built and form is maintained. The Bank of Utah Championship is not a major. It is a $6 million fall event at a desert course in Ivins, Utah. Scheffler competes there for the same reason he competes everywhere: because the golf is in front of him and the scoreboard requires an answer.

What's Next

Scheffler enters the fall as World No. 1 with his lead intact after the Presidents Cup week, during which he was noted as having a "complicated Sunday" by multiple outlets covering the event. Round 1 at the Bank of Utah Championship begins Thursday, October 1, and runs through Sunday at Black Desert Golf Course.
