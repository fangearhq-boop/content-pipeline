Bank of Utah Championship Preview — Black Desert's $6M FedExCup Fall Stop Opens Thursday

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | September 29, 2026

The Bank of Utah Championship at Black Desert Golf Course begins Thursday, October 1 in Ivins, Utah. With a $6 million purse and full FedExCup Fall points available, the third edition of this event draws a motivated field to one of the most visually dramatic venues on the PGA Tour schedule.

Black Desert is set in the red rock country of southern Utah, where ancient lava flows frame the fairways and the high desert elevation pushes ball flight several yards further than sea level. The course design rewards precision over power — knowing when to attack and when to lay back is worth more than an extra 20 yards off the tee.

The Defending Champion

Michael Brennan won the 2025 edition of the Bank of Utah Championship and returns this week to defend. Brennan's 2025 run at Black Desert featured consistent ballstriking, a hot putter in the final round, and smart course management across four days — a blueprint that translates well at any desert venue where the premium on positioning outweighs raw scoring numbers.

The course has rewarded similar profiles in both of its previous two editions. Players who can control their misses and work the ball in both directions on the windy high desert corridors tend to finish closer to the top than bombers who rely on distance to create scoring opportunities.

The Fall Stakes

The Bank of Utah Championship is the second stop of eight in the 2026 FedExCup Fall series. The fall schedule runs through the end of the year, with full-point events giving players on the bubble a direct path to improving their standing before the wrap-up events that determine 2027 PGA Tour status.

For players who finished the FedEx Cup Playoffs short of the top tier, the fall is the season. The Bank of Utah Championship draws a field that is genuinely motivated — players competing for status, not just paychecks. That competition tends to produce tighter leaderboards and fewer blowout rounds than signature events where star power dominates the coverage.

What's Next

Round 1 at Black Desert Golf Course tees off Thursday, October 1. The tournament runs through Sunday, October 4. FedExCup Fall points update after the final round, and the standings shift heading into the back half of the fall series.
