2027 Ryder Cup Preview — Furyk Leads USA to Adare Manor, Donald Returns for Europe

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | September 29, 2026

With the Presidents Cup concluded and the trophy back in American hands, the next significant event on the team golf calendar is already visible. The 2027 Ryder Cup will be played at Adare Manor in Adare, County Limerick, Ireland. Jim Furyk captains the United States. Luke Donald returns for Europe.

The gap between now and Adare Manor is approximately 12 months. In that window, rosters build through qualification, captains develop game plans, and the analysis of what happened at Medinah this week gets filtered into preparation for a more consequential contest.

What Medinah Showed

The 2026 Presidents Cup produced a result that leaves useful evidence for both camps. The International team, without the depth or infrastructure of a Ryder Cup European side, outplayed the United States for three consecutive days at a home venue before losing a Sunday singles session that the U.S. won by a considerable margin.

The European team at Adare Manor will be stronger. They will have better players, more match-play experience together, and a home crowd in Ireland that represents one of the most passionate golfing cultures in the world. They will have Luke Donald, who has captained this team since 2022 and knows exactly how to prepare for match play at this level.

What Medinah showed the Americans is that their Sunday depth advantage may not be sufficient if the team is three points down after three days. It also showed them what happens when the opposing captain uses his lineup effectively.

Furyk's Task

Jim Furyk's challenge at Adare Manor is the mirror image of the situation he watched unfold this week. He must build a team that does not need a Sunday comeback. He must identify pairings that work across formats — foursomes, fourballs, singles — and develop the team chemistry that the Americans have historically relied on only in their strongest years.

The PGA of America introduced a revised qualifying format for 2027 in response to criticism of how recent American Ryder Cup rosters were assembled. Furyk will have the benefit of a process that is at least partially updated. Whether it selects a better team is a question that answers itself on the first day at Adare Manor.

The Venue

Adare Manor is a luxury resort course in County Limerick that was redesigned specifically to host the Ryder Cup. The layout features parkland characteristics — tree-lined fairways, demanding approach angles, greens that reward touch — and the Irish weather introduces conditions that the American team will need to prepare for specifically. Wind, rain, soft ground, and low light are not standard preparation environments for a tour that primarily plays in mild, sunny conditions in the American southwest.

What's Next

The 2027 Ryder Cup qualifying process for the United States begins this fall. Every PGA Tour event from October onward contributes toward the points that will determine automatic qualification spots. For European players, the DP World Tour's Race to Dubai runs through the calendar year. The Ryder Cup roster conversation has already started.
