Von Dellingshausen at 16-Under: German Leads BMW Australian PGA Championship by Three Heading Into Sunday's Final Round

By Ryan Calloway

November 28, 2026

Nicolai Von Dellingshausen enters the final round of the BMW Australian PGA Championship with a three-shot lead over Alexander Noren after posting 16-under par through 54 holes at The Lakes Golf Club in Sydney. The German golfer has led this event since round 2 and extended his advantage through Saturday's third round, arriving at Sunday in firm control of a DP World Tour co-sanctioned event that has produced a compelling leaderboard across three days.

The Leaderboard After Round 3

Alexander Noren is second at 13-under, three shots off the pace. Tom McKibbin and Kiradech Aphibarnrat are tied for third at 12-under, keeping the chase group compact enough that a single swing can alter the standings. Haydn Barron, the amateur who led the event outright after the opening round with an 8-under 64, is at 11-under and remains inside the top five of the full professional field — a performance that has been one of the week's consistent storylines. Adam Scott sits at 9-under. Cameron Smith, who shot 64 in round 2, is at 10-under and faces a six-shot deficit heading into Sunday.

Von Dellingshausen has been in front or close to the top of this leaderboard since day two, and his round 3 score of 69 maintained the advantage he built earlier in the week. At 16-under with a three-shot cushion, he is well-positioned to claim the BMW Australian PGA Championship title on Sunday.

The Chasers' Paths

Noren at three back is the most realistic challenger. The Swede has the experience to assemble a low round when needed, and three shots on a course like The Lakes — where birdies are accessible and the front nine rewards aggressive play — is a gap that can evaporate quickly.

McKibbin and Aphibarnrat at four back face a steeper task, but four-shot swings in final rounds on this circuit are not unusual. McKibbin, the Northern Irishman who has played well throughout the week, will need to build momentum from the opening holes to stay in contention. Aphibarnrat is a consistent performer in these conditions and enters Sunday with nothing to lose.

Smith at six back would need a career-low round at The Lakes to overhaul Von Dellingshausen — theoretically possible but requiring near-perfect execution.

What's Next

The final round of the BMW Australian PGA Championship tees off Sunday, November 29, at The Lakes Golf Club in Sydney. Von Dellingshausen will play in the final group alongside his nearest challengers. The event concludes the Australian PGA leg of the Australasian swing before the tour moves to Kingston Heath in Melbourne for the Australian Open (December 3-6).
