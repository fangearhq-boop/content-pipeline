### STORY 2: PGA Tour CEO Hire

**Long-Form Post** (10:30 AM ET)
Window: 10:30 AM

🏌️ MAJOR STRUCTURAL CHANGE AT THE PGA TOUR

Jay Monahan, PGA Tour Commissioner since 2017, is stepping down at the end of 2026 when his contract expires. His tenure was defined by the LIV Golf disruption and years of contentious negotiations over professional golf's future.

The PGA Tour has responded to this transition by creating something new: a CEO role. Brian Rolapp — an NFL executive — has been hired as the Tour's first-ever chief executive officer. The CEO role is designed to handle commercial and business operations separately from Tour governance.

For fans, the question is what this means going forward. The PGA Tour enters 2027 with new leadership, a stabilized competitive landscape after LIV's collapse, and unresolved questions about where the top players compete and how media rights are distributed.

A new chapter begins.
