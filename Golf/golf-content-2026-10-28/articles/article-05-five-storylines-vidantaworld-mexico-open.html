Five Storylines to Watch at the VidantaWorld Mexico Open Starting Thursday

By Jake Torres

October 28, 2026

PGA Tour | VidantaWorld Mexico Open | Preview

The VidantaWorld Mexico Open starts Thursday at Vidanta Vallarta, and the tournament delivers a genuinely compelling week of PGA Tour golf. Here are five reasons this event should be on every golf fan's radar through the weekend.

No. 1 — Smotherman's Three-Peat Bid

Brandon Smotherman won the Bermuda Championship on Monday and his previous event the week before that. Three consecutive PGA Tour wins would put his name alongside some of the rarest streaks in the sport's history. This is the storyline of the week, and it starts when Smotherman walks onto the first tee Thursday morning. Every shot he hits will be watched in the context of a potential run that would define his career.

No. 2 — Wyndham Clark at the Top of His Game

The pre-tournament favorite has earned that label honestly. Three wins this season, consistent results across different course types, and the kind of mental fortitude that shows up on leaderboards when the pressure rises — Clark is exactly the kind of player who wins tournaments like this one. If Smotherman is the story of the week, Clark is the standard he will need to beat.

No. 3 — A Redesigned Course

Holes 4, 11, 13, and 15 at Vidanta Vallarta have all been reworked for this year's event. In a field of professionals who have played the old layout and prepared extensively for it, the changes introduce an element of the unknown — particularly for players who use course history heavily in their preparation. First-round scoring on the new holes will tell us which players adapted quickly and which ones are still finding their footing.

No. 4 — John Daly II on the Big Stage

The human interest angle of the week is John Daly II teeing it up among the best players on the PGA Tour. For fans who grew up watching his father's career, there is something undeniably compelling about watching the next generation take the stage. Daly II tees off at 10:44 AM ET Thursday — put his name on your radar early.

No. 5 — Rasmus Hojgaard as Your Sleeper Pick

The PGA Tour's own expert picks identified Hojgaard at +6600 as a player whose game fits the Vidanta Vallarta layout well. Sleeper picks at this price range exist because the market has not fully priced in a player's course-specific strengths, and tournament experts have flagged him specifically. If the leaderboard gets interesting on Saturday afternoon, check where Hojgaard sits.

What's Next

Round 1 of the VidantaWorld Mexico Open begins Thursday, October 29 at Vidanta Vallarta. Full results and leaderboard coverage will follow after Thursday's play concludes. This is a week worth following from the first tee shot.
