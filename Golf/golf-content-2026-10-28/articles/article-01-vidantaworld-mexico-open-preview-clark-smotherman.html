VidantaWorld Mexico Open Preview: Clark Favored, Smotherman Eyes Three Straight Wins

By Ryan Calloway

October 28, 2026

PGA Tour | VidantaWorld Mexico Open | Vidanta Vallarta

The 2026 VidantaWorld Mexico Open tees off Thursday at Vidanta Vallarta with a $6 million purse and 500 FedExCup Fall points on offer — and it arrives carrying one of the most compelling storylines of the fall season. Brandon Smotherman, who won the Bermuda Championship on Monday in a playoff, arrives at the venue with a chance to win three consecutive PGA Tour events.

Wyndham Clark enters as the pre-tournament favorite. Clark has been among the most consistent performers on the PGA Tour this season, recording three wins. He arrives at Vidanta Vallarta with his game in the kind of shape that has defined his best results: accurate off the tee, dialed in with his irons, and steady enough from 12 feet and in to close out tournaments under pressure.

A Reworked Course

This year's Mexico Open features a significantly updated layout. Holes 4, 11, 13, and 15 at Vidanta Vallarta have been reworked heading into the 2026 edition. The changes reshape multiple decision points on a course that was already regarded as one of the more demanding stops on the fall schedule, and players who have done their homework on the new designs should have an advantage through the first two rounds.

Defending champion Brian Campbell returns at odds of +22500 — a reflection of the field's overall depth rather than any doubt about his ability to contend again. Campbell's 2025 victory at this event was earned through excellent ball-striking and sharp putting on a course that punishes indecision. The challenge for any defending champion is managing the expectations that come with a prior win while treating the event as a fresh competition.

A Deep, Competitive Field

Beyond Clark, Smotherman, and Campbell, the Mexico Open field includes Tony Finau, Max Homa, Tom Kim, and John Daly II, among others. The combination of established tour veterans and emerging players creates a field with multiple legitimate paths to the trophy. Rasmus Hojgaard has been identified by PGA Tour experts as a sleeper at +6600 — a long-shot pick with a game that suits Vidanta Vallarta's layout.

FedExCup Fall points remain the primary carrot for the week. The 500 points available to the winner represent a significant boost in the standings for any player looking to improve their position heading into 2027. On a fall schedule that rewards consistency, Mexico is the kind of event that can reshape the standings in one week for a player who puts four rounds together.

What's Next

Round 1 at Vidanta Vallarta begins Thursday, October 29. Tee times are spread across the morning and early afternoon, with Clark, Smotherman, and Campbell among the marquee pairings to follow. Rounds 2 through 4 run Friday through Sunday. Check back throughout the week for round-by-round coverage and leaderboard updates.
