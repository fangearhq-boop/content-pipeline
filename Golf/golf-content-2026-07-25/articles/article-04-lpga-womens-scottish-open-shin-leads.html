Jenny Shin Leads Women's Scottish Open by Five — Round 3 Underway at Dundonald Links

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap

July 25, 2026

Jenny Shin stormed to a five-stroke lead at the halfway point of the ISPS HANDA Women's Scottish Open, and Round 3 is underway today at Dundonald Links in Scotland. Pajaree Anannarukarn and A Lim Kim are the closest challengers heading into the weekend at a tournament that serves as the direct lead-in to the next women's major championship on the calendar.

The AIG Women's Open — a major — follows on July 30 at a Lancashire venue in the United Kingdom. The field competing at the Women's Scottish Open this week is, in no small part, a preview of the contenders who will arrive for the major in the best form of the summer stretch.

Shin's Five-Stroke Lead

A five-stroke lead at the halfway point of a professional golf tournament is significant but not impenetrable — particularly on a Scottish links layout where the conditions can shift the competitive landscape in a single afternoon. Dundonald Links, a coastal course in Ayrshire, plays differently when the wind arrives and the rain complicates club selection and putting.

Shin has been consistent in her round-by-round performance to build the kind of cushion that puts her in control heading into the weekend. The gap between her and the chasing pack means her closest rivals need to gain strokes while Shin simultaneously loses them — a combination that requires both an excellent round from the pursuers and an imperfect round from the leader.

Anannarukarn, a Thai professional whose game is well-suited to demanding conditions, and A Lim Kim, the 2020 U.S. Women's Open champion, represent genuine threats if Shin's form dips. Both have the experience and the short game to make charges on difficult terrain.

The AIG Women's Open Countdown

The timing of the Women's Scottish Open — one week before the AIG Women's Open — gives the tournament an importance beyond its individual result. Players building form across links conditions this weekend carry that momentum directly into the major next week.

For context on how the 2026 women's major calendar has unfolded: Haeran Ryu won the Amundi Evian Championship on July 12 in the most recent major, giving the South Korean her second major championship title. The next major at Lancashire will add another name to the list of 2026's major champions.

Players who perform well at Dundonald Links this week will have a sense of confidence in links conditions — a quality that often determines major championship outcomes more than raw ball-striking ability.

What's Next

Round 3 is in progress today at Dundonald Links. Round 4 is Sunday, July 26. Shin enters the third round with her five-stroke lead as the clear favorite to claim the Women's Scottish Open title. Whether the lead holds through a full weekend of competitive links golf is Saturday's and Sunday's story to write.

Then, one week later, the AIG Women's Open begins. The competitors who best demonstrate their links game this week will have the first argument for major contention next week at Lancashire.
