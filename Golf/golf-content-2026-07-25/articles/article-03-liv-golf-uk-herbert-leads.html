Lucas Herbert's Bogey-Free 61 Sets the Pace at LIV Golf UK — DeChambeau One Back Entering Round 3

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap

July 25, 2026

Lucas Herbert arrived at JCB Golf & Country Club in Rocester, England, and immediately made his intentions clear. A bogey-free 61 in Round 1 — one of the most impressive individual rounds of the 2026 LIV Golf season — staked the Australian to a 13-under lead entering the third round, which is underway today at the LIV Golf United Kingdom event.

Bryson DeChambeau, whose own history with sub-65 rounds is well documented, sits one shot back at 12-under. Harold Varner III, representing team OKGC, is among the competitors in the hunt as the LIV Golf UK field enters the penultimate round of the weekend.

Herbert's Opening 61

A bogey-free round in professional golf at any level is notable. A bogey-free 61 at a LIV Golf event — where the combination of elite international players and no-cut pressure creates a competitive environment that rewards low scoring — is exceptional. Herbert moved through the JCB Golf & Country Club layout without a single dropped shot, making birdies at a rate that set the field a target no other player reached in Round 1.

Herbert, an Australian tour professional who has built a solid LIV Golf career, enters Round 3 with the kind of momentum that one-round leaders need going into the final two rounds. Whether he can protect the lead across Saturday and Sunday will define how the UK event is remembered.

DeChambeau in Pursuit

Bryson DeChambeau enters Round 3 one shot back — close enough to make Saturday's round decisive. DeChambeau, whose 59 at the Greenbrier in 2023 remains one of professional golf's most celebrated individual rounds, brings substantial low-round potential to any leaderboard he appears on. His 12-under total through two rounds is the result of consistent play rather than a single highlight performance.

The gap between Herbert and DeChambeau is one stroke — the narrowest possible margin that still gives the leader control. DeChambeau will need to birdie holes that Herbert doesn't to close the gap, then construct a final-round lead of his own.

LIV Golf's Late-Season Calendar

The UK event takes place on the LIV Golf schedule during what is becoming a competitive back half of the 2026 season, which runs from February 5 through August 30 across 14 events with 57 players. The no-cut, shotgun-start format keeps every player in the field for the full event, and the simultaneous team and individual competition adds a layer of scoring dynamics that differs from traditional stroke-play events.

The season-ending team championship remains in view for the top teams. The UK event, played against the backdrop of The Open Championship at Royal Birkdale ending just last Sunday, gives LIV Golf a moment in the spotlight while the golf world's attention remains on the United Kingdom.

What's Next

Round 3 at LIV Golf UK is underway today, Saturday July 25. The final round is Sunday. Herbert's one-stroke lead over DeChambeau entering the third round makes this a genuine two-player race at the top, with Varner and others attempting to stay in the conversation. By Sunday evening, the LIV Golf UK champion will be determined.
