Charles Schwab Cup Playoffs Begin: Stifel Charity Classic Opens Senior Golf's Championship Season

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | October 25, 2026

The postseason on PGA Tour Champions has arrived. The 2026 Charles Schwab Cup Playoffs are underway at Norwood Hills Country Club in St. Louis, Missouri, with the Stifel Charity Classic serving as the first of three events that will determine senior golf's champion of the year.

The $2.3 million event runs through Sunday, October 25, and the field carries 72 players who have earned their way into the playoffs through the 2026 PGA Tour Champions season. Every stroke carries Schwab Cup points, and with two more events still to follow, positioning matters as much as winning this week.

Stewart Cink's Season-Long Lead

Stewart Cink enters the playoffs as the Charles Schwab Cup season points leader — a testament to a remarkably consistent campaign across the full 2026 schedule. Cink, the former Open Championship winner, has been among the most reliable performers on the senior circuit this year, converting competitive rounds into top-ten finishes with the kind of regularity that builds commanding leads.

Holding the Schwab Cup lead entering the playoffs is a meaningful advantage, but it is by no means insurmountable. Three events remain, and the playoff structure awards enough points that any player in the 72-player field can still emerge with the Cup title. That is precisely what makes this week compelling.

Bjørn the Defending Champion

Thomas Bjørn arrives as defending champion, having won the 2025 Stifel Charity Classic at Norwood Hills at 12-under. The Danish veteran — a former European Ryder Cup captain and major champion contender throughout his career — brings local knowledge and a track record of performing when Schwab Cup points are at their most valuable.

For Bjørn, repeating at Norwood Hills would not only deliver another title but also vault him into serious Schwab Cup contention as the season reaches its most decisive stretch.

What's Next

The Stifel Charity Classic concludes Sunday. Following Norwood Hills, the Schwab Cup Playoffs continue at two more venues before the season champion is crowned. For the 72 players in this field, the postseason clock is ticking — and every shot at Norwood Hills shapes the path to the title. Senior golf's championship season is here, and the best players in the game over-50 are stepping up to decide who owns 2026.
