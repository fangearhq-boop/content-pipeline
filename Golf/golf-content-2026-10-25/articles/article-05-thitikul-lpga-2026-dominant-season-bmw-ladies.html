Jeeno Thitikul Is the LPGA's Best Player in 2026 — and the BMW Ladies Championship Is Next

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | October 25, 2026

The 2026 LPGA Tour season has a clear standard-bearer, and her name is Jeeno Thitikul. The Thai superstar has won four times this season — the most of any player on Tour — and has done so on stages ranging from her home country of Thailand to the Pacific shores of Hawaii. She is the defining player of a compelling LPGA season, and with the BMW Ladies Championship in South Korea approaching, her next opportunity to extend her lead is imminent.

A Season of Milestones

Thitikul's four victories this year include the Honda LPGA Thailand, where she claimed her first LPGA Tour title on home soil — a moment she described as deeply personal. The Honda victory was her eighth career LPGA win at the time.

Her most recent victory was the Lotte Championship in Hawaii, where she pulled clear of Ina Yoon by three shots to earn the $450,000 winner's share of the $3 million purse. Golf Channel noted at the time that it was her fourth LPGA Tour win of the 2026 season. Thitikul's ability to win in different conditions, on different continents, and against the full depth of the LPGA field reflects the breadth of her current game.

What Makes Her Dangerous

Thitikul's game is built on precision and aggression. She is among the best ball-strikers on the LPGA Tour, with a swing that generates reliable, consistent contact and distances. Her short game has evolved significantly over the past two seasons, adding a layer of scrambling ability that turns manageable misses into pars and close approaches into birdies. Combined with a competitive mindset that has always been one of her most-cited traits, the full package makes her extraordinarily difficult to beat when she is on form.

BMW Ladies Championship Ahead

The LPGA Tour's next stop is the BMW Ladies Championship in South Korea — a tournament on one of the sport's most passionate regional stages. Asian golf fans are among the most engaged in the world, and the BMW Ladies Championship routinely draws enormous galleries for an event that has produced memorable winning moments.

Thitikul will be among the favorites. Playing in Asia, where she is a household name across the continent, she tends to bring extra energy and focus to these stages. Whether she can extend her win total to five — and whether the rest of the field can find a way to stop her — will be the central storyline in South Korea.

What's Next

The LPGA Tour season continues with Korea and beyond. For Thitikul, 2026 has already been her most successful campaign. How many more wins she adds before the year closes will determine whether this becomes the signature season of her career. The competition is watching. So are the fans. And Thitikul, as she has done all year, will simply keep playing her game.
