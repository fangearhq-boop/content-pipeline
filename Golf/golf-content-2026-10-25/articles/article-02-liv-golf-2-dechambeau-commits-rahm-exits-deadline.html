LIV Golf 2.0 Deadline Day: DeChambeau Reportedly Commits, Rahm Formally Exits

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | October 25, 2026

Sunday, October 25, 2026 marks a pivotal day in professional golf's ongoing restructuring. The deadline for players to commit to LIV Golf 2.0 has passed, and the picture that has emerged draws a sharp divide between those going and those staying.

Jon Rahm, one of LIV Golf's most prominent players and a three-time individual season champion in the league, has formally departed. His separation agreement was finalized around October 14, and while his next step remains undetermined, his exit from LIV represents one of the most significant roster developments in the league's short history.

DeChambeau Stays With LIV

Bryson DeChambeau's situation is more complicated. His original LIV contract was among 19 rejected by a U.S. bankruptcy court during the league's restructuring process — a decision that effectively made him a free agent. Despite that legal reality, sources told ESPN that DeChambeau "remains committed to LIV Golf and is working diligently behind the scenes to bring LIV 2.0 to fruition."

If accurate, it means the former U.S. Open champion has chosen LIV 2.0 as his path forward in professional golf, at least for now. DeChambeau has not spoken publicly about his future plans since the 2026 LIV season concluded and his original contract expired.

The November 5 Hearing

The LIV Golf restructuring is not yet legally finalized. A U.S. bankruptcy judge is scheduled to hold a final approval hearing on November 5 — eleven days from today. Until that hearing concludes and the deal receives official court sanction, the league's 2027 structure remains formally unresolved.

BC Partners has been identified as providing the financial backing for LIV 2.0, reported at $300 million. The league's continued existence depends on that deal being ratified by the court and the remaining roster of committed players being sufficient to launch a commercially viable season.

Rahm's Open Questions

For Rahm, the path ahead is less clear. Three consecutive individual LIV season championships put him among the tour's biggest names, but his departure comes with PGA Tour reinstatement still unsettled. The process of returning to the traditional tour carries its own set of requirements and timelines.

Where Rahm lands — whether on the PGA Tour, the DP World Tour, or some other arrangement — will be one of professional golf's most anticipated announcements in the months ahead. His talent is undeniable. The question is simply which stage he performs on next.

What's Next

The November 5 bankruptcy hearing is the next major milestone for LIV Golf 2.0. The deadline that has now passed gives the league's organizers a clearer picture of which players are in, which are out, and what a 2027 roster might look like. The sport's parallel-universe structure — PGA Tour on one side, LIV on the other — continues to evolve with every passing week, and Sunday's deadline is the latest chapter in a story with no clear ending date in sight.
