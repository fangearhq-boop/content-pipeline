Bermuda Championship Final Round Preview: Smotherman and Power at 16-Under, Chasing Pack Ready

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | October 25, 2026

The 2026 Butterfield Bermuda Championship comes down to a final round at Port Royal Golf Course on Sunday, with Austin Smotherman and Seamus Power sharing the lead at 16-under 197 after 54 holes of compelling golf on the North Atlantic island.

Two shots back, Taylor Moore, Corey Conners, and Ben Silverman are all tied at 14-under, forming a chasing pack with every incentive to go low. In total, 12 players are within four shots of the lead — more than enough to ensure a drama-filled conclusion to the week.

Weather Complicates the Finish

Officials moved Sunday's tee times up after forecasters projected winds of up to 35 miles per hour. Players will head out in three-ball groupings, with the final pairing of Power, Smotherman, and Taylor Moore off No. 1 at 8:46 AM ET. The early start gives the field a chance to get more holes in before the worst of the conditions arrive.

Wind has already been a defining factor this week. Power's experience at Port Royal's par-3 16th on Saturday was illustrative — a chip 7-iron to a front pin wound up in a back bunker some 35 yards long when a gust caught the ball at the worst possible moment. Managing those moments with discipline and patience could be the difference between winning and falling off the leaderboard.

Two Compelling Stories at the Top

Smotherman enters Sunday having already written a significant chapter in his young career. Earlier this month, he claimed his first PGA Tour title at the Bank of Utah Championship. A second win this month would be an extraordinary October for any golfer. Smotherman has shown nerves of steel and a short game capable of getting through the trickiest of setups.

Power brings a different set of motivations. The Irish veteran has won this event before — he took the Bermuda Championship title in 2022 — and he understands Port Royal's particular demands. His path to Sunday's lead came through a smooth 67 on Saturday, one that kept him composed despite the island's notorious winds. A win here would deliver far more than just the trophy.

What's Next

Final round coverage airs on Golf Channel from 11:00 AM to 2:00 PM ET. The leaderboard is volatile enough that multiple scenarios are possible — a front-nine charge from the chasing pack, a steady wire-to-wire win by either co-leader, or a final-hole drama that has become something of a Port Royal tradition. Whichever player holes their final putt with the lowest score gets the $1.08 million winner's check and a significant boost in FedEx Cup Fall standings. Sunday in Bermuda will be worth watching closely.
