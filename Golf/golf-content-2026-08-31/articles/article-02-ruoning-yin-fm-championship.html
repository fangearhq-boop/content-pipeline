Ruoning Yin Wins FM Championship at TPC Boston — 6th Career LPGA Title with Clutch Finish

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | August 31, 2026

LPGA Tour · FM Championship · Ruoning Yin

Ruoning Yin of China claimed her sixth career LPGA Tour title at the 2026 FM Championship at TPC Boston, closing with a 2-under 70 on Sunday for a 14-under total of 274 — good for a two-shot victory over Hyo Joo Kim. The win came in the kind of fashion that separates champions: a birdie when she needed the lead, a rival's costly error, and a closing birdie on the 72nd hole to make the margin clear. Yin pockets $660,000 from the tournament's $4.4 million purse.

The Decisive Moments

The final round's drama unfolded in the back nine, where Yin and her playing partner Hyo Joo Kim were locked in a duel at the top of the leaderboard. The turning point arrived at the par-5 12th, where Yin ran in a 25-foot birdie putt to share the lead with Kim. Two players, one score, six holes left to play.

The match turned on the par-3 16th. Kim — who had been a formidable opponent all week — three-putted the green, missing a 9-foot par putt to hand the advantage back to Yin. From that point, the championship was Yin's to lose. She did not lose it. A closing birdie on the par-5 18th put a two-shot final margin on the scoreboard, leaving no doubt.

Kim finished at 12-under with a final-round 71. Allisen Corpuz came in alone at third at 11-under, a shot ahead of Rose Zhang and Sei Young Kim — both finishing at 10-under in a tie for fourth.

A Return to the Winner's Circle

The win marked Yin's first LPGA victory in approximately two years and her sixth overall on Tour. The 23-year-old from China has established herself as one of the most talented players of her generation — a player capable of winning on the grandest stages. The FM Championship is one of the LPGA's most prestigious non-major events, with a $4.4 million purse and consistent placement on the Tour's late-summer calendar. Winning it is a statement.

The tournament had navigated weather complications, with rain forcing rounds two and three to be played back-to-back on Saturday, compressing the tournament into an unusual format before Sunday's decisive round at TPC Boston in Norton, Massachusetts. Yin handled the disruption better than anyone.

The Defending Champion Context

Miranda Wang had won the 2025 FM Championship at a remarkable 20-under, edging Thitikul by one shot in a dominant performance. Yin's 14-under total on a trickier layout — breezy conditions made Sunday difficult — represents a strong performance in its own right, particularly given the clutch moments she delivered when the tournament was on the line.

What's Next

The LPGA Tour continues its fall schedule as the season enters its final stretch of 2026. For Ruoning Yin, the FM Championship title adds to a career that is still in its early chapters — at 23, she has already won six times on the highest stage in women's professional golf. The best may still be ahead of her.
