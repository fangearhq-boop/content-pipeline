PGA Tour Fall Schedule — Biltmore Championship, Presidents Cup at Medinah, and What Comes Next

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | August 31, 2026

PGA Tour · Presidents Cup · Fall Schedule

With Scottie Scheffler lifting the FedExCup trophy at East Lake Golf Club on Sunday, the 2026 PGA Tour playoffs are officially complete. But the calendar does not pause. The fall schedule arrives almost immediately, and it is loaded — headlined by a Presidents Cup at one of golf's most storied venues. Here is what comes next.

Biltmore Championship — September 17-20, Asheville, North Carolina

The fall stretch opens with the Biltmore Championship, a new addition to the PGA Tour's post-FedExCup schedule held at the historic Biltmore Estate in Asheville, North Carolina. Nestled in the Appalachian Mountains, the Biltmore Estate provides one of the most visually distinctive backdrops on Tour. The event gives players a competitive outlet immediately following the playoffs and offers the Asheville region a marquee professional golf event on a nationally significant stage. Details on the full field were still being finalized at press time.

Presidents Cup — September 24-27, Medinah Country Club, Illinois

The centerpiece of the fall golf calendar is the 2026 Presidents Cup at Medinah Country Club in suburban Chicago — a venue with deep golf history, most notably from the 2012 Ryder Cup, where Europe staged one of sport's most dramatic reversals. This year the format is Presidents Cup: United States versus an International team representing the rest of the world outside Europe.

The United States team, anchored by Scheffler as the world's No. 1 player and FedExCup champion, enters as a heavy favorite. The International team has historically struggled against American squads in the Presidents Cup, with the U.S. holding a commanding all-time record in the competition. The Medinah setting — and the memories it carries — ensures the environment will be intense regardless of the competitive ledger.

The Presidents Cup runs Thursday through Sunday, September 24-27, with foursomes, four-ball, and singles sessions across the four days.

Bank of Utah Championship — October 1-4, Ivins, Utah

The fall Western Swing continues the following week with the Bank of Utah Championship in Ivins, Utah — a scenic venue in the red rock desert of southwestern Utah. The event is part of the Tour's continued commitment to building competitive events in the fall months that maintain player engagement and sponsor revenue through the end of the calendar year.

The Bigger Picture

The fall schedule arrives after one of the most compelling PGA Tour summers in recent memory. Scheffler's comeback at East Lake — erasing a three-shot deficit with a final-round 66 — capped a season that also featured his continued dominance as the world's top-ranked player. On the LPGA side, Ruoning Yin's FM Championship victory adds a highlight to the women's game as it heads into its own fall stretch.

What's Next

For golf fans, the coming weeks deliver both competitive golf and the spectacle of team competition — a format that brings out a different kind of intensity from players who spend most of the year competing as individuals. The Presidents Cup at Medinah is the marquee event. The fall is far from over.
