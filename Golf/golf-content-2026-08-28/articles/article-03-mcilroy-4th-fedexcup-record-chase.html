McIlroy Chasing Unprecedented 4th FedExCup Title — Opens at -2 After Rocky Start

By Marcus Cole | August 28, 2026

Rory McIlroy came to East Lake Golf Club for the 2026 Tour Championship carrying the weight of a record that no player before him has ever achieved: four FedExCup titles. The three-time champion — having claimed the points-series crown in 2016, 2019, and 2022 — needs only one more to stand alone in the history of the format. But after Round 1, he has ground to make up.

McIlroy opened at 2-under par, including a stretch where he was 2-over before rallying. At T20, he sits six shots behind leader Min Woo Lee and three shots behind No. 1 seed Scottie Scheffler. Three rounds remain, and McIlroy has charged at East Lake before — but the task ahead requires an immediate response.

The Record in Context

Since the FedExCup was introduced in 2007, no player has won it four times. Tiger Woods, one of the format's early stars, won it twice. Phil Mickelson never won it. The three titles McIlroy already holds put him ahead of every player who has touched the tournament, and a fourth would separate him by a margin that would stand for years.

McIlroy is 37 years old, the reigning Masters champion, and a player whose game has shown no meaningful signs of decline. This is not a farewell tour. This is a player in his prime, with a clear target in mind, who needs the game he knows he has to show up for four rounds at East Lake.

The Round 1 Reality

The Tour Championship is played in a 30-player field with no cut — every competitor finishes all four rounds. That structure means every shot counts from the first tee on Thursday. McIlroy's early wobble, reaching 2-over before recovering, cost him valuable ground in a format where leaders tend to protect and extend their positions.

His eventual finish of 2-under showed the quality is there. The 8-under round that Min Woo Lee posted is the kind of benchmark McIlroy has the game to approach — but he needs to approach it in Round 2, not Round 4, if the record is going to stay in reach.

What's Next

Round 2 is underway at East Lake on August 28. McIlroy needs to make a significant move up the leaderboard to stay in realistic contention. The defending Tour Championship champion Tommy Fleetwood sits at -4, also well-placed.

The record is still there to be claimed. Three rounds of East Lake remain. McIlroy has done harder things in his career. But Friday is the day he has to respond — and respond loudly.
