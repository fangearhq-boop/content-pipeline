LPGA FM Championship Round 1: Choi and Yoshida Co-Lead at -3 at TPC Boston

By Ryan Calloway | August 28, 2026

R. Choi of Australia and Y. Yoshida of Japan share the Round 1 lead at the 2026 LPGA FM Championship, each posting 3-under par at TPC Boston in Norton, Massachusetts. A large and international group of five players sit one shot back at 2-under, setting up an open and competitive 54-hole chase toward Sunday's final round.

A Wide-Open Leaderboard

After 18 holes at TPC Boston, the leaderboard reads like a global representation of women's professional golf. Joining Choi and Yoshida one shot back at -2 are G. Dryburgh of Scotland, Hannah Green of Australia, S. Lee and Haeran Ryu of South Korea, and C. Iwai of Japan. The international depth of the top of the board reflects the LPGA Tour's standing as the premier global circuit for women's professional golf.

With defending champion Miranda Wang in the field, along with Atthaya Thitikul, Nelly Korda, and Georgia Hull — all capable of mounting major charges — the FM Championship has the ingredients for a compelling weekend.

Defending Champion Miranda Wang

Wang won the 2025 FM Championship at 20-under par, holding off Thitikul by a single shot to claim the title. The defence of that victory begins in Round 2 on Friday, with Wang needing to find her scoring to get into contention from what is likely at least a few shots off the pace after the opening round.

TPC Boston is a demanding championship course, and the par-scoring average over any Tour Championship event reflects the difficulty of consistently finding birdies. The three-under rounds posted by Choi and Yoshida represent genuine quality — not just a product of generous conditions.

The Stakes

The FM Championship carries a $4.4 million total purse, one of the larger prizes on the LPGA Tour calendar. Combined with its late-season positioning and strong field, it has established itself as a marquee event in the women's game. Winning here is a career-defining achievement for any LPGA player.

Choi and Yoshida begin Round 2 with the shared advantage of being at the top. Whether they can sustain that position against a chasing pack that includes multiple major champions and high-ranked competitors will define the tournament's weekend narrative.

What's Next

Round 2 of the LPGA FM Championship is underway at TPC Boston on August 28. Rounds 3 and 4 follow on August 29 and 30. With the leaderboard tightly packed after 18 holes, the weekend promises significant movement in all directions.

Watch for the defending champion, the pre-tournament favourites, and the two leaders to define the story as the FM Championship moves into its decisive phase.
