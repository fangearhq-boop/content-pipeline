LIV Golf Bankruptcy — 24 Days to Oct. 13 Player Commitment Deadline
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post LIV Golf's restructuring deadline is 24 days away — October 13. Here's what has to happen: players holding at least 2/3 of total claim dollar amounts AND at least 1/2 by headcount must commit to LIV 2.0 under the BC Partners deal. The deal offers players 52.5% ownership of the restructured tour. PIF exits entirely. The math runs through the top creditors. Jon Rahm is owed $7.47 million — the largest individual player claim. Bryson DeChambeau and Dustin Johnson are also major creditors. Their collective decision carries enormous weight. Rahm has been publicly noncommittal. He reached an agreement with the DP World Tour in May, paid his outstanding fines, and said this week he sees "opportunity" there. That language matters. If the threshold isn't met by October 13, the BC Partners deal is likely off. LIV 2.0 doesn't happen. And the tour that spent $4+ billion Saudi dollars on golf restructuring ends in bankruptcy liquidation. 24 days. --- ### Image Post Caption 24 days until LIV Golf's October 13 player commitment deadline.
