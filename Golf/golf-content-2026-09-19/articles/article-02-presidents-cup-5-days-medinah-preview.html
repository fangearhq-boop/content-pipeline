Presidents Cup 5 Days Out — Snedeker's USA Team Arrives at Medinah on Tuesday

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | September 19, 2026

Presidents Cup / Medinah / USA Team

The 2026 Presidents Cup is five days away. Practice rounds at Medinah Country Club begin Tuesday, September 22. Competition starts Thursday, September 24, when Brandt Snedeker's USA team opens against Geoff Ogilvy's International side in five four-ball matches — the first pairings announced shortly before the morning tee times.

The event runs through Sunday, September 27 with 30 total matches over four days. The USA enters this Presidents Cup with a 13-1-1 all-time record in the competition, a dominance that has defined the event's history and made every International win feel like a significant upset.

The Format and Schedule

Thursday features five four-ball matches beginning at 11:35 AM CDT. Friday follows with five foursomes matches starting at 1:05 PM CDT. Saturday splits into morning four-ball (four matches) and afternoon foursomes (four matches, beginning at 1:15 PM CDT). Sunday closes the competition with 12 singles matches, starting at 11:02 AM CDT through the final match going off at 1:19 PM CDT.

Pairings for each session are announced following the completion of the prior session, except for Thursday's opening matches, which will be revealed before competition begins. This means Sunday's 12 singles matches — often where Presidents Cup titles are clinched or overturned — are drawn after Saturday's final foursomes conclude.

The USA Team

Snedeker's USA automatic qualifiers are Scottie Scheffler, Cameron Young, Wyndham Clark, Russell Henley, Sam Burns, and Collin Morikawa. His six captain's picks are Chris Gotterup, Xander Schauffele, Justin Thomas, Patrick Cantlay, Jacob Bridgeman, and Jackson Koivun. Koivun, 21, is the youngest USA team member and is completing the Biltmore Championship this weekend before heading to Medinah on Tuesday.

Ogilvy's International team includes Adam Scott, Hideki Matsuyama, and Ryan Fox among the named members. The International side has won the Presidents Cup just once, in 1998 at Royal Melbourne, and drew in 2003 at Fancourt. Ogilvy, an Australian and a former major champion, is one of the more strategically experienced International captains the team has had in recent editions.

Why Medinah Matters

Medinah Country Club in Medinah, Illinois is one of golf's most significant match play venues. It hosted the 1999 Ryder Cup — the "War at Medinah" — where the USA mounted a historic Sunday singles comeback. The course presents a demanding test with its tree-lined layout and the added drama of No. 17, the par-3 over Lake Kadijah that has defined pivotal moments in previous team events here.

For a USA team that enters as heavy favorites based on its roster depth and historical record, Medinah is familiar territory. But the Presidents Cup has produced surprising International performances over the years, and Ogilvy's team will be motivated to end a long streak of American dominance on American soil.

What's Next

Both teams hold official practice rounds at Medinah on Tuesday September 22 and Wednesday September 23 (gates open 9 AM CDT each day). Competition begins Thursday September 24 with Thursday's pairings announced that morning. The Presidents Cup runs through Sunday September 27. NBC, Golf Channel, and Peacock share broadcast rights.
