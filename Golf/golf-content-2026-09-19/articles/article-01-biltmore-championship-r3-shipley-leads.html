Shipley at -15, Kohles One Back: Round 3 Is Live at the Biltmore Championship

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | September 19, 2026

PGA Tour / Biltmore Championship / Asheville

Neal Shipley posted a career-low 62 on Friday to take the 36-hole lead at the 2026 Biltmore Championship. He is at -15 through 36 holes at The Cliffs at Walnut Cove in Asheville, North Carolina — one shot ahead of Ben Kohles, who sits at -14. Round 3 is live today, Saturday September 19.

Shipley's 62 was the round of the tournament. It moved him past the co-leaders from Thursday — Max Greyserman and Kurt Kitayama, who had shared the overnight lead at -7 — and into a commanding position heading into the weekend. The gap between Shipley and the fourth-place cluster is meaningful: Ricky Castillo and Greyserman sit at -11, four shots back, with a group of players behind them that would need a near-perfect round just to get into contention by Sunday.

The Leaderboard Situation

The top of the Biltmore leaderboard through 36 holes: Shipley (-15), Kohles (-14), Castillo and Greyserman (-11). Jackson Koivun, the youngest member of the USA Presidents Cup team, sits at -7 through two rounds — 8 shots off the pace. He made the cut comfortably but would need something exceptional on Saturday to put himself in legitimate championship contention.

The preferred-lies rule that has been in place all week — applied due to a combination of rain and summer heat creating unusual turf conditions — continues through the final two rounds. Preferred lies on all four rounds is an unusual condition for a PGA Tour event and has contributed to scoring being lower than typical on this golf course. That said, Shipley's 62 was not purely a product of conditions — the scoring differential at the top of the board reflects genuinely excellent ball-striking and putting.

What a Shipley Win Would Mean

A victory for Shipley at the Biltmore Championship would be the kind of breakthrough story that defines FedExCup Fall runs. He is competing in the inaugural edition of a tournament that carries historical weight as the first PGA Tour event in western North Carolina since 1942 — 84 years between rounds of competitive golf at this level in the region. A champion here is not just winning a $5 million purse with $900,000 going to the winner; they are entering the record books of a course and a community that has waited decades for professional golf to return.

Kohles, one back, is a credible threat. He has been consistent all week and has the game to post a low number on a course playing with preferred lies. The Round 3 grouping — Shipley, Kohles, and Castillo teeing off together at 9:45 AM ET — sets up a competitive three-way battle in the final pairing for the afternoon broadcast.

What's Next

Round 3 of the Biltmore Championship is live Saturday September 19. NBC has coverage from noon to 12:30 PM ET, with Golf Channel picking up from 12:30 to 3 PM ET. The final round is Sunday September 20. The champion will be crowned at The Cliffs at Walnut Cove — the first PGA Tour winner in Asheville in 84 years.
