### STORY 3: LIV Golf Oct. 13 Deadline

**Long-Form Post**
LIV Golf's restructuring deadline is 24 days away — October 13.

Here's what has to happen: players holding at least 2/3 of total claim dollar amounts AND at least 1/2 by headcount must commit to LIV 2.0 under the BC Partners deal. The deal offers players 52.5% ownership of the restructured tour. PIF exits entirely.

The math runs through the top creditors. Jon Rahm is owed $7.47 million — the largest individual player claim. Bryson DeChambeau and Dustin Johnson are also major creditors. Their collective decision carries enormous weight.

Rahm has been publicly noncommittal. He reached an agreement with the DP World Tour in May, paid his outstanding fines, and said this week he sees "opportunity" there. That language matters.

If the threshold isn't met by October 13, the BC Partners deal is likely off. LIV 2.0 doesn't happen. And the tour that spent $4+ billion Saudi dollars on golf restructuring ends in bankruptcy liquidation.

24 days.

### STORY 5: Rahm Sees "Opportunity" on DP World Tour

**Facebook Post**
Jon Rahm said on September 15 that he sees "opportunity" on the DP World Tour. He's been noncommittal about LIV 2.0.

This matters. Rahm is LIV Golf's single largest individual creditor at $7.47 million. Under the BC Partners deal, his commitment is worth more than almost any other player's — because the threshold isn't just headcount, it's 2/3 of total claim dollar amounts.

Rahm reached an agreement with the DP World Tour earlier this year — paid his outstanding fines, committed to a required quota of tour events, and made himself eligible for the 2027 Ryder Cup for Europe in Ireland.

He has 24 days to make his October 13 call. His language suggests he already knows what he's going to do.
