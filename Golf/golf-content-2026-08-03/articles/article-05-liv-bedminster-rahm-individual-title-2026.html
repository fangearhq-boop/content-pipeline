LIV Golf New York: Rahm Closing In on Individual Title as League's Future Hangs in the Balance

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | August 3, 2026

Jon Rahm leads the 2026 LIV Golf individual standings. This week at Trump National Golf Club in Bedminster, New Jersey, he can clinch the title — putting himself more than 400 points clear of the field going into the final two regular-season events if Bryson DeChambeau finishes in a three-way tie for second or worse.

LIV Golf New York begins August 6 and runs through August 9. The field includes 57 players and 13 major champions. DeChambeau, Cameron Smith, and Tyrrell Hatton are all in the field and would need Rahm to fall back for any of them to stay in realistic contention for the individual title.

Rahm's Season in LIV

Rahm joined LIV Golf ahead of the 2024 season in one of the league's marquee signings. His 2026 individual campaign has built consistently through the year — strong finishes at the events that have been played, a lead in the standings that reflects accumulation over a full season rather than one outlier result.

Clinching at Bedminster would give him the individual championship with two events remaining, providing the cushion of mathematical certainty before Indianapolis (August 20-23) and whatever version of the Team Championship ultimately gets played — or does not.

The Event's Context

LIV Golf New York at Trump National Bedminster is one of three remaining regular-season events in the 2026 LIV calendar. The other two — LIV Golf Indianapolis and the Team Championship — are operating under a cloud of financial uncertainty. Two LIV events have already been cancelled this year: New Orleans and Michigan.

The league filed WARN Act notices in both the U.S. and the U.K. this month, the legal mechanism for notifying employees ahead of potential mass layoffs. Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, which committed approximately $600 million to fund the 2026 season, has delivered roughly $200 million of that total. The $400 million gap has not been publicly closed. LIV is seeking $250 to $350 million in outside investment and has not announced a new primary backer.

The Team Championship, designed to be the climax of the LIV competitive calendar, is described as "in serious jeopardy of not being played."

Bedminster as a Setting

Trump National Golf Club in Bedminster has hosted LIV Golf events before, and the course and venue are familiar to the players. The combination of Rahm's title race, a competitive field with significant prize money, and the backdrop of the league's existential questions gives this week a weight that extends beyond the leaderboard.

Players competing at Bedminster are doing so in a legitimate professional context — competing for real points and real money against the best players LIV has assembled. They are also doing so in a league whose near-term future is not publicly guaranteed. Both of those things are true simultaneously, and both of them are relevant to what LIV Golf New York represents as an event.

What's Next

If Rahm clinches the individual title at Bedminster, he does so as the clear dominant individual performer of the 2026 LIV season. Indianapolis follows on August 20-23. The Team Championship's status will likely become clearer — or not — in the days around those events. For now, Bedminster is where the golf happens, and Rahm's title run is the competitive story at its center.
