### STORY 3: LIV Golf Crisis — WARN Act Filed

#### Long-Form Post — 1:00 PM ET

LIV Golf filed WARN Act notices in both the United States and the United Kingdom this month.

The WARN Act requires organizations to give 60 or more days of advance notice before mass layoffs. Filing it is a legal signal that the organization is preparing for that possibility, even if it hasn't happened yet. LIV's spokesperson said there are "no changes to current workforce, operations, or schedule." But the filings speak their own language.

The financial picture: Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund committed approximately $600 million to fund the 2026 LIV season. It has delivered roughly $200 million. The $400 million gap is not bridged. LIV is operating on loans, not capital, as it seeks $250-350 million in outside investment to survive beyond this season.

The Team Championship — LIV's marquee season-ending event — is described by insiders as "in serious jeopardy of not being played."

LIV Golf New York begins this Thursday at Trump National in Bedminster. For now, the show goes on. But the WARN Act filings say what a lot of people inside and outside the league already suspected: this may not last much longer.
