Ruoning Yin Wins the Buick LPGA Shanghai for Her Second 2026 LPGA Title

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | October 24, 2026

Ruoning Yin is a two-time LPGA winner in 2026.

The Chinese star closed with a final-round 68 at the Buick LPGA Shanghai to finish at 11-under 277, winning by one shot over Hye Jin Choi in a tight conclusion to the $3.2 million event. Amy Yang finished third at 8-under 280, unable to close the gap over the final holes.

The win is Yin's second LPGA title of the 2026 season, confirming her place among the Tour's elite players heading into the final stretch of the year.

How the Week Unfolded

The Buick LPGA Shanghai featured no 36-hole cut, with 76 of 81 players completing all four rounds. That format rewards consistent performance across the full week — and Yin delivered it. Her 68 on Sunday wasn't flashy, but it was enough to hold off Choi, who was making her own push coming down the final stretch.

Yin earned $480,000 from the winner's share, a significant result that adds to what has been a strong 2026 for the 22-year-old.

Race to CME Globe Impact

Beyond the trophy and the check, Yin picks up 500 points in the Race to the CME Globe — the LPGA's season-long points competition that culminates at the CME Group Tour Championship in November. With multiple events remaining, those 500 points are a meaningful addition in what is becoming a competitive race to the season finale.

The LPGA Tour's annual trip to China at the Buick LPGA Shanghai has historically produced some compelling finishes, and the 2026 edition was no different.

What's Next

The LPGA Tour heads to South Korea for the BMW Ladies Championship, the next stop on the schedule. Yin will carry momentum from Shanghai into what remains of a strong 2026 season — and her Race to CME Globe position means she will be in contention when the year-end prize is eventually decided.
