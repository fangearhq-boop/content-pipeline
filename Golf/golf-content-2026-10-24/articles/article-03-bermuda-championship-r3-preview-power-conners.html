Bermuda Championship Round 3 Preview: Five Veterans Chase Two Rookies at Port Royal

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | October 24, 2026

Kensei Hirata and Chandler Blanchet hold the lead. What they do not hold is experience — at this event, in this pressure, at a course known for making leaders work for every stroke on Sunday.

That dynamic sets up Saturday's third round at Port Royal Golf Course as one of the more intriguing setups of the 2026 fall PGA Tour swing. Two first-time Bermuda participants share the top spot at 13-under 129. Five veterans are one shot back and hungry.

The Chasers

The group at 12-under is as varied as it is dangerous.

Seamus Power brings the kind of comfort with seaside, windswept conditions that most American players simply don't have. Born and raised in Ireland, Power has built his Tour career on ball-striking and composure in difficult weather. Bermuda in late October suits his profile well.

Corey Conners is the most reliable iron player in the group. The Canadian is not a flashy scorer, but he rarely makes the kind of mistake that costs a player a tournament. When a course like Port Royal demands precision — and it does — Conners is exactly the type of player who climbs leaderboards.

Eric Cole, Ben Silverman, and Danny Walker complete the quintet. All three have enough Tour experience to hold steady under pressure. None of them is someone the leaders can simply write off heading into Saturday.

The 18th Hole Question

Port Royal's finishing hole has been part of the conversation at every Bermuda Championship. The uphill par-4 18th is the kind of closing hole that rewards aggression from players with nothing to lose and punishes conservatism from leaders trying to protect.

Saturday and Sunday, that hole will matter. Players who can attack it — or at least neutralize it — will have a real advantage in the final 36 holes.

What's Next

Round 3 tees off Saturday, with the final round set for Sunday. The full-field cut was made at 5-under 137, with the weekend field narrowed to players who can genuinely contend. With two rookies leading and five proven players one back, Saturday at Port Royal has all the ingredients for leaderboard chaos. The 2026 Butterfield Bermuda Championship concludes October 25.
