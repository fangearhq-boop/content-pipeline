Stifel Charity Classic Opens the Charles Schwab Cup Playoffs at Norwood Hills

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | October 24, 2026

The PGA Tour Champions postseason is here.

The 2026 Stifel Charity Classic at Norwood Hills Country Club in St. Louis marks the opening event of the three-event Charles Schwab Cup playoff series. Seventy-two players who advanced from the regular season are competing for supremacy — and for the overall Schwab Cup title that goes to the best performer across all three playoff events.

Cink Enters With the Lead

Stewart Cink arrives in St. Louis as the overall Charles Schwab Cup points leader heading into the playoff. Cink, a former British Open champion who has built a consistent senior circuit career, is in the position every player in the field covets — leading the standings with three events left to play.

But the Schwab Cup format is designed to create drama. Points are compressed in the playoffs, meaning every player in the 72-man field can still realistically affect the overall standings. A hot week at Norwood Hills can move a player from middle of the pack to the cusp of the title.

Defending Champion Bjorn in the Field

Thomas Bjorn won the 2025 Stifel Charity Classic with a score of 12-under, setting the target for this year's field. The Danish veteran knows Norwood Hills and what it takes to win there, giving him a mental edge as he defends.

Bjorn's 2025 win was part of a broader Schwab Cup story — the event's winner doesn't always go on to win the overall title, but performing well here sets up momentum for the final two playoff events that follow.

The Norwood Hills Setting

Norwood Hills Country Club is a traditional parkland course in the St. Louis suburbs, built around classic Midwest tree-lined fairways and firm, fast greens. The course rewards accuracy over length — making it a venue that tends to favor the most consistent ball-strikers in the Champions Tour field.

What's Next

The Stifel Charity Classic runs October 23-25. Two more playoff events follow — the field's results here will shape the Schwab Cup standings heading into the final stretch. For Cink and the other leaders, this is their chance to start building a cushion. For everyone else, it's the moment to start closing the gap.
