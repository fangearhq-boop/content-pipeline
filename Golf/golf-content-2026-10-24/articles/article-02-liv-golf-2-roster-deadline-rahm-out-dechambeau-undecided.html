LIV Golf 2.0 Deadline Is Tomorrow: Rahm Out, DeChambeau Still Undecided

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | October 24, 2026

Tomorrow is the day LIV Golf 2.0 finds out who it actually is.

October 25 is the deadline for players to commit to the restructured breakaway league — a date that has already been extended once from its original October 13 target. BC Partners Credit is providing up to $300 million in financing. But as the clock winds down, the roster picture remains incomplete, and the league's most compelling name is still on the fence.

Who's Out

Several prominent players who were previously involved with or expected to consider LIV Golf have confirmed they will not be participating in LIV 2.0.

Jon Rahm, the former world No. 1 who made a high-profile move to LIV in 2024, is confirmed out. So are Louis Oosthuizen, Adrian Meronk, and David Puig — four names that would have lent significant credibility to the 2.0 product.

For players on the DP World Tour, the calculation is particularly stark: those who join LIV 2.0 will be barred from the DP World Tour, a policy that adds real cost to any decision to commit.

Who's In

The confirmed committed list is currently led by Cameron Tringale, Richard Bland, and Ben Campbell — capable players, but not the marquee names the league would prefer to lead its promotional push.

The hope for LIV 2.0 organizers is that tomorrow's deadline brings a late wave of commitments, and that BC Partners' financial backing proves persuasive to players who have been sitting on decisions.

DeChambeau: The Decision That Defines It

No single player's choice matters more than Bryson DeChambeau's. The power-hitting former U.S. Open champion has not committed and remains publicly undecided as of Friday. DeChambeau's presence — or absence — will shape how LIV 2.0 is perceived by the broader golf world.

BC Partners has the contractual ability to extend the deadline again if the roster does not reach a sufficient threshold. But every extension delays the league's ability to announce its product and begin building around it.

What's Next

The deadline arrives October 25. Whether BC Partners extends again or accepts the current roster will be clear within 24 hours. The outcome will define what LIV Golf 2.0 actually looks like — and whether it represents a meaningful competitive alternative or a smaller, quieter chapter than its predecessor.
