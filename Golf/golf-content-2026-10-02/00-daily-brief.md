## Today's Top Stories

### STORY 1: LIV Golf Bankruptcy — Players Owed Millions, Contracts Terminated
**Tier:** 1
**Category:** LIV Golf / Bankruptcy / Saudi Arabia / Contract Disputes
**Summary:** LIV Golf filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy on September 8, with liabilities of $500M–$1B against assets of $100M–$500M. All existing player contracts are terminated under the restructuring. Jon Rahm tops the owed list at $7.47M from this year alone, with more than $100M in future guarantees reportedly still outstanding. Saudi Arabia's PIF, which funded the tour since 2022, has agreed to provide a $49.6M DIP loan and will fully exit as backer. London PE firm BC Partners is financing a potential LIV 2.0 return in 2027.
**Angle:** A $5 billion breakaway tour in ruins — what happens to the players?

### STORY 2: Sergio Garcia Files to Exit LIV Golf Contract — Career at a Crossroads
**Tier:** 1
**Category:** Sergio Garcia / LIV Golf / Bankruptcy / Career
**Summary:** Sergio Garcia has filed legal papers seeking explicit termination — not just rejection — of his LIV Golf contract in the Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings. Under U.S. bankruptcy law, contract rejection is treated as a breach, not a clean termination. Garcia's lawyers argue the distinction matters for his career flexibility. The 2017 Masters winner plans to play the DP World Tour in 2027 and wants to be an "instant free agent." Garcia is optimistic LIV Golf will return in some form.
**Angle:** A Masters champion's career in limbo — and why the legal distinction matters.
