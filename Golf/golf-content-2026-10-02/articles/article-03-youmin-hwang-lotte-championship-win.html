Youmin Hwang Birdie-Bombs Her Way to Back-to-Back LOTTE Championship Titles in Hawaii

By Marcus Cole | October 2, 2026

The defending champion closed with five birdies in her final six holes — including four consecutive to end the round — to win at 17-under 271 at Hoakalei Country Club.

In a sport that rewards patience and precision, Youmin Hwang chose power and persistence on the back nine at Hoakalei Country Club on Sunday. The South Korean star made five birdies in her final six holes, including four consecutive birdies to close her round, shooting 5-under 67 to win the 2026 LOTTE Championship presented by Hoakalei at 17-under 271.

It was the third consecutive birdie-fueled finish that defined Hwang's week in Ewa Beach, Hawaii — and a repeat LOTTE title that cemented her status as the dominant force at this tournament. Youmin Hwang is the defending LOTTE Championship winner, and she has defended that title successfully.

The Closing Stretch

Through 12 holes of her final round, Hwang's position on the leaderboard was comfortable but not definitive. Then she birdied holes 13, 15, 16, 17, and 18 — four straight at the finish, turning a solid Sunday into something memorable. The combination of low scoring and clutch execution on the tournament's defining stretch is difficult to manufacture. Hwang made it look systematic.

After the opening round, A Lim Kim had led at 7-under 65. Nelly Korda, the marquee name in a strong field, entered the week as a favorite and finished tied for fourth — a strong result by conventional metrics but one that fell short of Hwang's relentless closing push.

The Course and the Stage

Hoakalei Country Club in Ewa Beach is one of the most picturesque stops on the LPGA Tour calendar — a par-72 layout at 6,530 yards built against the backdrop of Hawaii's coastline and volcanic terrain. The LOTTE Championship's purse of $3 million reflects its standing as a significant event in the LPGA season.

For Hwang, defending a title in Hawaii against the field assembled for the LOTTE Championship is a meaningful statement. Korda, among the game's most recognizable players, was unable to match the Korean's finishing tempo.

LPGA Tour Context

The LPGA Tour's October schedule continues with the Buick LPGA Shanghai on October 15-18, followed by the BMW Ladies Championship in South Korea on October 22-25, and the Maybank Championship from October 29-November 1 in Malaysia. The season's closing stretch shifts to Asia, where the international LPGA community's depth becomes particularly apparent.

What's Next

Hwang's back-to-back LOTTE wins will attract significant attention in the Korean golf community and beyond. The LPGA's Asian swing begins in two weeks. Whether Hwang can carry her Hawaii form to the continent where the deepest competition in women's golf resides is the next question — but she arrives at those events as a defending champion who knows how to close.
