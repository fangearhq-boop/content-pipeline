Bank of Utah Championship: Fisk and Jaeger Lead at 8-Under at Stunning Black Desert Resort

By Ryan Calloway | October 2, 2026

The PGA Tour's newest fall venue is delivering drama early — Black Desert Resort's red rock backdrop frames a FedEx Cup points race that matters for players fighting for 2027 status.

The PGA Tour's FedEx Cup Fall continued this week with the Bank of Utah Championship at Black Desert Resort in Ivins, Utah — one of two new events added to the 2026 fall schedule. Through the opening rounds, Steven Fisk and Stephan Jaeger have each carded 8-under 63 rounds to share the lead, with Sudarshan Yellamaraju and David Lipsky close behind.

Defending champion Michael Brennan is among the 120 players in the field, aiming to become the second consecutive player to successfully defend at this event — though the venue itself is new, the FedEx Cup points are as real as ever.

The New Venue

Black Desert Resort opened its doors to PGA Tour competition this year after being announced as a replacement for the Sanderson Farms Championship in Mississippi, which left the schedule after 2025. The course — carved into Utah's dramatic red rock canyon landscape near Zion National Park — has generated significant attention from players and media for its visual presentation.

The inaugural Biltmore Championship in Asheville kicked off the fall schedule in September with Jacob Bridgeman winning at 26-under 258. The Bank of Utah Championship is the second event of the fall run, followed by the Baycurrent Classic in Japan (Oct. 8-11), the Butterfield Bermuda Championship (Oct. 22-25), and the VidantaWorld Mexico Open (Oct. 29-Nov. 1).

Why This Week Matters

The FedEx Cup Fall carries 500 FedExCup points for winners — meaningful currency for players trying to secure their PGA Tour cards, improve their category status, or position themselves for the following season's elevated events. For players outside the top 125 in FedEx Cup points, each week's result can be the difference between full status and conditional exemptions in 2027.

Fisk and Jaeger, two of the tour's more consistent mid-tier competitors, have given themselves a strong position entering the weekend. Whether they can hold off a chasing pack over the final two rounds will determine how the Bank of Utah Championship's inaugural year is remembered.

What's Next

The Bank of Utah Championship concludes Sunday, October 4. A Fisk or Jaeger victory would represent a career-defining moment for either player in a newly significant event. For Black Desert Resort, a compelling finish would cement its standing as one of the Tour's most distinctive new venues. The FedEx Cup Fall continues with four more events through November — the points race is very much alive.
