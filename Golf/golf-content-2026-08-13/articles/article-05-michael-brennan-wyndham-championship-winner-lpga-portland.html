Michael Brennan Wins the Wyndham Championship at -22 — The Regular Season Ends With a Birdie on 18

By Jake Torres | August 13, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

The 2026 PGA Tour regular season ended the way it began: with players competing intensely for every possible position before the calendar closed on the postseason field. Michael Brennan made sure his chapter of that story ended well. He won the Wyndham Championship at Sedgefield Country Club in Greensboro, North Carolina, finishing at 22-under par after a final-round 64 that included a clutch birdie on the 72nd hole. He won by three shots.

Brennan earned $1,530,000 from the $8,500,000 purse — and more importantly, secured his place in the FedEx Cup Playoffs that began Thursday at TPC Southwind.

How the Wyndham Unfolded

The final-hole birdie was the signature moment of Brennan's week. Coming to the 18th with a lead to protect and a number to make, he converted — the kind of clutch execution that defines who walks off the 72nd green as a champion and who has to think about what went wrong on the flight home. Runner-up Beau Hossler finished three shots back, unable to close the gap despite a competitive final round.

The most remarkable scorecard of the week belonged to Ben James, who shot a 61 in the final round — one of the rounds of the year, a low number that put the tournament briefly in tension before Brennan's response made the margin clear. James finished third overall. Kevin Yu was fourth.

What the Wyndham Means

The Wyndham Championship has a specific place in the PGA Tour calendar. It is the final event of the regular season — the last opportunity for players hovering around the top 70 in FedExCup points to earn their way into the playoffs, or for players already locked in to improve their seeding before the postseason begins. The field playing in Greensboro was competing with one eye on the scoreboard and one eye on the points table.

With the Wyndham complete, the 2026 playoff field is set. The top 70 qualified. The seedings are locked in ahead of the FedEx St. Jude Championship, which opened Round 1 Thursday at TPC Southwind — the same day the Wyndham's trophy was still being polished.

LPGA Portland Classic: Round 1 Today

The women's game is also in postseason mode. The LPGA Tour's Standard Portland Classic began Round 1 on Thursday at Columbia Edgewater Country Club in Portland, Oregon — running concurrent with the opening of the FedEx St. Jude Championship. The Portland Classic is one of the LPGA's Pacific Northwest anchors, with meaningful world ranking points available and a field that reflects the full range of international women's tour golf.

The event runs through Sunday, August 16, and is followed the next week by the CPKC Women's Open — August 20-23 at Royal Mayfair Golf Club in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. August is the most concentrated period of competitive golf on both the PGA Tour and LPGA calendars. The season is entering its decisive stretch. It starts in Memphis. It starts in Portland. It starts now.
