Tom Kim did something remarkable today in Japan.

The 24-year-old South Korean fired a final-round 64 — five birdies and an eagle — to win gold at the 2026 Asian Games with an 18-under total, winning by three strokes. Kazuki Higa of Japan, Ding Wenyi of China, and Sadom Kaewkanjana of Thailand shared the runner-up spot.

### STORY 3: LIV Golf Player Deadline

**FB Long-Form Post**
Window: 1:00 PM ET

The clock is ticking for LIV Golf players.

October 18 is the deadline for players to confirm whether they're in for LIV 2.0 — a proposed relaunched version of the circuit backed by London-based BC Partners. The pitch: 10 events in 2027, split between international and US stops.

LIV Golf filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy on September 8. Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, which poured roughly $6 billion into the circuit since its 2022 launch, declared LIV Golf "no longer consistent" with its investment strategy and walked away.

The two biggest questions center on Jon Rahm and Bryson DeChambeau — LIV's two biggest stars. Rahm leads the 2026 LIV standings at 1,057 points and reportedly has $100 million in future guarantees outstanding. Both are linked to potential departures.

If Rahm and DeChambeau leave, the LIV 2.0 pitch gets significantly harder to sell.

15 days to choose. What do you think — does LIV Golf have a future?

### STORY 4: Sergio Garcia

**FB Long-Form Post**
Window: 3:30 PM ET

Sergio Garcia wants out of LIV Golf. The question is whether getting out will give him back what he lost by joining.

The 46-year-old Spaniard filed a motion in NJ bankruptcy court asking for the explicit termination of his LIV contract — not a rejection, but a termination. The legal distinction matters: a termination clearly establishes his free-agent status, which is critical for planning tournament schedules and sponsorships in 2027.

His stated destination is the DP World Tour. But SI.com reports that his Ryder Cup career — an enormous part of his legacy — "appears to be over."

Garcia has played in 10 Ryder Cups for Europe and holds the record for most points ever accumulated in the event. Since joining LIV in 2022, he has been ineligible for European Ryder Cup consideration. At 46, the path back to Ryder Cup standing before the 2027 event at Adare Manor in Ireland is extremely tight.

A 9-figure LIV deal that was supposed to secure his future has instead put his legacy at risk.
