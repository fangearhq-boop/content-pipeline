Bank of Utah Championship: Smotherman's Career-Low 62 Builds One-Shot Lead on Moving Day

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | October 3, 2026

Austin Smotherman has spent two rounds at Black Desert Resort looking like the best player in the field — and Saturday, he gets another chance to prove it.

Smotherman enters Moving Day at the Bank of Utah Championship at 17-under par, holding a one-shot lead over Kevin Roy after posting rounds of 63 and 62 at Black Desert Resort Golf Course in Ivins, Utah. The 62 in Round 2 was the lowest round of Smotherman's professional career.

His pursuit company will be familiar. Roy, who also sits comfortably ahead of the rest of the field, shared a flight with Smotherman to get to Utah — and will share the final pairing in Saturday's third round.

How Smotherman Got Here

Smotherman opened with a 63 in Round 1, moving quickly to the top of the leaderboard. But it was the second round that made everyone take notice.

A 62 — at any PGA Tour event, on any course — demands attention. At a FedEx Cup Fall event where the field includes veterans looking to build points and emerging players trying to prove they belong, a 62 sends a message.

Smotherman made it look efficient rather than electric. He took advantage of the birdie holes, avoided the bogeys that have trapped others, and turned what could have been a tense round into something approaching a statement.

He now leads at 17-under, with Roy — another player in strong form this week — the only one within striking distance.

Roy One Back

Kevin Roy has had a strong week in his own right. At 16-under, Roy is in position to make a serious run at the title, and the fact that he and Smotherman are paired together for the third round sets up one of the more intriguing final-group dynamics of the fall season.

The two players' shared travel arrangements have been noted by television broadcasters as one of the feel-good details of the week. Whether that familiarity helps or hurts either player under pressure on Sunday remains to be seen.

The Tournament at a Glance

The Bank of Utah Championship is a FedEx Cup Fall event, offering $5 million in prize money and 500 FedExCup points to the winner. The tournament runs October 1 through 4 at Black Desert Resort, which sits in the dramatic red-rock landscape of southwestern Utah near St. George.

Black Desert is one of the more visually striking venues on the PGA Tour calendar, and the course has challenged players throughout the week despite the low scoring — a testament to how well the leaders have played rather than any lack of difficulty.

What's Next

The final round is Sunday, October 4. Smotherman's one-shot cushion is meaningful but not comfortable — Roy has shown he can score, and anyone within five shots heading into Sunday should be considered a legitimate threat in scoring conditions that have been favorable all week.

For Smotherman, Sunday represents a chance to claim his first PGA Tour title. He has played the first 36 holes as well as anyone in the field could hope to, and the stage is set for a compelling conclusion at Black Desert Resort.
