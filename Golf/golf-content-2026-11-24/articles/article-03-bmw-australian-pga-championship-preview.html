BMW Australian PGA Championship Opens DP World Tour Season at The Lakes Sydney With Record Purse

By Jake Torres | November 24, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

The DP World Tour's new season begins Thursday in Sydney, and it arrives with a record prize fund and a historic venue. The BMW Australian PGA Championship runs from November 26 through November 29 at The Lakes Golf Club — the club's first time hosting the event since 1987 — carrying AU$2.5 million in total prize money, the largest purse in the tournament's 121-year history.

The event serves as the season opener for the DP World Tour, the annual tradition of beginning in Australia before the tour moves to its main European and international schedule. For Australian golf fans, the return to Sydney and to The Lakes provides a home-country showcase that the game's domestic audience has been waiting for.

The Venue and Its History

The Lakes Golf Club occupies a distinctive stretch of land in Sydney's eastern suburbs, a course that has long been considered one of Australia's finest and one that presents a comprehensive test of all parts of a player's game. Its return to the Australian PGA Championship after nearly four decades gives the event a different feel — a classic venue rather than a modern purpose-built track, one that rewards course management and precision over raw distance alone.

The challenge for players from outside Australia is familiarizing themselves with a course that domestic players know well. Min Woo Lee, one of the pre-tournament favorites, has the kind of adaptability and shot-making quality that translates across different conditions and surfaces.

Min Woo Lee as a Focal Point

Lee's status as a pre-tournament favourite reflects the form he has shown across multiple tours over recent seasons. The Western Australian has the game to win on any DP World Tour course, and home-country knowledge — even with The Lakes being in a different city from his home state — gives him a comfort factor that some international arrivals will not have.

Beyond Lee, the field for the Australian PGA Championship typically draws well from the European and Asian Tours, alongside members of the PGA Tour of Australasia. The record purse this year is likely to attract a stronger than usual international contingent.

What's Next

First-round play begins Thursday, November 26. The Australian PGA Championship serves as the opening act for a DP World Tour season that will look considerably different from previous years once former LIV Golf players begin integrating into the tour's ranks. For this week, though, the story belongs to The Lakes, to Sydney, to 121 years of a great Australian golf championship, and to whoever can claim the first title of the new season.
