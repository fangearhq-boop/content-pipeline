Jon Rahm Has Won Three Consecutive LIV Individual Championships. What Comes Next May Reshape the Sport.

By Jake Torres | August 12, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

Jon Rahm is the undisputed individual champion of LIV Golf — for the third consecutive year. He clinched that title at the LIV New York event last week, finishing the season's points race at 944.54 points, a 218-point margin over Bryson DeChambeau's 726.20. The remarkable detail: Rahm clinched it despite finishing just T41 at LIV New York. His lead was so large that it survived a poor individual result.

Three straight LIV Individual Championships. In a circuit that started in 2022, that's the full arc of the competition — Rahm has been at the top of it from the moment he joined in 2024.

A Record of Consistency That Has No Parallel in Professional Golf

The numbers from Rahm's time on LIV Golf are genuinely extraordinary. Since joining the circuit in 2024, Rahm has made 25 total starts. In those 25 starts, he has finished outside the top 10 exactly once. One result outside the top 10 in 25 starts — a consistency rate that no other active professional golfer, on any tour, can match across a comparable sample.

His 2026 LIV season through nine events: two wins (Hong Kong and Mexico City), four runner-up finishes, and eight top-10 results. He entered LIV New York with the points lead locked. T41 was not the outcome he wanted, but it did not matter.

The margin of 218 points over DeChambeau — a former major champion and a prolific winner in his own right — is the kind of buffer that doesn't accumulate through luck. It accumulates through relentless proximity to the top of each leaderboard, week after week, venue after venue.

Indianapolis Is Next

The LIV Golf Tour heads to Indianapolis for its next event — specifically Chatham Hills Golf Club in Westfield, Indiana, on August 20-23. It is the penultimate LIV event of the 2026 season before the circuit enters one of the most consequential offseasons in its short history.

For Rahm, Indianapolis represents another week of defending his standard. He will arrive as the acknowledged dominant force on the tour, with no individual title left to win this season — it's already his. The team component of LIV Golf continues, but the individual narrative for 2026 has already been written. Rahm wrote it across nine events and 25 combined career starts without losing a step.

The BC Partners Horizon

Beyond Indianapolis, the LIV Golf landscape is about to change in fundamental ways. The BC Partners deal — under which private equity firm BC Partners will assume operational control of LIV Golf as PIF, the Public Investment Fund of Saudi Arabia, exits its financial role — is expected to close in September 2026. PIF's exit from its active financial position in the tour marks the end of the circuit's founding structure.

What the post-PIF era means for LIV Golf's prize funds, team structure, and relationship with the PGA Tour and Official World Golf Rankings is unclear. There are conversations happening at every level of the sport about what a BC Partners-operated LIV Golf looks like — and whether that version is more or less likely to find a path toward some form of reconciliation or integration with the broader global tour ecosystem.

The questions are large ones. But heading into that uncertainty, LIV Golf's dominant individual player is going to Indianapolis as a three-time champion. Whatever the organizational future holds, Rahm's individual record will be part of the story that defines what LIV's first years actually produced.

What's Next
