LIV Golf New York Opens — Jon Rahm Is 219 Points Clear and Bidding for a Third Straight Individual Title

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | August 6, 2026

LIV Golf / Jon Rahm / Individual Championship / Bedminster

The LIV Golf circuit has returned to one of its most distinctive venues — and this week at Trump National Golf Club in Bedminster, New Jersey, the individual championship race has arrived at something approaching its conclusion.

Jon Rahm, captain of Legion XIII and the dominant force in LIV Golf competition, begins the week with 938.44 individual championship points — a lead of 219.04 points over second-placed Bryson DeChambeau (719.40 points). Lucas Herbert holds third at 531.23 points after winning two of the last four LIV events.

The math for Rahm's opponents is daunting. For DeChambeau to keep the individual title race alive, he needs to outscore Rahm by at least 19.04 points this week. Given Rahm's combination of consistency and course-suited game, that is a significant ask.

The Case for a Third Straight Title

Rahm won the LIV Individual Championship in 2024 — his first season on the circuit. He won it again in 2025. A third consecutive title would make him the undisputed standard-bearer of the LIV Golf era, the player whose name is synonymous with the league's individual competition in the same way Tiger Woods was synonymous with the FedExCup in its early years.

The Bedminster venue suits him. Trump National Golf Club has been stretched to 7,651 yards since 2023 — an increase of more than 125 yards from its original configuration — to accommodate the power game of LIV's field. Rahm is one of the tour's most complete players: elite iron play, elite wedge game, a short game that holds up under pressure. Length is not his only weapon, and on a course that demands precision as much as distance, that completeness is an advantage.

The Prize and the Stakes

The LIV Golf New York tournament offers $20 million in total prize money, split between individual ($4 million to the winner) and team ($10 million pool, $3 million to the winning team captain) competitions. The scale of the purse reflects LIV's continued commitment to offering premium competitive compensation, and a strong showing here — especially the individual title — carries financial implications beyond the single-event prize.

DeChambeau, who has made no secret of his desire to challenge Rahm for the individual crown, arrives with the motivation but likely not the mathematical runway. Lucas Herbert — whose recent form suggests he is peaking at the right moment — is the wildcard in the individual standings.

What's Next

The LIV Golf New York tournament runs through Sunday, August 9. If Rahm manages the week without a significant gap closing from DeChambeau, the individual championship question could effectively be resolved before LIV's season reaches its conclusion. Follow the leaderboard at Bedminster closely — the outcome here may determine whether this is a race that runs to the wire or one that ends early.
