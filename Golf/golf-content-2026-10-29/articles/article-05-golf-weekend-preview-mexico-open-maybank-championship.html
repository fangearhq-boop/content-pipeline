Golf Weekend Preview: Mexico Open and Maybank Championship Run Simultaneously This Week

By Marcus Cole

October 29, 2026

Golf Preview

Golf fans are getting an unusually rich weekend. Two major professional tournaments are running simultaneously — the VidantaWorld Mexico Open on the PGA Tour and the LPGA Maybank Championship — and both opened Thursday with storylines that will carry through to Sunday, November 1. Here is the full picture of what to watch.

VidantaWorld Mexico Open (PGA Tour)

The week's defining story in men's golf is Austin Smotherman's pursuit of a third consecutive PGA Tour victory. Smotherman won the Bermuda Championship in a playoff and followed it the very next week with another win, and now he has arrived at Vidanta Vallarta in Puerto Vallarta with an opportunity to do something historically rare in the modern game.

The field includes Wyndham Clark as the betting market's favorite, plus Tony Finau, Max Homa, and Tom Kim — all capable of winning any week they are playing their best golf. The coastal Vallarta course, with its history of low scoring and soft conditions, sets up a high-birdie environment that rewards players who can keep the ball in play and make putts when they matter.

Smotherman has relevant course history here — previous strong rounds at this venue add to the favorable matchup. But the larger story is whether any player in the field has enough game to beat a man who has won two weeks in a row. The Mexico Open runs through Sunday.

LPGA Maybank Championship

The first round of the Maybank Championship in Kuala Lumpur produced one of the more striking opening scorecards on the LPGA Tour this year. Pauline Roussin-Bouchard shot a 64 — 8-under — highlighted by three consecutive closing birdies, and leads by one stroke heading into Friday.

Charley Hull is in fourth place at 6-under, two strokes back and fully in the frame for a title run. Lottie Woad sits at 1-under with plenty of rounds remaining to make a move. Defending champion Miyu Yamashita is also in the field, bringing the knowledge of what it takes to win at the KLGCC West Course.

The Maybank Championship is one of the LPGA Tour's most significant international events, and this week's field and opening-round leaderboard suggest it will deliver the quality of competition the event deserves. The tournament concludes Sunday in Malaysia.

How to Follow Both

Both events overlap entirely this week, with tee times running throughout the day in their respective time zones. The Mexico Open's Puerto Vallarta location means afternoon and evening coverage in North American time zones, while the Maybank Championship in Kuala Lumpur produces results overnight or early morning for North American audiences. Golf Channel and the PGA Tour's streaming platform carry the Mexico Open coverage; the LPGA Tour's digital platforms and broadcast partners cover the Maybank Championship.

Leaderboard updates for both events are available through the PGA Tour's app and website and through the LPGA Tour's official channels. The key days to watch for results are Saturday and Sunday, when leaders separate from the field and final-round scenarios develop.

What's Next

Round 2 for both tournaments runs Friday. At the Mexico Open, the field chases Smotherman and the event's early leaderboard takes final shape before the cut. At the Maybank Championship, Hull will look to close the two-shot gap to Roussin-Bouchard while Woad and Yamashita try to get themselves into contention. The complete picture of both tournaments will be clearer by Friday evening. Sunday, November 1 will deliver the winners and complete a weekend of dual-event golf coverage unlike most of the professional calendar.
