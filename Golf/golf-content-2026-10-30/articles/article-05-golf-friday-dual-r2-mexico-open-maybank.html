Golf Friday: Two Tournaments Both in Round 2 — Your Complete Guide

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap

October 30, 2026

Friday is one of the best days of the golf week, and this Friday is better than most. Both the VidantaWorld Mexico Open on the PGA Tour and the LPGA Maybank Championship are in their second rounds simultaneously — one in Puerto Vallarta, one in Kuala Lumpur — giving golf fans two complete leaderboards and multiple compelling storylines to track across an all-day session.

VidantaWorld Mexico Open — Round 2 Underway

The Mexico Open's Round 2 at Vidanta Vallarta in Puerto Vallarta picks up with Karl Vilips holding a one-stroke lead at 9-under after a career-low 62 on Thursday. Kensei Hirata is at 8-under and one back. Max Homa sits at 5-under from his first-round 66.

The week's central story — Austin Smotherman's bid for three consecutive PGA Tour wins — is running alongside the Vilips leaderboard position. Smotherman has won his last two events on Tour and arrives at this one as professional golf's most watched player regardless of the leaderboard. Three consecutive wins in the modern era is historically rare, and every round he plays in Mexico City adds to that possibility.

Billy Horschel withdrew during Round 2 with dizziness and lightheadedness — a significant subtraction from the field, though the leaderboard battle at the top continues.

The cut follows Round 2's conclusion, determining who remains for the weekend.

LPGA Maybank Championship — Round 2 in Malaysia

In Kuala Lumpur, Pauline Roussin-Bouchard enters Round 2 at the KLGCC West Course with a one-stroke lead at 8-under, carrying Thursday's impressive 64 — highlighted by three closing birdies — into her second round. The LPGA Tour field at the Maybank Championship is deep enough that her lead, while meaningful, is not safe.

Charley Hull sits at 6-under and is the most credible threat to Roussin-Bouchard's position. Hull's experience in international LPGA competition and her comfort with pressure make two strokes a workable deficit. A hot second round changes the picture entirely.

Lottie Woad is inside the field. Defending champion Miyu Yamashita is working her way back into the conversation. The Maybank Championship's second round will begin sorting out who is genuinely in this title race.

What to Watch Friday

The Mexico Open and the Maybank Championship offer complementary viewing across different time zones — which means golf fans with flexible schedules or multiple screens can follow both tournaments simultaneously. The two events wrap up Sunday, November 1, with final rounds in both Puerto Vallarta and Kuala Lumpur.

From Mexico: track Vilips for the 36-hole leader story, and Smotherman's board position for the historic-streak narrative. From Malaysia: watch whether Roussin-Bouchard extends her lead or whether Hull and the rest of the field close the gap in Round 2.

The weekend sets up well from here. It starts today.
