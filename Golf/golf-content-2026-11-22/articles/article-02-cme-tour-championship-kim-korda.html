CME Group Tour Championship Final Round: A Lim Kim Leads, Korda's Hall of Fame Moment Awaits

By Jake Torres | November 22, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

The 2026 LPGA season closes Sunday at the CME Group Tour Championship in Naples, Florida, and it is delivering one of the most compelling final-round setups of the year. A Lim Kim holds an 18-under par lead entering the final round at Tiburón Golf Club, with Nelly Korda two strokes behind and an extraordinary prize within reach: a victory today would qualify Korda for the LPGA Hall of Fame.

The $11 million event, which offers $4 million to the winner, is simultaneously the richest event on the LPGA Tour and a potential landmark moment in Korda's career. World No. 1 for most of the 2026 season, Korda needs only to close a two-stroke gap over 18 holes to earn entry into the sport's most exclusive honor.

Kim's Week So Far

A Lim Kim has been the story of the week. In the second round, the South Korean star made a hole-in-one on the par-3 16th hole — a 7-iron from 168 yards — that became one of the week's defining images. She has built her lead methodically, playing controlled, consistent golf that has kept her ahead of a field packed with the Tour's best players entering the final round.

Atthaya Thitikul — known as Jeeno — is also positioned in the final group, adding further star power to what promises to be an absorbing final 18 holes. The defending champion has been in position all week to make a run at a second consecutive title.

Korda's Hall of Fame Chase

The Korda angle elevates Sunday's final round beyond a typical season-finale finish. Having led the world rankings throughout 2026 and accumulated the credentials of one of the LPGA Tour's dominant players of her era, Korda is two shots away from one of the most significant achievements in women's golf.

She has been in similar positions before — a competitor of Korda's caliber does not reach world No. 1 without the ability to perform under pressure. But with a Hall of Fame entry as the specific reward on this particular Sunday, the significance of every shot she plays will carry an extra weight that is difficult to replicate in ordinary circumstances.

What's Next

The final round will determine the LPGA Tour's season champion, the $4 million CME Group Tour Championship winner, and potentially a new Hall of Fame member. Whatever the outcome, the 2026 LPGA season will be remembered for delivering its signature moment exactly where it was supposed to — on the final Sunday, with everything on the line.
