LPGA Tour 2026 Season Wraps Up With $132 Million Purse and a Memorable Season Finale

By Jake Torres | November 22, 2026 | Golf Fanrecap

The 2026 LPGA Tour season closes on Sunday, November 22, at the CME Group Tour Championship in Naples, Florida, completing a 10-month run that spans from January 29 to today and distributes $132 million in total prize money. For a tour that has steadily grown its financial footprint over the past decade, the 2026 season represents the richest campaign in LPGA history.

Nelly Korda Leads the Year

Nelly Korda's sustained position at world No. 1 throughout the 2026 season has been the defining narrative of the year. The American star has been the benchmark against which every other player on Tour has been measured, and she enters the final round of the CME Group Tour Championship with an opportunity to close the year on its most significant note possible — a win that would qualify her for the LPGA Hall of Fame.

Korda's season reflects the kind of consistent excellence that pushes players into historical conversations. Her world ranking has never been seriously threatened, and the tournament victories she has accumulated have reinforced her standing as the best player of her generation.

Compelling Contenders Throughout the Year

Atthaya Thitikul — known in the golfing world as Jeeno — has been Korda's most consistent challenger, pushing the No. 1 player on numerous occasions and demonstrating the kind of ball-striking that makes her a legitimate threat at any tournament on any given week. A Lim Kim has emerged as a compelling contender as well, particularly in this week's CME Group Tour Championship, where her hole-in-one in the second round and 18-under par lead entering the final round have announced her as a player capable of winning the sport's biggest prizes.

What's Next

The CME Group Tour Championship final round will determine the season's last champion and potentially add a Hall of Fame member to the sport's history. As the 2026 season closes, the LPGA heads into an offseason with strong momentum — a record purse, a compelling group of top-10 players, and a finale that is delivering exactly the drama the sport needs to send fans into the winter months. The 2027 season will begin with the expectation that women's professional golf continues to grow in both prize money and competitive quality.
