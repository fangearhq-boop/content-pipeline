# Golf Daily Brief — 2026-11-22
**Brand:** Golf Fanrecap | **Date:** Sunday, November 22, 2026

## Top Stories

### STORY 1: LIV Golf Officially Winding Down — End of a $5 Billion Era
LIV Golf is shutting down. Its lawyers told a New Jersey bankruptcy court on November 5 that the league would not proceed with its planned LIV 2.0 relaunch. The bankruptcy filing from September 8 led to a November 10 Chapter 11 plan targeting a January liquidation. Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund changed its investment strategy in April and stopped funding after the 2026 season. A rescue deal from BC Partners — worth upward of $300 million — also fell through. The league laid off 289 employees and furloughed 22 more before filing for bankruptcy.

### STORY 2: CME Group Tour Championship Final Round — A Lim Kim Leads, Korda Chasing LPGA Hall of Fame
The 2026 LPGA season closes Sunday at the CME Group Tour Championship in Naples, Florida. A Lim Kim entered the final round at 18-under par, with Nelly Korda two strokes back. A Korda win would qualify her for the LPGA Hall of Fame. The $11 million event offers $4 million to the winner and runs November 19-22 at the Tiburón Golf Club. Jeeno Thitikul (Atthaya Thitikul) is also positioned in the final group.
- **Tier:** 1 | **Status:** FOLLOW UP

### STORY 3: Jon Rahm Open to Koepka-Style Deal for PGA Tour Return
Jon Rahm says he would accept the same penalties Brooks Koepka agreed to in order to return to the PGA Tour after his LIV Golf stint. Koepka's terms included a $5 million fine, no equity shares for five years, and no direct access to signature events or FedExCup bonus money. Rahm says he has "no idea" when he would be allowed back; the Tour's CEO said there are no current reinstatement plans, and the Returning Member Program is closed. Rahm plans to play the DP World Tour in 2027 while awaiting a ruling.
- **Tier:** 2 | **Status:** NEW

### STORY 4: PGA Tour Not Rushing LIV Reinstatement Decision — January Timeline
PGA Tour CEO Brian Rolapp confirmed the Tour is in no hurry to decide on the future of LIV players. The Returning Member Program that allowed Brooks Koepka's return is closed. Any ruling on LIV players rejoining the American circuit has been pushed to January 2027. LIV Golf's creditors have also subpoenaed the PGA Tour for documents; the Tour called the request a "desperate fishing expedition." A bankruptcy hearing is scheduled for November 24.
- **Tier:** 2 | **Status:** NEW

### STORY 5: LPGA Season in Review — $132 Million Purse, Season Ends November 22
The 2026 LPGA Tour season wraps up Sunday with the CME Group Tour Championship. It has been a season spanning from January 29 to November 22, with a total purse of $132 million — the richest in LPGA history. Nelly Korda has led the world rankings throughout the year, while Jeeno Thitikul and A Lim Kim have been consistent contenders. The year closes with one of golf's most compelling final-round storylines.
