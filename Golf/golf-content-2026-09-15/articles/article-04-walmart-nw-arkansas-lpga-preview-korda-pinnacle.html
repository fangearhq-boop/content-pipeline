LPGA Heads to Arkansas: Korda Leads the Field as NW Arkansas Championship Returns for Year 20

By Ryan Calloway

September 15, 2026

Ten days from now, the LPGA Tour will be in Rogers, Arkansas, playing the 20th edition of the Walmart NW Arkansas Championship Presented by P&G at Pinnacle Country Club. It is one of the Tour's most consistent stops, one of its most popular, and this year it arrives with particular stakes: a $3 million purse, 500 Race to CME Globe points, and the first competitive gathering of the full field since the Solheim Cup concluded in the Netherlands.

The player everyone will be watching is Nelly Korda.

The Tournament

Pinnacle Country Club in Rogers, Arkansas has hosted this event for two decades. The 54-hole stroke play format with no cut means the entire field plays three full rounds, and consistent ball-striking tends to separate the contenders from the field. At a course the players know well, the premiums are placed on accuracy and putting.

The $3 million purse is a meaningful figure on the LPGA Tour, and the 500 Race to CME Globe points make this event consequential for players chasing the year-end championship. With the season winding toward its conclusion, every point matters. Expect the leaders of the Globe standings to treat this event with the urgency it deserves.

Korda Returns

Nelly Korda is the world No. 1 and has been the dominant force in women's golf across the past two seasons. In 2026, she already owns two major titles — the Chevron Championship and the U.S. Women's Open — a pair of wins that placed her name in conversations about the greatest seasons in LPGA history.

The Solheim Cup, however, did not end the way Korda and Team USA had hoped. Europe won 15-13 at Bernardus Golf in the Netherlands, and USA lost despite having the deeper roster on paper. Korda competed as a key anchor of the American team, but the result was a loss, and she will arrive in Arkansas carrying the motivation of a competitor who was on the wrong side of a close team defeat.

That kind of competitive hunger tends to translate into results. Players who come off Solheim losses with a point to prove are often at their most dangerous in the weeks immediately following the event.

The Post-Solheim Field

Every LPGA player who competed at the Solheim Cup — for both Team USA and Team Europe — will be eligible to compete in Rogers, Arkansas. That means the post-Solheim narrative threads will continue to play out over 54 holes at Pinnacle. The European players who celebrated in the Netherlands will arrive with confidence. The American players will arrive with something to prove.

Among the names to watch beyond Korda: European Solheim performers who broke out during the week at Bernardus Golf will bring momentum into a competitive field. The full field announcement and tee times will be released in the coming days.

What's Next

The Walmart NW Arkansas Championship Presented by P&G runs September 25-27 at Pinnacle Country Club in Rogers, Arkansas. It is a genuine celebration of what this event has become — 20 years at the same venue, consistent crowds, and a field that takes the event seriously because the LPGA Tour has given it a purse and a points allocation that demands to be taken seriously.

For Nelly Korda, it is the next opportunity to remind the golf world why she has spent the last two years at the top of the rankings. For everyone else in the field, it is a chance to close the gap.
