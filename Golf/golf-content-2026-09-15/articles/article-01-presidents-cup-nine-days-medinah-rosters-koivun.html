Presidents Cup Is 9 Days Away: Full Rosters Set, Koivun Headlines Team USA's Picks

By Ryan Calloway

September 15, 2026

Nine days. That is all that separates the golf world from the first tee shot at Medinah Country Club, and the storylines for the 16th Presidents Cup are already fully formed.

USA captain Brandt Snedeker has named his complete 12-man squad, and International captain Geoff Ogilvy has done the same. The rosters are locked. The venue is ready. And one name above all others on the American side has captured the imagination of a sport that feeds on youth and fearlessness.

The American Roster

Snedeker's automatic qualifiers came through the FedEx Cup standings: Scottie Scheffler, Cameron Young, Wyndham Clark, Russell Henley, Sam Burns, and Collin Morikawa. All six are proven performers who have competed at this level before.

The captain's picks are where the story gets interesting. Snedeker added Chris Gotterup, Xander Schauffele, Justin Thomas, Patrick Cantlay, and Jacob Bridgeman — all reliable Presidents Cup contributors — and then made the pick that will define how his captaincy is remembered: 21-year-old Jackson Koivun.

Koivun turned professional out of Auburn earlier this year and has already shown flashes of the game that convinced Snedeker he belonged on this team. At Medinah, he will be the youngest player on either side. He will also be one of the most closely watched.

Schauffele and Cantlay each carry 10-4-0 records in Presidents Cup play. Thomas is 10-3-2. These are the cornerstones of the American lineup, the players Snedeker will rely on to win points in the formats where experience is premium currency.

The International Roster

Geoff Ogilvy's automatic qualifiers are Si Woo Kim, Hideki Matsuyama, Ryan Fox, Tom Kim, Min Woo Lee, and Adam Scott. Scott, the 2013 Masters champion, adds veteran presence to a squad that skews younger than in previous years.

Ogilvy's captain's picks are Corey Conners, Nico Echavarria, Sungjae Im, Ryo Hisatsune, Nick Taylor, and Christiaan Bezuidenhout. Si Woo Kim is the only International player ranked in the top 20 of the Official World Golf Ranking, a gap that reflects the depth advantage the United States brings to every edition of this event.

The Weight of History

The Americans have won 13 of the 15 editions of the Presidents Cup contested since 1994. That is not a footnote — it is the defining fact of the competition. Team International has kept matches close at times, and the 2019 edition in Melbourne went to the final day, but the Americans have closed out virtually every edition with authority.

Medinah adds another layer of high-stakes atmosphere. The club hosted Ryder Cups in 1991, 1999, and 2012 — the last of those producing one of the most memorable comebacks in team golf history, when Europe rallied on Sunday to reclaim the trophy. The course is built for drama.

What's Next

The Presidents Cup runs September 24-27 at Medinah Country Club in Medinah, Illinois. The opening ceremony and practice rounds begin earlier in the week. Team pairings for the foursomes and fourball sessions will be announced as the event draws closer.

For Jackson Koivun, the next nine days are preparation. For Brandt Snedeker, they are the final window to finalize strategies. And for anyone who follows golf, they are the countdown to what promises to be the most compelling week of the fall.
