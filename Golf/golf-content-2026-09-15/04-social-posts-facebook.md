### STORY 2: Scottie Scheffler Season Review

**FB Long-Form** | Window: 8:00 AM ET
The numbers are almost impossible to believe.

Scottie Scheffler closed out the 2026 PGA Tour season by winning the TOUR Championship at East Lake on August 30 — his second FedEx Cup title, joining Tiger Woods and Rory McIlroy as the only multiple winners. He shot a final-round 4-under 66 to win by three strokes over Viktor Hovland.

But the win was just the capstone on a historic season:
- $30,937,525 in official prize money — a new PGA Tour single-season earnings record
- $23 million in FedEx Cup bonus money
- $130 million-plus in career earnings — passing Tiger Woods' all-time mark of $120.99 million
- A 2026 scoring average of 67.96 — the lowest on Tour since 1983

Scheffler won three times in 2026: the American Express in January, the FedEx St. Jude Championship, and the TOUR Championship. In between, he posted five runner-up finishes in a 210-day stretch without a win — a stretch that kept the pressure off the label "dominant" just long enough before he reminded everyone exactly what he is.

For anyone who wonders what the greatest era of PGA Tour golf looks like: this is it.

### STORY 3: LIV Golf Season Concluded

**FB Long-Form** | Window: 10:30 AM ET
The 2026 LIV Golf season has ended — and the story off the course may be bigger than anything that happened on it.

On the golf side: Legion XIII, led by Jon Rahm, claimed the team championship in Indianapolis. Michael La Sasso made history by becoming the youngest winner in LIV Golf history at just 22 years old. The 2026 season also marked a significant format change, with events expanding to 72 holes of stroke play from the original 54-hole format.

But the real drama in 2026 has been financial and structural. LIV filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection on September 8, with the Saudi Public Investment Fund (PIF) providing $49.6 million in DIP (debtor-in-possession) financing to keep the circuit operating. BC Partners is leading restructuring discussions toward "LIV 2.0" — a restructured league in which players would hold majority equity ownership.

Before the season began, Brooks Koepka and Patrick Reed departed LIV to rejoin the PGA Tour. Jon Rahm, owed more than $100 million from his original signing deal, remains the most prominent figure in the ongoing creditor proceedings.

Four 2027 events are already announced: Saudi Arabia, Australia, South Africa, and Hong Kong.

**FB Long-Form** | Window: 8:00 AM ET

The Walmart NW Arkansas Championship Presented by P&G returns to Pinnacle Country Club in Rogers, Arkansas on September 25-27. This will be the 20th year the event has been held at Pinnacle — a genuine milestone for one of the Tour's most popular stops.

The purse is $3 million, with 500 Race to CME Globe points on the line for players chasing the year-end championship. The 54-hole, no-cut format means the field plays three full rounds, making consistent ball-striking the premium.

The star of the show: Nelly Korda. The world No. 1 — who has already won two majors in 2026 (the Chevron Championship and the U.S. Women's Open) — returns to competitive golf after helping anchor a USA team that fell short in the Solheim Cup. Korda will be motivated to reclaim momentum.
