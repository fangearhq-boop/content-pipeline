## Story Summaries

### STORY 1: Presidents Cup — 9 Days to Medinah
The 16th Presidents Cup runs September 24-27 at Medinah Country Club (Medinah, IL) — 9 days away. USA captain Brandt Snedeker's full 12-man squad: automatic qualifiers Scheffler, Cameron Young, Wyndham Clark, Russell Henley, Sam Burns, Collin Morikawa; captain's picks Gotterup, Schauffele, Thomas, Cantlay, Jacob Bridgeman, Jackson Koivun (21 years old, fresh from Auburn, maiden Presidents Cup). International captain Geoff Ogilvy's squad: auto qualifiers Si Woo Kim, Hideki Matsuyama, Ryan Fox, Tom Kim, Min Woo Lee, Adam Scott; picks Corey Conners, Nico Echavarria, Sungjae Im, Ryo Hisatsune, Nick Taylor, Christiaan Bezuidenhout. USA has won 13 of 15 editions. Medinah hosted Ryder Cups in 1991, 1999, and 2012.

### STORY 2: Scottie Scheffler's Historic 2026 Season
Scheffler won the TOUR Championship at East Lake on August 30, claiming his second FedEx Cup title and setting a new single-season PGA Tour earnings record of $30,937,525. With his $10M TOUR Championship purse and $23M FedEx Cup bonus, Scheffler has now surpassed Tiger Woods as the all-time PGA Tour money leader with $130M+ in career earnings (Woods' mark was $120.99M). He joins Woods (2007, 2009) and Rory McIlroy (2016, 2019, 2022) as the only multiple FedEx Cup winners. His 2026 scoring average of 67.96 was the Tour's lowest since 1983.

### STORY 3: LIV Golf Post-Season
The 2026 LIV Golf season concluded with the team championship in Indianapolis. Legion XIII (led by Jon Rahm) won the team title ($8.4M prize). Michael La Sasso became the youngest winner in LIV history at age 22. For the 2026 season, LIV expanded to 72-hole stroke play from the original 54-hole format. Brooks Koepka and Patrick Reed departed LIV before the 2026 season to rejoin the PGA Tour. LIV filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy on Sept. 8, with PIF providing $49.6M DIP financing; BC Partners is leading a "LIV 2.0" restructuring to give players majority equity. Four 2027 events already announced: Saudi Arabia, Australia, South Africa, Hong Kong.

### STORY 4: Walmart NW Arkansas Championship Preview
The Walmart NW Arkansas Championship Presented by P&G tees up September 25-27 at Pinnacle Country Club in Rogers, AR — the 20th year at the venue. The $3M purse event is 10 days away. Nelly Korda (world No. 1, two 2026 majors: Chevron + U.S. Women's Open) headlines the returning field after competing in the Solheim Cup. A 54-hole stroke play format with no cut; 500 Race to CME Globe points available.

### STORY 5: Lottie Woad — Europe's Solheim Hero
Twenty-one-year-old Lottie Woad (world No. 5, English) clinched Europe's 15-13 Solheim Cup victory over USA at Bernardus Golf in the Netherlands last week. Woad beat Auston Kim 2&1 in Match 8 to give Europe the points they needed. Her performances throughout the Solheim — aggressive, consistent, unfazed by the occasion — made her the week's breakout star. She is the next major talent on the LPGA Tour, already holding four Tour wins and a world ranking that belies her age. Next Solheim Cup: 2028.
