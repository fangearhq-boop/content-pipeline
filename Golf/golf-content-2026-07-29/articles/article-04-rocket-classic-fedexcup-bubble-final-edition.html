The Final Rocket Classic Starts Thursday — 500 Points and the FedExCup Bubble at Detroit Golf Club

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | July 29, 2026

Rocket Companies is exiting title sponsorship after 2026. The last Rocket Classic runs July 30–August 2 at Detroit Golf Club, offering 500 FedExCup points — the same haul that sent Jackson Koivun to the Playoffs after the 3M Open. Two regular-season events remain. The bubble math is now very clear.

The Rocket Classic at Detroit Golf Club has an end date. Rocket Companies announced it will not renew its title sponsorship after this season. That makes the 2026 edition — running July 30 through August 2 — the last time this tournament carries that name.

For players focused on FedExCup standings, the sponsorship history is a footnote. The 500 points on offer are not.

The Bubble Math

The FedExCup Playoffs begin with the FedEx St. Jude Championship on August 13–16 at TPC Southwind in Memphis. The top 70 players in FedExCup standings qualify. The top 50 advance to the BMW Championship at Bellerive (August 20–23). The top 30 make the Tour Championship at East Lake in Atlanta (August 27–30).

Two regular-season events remain to determine that top 70: the Rocket Classic this week and the Wyndham Championship (August 6–9 at Sedgefield Country Club in Greensboro, North Carolina). After Wyndham, the Playoff field is set.

Before the 3M Open, players including Brooks Koepka (82nd), Tony Finau (83rd), Andrew Novak (84th), and Billy Horschel (101st) were outside the top 70. The 3M Open results moved those positions. But with 500 points available at both Detroit and Greensboro, a win at either event can deliver a Playoff berth from well outside the bubble — as Koivun just demonstrated from entirely outside it.

Scottie Scheffler and the No. 1 Paradox

Scottie Scheffler remains the FedExCup No. 1 heading into Detroit. He also has five runner-up finishes in 2026 with one win — the American Express in January. His final-round 63 at the 3M Open last week was the best round of the field and it still wasn't enough, because Koivun opened with a 64 and built from there.

Scheffler being the FedExCup leader without wins is one of the stranger stories of the PGA Tour season. If he wins at Detroit, the narrative shifts. If he adds another runner-up, the story deepens.

What's Next

Round 1 at Detroit Golf Club is Thursday, July 30. The bubble watch will be live throughout the week as players fight for position with only two events left. After the Rocket Classic concludes, the Wyndham becomes the final regular-season decision point before the Playoffs field locks in.
