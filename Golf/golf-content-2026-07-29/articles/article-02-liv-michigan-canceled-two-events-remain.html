LIV Golf Cancels Michigan — The $40M Season Finale Is Off, Two Events Remain

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | July 29, 2026

LIV Golf's $40 million Michigan team championship has been officially canceled. The 2026 season — which opened with 14 events — now has two remaining. CEO Scott O'Neil could not guarantee those will happen either. There will be no LIV team champion in 2026.

The announcement was expected today, July 29. LIV Golf has canceled the Michigan event — the $40 million team championship that was scheduled for August 27–30 at The Cardinal at Saint John's Resort in Plymouth Township, Michigan.

It was supposed to be the season finale. It was supposed to crown a team champion. It is not going to happen.

This is the second cancellation of LIV's 2026 schedule. LIV New Orleans, originally set for June 25–28, was the first. The original 2026 calendar had 14 events. It is now down to 12 completed or surviving.

What's Left of the 2026 Season

Two events remain on the 2026 LIV schedule:

LIV Golf New York — Trump National Golf Club in Bedminster, New Jersey — runs August 6–9 with a $30 million purse. LIV Golf Indianapolis — The Club at Chatham Hills — runs August 20–23, also a $30 million event.

When asked directly whether he could guarantee those two remaining events would take place, CEO Scott O'Neil said he could not. That statement came at a captains' meeting O'Neil led in the days before the Michigan cancellation was formalized.

Martin Kaymer, captain of Cleeks GC, publicly put the odds of the Michigan event happening at five percent. "Highly unlikely," he said, after that same meeting. Kaymer turned out to be right.

What Michigan's Cancellation Means

Michigan was more than a regular-season event. It was the team championship finale — the event that would have determined which of LIV's teams ended the season as champions. With Michigan gone, there will be no team champion in 2026. That competitive structure, which LIV has promoted as a differentiating feature of its format, produces no champion this year.

The cancellation is also the clearest signal yet that LIV's financial situation is directly affecting its calendar. The league had zero infrastructure on site in Michigan as of late July. No hospitality structures, no stage, no signage, no bridge. Contractors had been left unpaid and had canceled travel. The event was not going to happen regardless of what was officially announced.

What's Next

LIV Bedminster begins August 6. Whether it proceeds as scheduled — and whether Indianapolis follows — depends on factors that LIV's own leadership has declined to guarantee. The league's financial position, covered separately, makes the remaining schedule genuinely uncertain.

Two events. A CEO who won't promise they happen. The 2026 LIV season is ending very differently from how it started.
