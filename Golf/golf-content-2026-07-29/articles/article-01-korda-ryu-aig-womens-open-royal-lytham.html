Korda and Ryu Tee Off Together Thursday — One Win Away From Rewriting Women's Golf History

By Marcus Cole | Golf Fanrecap | July 29, 2026

Nelly Korda and Haeran Ryu have split all four 2026 women's majors between them. They start Round 1 of the AIG Women's Open in the same group at 8:42 AM Thursday at Royal Lytham. One more major for either player equals three in a calendar year — a record Inbee Park set in 2013.

Nelly Korda won the Chevron Championship in April. She won the US Women's Open in June. Two of the year's four women's major titles belong to the world No. 1.

Haeran Ryu won the KPMG Women's PGA Championship. She won the Amundi Evian Championship. The other two belong to her.

On Thursday morning at Royal Lytham & St Annes, both players tee off together at 8:42 AM local time in the same first-round group. Lydia Ko — the 2024 AIG Women's Open champion — completes the trio.

What's on the Line

Three majors in a calendar year. Inbee Park did it in 2013 — she won the Kraft Nabisco Championship, the US Women's Open, and the Women's British Open in the same season. No player has matched that in the modern era. Korda and Ryu are each one win from equaling it.

Korda enters Royal Lytham as the betting favorite at 15-2. She has four career major titles heading into this week, and her form in 2026 has been dominant. Ryu is 12-1. She won in back-to-back starts at two different majors and has been among the most consistent performers of the LPGA season.

For either player, a win at Royal Lytham would not just be another major title. It would be a legitimate historical marker in women's professional golf.

The Course and the Field

Royal Lytham & St Annes last hosted the Women's Open in 2018 — Ariya Jutanugarn won that edition. The Lancashire links plays to par 71 at 6,601 yards and features 174 bunkers, one of the most bunker-dense layouts on the major rotation. This is the 50th Women's British Open and the 26th as a full LPGA major. The field is 144 players competing for a record $10 million purse.

The broader field includes Miyu Yamashita, the defending champion, who tees off at 1:15 PM alongside world No. 2 Atthaya Thitikul. Charley Hull, Hannah Green, and Brooke Henderson are grouped together at 1:26 PM. Rose Zhang is confirmed in the field.

What's Next

Round 1 is Thursday, July 30. The marquee group of Korda, Ryu, and Ko tees off at 8:42 AM UK local time, which is early morning in North America. Leaderboard updates throughout the day.

If either Korda or Ryu reaches Sunday at Royal Lytham in contention, the historical stakes attached to this week become the story driving coverage globally. Both players have handled championship moments well this season. The question is whether either can handle it when the history is this specific and this close.
