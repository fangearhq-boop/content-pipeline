LIV Golf New York Final Round: Niemann at -14 With Varner III Closing Fast

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | August 9, 2026

While the PGA Tour plays its final chapter of the regular season in Greensboro, LIV Golf is staging its own compelling Sunday finale 400 miles north at Trump National Golf Club in Bedminster, New Jersey.

Joaquin Niemann enters the final round at -14, holding a two-shot advantage over Harold Varner III. What looked like a runaway after 36 holes — Niemann shot 64-65 without a single bogey — became a proper Sunday chase after Varner III fired a 65 on Saturday to pull back to within reach.

Niemann's Commanding Start, Saturday's Reset

Niemann's opening two rounds were a masterclass in bogey-free golf. A 64 on Thursday, a 65 on Friday — two rounds with no blemishes, a display of ball-striking precision and putting confidence that put the field effectively in his rearview mirror before the weekend began.

Saturday changed the arithmetic. Niemann shot a 70, a solid round by any conventional measure but one that allowed the field to creep closer. The Chilean star still leads, but the cushion is two shots rather than a comfortable margin — and two shots over 18 holes at Trump National is not a lead that allows complacency.

Harold Varner III made sure of that. His 65 on Saturday was one of the better rounds of the week at Bedminster, a performance that pushed him from the fringe of contention to a genuine co-protagonism in Sunday's final act. Varner has had a career defined by moments when he looked like a future winner; Sunday at LIV Golf New York is another one of those moments.

Team Competition: Torque GC in Control

LIV Golf's team format adds a layer to the Sunday narrative that individual stroke play doesn't replicate. Niemann's team, Torque GC, enters Sunday with a 5-shot team lead over the field — a margin built largely on Niemann's dominant individual performance but one that reflects collective contributions as well.

The team competition plays out simultaneously with the individual leaderboard, creating scenarios where what's optimal for a player individually may interact with what's optimal for his team. Torque GC's 5-shot cushion gives Niemann room to play aggressively for the individual title without the team result being at immediate risk.

Two Worlds, One Sunday

The parallel finales — PGA Tour at Sedgefield, LIV Golf at Bedminster — create one of the more unusual days on the golf calendar. Both feature legitimate drama. Both feature players with something meaningful at stake. The FedExCup bubble at Sedgefield carries playoff-advancement consequences; the individual title at Bedminster carries the prestige of a LIV event win and the team prize for Torque GC.

For Niemann, a win in New York would add to what has been a strong 2026 LIV season. He began the week as one of the more in-form players on the LIV circuit, and his performance through 54 holes confirmed that he is playing with confidence and precision. The final 18 holes will test whether that level holds under pressure.

What's Next

The LIV Golf schedule continues after New York, with future events part of the 2026 calendar before the league turns its attention to the restructured 2027 season under the BC Partners deal expected to close in September. For Niemann and Varner III, Sunday is the only thing that matters — a two-shot difference and 18 holes between them and a trophy at Trump National Bedminster.
