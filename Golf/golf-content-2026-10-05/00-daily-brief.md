## Today's Top Stories

### STORY 1: LIV Golf Secures Enough Player Support for 2027 Relaunch with BC Partners
**Tier:** 1
**Category:** LIV Golf / Bankruptcy / BC Partners / 2027 Relaunch / Player Commitments
**Why it matters:** LIV Golf has received verbal commitments from enough players to clear the threshold required for the BC Partners restructuring deal to proceed. The deal requires 50% of players by count and two-thirds of total claim value. With 8 days until the October 13 deadline, sources report LIV is "comfortably above" the threshold. This is the biggest development in professional golf since the September 8 bankruptcy filing.
**Status:** FOLLOW UP

### STORY 2: Youmin Hwang Wins LOTTE Championship in Hawaii Comeback
**Tier:** 1
**Category:** LPGA Tour / LOTTE Championship / Youmin Hwang / Hawaii / First Win
**Why it matters:** Youmin Hwang, a 22-year-old South Korean competing on a sponsor exemption, won the LOTTE Championship at Hoakalei Country Club with a stunning final-round 67 — birdying five of her last six holes, including the final four, to close at 17-under 271 and win by one stroke over Hyo Joo Kim. Jeeno Thitikul, who led by three entering Sunday, finished back in the field. It is Hwang's first LPGA Tour title.
**Status:** FOLLOW UP

### STORY 4: Tom Kim Wins Asian Games Gold, Withdraws from Baycurrent Classic
**Tier:** 2
**Category:** PGA Tour / Tom Kim / Asian Games / South Korea / Military Service
**Why it matters:** Tom Kim won gold at the 2026 Asian Games at Kasugai Country Club, shooting a final-round 6-under 64 to win by three strokes at 18-under par. The victory exempts Kim from mandatory military service in South Korea, effectively securing his PGA Tour career. He subsequently withdrew from the Baycurrent Classic to recover from the gold medal run.
**Status:** NEW

### STORY 5: Jon Rahm Decision Looms — 8 Days Until LIV Deadline
**Tier:** 2
**Category:** Jon Rahm / LIV Golf / Bankruptcy / BC Partners / Deadline
