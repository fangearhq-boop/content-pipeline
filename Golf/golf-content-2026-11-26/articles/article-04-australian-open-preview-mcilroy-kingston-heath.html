Australian Open Dec 3-6 at Kingston Heath: McIlroy Headlines as Golf's Australian Season Peaks

By Ryan Calloway | November 26, 2026

The BMW Australian PGA Championship at The Lakes in Sydney wraps up Sunday. The Australian Open at Kingston Heath in Melbourne begins the following Thursday, December 3. For the players competing in both — and there are several — the southern hemisphere golf swing ends with its most prestigious stop.

Rory McIlroy leads the confirmed entries for the Australian Open, the event that has been elevated as a co-sanctioned DP World Tour and PGA Tour event since mid-2026. That co-sanctioned status has drawn international fields with genuine depth, and the December 3-6 staging at Kingston Heath marks the conclusion of the sport's international calendar year.

Kingston Heath's Reputation

Kingston Heath Golf Club in Melbourne's sandbelt district is widely regarded as one of the finest golf courses in the world — a mid-length parkland-sandbelt hybrid that rewards precision over power, features firm and fast conditions when the summer weather cooperates, and provides a constant test of shot-making through its small, contoured greens.

The venue gives the Australian Open its most distinctive identity: an event that feels different from the American-influenced events that dominate the northern hemisphere calendar, and that tests different skills from the marquee major venues. It is the kind of course where a crafty player with a creative short game can outperform a bomber whose game is built for wide fairways and soft conditions.

McIlroy, Smith, and Scott

McIlroy arrives having claimed his record-tying eighth Race to Dubai title during the European season. He is the marquee name in a field that also expects Cameron Smith and Adam Scott, both of whom are playing the BMW Australian PGA Championship this week at The Lakes.

Smith, the three-time Australian PGA champion, has spoken publicly about his desire to establish himself back in the elite of international golf following his return from outside the mainstream tours. A strong week at The Lakes, followed by a contention at Kingston Heath, would do more for that narrative than any press conference could. Adam Scott, at 46, plays because he still can — and still does it at a level that keeps him relevant in fields like this one.

What's Next

The Australian Open begins with the first round on Thursday, December 3 at Kingston Heath. It is the final major international golf event of 2026. For McIlroy, it is an opportunity to add another prestigious title to a season already defined by the Race to Dubai. For Smith and Scott, it is a home event at the sport's most beautiful level. For the rest of the international field, it is 72 holes on a course that tends to separate the best from everyone else in ways that feel conclusive by Sunday afternoon.
