Marco Penge Wins Four-Way RSM Classic Playoff to Save PGA Tour Card and Earn First Win

By Jake Torres | November 26, 2026

Marco Penge spent the week at Sea Island doing math. He was ranked 120th in the FedExCup Fall standings going into the RSM Classic — 21 spots outside the cutline for automatic 2027 PGA Tour status. By Monday evening, after a four-way playoff at No. 18 on the Plantation Course, the math had resolved in his favor in the most dramatic fashion possible.

Penge birdied the first extra playoff hole to claim his maiden PGA Tour victory, defeating Haotong Li, Max Greyserman, and Michael Thorbjornsen — three players who had also watched every scoreboard all week knowing their livelihoods were on the line. He earned a two-year Tour exemption through 2028 in the process.

The Stakes Were as High as Golf Gets

The RSM Classic is annually one of the most charged events on the PGA Tour calendar, not because of its prestige or its purse, but because of what it represents for the players on the bubble. The FedExCup Fall standings after the event determine which players retain full 2027 Tour status. Players ranked outside the top 100 must either win the event outright or receive a sponsor exemption or conditional status to remain on Tour.

For Penge, ranked 120th at the start of the week, the only viable path to saving his card was to win. He did exactly that. He was the only player in the week's field to climb into the top 100, pushing David Lipsky out of the automatic cutline with his victory.

Four Men, One Hole, One Career Defined

The playoff involved four players who had each found themselves tied at the conclusion of regulation — Penge, Li, Greyserman, and Thorbjornsen. All four had reason to want the win badly, and all four had the game to deliver it. Penge ended the drama quickly, hitting his approach close at No. 18 on the return to the Plantation Course and converting the birdie putt when none of his competitors could match the moment.

It was not a moment defined by brilliance; it was a moment defined by composure. In a playoff where four players had spent 72-plus holes performing under maximum pressure, the one who kept his nerve when the stakes were stated clearly was the one who went home a champion.

What's Next

Marco Penge carries his first PGA Tour victory and a secure card into an offseason that now looks entirely different from how it appeared 10 days ago. For David Lipsky, the bubble's other side is a painful landing, and his path back to full Tour status will require success on the conditional circuit or finding another route to membership. The PGA Tour's 2027 season schedule opens in January, and Penge will be there for it — with a winner's exemption and the confidence of a player who delivered when everything was on the line.
