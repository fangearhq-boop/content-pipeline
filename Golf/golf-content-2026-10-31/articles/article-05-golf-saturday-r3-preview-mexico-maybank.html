Golf Saturday Preview: R3 in Mexico and Kuala Lumpur — Novak Leads PGA, Yamashita LPGA

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap

October 31, 2026

Saturday brings Round 3 across two time zones and two tours. At VidantaWorld in Puerto Vallarta, Mexico, Andrew Novak carries a 3-shot lead into the PGA Tour's Mexico Open third round at -14. On the other side of the globe in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, defending champion Miyu Yamashita leads the LPGA's Maybank Championship at -13, 2 shots clear of the field.

Two tournaments, two leaders, one Saturday. Here is the full picture.

PGA Tour — VidantaWorld Mexico Open (Puerto Vallarta)

Andrew Novak's R2 course-record-tying 61 gave him a commanding 3-shot advantage that he carries into Saturday's moving day. Five players are tied at -11 — Ben Griffin, Kihei Akina, Matthieu Pavon, Adrien Dumont de Chassart, and Kensei Hirata — making the chasing pack both large and legitimate.

The Mexico Open adds an additional subplot: two-time defending champion Patrick Smotherman. He won here in 2024 and 2025. Whether he is positioned to make a run at a historic three-peat will become clear as Saturday's groups go out and the round develops. Novak is the leader and the center of attention, but Smotherman's presence in the field is the historical thread running through the week.

A 54-hole leader at a PGA Tour event wins roughly 60 percent of the time. Novak's task Saturday is to play smart, protect his margin, and not give the -11 group a look at the lead. One careless stretch in the middle of the round can bring a chase to within a shot or two. One clean stretch from any of the five chasers can do the same.

LPGA Tour — Maybank Championship (Kuala Lumpur)

Miyu Yamashita arrived in Malaysia as the defending champion and has played like it. Her R2 66 moved her to -13 through 36 holes, and she leads Julia López Ramírez and Pauline Roussin-Bouchard by 2 shots.

Roussin-Bouchard's bogey-free record through 36 holes is the conversation piece of the Maybank's midpoint. A player who hasn't conceded a shot to par across two rounds is in a different mental state than one who has been scrambling. She arrives at Saturday with a clean slate and a 2-shot gap to close. Yamashita's advantage is real, but it is not a guarantee in a field that includes A Lim Kim at -10 and Amy Yang, Charley Hull, Maja Stark, and Sophia Schubert at -9.

The Maybank's Saturday will tell us whether Yamashita can extend her lead, hold it, or face a genuine charge from the group behind her. Four of the five players at -9 are former major champions or established LPGA winners. The leaderboard has depth.

What to Watch Saturday

In Mexico: Can Novak shoot another clean round and put the tournament away, or will the -11 group close the gap? Watch whether Smotherman can use Saturday to write his way into the three-peat conversation.

In Kuala Lumpur: Can Yamashita defend with the authority her Friday performance suggested? And can Roussin-Bouchard extend her bogey-free record through a third round while putting the pressure on?

What's Next

Both tournaments are in R3 Saturday. The Mexico Open's Round 4 and the Maybank Championship's Round 4 both take place Sunday — meaning today's results will set up tomorrow's finish. Leaders who extend leads today give themselves margin to work with. Leaders who bleed shots open doors. Saturday golf is where championships are lost and where championship narratives take their final shape before the closing round.
