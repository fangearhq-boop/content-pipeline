CME Group Tour Championship 2026 Preview: Thitikul, Korda, and the LPGA's $11M Finale

By Jake Torres | Golf Fanrecap | November 17, 2026

The LPGA Tour's season finale arrives Thursday at Tiburon Golf Club in Naples, Florida — and the stage has never been bigger. The CME Group Tour Championship brings together the top 60 players on tour for a 72-hole stroke-play event with an $11 million purse, the richest in women's professional golf.

At the top of the field: defending champion Jeeno Thitikul and world No. 1 Nelly Korda. Both players arrive in peak form. The question is which of them holds the trophy on Sunday.

Thitikul: Defending and in Form

Jeeno Thitikul won the 2025 CME Group Tour Championship at a stunning 26-under par, a performance that underscored her status as one of the most dangerous players in women's golf when the conditions are right and the stakes are highest. She arrives this week as the defending champion and in excellent form.

Thitikul picked up her fourth LPGA victory of 2026 at the Lotte Championship, adding to a season that has consistently placed her inside the top five at major events. Her ball-striking has been among the best on tour all year, and Tiburon's demanding layout — with its narrow fairways and demanding approach angles — tends to reward precisely the type of controlled, strategic play that is Thitikul's hallmark.

No LPGA player has successfully defended a CME Group Tour Championship title in recent years. That narrative makes Thitikul's run this week all the more compelling.

Korda's Season-Defining Opportunity

Nelly Korda enters Naples as the world No. 1 and as one of the most dominant players in women's golf over the past two seasons. She has been a consistent presence at the top of leaderboards throughout 2026, and the CME represents a natural opportunity to cap a strong year with a marquee title.

Korda has not won the CME Group Tour Championship. For a player of her caliber — multiple major titles, a world No. 1 ranking, one of the sport's highest profiles — a Naples victory would add an important entry to her résumé and announce the season's close on her terms.

She arrives in Florida not chasing a ranking or a bonus pool. She is simply chasing a win.

The Field and the Format

The CME Group Tour Championship invites the top 60 players from the LPGA Tour's season points list, meaning every player in the field has earned her way here. The 72-hole stroke-play format over four days places a premium on sustained excellence — there is no cut, so every player tees off on Sunday, but the cumulative pressure of four competitive rounds in Naples has a way of separating the field.

Tiburon Golf Club, located at The Ritz-Carlton Golf Resort in Naples, has hosted the event multiple times and is well known to the LPGA's top players. Its Bermuda grass greens and subtropical conditions create a setting that is both beautiful and strategically demanding.

The $11 million purse makes the CME Group Tour Championship not only the richest event in women's golf but one of the largest prize pools in professional golf globally. The winner receives $2 million.

What's Next

The CME Group Tour Championship begins Thursday, November 19, at Tiburon Golf Club in Naples, Florida. The event runs through Sunday, November 22. With Thitikul defending and Korda hunting, the 2026 LPGA season appears set to end with the sport's two most compelling figures in direct competition at the highest stakes. For women's golf, there may not be a better way to close the year.
