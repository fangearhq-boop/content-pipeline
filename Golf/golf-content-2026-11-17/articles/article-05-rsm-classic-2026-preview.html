RSM Classic 2026 Preview: The PGA Tour's Season Finale at Sea Island

By Ryan Calloway | Golf Fanrecap | November 17, 2026

The PGA Tour's 2026 season ends where it has ended before: at The RSM Classic at Sea Island Golf Club on the Georgia coast, running November 19-22. With the FedEx Cup already claimed by Scottie Scheffler and the season's major championships long settled, Sea Island offers one final week for players to carve out a meaningful result before the books close on the year.

What's Still At Stake

For some players, the RSM Classic is about card security. The PGA Tour's membership system means that final season finishes can determine who keeps their cards for 2027, and a strong performance at Sea Island carries real career consequences for players on the bubble.

For others, the week represents a last chance to add a first PGA Tour victory to their résumé before year's end. First wins matter — for ranking, for exemptions, for the trajectory of a career. The RSM Classic has been a launching pad for emerging players before, and a relatively open field at a season finale creates opportunities that more loaded events do not.

The $7.4 million purse gives everyone in the field reason to bring their best to Georgia. Even for players with nothing specific at stake in the standings, $7.4 million is motivation enough.

Sea Island: A Worthy Setting

Sea Island Golf Club provides one of the most scenic settings on the PGA Tour calendar. The event uses two courses — the Plantation Course and the Seaside Course — in a split-field format for the first two rounds before the field consolidates for the weekend. Both layouts take advantage of the Georgia coast's natural surroundings, with the Seaside Course offering the more visually dramatic conditions thanks to its proximity to the Atlantic.

The tournament's November timing typically brings mild temperatures and calm conditions early in the week before coastal weather can introduce wind and variability late. Players who can manage the Bermuda grass greens that characterize both layouts and handle whatever conditions the final weekend brings tend to separate themselves here.

The Feel of a Season Finale

There is something distinct about the final event of a PGA Tour season. The FedEx Cup is gone. The major schedule is a distant memory. What remains is simply professional golf at a beautiful venue with a meaningful purse — no playoffs pressure, no historical weight, just the week itself.

RSM Classic champions have often been players who seized the moment when the field's stars were absent and the conditions were right. This year, with LIV Golf's collapse reshaping the broader landscape of the sport and the PGA Tour firmly in control of professional golf's future, the season finale carries extra symbolic weight even if the competitive stakes are limited.

What's Next

The RSM Classic tees off Thursday, November 19, at Sea Island Golf Club in St. Simons Island, Georgia, and concludes Sunday, November 22. When the final putt drops on Sunday afternoon, the 2026 PGA Tour season will be complete — the most dramatic in years, defined by LIV Golf's end, Scheffler's historic run, and the beginning of whatever comes next for professional golf. Sea Island may not be where any of those stories started. But it is where this chapter closes.
