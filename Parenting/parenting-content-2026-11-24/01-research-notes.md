### STORY 4: Black Friday Toy Safety Checklist

**Facts — HIGH confidence (CPSC official guidance + established standards):**
- Choking hazard label: toys for under-3 must be labeled with choking hazard warning if they contain small parts. — HIGH (CPSC official standard)
- Button battery danger: button/coin batteries can cause severe internal injuries if swallowed; must be in secured compartments. — HIGH (CPSC official)
- Noise hazard: toys over 85 dB can damage children's hearing. — HIGH (AAP/CPSC guidance)
- Magnetic ingestion: high-powered magnets in children's products are banned by CPSC; check for loose/accessible magnets. — HIGH (CPSC)
- 2026 recall context: magnetic robot toys (CHE-US, ~101K units, fatal ingestion risk — Nov 23 pipeline research); flammable pajamas (Qpancy, Nov 22); AI toys with unsafe content (Trouble in Toyland 2026, Nov 22). — HIGH (Nov 22-23 research)
- CPSC SaferProducts.gov: official recall database to check before buying. — HIGH (official)

**Sources:**

### STORY 5: Parenting Yourself Through the Holidays

**Facts — HIGH confidence:**
- AAP/Pediatrics study: caregiver mental health moderates how well parenting interventions work. Children of caregivers without mental health challenges benefited most from parenting programs. — HIGH (publications.aap.org)
- AAP recommendation: parents seeking mental health support should be connected to local resources, not just referred. — HIGH (AAP guidance)
- Pikes Peak Behavioral Health Group: local COS mental health provider, serves families. — HIGH (local provider)
- El Paso County Health: Behavioral health resources available, elpasocountyhealth.org. — HIGH (official county)

**Facts — MEDIUM confidence:**
- D11 family services: district provides social-emotional support resources for families. — MEDIUM (general district knowledge, not fetched)
