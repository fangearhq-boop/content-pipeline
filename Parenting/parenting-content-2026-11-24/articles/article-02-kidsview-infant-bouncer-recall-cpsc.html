Recall Alert: Kidsview Infant Bouncers Recalled After 7 Collapses, 2 Head Injuries

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | November 24, 2026

Tier 1 | National Parenting / Product Safety pillar

If you have a Kidsview infant bouncer at home, stop using it now.

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission announced a recall on November 19, 2026, covering approximately 412 Kidsview infant bouncers sold on Amazon. The bouncers can collapse during use, and the CPSC has received seven reports of collapse — including two incidents in which infants struck their head on the floor.

What the Recall Covers

The recall covers Kidsview infant bouncers sold through Amazon. If you purchased one of these products, the remedy is a full refund. The CPSC advises parents to stop using the bouncer immediately, regardless of whether it has shown any issues.

To start the refund process, visit cpsc.gov/recalls or contact the seller through your Amazon order history. The official CPSC recall page has the most current contact information and instructions.

A Difficult Month for Infant Products

This is the third infant or baby product recall COS Parenting has covered in the past two weeks. Earlier this month, the SHEIN Baby Rattle and Teether set was recalled over a choking hazard involving approximately 2,657 sets. The CHE-US magnetic robot toy recall — which covered more than 101,000 units — was announced on November 19, the same day as this Kidsview action.

The pattern underscores why it's worth checking cpsc.gov/recalls periodically, particularly for products purchased from third-party sellers on Amazon or similar platforms. The CPSC's SaferProducts.gov database is searchable by product type and brand.

How to Check Your Baby Gear

For families with infants, it's worth taking 10 minutes to run a quick check on the products you use most. At cpsc.gov, you can search by product category, brand, or keyword. If you've received recall notices by email from Amazon, those will also appear in your account notifications — but not all sellers proactively notify buyers, so an active search is more reliable.

The key categories to check regularly: infant bouncers and swings, bath seats, sleep products, and toys with small parts or magnetic components.

What's Next

The CPSC recall page at cpsc.gov/recalls is the primary source for all current and past recalls. Parents who have already purchased the Kidsview bouncer and want to initiate a refund should contact the seller through their Amazon order history or follow the instructions on the official recall page.

With Black Friday approaching this week, it's also a good time to check any toys or baby products you're planning to buy against the CPSC recall database before purchasing.
