## Story 4: AAP Iron Deficiency Screening Guidelines

**Source:** AAP news release (aap.org/en/news-room/news-releases/aap/2026/); Contemporary Pediatrics (Jun 29 and Sep 16, 2026); Texas Children's Hospital; GuidelineCentral; healthychildren.org
**Confidence:** HIGH

- Guideline released: June 2026, published July 2026 issue of Pediatrics [HIGH — multiple sources confirm]
- Replaces: 2010 guidance [HIGH]
- Screening test: CBC + serum ferritin (or hemoglobin alone if CBC/ferritin not available) [HIGH]
- Breastfed infants: Screen at 9–12 months [HIGH]
- Formula-fed infants: Screen at 15–18 months after transition to cow's milk [HIGH]
- Iron supplementation for breastfed term infants: 1 mg/kg/day by 4 months (may wait to 6 months when iron-containing complementary foods introduced) [HIGH]
- Preterm infants: Iron supplementation by 2 weeks of life [HIGH]
- No cow's milk before 12 months; excess milk intake after 12 months is a risk factor [HIGH]
- Children with ongoing risk: Recheck at each well visit through age 4 [HIGH]
- Menstruating adolescents: CBC + ferritin at least 1 year after menarche or by age 14 [MEDIUM — per practitioner summary]
