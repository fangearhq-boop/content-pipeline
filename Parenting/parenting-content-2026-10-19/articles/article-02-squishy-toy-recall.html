37,000 Squishy Toys Recalled Over Fatal Water Bead Hazard — Check Your Toy Bin Now

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | October 19, 2026

COLORADO SPRINGS — SAFETY

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission recalled approximately 37,000 ABC Trading squishy toys on October 1, 2026, due to a potentially fatal ingestion hazard. If you have these toys at home, the CPSC says to stop use immediately and discard them.

The two recalled products are the Squishy Dumpling (model number 3655) and the Crazy Squib Ball (model number 3984). Both were sold from April through June 2026 at ABC Trading stores in Los Angeles and at retailers nationwide, priced between 41 cents and $1.25.

Why These Toys Are Dangerous

The toys contain water beads that expand beyond the size permitted under the mandatory federal toy safety standard. If a child swallows one, the bead can continue expanding inside the body and cause a life-threatening intestinal blockage or obstruction.

Because of their low cost and small size, these toys may not stand out as a concern in a typical toy collection. The CPSC's recall of 37,000 units indicates wide enough distribution that families should check actively rather than assume they don't have one.

Medical providers advise: if you suspect a child has swallowed a water bead, do not induce vomiting. Go to the emergency room right away and tell medical staff what was swallowed so they can perform appropriate imaging.

What to Do

The CPSC's remedy instructions are:

  1. Stop use of the product immediately.
  2. Write "RECALLED" on the toy.
  3. Throw it away.
  4. Email a photo of the discarded toy to recallabc@gmail.com to receive a refund.

No injuries have been reported in connection with this specific recall, but the expansion behavior of the water beads makes these toys a clear hazard for young children.

What's Next

For full recall details and official guidance, visit CPSC.gov and search for "ABC Trading." The CPSC maintains a full searchable recall database at cpsc.gov/recalls. Signing up for CPSC recall alerts at cpsc.gov/en/Recalls/ puts new notices directly in your inbox as they're issued.

If your child has swallowed a water bead from any toy — recalled or not — contact your child's pediatrician or call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222 before heading to the emergency room for guidance on next steps.
