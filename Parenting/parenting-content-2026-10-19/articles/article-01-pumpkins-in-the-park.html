Pumpkins in the Park Is Back This Saturday — A Free Fall Festival the Whole Family Can Enjoy

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | October 19, 2026

COLORADO SPRINGS — LOCAL EVENTS

With D11 and D49 families in the middle of fall break, a free outdoor festival is landing at exactly the right time. Pumpkins in the Park returns to Bancroft Park in Old Colorado City this Saturday, October 24, from noon to 6 p.m., with no tickets or registration required.

The event is organized by the Old Colorado City shopping district and has been a neighborhood fixture for years. This year's lineup includes a pumpkin patch, live music, face painting, balloon animals, tie-dye, arts and crafts, a costume contest, a beer garden, and local vendors.

What to Expect

The costume contest is one of the event's most popular draws. If you've been procrastinating on costumes, this gives you a reason to pull something together a few days before Halloween. The contest is open to all ages.

The pumpkin patch and arts and crafts activities are well-suited to younger children. The vendor area and beer garden give adults something to do while kids explore. Live music runs throughout the afternoon.

Old Colorado City's Colorado Avenue strip runs right past Bancroft Park, so the festival connects naturally with the neighborhood's independent shops and restaurants. Parking is available along Colorado Avenue and on surrounding side streets.

What's Next

Pumpkins in the Park runs one afternoon only. For updated details, check the Old Colorado City shopping district at shopoldcoloradocity.com before heading out — event details can change. Bancroft Park is free to enter, and the surrounding neighborhood is walkable from the parking areas along Colorado Avenue.

Halloween falls on a Saturday this year, making the week of October 26 a natural second window for costumes and celebrations. Pumpkins in the Park on October 24 is a strong opening act for the week.
