### STORY 2: CPSC Recalls 37,000 ABC Trading Squishy Toys for Water Bead Hazard [T1 — NEW]
The CPSC recalled approximately 37,000 ABC Trading squishy toys on October 1, 2026. Two products: Squishy Dumpling (model 3655) and Crazy Squib Ball (model 3984). Water beads inside expand beyond the permitted size and pose a fatal ingestion risk. Sold April–June 2026 at ABC Trading stores and retailers nationwide for $0.41–$1.25. Stop use, mark "RECALLED," discard, and email recallabc@gmail.com with a photo for a refund. Confirmed via CPSC.gov.

### STORY 3: AAP and CDC Now Have Different Vaccine Schedules — What COS Families Should Know [T2 — NEW]
In January 2026, the CDC narrowed its routine childhood immunization schedule, removing several vaccines or moving them to "high-risk only." The AAP declined to follow, maintaining recommendations for 18 diseases for all children. The divergence affects COVID-19 (6–23 months), RSV, and HPV timing, among others. The AAP schedule is endorsed by 12 medical organizations including the AMA, AAFP, and ACOG.

### STORY 4: AAP Updates Iron Deficiency Screening Guidelines for Infants and Toddlers [T2 — NEW]
The AAP released new iron deficiency guidelines in June 2026 (published July 2026 issue of Pediatrics), replacing 2010 guidance. Expanded screening with CBC + serum ferritin: breastfed infants at 9–12 months, formula-fed infants at 15–18 months after transition to cow's milk. Breastfed infants should receive iron supplementation (1 mg/kg/day) by 4 months. Children with ongoing risk factors rescreened at each well visit through age 4.
