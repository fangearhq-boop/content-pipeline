### STORY 1: D11 Bond Election Day — COS Voters Decide $775M for School Buildings Today
**Tier:** 1 | **Pillar:** Local News | **Byline:** Sarah Morales

Today is Election Day in Colorado Springs. District 11 voters are deciding on Issue 4A, a $775 million bond that would fund the construction of a new Holmes Middle School, HVAC repairs at every D11 school, upgrades to Palmer High and Coronado High, and library renovations across the district. This is the first D11 capital bond since 2004. A 2021 bond attempt failed by just 11 votes. For the average homeowner, the estimated cost is about $260/year (roughly $22/month). Mail-in ballots can still be returned to drop boxes through 7 PM today — find locations at d11.org/bond. D20 also has a bond on today's ballot.

### STORY 2: Colorado Flu Season Update — First Pediatric Death Confirmed, Vaccines Available Now
**Tier:** 1 | **Pillar:** National Parenting | **Byline:** Jamie Rivera

Colorado health officials have confirmed the first reported pediatric influenza-associated death of the 2026-27 respiratory virus season in an elementary-aged child. The CDPHE reports that child had not received the 2026-27 flu vaccine. Last season was Colorado's worst on record for children: 8 deaths and 1,169 hospitalizations. The 2026-27 season is starting earlier than usual. Flu peaks in December-January; it's not too late to vaccinate now. The AAP recommends annual flu shots for all children ages 6 months and older. COS pharmacies and pediatric clinics have doses available.

- **Posting window:** 10:00 AM MT

### STORY 3: CPSC Recall Alert — Children's Bed Rails and Light-Up Toys to Check Now
**Tier:** 2 | **Pillar:** National Parenting | **Byline:** Sarah Morales

CPSC has issued multiple 2026 recalls and warnings on children's portable bed rails for entrapment and asphyxiation hazards, including recalls of HOMEAL and WYBITNY bed rails sold on Amazon, and warnings to stop using KOOLDOO, Famill, and Yulong Tengfei rails immediately. Separately, ZMC Group has expanded a recall of light-up children's toys due to battery ingestion risk. Families who purchased bed rails or light-up children's toys in 2025-2026 — especially from Amazon — should check cpsc.gov. Return or destroy affected products; do not donate. CPSC hotline: 1-800-638-2772.

- **Posting window:** 12:00 PM MT

### STORY 4: Veterans Day Weekend Guide for COS Families — Parade, 5K, and Free Museum Entry
**Tier:** 2 | **Pillar:** Local Events | **Byline:** Jamie Rivera

Veterans Day weekend in Colorado Springs is family-friendly and largely free. The In Their Honor Veterans Day Parade runs Saturday, November 7 at 11 AM, marching south along Tejon Street in downtown COS — free for all. Sunday, November 8: the Colorado Springs Veterans Day 5K (9-11 AM) includes a kids race for ages 15 and under, $15 registration. The ANA Money Museum (818 N. This weekend is a great chance to talk with kids about what Veterans Day means and honor the military community that defines so much of Colorado Springs.
