District 49 families have a no-school day coming up this Friday, November 6. It's a professional development day for D49 staff, which means students are home. If you're working Friday and haven't sorted out childcare yet, you have tonight and tomorrow morning to get that arranged.

D11 and D20 families: this is a D49-specific calendar item. Your district's schedule may differ. Check d11.org or asd20.org to confirm what's on your calendar this week.

What to Know About the D49 Board Meeting Thursday

The D49 Board of Education holds its regular meeting on Thursday, November 5. If you're a D49 parent who wants to follow district business or attend in person, the meeting agenda and access details are posted at d49.org. Board meetings are open to the public and include a public comment period for community members who want to address the board.

Board meetings are often the place where curriculum decisions, budget updates, and school-level changes get formal votes. If there are any items of interest to your family on the November 5 agenda, Thursday evening is the opportunity to be in the room or watch remotely.

Looking Ahead: Thanksgiving Break

For D49 families doing forward planning, Thanksgiving break runs November 23 through November 27. That's about three weeks out. If your work schedule during that week requires advance childcare planning, now is a reasonable time to start those conversations.

The stretch from now through Thanksgiving is relatively light on district-wide calendar items for D49, which makes this week's no-school day a bit of a standalone. After the holiday break, the December holiday calendar will pick up with its own set of early dismissals and no-school days — check d49.org for the full academic calendar if you want to map out the rest of the semester.

What's Next

For D49 families: confirm childcare for Friday if needed, and check d49.org if you want to tune into Thursday's board meeting. For families across all Colorado Springs districts: Veterans Day weekend is coming up Saturday and Sunday, with the In Their Honor Veterans Day Parade downtown on Saturday morning and a kids 5K on Sunday — both good family options if the weather cooperates.
