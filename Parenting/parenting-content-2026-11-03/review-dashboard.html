Colorado Flu Season Update — First Pediatric Death Confirmed, Vaccines Available Now
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Colorado health officials have confirmed the first child flu death of the 2026-27 season. The child was elementary school age and had not received this year's flu vaccine. We know this is hard to read. It's also information that matters. Last season was Colorado's worst on record for children: 8 kids died and 1,169 were hospitalized — more than any year since the state started tracking. The 2026-27 season is starting earlier than last year. Flu typically peaks in December and January, which means we're heading into the highest-risk window right now. The good news: it's not too late. The AAP recommends the annual flu shot for every child 6 months and older, and doses are available at most COS pharmacies and pediatric clinics. If your kids haven't gotten their flu shots yet, this week is a good time. Families whose kids have respiratory illness symptoms — fever, cough, body aches — should call their pediatrician before heading in. Testing is available. Have your kids gotten their flu shots yet this season? ---

CPSC Recall Alert — Children's Bed Rails and Light-Up Toys to Check Now
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post If you've bought children's bed rails or light-up kids' toys from Amazon in the past year or two, take a minute to check this. The Consumer Product Safety Commission has issued formal recalls for two brands of children's portable bed rails — HOMEAL and WYBITNY — due to a risk of entrapment and asphyxiation. Three other brands (KOOLDOO, Famill, and Yulong Tengfei) have received urgent stop-use warnings for the same hazard. All of these products were sold through Amazon. Separately, ZMC Group has expanded a recall on light-up children's toys over a battery ingestion risk. These toys have small, powerful batteries that can cause serious internal injuries if swallowed. What to do: stop using any recalled product immediately. Don't donate it — it may harm another family. Check cpsc.gov for the specific recall number and remedy options (refund, replacement). You can also search your Amazon order history by brand name. CPSC's hotline is 1-800-638-2772. Is there a recall here you weren't aware of? ---

Veterans Day Weekend Guide for COS Families — Parade, 5K, and Free Museum Entry
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Veterans Day weekend is coming up, and Colorado Springs has a few good options for families who want to get out and mark the occasion. On Saturday, November 7, the In Their Honor Veterans Day Parade kicks off at 11 AM in downtown COS, marching south down Tejon Street. It's free, it's family-friendly, and it's a meaningful hour for kids who are old enough to ask why we honor veterans. On Sunday, November 8, the Colorado Springs Veterans Day 5K runs from 9 to 11 AM. There's a kids race for ages 15 and under — registration is $15. If you want to add an indoor stop, the ANA Money Museum at 818 N. Cascade Ave. offers free admission for children 12 and under. They have exhibits on coins and currency history that are surprisingly kid-friendly. This is one of those weekends where Colorado Springs' deep military roots show up in a tangible way. It's a good opportunity to talk with kids about what service means and who in their community has served. What are your Veterans Day weekend plans? ---
