### STORY 3: CPSC Recalls Squishy Dumpling and Crazy Squib Ball Toys — Water Bead Hazard
**Tier:** 2 | **Type:** NEW STORY
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Pillar:** National Parenting (Product Safety)

ABC Trading is recalling approximately 37,000 units of Squishy Dumpling Toys and Crazy Squib Ball Toys. The toys contain water beads that violate mandatory safety standards — water beads are an ingestion hazard that can expand inside a child's body and cause serious internal injuries. Recalled October 2, 2026. Families who have these products should write "recalled" on the toy and email a photo for a refund.

**Key Facts:**
- Recall date: approximately October 2, 2026
- Products: Squishy Dumpling Toys + Crazy Squib Ball Toys (ABC Trading)
- Units: ~37,000
- Hazard: water beads violate mandatory safety standards; ingestion/expansion hazard
- Remedy: write "recalled" on toy, email photo for refund
- Source: cpsc.gov

**Sources:** cpsc.gov, abc27.com consumer recalls

### STORY 4: D11 Bond Measure 4A — Three Weeks to Election Day
**Tier:** 2 | **Type:** FOLLOW-UP (Oct 10 S1: D11 Bond explainer)
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Pillar:** Local News

With November 3 election day three weeks away, D11 families are starting to receive their mail ballots for Ballot Question 4A. The $775M bond measure funds HVAC/air quality improvements, safety and security upgrades, asbestos and lead mitigation, CTE (career-technical education) pathways, classroom modernization, and library renovations. The typical homeowner would pay approximately $260/year in additional property taxes. An independent oversight committee is required. D11 voted 6-1 to put the measure on the ballot in August 2026.

**Key Facts:**
- Election Day: November 3, 2026 (3 weeks away as of Oct 12)
- Measure: Ballot Question 4A, $775M
- Approved for ballot: Board voted 6-1, August 5, 2026
- Cost to typical homeowner: ~$260/year
- Funds: HVAC, safety/security, asbestos/lead, CTE, classrooms, libraries
- Independent oversight committee required
- Info: krdo.com, d11.org/4A

**Sources:** krdo.com Oct 9, gazette.com endorsement Oct 6
