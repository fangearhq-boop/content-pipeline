CPSC Recalls Squishy Dumpling and Crazy Squib Ball Toys Over Water Bead Hazard

By Jamie Rivera | October 12, 2026

About 37,000 Squishy Dumpling Toys and Crazy Squib Ball Toys have been recalled by ABC Trading after the CPSC determined the products contain water beads that violate mandatory safety standards. If your family has these products, here's what to do.

Why Water Beads Are Dangerous

Water beads — the small, colorful orbs that absorb liquid and expand — have been at the center of multiple CPSC recalls in recent years. The danger is straightforward: if a child swallows a water bead, it can absorb fluid inside the digestive tract and expand significantly, causing blockages and serious internal injuries. In severe cases, the injuries have required surgery.

Young children are especially at risk because the beads can look like candy or small toys, and their small size before hydration makes them easy to swallow without a caregiver noticing. The CPSC has consistently flagged products containing water beads as presenting an unacceptable risk to children.

What to Do If You Have These Toys

If your family purchased Squishy Dumpling Toys or Crazy Squib Ball Toys from ABC Trading, stop using them immediately. The CPSC-recommended remedy is to write "recalled" clearly on the toy and email a photo to the manufacturer to receive a refund.

For additional details, including contact information for ABC Trading, visit cpsc.gov and search for the recall. The CPSC maintains a comprehensive, searchable recall database that's updated regularly.

A Pattern of Water Bead Recalls

This is not the first water bead toy recall in 2026. The CPSC has been actively pursuing water bead products that fail to meet safety standards, and parents should be aware that similar-looking toys from other brands may also pose risks. If your child has any toy that contains small, round, absorbent beads, check cpsc.gov to confirm whether it's on the recall list.

The broader guidance from consumer safety organizations is consistent: water bead products should not be accessible to children under 10 unsupervised, and any product containing water beads that isn't specifically certified as meeting current CPSC standards should be treated with caution.

What's Next

The recall is active now. If you've recently gifted or received either of these products, check your home and follow the remedy steps above.
