D11 Bond Measure 4A: Mail Ballots Arriving Now as Election Day Approaches

By Sarah Morales | October 12, 2026

Colorado Springs School District 11's Ballot Question 4A goes before voters on November 3. With mail ballots arriving this week, families have three weeks to decide on the $775M bond measure that would fund safety upgrades, HVAC improvements, and other facility needs across the district.

What the Bond Would Fund

The $775M bond measure would fund improvements across District 11 facilities in several categories:

  • Safety and security upgrades across district schools
  • HVAC systems and air quality improvements
  • Asbestos and lead mitigation
  • Career-technical education (CTE) pathways and facilities
  • Classroom modernization
  • Library renovations
  • Prepayment of existing lease-purchase obligations

The district also notes that an independent oversight committee would be required to track how funds are spent.

What It Costs

The district estimates the measure would cost a typical homeowner approximately $260 per year in additional property taxes. The actual amount varies based on your home's assessed value. For a more precise estimate, El Paso County's online property tax estimator can help calculate your specific impact.

The Background

The District 11 school board voted 6-1 in August 2026 to place Ballot Question 4A on the November ballot. The measure has drawn endorsements from local organizations, including The Gazette's editorial board, which published its support in early October.

D11 covers most of the central and southeastern portions of Colorado Springs. With many school buildings aging and infrastructure needs growing, supporters of the measure argue the bond represents a long-deferred investment in the safety and functionality of district schools.

What's Next

Mail ballots are going out to registered voters in El Paso County this week. Election Day is November 3, 2026. Families can also return completed ballots early through drop boxes or in-person locations. For the full voter guide and official district information on Ballot Question 4A, visit d11.org.
