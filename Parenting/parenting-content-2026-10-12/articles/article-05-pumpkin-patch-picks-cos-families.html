Best Pumpkin Patches Near Colorado Springs for Families This Fall

By Jamie Rivera | October 12, 2026

With D49 fall break underway and peak pumpkin season here, Colorado Springs and nearby Monument have strong options for families looking for a genuine outdoor fall experience. Here are three worth your time this week.

Venetucci Farm Pumpkin Fest

Venetucci Farm is a historic, family-owned farm on the south end of Colorado Springs that has become one of the city's best-loved fall traditions. Their Pumpkin Fest runs on Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays through October 31.

The offerings include a genuine pumpkin patch, fresh cider, cornhole, Jenga, and the kind of relaxed outdoor atmosphere that's hard to replicate anywhere else in the area. It's the sort of place that feels distinctly Colorado — low-key, community-focused, and beautiful against the backdrop of a fall afternoon.

For families with kids of any age, this is a reliable choice for an afternoon out without much planning required. Bring cash or check the farm's website for current payment options and hours.

Colorado Kids Ranch — Monument

A short drive north of COS in Monument, Colorado Kids Ranch is particularly well-suited for families with younger children. The venue is designed with kids ages 2-12 in mind and remains open through October 31. Admission runs $18-20, and children 3 and under get in free.

The ranch is a focused, manageable size — the kind of experience that doesn't leave young kids overwhelmed or overloaded. For parents with toddlers who need stimulation but not a whole day's worth of activity, it fits the bill well.

Western Museum of Mining and Industry — Miner's Pumpkin Patch

If you want a fall outing that pairs history with pumpkins, the Western Museum of Mining and Industry is worth the trip. Every Saturday in October, the museum runs their Miner's Pumpkin Patch — which includes hayrides, a hay maze, and gold panning. It's a genuinely different experience from a conventional pumpkin patch and gives kids something educational to bring home alongside their pumpkin.

The museum is located just north of COS on the I-25 corridor. Check the museum's website for current hours and pricing before going on a Saturday.

What's Next

Pumpkin season peaks in the third and fourth weeks of October. D49 fall break runs through October 19, making this week the ideal time to schedule a farm outing before school resumes. D11 fall break follows October 19-23, so families in that district will have their window the following week. And for more COS fall events, the full October calendar is available at the Gazette's events section and visitcos.com.
