### STORY 3: Squishy Dumpling Water Bead Toy Recall

#### Long-Form Post — 12:00 PM MT

Parents, heads up on a product recall you'll want to check for.

ABC Trading has recalled approximately 37,000 Squishy Dumpling Toys and Crazy Squib Ball Toys — both contain water beads that violate mandatory safety standards. The concern with water beads isn't just a choking hazard. If a child swallows one, the beads can expand inside the digestive tract and cause serious internal injuries that sometimes require surgery.

If you have either of these products at home, the CPSC says to write "recalled" on the toy and email a photo for a refund. More details are at cpsc.gov.

Water bead toys have been a recurring concern for the CPSC over the past few years. This recall joins a growing list of similar products that have been pulled from shelves for the same reason.

### STORY 4: D11 Bond Measure 4A — 3 Weeks Out

#### Long-Form Post — 3:00 PM MT

D11 families, mail ballots for the November 3 election are going out this week — which means it's time to talk about Ballot Question 4A.

The $775M bond measure would fund safety and security improvements, HVAC and air quality upgrades, asbestos and lead removal, career-technical education (CTE) pathways, classroom modernizations, and library renovations across the district. The district school board approved putting this on the ballot 6-1 back in August.

What would it cost? The district estimates roughly $260 per year for a typical homeowner in additional property taxes. The measure also requires an independent oversight committee to track how the money gets used.

We covered the full explainer earlier this week. If you missed it, all the details are at d11.org or search "D11 4A."

### STORY 5: Pumpkin Patch Picks — COS Family Farms This Week

#### Long-Form Post — 5:00 PM MT

If you've got D49 kids home on fall break and you're looking for a reason to get outside, Colorado Springs has some genuinely good pumpkin patch options this week.

Venetucci Farm on the south side of town is one of the most beloved family spots in COS. Their Pumpkin Fest is open Fridays through Sundays through October 31 — think cider, Jenga, cornhole, and, of course, a pumpkin patch. It's a family-owned historic farm and the kind of place you actually want to come back to every year.

A little farther out, Colorado Kids Ranch in Monument is open through October 31. It's particularly good for younger kids (ages 2 and up). Admission runs $18-20, and children 3 and under get in free.

For something a little different, the Western Museum of Mining and Industry runs their Miner's Pumpkin Patch every Saturday in October — hayrides, a hay maze, and gold panning, all on the same outing.
