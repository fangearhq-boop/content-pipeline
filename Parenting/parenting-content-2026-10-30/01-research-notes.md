### STORY 1: Harkla Sensory Swing Recall

**Source:** cpsc.gov/Recalls (Oct 29, 2026), kiro7.com, abc27.com, wftv.com
**Confidence:** HIGH (CPSC official recall + multiple news outlets)

**Key Facts:**
- [HIGH] CPSC announced recall of Harkla children's compression sensory swings: October 29, 2026
- [HIGH] Approximately 31,075 units affected
- [HIGH] Hazard: 4-hole ceiling hook plate can separate from the mount, creating a fall hazard
- [HIGH] Only the 4-hole mounting plate version is affected; 2-hole version is SAFE
- [HIGH] 29 reported incidents; 14 injuries including 2 concussions and 1 broken arm
- [HIGH] Swings sold March 2020–December 2025 for approximately $100–$130
- [HIGH] Sold at Amazon, Harkla.co, Sensory Tool House, Hammer & Jacks, Loaded Sensory, and Renew
- [HIGH] Remedy: free hardware replacement kit at harkla.co/pages/ceiling-hook-recall-2026 or call 844-442-7552
- [HIGH] SKU affected: LS-COMP-1BLU or LS-COMP-1BLU-SW (4-hole version)
- [MEDIUM] Described as "fifth significant child product safety action in October 2026" — prior four: ONTHEWEI bath seat (Oct 9), Papablic bedside sleeper/bblüv Grow high chair (Oct 15), Lucakuins bath seat (Oct 27), Flyboss infant bouncer (Oct 29)

**Confidence:** HIGH (CDC primary source + multiple news outlets confirm case count)

**Key Facts:**
- [HIGH] As of October 22, 2026: 4,588 confirmed US measles cases
- [HIGH] Highest annual case count since measles was declared eliminated in 2000
- [HIGH] Roughly double 2025's total case count
- [HIGH] 3 deaths nationally in 2026
- [HIGH] Children aged 5–19 make up 42% of cases
- [HIGH] Children under 5 make up 17% of cases; 15% of under-5 cases required hospitalization
- [HIGH] 96% of 2026 patients were unvaccinated or had unknown vaccination status

- [HIGH] Down from 95.2% in 2019-20 (pre-pandemic level)
- [MEDIUM] Pennsylvania has the largest state outbreak (~1,222 cases as of Oct 15)
- [MEDIUM] Colorado-specific MMR rates or COS/D11 rates: not confirmed from single source
- [LOW] Local COS herd immunity threshold context: ~95% needed for measles (general immunology standard — do not present as COS-specific data)

### STORY 3: AAP Oral Health for Adolescents

**Source:** healthychildren.org/English/news article pointing to Pediatrics Oct 19, 2026 publication; AAP Section on Oral Health
**Confidence:** MEDIUM (single main article; Pediatrics publication confirmed)

**Key Facts:**
- [MEDIUM] Clinical report "Oral Health for Adolescents" published in Pediatrics: October 19, 2026
- [MEDIUM] Lead author: Dr. Fisher-Owens, AAP Section on Oral Health
- [MEDIUM] Pediatricians should take a dental history and do perioral/intraoral exam at annual teen well visits
- [MEDIUM] Screen for: dental pain, bleeding gums, dry mouth, changes in eating/drinking
- [MEDIUM] Report flags vaping, cannabis, and alcohol as oral health risk factors to assess during well visit
- [HIGH] Tooth decay (dental caries) remains the most common chronic disease in adolescents — well established
