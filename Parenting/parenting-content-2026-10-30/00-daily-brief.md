### STORY 3: AAP Says Pediatricians Should Check Your Teen's Teeth at Every Well Visit
- **Tier:** T2
- **Pillar:** National Parenting
- **Status:** NEW
- **Byline:** Jamie Rivera
- **Summary:** New AAP clinical report "Oral Health for Adolescents" published in Pediatrics October 19, 2026. Pediatricians should take a dental history and perform a perioral/intraoral exam at every teen well visit; screen for pain, bleeding gums, dry mouth, changes in eating/drinking. - **Action:** Ask your teen's pediatrician to assess oral health at next well visit; confirm teen has regular dental visits.
