D11 Bond Vote Closes in 4 Days — Here's How to Return Your Ballot by November 3

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting

October 30, 2026

The deadline to vote on the D11 $775 million school bond is November 3, 2026 — four days from today. If you haven't returned your mail-in ballot yet, it needs to be in a drop box by 7 PM on Election Day. This is a final-call reminder, not a deep dive into what the bond funds — we've covered that already. This is about making sure your ballot doesn't sit on a counter until it's too late.

How to Return Your Ballot

Colorado Springs is a mail-in ballot jurisdiction. Your ballot was mailed to your address as a registered voter within D11 boundaries. You have three options to return it:

The easiest: drop it in a designated drop box. Drop boxes are open now, 24 hours a day, through 7 PM on November 3. Locations are listed at d11.org/bond and on the El Paso County Clerk's website. You don't need a stamp, an envelope, or anything other than your completed, signed ballot.

You can also mail it back, but only if it will arrive by Election Day — postmarked by November 3. With four days left and weekend mail service, mailing is now the higher-risk option. A drop box is faster and certain.

In-person voting at vote centers is also available on Election Day, November 3, if you prefer that route or need a replacement ballot. Vote center locations are listed on the El Paso County Clerk's site.

What You're Voting On

The D11 bond would authorize $775 million for school facility improvements. The major components we've covered in detail this month include HVAC replacements across aging buildings, classroom modernization, and library renovations. The estimated tax impact is approximately $6.50 per month for every $100,000 of assessed home value.

D11 hasn't passed a bond since approximately 2003. The most recent attempt failed narrowly. With about 24,000 students in the district, this vote determines what condition those school buildings will be in for the next generation of families.

More detail at d11.org/bond, including the full tax calculator and project list by school.

What's Next

Election night results will begin reporting after 7 PM on November 3 at the El Paso County Clerk's website and local news stations. D11 will also communicate results through its district channels. We'll have a follow-up once results are in.

If your ballot is already in a drop box or in the mail, you're done. If it's on your counter, now is a good time to sign it and make the drop before the weekend gets busy.
