### Build-A-Bear Heartwarming Hugs Bears Recall
- **Units:** ~36,000
- **Hazard:** Zipper slider on side pouch (holds 2.5 lbs ceramic heating/cooling beads) can detach — choking hazard
- **Sold:** January–March 2026 at Build-A-Bear Workshop stores, approximately $48
- **Incidents:** 1 zipper detachment in UK; 0 U.S. injuries
- **Action:** Stop use immediately; return to any Build-A-Bear store for full refund or gift card
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Sources:** CPSC.gov, NBC News, Newsweek

### PIXLABBY Magnetic Fidget Sliders Recall
- **Units:** ~860
- **Sold:** Amazon, October 2025–May 2026, approximately $10
- **Hazard:** Silicone casing removable by children; releases high-powered magnets. Multiple magnets swallowed attract across intestinal walls → perforation, twisting, blockages, blood poisoning, death
- **Incidents:** 1 child hospitalized after ingesting 2 magnets
- **Action:** Stop use; photograph disposal; contact PIXLABBY for full refund
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Sources:** CPSC.gov direct, ConsumerAffairs Aug 7 roundup

### Wnttmt Baby Walkers Recall (also Aug 6 batch — secondary mention only)
- **Units:** ~1,310 sold on Amazon
- **Hazard:** Can pass through standard doorway AND fail to stop at step edge — stair fall risk

## National Parenting Research

### Toddler Foods Ultra-Processed Study
- **Research group:** University of Texas at Austin
- **Scope:** 2,783 toddler food products audited from 21 Austin-area grocery stores
- **Key findings:**
  - 81% of products marketed for ages 6-36 months were ultra-processed
  - 49% failed at least one WHO nutrient recommendation for this age group
  - 17% exceeded sugar limits
  - 25% exceeded sodium limits
  - 11% exceeded fat recommendations
  - 12% were too calorie-dense
- **Presented at:** American Society of Nutrition NUTRITION 2026 annual conference
- **Implications:** Displace vital nutrients for brain development; linked to childhood obesity, type 2 diabetes, heart disease
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Sources:** Forbes Aug 1, ScienceDaily July 27, EurekAlert, CBS42, UT Austin CNS
