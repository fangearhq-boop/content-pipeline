**Tier:** 1
**Type:** NEW STORY

**Angle:** CPSC recall: approximately 860 PIXLABBY Magnetic Fidget Sliders sold on Amazon (October 2025–May 2026, approximately $10). ### STORY 4: 81% of Toddler Foods at the Grocery Store Are Ultra-Processed, New Study Finds
**Tier:** 2
**Type:** NEW STORY
**Content Pillar:** National Parenting
**Angle:** University of Texas at Austin researchers audited 2,783 toddler food products from 21 grocery stores and found that 81% were ultra-processed. Nearly half (49%) failed at least one WHO nutritional recommendation for children ages 6-36 months. Among the failures: 17% exceeded sugar limits, 25% exceeded sodium limits, 11% had excessive fat, and 12% were too calorie-dense. Study was presented at the American Society of Nutrition's NUTRITION 2026 annual conference. Researchers note these foods displace vital nutrients needed for brain development and are linked to childhood obesity, type 2 diabetes, and heart disease. Practical shopping guidance for Colorado Springs parents.
