### STORY 2: PIXLABBY Magnetic Fidget Sliders Recalled — Child Hospitalized
**Tier:** 1
**Type:** NEW STORY
**Content Pillar:** National Parenting
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Content:** Article + 2 X Text Posts + FB Long-Form + FB Image Caption + 2 Image Concepts

**Key Facts (HIGH confidence):**
- ~860 units; Amazon; October 2025–May 2026; ~$10
- Hazard: Silicone casing removable → exposes high-powered magnets; multiple magnets swallowed attract across intestinal walls → perforation, blockages, blood poisoning, death
- 1 child hospitalized after ingesting 2 magnets
- Action: Stop use; photograph disposal; contact PIXLABBY for full refund
- Sources: CPSC.gov, ConsumerAffairs Aug 7

**Narrative angle:** This is an urgent safety story. High-powered magnet ingestion is one of the most dangerous pediatric emergencies. The CPSC has been working on magnet toy regulations for years. One child is already hospitalized. Even though the unit count is small (~860), these were inexpensive Amazon items that could be in any home.

### STORY 4: 81% of Toddler Foods Are Ultra-Processed
**Tier:** 2
**Type:** NEW STORY
**Content Pillar:** National Parenting
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Content:** Article + 1 X Text Post + FB Long-Form + FB Image Caption + 2 Image Concepts

**Key Facts (HIGH confidence):**
- 81% of 2,783 toddler food products audited were ultra-processed (ages 6-36 months)
- 49% failed at least one WHO nutrient recommendation
- Breakdown: 17% too much sugar; 25% too much sodium; 11% too much fat; 12% too calorie-dense
- University of Texas at Austin; presented at NUTRITION 2026 (American Society of Nutrition)
- Sources: Forbes Aug 1, ScienceDaily July 27, EurekAlert, CBS42, UT Austin CNS

**Narrative angle:** Back-to-school season + weaning/toddler food overlap means this study has immediate practical relevance for Colorado Springs families introducing solid foods. Key message is not to panic but to use the label — this is a teachable opportunity to read nutrition facts.
