### STORY 2: CPSC Recalls: B. Childhood Strollers, Bellabu Bear Kids' Robes, Golden Link LED Cups
- **Tier:** 2
- **Pillar:** National Parenting
- **Status:** NEW STORY
- **Angle:** Three new CPSC recalls and warnings parents in Colorado Springs should know about this weekend. B. Childhood strollers sold on SHEIN: stop use immediately due to entrapment hazard (opening between seat and grab bar can trap an infant). Bellabu Bear children's robes (sold at Saks Fifth Avenue and online): recalled for burn hazard, violating mandatory sleepwear standards. Golden Link Fantastic Four cups and Captain America containers with LED lights: recalled due to serious injury/death risk from button battery ingestion. All three are products that may be in COS homes.

### STORY 3: D11 Board Votes Tuesday on $775M Bond — First in Over 20 Years
- **Tier:** 2
- **Pillar:** Local News
- **Status:** FOLLOW-UP
- **Angle:** Colorado Springs School District 11's board of education meets Tuesday, August 5 at 5:30 PM to vote on whether to place a $775 million bond measure on the November 2026 ballot. If approved by the board, voters will decide in November. The bond would fund HVAC upgrades, safety improvements, school modernizations, and new construction — the first major D11 bond effort in over 20 years. Cost to homeowners: approximately $6.50/month per $100,000 of assessed property value. D11's first day of school is August 12, one week from Tuesday.
