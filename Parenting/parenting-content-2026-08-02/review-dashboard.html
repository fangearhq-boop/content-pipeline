CPSC Recalls: B. Childhood Strollers, Bellabu Bear Kids' Robes, Golden Link LED Cups
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Three products you'll want to check if you have them at home. First: B. Childhood "High Landscape" strollers sold on SHEIN. The CPSC has issued a stop-use warning because an opening between the seat and the grab bar can allow an infant to become entrapped. If you have one of these strollers, stop using it immediately and check cpsc.gov/Recalls for the full notice and remedy information. Second: Bellabu Bear children's robes. These were sold at Saks Fifth Avenue and through online retailers between January 2024 and July 2025. They've been recalled for a burn hazard — the robes don't meet mandatory standards for children's sleepwear. Contact information for the recall is on the CPSC site. Third: Golden Link Fantastic Four cups and Captain America containers with LED lights. The button batteries in the LED components pose a serious risk of injury or death if a child ingests one. These are sold as cups and food containers — the kind that might look like a kid's water bottle or snack holder. If you're unsure whether something you own has been recalled, cpsc.gov/Recalls has a full searchable database. The CPSC also has a free recall alert system you can subscribe to at their site. ### Long-Form Post Colorado Springs School District 11's board of education meets this Tuesday, August 5, at 5:30 p.m. On the agenda: a vote on whether to place a $775 million bond measure on the November 2026 ballot. If the board votes yes, voters will decide in November whether to approve the bond. The money would fund facility improvements across D11 schools — HVAC upgrades in buildings that have gone decades without them, safety and security upgrades, school modernizations, and new construction where needed. For context: this would be D11's first major bond measure in more than 20 years. The estimated cost to homeowners is roughly $6.50 per month for every $100,000 of assessed property value. D11's first day of school is August 12 — just one week after Tuesday's vote. Whatever the board decides, families will be heading back to those same buildings in 10 days. If you want to learn more before Tuesday, d112026bondplanning.com has information about what the bond would fund and why D11 says it's needed. The board meeting is public, and the vote is typically the final agenda item. COS School Start Dates 2026 — All Three Districts at a Glance
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post If you're still not sure exactly when school starts in Colorado Springs this year, here's a quick-reference breakdown. D49 (Falcon School District 49): Kindergarteners, 6th graders, and 9th graders — kids entering a new school building for the first time — start this Monday, August 3. All other D49 students start Tuesday, August 4. D11 (Colorado Springs School District 11): First day is August 12. D20 (Academy School District 20): Student first day is August 17–18. (Worth noting: August 13 is a staff and professional development day — you may see that date referenced, but students don't start until the 17th.) That means three different first-day weekends across about two weeks: D49 families gear up now, D11 families have 10 days, and D20 families have until August 16 to hit the back-to-school checklist. Before the first day, it's worth double-checking: supply list completed, emergency contacts updated in your school's system, transportation route confirmed (especially if the route changed from last year), and school start/end times verified — many schools adjust those slightly from year to year.
