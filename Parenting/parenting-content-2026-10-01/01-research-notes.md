## Story 4: AAP 2026 Vaccine Schedule

**Sources:** ajmc.com, coloradosun.com (Jan. 7, 2026), healthychildren.org/aapnews, cdphe.colorado.gov

### Key Facts
- AAP 2026 schedule: Recommends vaccines for 17 diseases (some sources: 18)
- CDC 2026 schedule: Fewer diseases; dropped hepatitis A, hepatitis B, meningococcal disease, rotavirus, flu, RSV (and at least one additional vaccine)
- Difference: AAP recommends approximately 7 more vaccines than CDC
- COVID-19 vaccine: AAP recommends for children ages 6–23 months
- Endorsements: 12 national health organizations + 28 states back AAP schedule
- Colorado CDPHE: Urges families to follow AAP schedule, not CDC
- Source detail: coloradosun.com Jan. 7, 2026 article confirmed Colorado's CDPHE position
- Key context: The divergence between AAP and CDC is relatively new in 2026; historically they aligned
