## Story 2 — CPSC Infant Recalls

### Long-Form Post

COS parents with infants — this one's urgent.

The Consumer Product Safety Commission has recalled three infant walker brands and issued a stop-use warning for an infant swing this month. If you've recently purchased a baby walker or infant swing on Amazon or Walmart, check your order history.

Here's what's been recalled:

**Infant walkers to stop using immediately:**
- Wnttmt Baby Walkers (sold as WURUI.BABY, Amazon) — ~1,310 units. Fall and entrapment hazard; can fit through a standard doorway and fail to stop at a step's edge.
- Uuoeebb Infant Walkers (sold by BaoD, Amazon) — entrapment hazard; leg openings allow a child to slip until their head can become trapped.
- WonderStone Infant Walkers (sold by Wonder Stone Toys, Walmart.com) — fall hazard; same doorway and step-edge failure.

**Infant swing — stop use warning:**
- Bimuva Infant Swings (Amazon, sold Oct 2024–Aug 2025, ~$100) — about 2,970 units. The incline angle exceeds the 10° safety standard. Violates the Safe Sleep for Babies Act. Suffocation risk. Do not use, do not resell, do not give away.

All walker recalls involve products that violate the federal mandatory safety standard. The full details, including how to get a refund, are at cpsc.gov.

Have any of these products? What did you do with it? Share so other parents know what to look for.

## Story 3 — Bear Creek Nature Center 50th Anniversary

### Long-Form Post

Free family day alert: Bear Creek Nature Center is turning 50, and it's throwing a party. 🐾

Mark your calendar for Saturday, August 22, 10 AM to 2 PM at Bear Creek Nature Center (245 Bear Creek Road). The whole event is free to the public, which makes it one of the better COS family outings this month.

One heads-up: the Living Nature Hike requires pre-registration. If you want in on that, secure your spot before August 22. El Paso County Parks and Recreation is hosting — check elpasoco.com for registration details.

## Story 4 — AAP Flu Vaccine 2026-27

### Long-Form Post

The American Academy of Pediatrics just released its flu vaccine guidance for the 2026–27 season — and the message is clear: get your kids vaccinated, and do it before October ends. 💉

The AAP recommends annual flu vaccination for all children 6 months and older who don't have a medical contraindication. There's no preference between vaccine formulations — any licensed flu vaccine appropriate for your child's age and health status counts. The goal is to get them protected before flu season picks up.

Here's the context that makes this hit harder: last season (2025–26) was a high-severity flu year. 190 children in the United States died from influenza. Of those, 85% were not vaccinated.

This back-to-school season in Colorado Springs is a natural checkpoint. Kids are back in classrooms, exposure risks go up, and October is still close enough that there's time to get ahead of it. Most pediatricians and many pharmacies now offer flu shots — a quick call to your child's doctor or a walk-in pharmacy appointment is usually all it takes.

## Story 5 — Back-to-School Anxiety Tips

### Long-Form Post

D11's first day is today — and if your household this morning felt like controlled chaos mixed with a little dread, you're in good company. 💙

Back-to-school anxiety is real, and it doesn't just hit the kids. Parents carry it too — and local advocates in Colorado Springs point out that kids often pick up on their parents' stress. So the first tip is almost uncomfortably simple: manage your own nerves, and you help your child manage theirs.

The team at Safe Passage, a Colorado Springs children's advocacy organization, shared some practical guidance this back-to-school season:

**What helps:**
- Start conversations early, not the morning of — don't surprise kids with schedule changes or transitions
- Pack the bag and choose tomorrow's clothes the night before; a visual routine lowers morning stress for everyone
- Do a daily check-in, but make it specific — "What was one good moment?" gets more out of kids than "How was school?"
- Brief check-ins after school work better than long debrief sessions

**When to pay closer attention:**
If anxiety doesn't settle as routines kick in, or if it starts affecting sleep, school attendance, or your child's relationships at home, it's worth getting more support. Changes that last several weeks are a signal.

Colorado has a 24/7 resource if you need it: call or text 988 for the Mental Health Lifeline.

Here's a question to kick off the conversation: first day jitters or first day excitement — which is your kid?
