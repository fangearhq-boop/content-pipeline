### STORY 5: Back-to-School Anxiety Is Real — What COS Parents Can Do Today

**Tier:** 2
**Pillar:** Evergreen
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Status:** NEW STORY

**What:** With D11 starting today and D20 coming Aug 17, back-to-school anxiety is a real issue for kids and parents alike. Local experts and Colorado-based advocates share practical tips: start routines early, keep daily check-ins short and specific, watch for warning signs, and know that parental stress transfers to kids. Colorado 988 Mental Health Lifeline is available 24/7.

**Why now:** D11 first day is today; D20 starts Aug 17. Evergreen but maximally timely on day-one.

**Key facts:**
- Expert cited: Safe Passage, a Colorado Springs children's advocacy center
- Key tips: communicate about transitions; establish routines before school starts; choose clothes/pack bags night before; avoid surprising kids about schedule changes
- Parental stress matters: children take emotional cues from parents; high parental stress increases child anxiety
- Daily check-in: brief, specific questions ("What was one good moment today?") work better than broad questions
- Warning signs: lasting changes (several weeks) affecting sleep, school attendance, or home relationships warrant additional support
- Colorado 988 Mental Health Lifeline: call or text 988, available 24/7
- Source: KOAA (koaa.com), Fox21 (fox21news.com), Colorado Parent (coloradoparent.com)
