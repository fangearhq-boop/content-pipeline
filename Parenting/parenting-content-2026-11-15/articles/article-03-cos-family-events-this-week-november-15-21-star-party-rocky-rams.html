COS Family Events This Week: Star Party Tuesday, Rocky Ram's Holiday Spectacular Saturday

By Jamie Rivera | November 15, 2026 | COS Parenting

It's the last full week before Thanksgiving break — and there are two standout family events worth putting on the calendar. A free astronomy night at UCCS on Tuesday and an early holiday celebration on Pikes Peak on Saturday. Here's what to know about each one.

Tuesday, November 18 — Family Star Party: Voyager Anniversary at UCCS

The Colorado Springs Astronomical Society is hosting a free public star party at UCCS Centennial Hall on Tuesday, November 18, starting at 6:00 PM. The theme this year is the Voyager spacecraft's Golden Record — the message humanity sent into space in 1977, now well past the edge of our solar system.

Expect real stargazing with telescopes (weather permitting), kid-friendly activities around the Golden Record and what it contains, and the kind of event that tends to leave both kids and adults asking more questions than they started with. It's free, it's open to the public, and UCCS Centennial Hall is centrally located in Colorado Springs.

This kind of event is relatively rare — a legitimate free astronomy program with organized activities, not just a "come look through a telescope" setup. If you've got kids with any interest in space, it's worth the trip.

Saturday, November 21 — Rocky Ram's Holiday Spectacular on Pikes Peak

Rocky Ram's Holiday Spectacular at Crystal Reservoir on Pikes Peak is a COS tradition that kicks off the holiday season in a setting you can't replicate. Photos with Santa and Mrs. Claus, kid-friendly games, and holiday activities — all with Pikes Peak as the backdrop.

Crystal Reservoir sits along the Pikes Peak Highway, which means the event brings a mountain experience that's distinctive to COS. If you haven't been, it's the kind of outing that shows up in a lot of family photos years later. Check the Pikes Peak Highway schedule and weather conditions before heading up — mountain events at elevation can be affected by late-season weather.

Also This Week: D49 Board Work Session

D49 families: the district has a Board of Education Work Session on Tuesday, November 18 from 6:30 to 9:30 PM at Creekside Success Center. Work sessions are open to the public, though they typically follow a different format than regular board meetings. Worth checking the D49 website at d49.org for the agenda if there's a topic on your radar.

Thanksgiving Break — All Three Districts

D11, D20, and D49 families: Thanksgiving break runs November 23 through November 27 for all three districts. This week is the last full school week before that break. The calendar goes school → Thanksgiving → back to school December 1 for a brief stretch before winter break approaches.

What's Next

After Thanksgiving, COS family events quickly shift into full holiday season programming — tree lightings, winter markets, school performances, and the December event calendar. Rocky Ram's on Saturday is a good early warm-up. For a running list of COS family events, local calendars like Happenin' COS track new additions regularly as venues finalize their December programs.
