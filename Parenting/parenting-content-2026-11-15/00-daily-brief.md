### STORY 1: D11 Bond Certified — What the $775M Win Means for Families Now
**Tier: 1 | Type: FOLLOW-UP (Nov 13 S2: D11 bond passes → what's next)**
**Pillar: Local News**
- **Posting window:** 8:00 AM MT

District 11 voters approved the $775 million Issue 4A bond in the November 3 election — the first D11 bond since 2006 and the largest single bond in El Paso County history. With approximately 51.68% of voters in favor in unofficial results, the measure is on track for certification. The board has committed to redirecting mill levy override funds to teacher pay if the bond passes. Major projects include an $80 million rebuild of Holmes Middle School. The board also proposed a citizen oversight committee for bond spending. This story looks at what the certification means for the construction timeline and what families can expect over the coming years.

### STORY 2: CPSC Recalls: SHEIN Baby Rattles and Kakgold RC Cars — Two New Hazards to Know About
**Tier: 1 | Type: NEW STORY**
**Pillar: National Parenting**
- **Posting window:** 10:00 AM MT

The CPSC posted two children's product recalls on November 12, 2026 that COS families should know about before the holiday shopping season. SHEIN baby rattle and teether sets sold on US.Shein.com between November 2025 and July 2026 (about 2,657 units) have knobs that can detach and pose a choking hazard for children under 3. Kakgold 4WD remote-controlled cars (about 294 units) contain button cell and lithium-ion batteries that children can access, posing an ingestion and injury risk. Both recalls require consumers to stop use immediately and contact the company for a remedy.

### STORY 4: Colorado's Flu Season Is Starting Early — What COS Families Need to Know
**Tier: 2 | Type: NEW STORY**
**Pillar: National Parenting**
- **Posting window:** 3:00 PM MT

Colorado health officials confirmed the first reported pediatric influenza-associated death of the 2026–27 season in an elementary-aged child who had not received the current flu vaccine. Last season was Colorado's worst on record for flu hospitalizations — 5,931 statewide, 1,169 in children, 8 pediatric deaths. The 2026–27 season is showing early activity. Flu vaccination rates for children 6 months to 9 years old remain at approximately 36% — far below what's needed for community-level protection. CDPHE is also sending RSV immunization reminders to families of children under 8 months. Sources include CDPHE, Denver7, CPR News.

### STORY 5: D20 Families: What to Know About the District's New Interim Leader and School Builds
**Tier: 2 | Type: NEW STORY**
**Pillar: Local News**
- **Posting window:** 5:00 PM MT

Academy District 20 is a year into a leadership transition following the board's vote to part ways with former superintendent Jinger Haberer. The board unanimously selected Dr. Susan Field, previously assistant superintendent for learning services, as interim superintendent through the 2026–27 school year. D20 also broke ground on a new pre-K through 8th grade school at the Air Force Academy, funded by a $42.7 million Department of Defense award — a significant addition for USAFA families in the district. D20 maintained its "accreditation with distinction" academic rating in preliminary 2025–26 state performance results.
