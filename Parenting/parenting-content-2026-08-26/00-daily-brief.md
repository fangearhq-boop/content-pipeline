### STORY 2: CPSC Recall — 213,500 Goody King Magnetic Building Cubes and Blocks — TIER 1
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Angle:** NEW STORY — CPSC recalled Goody King Magnetic Building Cubes and Blocks (Yi Suen Commerce) — 213,500 units sold on Amazon January 2024–July 2026. Sets of 45, 56, 100, 120, 150, and 300 cubes. Cubes can break open, releasing high-powered magnets. Two children required surgery to remove ingested magnets. Hazard: magnets can perforate intestines, cause blood poisoning, or death if multiple ingested. Stop using immediately; contact Yi Suen Commerce for full refund + prepaid shipping label.
