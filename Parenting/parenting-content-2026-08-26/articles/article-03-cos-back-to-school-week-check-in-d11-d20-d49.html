COS Back-to-School Check-In: Where D11, D20, and D49 Families Stand This Week

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | August 26, 2026

Colorado Springs families are spread across different stages of the 2026–2027 school year right now, depending on which district their children attend. Some families are wrapping up week four. Others are just getting through week two. Here is a quick district-by-district check-in, along with some practical suggestions for this point in the adjustment period.

Quick Reference

  • D49 (Falcon): Started Aug 3-4 — Week 4 as of Aug 26
  • D11: Started Aug 12 — Week 3 as of Aug 26 (Monday late starts continue all year)
  • D20: Started Aug 17-18 — Week 2 as of Aug 26
  • D11 enrollment: Still open — d11.org or 1033 N. Franklin St., Mon–Fri 8 AM–4 PM MT
  • D11 communication: ParentSquare
  • D20 communication: ParentSquare
  • D49 communication: Check d49.org for family portal

D49 Families: Week Four

Falcon School District 49 families are the furthest into the year. Students who started August 3 (kindergartners, 6th graders, and 9th graders) have been in school for more than three weeks. Everyone else began August 4.

By week four, most families have moved through the initial adjustment and are now in the pattern that will largely define the school year. Homework routines are either working or revealing where they need to change. After-school schedules — sports, activities, childcare arrangements — are in full swing. If something is not working at this stage, it is worth addressing now rather than waiting for a bigger disruption.

D49's Student Success Center opened this school year with expanded preschool services (Monday through Thursday, available to families district-wide) and relocated Elevates 18–21 Transition Services. If your family uses those programs, week four marks the earliest point at which families are reliably in the full routine.

D11 Families: Week Three

Colorado Springs School District 11 families started August 12, with a Jump Start Day on August 11 for kindergartners, 6th graders, and 9th graders. Week three is typically when the novelty of school has worn off and the routine is either taking hold or not.

A reminder for D11 families: Monday late-start days are built into the district calendar all year, not just the first few weeks. Each Monday, students arrive later to allow for teacher professional development before school begins. Check your specific school's schedule for the exact late-start time, as it varies by grade level.

D11 enrollment is still open for the 2026–2027 school year. If you need to enroll or make changes, visit d11.org or go in person to 1033 N. Franklin Street, open Monday through Friday 8 AM to 4 PM MT.

D20 Families: Week Two

Academy School District 20 families are the most recently started, with preschool, kindergarten, 6th grade, and 9th grade students beginning August 17 and all other students starting August 18. Week two is the first week where the full enrollment — all grades, all buildings — is in the routine together.

For D20 families, week two is when many children are still adjusting. The excitement of the first week has passed, and the consistency of the routine is being tested. This is a normal adjustment point. Some children, particularly in the lower elementary grades, may still be showing signs of back-to-school anxiety — difficulty at drop-off, complaints about minor ailments, lower energy in the evenings.

Practical Suggestions for Weeks Two Through Four

Regardless of district, a few things are worth doing at this stage of the school year. Set a consistent homework location — the same spot, the same time, each day — and protect that routine from interruptions. Check in with your child's teacher if early academic or social concerns have appeared; most teachers are responsive via ParentSquare or email during the first month.

Confirm that your family is connected to your district's communication system. D11 and D20 use ParentSquare for alerts and messages. D49 families should verify they are set up in the d49.org family portal. Missing a Monday late-start notice or a schedule change because a parent account was not activated is a preventable problem.

What's Next

The first round of parent-teacher conferences typically happens in October for most COS districts. For D20 families just entering week two, this will be their first formal progress check-in with teachers. For D49 families in week four, the conference calendar may already have dates posted. Check your district's calendar now so the date does not sneak up on you.
