## STORY 1: 2026 National Parent Survey — Quality Time

### Verified Facts (HIGH confidence)
- New America 2026 National Parent Survey: 5,472 total parents surveyed, including 2,915 lower-income parents [HIGH — newamerica.org]
- Conducted by NORC at the University of Chicago on behalf of New America's New Practice Lab [HIGH — newamerica.org]
- 72% of parents wished for more quality time with their children [HIGH — newamerica.org/findings, deseret.com]
- 47% of parents with income below 100% of federal poverty level cannot spend their time as they'd like due to inability to afford to [HIGH — newamerica.org]
- 24% of working lower-income parents shifted to gig/platform work for greater flexibility vs. 10% of higher-income parents [HIGH — newamerica.org]
- Over half of parents cited cost as primary barrier to ideal child care [HIGH — newamerica.org]
- 43% of moms said they struggled to meet family's basic needs "often" or "sometimes" in the past year; 34% of dads [HIGH — newamerica.org]

- Colorado Springs infant center-based care can exceed $1,200/month [HIGH — prior pipeline research Aug 24]

## STORY 2: Goody King Magnetic Building Cubes Recall

### Verified Facts (HIGH confidence)
- Product: Goody King Magnetic Building Cubes and Blocks, imported by Yi Suen Commerce [HIGH — CPSC.gov official recall page]
- Units recalled: approximately 213,500 [HIGH — CPSC.gov, ABC News, NBC26, WFLA, WSPA]
- Sold on Amazon between January 2024 and July 2026 [HIGH — CPSC.gov]
- Sets available in counts of 45, 56, 100, 120, 150, and 300 cubes [HIGH — CPSC.gov]
- Themes include forests, dinosaurs, and unicorns [HIGH — CPSC.gov summary]
- Two children required surgery to remove ingested magnets [HIGH — ABC News, WFLA, WSPA]
- Hazard: cubes can break or open, releasing high-powered magnets; if multiple magnets ingested they attract each other, can perforate intestines, cause twisting/blockage, blood poisoning, and death [HIGH — CPSC.gov]
- Remedy: stop using immediately; contact Yi Suen Commerce for full refund + prepaid shipping label [HIGH — CPSC.gov]

### Distinction from Prior Coverage
- Aug 24 S2 covered Gagaku Ice Cream Toy Playsets (magnets) + ABC Trading (battery toys) — different products from different companies. Goody King is a distinct new recall.

## STORY 5: Back-to-School Sleep Tips

### Verified Facts (HIGH confidence)
- AAP sleep recommendations for school-age children: ages 6–12: 9–12 hours/night; teens 13–18: 8–10 hours/night [HIGH — established AAP clinical practice guideline, well-documented]
- AAP recommends no screens 30-60 minutes before bedtime [HIGH — AAP policy/established guideline]
- Morning light exposure helps reset circadian rhythm [HIGH — established sleep science consensus]
- Colorado Springs elevation: approximately 6,035 feet above sea level [HIGH — established geographic fact]
- Altitude can affect sleep quality, particularly for those not acclimatized [MEDIUM — general medical knowledge; altitude sleep disruption is documented but individual effects vary]
