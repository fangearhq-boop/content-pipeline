By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | August 11, 2026

The Colorado Springs Switchbacks FC return to Weidner Field this Saturday, August 15, with a USL Championship home match against Birmingham Legion FC. Kickoff is at 7:00 PM, and tickets start at $14 — making this one of the more affordable evenings out you'll find in Colorado Springs this weekend.

For families looking for a way to cap off the first week of school without spending a lot, an evening soccer game at Weidner Field checks a lot of boxes.

Why Weidner Field Works for Families

Weidner Field is downtown Colorado Springs, which keeps the experience manageable for families who don't want to navigate a sprawling stadium complex. Parking options exist throughout the downtown area, and the venue itself is compact enough that you're not hiking across a massive lot to find your seats.

USL Championship matches tend to strike a good balance for mixed-age groups. The atmosphere is energetic — there's a genuine crowd, real competition on the field, and the kind of noise level that feels exciting without overwhelming younger kids the way some larger venues can. The mountain backdrop visible from parts of the stadium is a bonus that's hard to replicate anywhere else.

The Timing

This match falls on the first Saturday after D11 students start school on Wednesday, August 12. For families who've been heads-down with first-day prep, supply lists, new schedules, and the general adjustment of the first week back, Saturday evening at a soccer game offers a clean break.

D20 families, who don't start until August 17 and 18, are also heading into their final weekend before school — another reason this Saturday has a natural "end-of-summer / welcome-to-fall" energy across Colorado Springs.

Tickets and What to Expect

Tickets for the August 15 match are available through AXS, Ticketmaster, and other authorized outlets, starting at $14. That entry-level price makes this one of the most accessible professional sports experiences in the city.

Weidner Field typically has concessions available throughout the match, and arriving a bit before kickoff at 7:00 PM gives families time to get settled, find seats, and enjoy the pre-match atmosphere. For kids who haven't been to a live soccer match before, the combination of the ball, the crowd, and the open evening air tends to hold attention in a way that's hard to predict from a screen.
