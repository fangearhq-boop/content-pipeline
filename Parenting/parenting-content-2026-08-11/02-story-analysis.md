### STORY 4: AAP Screen Time Limits

**Tier:** 2
**Content Pillar:** National Parenting
**Posting Windows:** 3:00 PM MT (S4-A), 8:00 PM MT (S4-B)

**Key Narrative:** AAP-backed report says counting screen hours is no longer the right framework. What matters is the type and context: co-viewing, educational content, relational use vs. passive scrolling or gaming for avoidance. Dr. Munzer: "Simply taking devices away or enforcing rigid rules can backfire." New advice: model behavior, watch together, ask about what they're viewing.

**Priority 2 claims (MEDIUM):** Review covers hundreds of studies / 20 years; Munzer quote attributed to ABC News report
**Audience hook:** You've been counting hours wrong. The AAP says it's about what they're doing, not how long they're doing it — and that rigid limits backfire.

**What's Next:** Ongoing parenting issue; consider follow-up when AAP publishes formal updated policy.
