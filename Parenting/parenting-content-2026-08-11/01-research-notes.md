## STORY 1: Liuqibax Bed Rail Recall

### Verified Facts
- Product name: Liuqibax adult portable bed rails, Model BR-11 — **HIGH** (Source 1)
- Seller: Luqiba (sold as "Liuqibax" on Amazon) — **HIGH** (Source 1)
- Units recalled: About 12,000 — **HIGH** (Source 1, 2)
- Hazard: Entrapment within rail or between rail and mattress; risk of asphyxiation death — **HIGH** (Source 1)
- Additional violation: Rails lack required hazard warning labels — **HIGH** (Source 1)
- Sales channel: Amazon.com — **HIGH** (Source 1)
- Sales period: July 2024 through May 2026 — **HIGH** (Source 1)
- Price: About $60 — **HIGH** (Source 1)
- Description: Black or white, about 30.5 inches wide x 40.5 inches tall, weighs 6 pounds — **HIGH** (Source 1)
- Injuries: No injuries reported — **HIGH** (Source 1)
- Consumer action: Stop use immediately; contact Luqiba for full refund — **HIGH** (Source 1)
- Refund process: Dismantle rail, cut safety strap/storage bag, write "RECALLED" on upper and lower rails in permanent marker, email photo to Liuqibax@outlook.com — **HIGH** (Source 1)
- CPSC.gov recall page confirms this is a MANDATORY standard violation — **HIGH** (Source 1)
