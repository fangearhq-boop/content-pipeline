If you bought a Chillife 5-in-1 Montessori Baby Toy Set on Amazon at any point between July 2023 and April 2026, stop using it right now. The Consumer Product Safety Commission has recalled more than 20,000 of these popular sets over a serious choking hazard — and the remedy requires you to destroy the product before you can claim a refund.

The recall covers 20,134 units. The hazard is specific to the pull-string teether included in the set: its silicone strings are longer and thinner than federal safety standards allow, and they can extend far enough to reach the back of a child's throat. The CPSC has documented 32 incidents connected to this type of hazard.

What's in the Set

The recalled set includes five pieces: the pull-string teether (the component at the center of the recall), circular stacking blocks, a sensory toy, a shape sorting cube with blocks, and a suction cup spinner. The sets were sold on Amazon under the brand name "Chillife Official" and retailed for about $27.

The entire set is subject to the recall — not just the teether — though the teether is the component that poses the direct choking risk. Families who purchased the set should set all five pieces aside.

What To Do Right Now

The CPSC remedy for this recall is more involved than a standard "stop use and return" process. Here's what it requires, in order:

  1. Stop using the toy set immediately — especially the pull-string teether.
  2. Cut all silicone strings off the teether to render it non-functional.
  3. Write "DESTROYED" on the toy in permanent marker.
  4. Photograph the destroyed toy to document the destruction.
  5. Email the photo to the company to receive a partial refund.

Do not donate the set. Do not pass it on to another family or a secondhand store. The remedy process requires proof of destruction, and a toy passed along in good faith could end up in the hands of another child.

How To Verify Your Set

If you're not certain whether the toy set in your home is the recalled product, the CPSC recall listing is the definitive source. Visit cpsc.gov and search "Chillife Montessori" to access the full recall notice, which includes product photos and additional identifying information. The sets were sold exclusively on Amazon, so if you purchased a Chillife Montessori baby toy set through any other retailer, you're looking at a different product.

For families who received the set as a gift, it's worth checking with anyone who may have given it. The sets were popular during the holiday seasons of 2023 and 2024 given the price point and the "Montessori" branding, which has broad appeal.

What's Next

The CPSC publishes all active recalls at cpsc.gov, and their recall alert system allows families to sign up for email notifications. For Colorado Springs parents who want to stay current on product safety issues — particularly for infant and toddler toys — it's worth bookmarking the CPSC recall page or subscribing to alerts. If your child has shown any choking symptoms or difficulty swallowing that you can't explain, contact your pediatrician. For recall questions, reach the CPSC directly at 800-638-2772.
