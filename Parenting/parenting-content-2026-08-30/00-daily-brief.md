### STORY 1: Chillife 5-in-1 Montessori Baby Toy Sets Recalled — Choking Hazard Silicone Strings
**Tier:** 1 — Breaking/Trending
**Platforms:** X, Facebook, Article
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Pillar:** National Parenting / Safety

The Consumer Product Safety Commission has announced a recall of approximately 20,134 Chillife 5-in-1 Montessori Baby Toy Sets due to a dangerous choking hazard. The toy sets contain a pull-string teething toy with silicone strings that are longer and thinner than allowed by federal standards and can reach the back of a child's throat, posing a risk of respiratory distress and deadly choking. The toys were sold exclusively on Amazon from July 2023 to April 2026 for about $27.
