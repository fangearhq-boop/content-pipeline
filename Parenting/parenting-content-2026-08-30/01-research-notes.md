### STORY 1: Chillife 5-in-1 Montessori Baby Toy Sets Recall

**Sources:**
- cpsc.gov: "Montessori Baby Toy Sets Recalled Due to Risk of Serious Injury or Death from Choking; Violate Mandatory Standard for Toys; Sold on Amazon by Chillife Official"
- kiro7.com: "Recall alert: 20K Montessori baby toy sets recalled over choking hazard" — 20,134 units
- consumeraffairs.com: "Consumer product safety recall roundup for August 28, 2026" — confirmed alongside other recalls
- yahoo.com: "Baby toys sold on Amazon recalled in the US after 32 reported choking incidents" — 32 incidents
- actionewsjax.com: confirms 20,134 units, choking hazard from long silicone strings

**Key Facts (HIGH confidence):**
- 20,134 units recalled [kiro7.com, actionewsjax.com — both citing CPSC]
- Product: Chillife 5-in-1 Montessori Baby Toy Sets — includes pull-string teether, stacking blocks, sensory toy, shape sorting cube, suction cup spinner [cpsc.gov]
- Hazard: Silicone strings are longer and thinner than allowed; can reach back of child's throat [cpsc.gov]
- Where sold: Amazon exclusively, July 2023–April 2026 [cpsc.gov]
- Price: About $27 [cpsc.gov]
- Remedy: Stop use, cut all silicone strings, write "DESTROYED" on toy, photograph, email to company for partial refund [cpsc.gov]

**Key Facts (MEDIUM confidence):**
- 32 reported incidents [yahoo.com — from CPSC report]

### STORY 3: Back-to-School Anxiety

**Sources:**
- childmind.org: "Managing Back-to-School Anxiety" — predictability is best antidote; anxiety fades when routines become familiar
- hopkinsmedicine.org: "5 Tips to Ease Back-to-School Anxiety" — 5 practical tips
- geisinger.org: "Navigating Back-to-School Anxiety" (Aug 5, 2026)
- massgeneralbrigham.org: "Back-To-School Anxiety" — if anxiety persists 2-3 weeks interfering with daily life, seek professional help
- aupairinamerica.com: "Back-to-School Anxiety: How to Help Your Child Feel Confident" (Aug 11, 2026)
- valleynewslive.com: "Experts give tips on dealing with back to school anxiety" (Aug 26, 2026)

**Key Facts (HIGH confidence):**
- Anxiety is normal at start of school year; for most children it fades as routines form [childmind.org]
- Predictability is one of the best antidotes for anxiety [childmind.org]
- Consistent bedtime, meal times, homework schedule help [childmind.org]
- Open, judgment-free conversations help [childmind.org]
- Warning signs: anxiety persisting 2-3+ weeks, interfering with daily life, sleep problems, major behavior changes [massgeneralbrigham.org]
- Calm coping techniques: deep breaths, muscle squeezes [geisinger.org]
