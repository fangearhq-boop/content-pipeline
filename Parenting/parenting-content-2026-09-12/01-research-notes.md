## Story 1: Aila+Aiden Baby Lounger CPSC Warning

**Verified Facts (HIGH confidence):**
- CPSC issued a warning (not a recall — company is out of business) to stop using Aila+Aiden Baby Loungers immediately (cpsc.gov)
- Model No: Nest1; "Aila+Aiden," "Baby Lounger" and "Model No: Nest1" printed on a label stitched on the outside
- Product sold on Amazon.com from December 2023 through June 2026 for approximately $46 (cpsc.gov)
- Hazards: sides too low to contain an infant; foot opening openings too wide (wider than allowed); no stand — creating fall and entrapment hazards (cpsc.gov)
- Violates mandatory standard for infant sleep products (cpsc.gov)
- Aila+Aiden is out of business — no refunds available (cpsc.gov, Medical Daily)
- Required consumer action: stop using immediately; remove foam padding; cut cover in half; dispose (cpsc.gov)
- Do not sell or give away (cpsc.gov)
- Sold by Amazon.com and potentially other third-party sellers (cpsc.gov)

**MEDIUM confidence:**
- May have been sold in other colors/prints not shown in official CPSC photos (Medical Daily — "Federal Baby Lounger Warning Covers Colors and Prints the Official Photo Never Showed")
