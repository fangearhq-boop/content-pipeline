CPSC Warns Parents to Destroy Aila+Aiden Baby Loungers — No Refund Available (TIER 1)
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post **Window:** 10:00 AM MT If you have an Aila+Aiden Baby Lounger in your home, the Consumer Product Safety Commission wants you to stop using it right now. The CPSC issued a safety warning this week against the lounger (Model No: Nest1), which was sold on Amazon from December 2023 through June 2026 for about $46. The problem: the sides are too low to safely contain an infant, the openings at the foot are wider than allowed by mandatory infant sleep product standards, and there is no stand — creating serious fall and entrapment hazards. Here is what makes this situation harder than most recalls: the company, Aila+Aiden, is out of business. There is no refund available. If you have this product, you need to remove the foam padding, cut the cover in half, and throw it away. Do not sell it. Do not donate it. Destroy it. The product label reads "Aila+Aiden," "Baby Lounger," and "Model No: Nest1" on a tag stitched to the outside of the cover. Full details at cpsc.gov/Recalls. Have you seen this product in your area? If so, share this post so other Colorado Springs families can check their homes. --- ### Image Post Caption **Window:** 10:30 AM MT CPSC safety warning: Aila+Aiden Baby Loungers (Model Nest1) must be destroyed immediately. Pikes Peak Regional Airshow — COS's Biggest Family Aviation Event Returns Next Weekend (TIER 1)
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post **Window:** 5:00 PM MT The Pikes Peak Regional Airshow is back next weekend, and it is worth planning for now. The event runs Saturday and Sunday, September 19-20, 2026, at the Colorado Springs Airport at 7330 Embraer Heights. Gates open at 8:30 AM and aerial performances run from 10:50 AM to 2:25 PM both days. The show includes WWII warbirds, modern military jets, precision aerobatics, kid zones, veteran recognition, and static displays of vintage and active-duty aircraft. Parking is free. Here is the critical detail for Colorado Springs families: tickets are only available online. They are not sold at the gate. And the airshow has sold out in previous years. Adult tickets start at $53.20; children start at $42.18. Preferred seating is $80.75, and a VIP Canteen Experience (which includes food, drinks, and shade) runs $204.17. Tickets are available at pprairshow.org. This is a great one for families with kids who are into planes, history, or just want a full day outdoors next weekend. Are your kids into aviation? September Rhythm — How COS Families Can Lock In a Fall Routine One Month In (TIER 2)
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post **Window:** 12:00 PM MT About one month in. How are the routines holding? All three Colorado Springs school districts are well into the 2026-27 school year now — D49 started August 3, D11 August 12, and D20 August 17. That means families are past the honeymoon phase and settling into what actually works (or doesn't) for their household. Month one is often where things start to slip. The kids are tired. The novelty of the new school year has worn off. The schedule that looked clean in August is running up against reality. That is completely normal, and it is also a good moment to reset. A few things that tend to help: a consistent wake time (even on weekends) matters more than a strict bedtime, because sleep timing is what drives morning energy. After-school decompression time before homework — even 20 minutes — tends to improve focus and reduce conflict. D11 families: your Monday late starts are built into the calendar and are a good reset point for the week. October is coming, and with it, parent-teacher conferences across all three districts. The routines you build this month will set the tone for that conversation. What has worked for your family this fall? Colorado Springs September Outdoor Guide: Your Family's Last Big Outdoor Weekend (TIER 2)
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post **Window:** 3:00 PM MT September might be Colorado Springs' best month for outdoor family time, and most of it is free. The average high this month is around 74 degrees, with only about three rainy days. The summer crowds have thinned, and the trails are still fully accessible. It's the sweet spot before October brings shorter days and colder temperatures. Where to take the family this month: **Garden of the Gods:** Paved and unpaved trails, open from sunrise to 10 PM, free. The rock formations are incredible any time of year but hit different in September light. **America the Beautiful Park:** Free access to the Quirks interactive art installation, hammocks, spinner bowls, and open green space. **North Cheyenne Cañon Park:** Free, with trails ranging from easy walks to challenging climbs. Waterfall views are worth the walk. And coming up: - Dahlia Days: Sept 12 and 19 at Gather Mountain Blooms ($10 admission) - Pikes Peak Regional Airshow: Sept 19-20 at COS Airport (tickets online at pprairshow.org) Colorado Springs families, what is your go-to September outdoor spot?
