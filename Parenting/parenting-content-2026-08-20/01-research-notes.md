### Story 5: AAP NICU/Parental Mental Health
- AAP Pediatrics, Volume 158, Issue 2, August 2026
- Publication: "The Importance of Addressing Parental Mental Health for Children Born Preterm"
- Key finding: Parental mental health (anxiety, stress) significantly impacts outcomes for preterm children; integration of mental health support in NICU care recommended
- AAP recommendation: Pediatric medical homes should address parental mental health proactively
- COS NICU resources: UCHealth Memorial Hospital NICU, Centura St. Francis Medical Center NICU
