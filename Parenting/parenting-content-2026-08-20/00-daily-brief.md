### STORY 2: Product Safety Alert — 3 Recalls COS Parents Must Check Now
**Tier:** 1 | **Byline:** Jamie Rivera | **Pillar:** National Parenting
CPSC August 2026 recalls requiring COS parent action: (1) PIXLABBY silicone magnetic fidget sliders — recalled after a child swallowed the magnets, which can cause intestinal perforations, blockages, blood poisoning and death; sold on Amazon. (2) Wnttmt baby walkers (about 1,310 units) — can fit through a standard doorway and fail to stop at stair edges, sold on Amazon. (3) Michley Children's Pajamas sold on SHEIN.com — fail mandatory flammability standards, creating a burn injury risk. All three recalls have stop-use advisories; parents should check cpsc.gov for full details.

### STORY 5: AAP on NICU Parental Mental Health — What New Research Means for Colorado Springs Families
**Tier:** 2 | **Byline:** Sarah Morales | **Pillar:** National Parenting
The American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) published new research in August 2026 (Pediatrics Vol. 158, Issue 2) on the importance of addressing parental mental health for children born preterm. The research highlights anxiety and mental health challenges faced by parents of NICU babies, and calls for family partnership in neonatal care. COS families with NICU experience can connect with UCHealth Memorial Hospital and Centura St. Francis hospital resources; the AAP recommends parents proactively ask their pediatrician for a mental health referral if NICU trauma is affecting them.
