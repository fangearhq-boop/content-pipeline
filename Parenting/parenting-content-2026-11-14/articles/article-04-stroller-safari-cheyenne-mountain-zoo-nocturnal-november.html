Sunday Morning at the Zoo: Stroller Safari Nocturnal November at Cheyenne Mountain Zoo

By Jamie Rivera | November 14, 2026 | COS Parenting

If you're looking for something to do with a toddler or baby tomorrow morning, the Cheyenne Mountain Zoo has a program designed exactly for the stroller set. Stroller Safari: Nocturnal November runs Sunday, November 15, starting at 9 AM. It's one of the zoo's regularly scheduled programs for families with young children who aren't yet ready for a full-day zoo experience — shorter, guided, and built around animal encounters that work well for the under-3 crowd.

What Stroller Safari Is

Stroller Safari programs at the Cheyenne Mountain Zoo are guided visits designed for infants and toddlers with caregivers. The November edition is themed around nocturnal animals — the zoo features exhibits with animals that are most active at night, and the Nocturnal November programming is built around that. For young kids who can't process a full multi-hour zoo visit, this format works: shorter sessions, age-appropriate educational content, and hands-on elements that hold short attention spans.

The 9 AM start time is worth noting. Zoo mornings are quieter than afternoons, the animals tend to be more active in the cooler morning hours, and stroller navigation is easier when the crowds haven't built up yet. For families with kids who nap in the late morning, it's also a good window to get out and back before that schedule gets disrupted.

Timing and What to Know Before You Go

The Cheyenne Mountain Zoo is at 4250 Cheyenne Mountain Zoo Rd., Colorado Springs. Standard zoo admission applies — check the zoo's website at cmzoo.org for current pricing and whether Stroller Safari requires separate registration or is included with admission. Programs like this sometimes fill quickly, so confirming your spot before Sunday morning is worth the two minutes it takes.

Dress for cool weather — it's mid-November in COS, and the zoo is at elevation. If you're bringing a stroller, the zoo's pathways are paved and stroller-friendly throughout the main areas. Layers on the kids, snacks for the stroller set, and you're set for a solid Sunday morning.

The Bigger Picture: Zoo Before the Holiday Season Begins

The Cheyenne Mountain Zoo's holiday programming is coming soon. When it kicks in, the zoo's weekend foot traffic increases significantly. This Sunday, November 15, is one of the last quieter weekend mornings before that shift. If you've been meaning to get to the zoo this fall and haven't made it, tomorrow is a natural window.

What's Next

The Cheyenne Mountain Zoo's holiday lights season and electric events are on the near horizon for COS families — keep an eye on cmzoo.org for dates as they're announced. For families with young children, Stroller Safari programs run on a regular schedule through the winter months as well. If Nocturnal November works for your family, it's worth bookmarking the zoo's program calendar for the December and January editions. COS Parenting will post updates as holiday-season zoo programming is confirmed.
