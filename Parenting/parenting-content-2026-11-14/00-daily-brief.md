### STORY 3: Taleco Gear Baby Jumpers and Swings Recalled — 41,100 Units, Fall and Strangulation Hazard
**Tier: 1 | Type: NEW (National Parenting — CPSC Recall)**
- **Posting window:** 12:00 PM MT

The Consumer Product Safety Commission recalled approximately 41,100 Taleco Gear baby jumpers and infant swings (recall #26-688, issued August 13, 2026). The products were sold on Amazon, Walmart, and TalecoGear.com from April 2021 through July 2026. The recalled items pose fall, impact, and strangulation hazards. CPSC received more than 52 incident reports and at least 32 injuries, including head bumps from falls. Consumers should immediately stop using the products and contact Taleco Gear for a full refund — the item must be destroyed, not donated or resold.

### STORY 5: What to Actually Buy Kids for the Holidays — Age-Appropriate Toys and What Actually Works
**Tier: 2 | Type: NEW (Evergreen)**
- **Posting window:** 5:00 PM MT

With holiday shopping underway and multiple baby product recalls this season, it's worth revisiting what actually makes a good toy purchase for young kids. Age range labels matter more than most parents realize — products designed for kids 3+ are tested for small-part safety at that threshold, not younger. For infants and toddlers, look for large-piece construction, no strings or cords over 12 inches, and materials that can be sanitized. For school-age kids, open-ended items like building sets, art supplies, and board games consistently outperform newer alternatives for sustained engagement. The simplest test: if you can't describe what a child actually does with it, skip it.
