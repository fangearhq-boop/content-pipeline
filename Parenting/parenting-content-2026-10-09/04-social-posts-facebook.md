### STORY 3: SHEIN Spiral Toys CPSC Recall

#### Long-Form Post — 10:30 AM MT

If you ordered any spiral or coil toys from SHEIN between last September and this past May, check your order history.

The Consumer Product Safety Commission issued a recall on September 3, 2026, for SHEIN Spiral Toys — about 963 units sold on SHEIN.com for roughly $6-$16. The problem: small ball components can detach from the toy, which is a choking hazard for children under 3 years old and violates the federal small parts ban.

No injuries have been reported, but the remedy is clear: stop using the toy immediately. SHEIN is offering a full refund.

To get your refund, contact SHEIN directly:
📞 833-853-8668
📧 uscsteam@shein.com

If you've already tossed the toy, still reach out — they can typically process a refund with your order number.

Safety recall info is worth sharing with other parents, especially since these were sold online and may be in homes across Colorado Springs. Tag a parent who shops on SHEIN. ⚠️

### STORY 5: Where to Get Flu Shots in Colorado Springs

#### Long-Form Post — 3:00 PM MT

Yesterday we shared the AAP's recommendation to get kids vaccinated against flu by the end of October. Today let's make it practical: where do you actually go in Colorado Springs?

Colorado had five pediatric flu deaths in the 2025-26 season, and the state's child vaccination rate last year was about 34% — well below the national average. Getting ahead of it now matters.

Here are your local options:

**Penrose-St. Francis Health Services** — Multiple COS locations, walk-in flu clinics. Call (719) 776-5000 to find the location nearest you.

**UCHealth Memorial Hospital** — Flu shot clinics at multiple locations. Check uchealth.org for current availability.

**Walgreens, CVS, Safeway Pharmacy** — Walk-in shots for most kids ages 4 and up (some 3+ with a note). Most take insurance; out-of-pocket is usually under $20 without coverage.

**El Paso County Public Health** — Free or low-cost flu vaccines for families without insurance. Call (719) 578-3199.

**School-based clinics** — D11 and D49 schools sometimes run flu shot days — watch for flyers from your school nurse.

A few tips: schedule kids and adults together to make one trip count, and ask about the nasal spray option (FluMist) if your child is needle-averse — it's approved for healthy kids ages 2-49.
