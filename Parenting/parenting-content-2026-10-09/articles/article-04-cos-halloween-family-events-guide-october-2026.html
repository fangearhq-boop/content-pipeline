Halloween Family Events in Colorado Springs — October 2026 Guide

By Sarah Morales | October 9, 2026

Colorado Springs, Colorado

October is a strong month for family events in Colorado Springs, and Halloween programming kicks off this weekend with several options running through the end of the month. Whether you're looking for something free and low-key or a full costumed outing with the kids, the calendar has enough options to fill multiple weekends before Halloween arrives.

KOA Colorado Springs Halloween Weekends

The KOA campground in Colorado Springs is running Halloween-themed weekends this month, starting with this weekend, October 9-11. The campground opens up its Halloween activities to both campers staying on-site and day visitors. Events include trick-or-treating throughout the grounds, a heated pool, costume contests, and fall-themed activities for kids.

This is one of the better options for families with younger children who want a structured, low-stakes Halloween experience without the intensity of a haunted attraction. The heated pool makes it an easy sell even for kids who aren't Halloween-obsessed. A second weekend of events runs October 16-18.

Check the KOA Colorado Springs website for day visitor pricing, hours, and any registration requirements.

Pumpkins in the Park — October 24 at Bancroft Park

Colorado Springs Parks and Recreation hosts Pumpkins in the Park on Saturday, October 24, at Bancroft Park in Old Colorado City. The event runs from noon to 6 pm and is free to attend. Activities include pumpkin decorating, fall-themed games, and family activities in one of Colorado Springs' most walkable neighborhood parks.

Bancroft Park is located at 2600 West Colorado Avenue. The Old Colorado City neighborhood around the park has independent shops and restaurants if you want to extend the outing. Parking is available along Colorado Avenue and on surrounding streets.

This event is well-suited for families with children in the elementary range — young enough to love it, old enough not to need a stroller in a park setting.

Trunk or Treat at Garden of the Gods — October 25

The Garden of the Gods Visitor and Nature Center hosts a Trunk or Treat event on Sunday, October 25. The event is family-friendly and takes place during daytime hours. The backdrop — red sandstone formations against October blue sky — makes this one of the more visually striking settings for a Halloween event in the region.

Admission to Garden of the Gods itself is always free, though the Trunk or Treat event at the visitor center may have its own logistics. Check the Garden of the Gods website or call the visitor center ahead of time to confirm times and any participation details.

Boo at the Zoo — Cheyenne Mountain Zoo

Cheyenne Mountain Zoo runs Boo at the Zoo across multiple October weekends. The event includes trick-or-treating stations throughout the zoo, seasonal enrichment activities for the animals, and a costumed atmosphere that works well for younger children who find full-scale haunted attractions too intense.

Boo at the Zoo requires tickets beyond regular zoo admission. Cheyenne Mountain Zoo is located at 4250 Cheyenne Mountain Zoo Road in Colorado Springs. Check the zoo's website for specific dates, ticket prices, and age recommendations for the different elements of the event.

What's Next

Halloween falls on a Saturday this year, so the last weekend of October doubles as the main event. KOA's final Halloween weekend may run October 23-25 — check their schedule for confirmation. For families wanting even more options, the Colorado Springs area has neighborhood trunk-or-treat events organized through schools, churches, and community organizations throughout the month — watch your school's communications and neighborhood Facebook groups for local listings.
