D49 Fall Break Starts Sunday — D11 Break is October 19: What COS Families Need to Know

By Sarah Morales | October 9, 2026

Colorado Springs, Colorado

Falcon School District 49 families have three days until fall break begins. Break starts Sunday, October 12, with students returning to school on Monday, October 20. For D11 families in Colorado Springs, the wait is a little longer — fall break runs October 19-23, with students back on Monday, October 26.

That staggered timing means the two largest Colorado Springs-area districts won't be on break at the same time, which has practical implications for families with kids in different schools and for local childcare availability. If you're looking at childcare or camps for fall break, availability can fill faster than expected — particularly for D49, where Sunday is the start.

Planning Ideas for Fall Break in Colorado Springs

October is one of the best months to be in Colorado Springs, and fall break aligns with peak fall color season in the region. A few options within easy reach:

The Garden of the Gods is free and reaches peak foliage in mid-October. The visitor center has year-round exhibits and ranger-led programs, and the rock formations are as striking in fall as any season. For families who want more structure, the Nature Center offers programs for elementary-age kids.

The Cheyenne Mountain Zoo runs Boo at the Zoo on multiple October weekends, including during D49's break. Tickets are required beyond regular admission; check the zoo's website for times and pricing.

The Royal Gorge is about 45 minutes from Colorado Springs and offers dramatic canyon views from the bridge and the gondola. It's a good half-day trip for families with kids who are past the toddler stage.

For a lower-key week, the COS park system has dozens of trails and playgrounds that are rarely crowded in October. Monument Valley Park along Monument Creek is walkable from many central Colorado Springs neighborhoods and is one of the city's underrated fall spots.

Childcare During Fall Break

If fall break means a childcare gap, a few Colorado Springs options worth checking:

The YMCA of the Pikes Peak Region runs school break camps at multiple locations. Registration fills, so call or check online now if you need spots. The Boys and Girls Club of the Pikes Peak Region also runs day programs during school breaks; eligibility requirements vary by site.

Some D11 and D49 elementary schools coordinate informal playdate networks through their parent organizations — check your school's Facebook group or contact your school's parent-teacher organization if you need referrals to other families in the same situation.

What's Next

Both breaks land during prime October weather in Colorado Springs. If the family trip is still unplanned, there's still time to pull something together — whether that's a drive up the Pikes Peak Highway before it closes for the season or a few days exploring the trails and parks that don't require a reservation. Check with local museums, too: the Western Museum of Mining and Industry and the Colorado Springs Fine Arts Center at Colorado College often have school break programming.
