## STORY 3: SHEIN Spiral Toys CPSC Recall

**Sources searched:** CPSC.gov recall database
**Findings (from CPSC recall Sept 3, 2026):**
- Product: SHEIN Spiral Toys (also described as coil/spring toys)
- Units recalled: approximately 963
- Hazard: Small ball components detach from the toy, violating the federal small parts ban (16 CFR 1501)
- Hazard type: Choking hazard for children under 3 years old
- Sold on: SHEIN.com
- Price range: approximately $6–$16
- Sale period: September 2025 through May 2026
- Remedy: Full refund. Stop use immediately.
- Consumer contact: 833-853-8668 or uscsteam@shein.com
- No injuries reported at time of recall
- SHEIN is cooperating with CPSC

**Confidence:** HIGH (official CPSC recall record; phone number and email verified)

## STORY 5: Flu Shots in Colorado Springs

**Sources searched:** Colorado CDPHE, AAP flu guidelines 2026-27, Penrose-St. Francis Health Services, UCHealth, local pharmacy info
**Findings:**
- AAP 2026-27 recommendation: flu vaccine for all children 6 months and older; get vaccinated by end of October for best protection
- Colorado 2025-26 season: 5 pediatric deaths from influenza (CDPHE data); 34.4% child vaccination rate (below national average of ~52%)
- 2026-27 flu vaccine: updated formulation to match circulating strains; both flu shot and nasal spray (FluMist) available; FluMist approved for healthy kids 2-49
- **COS locations:**
  - Penrose-St. Francis Health Services: multiple locations, walk-in flu clinics; call (719) 776-5000 for locations/hours
  - UCHealth Memorial Hospital: flu shot clinics; check uchealth.org/flu-shot
  - Walgreens, CVS, Safeway pharmacy: walk-in flu shots for ages 4+ (some 3+ with prescription); most take insurance
  - El Paso County Public Health: free/low-cost flu vaccines for uninsured families; (719) 578-3199
  - D11, D49 schools may run school-based flu clinics — check school nurse communications
  - University of Colorado at Colorado Springs (UCCS): community flu clinics fall 2026 for families of students/staff and public
- Best practice: schedule both kids and adults together; takes about 15 minutes

**Confidence:** MEDIUM (specific clinic hours/availability change; recommend parents call ahead)
