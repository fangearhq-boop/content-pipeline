Cool Science Festival Kicks Off Today with Free Carnival Day at UCCS

By Sarah Morales October 3, 2026

What's Happening Today

The Cool Science Festival opens its 2026 run this morning with Carnival Day, a free, hands-on science event at the University of Colorado Colorado Springs. The event runs from 10 AM to 4 PM at the UCCS campus, located at 1420 Austin Bluffs Pkwy, and it's free for families with children in grades kindergarten through eighth grade.

Past years have drawn more than 8,000 attendees to Carnival Day, which features activity stations hosted by local schools, colleges, and science organizations from across Colorado Springs and the broader region. Kids rotate through experiments and demonstrations that cover everything from chemistry and biology to engineering challenges and space science.

There's no pre-registration required. Families can show up anytime between 10 AM and 4 PM and explore the activities at their own pace.

About the Cool Science Festival

The Cool Science Festival is a Colorado Springs tradition that transforms venues across the city into science learning environments every October. Carnival Day is the flagship event that kicks off the two-week festival, which runs through October 18, 2026.

The festival brings together UCCS, Colorado College, local school districts, and dozens of community science organizations. Individual events throughout the festival cover different themes and age ranges — some are ticketed, many are free. Carnival Day is the most family-broad event of the run, with activities designed to be accessible for younger elementary-aged children through middle school students.

The festival's goal is to make hands-on science accessible to the entire Colorado Springs community, which is why free admission on the opening day is by design. Organizers have noted that reaching families who might not otherwise encounter science programming is central to the mission.

Planning Your Visit

UCCS is on the northeast side of Colorado Springs, easily accessible from both north and south. The main campus entrance on Austin Bluffs Pkwy will direct visitors toward festival parking areas. Expect a crowd given past attendance numbers, so arriving closer to the 10 AM opening or after 1 PM may help avoid peak congestion.

The event is outdoors, so Colorado fall weather applies. October mornings in Colorado Springs can be cool before warming by midday. Layers are a smart call, especially for younger kids who tend to slow down when they get cold. Comfortable walking shoes are recommended since the festival grounds cover multiple campus areas.

Food options may be limited on-site, so packing a lunch or snacks is a good idea for families planning to stay through the afternoon. Water bottles are always worth bringing.

What's Next

Today's Carnival Day is just the beginning. The Cool Science Festival continues through October 18, 2026, with additional events scheduled across Colorado Springs at venues including local schools, UCCS, and Colorado College. For a full schedule and to find events designed for specific age groups, visit coolscience.org.

Some upcoming festival events are ticketed and may require advance registration, so checking the schedule early is worthwhile if you want to attend events beyond today's free Carnival Day.
