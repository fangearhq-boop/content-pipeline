D11 Day at Air Force vs. Navy: Families Invited to Falcon Stadium Today

By Sarah Morales October 3, 2026

The Game and the Invitation

Colorado Springs' D11 school district is participating in D11 Day at today's Air Force vs. Navy football game at Falcon Stadium on the USAF Academy campus. Kickoff is at 10:00 AM MT, and the game is broadcast on CBS for families watching from home.

The matchup is the opening game of this season's Commander-in-Chief's Trophy series — the annual competition among Air Force, Army, and Navy that's been played since 1972. It's one of the most tradition-rich rivalries in college football, and it takes place right here in Colorado Springs.

Tickets are available through the Air Force Athletics website starting at $73. Families looking for a fall morning outing can be seated and back home for lunch — with the early kickoff, it's a manageable game-day experience for kids of most ages.

About the Commander-in-Chief's Trophy

The Commander-in-Chief's Trophy is awarded each season to the service academy with the best head-to-head record against the other two. Air Force, Army, and Navy each play the other two in a round-robin series, with the trophy going to whoever accumulates the best record — or in the case of ties, decided by tiebreaker criteria.

The series dates back to 1972 and reflects the unique character of the three academies, each training future military officers while fielding Division I football teams. Playing at Falcon Stadium, which sits on the Academy grounds north of Colorado Springs with Pikes Peak visible in the distance, gives the game a setting that's hard to replicate anywhere else in college football.

Navy won the most recent Air Force vs. Navy head-to-head matchup 34-31, which adds some competitive urgency to today's game from the Air Force side. Army plays both Air Force and Navy later in the season, so today's result will matter for the final trophy standings.

Attending with Families

Falcon Stadium is a family-friendly venue with good sight lines throughout. The 10 AM kickoff means the stadium will be filling up by 9 AM or earlier for parking and entry. The USAF Academy campus requires all visitors to follow access procedures — check goairforcefalcons.com for current gate and entry guidelines before you arrive, especially if you're bringing children.

October mornings at the Academy elevation can start cool and warm up quickly by late morning. Layers are useful. The game typically runs about three hours, so plan for return traffic in the early afternoon.

For D11 families, today is a designated D11 Day, which may include specific recognition, discounts, or activities for district community members — check d11.org or the Air Force Athletics ticket page for any D11-specific details before heading out.

What's Next

If you can't make it to the stadium, today's Air Force vs. Navy game is live on CBS at 10:00 AM MT. The Commander-in-Chief's Trophy series continues later this fall with Army games against both Air Force and Navy — the full trophy race won't be settled until all three matchups are complete.

For tickets, stadium information, and the full Air Force football schedule, visit goairforcefalcons.com.
