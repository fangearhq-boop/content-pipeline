  - Products: Personalized baby bibs and stroller bags
  - Hazard: Snap attachment can detach, creating choking hazard — risk of serious injury or death
  - Sold on: Etsy.com

  - Hazard: Burn hazard — products violate mandatory federal flammability standards for children's sleepwear
