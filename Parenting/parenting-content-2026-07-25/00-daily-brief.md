### STORY 1: Two CPSC Recalls to Know This Weekend — Peony Design Bibs and Bellabu Bear Robes

**Tier:** 1
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Posting window:** 8:00 AM MT (safety first)

The CPSC has announced two separate recalls relevant to Colorado Springs families this month. Peony Design is recalling personalized baby bibs and stroller bags after the snap attachment can detach, creating a choking hazard with risk of serious injury or death. The products were sold on Etsy.com from October 2025 through July 2026, priced $15–$45; consumers should remove them from children immediately and contact Peony Design for a full refund. Bellabu Bear is separately recalling children's robes due to a burn hazard — the products violate mandatory standards for children's sleepwear. Both recalls are active and warrant immediate parent attention.

**Key facts:**
- Peony Design: snap detaches → choking hazard; sold Etsy Oct 2025–Jul 2026; price $15–$45; full refund
- Bellabu Bear: burn hazard; violates children's sleepwear standards; stop using immediately
- Both recalls: active CPSC listings
- Source: CPSC.gov (cpsc.gov/Recalls)
