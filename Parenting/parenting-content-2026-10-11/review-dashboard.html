Avengers Infinity Gauntlet Medal Recall — Dangerous Magnets
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post If your kid is into virtual racing challenges, heads up on a recall you'll want to know about. The Conqueror recalled approximately 476 Avengers Infinity Gauntlet licensed medals on October 8th. The medals contain high-powered magnets that can detach from the product — and magnet ingestion is one of the more serious child safety hazards out there. When two or more magnets are swallowed, they can attract through intestinal walls and cause tears, blockages, or worse. The CPSC noted three reports of magnets detaching, and no injuries so far. The medal is gold-colored with colorful gems, sold in a black package marked "Marvel Avengers." It was available through The Conqueror's website and app from March through May 2026 for around $50. If you have one: stop using it and keep it away from kids. You can get a full refund or a replacement medal by contacting The Conqueror at 833-799-0368 (Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM ET) or through the recall page at theconqueror.events/recall-inf. Has your family done any of The Conqueror's virtual race challenges? Would you have known to check the recall site for a product like this? ---

Sweet Cubby Loungewear Recall — Flammable Burn Risk
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post If you've shopped Shein for kids' loungewear, this is one to check. The CPSC recalled approximately 12,461 Sweet Cubby children's loungewear sets on September 11th. The two-piece sets — a short-sleeved shirt with matching shorts — were sold on Shein.com from June 2025 through May 2026 for $8 to $12. They fail the federal flammability standard for children's sleepwear, which means they pose a burn risk. The recalled sets are in sizes 5-6Y through 13-14Y. Check the neck and seam labels for batch number 272779 — that's the identifier for the recalled product. What to do: stop using the sets and keep them away from your kids. Email SweetCubby@163.com to request a full refund. You'll need to cut the sets in half and send a photo of the destroyed product before they process it. No injuries have been reported, but the sizing range covers a lot of families. If you're not sure whether you have this product, it's worth digging through the kids' drawer. Have you run into any Shein clothing recalls before? This is one where the price point makes the risk easy to miss. ---

Halloween Safety Checklist for COS Families
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Two weeks to Halloween, which means it's a good time to run through the checklist before the rush of the last weekend. Colorado Springs Police and the Red Cross put out similar guidance every year, and honestly the reminders are worth it — kids are more than twice as likely to be hit by a car and killed on Halloween than on any other day of the year. That stat is real and worth sharing. Here's the checklist we use: 🔦 **Visibility first.** Dark costumes need glow sticks, flashlights, or reflective add-ons. Reflective tape on bags costs almost nothing. Face makeup instead of masks helps kids see around them and cars see them. 🗺️ **Plan the route.** Know where you're going before you leave. Stick to familiar neighborhoods. Set a time to be home if your older kids are going with a group. 🚗 **Watch the streets.** Cross at corners and look both ways — even in a neighborhood with low traffic. Drivers in Colorado have been urged to slow down through residential areas on Halloween night. 🚫 **Never go inside.** Candy comes from the door. That's the rule, even if someone invites them in. 🍬 **Inspect candy before eating.** Anything unwrapped or homemade goes in the trash, full stop. What's the most important safety tip you actually make your kids follow every year? ---
