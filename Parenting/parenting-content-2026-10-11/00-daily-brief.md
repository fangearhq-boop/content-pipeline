### STORY 3: Sweet Cubby Loungewear Recall — Flammable Burn Risk

- **Type:** National Parenting — Product Safety / CPSC Recall
- **Tier:** 2
- **Status:** NEW
- **Byline:** Jamie Rivera
- **Summary:** CPSC recalled ~12,461 Sweet Cubby children's loungewear sets on September 11, 2026. Two-piece sets (short-sleeved shirt + matching shorts) in sizes 5-6Y through 13-14Y fail the federal flammability standard for children's sleepwear — burn risk.
