Avengers Infinity Gauntlet Medal Recalled Over Dangerous Magnet Hazard

By Jamie Rivera | October 11, 2026

Product Safety | CPSC Recall

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission announced a recall of approximately 476 Avengers Infinity Gauntlet licensed medals on October 8, 2026. The medals, sold by The Conqueror through its virtual race app and events website, contain high-powered magnets that can detach from the product. Magnet ingestion is one of the most serious hazards in children's product safety — when a child swallows two or more small magnets, they can attract through the walls of the intestines, causing tears, blockages, or internal bleeding that requires emergency surgery.

The CPSC has received three reports of magnets detaching from the medal. No injuries have been reported, but the agency and The Conqueror are asking all owners to stop using the product and keep it away from children immediately.

What the Medal Looks Like

The recalled medal is gold-colored with colorful gemstone-style decorations on the front. It came in a black package labeled "Marvel Avengers." It was sold as a finisher medal for The Conqueror's virtual fitness challenges — customers complete a set distance goal and receive the medal as a reward. The product was available through The Conqueror's events website and the Conqueror App from March through May 2026, priced at approximately $50.

What to Do If You Have This Medal

The CPSC's guidance is straightforward: stop using the medal now and do not allow children to handle it. The Conqueror is offering either a full refund or a replacement medal.

To process your remedy, you'll be asked to cut the ribbon off the medal and write "RECALLED" in permanent marker on both the medal and the ribbon. Then discard the medal and email a photo of the disposed product to The Conqueror through the recall page. The company can be reached by phone at 833-799-0368, Monday through Friday from 9 AM to 5 PM ET, or online at theconqueror.events/recall-inf.

This notice also applies to medals purchased or received as gifts and set aside — if you completed a Conqueror challenge earlier this year and received this medal, check whether it's been stored somewhere accessible to children in your home.

Why Magnet Ingestion Is So Dangerous

Loose high-powered magnets are a well-documented child safety hazard. The CPSC has issued multiple warnings on this category over the years, and mandatory standards exist for a reason: children who swallow even small magnets may not show symptoms immediately, which can delay treatment. When multiple magnets are swallowed — or when a magnet is swallowed and a metallic object is already in the digestive tract — the attraction between them creates pressure on the intestinal wall that can cause perforation within hours. Surgical intervention is often required.

The danger is especially high for younger children who may not be able to explain what they swallowed, and for older children who may handle the medal as a collectible without a parent's knowledge.

What's Next

If you or someone in your family completed a Conqueror challenge this spring, check your medal. The recall covers units sold March through May 2026. Contact The Conqueror at 833-799-0368 or theconqueror.events/recall-inf to start the refund or replacement process.
