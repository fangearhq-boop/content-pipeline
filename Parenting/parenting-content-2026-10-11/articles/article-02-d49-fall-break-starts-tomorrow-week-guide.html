D49 Fall Break Starts Monday: A Week-by-Week Guide for Colorado Springs Families

By Sarah Morales | October 11, 2026

Local News | Colorado Springs Schools

If your kids are in D49, they're not going to school tomorrow. Fall break for the Falcon School District 49 starts Monday, October 12, and runs through October 19 — they're back in class on October 20. That's eight school days and two weekends, which means you've got some planning to do today if you haven't already.

D11 families have a little more time — Harrison School District 11's break runs October 19 through 23, with students returning October 26.

Free (and Almost Free) Options This Week

The Cool Science Festival is running through October 18 at venues around Colorado Springs. It's one of the better free options for families with elementary-age kids during break week, with hands-on activities and multiple locations to spread out the visits. Check the festival website for the full venue list and any registration requirements.

Garden of the Gods is free to access, as always, and mid-October is genuinely one of the best times to be on those trails. The fall color peaks around this time in the Front Range, and the crowds are lighter than summer weekends. It works for nearly every age — stroller-friendly paths near the visitor center for little ones, longer hikes toward Siamese Twins for older kids who want a challenge.

The YMCA of the Pikes Peak Region typically runs drop-in programming during school breaks. Check with your specific location for fall break hours and offerings, since the schedule varies by branch.

Tickets Required

Boo at the Zoo at the Cheyenne Mountain Zoo runs every October weekend through the end of the month. It's one of the consistently popular fall family events in COS — lights, activities, and a very manageable experience for younger kids who aren't ready for full-on Halloween intensity. Tickets sell out on popular weekends, so don't wait to book if you're aiming for this coming Saturday or Sunday.

The KOA Holiday campground in Fountain is running themed Halloween weekends all month. October 16–18 is their "Boo-tastic Science Spree" weekend, with trick-or-treating around the campgrounds, campsite decorating, and family activities. You don't have to be camping to enjoy many of the activities — check their site for day visitor options.

Coming Up at the End of October

If you're planning ahead for D11 break week (October 19–23), or just looking at the full month, a few events worth noting: Pumpkins in the Park at Bancroft Park is on October 24, free from noon to 6 PM. The Garden of the Gods Trunk-or-Treat is October 25 at the visitor center. Those fall outside D49's break but land squarely in D11's, and they're worth the drive even if you're not in D11.

The Boys and Girls Club of the Pikes Peak Region is also open during break for registered members — if your family is enrolled, check with your club location about fall break hours.

What's Next

D49 students return October 20. D11 break begins October 19 — COS Parenting will have a full week guide for D11 families closer to that date. Keep an eye on our site for event updates and any changes to local attraction schedules during break week. Enjoy the time.
