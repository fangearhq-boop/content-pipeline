Halloween Week in Colorado Springs: What's Happening October 12–18

By Sarah Morales | October 11, 2026

Local Events | Colorado Springs

D49 fall break starts Monday, and that means you've got a full week to work with. October in Colorado Springs has a lot going on — here's what's actually happening this week, broken down by cost and effort level so you can pick what fits your family.

Free: Cool Science Festival (Through October 18)

The Cool Science Festival is running through October 18 at venues around COS. It's one of the more underrated family weeks in the city — hands-on activities at multiple locations, free admission, and a range of programming that works for different ages. Check the festival website for the full venue list and any sessions that require registration.

If you have younger kids who get bored quickly, spread the visits out across a few days rather than trying to hit everything at once. The multi-venue format makes it easy to treat each location as a half-day outing.

Free: Garden of the Gods (Every Day)

The park is always free, and mid-October is one of the best times to go. Fall foliage in the Front Range peaks around this window, and the light hits the red rocks differently than in summer. Bring a stroller for the accessible paths near the visitor center, or head toward the Siamese Twins or Perkins Central Garden Trail for a longer loop with older kids. Parking can fill on weekends — arrive early or use the east entrance off 30th Street.

Tickets Required: Boo at the Zoo

Cheyenne Mountain Zoo's Boo at the Zoo runs every October weekend through October 31. It's consistently one of the most popular fall family events in COS, and it does sell out — especially for the final weekends closer to Halloween. If you're planning to go this coming Saturday or Sunday, get tickets before they're gone. The experience is designed for younger kids (think jack-o-lanterns, lights, costumed characters) and is manageable even for toddlers.

KOA Halloween Weekend: Oct 16–18 "Boo-tastic Science Spree"

The KOA Holiday campground in Fountain runs themed Halloween weekends all month, and October 16–18 is their "Boo-tastic Science Spree" theme. Activities include trick-or-treating around the campgrounds, campsite decorating, and family-friendly events throughout the weekend. You don't have to book a campsite to participate in everything — check the KOA website for day visitor options and pricing for this specific weekend.

Coming Up Later in October

A few events outside this week's window worth planning ahead for:

  • Pumpkins in the Park: October 24, Bancroft Park — free, noon to 6 PM.
  • Emma Crawford Coffin Races: October 24, Manitou Springs — a COS tradition for older kids and adults.
  • Garden of the Gods Trunk-or-Treat: October 25 at the visitor center — free.

If you have D11 kids, their fall break runs October 19–23, so those events land right in the middle of their break week.
