### STORY 1: CPSC Recalls Two Products Over Button Battery Danger

**Freshness tags:** CPSC-recall, button-battery, child-safety
**Pillar:** National Parenting | **Tier:** 1

**Summary:** CPSC recalled two products on or around August 20, 2026 — JINHEZO Mini Waterproof LED Tea Lights and OKK Trading Easter LED Headbands — because button batteries are accessible to children. A swallowed button battery can cause fatal internal burns within hours. These are separate from the Aug 20-21 baby lounger recalls.

**Relevance to COS families:** Button battery products are common in Colorado Springs homes. Both items are likely purchased through Amazon or online retailers. Parents and grandparents need to know the hazard is invisible until it's too late.

4. What to do if you find these products at home
