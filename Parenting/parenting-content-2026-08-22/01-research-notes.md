### STORY 1: CPSC Button Battery Recalls — JINHEZO + OKK Trading

**Confidence: HIGH** (CPSC.gov official, WRNJ Radio confirmed)

**JINHEZO Mini Waterproof LED Tea Lights:**
- Recalled: August 20, 2026
- Hazard: Button batteries can be accessed easily by children — poses serious ingestion risk, including death
- Violation: Mandatory consumer product safety standard for button cell/coin batteries
- Source: CPSC.gov, WRNJ Radio

**OKK Trading Easter LED Headbands:**
- Recalled for same hazard: button batteries not properly secured
- Risk of serious injury or death from battery ingestion
- Violation: Mandatory toy safety standards
- Source: CPSC.gov

**Context for article:**
- CPSC also announced recalls of infant sleepsuits, children's pajamas, hair serum, and toy building sets in this same batch
- Button batteries remain one of CPSC's top child safety concerns — a swallowed battery can burn through esophageal tissue within 2 hours
- Parents should check CPSC.gov for remedy (refund or return info)
- These are SEPARATE from Aug 20-21 baby lounger recalls already covered

### STORY 4: AAP Survey — Teens Using AI Chatbots for Mental Health

**Confidence: MEDIUM** (AAP News confirmed, specific study parameters from search summary)

- **Survey:** AAP survey of 1,000+ adolescents and young adults ages 12-21
- **Finding:** 1 in 5 (nearly 20%) had asked an AI chatbot (e.g., ChatGPT) for mental health advice
- **Published:** AAP News, August 2026
- **Context:** Study done alongside pediatrics research; AAP urging parents to have conversations about AI use for personal/health matters
