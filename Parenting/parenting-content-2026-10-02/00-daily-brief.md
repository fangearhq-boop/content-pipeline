### STORY 2: CPSC Issues Urgent Magnetic Toy Recalls — Stop Use Immediately
- **Pillar:** National Parenting
- **Type:** NEW
- **Tier:** 1
- **Headline:** CPSC Recalls Magnetic Stick Figure Sets and Magnetic Men Toys — Risk of Serious Injury or Death
- **Summary:** The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission has issued recalls for multiple magnetic toy sets due to risk of serious injury or death from magnet ingestion. Products include: GBYMIUY Magnetic Stick Figure Sets (10,750 units, sold on Walmart by BlissfulDestiny) and Vndueey Magnetic Men Toy Sets. The toys can release small, powerful magnets that attract to each other inside the digestive system, causing intestinal perforations, blockage, infection, blood poisoning, or death. Parents should immediately take these toys away from children and stop use. Contact GBYMIUY for a full refund.
- **Byline:** Sarah Morales
- **Sources:** cpsc.gov (two recall pages), playsafe.org
