Rock Ledge Ranch Harvest Festival Is This Saturday — Wagon Rides, Pumpkins, and Pie

By Jamie Rivera | October 2, 2026

COLORADO SPRINGS —

Rock Ledge Ranch Historic Site is hosting its annual Harvest Festival on Saturday, October 3, from 10 AM to 4 PM. The event is one of the more genuinely immersive fall experiences in Colorado Springs — a working 19th-century ranch setting that puts families in the middle of cider-making, blacksmithing, and horse-drawn wagon rides without the manufactured feel of a commercial pumpkin patch.

The ranch is located at 3105 Gateway Road at the base of Garden of the Gods, making it a natural stop for families who want to combine the festival with a walk through the park.

What's Happening at the Festival

Activities at the 2026 Harvest Festival include horse-drawn wagon rides, live old-time music, cider-making demonstrations, tours of historic homes on the property, blacksmith demonstrations, a candy scramble for kids, and pie on the lawn. Pumpkins are available to purchase for $6 each to take home.

The historic homes at Rock Ledge Ranch span three distinct eras — the Galloway homestead (1860s), the Chambers Ranch (1880s), and the Rock Ledge Estate (early 1900s). Staff and volunteers perform the Harvest Festival in period-appropriate settings, which gives kids an unusually concrete sense of how agricultural life looked across different generations.

Admission and Logistics

Admission is priced on a sliding scale. Children two and under get in free, which makes this accessible for families with babies and toddlers alongside older kids. What's Next

The Harvest Festival is a one-day event — Saturday, October 3 only. If you're planning your October weekend more broadly, Venetucci Farm's Pumpkin Fest runs both Saturday and Sunday this weekend and offers pumpkin picking for $5 per person (ages 8+). The Cool Science Festival continues through October 18 at multiple Colorado Springs venues. And starting later in the month, Boo at the Zoo runs October 20-22 and October 27-29 for a Halloween-themed Colorado Springs experience.
