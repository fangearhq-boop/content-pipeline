Academy District 20 Earns 18th Consecutive Top Accreditation — October Events for D20 Families

By Jamie Rivera | October 2, 2026

COLORADO SPRINGS —

Academy School District 20 has earned Colorado's highest district accreditation rating for the 18th consecutive year, the district announced this fall. The milestone is notable precisely because of its consistency — 18 years is a long track record of meeting the state's top standards for academic performance across all 40 schools and more than 26,000 students in the district.

Colorado's accreditation system measures school quality on a five-tier scale. The highest tier, which D20 holds, reflects strong academic outcomes, growth measures, and school performance across the full district. Maintaining that rating across nearly two decades — through changes in state standards, curriculum, and student population — is a genuine achievement.

What D20 Families Need to Know This Week

Today (Friday, October 2) is a busy one for Academy District 20 families. There's a 2-Hour PLC Late Start this morning, which affects drop-off schedules districtwide. The district is also running a Parent Academy session today — "From Freshman Year to Graduation" — designed for families of current or upcoming high schoolers who want to understand how to navigate the D20 college and career prep resources.

Also today: the D20 Drive-Thru Toiletry Drive runs from 11 AM to 1 PM at Academy Endeavour Elementary School. If you have unused toiletries to donate, this is a convenient drop-off option.

The October Calendar for D20

Beyond today's schedule, the month ahead includes several dates D20 families should have on their radar. Quarter 1 ends October 9 — the last day to check grades before the report card cycle closes. Parent-Teacher Conferences fall on October 13 and 14, with schools closed to students on October 15 and 16. That makes the week of October 13 a shortened school week.
