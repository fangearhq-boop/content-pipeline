### STORY 2: CPSC Magnetic Toy Recalls

**LONG-FORM POST:**
Heads up, families — this is worth checking your toy bins today.

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) has issued recalls for two magnetic toy sets this week:

1. **GBYMIUY Magnetic Stick Figure Sets** — about 10,750 units, sold on Walmart by BlissfulDestiny. These were recalled October 1, 2026.
2. **Vndueey Magnetic Men Toy Sets** — also recalled due to the same hazard.

The problem with both: the toys can release small, high-powered magnets that children can swallow. Once ingested, those magnets attract to each other inside the digestive system — causing intestinal perforations, blockage, infection, blood poisoning, or death. This isn't a warning to be cautious; it's a "stop use immediately" situation.

If you have either of these, take them away from your kids right now. Contact GBYMIUY for a full refund.

This is part of a pattern we've been tracking — magnetic toys sold through online marketplaces are increasingly the subject of recalls because they can slip through quality controls. If you have magnetic toys at home that seem oddly powerful for small pieces, it's worth a second look.

The CPSC recall pages are at cpsc.gov/Recalls.

Have you seen these toys at your house or in your kids' friend groups?

### STORY 3: Rock Ledge Ranch Harvest Festival

**LONG-FORM POST:**
If you're looking for a classic Saturday morning plan, Rock Ledge Ranch is ready for you tomorrow.

The Harvest Festival at Rock Ledge Ranch Historic Site runs Saturday, October 3, from 10 AM to 4 PM at 3105 Gateway Road. It's one of those events that feels genuinely old-fashioned in the best way — wagon rides pulled by horses, live old-time music, cider-making demonstrations, a candy scramble for the kids, and tours of the historic homes and blacksmith on site.

Here's the quick breakdown:

- Pumpkins available to take home for $6 each
- (719) 578-6777 for questions

It's genuinely a fun few hours that doesn't require a lot of planning or advance tickets — though showing up early means you'll get a wagon ride before the afternoon rush.
