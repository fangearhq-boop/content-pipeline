### STORY 2: CPSC Magnetic Toy Recalls

**Sources consulted:**
- cpsc.gov — "Magnetic Stick Figure Sets Recalled Due to Risk of Serious Injury or Death from Magnet Ingestion; Violate Mandatory Standard for Toys; Sold on Walmart by BlissfulDestiny" (GBYMIUY, 10,750 units)
- cpsc.gov — "Vndueey Magnetic Men Toy Sets Recalled Due to Risk of Serious Injury or Death from Magnet Ingestion; Violates Mandatory Standard for Toys"
- cpsc.gov — "CPSC Warns Consumers to Immediately Stop Using Vndueey Magnet Men Toy Sets..."
- playsafe.org — "Toy Recalls - 2026 Recalls"

**Verified facts (HIGH):**
- GBYMIUY Magnetic Stick Figure Sets recalled October 1, 2026 — cpsc.gov
- 10,750 units affected (GBYMIUY/BlissfulDestiny Walmart listing) — cpsc.gov
- Vndueey Magnetic Men Toy Sets also recalled — cpsc.gov
- Hazard: loose high-powered magnets that can be ingested — cpsc.gov
- Ingestion risk: magnets attract inside digestive system, causing intestinal perforations, blockage, infection, blood poisoning, death — cpsc.gov
- Action: take away from children immediately, stop use, contact for full refund — cpsc.gov
- GBYMIUY contact required for refund — cpsc.gov

**Verified facts (MEDIUM):**
- Other similar recalls also issued in 2026: Generic Magnetic Ball Sets (Ritons, Amazon), GLMZZ Fidget Magnet Ball Toys — cpsc.gov (pattern of multiple brands)
