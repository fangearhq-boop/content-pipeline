### STORY 2: CPSC November Recalls: Baby Brezza Sterilizers and Arlopu Slide Playsets
**Tier:** 1 | **Pillar:** National Parenting | **Byline:** Jamie Rivera

Two significant CPSC recalls announced November 5, 2026 affect widely sold baby and children's products. Baby Brezza SuperFast Sterilizer Dryers (~29,000 units): overheating and melting plastic housing risk; free replacement available. Arlopu children's slide playsets (~10,318 units sold on Amazon/Walmart): head and neck entrapment hazard with multiple deadly openings. Stop use immediately.

**Angles:** What to do with each recalled item; how to register; COS families who bought these on Amazon need to act.
