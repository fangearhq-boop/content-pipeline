### STORY 2: CPSC Recalls Magnetic Stick Figure Toy Sets — Loose Magnets Pose Deadly Swallowing Risk
- **Tier:** T1
- **Type:** NEW
- **Pillar:** National Parenting (Product Safety)
- **Byline:** Jamie Rivera
- **Summary:** CPSC recalled approximately 10,750 GBYMIUY Magnetic Stick Figure Toy Sets on October 1, 2026. Sold on Amazon April–July 2026 by Ningbo Jiboyou Trading Co. (GBYMIUY). Each pack contains 30 figures with small high-powered magnets in hands and feet; sets can shed loose magnets that, if swallowed, pull together inside the body causing internal perforations, intestinal blockage, blood poisoning, and death. No incidents reported. Remedy: remove from children immediately, contact zhangfa0426@outlook.com for full refund (submit photo of disposed product). Product ID "GBYMIUY" and "X004TVHD2Z" on packaging.
- **Key Facts:** ~10,750 units; sold Amazon April–July 2026 by GBYMIUY/Ningbo Jiboyou; magnets in hands/feet of 30 figures per pack; loose magnet ingestion can cause perforations, intestinal twisting, blood poisoning, death; no incidents reported at time of recall; refund: zhangfa0426@outlook.com; product ID: GBYMIUY, X004TVHD2Z

### STORY 4: Don't Wait on the Flu Vaccine — Why October Is the Deadline for Kids Under 8
- **Tier:** T2
- **Type:** NEW (Evergreen, timely October action item)
- **Pillar:** National Parenting
- **Byline:** Jamie Rivera
- **Summary:** Children ages 6 months through 8 years who are getting the flu vaccine for the first time — or who received only one dose last season — need TWO doses at least four weeks apart. To get both doses before flu season peaks (typically late October through February), families need to get the first dose now. Kids who got two doses last year and children 9 and older need only one dose. AAP recommends all children 6 months and older get the annual flu vaccine. October is the target month for first dose to ensure protection by November.
- **Key Facts:** Children 6mo–8yr: 2 doses if first time or only 1 dose last year; at least 4 weeks apart; October deadline to get both before flu season peaks; kids 9+ and those who got 2 doses last year: 1 dose; AAP recommends flu vaccine all children 6mo+; 2026-27 season; check pediatrician or pharmacy
- **Sources:** aap.org, healthychildren.org

### STORY 5: CPSC Warns: Stop Using Mbabyjon Crib Bumpers — Seller Refused to Recall
- **Tier:** T2
- **Type:** NEW
- **Pillar:** National Parenting (Product Safety)
- **Byline:** Sarah Morales
- **Summary:** CPSC issued a consumer warning (NOT a voluntary recall) on October 1, 2026 against Mbabyjon padded crib bumpers sold by Mbabyjon of Newark, Delaware. The bumpers violate the federal Safe Sleep for Babies Act ban on padded crib bumpers and can obstruct an infant's airway, posing a serious suffocation risk. The seller has NOT agreed to a recall or remedy. Families should stop using the bumpers immediately, dispose of them, and NOT sell or give them away. A firm, flat sleep surface with only a fitted sheet — no bumpers, blankets, pillows, or positioners — is the only safe option for infant sleep.
- **Key Facts:** CPSC WARNING (not recall) Oct 1, 2026; Mbabyjon crib bumpers; Newark, DE seller; violates Safe Sleep for Babies Act ban on padded crib bumpers; suffocation risk (can block airway); seller refused to recall; no remedy available; stop use immediately, dispose, do not resell; AAP safe sleep: firm flat surface, fitted sheet only
- **Sources:** cpsc.gov, healthychildren.org
