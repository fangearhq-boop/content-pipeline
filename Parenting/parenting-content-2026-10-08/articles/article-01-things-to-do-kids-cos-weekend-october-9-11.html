Things to Do with Kids in Colorado Springs This Weekend (October 9–11)

By Sarah Morales | October 8, 2026

Colorado Springs, CO

The weekend weather is cooperating and Colorado Springs has a solid lineup of free and low-cost options for families. Here's what's worth showing up for between Friday and Sunday.

Friday, October 9

Free Halloween Costume Exchange — Deerfield Hills Community Center

Kick off the weekend at the free Halloween Costume Exchange at Deerfield Hills Community Center, Friday, October 9, from 4 to 6 p.m. No registration is required. The event includes crafts, activities, games, and light refreshments. It's a straightforward way to pick up costumes for the upcoming holiday without the full retail price tag.

Saturday, October 10

National Fossil Day at Garden of the Gods — Free

The 12th Annual National Fossil Day celebration takes place at the Garden of the Gods Visitor and Nature Center (1805 N. 30th Street) on Saturday, October 10, from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. Admission is free. The event features hands-on fossil and natural history activities for all ages, giveaways, and live animals from Nature's Educators. No reservations are required.

This is one of the stronger recurring fall events in Colorado Springs for families with curious kids — the Garden of the Gods backdrop doesn't hurt either.

9th Annual FamilyFest at Soccerhaus — $5 Online, Kids 12 and Under Free

Also on Saturday: the 9th Annual FamilyFest at Soccerhaus Sports and Event Center (4845 List Drive), running noon to 5 p.m. Tickets are $5 online or $8 at the door; children 12 and under get in free. Families can explore local resources, connect with community organizations, and take in the event's broader program. It's a good one for families who want a structured, indoor option alongside the outdoor picks.

Pedal Pikes Peak — Bike-Only Morning Access

If your family bikes, Pikes Peak is running its seasonal early-access mornings this weekend. The gateway opens at 7 a.m. on Saturday and Sunday with no vehicle traffic until 9 a.m. It's a rare opportunity to ride the lower sections of the Pikes Peak Highway without sharing the road with cars. Check current Pikes Peak toll and access schedules before heading out.

All Weekend

Cool Science Festival — Through October 18

The Colorado Springs Cool Science Festival is running now through October 18. Most events are free, with activities spread across multiple venues. The festival covers science, technology, nature, and hands-on learning for kids and adults. Check the full schedule at coolscience.org for specific events happening this weekend.

Miners' Pumpkin Patch at WMMI — Every Saturday in October

The Western Museum of Mining and Industry (just north of Colorado Springs) runs its Miners' Pumpkin Patch every Saturday in October, with hayrides, food trucks, animal encounters, and pumpkin patch games. Check wmmi.org for hours and current admission pricing.

What's Next

Looking beyond this weekend: Pumpkins in the Park returns to Bancroft Park in Old Colorado City on October 24 (free). The Emma Crawford Coffin Races in Manitou Springs are also October 24. Boo at the Zoo runs through October 31 at Cheyenne Mountain Zoo.
