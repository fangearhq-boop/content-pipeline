Rock Ledge Ranch Harvest Festival 2026: What to Expect on October 3

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | September 26, 2026

One week from today, Rock Ledge Ranch Historic Site will host its annual Harvest Festival — a full-day outdoor event that's become one of the most reliable fall traditions on the Colorado Springs family calendar. It's happening Saturday, October 3, from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM at 3105 Gateway Road, at the east entrance to Garden of the Gods.

If you've never been, here's what makes it worth the trip: this isn't a commercial pumpkin patch with a parking lot. Rock Ledge Ranch is a working historic site that recreates life on the Pikes Peak frontier, and the Harvest Festival layers seasonal activities on top of that backdrop in a way that's genuinely engaging for kids and adults alike.

What's There

The day's lineup includes:

  • Wagon rides around the historic ranch property
  • Live old-time music
  • Pumpkins to take home — $5 each
  • Candy scramble for kids
  • Festival games
  • Cider-making demonstrations and a deep dive into the history of apples in the Pikes Peak Region
  • Food trucks plus pie and warm cider on the lawn
  • Tours of the historic homes on the property
  • Blacksmith demonstration
  • General Store selling ranch-made souvenirs

Count on at least three hours if you want to work through most of it — the cider history presentation alone tends to run longer than families expect because the kids get curious.

Cost and Logistics

Tickets are priced between $4 and $8 per person, with children two and under admitted free. The exact per-age pricing is available at the gate. There's no reservation required — it's a walk-up event.

Rock Ledge Ranch is located at 3105 Gateway Road, adjacent to the Garden of the Gods main entrance on the east side. Parking is available on site. The site is ADA accessible for most areas, though the historic property has some uneven terrain.

Planning Your Day

October 3 is also the opening day of the Colorado Cool Science Festival's Big Carnival Day (free, 10am–4pm, different venue). If you're trying to hit both — possible, since they run the same hours — the Harvest Festival has the better lunch/food-truck setup, while Cool Science is the better choice for a longer interactive activity session with older kids. Families with toddlers and young children tend to find Rock Ledge Ranch's pacing more manageable.

Weather in Colorado Springs in early October runs in the 60s during the day with occasional wind. Dress in layers and bring a jacket for the wagon ride.

What's Next

Check rockledgeranch.com and coloradosprings.gov for the most current event details and any weather-related updates closer to October 3. Rock Ledge Ranch also hosts other seasonal programming through the fall — if the Harvest Festival is a hit, it's worth checking what else is scheduled.

October is peak season for family outdoor events in Colorado Springs. Between the Harvest Festival, Cool Science, Gather Mountain Blooms Oktoberfest, and the Emma Crawford Coffin Races later in the month (October 24 in Manitou Springs), there's a solid lineup of low-cost, outdoor options for families through the end of the month.
