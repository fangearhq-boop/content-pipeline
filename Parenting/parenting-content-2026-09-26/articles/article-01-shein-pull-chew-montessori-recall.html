SHEIN Pull & Chew Montessori Teething Toy Recall: What Colorado Springs Parents Need to Know

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | September 26, 2026

The Consumer Product Safety Commission has issued a recall for SHEIN Pull & Chew Montessori Teething Toys, and parents who purchased these products need to act now. About 644 units are affected. The hazard is serious: the silicone strings on the toys are smaller and longer than safety standards allow, and the CPSC warns they can reach the back of a young child's throat and become lodged, causing respiratory distress and posing a deadly choking risk.

No injuries have been reported so far. That means there's still time to prevent one — but only if families stop using these products today.

Which Products Are Recalled

The recalled items are SHEIN Pull & Chew Montessori Teething Toys. They're made of silicone and come in four colors: green, pink, yellow, and blue. Each toy has four strings in varying colors. The products were sold in a clear and white plastic pouch labeled "BABY PRODUCTS" and "Crafted in Gentle Care."

They were sold exclusively on SHEIN.com from December 2025 through April 2026 for approximately $10 each.

This recall is separate from the SHEIN Spiral Toys recall issued earlier in September. If you purchased any SHEIN baby or toddler products in the past year, check each item against the description above.

What to Do If You Have These Toys

  1. Stop using them immediately. Take them away from children right away.
  2. Cut the silicone strings. The CPSC requires destruction of the toy before a refund is issued.
  3. Email productrecall@shein.com with a photo of the destroyed toy. SHEIN will issue a full refund. You do not need to mail the toy back.

This recall also applies in Canada. If you shared or gifted one of these products, let the recipient know as well.

Why Silicone String Toys Carry This Risk

The mandatory federal safety standard for toys sets specific limits on how long and how thin silicone strings on teething products can be, precisely because of the anatomy of an infant's airway. A string that passes the choking hazard test by overall size may still be dangerous if it's long and thin enough to compress and travel down the throat.

The CPSC's finding is that these specific toys violate that standard. Even toys that look similar to others on the market can differ in dangerous ways based on manufacturing tolerances.

What's Next

The CPSC recall is live at cpsc.gov — search for "SHEIN Pull and Chew" to find the official notice and bookmark it for reference. If you have questions about whether your specific product is affected, you can also contact SHEIN customer service directly through the recall email address.

For future purchases, the CPSC's recalls database (cpsc.gov/recalls) lets parents search by product type, brand, and hazard. Signing up for recall notifications means you won't have to check manually — the alerts come to you. In a house with young children, that's a five-minute setup worth doing today.
