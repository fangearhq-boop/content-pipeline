- cdc.gov/vaccines-for-children/hcp/vaccines-provided: VFC vaccine list

**Key Facts Confirmed:**
- COVID-19 vaccines added to VFC program for 2026-27 contract year
- Providers can now request vaccines through the VFC system
- CDC has NOT confirmed a shipment timeline
- VFC program provides vaccines free to: Medicaid-enrolled children, uninsured children, underinsured children (through FQHCs), and American Indian/Alaska Native children
- Estimated ~half of all U.S. children qualify for VFC
- Delay means low-income children may receive shots weeks/months after privately insured peers

**Note on AAP:** AAP's 2026-27 COVID vaccine recommendations were covered September 25. Today's angle focuses specifically on VFC access/delays, not general recommendations.
