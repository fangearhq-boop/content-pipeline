Fall 2026 Toy Recalls Colorado Springs Parents Need to Know Before Holiday Shopping Starts

By Sarah Morales | November 13, 2026

National Parenting

Holiday gifting season is here, and before you buy, wrap, or receive baby and toddler products this year, there are several Consumer Product Safety Commission recalls from this fall that you need to know about.

These aren't recalls from years ago. They're from the past few months — which means products that were sold on major online platforms and may have been gifted at baby showers or purchased as early holiday gifts are still in homes right now.

SHEIN Pull and Chew Montessori Teething Toys (Recall #26-782)

This is the highest-priority recall on the list for families with infants and young toddlers.

SHEIN Distribution Corporation recalled its Pull and Chew Montessori Teething Toys on September 17, 2026 (CPSC Recall #26-782). Approximately 644 units were sold on SHEIN.com between December 2025 and April 2026 for approximately $10.

The hazard: the silicone strings on these toys can reach a baby's throat and cause choking or respiratory distress. This is a serious risk for the age group these toys are marketed to.

If you have one, stop use immediately. SHEIN is offering a full refund. Contact SHEIN directly through their website with your order information.

HABA USA Rainbow Rattle Grasping and Teething Toys

HABA USA — a German toy brand with a strong reputation in the natural toy market — recalled its Rainbow Rattle Grasping and Teething Toys for choking and ingestion hazards. HABA products are sold through specialty toy stores, natural product retailers, and online marketplaces, and they're commonly gifted as premium baby shower gifts.

If you have a HABA Rainbow Rattle or received one recently, check the CPSC recall page for the specific models affected and the available remedy.

Costway Babyjoy High Chairs

Costway's Babyjoy high chairs were recalled for entrapment and suffocation hazards. This recall affects a different age group than the teething toys — toddlers who've moved to solid foods. Costway products are sold on Amazon and other major online platforms at budget-friendly price points, which means they're widely distributed.

If you have a Costway Babyjoy high chair, stop use and check the CPSC recall page for the specific models and available remedy.

Note: The Costway recall was confirmed through news reporting. Verify specific model numbers and remedy details directly at cpsc.gov/Recalls before taking action.

How to Check Any Product

The CPSC maintains a searchable recall database at cpsc.gov/Recalls. You can search by product name, company, or hazard type. It's worth bookmarking, especially before holiday shopping season.

A few practical steps for COS families right now:

  • Check any SHEIN baby products purchased in the last year against the recall list
  • If you've received baby shower gifts recently, cross-reference the product names and brands
  • If you're shopping for infant and toddler gifts this holiday season, check the recall database before you buy

What's Next

The CPSC issues new recalls on a rolling basis throughout the year. During the holiday season, the volume of toy-related recalls typically increases as manufacturers rush products to market. Signing up for CPSC recall alerts at cpsc.gov/Recalls will push notifications to your email when new recalls are issued — a low-effort way to stay current without having to check manually.

If you have a product that's been recalled and need help with the remedy process, the CPSC hotline is 1-800-638-2772.
