### STORY 3: Fall 2026 Baby and Toddler Toy Recalls: What Colorado Springs Parents Need to Check Right Now
**Tier: 2 | Type: NEW**
- **Posting window:** 12:00 PM MT

The Consumer Product Safety Commission has issued multiple recalls of baby and toddler toys this fall. The highest-priority: SHEIN Distribution Corporation's Pull and Chew Montessori Teething Toys (recall #26-782, September 17, 2026 — about 644 units sold on SHEIN.com from Dec 2025 through April 2026 for ~$10; silicone strings can reach the throat and cause choking). HABA USA also recalled Rainbow Rattle Grasping and Teething Toys for choking and ingestion hazards. Costway's Babyjoy High Chairs were recalled for entrapment and suffocation hazards. Parents who purchased any of these products from online marketplaces should check CPSC.gov/Recalls immediately and stop use.

### STORY 4: The AAP Just Changed Screen Time Guidelines — Here's What Colorado Springs Families Actually Need to Know
**Tier: 2 | Type: NEW**
- **Posting window:** 3:00 PM MT

The American Academy of Pediatrics updated its screen time guidance in early 2026, moving away from strict hour-based limits for older children and toward a more holistic framework. The new guidance uses a "5 Cs" model — Child, Content, Calm, Crowding out, and Communication — with the central question being whether screens are displacing sleep, outdoor time, movement, or in-person connection rather than a focus on counting minutes. For children under 18 months, the guidance still recommends avoiding screens except video chat. For ages 2-5, up to one hour of high-quality content daily. For school-age children, the focus shifts from counting minutes to evaluating what screens are replacing. This is a meaningful change for Colorado Springs parents who have been trying to enforce arbitrary hour limits.
