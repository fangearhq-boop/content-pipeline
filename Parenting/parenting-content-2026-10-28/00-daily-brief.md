### STORY 1: AAP Updates Pediatric UTI Guidelines — New Age Range, Drops Race as Factor
**Tier:** T1
**Type:** NEW (not previously covered; AAP October 2026 Pediatrics journal publication)
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Posting window:** 8:00 AM MT

The American Academy of Pediatrics published updated clinical practice guidelines for urinary tract infections (UTIs) in children in the October 2026 issue of Pediatrics. The new guidance extends the covered age range to 8 days through 5 years (expanded from the prior version) and drops race as a clinical risk factor — a significant change from older guidance. The update also revises recommendations on diagnosis, treatment, imaging, and prevention. UTIs are frequently missed in young children; these guidelines tell parents what to expect at the pediatrician. Source: AAP HealthyChildren.org, publications.aap.org/pediatrics.

### STORY 3: COS Man Hosts Sensory-Friendly Haunted Forest for Neurodivergent Kids
**Tier:** T2
**Type:** NEW (distinct from all prior Halloween stories — this is specifically about accessibility/neurodivergent families)
**Pillar:** Local News / Local Events
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Posting window:** 3:00 PM MT

KRDO published an October 28, 2026 story about a Cascade, CO man who has created a sensory-friendly Haunted Forest for children with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD), welcoming a field trip from RiseUp School — a Colorado Springs school serving students with IDD. The Haunted Forest has operated for about five years. RiseUp principal Kathy Stults praised the experience. Many RiseUp students are non-verbal; traditional Halloween settings with crowds, strobe lights, and loud noise are not accessible. This story is timely (3 days before Halloween) and speaks directly to COS families of neurodivergent children. Source: KRDO.com, October 28, 2026.
