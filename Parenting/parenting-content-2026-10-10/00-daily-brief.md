### STORY 1: D11 Bond Measure 4A — $775M on the November Ballot

- **Tier:** T1
- **Type:** Local News
- **Byline:** Sarah Morales
- **Summary:** Colorado Springs School District 11 has a $775 million bond measure on the November 3, 2026 ballot. The board voted 6-1 on August 5, 2026 to refer the measure. Funds would address safety and security upgrades, HVAC and air filtration replacement, asbestos and lead pipe removal, CTE program renovations, classroom modernization, and library renovations. The bond also prepays existing lease-purchase obligations, freeing up mill levy override funds for teacher pay. Estimated cost to homeowners: approximately $260/year (~$6.50/month per $100,000 of assessed property value). An independent citizens' oversight committee is required.
- **Key Facts:** $775M bond; November 3, 2026 ballot; board vote 6-1 Aug 5, 2026; ~$260/year typical homeowner; uses: safety/security, HVAC/air filtration, asbestos/lead, CTE, classrooms, libraries; prepays lease-purchase obligations; independent oversight committee required

### STORY 2: CPSC Recalls Two Baby Bath Seat Models — Drowning and Entrapment Risks

- **Tier:** T1
- **Type:** National Parenting / Safety
- **Byline:** Jamie Rivera
- **Summary:** Two baby bath seat models have been recalled due to serious safety hazards. The YCXXKJ Baby Bath Seats, sold on Amazon by BenTalk, were recalled for drowning and entrapment risk — they violate the mandatory federal safety standard. A second recall covers ONTHEWEI bath seats (October 8, 2026): more than 2,000 units sold on Amazon that are unstable and tip over, creating a drowning risk; no injuries have been reported for this model. Parents should stop using both products immediately and follow CPSC remedy instructions.
- **Key Facts:** YCXXKJ recall: drowning/entrapment, violates mandatory standard, sold Amazon/BenTalk; ONTHEWEI recall Oct 8 2026: >2,000 units Amazon, tips over, no injuries; both: stop use immediately, contact CPSC for remedy
- **Sources:** cpsc.gov, consumeraffairs.com (Oct 9 roundup)

### STORY 5: BabyBond Retractable Safety Gate Recalled — Entrapment Hazard

- **Tier:** T2
- **Type:** National Parenting / Safety
- **Byline:** Sarah Morales
- **Summary:** Infant Innovations dba BabyBond has recalled approximately 280 retractable safety gates (Recall #26-168, issued January 8, 2026). The black/gray/white mesh gates (model SH20.006DB) are 33 inches high and adjustable up to 55 inches wide. The hazard: a child's torso can pass through the gap between the gate and the floor, creating an entrapment risk. The gates were sold on Target.com from November 2024 through October 2025 for approximately $50. No incidents have been reported. The remedy is to stop use and return to any Target store for a full refund.
- **Key Facts:** Recall #26-168; Jan 8, 2026; Infant Innovations dba BabyBond; ~280 units; model SH20.006DB; black/gray/white mesh; 33" high, up to 55" wide; sold Target.com Nov 2024–Oct 2025 ~$50; hazard: child torso passes through floor gap; no incidents; remedy: return to any Target for full refund
- **Sources:** cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/Infant-Innovations-dba-BabyBond
