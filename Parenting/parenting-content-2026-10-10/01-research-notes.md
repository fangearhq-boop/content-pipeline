### STORY 5: BabyBond Retractable Safety Gate Recall

**Sources:**
- cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/Infant-Innovations-dba-BabyBond (official CPSC recall page)

**Verified Facts:**
- Official recall number: #26-168
- Recall issued: January 8, 2026
- Company: Infant Innovations LLC dba BabyBond, Sheridan, WY
- Units recalled: approximately 280
- Product: retractable mesh safety gate, model SH20.006DB
- Colors: black, gray, white mesh
- Dimensions: 33 inches high, adjustable width up to 55 inches
- Hazard: gap between gate bottom and floor large enough for child's torso — entrapment risk
- Sale location: Target.com
- Sale period: November 2024 through October 2025
- Price: approximately $50
- Reported incidents: none
- Remedy: stop use; return to any Target store for full refund (no receipt needed)
