D11 Fall Break Starts October 19 — A Planning Guide for Colorado Springs Families

By Sarah Morales | October 10, 2026

Local Events

Colorado Springs School District 11 students have fall break coming up in nine days. The break runs October 19–23, with kids returning to school on Monday, October 26. That's a full week to fill — and COS has plenty of options to make it worth planning ahead.

If you're in D49, you're already out — that district's break starts Sunday, October 12. D11 families have a bit more runway, which means there's still time to nail down plans before the week arrives.

COS Activities for Fall Break Week

Boo at the Zoo — Cheyenne Mountain Zoo

Cheyenne Mountain Zoo's Boo at the Zoo runs through October and is one of the most popular family events in Colorado Springs every fall. It's a great fit for kids of most ages, and the zoo's mountain setting makes the fall atmosphere feel like a bonus. Check the zoo's website for specific dates and ticket availability before you go — popular evenings can sell out.

Garden of the Gods

Garden of the Gods is free, always open, and genuinely spectacular in October. The red sandstone formations against autumn foliage and Colorado blue skies is a combination that doesn't get old. The Visitor and Nature Center has exhibits appropriate for younger kids, and the trail network accommodates everything from stroller walks to longer hikes. Parking fills up fast on weekends and holidays — early morning arrivals beat the crowds.

YMCA Drop-In Programs

Colorado Springs YMCA locations typically offer drop-in programming during school breaks. It's worth calling your nearest branch to confirm what's scheduled for fall break week — programs vary by location and can book up, especially for after-care-style structured activities.

Boys and Girls Club

Boys and Girls Club of the Pikes Peak Region offers structured programming for kids during school break periods. Check with your local club for fall break week availability and any registration requirements.

The Halloween Season Overlap

D11's fall break lands right in the middle of peak Halloween season in Colorado Springs. Several neighborhood trunk-or-treats, the Pikes Peak Library District's Halloween programming, and local parks and recreation events typically fill the last two weeks of October. It's worth scanning city and library event calendars for the week of October 19–23 — there are usually free or low-cost family options that don't require much advance booking.

What's Next

D11's official calendar is at d11.org. For Cheyenne Mountain Zoo tickets and Boo at the Zoo dates, check cmzoo.org. If you want a broader look at what's happening in COS during fall break week, Pikes Peak Library District and Colorado Springs Parks, Recreation and Cultural Services both post updated event calendars closer to the break.
