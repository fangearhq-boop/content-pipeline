CPSC Recall Alert: HAUOMS 8-Drawer Dresser Poses Tip-Over Risk to Young Children

By Jamie Rivera | November 12, 2026 | COS Parenting

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission has issued a recall for the HAUOMS 8-Drawer Dresser due to a tip-over hazard that poses a serious crush risk to young children. The recall, issued November 5, 2026, covers approximately 1,392 units sold on Amazon. If you purchased this dresser, you should stop using it immediately.

What's the Problem

The HAUOMS 8-Drawer Dresser can tip forward when one of its drawers is extended. This is a known and persistent category of furniture hazard — drawers act as a lever, shifting the dresser's center of gravity forward when pulled out, particularly if a child grabs the drawer face or climbs on an open drawer. The result is a rapid tip-over that can cause severe or fatal crush injuries to young children.

The CPSC has worked for years to address tip-over hazards in dressers and other furniture. The HAUOMS recall follows a pattern of units reaching consumers through major online retailers before tip-over risks are identified in post-market testing or incident reports.

How to Check if You Have This Dresser

The model SKU for the recalled dresser is AJ_DJ504298_1. This number is typically found on a sticker on the back or bottom of the dresser. If you purchased a HAUOMS 8-drawer dresser on Amazon in the past year for approximately $150, check the sticker before using the dresser again.

If the SKU matches, do not anchor the dresser to a wall and continue using it — the remedy in this recall is a full refund, not an anchoring fix. The structure of the dresser has been determined to present a hazard that anchoring alone does not fully address.

How to Claim the Refund

To receive the full refund, consumers should stop using the dresser immediately and email a photo of the product to recall@hauoms.com. CPSC recall page: cpsc.gov/node/67710.

Tip-Over Safety in Your Home

This recall is a good time to do a quick check of dressers and furniture throughout your home, regardless of brand. The CPSC recommends anchoring all dressers to a wall stud using an anti-tip device or furniture anchor strap — these are inexpensive and widely available. Any dresser in a child's room, or in a room children access, should be anchored. Drawers should also be kept closed when not in use.

What's Next

If you have the recalled HAUOMS dresser, the steps are straightforward: stop using it, check the SKU on the back or bottom, and email recall@hauoms.com with a photo to begin the refund process. The CPSC's full recall listing is at cpsc.gov. For other recent recalls affecting children's products, check the CPSC's recall page regularly or sign up for CPSC recall email alerts at cpsc.gov.
