Things to Do with Kids in Colorado Springs This Weekend (November 13–15)

By Sarah Morales | November 12, 2026 | COS Parenting

The weekend of November 13–15 brings a strong mix of free and low-cost options for COS families. We've got a full-weekend event at a major venue, a Saturday packed with activities across multiple locations, and the city's outdoor ice rink officially open for the season. Here's the rundown.

Colorado Springs Home Show — Free All Weekend

The Colorado Springs Home Show runs Friday through Sunday at the Norris Penrose Event Center with no admission charge. It's a surprisingly kid-friendly outing for families with curious kids who like to explore — expect booths, demonstrations, and the kind of "how does this work" conversations that keep children engaged longer than you'd expect. The event runs all three days, so you can go whenever it fits your schedule best.

Holiday Character Stroll — Saturday, Nov. 14, 11 AM–1 PM

Acacia Park hosts the Holiday Character Stroll on Saturday, November 14, from 11 AM to 1 PM. The event is free and open to all ages — holiday characters in costume will be on hand for photos and interactions. Acacia Park is at 115 E. Platte Ave in downtown COS, so it pairs well with a trip to the nearby holiday market or ice rink.

This is one of the strongest free picks of the weekend for families with young children. It runs just two hours, which keeps it manageable even with toddlers in tow.

Garden of the Gods History Day — Saturday, Nov. 14, 10 AM–2 PM

The Garden of the Gods Visitor Center hosts History Day on Saturday from 10 AM to 2 PM — also free. It's a good overlap with the Character Stroll if you want to split the day: History Day runs earlier in the morning, leaving time to get downtown for the Stroll at 11 AM. Programming typically includes hands-on activities and exhibits that connect kids to the geology and history of one of COS's most distinctive landmarks.

Anastasia Musical Youth Edition — Saturday and Sunday at ENT Center

The Anastasia Musical Youth Edition opened at the ENT Center at UCCS on Thursday and continues through the weekend. If you're looking for a ticketed performance to anchor the weekend — especially for kids who love musical theater or have read the story — this is a good fit. Tickets are available through the ENT Center box office. Check availability early, as weekend performances for youth productions at this venue tend to sell out.

Acacia Park Ice Rink — Just Opened

The Acacia Park ice rink opened Thursday, November 13 — the first days of the season. It's located right downtown, and admission for kids 4 and under is free. If you haven't been yet, the opening weekend is a great time to go before lines build up in December. Skate rental is available on-site.
