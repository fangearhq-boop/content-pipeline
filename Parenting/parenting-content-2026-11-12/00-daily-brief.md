### STORY 2: D11's $775 Million Bond Passes: What It Means for Colorado Springs Schools
**Tier: 1 | Type: NEW**
**Posting Window: 10:00 AM MT**

Voters approved Colorado Springs School District 11's $775 million bond measure in the November 2026 election, the first D11 bond since 2006. The bond funds major facility projects including an $80 million rebuild of Holmes Middle School and $24.18 million in renovations at MacLaren STEM Academy. A citizen oversight committee will monitor spending. Families across D11 will start to see project timelines and community engagement sessions in coming months.

### STORY 3: AAP: When a Parent Has a Substance Use Disorder, Here's How Pediatricians Can Help
**Tier: 1 | Type: NEW**
**Posting Window: 12:00 PM MT**

The American Academy of Pediatrics published new guidance on October 26, 2026, ahead of the December 2026 issue of Pediatrics, calling on pediatricians to take a nonjudgmental approach when a parent has a substance use disorder. The guidance emphasizes building trusted relationships with families to support children's developmental and emotional needs. Research shows children in these households face elevated risks for developmental delays, trauma, and behavioral challenges — but early pediatric intervention can make a significant difference.

### STORY 4: CPSC Recall Alert: HAUOMS 8-Drawer Dresser Poses Tip-Over Risk to Young Children
**Tier: 2 | Type: NEW**
**Posting Window: 3:00 PM MT**

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission announced a recall of HAUOMS 8-Drawer Dressers (Recall #27-068, issued November 5, 2026). Approximately 1,392 units were sold on Amazon for about $150 (SKU AJ_DJ504298_1). The dresser can tip over when a drawer is extended, posing a serious crush hazard to young children. The remedy is a full refund — consumers should stop using the dresser immediately and email a photo to recall@hauoms.com.

**Sources:**
- CPSC Recall #27-068: https://www.cpsc.gov/node/67710 [OFFICIAL]
