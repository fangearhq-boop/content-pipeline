**HIGH confidence:**

  - Sources: AAP Publications (Pediatrics journal), CIDRAP, Clinical Advisor, AAP.org
- 190 pediatric deaths from flu nationally in 2025-2026 season
  - Source: AAP policy statement, CIDRAP coverage
- 85% of children who died from flu last season were unvaccinated
  - Source: AAP policy statement
- Only 49.4% of children ages 6 months through 17 years were vaccinated through May 2026
  - Source: AAP policy statement / CIDRAP
- All children 6 months+ without medical contraindication should receive annual flu vaccine
  - Source: AAP policy statement
- Children 6 months through 8 years needing two doses: first-time recipients, received only 1 prior dose before July 1, 2026, or unknown vaccination history; doses at least 4 weeks apart
  - Source: AAP policy statement, Clinical Advisor

  - Source: AAP policy statement
