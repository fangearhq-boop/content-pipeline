Cheyenne Mountain Zoo Opens Little Paws Nature Preschool — August 24
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Colorado Springs parents, this one is worth bookmarking. The Cheyenne Mountain Zoo is opening its first-ever state-accredited outdoor preschool next Monday, August 24 — and registration is still open. Little Paws Preschool is not a summer camp or a zoo class. It is a full preschool program, accredited by the Colorado Department of Early Childhood, with state-licensed teachers and a calendar that follows Cheyenne Mountain School District 12. The difference is the classroom: zoo trails, animal habitats, and outdoor learning environments replace four walls and fluorescent lights. There are two age tracks: 3-year-olds: Tuesdays and Thursdays, 9 AM to noon, $220.75 per month 4-5-year-olds: Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays, 9 AM to 1 PM, $460.75 per month Class sizes are approximately 15 students. Because the program is accredited and calendar-synced with a local school district, it qualifies as a formal early childhood education program — not just enrichment. If you have a preschool-age child and have been looking for something genuinely different in COS, this is worth a call. Email LittlePawsPreschool@cmzoo.org to request information and an application. Has anyone enrolled already? Drop a comment and let families know what you've heard. ---

AAP Releases 2026-27 Flu Vaccine Guidelines — 190 Children Died Last Season
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post The American Academy of Pediatrics just released its flu vaccine guidelines for the 2026-27 season. Here is what Colorado Springs families need to know before fall arrives. First, last season's numbers: 190 children died of influenza in the United States. Of those deaths, 85% occurred in unvaccinated children. Only 49.4% of children ages 6 months through 17 years were vaccinated through May 2026 — well below what pediatricians say is needed for meaningful community protection. The AAP's recommendation is unchanged in principle but now backed by sharper data: every child 6 months and older without a medical contraindication should receive an annual flu vaccine as soon as it becomes available this fall. There is one critical detail about dosing that many parents do not know: If your child is between 6 months and 8 years old AND any of the following applies to them, they need TWO doses this season, at least 4 weeks apart: - They have never received a flu vaccine before - They have received only 1 dose total in their entire history (before July 1, 2026) - You are unsure of their vaccination history If they do not meet any of those criteria, a single dose is sufficient. The AAP says there is no preference for a specific brand or formulation — any licensed, age-appropriate vaccine is appropriate. Do not delay if your preferred type is unavailable. In Colorado Springs, flu vaccines typically become available at pediatricians, Walgreens, King Soopers pharmacies, and through El Paso County Public Health starting in September. The AAP recommends completing your child's full course by the end of October. With D20 starting tomorrow and the full school year underway, scheduling flu vaccines in the next few weeks is a practical step most families can make time for. Questions about the two-dose rule? Drop them in the comments. ---

Two More Baby Product Recalls — Vevor Swings and Finger Lights
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Two more baby and children's product recalls COS parents should know about — both from August 2026 CPSC announcements and neither covered in our recent recall roundups. VEVOR BABY SWINGS (Sanven Technology) — SUFFOCATION HAZARD This recall is serious because the product was never safe to begin with: Vevor baby swings violate the mandatory federal standard for infant sleep products AND the inclined sleepers ban. Both of those standards exist because angled, padded products have been linked to infant suffocation. Details: - Sold at Vevor.com and Amazon.com between January and August 2025 - Price range: $65-$80 - No injuries reported in the US — but the product is illegal under federal safety law - Action: Stop using immediately. Contact Vevor for instructions. FINGER LIGHTS — BUTTON BATTERY HAZARD Two brands are affected: POPOOO Jungle Safari LED Finger Lights (~62,490 units) and Delightmi Oitnlaughter LED Finger Lights (~1,012 units). Children can access the internal button cell batteries too easily. Button batteries are a hazard that pediatric ER staff take extremely seriously. When swallowed, they can cause severe internal chemical burns and death within hours — faster than most parents realize. If your child swallows one, go to the emergency room immediately. Do not wait to see if symptoms develop. Both products were sold on Amazon.com. Check your home for both. For the complete list of COS-relevant recalls we have covered this month — including the Taleco Gear baby jumpers/swings (Aug 15), PIXLABBY magnetic fidget sliders (Aug 8), GOPO Toys teething toys (Aug 6), and Build-A-Bear Heartwarming Hugs Bears (Aug 8) — see the COS Parenting page. What other product safety questions do Colorado Springs families have? ---

D20 Starts Tomorrow — Final Checklist for Academy District 20 Families
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Academy District 20 families — tomorrow is Day 1. If you have been following the COS school calendar, you know D49 started August 3 and D11 started August 12. Tomorrow, D20 joins them. Here is the schedule one more time: MONDAY, AUGUST 17 First day for: preschool, kindergarten, 6th grade, and 9th grade students TUESDAY, AUGUST 18 First day for: all other grades The two-wave start is intentional. The staggered approach gives teachers and staff a smaller group to welcome on each first day, making the transition smoother — especially for students entering a new building. Tonight's checklist for D20 families: - Confirm your transportation arrangement (bus route, carpool, or drop-off location) - Log in to the parent portal and check for any supply lists from specific teachers - Make sure your child knows which day is their first day - Set wake-up times for the earlier school-year routine — going back an hour can take a few days to adjust - Pack the backpack tonight One more note for families on the west side: D20 broke ground on August 3 on the new Douglass Valley Elementary School, which will eventually replace and expand the existing building. That construction is underway, but the existing school continues its normal operation this year. Tomorrow morning, Colorado Springs will be fully back in school. D20 parents — you've got this.
