### STORY 2: CPSC Recall — PIXLABBY Magnetic Fidget Sliders

**Source 1:** CPSC.gov — Official recall page
- Product: PIXLABBY Silicone Magnetic Fidget Sliders
- Units sold: ~860
- Sold on: Amazon.com
- Sale period: October 2025 through May 2026
- Price: approximately $10
- Hazard: Violate mandatory toy standard; contain two high-powered magnets that can be liberated; magnets can attract each other in digestive system causing perforations, blockages, blood poisoning, and death
- Incident: One child removed silicone casing, accessed two magnets inside, ingested them; child was hospitalized
- Remedy: Stop use immediately; contact PIXLABBY for full refund; dispose and submit photo to ah1998zxc@gmail.com

**Confidence:** HIGH — Official CPSC.gov recall page

### STORY 3: NSF 2026 Sleep in America Poll

**Source 1:** National Sleep Foundation — 2026 Sleep in America Poll
- 44% of children do not consistently receive the recommended amount of sleep for their age
- Parents underestimate sleep needs by more than one hour per day across all age groups
- 70% of parents: poor sleep negatively affects their child's mood and daytime functioning
- 86% of parents: good sleep improves their child's behavior and mood

**Source 2:** PMC (research) — Sleep in children ages 6-12
- Sleep essential for cognition, emotional regulation, behavior, physical health

**Source 3:** Frontiers in Sports and Active Living — Exercise and sleep in children
- Exercise training improves sleep quality in children
- After 8 weeks of exercise training, sleep habits improved
- Stress perception negatively correlated with volume of moderate-vigorous exercise

**Confidence:** HIGH for NSF poll data; MEDIUM for exercise-sleep link (single research source)
