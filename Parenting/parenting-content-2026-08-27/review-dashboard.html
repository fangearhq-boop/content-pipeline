### Long-Form Post If your family is connected to Orton Academy or Trailblazer Elementary in Colorado Springs, here's an update worth knowing. Colorado Springs School District 11 has confirmed that Orton Academy, a D11 charter school, will share space at Trailblazer Elementary for the 2026-27 school year. The arrangement takes effect with this school year, which means both school communities are now operating from the same building. Charter school co-location is not unusual in D11 — it's a resource-sharing model the district has used before. But it does change logistics for families: drop-off and pick-up patterns, access to common areas, and the general rhythm of a school day can look different when two school communities share a facility. If your family is affected and you haven't yet connected with your school about the specifics, now's a good time to reach out. Your school's front office is the best starting point for questions about schedules, drop-off timing, or any questions the co-location raises. Are you in one of these school communities? CPSC Recall — PIXLABBY Magnetic Fidget Sliders (Magnet Ingestion Risk)
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post There's a CPSC recall COS families should know about right now — especially if you've ordered toys from Amazon in the past year. PIXLABBY Silicone Magnetic Fidget Sliders have been recalled by the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission. About 860 units were sold on Amazon.com between October 2025 and May 2026, priced at approximately $10 each. Here's the problem: the sliders contain two high-powered magnets inside a silicone casing. A child was able to remove the casing, access the magnets, and swallow them. That child was hospitalized. High-powered magnets are particularly dangerous when swallowed — they can attract each other through intestinal walls, causing perforations, blockages, and infections that can be life-threatening. If you have these in your home, stop using them immediately. Keep them away from children. Contact PIXLABBY for a full refund, and you'll be asked to dispose of them and send a photo of the disposed product to ah1998zxc@gmail.com to receive your refund. This is the third magnetic toy recall in Colorado Springs families' feeds in recent weeks — a reminder to do a quick check of any magnet-based toys in your home, regardless of brand. Have you seen any of these in a store or online recently? Tag a friend who might need to know. ### Image Post Caption RECALL: PIXLABBY magnetic fidget sliders — high-powered magnets, one child hospitalized. Stop use, contact seller for full refund.

NSF 2026 Sleep Poll — 44% of Kids Aren't Getting Enough Sleep
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post If you've been wondering whether your kids are getting enough sleep — there's a good chance they're not, and you might be underestimating how much they need. The National Sleep Foundation's 2026 Sleep in America Poll found that 44% of children don't consistently get the recommended amount of sleep for their age. That's nearly half of all kids. And parents are regularly underestimating sleep needs by more than an hour per day across every age group. The effects are visible: nearly 70% of parents say poor sleep directly affects their child's mood and daytime functioning. Eighty-six percent say good sleep improves behavior. So the problem isn't knowledge — most parents know sleep matters. The gap is in how many hours it actually takes. Here's something that helps, according to recent research: regular exercise. Kids who got consistent moderate-to-vigorous physical activity showed improved sleep habits after eight weeks. We're heading into fall sports season and cooler weather — there's a window to build that routine before school nights get harder. What's working for sleep routines in your house right now? ### Image Post Caption NSF poll: 44% of kids aren't getting enough sleep, and parents underestimate needs by over an hour a day. Free School Support Resources COS Families Should Know
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post We're about two to three weeks into the 2026-27 school year depending on your district, and this is usually when families figure out what they need and don't yet have. Here's a quick round-up of free and low-cost resources available to Colorado Springs families right now: **School supplies — D11:** Colorado Springs School District 11 partnered with Edukit to deliver free, grade-specific school supply kits to more than 14,000 K–8 students. If your student is in D11 and didn't receive a kit on the first day, reach out to your school's front office. **Immunization records:** If you're still sorting out vaccination paperwork, El Paso County Public Health (elpasocountyhealth.org) can help with school immunization records and requirements. **Childcare assistance:** The Colorado Child Care Assistance Program (CCAP) is available to income-eligible families in El Paso County. Call 719-444-5000 to find out if you qualify. **Paid family leave:** Colorado's FAMLI program offers paid leave for family and medical reasons. If you have a new baby, a sick child, or a family medical event this year, visit colorado.gov/famli to understand your options. **Homework help and free programs:** Every PPLD branch offers free resources — homework help, after-school programming, and reading materials. Visit ppld.org to find events at your nearest location.
