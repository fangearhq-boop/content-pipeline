Things to Do with Kids in Colorado Springs This Weekend (August 28–30)

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | August 27, 2026

Late August in Colorado Springs has a particular kind of energy — summer's still showing up but the school-year rhythm has set in, and weekends feel a little more earned. This one has some genuinely good options, including a free Friday evening event and a Saturday morning that can send your family into the weekend feeling like they actually did something.

Here are six picks for August 28–30. As always, check times and ticket availability before you head out. For the full list of things to do with kids in Colorado Springs this weekend, Springs Daily keeps the running calendar updated.

Wings of Change Grand Reveal — Friday, Aug 28, 5:30–7 p.m.

Start the weekend right with a free evening event at the Colorado Springs Pioneers Museum lawn, 1 Lake Ave. The Wings of Change installation — featuring artist-designed butterfly and dragonfly sculptures placed across the city — holds its grand reveal Friday evening from 5:30 to 7 p.m. It's an outdoor event on a beautiful lawn that works for kids of all ages, and the timing (early evening, not too late) makes it easy to fit before dinner or after. This is the strongest free pick of the weekend.

Story and Crafts with Cynde — Through Saturday, Aug 31 (Free)

The Pikes Peak Library District is running Story and Crafts with Cynde at Reading at the Rock Library through Saturday, August 31. The program includes stories, songs, rhymes, and movement — a format that works especially well for younger kids who need the combination of sitting and moving. PPLD programs are free and open to all. Check ppld.org for specific times before you go.

If your family is looking for free things to do this weekend in Colorado Springs, library programs are consistently among the best options.

Rocky Mountain 5K by YMCA — Saturday, Aug 29, 8 a.m.

America the Beautiful Park (134 Cimino Dr) hosts the Rocky Mountain 5K by YMCA on Saturday morning at 8 a.m. If you have kids who are old enough to run or walk a 5K — or a family that just enjoys the race-day energy — this is a solid Saturday morning. The park is a beautiful venue, and the timing means you're back home before the heat builds. Check the Colorado Springs YMCA website for registration and participant details.

Sunflower Days at Gather Mountain Blooms — Saturday, Aug 29, 8 a.m.–noon

Gather Mountain Blooms is hosting Sunflower Days on Saturday morning from 8 a.m. to noon — a relaxed summer experience surrounded by blooming fields. This is the kind of outing that works well for mixed-age families and is genuinely photogenic. Check the Gather Mountain Blooms website for ticket pricing and exact location before you go.

U.S. Olympic and Paralympic Museum — All Weekend

If you haven't been to the museum at 1 Olympic Plaza lately — or if you have visiting family this weekend — it remains one of the best family outings Colorado Springs offers. Guided experiences run daily starting at noon and last about 90 minutes. It's impressive for kids who love sports, history, or anything competitive. Admission fees apply; check usopm.org for current pricing and booking.

Spirit of the South at Cottonwood Center for the Arts — Through Saturday

The Spirit of the South exhibition at the Cottonwood Center for the Arts runs through Saturday, August 29. It's a free-to-browse art gallery experience that pairs well with lunch downtown. The Cottonwood Center is at 427 E. Colorado Ave — worth a stop if you're in that part of the city.

What's Next

Garden of the Gods (1805 N. 30th St) is always free and always open — the kind of backup plan that's also the right call. For everything happening this weekend in Colorado Springs, Springs Daily's full calendar has the complete picture.

Enjoy the weekend, COS families. You've earned it.
