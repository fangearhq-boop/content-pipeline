### STORY 1: D11/D20 Schools Forfeit vs. Lutheran HS

**Long-Form — 8:00 AM MT**
Something significant is unfolding in Colorado Springs high school sports — and it's a conversation families across COS will want to understand.

On Monday, District 11 announced that Coronado High School will not play its Thursday, October 15 game against Lutheran High School. The decision follows a similar move by Academy District 20, whose Air Academy High School forfeited its homecoming game earlier this month.

Both districts point to racism allegations against Lutheran players. Sand Creek High School and Rampart High School have alleged that Lutheran players made racist remarks and gestures during games. Lutheran has denied the allegations. A CHSAA investigation is currently underway.

D11 issued a clear statement: it asked both Lutheran High School and CHSAA for a strong, unequivocal statement denouncing racism. When neither responded, D11 determined that proceeding with the game wasn't consistent with its values of respect, dignity, and equal and fair play.

D11 was careful to note it is not making a determination on the allegations themselves — the investigation is ongoing. The district is simply drawing a line on the kind of environment it will participate in.

We'll keep watching how this unfolds. How are you talking to your kids about this situation?

### STORY 3: Mbabyjon Crib Bumper Recall

**Long-Form — 12:00 PM MT**
Quick safety heads-up for families with young children: the CPSC has issued a stop-use notice for Mbabyjon padded crib bumpers.

Padded crib bumpers are banned in the United States under the Safe Sleep for Babies Act. If you have Mbabyjon padded crib bumpers in your child's sleep space, the CPSC says to stop using them immediately and dispose of them.

Safe sleep guidelines are clear: your baby's sleep space should be firm, flat, and free of loose items — that means no bumpers, no pillows, no blankets in the crib. The only things that belong in a safe sleep space are a fitted sheet and a sleep sack or swaddle if needed.

This comes in the same month the CPSC also recalled multiple baby bath seat products. It's a good time to do a quick audit of your nursery and make sure everything you're using meets current safety standards.

Got questions about safe sleep? Healthychildren.org (the AAP's parent resource) is one of the best places to start.

### STORY 5: D11 Bond 4A Mail Ballots

**Long-Form — 5:00 PM MT**
If you're a D11 family and registered voter in El Paso County, your mail ballot for the November 3 election should be arriving this week — and Measure 4A is on it.

Here's the quick version of what it asks: Should D11 borrow $775 million to fund school safety and security upgrades, HVAC and air quality improvements, asbestos removal, career and technical education programs, updated classrooms, and new and renovated school libraries?

The estimated cost to a typical homeowner is around $260 per year. The district has committed to independent oversight of how the funds are used.

Voters have three weeks to research, make a decision, and return their ballot. Drop boxes open around October 14. You can also return your ballot by mail.

D11's enrollment has been declining in recent years, and the district argues the bond is about bringing aging school buildings up to a standard that serves students — many of the buildings targeted are several decades old. Opponents, where they've spoken, have raised concerns about the cost.

If you have questions about what's on your ballot, d11.org/4A has information from the district's perspective.
