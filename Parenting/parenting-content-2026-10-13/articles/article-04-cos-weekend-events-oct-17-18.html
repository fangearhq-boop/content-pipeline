Family Weekend Guide: Colorado Springs Events for October 17–18, 2026

By Jamie Rivera | October 13, 2026 | Local Events

Colorado Springs has a genuinely packed family weekend ahead. Whether your kids are into LEGO, robotics, Halloween, cultural celebrations, or just want to show off their dog's costume, there are real options across both Saturday and Sunday — several of them free. Here's what's happening and what you need to know before heading out.

Saturday, October 17

Colorado Springs Brick Convention

LEGO fans, this is your weekend. The Colorado Springs Brick Convention is at the Colorado Springs Events Center on Saturday with morning and afternoon sessions. Tickets are $17.46. It's a hands-on convention with displays, builds, and vendors — the kind of event that tends to run long once kids get inside.

Free Robotics Workshop for Kids

A free robotics workshop for kids ages five through 14 is running Saturday at the DoubleTree by Hilton, starting at 11 AM. It's hands-on and free, which makes it a strong contender for families with elementary and middle school-age kids. Spots have a way of filling up at events like this — register early if you can.

Día de la Catrina at Crisálida

Also on Saturday starting at noon: Día de la Catrina at Crisálida, a family event celebrating Día de los Muertos culture with activities, entertainment, food, and mocktails. Registration is required for this one. It's a beautiful cultural event that's appropriate for a wide range of ages and tends to be one of the more memorable things on the COS October calendar.

Halloween Trunk-or-Treat Events

Saturday has multiple trunk-or-treat options across COS:

  • Halloween Trick or Treat Mingle at Citadel Mall — Free, starting at 11 AM
  • Hope COS trunk-or-treat — 4 PM
  • Rex Environmental trunk-or-treat — 1 PM

If your kids are trick-or-treating age and you want a lower-pressure Halloween event before October 31, Saturday gives you options across different parts of the day and different areas of the city.

Sunday, October 18

Barks and Boos Fall Fest

Sunday brings Barks and Boos Fall Fest, a free event running from 11 AM to 3 PM featuring a pup parade and a dog costume contest. If your family has a dog, this is the event for you. It's also a solid low-key option for families who want to be outside without a packed schedule.

Planning Notes

Saturday is a heavy day with a lot of overlap in timing. If you're trying to hit more than one event, plan your route — Citadel Mall and the DoubleTree are both in the central-north part of the city, while Crisálida is a separate venue. Check registration requirements before Saturday morning to avoid showing up to a sold-out event.

The Brick Convention's $17.46 ticket price applies per person, so it's worth checking whether there's a family rate or if kids under a certain age are free before you finalize your group. The Events Center website or the convention's own page will have the most current ticket details.

For Día de la Catrina, registration being required typically means spots are limited. If this is on your list, register now rather than waiting for the weekend.

What's Next

October 31 is about two and a half weeks away. COS neighborhoods and event venues typically have strong Halloween weekend programming the final week of October. Watch the Gazette's Springs Scene section and visitcos.com for additions to the calendar.

The Cool Science Festival also closes this Sunday, October 18 — so if your family is planning to attend that, Sunday is the last day. Check visitcos.com for remaining events and confirm registration requirements before heading out.
