### STORY 1: D11, D20 Schools Forfeit Football Games vs. Lutheran HS Over Racism Allegations
**Tier:** T1
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Pillar:** Local News
**Type:** NEW STORY
**Posting Window:** 8:00 AM MT

D11 announced on October 12 that Coronado High School will not play its Thursday, October 15 game against Lutheran High School, citing racism allegations and the lack of a public statement denouncing racism from either Lutheran or CHSAA. Academy District 20's Air Academy High School made a similar decision earlier, forfeiting its homecoming game. The allegations — from Sand Creek High School and Rampart High School — claim Luther players made racist remarks and gestures during football games. Lutheran has denied the allegations.

### STORY 3: CPSC Recalls Mbabyjon Crib Bumpers — Stop Use Immediately
**Tier:** T2
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Pillar:** National Parenting (Product Safety)
**Type:** NEW STORY
**Posting Window:** 12:00 PM MT

The CPSC issued a stop-use notice for Mbabyjon padded crib bumpers in early October, citing violation of the Safe Sleep for Babies Act. Padded crib bumpers are banned in the United States. Parents who have these products in their child's crib should remove and dispose of them immediately.

**Key Facts (HIGH confidence):**
- CPSC issued stop-use notice for Mbabyjon padded crib bumpers — source: consumeraffairs.com (Oct 9 recall roundup)
