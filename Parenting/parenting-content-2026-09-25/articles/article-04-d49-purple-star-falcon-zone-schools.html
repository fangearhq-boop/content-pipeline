Every school in D49's Falcon Zone has earned the Colorado Department of Education's Purple Star designation — a recognition that means a great deal to the thousands of military families whose kids attend those schools.

Colorado Springs is home to one of the largest concentrations of military families in Colorado, with Fort Carson and the Air Force Academy both in the area. For families that move frequently, experience deployment cycles, and navigate the challenges that come with military life, having schools specifically equipped and recognized for that support is more than symbolic.

What the Purple Star Designation Means

The Colorado Purple Star program is administered by the Colorado Department of Education and recognizes schools that demonstrate a commitment to serving military-connected students. To earn the designation, schools must have specific staff trained in military family support, resources for students experiencing transitions, and documented programs that address the unique challenges military kids face — including frequent school changes, extended parent absences, and the social adjustments that come with moving.

Earning the designation isn't a one-time checkbox. Schools are expected to maintain the programs and resources that earned them the recognition. Having all seven Falcon Zone schools hold it means military families in that part of Colorado Springs can expect a consistent level of support across every building in the zone.

D49 Recognition in September 2026

The Purple Star news comes alongside another D49 milestone from earlier this month. On September 10, 2026, D49's Board of Education held its Fantastic 49 recognition event, honoring two Colorado-based companies and two members of Team 49 — the district's network of community partners — for their contributions to the district and its students.

The recognition events reflect a district that's been active on multiple fronts heading into the 2026-27 school year. D49 also opened a new Student Success Center this fall, expanding access to specialized programming at the former Falcon Elementary building.

For Military Families in the Falcon Zone

If you're a military family new to Colorado Springs — or if you're considering the Falcon Zone for your kids — the Purple Star designation is a signal that the schools have infrastructure for the kind of transitions and support needs that come with military life. It doesn't eliminate every challenge, but it means someone at the school has been trained to understand them.

D49's website at d49.org is the best resource for specific information about each school's programs, enrollment, and support services for military-connected families.

What's Next

Families interested in D49's Falcon Zone schools can find enrollment and school-specific information at d49.org. Colorado Springs parents with questions about the Purple Star program can also visit the Colorado Department of Education's website. D49 continues enrollment on a rolling basis for families new to the district throughout the school year.
