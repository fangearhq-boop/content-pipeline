The Colorado Cool Science Festival is back for 2026, running October 3 through 18 in Colorado Springs. The highlight for families is the Big Carnival Day on Saturday, October 3 — a free, full-day event from 10 AM to 4 PM with free parking included. It's one of the best free family days of the fall season in COS, and it's aimed squarely at K–8 kids.

What to Expect at Carnival Day

Carnival Day is the festival's biggest public event — the one with the open-access format and the crowd of excited kids. Hands-on activities include rocket building and launches, robotics demonstrations, slime labs, drone flights, virtual reality stations, and science magic shows. The mix is designed for kids across a wide age range, from early elementary through middle school.

The free admission and free parking make it an easy yes for families. There's no pre-registration required for general attendance, but the venue fills up, especially in the late morning. Arriving near the 10 AM opening gives you the most time before crowds build.

The Bigger Festival: Oct 3–18

Carnival Day kicks off a two-week run of science programming across Colorado Springs. The full festival schedule includes additional events and programming from October 3 through 18, with events at various venues in the area. Some events within the festival may have separate registration or limited capacity, so checking coolscience.org in advance is the best way to plan a full schedule.

The festival has a strong K–8 orientation, which reflects its roots as a community science education initiative. If your school hasn't mentioned it, it's worth asking — many local schools participate in festival programming during the school day, and the public events on weekends are separate and open to all.

Why This Is Worth Your Saturday

Fall in Colorado Springs fills up fast with competing events — and October 3 has a few of them. But the Cool Science Festival's free Carnival Day is one of those events where the combination of zero cost, hands-on engagement, and genuine educational value is genuinely hard to beat. Kids who are indifferent to science textbooks often find something at a festival like this that clicks for them — a rocket launch, a slime experiment, a robot they can actually control.

For parents who've been looking for an activity that feels substantive beyond the typical fall outing, this is a good choice.

What's Next

The full festival schedule and event details are available at coolscience.org. Carnival Day is Saturday, October 3, 10 AM–4 PM, free admission, free parking. The festival continues through October 18. Check the website for additional programming throughout the two-week run.
