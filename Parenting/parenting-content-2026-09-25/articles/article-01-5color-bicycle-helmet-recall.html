The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission issued a recall on September 24, 2026 for approximately 324 units of the 5Color bicycle helmet, size Small. The helmet fails to meet federal safety standards for bicycle helmets — the mandatory requirements that ensure a helmet will actually protect a child's head in a crash.

If your child has one of these helmets, stop using it now. This is not a precautionary soft-advisory — it's a formal CPSC recall based on the product's failure to meet mandatory federal requirements.

How to Identify the Recalled Helmet

The recalled helmet is size Small, pink, and fits head circumferences between 18 and 22 inches. The model number is YD-001 and the lot number is YD-260320. These were sold through Amazon in May 2026, priced at approximately $25 to $26.

Check the helmet itself for the model and lot numbers — they should be printed on a label inside or on the product. If you're unsure whether you purchased this specific model, CPSC.gov lists the full recall details.

What to Do Now

The CPSC's guidance is clear: stop use immediately and cut the straps so the helmet cannot accidentally be used again. To receive a refund, email a photo of the helmet to FiveColorCS@163.com. You can also reach the company directly at 833-382-6461, Monday through Friday from 9 AM to 2 PM CT.

No injuries have been reported as of the recall date, but the product's failure to meet mandatory safety standards means there's no way to verify it will protect in a real impact. It's not worth the risk.

Why Helmet Safety Standards Matter

Federal bicycle helmet safety standards set by the CPSC specify minimum requirements for impact absorption, strap strength, and retention system integrity. A helmet that fails these tests may look identical to a compliant one but perform differently in a crash — which is precisely why the CPSC requires mandatory certification before helmets can be sold.

When shopping for a replacement, look for helmets that carry CPSC certification (it will be noted on the label). National brands sold at major retailers are required to meet these standards, but smaller online sellers sometimes list products that haven't been properly tested.

What's Next

If you purchased this helmet, contact FiveColorCS@163.com with a photo of the product to begin the refund process. The company is available by phone at 833-382-6461 during weekday business hours. The full recall notice is available at CPSC.gov. Colorado Springs families with questions about product safety can also find recall alerts at cpsc.gov/recalls.
