### STORY 1: HARPPA Nordi Toddler Tower Stool Recall

**Tier:** 1 (Product Safety — stop use immediately)
**Pillar:** National Parenting (Product Safety)
**Posting Window:** 8:00 AM MT
**Primary Angle:** HARPPA recalled ~47,000 Nordi Foldable Toddler Tower Stools after reports of collapse, tip-over, and entrapment. A child's torso can fit through the openings, posing risk of serious injury or death. Free repair kit available.
**Secondary Angle:** Parents who bought the $130 stools on Amazon or Harppababy.com between September 2023 and June 2026 should stop use and contact HARPPA.
**Hook:** If you have a HARPPA Nordi Toddler Tower at home — stop using it now.
