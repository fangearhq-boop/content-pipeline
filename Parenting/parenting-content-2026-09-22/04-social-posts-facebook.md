### STORY 1: HARPPA Toddler Tower Stool Recall

**Long-Form [8:00 AM MT]**

If there is a HARPPA Nordi Foldable Toddler Tower in your home, stop using it now.

The Consumer Product Safety Commission and HARPPA issued a recall on July 30, 2026 for approximately 47,000 Nordi Foldable Toddler Tower Stools (Model HANS0002). The stools can collapse or tip over while in use — and because a child's torso can fit through the openings on the front and back sides, the risk is serious. HARPPA has received three reports of collapse, resulting in injuries to two children.

These stools were sold online at Harppababy.com and Amazon.com between September 2023 and June 2026, priced at approximately $130.

The good news: HARPPA is offering a free repair kit — including protective nets, stabilizing feet, and installation instructions — at no cost and with free shipping. You do not need to return the stool.

To request your free repair kit: visit harppababy.com/pages/2026-harppa-nordi-foldable-toddler-tower-model-hans0002-voluntary-recall or go to cpsc.gov and search for "HARPPA."

### STORY 2: Cool Science Festival Oct. 3-18

**Long-Form [10:00 AM MT]**

Mark your calendars — one of Colorado Springs' favorite family events is almost here.

The Colorado Springs Cool Science Festival returns October 3-18, 2026, bringing 16 days of STEAM events across the Pikes Peak Region. Most events are free, and the festival is designed for all ages — from curious kindergarteners to adults who never outgrew their love of science.

The highlight for younger families is Cool Science Carnival Day at UCCS on Saturday, October 3. This free event (yes, even parking is free) transforms the campus into a hands-on science playground for K-8 students. Expect science magic shows, lab tours, rockets, slime, drones, live animals, virtual reality, robots, liquid nitrogen treats, and dozens of other activities.

Beyond Carnival Day, the festival hosts events at locations throughout Colorado Springs and the Pikes Peak Region through October 18. Some events may have a small fee, but the majority are offered free of charge.

For the full schedule and to plan your visit: coolscience.org

Are your children interested in science or engineering? This festival is one of the best free resources Colorado Springs has to offer.

### STORY 3: D49 POWER Zone Marzano Milestone

**Long-Form [12:00 PM MT]**

Every one of District 49's POWER Zone schools moved up this year — and that's worth celebrating.

All seven schools in the D49 POWER Zone advanced one level on the Marzano High Reliability Schools (HRS) framework during the 2025-2026 school year. The achievement was recognized at the Fantastic 49 presentation on September 10, where D49 honored school leaders and two Colorado-based partner firms.

The POWER Zone is a group of seven schools in the northwest part of District 49: Vista Ridge High School, Skyview Middle School, Inspiration View Elementary School, Falcon Homeschool Program, Ridgeview Elementary School, ALLIES, and Odyssey Elementary School. Zone Superintendent Theresa Ritz leads the group.

The Marzano High Reliability Schools framework measures how consistently schools provide high-quality instruction and learning experiences — and advancing on it is not automatic. Schools must demonstrate documented improvement in both processes and outcomes.

For families with students in D49's northwest schools, this is a meaningful signal about the direction the district is heading.

### STORY 4: America 250 / Colorado 150 Events

**Long-Form [3:00 PM MT]**

Colorado is turning 150 years old in 2026 — and there are free family events happening right here in Colorado Springs to mark the occasion.

As part of the America 250/Colorado 150 dual celebration, the Colorado Springs Pioneers Museum is hosting the "We The People: Colorado at 150" lecture series throughout the fall. The next lecture is October 17, 2026 at 2 PM at the museum, exploring the diverse experiences and voices of Coloradans across the state's 150-year history.

The Pioneers Museum at 215 S Tejon Street is free every day — making it one of the most accessible cultural resources in Colorado Springs. The America 250/Colorado 150 programming adds a seasonal reason to visit, especially with children who are studying Colorado history in school.

For families who want a more active experience, downtown walking history tours — "Seeing History in Place" — are available through Downtown CS for $10 per person.

More information about America 250/Colorado 150 events in the city can be found at coloradosprings.gov/America250Colorado150.

### STORY 5: Trackchair Program

**Long-Form [5:00 PM MT]**

Colorado Springs' most inclusive outdoor adventure program just got bigger.

The PRCS Trackchair Program expanded in 2026 to offer two guided hikes daily, Wednesday through Sunday, at four open spaces across Colorado Springs: Ute Valley Park, Red Rock Canyon Open Space, Blodgett Open Space, and Bluestem Prairie Open Space.

Trackchairs are all-terrain power wheelchairs that allow people with mobility limitations to access trails they otherwise could not. The PRCS program provides the chairs and guides — at no cost — so that more families can explore Colorado Springs' remarkable outdoor spaces together.

The expansion is part of a broader investment the city made in parks and trails in 2026, which also includes new trail connections, wayfinding improvements, and Rock Island Trail upgrades.

Whether your family includes someone who needs accessible options, or you simply want to experience COS open spaces in a new way, the Trackchair program is worth knowing about.

For more information: coloradosprings.gov/PRCS
