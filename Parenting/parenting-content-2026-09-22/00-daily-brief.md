### STORY 1: HARPPA Recalls Nordi Toddler Tower Stools — Collapse and Entrapment Hazard
**Type:** NEW
**Tier:** 1 (Product safety recall — stop use immediately)
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Angle:** The CPSC and HARPPA recalled about 47,000 Nordi Foldable Toddler Tower Stools due to collapse, tip-over, and entrapment hazards. Three reports of collapse, two injuries. A child's torso can fit through the openings, posing a risk of serious injury or death. Free repair kit available.
