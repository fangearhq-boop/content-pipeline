## STORY 1: HARPPA Nordi Toddler Tower Stool Recall

**Key Facts:**
- Recall announced: July 30, 2026 [HIGH — cpsc.gov]
- Recalled product: HARPPA Nordi Foldable Toddler Tower Stools, Model HANS0002 [HIGH — cpsc.gov]
- Units recalled: approximately 47,000 [HIGH — cpsc.gov, thedailynewsonline.com]
- Hazard: tower stools can collapse or tip over; child's torso can fit through front/back openings — risk of serious injury and death from tip-over, fall, and entrapment [HIGH — cpsc.gov]
- Injuries reported: 3 reports of collapse/tip-over, resulting in 2 injuries (scrapes, cuts, bruises); no deaths [HIGH — cpsc.gov]
- Sold at: Harppababy.com and Amazon.com [HIGH — cpsc.gov]
- Sale period: September 2023 through June 2026 [HIGH — cpsc.gov]
- Price: approximately $130 [HIGH — cpsc.gov]
- Remedy: stop use immediately; contact HARPPA for a FREE repair kit (protective nets, stabilizing feet, installation instructions) including free shipping [HIGH — cpsc.gov, harppababy.com]
- Contact: harppababy.com/pages/2026-harppa-nordi-foldable-toddler-tower-model-hans0002-voluntary-recall [HIGH]
- Model number: HANS0002 [HIGH — cpsc.gov]

Note: This recall was July 30, 2026 — approximately 2 months ago. Not previously covered in COS Parenting pipeline. Including as timely safety reminder, especially as school year is underway and families may have purchased this item.
