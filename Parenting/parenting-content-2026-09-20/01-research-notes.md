## STORY 1: Jerify Magnetic Stick Figure Toy Recall

**Key Facts:**
- Product name: Jerify Magnetic Stick Figure Toy Sets [HIGH — cpsc.gov]
- Manufacturer/seller: Colerinsec [HIGH — cpsc.gov]
- Recall date: September 17, 2026 [HIGH — cpsc.gov]
- Units sold: ~38,507 [HIGH — cpsc.gov/foodpoisoningbulletin]
- Sale location: Amazon.com exclusively [HIGH — cpsc.gov]
- Sale dates: October 2022 through May 2026 [HIGH — cpsc.gov]
- Sale price: ~$21 [HIGH — cpsc.gov]
- Hazard: Magnets in hands and feet can detach; swallowed magnets attract each other/metal, cause intestinal perforations, twisting, blockage, blood poisoning, death [HIGH — cpsc.gov]
- Incidents: Two reports of children who swallowed magnets; both hospitalized [HIGH — cpsc.gov]
- Description: 30-piece sets; figures ~2.7" tall × 2.5" wide; ten colors; human figure shape [HIGH — cpsc.gov]
- Action: Stop use immediately; take away from children; contact Colerinsec for full refund; dispose of toy; submit photo of disposed toy [HIGH — cpsc.gov]
- Contact for refund: Colerinsec [HIGH — cpsc.gov]
