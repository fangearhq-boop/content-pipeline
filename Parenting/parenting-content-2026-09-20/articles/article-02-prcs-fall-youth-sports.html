Fall Youth Sports Registration Open in Colorado Springs: Soccer, Football, and More Through PRCS

By Sarah Morales | September 20, 2026

Local Events | Colorado Springs

If your family has been looking for a fall sports program in Colorado Springs, registration is open now through the city's Parks, Recreation and Cultural Services (PRCS) department. The fall season includes soccer, flag football, and tackle football across a wide range of ages and grade levels — and some divisions are already filling up.

PRCS runs one of the most accessible youth sports programs in the region, offering structured leagues with trained officials, age-appropriate divisions, and games held at city parks and fields across Colorado Springs. For many families, it's the first organized sports experience their kids have outside of school PE.

What's on Offer This Fall

The fall 2026 PRCS youth sports lineup covers three sports with grade-based divisions:

Soccer is the broadest program, open from PreK through 9th grade. For younger children — especially PreK through 2nd grade — the focus is on learning to love the game: basic movement, passing, and small-sided games. As kids move into the upper elementary and middle school divisions, the format becomes more competitive, with full-sided games and formal standings.

Flag Football runs for grades 1 through 8. This is a non-contact version of the game that still teaches the fundamentals: routes, snaps, reading a defense, and working as a unit. It's a popular choice for families who want their kids involved in football but aren't ready — or don't want — full-contact play.

Tackle Football is available for grades 3 through 8. For families who want the full game experience, PRCS offers tackle football with proper equipment and coaching. This program follows standard youth football formats with practices and weekend games.

How to Register

Registration is handled online at coloradosprings.gov/youthsports. The site lists available divisions, fees, practice start dates, and game locations. If you prefer to talk through options first or have questions about which division fits your child's age and experience level, you can reach the city's Sports Office directly at (719) 385-5940.

PRCS has noted that spaces are limited in some divisions, so families interested in a particular program should register sooner rather than later. Fall sports in Colorado Springs tend to draw strong participation, and some age groups fill quickly once school starts and schedules settle.

Why PRCS Programs Stand Out

One of the practical advantages of PRCS youth sports is the range of access options. Programs are offered at price points that aim to keep participation feasible for most Colorado Springs families, and the city's parks system means games and practices are spread across multiple locations rather than concentrated at a single facility.

For younger children especially, organized sports through PRCS can be a lower-stakes entry point — the leagues are designed with development in mind, not just competition. Kids get field time, exposure to teammates from different schools and neighborhoods, and the basics of working within a structured game.

For families new to Colorado Springs, PRCS sports also serve as a way to connect with other local families in a consistent, weekly setting that extends through the fall season.

What's Next

PRCS youth sports are one of several fall recreational programs the department runs each season. If soccer or football aren't the right fit, the city also offers other recreational activities through the same system — checking coloradosprings.gov/recreation gives families a full view of what's available this fall.

For families who've already registered, practice start dates and field assignments are posted through the PRCS registration portal once divisions are set. Questions about specific programs or accommodations can go to the Sports Office at (719) 385-5940.
