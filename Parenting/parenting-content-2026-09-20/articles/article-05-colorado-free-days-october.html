Free Museum Days in Colorado This October: What COS Families Should Know

By Jamie Rivera | September 20, 2026

Evergreen | Colorado Springs

October is one of the better months to take advantage of free museum access in Colorado — and for Colorado Springs families, the options are better than most people realize. Between the Colorado Springs Pioneers Museum's everyday free admission and the statewide Colorado Free Days program, there are real opportunities to get kids into museums without spending much of anything.

Here's what's available this fall and how to find the full list of dates.

Colorado Springs Pioneers Museum — Free Every Day

The Colorado Springs Pioneers Museum at 215 S Tejon Street is free every day it's open, no voucher or reservation required. That's one of the few local museums where there's no cost barrier at all — you can walk in on any visit without planning around a specific free date.

The museum covers the history of Colorado Springs and the Pikes Peak region, with rotating exhibits alongside its permanent collection. It's housed in the historic El Paso County Courthouse building downtown — the building itself is worth seeing. Exhibits tend to include hands-on elements and displays that hold kids' attention, and the downtown location makes it easy to pair with a walk or lunch nearby.

Hours vary by day, so it's worth checking the museum's website or calling ahead, especially for holiday weekends. The museum is operated by the City of Colorado Springs.

Colorado Free Days — Statewide Program

Colorado runs a statewide program where participating institutions offer free admission on specific scheduled dates throughout the year. The program typically includes science centers, natural history museums, botanical gardens, and other venues — several of which are within a reasonable drive from Colorado Springs.

October has some of the best free day dates in the program's calendar. Institutions that have historically participated include the Denver Museum of Nature and Science, the Colorado Springs Fine Arts Center, and other regional venues. The specific institutions and dates change year to year, so checking the current list is essential before planning a trip.

The full 2026 Colorado Free Days schedule is available at coloradoparent.com and uncovercolorado.com — both sites maintain updated annual guides. The Colorado Tourism Office also publishes participation information at colorado.com.

Making the Most of Free Access

A few practical notes for planning around free days: popular institutions like the Denver Museum of Nature and Science can get crowded on free admission days, especially on weekends. Going earlier in the day or on a weekday free date tends to make for a better visit if your schedule allows.

For the Pioneers Museum in COS, everyday free admission means you can treat it like a regular weekend outing without any planning pressure. It's one of the more underused local resources for families — it rarely feels crowded, the building is beautiful, and there's enough variety in the exhibits to work for a range of ages.

If you're driving to Denver for a free day at a larger institution, it's also worth checking whether other participating venues nearby have the same date — sometimes two or three places are free on the same day, which turns a day trip into a full outing.

What's Next

The Colorado Free Days guides at coloradoparent.com and uncovercolorado.com are updated for each year and are worth bookmarking before October fills up with other commitments. Dates for 2026 are already listed.

For COS families looking for additional free or low-cost fall activities, the city's parks system and PRCS department also run seasonal programming throughout October. And if your family is interested in the Colorado Springs Fine Arts Center — which has participated in the statewide free days program — check their schedule at csfineartscenter.org for current programming and admission information.
