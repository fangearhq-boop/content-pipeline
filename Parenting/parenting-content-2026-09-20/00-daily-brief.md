### STORY 1: Jerify Magnetic Stick Figure Toys Recalled — 38,507 Units, Children Hospitalized
**Type:** NEW STORY
**Pillar:** National Parenting (Product Safety)
**Tier:** 1 (Urgent recall)
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Angle:** CPSC recalled Jerify Magnetic Stick Figure Toy Sets on September 17, 2026 after two children swallowed magnets and were hospitalized. Sold on Amazon Oct 2022-May 2026 for ~$21. Magnets can detach from limbs, be swallowed, and cause perforations, intestinal blockage, blood poisoning, or death.
**Action:** Stop use immediately; contact Colerinsec for full refund; dispose of toy and submit photo of disposal.
