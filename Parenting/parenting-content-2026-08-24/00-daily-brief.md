### STORY 2: CPSC Recalls Alert — Magnet Toys and Battery Toys Pose Deadly Hazard — TIER 1
**Angle:** Two CPSC recalls COS parents need to know about now. (1) Gagaku Children's Ice Cream Toy Playsets, sold on Amazon by Brandworl.USA, recalled for magnet ingestion hazard — magnets that detach can cause intestinal perforations, blockage, and death if swallowed. (2) ABC Trading recalled three children's toys (toy headband model 6300RP; Electronic Pet Cage-Dinosaur Tribes model 8266/ZH998-22; My Pet Bird Cute Bird Tribes ZH998-23) due to button cell battery compartments children can easily access — button battery ingestion can cause internal chemical burns and death. ABC Trading toys sold at TOYZ and Joissu stores nationwide, November 2022–October 2025, $5–$9 each. Stop use immediately; contact retailers for refund.
