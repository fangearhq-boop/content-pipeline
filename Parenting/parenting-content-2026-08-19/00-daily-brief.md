### STORY 1: CPSC Recall — Taleco Gear Baby Jumpers and Swings (52 Incidents, 32 Injuries)
**Tier:** 1 | **Byline:** Jamie Rivera | **Pillar:** National Parenting
CPSC recalled Taleco Gear Baby Jumpers, Baby Swings, and 2-in-1 Baby Jumper & Swings on August 13, 2026. At least 52 hazard incidents reported, including at least 32 injuries — head bumps and bloody noses among them. Hazards: fall and strangulation. Sold on Amazon.com, Walmart.com, and TalecoGear.com from April 2021 through July 2026, priced $65–$120. Families should stop use immediately.

**Tier:** 1 | **Byline:** Sarah Morales | **Pillar:** National Parenting

### STORY 4: AAP Screen Time Guidelines — What Parents Need to Know
**Tier:** 2 | **Byline:** Sarah Morales | **Pillar:** National Parenting
The American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) has updated its approach to screen time — moving from strict time limits toward a quality-first framework. Under the updated guidance: no screens before 18 months (except video chats); up to 1 hour/day of high-quality content for ages 2–5; for older kids, focus on what they watch and whether parents are watching with them. With school back in session and screen habits shifting, now is the right time for COS families to revisit the guidance.
