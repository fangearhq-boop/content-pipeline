### STORY 3: Aedyn's Backpack Club

#### Long-Form Post — 12:00 PM MT

If you haven't heard of Aedyn's Backpack Club, we're here to fix that.

Every August, Aedyn's Backpack Club distributes free backpacks loaded with school supplies to PreK–12 students here in Colorado Springs. It's completely free. No income documentation required at the door — just bring your kids. They do first-come, first-served, so you'll want to arrive when they open.

The organization is named after Aedyn, and it's entirely community-driven. They serve kids across COS grade levels, and the kiddos need to be present to get a backpack.

### STORY 4: Packz 4 Kidz COS

#### Long-Form Post — 3:00 PM MT

Back-to-school backpack drives in Colorado Springs — there are more than most families realize, and one of the ones we want to highlight today is Packz 4 Kidz. 🎒

Packz 4 Kidz is run by Kenzi's Causes and specifically serves Colorado Springs. Their goal each year is to get free backpacks and school supplies into the hands of 400 kids here. They serve students from early childhood education all the way through 12th grade.

To receive a backpack, your family needs to be low-income, and the child needs to be present to pick out their own bag. For very young children, a birth certificate works if they can't be there. The organization works on a first-come, first-served basis.

We don't have the confirmed 2026 COS event date and location as of today, so we're pointing families directly to kenziscauses.org/packz-4-kidz-colorado-springs/ for the most current information. If you know someone who could use this resource, this is a good one to share.

### STORY 5: D20 Bus Assignments Live August 1

#### Long-Form Post — 5:00 PM MT

D20 families — this one's for you. 🚌

Bus assignments for the 2026–27 school year go live on August 1 through the Extended Parent Portal at asd20.org. That's this Friday.

Here's how to find yours: log into the Extended Parent Portal, click "Bus Routing," select your student's name, then click the "Bus Routing" tab. You'll see your bus number, stop location, and pickup and drop-off times for the new school year.

If you haven't already, Transportation Space Available requests are also open via the same portal — those opened July 20. Head there if you want to request a bus route change before school starts.

D20's first day is August 17 (orientation) and August 18 (first full day). That gives you a couple of weeks after Friday to verify your route before the school year kicks off.
