Aedyn's Backpack Club Gives Free Backpacks to Colorado Springs Kids Each August

By Sarah Morales | July 29, 2026

Colorado Springs, CO

Aedyn's Backpack Club is a Colorado Springs organization that distributes free backpacks filled with school supplies to local students every August. It's entirely free, requires no advance registration, and serves PreK through 12th grade. If you haven't heard of it, you're not alone — it's one of those COS resources that regularly flies under the radar until someone tells you about it.

How It Works

The event operates on a first-come, first-served basis. Students must be present to receive a backpack — families can't pick one up on behalf of a child who isn't there. The distribution is typically held in August to align with back-to-school season across Colorado Springs's three main districts (D49 starts August 3–4, D11 on August 12, D20 on August 17–18).

There's no income requirement to participate. The event is open to COS students across grade levels, from the youngest elementary schoolers through high school seniors.

About the Organization

Aedyn's Backpack Club is named after Aedyn, the person who inspired the effort. It's a community-run initiative focused on making sure Colorado Springs kids have what they need to walk into school on day one. The supplies included typically cover the basics — folders, pencils, notebooks — alongside the backpack itself.

Organizations like this one operate on donated supplies and volunteer energy, and they fill a gap that becomes clearest in August: not every family can cover back-to-school costs before the first day, and having a filled backpack makes a real difference for kids starting a new school year.
