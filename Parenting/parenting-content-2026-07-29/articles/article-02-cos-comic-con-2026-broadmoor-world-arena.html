Colorado Springs Comic Con Returns August 21–23 at Broadmoor World Arena

By Jamie Rivera | July 29, 2026

Colorado Springs, CO

Colorado Springs Comic Con 2026 takes place August 21–23 at Broadmoor World Arena. Friday evening kicks things off at 3 p.m., with full days running Saturday and Sunday. Tickets are available through AXS.com and at the Pikes Peak Center box office. Three-day passes and single-day tickets are both options.

Families have about three weeks to plan — which means there's time to organize costumes, figure out which days to attend, and decide what the kids most want to see.

The Schedule

Friday, August 21 opens at 3:00 p.m. — good for families who can leave work or school pickup early and want to get a look at the floor before weekend crowds arrive. Saturday and Sunday are full convention days, typically running from morning through early evening.

For full programming details, panel schedules, and the guest list, the official source is cscomiccon.com. That's also where families can verify current face-value ticket pricing. Some secondary market sites are listing significantly higher resale prices — check the official site to avoid overpaying.

Getting Tickets

Tickets are sold through AXS.com and at the Pikes Peak Center box office. Both 3-day passes and single-day options are available, which gives families flexibility. If you're only able to make one day, a single-day ticket lets you pick the day that works for your schedule without paying for the full run.

Broadmoor World Arena is a major venue with ample parking and the scale to handle a large weekend event. It's a comfortable setting for families bringing younger kids who may need more space to move around than a smaller venue would allow.

Cosplay and Kids

Comic Con is a cosplay-friendly event, and three weeks is a workable runway for family costumes. You don't need anything elaborate — a superhero cape, a wizard robe, or a game character color scheme with face paint is enough to get into the spirit. Many families with younger kids find that even simple costumes significantly increase the kids' engagement and their own enjoyment of the event.

The convention floor features vendors, artist alleys, and exhibitors alongside the panel programming. Depending on what your kids are into — comics, anime, video games, fantasy, science fiction — there's usually something on the floor for each interest.
