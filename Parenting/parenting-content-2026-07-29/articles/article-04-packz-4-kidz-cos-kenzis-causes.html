Packz 4 Kidz: Free Backpacks for 400 Colorado Springs Kids via Kenzi's Causes

By Jamie Rivera | July 29, 2026

Colorado Springs, CO

Packz 4 Kidz is a Colorado Springs backpack drive run by Kenzi's Causes. Each year, the program aims to provide free backpacks and school supplies to 400 COS students — from early childhood education through 12th grade — at no cost to families. If your household could use the help this back-to-school season, or if you know a family who could, this is one to know about.

Who It Serves

Packz 4 Kidz specifically targets low-income Colorado Springs families. The program covers students at every level — from the youngest ECE learners through high school seniors — because back-to-school supply costs don't disappear as kids get older. For older students especially, backpacks and supplies are a real expense that isn't always easy to cover.

The organization operates on a first-come, first-served basis. Students need to be present to select their own backpack. For very young children who can't attend, a birth certificate is an accepted alternative so the family can still access the resource.

The Goal: 400 Kids

Kenzi's Causes sets a concrete goal each year — 400 COS kids equipped with backpacks and supplies — which gives the program a clear benchmark and helps donors understand the scale they're supporting. That number represents real families across Colorado Springs who start the school year a little more prepared.

Kenzi's Causes is the parent organization behind Packz 4 Kidz. The COS program is specifically focused on the Colorado Springs community, separate from any other regional events the organization may run.
