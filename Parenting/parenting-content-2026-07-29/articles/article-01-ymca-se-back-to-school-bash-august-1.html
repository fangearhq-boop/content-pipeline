Free Back to School Bash at SE Colorado Springs YMCA This Saturday — Backpacks, Pool Time, and More

By Sarah Morales | July 29, 2026

Colorado Springs, CO

Colorado Springs families have one more free back-to-school event to add to the calendar before D49's first day on Monday. The Southeast Armed Services YMCA is hosting a Back to School Bash on Saturday, August 1, at 2190 Jet Wing Dr — and it includes free backpacks, pool access, food, games, and giveaways for PreK through 12th grade students.

Two Windows, Same Day

The event runs in two back-to-back windows. Military and veteran families get the first slot, from 1 to 3:30 p.m. MT. At 3:30 p.m., the event opens to all Southeast Colorado Springs families and runs until 5:00 p.m.

You don't need to register in advance to attend. Backpacks are available while supplies last, first-come, first-served, so families who want to maximize their chances of getting one should plan to arrive when their window opens.

To receive a backpack, the student must be present and enrolled in a local Colorado Springs school. PreK through 12th grade students are eligible.

What's There

Beyond the backpacks, the event includes pool access, outdoor games, food, and giveaways. For families whose kids have been out of school activities all summer, an afternoon at the pool with community events around it is a solid send-off before the school year starts.

The Southeast Armed Services YMCA primarily serves military and veteran communities in the Southeast Colorado Springs corridor — hence the dedicated window for those families at the start of the event. The facility is located in the 80916 zip code near Peterson Space Force Base.

Not the Same as Backpack Bash Central

If you've been following back-to-school events this week, you may have seen coverage of Backpack Bash Central (cosbackpackbash.com), which runs Saturday August 1 from 9 a.m. to noon. That's a different event, organized by a different group, at a different location. Both are free. Both are Saturday. They don't overlap in time.

If your family already attended or is going to Backpack Bash Central in the morning, the Armed Services YMCA event is a separate afternoon option — particularly useful if you didn't get to the morning event in time for supplies.

What's Next

D49's first day is Monday, August 3 for kindergarteners, 6th graders, and 9th graders, with all other students starting August 4. D11 follows on August 12 and D20 on August 17–18. If your family still needs backpacks or supplies, Saturday's Armed Services YMCA event is one of the last free opportunities before D49's doors open Monday morning.
