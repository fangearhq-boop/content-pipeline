D20 Bus Assignments Go Live August 1 — Here's How to Find Yours

By Sarah Morales | July 29, 2026

Colorado Springs, CO

Academy School District 20 families: bus assignments for the 2026–27 school year are available starting Friday, August 1, through the Extended Parent Portal at asd20.org. If your student rides the bus, logging in Friday is the fastest way to confirm their route before the school year starts August 17.

How to Find Your Bus Assignment

The process takes about a minute once you're logged in. Here's the path:

  1. Go to asd20.org and log into the Extended Parent Portal
  2. Click "Bus Routing"
  3. Select your student's name
  4. Click the "Bus Routing" tab

From there, you'll see your student's bus number, the stop location, and pickup and drop-off times for the new year. If you have more than one student in D20, you can switch between their names to pull up each one.

Transportation Space Available Requests

If you need to request a route change, Transportation Space Available requests opened July 20 via the same Extended Parent Portal. That same Bus Routing section handles both the assignment lookup and any modification requests.

Space Available requests are not guaranteed — they depend on route capacity — but submitting one now gives D20 transportation the maximum lead time to evaluate it before the school year starts. If you've been meaning to submit one, the window is already open.

What's Included in the Assignment

The Bus Routing tab shows the specific information families need to plan the first week of school:

  • Bus number
  • Stop location (address or cross street)
  • Pickup time in the morning
  • Drop-off time in the afternoon

That information is what determines your morning routine, so it's worth pulling up and confirming before D20's first day rather than trying to figure it out in real time on August 17.

What's Next

D20's bus assignments go live Friday, August 1, with the school year starting two and a half weeks later on August 17. If your student's assignment looks wrong or you need to request a route change, the Extended Parent Portal at asd20.org is where all of it happens. Checking early leaves you time to sort out any issues before the first morning rush.
