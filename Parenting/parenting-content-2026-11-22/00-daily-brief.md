### STORY 2: CPSC Recalls Qpancy Children's Pajamas Over Burn Hazard — Check Before the Holiday Season
**Tier:** 1
**Type:** NEW STORY (distinct from previous pajama recalls — different brand, Recall #27-094)
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Posting Window:** 10:00 AM MT

The Consumer Product Safety Commission announced a recall of approximately 1,309 Qpancy children's pajama sets on November 19, 2026 (Recall #27-094), because they violate the mandatory flammability standard for children's sleepwear — posing a risk of serious burn injuries or death. The sets were sold on Amazon from March through August 2026 for approximately $20. Parents who purchased Qpancy pajama sets should stop dressing children in them immediately and contact Qpancy for a full refund. With the holiday season bringing new pajama gifts and cozy family traditions, the CPSC is urging parents to verify that any children's sleepwear they buy carries the proper flammability label — either "flame resistant" or "snug-fitting."

The American Academy of Pediatrics published new guidance in October 2026 on "safe and equitable pediatric use of generative AI" — meaning the AI tools increasingly used by doctors, nurses, and clinical staff during children's appointments. The guidance covers how pediatricians should use AI for things like visit note-taking and clinical decision support, while making clear that families deserve transparency when AI tools are involved in their child's care.
