D49 Opens Two New Preschool Classrooms — More Early Childhood Options for East Colorado Springs Families

By Sarah Morales

September 1, 2026

Local Schools

Families in east Colorado Springs looking for preschool options have a new reason to check with Falcon School District 49 this fall. D49 opened two new preschool classrooms at its Student Success Center to start the 2026–27 school year, expanding early childhood education in the Falcon corridor.

The expansion adds capacity at a time when preschool waitlists across Colorado Springs remain a persistent challenge for many families. For parents who haven't yet found a preschool spot this year, it's worth calling D49 directly to ask about current availability.

What the Expansion Means for COS Families

D49's Student Success Center is located in east Colorado Springs and serves families throughout the Falcon area. The addition of two new preschool classrooms follows the district's broader commitment to early childhood education, which it has been expanding over the past several school years.

Early childhood programs in public school districts typically serve children ages 3–5, including students who qualify for special education services as well as general education preschool students. Spots in district-run preschool programs can fill quickly, but districts often maintain rolling enrollment throughout the school year for families who qualify.

It's worth noting that early enrollment is always preferable, but families who contact D49 now — even after school has already started — may still find placement options, particularly if their child has an IEP or early intervention referral.

Early Childhood Options Across COS Districts

All three major Colorado Springs school districts offer early childhood programs, though structure, eligibility, and availability vary. D11 offers the Colorado Preschool Program (CPP) at multiple sites across central and south Colorado Springs. D20 operates preschool programs at several of its elementary schools, with priority given to children with disabilities and those who meet at-risk criteria. D49's new classrooms join its existing early childhood offerings.

Private preschool and licensed childcare options also operate throughout Colorado Springs. For families whose children don't qualify for district programs, Colorado's Child Care Assistance Program (CCAP) may help offset costs at licensed providers.

What's Next

Families interested in D49's new preschool classrooms should visit d49.org or call the district's Early Childhood Education department directly for availability and enrollment information. D11 early childhood program information is at d11.org, and D20 preschool information is at asd20.org. If you're not sure which district serves your address, use your street address on the relevant district website's school finder tool.
