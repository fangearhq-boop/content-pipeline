Target Recalls 211,000 Cat & Jack Toddler Sandals Over Choking Hazard

By Sarah Morales

September 1, 2026

Product Safety

If you bought Cat & Jack toddler sandals at Target this spring, check the closet before your child puts them on again. The CPSC has announced a recall of approximately 211,000 pairs of Cat & Jack toddler girls' sandals due to a choking hazard — the decorative plastic pearls on the shoes can detach, posing a risk of serious injury or death.

Target received 23 reports of the pearls falling off the sandals. No injuries have been reported, but the CPSC classified the detached pearls as a risk of serious injury or death given the age group affected — toddlers in sizes 5T through 12T.

Identifying the Recalled Sandals

The recalled sandals are tan with two raffia straps, gold buckles, and small decorative plastic pearls. They were sold in sizes 5T through 12T. If the sandals in your home match this description — tan, raffia, gold buckle, pearls — and were purchased between January and May 2026, they are likely part of this recall.

No receipt is necessary to return the sandals.

How to Get Your Refund

Stop using the sandals immediately. You have two options to receive your full refund:

Return them to any Target store. Bring the sandals to the customer service desk for a full refund. No receipt is needed. You can also call Target at 1-800-591-3869 (available 7 AM to 10 PM CT daily) to arrange a mail return with a prepaid label and a mailed refund.

Target.com also has a dedicated recall page with return instructions at target.com/help/article/000225695.

What's Next

With 211,000 pairs sold across Target stores and Target.com from January through May, these sandals are common in Colorado Springs households. If you purchased them as a gift for another family, reach out to let them know about the recall. The CPSC's full recall notice is available at cpsc.gov. For additional questions, Target's recall line is 1-800-591-3869.
