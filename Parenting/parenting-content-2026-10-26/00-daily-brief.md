### STORY 1: Costway High Chair Recalled — 3,332 Units Sold at Walmart and Target
**Tier:** T1
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Type:** NEW
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Posting window:** 8:00 AM MT

CPSC announced a recall of Costway convertible high chairs on October 22, 2026. Approximately 3,332 units were sold at Walmart and Target. The chairs lack a passive restraint, creating a fall and entrapment risk for babies. CPSC says the chairs present a risk of serious injury or death. Consumers should stop use immediately. Recall remedy is a full refund.

### STORY 2: D11 Bond Countdown — One Week Until Nov. 3 Vote
**Tier:** T2
**Pillar:** Local News
**Type:** FOLLOW-UP (2026-10-24 S2: D11 bond deep-dive → one week out, how to vote)
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Posting window:** 10:00 AM MT

D11's $775 million bond is on the November 3, 2026 coordinated election ballot — one week away. First new D11 bond since 2004. Projects include Palmer High School rebuild, Holmes Middle School rebuild, Coronado High School HVAC, and districtwide upgrades. Tax impact: ~$6.50/month per $100,000 of assessed property value. If approved, the bond frees operating funds to support a $10,000 raise for all D11 teachers. Gazette endorsed the bond. Vote by dropping your ballot at a D11 school or El Paso County Clerk location by Nov 3.

### STORY 4: CPSC Recalls ONTHEWEI Baby Bath Seats — Tip-Over Risk on Amazon
**Tier:** T2
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Type:** NEW
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Posting window:** 10:00 AM MT

CPSC recalled ONTHEWEI baby bath seats (approximately 2,000 units sold on Amazon) in October 2026. The seats have a tip-over instability hazard that poses a drowning risk to infants.
