### STORY 2: Two Teething Toy Sets Recalled — Choking Hazard to Infants
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Tier:** 1
**Scheduled:** 10:00 AM MT
**Byline:** Sarah Morales

CPSC recalled two pull-string teething toy sets sold on Amazon on July 23, 2026. Both the CuddleCubs Creations highchair teething toy set and the Aojieni Sili Factory pull-string teething toy violate mandatory toy safety standards: their silicone strings are smaller and longer than permitted and can reach the back of an infant's throat, posing a choking and respiratory-distress hazard. Parents should stop using both immediately.

**Key Facts:**
- CuddleCubs Creations highchair teething toy set (BlueOrange/YellowOrange): sold by YouRfocus on Amazon; Dec 2024–Apr 2026; $15–$19; refund: cut all silicone strings, write "DESTROYED" on body, send photo to cuddlecubscreation@126.com
- Aojieni Sili Factory Pull String Teething Toys: sold on Amazon; May 2025–Apr 2026; $11–$15; 1 report of strings reaching throat (gagging, no injury); refund: cut strings, write "RECALLED" on body, send photo to info@silifactory.com
- Both: silicone strings too small/long per mandatory toy standard; can reach back of infant's throat; choking and respiratory distress hazard
- CPSC recall date: July 23, 2026
- Source: cpsc.gov, local3news.com, wrnjradio.com

### STORY 4: Pediatricians Issue Infant Heat Safety Rules — Heat Advisory in Effect Today
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Tier:** 1
**Scheduled:** 3:00 PM MT
**Byline:** Sarah Morales

Pediatricians nationwide issued detailed heat safety guidance for infants and toddlers during summer 2026's heat waves. The guidance is directly applicable to Colorado Springs today: a NWS Heat Advisory is in effect from 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. MDT with a high of 97 degrees F and extreme UV index of 12. Key rules include: never cover a stroller with a blanket (even thin cloth traps heat inside); use the stroller's built-in canopy instead; check metal parts before placing a child in a stroller or car seat; and never leave a child in a car, even briefly, even in shade.

**Key Facts:**
- NWS Heat Advisory: in effect today July 26, 10 a.m.–9 p.m. MDT; high 97 degrees F; 10% chance of afternoon storms after 2 p.m.
- UV Index: extreme (peak UV 12) for Colorado Springs today (elevation 6,035 ft amplifies UV)
- Pediatrician guidance (widely syndicated July 20–21, 2026):
  1. Do NOT cover a stroller with a blanket — even lightweight coverings prevent air circulation and trap heat inside
  2. Use the stroller's built-in canopy for shade
  3. Check metal stroller parts, car seat buckles, and playground equipment — they absorb heat and can burn skin
  4. Run AC or open windows for 10–20 seconds before placing child in a car
  5. Pediatricians now routinely include heat as a safety topic alongside drowning and choking
- Best outdoor window for COS families today: 9 a.m.–11 a.m.
- Source: wusf.org July 20, wlrn.org July 21, local10.com July 20, krdo.com July 25, kktv.com July 26
