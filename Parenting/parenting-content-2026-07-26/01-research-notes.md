**Key Facts — Pediatrician guidance (HIGH confidence — 4+ sources):**
1. Do NOT cover stroller with blanket — even thin cloth prevents air circulation, traps heat
2. Use the stroller's built-in canopy for shade
3. Check metal parts (stroller, car seat, playground equipment) before placing child — absorb heat, can burn skin
4. Run AC or open windows 10–20 seconds before placing child in car
5. Pediatricians now include heat as standard safety topic alongside drowning and choking
