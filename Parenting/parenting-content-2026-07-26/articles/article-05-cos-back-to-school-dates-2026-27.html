COS School Start Dates for 2026-27 -- D11 August 12, D20 August 17-18, D49 in August

By Jamie Rivera July 26, 2026

The Dates for Each COS District

Colorado Springs families have fewer than three weeks until the 2026-27 school year begins. Here are the confirmed first-day dates for each of the three main Colorado Springs-area districts.

Colorado Springs School District 11

District 11 students in grades kindergarten through 12 return to school on Wednesday, August 12, 2026. This is the first day for all K-12 grades at D11. The last day of school for the 2026-27 year is May 25, 2027.

D11 is the earliest of the three districts to start this year. Families in D11 have less than three weeks from today to finalize school supplies, routines, and before-and-after-school arrangements.

Academy District 20

Academy District 20 has a two-day rollout for its first week back. Preschool, elementary, 6th grade, and 9th grade students begin on August 17, 2026. All remaining D20 students join on August 18, 2026. D20's slightly later start date gives families an additional five days compared to D11.

District 49

District 49 also opens in August 2026. The specific first day of school for D49 students was not confirmed at the time of publication -- families should check the district calendar directly at d49.org/discover-d49/district-calendar for the exact date.

D49 is also opening the new Student Success Center this August. The center adds two early childhood education classrooms available to D49 families districtwide, running on a Monday through Thursday schedule. More information is at d49.org.

D49 Student Success Center Opens in August

In addition to the regular school start date, D49 is opening the Student Success Center at the start of the 2026-27 school year. The center features two new early childhood education classrooms open to D49 families throughout the district. The program runs Monday through Thursday. This is an expansion of D49's early childhood offerings beyond its individual school campuses.

What's Next

School supply lists for D11 and D20 are typically published two to three weeks before the first day. At this point in the calendar, families can expect lists to have been posted or to appear imminently on each district's website. D11 lists are at d11.org, D20 lists are at asd20.org, and D49 lists are at d49.org.

COS Parenting will cover back-to-school resources, school supply roundups, and first-day guides in the weeks ahead. For families with children in early childhood programs, the D49 Student Success Center details are at d49.org.
