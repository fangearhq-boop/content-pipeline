Colorado Springs Sunday Market Is at Acacia Park Today — Free, 9 a.m. to 2 p.m.

By Jamie Rivera July 26, 2026

What the Sunday Market Offers

The Colorado Springs Sunday Market returns to Acacia Park today, July 26, from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. The market is free to attend and brings more than 60 vendors to downtown Colorado Springs each week, selling Colorado-grown produce, artisan foods, handmade goods, and live local music.

Acacia Park is located at 115 E Platte Ave in downtown Colorado Springs. The market runs every Sunday through October 25, 2026, and has become a regular weekend destination for families throughout the summer.

Plan Around the Heat Advisory

The National Weather Service has issued a Heat Advisory for the Colorado Springs area for today, July 26, from 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. MDT. The forecast high is 97 degrees F, with an extreme UV index. There is a 10 percent chance of afternoon showers and thunderstorms after 2 p.m.

The Sunday Market runs from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. -- its closing time coincides exactly with when the afternoon storm window begins, and the worst heat peaks in the midday window. Families who plan to attend should arrive as close to the opening as possible to shop before temperatures climb. The best outdoor window today is the 9 a.m. to 11 a.m. period before the heat advisory fully sets in.

The NWS advises wearing sunscreen, staying hydrated even if you do not feel thirsty, and avoiding strenuous activity during mid-afternoon hours.

What to Expect at the Market

The Sunday Market features a broad mix of vendors from across the region. Products typically include seasonal fresh produce from Colorado farms, locally made jams and honeys, baked goods, fresh herbs, prepared foods, handmade jewelry, pottery, clothing, and other artisan crafts. Live music is a consistent fixture of the market, with local musicians performing during market hours.

The market draws families with young children regularly. Downtown Colorado Springs has several walkable spots nearby, and Acacia Park itself provides some tree shade for early-morning browsing. Strollers are welcome throughout the market grounds.
