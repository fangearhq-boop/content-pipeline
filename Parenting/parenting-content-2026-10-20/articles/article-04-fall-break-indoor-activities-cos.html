Fall Break Indoor Activities in Colorado Springs: Day 2 Options for D11 and D49 Families

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | October 20, 2026

D11 and D49 families are on day two of fall break today, with three more days to fill through Friday, October 23. Yesterday's coverage focused on COS trail options — today we're going inside. Whether it's the weather, a kid who already ran three miles yesterday, or just a change of pace, Colorado Springs has more quality indoor options than most families cycle through in a typical week.

Arts and Culture

The Colorado Springs Fine Arts Center at Colorado College (30 W. Dale St.) is genuinely kid-friendly in a way that art museums don't always announce clearly. The Taylor Museum's collection spans western American art, photography, and rotating contemporary exhibits. Kids who've never been tend to engage better than parents expect. Admission is free for Colorado College students and affordable for families — check coloradocollege.edu/fac for current ticket prices and hours.

The American Numismatic Museum (818 N. Cascade Ave.) is one of COS's underrated free attractions. It covers the history of money from ancient coins to modern currency, and it's more visually interesting than it sounds. Free admission. Smaller than a full-day museum, but a solid 90-minute outing for families with older elementary kids.

Active Indoor Options

Skyzone in Colorado Springs is the trampoline park option for families who need their kids to physically exhaust themselves before noon. Toddler time, open jump, dodgeball, and foam pit sections. Book a time slot online — walk-in availability varies and fall break week tends to be busy.

Cave of the Winds in Manitou Springs (100 Cave of the Winds Rd., Manitou Springs) offers guided underground tours that work regardless of weather — temperature inside the cave stays around 54°F year-round, which feels like an adventure and requires a jacket. The Wind Walker Challenge, an outdoor aerial course, is weather-dependent, but the cave tours themselves aren't. Check caveofthewinds.com for current tour availability and prices.

Library Programs

Pikes Peak Library District branches are running fall programs this week for school-age kids and teens. Programs vary by branch — check ppld.org for the current event calendar and filter by your nearest location. Library programs are free and worth the 30 seconds it takes to look up the schedule. Some branches have maker spaces and STEM activities that run as drop-in sessions during school break weeks.
