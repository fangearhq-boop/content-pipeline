FDA Recalls Babies' Magic Tea Gripe Water: Undeclared Alcohol in Infant Supplement

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | October 20, 2026

The FDA issued a recall alert on October 7 for Babies' Magic Tea Gripe Water, lot number 25122bw, after testing found undeclared substances in the product. The affected lot contains 8.3% ethanol — alcohol — along with methanol and isopropanol, none of which are declared on the label. All three substances are inappropriate in an infant supplement.

If you have this product at home, the guidance is straightforward: stop using it now. Do not return it to a retail store. Dispose of it or contact the seller — the product is sold on Amazon and through the Secrets of Tea brand — for guidance on returns or refunds. The FDA has not reported any injuries associated with the affected lot.

Where the Product Is Sold

The recalled lot is sold through Amazon and through the Secrets of Tea seller account. If you purchased Babies' Magic Tea Gripe Water from either source, check the lot number printed on the bottle. Lot 25122bw is the affected product. If your lot number matches, follow the disposal guidance above and reach out to the seller for a refund.

Gripe water products are commonly used by parents of infants to address colic, gas, and stomach discomfort. The presence of undeclared alcohol at any concentration in a product intended for infants is a serious concern. Methanol and isopropanol, even in small amounts, carry additional risks in this age group.

FDA Recall Background

The FDA's October 7 alert identified the issue through routine product testing. The recall is classified as a voluntary action. No injuries have been reported in connection with lot 25122bw. The FDA recommends that consumers who purchased the affected lot contact the seller rather than returning the product to a pharmacy or retail store, as most retail locations did not carry this particular seller's inventory.

What's Next

If you've already used the affected lot, monitor your infant for any unusual symptoms and contact your pediatrician with questions. For the recall status and additional FDA guidance, visit fda.gov and search for the Babies' Magic Tea recall. If you're looking for an alternative gripe water product, ask your pediatrician — they can recommend options that have been reviewed for your child's specific age and weight.
