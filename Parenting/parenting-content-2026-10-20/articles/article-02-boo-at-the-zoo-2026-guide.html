Boo at the Zoo 2026: Everything You Need to Know Before This Weekend

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | October 20, 2026

Boo at the Zoo returns to Cheyenne Mountain Zoo this Friday through Sunday — October 23, 24, and 25 — from 4:00 to 8:30 PM each night. It's the biggest family Halloween event in Colorado Springs, and it's the kind of thing worth planning around: costumes, cooler evening air, animals in their evening routines, and trick-or-treat stations scattered across the zoo's hillside paths.

Advance tickets are available now at cmzoo.org/boo. CMZ members save $5. This event has sold out in previous years — buying ahead saves you from finding out the night is closed when you pull into the parking lot.

What to Expect

Boo at the Zoo is a ticketed evening event, so the zoo looks and feels different from a daytime visit. Lighting is warmer and more atmospheric, the paths are less crowded than peak daytime hours, and many of the animals are more active in the cooler evening temperatures. Trick-or-treat stations are set up throughout the zoo with candy and, at some stations, non-food treat options for children with allergies.

Costumes are strongly encouraged — both for kids and adults. A good portion of the fun is seeing what other families brought. The animals, for their part, do not dress up and are not consulted on the event theme.

Parking at Cheyenne Mountain Zoo fills up for popular evening events. Arriving near the 4:00 PM opening time gives you the best chance at a close spot. The zoo is on a hillside — strollers work fine on the main paths but some areas require navigating steps or steep sections. Check the CMZ website's accessibility map if you need the most level routes.

October evenings in Colorado Springs can be cold. The zoo's elevation means temperatures drop faster than in the city. Bring layers — costumes over warm base layers tend to work better than costumes over nothing. If your child's costume is a thin polyester superhero suit, a fleece underneath and a jacket that can go on and off will save the night.

Fall Break Timing

D11 and D49 families are on fall break through October 23, which means Friday's opening night falls on the last day of the break. Families who've been looking for the weekend's big activity have it — and D49 families whose break started October 13 have had almost two weeks to build anticipation. It's a natural anchor for a fall break that needed one.

What's Next

Tickets at cmzoo.org/boo. Cheyenne Mountain Zoo's full Boo at the Zoo page has current pricing, a map of trick-or-treat station locations, and accessibility details. Saturday and Sunday nights (October 24–25) are typically the busiest — if you have flexibility, Friday evening often has shorter lines at the treat stations.
