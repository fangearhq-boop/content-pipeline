Skate in the Park Is Back at Acacia Park — Kids Under 5 Skate Free This Winter

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | November 17, 2026

Skate in the Park opened its 15th season at Acacia Park in downtown Colorado Springs on Friday, November 13, and it's running daily through January 2027. The rink is part of the Acacia Park Holiday Celebration, a City of Colorado Springs Parks, Recreation & Cultural Services program that marks the official start of the holiday season downtown.

What It Costs

Admission is $15 per person and includes skate rental. There's no need to bring your own skates — rental is part of the flat admission price. Children ages 4 and under skate free when accompanied by a paying adult. Active-duty military and first responders receive a $2 discount.

For families with two or more adults and multiple kids, the per-person flat rate with rental included tends to be more cost-effective than similar rinks that separate the skate rental from the admission fee.

Why It Works for Families with Young Kids

The free-for-ages-4-and-under policy is the main reason Skate in the Park shows up consistently on COS family recommendations. Ice skating with a toddler is a high-effort, low-duration activity by definition — a 20-minute skate session is a success. Not having to pay full admission for that session makes the whole outing feel worth the logistics.

For older kids trying skating for the first time, the included rental removes the barrier of figuring out shoe size and rental lines separately. You show up, pay, pick up skates at the rental counter, and you're on the ice.

What's the Setting Like

Acacia Park is in the heart of downtown Colorado Springs, and the holiday decoration that goes up around Thanksgiving transforms the area into one of the more visually appealing spots in the city this time of year. The rink is managed by the city parks program, which means it's reliably open during posted hours and has consistent supervision.

The 15th season milestone is worth noting: this program has been a consistent part of Colorado Springs winters for a long time, and that track record shows in how it runs.

What's Next

Skate in the Park runs daily through January 2027. Hours and any closures for special events will be posted on the City of Colorado Springs Parks website at coloradosprings.gov. It's worth checking before a weekend trip, especially around holidays when hours may shift.
