Things to Do with Kids in Colorado Springs This Weekend (November 21–23)

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | November 17, 2026

The weekend of November 21–23 kicks off the stretch that takes Colorado Springs families into Thanksgiving week, and there are a few things worth getting on the calendar now. From ice skating downtown to holiday markets and craft fairs, here's what's happening and what you need to know before you go.

For the full list of things to do with kids in Colorado Springs this weekend, and for free things to do this weekend, check the regularly updated Springs Daily guides. And if you want to see everything happening this weekend, that page has the broader picture beyond family-specific events.

Skate in the Park — Acacia Park

The strongest anchor for a free family outing this weekend is Skate in the Park at Acacia Park in downtown Colorado Springs, which opened its 15th season on November 13 and is running daily through January 2027. It's part of the Acacia Park Holiday Celebration, and the holiday atmosphere downtown makes it feel like more than just a skating outing.

What you need to know: Admission is $15 per person and includes skate rental — no need to bring your own skates. Children ages 4 and under skate free when they come with a paid adult. Active-duty military and first responders get $2 off. It's a City of Colorado Springs Parks, Recreation & Cultural Services program, which means it's well-managed and reliable.

For families with kids who haven't tried ice skating yet, this is a low-stakes introduction: skate rental is included in the flat price, the rink is supervised, and the free-for-toddlers policy means you're not paying full admission for a 20-minute skate with a 3-year-old.

Vintage Market Days — Norris Penrose Event Center

Vintage Market Days runs Friday through Sunday (November 21–23) at the Norris Penrose Event Center on Lower Gold Camp Road. The market combines vintage items, boutique goods, and holiday finds across a large indoor/outdoor venue. Kids typically get in free; adults pay around $5 at the door. It's worth getting there earlier in the weekend when selection is best.

Confirm specific hours and admission pricing at norrispenrose.com/events before you go, as these may update close to the date.

Saturday Holiday Craft Fairs

Saturday, November 22 has two craft fairs running simultaneously around Colorado Springs:

  • PPBC Holiday Craft Fair — 9 AM at East United Methodist Church, 1505 E Monument St, Colorado Springs. Free or low-cost entry. Good for local artisan gifts.
  • Rampart Craft Bazaar — 8250 Lexington Dr, Colorado Springs. Free entry. Local vendors with holiday goods.

Both events are on the smaller side and work well as a morning outing before Skate in the Park or the market. Confirm hours with the organizers before heading out, as times can shift close to the event date.
