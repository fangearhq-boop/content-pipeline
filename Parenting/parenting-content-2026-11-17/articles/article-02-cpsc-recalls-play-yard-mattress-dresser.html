CPSC Recalls Play Yard Mattresses and Dressers: What Colorado Springs Families Need to Know

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | November 17, 2026

Two more CPSC recalls came through this week affecting products that sit in a lot of Colorado Springs homes — a play yard mattress and a multi-drawer dresser. If you have either product, here's what you need to know and what to do right now.

Mommy & Me Pack & Play Mattresses

The Consumer Product Safety Commission has recalled Mommy & Me Pack & Play mattresses for suffocation and entrapment hazards in play yards. Play yard mattresses are used by many families from infancy through early toddlerhood — often as a travel sleep space or secondary crib alternative.

Suffocation and entrapment are among the highest-severity hazard categories in CPSC recalls. If you have a Mommy & Me Pack & Play mattress, stop using it immediately. Visit cpsc.gov/Recalls to find the specific recall entry and the remedy — this will typically be a refund or a replacement product.

HAUOMS Eight-Drawer Dressers

The CPSC has also recalled HAUOMS eight-drawer dressers — 1,392 units — for tip-over and child entrapment risk. Dresser tip-overs are a leading cause of child injury in the home; heavy furniture can fall when a child pulls on a drawer, and entrapment underneath can happen quickly.

If you have a HAUOMS eight-drawer dresser, check the recall page at cpsc.gov/Recalls to confirm whether your model is affected. While waiting for a remedy, anchor the dresser to the wall with a furniture anti-tip strap if possible — hardware stores and Amazon carry inexpensive options.

How to Stay Current on CPSC Recalls

This is now the third week in a row with CPSC recalls affecting families with young children. November has seen recalls on infant walkers, safety gates, soother clips, teether sets, RC car batteries, play yard mattresses, and dressers. The pace isn't unusual — the CPSC issues recalls year-round — but it underscores why keeping the recall database bookmarked is worth the effort.

The cpsc.gov/Recalls database is searchable by product category, brand, and recall number. You can also sign up for email alerts on the site. Five minutes spent there now can save a lot of worry later.
