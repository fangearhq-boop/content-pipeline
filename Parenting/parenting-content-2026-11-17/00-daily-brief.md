### STORY 4: AAP 2026–27 Flu Guidance — What COS Families Need to Know Right Now

**Tier:** 2
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Posting window:** 3:00 PM MT
**Type:** NEW STORY

**Summary:** The American Academy of Pediatrics has updated its flu guidance for 2026–27, and Colorado Springs families should know the key changes. All children 6 months and older are recommended for annual flu vaccination. Children ages 6 months–8 years getting their first flu shot this season (or who only had one dose before July 1, 2026) need two doses. Colorado's 2026–27 season is off to an early, concerning start.

**Key Facts:**
- AAP recommends annual flu vaccination for all children 6 months and older — 2026–27 policy, Pediatrics journal
- Children 6 months–8 years, first time vaccinated (or one dose before July 1, 2026): need 2 doses this season
- 2025–26 season: 190 pediatric flu deaths nationally; only 49.4% of children vaccinated
- Colorado 2026–27: first pediatric death already reported (elementary-aged, unvaccinated)
- Antivirals updated: baloxavir now available as generic for 2026–27
- All 2026–27 U.S. flu vaccines are trivalent with updated compositions

**Sources:**
