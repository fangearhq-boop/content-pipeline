### STORY 1: Kith Children's Sleepwear Recall — Burn Hazard (TIER 1)
**Status:** NEW STORY
**Pillar:** National Parenting (Product Safety)
Kids Kithmas Plaid Lounge Sets recalled by Kith Retail due to violating mandatory flammability standards for children's sleepwear. About 130 sets sold at Kith stores and online from December 2025 through January 2026 for approximately $75. Consumers should immediately stop use, cut the garments in half, photograph the destruction, and email recalls@kithnyc.com for a full refund via gift card. No injuries reported. Burn hazard from loose-fitting sleepwear contacting ignition sources (stoves, candles, matches).

### STORY 2: Dresser Tip-Over Recalls — Two Brands, Same Deadly Risk (TIER 1)
**Status:** NEW STORY
**Pillar:** National Parenting (Product Safety)
Two separate dresser recalls both involve 6-drawer units that can tip over and trap or crush children. Gizoon Direct recalled 6-drawer double dressers due to tip-over and entrapment hazards. Nanjing Wu Hai (Alanca brand) recalled 6-drawer wood dressers because they do not meet federal clothing storage unit standards. Stop using immediately if not anchored to a wall. Children have been seriously injured and killed by dresser tip-overs nationally. Contact manufacturers for refunds.
