CPSC Issues Safety Warning on Lucakuins Infant Bath Seats
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post If you have a Lucakuins infant bath seat, the CPSC wants you to stop using it right now. 🚨 The agency issued a safety warning — not a full recall — after the company declined to cooperate with a voluntary recall process. That's the same path we saw with the bblüv high chair two weeks ago. The hazards are drowning and entrapment, which means this is a serious enough risk that CPSC went public even without company cooperation. This is now the fourth baby product safety action in roughly two weeks in the COS Parenting pipeline — following ONTHEWEI bath seats (Oct 9), Papablic bedside sleepers (Oct 15), and bblüv high chairs (Oct 15). The pattern is worth paying attention to. What to do: stop using the product immediately. Set it aside, don't pass it on to another family, and visit CPSC.gov or search "Lucakuins CPSC" for the full warning and remedy details. Have you done a recent check of baby gear in your home for open recalls or warnings? ---

D49 Earns Nine More Purple Star Designations
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post If your family is military-connected, here's some good news from D49 this week. 🌟 District 49 just earned nine more Purple Star School designations from the Colorado Department of Education, putting it among the largest Purple Star program footprints in the state. For families who haven't come across this designation before: Purple Star recognizes schools that actively support military-connected students — the kids whose parents are at Fort Carson or Peterson Space Force Base, who may have moved three times in four years, or who are going through a parent's deployment. What the designation actually means on the ground: a school with Purple Star status has a designated military family point of contact, staff trained in military family transitions, and community connections to support students who are new to the district or preparing to leave. D49 has long served a significant military family population because of its geographic footprint near the bases. The expansion of Purple Star campuses means more families will have a recognized, structured support system in place from the first day their kids walk in the door. Does your family use a D49 Purple Star school? We'd love to hear what the support has looked like. ---

D11 Schools Off Colorado Watchlist — No High School in Priority Improvement Status
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Here's a D11 milestone that didn't get enough attention this fall: for the first time in about a decade, no Colorado Springs School District 11 high school is on the state's accountability watchlist. Mitchell High School moved out of Priority Improvement status — a significant shift for a campus that had faced persistent accountability pressure. Ten D11 schools improved their performance ratings in the state's most recent report. No D11 high school is now in either the Priority Improvement or Turnaround category. For D11 families and especially Mitchell families, this represents years of work by teachers, administrators, and students. The state accountability system exists specifically to flag when schools aren't meeting performance thresholds — moving off the list isn't just paperwork, it reflects real gains in the data. The district still has work ahead, and the accountability system will continue to track progress. But this is the kind of milestone worth sharing with the families who've stayed in D11 schools through harder years. Did your family stick with a D11 school during the tougher accountability years? What made you stay? ---

D11 Bond: 7 Days to Vote — How to Return Your Ballot
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post The D11 bond vote is seven days away, and if you're planning to vote, it's worth knowing exactly how the process works right now. Colorado sends mail ballots automatically — yours should already be in your house. You've got a few options for returning it: **Drop boxes:** El Paso County Clerk drop boxes are open now, ahead of Election Day. You can also drop your ballot at any D11 school location. These are the fastest options. **Mail:** You can still mail your ballot, but it needs to be postmarked by November 3 to count. With a week left, mailing it today or tomorrow is fine — but waiting until Election Day is risky. **In person:** If you haven't received your ballot or lost it, you can go to the El Paso County Clerk's office to get a replacement and vote in person. **Check your status:** El Paso County's election page lets you track whether your returned ballot has been received and counted. Worth checking after you drop it off. The bond itself is $775 million — Palmer HS rebuild, Holmes MS rebuild, Coronado HVAC upgrades, and districtwide improvements. For those keeping track, we covered what the bond funds in detail on Monday. Have you already returned your D11 ballot, or are you still sitting on it? ---
