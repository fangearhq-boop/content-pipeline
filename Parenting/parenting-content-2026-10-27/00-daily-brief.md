### STORY 1: CPSC Issues Safety Warning on Lucakuins Infant Bath Seats
**Tier:** T1
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Type:** NEW
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Posting window:** 8:00 AM MT

The CPSC has issued a safety warning on Lucakuins infant bath seats, directing consumers to stop using the product immediately due to drowning and entrapment hazards. Unlike a full recall, this is a CPSC warning after the company did not cooperate with a voluntary recall. Parents should stop use immediately and check for the product in their bathrooms. This is the fourth baby bath and sleep product safety action in approximately two weeks, following ONTHEWEI bath seats (Oct 9), Papablic bedside sleepers (Oct 15), and the bblüv high chair (Oct 15).

### STORY 2: D49 Earns Nine More Purple Star Designations
**Tier:** T2
**Pillar:** Local News
**Type:** NEW
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Posting window:** 10:00 AM MT

District 49 has received nine additional Purple Star School designations from the Colorado Department of Education, expanding its total to one of the largest Purple Star program footprints in the state. The Purple Star designation recognizes schools that support military-connected students and families with dedicated staff contacts, transition support, and community outreach. D49 serves a large military family population due to its proximity to Fort Carson and Peterson Space Force Base. The expanded designations mean more campuses now have specific resources for families dealing with deployment, frequent moves, and reintegration.

### STORY 3: D11 Schools Off Colorado Watchlist — No High School in Priority Improvement Status
**Tier:** T2
**Pillar:** Local News
**Type:** NEW
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Posting window:** 8:00 AM MT

For the first time in approximately a decade, no Colorado Springs School District 11 high school is on the state's Accountability Clock or Priority Improvement watchlist. Mitchell High School, which was previously flagged in the Priority Improvement category, has moved out of that designation following gains in academic performance. Ten D11 schools improved their ratings in the most recent state report. The shift marks a notable change for a district that has faced persistent accountability pressure at the high school level.
