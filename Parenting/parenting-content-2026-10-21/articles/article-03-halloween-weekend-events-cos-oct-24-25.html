Coffin Races, Candy Crawls, and More: Your Family's Halloween Weekend Guide for COS — Oct 24-25

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | October 21, 2026

The last big Halloween weekend before October 31 is packed across Colorado Springs and the Pikes Peak region — from a 32-year-old coffin-racing tradition to monster trucks to ballet.

This Saturday and Sunday mark the final full Halloween weekend before October 31, and Colorado Springs delivers. Whether your family wants the full Halloween spectacle, a free afternoon with candy, or something unexpectedly sophisticated, there are options across every age range and budget.

Here's what's happening, organized to help you plan.

The Can't-Miss: Emma Crawford Coffin Races — Saturday, Oct 24

If you've never been to the Emma Crawford Coffin Races in Manitou Springs, this is the year to go. Now in its 32nd year, the event draws roughly 10,000 spectators to watch teams of five race hand-decorated coffins uphill through the historic downtown. The race honors the legend of Emma Crawford, whose casket reportedly washed down a mountain during a flood in the early 1900s.

The racing runs from 10 AM to 5 PM on Saturday, October 24. Road closures in Manitou Springs begin at 9 AM, so plan to arrive early. Admission is free. Prizes go to the fastest team and to winners in categories including Best Emma, Best Coffin, and Best Entourage. It's one of the more genuinely weird and wonderful Colorado traditions, and it's completely free — which makes it one of the best family outings of the fall season.

Free Events This Weekend

Beyond the coffin races, there are several free options across the weekend:

  • Downtown COS Candy Crawl — Saturday, Oct 24, 2-5 PM. More than 20 downtown Colorado Springs businesses participate in this family-friendly trick-or-treat walkabout. Good for toddlers through elementary-age kids.
  • Pumpkins in the Park — Saturday, Oct 24, noon-6 PM, Bancroft Park/Old Colorado City. Free, community-focused fall festival.
  • First Pres Trunk or Treat — Saturday, Oct 24, 3-5 PM. Free, family-friendly.
  • Garden of the Gods Trunk or Treat — Sunday, Oct 25, 1 PM. Free.

A Note on the Little Ones

Families with toddlers and preschoolers should note that the Little Learners: Monster Mash event — featuring a spooky story time, monster dance party, and cookie decorating for ages 3 to 5 — is happening tomorrow, Wednesday, October 22. Check the current listing for time and location. D11's fall break wraps up Friday, October 23, so this midweek event is well-timed for families still off school.

What's Next

Halloween itself falls on Saturday, October 31 this year — 10 days away. Most trunk-or-treats and neighborhood events cluster the two Saturdays before (this weekend and next). After this weekend, COS events will shift to Oct 30-31 neighborhood trick-or-treating, the Boo at the Zoo final nights, and school and church-sponsored parties. Check visitcos.com for a running list of events as the week progresses. If you're heading to Venetucci Farm or Broadmoor World Arena this weekend, confirm current ticket pricing before you go — some events have adjusted pricing from prior years.
