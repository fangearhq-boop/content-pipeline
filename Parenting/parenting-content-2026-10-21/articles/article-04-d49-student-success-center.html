D49 Opened a New Student Success Center This Fall — Here's What Families Need to Know

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | October 21, 2026

District 49's Student Success Center brought several specialized programs into one permanent facility this August — a change that affects families in PEAK, Elevates, preschool, and the Base49 care program.

When school started this August, District 49 quietly opened a significant new facility: the Student Success Center, located at the former Falcon Elementary site. The change brings several of the district's specialized programs — ones that had previously been scattered across aging temporary structures — into one permanent building.

If your family is enrolled in or considering any of these programs, here's what changed and what to know heading into the second quarter of the school year.

What Programs Are at the Student Success Center

The SSC houses four distinct program areas:

  • PEAK (gifted and advanced learning) — D49's program for students identified as gifted or in need of advanced instruction is now based at the SSC.
  • Elevates (specialized instruction and life skills) — The program serving students who need specialized instruction, including life-skills classes, has a dedicated space in the new facility.
  • Preschool — Two full-time preschool classrooms operate at the SSC.
  • Base49 (before and after school care) — The district's before- and after-care program is also based at the SSC.

The building includes a full gymnasium, cafeteria, and life-skills equipment — the kind of purpose-built infrastructure that supports these programs in ways modular classrooms could not.

What Happened to Falcon Elementary

Falcon Elementary School of Technology, which was previously at this location, has relocated. The school is now operating at the Bennett Ranch Elementary campus. D49 communications from the start of the school year confirm the transition, and the updated campus assignments are reflected in the district's website at d49.org.

The consolidation reflects a broader D49 goal: moving specialized programs out of aging modular buildings and into a facility designed to serve them long-term. For PEAK and Elevates families in particular, the move to a permanent building with appropriate space and equipment is a meaningful upgrade.

D49 Board Meets Today

The District 49 board of education holds its regular meeting today, October 21. Families interested in district updates — including any agenda items related to the SSC or program operations — can check d49.org for the meeting agenda and public comment information.

What's Next

For D49 families new to any of the Student Success Center programs, the district's main website (d49.org) has the most current contact information, transportation details, and program enrollment guidance. If you have questions specific to PEAK eligibility, Elevates services, preschool registration, or Base49 scheduling, reaching out directly to the SSC or the district's student services office is the best starting point. The facility represents a long-term commitment to these programs — and for families who've navigated temporary modular classrooms in prior years, that permanence is worth noting.
