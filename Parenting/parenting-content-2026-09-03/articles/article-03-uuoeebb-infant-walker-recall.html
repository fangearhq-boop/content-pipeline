CPSC Recalls About 2,650 Uuoeebb Infant Walkers — Stop Using Immediately

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | September 3, 2026

The Uuoeebb Infant Walker, sold on Amazon by BaoD from December 2024 through September 2025, has been recalled because it can roll through a standard doorway and fail to stop at the edge of a step — and leg openings are large enough to entrap a child's head. Families should stop using the walker immediately and contact the seller for a full refund.

The Consumer Product Safety Commission has announced a recall of approximately 2,650 Uuoeebb Infant Walkers after determining the product violates the mandatory safety standard for infant walkers in two dangerous ways.

First: the walker can fit through a standard doorway and doesn't stop at the edge of a step, creating a fall hazard. Second: the leg openings are wide enough that a child can slip down into the walker until their head becomes entrapped. These aren't design quirks — the CPSC issued a stop-use warning even before the formal recall, and the formal recall is now in place.

How to Identify the Product

The recalled walkers are collapsible and have a round base with eight small wheels and seven adjustable height settings. They come in gray, black, and pink. The safest way to confirm your walker is the affected unit is to check under the seat for a tracking label that reads "Production Batch: 7654." If yours shows that batch number, the recall applies to you.

What to Do Right Now

The CPSC's instructions are straightforward. Stop using the walker immediately. Then disassemble it and remove the fabric seat. Write the word "Recalled" on the top of the tray in permanent marker. Take a photo of the walker in this state and email it to Uuoeebbrecalls@outlook.com. BaoD will process a full refund once they receive the photo.

The full recall listing is at cpsc.gov under Recall No. 26-141.

A Note About Infant Walkers in General

Infant walkers have a complicated safety history. The American Academy of Pediatrics has recommended against using them for years, citing fall hazards, delayed motor development, and the exact entrapment issues described in this recall. If you're looking for alternatives that encourage movement in infants who aren't yet walking, stationary activity centers and floor play mats are the options most pediatricians recommend.

That's not a judgment on any family's choices — it's just useful context as you decide what to replace this with.

What's Next

If you bought an Uuoeebb infant walker from Amazon between December 2024 and September 2025, contact BaoD via Uuoeebbrecalls@outlook.com now. The refund process is simple and doesn't require you to return the product — just the photo. Share this recall with parent groups and friends who may have this product in their homes.
