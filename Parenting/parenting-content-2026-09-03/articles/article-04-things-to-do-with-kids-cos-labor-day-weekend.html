Things to Do with Kids in Colorado Springs This Weekend (Sept 4–6, 2026)

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | September 3, 2026

Labor Day weekend in Colorado Springs means one thing above all others: 65 hot air balloons floating above Memorial Park. But the 50th annual Lift Off isn't the only thing on the docket. Here's what's worth your time from Friday through Sunday — and into Labor Day Monday.

This is a long weekend, and Colorado Springs has a lot going on. The full full list of things to do with kids in Colorado Springs this weekend is at Springs Daily, but here's what we think is worth building your weekend around.

Two of the picks are free. One requires a drive into the mountains. All of them are genuinely worth it.

Labor Day Lift Off — 50th Anniversary

The Labor Day Lift Off turns 50 this year, and it remains one of the most spectacular free family events in the state of Colorado. About 65 hot air balloons gather at Memorial Park for mass launches each morning — Saturday, Sunday, and Monday. Balloon launches start at 7 AM, and the recommendation is to arrive by 6:30 AM if you want a prime viewing spot before the chase vehicles and crowds fill the park.

Beyond the morning launches, the festival includes USAF Wings of Blue skydiving demonstrations, live music throughout the day, and food vendors across the park. Saturday and Sunday evenings feature night glows, when the grounded balloon envelopes are lit from within after dark — an effect that young kids find genuinely magical.

New this year: Orangetheory is hosting a 5K race on Sunday morning around Prospect Lake. If anyone in your group is a runner, it's a fun way to start the day before the balloons go up.

  • When: Saturday–Monday, Sept 5–7. Balloon launches at 7 AM daily.
  • Where: Memorial Park, Colorado Springs
  • Cost: Free
  • Ages: All ages. Stroller-accessible.

Commonwheel Artists Co-op Art Festival — Manitou Springs

Running concurrently with the Lift Off, the Commonwheel Art Festival fills Memorial Park in Manitou Springs (not the same Memorial Park — this one is at 502 Manitou Ave.) with more than 100 artist booths, live local music, kids activities, and prize drawings. It runs Saturday through Monday from 10 AM to 5 PM each day, and admission is free.

If you're looking for something lower-key than the balloon chaos, Commonwheel makes for a pleasant morning — let the kids participate in the activities, pick out a piece of art, and walk along Fountain Creek, which runs adjacent to the park.

  • When: Saturday–Monday, Sept 5–7. 10 AM – 5 PM daily.
  • Where: Memorial Park, Manitou Springs (502 Manitou Ave.)
  • Cost: Free
  • Ages: All ages.

Victor Plein Air Show and Sale

About an hour south of Colorado Springs, the historic mining town of Victor is hosting its annual nationally recognized plein air art show and sale Saturday through Monday at the Victor Elks Lodge (181 N 3rd St). If you've never driven up to Victor, Labor Day weekend is a good excuse — the mountain scenery through Cripple Creek is striking, the town is small and walkable, and the art show is open to the public.

This one's more of a half-day trip than a quick stop, but it pairs well with a drive if you want to give the family something different from the Memorial Park crowds.

  • When: Saturday–Monday, Sept 5–7
  • Where: Victor Elks Lodge, 181 N 3rd St, Victor, CO
  • Cost: Free to browse
  • Ages: Best for older kids who can handle a gallery-style setting
  • Drive time: About 1 hour from downtown Colorado Springs

Outdoor Play and Hiking — Always on the List

The weather in early September in Colorado Springs is about as good as it gets — typically warm but not hot, with manageable afternoon wind. If you'd rather skip the events and just get outside, Cheyenne Mountain State Park is a reliable pick for families with kids who can handle a moderate trail. America the Beautiful Park, adjacent to Prospect Lake in downtown, is free, always open, and has the kind of interactive play installations that eat up an afternoon without costing anything.
