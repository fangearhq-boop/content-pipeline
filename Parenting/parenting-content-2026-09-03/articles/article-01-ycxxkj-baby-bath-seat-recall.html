CPSC Recalls About 8,960 YCXXKJ Baby Bath Seats Over Drowning Risk

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | September 3, 2026

The YCXXKJ Baby Bath Seat, model YD-1958, has been recalled because it's unstable and can tip over during use, posing a risk of serious injury or death from drowning. Families who bought this on Amazon should stop using it now.

If you've got a baby bath seat from Amazon that you bought in the last couple of years, it's worth checking the brand name. The Consumer Product Safety Commission has announced the recall of approximately 8,960 YCXXKJ Baby Bath Seats after finding that they violate the mandatory safety standard for infant bath seats.

The seats are unstable and can tip over while a child is in them. That instability creates a drowning risk that the CPSC says is serious enough to stop use immediately. No injuries have been reported, but the agency is urging families who have this product to act now.

What the Product Looks Like

The recalled bath seat comes in four colors: blue, gray, pink, and yellow. It has two detachable arms that function as a restraint, four suction cups on the bottom of the seat, and three toy attachments — a duck, a turtle, and a whale. If you're not sure whether your seat is the affected model, check the back of the seat for a tracking label that reads "Model: YD-1958."

How to Get Your Refund

Here's exactly what the CPSC says to do:

First, stop using the seat right away. Then, send two photos to BenTalk at bentalkhere@outlook.com. The first photo should show the seat from the front with the word "Recalled" written on it in permanent marker. The second photo should show the seat disassembled. BenTalk will process a full refund once they receive your photos.

If you'd rather call, BenTalk's customer line is 213-579-2588. They're available Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM Eastern time.

You can also find the full recall listing at cpsc.gov (Recall No. 26-146).

What's Next

If you bought this bath seat from Amazon between May 2024 and October 2025, don't wait. Contact BenTalk, get the refund started, and don't put the seat back in use. If you're not sure where to find a safe replacement, look for bath seats that carry the ASTM International infant bath seat safety standard (ASTM F1967) on their packaging — that's the baseline you want to see.
