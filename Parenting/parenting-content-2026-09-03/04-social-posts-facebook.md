## STORY 1: YCXXKJ Baby Bath Seat Recall

**Long-Form Post**

If you've got a baby bath seat from Amazon with the name YCXXKJ on it, please check this right now.

The CPSC has recalled about 8,960 YCXXKJ Baby Bath Seats (model YD-1958) after finding they can tip over while in use — posing a risk of serious injury or death from drowning. These were sold on Amazon from May 2024 through October 2025 for $34 to $40. Colors were blue, gray, pink, and yellow. The model number appears on a tracking label on the back.

Here's what you need to do: Stop using the seat immediately. To get your full refund, email two photos to bentalkhere@outlook.com — the first with "Recalled" written on the front in permanent marker, the second showing the seat disassembled. You can also call BenTalk at 213-579-2588, Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM ET.

The recall number is 26-146. Details are at cpsc.gov.

Drop this post in any Colorado Springs parent group you're in — we want every family who has this seat to see it. Have you checked yours?

## STORY 2: AAP 2026-2027 Flu Vaccine Guidance

**Long-Form Post**

September is here, which means flu season is coming — and the American Academy of Pediatrics wants every family to get ahead of it now.

The AAP just released its 2026-27 influenza guidance, recommending annual flu vaccination for all kids starting at 6 months of age. The goal is to get everyone vaccinated by the end of October, before flu season typically peaks. If your child is under 9 and has never had a flu shot before, they'll need two doses this year — so it's worth scheduling early.

Why does it matter? Last season was rough. The 2025-26 flu season was high-severity, with 190 pediatric deaths in the U.S. Of those children, 85% had not been vaccinated. And CDC researchers found that flu vaccines reduce the risk of a child dying from influenza by 80%.

In Colorado Springs — at over 6,000 feet — respiratory illnesses can hit kids harder. Back-to-school season across D11, D20, and D49 means more exposure, not less. The timing is right to get this taken care of.

Check in with your pediatrician or a local pharmacy this month. Have your kids had their flu shots scheduled yet?

## STORY 3: Uuoeebb Infant Walker Recall

**Long-Form Post**

Another walker recall — and this one has a deadly hazard that the CPSC says parents need to act on immediately.

The YCXXKJ Uuoeebb Infant Walker — sold on Amazon by a seller called BaoD from December 2024 through September 2025 — has been recalled because it can roll through a standard doorway and fail to stop at the edge of a step. That's a fall hazard. And the leg openings are large enough that a child can slip through until their head becomes trapped. About 2,650 units are affected. They're collapsible walkers with a round base, eight wheels, and seven height settings — sold in gray, black, and pink for $60 to $90.

Here's what to do: Stop using it immediately. Disassemble the walker, remove the fabric seat, and write "Recalled" on the top of the tray. Then email a photo to Uuoeebbrecalls@outlook.com for a full refund. The CPSC recall number is 26-141.

If you're not sure whether your walker is affected, look for the tracking label under the seat that reads "Production Batch: 7654."

Have you checked your walker? Tag a parent friend who might have one.

## STORY 4: Weekend Roundup — Things to Do Sept 4–6, 2026

**Long-Form Post**

It's a big Labor Day weekend in Colorado Springs — and if you're wondering what to do with the kids, we've put the whole guide together for you.

The centerpiece is the 50th annual Labor Day Lift Off at Memorial Park, running Saturday through Monday. Free admission, roughly 65 hot air balloons, and daily launches starting at 7 AM — get there by 6:30 if you want a good spot. The balloons light up after dark on Saturday and Sunday evenings for the night glows. There are live music and food vendors throughout the weekend, plus USAF Wings of Blue skydiving demonstrations. And new this year, Orangetheory is hosting a 5K race on Sunday morning around Prospect Lake.

If you're looking for something a little different, the Commonwheel Art Festival is happening in Manitou Springs the same weekend — Saturday through Monday, 10 AM to 5 PM, free. More than 100 artists, live music, and kids activities in Memorial Park in Manitou.

Feeling adventurous? The Victor Plein Air Show & Sale at the Victor Elks Lodge (181 N 3rd St, Victor) runs all three days and makes for a scenic mountain drive — about an hour from COS.

## STORY 5: America the Beautiful Park

**Long-Form Post**

We talk a lot about Cheyenne Mountain Zoo and Garden of the Gods — and both are worth every trip. But there's a free, downtown Colorado Springs park that deserves more attention: America the Beautiful Park.

It's on the western edge of downtown, right next to Prospect Lake. The interactive art installations there — part of the "Quirks" project — include spinner bowls, hammocks, and colorful tactile elements that kids genuinely love. Toddlers can spin themselves silly. Older kids can wander, climb, and explore. You can bring a picnic, walk the path around Prospect Lake, or just let the afternoon disappear.

It's one of those spots where the kids burn energy, the grown-ups actually relax, and nobody has to spend money to make it happen.

The park is especially nice in September — the summer crowds thin out, the temperatures are manageable, and on a clear day you get a view of Pikes Peak that's hard to beat.
