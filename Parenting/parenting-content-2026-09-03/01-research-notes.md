# Research Notes — September 3, 2026

## Story 1: YCXXKJ Baby Bath Seat Recall

**Source:** CPSC.gov — Recall No. 26-146
**Confidence:** HIGH

- Product: YCXXKJ Baby Bath Seat, Model YD-1958
- Units recalled: approximately 8,960
- Hazard: Unstable — can tip over while in use, posing risk of serious injury or death from drowning
- No incidents or injuries reported at time of recall
- Colors: blue, gray, pink, yellow
- Features: two detachable arms (restraint), four suction cups on bottom, three toys (duck, turtle, whale)
- Tracking label on back: "Model: YD-1958"
- Sold: Amazon.com by BenTalk (also listed as "BenTalk" seller account)
- Sale dates: May 2024 through October 2025
- Price: $34 to $40
- Remedy: Full refund. Stop using immediately. Send two photos to bentalkhere@outlook.com (photo 1: write "Recalled" on front in permanent marker; photo 2: disassembled bath seat). Or call 213-579-2588, M–F 9 AM–5 PM ET.
- CPSC X post confirmed: "RECALL: YCXXKJ Baby Bath Seats"

## Story 2: AAP 2026-2027 Influenza Vaccine Recommendations

**Source:** AAP (publications.aap.org), HealthyChildren.org, Clinical Advisor
**Confidence:** HIGH

- Policy: Annual influenza vaccination of all children without medical contraindications, starting at 6 months of age
- Updated guidance released for 2026-27 season (policy statement doi: 10.1542/peds.2026-078778)
- Vaccine type: any licensed, age- and health-status-appropriate vaccine; no preference for product or formulation
- Timing: Ideally received by end of October; offer as soon as vaccine is available
- Children who need two doses (first-time vaccinees under age 9) should be prioritized for early scheduling
- Context: 2025-26 season was high-severity — 190 pediatric deaths; 85% of children who died were not vaccinated
- CDC data: flu vaccines reduce pediatric flu death risk by 80%
- Additional update: detailed new guidance for immunocompromised children
- Vaccines for Children program: flu shots available at no cost for eligible kids

## Story 3: Uuoeebb Infant Walker Recall

**Source:** CPSC.gov — Recall No. 26-141
**Confidence:** HIGH

- Product: Uuoeebb Infant Walkers
- Units recalled: approximately 2,650
- Manufacturer: Hunan Suihuo E-commerce Co., Ltd., China (doing business as BaoD)
- Hazard: Walker can fit through a standard doorway and fails to stop at edge of a step → deadly fall hazard; leg openings allow child to slip down until head becomes entrapped → death risk
- Design: Collapsible, round base, 8 wheels, 7 adjustable height settings
- Colors: gray, black, pink
- Tracking label: "Production Batch: 7654" under the seat
- Sold: Amazon.com by BaoD, December 2024 through September 2025
- Price: $60 to $90
- Remedy: Stop using immediately. Disassemble walker, remove fabric seat, write "Recalled" on top of tray, email photo to Uuoeebbrecalls@outlook.com for full refund.
- CPSC also issued a separate Warning (before the formal recall) instructing consumers to stop immediately.

## Story 5: America the Beautiful Park, Colorado Springs

**Source:** VisitCOS.com, Springs Event Hub
**Confidence:** MEDIUM (park features confirmed; some details are general knowledge)

- Location: Western edge of downtown Colorado Springs; adjacent to Prospect Lake
- Free admission, open year-round
- Features:
  - Interactive art installation "Quirks" — community art project with colorful, tactile elements
  - Hammocks
  - Spinner bowls
  - Turntables
  - Open lawn space for picnicking and play
