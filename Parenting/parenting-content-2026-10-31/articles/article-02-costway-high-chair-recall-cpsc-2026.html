CPSC Recalls About 3,332 Costway Convertible High Chairs for Entrapment and Fall Hazards

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting

October 31, 2026

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission issued a recall of approximately 3,332 Costway convertible high chairs on October 22, citing entrapment and fall hazards that violate the federal mandatory safety standard for high chairs. If you have a Costway high chair that converts into a table-and-chair set, stop using it now and check the CPSC website for remedy information.

What's Being Recalled

The recall covers Costway convertible high chairs — models that convert into a small table and chair set as a child grows. The CPSC assigned this recall number 27-040. The chairs were manufactured in China.

The CPSC states that the chairs violate the mandatory federal safety standard for high chairs, creating both an entrapment hazard and a fall risk. Parents should stop using the recalled chairs immediately, regardless of whether they've noticed any problems.

What to Do

The remedy for this recall is replacement. To claim it, contact Costway or visit the CPSC recall page directly at cpsc.gov for full instructions on how to return or exchange the product. The CPSC recall listing provides the most current contact information and remedy details.

If you're not sure whether your chair is the recalled model, the safest approach is to stop using it until you've confirmed with the manufacturer. High chairs that convert to table sets are the affected style — standard high chairs that don't convert are not part of this recall.
