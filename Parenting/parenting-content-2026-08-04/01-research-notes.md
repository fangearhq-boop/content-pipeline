### STORY 2: CPSC Recall — TOMY Boon PIVOT Toddler Tower Step Stools

**Confidence: HIGH** (CPSC.gov official recall page, Consumer Reports)

- **Product:** TOMY Boon PIVOT Collapsible Toddler Tower Kitchen Step Stools [HIGH]
- **Units recalled:** 116,908 [HIGH]
- **Hazard:** Tip-over and fall hazard — towers can tip while a toddler is standing on them; risk of serious injury and death [HIGH]
- **Incidents/injuries:** 11 reports of instability or leaning; no injuries yet reported (as of recall date) [HIGH]
- **Where sold:** Amazon, Target, Babylist [HIGH]
- **Sale period:** January 2023 through June 2026 [HIGH]
- **Price:** Approximately $150 [HIGH]
- **Remedy:** Stop using immediately; TOMY will provide a free stabilizing repair part and instructions [HIGH]
- **What to do:** Contact TOMY for free repair part before resuming use [HIGH]
- **CPSC page:** cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/TOMY-Recalls-Boon-PIVOT... [HIGH]

**Broader toddler tower recall context (HIGH — Consumer Reports):**
- 11 total toddler tower product recalls since September 2025 [HIGH]
- Combined total units across all 11 brands: 434,176 [HIGH]
- Combined documented incidents: 141 [HIGH]
- Combined child injuries: 54 [HIGH]
- Consumer Reports is urging the CPSC to finalize a mandatory safety standard for this product category [HIGH]

**Also active — related recalls worth monitoring (MEDIUM — prior pipeline coverage):**

- Sanven/Vevor Baby Swings: suffocation hazard, inclined sleeper ban
