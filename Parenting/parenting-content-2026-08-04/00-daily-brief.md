### STORY 2: CPSC Recall — TOMY Boon PIVOT Toddler Tower Step Stools (116,908 Units)
- **Tier:** 1 (Breaking/National Parenting)
- **Pillar:** National Parenting
- **Classification:** NEW STORY (different from Aug 3 S2: infant walkers and bouncers; today's story covers toddler tower/kitchen step stools — distinct product category)
- **Byline:** Jamie Rivera
- **Posting Window:** 10:00 AM MT

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission has recalled 116,908 TOMY Boon PIVOT Collapsible Toddler Tower Kitchen Step Stools due to tip-over and fall hazards that risk serious injury or death. The stools were sold at Amazon, Target, and Babylist from January 2023 through June 2026 for approximately $150. Families should stop using the stool immediately and contact TOMY for a free stabilizing repair part. Context: This is the 11th recall of a toddler tower product since September 2025; across all 11 recalls, 434,176 units have been recalled and 54 child injuries documented. Consumer Reports is pushing the CPSC to finalize a mandatory safety standard.

### STORY 3: D11 Bond Vote Is Tomorrow — Last Chance to Know What's at Stake
- **Tier:** 2 (Local News / Time-Sensitive)
- **Pillar:** Local News
- **Classification:** FOLLOW-UP (Aug 3, 2026 — S3: D11 bond vote 2 days away; Aug 2, 2026 — S3: 3 days out; Aug 1, 2026 — S5: 4 days out)
- **Byline:** Sarah Morales
- **Posting Window:** 12:00 PM MT

The D11 Board of Education votes tomorrow, Wednesday August 5 at 5:30 PM, on whether to place a $775 million bond measure on the November general election ballot. If the board approves putting it to voters, it would be D11's first major bond measure in more than 20 years. The bond would fund HVAC upgrades, school safety improvements, building modernization, and one new construction. The average D11 building is 58 years old, and the district's identified repair backlog exceeds $422 million — against an annual capital budget of only $17 million. The estimated taxpayer cost: approximately $6.50 per month per $100,000 of assessed home value. D11's first day of school is August 12.
