### STORY 1: Liam Stewart School Zone Act (HB26-1318)

**Law name:** Liam Stewart School Zone Act — **HIGH** (bill number HB26-1318, multiple sources)
**Effective date:** August 12, 2026 — **HIGH** (KDVR, Yellow Scene, CBS Colorado, Colorado Senate Democrats)
**Named for:** Liam Stewart, Littleton middle schooler struck and killed while biking to school 2023 — **HIGH** (KDVR confirmed)
**School zone expansion:** School zones defined as roadways within at least 1,000 feet of school property boundary — **HIGH** (KDVR, CBS Colorado)
**Fines:** Fines doubled within school zones — **HIGH** (KDVR)
**School streets:** New "school streets" designation — roadways immediately adjacent to school property, max 10 mph, vehicles must yield to all pedestrians and cyclists — **HIGH** (KDVR)
**Community process for zone reduction:** Communities wanting to reduce zone below 1,000 feet must hold public hearing first — **HIGH** (CBS Colorado)
**Signed into law:** May 29, 2026 — **HIGH** (Colorado Senate Democrats release)
**Sponsors:** Representatives Kenny Nguyen and Meg Froelich — **HIGH** (Colorado Senate Democrats)
**House vote:** Passed 42-21 — **HIGH** (Colorado Senate Democrats)
**D11 first day:** August 12, 2026 — **HIGH** (confirmed prior pipelines)
**School bus law note:** Since May 2025, ALL drivers in ALL directions must stop at least 20 feet from school bus with red flashing lights; painted medians no longer permit passing — **HIGH** (Colorado State Patrol)

Sources: KDVR, Yellow Scene (Aug 8), CBS Colorado, Colorado Senate Democrats, HB26-1318 full text

### STORY 5: Toddler Foods Ultra-Processed Study

**Finding:** 81% of toddler food products audited were ultra-processed — **HIGH** (Pediatrics/AAP journal)
**Products audited:** Nearly 2,800 toddler food products from grocery stores — **HIGH** (Pediatrics)
**Study context:** Published in Pediatrics (the AAP journal) — **HIGH** (AAP publications)
**Study location:** Conducted in Texas (grocery store audit) — **MEDIUM** (ConsumerAffairs/research summary)
**Issue:** Packaging/labeling often makes products appear healthier than they are — **HIGH** (study finding)
**Definition:** Ultra-processed = industrial formulations with little whole food; often high in sugar, sodium, artificial additives — **MEDIUM** (contextual — standard NOVA definition)
**AAP guidance note:** AAP has consistently recommended whole foods and limiting ultra-processed foods for young children — **HIGH** (healthychildren.org)

Sources: AAP Pediatrics journal Volume 158 Issue 2, AAP news releases
