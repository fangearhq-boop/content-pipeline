CPSC Warning: Stop Using Flyboss 3-in-1 Infant Bouncers Immediately

By Jamie Rivera | October 29, 2026 | COS Parenting

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission has issued a warning for Flyboss 3-in-1 Infant Bouncers: the product can collapse during use, creating a fall hazard for infants. If you have one, stop using it now.

Approximately 14,200 units are affected. The CPSC received 191 reports of collapse incidents involving the bouncers. In 115 of those cases, an infant's head struck the surface below. No fatal injuries have been publicly reported, but the incident volume is significant — and the risk is real enough that the CPSC is urging immediate cessation of use.

What to Do Right Now

If you have a Flyboss 3-in-1 Infant Bouncer, stop using it today. Do not use it until you've checked the CPSC website at cpsc.gov for current remedy information, which may include a refund, repair, or replacement depending on what the company makes available. The CPSC's product recall and warning database is searchable by product name.

If you're not certain whether you have this specific product, the bouncer is sold under the Flyboss brand as a 3-in-1 model. Check the packaging or product listing where you purchased it to confirm the brand and model name.

What's Next

Visit cpsc.gov and search "Flyboss" to access the current warning information and any available remedy. If your product is confirmed affected and no remedy is yet available, keep the product out of reach and check back within the week.
