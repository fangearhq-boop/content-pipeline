### STORY 1: AAP Updates Flu and RSV Guidance — Respiratory Season Is Here
**Tier:** T1
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Type:** NEW (not previously covered; distinct from Oct 28 UTI, Oct 21 COVID vaccine stories)
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Posting window:** 8:00 AM MT

The American Academy of Pediatrics has issued updated guidance for the 2026-27 respiratory virus season covering both flu and RSV. Flu: the AAP recommends annual influenza vaccination for all children 6 months and older without medical contraindication — the recommendation is unchanged, but the urgency is real. The 2025-26 flu season was among the most severe on record for children, with at least 190 pediatric deaths, yet only 49.4 percent of children were vaccinated. The 2026-27 vaccines are all trivalent; the AAP does not favor one product over another. For RSV: updated guidance (published October 26 in Pediatrics) recommends immunization for all infants under 8 months entering their first RSV season, and expands high-risk criteria for second-season RSV protection to children aged 8-19 months at elevated risk. Sources: AAP healthychildren.org, Pediatrics (Oct 26, 2026), Contemporary Pediatrics, Drug Topics.

### STORY 2: CPSC Warns Parents to Stop Using Flyboss 3-in-1 Infant Bouncers
**Tier:** T1
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Type:** NEW (not previously covered; prior CPSC: Lucakuins Oct 27, Papablic/ONTHEWEI prior weeks)
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Posting window:** 10:00 AM MT

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission has issued a warning for approximately 14,200 Flyboss 3-in-1 Infant Bouncers: the bouncers can collapse during use, creating a fall hazard for infants. The CPSC received 191 reports of collapse incidents, 115 of which involved an infant's head striking the surface below. Parents should stop using the product immediately and visit cpsc.gov for remedy information. This is the latest in a string of infant product safety actions in October 2026. Sources: Consumer Affairs, CPSC.gov.
