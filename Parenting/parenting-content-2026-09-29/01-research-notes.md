### STORY 5: AAP Screen Time Guidelines 2026 Update
**Status:** NEW
**Sources:**
- https://health.choc.org/updated-aap-recommendations-for-screen-time/ — CHOC Children's Health Hub, citing updated AAP recommendations
- https://drlookai.ai/blogs/blogs/aap-screen-time-guidelines-2026 — AAP Screen Time Guidelines 2026 summary

**Key Facts (HIGH confidence — multiple sources consistent):**
- AAP shifted away from the "2-hour rule" for school-age children and teens in 2026
- New framework: quality, context, and conversation rather than strict time limits
- Under 18 months: no screens (rule unchanged)
- Ages 2–5: up to 1 hour/day of high-quality content with parent co-viewing
- School age and teens: no universal hour cap; focus on what screens REPLACE (sleep, physical activity, homework, face-to-face time)
- Strong recommendation: no screens in bedrooms; screen-free buffer before bedtime
- Sleep harm is consistently documented: screens in the hour before bed delay sleep and reduce sleep depth (blue light suppresses melatonin)
- Key shift in philosophy: "How are you using it?" replaces "How long are you using it?"

**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Freshness:** 2026 update — parents broadly asking about this; evergreen with a news hook.
