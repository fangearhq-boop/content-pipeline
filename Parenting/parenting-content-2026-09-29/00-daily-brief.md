### STORY 5: AAP Updates Screen Time Rules — It's About Quality, Not Just Clock Hours
**Type:** NEW
**Tier:** 2 (National Parenting)
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Angle:** The American Academy of Pediatrics has moved away from its old "2-hour rule" for school-age kids and teens. The updated 2026 framework focuses on quality, context, and what screens replace — not rigid time caps. Key changes: no screens under 18 months; ages 2–5 get up to 1 hour/day of quality content with parents co-viewing; school-age and teen caps removed in favor of conversation about sleep, activity, and face-to-face time. One rule holds firm: no screens in bedrooms, especially before sleep.
**Sources:** AAP via CHOC Children's Health Hub (health.choc.org), drlookai.ai/blogs/blogs/aap-screen-time-guidelines-2026
**Posting Window:** 5:00 PM MT
