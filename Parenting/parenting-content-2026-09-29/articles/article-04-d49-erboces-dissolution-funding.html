D49 to Absorb $53 Million After ERBOCES Dissolution — What Colorado Springs Families Need to Know

By Jamie Rivera | September 29, 2026

Local News | D49 Schools

A significant change is underway in Colorado Springs' District 49. The D49 Board of Education voted to dissolve the Education ReEnvisioned Board of Cooperative Education Services — known as ERBOCES — and absorb approximately $53 million in funding that had been managed through the organization. The Colorado Department of Education directed the change.

The Colorado Springs Gazette first reported this on September 11. If you're a D49 family and haven't heard about it yet, here's what we know and where to find updates.

What Was ERBOCES?

ERBOCES — the Education ReEnvisioned Board of Cooperative Education Services — was an organizational partner that worked alongside D49 to provide charter and specialized education programs. Boards of Cooperative Educational Services (BOCES) are a Colorado mechanism that allows districts to share or contract educational services, often serving students with specialized needs or families choosing alternative school structures.

ERBOCES had managed a significant pool of funding — approximately $53 million — connected to those programs. With the board's vote to dissolve it, that funding and the programs it supported transition back to D49's direct oversight.

What This Means for Families

If your children attend a standard D49 neighborhood school, the day-to-day impact of this change is likely minimal in the near term. The dissolution primarily affects families using specialized programs, charter schools, or services that were administered through ERBOCES.

For those families, it's worth knowing that the programs themselves are not necessarily going away — the change is in who administers them and manages the associated funding. D49 is now taking direct ownership of programs that were previously operating under ERBOCES's structure.

What specifically changes for individual programs, what the transition timeline looks like, and how families can expect to interact differently with their school or program administrators are questions that D49 is best positioned to answer directly.
