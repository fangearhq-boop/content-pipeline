### STORY 5: AAP's 2026 Screen Time Update — Quality Over Limits This Fall
**Type:** NEW STORY
**Tier:** 2 (National Parenting — timely with fall/indoor season)
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Angle:** The American Academy of Pediatrics updated its screen time guidelines in 2026 to emphasize quality, context and conversation over strict time caps. Key rules: no screens for children under 18 months (except video calls); 1 hour per day of high-quality programming for ages 2-5; age 6+ should maintain consistent limits that protect sleep and physical activity. Timed with fall's shift to more indoor time.
