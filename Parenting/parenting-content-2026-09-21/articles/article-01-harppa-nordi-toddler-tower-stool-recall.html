HARPPA Nordi Toddler Tower Stool Recalled: What Colorado Springs Parents Need to Know

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | September 21, 2026

National Parenting — Product Safety

If you have a HARPPA Nordi Foldable Toddler Tower Stool in your home, stop using it now. The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission has issued a recall for approximately 47,166 units of this product due to serious collapse, tip-over, and entrapment hazards.

The recalled stool — model HANS0002 — can collapse or tip over while a child is using it. More alarming: a child's torso can fit through the front or back openings of the stool, creating an entrapment risk. The CPSC has received three reports of incidents, including two injuries involving scrapes, cuts, and bruises.

What's Being Recalled

The HARPPA Nordi Foldable Toddler Tower Stool is a kitchen helper stool designed to let young children reach countertop height while working alongside adults. The recalled units are white, black, and blue with a natural wood-colored step, platform, and safety bars. The stool measures approximately 18 inches deep, 18 inches wide, and 38.6 inches tall.

These stools were sold on Amazon.com and harppababy.com from September 2023 through June 2026 at a price of around $130. To confirm whether your stool is affected, check the label on the underside of the stool platform for model number HANS0002.

What the Hazard Is

The CPSC identified two separate hazard types with this product. First, the structure can collapse or tip over while a child is using it. Second — and critically — a child's torso can fit through the front or back openings of the stool frame, creating an entrapment risk that could lead to serious injury.

There have been three reported incidents, resulting in two injuries. The injuries documented include scrapes, cuts, and bruises. The agency notes this recall as presenting a risk of serious injury, and families should treat it accordingly.

How to Get the Free Repair Kit

HARPPA is providing a free remedy for affected families. The repair kit includes protective nets, stabilizing feet, and installation instructions. Shipping is also free.

To request your repair kit, visit harppababy.com. You'll need to have your model number on hand (HANS0002, found on the label on the underside of the stool platform).

Do not continue using the stool before the repair kit is installed. The CPSC advises stopping use immediately.

What's Next

If your stool matches the recall, stop using it today and request the free repair kit at harppababy.com. Once your kit arrives, follow the provided installation instructions before allowing your child to use the stool again.

For the full official recall notice, including photos and additional contact information, visit the CPSC recall page at cpsc.gov. COS families who have questions can also reach HARPPA directly through the harppababy.com website.

If you have friends or neighbors in Colorado Springs with toddlers, it's worth sharing the recall information — the stool was widely available on Amazon and may be in homes that haven't seen the CPSC notice yet.
