Orton Academy Moves to Trailblazer Elementary for the 2026-27 School Year

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | September 21, 2026

Local News

Colorado Springs School District 11 families who have a child with dyslexia or a reading difference have a new school location to note. Orton Academy — a K-8 charter school that specializes in dyslexia education — is moving into Trailblazer Elementary for the 2026-27 school year. The school's staff, students, and programming will remain entirely separate within the shared building.

The move was reported earlier this year by the Colorado Springs Gazette and reflects the kind of logistical shift that doesn't change what a school does, but does change where families drop off and pick up. If you have a child enrolled at Orton Academy, or if you've been researching specialized schools for a child who struggles with reading, here's what you need to know.

What Is Orton Academy

Orton Academy is a public charter school in Colorado Springs that opened in 2020 with a specific focus: educating students who have dyslexia or related reading differences. It serves students in kindergarten through eighth grade, using structured literacy approaches designed for students whose brains process written language differently.

The school has been chartered within D11 since 2024. Charter schools in Colorado operate with more autonomy than traditional district schools — they set their own instructional approaches and hire their own staff — but they're publicly funded and open to families within the district without a tuition cost.

Orton Academy fills a real gap in COS's school landscape. Dyslexia affects a significant portion of school-age children, and most families navigate it through reading interventions, tutoring, or IEP services within traditional schools. A school built specifically around how students with dyslexia learn is a different kind of option.

What the Move Means

Starting with the 2026-27 school year, Orton Academy is operating out of the Trailblazer Elementary building. The two schools share the facility, but they function independently. Orton Academy students go to their own classrooms, taught by Orton Academy staff, following Orton Academy's curriculum and schedule. Trailblazer Elementary continues as its own school alongside them.

This kind of co-location is not unusual for charter schools. Buildings that have capacity can house more than one school, each with its own identity and operations. For families with children at Orton Academy, the key change is practical: a new drop-off address, new daily logistics around the Trailblazer building location.

What Stays the Same

The move doesn't change Orton Academy's mission, curriculum, or the staff working with students. The dyslexia-focused instructional approach, the school's charter with D11, and the grade span (K-8) all remain in place. If your child is already enrolled, the programming they signed up for continues — just in a new building.

What's Next

If your child is currently enrolled at Orton Academy, connect with the school directly for any updated logistics around the Trailblazer Elementary location — drop-off and pick-up procedures, building access, and scheduling details. The school's official information is available through D11 at d11.org.

If you've been researching schools in Colorado Springs for a child with dyslexia or a reading difference, Orton Academy is worth a closer look. Its charter status means it's publicly funded and open to D11 families without cost. You can find more information through the D11 website or reach out to the school directly.
