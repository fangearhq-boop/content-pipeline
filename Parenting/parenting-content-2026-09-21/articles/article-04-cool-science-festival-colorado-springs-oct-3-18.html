Colorado Springs Cool Science Festival Returns Oct. 3–18 with 16 Days of Free STEAM Activities

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | September 21, 2026

Local Events

If you're building out your fall family calendar, here's the anchor event. The Colorado Springs Cool Science Festival runs October 3-18, 2026 — 16-plus days of science, technology, engineering, art, and math activities spread across the city, with most events free and open to all ages.

The festival is hosted by Cool Science, a local nonprofit, and it's been a staple for COS families for years. Whether your kids are into rockets, robots, animals, or chemistry experiments, there's something on the schedule for them. The festival kicks off with Cool Science Carnival Day at UCCS on Saturday, October 3 — free admission, free parking, and a full day of activities designed primarily for K-8 students.

Carnival Day: The Opening Act

Carnival Day on October 3 at the UCCS campus is where the festival opens with a bang. This is a large-scale, free, family-friendly event designed for kids in kindergarten through eighth grade, though all ages are genuinely welcome.

The lineup is impressive: science magic shows, university lab tours, rocket launches, slime stations, drones, robots, virtual reality experiences, liquid nitrogen treats, and live animals. Admission is free and parking is free — which makes it a rare full-day family outing that won't dent the weekend budget.

UCCS is one of the larger venues in COS for public events, and Carnival Day makes good use of the campus. It's worth arriving with some time to explore, because there are enough activity stations that a curious eight-year-old could easily spend three or four hours working through them.

The Rest of the Festival

Beyond Carnival Day, the festival runs through October 18 with events distributed across Colorado Springs at various venues, organizations, and institutions. Most events are free. A small number may include a fee to cover the costs of hosting the activity.

Events span the full range of STEAM subjects and are designed with K-8 students as the primary audience, though many are open and relevant to older students and adults as well. The full schedule is updated regularly at coolscience.org — it's worth bookmarking and checking back as new events are added.

Why It's Worth Planning Around

The Cool Science Festival lines up well with the start of fall, when the days shorten and families start looking for structured indoor and outdoor activities. For parents who want their kids exposed to science and STEAM concepts outside of school, this is one of the few times in the year when that exposure comes in genuinely engaging, hands-on formats — not worksheets and textbooks but rockets, live animals, and liquid nitrogen.

For COS families who haven't attended before, Carnival Day is the easiest entry point. It's a single date, a single venue, and it's free from start to finish. After that, the rest of the festival's two-plus weeks give families flexibility to attend additional events that match their kids' specific interests.

What's Next

Carnival Day is October 3 at UCCS — mark it now. For the full festival schedule and to find events throughout Colorado Springs from October 3-18, visit coolscience.org.

No registration is required for Carnival Day, but check individual event listings on the website for any events that might need advance sign-up or have limited capacity. And if there are events at local schools, libraries, or community centers during the festival window, the Cool Science website is the most reliable place to find them as they're added to the schedule.
