### STORY 5: AAP Screen Time Guidelines

**Source 1:** health.choc.org/updated-aap-recommendations-for-screen-time
- CHOC (Children's Health) article on AAP updated screen time recommendations
- AAP 2026 update: prioritizes quality, context and conversation over strict time limits
- Key recommendations:
  - Under 18 months: no screens except video calls (FaceTime, etc.)
  - Ages 2-5: limit to 1 hour/day of high-quality programming
  - Ages 6+: consistent limits protecting sleep, physical activity, family time
- Confidence: HIGH (CHOC is official children's hospital citing AAP guidelines)

**Source 2:** aap.org/en/news-room/news-releases-from-pediatrics/
- AAP official news releases
- Pediatrics journal Volume 158, Issue 3 (September 2026)
- Confidence: HIGH (official AAP source)

**KEY FACTS (HIGH confidence):**
- Organization: American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP)
- Update year: 2026
- Key shift: from strict time limits to quality + context + conversation focus
- Under 18 months: no screens (except video calls)
- Ages 2-5: max 1 hour/day of high-quality content
- Ages 6+: consistent limits; screens should not displace sleep, physical activity, homework
- Co-viewing encouraged: watch with children, discuss what you're watching
- Confidence: HIGH
