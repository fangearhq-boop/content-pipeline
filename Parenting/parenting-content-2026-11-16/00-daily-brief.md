### STORY 4: ADHD + Screen Time — New AAP-Supported Guide for COS Families

**Tier:** 2
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Posting window:** 3:00 PM MT
**Type:** NEW STORY

**Summary:** A new joint report from Understood and the American Academy of Pediatrics (published today, Nov 16) focuses specifically on helping parents of elementary-aged children with ADHD reduce screen-time conflict. Different from the AAP 5 Cs general framework (Oct 2026), this guide addresses why ADHD brains respond differently to screen limits and gives practical strategies for transitions, dopamine-seeking behavior, and de-escalating conflict at screen-off time.

**Key Facts:**
- Published: Nov 16, 2026 (today) via Understood + AAP joint guidance
- Target: parents of school-age children with ADHD
- Key insight: ADHD brains have dopamine dysregulation that makes screen transitions harder
- Distinct from general 5 Cs framework (Oct 2026) — ADHD-specific guidance
- Actionable strategies: transition warnings, visual timers, co-regulation, "earned" vs "time-limited" framing

**Sources:**
