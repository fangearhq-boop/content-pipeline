Your COS Thanksgiving Week Family Guide: Turkey Trot, Cranksgiving, the Nutcracker, and More

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | November 16, 2026

Thanksgiving week in Colorado Springs has a lot going on for families — from a 29-year-old race tradition to a kids' cooking camp, a food-drive bike ride, and the Nutcracker. Here's what's happening and what you need to know to plan your week.

Turkey Trot 5K — Thursday, Nov. 26

The Turkey Trot 5K returns for its 29th year on Thanksgiving morning at Briargate YMCA. This is Colorado Springs's signature family Thanksgiving event, and it earns that reputation: it's stroller-friendly, dog-friendly, and designed so everyone can participate at their own pace.

The Kids Tiny Trot kicks off at 8:15 AM. The main 5K run starts at 9 AM. Registration is $54.25 for adults and $43.65 for youth ages 6–19. If you're planning to come as a family, register in advance — this event draws a crowd.

Details and registration are available at visitcos.com.

Cranksgiving Food-Drive Bike Ride — Saturday, Nov. 21

Five days before Thanksgiving, Cranksgiving brings its annual food-drive bike ride to Red Leg Brewing Co. The ride starts at 9 AM and is family-friendly. Participants gather non-perishable food donations as part of the ride — a good way to get outside, do something active, and give back before the holiday.

Check the Cranksgiving event page on Eventbrite for registration details and the most current information.

Chefs' Kitchen Kids Thanksgiving Mini-Camp — Monday, Nov. 23

If your kids are off school during Thanksgiving week and you're looking for something structured, Chefs' Kitchen is running a Kids Thanksgiving Mini-Camp on Monday, Nov. 23, from 9 AM to noon. Cost is $235. The camp focuses on holiday baking and cooking — a hands-on option for older kids who want to actually make something for the holiday.

Contact Chefs' Kitchen directly to confirm availability and registration details before the break.

The Nutcracker — Saturday and Sunday, Nov. 27–28

The Colorado Springs Philharmonic presents the Nutcracker at Pikes Peak Center on Nov. 27 and Nov. 28. For families who make the Nutcracker part of their holiday tradition, this is the year's performance. Tickets are available through the Pikes Peak Center box office.

If this is your first time bringing kids to the Nutcracker, Nov. 27 or 28 is a good entry point — the story is visual and approachable for children as young as 4 or 5.

What's Next

Thanksgiving week is 10 days away. If the Turkey Trot is on your list, registration in advance is the move — it fills up. For the Cranksgiving ride, check the Eventbrite listing for current registration status. The Nutcracker and cooking camp both benefit from early booking.
