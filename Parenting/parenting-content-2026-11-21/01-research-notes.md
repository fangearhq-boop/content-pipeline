## Story 2: FZChenrry Magnetic Fidget Ball Toys CPSC Warning (#27-095)

**Confidence:** HIGH

**Key Facts:**
- CPSC Warning #27-095 announced November 19, 2026
- Product: FZChenrry Magnetic Fidget Ball Toys — 8 textured silicone magnetic balls, purple, sold in oval black zippered case
- Seller: Chanlee (on Amazon)
- Units: ~1,372 sets sold
- Sale period: Amazon, February 2025–September 2026
- Price: ~$16/set
- Status: Seller REFUSED voluntary recall — CPSC issued warning, NOT a recall
- Hazard: high-powered magnets violate mandatory toy standard (federal magnet standard); ingesting two or more magnets can cause intestinal perforations, intestinal tears — potentially fatal without surgery
- Remedy: NO recall, NO refund, NO replacement; CPSC advises stop using, do not sell or give away; dispose of product safely
- Note: This is a warning, not a recall. Families should check Amazon orders from Feb 2025–Sep 2026.

**Story History Check:** NEW STORY — distinct from Nov 20 S1 (CHE-US magnetic robot toys are stick figure robots, different form factor and seller; CHE-US = Guangzhou Shian Trading Co., FZChenrry = Chanlee); two separate CPSC enforcement actions covering different magnetic toy products in the same week. Important to cover both.

## Story 3: COS Childcare Shortage / Childcare Desert

**Confidence:** HIGH

**Key Facts:**
- Colorado Springs has approximately 47,000 children under age 5
- Licensed childcare capacity: approximately 18,400 spots — a gap of roughly 28,600 seats
- Worst-affected areas: East Colorado Springs, Southeast Colorado Springs, Fountain-Fort Carson corridor
- COS City Council approved a new zoning ordinance allowing childcare facilities in additional building types (commercial, industrial, mixed-use zones that previously restricted them)
- New loan program established to help childcare providers offset startup and operating costs
- Statewide: approximately 14,000 Colorado children on the childcare subsidy waitlist (Colorado Sun, April 2026)
- Childcare deserts defined as areas where there are more than three children per licensed childcare slot

**Story History Check:** NEW STORY — not covered in Nov 19 or Nov 20 runs; city council zoning ordinance and loan program are new policy actions to cover.
