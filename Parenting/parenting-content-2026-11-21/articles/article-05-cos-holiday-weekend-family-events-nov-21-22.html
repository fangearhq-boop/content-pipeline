COS Family Weekend Guide: Vintage Markets, Ice Skating, and Holiday Lights (Nov 21–22)

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | November 21, 2026

It's the first real holiday weekend of the season in Colorado Springs, and there's enough going on that you won't have to drive far or spend a lot to have a solid family day. Here's what's happening November 21 and 22 — with details on cost, age ranges, and logistics so you can plan without guessing.

Vintage Market Days: "Rocking Around the Christmas Tree"

Vintage Market Days runs Friday through Sunday, November 20–22, at the Norris Penrose Event Center (1045 Lower Gold Camp Road). Saturday and Sunday admission is $12 per adult; a weekend pass covering all three days is $17. Children 12 and under are free.

The event features more than 150 vendors selling vintage goods, holiday gifts, artisan items, and food. It's a large indoor event at an enclosed venue, which makes it a reasonable option on a cold weekend day. The "Rocking Around the Christmas Tree" theme means holiday-focused inventory — if you're doing any holiday shopping, this is worth checking before the season's larger holiday markets open.

Hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM on Saturday and Sunday. Norris Penrose has ample parking, and the venue is accessible. The Lower Gold Camp Road location is on the southwest side of the city — plan about 10–15 minutes from most parts of COS.

Skate in the Park

The outdoor rink at Acacia Park in downtown Colorado Springs is in its 12th season and is open now through approximately January 2027. Admission is $15 per person and includes skate rental. Kids ages four and under skate free.

Tickets are sold at the door — no advance purchase. If you've never taken kids skating in Colorado Springs, this is the go-to option. It's centrally located, the skate rental is included in admission, and it pairs naturally with a stop at the downtown coffee shops and restaurants within walking distance on Tejon Street.

If the weekend has clear skies and cold temperatures, afternoon rink conditions are typically good. Bundle up — it's outdoor skating with mountain weather.

Broadmoor Holiday Lights

The Broadmoor hotel's holiday lights experience is running through approximately March 1, 2027, and it's visible from the public streets and paths around the property. This is a free option — drive or walk the perimeter near Lake Avenue — and it works especially well as a post-dinner outing or a final stop after the market or the rink.

The Broadmoor's holiday decorations are extensive and traditionally include lit trees, displays along the main drive, and illuminated areas around the lake. It's a classic COS winter tradition that doesn't require tickets or timing.
