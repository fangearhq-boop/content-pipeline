D11's Galileo Middle School Opens Ascend Food Pantry and Community Garden

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | September 27, 2026

Colorado Springs has a new neighborhood resource worth knowing about. Galileo Middle School, part of School District 11, celebrated the opening of its "Ascend" food pantry and community garden this week in partnership with Joint Initiatives for Youth and Families. The pantry operates market-style — families walk in, browse shelves stocked with fresh produce, bread, and household staples, and take what they need.

It's not a donations-box model or a once-a-month pickup. The goal is to operate more like a neighborhood market — dignified, practical, and accessible to the families who need it most.

Community Garden That Feeds the Shelf

The pantry is connected to something that has been growing quietly at the school for the past year: a community garden that produced more than 900 pounds of fresh produce this season. The harvest goes directly into the pantry shelves and is shared with the broader school community. That connection between a school garden and a school pantry is still relatively rare — and it's one of the reasons the Ascend program has drawn attention from multiple Colorado Springs TV stations this week.

Twenty-two families have been served since the pantry opened, with plans already in place to expand access to King Palmer Elementary next.

The Bigger Vision: Ascend Community Center

The food pantry and garden are described as Phase One of a larger plan to transform Galileo Middle School into the Ascend Community Center. Future phases include laundry services, additional garden beds, and other wraparound resources for the surrounding neighborhood. The partnership with Joint Initiatives for Youth and Families brings nonprofit expertise in community-centered service design to what has historically been a school building.

D11 families in the Galileo Middle School zone can access the pantry during school hours. Families interested in learning more about the Ascend program or in referring someone who could benefit can reach out to Galileo Middle School directly through the D11 website at d11.org.

What's Next

The Galileo Ascend program represents a model that other COS schools and nonprofits may look to as a template for combining educational facilities with community services. If you've been looking for ways to support the program — whether as a volunteer, a garden contributor, or a donor to Joint Initiatives for Youth and Families — the program is in early growth mode and those contributions carry real weight right now. Watch for additional announcements as Phase Two planning begins and expansion to King Palmer takes shape.
