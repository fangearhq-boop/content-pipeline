D20 Parent Academy: Free High School Planning Event Monday, September 29

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | September 27, 2026

Academy District 20 is hosting a free Parent Academy session on Monday, September 29, at 6:00 p.m. at the district administration building at 1110 Chapel Hills Drive. The event is designed for families with current or incoming D20 high school students.

The session title is "From Freshman Year to Graduation — Planning with Purpose." It will cover D20's high school graduation requirements, key academic milestones across the four years of high school, and practical guidance on how families can support their students in staying on track.

Who Should Attend

The Parent Academy is geared toward families who want a clearer picture of the D20 high school pathway — whether your student is just entering high school or you're a year or two in and still figuring out how all the requirements fit together. Topics like graduation credit requirements, elective sequencing, and the connection between course choices and post-graduation options (college, career pathways, military) can feel overwhelming without a structured overview, which is exactly what this event provides.

Parents new to D20, parents who moved from another district with different graduation requirements, and families whose students are considering dual enrollment or Advanced Placement courses will all likely find useful information.

Event Details

The event is free to attend. Verify the most current information — including any registration details — at asd20.org/parent-academy/ before Monday.
