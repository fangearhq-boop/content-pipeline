Cheyenne Mountain Zoo Stroller Safaris Start Oct. 1 — "All Things Orange in October" for Ages 1-4

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | September 27, 2026

If you have a toddler or baby in the house, Cheyenne Mountain Zoo has something new this fall worth putting on the calendar. Starting Wednesday, October 1, the zoo is launching its "All Things Orange in October" Stroller Safaris — a guided program designed specifically for children ages 1 to 4, with an adult companion.

The first session runs 9 to 10 a.m. on October 1, with additional dates on October 7, 16, and 24 at the same time.

What Happens During a Stroller Safari

Each session lasts about an hour and includes three parts: a sensory activity, a meet-and-greet with one of the zoo's animal ambassadors, and an animal feeding experience. The small group format and informal pace make this a good fit for younger children who aren't ready for a full zoo visit but are ready to engage with animals in a more contained, structured way.

The October theme — "All Things Orange" — ties the animal and sensory programming to fall colors, seasonal elements, and the zoo's autumn environment. Expect pumpkins to make an appearance.

Cost and Registration

Stroller Safari tickets cost $4.75 for zoo members and $20.75 for non-members. The non-member price includes zoo admission, so families can extend their visit after the program if they'd like. Babies under 1 year old are free.

Registration is required in advance. You can sign up at cmzoo.org/stroller, call 719-424-7827, or email edprograms@cmzoo.org.
