ABC Trading Light-Up Toy Recall: 43,674 Units Recalled Over Button Battery Death Risk

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | September 27, 2026

The Consumer Product Safety Commission issued a recall on September 24, 2026 for ABC Trading light-up children's toys after the battery compartments were found to violate mandatory toy safety standards. Children can access the button cell batteries inside, posing a risk of serious injury or death: button batteries cause rapid, severe internal chemical burns when swallowed, and can be fatal within hours if not immediately treated.

Approximately 43,674 units are included in this recall. If you have any of these products in your home, stop using them now.

Which Products Are Recalled

Four types of ABC Trading light-up toys are affected:

  • Light-up glasses
  • Crown headbands
  • Sparkle headbands
  • Light-up ties

To check whether your product is included, look for the model number on the outer bag label. Recalled units carry model numbers 6602, HD-10, or L-508. The label also says "MADE IN CHINA, Importer: ABC TRADING, INC."

These toys sold for approximately $1 each at ABC Trading stores in Los Angeles and specialty stores across the country, as well as on abctradinginc.com. Sales ran from September 2016 through May 2026. Given their low price point and novelty appeal, they appeared widely in party favor bags, Halloween treat bags, and holiday stocking stuffers over those years.

The Button Battery Hazard

Button batteries — also called coin cell batteries — are used in small electronics and are among the most dangerous household hazards for young children. They are small enough to swallow easily, and once lodged in the esophagus, they generate an electrical current that causes chemical burns to surrounding tissue. The damage can begin within two hours of ingestion and can be fatal if not treated immediately.

Mandatory federal toy safety standards include specific requirements for battery compartment access precisely to prevent children from reaching these batteries. The ABC Trading products at issue do not meet those requirements.

What to Do If You Have These Toys

  1. Stop using them immediately. Remove them from reach of children.
  2. Dispose of the product. Place it in the trash.
  3. Photograph the disposed product (in the trash or visibly destroyed).
  4. Email recallabc@gmail.com with the photograph to receive a full refund. No mailing is required.

What's Next

The full recall notice is available at cpsc.gov — search "ABC Trading light-up toys" to find it. With Halloween approaching, it's a good time to check any novelty or light-up party favor items in your home, especially inexpensive ones that may have come from bulk or specialty stores. If you've passed any of these products on to other families, let them know.

The CPSC's recall database at cpsc.gov/recalls lets you search by product type, brand, or hazard. Signing up for recall email alerts means you won't have to check manually — they'll arrive in your inbox automatically.
