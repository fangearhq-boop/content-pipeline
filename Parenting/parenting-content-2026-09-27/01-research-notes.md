## Key Facts by Story

### S1: ABC Trading Light-Up Toys Recall (Confirmed HIGH)
- Recall date: September 24, 2026 (CPSC.gov)
- Units: ~43,674
- Products: light-up glasses, crown headbands, sparkle headbands, light-up ties
- Identification: models 6602, HD-10, or L-508 on outer bag; "MADE IN CHINA, Importer: ABC TRADING, INC."
- Hazard: Button/coin cell batteries accessible to children; fatal if swallowed (internal chemical burns)
- Sold: ~$1 each at ABC Trading stores (LA) and specialty stores nationwide + abctradinginc.com
- Sale dates: September 2016 through May 2026
- Action: Stop use, photograph disposed product in trash, email recallabc@gmail.com for full refund
- Note: Also on Sep 24, Yuyitop Aitjunz 8-Drawer Dressers recalled (~25,073 units, tip-over/entrapment, STURDY Act violation, sold Amazon/Wayfair/TEMU/Walmart). Free retrofit kit available.
