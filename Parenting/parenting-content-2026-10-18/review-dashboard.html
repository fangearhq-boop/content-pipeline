Primark 101 Dalmatians Coin Purse Recalled for Phthalate Hazard [T1 — NEW]
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Heads up, Colorado Springs parents — if you picked up a 101 Dalmatians coin purse from Primark in the last several months, you'll want to check it. 🚨 The Consumer Product Safety Commission announced a recall on October 15 for about 2,100 Primark 101 Dalmatians faux-leather coin purses. The issue is phthalates — chemicals found at levels above what's permitted, which can be harmful to young children through ingestion. The purses were sold at Primark stores in the Northeast, Florida, Maryland, and Illinois from March through June 2026 for about $9. Here's how to identify the recalled item: the model number 2595301 is on the tag attached to the zip puller. If it matches, stop letting kids use it and return it to any Primark store for a full refund. The CPSC says no injuries have been reported, but the risk is real. The same purse was recalled in the UK back in September, so this has been on the radar for a while. Did you have this item in your home?

Xalirium Portable Bed Rails Recalled for Entrapment Risk [T2 — NEW]
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Another CPSC recall from October 15 that families should know about — especially if grandparents or other caregivers use portable bed rails at home. 🛏️ The CPSC recalled about 200 usOpal Xalirium portable bed rails due to entrapment and asphyxiation risk. The rails fail the federal mandatory safety standard for adult portable bed rails and they're missing required hazard warning labels. They were sold on Amazon from February through August 2026 for around $60. The recall is specifically for bed rails without vertical bars inside the opening — that's the detail to check. If you have them, stop using them and contact usOpal for a full refund. No injuries have been reported. These might be in your home if you have elderly parents, grandparents visiting, or anyone using a bed rail for nighttime safety. Worth a quick check. Is this something your family uses?

How COS School Districts Are Embracing Fall Breaks [T2 — NEW]
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Colorado Springs families have noticed: fall break is getting bigger across our school districts. The Gazette published a piece this weekend looking at how D11, D49, and D20 have each developed their own version of fall break. D11's one-week break (Oct 19-23) is the most recent addition — the district has even added a free literacy program this year for kindergarteners who want extra reading support. D49 runs two full weeks off, which means a longer overall school year to compensate. D20 structured its break around parent-teacher conferences, calling it an "end of quarter" break. The community response has generally been positive. Parents like having a mid-fall recharge, and the Gazette notes that general consensus is the schedule should stay as it is. There's a real argument that the pace of modern school calendars benefits from a mid-fall pause — and COS districts seem to agree. It's worth knowing your district's specific schedule and planning accordingly. D49 families still have several days of break remaining. D11 families start theirs tomorrow. How do you feel about fall break in COS — is the schedule working for your family?
