### STORY 2: Primark 101 Dalmatians Coin Purse Recalled for Phthalate Hazard [T1 — NEW]
The CPSC announced a recall of approximately 2,100 Primark 101 Dalmatians faux leather coin purses (recall #27-028, dated October 15, 2026) due to phthalate levels that pose a risk to young children through ingestion. The purses were sold at Primark stores in the Northeast, Florida, Maryland, and Illinois from March through June 2026 for about $9. The model number 2595301 is on the tag attached to the zip puller. Consumers should stop use, keep away from children, and return to a Primark store for a full refund. No injuries reported.

### STORY 3: Xalirium Portable Bed Rails Recalled for Entrapment Risk [T2 — NEW]
The CPSC recalled approximately 200 usOpal Xalirium portable bed rails (October 15, 2026) due to entrapment and asphyxiation risk. The rails fail the federal mandatory standard for adult portable bed rails and lack required hazard warning labels. Sold on Amazon.com from February through August 2026 for about $60. No injuries reported. Consumers should stop use immediately and contact usOpal for a full refund.
