### STORY 4: AAP 2026-27 Flu Vaccine Guidance

**Two-dose requirement:**
- Who needs 2 doses: children ages 6 months–8 years who (a) are getting flu vaccine for the first time, OR (b) received only 1 dose ever before July 1, 2026, OR (c) vaccination history unknown
- Timing: at least 4 weeks apart
- Ideal completion: by end of October
- Same brand/formulation NOT required for both doses

**Vaccine formulation:**
- All 2026-27 US flu vaccines: trivalent, updated compositions
- No preference between egg-based, cell culture-based, recombinant, or LAIV formulations

**This is distinct from:** AAP COVID vaccine guidance covered Sep 25 (that was about COVID-19 VFC delays)

**Confidence:** HIGH on two-dose requirements, timing, formulation (healthychildren.org, contemporarypediatrics.com — AAP official sources)
