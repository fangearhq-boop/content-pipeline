Mikario Trading Baby Gym Recalled — Suffocation Hazard, Sold on TikTok Shop - **Tier:** 1 - **Angle:** CPSC recalls Mikario Trading 5-in-1 Convertible Baby Gyms — sold exclusively on TikTok Shop from May through October 2025 for about $50. The convertible mat can function as a tummy-time pillow, but the floor mat can obstruct an infant's breathing, violating the mandatory safety standard for infant support cushions. The pink units are Item No. CBPBG-PIN. Action: stop use immediately, destroy (cut mat in half), photograph the destruction, and contact Mikario Trading at mikariorecall@gmail.com or 209-232-8655 (Mon–Fri, 7 a.m.–1 p.m. ET) for a full refund. TikTok Shop angle is noteworthy — parents who shop via social commerce may not realize products there carry CPSC recall risk.

AAP Updates Flu Vaccine Guidance — Some Kids Need Two Doses This Season
NOT VERIFIEDBRIEF
AAP Updates Flu Vaccine Guidance — Some Kids Need Two Doses This Season - **Tier:** 2 - **Angle:** The American Academy of Pediatrics has released its 2026-27 influenza prevention and treatment recommendations. Key change for parents: children ages 6 months through 8 years who are receiving the flu vaccine for the first time, who received only one dose ever before July 1, 2026, or whose vaccination history is unknown, need two doses this season at least four weeks apart. Both doses should ideally be completed by the end of October. The two doses don't need to be the same brand or formulation. All 2026-27 flu vaccines in the U.S. are trivalent with updated compositions. This is separate from the AAP COVID vaccine guidance covered earlier this week.
