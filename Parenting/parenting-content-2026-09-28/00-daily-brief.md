### STORY 4: AAP Updates Flu Vaccine Guidance — Some Kids Need Two Doses This Season

- **Tier:** T2
- **Pillar:** National Parenting (Health)
- **Byline:** Sarah Morales
- **Angle:** The American Academy of Pediatrics has released its 2026-27 influenza prevention and treatment recommendations. Key change for parents: children ages 6 months through 8 years who are receiving the flu vaccine for the first time, who received only one dose ever before July 1, 2026, or whose vaccination history is unknown, need two doses this season at least four weeks apart. Both doses should ideally be completed by the end of October. The two doses don't need to be the same brand or formulation. All 2026-27 flu vaccines in the U.S. are trivalent with updated compositions. This is separate from the AAP COVID vaccine guidance covered earlier this week.
