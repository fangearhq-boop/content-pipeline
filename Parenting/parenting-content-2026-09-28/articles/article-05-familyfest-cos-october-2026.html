FamilyFest Returns to Colorado Springs on October 10 — Free for Kids 12 and Under

By Jamie Rivera | September 28, 2026

FamilyFest is coming back to Colorado Springs on Saturday, October 10, and admission is free for every child 12 and under. The event runs from noon to 5 p.m. at 4845 List Drive, organized by ExpoPros. It's shaping up to be a busy Saturday for families in the Pikes Peak region, but FamilyFest's free admission model makes it one of the easier calls on a weekend with several options competing for your family's afternoon.
