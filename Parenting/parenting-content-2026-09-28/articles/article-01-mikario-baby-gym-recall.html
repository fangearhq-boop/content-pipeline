CPSC Recalls Mikario 5-in-1 Baby Gym Sold on TikTok Shop — Suffocation Hazard

By Jamie Rivera | September 28, 2026

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission has recalled the Mikario Trading 5-in-1 Convertible Baby Gym after determining its floor mat can obstruct an infant's breathing. The product violates the mandatory federal safety standard for infant support cushions, and the CPSC is directing parents to stop using it immediately.

The recall affects the pink units only, sold exclusively on TikTok Shop from May through October 2025 at roughly $50. If you purchased this product through TikTok Shop during that window, it's worth checking your records — the item number on recalled units is CBPBG-PIN.

What the Recall Covers

The gym converts between an overhead activity center and a tummy-time pillow mat — that's where the hazard lives. When used in its floor mat configuration, the mat can block an infant's airway in a way that violates the mandatory safety standard for infant support cushions. The CPSC classifies this as a suffocation risk.

The recalled units are the pink colorway only. The item number, CBPBG-PIN, should appear on the product's label or packaging if you still have it. The product was never listed on Amazon, Target, or other major retailers — TikTok Shop was the exclusive sales channel, which means the typical retailer-recall notification channels didn't apply here.

What Parents Need to Do Now

The CPSC's required action is specific: don't just set the product aside.

  1. Stop use immediately. Don't let your baby use the mat in any configuration.
  2. Destroy the floor mat. Cut it in half. This isn't optional — the CPSC requires destruction to ensure the product can't be passed along or resold.
  3. Photograph the cut mat. You'll need photographic proof of destruction to receive your refund.
  4. Contact Mikario Trading at mikariorecall@gmail.com or by phone at 209-232-8655. Phone hours are Monday through Friday, 7 a.m. to 1 p.m. ET. They're offering a full refund with proof of destruction.

If you've already given this product to someone else — a family member, a friend, a neighbor — please reach out to them directly. The recall applies to every unit sold during that window, regardless of who currently has it.

A Note on TikTok Shop Purchases

This recall is a useful reminder that products purchased through social commerce platforms carry the same federal safety obligations as products from any other retailer. The CPSC's jurisdiction doesn't stop at Amazon's checkout page. If a product sold on TikTok Shop, Instagram, or any other platform fails a mandatory safety standard, it's subject to recall just the same.

The practical challenge is notification. When you buy something at Target and there's a recall, Target often sends you a direct email. Social commerce platforms don't always have the same infrastructure for that. That means parents who shop through TikTok Shop may not automatically receive recall notices the way they would with a traditional retailer — which is part of why the CPSC publicizes these recalls directly.

The CPSC's full recall notice is available at cpsc.gov.

What's Next

If you have a Mikario 5-in-1 Baby Gym (pink units), the next step is immediate: stop use, cut and photograph the mat, and contact Mikario for your refund. The CPSC does not report any injuries associated with this product in the available recall notice, but the hazard is real and the required action is clear. Parents who bought this product on TikTok Shop between May and October 2025 and are unsure whether their unit is affected should check the item number on the label — CBPBG-PIN is the recalled unit.
