### Story 1: Mikario Baby Gym Recall — LONG-FORM
**Posting window:** 8:00 AM MT

🚨 If you bought a baby gym on TikTok Shop in the past year, stop and read this.

The CPSC has recalled the Mikario Trading 5-in-1 Convertible Baby Gym — the pink units, sold exclusively on TikTok Shop from May through October 2025 for about $50. The problem: when the gym converts into a tummy-time pillow mat, the floor mat can obstruct an infant's breathing. It violates the mandatory safety standard for infant support cushions, which means suffocation risk.

Here's what to do right now: Stop using it immediately. Cut the mat in half — seriously, destroy it — and photograph the cut mat as proof. Then contact Mikario Trading at mikariorecall@gmail.com or by phone at 209-232-8655 (Monday through Friday, 7 a.m. to 1 p.m. ET) to request a full refund. The item number on pink units is CBPBG-PIN.

The TikTok Shop angle is worth noting: parents who discover and buy products through social media may not think of those purchases the same way they would an Amazon or Target buy, but CPSC recalls apply equally. If you've gifted one of these or passed it along, please share this with that family too. 🙏

That said, parents who attend board meetings or track district decisions may notice a different dynamic in the coming months as both roles settle.

### Story 3: Venetucci Farm Pumpkin Fest — LONG-FORM
**Posting window:** 3:00 PM MT

It's finally here — Venetucci Farm Pumpkin Fest is back, and this year they're celebrating 90 years! 🎃

The season runs every Friday, Saturday, and Sunday from October 1 through October 29 at the farm at 5210 S. US-85-87 in Colorado Springs. Hours are 10 AM to 4 PM. Admission is $5 per person, and kids 6 and under get in free. Pumpkins are $10 any size, which is genuinely one of the best deals in Colorado Springs fall.

Every weekend you'll find the pumpkin patch, hayrides, a scarecrow trail, games, concessions, and fall photo areas. Saturdays and Sundays add a craft fair in the barn and farm animals — solid for younger kiddos. Three themed weekends make it worth planning multiple trips if your family is into it: October 10–11 is Great Pumpkin Weekend (Charlie Brown reading nook, pumpkin beer for the grown-ups, guess-the-weight contest), October 17–18 is Scarecrows and Flannel, and October 30–31 is Costume Party and Pup-Kin Fest — trick-or-treating for kids and dogs.

Venetucci Farm is one of those COS traditions that doesn't need much selling. If you haven't been, it's worth a Friday afternoon trip before the weekend crowds hit.

Which themed weekend is your family most excited about — Great Pumpkin, Scarecrows and Flannel, or the Pup-Kin Fest?

### Story 4: AAP Flu Vaccine Guidance — LONG-FORM
**Posting window:** 12:00 PM MT

Flu season is coming, and the American Academy of Pediatrics has updated its 2026–27 guidance — there's a key change that affects a lot of Colorado Springs families with younger kids. 💉

If your child is between 6 months and 8 years old, they may need two flu shots this season instead of one. The two-dose requirement applies if they're getting the flu vaccine for the first time, if they received only one dose ever before July 1, 2026, or if their vaccine history is unknown. The doses need to be at least four weeks apart, and ideally both should be done by the end of October to build protection before flu season peaks.

A few things worth knowing: The two doses don't need to be the same brand or formulation. All 2026–27 flu vaccines in the U.S. are trivalent with updated compositions, so whatever your pediatrician has available will work. This guidance is also separate from the COVID vaccine updates that came out earlier this week — two different schedules to track.

If you're not sure whether your child qualifies for two doses, your pediatrician or a local pharmacy can check their vaccination history. The earlier you schedule, the easier it'll be to get both doses in before the October deadline.
