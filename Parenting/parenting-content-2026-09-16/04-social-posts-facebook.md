### STORY 2: Three Dresser Tip-Over Recalls

**Long-Form Post**
*Window: 10:00 AM MT*

Important safety alert for Colorado Springs families with young kids: three brands of six-drawer dressers were recalled this month for tip-over and entrapment hazards — and together they add up to more than 16,000 units in homes across the country.

Here's what's been recalled and where they were sold:

🔹 FJTJBSI — about 9,000 units, sold on Amazon.com from November 2024 through July 2026 for around $170. Remedy: write "RECALLED" in permanent marker on the dresser, then email a photo to FJTJBSIrecall@outlook.com for your refund.
🔹 Gizoon — about 4,396 units, sold on Amazon from May 2025 through August 2026 for $138 to $171.
🔹 Alanca — about 261 units, sold on Wayfair from April 2025 through June 2026 for $206 to $240.

All three violate the mandatory federal safety standard under the STURDY Act, which requires dressers to resist tipping when a child climbs or pulls on them.

If you have one of these dressers and it is not currently secured to the wall, stop using it and move it out of any area where children can access it. Full recall details are at cpsc.gov — search the brand name plus "dresser recall."

Have you anchored your kids' dressers to the wall? It's worth double-checking today.

### STORY 3: D49 Student Success Center

**Long-Form Post**
*Window: 8:00 AM MT*

Good news for families in District 49: the D49 Student Success Center is open.

Two new early childhood classrooms launched at the start of this school year at the former Falcon Elementary building, expanding the center's capacity for preschool and the Base49 program. The Student Success Center is designed to serve as a hub for specialized learning — a single location where D49's most intensive early childhood and support programs can operate under one roof.

There's more milestone news from D49 this month: nine additional campuses have been named Colorado Purple Star Schools by the Colorado Department of Education, recognizing their exceptional support of military-connected students and families. For a community with significant ties to Fort Carson and Peterson Space Force Base, this recognition matters.

### STORY 4: COS Parks Master Plan

**Long-Form Post**
*Window: 12:00 PM MT*

If you've ever pushed a stroller down a cracked trail, wished your neighborhood had a better playground, or wanted more shaded picnic areas at a city park — here's your chance to actually say something about it.

Colorado Springs is in the final stretch of public comments on its Draft Park System Master Plan, and the deadline is September 22. That's six days from today. The plan maps out the city's priorities for parks, trails, and open spaces for years to come, and the Parks Advisory Board reviewed the draft on September 10.

For context on what's at stake: Colorado Springs Parks, Recreation and Cultural Services operates three community centers, seven sports complexes, and five program areas, and serves more than 300,000 residents each year. This master plan will shape how that system grows (or doesn't) over the next decade.

You can read the draft and submit feedback at coloradosprings.gov/ParkSystemMasterPlan. It takes less time than you'd think.

### STORY 5: Fall Events Roundup

**Long-Form Post**
*Window: 3:00 PM MT*

It's a big week for Colorado Springs families, and we've pulled together everything worth knowing about before the weekend:

🐾 **Bark at Briargate** — This Saturday, September 19, from 11 AM to 3 PM. Free admission. Features on-site dog adoptions (great if your family has been thinking about adding a dog), pet-focused vendors, activities, and giveaways. Fun for kids and anyone who loves dogs.
