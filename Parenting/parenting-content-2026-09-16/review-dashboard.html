Three Dresser Tip-Over Recalls — FJTJBSI, Gizoon, Alanca (STURDY Act)
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post *Window: 10:00 AM MT* Important safety alert for Colorado Springs families with young kids: three brands of six-drawer dressers were recalled this month for tip-over and entrapment hazards — and together they add up to more than 16,000 units in homes across the country. Here's what's been recalled and where they were sold: 🔹 FJTJBSI — about 9,000 units, sold on Amazon.com from November 2024 through July 2026 for around $170. Remedy: write "RECALLED" in permanent marker on the dresser, then email a photo to FJTJBSIrecall@outlook.com for your refund. 🔹 Gizoon — about 4,396 units, sold on Amazon from May 2025 through August 2026 for $138 to $171. 🔹 Alanca — about 261 units, sold on Wayfair from April 2025 through June 2026 for $206 to $240. All three violate the mandatory federal safety standard under the STURDY Act, which requires dressers to resist tipping when a child climbs or pulls on them. If you have one of these dressers and it is not currently secured to the wall, stop using it and move it out of any area where children can access it. Full recall details are at cpsc.gov — search the brand name plus "dresser recall." Have you anchored your kids' dressers to the wall? It's worth double-checking today. --- ### Image Post Caption *Window: 10:00 AM MT* Recall alert: three dresser brands (FJTJBSI, Gizoon, Alanca) recalled for tip-over hazard — 16,000+ units. All sold on Amazon or Wayfair 2024-2026. Stop using if not wall-anchored. Full details at cpsc.gov.

### Long-Form Post *Window: 12:00 PM MT* If you've ever pushed a stroller down a cracked trail, wished your neighborhood had a better playground, or wanted more shaded picnic areas at a city park — here's your chance to actually say something about it. Colorado Springs is in the final stretch of public comments on its Draft Park System Master Plan, and the deadline is September 22. That's six days from today.
