### STORY 4: Target Gigglescape Under the Sea Popping Toy Recall

**HIGH confidence:**
- Recall issued 2026 (recall number 26-598)
- Units: approximately 49,000
- Product: Gigglescape Under the Sea Popping Toy (blue whale-shaped, clear dome with small plastic balls, plunger on top)
- Sold: exclusively Target stores nationwide
- Sold: August 2025 through January 2026
- Price: approximately $10
- Hazard: clear plastic dome can detach, exposing small plastic balls (choking hazard)
- Reports: 9 reports of dome detaching, 1 child began to choke
- Consumer action: stop use immediately; return to any Target store for full refund OR contact Target for prepaid return label
- Identifier: "Gigglescape" name on packaging and imprinted on bottom of blue plastic base

### STORY 5: Back-to-School Sleep Reset

**HIGH confidence (AAP-backed general guidance):**
- Kids ages 6-12: 9-12 hours sleep per night
- Kids ages 13-18: 8-10 hours sleep per night
- Recommendation to shift bedtime 15 minutes earlier per night to reset
- Recommendation: screens off 60 minutes before bed
- Blackout curtains help offset late summer sunsets
