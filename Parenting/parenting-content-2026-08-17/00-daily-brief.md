### STORY 2: CPSC Warning — Budget Baby Boost Crib Bumpers (Suffocation Hazard)

- **Content Pillar:** National Parenting
- **Tier:** 1
- **Byline:** Sarah Morales
- **Angle:** NEW STORY — August 6, 2026: CPSC warns consumers to stop using Budget Baby Boost Crib Bumpers immediately due to suffocation risk. ### STORY 5: Back-to-School Sleep Reset — What COS Pediatricians Recommend

- **Content Pillar:** National Parenting
- **Tier:** 2
- **Byline:** Jamie Rivera
- **Angle:** NEW STORY (evergreen with local timing hook) — D20 opens today, D11 opened Aug 12, D49 opened Aug 4 — most COS families are one week or less into the school routine. Pediatric sleep guidance: kids need 9-12 hours (ages 6-12) and 8-10 hours (ages 13-18); shift bedtime by 15 minutes earlier each night for a week; blackout curtains help with COS's late summer sunsets; screens off 60 minutes before bed. Tips are AAP-backed and useful for the entire first week of school when routines are still resetting.
