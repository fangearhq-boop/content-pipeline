COS Family Weekend Aug 22-23: Bear Creek's 50th Bear-thday, Sunflower Days, and Comic Con

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | August 17, 2026

The first weekend after all three COS districts are back in school tends to feel a little sideways — kids are tired, routines aren't settled, and nobody wants to plan a five-item schedule. This weekend is easier than most, because one event does most of the work for you: Bear Creek Nature Center is turning 50 on Saturday.

Saturday: Bear Creek Nature Center 50th "Bear-thday" Party

Bear Creek Nature Center opened in 1976 as the Solar Trails Center — Colorado's first nature center. Saturday, August 22, from 10 AM to 2 PM, the center is throwing itself a birthday party at 245 Bear Creek Road. It's free.

Programming includes living-history hikes, nature activities, book readings, art, educational exhibits, food, community partners, and a ribbon-cutting for the brand-new Life in the Shadows exhibit. The Living Nature Hike requires pre-registration; everything else is walk-up. The El Paso County Board of Commissioners formally declared August 22 a day of celebration to mark the milestone.

This is the standout free pick of the month. Bear Creek is easy to reach from most of central and southwest COS, parking is straightforward, and the mix of hikes and indoor exhibits makes it work for kids across a wide age range.

All Weekend: Sunflower Days at Gather Mountain Blooms

Sunflower Days runs August 15 through August 29 at Gather Mountain Blooms, 5210 South U.S. Highway 85 87. Tickets range from $0 to $11.17; children under 6 are free. There's floral-inspired cocktails, coffee from Good Gracious Coffee, brunch from 4th Avenue Crepes, and a small artisan makers market. If you go this weekend and want it to feel like a summer morning, arrive early — 8 AM to 10 AM slots have the best light and the calmest crowds.

Aug 21-23: Colorado Springs Comic Con

For older kids and comic-obsessed families, Colorado Springs Comic Con runs Aug 21 at 3 PM and Aug 22-23 starting at 10 AM at the Broadmoor World Arena, 3185 Venetucci Blvd. Family-friendly with cosplay, panels, and vendor booths.
