D20 First Day of School: Preschool, K, 6th, and 9th Grade Start Today (August 17, 2026)

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | August 17, 2026

Today is the first day of the 2026-27 school year for Academy District 20 preschool, kindergarten, 6th grade, and 9th grade students. All other D20 grades — 1st through 5th, 7th through 8th, and 10th through 12th — start tomorrow, Tuesday August 18. If you have kids in more than one grade band, you're running a two-day rollout this week.

The Staggered Start Is Intentional

D20 splits the first day for a reason. New-to-building students — the incoming kindergarteners at every elementary school, the incoming 6th graders at every middle school, and the incoming 9th graders at every high school — get a full day with a smaller population before older siblings, upperclassmen, and everyone else arrives on Tuesday. Fewer bodies in the hallway means more time with counselors, more time to find lockers and lunch tables, and less pressure on the first ride of the school bus.

Preschool joins Monday for the same reason: smaller class rosters, easier transitions, more one-on-one time with new teachers.

COS Back-to-School Snapshot

D20 is the last of the three big COS-area districts to open. Colorado Springs D11 started August 12 (with Jumpstart Day for K, 6th, and 9th on August 11). District 49 started earlier still, on August 4. That means today is when the whole city fully switches into school-year rhythm. Morning traffic on Powers, Interquest, and the north-end corridors will be heavier through the week; drop-off lines will be longest at buildings serving mixed grade bands where Monday and Tuesday families overlap.

What's Next

Tomorrow, August 18, brings the rest of D20's roughly 26,000 students back to buildings — the first day with the full district in-session. Watch for building-specific communications about parking, bus routes, and morning arrival windows; those tend to arrive in the parent portal in the first 48 hours. If you're new to the district, save the D20 main line and your school's front office number in your phone before the week runs away from you.
