# Story Analysis — COS Parenting
**Date:** 2026-08-17

---

### STORY 1: D20 First Day of School

- **Primary Angle:** Live coverage of Colorado's largest COS-area district on opening day.
- **Key Tension:** Staggered schedule — some families sending kids Monday, others Tuesday. Confusion possible if families weren't paying attention.
- **Audience Hook:** "Today's the day, D20 families. Here's the full schedule for both today and tomorrow."
- **Content Required:** Article + 2 X posts + FB Long-Form + FB Image Caption + 2 image concepts (Gemini base_only)
- **Byline:** Jamie Rivera
- **Voice Check:** Warm-but-organized tone. Use "families" not "moms and dads." Explicitly call out the two dates so nobody misses their kid's day.

### STORY 2: Budget Baby Boost Crib Bumpers CPSC Warning

- **Primary Angle:** Federal safety warning; must-read for any COS family with an infant.
- **Key Tension:** Crib bumpers have been federally banned since 2022, but they still turn up on secondary markets, in gift boxes, from well-meaning grandparents. Parents may not know the product is illegal AND lethal.
- **Audience Hook:** "If you have this brand of crib bumper — or ANY crib bumper — stop using it. Here's what the CPSC just said."
- **Content Required:** Article + 2 X posts + FB Long-Form + FB Image Caption + 2 image concepts (Gemini base_only)
- **Byline:** Sarah Morales
- **Voice Check:** Serious and calm — this is a safety piece. No exclamation marks in article. Do NOT scare-monger; be direct.

### STORY 3: Weekend Events Aug 22-23

- **Primary Angle:** Weekend planner — three anchor events with Bear Creek 50th "Bear-thday" as the standout free pick.
- **Key Tension:** Late summer + back-to-school routines = parents crave low-effort weekend plans. Bear Creek is walkable, free, family-friendly, once-in-a-generation.
- **Audience Hook:** "Bear Creek Nature Center is turning 50 this Saturday. Here's how to make it a family morning."
- **Content Required:** Article + 1 X post + FB Long-Form + FB Image Caption + 2 image concepts (Gemini base_only)
- **Byline:** Jamie Rivera
- **Voice Check:** Enthusiastic without being over-the-top. "Free," "walking distance," and "kids under 6 free" are the trust signals.

### STORY 5: Back-to-School Sleep Reset

- **Primary Angle:** Evergreen sleep guidance timed to the first week of school in all three main COS districts.
- **Key Tension:** Nobody wants to be the parent who dropped bedtime enforcement over summer and is now paying the price at 6:45 AM every weekday.
- **Audience Hook:** "One week in — if bedtime is a battle right now, here's how pediatricians recommend fixing it."
- **Content Required:** Article + 1 X post + FB Long-Form + FB Image Caption + 2 image concepts (Gemini base_only)
- **Byline:** Jamie Rivera
