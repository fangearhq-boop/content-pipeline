Pediatric sleep guidance: kids need 9-12 hours (ages 6-12) and 8-10 hours (ages 13-18); shift bedtime by 15 minutes earlier each night for a week; blackout curtains help with COS's late summer sunsets; screens off 60 minutes before bed.
