Colorado Turns 150 Today — Free Family Celebration at Pioneers Museum in COS Through 4 p.m.

By Jamie Rivera | August 1, 2026

Colorado Springs, CO

Colorado became a state on August 1, 1876. That was 150 years ago today, which makes this Colorado's sesquicentennial — a milestone the state has been building toward all year. In Colorado Springs, the celebration is happening right now, and it is free.

The Colorado Springs Pioneers Museum, at 215 S. Tejon Street in downtown Colorado Springs, is hosting a full day of family activities through 4 p.m. today. There is no admission charge, and no advance registration is required. Families can park downtown and walk to the museum.

What's at the Museum Today

The event lineup includes live music from the United States Air Force Academy's "Wild Blue" ensemble — a performance group based at the Academy that plays a range of American musical styles. Food trucks are on-site, and there is birthday cake. The Pikes Peak Library District is running a historical photo booth with Colorado-themed imagery and backdrops. Wishing Star Farms has brought a petting zoo for families with young children.

For families looking for something beyond the activities, the museum is also opening a new exhibit today: "Freedom to Thrive: The American Experiment in Colorado Springs." The exhibit examines the stories of people in Colorado Springs who faced obstacles and opportunities related to freedom over the course of the community's history. The opening coincides with Colorado Day intentionally — the museum has been building toward this exhibit as part of the state's 150th anniversary programming.

The Context: Colorado's 150th Year

Colorado was admitted to the Union as the 38th state on August 1, 1876, earning it the nickname "The Centennial State" — it joined during the nation's centennial year. The 150th anniversary falls in 2026, which also marks the 250th anniversary of American independence, creating a statewide dual celebration that Colorado Springs has marked throughout the summer.

The City of Colorado Springs has maintained a Colorado 150 programming page at coloradosprings.gov/America250Colorado150 throughout the year. Today's Pioneers Museum event is among the signature local celebrations of the milestone. The museum itself has deep roots in the city's founding story and serves as a fitting venue for a day that is as much about reflecting on where Colorado Springs came from as it is about celebrating where it is today.

What's Next

Today's event runs through 4 p.m. at the Colorado Springs Pioneers Museum, 215 S. Tejon Street. It is free and open to the public. The museum's regular programming — including the new "Freedom to Thrive" exhibit — continues beyond today. Hours and details at cspm.org.

Colorado's 150th statehood anniversary programming continues across the state through the year. For families who miss today's event, the museum and the city's America 250 / Colorado 150 pages will have ongoing programming details through the fall.
