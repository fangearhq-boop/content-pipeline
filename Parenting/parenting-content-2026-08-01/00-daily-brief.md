### STORY 5: D11 Bond Vote Is August 5 — Four Days Away
- **Tier:** 2
- **Pillar:** Local News
- **Angle:** FOLLOW-UP (July 30 Story 2) — The D11 Board of Education votes August 5 on whether to place the $775 million bond on the November 2026 ballot. Four days away. The bond would fund air conditioning for D11 schools between 50–100 years old that currently lack AC, and include teacher pay increases. Tax impact if approved and passed by voters: approximately $6.50 more per month per $100,000 of assessed property value. Also notable: Jenkins Middle School (D11) is expected to reopen for students this fall after a roughly 18-month closure due to structural failures (cracking/separating floors and walls). 6th and 7th graders spent 2025–26 in portable classrooms; 8th graders were relocated to Doherty High School. Both stories tie back to the state of D11 facilities. Board meeting info at d11.org.
- **Story History:** FOLLOW-UP from July 30 Story 2 (D11 bond overview)
