### STORY 2: CPSC Recalls — Baby Swings, Bath Seats, Magnetic Sets

**HIGH confidence facts:**
- **VEVOR/Sanven Technology Baby Swings:** Recalled for suffocation hazard; violates mandatory standard for infant sleep products and the inclined sleepers ban; sold on Amazon (CPSC.gov; Consumer Reports 2026 roundup — confirmed 2026 recall)
- **NFSVLB Baby Bath Seats:** Recalled for risk of drowning and entrapment; sold on Amazon; violates mandatory standard for infant bath seats (CPSC.gov; Consumer Reports 2026 roundup — confirmed 2026 recall)
- **Ocensmice Magnetic Building Stick and Ball Sets (Amazon):** CPSC warning (not full recall but CPSC safety warning); magnets exceed federal strength limits; swallowed magnets can attract through intestinal walls causing perforation or death (CPSC.gov advisory — confirmed 2026)
- For all three: check for products at home and stop use; report to CPSC.gov for remedies; full recall details at CPSC.gov/Recalls
- Magnet safety: swallowed high-powered magnets are a leading cause of serious intestinal injury in children; even one magnet that attracts to another through intestinal walls can cause perforation, obstruction, or death — federal mandatory magnet standard was updated in 2022 and products must comply (CPSC magnet guidance)

**MEDIUM confidence facts:**
- Specific unit counts, sale dates, and remedy details for VEVOR and NFSVLB not pinpointed in search results — full details at CPSC.gov/Recalls
- Ocensmice is a CPSC warning rather than a formal recall per search results

**Sources:** CPSC.gov/Recalls, Consumer Reports 2026 baby product recall roundup, CPSC magnet safety guidance

### STORY 5: D11 Bond Vote August 5
