## Story 2: CPSC Recalls — GOPO Teething Toys & HARPPA Toddler Tower Stools

### GOPO Toys Pull String Teething Toys (HIGH urgency)
- Hazard: Silicone strings exceed permitted length AND fall below minimum size → reach back of child's throat → choking/respiratory distress; RISK OF DEATH
- Units: ~70,410
- Sold: Amazon.com, August 2023–March 2026, $11–$15
- Incidents: 3 reported (choking/respiratory distress)
- Remedy: Stop use immediately. Cut all silicone strings, write "DESTROYED" across toy body in permanent marker, photograph, email to recalls@gopotoys.com for full refund
- Source: CPSC.gov, Fox Business
- Confidence: HIGH

### HARPPA Nordi Toddler Tower Stool (Model HANS0002)
- Hazard: Can collapse or tip over; child's torso can fit through front/back openings → entrapment + fall risk; RISK OF DEATH
- Units: ~41,166
- Sold: Harppababy.com and Amazon.com, September 2023–June 2026, ~$130
- Description: White, black and blue with natural wood; ~18"D × 18"W × 38.6"H; "MODEL No.: HANS0002" on label underside
- Incidents: 3 reports of collapsing/tipping; 2 injuries (scrapes, cuts, bruises)
- Remedy: Contact HARPPA for FREE repair kit (protective nets + stabilizing feet + instructions, free shipping)
- Source: CPSC.gov, WKYC report
- Confidence: HIGH

## Story 5: Screen Time & Child Mental Health Study 2026

### Verified Facts (HIGH confidence)
- Published: 2026 in *Humanities and Social Sciences Communications* (Nature portfolio)
- Sample: 50,231 U.S. children and adolescents ages 6–17 (National Survey of Children's Health data)
- Key finding 1: 4+ hours daily entertainment screen time → 45% higher anxiety risk
- Key finding 2: 4+ hours daily entertainment screen time → 61% higher depression risk
- Mechanism: sleep displacement is the key pathway; screens displace sleep and physical activity
- Highest-risk behavior: bedroom screens after 9 PM (sleep displacement, not primarily content)
- Policy: HHS/Surgeon General calling for urgent action
- Source: Nature/HSSC (DOI not retrieved but publication confirmed), Medical Daily summary, HHS press release, Roya Health parent guide
- Confidence: HIGH
- Note: Distinct from Aug 11's AAP screen time story (which focused on type/context framework, not hour-count risk percentages)

### Parent Takeaways (AAP-aligned):
- Device-free bedrooms, especially after 9 PM
- 1-hour cap per day for ages 2–5
- No screens before 18 months
