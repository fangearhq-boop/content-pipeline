Two Squishy Dumpling Toy Lines Recalled Over Deadly Water Bead Hazard — Check Your Kids' Toy Bins

By Jamie Rivera October 5, 2026

Two Recalls, Same Hazard

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission issued two separate recalls on October 1, 2026, targeting squishy dumpling toys that contain water beads expanding beyond safe limits. Both recalls cover products currently or recently in circulation, and families should check their children's toy bins immediately.

The first recall involves LLK Trading's Mochi Malrang Mystery Dumpling Toys — a translucent red squishy toy with a cartoon face, packaged in a tan plastic container shaped like a bamboo steamer. Approximately 480 units were sold at Easy Wholesale in San Jose, California in July 2026 for about $10 each.

The second recall involves ABC Trading's Squishy Dumpling Toys (model 3655) and Crazy Squib Ball Toys (model 3984) — multi-colored translucent toys filled with glitter and water beads, available in several colors. About 37,000 units are affected in this recall.

Why Water Beads Are Dangerous

Water beads expand significantly when they absorb moisture. Both recalled products contain water beads that expand beyond the size permitted under mandatory federal toy safety standards. If a child swallows a water bead, it continues to absorb liquid inside the body, causing it to swell further.

The hazard is serious: ingested water beads can cause choking, intestinal obstruction, severe vomiting, dehydration, and in the most serious cases, death. Neither recall had reported any incidents or injuries as of the October 1 recall date — but that is the nature of recalls issued to get products out of homes before injuries occur.

The CPSC has now issued five total recalls covering approximately 53,000 squishy dumpling and bun toys since September 3, reflecting a pattern of similar products violating the same mandatory safety standard.

What to Do Right Now

If you have either product in your home, stop use immediately and keep it away from children.

For LLK Trading Mochi Malrang Mystery Dumpling Toys:

  • Write "RECALLED" in permanent marker on the toy and its packaging
  • Take a photo
  • Email llktradinginc@gmail.com to request a full refund

For ABC Trading Squishy Dumpling Toys and Crazy Squib Ball Toys:

  • Write "recalled" on the toy
  • Email recallabc@gmail.com with a photo
  • A full refund will be issued

Full recall details, including product photos to help identify affected toys, are available at cpsc.gov/Recalls.

What's Next

The CPSC has flagged this category of toys as an ongoing safety concern, and more recalls in this product category are possible. Parents in Colorado Springs can sign up for CPSC recall alerts at cpsc.gov/recalls/stay-informed to receive notifications for future product safety announcements. If your child has swallowed any small toy component and is experiencing symptoms, contact Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222 or go to the nearest emergency room.
