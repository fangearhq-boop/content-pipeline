D11 Fall Break Is October 19–23 — Activities to Fill the Week in Colorado Springs

By Sarah Morales October 5, 2026

Mark Your Calendar

Colorado Springs School District 11 families have fall break from Monday, October 19 through Friday, October 23 — five days with no school. For families in D11, that break is two weeks away from today. AcademyACL, a Colorado Springs charter school, also has fall break scheduled for October 19–23. D20 and D49 families should confirm their own district's dates at d20.org or d49.org, as break weeks vary by district.

October 19–23 lands in the sweet spot of Colorado Springs fall: peak foliage is typically mid-October at local parks, Halloween events are ramping up, and daytime temperatures are still comfortable for outdoor activities. Evening temperatures can dip into the 40s by late October, so plan layers for any after-dark events.

What's Happening That Week in Colorado Springs

Several family-friendly activities run through or alongside fall break week:

Boo at the Zoo (Cheyenne Mountain Zoo, ongoing through October 31): Kids can trick-or-treat in costume at station throughout the zoo. There's also a haunted house section for families who want a slightly spookier experience. The zoo is open daily, making it a reliable option for any day of break week. Confirm current pricing and hours at cmzoo.org before you go.

Garden of the Gods (free, open daily): The red rock park typically hits its peak fall color display around mid-October, when the surrounding scrub oak and cottonwood trees turn. The Visitor and Nature Center is free, and the park itself requires no admission. It's one of the best free family activities in Colorado Springs in October.

Local pumpkin patches and fall festivals: Several farms and community organizations in the Colorado Springs area run pumpkin patches through late October. Check the Gazette's local events calendar for specific locations and hours as break week approaches.

Planning the Weekend After Break

The weekend immediately following fall break — Saturday, October 24 — has two notable events worth planning ahead for:

Pumpkins in the Park at Bancroft Park in Old Colorado City is a free community festival featuring massive carved gourds, live music, and a costume contest. It's one of the best free family events in the Colorado Springs fall calendar and a natural way to close out a fall break week.

Emma Crawford Coffin Races in Manitou Springs, also October 24, is one of the more uniquely Colorado Halloween traditions — teams decorate coffins and race them down Manitou Avenue. It's free to watch and genuinely memorable for families with older kids.
