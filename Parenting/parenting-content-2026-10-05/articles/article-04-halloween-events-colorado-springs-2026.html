Halloween in Colorado Springs: Best Family Events for October 2026

By Jamie Rivera October 5, 2026

A Month of Options

October is one of the most activity-rich months of the year for families in Colorado Springs. Between free community festivals, zoo events, and local cultural traditions, there's a solid lineup for families with kids of all ages — from toddlers in pumpkin costumes to older kids who want something a little spookier.

One planning note: late October in Colorado Springs can bring significant temperature swings. Daytime highs in the upper 50s are common by the last week of October, but evening temperatures regularly drop into the low 30s. For any outdoor evening event, plan costumes and layers accordingly.

Pumpkins in the Park — Free Community Festival

Pumpkins in the Park at Bancroft Park in Old Colorado City is one of the best free family events in the Colorado Springs fall calendar. The event takes place on Saturday, October 24, and features massive carved and decorated gourds on display, live music, and a costume contest. It draws families from across the city each year and works well for all ages — there's enough spectacle for toddlers and enough community energy for older kids and parents alike.

Bancroft Park is located in Old Colorado City, a walkable neighborhood with restaurants and shops nearby. Plan for parking to be busy — Old Colorado City is a popular destination on fall Saturdays.

Boo at the Bridge — Ticketed Family Experience

Boo at the Bridge takes place on Wednesday, October 22, from 4 to 7:30 PM at Royal Gorge Bridge and Park. Tickets are $13. The event includes trick-or-treating, games, and live entertainment at one of Colorado's most scenic venues. The gorge setting adds a natural Halloween atmosphere — dramatic, elevated, and genuinely memorable.

This is a mid-week event, which means it works well for fall break families and those who can make a weeknight outing. Verify current pricing and ticket availability at royalgorgebridge.com before planning.

Boo at the Zoo — All Month at Cheyenne Mountain Zoo

Cheyenne Mountain Zoo's Boo at the Zoo runs through October 31. Kids can trick-or-treat at stations throughout the zoo while dressed in costume, moving through exhibits that include the zoo's resident animals as a backdrop. For families who want a slightly more intense experience, there's a haunted section available as well.

The zoo sits at elevation on the side of Cheyenne Mountain, which means temperatures there run a few degrees cooler than downtown Colorado Springs — especially in the evenings. Confirm current admission rates and hours at cmzoo.org. The zoo is a membership destination for many COS families, making Boo at the Zoo a recurring seasonal value for regular members.

Halloween History Detectives — Free at Pioneers Museum

Halloween History Detectives at the Colorado Springs Pioneers Museum on October 25 is a low-key option that works especially well for curious kids who want more substance with their Halloween. The event lets families trick-or-treat for local history facts and complete hands-on crafts throughout the museum. Admission to the museum is free. It's a good pick for families with school-age kids who want to anchor the holiday in something local.
