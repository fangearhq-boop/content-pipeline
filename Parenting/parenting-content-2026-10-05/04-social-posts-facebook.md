### STORY 1: D11 $775M Bond Measure

**Long-Form Post (8:30 AM MT)**

Colorado Springs D11 families, there's something important on the November ballot that affects every family in the district.

On August 5, the D11 Board of Education voted 6-1 to place a $775 million bond measure — Ballot Question 4A — on the November general election ballot. It would be the first voter-approved bond in the district since 2004.

What it covers: repairs at every D11 school. That means new HVAC systems, asbestos removal, modernized classrooms, a full rebuild of one school, and finally getting air conditioning into Coronado High School. The district says the average homeowner would pay approximately $260 per year if the measure passes.

October is the time to get informed before ballots arrive. We'll be covering this one closely in the weeks ahead.

### STORY 2: CPSC Squishy Dumpling Toy Recalls

**Long-Form Post (10:30 AM MT)**

If your kids have squishy dumpling toys, check the toy bin today.

The CPSC issued two separate recalls on October 1 covering these types of toys. LLK Trading recalled Mochi Malrang Mystery Dumpling Toys — a translucent red squishy dumpling in a bamboo steamer container, sold at Easy Wholesale in San Jose in July for about $10. About 480 units are affected.

ABC Trading recalled around 37,000 Squishy Dumpling Toys (model 3655) and Crazy Squib Ball Toys (model 3984) — multi-color translucent toys with glitter and water beads inside.

Both recalls involve the same hazard: the water beads inside expand beyond the safe limit. If a child swallows one, it can cause choking, intestinal obstruction, severe vomiting, and dehydration — and in serious cases, can be fatal.

Stop use immediately. For each product, write "RECALLED" on the toy and contact the company for a full refund:
- LLK Trading: llktradinginc@gmail.com
- ABC Trading: recallabc@gmail.com

CPSC has now issued five recalls covering about 53,000 of these toys since September 3. This one matters — please share it.

### STORY 5: Parent-Teacher Conference Season

**Long-Form Post (5:30 PM MT)**

It's parent-teacher conference season in Colorado Springs, and the first round of fall PTCs is just around the corner for many families.

CIVA Charter School has conferences scheduled for October 14 (5–7:30 PM, school in session) and October 15 (4–7:30 PM, no school), with a follow-up round on October 23 and 24. D11's school year calendar includes two designated parent-teacher conference days — individual schools typically set specific times and sign-up windows.

If you haven't done a fall PTC in a while, here's what makes them worth preparing for:

- **Come with a question.** Teachers appreciate specificity: "How does my child do with transitions?" goes further than "How are they doing?"
- **Ask what support looks like at home.** Teachers are more likely to have practical suggestions than you'd expect.
- **Ask about the second half of the semester.** Fall PTCs are early enough that there's real time to course-correct before semester grades close.
- **Take notes.** You won't remember everything, and a quick note on your phone is enough.

Check your school's app or website for sign-up windows — many schools open scheduling 1–2 weeks before conferences.
