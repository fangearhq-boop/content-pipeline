### STORY 1: D11's $775M Bond Measure Is on the November Ballot — What COS Families Need to Know
**Type:** NEW
**Tier:** 1 (Major local school district ballot measure — direct financial impact on COS families)
**Pillar:** Local News
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Angle:** Colorado Springs School District 11's Board of Education voted 6-1 on August 5 to place a $775 million bond measure on the November 2026 general election ballot. The measure, Ballot Question 4A, would fund repairs at every D11 school — HVAC upgrades, asbestos removal, modernized classrooms, a full school rebuild, and air conditioning at Coronado High School. If approved, the average homeowner in D11 would pay approximately $260/year. It would be the district's first voter-approved bond since 2004. October is the time for COS families to get informed before November ballots arrive.

### STORY 2: Two Squishy Dumpling Toy Lines Recalled Over Deadly Water Bead Hazard — Check Your Kids' Toy Bins
**Type:** NEW
**Tier:** 1 (CPSC recall — safety story, mandatory Tier 1)
**Pillar:** National Parenting (Product Safety)
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Angle:** Two separate CPSC recalls issued October 1, 2026 target squishy dumpling toys containing water beads that expand beyond safe limits. LLK Trading recalled 480 Mochi Malrang Mystery Dumpling Toys (translucent red, cartoon face, $10) sold at Easy Wholesale in San Jose in July 2026. ABC Trading recalled about 37,000 Squishy Dumpling Toys (model 3655) and Crazy Squib Ball Toys (model 3984), multi-color translucent toys filled with glitter and water beads. All pose ingestion, choking, and intestinal obstruction hazards — water beads can cause severe vomiting, dehydration, and death if swallowed. Stop use immediately; contact each company for a full refund. CPSC has now issued five recalls covering ~53,000 such toys since September 3.
**Sources:** cpsc.gov/Recalls (LLK and ABC Trading notices), fox13news.com, citizen.com, recalltimes.com
