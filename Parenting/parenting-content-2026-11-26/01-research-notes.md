### STORY 2: Qpancy Children's Pajama Recall

**Key Facts:**

- CPSC announced recall of approximately 1,309 Qpancy children's pajama sets on Nov. 19, 2026 — HIGH
- Recall number: 27-094 — HIGH (consistent across sources)
- Hazard: Sets fail mandatory flammability requirements for children's sleepwear; pose risk of serious burn injury or death — HIGH (CPSC.gov official recall page)
- Products: Long-sleeve, button-front tops with front pocket and shorts, plus matching doll pajamas — HIGH
- Sizes: 4-5T through 10-11Y — HIGH
- Prints: Multicolor cats, pink bows and stripes — HIGH
- Sold on Amazon from March through August 2026 for approximately $20 — HIGH
- Seller: Shanghai Shengkailong Trading Co., Ltd. (doing business as Qpancy), China — HIGH
- No injuries or incidents reported — HIGH
- Consumer action: Stop use; email qpancyrecall@outlook.com for refund; cut garments in half, send photo, then discard — HIGH
- Recall page: https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Qpancy-Recalls-Childrens-Pajamas-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Burn-Hazard-Violate-Mandatory-Standards-for-Childrens-Sleepwear — HIGH

### STORY 4: Colorado Springs School Zones Expansion

**Key Facts:**

- Before 2023, school zones were largely limited to elementary schools in Colorado Springs — HIGH
- A Doherty High School student was struck and killed by a vehicle near the school in 2023, prompting the expansion — HIGH (Gazette, KOAA)
- City launched Safe Streets COS program — includes school zone enhancements, roadway/intersection improvements, trail crossing projects, and partnerships with CSPD on speed safety cameras — HIGH (coloradosprings.gov)
- New zones being added to multiple middle and high schools: Vista Ridge, Sierra, Cheyenne Mountain, Mann, Panorama, West, Palmer High School among others — MEDIUM (Gazette Feb. 2026 report; some may not be complete yet)
- Flashers at Sierra High School operational for 2026-27 school year — MEDIUM
- Installation at Panorama Middle School set for completion "by end of summer" — MEDIUM (timing may have passed; treat as "recently completed")
- New zones affect D11, D20, and D49 schools — HIGH
- State bill HB26-1318 "Traffic Safety Near Schools" heard by legislature in 2026 — MEDIUM (outcome unknown)
