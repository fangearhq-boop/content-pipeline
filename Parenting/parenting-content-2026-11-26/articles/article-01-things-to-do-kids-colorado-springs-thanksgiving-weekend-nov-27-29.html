Things to Do with Kids in Colorado Springs This Weekend (Nov 27–29)

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | November 26, 2026

Thanksgiving has come and gone, and now you've got three full days of holiday weekend stretching out ahead of you with kids who need somewhere to go. Good news: Colorado Springs delivers. Whether you're looking for free outdoor adventures, some festive shopping fun, or a chance to get your skates on for the first time this season, there's something happening this weekend that works for the whole family.

Here's your guide to the best things to do with kids in Colorado Springs this weekend, Nov. 27–29. For the full list of everything happening, check out everything to do with kids in Colorado Springs this weekend, and don't miss the free things to do this weekend if you're keeping it budget-friendly.

1. Skate in the Park at Acacia Park

When: Open most days through Jan. 31; check for Thanksgiving weekend hours
Where: Acacia Park, downtown Colorado Springs
Cost: $13 per person, includes skate rentals

The ice rink at Acacia Park is one of the best spots to kick off the holiday season with kids. It's right in the heart of downtown, easy to get to, and the rental skates are included in the admission price. Even first-timers can give it a go — the rink is sized for all skill levels. Bundle up and make an afternoon of it.

2. Colorado Country Christmas Gift Show

When: Fri. Nov. 27, 10 a.m.–7 p.m.; Sat. Nov. 28, 10 a.m.–7 p.m.; Sun. Nov. 29, 10 a.m.–5 p.m.
Where: Colorado Springs Event Center, 3960 Palmer Park Blvd.
Cost: Check the event website for admission pricing

This popular holiday market brings dozens of local and regional vendors together for a weekend of festive shopping. Santa appearances are on the schedule, making it a fun family stop even if you're not in full shopping mode. It's a good way to ease the kids into the holiday spirit while you check a few gifts off the list.

3. Manitou Springs Elf Hunt

When: Through Dec. 20, all day, every day
Where: Downtown Manitou Springs
Cost: Free

The Manitou Elf Hunt is exactly what it sounds like: a free holiday scavenger hunt through the shops and streets of downtown Manitou Springs, where little elves have been hidden for kids to find. It's one of those low-key, low-cost activities that kids genuinely love, and it doubles as a great reason to explore the quirky charm of Manitou before the busiest shopping weeks of December. Plan an hour or two and pair it with a stop for hot chocolate. For more free things to do this weekend across Colorado Springs, check out the full guide.

4. Garden of the Gods

When: Open daily
Where: 1805 N. 30th St., Colorado Springs
Cost: Free

You don't need a reason to go to Garden of the Gods, but "post-Thanksgiving energy to burn" is a pretty good one. The park is open every day, free to enter, and the red rock formations never get old. Even a 30-minute walk through the main loop is enough to get some fresh air and remind yourself — and the kids — that Colorado is a pretty special place to live. The Visitor and Nature Center is also worth a stop if the weather turns cold.

5. Pro Rodeo Hall of Fame and Museum

When: Open daily; check website for holiday weekend hours
Where: 101 Pro Rodeo Dr., Colorado Springs
Cost: Check website for current admission pricing

If you've got curious kids who've never been, the Pro Rodeo Hall of Fame is one of Colorado Springs' most underrated family outings. It's warm, interactive, and genuinely interesting for all ages. The exhibits on rodeo history, animal care, and Western heritage keep kids engaged longer than you might expect. A solid rainy-day or cold-weather option.
