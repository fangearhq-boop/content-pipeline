CPSC Recalls 1,309 Qpancy Children’s Pajamas from Amazon Over Burn Hazard

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | November 26, 2026

If you bought children's pajamas from a brand called Qpancy on Amazon this year, check your kids' dresser before bedtime tonight. The Consumer Product Safety Commission announced a recall of approximately 1,309 Qpancy children's pajama sets on Nov. 19, 2026, because the sets fail mandatory federal flammability standards for children's sleepwear — meaning they pose a risk of serious burn injury.

The recall number is 27-094, and the full details are available at cpsc.gov.

No injuries have been reported before the recall announcement, but the CPSC says families should stop using the pajamas immediately.

Why It Matters

Federal flammability standards for children's sleepwear exist because young children are at higher risk of injury from clothing fires, and pajamas are worn during sleep when a fire could go undetected longer. When sleepwear fails these standards, it can ignite and burn more quickly than compliant fabric.

The pajamas were sold during the spring and summer months — which means many families may have bought them for back-to-school or general use and they're still in circulation. With the holiday season now underway, pajama sets are common gifts, so it's also worth flagging this to anyone who might be shopping for similar items.

What to Do

If you have these pajamas:

  1. Stop using them immediately.
  2. Email Qpancy at qpancyrecall@outlook.com to request a full refund.
  3. Cut the pajamas in half, take a photo of the destroyed garment, and send the photo to Qpancy.
  4. Discard the cut-up pajamas.

You don't need a receipt to get the refund — the company is asking for the photo of destruction as proof.

What's Next

If you're shopping for children's sleepwear this holiday season, look for pajamas that are labeled "flame resistant" or made from naturally tight-fitting fabrics that meet federal flammability requirements. The CPSC maintains a full recall list at cpsc.gov where you can search any product by brand or category. It's worth bookmarking — especially as holiday gifts start arriving. If you're unsure whether a product has been recalled, that's your first stop.
