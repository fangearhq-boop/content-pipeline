CPSC Recall Alert: Check Your Infant Walkers and Baby Products Before Back-to-School Season

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | August 3, 2026

National Parenting

Summer is peak season for U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission recalls — and this one has three separate actions worth knowing about if you have a baby or toddler at home. As the school-year chaos picks back up for older kids, it's easy to miss a recall notice that arrived during a busy week in July. Here's what was flagged and what to do if you have one of these products.

WonderStone Infant Walkers (Models 616 and 616-1)

The CPSC issued a full recall on July 10 for WonderStone infant walkers, covering approximately 70 units sold through Walmart.com in April 2026 at around $80 each. The walkers come in green and pink, with a gray fabric seat, white tray, detachable music box, and six wheels. The recall covers both Models 616 and 616-1.

The hazard is serious: these walkers fit through a standard doorway — which means a child can roll right out of a room — and they fail to stop at a step edge, removing the only barrier between the walker and a stair fall. The CPSC notes the risk includes serious injury or death. If you bought one of these on Walmart.com this spring, stop using it immediately, disassemble it, cut the seat, write "Recalled" on the tray, and email a photo to wonderstonerecall@outlook.com to receive a full refund.

Babypark 3-in-1 Soft Carriers

On July 2, the CPSC issued a warning for Babypark 3-in-1 Soft Infant and Toddler Carriers sold on Amazon between April 2024 and May 2026, priced around $27. The carriers have leg openings large enough for an infant to slip through, posing a fall hazard. This violates the mandatory federal safety standard for soft infant and toddler carriers.

Stop use immediately if you have this carrier. The CPSC Warnings page at cpsc.gov has full details and photos for identification.

Target Gigglescape Under the Sea Popping Toy

Target recalled about 49,000 units of the Gigglescape Under the Sea Popping Toy (CPSC Recall #26-598) on July 2. The toy was sold at Target stores from August 2025 through January 2026 for around $10. It's a whale-shaped toy with a clear plastic dome on top and small plastic balls inside.

The plastic dome can detach from the base, exposing the balls inside — a choking hazard for young children. The CPSC received nine incident reports, including one child who choked on a ball. The product is identified by "Gigglescape" printed on the front of the packaging and on the underside of the blue base. Return it to any Target store for a full refund — no receipt needed for recalled items.

How to Check Any Product

The CPSC's full recall database is at cpsc.gov. You can search by product name, brand, or category. Infant walkers are one of the CPSC's most consistently recalled product categories — they're responsible for thousands of emergency room visits annually, and the agency has been pushing for stronger mandatory standards for years. If you have any infant walker, it's worth a quick search regardless of brand.

For soft carriers, the CPSC's SaferProducts.gov page also allows consumers to search by product type and report incidents.

What's Next

The CPSC releases recall notices throughout the year, with summer typically seeing higher volume as new product lines hit the market. Bookmarking cpsc.gov and checking it periodically — especially after purchasing baby or toddler products online — is the most reliable way to stay current. If a product you own is recalled and the recall isn't caught until months later, most manufacturers still honor refunds and remedies.
