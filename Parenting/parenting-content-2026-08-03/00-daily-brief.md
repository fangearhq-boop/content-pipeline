### STORY 3: D11 Bond Vote Is Two Days Away — What Wednesday's $775M Decision Means for COS Families
- **Tier:** 2 (Local News)
- **Pillar:** Local News

- **Byline:** Sarah Morales
- **Posting Window:** 12:00 PM MT

District 11's $775 million bond vote is Wednesday, August 5 at 5:30 PM. If approved, it would be the first major D11 bond measure in more than 20 years. At approximately $6.50 per month per $100,000 of home value, the bond would fund HVAC upgrades, school safety improvements, modernization projects, and one new construction. D11's first day of school is August 12 — one week after the vote. This brief covers what the bond covers, where to vote, and why the timing matters.
