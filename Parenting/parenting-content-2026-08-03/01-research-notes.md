## STORY 5: AAP Screen Time 5 C's

**Status:** CONFIRMED (HIGH confidence — official AAP guidance)

**Framework:** American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) replaced old blanket rules ("no screens under 2," "one hour/day under 5") with the 5 C's framework, which is designed for real family life.

**The 5 C's:**
1. **Child** — What are this child's individual needs, maturity, and sleep habits? Screen time affects a 4-year-old very differently from a 9-year-old.
2. **Content** — Is it educational, creative, social, or passive? Not all screen time is equal.
3. **Context** — Are they watching alone or together? School night or weekend? Reward or default?
4. **Communication** — Are you talking with kids about what they watch and why? The conversation matters more than the timer.
5. **Crowd** — Who influences what your child watches? Friends, algorithms, group chats all play a role.

**Key point:** The AAP no longer sets a magic number because research shows the relationship to media matters more than total minutes. The 5 C's help identify which variable is off.

**Resource:** healthychildren.org (AAP's parent-facing site) has full interactive 5 C's resource.

**Source confidence:** HIGH — official AAP policy guidance, widely reported.
