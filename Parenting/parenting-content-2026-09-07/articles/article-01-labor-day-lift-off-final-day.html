Today marks the final day of the Labor Day Lift Off, Colorado Springs' beloved hot air balloon festival that has grown over 50 years into one of the region's most anticipated family events. If you made it out Saturday or Sunday, you know what the energy is like. If you haven't been yet, Monday is your last chance.

The festival takes place at Prospect Lake in Memorial Park — a location that gives families the open sky views needed to fully appreciate about 60 hot air balloons in action. Admission is free, and there is no requirement to pre-purchase tickets for general access.

What's on Tap Today

The final day typically features two signature events:

The morning mass ascension, weather permitting, is the festival's most spectacular moment. Balloons inflate and launch from the park in sequence, filling the sky over Colorado Springs. Conditions have to cooperate — wind and weather make the call — but when it happens, it's the kind of thing kids remember for years. Get there early if you want a front-row view.

The evening Balloon Glow is the festival's closing act, and it's genuinely magical. Balloons stay tethered to the ground and light up from their burners after dark. The effect — dozens of glowing orbs against the night sky — is worth staying out past bedtime for, especially for younger kids who will be completely transfixed. Check the official festival page for tonight's schedule and any weather updates.

Labor Day Context for COS Families

For families in D11, D20, and D49, today is the last school-free day of the Labor Day weekend. School resumes tomorrow for all three districts. That makes today a natural final-day opportunity to squeeze in an outdoor experience before the fall school routine takes over for the week.

Parking fills quickly around Prospect Lake. The park is accessible by foot or bike if you're within range, and nearby street parking tends to be available earlier in the day. Bring sunscreen and layers — September mornings in Colorado Springs can be cool, but midday can warm up quickly at altitude.

What's Next

The Labor Day Lift Off returns in 2027. If you miss today's final events, the festival's official channels will typically post any recap highlights and early information for next year as the season winds down.

Looking ahead: Fiestas Patrias comes to Colorado Springs this Saturday, September 12 — another free family event in downtown COS. And for families with kids in school sports or activities, fall season schedules are filling in quickly across all three districts.
