Colorado Springs families have a free family outing to look forward to this Saturday: the annual Fiestas Patrias celebration comes to COS on September 12, bringing live music, cultural performances, food, and activities for all ages to the downtown area.

Fiestas Patrias — which translates to "homeland celebrations" — marks the national independence days of Mexico and several other Latin American countries, which fall in mid-September. It's one of the most significant cultural events in the Hispanic community, and in Colorado Springs, the Fiestas Patrias Colorado festival has grown into a broader community celebration that draws families from across the Pikes Peak region.

What to Expect

The festival takes place in and around the Colorado Springs Pioneers Museum and the surrounding downtown area. The lineup typically includes:

  • Live music from regional musicians, with dancing encouraged
  • Lucha libre — traditional Mexican wrestling performed by masked wrestlers. This is a crowd favorite for kids, who tend to be completely captivated by the athletic spectacle
  • Food vendors offering a wide range of traditional dishes
  • Cultural booths with heritage crafts, information, and activities
  • A festive atmosphere decorated with traditional colors and papel picado

Admission is free, making this a high-value outing for families. The event draws people from diverse backgrounds — it's a genuine community celebration, not a niche cultural event, and Colorado Springs' large Hispanic community makes it one of the most well-attended festivals of the fall season.

Why This Is Worth Your Saturday

September is Hispanic Heritage Month, which runs September 15 through October 15. Fiestas Patrias falls at the very beginning of that window and offers families a direct, experiential way to engage with the culture and history that is a meaningful part of Colorado Springs' identity.

For families with kids in D11, D20, or D49, many schools incorporate Hispanic Heritage Month into their fall curriculum. Attending Fiestas Patrias gives kids real-world context for what they're learning in class — and they'll have a much easier time connecting to abstract history lessons after experiencing the food, music, and celebration in person.

What's Next

Fiestas Patrias 2026 is Saturday, September 12 in downtown Colorado Springs. Check fiestaspatriascolorado.com for the current schedule of performances and any updates on timing. Arrive with some cash for food vendors, comfortable walking shoes, and the willingness to stay for the lucha libre — the kids won't let you leave before it's done.
