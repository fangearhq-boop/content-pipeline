D11 and D49 Fall Break Ends Today — Schools Back Monday, Oct. 26

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | October 25, 2026

Today is the last day of fall break for families in Colorado Springs School District 11 and Academy School District 49. Both districts return to school on Monday, October 26.

D11's fall break ran October 19 through 23. D49's break ran October 13 through 23. If you're in D20, your district was on a different calendar this fall and is already back in session. Charter school families should verify return dates with their specific school.

Sunday Night Prep — A Short Checklist

Whether fall break felt like a long week or a quick exhale, the Sunday-before-school routine is the same: a few small things done tonight prevent Monday morning chaos.

Backpacks

Books, folders, permission slips, library books — if any of these came home during break, get them back in the bag tonight. It takes three minutes and saves ten tomorrow.

Lunches

Check what's in the refrigerator and pantry before bed. If your child packs, plan out what goes in tomorrow. If they eat school lunch, confirm whether the account has money. Five minutes of checking now avoids a last-minute scramble at 7 AM.

Bedtime

A week of later nights means most kids will resist an earlier bedtime tonight. It's worth the mild argument. Getting to sleep 30 to 45 minutes earlier than they want to pays off in how they feel Monday morning and for the rest of the week.

Clothes and Shoes

Lay them out tonight. Not because mornings are hard, but because they don't have to be. This one small act removes an entire category of decision-making from 7:15 AM.

The Transition Back

Some kids walk back into school like nothing changed. Others need a day or two to re-settle. Both are normal. A week away from a routine — especially a week with later bedtimes, less structure, and more screen time — takes a few days to shake off.

If your child tends to resist transitions, it can help to mention tonight that school starts again tomorrow, and to keep the evening calm. Not a big deal, just a heads-up.

What's Next

The next major milestone on D11's calendar is the November 3 bond election. D11 voters will decide on a $775 million bond measure — the first D11 bond since 2004 — that would fund projects including HVAC replacement at Coronado High School and rebuilds at Holmes Middle School and Palmer High School. If you haven't looked into it yet, early voting is underway in El Paso County.
