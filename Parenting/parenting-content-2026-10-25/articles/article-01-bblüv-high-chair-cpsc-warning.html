CPSC Warning: Stop Using the bblüv Grow High Chair Immediately

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | October 25, 2026

If you own the bblüv Grow high chair, the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission has a direct message for you: stop using it, do not sell it, do not give it away, and dispose of it.

On October 15, 2026, the CPSC issued Warning #27-031 against the bblüv Grow high chair, citing serious fall and entrapment hazards. The problem is specific: the restraint system was not pre-assembled during manufacturing, meaning the harness meant to keep a child secured in the seat was never functional. The product violates the mandatory federal safety standard for high chairs.

Why This Is a Warning, Not a Recall

Most product safety actions are voluntary recalls — the manufacturer cooperates with the CPSC to notify customers and offer a remedy. This situation is different. bblüv Group declined to agree to an acceptable corrective action plan, which is why CPSC issued a unilateral warning rather than a standard recall announcement.

That distinction matters: it means there is no official remedy program in place, no exchange offer, and no repair kit coming. CPSC's guidance is simply to stop using the product and get it out of your home.

At the time the warning was issued, zero injuries or incidents had been confirmed. The CPSC acts on hazard potential, not just reported injuries — and a restraint system that was never assembled represents a clear failure for a product designed to hold infants and toddlers in place.

What to Do If You Own This Product

  • Stop using the bblüv Grow high chair immediately.
  • Do not attempt to fix it yourself. The issue is in the manufacturing process, not user assembly.
  • Do not give it away or sell it.
  • Dispose of the product. If possible, cut or dismantle it so it cannot be reused.
  • Check cpsc.gov for updates — bblüv Group may eventually offer a remedy, and the warning page will reflect any changes.
  • If you purchased the chair from a retailer, contact them about a refund. Keep your receipt or order confirmation if you have it.

Where This Product Was Sold

The bblüv Grow high chair was manufactured in China and sold by third-party sellers through various online platforms. If you are unsure whether the high chair in your home is the bblüv Grow model, check the underside of the seat or the product box for the brand name and model number.

A separate warning about a bblüv convertible high chair (model B0179) was also issued by Health Canada in 2025 for entrapment hazards. If you have any bblüv high chair product, verify the model against both the CPSC warning and Health Canada's notice.

What's Next

The CPSC warning page at cpsc.gov is the best place to check for any updates on this product, including if bblüv Group eventually agrees to a remedy. If you have questions or want to report an incident, the CPSC's SaferProducts.gov portal accepts consumer reports.

If you know families with young children who may own this product, share this information. Product warnings like this one don't always make national news, and many families won't see it unless someone passes it along.
