### STORY 5: Halloween Night Safety — Last-Week Checklist for COS Families
- **Tier:** 2 (Today)
- **Pillar:** National Parenting
- **Byline:** Sarah Morales

- **Post Time:** 5:00 PM MT
- **Summary:** With Halloween six days out, it's time for the final safety prep. Child pedestrian fatality risk is statistically 43% higher on Halloween than on other fall evenings (Safe Kids Worldwide). CSPD recommends children under 12 trick-or-treat with adult supervision. Key tips: reflective tape on costumes, flashlights or glow sticks, face paint over full masks that limit vision, crossing only at crosswalks and lit intersections, staying in familiar neighborhoods.
