Things to Do with Kids in Colorado Springs This Weekend (November 20–22)

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | November 19, 2026

This is the weekend the holiday season announces itself in Colorado Springs. Between a free kickoff event at the Broadmoor, a full-weekend holiday market, the Trans-Siberian Orchestra, and a live theater production that runs through December, there's a lot going on. Here's what families with kids should know about this weekend's options.

Broadmoor Holiday Kickoff — Sunday, Nov. 22, 4 PM (Free)

The Broadmoor (1 Lake Ave.) kicks off its holiday season Sunday afternoon. The public portions are free, all ages welcome. This is the kind of Colorado Springs tradition that works as a first holiday outing of the season — outdoor, low-key, and genuinely beautiful. The Broadmoor's property goes all-out on holiday lighting and the energy of opening night carries through. No tickets needed for the public outdoor portions; arrive by 4 PM for the full experience.

Vintage Market Days: "Rocking Around the Christmas Tree" — Fri–Sun, Nov. 20–22

The holiday-themed Vintage Market Days market runs all three days at the Norris Penrose Event Center (1045 Lower Gold Camp Rd.). It's stroller-friendly and runs the full weekend, so you can pick the day that works. Friday pricing runs higher — roughly $17 — while Saturday and Sunday are approximately $12 based on prior-year data. Confirm current pricing and hours at vintagemarketdays.com. Expect holiday gifts, vintage finds, and home goods across indoor and outdoor vendor booths.

Trans-Siberian Orchestra — Sunday, Nov. 22

The Trans-Siberian Orchestra's 30th anniversary tour of "The Ghosts of Christmas Eve" hits the Broadmoor World Arena (3185 Venetucci Blvd.) on Sunday with two shows: 2:30 PM and 7:30 PM. Tickets start around $79 on AXS. This is a full rock-orchestra production with serious light and sound production values. Best for kids 6 and up who can handle the volume and intensity, but younger kids who love lights and big spectacle often do fine too — parents know their kids. Tickets at broadmoorworldarena.com.

Irving Berlin's White Christmas — Opens Nov. 19, Runs Through Dec. 27

Colorado College's Fine Arts Center (JiGaSa Theatre) opened Irving Berlin's White Christmas on November 19 and runs the production through December 27. This is the opening weekend if you want to catch it early. Tickets are sold through the Fine Arts Center — check pricing and availability at fac.coloradocollege.edu. Best for ages 7 and up. The run is long, so there's no urgency to go this weekend, but the opening-weekend shows often have an energy that the later ones don't.

Skate in the Park — Ongoing Through Jan. 2027

Acacia Park's outdoor skating rink is open daily through January 2027. It's $15 per person including skate rental, and kids 4 and under skate free with a paying adult. Active-duty military and first responders get $2 off. If you've already been once this season, it's still worth factoring into a weekend plan — it's a reliable go-to when other plans fall through or the weather is cooperating.
