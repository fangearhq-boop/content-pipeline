COS Holiday Season Preview: Flying W Dinner Show, Royal Gorge Santa Express, Festival of Lights

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | November 19, 2026

The major Colorado Springs holiday events kick off in about two weeks. Three in particular are worth getting on the calendar now — two of them sell out well in advance. Here's what to know about the Flying W Ranch Cowboy Christmas Jubilee, the Royal Gorge Route Railroad Santa Express, and the Festival of Lights Parade.

The Flying W Wranglers have been putting on the Cowboy Christmas Jubilee for decades, and it remains a Colorado Springs holiday tradition that many families build into their annual calendar. The format is a dinner show: a western-style meal (smoked Angus steak with sides) followed by a Christmas performance from the Wranglers, who blend classic cowboy music with holiday songs. The venue is a working ranch in the northwest part of the city.

Multiple seatings run each evening, so there's some flexibility, but popular dates — especially weekends and the week before Christmas — book up early. The 2026 prices listed above are approximate from prior seasons; confirm current pricing at flyingw.com/christmas/ before booking. If you have out-of-town family visiting over the holidays, this is typically the kind of COS experience they don't have at home.

NoteThis is Royal Gorge Railroad in Cañon City, not Pikes Peak

The Santa Express is run by the Royal Gorge Route Railroad and departs from Cañon City, about 45 minutes southwest of Colorado Springs. This is a point worth clarifying upfront: it's not a Pikes Peak train. The Royal Gorge trip goes through the canyon along the Arkansas River and runs a holiday-themed excursion with Santa on board.

Ticket prices are higher than a typical holiday activity, and dates sell out quickly — often well before December. If you want to do this one, booking at royalgorgeroute.com/santa-express now rather than waiting is worth the planning effort. This is an experience that works well for children roughly ages 2 and up who can sit for a train ride, and it's genuinely memorable for kids who are excited about trains or Santa visits.

The Festival of Lights Parade on Tejon Street is now in its 42nd year. The parade starts at 5:50 PM and winds through downtown Colorado Springs. It's free, it works for all ages, and the downtown Tejon Street setting makes it easy to combine with dinner at one of the nearby restaurants. For families who like building a full outing around it: the Colorado Springs Pioneers Museum runs a Family Fun Day on the same block from 10 AM to 2 PM, which gives you a full afternoon-to-evening run if you want it.

Festival of Lights details are at coloradospringsfestivaloflights.com.

What's Next

These three events bracket the first two weeks of December. If you're planning to do all three, the booking priority is Santa Express first (highest demand, furthest out), then Flying W (popular weekends book out in November), then Festival of Lights (free, no advance booking needed). The Cheyenne Mountain Zoo's Electric Safari also opens December 2 — that one was covered separately this week and also requires advance tickets at cmzoo.org. December in COS has a full calendar this year.
