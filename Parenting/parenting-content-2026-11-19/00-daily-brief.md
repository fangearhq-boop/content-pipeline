Two toy recalls worth flagging as the holiday shopping season gets underway. The HABA Rainbow Rattle Grasping and Teething Toy (recall #26-719, August 27, 2026) is recalled for a choking hazard — the elastic cord knot can come untied, releasing small parts. About 21,758 units were sold on Amazon between July 2023 and December 2025 for about $10. Remedy is a free replacement. Both are worth checking against cpsc.gov/Recalls as families sort holiday wish lists and clean out toy bins.

### STORY 2: AAP 2026 — Mental and Emotional Health Belongs in Every Pediatric Visit
**Tier:** 2
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Type:** NEW STORY
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Posting Window:** 10:00 AM MT

**Summary:**
The American Academy of Pediatrics published a new framework in the May 2026 issue of Pediatrics expanding the standard for mental and emotional health care in pediatric visits. Where previous guidance focused on screening schedules (anxiety screening annually 8–18; depression and suicide risk starting 12; substance use annually after 11), the 2026 framework shifts the expectation further: mental, emotional, and relational health should be incorporated into every pediatric visit as a matter of routine promotion — not only when there is a concern. The approach includes anticipatory guidance, surveillance, screening, and brief interventions, delivered in trauma-informed and equitable ways. Parents can ask their pediatrician directly at the next well-child appointment.

### STORY 4: D49 Purple Star Schools — All 7 Falcon Zone Schools Recognized
**Tier:** 2
**Pillar:** Local News
**Type:** NEW STORY
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Posting Window:** 3:00 PM MT

**Summary:**
All seven schools in District 49's Falcon Zone now carry the Colorado Purple Star designation, which recognizes schools that provide strong support systems for military-connected families. The designations came through the April 7, 2026 ceremony — part of Colorado's second annual Purple Star round under HB24-1076. Twenty-two additional Colorado schools were recognized that day; multiple D49 campuses were among them, completing all seven in the Falcon Zone. The Purple Star program requires a designated staff contact for military families, adjustment support programs, and staff training. The designation is valid for three years. D49's Board of Education unanimously passed a Purple Star resolution on December 4, 2025.
