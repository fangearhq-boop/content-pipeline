**Confidence:** HIGH

**Sources:**

- AAP News Release (2025 screening report background): "American Academy of Pediatrics Offers Guidance on Screening Children for Mental Health, Emotional, and Behavioral Problems" — https://www.aap.org/en/news-room/news-releases/aap/2025/american-academy-of-pediatrics-offers-guidance-on-screening-children-for-mental-emotional-behavioral-health-disorders/
- HealthyChildren.org: "Integrating Mental, Emotional and Relational Health into Every Well-Child Visit" — https://www.healthychildren.org/English/news/Pages/integrating-mental-emotional-and-relational-health-into-every-well-child-visit-new-AAP-report.aspx

**Key facts verified:**
- May 2026 Pediatrics publication: "Framework for Approaching Healthy Mental and Emotional Development in Pediatrics"
- Key shift: mental and emotional development should be incorporated into every pediatric visit (not only at concern points)
- Approach: anticipatory guidance, surveillance, screening, brief interventions — trauma-informed, equitable
- Screening schedule (from 2025 clinical report): caregiver postpartum depression within first month of life; child screening at 6, 12, 24, 36 months, then annually after 3; anxiety annually ages 8–18; depression + suicide risk starting 12; substance use annually after 11
