Fall Parent-Teacher Conferences Are Coming: How COS Families Can Prepare Now

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | September 11, 2026

Where COS Districts Stand

  • Falcon School District 49: Approximately week 7 (started August 3)
  • Colorado Springs School District 11: Approximately week 5 (started August 12)
  • Academy School District 20: Approximately week 4 (started August 17)
  • Fall conferences: Typically scheduled in October across D11, D20, and D49
  • Prep tip: Email your child's teacher for an informal check-in before the formal conference

For Colorado Springs families, fall parent-teacher conferences in D11, D20, and D49 are typically 4 to 8 weeks out at this point in the school year. That seems like a comfortable distance — until you realize that October will arrive before most families have had time to think about what they want to say in 20 minutes.

The families who get the most from fall conferences are the ones who started preparing in September. Here is what that preparation looks like.

Start a Notes Document This Week

Teachers see between 20 and 35 students each day. They notice patterns in the classroom; you notice patterns at home. Those home observations are valuable data that your child's teacher may not have, and a brief, organized notes document makes it easy to share them during the conference.

Consider jotting down: any shifts in your child's attitude toward school since the year started, specific subjects or assignments that seem to be generating frustration or excitement, changes in sleep or appetite that might be school-related, and any social dynamics your child has mentioned.

You do not need a formal document — a few notes in your phone will do. The goal is to arrive at the conference with specifics, not just a general sense that "things are going okay" or "things have felt hard."

Email the Teacher Now, Before the Conference

A brief, proactive email to your child's teacher in September — not to complain, just to check in — can surface issues weeks before a formal conference. Most teachers appreciate the outreach. A message as simple as "We're a few weeks in and I wanted to ask how things are going from your side" is enough.

If your child has an IEP, a 504 plan, or any accommodation in place, this is also a good moment to confirm that those supports are actively in place and being tracked. Discovering at the October conference that an accommodation was not being implemented is a harder conversation than catching it in September when there is still time to course-correct.

Prepare Your Questions in Advance

Conference time is short — typically 15 to 20 minutes. The families who leave with the most useful information are the ones who arrive with specific questions. Some reliable ones:

  • Is my child meeting grade-level academic benchmarks in reading and math?
  • What does success look like in this class this year?
  • Are there any patterns in social or behavioral areas I should know about?
  • Is there anything specific I can reinforce or support at home?
  • If my child needs additional support, what resources does the school have?

If you have a specific concern — a topic your child struggles with, a friendship situation, a recent change at home — state it plainly and early in the conference. Teachers are skilled at addressing concrete concerns; they are less able to help with "things just feel off."

For Families With Multiple Children

If you have children at multiple grade levels or multiple schools in the same district, conference season can get logistically complex quickly. D11, D20, and D49 all typically hold fall conferences in October, though the specific dates vary by school. Now is a reasonable time to reach out to each school to confirm scheduled conference dates and sign up for time slots before they fill.

What's Next

Fall conferences in COS typically lead directly into the Thanksgiving holiday block — which means if concerns surface in October, there is limited time before the semester break to address them. Using September to build a communication channel with teachers gives families a head start. Check your child's school website or call the front office to confirm 2026-27 conference dates for your specific building.
