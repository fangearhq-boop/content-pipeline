### STORY 1: CPSC Recall — Ricky Joy Sour Crush Candy Bottles (2.3 Million Units) (TIER 1)
**Status:** NEW STORY — CPSC recall issued September 10, 2026
**Pillar:** National Parenting (Safety Recall)
**Angle:** URGENT — The CPSC recalled about 2.3 million Ricky Joy Sour Crush Rolling Liquid Candy Bottles on September 10, 2026. The rolling ball on each bottle can detach, posing a serious choking hazard for children. Bottles were sold at H-E-B, Forman Mill, and online at Walmart.com, CandyFunHouse.com, and RickyJoy.com from January 2023 through May 2026 for about $3 (single) or $13-$32 (packs). Flavors: blue raspberry, strawberry, green apple, watermelon. Parents should stop use immediately and contact Ricky Joy for a full refund at 404-975-0352 (M-F 8 AM–5 PM ET) or rickyjoy.com (click "Recall Information"). No injuries reported. A prior rollerball candy recall (Slime Licker, 70M units, 2024) resulted in one death — this product type is high-risk.
**Byline:** Sarah Morales

### STORY 2: RSV Season 2026-27 Is Starting — What Colorado Springs Families Need to Know (TIER 1)
**Status:** NEW STORY — National parenting/health, seasonally urgent
**Pillar:** National Parenting (Health)
**Angle:** September marks the beginning of Colorado's RSV season. The AAP updated its 2026-27 RSV prevention guidelines with expanded high-risk criteria for children aged 8–19 months. RSV immunization (nirsevimab or clesrovimab monoclonal antibodies) is recommended for all infants under 8 months entering their first RSV season. The September window is also when pregnant parents should receive RSV vaccination to pass protection to newborns. COS families with infants or high-risk toddlers should talk to their pediatrician now before RSV activity picks up in October-November. Key high-risk expanded categories: preterm (under 32 weeks), congenital heart disease, pulmonary/neuromuscular disorders, Down syndrome.
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera

### STORY 5: Finger Light Toys Recalled Over Battery Danger to Kids (TIER 2)
**Status:** NEW STORY — CPSC recall issued September 10, 2026
**Pillar:** National Parenting (Safety Recall)
**Angle:** Also on September 10, the CPSC recalled Cade California Electronic Finger Light Toys sold on Amazon due to button cell battery ingestion risk. The toys contain easily accessible button cell/coin batteries that can be swallowed by children, causing serious internal chemical burns or death if lodged in the throat. Parents who purchased these toys should stop letting children use them immediately and remove the batteries. Button cell battery ingestion is one of the leading causes of serious childhood injury from toys. Link to CPSC page and safe storage guidance.
