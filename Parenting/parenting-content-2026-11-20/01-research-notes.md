### STORY 1: CHE-US Magnetic Robot Toy CPSC Warning
**Confidence:** HIGH
**Source URLs:**
- https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/CPSC-Warns-Consumers-to-Immediately-Stop-Using-CHE-US-Magnetic-Stick-Figure-Robot-Toys (official CPSC warning page)
- https://www.cpsc.gov/s3fs-public/2026-10-29-che-us-magnetic-toy-warning.pdf

**Key Facts:**
- Product: CHE-US magnetic stick figure robot toys (also branded CHESLICR)
- Units: ~101,282 sold on Amazon
- Seller: Guangzhou Shian Trading Co., Ltd.
- Model code: X004N3MGCB (found on packaging)
- Sold in sets of 5 or 10, price: ~$9
- Hazard: Arms and legs can be pulled apart, releasing high-powered magnets that exceed the federal toy safety standard (ASTM F963) and that fit within the small parts cylinder (choking hazard for under age 3)
- Injury risk: If two or more magnets are swallowed, they can attract each other through intestinal walls, causing perforations, blockages, or sepsis — potentially fatal without surgery
- Seller refused voluntary recall; no refund available
- CPSC action: Formal warning (not a recall); consumers told to dispose of product immediately
- Sale period: Available on Amazon; still potentially listed

**Research Notes:**
- High-powered magnet toy ingestion injuries are well-documented in medical literature; CPSC has previously recalled similar products (e.g., Zen Magnets 2021)
- The refusal-to-recall situation is notable — CPSC must issue a warning rather than the typical recall mechanism, which limits consumer remedies
- No injury reports cited in the warning, but precautionary

- This is planning-stage — no immediate impact on families, but important context for D11 decisions in the spring

### STORY 3: AAP + Understood.org ADHD Screen Time Guidance for Ages 5–10
**Confidence:** HIGH
**Source URLs:**
- https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/understood-org-aap-adhd-screen-time-guidance-november-2026
- https://www.understood.org/en/articles/adhd-screen-time-guidance-aap
- https://www.aap.org/en/news-room/news-releases/2026/aap-understood-adhd-screen-time/

**Key Facts:**
- Release date: November 16, 2026
- Published by: Understood.org, in partnership with the American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP)
- Target audience: Parents of children ages 5–10 with ADHD
- First guidance: First expert-backed, ADHD-specific screen time document for this age group
- Key message: Context and content matter more than counting minutes
- Addresses myth: Screens do NOT cause ADHD
- Addresses parental guilt: Many families with ADHD kids feel excessive guilt about screen use — this paper explicitly aims to relieve it
- Gap filled: Existing AAP general screen time guidance covers all kids; this fills the gap for ADHD-specific families
- No specific screen time minute limits given (intentional — ADHD affects executive function differently than neurotypical kids)

**Research Notes:**
- Understood.org is a well-regarded nonprofit focused on learning and thinking differences; AAP partnership gives it clinical credibility
- ADHD affects ~9.4% of US children ages 2–17 (CDC estimate); in Colorado Springs metro area, that translates to a substantial number of families
- Paper is freely available on Understood.org

### STORY 5: D11 $775M Bond Measure 4A Passes
**Confidence:** HIGH
**Source URLs:**
- https://gazette.com/education/d11-bond-4a-results-november-2026/
- https://www.krdo.com/news/local-news/d11-bond-measure-4a-passes-colorado-springs/

**Key Facts:**
- Ballot item: Issue 4A
- Amount: $775 million
- District: Colorado Springs School District 11
- Result: Approved by voters
- Yes votes in early results: Nearly 50,000
- Status: Confirmed
- Use of funds: Capital improvements across all D11 campuses — aging buildings, deferred maintenance, facility upgrades
- Historical context: First D11 bond to pass in 20 years; largest school bond in El Paso County history
- Timeline: No specific campus timelines announced yet

**Research Notes:**
- The bond's passage comes alongside the separate capacity/enrollment story (Story 2), which could appear contradictory — voters approving $775M while 11 schools are underused; worth acknowledging this in the article
- Bond funding is capital only — cannot be used for salaries or operations
- Families in D11 will see building improvements but timeline decisions come later; most families won't see immediate visible changes
