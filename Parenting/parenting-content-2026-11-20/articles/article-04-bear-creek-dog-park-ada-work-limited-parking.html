Bear Creek Regional Dog Park ADA Work Underway — Plan Ahead for Limited Parking Through Dec 18

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | November 20, 2026

El Paso County started ADA accessibility work at Bear Creek Regional Dog Park on November 19. The project runs through December 18, 2026. On-site parking is limited for the duration of the work, and families who make regular trips to the park with kids and dogs will need to plan around it.

The improvements are upgrades to the parking areas and the entrance path to bring them into compliance with ADA accessibility standards. When the work is done, the park will be more accessible for families who bring strollers or have family members with mobility needs. For now, the construction means you'll need to park in one of the overflow lots the county has set up.

Overflow Parking Options

El Paso County designated three overflow parking areas for the duration of construction.

Overflow Location Notes
Parks Headquarters Adjacent to the dog park; closest overflow option
Bear Creek Regional Park main lot Main lot for the broader park; short walk to dog park
North side of the dog park Secondary parking on the dog park property

All three locations are on or immediately adjacent to the Bear Creek Regional Park property. The county hasn't specified which is closest or most convenient for specific entrances, so it's worth arriving with extra time on your first visit during the construction period to get oriented.

What's Staying Open

The dog park itself is staying open. The ADA construction is focused on the parking area and entrance path — not on the off-leash areas, fencing, or other amenities inside the park. If you can get there, your dogs and kids can still use the park the same way they usually do.

The timing isn't ideal. November and December are popular months for dog park visits as families try to get outdoor time before the holidays fill up. And the construction runs right through the period when weekend trips to the dog park tend to cluster. Building in an extra few minutes for parking is the main adjustment.

Why This Work Is Happening

ADA accessibility improvements at parks and recreation facilities are an ongoing part of how El Paso County maintains its parks system. The Bear Creek Regional Dog Park is one of the most heavily used off-leash parks in the Colorado Springs area, and the accessibility upgrades specifically address the parking and entry experience — which are often the most significant barriers for people with mobility needs.

When the work is complete in December, the park will have accessible parking spaces and an accessible path from parking to the dog park entrance. That's a meaningful improvement for families who visit with mobility aids, strollers, or other access needs.

What's Next

Construction wraps on December 18, 2026. If you're a regular at Bear Creek Regional Dog Park, the simplest approach is to use the Parks Headquarters lot as your default for the next few weeks and check the El Paso County Parks website at elpasoco.com for any updates if you have questions about access. The park is open during construction — just budget a bit more time to get parked.
