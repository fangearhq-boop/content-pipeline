### STORY 4: CPSC Summer 2026 Children's Product Recalls

**HIGH confidence facts:**

**Babypark 3-in-1 Soft Infant/Toddler Carriers:**
- Hazard: leg openings too large — suffocation/fall risk for infants
- Sold: Amazon.com, approximately April 2024–May 2026
- Action: stop use immediately; contact seller for refund
- Source: CPSC.gov

**POPOOO/Delightmi Jungle Safari LED Finger Lights:**
- Hazard: accessible button cell battery compartment — ingestion risk for young children
- Sold: approximately July 2025–April 2026
- Action: keep away from children; contact seller
- Source: CPSC.gov

**TOMY Boon PIVOT Toddler Tower/Kitchen Step Stools:**
- Hazard: product shifts and leans when child is on it — fall risk; 11 reports of incidents
- Sold: Target.com, Amazon, Babylist; January 2023–June 2026
- Action: stop use; contact TOMY/Boon for remedy
- Source: CPSC.gov, Medical Daily

**Target Gigglescape Under the Sea Popping Toys:**
- Hazard: dome detaches, exposing small balls — choking hazard; 9 reports including 1 choking incident
- Sold: Target stores and Target.com
- Action: stop use; return to Target for refund
- Source: CPSC.gov

**Peony Design Personalized Baby Bibs/Stroller Bags:**
- Recall date: July 23, 2026
- Hazard: snap can detach from fabric — choking hazard for infants
- Action: stop use; contact Peony Design for refund
- Source: CPSC.gov

**Sources:** CPSC.gov (multiple recall pages), Medical Daily, The BayNet, getkeptapp.com
