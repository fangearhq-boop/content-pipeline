### STORY 5: Colorado's Gov. Polis Among Six Governors Urging AAP to Add Early Math to Well-Child Visits
- **Tier:** 2
- **Pillar:** National Parenting
- **Angle:** FOLLOW-UP / EXPANDED (July 28 Story 4 covered this story; new angle for July 30: what COS parents can do now) — On July 23, 2026, six governors — including Colorado's Jared Polis — sent a letter to the American Academy of Pediatrics urging formal early numeracy guidance modeled on the AAP's existing literacy program. The practical angle for COS families: ask your child's pediatrician about early math milestones at your next well-child visit. Counting, sorting, and simple number games build the numeracy skills that predict long-term academic success. This is especially timely with D49's first day of school four days away.
