D11 Considers $775 Million Bond Measure — Board Votes August 5 - **Tier:** 1 - **Angle:** NEW STORY — Colorado Springs School District 11 is weighing a $775 million bond measure, its first bond in more than 20 years (the last passed in 2004). The D11 board votes August 5 on whether to place the bond on the November ballot. The measure would fund air conditioning for schools 50 to 100 years old and includes teacher pay increases. Estimated tax impact: approximately $6.50 per month per $100,000 of property value. D11 serves the central Colorado Springs area. Sources: Colorado Springs Gazette (July 29, 2026), KRDO, KOAA.
