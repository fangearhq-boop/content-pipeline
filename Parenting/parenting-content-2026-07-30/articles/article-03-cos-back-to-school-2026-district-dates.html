COS Back-to-School 2026: D49 Starts Monday, D20 on August 13, D11 Date Still Pending

By Jamie Rivera | July 30, 2026

Colorado Springs, CO

Four days. That's how long D49 families have before the 2026–27 school year begins. The three major Colorado Springs-area school districts are all at different points in the back-to-school timeline, and if you haven't confirmed your child's first day, this is the moment to do it.

Quick Reference: COS Back-to-School Dates 2026–27

District First Day Notes
Falcon District 49 (D49) August 3 (K, 6th, 9th grade) All other students: August 4. Preschool: August 10.
Academy District 20 (D20) August 13 Verify at asd20.org
Colorado Springs D11 TBD Board meeting August 5; check d11.org for updates

D49 — Starts Monday, August 3

Falcon School District 49 uses a staggered opening. Kindergarteners, incoming 6th graders, and incoming 9th graders report on Monday, August 3 — giving new-to-building students a day to orient before the full school population arrives. All other D49 students start Tuesday, August 4.

D49 preschool families have a separate timeline: preschool begins August 10, also staggered. If your child is entering D49 preschool, confirm your specific schedule with your school, as preschool transitions often involve additional orientation days or half-day starts.

D49 resources including transportation routes, open houses, and supply lists are available at d49.org. With the first day four days away, this is the time to confirm school assignments, bus stops, and any outstanding registration items.

One practical note for D49 families: the Backpack Bash Central event is this Saturday, August 1 — 9 a.m. to noon at Mitchell High School — offering free backpacks for PreK through 12th grade students enrolled in any local public or charter school. It's two days before D49's first day of school and the last remaining Backpack Bash location of the season.

D20 — Starts August 13

Academy District 20 families have about two weeks before the 2026–27 school year begins. The district's first day is August 13. If you're a D20 family who recently enrolled — through School Choice or otherwise — confirm your transportation arrangements directly with the school, as district transportation deadlines for new students have already passed for the fall. Check asd20.org for school-specific open house schedules and any pre-start resources.

D11 — Date Still Pending

Colorado Springs School District 11 has not yet announced its first day for the 2026–27 school year. The district's board has several significant items in progress — including the vote on August 5 about the $775 million bond measure — and the fall calendar is expected to be confirmed following those board-level decisions. D11 families have more time before school starts than D49 or D20 families, but should check d11.org regularly for the first-day announcement as August approaches.

What's Next

D49 families should treat this weekend as their final prep window — confirm your child's school, bus route, and schedule, and consider the Backpack Bash on Saturday morning for free back-to-school gear. D20 families have until August 13, giving a little more runway for open houses and supply shopping. D11 families should check d11.org for the start-date announcement in the coming days. All three districts' official websites are the most reliable sources for any last-minute schedule changes.
