Five Children's Product Recalls COS Parents Should Check Before School Starts

By Sarah Morales | July 30, 2026

Colorado Springs, CO

Back-to-school season is a natural moment to do a quick check of the gear in your home, and this summer the Consumer Product Safety Commission has issued or confirmed recalls on five children's products that are worth knowing about. The range spans infant carriers, step stools, finger lights, popping toys, and baby bibs — product types common enough that many Colorado Springs families may have at least one of them somewhere.

Here's what to look for and what to do if you have any of these items.

Babypark 3-in-1 Soft Infant and Toddler Carriers

These baby carriers have leg openings that are too large for infant bodies, creating a suffocation and fall hazard. The carriers were sold on Amazon.com from approximately April 2024 through May 2026. If you have a Babypark 3-in-1 carrier, stop using it immediately and contact the seller for a refund. The full recall details are at cpsc.gov.

POPOOO / Delightmi Jungle Safari LED Finger Lights

These LED finger lights — sold under the POPOOO and Delightmi brand names — have button cell battery compartments that young children can access. Button cell batteries are a serious ingestion hazard and can cause severe internal injuries if swallowed. The lights were sold from approximately July 2025 through April 2026. Keep them away from children and contact the seller for remedy information. Details are at cpsc.gov.

TOMY Boon PIVOT Toddler Tower and Kitchen Step Stools

This recall has 11 reported incidents. The TOMY Boon PIVOT toddler tower — a kitchen step stool designed to let children stand at counter height — shifts and leans when a child is standing on it, creating a fall hazard. The product was sold at Target.com, Amazon, and Babylist from January 2023 through June 2026. If you have one, stop using it and contact TOMY or Boon for remedy information. CPSC.gov has the full recall listing.

Because these were sold at major retailers over a multi-year period, they have broader distribution than many recalls. If you bought a PIVOT toddler tower for your kitchen in the last few years, it's worth confirming whether it's the affected model.

Target Gigglescape Under the Sea Popping Toys

This recall comes with a confirmed choking incident. The dome on the Target Gigglescape Under the Sea popping toy can detach from the base, exposing small balls inside that are a choking hazard for young children. There have been nine reported incidents, including one confirmed choking. The toys were sold at Target stores and on Target.com. Return the product to any Target store for a full refund.

Peony Design Personalized Baby Bibs and Stroller Bags

Peony Design issued a recall on July 23, 2026 for personalized baby bibs and stroller bags. The snaps on these products can detach from the fabric, creating a choking hazard for infants. If you purchased personalized bibs or stroller bags from Peony Design, stop using them and contact Peony Design directly for a refund. The recall notice is at cpsc.gov.

How to Check Your Products

The CPSC maintains a searchable database of all current and recent recalls at cpsc.gov. You can search by brand name, product type, or keyword. If you buy baby gear secondhand — at consignment sales, on Facebook Marketplace, or from resale apps — a 30-second cpsc.gov search before using any item with a young child is one of the quickest safety checks available.

For any of the products listed above, the action is the same: stop using it, then check cpsc.gov for the specific recall number and remedy process. Refunds are the most common remedy, but the steps vary by product and brand.

What's Next

If you have any of these five products, stop use now and follow the remedy steps at cpsc.gov. Sign up for CPSC recall alerts at cpsc.gov/recalls to receive notifications about future recalls by email. The CPSC issues dozens of recall notices each year across all product categories — a subscription ensures you hear about new ones as they're issued rather than weeks or months later.
