Old Colorado City Christkindlmarket Returns Dec. 4 — Bigger and Better for 2026

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | November 27, 2026

Colorado Springs families looking for a free, festive holiday outing in December have a new tradition to build on. The Old Colorado City Christkindlmarket returns for its second year on December 4, and the organizers have expanded the event with more vendors, more dates, and what promises to be a bigger footprint at Bancroft Park.

The market runs three separate weekends through December 23 — plenty of opportunities to fit it into a busy holiday schedule. It's free to attend, it's in one of COS's most charming neighborhoods, and it's exactly the kind of local event worth putting on the family calendar before the month fills up.

The park is in the heart of the Old Colorado City neighborhood, which means a Christkindlmarket visit pairs naturally with a walk along Colorado Avenue's independent shops and restaurants before or after. If you're making a full day of it, the neighborhood's got the infrastructure to support one.

What to Expect

Christkindlmarkets are European-style holiday markets that focus on locally made goods, seasonal food, and the kind of atmosphere you can't replicate at a mall. The 2025 inaugural Colorado Springs edition drew thousands of visitors and established this as a legitimate addition to COS's holiday calendar. The 2026 expansion — more vendors, three full weekends instead of two — reflects how well it was received.

For families with young children, the market's outdoor, walkable format is generally easier to manage than indoor shopping centers: more room to move, fresh air, and the ability to leave when you need to. The 8 p.m. closing time means evening visits are an option for families whose kids do better after nap time.

Timing Tips

If you want to avoid the heaviest crowds, the mid-December window (Dec. 9–13) tends to be less busy than opening weekend or the final-week rush. Opening weekend (Dec. 4–6) will draw the most first-timers and media attention, and the final weekend (Dec. 16–23) will be the most festive but also the busiest as Christmas approaches.

Afternoons on weekdays (Dec. 9–13 runs Thursday through Monday) are typically less crowded than weekend evenings. If you have flexibility in your schedule, that's your best bet for a relaxed visit.

What's Next

The Old Colorado City Christkindlmarket is free to attend — no tickets or registration required. Check visitcos.com and the Old Colorado City Christkindlmarket's own channels closer to opening day for any vendor announcements or special events during each weekend. It's shaping up to be one of the holiday season's best free outings for COS families.
