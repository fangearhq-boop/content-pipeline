### STORY 2: Taleco Gear Baby Jumpers and Swings Recalled — ~41,100 Units, Fall and Strangulation Hazards
**Tier:** 1
**Pillar:** National Parenting (Safety / CPSC)
**Angle:** NEW STORY — CPSC recall issued August 13, 2026: Taleco Gear baby jumpers and swings (~41,100 units in the US, 150 in Canada). Model number is on fabric wash/care label inside seat cover.

### STORY 5: AAP Updates Screen Time Guidelines — What Colorado Springs Parents Need to Know
**Tier:** 2
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Angle:** NEW STORY — The American Academy of Pediatrics has updated its screen time guidelines for children. Key recommendations: no screens before 18 months (except video calls); one hour of high-quality programming daily for ages 2-5; for older kids, balance screens with sleep, physical activity, family time, and free play. Device-free bedrooms and mealtimes. Co-viewing and modeling healthy digital habits are now central to AAP guidance.
