December in Colorado Springs: Electric Safari, Festival of Lights, Christkindlmarket, and More

By Sarah Morales | November 25, 2026 | COS Parenting

December in Colorado Springs has a lot going on for families, and planning a little in advance makes the difference between catching the best events and showing up to find them fully booked. Here's what's coming up in the next few weeks — with the details you need to actually make them happen.

Cheyenne Mountain Zoo Electric Safari — Opens December 2

The Cheyenne Mountain Zoo's holiday lights event opens December 2 and runs through January 1, 2027. If you've been before, you already know it's worth going — thousands of lights throughout the zoo grounds, animal encounters in the evening, and the unique experience of visiting the zoo after dark in December.

One thing to plan for: the zoo is shifting to early closings for setup. Starting November 26 — that's tomorrow — the zoo closes at 3 PM and continues to close at 3 PM through the end of the Electric Safari season in January. So if you're planning a daytime zoo trip between now and January, give yourself enough time to get in and get out.

Electric Safari reservations are recommended — visit cmzoo.org to check availability and book. Members can typically attend for free with a reservation, so it's worth checking your membership status before you go.

Festival of Lights Parade — December 5

The Festival of Lights Parade returns for its 36th year on December 5 at 5:50 PM on Tejon Street in downtown Colorado Springs. It's free, it's outdoors, and it involves a whole lot of lit-up floats on a cold December night — which is exactly the kind of thing that lands as a holiday memory for kids.

Tejon Street runs through downtown COS, and the parade is an easy evening outing that doesn't require tickets or advance planning. Bring layers — December evenings in Colorado Springs are cold — and get there a few minutes before 5:50 PM for a good spot along the route. The parade usually moves from north to south along Tejon, so the blocks near the Antlers or Acacia Park tend to have good viewing.

Christkindlmarket — December 4 Through 23

Christkindlmarket at Bancroft Park (2408 W Colorado Ave) runs December 4–6, 9–13, and 16–23 this year. Hours are noon to 8 PM daily. Admission is free.

It's not a continuous run — there are gaps between the three blocks of dates, so check the schedule before you plan your trip. The market has a European outdoor market feel — vendors selling holiday crafts, ornaments, and food, with hot drinks and a good atmosphere for browsing. The free admission makes it easy to visit more than once if you're trying to cover different weekends.

Bancroft Park is in Old Colorado City, which has its own walkable neighborhood energy. It's a good afternoon-to-early-evening outing, especially on the first weekend of December when the Christmas Stroll is also happening nearby.

Manitou Springs Elf Hunt — Returning This Season

The Manitou Springs Elf Hunt is an annual free scavenger hunt that runs through the shops and streets of Manitou Springs during the holiday season. Participants pick up a map, hunt for hidden elves in participating businesses, and are entered into weekly prize drawings. It's the kind of low-pressure, free activity that young kids genuinely enjoy, and it gives you an excuse to walk through Manitou in December, which is a pleasant thing to do anyway.

The 2026 dates haven't been officially announced as of publication. Check manitousprings.org for the full schedule and participation details before you head up the canyon.

What's Next

Mark your December calendar now. Electric Safari reservations at cmzoo.org fill up quickly. Festival of Lights Parade on December 5 — no tickets needed, just show up on Tejon Street. Christkindlmarket at Bancroft Park runs December 4–6, 9–13, and 16–23 (noon–8 PM, free). For Manitou Springs Elf Hunt dates, check manitousprings.org.
