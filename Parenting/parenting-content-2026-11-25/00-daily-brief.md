**Freshness Tag:** TODAY
**Tier:** 1
**Assigned Platforms:** X (2 posts) + Facebook Long-Form + Facebook Image Caption + Image Concepts + Article
**Priority Score:** 10
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Story Type:** NEW STORY — National Parenting pillar

**Summary:** A JAMA Pediatrics study published November 13, 2026 (255 children, K-6, doi: 10.1001/jamapediatrics.2026.5973) found that four common classroom accommodations — extended time, frequent breaks, standing desks, and low-distraction workspace — produced no measurable improvement in outcomes for children with ADHD. Some even reduced academic productivity. Behavioral interventions cut rule violations 37%; stimulant medication cut violations 61% and increased correct completed assignments 22%. Caveat: the study used a summer treatment program setting, not a regular classroom.

Stop use immediately, cut fabric in half, dispose.
