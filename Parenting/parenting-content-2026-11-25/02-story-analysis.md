### STORY 1: ADHD Classroom Accommodations Study

**Freshness Tag:** TODAY
**Tier:** 1
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Pillar:** National Parenting

**Summary:** A JAMA Pediatrics study published November 13, 2026 found that four common classroom accommodations for ADHD — extended time, frequent breaks, standing desk, low-distraction workspace — produced no measurable improvement in outcomes. Behavioral interventions and medication outperformed all four. The study involved 255 children in a summer treatment setting.

**Relevance:** This directly challenges something many COS parents have been told at IEP/504 meetings. It's genuinely actionable, potentially surprising, and will generate conversation among parents and educators.

**Angles:**
1. The finding itself — none of the four accommodations worked as expected
2. What DOES work — behavioral interventions + medication data
3. The caveat — summer program setting, not a regular school year
4. What this means practically for parents navigating IEPs and 504s
5. The larger question: are schools over-relying on accommodations instead of interventions?

### STORY 2: Cipton Bike Helmet Recall + Bambora Sling Warning

**Freshness Tag:** TODAY
**Tier:** 1
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Pillar:** National Parenting

**Summary:** Two safety alerts this week. CPSC recalled ~16,730 Cipton youth bicycle helmets (model CPSK300CC) on November 19 — sold at Dick's and JCPenney, fail safety certification, full refund available. CPSC issued a warning (not formal recall) on November 12 for Bambora sling carriers — restraint strap can separate, fall hazard for infants, no refund because company did not cooperate.
