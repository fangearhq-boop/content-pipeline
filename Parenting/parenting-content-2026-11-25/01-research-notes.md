### STORY 1: ADHD Classroom Accommodations Study
**Story Type:** NEW STORY — National Parenting pillar
**Confidence:** HIGH (JAMA Pediatrics peer-reviewed, multi-source)

**Key Facts:**
- JAMA Pediatrics study published **November 13, 2026** — HIGH
- **255 children**, K–6, enrolled in summer treatment program 2022–2025 — HIGH
- Four accommodations tested: extended time, frequent breaks, standing desk, low-distraction workspace — HIGH
- **None of the four accommodations** produced measurable improvement in outcomes — HIGH
- Some accommodations actually **reduced academic productivity** — HIGH
- Behavioral interventions cut rule violations **37%** — HIGH
- Stimulant medication cut violations **61%** and increased correct completed assignments **22%** — HIGH
- Setting caveat: summer treatment program, not a regular classroom — HIGH
- DOI: **10.1001/jamapediatrics.2026.5973** — HIGH

**Sources:**
