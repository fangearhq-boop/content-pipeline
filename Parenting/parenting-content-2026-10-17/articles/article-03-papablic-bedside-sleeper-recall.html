Recall Alert: Papablic Bedside Sleepers Pose Suffocation and Entrapment Risk

By Sarah Morales

October 17, 2026 — Colorado Springs, CO

The Consumer Product Safety Commission recalled Papablic convertible bedside sleepers on October 15, 2026. About 248 units are affected. If you have one of these products, stop using it immediately.

What the Hazard Is

The recall covers Papablic convertible bedside sleeper/play yards. The CPSC identified two failure modes:

  • The mattress can shift and create a gap between the mattress and the side of the product. An infant can roll or shift into that gap and become trapped, creating a suffocation hazard.
  • The latch mechanism can fail, causing the side of the product to collapse. This can result in infant entrapment or injury.

Infant sleep product hazards of this type are taken seriously by the CPSC because the risk window is during overnight sleep, when caregivers may not be immediately aware something has gone wrong. The recall was issued two days ago — it's recent, and many families may not have seen it yet.

Who This Affects

About 248 units were sold on Amazon and through baby product retailers. The product is marketed as a convertible bedside sleeper and play yard — a dual-use item that parents often use for the newborn and early infant stage to keep the baby accessible during nighttime feedings without bed-sharing.

If you purchased a Papablic bedside sleeper, check cpsc.gov/Recalls to confirm whether your specific model is included in the recall. The full product description, model numbers, and recall details are posted there.

What to Do

The CPSC's recommended remedy:

  1. Stop using the product immediately.
  2. Disassemble it.
  3. Cut the mattress in half and cut the fabric in half — this renders it non-functional and ensures it won't be passed along or put back into use.
  4. Take photos showing the cut mattress and fabric.
  5. Email the photos to support@papablic.com to receive a full refund.

The destruction step is standard in infant sleep product recalls. It prevents the recalled unit from being donated, sold secondhand, or used by another family who might not know about the hazard.

A Note on Secondhand Products

This is a good opportunity to check any infant sleep products you received secondhand — from family, friends, Facebook Marketplace, or thrift stores. Recalls don't always follow a product through resale, and the original purchaser may not have been notified. Checking cpsc.gov/Recalls before using any secondhand infant sleep product is a straightforward habit worth building.

What's Next

Stop using any Papablic convertible bedside sleeper immediately. Disassemble it, cut the mattress and fabric in half, and email photos to support@papablic.com for a full refund. The full recall notice — including model numbers and additional details — is available at cpsc.gov/Recalls. The recall was issued October 15, 2026.
