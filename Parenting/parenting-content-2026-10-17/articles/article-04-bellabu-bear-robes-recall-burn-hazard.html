Recall Alert: Bellabu Bear Children's Robes Fail Flammability Standards

By Jamie Rivera

October 17, 2026 — Colorado Springs, CO

The Consumer Product Safety Commission has recalled about 1,300 Bellabu Bear bamboo sherpa robes for children under recall number 26-013. The robes don't meet the mandatory federal flammability standard for children's sleepwear, which means they pose a burn hazard. If you have one, stop using it.

What's Being Recalled

The recall covers Bellabu Bear bamboo sherpa robes in sizes S/M and M/L. The robes were sold at Saks Fifth Avenue and children's boutiques from January 2024 through July 2025. The retail price was approximately $60.

About 1,300 units are included in the recall. No injuries have been reported.

What the Hazard Is

Children's sleepwear sold in the United States must meet federal flammability standards under the Flammable Fabrics Act. The standards exist because children's sleep environments — candles, space heaters, fireplaces, stoves — can expose sleepwear to open flame or intense heat. Fabric that ignites easily and burns quickly is significantly more dangerous than fabric that passes the standard.

Bamboo fabric, including bamboo sherpa blends, can be manufactured to meet flammability standards, but these particular robes don't. The hazard is not theoretical — it's the reason the standard exists and why the CPSC enforces it for children's sleepwear specifically.

The robes are being recalled as sleepwear. If you've been using yours as a robe after bath time rather than overnight, the same flammability concern applies if your child is near any heat source while wearing it.

Who This Affects

Parents who purchased a Bellabu Bear bamboo sherpa robe between January 2024 and July 2025 — through Saks Fifth Avenue or a children's boutique — should check whether their product is included in the recall.

Because these were sold through specialty retailers rather than mass-market chains, this recall may have lower visibility than larger-brand recalls. If you know another family who shops at Saks or children's boutiques, it's worth mentioning.

What to Do

Stop using the robe immediately. Return it to the retailer where you purchased it for a full refund.

For return instructions and to confirm your specific product is covered, visit:

  • bellabubear.com/pages/recall — the brand's own recall page
  • cpsc.gov/Recalls — the official CPSC notice (recall #26-013)

If you purchased through Saks Fifth Avenue, the retailer should be able to process the return directly. For boutique purchases, the recall page has contact information for the brand.

What's Next

Stop using any Bellabu Bear bamboo sherpa robe and return it for a full refund. The recall is CPSC number 26-013.
