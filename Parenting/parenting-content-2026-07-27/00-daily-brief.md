### STORY 2: Target Cat & Jack Toddler Girls' Sandals Recall (#26-629)

**Tier:** T1 | **Pillar:** National Parenting | **Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Posting Window:** 8:00 AM MT (urgent safety, first slot)

CPSC recall #26-629. Target is recalling approximately 211,000 pairs of Cat & Jack brand toddler girls' sandals. The plastic decorative pearls on the raffia straps can detach, posing a choking hazard to young children.

- **Product:** Tan sandals with gold buckles and raffia straps with decorative plastic pearls
- **Sizes:** 5T–12T
- **Price:** ~$22
- **Sold:** January–May 2026 at Target stores nationwide and Target.com
- **Reports:** 23 reports of pearls detaching; no injuries reported
- **Remedy:** Return to any Target store for a full refund; no receipt needed

Story history: NEW. No prior recall coverage for this product.

### STORY 4: Aspen Acres Fire — Evacuations Lifted, Families Returning Home

**Tier:** T2 | **Pillar:** Local News | **Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Posting Window:** 12:00 PM MT (midday news slot)

The Aspen Acres Fire (south of Colorado Springs) is now 61% contained at 101,879 acres, making it the 9th largest wildfire in Colorado history by acreage. Evacuation orders have been lifted for Wetmore, Beulah, Rye, San Isabel, and Colorado City. Families are returning to their properties.

Flash flood and debris flow risk remains elevated in burn scar areas during afternoon thunderstorms. Today's heat advisory (94–100°F) with expected afternoon storms (standard summer pattern) makes this risk active today.

Story history: FOLLOW-UP. No prior Aspen Acres Fire coverage in July 25–26 runs. Fire has been ongoing; this is first day COS Parenting covers it due to evacuation-lifting milestone making it locally actionable.

### STORY 5: Mikrdoo Children's Pajamas CPSC Safety Warning

**Tier:** T2 | **Pillar:** National Parenting | **Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Posting Window:** 5:00 PM MT (evening family content slot)

CPSC issued a safety warning (not a full recall) for Mikrdoo brand children's pajamas. The pajamas violate the federal flammability standard for children's sleepwear. They were sold on Amazon from August 2025 through May 2026 via SHEIN as a third-party seller at $18–$20.

- **Action:** Stop using immediately. Do NOT donate. Destroy the pajamas.
- **Why now:** Back-to-school shopping is underway; families may be replacing old sleepwear or buying new.

Note: This is a CPSC Safety Warning, not a formal recall (important distinction for article framing).

Story history: NEW. No prior pajama/sleepwear coverage in July 25–26.
