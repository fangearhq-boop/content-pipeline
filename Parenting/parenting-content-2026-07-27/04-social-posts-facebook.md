### STORY 2: Target Cat & Jack Sandals Recall (#26-629)

#### Long-Form Post
**Time:** 8:00 AM MT | **Tier:** T1

If you picked up a pair of Cat & Jack sandals from Target this spring, it's worth a quick check of your toddler's closet.

The CPSC just issued recall #26-629 for approximately 211,000 pairs of Cat & Jack toddler girls' sandals sold at Target between January and May 2026. The issue: the decorative plastic pearls on the raffia straps can detach. Those pearls pose a choking hazard for young children.

Here's what to look for: tan sandals with gold buckles and raffia straps with small plastic pearl beads. Sizes 5T through 12T. The sandals were priced around $22 and sold in Target stores and on Target.com.

If you've got a pair, return them to any Target store for a full refund. You don't need a receipt. There have been 23 reports of pearls detaching — no injuries so far, which is the good news, but the recall is worth acting on.

Tag a parent who might have these. 👟

Did you or anyone you know buy these sandals this spring?

### STORY 4: Aspen Acres Fire — Evacuations Lifted, Families Return

#### Long-Form Post
**Time:** 12:00 PM MT | **Tier:** T2

Good news for families south of Colorado Springs: the Aspen Acres Fire has reached 61% containment, and evacuation orders have been lifted for five communities — Wetmore, Beulah, Rye, San Isabel, and Colorado City. Families who had to leave their homes are heading back today.

The fire is now at 101,879 acres, making it the 9th largest wildfire in Colorado history. More than 180 structures were destroyed, including 55+ homes. It's been a devastating stretch for those communities, and the return home is welcome news even if it comes with grief over what was lost.

There's one important thing families going back need to know: burn scar areas have elevated flash flood and debris flow risk. This is standard after a large fire, and it's especially relevant right now because summer afternoon thunderstorms are expected today, as they typically are in July. If you're returning to a property near the burn scar, stay alert to flood watches and move away from low-lying areas during storms.

We're thinking of everyone in those communities today. 🙏

Are you, or is anyone you know, returning home after the Aspen Acres evacuations?

### STORY 5: Mikrdoo Children's Pajamas CPSC Safety Warning

#### Long-Form Post
**Time:** 5:00 PM MT | **Tier:** T2

If you bought children's pajamas on Amazon in the past year, it's worth checking the brand.

The CPSC has issued a safety warning — not a full recall, but a formal warning — for Mikrdoo brand children's pajamas sold on Amazon from August 2025 through May 2026. The seller was SHEIN acting as a third-party seller on the platform. The pajamas were priced around $18–$20.

The issue is flammability: these pajamas violate the federal safety standard for children's sleepwear. That standard exists specifically to reduce burn injuries. Pajamas that fail it can ignite more easily and burn faster than they should.

Here's the part worth paying attention to: the CPSC says to stop using them immediately, and to NOT donate them. The reason matters — if you donate pajamas that fail flammability standards, another family ends up with the same risk. The guidance is to destroy them.

With back-to-school shopping underway for a lot of families, this is a good moment to check what's in your kids' drawers before restocking. 🛒

Have you come across this brand? Worth a check of your Amazon order history from the past year.
