CPSC Issues Safety Warning for Mikrdoo Children's Pajamas Sold on Amazon

By Sarah Morales July 27, 2026

What the Warning Covers

The Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) has issued a safety warning for Mikrdoo brand children's pajamas. These pajamas fail to meet the federal flammability standard for children's sleepwear — a mandatory safety requirement that exists specifically to reduce burn injuries in children who encounter open flames or heat sources while wearing sleepwear.

This is a CPSC Safety Warning, not a formal recall. The distinction matters because it affects the remedy path: there is no formal return-for-refund program here the way there is with a full CPSC recall. The action CPSC recommends is to stop using the pajamas immediately, not donate them, and destroy them.

Why Flammability Standards Exist

The federal flammability standard for children's sleepwear (16 CFR Part 1615 and 1616) has been in place since the 1970s. It covers sleepwear for children ages nine months through 14 years. The standard requires that children's pajamas either be made from inherently flame-resistant fabric or be treated with a flame-retardant finish — and it sets specific test requirements for how those fabrics perform when exposed to flame.

The reason the standard is specific to sleepwear is that children are asleep and less responsive when wearing it, and bedroom environments can have fire risk sources — candles, space heaters, fireplaces. Pajamas that fail the standard can ignite more easily and burn faster, increasing the risk of serious burn injury before a child or adult can respond.

Why Not Donate Them

The instruction to not donate the pajamas is the part of this warning that's easy to overlook. The instinct when getting rid of usable-looking clothing is to donate it. But pajamas that violate flammability standards pose the same risk to the next child who wears them. Donating them moves the risk rather than eliminating it. The CPSC guidance is to destroy them — cut them up or otherwise render them unwearable before disposal.

Why This Comes Up Now

Back-to-school shopping is underway for most Colorado Springs families. That shopping often includes replacing worn-out or outgrown sleepwear alongside school supplies and clothing. Families who are buying new pajamas in July and August are also in the window when they might be pulling older pajamas out of drawers to assess what's still usable — which is exactly the moment to catch a pair of Mikrdoo pajamas before they go back into the rotation.

The sale period for these pajamas was August 2025 through May 2026. If you bought pajamas on Amazon from SHEIN in roughly the past year, it's worth checking the brand label.

What's Next

If you have Mikrdoo pajamas from Amazon in this sale window, stop using them and destroy them. Do not donate them. The full CPSC safety warning is available at cpsc.gov for families who want to see the official documentation or verify the product description. For anyone who bought through Amazon, checking your order history from August 2025 onward is the quickest way to confirm whether you purchased these pajamas. If you have questions about a specific purchase, Amazon's customer service may be able to help with additional information on the transaction.
