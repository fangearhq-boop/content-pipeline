- **Classification:** FOLLOW-UP (Aug 4, 2026 — S3: D11 Bond Vote Tomorrow preview)
- **Byline:** Sarah Morales
- **Posting Window:** 8:00 AM MT

The D11 Board of Education votes tonight, Wednesday August 5 at 5:30 PM, on whether to place a $775 million bond measure on the November general election ballot. This is the most significant vote for D11 families in more than 20 years. The bond would fund HVAC upgrades, school safety improvements, building modernization, and one new construction. The average D11 building is 58 years old, with a documented repair backlog exceeding $422 million — against an annual capital budget of only $17 million. If the board approves the ballot referral, COS voters will decide in November. Estimated taxpayer cost: approximately $6.50 per month per $100,000 of assessed home value. D11 first day of school: August 12.

### STORY 4: The AAP Just Changed How We Think About Screen Time — 2026 Update
- **Tier:** 2 (National Parenting)
- **Pillar:** National Parenting
- **Classification:** NEW STORY (2026 AAP screen time guideline update — different from any prior coverage)
- **Byline:** Jamie Rivera
- **Posting Window:** 3:00 PM MT

In January 2026, the American Academy of Pediatrics officially moved away from rigid hour-based screen time limits for children ages 6 and up. The new guidance prioritizes quality, context, and whether screen use is replacing important activities like sleep, physical play, or face-to-face interaction. The framework is called the "5 Cs." The 18-months-and-under and ages 2–5 guidelines remain similar (no screens before 18 months; 1 hour of high-quality content for ages 2–5), but the approach for school-age children now focuses on habit quality over clock-watching.

### STORY 5: CPSC Summer Baby Safety Alert: Vevor Swings and Baby Lounger Recalls
- **Tier:** 2 (National Parenting / Safety)
- **Pillar:** National Parenting
- **Classification:** NEW STORY (Vevor/baby lounger recalls — distinct from Aug 4 TOMY toddler tower recall)
- **Byline:** Sarah Morales
- **Posting Window:** 5:00 PM MT

The CPSC has active recalls on multiple infant sleep and support products this summer. Key items: Vevor baby swings (inclined sleeper ban violation — suffocation hazard); Cpzzkq brand baby loungers sold on Amazon by CetoPMax (expanded recall — suffocation hazard, fails mandatory infant support cushion standard); Vevor baby loungers (entrapment and fall hazards). These products were all marketed to families of newborns and infants under 1 year. If you or someone you know has any of these products, stop using them immediately. All three have CPSC recall actions in 2026.
