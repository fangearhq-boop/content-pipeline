## STORY 1: Skip Hop Elmo Teether Recall

**Sources:** CPSC.gov, WFTV/Action News Jax/KIRO7, ABC27

**Core facts (HIGH confidence):**
- Product: Skip Hop Baby Sesame Street Elmo Silicone Teether, model 9R263210
- Recall date: September 3, 2026
- Units affected: approximately 22,660 U.S. units; ~3,177 additional in Canada
- Hazard: Black eyes can detach from teether when FROZEN — choking hazard
- Teether shape: Elmo's face (red, orange, white) with textured key (blue) and fabric crayon box (beige/blue/green/yellow/orange/red)
- Incident reports: 4 consumer reports of eyes detaching; no injuries reported
- Remedy: Stop use immediately, contact Skip Hop at skiphoprecall.com for refund in form of a gift card
- Gift card usable at: Carter's, Skip Hop, OshKosh B'gosh, Little Planet stores (all William Carter Company brands)
- CPSC page: cpsc.gov/Recalls
- Note: Do NOT freeze silicone teethers — key consumer safety message

**Sources:** cpsc.gov, wftv.com, actionnewsjax.com, kiro7.com, abc27.com

## STORY 4: Child Passenger Safety Week

**Core facts (HIGH confidence):**
- National Child Passenger Safety Week: September 20-26, 2026
- CDC statistic: More than 1,000 child passengers ages 14 and younger killed in U.S. motor vehicle crashes in 2023
- 43% of those children were not buckled up (CDC)
- The week promotes free car seat check events nationwide
- Key message: Many car seats are installed incorrectly or children are in wrong-size seats
- Colorado law requires: children under 8 in proper child restraint seat; rear-facing until age 2 recommended
- Free car seat checks: Contact your local fire station or Safe Kids Colorado chapter for COS events

**Sources:** wymt.com (health dept announcement), CDC data, general knowledge
