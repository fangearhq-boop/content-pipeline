### STORY 1: Two Toddler Kitchen Towers Recalled — More Than 207,000 Units Combined
**Tier:** 1 | **Byline:** Jamie Rivera | **Pillar:** National Parenting (Product Recall)
**Status:** NEW STORY

Two separate kitchen toddler tower recalls have been announced by the CPSC, together covering more than 207,000 units sold on Amazon and major retailers.

**Boon PIVOT Collapsible Toddler Tower** (TOMY): 116,908 units recalled. The tower can tip over while in use, and a child's torso can fit through openings on the front and back sides, posing a risk of serious injury and death. Sold at Target.com, Amazon.com, and Babylist.com from January 2023 through June 2026 for ~$150. No injuries reported. Free repair kit available: recall.tomy.com/pivot.

**Woodure Toddler Kitchen Step Stools** (Shenzhen Muqiqu Creative Development): ~91,000 units recalled. Models WD1764, WD1357, WD1720. Can collapse or tip over; torso-entrapment hazard. Sold on Amazon July 2024 through June 2026, $60–$100. 22 instability/tip-over reports; 15 injuries (scrapes, cuts, bruises). Free repair kit: wwoodure.com/recall.

**Key Facts:**
- Boon PIVOT: 116,908 units; TOMY recall; Target/Amazon/Babylist Jan 2023–Jun 2026; ~$150; free repair kit (recall.tomy.com/pivot)
- Woodure: ~91,000 units; Amazon Jul 2024–Jun 2026; $60–$100; 15 injuries; free repair kit (wwoodure.com/recall)
- Both: stop use immediately, store away from children until repaired
- Total combined: ~207,908 units
