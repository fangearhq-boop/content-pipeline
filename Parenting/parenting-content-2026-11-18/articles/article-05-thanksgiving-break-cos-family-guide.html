Thanksgiving Break in Colorado Springs: What to Do With the Kids Nov. 24–28

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | November 18, 2026

Thanksgiving break starts November 24 for most families in D11, D20, and D49 — five days with kids at home and a holiday in the middle. If you're looking to build some structure into the week, here's a guide to what's happening in and around Colorado Springs.

Thanksgiving Morning: Turkey Trot at Briargate YMCA

The annual Turkey Trot 5K and Kids' Tiny Trot returns to the Briargate YMCA at 4025 Family Place on Thanksgiving morning. The Tiny Trot kicks off at 8:15 a.m. and the 5K follows at 9:00 a.m. It's a well-established COS tradition that tends to draw hundreds of families from across the community.

Pricing: $43.65 for ages 6–19 and $54.25 for ages 20 and up. The event is stroller-friendly and dog-friendly — a good combination for families with kids at multiple ages or stages. Register at the YMCA or through the visitcos.com event listing.

Thanksgiving Morning: Colorado Wolf and Wildlife Center

For families who want something a little more unusual, the Colorado Wolf and Wildlife Center in Divide — about 40 minutes from Colorado Springs — hosts a Turkey Toss on Thanksgiving morning from 9 to 11 a.m. The event gives guests a close-up look as the wolves and other animals receive Thanksgiving-themed enrichment feeding. Pricing: ages 6–11 are $20, ages 12 and older are $40. It's a memorable alternative for families who want to add something different to the morning.

Skate in the Park at Acacia Park opened November 13 for its 15th season and runs daily through January 2027. Admission is $15 per person including skate rental, and children 4 and under are free with a paying adult. It's worth checking hours around Thanksgiving Day specifically — holiday hours may vary.

What's Next

Thanksgiving break starts November 24. For the Turkey Trot, register in advance at visitcos.com to secure your spot — it fills up. For the Wolf Center Turkey Toss, call ahead or check the Colorado Wolf and Wildlife Center's website to confirm availability and reserve. For everything else on the list, checking current hours before you go is always a good call during the holiday week.
