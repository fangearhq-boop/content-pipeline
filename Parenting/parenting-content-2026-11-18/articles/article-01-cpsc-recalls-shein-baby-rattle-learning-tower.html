CPSC Recall Alert: SHEIN Baby Rattle Sets Recalled Over Choking Hazard for Kids Under 3

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | November 18, 2026

Two new product recalls from the Consumer Product Safety Commission affect COS families with young children. The higher-urgency of the two involves SHEIN's Baby Rattle and Teether Toy Sets — approximately 2,657 units sold online for about $10 — which have been recalled because small parts can detach and pose a choking risk for children under 3. If you have one, stop use immediately.

The SHEIN Baby Rattle and Teether Recall

Recall number 27-083 covers SHEIN Baby Rattle and Teether Toy Sets sold on US.Shein.com between November 2025 and July 2026. The sets include toys with purple and orange decorative knobs that can detach during use. Detached small parts create a choking hazard for children under 3 years old — CPSC's highest-priority age group for this type of hazard.

No injuries have been reported in connection with this recall. SHEIN is offering a full refund as the remedy. To receive it, you'll need to confirm that you've disposed of the product.

To start the refund process or confirm your item is affected, visit cpsc.gov/Recalls and search for recall number 27-083, or look for SHEIN Baby Rattle in the recall database.

Little Partners Grow N Stow Learning Tower Recall

The Consumer Product Safety Commission has also recalled the Little Partners "Grow N Stow" Folding Learning Tower due to a fall hazard. These towers — designed to let young children safely reach countertop height while adults cook or prepare food — have become popular in COS households with toddlers and preschoolers. If you have one, stop use and check cpsc.gov/Recalls for the specific remedy steps.

How to Stay Current on CPSC Recalls

The CPSC issues recalls regularly, and it can be easy to miss them in a busy week. The quickest habit: bookmark cpsc.gov/Recalls and check it monthly. Recalls are listed in reverse chronological order, so a quick scroll takes about two minutes. You can also sign up for email alerts on the CPSC website to receive notifications by product category.

What's Next

If you have the SHEIN Baby Rattle and Teether Toy Set, stop use today and visit cpsc.gov/Recalls to start the refund process. If you have a Little Partners Grow N Stow Learning Tower, do the same. When in doubt about any child product, check the CPSC database — it's the official record and the fastest way to know if something in your home is affected by an active recall.
