Over 100 Families Pack a D11 Meeting — Here's What the Boundary Redrawing Is About

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | November 18, 2026

Colorado Springs School District 11 is reviewing its school boundaries — and families are paying attention. More than 100 northwest-area residents recently turned out to a community meeting at Trailblazer Elementary to ask questions and push back on what they're hearing. For COS families with kids in D11 schools, here's what the review is about and why it matters.

Why D11 Is Reviewing Boundaries Now

The numbers are the starting point. D11 has lost 21.6% of its student population over the last decade, dropping from a larger enrollment base to approximately 22,265 students today. During the same period, D49 (Falcon School District) has grown 65.5%. Regionally, D20 is now the largest district in the Colorado Springs area, with D49 second and D11 14th in size statewide as of January 2025 state data.

When a district's enrollment falls significantly, its existing attendance boundaries can produce lopsided results — some schools underfull, others at capacity, transportation routes that no longer make geographic sense. Boundary reviews are a standard administrative response to these shifts. D11 administrators are also revisiting student transfer policies as part of the same process.

What Families Are Asking About

The Trailblazer Elementary meeting drew more than 100 northwest-area residents, reflecting how directly families feel the potential impact of boundary changes. The northwest portion of D11 has seen some of the most visible enrollment shifts as development patterns have drawn families toward D20 and D49. Questions at community meetings typically focus on whether children currently at a school would be reassigned under new boundaries, what the transfer process would look like, and how transportation routes would change.

No specific vote or timeline has been confirmed. The community engagement process is still open, which means now is the meaningful window for D11 families to attend a meeting, review any materials the district has shared, and make their preferences heard before decisions are finalized.

How This Differs From the Bond Accountability Story

This boundary review is a separate issue from the D11 bond accountability concerns that surfaced earlier this month. The bond process relates to how the district spends its approved capital funds — specifically on school rebuilds and renovations like the Holmes Middle School and MacLaren Elementary projects. The boundary redrawing is an enrollment and attendance zone question. They're both active D11 issues, but they involve different district decisions and different community processes.

What's Next

D11 community engagement meetings on the boundary review are ongoing. To stay current on dates and locations, check the D11 district website at d11.org. If your family is in the northwest attendance area, or if you have children at a D11 school that might be affected by enrollment shifts, attending an upcoming meeting is the most direct way to get information and have your questions answered by district administrators.
