Electric Safari 2026: What Families Need to Know Before Tickets Sell Out

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | November 18, 2026

Two weeks from today, Cheyenne Mountain Zoo lights up for Electric Safari — one of the most popular family events in Colorado Springs and the No. 2 zoo lights experience in North America according to USA TODAY. The event opens December 2, 2026 and runs through January 1, 2027. If you're planning to take your family, the most important thing to know right now: advance tickets are required, and they sell out fast.

What to Expect

Electric Safari transforms the zoo's 50-plus acres into an immersive holiday lights experience with more than 85 light sculptures — many of them animal-themed in keeping with the zoo's character. The event includes illuminated animal inflatables, a holiday-safari drone show, and Santa at Safari Lodge. The scale of the event is one of the things that sets it apart from smaller COS holiday light displays: it takes the better part of an evening to walk through, and there's enough variety to keep kids of different ages engaged.

The zoo earned its USA TODAY ranking by consistently updating and expanding the display. This is the seventh straight year it has ranked in the top three nationally, so the reputation isn't just marketing — COS families who've been before tend to go back.

Tickets and Planning

Advance tickets are sold at cmzoo.org. December dates around winter break — particularly the week of December 22–January 1 — historically sell out several weeks ahead of time. If you have flexibility, weeknights in early to mid-December tend to have more availability and slightly smaller crowds than weekends.

Zoo members have access to member-exclusive pricing and sometimes earlier reservation windows, so if you have a membership, check your member benefits before buying general admission tickets.

Tips for Families With Young Kids

Strollers are generally welcome along the paved main paths. Dress in warm layers — evenings at Cheyenne Mountain Zoo in December are cold, and the zoo is at elevation. Bring a carrier for toddlers if you have a long-distance walker who might tire midway through. Many families find the experience runs about two to three hours depending on how much time kids want to spend looking at favorite sculptures.

What's Next

Visit cmzoo.org to check available dates and purchase tickets. December fills quickly, especially the holiday stretch from December 22 through January 1. If you have a zoo membership, log in to your account first to access member ticket pricing. The earlier you book, the more date flexibility you'll have — this is one COS December activity that rewards planning ahead.
