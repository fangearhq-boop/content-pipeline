### STORY 1: CPSC Recalls — Baby Rattle Sets and Learning Tower
**Tier:** 1
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Type:** NEW STORY
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Posting Window:** 8:00 AM MT

Three new CPSC recalls affect COS families with young children. SHEIN has recalled approximately 2,657 Baby Rattle and Teether Toy Sets sold on US.Shein.com between November 2025 and July 2026 for approximately $10; purple and orange knobs can detach, posing a choking hazard for children under 3. The recall number is 27-083; the remedy is a full refund. Little Partners has separately recalled its "Grow N Stow" Folding Learning Tower due to a fall hazard. If you have either product at home, stop use immediately and check cpsc.gov/Recalls for remedy steps.
