**Sources:** CPSC.gov, ABC News, Fox26, Fox5, WFTV, WFLA

- **HIGH:** Recall of Goody King Magnetic Building Cubes and Blocks — imported by Yi Suen Commerce — CPSC.gov, ABC News confirmed
- **HIGH:** Total units: approximately 213,500 — CPSC.gov, WFTV
- **HIGH:** Sold on Amazon from January 2024 through July 2026 — CPSC.gov
- **HIGH:** Price range: $17-$50 — CPSC.gov
- **HIGH:** Set sizes: 45, 56, 100, 120, 150, and 300 pieces — CPSC.gov
- **HIGH:** 2 children swallowed magnets and underwent surgery — CPSC.gov, multiple news sources
- **HIGH:** 27 additional reports of blocks breaking or magnets becoming loose — CPSC.gov

## Story 5: RSV Season Prep

**Sources:** AAP, CDC, CPSC; vaccineadvisor.com, aap.org RSV prevention page

### Verified Facts
- **HIGH:** AAP recommends nirsevimab (Beyfortus) or clesrovimab for infants under 8 months during RSV season — AAP RSV Prevention page confirmed
- **HIGH:** Maternal RSV vaccination (Abrysvo) is an alternative to infant immunization — AAP confirmed
- **HIGH:** Children 8-19 months at high risk should also receive RSV immunization — AAP 2026 recommendations
- **HIGH:** RSV causes approximately 58,000-80,000 hospitalizations in U.S. children under 5 annually — AAP/CDC
- **HIGH:** RSV season in Colorado typically runs late October through January — standard Colorado health pattern (MEDIUM - exact dates vary by year)
- **MEDIUM:** Colorado's RSV season timing: fall school start correlates with RSV spread increase
