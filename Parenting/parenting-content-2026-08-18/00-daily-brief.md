### STORY 2: CPSC Urgent Warning — Slide Playset Kills 17-Month-Old, Destroy Immediately
**Tier:** 1 | **Byline:** Jamie Rivera | **Pillar:** National Parenting
On August 13, 2026, the CPSC issued an urgent safety warning — not a traditional recall — about Wenzhou Yidian Model WPX-4 indoor/outdoor slide playsets after a 17-month-old child died from head and neck entrapment. The colorful plastic playsets include a tunnel, slide, and basketball hoop, and come in red, navy, coffee, gray, green, and yellow. The manufacturer has refused to cooperate with a recall. STOP USING IMMEDIATELY — dispose in trash, do not sell or give away.

### STORY 3: Goody King Magnetic Cubes Recall — 2 Kids Had Surgery, 213K Units
**Tier:** 2 | **Byline:** Sarah Morales | **Pillar:** National Parenting
The CPSC recalled 213,500 Goody King Magnetic Building Cubes and Blocks (imported by Yi Suen Commerce) on August 13, 2026. Two children underwent surgery to remove swallowed magnets; 27 additional incidents of blocks breaking and magnets becoming loose. Sets of 45 to 300 pieces were sold on Amazon from January 2024 through July 2026 for $17-$50. Contact Yi Suen Commerce for a prepaid return label and full refund.

### STORY 4: First-Week Survival Guide — All COS School Districts Now in Session
**Tier:** 2 | **Byline:** Jamie Rivera | **Pillar:** Evergreen
With D49 (Aug 4), D11 (Aug 12), and D20 (Aug 17-18) now all in session, COS families enter the critical first-week adjustment period. Practical guidance: set consistent wake times, prep lunches the night before, allow 15-30 minutes of decompression after school before homework, watch for first-week anxiety in kids changing buildings (K, 6th, 9th graders), and keep routines predictable. First weeks set habits for the whole year.

### STORY 5: RSV Season Is 8 Weeks Out — What COS Families Need to Know Now
**Tier:** 2 | **Byline:** Sarah Morales | **Pillar:** National Parenting
RSV season typically begins in Colorado in late October, peaking December-January. With all COS schools now in session and kids sharing classrooms again, families with infants and high-risk toddlers should act now. The AAP recommends nirsevimab or clesrovimab for infants under 8 months during RSV season. Maternal RSV vaccination (Abrysvo) is an alternative. Know when to call your pediatrician and what symptoms to watch for.
