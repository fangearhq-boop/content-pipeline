Bark at Briargate: Free Dog Adoption Event in Colorado Springs Today

By Jamie Rivera | September 19, 2026

Local Events

If your family has been thinking about adding a dog, or just wants a reason to get outside on a September Saturday, you've got a good one today. Bark at Briargate runs from 11 AM to 3 PM at The Promenade Shops at Briargate — and it's completely free.

The event brings together adoptable dogs, pet-focused vendors, giveaways, and family-friendly activities, all in one of Colorado Springs' most popular outdoor shopping centers. It's organized in partnership with the LovePup Foundation, a local nonprofit working to place dogs in permanent homes. If your kids have been making the case for a family dog, this is as low-pressure an introduction as you'll find.

What to Expect

Bark at Briargate is designed for families, dog lovers, and anyone curious about pet adoption. You'll find adoptable dogs available for meet-and-greets throughout the afternoon. LovePup Foundation staff will be on hand to answer questions about specific dogs, the adoption process, and what it takes to bring a new pet home.

Beyond adoptions, the event features pet-focused vendors — think treats, accessories, and small-business pet brands — plus giveaways and activities throughout the four-hour window. You don't need to arrive right at 11 AM; the event is designed to be drop-in friendly, so come when it works for your family's Saturday.

About the LovePup Foundation

The LovePup Foundation partners with events like Bark at Briargate to connect adoptable dogs with Colorado Springs families. All dogs at on-site adoptions go through LovePup's standard screening and preparation process. If you meet a dog today and want to move forward, LovePup staff can walk you through the next steps on site.

Not ready to adopt? That's fine too. The event is equally worth attending just to browse, meet some dogs, and support a local organization doing good work. It costs nothing to show up.

Bring a Donation If You Can

Families are encouraged to bring a new, unused pet supply item as a donation for LovePup. Think dog food, treats, toys, leashes, or other basic supplies. It's a suggested contribution, not a requirement — your presence alone is welcome.

Getting There

The Promenade Shops at Briargate is located in northern Colorado Springs, easily accessible from Powers Boulevard and Briargate Parkway. Parking is free and plentiful throughout the shopping center. The outdoor plaza layout makes it comfortable to move around with strollers or younger kids.

What's Next

Bark at Briargate runs through 3 PM today. If your family is looking ahead at October, Colorado Springs has a strong lineup of fall events coming up — including pumpkin patches, Halloween festivals, and outdoor screenings. Keep an eye on COS Parenting for the full fall guide.

If you're seriously considering dog adoption, the LovePup Foundation is a good place to start. You can connect with them directly at today's event or follow up through their website for more information on available dogs and the adoption process.
