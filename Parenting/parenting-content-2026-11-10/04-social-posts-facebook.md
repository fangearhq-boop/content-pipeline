### STORY 5: Holiday Toy Safety

#### Long-Form Post — 5:00 PM MT

**Long-Form Post (5:00 PM MT)**

Holiday shopping is about to kick into high gear, and before you fill up that cart, there are a few things worth checking first.

The AAP and CPSC release toy safety guidance every year — and there are a handful of things that trip parents up most often. First: age labels. That "3+" sticker isn't just a suggestion. It's a choking hazard warning, and it matters even if your kid seems mature.

Button and coin batteries are the top CPSC safety concern right now. They're in everything — remote controls, greeting cards, small toys. If a child swallows one, it can cause severe internal damage within two hours. Check that any toy with batteries has a secure, screw-locked battery compartment.

Buying secondhand for the holidays? We love it. Just check cpsc.gov/recalls before you buy. Recalls follow the product, not the original buyer — and there are several active 2026 baby product recalls on the list right now.

If you have a safety question or think a product should be recalled, you can call the CPSC hotline directly: 1-800-638-2772.
