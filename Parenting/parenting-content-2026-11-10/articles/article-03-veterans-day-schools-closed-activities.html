Veterans Day Is Tomorrow: Schools Are Closed in D11, D20, and D49 — Here's What to Do with the Day

By Sarah Morales | November 10, 2026

Local Events

Veterans Day is Wednesday, November 11 — a federal holiday that closes schools across Colorado Springs. D11, D20, and D49 are all out for the day. If you're suddenly managing a mid-week day off with kids, there are some genuinely good options in Colorado Springs that also carry real meaning.

COS has one of the largest active-duty military communities in the country. Fort Carson, the United States Air Force Academy, Peterson Space Force Base, Schriever Space Force Base, and Cheyenne Mountain Space Force Station are all within or near the city. Veterans Day hits differently here — there's a good chance you know someone it's honoring.

Free Things to Do in Colorado Springs on Veterans Day

National Museum of World War II Aviation

The National Museum of World War II Aviation offers free admission on Veterans Day. The museum houses an extraordinary collection of flying WWII aircraft — not just static displays, but aircraft that actually fly. It's one of Colorado Springs' most underrated family attractions, and Veterans Day is an excellent time to visit with kids of any age. The museum is located at the Colorado Springs Airport complex.

United States Air Force Academy

The Air Force Academy campus is open to the public, and Veterans Day is a meaningful day to visit. The Cadet Area, the iconic Cadet Chapel, and the Visitor Center are accessible to civilian families. Kids who are interested in planes, military history, or just want to see one of the most distinctive campuses in the country will find plenty to take in.

Practical Tips for the Day

  • Call ahead for museum hours. While free admission on Veterans Day is an established practice at the WWII Aviation Museum, confirm current hours before you go.
  • USAFA security checkpoint. Visiting the Air Force Academy requires passing through a security gate. Have a valid ID ready. Children generally don't need separate ID, but adult drivers do.
  • Many businesses are open. Veterans Day closes federal offices and schools, but most retail businesses and restaurants are open. It's a good day for a family outing without the full weekend crowds.
  • Thank a veteran. If you have family, friends, or neighbors who have served, today is a simple day to say so. Kids who grow up around that gratitude tend to carry it.

What's Next

Schools are back in session on Thursday, November 12. In the meantime, Veterans Day is a rare opportunity to do something meaningful with kids on a weekday — a free museum visit, a walk through the Academy campus, or a quiet conversation about why the holiday exists. Those tend to stick.
