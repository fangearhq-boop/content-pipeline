### STORY 1: WPX-4 Slide Playset CPSC Warning
**Sources:** CPSC.gov official warning, Click2Houston, The Hill
**Key facts (HIGH confidence):**
- Product: Model WPX-4 Slide Playsets — indoor/outdoor plastic playsets with tunnel, slide, and basketball hoop
- Manufacturer: Wenzhou Yidian Amusement Equipment Co., Ltd. (China)
- Hazard: Multiple openings where a child's torso can pass through but the head cannot — head and neck entrapment risk
- Incident: 1 confirmed death — a 17-month-old child died in an entrapment incident
- CPSC action: This is a WARNING issued August 13, 2026 — CPSC urges consumers to stop use immediately and DISPOSE of the product
- Critical: Do NOT sell or give away — the product should be destroyed/disposed
- No refund mechanism announced yet (this is a warning, not a formal recall with remedy)
- URL: CPSC.gov warning page issued August 13, 2026

### STORY 2: Goody King Magnetic Building Cubes Recall
**Sources:** CPSC.gov official recall, ABC News, Fox Business
**Key facts (HIGH confidence):**
- Product: Goody King-branded Magnetic Building Cubes and Blocks
- Importer: Yi Suen Commerce, Hong Kong
- Units: approximately 213,500
- Sets: 45, 56, 100, 120, 150, and 300 cubes; themed (forests, dinosaurs, unicorns)
- Sold: exclusively on Amazon.com between January 2024 and July 2026
- Hazard: Cubes can break or open, exposing high-powered magnets; if 2+ magnets swallowed, they attract through intestinal walls causing perforations, twisting, or blockage — can cause blood poisoning and death
- Incidents: At least 27 reports of cubes breaking; 2 children required surgery to remove swallowed magnets
- Action: Stop use immediately; contact Yi Suen Commerce for full refund; company sends prepaid shipping label after consumer registers for recall; refund issued after company receives returned product
- Recall date: August 13, 2026
