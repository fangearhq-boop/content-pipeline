Goody King Magnetic Building Cubes Recalled — 213,500 Units Sold on Amazon, 2 Children Required Surgery

By Sarah Morales | August 14, 2026 | COS Parenting

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission announced a formal recall on August 13, 2026 of Goody King-branded Magnetic Building Cubes and Blocks. Approximately 213,500 units are affected. If your family bought any of these sets on Amazon between January 2024 and July 2026, stop use immediately and contact the importer for a full refund.

The recall was issued after at least 27 reports of the cubes breaking apart and two documented cases of children requiring surgery to remove swallowed magnets.

Which Products Are Affected

The recall covers Goody King-branded Magnetic Building Cubes and Blocks sold exclusively through Amazon.com. The sets came in a range of sizes and themes:

  • Set sizes: 45, 56, 100, 120, 150, and 300 cubes
  • Themes: forests, dinosaurs, and unicorns
  • Importer: Yi Suen Commerce, based in Hong Kong
  • Sale window: January 2024 through July 2026 on Amazon.com

If your family purchased any Goody King magnetic cube set from Amazon during that period, assume it is covered by this recall. Remove the sets from children's reach immediately while you process the return.

Why Magnetic Toy Recalls Are Serious

Magnetic building toy recalls often raise a question for parents: aren't magnets everywhere? What makes these different?

High-powered rare earth magnets, which are used in many modern magnetic building toys, generate forces far stronger than the toy magnets most parents remember from childhood. The danger isn't swallowing a single magnet — a single magnet typically passes through the digestive system without complication. The danger is swallowing two or more magnets separately, or a magnet and a ferrous metal piece.

When that happens, the magnets can attract to each other through the walls of the intestines. That attraction can cause the intestinal walls to compress, perforate, twist, or become blocked. The resulting injuries are internal and serious: perforations can cause infections and blood poisoning; blockages can cut off blood supply to sections of the intestine. Surgery is often required to correct the damage, and in the most severe cases the injuries can be life-threatening.

In the case of the Goody King cubes, the hazard is compounded by the cubes' tendency to break apart. A cube that appears intact may crack open during play, exposing the magnet inside — and a child may swallow the magnet without an adult noticing immediately. That's how the 27 reported incidents occurred.

How to Get Your Refund

The remedy for this recall is a full refund. Here's how the process works:

Contact Yi Suen Commerce to register for the recall. Once you've registered, the company will send you a prepaid shipping label so you can return the set at no cost. After Yi Suen Commerce receives your returned product, the refund is issued. The CPSC announcement does not specify refund method or timeline, but these details are typically available through the importer directly.

While you're waiting to complete the return, keep the product away from children. If you've already thrown the set away or can't locate it, contact Yi Suen Commerce anyway to see whether the refund process has any accommodations for products that can't be returned.

If Your Child Has Swallowed a Magnet

If you believe your child may have swallowed a magnet — or more than one — seek emergency medical care immediately. Do not wait for symptoms. Magnetic ingestion injuries can be internal and may not show obvious signs right away. Emergency physicians can use imaging to locate magnets and determine the appropriate course of action.

Do not try to induce vomiting. Do not give laxatives. Medical evaluation is the only appropriate response.

What's Next

The CPSC recall page for this product will be updated as more information becomes available, including any clarifications on the refund process from Yi Suen Commerce. Families can find the current recall listing at CPSC.gov by searching for "Goody King."

If you purchased these sets as gifts for other families, reach out to let them know. The recall covers all sales from Amazon during the nearly two-and-a-half-year window between January 2024 and July 2026, so there are a significant number of families across Colorado and the country who may still have these sets in their toy bins without knowing about the recall.

Magnetic building toys remain popular and most reputable brands build their sets to meet ASTM safety standards for high-powered magnets. If your family wants to continue with magnetic building play, look for sets that are explicitly ASTM F963 compliant — a standard developed specifically to address high-powered magnet hazards in children's toys.
