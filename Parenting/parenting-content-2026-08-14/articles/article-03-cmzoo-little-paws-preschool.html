Colorado's First Zoo-Based Outdoor Preschool Opens August 24 at Cheyenne Mountain Zoo

By Jamie Rivera | August 14, 2026 | COS Parenting

If you've got a 3- to 5-year-old and you've been looking for a preschool setting that's genuinely different, there's something new in Colorado Springs worth knowing about.

Little Paws Preschool at Cheyenne Mountain Zoo opens its inaugural class on Monday, August 24 — making it Colorado's first state-accredited, nature-based outdoor preschool inside a zoo. Enrollment is still open as of today.

What Little Paws Preschool Is

Little Paws Preschool is a licensed preschool program that operates entirely outdoors on the grounds of Cheyenne Mountain Zoo. It's accredited by the Colorado Department of Early Childhood, and all teachers hold early childhood credentials that meet Colorado state licensure requirements. It isn't a zoo program that borrows preschool language — it's a fully licensed preschool that happens to be located inside a zoo.

The curriculum is play-based. Children learn through outdoor exploration rather than structured desk time. The program focuses on critical thinking, collaboration, emotional intelligence, and resilience — all developed through direct interaction with the outdoor zoo environment. Classes are kept small, at approximately 15 students each.

Why Outdoor and Nature-Based Preschool

Research on outdoor and nature-based early childhood education has grown substantially over the past decade. Studies consistently find that children who spend significant time in natural outdoor environments show higher levels of physical activity, reduced stress, stronger attention spans, and more developed gross motor skills compared to peers in traditional indoor-only settings.

Nature-based programs also tend to support the kind of open-ended, child-directed play that developmental researchers associate with creativity and problem-solving capacity. When children are exploring a real environment — one that includes animals, plants, weather, terrain, and unpredictability — they're practicing adaptation, observation, and persistence in ways that structured indoor activities don't easily replicate.

A zoo adds a specific dimension to that environment. Children interact with living animals, observe natural behaviors, and build a sense of connection to wildlife that's hard to develop from books or screens. For Colorado Springs families who already visit Cheyenne Mountain Zoo, the idea of their preschooler spending two or three mornings a week learning in that space is likely immediately intuitive.

A Unique Option for COS Families

Colorado Springs has a number of strong preschool options across D11, D20, and D49, as well as private programs. What's notable about Little Paws is what it adds to that landscape rather than what it competes with.

State accreditation matters for families who want assurance that the program meets Colorado's educational standards — it means the curriculum and teacher qualifications have been evaluated and approved, not just self-certified. The small class size of approximately 15 students is meaningful for parents who've experienced the difference between a 12-child classroom and a 22-child one at this age. And the zoo location is genuinely one-of-a-kind in Colorado.

It's not the right fit for every family. The schedule requires two or three weekday mornings of drop-off and pickup at Cheyenne Mountain Zoo, which adds commute considerations depending on where you live in COS. And at $220.75 to $460.75 per month, it's a real financial commitment. But for families with the flexibility and the interest, it's a program worth knowing about.

What's Next

The inaugural class opens August 24. If you're considering enrolling your child, reaching out now is the right move — enrollment is still open, but space is limited by the program's class-size design.

To get more information or begin the enrollment process, contact Little Paws Preschool directly at littlepawspreschool@cmzoo.org. The Cheyenne Mountain Zoo website (cmzoo.org) also has information on the program.

For COS parents who haven't found the right preschool fit yet, or who've been curious about nature-based learning, this is genuinely new ground for Colorado Springs — and it's worth a look before the August 24 opening date arrives.
