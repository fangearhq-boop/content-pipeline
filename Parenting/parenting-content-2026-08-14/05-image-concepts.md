## STORY 1: WPX-4 Slide Playset CPSC Warning

**Purpose:** X/Facebook post hero
**Prompt:**

The playset is colorful and toy-like but photographed from a slight low angle that emphasizes its size relative to a small child. Mood: cautionary, warm afternoon light with slight shadows.
