### STORY 1: CPSC Warns Parents to Stop Using WPX-4 Slide Playsets — 17-Month-Old Died
**Tier:** 1
**Pillar:** National Parenting (Safety / CPSC)
**Angle:** NEW STORY — CPSC issued an urgent warning August 13 to immediately stop using Model WPX-4 Slide Playsets (indoor/outdoor plastic playset with tunnel, slide, basketball hoop) made by Wenzhou Yidian Amusement Equipment. ### STORY 2: Goody King Magnetic Building Cubes Recalled — 213,500 Units, 2 Kids Required Surgery
**Tier:** 1
**Pillar:** National Parenting (Safety / CPSC)
**Angle:** NEW STORY — CPSC recall issued August 13: Goody King-branded Magnetic Building Cubes and Blocks (~213,500 units sold exclusively on Amazon.com January 2024–July 2026). The cubes can break apart, exposing high-powered internal magnets. If a child swallows 2+ magnets, they attract through intestinal walls, causing perforation, twisting, or blockage. At least 27 cube-break reports; 2 children required surgery to remove swallowed magnets. Action: stop use; contact Yi Suen Commerce for a full refund (prepaid shipping label provided after registering).
- **Posting window:** 10:00 AM MT
**Byline:** Sarah Morales

### STORY 4: Jenkins Middle School Opens New Wing After 1.5 Years of Closure
**Tier:** 2
**Pillar:** Local News
**Angle:** NEW STORY — D11 Jenkins Middle School held its ribbon cutting on August 12, 2026 (the same day as D11's first day of school), fully reopening after roughly 18 months of closure due to structural safety concerns. The original building had never been properly anchored to its foundation, causing cracking and separating floors and walls. Crews demolished the 7th and 8th grade wing and built a new one: 20,000 square feet, 20 new classrooms, anchored to a new stable foundation. Groundbreaking: October 2025. During the closure, 6th and 7th graders were in portable classrooms in the parking lot; 8th graders attended Doherty High School.
- **Posting window:** 3:00 PM MT
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
