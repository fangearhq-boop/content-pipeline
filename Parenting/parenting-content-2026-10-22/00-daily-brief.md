### STORY 4: Halloween Pedestrian Safety — One Week to Halloween
- **Tier:** 2 (Today)
- **Pillar:** National Parenting
- **Byline:** Jamie Rivera
- **Tag:** NEW
- **Post Time:** 3:00 PM MT
- **Summary:** Halloween night is statistically one of the most dangerous evenings of the year for child pedestrians — the fatality risk for child pedestrians is about 43% higher on Halloween than on other fall evenings. CSPD recommends children under 12 trick-or-treat with adult supervision. Safety tips: wear reflective tape on costumes, carry flashlights or glow sticks, choose face paint over full-face masks that limit vision, stay on sidewalks, cross only at crosswalks, and stick to lit neighborhoods. Go during daylight hours when possible (after school until 8 PM).
- **Sources:** Safe Kids Worldwide Halloween data, CSPD 2026 seasonal safety messaging, AAP Halloween safety guidelines
