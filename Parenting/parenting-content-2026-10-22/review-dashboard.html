AAP: All Kids 6 Months and Older Should Get Flu Vaccine Now
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Flu season is hitting early this year, and it's time to get your kids vaccinated if you haven't already. The American Academy of Pediatrics released its 2026–27 guidance this summer: flu vaccine is recommended for every child 6 months and older, ideally before the season peaks. After last year's severe season — 190 pediatric deaths nationally — the AAP is emphasizing urgency. Colorado already confirmed its first child flu death of this season earlier this week. That's a reminder that flu isn't just a bad cold for kids. Two things to do this week: 1. Schedule a flu shot for every family member 6 months and older. 2. Ask your pediatrician about nirsevimab (Beyfortus) for infants under 8 months — the AAP updated its RSV recommendation too. Walk-ins are available at most COS pharmacies and urgent care clinics. Your pediatrician's office can usually get you in within a day or two. Have you gotten your kids vaccinated for flu yet this season? --- ### Image Post Caption Flu season is here early — the AAP says every child 6 months and older should be vaccinated now. Halloween Pedestrian Safety — One Week to Halloween
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post One week until Halloween, and we want every kid to make it home safe with their candy haul. Here's the thing: Halloween night is genuinely one of the most dangerous evenings of the year for child pedestrians. Research consistently shows fatality risk for kids runs about 43% higher on October 31 than on comparable fall evenings. The most important safety habits: 🔦 Carry a flashlight or glow sticks — be visible, not just visible-ish 🔁 Add reflective tape to costumes and bags 😊 Choose face paint or a partial mask over a full-face mask — kids can't see cars they can't see 🚦 Cross only at crosswalks and look both ways (yes, even older kids) 👨‍👩‍👧 Kids under 12 should have an adult with them — CSPD recommends it Go out earlier when it's still light. Most trick-or-treating in COS wraps by 8 PM anyway. What's your family's Halloween safety routine? --- ### Image Post Caption One week to Halloween — a quick reminder that Oct. 31 is one of the highest-risk nights of the year for child pedestrians. Venetucci Farm — Free Pumpkins for Kindergartners, Final Weekends
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post If you have a kindergartner in your family, this weekend is your window. Venetucci Farm's annual free-pumpkin tradition continues this Saturday and Sunday (October 24–25), open 9:30 AM–4 PM. The farm is at 5210 S US Highway 85, just south of town. Every kindergartner gets a free pumpkin to take home — a tradition that's been happening in Colorado Springs for over 70 years. There's something genuinely special about watching a 5-year-old pick out their own pumpkin from a real farm. General admission is open to all families, not just kindergartners. It's a lovely fall outing even if your kids are older. After this weekend, the final day is October 31 before the season wraps up. Have you taken your kids to Venetucci Farm? It's one of those COS traditions that feels like fall. --- ### Image Post Caption Venetucci Farm gives free pumpkins to kindergartners this weekend — Saturday and Sunday, Oct. 24–25, 9:30am–4pm at 5210 S US Hwy 85.
