CPSC Recalls Uuoeebb Infant Walkers Sold on Amazon — Fall and Entrapment Hazards

By Sarah Morales | September 13, 2026 | COS Parenting

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission has issued a recall for Uuoeebb Infant Walkers sold on Amazon by the seller BaoD. The walkers have been found to violate the mandatory federal safety standard for infant walkers and pose a risk of serious injury or death from fall and entrapment hazards.

If you purchased one of these walkers, stop using it right away and check cpsc.gov/Recalls for the full recall details and available remedy.

The Ongoing Issue with Infant Walkers

Infant walkers have been a safety concern for decades. The federal government has required all infant walkers sold in the U.S. to meet a mandatory safety standard since 2010. Despite that standard, walkers that fail to meet it — often sold through online marketplaces by third-party sellers — continue to surface in CPSC recalls.

The primary hazards are consistent: walkers can tip over on uneven surfaces or near stairs, and their design can allow infants to move quickly into dangerous areas before a caregiver can intervene. The entrapment risk specifically relates to how the walker's frame interacts with a child's body or with household items.

The American Academy of Pediatrics advises against using infant walkers entirely, noting that they do not actually help children learn to walk and carry significant injury risks. If you're looking for alternatives that support infant movement and development, stationary activity centers (sometimes called "exersaucers") do not carry the same mobility risk.

Checking Your Amazon Purchase History

If you're not sure whether you purchased the recalled model, check your Amazon order history. The seller BaoD and the brand name Uuoeebb should help identify the product. The full recall listing at cpsc.gov/Recalls will include any identifying details such as model numbers or product photos to help confirm whether your walker is affected.

What's Next

Visit cpsc.gov/Recalls for the complete official recall notice, including remedy instructions and contact information for the recall. If you purchased the walker on Amazon, Amazon's recall portal may also provide options for a refund or replacement. Colorado Springs parents should also share this information with other families — infant walkers purchased as gifts or hand-me-downs may be in circulation without the original buyer being aware of the recall.
