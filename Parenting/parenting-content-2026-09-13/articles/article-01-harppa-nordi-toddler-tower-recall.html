CPSC Recalls Harppa Nordi Toddler Tower Stools Over Entrapment and Fall Hazards

By Sarah Morales | September 13, 2026 | COS Parenting

If you have a Harppa Nordi Foldable Toddler Tower Stool in your home, stop using it now. The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission has recalled 47,166 of these stools due to entrapment and fall hazards that pose a risk of serious injury or death to young children.

The recalled model — HANS0002 — can collapse or tip over while in use. More critically, a child's torso can fit through the openings on the front and back sides of the stool, creating an entrapment risk. CPSC has received three reports of the stools collapsing or tipping, resulting in two injuries including scrapes, cuts, and bruises.

How to Identify the Recalled Stool

The recalled stools are white, black, and blue with natural wood color on the step, platform, and safety bars. They measure approximately 18 inches deep, 18 inches wide, and 38.6 inches tall. The model number HANS0002 is printed on a label on the underside of the stool's platform. If you see that number, your stool is part of this recall.

What to Do

Stop using the stool immediately and keep it away from children until you have the repair kit installed. HARPPA is providing a free remedy that includes protective nets, stabilizing feet, and installation instructions. Shipping is also free.

You can request the repair kit three ways:

  • Call 833-471-9321, Monday through Friday, noon to 8 PM ET
  • Email recall@harppababy.com
  • Visit the recall page at harppababy.com

Full official recall details are also available at cpsc.gov/Recalls.

What's Next

If you purchased a Harppa Nordi stool on Amazon or directly from Harppababy.com, check the model number now. This recall affects stools sold over nearly three years, so it may be in the hands of families who no longer have the original packaging or receipt.
