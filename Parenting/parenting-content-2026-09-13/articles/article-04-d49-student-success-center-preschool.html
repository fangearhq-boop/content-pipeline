D49 Opens Two New Preschool Classrooms at Student Success Center for 2026–27

By Jamie Rivera | September 13, 2026 | COS Parenting

Falcon School District 49 started this school year with two new preschool classrooms open at its Student Success Center — an expansion that reflects growing demand for early childhood programming in Colorado Springs' fastest-growing school district.

The new classrooms are part of D49's broader investment in early learning, and they join existing programs at the Student Success Center, which has served as a hub for specialized and early education services in the district. D49 students returned to school on August 3, the earliest start date among the three major COS school districts.

Why It Matters for COS Families

Access to quality preschool in Colorado Springs has been a recurring topic for families in the eastern part of the city, where D49 serves a large and growing population. Adding two classrooms may seem incremental, but it represents real additional capacity for families who want to enroll their youngest children in a structured early learning environment before kindergarten.

Research consistently shows that early childhood education — particularly in the preschool years — has lasting effects on academic readiness, social development, and school success. For Colorado Springs families, having more options closer to home matters.

About the Student Success Center

The Student Success Center is D49's facility dedicated to specialized and early learning programs. It serves students who benefit from intensive or individualized support, as well as the district's youngest learners beginning their educational journey. The center opened at the start of the 2026–27 school year, and the two new preschool classrooms are among its newest additions.

D49 serves families in the eastern Colorado Springs area, including parts of Falcon, Peyton, and Calhan. It has been one of the faster-growing districts in El Paso County over the past decade, and its student population includes a significant number of military families from nearby installations.

What About D11 and D20?

Colorado Springs families in other parts of the city should check with their own district for early childhood enrollment information. D11 started its school year on August 12, and D20 began August 17. Each district has its own preschool program structure and enrollment process, and availability can vary by school and location.

For any district, September is often a good time to begin conversations about preschool enrollment for the following school year, as spots in specialized or subsidized programs can fill quickly.

What's Next

Families interested in D49's preschool programs or the Student Success Center can find current information at d49.org. The district's enrollment office can direct you to the right contact for early childhood program availability. For D11 and D20 preschool information, visit d11.org and asd20.org respectively.
