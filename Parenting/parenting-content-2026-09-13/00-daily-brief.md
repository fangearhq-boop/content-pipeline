### STORY 1: CPSC Recall — Harppa Nordi Foldable Toddler Tower Stools (Model HANS0002)

**Tier:** 1
**Type:** NEW
**Pillar:** National Parenting (CPSC Safety)
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Platforms:** X, FB, Article

CPSC has recalled 47,166 Harppa Nordi Foldable Toddler Tower Stools (Model HANS0002) due to entrapment and fall hazards. The stools can collapse or tip over while in use, and a child's torso can fit through the openings on the front and back sides. Three incidents have been reported, resulting in two injuries (scrapes, cuts, bruises). The stools were sold at Harppababy.com and Amazon.com from September 2023 through June 2026 for approximately $130. HARPPA is offering a free repair kit (protective nets, stabilizing feet) including shipping. Contact: 833-471-9321 (noon–8 PM ET Mon–Fri), recall@harppababy.com, or harppababy.com.

Sources: CPSC.gov, harppababy.com, medicaldaily.com, karmactive.com

### STORY 3: AAP 2026–27 Flu Vaccine Recommendations — All Children 6 Months and Older

**Tier:** 1
**Type:** NEW
**Pillar:** National Parenting (AAP Health)
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Platforms:** X, FB, Article

The American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) released updated flu vaccine recommendations for the 2026–27 season. The AAP urges annual flu vaccination for all children aged 6 months and older. Vaccines should ideally be received by the end of October. Children 6 months through 8 years who are receiving flu vaccine for the first time — or who received only one dose ever before July 1, 2026 — need two doses at least 4 weeks apart. Important update: injectable flu vaccines in 2026–27 carry updated prescribing information noting an increased risk of febrile seizures in children under 5 (estimated one per 22,624–41,170 doses). The AAP says the benefits of vaccination significantly outweigh this risk.

Sources: healthychildren.org, AAP Publications (Pediatrics journal), cppdocs.org, CIDRAP

### STORY 5: CPSC Recall — Uuoeebb Infant Walkers (Amazon/BaoD) — Fall and Entrapment Hazard

**Tier:** 2
**Type:** NEW
**Pillar:** National Parenting (CPSC Safety)
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Platforms:** X, FB, Article

CPSC has recalled Uuoeebb Infant Walkers sold on Amazon by BaoD. The walkers violate the mandatory federal safety standard for infant walkers and pose a risk of serious injury or death from fall and entrapment hazards. Parents should stop using the product immediately. Full details and remedy information are available at cpsc.gov/Recalls.

Sources: CPSC.gov/Recalls (Uuoeebb Infant Walkers recalled), foxbusiness.com
