## Key Facts Verified

### Story 1: Harppa Nordi Recall
- **Model:** HANS0002
- **Units recalled:** 47,166
- **Hazard:** Collapse/tip over; child's torso can fit through front/back openings (entrapment + fall)
- **Incidents:** 3 reported; 2 injuries (scrapes, cuts, bruises)
- **Price at sale:** ~$130
- **Sold:** Harppababy.com + Amazon.com, September 2023–June 2026
- **Colors/specs:** White, black and blue with natural wood; 18"D x 18"W x 38.6"T; label under platform reads "MODEL No.: HANS0002"
- **Remedy:** Free repair kit (protective nets, stabilizing feet + instructions), free shipping
- **Contact:** 833-471-9321 (noon–8 PM ET Mon–Fri); recall@harppababy.com; harppababy.com
- **Source confidence:** HIGH (CPSC.gov primary source confirmed via multiple secondaries)

### Story 3: AAP Flu 2026–27
- **Recommendation:** Annual flu vaccine for ALL children 6 months and older
- **Timing:** By end of October ideally
- **Two-dose note:** Children 6 months–8 years, first time OR only 1 dose ever before July 1, 2026 → need 2 doses, at least 4 weeks apart
- **New safety update:** Injectable flu vaccines 2026–27 — updated prescribing info — increased risk of febrile seizures in children under 5; estimated 1 per 41,170 doses (one study) and 1 per 22,624 doses (second study)
- **AAP position:** Benefits of vaccination significantly outweigh febrile seizure risk
- **Source confidence:** HIGH (AAP primary source via CIDRAP, healthychildren.org, Pediatrics journal)
