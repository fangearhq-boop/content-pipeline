### STORY 5: AAP + Apple Family Media Plan — iOS 27 Back-to-School [TIER 2]
**Headline:** Apple Built the Pediatrician's Advice Into Your Kid's iPhone. Here's What Changes This Fall.
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Who/What:** Apple and the American Academy of Pediatrics announced at WWDC on June 8, 2026 that Apple is adapting the AAP Family Media Plan into iOS 27 device settings (iOS 27/iPadOS 27/macOS 27, releasing fall 2026). New parental features: "Ask to Browse" (child cannot access any Safari site until parent approves), mandatory Child Account for under-13, Time Allowances by app category, Contact Approval (new contacts require parent sign-off), Expanded Communication Safety (detects gore and violence, not just nudity). AAP Family Media Plan tool still free at HealthyChildren.org/MediaUsePlan. Back-to-school is the AAP's recommended moment to reset family media agreements.
**Key facts:** WWDC June 8, 2026; iOS 27 fall 2026; Ask to Browse; Child Account required for under-13; Contact Approval; Time Allowances by category; Expanded Communication Safety; free AAP plan tool at HealthyChildren.org/MediaUsePlan; D11 first day Wed Aug 12 is the back-to-school hook
