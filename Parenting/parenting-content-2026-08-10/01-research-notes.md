**Recall announcement date:** August 6, 2026 (part of CPSC's 17-recall batch) — **HIGH** (CPSC.gov)

**A2batt EEMB Lithium-ion Coin Battery Chargers:**
- Units: Approximately 4,930 — **HIGH** (CPSC.gov)
- Product: EEMB Lithium-ion Coin Battery Chargers, model SKLC-0420-0040 (includes charger, 4 rechargeable CR2032 batteries, USB cable) — **HIGH** (CPSC.gov)
- Sold: Amazon.com, August 2024 – March 2026, approximately $14 — **HIGH** (CPSC.gov)
- Hazard: Coin batteries accessible to children; packaging does not meet child-resistant design standards and lacks required warnings under Reese's Law; swallowed coin batteries cause internal chemical burns and can be fatal — **HIGH** (CPSC.gov)
- Incidents/injuries: None reported — **HIGH** (CPSC.gov)
- Remedy: Full refund; write "RECALL" on the charger; contact A2batt for return instructions — **HIGH** (CPSC.gov)
- CPSC URL: cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/A2batt-Recalls-EEMB-Lithium-Coin-Battery-Chargers — **HIGH**

**KC Imports and Exports Mini Laser Pointer Keychains:**
- Units: Approximately 9,600 — **HIGH** (CPSC.gov)
- Product: Laser & LED Light Mini Laser Pointer Keychains, model KKC-6071 — **HIGH** (CPSC.gov)
- Sold: Gas stations, discount stores nationwide, and online at divaatheartsj.com, June 2025 – June 2026, approximately $0.42 per unit — **HIGH** (CPSC.gov)
- Hazard: Button battery accessible to children; packaging lacks required Reese's Law warnings; ingested button batteries cause internal burns — **HIGH** (CPSC.gov)
- Incidents/injuries: None reported — **HIGH** (CPSC.gov)

**Reese's Law context:** Reese's Law (enacted 2022) requires child-resistant packaging and specific warnings on consumer products containing button/coin batteries after multiple child deaths — **HIGH** (CPSC.gov)
