## STORY 1: Walmart Mainstays 9-Drawer Fabric Dresser Recall

**Source:** CPSC.gov (official recall pages, confirmed HIGH)
- Product: Mainstays 9-Drawer Fabric Dresser (black metal frame, nine brown or black fabric drawers with handles)
- Dimensions: ~40 inches long, 13.75 inches wide, 45 inches tall, 32 pounds
- Price: approximately $80
- Sold: Walmart stores nationwide and online at Walmart.com, September 2023–March 2026 (HIGH)
- Original recall: approximately 165,250 units, May 2026 (HIGH)
- Reannouncement: Walmart distributed recalled dressers post-recall through liquidators (HIGH — KIRO 7 News)
- Hazard: tip-over and entrapment hazard when not anchored to wall; violates STURDY Act mandatory standard (HIGH)
- Injury/Death risk: serious injury or death from tip-over crushing child (HIGH)
- Manufacturer: Hop Thang Interior Wood Co. Ltd., Vietnam (MEDIUM — single source)
- Remedy: full refund from Walmart; stop using if not anchored, place out of children's reach (HIGH)
- Contact: Walmart at 1-800-925-6278 or Walmart.com/recalls (MEDIUM — inferred from standard Walmart recall procedure)

## STORY 4: AAP 2026 Screen Time Guidelines

**Source:** EdSurge (Feb 2026 AAP update), AAP.org, Dr.Look AI, Jellies App (MEDIUM — reported but not directly from aap.org page)
- Updated guidance: February 2026 (MEDIUM — EdSurge reporting)
- Framework shift: away from hour-based limits, toward quality/context/conversation (HIGH — widely reported)
- No screens before 18 months (HIGH — longstanding AAP rule, confirmed 2026 update)
- Ages 2–5: up to 1 hour per day of high-quality content (HIGH)
- Older kids: no specific hourly limit but consistent family boundaries (MEDIUM)
- Core question AAP now asks: "What did this replace?" not "How many minutes?" (MEDIUM — EdSurge)
- Phones/tablets out of bedrooms at night = single most protective habit for sleep and mental health (HIGH — widely reported)
- Sleep recommendations: ages 6–13: 9–11 hrs/night; teens 14–17: 8–10 hrs/night (HIGH — AAP endorsed)
- Back-to-school timing: families often restart screen habits in September; good moment for a reset
