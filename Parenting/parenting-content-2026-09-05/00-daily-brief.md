### STORY 4: AAP 2026 Screen Time Guide: Stop Counting Minutes, Start Asking "What Did It Replace?" (TIER 2)
The American Academy of Pediatrics released updated screen time guidance in early 2026 that shifts away from hour-based limits toward a framework centered on quality, context, and what screens replace. Key rules: no screens before 18 months; one hour of high-quality content per day for ages 2–5; for older kids, no time limit but phones/tablets out of bedrooms at night. The single most protective screen habit for children's sleep and mental health? Devices out of the bedroom at bedtime.
