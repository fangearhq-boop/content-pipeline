The Labor Day Lift Off Balloon Glow Is Tonight — What Families Need to Know Before Heading to Memorial Park

By Jamie Rivera | September 5, 2026 | Local Events

The 50th annual Colorado Springs Labor Day Lift Off is officially underway, and tonight is one of the highlights of the entire weekend. Starting at dusk, all 65 hot air balloons at Memorial Park will ignite their burners at the same time — lighting up the park with color and fire in what's known as the Balloon Glow. Admission is free, parking starts at $5, and if you haven't been to a Balloon Glow before, this is one of those COS events that families tend to talk about for years.

What the Balloon Glow Actually Is

A Balloon Glow is exactly what it sounds like: tethered hot air balloons, inflated and anchored in place, illuminated from the inside by their propane burners. When 65 of them fire at once — or in a ripple, depending on how the evening is choreographed — the effect is something between a campfire and a light show, with massive fabric envelopes glowing orange, gold, and red against a darkening sky.

The Balloon Glow happens at dusk both Saturday and Sunday evenings, so families have two chances to see it this weekend. Tonight's version is the first of the two, and the park is typically less crowded Saturday evening than Sunday. If you can make tonight work, it's worth doing.

Logistics for Tonight

Here's what to know if you're heading out:

  • Admission: Free. No tickets needed to watch the Balloon Glow.
  • Parking: The East Lot is $5. Standard pre-ordered lots are $15. VIP and Aquatics parking is $20. Arrive earlier than you think you need to — parking fills up as dusk approaches.
  • Timing: The Balloon Glow is timed with sunset, which shifts slightly each evening. Arriving by 7 PM gives families a comfortable window to find a spot before the glow begins.
  • Tethered balloon rides: Rainbow Ryders is offering tethered rides for kids ages five and older. These are separate from the free admission and have limited availability.

The Rest of the Weekend

The Lift Off isn't just the Balloon Glow. Here's what else is happening this weekend that families should know about:

Morning ascensions launch at 7 AM on Saturday, Sunday, and Monday. If you want to be close to the launch area, arrive by 6:30 AM. The ascensions are weather-dependent and can be canceled, so check the official event channels the morning of.

Wings of Blue, the U.S. Air Force's parachute demonstration team out of the Air Force Academy, is scheduled to perform this weekend. If your kids haven't seen a skydiving demonstration before, it's a striking contrast to the balloons — quiet color versus fast-moving precision.

The Grizzly drone show is also on the schedule. Drone shows have become a regular feature at large Colorado Springs outdoor events over the past two years, and they're particularly good for younger kids who might not stay engaged through a long evening program.

The 2026 festival also introduced some new elements this year, including an Overlook Club with elevated seating and a north-field expansion that adds room for more attendees. Organizers brought in new special-shape balloons as part of the 50th anniversary programming.

Sunday morning also includes the Orangetheory 5K at Prospect Lake for families who want to run before the day's events kick off.

Tips for Families with Young Kids

Memorial Park is one of the better outdoor event venues in COS for families with young children — it's flat, stroller-accessible, and has enough open space that you don't feel hemmed in. That said, Saturday evening Balloon Glow crowds are large, and navigating the park in low light with small kids requires some planning.

Bring a blanket or camp chairs. The best Balloon Glow viewing is from open grass, not standing in a packed crowd. The park has permanent restroom facilities, though lines grow as the event gets going. Plan accordingly. If you're bringing kids under three, the noise from the propane burners is louder than expected — bring ear protection if your child is sensitive to sudden sounds.

What's Next

The Lift Off continues through Monday, Labor Day, with morning ascensions each day and the second Balloon Glow on Sunday evening. Morning ascension is the best time to see the balloons in flight, not just tethered — if your family is up for an early morning, Sunday at 6:30 AM is worth the alarm. The full event schedule is available through the official Labor Day Lift Off channels and Visit COS.
