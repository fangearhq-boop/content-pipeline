Walmart's Mainstays 9-Drawer Dresser Is Recalled — Again. Here's What Colorado Springs Families Need to Know

By Sarah Morales | September 5, 2026 | Safety

If you bought a Mainstays 9-drawer fabric dresser from Walmart between September 2023 and March 2026, stop using it now. The dresser has been recalled because it can tip over and trap or crush a child — and if you missed the original May 2026 recall, you're not alone. Walmart continued distributing these dressers through liquidators after the initial recall was issued, which means some families purchased them without knowing the product was already flagged as dangerous.

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) has stepped in to require the recall, citing a violation of the STURDY Act — the mandatory federal standard designed to prevent tip-over deaths in children's furniture. Tip-over tragedies are among the most preventable child fatalities, and the CPSC takes these violations seriously.

What the Dresser Looks Like

The recalled dresser has a black metal frame with nine fabric drawers in brown or black. It stands about 45 inches tall, is roughly 40 inches long and 13.75 inches wide, and weighs approximately 32 pounds. The dresser was sold at Walmart stores nationwide and on Walmart.com for around $80. The original recall in May 2026 covered approximately 165,250 units.

The manufacturer is Hop Thang Interior Wood Co. Ltd., based in Vietnam.

Why It's Dangerous

The hazard is straightforward: when not anchored to a wall, this dresser can tip forward. If a child climbs on a drawer, pulls on it, or simply leans against it, the whole unit can fall. At 32 pounds, that's enough weight to trap or seriously injure a toddler. The CPSC's mandatory STURDY Act standard requires furniture to resist tip-over under specified conditions — this dresser doesn't meet that standard.

The reissued warning adds urgency because the post-recall liquidator sales mean some families bought these dressers in good faith, with no idea a safety recall had already been issued. The dresser may not have come with any recall notice at the point of sale.

What to Do Right Now

If you have one of these dressers in your home, here's what the CPSC and Walmart are asking you to do:

  • Stop using the dresser immediately if it isn't secured to a wall with a tip restraint.
  • Move it somewhere children can't access it — out of a child's bedroom, a playroom, or any space where kids are regularly present.
  • Contact Walmart for a full refund. You can call 1-800-925-6278 or visit Walmart.com/recalls.

You don't need to have the original receipt. Walmart is issuing full refunds on recalled units.

A Broader Pattern Worth Knowing

This is the third furniture or product recall covered in COS Parenting over the past week — after a teether choking hazard on September 4 and a bath seat drowning risk on September 3. These aren't unrelated coincidences; the CPSC regularly identifies hazardous products and issues recalls, and September tends to be an active month as back-to-school routines put more eyes on household items.

Tip-over furniture deaths are documented and preventable. The CPSC estimates that a child dies from furniture tip-over every two weeks in the United States. Wall-anchoring hardware typically costs a few dollars and takes less than 30 minutes to install. If you have any tall, freestanding furniture in your home — not just this dresser — this is a good moment to check whether it's secured.

The STURDY Act, which became law in 2022, set mandatory stability requirements for clothing storage units. This recall is one of the first high-profile enforcement actions tied specifically to that standard.

What's Next

If you're unsure whether your furniture meets tip-over standards, the CPSC's website (cpsc.gov) has guidance on wall anchoring and a searchable recall database. Colorado Springs families can also call Walmart's dedicated recall line at 1-800-925-6278 to confirm refund eligibility and get instructions on how to return or dispose of the dresser. Don't wait — if the dresser is in a child's room right now, move it today.
