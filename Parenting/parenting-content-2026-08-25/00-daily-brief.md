### STORY 3: AAP 2026 Screen Time Guidelines: The 5 C's Framework Is Here — TIER 2
**Angle:** In February 2026, the American Academy of Pediatrics officially retired the two-hour-per-day screen time limit for children ages 2 and up. The new framework centers on five C's: Child, Content, Calm, Crowding Out, and Communication. Key rules still in place: no screens before 18 months; no more than one hour of high-quality content per day for ages 2–5. A March 2026 study found that solitary screen time can cause harm in children with developmental vulnerabilities at just 10–30 minutes per day — co-viewing with a caregiver changes the outcome significantly.
