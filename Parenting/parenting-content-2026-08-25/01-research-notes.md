## Story 2: CPSC Recalls — Baby Loungers and Infant Walkers

**CooCooBaby Baby Loungers (HIGH):**
- Recall date: June 18, 2026
- Source: CPSC.gov/Recalls/2026
- Products: Classic and Deluxe Baby Loungers
- Units: approximately 2,355
- Sale period: December 2024 – March 2026
- Price range: $35–$70
- Hazards: (1) sides shorter than minimum height limit → infant could fall out; (2) sleeping pad thickness exceeds maximum limit → suffocation risk; (3) infant can fall through enclosed opening at foot of lounger
- No reported injuries at time of recall
- Action: Stop use immediately; destroy by cutting up sides + sleeping pad; email photo to support@coocoobabyofficial.com for full refund

**Uuoeebb Infant Walkers (HIGH):**
- Recall date: December 11, 2025 (CPSC recall)
- Source: CPSC.gov/Recalls/2026 (also listed as 2026 CPSC recall page); cpsc.gov Warnings page
- Product: Collapsible round-base walker, 8 wheels, 7 adjustable height settings; colors: gray, black, pink
- Units: approximately 2,650
- Sold by: BaoD on Amazon
- Sale period: December 2024 – September 2025
- Price range: $60–$90
- Tracking label: "Production Batch: 7654" under seat
- Hazards: (1) fits through standard doorway → can fall down stairs; (2) fails to stop at step edge; (3) leg openings allow child to slip through → head entrapment
- No injuries reported
- Action: Stop use immediately; contact BaoD for full refund

## Story 3: AAP 2026 Screen Time Guidelines

**Key facts (HIGH):**
- Update date: February 2026
- Change: AAP officially retired the two-hour-per-day screen time limit for children ages 2+
- New framework: 5 C's — Child, Content, Calm, Crowding Out, Communication
- Still in effect: No screens before 18 months; no more than 1 hour high-quality content/day for ages 2–5
- Family Media Plan: consistent, family-agreed limits for older children
- March 2026 study: solitary screen time harmful for children with developmental vulnerabilities at just 10–30 minutes/day; co-viewing significantly changes outcome
- Colorado Springs note: applies to all families; El Paso County Resources available through EFCS (El Paso County Family Crisis Services) and pediatricians
