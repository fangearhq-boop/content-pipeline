### STORY 2: Primark 101 Dalmatians Recall Alert

#### Long-Form Post — 10:00 AM MT

```
Recall alert for Colorado Springs parents: if you purchased a Primark 101 Dalmatians coin purse for your child, stop use and return it now. ⚠️

The CPSC issued this recall on October 15. About 2,100 of the black faux leather coin purses — Model No. 2595301, sold at Primark stores for about $9 — were found to contain phthalate levels above the legal limit. Phthalates are chemicals that can harm children's development with sufficient exposure, particularly through mouthing small items or ingesting pieces of the material.

These purses were sold at Primark locations in the Northeast, Florida, Maryland, and Illinois from March through June 2026. If you purchased one, bring it back to any Primark store for a full refund in your original payment method. No injuries have been reported.

This is the same product that was recalled in the UK in September — the US recall follows the same model number. Full CPSC recall notice is at the link in bio.

Did you see this recall? Share so other families can check their bags.
```

### STORY 4: LandTale Bed Rail Recall

#### Long-Form Post — 5:00 PM MT

```
Recall notice for Colorado Springs parents: if you are using LandTale adult portable bed rails — especially to help keep young children safe in bed — stop use immediately. ⚠️

YunHeng recalled about 730 of these rails on October 1. The product (SKU EBR001) was sold on Amazon for about $43 from February through June 2026. The recall was issued because the rails pose an entrapment risk — a child or adult can become trapped within the rail or between the rail and the mattress — and they lack required hazard warning labels. The product also fails to meet the mandatory federal safety standard for this type of product.

To get a full refund, contact YunHeng at LandtaleBedRailsRecall@outlook.com. Proof of destruction — a photo of the damaged product — is required before the refund is processed. No injuries have been reported.

The full CPSC recall notice (October 1, 2026) is at the link in bio.

Are you using bed rails at home? Worth double-checking the brand and model against the CPSC recall database.
```

### STORY 5: Halloween Safety Tips

#### Long-Form Post — 8:00 PM MT

```
Halloween is two weeks from tomorrow, and it's the right time to think through your family's trick-or-treat game plan before the night sneaks up on you. 🎃

A few things worth doing now: Check that costumes don't have trailing fabric or shoes that don't fit for a long night of walking. If there's a mask involved, test whether your child can actually see clearly in it — consider face paint as an alternative that doesn't block peripheral vision. For visibility, glow sticks and reflective tape are both more reliable than glow-in-the-dark fabric, especially on darker streets.

On the night itself, stick to familiar well-lit neighborhoods, set a clear meeting point if older kids are going with friends, and inspect all candy before anyone eats it — especially anything unwrapped or in packaging that looks unusual. These aren't new tips, but they're worth running through with your kids before Oct 31. 🕯️

If your family is planning on Boo at the Zoo or a trunk-or-treat this year, check venue rules in advance — most require advance tickets.
```
