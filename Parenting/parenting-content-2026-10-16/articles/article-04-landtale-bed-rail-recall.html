LandTale Bed Rail Recall: Entrapment and Asphyxiation Risk Prompts CPSC Action

By Jamie Rivera | October 16, 2026 | National Parenting

YunHeng recalled about 730 LandTale adult portable bed rails on October 1 due to entrapment and asphyxiation risks. The product fails to meet the mandatory federal safety standard for bed rails and lacks required hazard warning labels. For families using these rails to help keep young children safe in bed, the recall action is clear: stop use immediately.

Recall at a Glance

Detail Information
Recall date October 1, 2026
Recalling company YunHeng
Product LandTale adult portable bed rails
SKU EBR001
Units recalled Approximately 730
Approximate retail price $43
Sale period February 2026 through June 2026
Sold at Amazon
Hazard Entrapment within the rail or between the rail and mattress; asphyxiation risk; violates mandatory federal safety standard; lacks required hazard warning labels
Remedy Stop use immediately; contact YunHeng for full refund (photo proof of product destruction required)
Contact LandtaleBedRailsRecall@outlook.com
Injuries reported None

Understanding the Risk

The CPSC recall identifies two hazards with these bed rails: entrapment within the rail itself, and entrapment in the gap between the rail and the mattress. Both scenarios present a suffocation risk, particularly for young children or older adults who may not be able to free themselves. The product also fails to meet the federal safety standard for portable bed rails (16 CFR Part 1224) and was sold without the required hazard warning labels that alert users to these risks.

Portable bed rails marketed as "adult" products are sometimes used in family households to help keep young children from rolling out of bed. If you purchased this product for that purpose, this recall applies and the remedy is the same: stop use and contact YunHeng for a refund.

How to Get Your Refund

To receive a full refund, contact YunHeng at LandtaleBedRailsRecall@outlook.com. A photo showing the destroyed product is required before the refund is processed. The CPSC's recommendation is to stop use immediately and keep the product out of service — not to continue using it while waiting for the refund to process.

The full CPSC recall notice is available at cpsc.gov. The recall covers only SKU EBR001 sold between February and June 2026 on Amazon.

What's Next

If you need a replacement product for keeping a child safe in bed, look for bed rails that explicitly state CPSC compliance and display the required hazard warning language on the packaging. For a complete list of recalled children's and household products, Colorado Springs parents can search the CPSC recall database at cpsc.gov/Recalls by product type or date. If you have additional questions about this recall, contact YunHeng at LandtaleBedRailsRecall@outlook.com.
