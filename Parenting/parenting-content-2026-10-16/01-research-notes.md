## STORY 2: Primark 101 Dalmatians Coin Purse Recall

**Verified Facts (HIGH confidence):**
- Recall date: October 15, 2026 (CPSC)
- Recall number: 27-028 (CPSC)
- Units recalled: approximately 2,100
- Product: Primark 101 Dalmatians faux leather children's coin purses, Model No. 2595301
- Price: approximately $9
- Sale period: March 2026 through June 2026
- Sale locations: Primark stores in the Northeast and in Florida, Maryland, and Illinois
- Hazard: Contains phthalate concentrations above permitted levels; phthalates can be harmful to young children with sufficient exposure
- Remedy: Return to any Primark store for full refund in original form of payment
- UK recall: September 4, 2026 (same model number)
- Injuries reported: None

**Sources:** cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Primark-101-Dalmatians..., easternherald.com, britbrief.co.uk

## STORY 4: LandTale Bed Rail Recall

**Verified Facts (HIGH confidence):**
- Recall date: October 1, 2026 (CPSC)
- Company: YunHeng
- Product: LandTale adult portable bed rails, SKU EBR001
- Units recalled: approximately 730
- Price: approximately $43
- Sale period: February 2026 through June 2026
- Sale locations: Amazon
- Hazard: Entrapment within rail or between rail and mattress; asphyxiation risk; violates mandatory federal standard; lacks required hazard warning labels
- Remedy: Stop use immediately; contact YunHeng at LandtaleBedRailsRecall@outlook.com for full refund; must destroy product and send photo as proof
- Injuries reported: None

**Sources:** cpsc.gov, threeheartshome.com
