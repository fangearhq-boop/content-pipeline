### STORY 2: Recall Alert: Primark's 101 Dalmatians Coin Purses Contain Toxic Chemicals
**Tier:** 1
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Status:** NEW (CPSC recall Oct 15, 2026)

The CPSC recalled about 2,100 Primark 101 Dalmatians faux leather children's coin purses on October 15 after testing found phthalate levels above legal limits. Phthalates can harm children's development when they're exposed to sufficient amounts — especially through mouthing or ingesting small pieces. The black purses (Model No. 2595301, ~$9) were sold at Primark stores in the Northeast, Florida, Maryland, and Illinois from March–June 2026. Full refund at Primark stores. UK recalled the same item in September.

Sources: cpsc.gov, EasternHerald.com, BritBrief.co.uk

### STORY 4: Bed Rail Recall: LandTale Adult Portable Bed Rails Pose Entrapment Risk
**Tier:** 2
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Status:** NEW (CPSC recall Oct 1, 2026)

YunHeng recalled about 730 LandTale adult portable bed rails on October 1 due to entrapment and asphyxiation risks. The rails — sold on Amazon for about $43 from February–June 2026 — don't meet federal standards and lack required hazard warning labels. For families using bed rails to keep young children safe at night, this recall is important: stop use immediately and contact LandtaleBedRailsRecall@outlook.com for a full refund (photo proof of destruction required).

Sources: cpsc.gov, threeheartshome.com

### STORY 5: Halloween Safety Tips for Colorado Springs Families
**Tier:** 2
**Pillar:** Evergreen
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Status:** NEW (Timely — Halloween Oct 31, 2 weeks away)

With Halloween two weeks out, now's the time for COS parents to review trick-or-treat safety. Key tips: glow sticks and reflective tape on costumes (not just face paint), stick to familiar well-lit neighborhoods, inspect all candy before kids eat it, avoid masks that limit vision (try face paint instead), and set a curfew. Costume safety: no trailing fabric, shoes that fit for walking, and flame-resistant materials. If your kids are going to Boo at the Zoo or a trunk-or-treat, review venue rules in advance.

Sources: AAP.org (trick-or-treat safety), CPSC.gov (Halloween safety), cmzoo.org
