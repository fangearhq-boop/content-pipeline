Things to Do with Kids in Colorado Springs This Weekend (September 11–13)

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | September 10, 2026

Family Events | Colorado Springs | Weekend | Things to Do | Kids

September is settling in, the back-to-school rush is officially over, and this weekend is an excellent one to get outside with your family. The weather in Colorado Springs this time of year tends to hold — sunny days, cooler mornings, and that fall light that makes everything look a little warmer than it actually is. Here's what's worth putting on your calendar for September 11 through 13.

Saturday: Dahlia Days at Gather Mountain Blooms

Dahlia Days at Gather Mountain Blooms is one of those COS-area experiences that flies under the radar and deserves a lot more attention. The farm at 5210 S. US-85/87 opens its dahlia fields in late summer and early fall, and this Saturday — September 12 — is a strong time to catch them. Late-season dahlias come in colors that look almost digital: deep purples, burnt oranges, hot pinks, and clean whites, often all on the same stem.

Admission is $10 per person. You can cut your own blooms, wander the rows, and take as many photos as you want. It's the kind of afternoon that's low-stress for parents and genuinely engaging for kids, especially those in the 5-and-up range who can walk the rows and pick what catches their eye.

Gather Mountain Blooms also has additional events later in the fall — Oktoberfest runs October 3-4 if you want to come back for round two.

Sunday: Pikes Peak Youth Fishing Derby at Crystal Creek Reservoir

If you have a kid between ages 6 and 15, Sunday is a great morning to head out to Crystal Creek Reservoir for the Pikes Peak Youth Fishing Derby. The event is free, the setting is beautiful, and it's exactly the kind of outdoor experience that sits in kids' memories well after the weekend is over.

Crystal Creek Reservoir is southwest of Colorado Springs, at the base of Pikes Peak — the views alone are worth the drive. Bring your own equipment if you have it, but check with event organizers about gear lending if you're newer to fishing. Kids don't need to catch anything for the morning to feel like a win.

Free Options All Weekend

The free things to do this weekend in Colorado Springs are plentiful this time of year, and several are as good as any ticketed event on the list.

Monument Valley Park is at its most pleasant in September — cooler than July, but still green, with the fountain running and plenty of room to run. Garden of the Gods remains free, and the visitor center is worth a stop. The Pikes Peak Greenway Trail is a good option if your family has bikes, running strollers, or just wants a flat, easy walk. For younger kids, the Pikes Peak Library District branches have weekend programming and drop-in story times — check the library calendar for your closest location.
