## Story 1: CPSC Blossom Loungewear Recall

- **Brand:** Blossom (sold as Blossom OCC) [HIGH]
- **Hazard:** Violates mandatory federal flammability standard for children's sleepwear; risk of burn injury or death [HIGH]
- **Products:** Two-piece loungewear sets (short-sleeved or long-sleeved button-up shirt with matching shorts or pants) [HIGH]
- **Sizes affected:** 12M through 12Y [HIGH]
- **Designs (short-sleeved):** White, navy, light blue, light pink, lilac — fireworks, multi-stripes, tulips, sailboats [HIGH]
- **Designs (long-sleeved):** "Letter to Santa" and "Santa paws" holiday designs [HIGH]
- **Consumer action:** Stop use immediately; destroy by cutting garments in half; photograph destroyed items; email sales@blossomocc.com for full refund or store credit [HIGH]
- **Source:** cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/Blossom-Recalls-Childrens-Loungewear [HIGH]
- **Additional recall (companion):** Bellabu Bear bamboo sherpa robes (burn hazard, Saks Fifth Avenue and online, ~$60, sizes S/M and M/L) — cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/Bellabu-Bear-Recalls-Childrens-Robes [HIGH]
- **Date window:** 2026 recall confirmed; exact announcement date Sep 25-30 [MEDIUM]
