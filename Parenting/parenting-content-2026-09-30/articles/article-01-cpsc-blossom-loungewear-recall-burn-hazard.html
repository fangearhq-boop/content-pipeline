CPSC Recalls Blossom Children's Loungewear — Burn Hazard, Sizes 12M to 12Y

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | September 30, 2026

Colorado Springs parents with young children should check their kids' pajama drawers. The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission has recalled Blossom OCC two-piece children's loungewear sets because the garments violate mandatory federal flammability standards for children's sleepwear, posing a serious risk of burn injury or death.

The recall covers a wide size range — 12 months through size 12 years — meaning it affects families with infants, toddlers, school-age children, and tweens.

Which Products Are Affected

Two styles of Blossom loungewear sets are included in the recall:

Short-sleeved sets in five colors — white, navy, light blue, light pink, and lilac — featuring designs including fireworks, multi-stripes, tulips, and sailboats.

Long-sleeved sets including holiday designs: a "letter to Santa" pattern and a "Santa paws" holiday print. These holiday designs are worth flagging in particular, as some families may have already purchased them ahead of the holiday season or may find them in shopping carts right now if they haven't seen the recall notice.

All affected garments are sold as two-piece sets: a button-up shirt with matching shorts or pants.

What to Do

Quick Reference: Blossom Loungewear Recall Steps
Step Action
1 Stop use immediately — do not allow children to wear the recalled garments
2 Destroy the garments by cutting them in half
3 Photograph the destroyed items
4 Email the photo to sales@blossomocc.com for a full refund in your original payment form or store credit

Full details are available at cpsc.gov/Recalls.

A Second Recall to Know

A separate 2026 CPSC recall also involves children's sleepwear with the same type of flammability violation: Bellabu Bear bamboo sherpa children's robes in sizes S/M and M/L. Those robes were sold at Saks Fifth Avenue and online for approximately $60. Families who purchased Bellabu Bear robes should check cpsc.gov/Recalls for the specific recall instructions.

What's Next

If you have Blossom loungewear sets in your home, act now before these items are forgotten in a dresser drawer and worn again. The CPSC recall process is straightforward — destroy, photograph, and email for a refund. Share this information with other Colorado Springs families who may have purchased these products, particularly anyone shopping for children's holiday clothing this fall.
