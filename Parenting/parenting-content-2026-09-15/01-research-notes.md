### STORY 3: OKK Rainbow Mystery Squishy Bun Toys Recall

**Key Facts (all HIGH confidence):**
- Recall: OKK Trading Recalls Rainbow Mystery Squishy Bun Toys Due to Risk of Serious Injury or Death from Water Bead Ingestion; Violate Mandatory Standard for Toys — cpsc.gov
- Units: ~7,200 — CPSC.gov, Fox Business, News10
- Product: Rainbow Mystery Squishy Bun Toy, model D08004, translucent pink with glitter and water beads — CPSC.gov
- Violation: Water beads inside expand larger than permitted by mandatory toy safety standard — CPSC.gov, USCPSC X post
- Hazard: Ingestion, choking, intestinal obstruction; can cause severe discomfort, vomiting, dehydration, risk of death — CPSC.gov
- Sales: Various third-party stores, May through August 2026 — CPSC.gov
- Action: Stop use; write "RECALLED" on toy + container, throw away, email photo to recall@okktoys.com for full refund — CPSC.gov
- Injuries: None reported — CPSC.gov
