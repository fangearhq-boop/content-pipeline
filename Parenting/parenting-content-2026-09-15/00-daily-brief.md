### STORY 3: OKK Rainbow Mystery Squishy Bun Toys Recalled — Water Bead Ingestion Risk
- **Tier:** 1
- **Type:** NEW STORY
- **Pillar:** National Parenting
- **Angle:** OKK Trading is recalling about 7,200 Rainbow Mystery Squishy Bun Toys (model D08004, translucent pink with glitter and water beads) because water beads inside expand larger than permitted, posing ingestion, choking, and intestinal obstruction hazards. No injuries reported.

### STORY 4: Lontcs Busy Boards Recalled for Lead Paint Violations

- **Pillar:** National Parenting
- **Angle:** SQT LLC recalls Lontcs Busy Boards sold on Amazon (May 2024 – July 2026, ~$31) for violating the federal lead paint ban.
