### STORY 1: CMZoo Giraffe Center Opens TODAY

**FB Long-Form — 8:00 AM MT**

### STORY 2: SHEIN Spiral Toy Recall

**FB Long-Form — 10:00 AM MT**
If you've ordered a spiral toy from SHEIN in the past year, this is worth knowing about.

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission announced that SHEIN Distribution Corporation is recalling about 963 spiral toys because the small ball components pose a choking hazard to children under 3. The toy features three, five, or seven stacked colored platforms with yellow plastic balls topped with bear-shaped heads. Those balls violate the federal small ball ban for toys intended for children younger than 3 — meaning they're small enough to fully block a child's airway if swallowed.

The toys were sold exclusively on SHEIN.com from September 2025 through May 2026, priced between $6 and $16. This is SHEIN's fourth recall for small ball violations since August 2025.

No injuries have been reported. But if your family has this toy, stop using it now and contact SHEIN for a full refund. The recall page is on CPSC.gov under "SHEIN Distribution Corporation Recalls Spiral Toys."

Have you seen this toy before? Worth sharing with parents who order frequently from SHEIN.

### STORY 3: OKK Rainbow Mystery Squishy Bun Toys Recall

**FB Long-Form — 12:00 PM MT**
Water bead toys have been a subject of serious safety concern for several years now, and here's another reason for Colorado Springs parents to check what's in the toy bin.

OKK Trading is recalling about 7,200 Rainbow Mystery Squishy Bun Toys (model D08004) — translucent pink squishy toys that contain glitter and water beads. The recall was triggered because the water beads inside these toys expand to a size that exceeds the permitted limit under the mandatory toy safety standard. That's a significant problem: if a child swallows even one of these beads, it can expand inside the body, causing intestinal obstruction, severe discomfort, vomiting, dehydration, and in serious cases, a risk of death.

These toys were sold at various third-party stores nationwide from May through August 2026. If your family has one — or if you've seen it at a relative's home — here's what to do: write "RECALLED" in permanent marker on both the toy and its plastic container, throw it away, and email a photo to recall@okktoys.com to receive a full refund. No injuries have been reported.

Water bead toys in general are worth a close look in your household — the CPSC has flagged multiple products in this category over the past two years.

### STORY 4: Lontcs Busy Board Lead Paint Recall

**FB Long-Form — 5:00 PM MT**
One more product recall to keep on your radar this week.

SQT LLC is recalling Lontcs Busy Boards sold on Amazon because they violate the federal lead paint ban. The boards were available on Amazon from May 2024 through July 2026, priced around $31. Lead is a serious health hazard for young children — even low levels of exposure can cause developmental delays, learning difficulties, and behavioral problems. There are no safe levels of lead for children under 6.

If your family purchased a Lontcs Busy Board from Amazon, stop using it right away. Destroy the toy by unscrewing its components, write "RECALLED" in permanent marker on the board itself, take a photo of the destroyed product, and email it to Lontcs@163.com for a full refund.

The recall page is on CPSC.gov — search for "SQT Recalls Lontcs Busy Boards."

Are you using any Amazon-purchased busy boards or activity boards with young children? It's worth double-checking.
