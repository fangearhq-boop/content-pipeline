Free Family Day: Rocky Mountain PBS KIDS Fest Comes to Panorama Park September 19

By Sarah Morales | September 6, 2026

Rocky Mountain PBS is bringing a free family event to Panorama Park in Colorado Springs on Saturday, September 19, from 10 AM to 2 PM. No tickets, no cost — a full morning of PBS KIDS fun for families with young children.

Two weeks out is a good time to put something on the calendar, and Rocky Mountain PBS KIDS Fest is worth the reminder. The event is coming to Panorama Park in Colorado Springs on Saturday, September 19 — free to attend, running from 10 AM to 2 PM, and designed for families with young kids.

September has no shortage of events on the COS family calendar, but free events with a four-hour window in a well-maintained park tend to stand out. Panorama Park is a good fit for this kind of thing — accessible, flat enough for strollers and little legs, and centrally located without the parking chaos of some of the downtown venues.

What Is PBS KIDS Fest

Rocky Mountain PBS KIDS Fest is a community event organized by Rocky Mountain PBS to bring their kids programming to life. Events in this format typically feature activities connected to PBS KIDS characters and educational themes — crafts, interactive booths, live entertainment — in a family-friendly outdoor setting. For young kids who watch PBS KIDS programming, it's a chance to engage with characters they recognize in a way that goes beyond the screen.

Rocky Mountain PBS serves Colorado Springs through their local broadcast and streaming programming. The KIDS Fest format is their way of showing up in communities in person, and the Colorado Springs stop on their calendar is worth taking seriously.

Why It's Worth the Trip

Free events with a clear end time are genuinely useful for parents. The four-hour window — 10 AM to 2 PM — is long enough to make the trip feel worthwhile but short enough that you're not managing exhausted kids through an entire day. Panorama Park gives families room to spread out, and the outdoor setting means kids can decompress between activities without feeling cooped up.

The timing also works well for the September calendar. The Pikes Peak Regional Airshow is the following weekend (September 19-20), and PBS KIDS Fest lands right in the gap between Labor Day weekend and the airshow. It's a good midweek-to-weekend bridge for families trying to fill out September.
