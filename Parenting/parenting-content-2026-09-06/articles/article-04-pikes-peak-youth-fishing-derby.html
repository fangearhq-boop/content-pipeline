Pikes Peak Youth Fishing Derby September 12 — Ages 6 and Up at Crystal Reservoir

By Jamie Rivera | September 6, 2026

Pikes Peak – America's Mountain is hosting a Youth Fishing Derby on Saturday, September 12, from 8:30 AM to 11:00 AM at Crystal Reservoir. The event is open to kids ages 6 and up, and it's set against one of the most scenic backdrops in Colorado Springs.

Next Saturday, September 12, is the Pikes Peak Youth Fishing Derby at Crystal Reservoir — and it's the kind of event that tends to leave an impression on kids who haven't spent much time fishing before.

Crystal Reservoir sits at roughly 7,500 feet elevation on the Pikes Peak Highway, surrounded by granite rock formations, pine forest, and the mountain itself looming overhead. It's not a generic park pond. It's a genuine Colorado mountain setting, and doing something as tactile and active as fishing in that environment tends to make an impression on kids in a way that a trip to a regular park simply doesn't.

Event Details

The derby is hosted by Pikes Peak – America's Mountain, the authority that manages the mountain and its facilities. It's scheduled for 8:30 AM to 11:00 AM, which is a useful window — early enough that you're done before the mountain gets crowded with afternoon visitors, and short enough that attention spans don't max out.

The event is open to kids ages 6 and up. If you have younger children who want to participate, keep in mind that the age floor is there for good reason — fishing requires patience and a degree of motor coordination that tends to kick in around age 6. For older kids and teens, this is a low-stakes, structured activity with a clear purpose and a defined end.

Getting There

Crystal Reservoir is accessible via the Pikes Peak Highway. The highway has a toll, so factor that into your planning. The reservoir is one of the early stops on the highway — you won't need to drive to the summit to reach it, which makes this more accessible for families who aren't sure if younger kids are ready for a summit trip.

An 8:30 AM start means leaving Colorado Springs by 7:30 AM at the latest to account for the drive and the toll gate. Check the Pikes Peak Highway's website for toll rates and any road conditions before you leave, as mountain road status can shift quickly in September.

What's Next

September 12 is one week out. Check the Pikes Peak – America's Mountain website for any registration requirements, gear recommendations, or updates to the event format before next Saturday. The highway typically advises visitors to check road and weather conditions before heading up — mountain mornings in September can be cooler than expected at 7,500 feet, so pack an extra layer for the kids. If fishing gear is required and your family doesn't have rods, check whether the event provides equipment or whether rentals are available through the host organization before you plan your morning around it.
