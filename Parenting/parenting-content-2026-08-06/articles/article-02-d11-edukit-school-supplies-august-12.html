Colorado Springs District 11 families heading into the 2026-2027 school year can cross one thing off their August checklist: buying school supplies for K-8 students.

D11 is partnering with Edukit this year to provide free, grade-specific school supply kits for more than 14,000 K-8 students. The kits are delivered directly to classrooms and will be waiting for students when they arrive on the first day of school, Wednesday, August 12.

Parents do not need to order the kits, sign up for anything, or pick them up. The program handles all of it.

What the Kits Include

The Edukit kits are grade-specific, meaning each grade level receives supplies appropriate for that year's coursework. A kindergartner's kit will look different from a sixth-grader's. The exact contents vary by grade, but the program is designed to cover the standard supplies that appear on most back-to-school lists.

One Important Caveat

The Edukit supply program does not replace your child's teacher-issued supply list. Teachers frequently request specific items — particular brands of notebooks, specific colored folders, or materials unique to their classroom — that may not be included in the standard kit.

If your child's teacher has sent home or posted a supply list, review it. The Edukit kit should cover the basics, but the teacher's list is the authoritative guide for what to bring.

What's Next

D11 first day is August 12, 2026. If your child attends a D11 K-8 school, no supply shopping is required for basic materials — though checking the teacher's specific list before the first week is still recommended. For more information, visit d11.org.
