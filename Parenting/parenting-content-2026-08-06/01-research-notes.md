### STORY 4: GOPO Toys Teething Recall

**Verified Facts (HIGH confidence):**
- CPSC recall announced June 19, 2026 — CPSC Twitter / CPSC.gov
- Product: GOPO Toys Pull String Teething Toys
- Units recalled: approximately 70,000 — Fox Business, Yahoo News, CPSC.gov
- Hazard: silicone strings smaller and longer than permitted by mandatory toy safety standards — CPSC.gov
- Three reported incidents: strings reached back of child's throat, causing choking or respiratory distress — CPSC.gov
- Sold on Amazon.com from August 2023 through March 2026 — CPSC.gov
- Price: $11 to $15 — CPSC.gov
- Description: off-white disc with grey ball center, six pull-string "tentacles," seven push buttons — CPSC.gov
- Remedy: stop use, cut all silicone strings, write "DESTROYED," photograph, email to recalls@gopotoys.com for full refund — CPSC.gov

**Sources:** cpsc.gov, foxbusiness.com, yahoo.com/news, livenowfox.com
