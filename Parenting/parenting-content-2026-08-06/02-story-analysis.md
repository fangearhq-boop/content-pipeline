### STORY 1: D11 Bond Vote Approved — $775M to November Ballot

**Tier:** 1
**Type:** FOLLOW-UP
**News Hook:** The D11 board voted YES Wednesday night — $775M bond now goes to voters in November.
**Key Narrative:** This is the culmination of months of planning and the Aug 5 board meeting. The vote was expected but the outcome matters enormously for COS families. Buildings averaging 58 years old. $422M deferred maintenance. Now voters will decide whether to fund it. First day of school is one week away (Aug 12).
**Why This Matters:** Every D11 family is affected — from crumbling infrastructure to teacher pay. This is the most significant education finance vote COS has seen in years.
**Audience Angle:** Parent-forward: what does a YES vote mean for my child's school? What happens in November? What does "repairs at every school" actually look like?
**Content Direction:** Lead with the vote result. Pivot quickly to what it means for families. Include the key numbers ($775M, $6.50/mo per $100K, Aug 12 first day context). End with what's next: voter decision in November.

### STORY 4: GOPO Toys Teething Toy Recall

**Tier:** 2
**Type:** NEW STORY
**News Hook:** 70,000 GOPO teething toys pulled — three reports of silicone strings causing choking.
**Key Narrative:** The toy looks harmless — a soft silicone pull-string teether. The hazard is specific: the strings are too long and thin, allowing them to reach the back of a child's throat. This was on Amazon for years. Parents of infants and toddlers need to check.
**Why This Matters:** Product recalls for infant toys are the highest-stakes recalls in the parenting space. Three choking incidents make this urgent.
**Audience Angle:** Safety-first. Give parents exactly what they need to act: what it looks like, where it was sold, what to do.
**Content Direction:** Brief product description. Clear hazard explanation (no jargon). Three incidents. What to do step by step. Full refund available.
