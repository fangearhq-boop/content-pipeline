### STORY 3: Two High Chair Hazards — Bicystar Recalled, Babimoni Warning Issued by CPSC
- **Tier:** 1
- **Pillar:** National Parenting
- **Angle:** NEW STORY — Two high chair products present serious hazards to infants and toddlers. (1) Bicystar Children's High Chairs: CPSC Recall #26-290 — model TB-HC6900, approximately 150 units, sold exclusively on Amazon.com in September 2025; gray metal frame with cushioned seat cover; dual hazard: no attached crotch restraint (fall risk) and fatal head entrapment gap between seat and tray; stop use immediately; contact seller for full refund. Ltd., China) refused the CPSC's recall request; CPSC urges immediate stop use; no refund mechanism established.

- **Tier:** 2

- **Angle:** NEW STORY — On July 23, 2026, six governors co-signed a letter to the American Academy of Pediatrics urging the AAP to develop clinical guidance on early numeracy, modeled on the AAP's existing "literacy prescription" program that made reading a standard part of pediatric well-child visits. Colorado Gov. Jared Polis is among the six signatories, alongside governors of Rhode Island, Maryland, Connecticut, Delaware, and North Carolina. ### STORY 5: Burn Scar Flash Flood Risk — What COS Families Need to Know This Monsoon Season
- **Tier:** 2
- **Pillar:** Local News
- **Angle:** FOLLOW-UP (July 27 Story 4 — Aspen Acres Fire) — As monsoon season brings afternoon thunderstorms to southern Colorado, the Aspen Acres burn scar presents a new hazard for families near or south of Colorado Springs. Evacuations have been lifted from Wetmore, Beulah, Rye, San Isabel, and Colorado City, and the fire (101,921 acres, 61% contained, 9th largest CO fire on record) is decreasing in activity. But burned soil behaves like a waxy surface — it repels water rather than absorbing it. As little as 0.5 inches of rain per hour can trigger flash floods and debris flows in burn scar areas with little to no warning. CDOT has warned drivers of flash flooding, mudslides, and reduced visibility on state highways near burn scar zones. Families should avoid canyon roads and burn scar areas during afternoon storms, even when skies directly above appear clear.
