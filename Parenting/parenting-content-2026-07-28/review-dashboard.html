Gov. Polis Among Six Governors Asking AAP to Make Math Part of Well-Child Visits - **Tier:** 2 - **Angle:** NEW STORY — On July 23, 2026, six governors co-signed a letter to the American Academy of Pediatrics urging the AAP to develop clinical guidance on early numeracy, modeled on the AAP's existing "literacy prescription" program that made reading a standard part of pediatric well-child visits. Colorado Gov. Jared Polis is among the six signatories, alongside governors of Rhode Island, Maryland, Connecticut, Delaware, and North Carolina. Research shows math skills at kindergarten entry are strong predictors of long-term academic success.

Burn Scar Flash Flood Risk — What COS Families Need to Know This Monsoon Season
NOT VERIFIEDBRIEF
Burn Scar Flash Flood Risk — What COS Families Need to Know This Monsoon Season - **Tier:** 2 - **Angle:** FOLLOW-UP (July 27 Story 4 — Aspen Acres Fire) — As monsoon season brings afternoon thunderstorms to southern Colorado, the Aspen Acres burn scar presents a new hazard for families near or south of Colorado Springs. Evacuations have been lifted from Wetmore, Beulah, Rye, San Isabel, and Colorado City, and the fire (101,921 acres, 61% contained, 9th largest CO fire on record) is decreasing in activity. But burned soil behaves like a waxy surface — it repels water rather than absorbing it. As little as 0.5 inches of rain per hour can trigger flash floods and debris flows in burn scar areas with little to no warning. CDOT has warned drivers of flash flooding, mudslides, and reduced visibility on state highways near burn scar zones. Families should avoid canyon roads and burn scar areas during afternoon storms, even when skies directly above appear clear.
