### STORY 3: High Chair Hazards

**HIGH confidence facts — Bicystar:**
- Recall #: CPSC #26-290 (cpsc.gov confirmed URL)
- Brand: Bicystar Children's High Chairs
- Model: TB-HC6900 (printed on label stitched to back of seat cover)
- Units: approximately 150
- Sold: Amazon.com, September 2025 only (specific price not confirmed)
- Description: gray chair with metal frame, cushioned seat cover, tray
- Hazard 1: No attached crotch restraint → fall hazard
- Hazard 2: head entrapment gap between seat and tray → fatal entrapment hazard
- Consumer action: Stop use immediately; contact seller for refund
- Source: cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/Bicystar-High-Chairs-Recalled

**HIGH confidence facts — Babimoni:**
- Type: CPSC Safety Warning (NOT a traditional recall — manufacturer refused)
- Brand: Babimoni Multifunctional High Chairs
- Manufacturer: Ningbo Yibai Children Products Co. Ltd. (China)
- Model: YB-H1234
- Units: approximately 350
- Sold: Amazon.com, March 2024 – July 2024
- Hazards: tipping, unattached restraints, tray disengagement, failing locks, fatal head entrapment gap
- Consumer action: Stop use immediately; no refund available through standard channels (manufacturer refused recall)
- Source: cpsc.gov/Warnings/2025/CPSC-Warns-Consumers Babimoni

**Sources:** cpsc.gov (Bicystar recall URL, Babimoni warning URL), WBIW sweeping high chair recalls coverage July 28
