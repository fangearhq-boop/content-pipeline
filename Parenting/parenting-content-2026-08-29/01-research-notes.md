## STORY 1: Kmaier Infant Walker Recall

### Recall Details (HIGH confidence — official CPSC.gov source)
- **Recalled by:** Kmaier (importer) / sold via Target.com
- **Product:** Kmaier infant walkers (round base, 6 wheels, adjustable height 9 levels)
- **Colors:** Pink, green, white
- **Model:** RV001 (label under the seat)
- **Units:** approximately 243 walkers
- **Sold:** Target.com, June 2026
- **Price:** $62 to $70
- **Hazard:** Can pass through a standard doorway AND fails to stop at the edge of a step — deadly fall hazard
- **Violation:** Mandatory federal standard for infant walkers
- **Remedy:** Stop use immediately; disassemble; destroy by cutting fabric seat and writing "Recalled" on tray in permanent marker; send photo to TargetplusSJ@outlook.com for full refund

### Why It Matters
- Infant walkers are already a high-risk product category (AAP has long recommended against them)
- Fall-related injuries from walkers can be severe, including head injuries and drowning
- Target.com purchasers may not have seen the recall announcement

## STORY 2: Little Loves & Co. Youth Clothing Drawstring Recall

### Recall Details (HIGH confidence — CPSC)
- **Recalled by:** Little Loves & Co.
- **Product:** Youth clothing sets (specific styles with waist drawstrings)
- **Units:** approximately 590 garments
- **Sold:** Littlelovesandco.com and Whatnot.com
- **Dates:** May 2025 through July 2026
- **Price:** $15 to $30
- **Hazard:** Waist drawstrings can snag on objects (playground equipment, bus doors, etc.) and trap children — entrapment hazard
- **Remedy:** Stop using immediately; contact Little Loves & Co. for remedy information

### Context
- Federal safety standards ban drawstrings on children's upper outerwear (hoods/necks); waist drawstrings on children's garments are also regulated
- Online marketplace purchases (Whatnot.com) often don't have the same safety screening as major retailers
- This recall is distinct from: PIXLABBY (Aug 27), Goody King (Aug 26), ABC Trading/Gagaku (Aug 24), Vevor baby swing (Aug 28), Liizousuda paint thinner (Aug 28)

## STORY 3: HABA Rainbow Rattle Grasping and Teething Toy Recall

### Recall Details (HIGH confidence — official CPSC.gov source)
- **Recalled by:** Habermaass Corporation (HABA USA)
- **Product:** Rainbow Rattle Grasping and Teething Toy
- **Item number:** 2012261001, batch code 0925
- **Units:** approximately 2,000
- **Sold:** Amazon.com, Target.com, Bellalunatoys.com, Habausa.com, Nordstrom.com, and specialty toy stores nationwide
- **Dates:** December 2025 through July 2026
- **Price:** approximately $13
- **Hazard:** Glued knot on elastic cord can become untied, exposing small wooden and plastic pieces → choking and ingestion hazard
- **Reports:** 2 reports of knots becoming untied; no injuries
- **Announced:** August 27, 2026
- **Remedy:** Full refund — contact HABA USA at 800-468-6873 (Mon–Fri, 9am–4pm ET), recall@habausa.com, or habausa.com/recalls

### Why It Matters
- HABA is a premium wooden toy brand popular among parents seeking natural-material toys
- These toys are marketed to infants in the grasping/mouthing developmental stage — highest-risk age group for choking
- Sold through Amazon, Target, and Nordstrom — widely purchased as baby gifts
- Parents who received it as a gift may not be aware of the recall

### Distinction From Prior Coverage
- Not a walker recall (S1: Kmaier)
- Not a clothing/drawstring recall (S2: Little Loves)
- Not the prior teething toy recalls covered ~Aug 15, 2026 (CuddleCubs/Aojieni — silicone string toys, different hazard)
- HABA recall announced August 27, 2026 — new this week; not yet covered in pipeline

## STORY 5: D11 Free School Supplies — Edukit Program

### Program Details (HIGH confidence — multiple COS sources)
- **District:** Colorado Springs School District 11 (D11)
- **Partner:** Edukit
- **Beneficiaries:** More than 14,000 kindergarten through 8th-grade students
- **What:** Free, grade-specific school supply kits
- **Delivery:** Kits delivered to students on the first day of school (August 12, 2026)

### Why It Matters
- Eliminates the financial burden of back-to-school supply shopping for families
- Grade-specific kits ensure students have the right tools for their level
- School is now in its 3rd week; the program has already delivered supplies to students

### COS Context
- D11 serves a diverse, mixed-income urban population
- This program distinguishes D11's back-to-school approach from D20/D49
