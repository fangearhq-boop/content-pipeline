### STORY 1: Kmaier Infant Walker Recalled — Fall Hazard (CPSC)
**Tier:** 1 — Breaking/Trending (Safety Recall)
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Platforms:** X, Facebook, Article
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera

CPSC has recalled Kmaier-brand infant walkers (model RV001) due to a serious fall hazard. The walkers can pass through a standard doorway and fail to stop at the edge of a step, creating a deadly fall risk. About 243 walkers were sold on Target.com in June 2026 for $62 to $70. Remedy: stop use immediately, disassemble and destroy the walker, send photo to TargetplusSJ@outlook.com for a full refund.

### STORY 3: HABA Rainbow Rattle Grasping and Teething Toy Recalled — Choking Hazard (CPSC)
**Tier:** 2 — Today/Upcoming (Safety Recall)
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Platforms:** X, Facebook, Article
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera

CPSC announced on August 27, 2026 that HABA USA (Habermaass Corporation) is recalling its Rainbow Rattle Grasping and Teething Toy (item 2012261001, batch code 0925) due to a choking and ingestion hazard. The glued knot on the toy's elastic cord can come untied, exposing small wooden and plastic pieces to infants. About 2,000 units were sold at Amazon, Target, Nordstrom, and specialty toy stores nationwide from December 2025 through July 2026 for approximately $13. Remedy: stop use immediately, contact HABA USA at 800-468-6873, recall@habausa.com, or habausa.com/recalls for a full refund.
