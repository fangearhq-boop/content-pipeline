### STORY 4: AAP 2026 Screen Time Guidelines — What Actually Changed (Tier 2)
The American Academy of Pediatrics published a major policy update in January 2026, retiring the rigid two-hours-per-day rule for children ages 2 and up. New framework: the "5 C's" (Child, Content, Calm, Crowding Out, Communication). Under 18 months: no screens (except video chat). Ages 2-5: one hour of high-quality content. Ages 6+: family-agreed limits with a Family Media Plan. Quality and context now matter more than clock time.
