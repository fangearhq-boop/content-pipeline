D49 Opens Student Success Center: What It Means for Families With Special Education Needs

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting

August 28, 2026

District 49 has opened its new Student Success Center (SSC) for the 2026-27 school year — and for families navigating special education services, it's a change worth knowing about.

The SSC is housed at the site of the former Falcon Elementary School of Technology and brings together several support programs that were previously distributed across the district. The goal is clearer coordination: one location, one hub, and easier access for the students and families who need these services most.

What's at the Student Success Center

The SSC opens with a strong lineup of programs and staff:

  • Full-time preschool classrooms: Two classrooms run Monday through Thursday, open to families throughout the district (not limited to families in the Falcon attendance zone).
  • Elevates 18-21 Transition Services: The Elevates program serves students ages 18-21, providing transition services focused on independence, employment, and post-secondary options. Relocating this program to the SSC means students now receive services within the district rather than through outside community agencies.
  • Motor Team: Physical therapists, occupational therapists, and their assistants are based at the SSC, making it easier to coordinate with preschool and other programs in the building.
  • Assistive Technology Team: Students who rely on tech supports for communication, learning, or mobility can work with the AT team at the SSC.
  • SWAP Coordinator: The School to Workforce Alliance Program coordinator is also based here, supporting students moving toward employment.
  • Homebound Special Education Teacher: The SSC is home base for the teacher serving students who receive instruction at home due to medical or other qualifying circumstances.

Why This Matters for Families

Previously, D49 families working with multiple programs could find themselves moving between different schools and buildings for services. A unified center changes that dynamic. Coordination between the Motor Team, the AT Team, preschool teachers, and transition services becomes more natural when everyone is in the same building.

For families new to D49's special education system — or who have children who may eventually need these services — the SSC is a good place to start asking questions. The district's website at d49.org has information on enrollment, evaluations, and how to connect with the SSC team.
