### STORY 1: Skip Hop Elmo Teether Recall — Choking Hazard Warning (TIER 1)
**Status:** NEW STORY
**Pillar:** National Parenting
**Angle:** CPSC recall issued September 3, 2026 for Skip Hop Baby Sesame Street Elmo Silicone Teethers. About 22,660 units recalled in the U.S. The black eyes can detach from the teether if frozen, posing a serious choking hazard for infants. Sold at Carter's stores and online, and skiphop.com from January 2025 to March 2026, priced at approximately $12. Consumers should stop use immediately. Refund (gift card) available at skiphoprecall.com or call 800-692-4674.
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
