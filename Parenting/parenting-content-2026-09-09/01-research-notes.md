### STORY 1: Skip Hop Elmo Teether Recall

**Source 1:** CPSC.gov — "Skip Hop Recalls Baby Sesame Street Elmo Silicone Teethers Due to Risk of Serious Injury or Death from Choking Hazard"
- Recall date: September 3, 2026
- Units: About 22,660 in the U.S. (about 3,177 in Canada)
- Hazard: Black eyes can detach from the teether if frozen, posing choking hazard
- Description: Elmo's face shape (red/orange/white with black eyes), includes textured key (blue) and fabric crayon box
- Model number: 9R263210
- Sold at: Carter's stores and Carter's online shop; skiphop.com
- Sale period: January 2025 to March 2026
- Price: Approximately $12
- Incidents: Four consumer reports of eyes detaching; no injuries reported
- Remedy: Stop use. Contact Skip Hop for refund as gift card (usable at Carter's, OshKosh B'gosh, Little Planet, Skip Hop)
- Consumer contact: skiphoprecall.com | www.skiphop.com ("Product Recalls" footer) | 800-692-4674 (Mon–Fri, 9 AM–5:30 PM ET)
