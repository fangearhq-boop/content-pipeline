Your Colorado Springs December Holiday Events Guide for Families

By Sarah Morales | COS Parenting | November 28, 2026

Thanksgiving is officially behind us, which means Colorado Springs is about to shift into full holiday mode. December in COS is genuinely one of the best months of the year for families — the mountains are snowy, the city lights up, and there's a calendar full of events that have been running long enough that your parents probably took you to some of them.

Here's what's coming up. Some of these start this week, so get the calendar out.

Cheyenne Mountain Zoo Electric Safari (Opens December 2)

The Electric Safari at Cheyenne Mountain Zoo opens this Tuesday, December 2, and runs through January 1, 2027 — with one important exception: it's closed on December 24. General public entry begins at 5 p.m.; Zoo members can enter starting at 4 p.m.

Advance timed e-tickets are required, and the CPSC advises that popular nights do sell out. Electric Safari is free for Zoo members, but members still need to reserve their timed tickets. Rideshare tickets are available as a backup option and don't sell out.

The Electric Safari is one of the most popular holiday events in the region. If you're planning to go on a weekend, buy tickets early. Tickets at cmzoo.org/electric.

Christmas at Flying W Ranch (December 3–23)

Flying W Ranch runs its Christmas dinner show from December 3 through December 23. It's one of Colorado Springs' most beloved winter traditions — a western-style holiday evening with dinner and a show at the historic ranch at the base of Pikes Peak. Check the Flying W Ranch website for available showtimes and tickets, as evening slots fill quickly in December.

Festival of Lights Family Fun Day (December 5, Free)

The Colorado Springs Pioneers Museum hosts its annual Festival of Lights Family Fun Day on Saturday, December 5, from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. Admission is free. This event has been a COS holiday tradition for over 40 years — it's timed to kick off the same day as the Festival of Lights Parade in the evening, so it's easy to build a full holiday day around it.

The museum is at 215 S. Tejon Street in downtown Colorado Springs. No registration required. Details at cspm.org.

Festival of Lights Parade (December 5, 5:50 PM)

The Festival of Lights Parade steps off at 5:50 p.m. on December 5 on Tejon Street in downtown Colorado Springs. This is one of the signature holiday events in the city — a lighted parade through downtown that draws large crowds. If you're combining it with the Family Fun Day at the museum, plan to grab dinner or a spot on the parade route between the two events.

Youth Symphony Holiday Pops and Children's Chorale

Two performances for families who want to combine the holidays with live music:

  • Youth Symphony Holiday Pops — December 5 at the Pikes Peak Center
  • Children's Chorale Holiday Show — December 6, 2 p.m. at the Pikes Peak Center, $26 per ticket

Check the Pikes Peak Center box office for current availability and pricing on the Youth Symphony concert.

North Pole — Santa's Workshop (Through December 24)

North Pole — Santa's Workshop, the historic holiday amusement park at the foot of Pikes Peak on Highway 24, is open through December 24. It's closed on Tuesdays and Wednesdays except during holiday weeks. Rides close at 4:45 p.m. during the season. It's one of the most uniquely Colorado Springs holiday experiences you can give your kids, especially if they haven't been before.

What's Next

December fills up fast in Colorado Springs, and several of these events — especially the Electric Safari and Flying W — book out well in advance. The first week of December alone has the Electric Safari opening, Flying W starting, the Festival of Lights Family Fun Day, the Festival of Lights Parade, and two concerts. Put the dates on the calendar this weekend, buy tickets for anything that requires them, and enjoy what is genuinely one of the best holiday seasons in the state.
