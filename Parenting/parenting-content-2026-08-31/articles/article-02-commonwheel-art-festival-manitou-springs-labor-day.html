52nd Commonwheel Art Festival Returns to Manitou Springs for Labor Day Weekend

By Sarah Morales

August 31, 2026

COS Family Events

Labor Day weekend in the Pikes Peak region has no shortage of options — but for families who want a free, art-forward outing that works for all ages, the Commonwheel Artists Labor Day Art Festival in Manitou Springs belongs at the top of the list.

Now in its 52nd year, the Commonwheel Artists Co-op festival runs September 5, 6, and 7 at Memorial Park in historic Manitou Springs, just minutes west of Colorado Springs. Admission is free, hours are 10 AM to 5 PM each day, and the event draws more than 12,000 visitors over the holiday weekend.

What to Expect

The festival features over 100 booths of juried fine arts and crafts — meaning every artist has been selected through a competitive review process for quality and originality. Visitors can browse and purchase original paintings, photography, ceramics, jewelry, sculpture, fiber art, and more from Colorado artists and makers from across the region.

Live original music plays throughout the weekend, along with kids activities and food vendors. The setting — Memorial Park against the backdrop of historic Manitou Springs and Pikes Peak in the distance — adds an atmosphere that no indoor event can replicate.

A Good Fit for Colorado Springs Families

Manitou Springs is a short drive from all parts of Colorado Springs — typically 10 to 20 minutes from most neighborhoods. Memorial Park sits near the base of the mountains, and the surrounding Manitou area has its own walkable shops, restaurants, and arcade for families who want to make a full day of it.

The free admission and outdoor setting make Commonwheel especially accessible for families with young children. You can browse for an hour or spend the full day depending on energy and interest. With kids activities built into the programming, there is enough to keep younger visitors engaged.

Tips for Your Visit

Arrive early on Saturday for the best parking options and the most relaxed browsing. Sunday and Monday tend to be somewhat calmer after the Saturday rush. Bring sunscreen, water, and cash — many artists appreciate it, though most also accept card payments. Strollers are welcome and navigate the park easily.

If you want to coordinate with the Labor Day Lift Off balloon festival at the other Memorial Park (in Colorado Springs), note that the two events are at different locations: Lift Off is at Memorial Park in Colorado Springs, while Commonwheel is at Memorial Park in Manitou Springs.
