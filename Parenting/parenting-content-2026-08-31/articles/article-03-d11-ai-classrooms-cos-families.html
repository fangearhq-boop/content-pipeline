Colorado Springs School District 11 Integrates AI in Classrooms — What Families Need to Know

By Jamie Rivera

August 31, 2026

COS Education News

As students across Colorado Springs head into the 2026–27 school year, families in Colorado Springs School District 11 are stepping into a classroom that looks a bit different than the one from last year. D11 has rolled out artificial intelligence tools through a platform called Power School AI, integrating AI-assisted learning into classrooms across the district.

For families who have been watching AI develop in education from the sidelines, D11's implementation is a concrete example of what that shift looks like in practice — in Colorado Springs schools.

What Power School AI Does

Power School AI is a classroom management and learning platform that uses artificial intelligence to support teachers and students. In D11's implementation, the system is designed to enhance the learning experience while building safeguards around student data privacy.

According to district reporting, the platform aims to strengthen — not replace — the relationship between teachers and students. Teachers retain control of their classrooms, and the AI functions as a support layer for identifying learning patterns, personalizing instruction, and reducing administrative workload for educators.

The emphasis on data privacy is a central part of how D11 has framed the rollout. Student information processed through the platform is protected under the district's data governance policies, and families can contact D11 directly for specifics about what data is collected and how it is used.

For parents who are new to AI in education, the most important questions to ask are practical ones: What data is being collected about my child? Who has access to it? How will the AI be used in my child's specific class? These are questions your child's teacher or school administrator can answer directly.

The Broader Picture for COS Education

D11 is not alone in this direction — school districts across Colorado and the country are evaluating and adopting AI tools, and the pace of adoption is accelerating. What sets D11's approach apart, according to district communications, is the emphasis on teacher trust and transparency: the technology is positioned as a tool teachers choose to use, not a system imposed over them.

For students, the day-to-day experience of AI in the classroom may vary considerably by teacher and grade level. Some will encounter AI-assisted feedback on writing assignments. Others might interact with the platform through personalized learning recommendations. The specific experience depends on how individual teachers choose to implement the tools available to them.

What's Next

D11's AI rollout is underway for the 2026–27 school year. Families with specific questions about how AI is being used in their child's classroom should reach out to their school directly.
