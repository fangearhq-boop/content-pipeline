Fall Youth Sports Registration Is Open in Colorado Springs — Here's What Families Need to Know

By Jamie Rivera

August 31, 2026

COS Family Activities

If your child has been talking about flag football, soccer, or tackle football this fall, registration windows are open now through Colorado Springs Parks, Recreation and Cultural Services. The city's fall youth sports programs are a practical, low-barrier way to get kids active after school and on weekends — and several programs are filling quickly as the back-to-school season ramps up.

For families who are new to registering through the city, the process runs through ColoradoSprings.gov/YouthSports, where you can see available programs, age divisions, and registration deadlines in one place.

What's Available This Fall

Colorado Springs PRCS offers several fall team sports for youth of varying ages. The most popular fall offerings include:

Flag Football — A non-contact version of the game designed for younger players and beginners. Flag football emphasizes fundamentals, teamwork, and game play without the physical demands of tackle. It's a strong option for kids who want to try football for the first time.

Youth Soccer — One of the most participated sports in the PRCS fall lineup, youth soccer is organized by age division and skill level. Games are typically played on weekends at city park fields throughout Colorado Springs.

Tackle Football — For older youth who want a more competitive football experience. Tackle programs have specific age and weight requirements, and registration tends to fill faster than other offerings given lower program capacity.

Why Register Through the City

Colorado Springs PRCS programs are one of the most accessible youth sports options in the area. The city keeps participation costs lower than many private leagues, the programs are organized on city park fields across different neighborhoods, and they require no prior experience. For families who want their child to try a sport before committing to a more intensive club program, PRCS is often the right first step.

The city also manages registration, scheduling, and communication through a central portal, which simplifies coordination for parents managing multiple children or busy fall calendars.

A Note on Timing

Fall sports registration operates on rolling deadlines, and popular divisions — especially in flag football and tackle football — can reach capacity before official close dates. If your child is interested in a specific sport, registering now rather than waiting until after Labor Day weekend is the safer approach. Once a division fills, the city maintains waitlists, but waitlist spots are not guaranteed.

Game schedules for the fall season typically begin in September after Labor Day weekend, with most programs running through October and into early November depending on the sport and division.

What's Next

Visit ColoradoSprings.gov/YouthSports to see which programs are currently open, check age and eligibility requirements, and complete registration for your child. If you have questions about a specific program, PRCS staff can be reached through the city's website.
