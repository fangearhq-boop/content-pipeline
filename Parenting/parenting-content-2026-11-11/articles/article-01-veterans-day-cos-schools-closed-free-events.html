Veterans Day 2026: D11, D20, and D49 Schools Are Closed — Here's What's Happening in COS Today

By Sarah Morales | November 11, 2026 | COS Parenting

Local Events

If you're waking up to a quieter-than-usual Wednesday morning, here's the confirmed situation: Colorado Springs School District 11, Academy School District 20, and School District 49 are all closed today for Veterans Day. City of Colorado Springs offices and El Paso County government buildings are also closed.

That means a lot of COS families have an unexpected free weekday. The good news is there's genuinely a lot to do — and some of it is free.

What's Open Today

Memorial Park Veterans Day Ceremony — 11 AM
The annual Veterans Day ceremony at Memorial Park is this morning at 11 AM. It's a meaningful, low-key event that's appropriate for kids of most ages. If you've never taken your children, this is a good year to start.

WWII Aviation Museum — Free Admission
The WWII Aviation Museum at the Colorado Springs Airport is offering free admission today in honor of Veterans Day. The museum has aircraft, exhibits, and hands-on areas that work well for school-age kids, especially those interested in history or aviation. Admission is normally charged, so today is a good opportunity to visit if you haven't been.

U.S. Air Force Academy — Open to Visitors
The USAFA is open to the public today. The Visitor Center, Cadet Chapel, and outdoor areas are accessible. The chapel alone is worth the trip if your kids have never seen it. The Academy doesn't charge general admission, so this is always a free option when it's open.

What's Closed

Beyond schools, most government services won't be running today. City Hall, the main public library branches (call ahead to confirm individual hours), and many non-emergency government services are observing the federal holiday. The post office is closed. Most private businesses and restaurants are open on their normal schedules.

Making the Day Meaningful

Colorado Springs is one of the most military-connected communities in the country, with Fort Carson, the Air Force Academy, Peterson Space Force Base, Schriever Space Force Base, and Cheyenne Mountain Space Force Station all in or near the city. For many COS families, Veterans Day isn't a distant concept — it's something tied to people they know.

Today's a good day to have a conversation with your kids about what Veterans Day means. Even a simple call to a grandparent or family friend who served carries weight. If you're heading to the Memorial Park ceremony or the WWII Aviation Museum, talk to your kids beforehand about what they're going to see and why they're going.

Ages 3–5 can understand "today we say thank you to people who kept us safe." School-age kids can handle more context — the different military branches, what service involves, why we have a specific holiday for it. Teens can go deeper into the history and their own family's connection to service.

What's Next

Schools reopen Thursday, November 12 across D11, D20, and D49. If you're looking ahead, the COS holiday season kicks into gear Thursday as well — Acacia Park's ice rink opens its season, and the Holiday Character Stroll is Friday, November 14 from 11 AM to 1 PM at Acacia Park.
