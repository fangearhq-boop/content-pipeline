4 Children's Products Recalled in November: What COS Parents Need to Check Now

By Jamie Rivera | November 11, 2026 | COS Parenting

National Parenting

Four children's and baby products were recalled in the first week of November 2026, all posted to the Consumer Product Safety Commission's official recall database on November 5. The recalls span magnetic building toys, outdoor play equipment, nursing pillows, and a baby care appliance. Here's what was recalled, what the specific hazard is, and what to do if you have one of these products at home.

Magnetopia Magnetic Building Cubes and Blocks

Hazard: Magnet ingestion risk. Loose or swallowed magnets can attract through intestinal walls, causing serious internal injury or death. This is one of the most dangerous toy hazards the CPSC tracks — magnet ingestion injuries require immediate surgical intervention and can be fatal.

What to do: Stop use immediately. Do not let children play with these products. Contact Magnetopia for information on refunds or remedies. If your child has swallowed a magnet or you suspect they have, seek emergency medical care immediately.

High-powered magnetic toy sets have been a recurring CPSC concern for years. This recall is a reminder to check any magnetic building toy in your home — not just this brand — to confirm it's not on a recall list.

Arlopu Slide Playsets

Hazard: Head and neck entrapment. Openings in the product can allow a child's torso to pass through while the head or neck becomes trapped, creating a strangulation risk. The recall covers approximately 10,318 units.

What to do: Stop use immediately and contact Arlopu for a refund or replacement. These products should not be used until a remedy is in place.

Kikea Store X-VESOLJE Nursing Pillows

Hazard: Suffocation risk. The nursing pillows violate mandatory safety standards for nursing pillows, posing a risk of serious injury or death from suffocation.

What to do: Stop use immediately. Do not use these nursing pillows with infants. Contact the retailer or CPSC for remedy information. If you purchased this product through a third-party marketplace, check the product listing for brand and model details.

Baby Brezza SuperFast Sterilizer Dryers

Hazard: Burn risk. The sterilizer dryers can overheat while plugged in and operating. If the surface is handled during or after operation, it poses a burn injury risk to adults and children.

What to do: Stop use immediately. Unplug the unit. Contact Baby Brezza for a refund or replacement.

How to Check for Recalls

The CPSC maintains a searchable recall database at cpsc.gov. You can search by product type, brand name, or browse by date. Recalls are added frequently — the CPSC posted 194 consumer product recalls in a recent 120-day window.

You can also call the CPSC recall hotline at 1-800-638-2772 (Monday through Friday, 10 AM to 6 PM ET) to report an unsafe product or ask about a specific recall.

Bookmarking cpsc.gov and checking it monthly — especially before the holiday gift-giving season — is one of the simplest ways to stay current on product safety. The holiday shopping season is when parents are most likely to purchase the kinds of items that show up in recall lists.

What's Next

The CPSC adds new recalls weekly. With the holiday season approaching, it's worth doing a sweep of your home's children's products now — especially any magnetic building sets, infant sleep accessories, or outdoor play equipment purchased in the past year or two. For the most current list, visit cpsc.gov or follow the CPSC on social media where recalls are posted as they're announced.
