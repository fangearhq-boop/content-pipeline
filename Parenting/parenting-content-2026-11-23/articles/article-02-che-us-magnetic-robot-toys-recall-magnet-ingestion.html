CPSC Recalls 101,000+ CHE-US Magnetic Robot Toys — Swallowing Two Magnets Can Be Fatal

By Sarah Morales November 23, 2026

Colorado Springs parents doing holiday shopping on Amazon should be aware of a significant recall announced this week — and a pattern that has emerged around magnetic toys in November 2026.

The Consumer Product Safety Commission has recalled approximately 101,282 CHE-US magnetic robot and stick figure toy sets (Recall #27-096), sold on Amazon from April 2025 through August 2026. The sets contain high-powered magnets that violate the mandatory toy safety standard. If a child swallows two or more of the magnets, they can attract each other through intestinal walls, causing perforations and tears that require emergency surgery — and can be fatal if not treated immediately.

What Was Recalled

The recalled products are CHE-US magnetic stick figure and robot toy sets. They were sold in packs of 5 or 10 on Amazon from April 2025 through August 2026 at prices ranging from $9 to $18. The packaging includes the identifier code X004N3MGCB.

Parents who purchased these sets should stop use immediately. Contact CHE-US for a full refund and do not donate, sell, or give away the product.

Why Magnetic Toy Hazards Are So Serious

High-powered magnets in toys are among the most dangerous hazards in the toy safety system — not because they cause immediate visible harm, but because the injury happens internally and may not be detected quickly enough. When a child swallows a single magnet, it can pass through the digestive system without issue. The danger comes when two or more magnets are swallowed at different times. Each magnet seeks out the others through intestinal walls, clamping the tissue between them. This causes pressure necrosis — tissue death — along the intestinal wall, which can lead to bowel perforation, sepsis, and death.

Pediatric gastroenterologists and emergency physicians recognize this as a time-sensitive emergency: the sooner the ingestion is treated, the lower the likelihood of serious injury. If a child is known or suspected to have swallowed magnets, go to an emergency room immediately — do not wait for symptoms.

A Pattern This Week

Wednesday's CPSC action on FZChenrry Magnetic Fidget Ball toys (Warning #27-095) and today's CHE-US recall are two separate products from two separate sellers — but the same underlying hazard. Both were sold on Amazon at low price points that make them common impulse buys and gift options. Neither set of products has the safety certification required under federal toy standards.

The CPSC has taken action against high-powered magnetic toy products repeatedly over the past decade. They remain a persistent problem because new products enter the Amazon marketplace faster than enforcement can keep up. When shopping for magnetic toys of any kind for children, verifying the product against CPSC.gov/Recalls before purchase is the most reliable safety check available to families.

What's Next

If you have CHE-US magnetic robot or stick figure toy sets (packaging code X004N3MGCB) at home, stop use immediately and contact CHE-US for a full refund. Do not donate or resell these products. The full recall notice, including contact information for CHE-US, is available at CPSC.gov under Recall #27-096.

Parents should also review the Nov 21 coverage of the FZChenrry Magnetic Fidget Ball warning (#27-095) to check whether that product is in their home as well. Both sets were sold on Amazon and both are in the common holiday gift price range.
