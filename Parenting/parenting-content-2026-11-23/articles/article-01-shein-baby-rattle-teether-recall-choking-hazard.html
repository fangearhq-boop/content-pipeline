CPSC Recalls ~2,657 SHEIN Baby Rattle and Teether Sets — Choking Hazard for Kids Under 3

By Jamie Rivera November 23, 2026

If you've purchased baby or toddler toys from SHEIN in the past year, there's a recall you need to know about before the holidays get into full swing.

The Consumer Product Safety Commission announced on November 12, 2026, that approximately 2,657 SHEIN Baby Rattle and Teether Toy Sets are being recalled. The reason: purple and orange knobs on the sets can detach, creating a choking hazard for children under 3 years old. No injuries have been reported, but the CPSC has determined the product violates the mandatory small parts ban for toys intended for young children.

What Was Sold and When

The sets were sold exclusively on SHEIN.com from November 2025 through July 2026 at approximately $10. The recalled product can be identified by the SKU number on the packaging: sk25070936134444119.

SHEIN is a large online fashion and lifestyle retailer that also sells baby and children's products. The low price point means these sets were accessible to a wide range of shoppers and may have been given as gifts.

What Colorado Springs Parents Should Do

The CPSC's guidance is clear: stop dressing or using these sets immediately and contact SHEIN for a full refund.

A Holiday Shopping Reminder

Choking hazards are among the most common — and most preventable — toy-related injuries for children under 3. Any small part that can detach from a toy, fit inside a toilet paper tube, or fit completely in a young child's mouth is a potential hazard under federal safety standards.

Before purchasing any toy for an infant or toddler as a holiday gift this season, it's worth taking 30 seconds to check the product at CPSC.gov/Recalls. The database is searchable by brand or product name and is updated whenever new recalls are announced.

CPSC also offers a free recall notification service at SaferProducts.gov — parents can sign up to receive email alerts for new recalls in categories they care about.
