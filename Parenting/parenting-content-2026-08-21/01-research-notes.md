## Story 5: CDC Flu Vaccine Study — 80% Death Risk Reduction

**Confidence:** HIGH (published in AAP Pediatrics journal)

- **Study:** CDC study published in Pediatrics (AAP), August 2026
- **Finding:** Flu vaccines reduce children's and teens' risk of dying from flu by 80%
- **2026–27 flu season update:** Injectable flu vaccines carry updated prescribing information for children under 5 — increased febrile seizure risk observed in postmarketing studies from 2023–24 and 2024–25 seasons
- **AAP position:** Still recommends routine flu vaccination for all children 6 months and older
- **AAP additional guidance:** Updated recommendations for immunocompromised children (from AAP News, August 2026)
- **Action for COS parents:** Talk to pediatrician by September; flu shots typically available early October through pharmacies and pediatric offices
- Local COS options: UCHealth pediatric clinics, Centura Health locations, CVS/Walgreens pharmacy clinics in COS
- Source: AAP Pediatrics Vol. 158, AAP News August 2026.

**Key facts (HIGH priority):**
- 80% reduction in flu death risk (CDC study in Pediatrics)
- Updated febrile seizure warning for under-5 injectable vaccines (2023–25 postmarketing data)
- AAP recommends flu shots for all kids 6 months and older
