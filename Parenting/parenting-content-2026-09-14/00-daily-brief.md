### STORY 1: Ricky Joy Sour Crush Candy Bottles Recalled — 2.3 Million Units, Choking Hazard
- **Tier:** 1
- **Pillar:** National Parenting (CPSC Safety)
- **Type:** NEW
- **Byline:** Jamie Rivera
- **Summary:** The CPSC and Ricky Joy Co. have recalled more than 2.3 million Sour Crush Rolling Liquid Candy bottles because the rollerball top can detach, creating a choking hazard. The 1.85-oz plastic bottles were sold in blue raspberry, strawberry, green apple, and watermelon flavors, individually and in 3- or 12-bottle packs. Sold at H-E-B, Forman Mills, Walmart.com, CandyFunHouse.com, and RickyJoy.com from January 2023 through May 2026 for $3–$32. No injuries reported. Remedy: stop use, take from children, request full refund at rickyjoy.com/pages/recall or call 404-975-0352 (8 AM–5 PM ET, Mon–Fri).
- **Posting Window:** 8:00 AM MT

### STORY 2: Finger Light Toys Recalled — Button Batteries Can Be Accessed by Children
- **Tier:** 1
- **Pillar:** National Parenting (CPSC Safety)
- **Type:** NEW
- **Byline:** Sarah Morales
- **Summary:** Multiple companies — including Cade California Electronic (CPSC, ~179,739 units) and others sold on Amazon — have recalled projecting finger light toys because children can easily access the button cell batteries inside. Button cell batteries can cause severe internal chemical burns and death if swallowed, even briefly. Sold on Amazon from 2015–July 2026 for $5–$16. Remedy: stop using immediately, email proof of disposal photo for full refund.
- **Posting Window:** 10:00 AM MT

### STORY 5: Back-to-School Screen Time Guide: What COS Families Should Know This Fall
- **Tier:** 2
- **Pillar:** National Parenting
- **Type:** NEW
- **Byline:** Jamie Rivera
- **Summary:** With schools a month into the 2026-27 year, COS families face a familiar challenge: managing screen time as homework, downtime, and fall routines collide. Research published in major pediatrics journals shows screens before bedtime extend sleep latency by an average of 23 minutes. The AAP recommends no screens for children under 18 months, one hour of quality content daily for ages 2-5, and prioritizing quality and conversation over strict time limits for older kids. For COS families: D11 is in week 5, D49 in week 6, D20 in week 4 — and routines are often still being established.
