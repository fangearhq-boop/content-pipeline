### STORY 5: Back-to-School Screen Time/Sleep Research

**Sources:**
- PMC/NCBI: "The effect of technology use on sleep quality in primary school-aged children" (2026)
- PMC/NCBI: "Prolonged screen time is associated with increased prevalence of sleep disorders in children" (2026)
- AAP: "Screen Time Affecting Sleep" (healthychildren.org/aap.org)
- AAP: "Sleep Duration, Restfulness, and Screens in the Sleep Environment" (Pediatrics journal)
- CHOC Children's: "Updated AAP recommendations for screen time"

**Key Facts (HIGH — from AAP.org/healthychildren.org):**
- AAP: No screens for children under 18 months
- AAP: Ages 2-5: 1 hour per day of high-quality content
- AAP: Ages 6+: prioritize quality and conversation over strict limits
- Sleep recommendation: 6-13yo need 9-11 hours/night; teens 14-17 need 8-10 hours/night

**Key Facts (HIGH — from peer-reviewed research, 2026):**
- Screen use extended sleep latency by 84% on average (equivalent to ~23.1 minutes delayed sleep onset)
- Strong correlation between screen use and poor sleep habits in elementary school children
- Screen use 1 hour before bedtime decreases quality and quantity of sleep
