### STORY 1: Labor Day Lift Off 2026 (Tier 1)

**Long-Form Post (8:00 AM MT)**

If you haven't made Labor Day plans yet — this is your sign. 🎈

The 50th annual Colorado Springs Labor Day Lift Off is happening September 5–7 at Memorial Park, and it's completely free for families. We're talking roughly 65 hot air balloons launching every morning starting at 7 AM (get there by 6:30 to grab a good spot), plus evening night glow spectacles where the balloons light up after dark — one of the most visually stunning things you can do with kids in this city.

New this year: the festival expands onto the park's north field, there's a brand-new Overlook Club with elevated seating (ticketed), special-shape balloons make their debut, and Orangetheory is hosting a 5K on Sunday morning around Prospect Lake if your family wants to make it a full morning. There'll also be USAF skydiving demos, live music, and the usual rows of food vendors.

Balloon rides are available through vendors like Rainbow Ryders — flights run 45 minutes to an hour and include a celebratory toast and flight certificate. Worth looking into if you've got older kids or want a one-of-a-kind experience this weekend.

Three days, free admission, Memorial Park. Whether you catch morning launches or the night glow, it's a September COS tradition worth making time for.

### STORY 2: Imagination Library — 3 Millionth Book (Tier 1)

**Long-Form Post (10:00 AM MT)**

Sometimes a number just stops you. 📚

This week, Governor Jared Polis handed out the 3 millionth book delivered to a Colorado child through the Imagination Library — and he did it right here in Colorado Springs, at the Early Connections location on Rio Grande Street.

The Imagination Library mails a free, age-appropriate book to enrolled children every single month, from birth until they start school. The program has grown to reach more than 89,000 Colorado kids — that's 26% of all children in the state ages zero to five. And new this year: bilingual Spanish/English book options are now available, with nearly 18,500 bilingual books already mailed in 2026 alone.

If your child isn't enrolled yet, it's free and takes minutes to sign up at imaginationlibrarycolorado.org. You don't need to earn a certain income or live in a specific neighborhood. You just sign up, and the books show up.

Early childhood literacy is one of those things where consistency matters more than anything else — and a book a month, every month, from birth, is exactly that kind of consistency.

Did you know about the Imagination Library before today? Is your family already enrolled? 💙

For families still getting their bearings on the new school year — here's your Colorado Springs district quick-reference:

If you have a middle or high schooler interested in STEM pathways, it's worth exploring what D11 offers in that space.

If you're new to your district or just want to know more about what's available, each district's website (d11.org, asd20.org, d49.org) has enrollment guides, program lists, and family resource pages.

**Long-Form Post (3:00 PM MT)**

Something fun coming up next week if you're looking for a way to keep the after-school momentum going. 🌿

The Dot Days Community Festival runs September 9–11 right here in Colorado Springs, with programming designed for families and kids of all ages. Sessions include Cultural Connections, Citizen Science, and Our Place in Nature — each one a different way to engage kids with the world around them in a hands-on, accessible setting.

The Colorado Springs Pioneers Museum at Boulder Park is part of the mix, and the week runs into a "We The People: Colorado at 150" lecture series on September 12 at 2 PM if you've got a history-curious kid.

It's the kind of event that doesn't require a whole day of logistics — a session here or there works perfectly. Worth putting on the calendar for next week.
