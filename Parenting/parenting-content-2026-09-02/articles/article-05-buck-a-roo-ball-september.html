Buck-A-Roo Ball Returns September 12 — Western Evening for Moms and Sons at the ProRodeo Hall of Fame

By Jamie Rivera | COS Parenting | September 2, 2026

Co Springs Mom Collective's 3rd annual event gives moms and their sons an intentional, western-themed evening in one of Colorado Springs' most unique venues.

Sometimes the best family memories come from events built specifically around one relationship. The 3rd annual Buck-A-Roo Ball is one of those events — a western-themed evening designed for moms and their sons, hosted by Co Springs Mom Collective on September 12 at the ProRodeo Hall of Fame and Museum of the American Cowboy in Colorado Springs.

Now in its third year, the Buck-A-Roo Ball has built a reputation as one of the more creative and intentional parent-child events in Colorado Springs. Here's what to know if you're considering it.

The Event

The Buck-A-Roo Ball is a ticketed evening event organized by Co Springs Mom Collective, a Colorado Springs-based community for local parents. The format is western-themed and includes dinner, dancing, and activities — all within the walls of the ProRodeo Hall of Fame and Museum of the American Cowboy, which provides a setting that's genuinely one-of-a-kind in Colorado Springs.

Western attire is part of the fun — expect boots, belt buckles, and kids who may or may not take their hats off inside. The event runs in the evening, which gives it a different feel from daytime festivals: a little more dressed-up, a little more intentional, and a memorable night out rather than an afternoon activity.

About the Venue

The ProRodeo Hall of Fame and Museum of the American Cowboy is located in Colorado Springs and serves as the official hall of fame for professional rodeo. It's a genuine Colorado institution — and an unusual choice for a kids' event in the best possible way. The museum's exhibits provide built-in conversation starters, and the building itself has a character that most event venues don't come close to matching.

Intentional Parent-Child Time in Colorado Springs

One thing the Buck-A-Roo Ball does well is give parents and children a specific context to be present together — not just in the same place at the same time, but doing something with a beginning, middle, and end. Research on parent-child connection consistently points to the value of one-on-one time with a defined activity rather than general togetherness. Events like this create the structure that makes those moments easier to have.

For families where schedules are tight and carved-out time is rare, an evening event like the Buck-A-Roo Ball gives September a memory that sticks.

What's Next

The Buck-A-Roo Ball is September 12 at the ProRodeo Hall of Fame in Colorado Springs. Tickets are required and are available through Co Springs Mom Collective — check their Instagram or Facebook page for current availability and pricing, as this event has sold out in previous years.

If tickets are no longer available, Co Springs Mom Collective also hosts other Colorado Springs parent-child events throughout the year worth following for future opportunities.
