### Story 4 — Baby Bath Seat Recall + Dresser Tip-Over Warning
**Tier:** T2
**Pillar:** National Parenting (Product Safety)

Two CPSC actions relevant to COS families this week: (1) ONTHEWEI baby bath seats — about 2,140 units recalled because the seats are unstable and can tip over during use, violating the mandatory infant bath seat standard. No injuries reported. Owners should stop using immediately and contact the company for a refund. (2) Jocoevol 6-drawer dressers — CPSC warned consumers to stop using these because they violate the mandatory standard for clothing storage units, posing tip-over and entrapment risks to young children. Source: cpsc.gov/Recalls, consumeraffairs.com Oct 9.
