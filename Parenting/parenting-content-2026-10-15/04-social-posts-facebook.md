## STORY 4: ONTHEWEI Baby Bath Seats Recalled and Dresser Warning Issued: What Parents Need to Know

### Long-Form Post -- 3:00 PM MT

```
Two product safety notices families with young children need to know about this week.

ONTHEWEI Baby Bath Seats (recalled): About 2,140 units have been recalled by the CPSC because the seats are unstable and can tip over during use, violating the mandatory infant bath seat standard. If you own one, stop using it immediately. No injuries have been reported, but a tipping bath seat during infant bathing is a drowning risk. Contact the company for a full refund — and find details at cpsc.gov/Recalls.

Jocoevol 6-Drawer Dressers (warning): The CPSC has warned consumers to stop using these dressers because they violate the mandatory clothing storage unit standard for tip-over and entrapment risks. Dresser tip-overs are one of the leading causes of furniture-related fatalities in young children. If you have one in a child's bedroom, anchor it to the wall now or stop using it.

Neither recall required a major purchase — a bath seat or a dresser — but the hazards are serious for the smallest kids in your home.

Do you have any of these products at home? 👀
```

## STORY 5: The AAP Says Kids Should Get Their Flu Shot by End of October. Here's What Parents Need to Know.

### Long-Form Post -- 5:00 PM MT

```
October is almost halfway over — and the American Academy of Pediatrics says the clock is ticking on flu shots for kids.

The AAP's 2026–27 influenza guidelines recommend that all children 6 months and older (without medical contraindications) receive their annual flu vaccine, and the ideal window to get it done is by the end of October.

Here's the thing parents of younger kids especially need to know: if your child is getting the flu vaccine for the very first time, they need two doses, four weeks apart. That means if you haven't started yet, you're already tight on timing before peak flu season kicks in.

Your pediatrician's office, most pharmacies, and many school-based health programs in COS offer the vaccine. Kids under 8 who need both doses can often get the second one booked at the same appointment.

Have your kids gotten their flu shot yet this season? 💉
```
