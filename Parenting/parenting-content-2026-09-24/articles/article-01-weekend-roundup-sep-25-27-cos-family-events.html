Colorado Springs Oktoberfest — Free All Weekend at WMMI

The 14th Annual Colorado Springs Oktoberfest runs Friday through Sunday at the Western Museum of Mining and Industry, 225 North Gate Blvd. General admission is free, and the event is explicitly family-friendly — this is not just a beer tent. Friday evening kicks off at 5 PM with a keg-tap ceremony at 5:25 PM. Saturday and Sunday's Dachshund Dash starts at 2 PM each day, which tends to be a genuine crowd favorite for families with younger kids.

The festival runs inside a 6,000-square-foot tent with German food, music, stein-hoisting competitions, and costume contests. It draws roughly 8,000 attendees over the weekend. Parking and some activities may carry an additional cost. More details are at csoktoberfest.com.

Harvest Hootenanny — Final Day of You-Pick Flowers at Gather Mountain Blooms

If your family has been meaning to get to Gather Mountain Blooms this fall, Saturday morning is the last chance. The Harvest Hootenanny on September 26 marks the final day of the 2026 you-pick flower season at the Venetucci Farm location, 5210 S. US Hwy 85/87. The event runs 8 AM to noon.

Admission is $5.72 per person, with children ages 3 and under free. This particular Saturday is also the first weekend pumpkins are available, adding a harvest element to the usual dahlia and flower experience. The morning includes live music and an artisan market. Tickets are available at gathermountainblooms.com; the farm recommends booking in advance since capacity is limited.

The early morning timing works well for families with young children, and the farm paths can typically accommodate strollers. Bring layers — early mornings in Colorado Springs are dipping into the 50s this week.

Bristol Brewing Oktoberfest — Free Family Session at Ivywild

The Bristol Brewing Oktoberfest family session runs 11 AM to 3 PM on Saturday at Ivywild School, 1604 S. Cascade Ave. General admission is free. The afternoon includes a live polka band, a Bavarian dance team, German food, and contests with prizes. VIP tickets ($85) are available at bristolbrewing.com for those who want the full package, but the free general admission session is well-suited to families with kids.

One useful note: the free outdoor movie at Ivywild's Schoolyard screens Saturday evening at the same location. If you're already there for the afternoon Oktoberfest, staying for the evening movie is an easy addition. Check ivywildschool.com for the film title and start time.

Poor Richard's Kids Playground — Grand Reopening Saturday

Poor Richard's Restaurant, 320 N. Tejon St., is reopening its kids' playground on Saturday, September 26. This downtown location has long been a family destination in Colorado Springs, and the playground reopening adds back a feature families have been waiting on. Verify the details — including hours and any associated cost — at poorrichardscs.com before heading over.
