HALO Dream Magic Sleepsuit Recalled: 45,000 Units Pulled Due to Choking Hazard

By Jamie Rivera | September 24, 2026

National Parenting | Product Safety

HALO Innovations has recalled approximately 45,000 Dream Magic Sleepsuits after the CPSC received reports that the zipper head can detach from the garment, creating a choking hazard for infants. The recalled units were sold between September 2025 and February 2026 at Amazon, Walmart, and Target at approximately $50 each.

If you purchased a HALO Dream Magic Sleepsuit during that window, the first step is to check the batch code on the garment's tag. This recall covers three specific batch codes — PO30592, PO30641, and PO30685 — and does not apply to units with other batch codes.

What to Do if You Have an Affected Sleepsuit

The CPSC recall instructions are specific about the steps. First, stop using the sleepsuit immediately. Second, go to sleepsuitrecall.com and complete the registration. After registering, write the word "RECALL" in permanent marker on the front of the garment, then remove the zipper heads to prevent the product from being reused. Finally, mail the garment back according to the instructions you receive after registration, and you'll receive either a full refund or a replacement product.

The registration process at sleepsuitrecall.com is designed to be straightforward. You'll need the batch code from your garment's tag and your purchase information. If you've already discarded the tag, the batch code can sometimes still be traced through your purchase receipt from Amazon, Walmart, or Target.

About the Reports

The CPSC received 15 reports of zipper heads detaching before the recall was announced. No injuries were reported in connection with any of those incidents. The recall is a precautionary action given that a detached zipper head from a small garment is a potential choking hazard for the age group that typically wears a sleepsuit.

HALO Innovations is a well-known infant sleep product company, and this recall covers only the Dream Magic Sleepsuit product line in the affected batch codes — it does not affect other HALO products.

What's Next

The CPSC maintains a complete and up-to-date list of all active recalls at cpsc.gov/Recalls. If you have any question about whether a specific item is affected, that's the authoritative source. If your child has used a recalled product and you have concerns about potential exposure to the hazard, consult your pediatrician.

For questions about this recall specifically, contact sleepsuitrecall.com or HALO Innovations directly through their official channels.
