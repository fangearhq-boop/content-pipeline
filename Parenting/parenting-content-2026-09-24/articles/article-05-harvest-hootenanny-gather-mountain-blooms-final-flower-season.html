Harvest Hootenanny at Gather Mountain Blooms: Last Chance for You-Pick Flowers This Season

By Sarah Morales | September 24, 2026

Local Events

If your family has been putting off a trip to Gather Mountain Blooms, Saturday morning is the last opportunity this season. The Harvest Hootenanny on September 26 marks the final day of the 2026 you-pick flower season at the Venetucci Farm location, and it comes with a few additions that make it worth the early wake-up.

What to Expect

The Harvest Hootenanny combines the usual you-pick experience with a few seasonal additions. The morning of September 26 is the first time pumpkins are available at Gather Mountain Blooms for the 2026 season — so families who want to pick both flowers and a pumpkin can do both in one visit. Live music runs through the morning, and an artisan market rounds out the event alongside the flower fields.

The you-pick concept is hands-on in a way that works well for kids of a range of ages. You cut what you want from the fields and pay by the stem, which gives families flexibility to spend as much or as little as they'd like beyond the admission fee. Dahlias have been the centerpiece of late-season visits, and Saturday marks the close of that chapter for the year.

Capacity is limited, and Gather Mountain Blooms recommends booking tickets in advance at gathermountainblooms.com. The four-hour window — 8 AM to noon — aligns well with the schedule of families with younger children, who tend to be at their best in the morning hours.

Practical Notes

Bring layers. Mornings in Colorado Springs are dipping into the 40s and 50s this week, and an outdoor flower farm at 8 AM will feel it. By the end of the event closer to noon, temperatures should be pleasant. Strollers can generally navigate farm paths, but confirming terrain details with the farm before you go is a good idea if mobility is a consideration.

The Venetucci Farm area on the south side of Colorado Springs is accessible from multiple directions. The address is 5210 S. U.S. Hwy 85/87. Allow a few extra minutes for parking and getting to the fields, especially early in the event when arrival tends to be concentrated.

For families who want to extend the day, the Bristol Brewing Oktoberfest family session runs from 11 AM to 3 PM at Ivywild School (1604 S. Cascade Ave.) — free general admission, polka band, and German food. That's a workable afternoon addition after the Harvest Hootenanny wraps at noon.

What's Next

Once the Harvest Hootenanny wraps Saturday, Gather Mountain Blooms transitions into its fall harvest season, with pumpkins taking center stage through October. Watch gathermountainblooms.com for updates on fall picking availability and any additional events.
