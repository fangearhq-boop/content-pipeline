## STORY 2: HALO Dream Magic Sleepsuits — CPSC Recall (Choking Hazard)

**Tier:** 1
**Pillar:** National Parenting (CPSC Product Safety)
**Byline:** Jamie Rivera
**Posting window:** 10:00 AM MT
**Angle:** 45,000 units recalled — zipper head detaches = choking hazard. Sold at Amazon, Walmart, and Target Sep 2025–Feb 2026. Batch codes to check. Register at sleepsuitrecall.com.
**Key facts:**
- Units recalled: ~45,000
- Hazard: Zipper head detaches — choking hazard for infants
- Sold: Amazon, Walmart, Target; September 2025 – February 2026; ~$50
- Affected batch codes: PO30592, PO30641, PO30685 (on garment tag)
- Reports: 15 zipper-detachment reports; zero injuries
- Action: Stop use, register at sleepsuitrecall.com, write "RECALL" in marker, mail back for refund/replacement
**Sources:** cpsc.gov/Recalls, wgntv.com, CBS News

## STORY 3: Tuyedoqe Travel Bassinets — CPSC Stop-Use Warning (Company Refused Recall)

**Tier:** 1
**Pillar:** National Parenting (CPSC Product Safety)
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Posting window:** 12:00 PM MT
**Angle:** CPSC warning (not a recall) — Tuyedoqe refused to issue a voluntary recall, so there is NO refund program. Extra urgency: strangulation + fall hazard. ~250 units on Amazon.
**Key facts:**
- Product: Tuyedoqe travel bassinets
- Units: ~250 (sold on Amazon, April–October 2025, ~$40)
- Hazards: Strangulation risk (restraint straps), fall hazard (sides too low), unsafe on elevated surfaces (no stand)
- Manufacturer (Tuyedoqe, China): Refused voluntary recall — CPSC issued Notice of Violation
- Action: Stop use immediately; NO refund program; contact CPSC directly at cpsc.gov
**Sources:** cpsc.gov (Notice of Violation, 2026)
