### STORY 2: Colorado Flu Season Is Starting Early — Why Kids Need Their Shots Now
**Tier:** 1 | **Pillar:** National Parenting | **Byline:** Jamie Rivera

Colorado's 2026-27 flu season is starting earlier than last year. Last season, 8 Colorado children died from flu, and 1,169 children were hospitalized — the highest total since the state began tracking data in 2004. The updated 2026-27 vaccine targets subclade K, the dominant variant from last year. AAP recommends flu shots for all children 6 months and older. CDPHE and pediatricians urge getting vaccinated before Thanksgiving gatherings. Most COS pharmacies and pediatric clinics carry the vaccine now.

### STORY 3: Day 1 After DST — How to Help Tired Kids This Week
**Tier:** 2 | **Pillar:** National Parenting | **Byline:** Sarah Morales

Today is the first Monday after clocks fell back. Kids are arriving at school an hour earlier on their body clock. School-age children take 5-7 days to fully adjust; babies and toddlers take 7-10 days. This week: keep bedtimes consistent (don't go earlier just because it's dark), get 10 minutes of morning sunlight with kids, push screens back after dinner, and expect grumpy Monday mornings. It gets better by the end of the week.

- **Posting window:** 8:00 AM MT
