Liberty High School Hosts Free Community Car Show September 26 — CTE Program Puts Student Work on Display

By Jamie Rivera | September 23, 2026

Local News | School District | D20

Colorado Springs families have a free reason to head to north Colorado Springs this Saturday. Liberty High School in Academy District 20 is hosting the Liberty Lancers Car Show on September 26 from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. at 8720 Scarborough Dr.

The event is free for spectators and showcases the school's Career and Technical Education program — specifically the Automotive Service Technology track, which trains students in hands-on vehicle repair and maintenance. Families don't need to own a car or know anything about engines to have a good time. Food trucks, a demolition demo, and the Colorado State Patrol's modified Mustang are part of the lineup.

What's Happening at the Show

The car show runs from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. and is free for everyone attending as spectators. There's no admission charge and no registration required to browse the vehicles on display.

Here's what the event includes:

Feature Details
Car competition Vehicles judged with prizes from industry partners
Food trucks On-site throughout the event
Demolition demo Live demonstration during the show
Special display Colorado State Patrol modified Mustang
Spectator admission Free

Families who want to enter their own vehicle can do so during check-in from 8:30 to 10 a.m. Entry fees are $5 for students and $10 for non-students, and payment is cash only. Entered vehicles compete for prizes donated by automotive industry partners.

About the D20 CTE Automotive Program

The Liberty High School Automotive Service Technology program is part of Academy District 20's districtwide CTE offerings. Students in the program learn real-world automotive skills — diagnostics, repair, maintenance — in a hands-on environment. Events like this car show give students the opportunity to share what they've learned with the broader community, while also connecting with industry partners who help support the program.

CTE programs have grown significantly in Colorado Springs school districts over the past several years. The emphasis on trades and technical skills reflects both the regional workforce demand and a recognition that four-year college isn't the right path for every student. An event like the Liberty Lancers Car Show makes that work visible to families who might not know these programs exist — or who might have a kid who'd thrive in one.

Getting There

Liberty High School is located at 8720 Scarborough Dr, Colorado Springs. The school is in the northern part of the city, near Briargate. Parking is available on site. The event runs rain or shine, though families should check the school's website or the D20 calendar at asd20.org if conditions change.
