Free Family Fun Festival Comes to Colorado Springs on October 17 — Games, Bounce Houses, Escape Rooms

By Sarah Morales | September 23, 2026

Local Events | Colorado Springs

Colorado Springs families have another completely free afternoon to look forward to in October. The Family Fun Festival comes to Family Worship Center on Saturday, October 17, from noon to 4 p.m. Admission is free and the event is open to all ages.

The event packs four hours with activities — including games, food, bounce houses, escape rooms, and cultural programming — without charging families anything to walk through the door. That accessibility makes it one of the more notable family events on the October calendar for Colorado Springs.

What Families Can Expect

The Family Fun Festival lineup includes the kinds of activities that work across a wide age range. Younger kids tend to gravitate toward bounce houses and open play, while older kids and adults can try escape rooms, which require problem-solving and teamwork. Games are available throughout the event, and food will be on hand so families don't need to make a separate stop.

The cultural activities component is worth highlighting. Events that incorporate cultural programming give families a chance to experience something outside their usual routine — music, dance, crafts, or food from different traditions — and that kind of variety tends to make a family event more memorable than one that sticks to the standard bounce-house-and-face-paint formula.

Because the event is four hours long and admission is free, it's a low-commitment way to fill an afternoon. Families can pop in for an hour or stay the full run. Either way, there's no ticket to buy and no registration required.

An October Full of Free Family Events in COS

The Family Fun Festival on October 17 isn't the only free family event in Colorado Springs this month. Colorado Springs FamilyFest (October 10, noon to 5 p.m., 4845 List Dr) precedes it by a week. The Colorado Springs Cool Science Festival runs October 3 through 18 with free STEAM events across the Pikes Peak region, anchored by the free Cool Science Carnival Day at UCCS on October 3.

Taken together, Colorado Springs families have meaningful free options almost every weekend in October. It's a good moment to build a family activity calendar for the month before the schedules fill up with school commitments and the weather shifts toward the unpredictable Colorado fall pattern.
