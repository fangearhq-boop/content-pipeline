### STORY 1: Jocoevol Dresser CPSC Warning

**Long-Form Post (8:00 AM MT)**

COS parents — if you bought a dresser on Amazon in the last couple of years, please read this. 🛑

The CPSC issued a warning on September 17, 2026, urging all consumers to immediately stop using Jocoevol 6-Drawer Dressers. These dressers — sold on Amazon.com from about December 2024 through June 2026 for around $160 — violate the mandatory federal safety standard for clothing storage units.

The danger is tip-over and child entrapment. A six-drawer dresser can weigh 60 to 100 pounds when full. If a child pulls on the drawers or tries to climb it, the dresser can tip. The consequences — crushing injuries, head trauma, suffocation — can be catastrophic.

**What to do if you have a Jocoevol 6-Drawer Dresser:**
1. Stop using it immediately.
2. If you can anchor it securely to a wall stud, do that now.
3. If you can't anchor it safely, dispose of it according to your local requirements.
4. Do not sell it or give it away.

Note: unlike a formal recall, this is a CPSC warning. That means there's no refund program from Jocoevol or Amazon — your options are anchor it or dispose of it.

If you're not sure how to anchor furniture safely, CPSC's AnchorIt.gov has free guidance and, for qualifying families, free anchor kits.

### STORY 5: D11 Open Enrollment Deadline Is October 1

**Long-Form Post (12:00 PM MT)**

Heads up, D11 families — the open enrollment deadline is one week away. 📅

If your child is currently enrolled in a Colorado Springs School District 11 school and you want to transfer them to a different D11 school, your application must be submitted by October 1. After that date, you'll need to wait for the next enrollment cycle.

If you're new to District 11 — meaning your child has never been enrolled in D11 — you can apply year-round. The October 1 deadline only affects current D11 families requesting a school transfer.

A few things worth knowing before you apply:
- Transportation to a choice school is generally your responsibility, not the district's.
- Some specialty programs have additional requirements (auditions, assessments, or separate applications).
- Seats are based on availability — applying earlier in any window improves your odds.

To submit an open enrollment application, visit d11.org. And if you have questions about a specific school's programs, the school's main office is usually the fastest place to get accurate, current information.
