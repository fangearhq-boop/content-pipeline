CPSC Warns: Stop Using Jocoevol 6-Drawer Dressers Now
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post COS parents — if you bought a dresser on Amazon in the last couple of years, please read this. 🛑 The CPSC issued a warning on September 17, 2026, urging all consumers to immediately stop using Jocoevol 6-Drawer Dressers. These dressers — sold on Amazon.com from about December 2024 through June 2026 for around $160 — violate the mandatory federal safety standard for clothing storage units. The danger is tip-over and child entrapment. A six-drawer dresser can weigh 60 to 100 pounds when full. If a child pulls on the drawers or tries to climb it, the dresser can tip. The consequences — crushing injuries, head trauma, suffocation — can be catastrophic. **What to do if you have a Jocoevol 6-Drawer Dresser:** 1. Stop using it immediately. 2. If you can anchor it securely to a wall stud, do that now. 3. If you can't anchor it safely, dispose of it according to your local requirements. 4. Do not sell it or give it away. Note: unlike a formal recall, this is a CPSC warning. That means there's no refund program from Jocoevol or Amazon — your options are anchor it or dispose of it. If you're not sure how to anchor furniture safely, CPSC's AnchorIt.gov has free guidance and, for qualifying families, free anchor kits. Do you anchor the furniture in your kids' rooms? We're curious how many families have done this step. --- ### Image Post Caption CPSC WARNING: Stop using Jocoevol 6-Drawer Dressers. 🛑 Sold on Amazon, Dec 2024–Jun 2026, ~$160. Violates mandatory safety standard — tip-over and child entrapment risk. No refund available; dispose or anchor to wall. Do you have furniture in your kids' rooms that isn't anchored?

D11 Open Enrollment Deadline Is October 1
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Heads up, D11 families — the open enrollment deadline is one week away. 📅 If your child is currently enrolled in a Colorado Springs School District 11 school and you want to transfer them to a different D11 school, your application must be submitted by October 1. After that date, you'll need to wait for the next enrollment cycle. If you're new to District 11 — meaning your child has never been enrolled in D11 — you can apply year-round. The October 1 deadline only affects current D11 families requesting a school transfer. A few things worth knowing before you apply: - Transportation to a choice school is generally your responsibility, not the district's. - Some specialty programs have additional requirements (auditions, assessments, or separate applications). - Seats are based on availability — applying earlier in any window improves your odds. To submit an open enrollment application, visit d11.org. And if you have questions about a specific school's programs, the school's main office is usually the fastest place to get accurate, current information.
