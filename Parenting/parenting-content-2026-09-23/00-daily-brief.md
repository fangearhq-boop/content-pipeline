### STORY 1: CPSC Warns: Stop Using Jocoevol 6-Drawer Dressers Now
**Type:** NEW
**Tier:** 1 (Product safety warning — stop use immediately)
**Byline:** Sarah Morales
**Angle:** The CPSC issued a warning on September 17, 2026, urging consumers to immediately stop using Jocoevol 6-Drawer Dressers because they violate the mandatory federal standard for clothing storage units and pose a serious tip-over and child entrapment hazard. The units were sold on Amazon from about December 2024 through June 2026 for about $160. No refund program — families must dispose or anchor.
