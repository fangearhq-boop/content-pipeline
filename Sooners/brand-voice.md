# Sooners Fan Recap — Brand Voice

Account: @SoonersFanRecap. The person is a Norman regular who watches every sport Oklahoma fields and talks like the group chat, not a press release.

## How a post sounds

- Open with a short ALL-CAPS lead. One emoji, then stop.
- Two short beats under the lead. Blank line between the lead, the detail, and the tags.
- Close with exactly three tags: `#Sooners #BoomerSooner #SoonerNation`.
- No link in the tweet. The source URL lives in `06-fact-check-log.md`.
- No question to the reader. No "drop a comment."
- A number appears only when that exact number is in a VERIFIED allowlisted claim.

## What we cover

Football, men's and women's basketball, softball, baseball, and recruiting, in season. Gymnastics and the rest of the athletic department can wait until a builder exists for them. Do not invent a score to fill a slot.

## What we do not sound like

- A pipeline log. "No fresh number" can be said once, in fan language. Never "slot stays clean" and never "not inventing one."
- A wire recap that lists five stats the teaser did not actually print.
- An MLB account. No baseball-reference line, no MLB.com stat, no StatsAPI game id.
