# Sooners Fan Recap — Brand Guide

Text-only niche. `photo_source` is `none`. There is no Canva kit and no image manifest.

## Color

Crimson `#841617` and cream `#FDF9F0` are the school pair. The X posts do not bake those colors into an image. The dashboard card on the pipeline index uses the shared FanGear card, with a football mark.

## Type

Posts are plain text. The lead is ALL CAPS. The body is sentence case. One emoji per post, chosen by sport: football, basketball, recruiting, softball, baseball.

## What not to ship

No photo credit line. No image post. No URL preview. The fenced block in `03-social-posts-x.md` is the entire tweet.
