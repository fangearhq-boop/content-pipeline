Verstappen Wins the Inaugural Madring Grand Prix — First F1 Victory at Madrid's New Circuit

By Ryan Calloway | September 13, 2026

Max Verstappen drove from third on the grid to become the first-ever Formula 1 winner at the Madring, with Charles Leclerc's retirement adding drama to Madrid's long-awaited F1 return.

History was made at the Madring on Sunday afternoon.

Max Verstappen claimed victory in the inaugural Madrid Grand Prix, becoming the first driver to win an F1 race at Madrid's brand-new circuit. Starting from third on the grid — behind McLaren's pole-sitter Lando Norris and championship leader Kimi Antonelli of Mercedes — the four-time world champion executed a composed race to take the victory at the Madring on Round 14 of the 2026 Formula 1 season.

The race was not without drama. Charles Leclerc, who had shown strong pace throughout the weekend for Ferrari — qualifying fifth and running competitively through the opening stints — retired from the event, ending Ferrari's hopes of a home-circuit result at the circuit built into IFEMA Madrid's exhibition grounds.

A New Circuit Delivers

The Madring was always going to be an unknown quantity heading into its debut Formula 1 race. No team had historical data from the 5.47km circuit, which features 22 corners, two underground tunnels, and the unique La Monumental banked section at Turn 12. Practice sessions, qualifying, and the race itself all took place on a track where instinct, adaptability, and real-time setup work mattered as much as raw pace.

Verstappen's ability to win on unfamiliar territory is, by this point, a well-established signature. The Red Bull driver has repeatedly shown throughout his career that circuits others find challenging become opportunities for him to assert his racecraft.

Starting from third — behind Norris on pole (1m31.824s) and Antonelli (+0.011s) — Verstappen tracked the leaders through the opening laps before finding his moment as the race unfolded across the Madring's complex layout.

Leclerc's Retirement

Charles Leclerc's day ended early, adding to what has been a difficult run of results for Ferrari in terms of reliability in 2026. Leclerc had shown genuine pace during the Madring weekend — fastest in free practice sessions alongside Antonelli — and qualified fifth, just behind his teammate Lewis Hamilton in fourth. His retirement denied Ferrari a strong result on a circuit where both cars appeared competitive.

Hamilton, who memorably crashed his Ferrari in FP3 before ignoring team instructions to stop the car and driving a lap back to the pits, had recovered to start fourth. His race at the Madring will be part of the post-race discussion for how Ferrari's inaugural Madring weekend played out.

A Historic Milestone for Madrid

The significance of the Madring's debut cannot be understated in a Spanish context. The last Formula 1 race in the Madrid area was the 1981 Spanish Grand Prix at the Jarama circuit, 45 years ago. Verstappen's victory is therefore the first Formula 1 win registered in the Madrid area in nearly half a century.

The race attracted enormous attention in Spain, with the Madring's location at IFEMA Madrid — an accessible urban circuit replacing the traditional Barcelona-Catalunya venue on the F1 calendar — representing a significant shift in how Formula 1 presents itself to Spanish fans.

What's Next

The championship standings will be updated following the Madring result, with the implications for Antonelli's lead over Russell and other title contenders becoming clear in the hours after the race. Round 15 of the 2026 Formula 1 season continues the run-in to the championship finale. With 9 rounds remaining after Madring and the points gap now reshuffled, the final third of the 2026 season is officially underway.
