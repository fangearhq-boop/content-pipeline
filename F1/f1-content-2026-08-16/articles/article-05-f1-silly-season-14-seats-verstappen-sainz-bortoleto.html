F1 Silly Season Reaches Peak Chaos: 14 of 20 2027 Seats Are Still Open

By Marcus Cole | August 16, 2026

GPFans' August 16 Transfer Roundup opened with a number that defines the state of the silly season: 14 seats. Not 4. Not 6. Fourteen of twenty 2027 race seats remain unconfirmed, driven entirely by a Verstappen domino that hasn't fallen. Factories reopen tomorrow. The Dutch GP is in five days. Something has to give.

On the last day before Formula 1 factories reopen for Dutch GP preparations, GPFans published their weekly Transfer Roundup. The headline: "Verstappen McLaren warning, Audi statement and FOURTEEN seats still open." That number — 14 — is the most accurate summary of where the 2027 F1 silly season stands as of today.

Fourteen of twenty race seats for next season are not yet 100% confirmed. That's 70 percent of the grid unsettled at a point in August when most previous silly seasons have resolved the majority of lineup questions. The reason for the extraordinary uncertainty is straightforward: Max Verstappen hasn't made a decision.

The Verstappen Domino

Wherever Verstappen goes, multiple seats open and close simultaneously. If he joins McLaren, Norris or Piastri — both under contract through 2027 minimum — faces a renegotiation or departure. Red Bull then needs a replacement for arguably the best driver of his generation. Multiple teams in the midfield and at the top are holding contingency plans that hinge on what Verstappen decides.

Verstappen's exit clause is active through October. The Dutch GP in five days is the most emotionally loaded moment in the window. An insider told GPFans today that his bond with Red Bull is "broken." Red Bull made a fresh contract offer. Verstappen has not responded.

McLaren remains the most-reported destination. ESPN sources describe those rumours as "wide of the mark" — a notable counterpoint. Mercedes is the second major candidate, with both 2027 Mercedes seats officially listed as not yet confirmed by F1.

What Is Confirmed

Amid the chaos, some decisions have been made. Gabriel Bortoleto used this week to clarify that his Audi contract runs "long-term" through at least 2028. Rumours had linked him to McLaren and others. He shut them down directly, confirming Audi's driver lineup is set for the foreseeable future alongside Nico Hulkenberg.

Carlos Sainz is close to resolution. According to Spanish outlet Marca, cited by GPFans, Sainz is in the "final stages of negotiations with Williams for a new one-year contract" for 2027. Sainz has been linked to Audi but Bortoleto's confirmation effectively closes that path. Williams appears to be his destination.

George Russell is engaged to Carmen Montero Mundt — that much was confirmed on August 9, ring and all, with an oval yellow diamond. His contract situation with Mercedes is less settled: the team reportedly hasn't rushed his renewal while pursuing Verstappen, which tells you everything about their priorities.

Lewis Hamilton is confirmed at Ferrari through at least 2027 (his third year at the Scuderia). Lando Norris is confirmed at McLaren through at least the end of 2027.

What's Next

Factories reopen Monday. Teams arrive at Zandvoort through the week. The Dutch GP — the last-ever race at this circuit — runs August 21-23. Verstappen called it his "last home race." The exit clause window is active. If there was ever a moment for an announcement, the emotional weight of Zandvoort makes this weekend the most likely one on the calendar. Whether he announces there, or waits and extends the uncertainty through October, the silly season cannot fully resolve until he acts. Fourteen seats are waiting on one decision.
