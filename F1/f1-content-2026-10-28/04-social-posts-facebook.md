### STORY 5: Mexico City GP Preview

**Long-Form Post**
Posting Window: 5:00 PM ET

The Mexico City Grand Prix is unlike any race on the F1 calendar — and this year, it could crown a world champion.

Here's what makes Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez so special:

🏔️ ALTITUDE: At 2,240 meters above sea level, Mexico City sits higher than almost any major motorsport venue in the world. The thin air reduces aerodynamic downforce, cuts engine power output, and demands more aggressive brake cooling strategies. Teams spend months of simulation time preparing for the specific conditions here.

🌬️ STRATEGY: With lower downforce efficiency, tire degradation behaves differently in Mexico than at most circuits. Teams often run higher wing angles than the numbers would suggest — the drag penalty is smaller when the air is thin — and pit windows can shift depending on how drivers manage their front tires through the quick Esses section.

🎃 DÍA DE MUERTOS: This year's race weekend coincides with one of Mexico's most iconic cultural celebrations. The atmosphere inside the stadium section of the circuit is electric at the best of times — during Día de Muertos, it becomes something else entirely.

And on Sunday, the 2026 world championship could be decided.
