Sergio Pérez Marks His 300th Formula 1 Start at His Home Race in Mexico City

By Elena Voss

October 28, 2026

Sergio Pérez | Mexico City GP | Milestone

Sergio Pérez will line up for the Mexico City Grand Prix this Sunday as a 300-start Formula 1 veteran — and he could not have chosen a better venue for the milestone. The Guadalajara-born driver will reach that landmark at Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez, his home circuit, in front of the Mexican fans who have supported him since the beginning of his career.

From a driver who had to fight for every opportunity in the early years of his career to one of the most experienced racers on the grid, Pérez's path to 300 starts is a story of persistence, skill, and the kind of resilience that outlasts doubt.

From Outsider to Icon

Pérez made his Formula 1 debut in 2011 with Sauber and spent years proving his worth to teams that often overlooked him. His reputation as a tire management specialist and a tactical race driver earned him victories and podiums across a career that has outlasted drivers far more hyped than him at the time of their debuts.

He silenced the retirement speculation ahead of this weekend's race. When asked about ongoing talk that his career could be drawing to a close, Pérez said he has changed the way he thinks about the intensity of Formula 1 scrutiny and that he remains fully motivated — particularly with a milestone home race on the horizon.

For Mexican fans, Pérez is more than a racing driver. He is a symbol of what their country can produce in the elite tier of motorsport, and the reception he receives each year at the Hermanos Rodriguez circuit reflects the depth of that connection. 300 starts in Formula 1 is an extraordinary achievement, and the crowd in the stadium section will make sure he knows it this weekend.

Looking Ahead

At Cadillac, Pérez is part of a team that is still developing its F1 identity. His experience — 300 races worth of race craft, strategy reads, and technical feedback — is exactly the kind of resource that a growing constructor needs. Whatever his future beyond this season, the weekend in Mexico City carries the weight of an entire career's worth of moments.

The stage at Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez has been set for years. On Sunday, it becomes the venue for a 300-race salute — and no driver in the history of Mexican motorsport deserves it more than Checo.

What's Next

Pérez takes to the track Friday in free practice, with qualifying Saturday and the race on Sunday, November 1. His Cadillac team is expected to run Colton Herta in FP1 for the mandatory rookie session, with Pérez returning to the car for FP2. The milestone 300th start comes in the most appropriate setting imaginable — home.
