### STORY 4: Câmara's Dilemma

**Long-Form Post** | Window: 10:00 AM ET
Rafael Câmara is living the dream of every young racing driver — but it comes with a complicated decision. The Brazilian rookie is Haas's Formula 1 driver for the final two rounds of the 2026 season, Qatar and Abu Dhabi. At the same time, he leads the Formula 2 championship by just seven points over Red Bull reserve Nikita Tsolov.

Here's the problem: both Qatar and Abu Dhabi host Formula 2 rounds on the same weekends as the F1 races. Câmara physically cannot compete in both. If he chooses Formula 1, he cedes the F2 title fight to Tsolov. If he chooses Formula 2, Haas needs to find another driver.

Câmara's debut at Interlagos was difficult — P20 in demanding circumstances with minimal preparation time. His Las Vegas appearance was his chance to show more. What comes next for Qatar is genuinely unresolved.

### STORY 5: Antonelli Season Review

**Long-Form Post** | Window: 1:00 PM ET
Kimi Antonelli arrived in Formula 1 as one of the most hyped prospects the sport had seen in years. He leaves his debut season as world champion.

The 2026 title — 402 points, clinched at Mexico City four races before the season's end — represents one of the most remarkable debut campaigns in Formula 1 history. His 98-point margin over Max Verstappen in the final standings tells a story of dominant, consistent performance from a 20-year-old driving in his first year at the top level.

As Formula 1 heads to Qatar for the penultimate round, Antonelli gets to race with the pressure of the title fully off. For the paddock — and for anyone who follows the sport — these final two rounds are a chance to watch the 2026 world champion race freely.
