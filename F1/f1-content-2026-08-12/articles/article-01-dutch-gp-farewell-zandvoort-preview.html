Nine Days Until Zandvoort's Last F1 Race — and It's the Most Loaded Weekend of the Year

By Elena Voss | August 12, 2026 | F1 Fanrecap

Nine days from now, Formula 1 arrives at Circuit Zandvoort for the last time. The Dutch Grand Prix on August 21-23 is a farewell event — the final F1 race the circuit will ever host — and it arrives surrounded by more storylines than almost any other race on the calendar this year.

It is also a sprint weekend: the first-ever sprint format at Zandvoort. The factory summer shutdowns end August 17, giving teams just four days to prepare before the weekend begins. Tickets sold out. DJ Martin Garrix is performing. The Dutch fans, who have packed the grandstands since F1 returned to Zandvoort in 2021, are ready for a sendoff worthy of the circuit's history.

The Backstory: Why Zandvoort Is Leaving

The Dutch Grand Prix returned to the calendar in 2021 after a decades-long absence, driven largely by the global surge in F1's popularity and Max Verstappen's rise as a Dutch sporting icon. The circuit, with its banked corners and passionate orange-clad crowd, became one of the most electric venues on the calendar almost immediately.

But the farewell has been planned. The Dutch GP's contract with Formula 1 ran its course, and no extension was reached. August 23, 2026 will be the last F1 race held at Zandvoort, at least for the foreseeable future. The circuit's farewell comes as the sport has no shortage of venues competing for calendar slots.

The Sprint Format Debut

This year's Dutch GP is one of five sprint weekends on the 2026 calendar — and the first time Zandvoort has hosted the format. The sprint race weekend structure runs across three days: practice and sprint qualifying on Friday, then sprint race and grand prix qualifying on Saturday, before the grand prix itself on Sunday at 9 a.m. ET.

The circuit's tight, technical layout and the famous Arie Luyendyk banking at Turn 3 make it a fascinating backdrop for a sprint race. Teams will have less time to develop setups after the factory reopens on August 17. Four days from factory to race day is a compressed timeline, and decisions made during the limited practice session on Friday will matter enormously.

The Verstappen Factor

The race takes on additional significance given what is happening with the man the Dutch crowd comes to see. Max Verstappen's exit clause from Red Bull Racing is now officially active, triggered when he entered the summer break 6th in the championship — roughly 110 points behind leader Kimi Antonelli. He has until October to formally decide.

His home race is effectively the opening act of that October countdown. Whether Verstappen races at Zandvoort wearing Red Bull colors for the last time, or as a man who has already made his decision privately, the energy around him will be unlike anything else on the 2026 calendar.

What's Next

The sprint race at Zandvoort is scheduled for Saturday, August 22. The grand prix follows on Sunday, August 23 at 9 a.m. ET. The championship picture — with Antonelli holding a 50-point lead over Hamilton — enters the second half of the season in one of the most compelling positions in recent memory. Nine days until it all begins again.
