Ferrari Seek Las Vegas Redemption as Leclerc and Hamilton Target a Strong Season Close

By Elena Voss | F1 Fanrecap | November 17, 2026

Ferrari arrive at the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix with a point to prove.

The Scuderia have experienced a complicated season — moments of brilliance punctuated by setbacks that have prevented them from fully challenging Mercedes' dominance. Most recently, Charles Leclerc's demotion after a time penalty at the Brazilian Grand Prix was a bitter pill for a team that expected to be closer to the sharp end of the championship fight by this point in the year.

With three rounds left, Las Vegas represents an opportunity to close out 2026 on a positive note and build momentum heading into the winter development program for 2027.

Leclerc's Difficult Stretch

Charles Leclerc is one of Formula 1's most naturally gifted drivers, and his frustration at Ferrari's 2026 performance has been visible at times. His penalty-related demotion at Brazil — which stripped him of a result he had earned on track — was emblematic of a year in which the technical and strategic pieces have not always fallen together for the Italian squad.

At the same time, Leclerc has shown flashes of the form that makes him a contender on any given weekend. Las Vegas, with its combination of pure speed requirements and technical precision through the slower sections, could suit his driving style and give him a chance to deliver a season-defining performance in the closing rounds.

Hamilton's Ferrari Chapter

Lewis Hamilton's decision to join Ferrari — and his subsequent 2026 campaign in Maranello — represents one of the great storylines of the season. The seven-time champion brought his experience, his preparation standards, and his competitive drive to a team hungry for success.

The results have been uneven, as is often the case when a driver joins a new team and must adapt to different car behaviors and team culture. But Hamilton's presence has raised Ferrari's standards in ways that extend beyond the lap timer, and the relationship has potential that may take another season to fully unlock.

What Las Vegas Needs to Deliver

For Ferrari, Las Vegas needs to produce a performance that reminds the paddock — and the sport's global fanbase — that this is still a team capable of winning. The SF-26, when it works at its best, is a fast and capable car. Finding that best in the final three races is the goal.

What's Next

Thursday's practice sessions at the Strip Circuit will give Ferrari their first read on how their car suits the Las Vegas layout in 2026 trim. If Leclerc and Hamilton can find pace quickly and the team delivers clean strategy calls, a strong result is achievable. After a season of near-misses and difficult weekends, the neon lights of Las Vegas could yet provide the kind of moment Ferrari have been chasing all year. Three races remain. The story is not finished.
