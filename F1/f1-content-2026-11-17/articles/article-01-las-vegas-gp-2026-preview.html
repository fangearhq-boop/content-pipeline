Las Vegas Grand Prix 2026 Preview: P2 Battle, McLaren Momentum, and the Final Neon Showdown

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | November 17, 2026

The Las Vegas Grand Prix is two days away, and while Kimi Antonelli has already secured his place as the 2026 Formula 1 World Champion, the season is very much alive.

Round 21 of 23 on the 2026 calendar brings Formula 1 back to the neon-lit Las Vegas Strip Circuit for another Saturday night race under the Nevada sky. Practice sessions begin on Thursday, November 19, with the race scheduled for 20:00 PST on Saturday, November 21 — that's 04:00 GMT for fans watching across the Atlantic.

What's Still at Stake

Antonelli clinched his championship at the Mexico City Grand Prix on November 1, finishing third to secure the title with four races to spare. But the remaining three rounds — Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi — carry genuine significance for everyone else.

At the top of the standings battle that still matters, Max Verstappen and George Russell are separated by points in the fight for P2 in the Drivers' Championship. For two of the sport's most competitive personalities, that sub-championship represents meaningful hardware at the end of a long and demanding season.

Lower down the grid, teams and drivers are fighting for constructors' points that have implications for prize money, development resources, and prestige heading into the 2027 season.

The Circuit and Its Challenges

The Las Vegas Strip Circuit is unlike any other on the Formula 1 calendar. The 50-lap race takes teams through some of the world's most famous streets at speeds that highlight every aerodynamic and power unit advantage. The long straights reward powerful engines, while the technical sections demand precise setup and driver skill.

Teams will spend the two practice sessions on Thursday gathering tire data on the abrasive Nevada asphalt, which can behave differently from track to track. The unique night conditions — where the surface temperature drops significantly from day to night — add another layer of complexity to strategy calls.

Contenders to Watch

Lando Norris arrives in Las Vegas on the back of a sensational wet-weather victory in Brazil, and McLaren's MCL40 has the high-speed pace to threaten at the front. Verstappen finished second in Brazil and brings Red Bull's experience on power-sensitive circuits. And Ferrari, despite a difficult recent run, have the talent in Leclerc and Hamilton to spring a surprise on this stage.

What's Next

Thursday practice arrives in 48 hours, and by Saturday evening the Las Vegas Grand Prix will have produced its own chapter in the 2026 season story. After that, two races remain — Qatar and Abu Dhabi — before the books close on this remarkable campaign. For Antonelli, it is a victory lap. For everyone else, it is three more chances to make their mark on a season that will be remembered for generations.
