Verstappen vs. Russell: The P2 Drivers' Battle Heads to Las Vegas With Three Races Left

By Marcus Cole | F1 Fanrecap | November 17, 2026

The 2026 Formula 1 Drivers' Championship belongs to Kimi Antonelli. But with three races remaining, there is still a championship battle worth watching.

Max Verstappen and George Russell are locked in a close fight for P2 in the final standings — a battle that matters enormously to both drivers, to their respective teams, and to the sport's historical record. Las Vegas, where practice begins Thursday and the race takes place Saturday night, is the next proving ground.

The Stakes of P2

For Verstappen, finishing second in the world standings would represent a significant achievement in a season where Mercedes has dominated. Red Bull have won races — Verstappen took victory at the Mexico City Grand Prix on November 1 — but the overall pace advantage has been with the Silver Arrows. Holding P2 at the end of the year would confirm Verstappen's status as the best of the rest in a year where "best" is a high bar.

For Russell, climbing to P2 would represent a career-best result in the Drivers' Championship and validate a campaign in which he has been competitive and consistent if not quite able to match Antonelli's pace week to week. Mercedes winning the Constructors' title at Brazil has already delivered team glory — now Russell wants personal glory.

Las Vegas as a Circuit

The Strip circuit's character could play into this battle in meaningful ways. The long, high-speed sections that characterize the Vegas layout tend to reward cars with strong straight-line performance and efficient aerodynamics. Red Bull and Mercedes both have capable packages here, so the battle may come down to individual qualifying laps and race management rather than a clear car advantage.

Tire management on the abrasive Las Vegas asphalt is another variable. Drivers who can nurse their tires over long stints — Verstappen's forte throughout his career — may find extra opportunity to manage gaps strategically.

The Mathematical Picture

Three rounds of racing remain: Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi. With 75 potential points still available from the remaining races plus bonus points for fastest lap, neither driver can be mathematically certain of their final position. Every race start, every overtake, and every strategy call carries consequence through to the Abu Dhabi season finale.

What's Next

The Las Vegas Grand Prix will provide the first of three decisive data points in the Verstappen-Russell P2 battle. How both drivers perform under the pressure of a sub-championship fight — where the top result is already claimed, but the next result very much is not — will tell us something important about how each of them handles the particular demands of this stage of a season. Watch closely on Saturday night under the neon.
