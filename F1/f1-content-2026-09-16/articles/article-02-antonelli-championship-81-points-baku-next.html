81 Points Clear With 9 Rounds Left — Antonelli Is One Weekend Away From Making History in Baku

By Marcus Cole | F1 Fanrecap | September 16, 2026

The 2026 Formula 1 Drivers' Championship is Kimi Antonelli's to lose. After 14 rounds, the 20-year-old Mercedes driver sits on 292 points, 81 clear of his own teammate George Russell. With 233 points remaining across nine rounds — including one sprint — the arithmetic has swung overwhelmingly in his favor.

His numbers this season border on historic: eight race victories, six pole positions, 12 podiums in 14 races. At no point has he trailed in the championship. At no point has the gap between him and the field looked anything less than commanding.

When Could He Clinch?

The earliest Antonelli can mathematically seal the title is the Singapore Grand Prix. To clinch there, he would need the race results to move in his favor over the Azerbaijan, Singapore lead-up period. But even without a premature conclusion, the championship appears certain barring an extraordinary collapse from a driver who hasn't shown any tendency toward one.

The next race is the Azerbaijan Grand Prix in Baku on September 26 — a Saturday race for the first time in the circuit's history, moved from its usual Sunday slot to avoid a clash with Azerbaijan Remembrance Day. The Baku street circuit, with its long main straight and unique power-circuit character, is a venue where results can be unpredictable. But Antonelli has not shown vulnerability on any circuit this season.

The Teammate Storyline

One of the more interesting subplots of Antonelli's title run is that his closest competitor is his own Mercedes teammate. George Russell, a former world champion and one of the sport's most complete drivers, has been exceptional this season — but simply hasn't been able to match Antonelli's pace or consistency. That both Mercedes drivers are in the top two says as much about the W15's dominance as it does about either driver individually.

The constructors' championship picture reinforces that reading: Mercedes leads the team standings with 503 points — 145 clear of Ferrari in second place.

What's Next

Baku on September 26 is the next checkpoint. Antonelli needs to keep doing what he has been doing all season — converting qualifying pace into race victories and managing the championship lead with composure. Nine rounds to go. The youngest world champion in Formula 1 history may be only a handful of weekends away.
