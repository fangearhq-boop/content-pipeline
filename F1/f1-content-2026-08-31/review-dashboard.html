Monaco GP ICA Appeal — Verdict Still Pending After August 25 Hearing
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post The Monaco Grand Prix might not be fully settled until mid-September. The FIA's International Court of Appeal heard arguments from McLaren and Red Bull on August 25 in Paris — but the judges have indicated the final verdict will take two to three more weeks. That puts the ruling in the week of September 8-15. Here's the background: Pierre Gasly received two 5-second penalties for pitlane speeding in Monaco, measured at 60.1 km/h and 60.4 km/h. That dropped him from third to seventh. Alpine appealed, and stewards overturned the penalties after FOM admitted the pitlane speed measurement reference point at Monaco's unique pit entry was inaccurate — meaning Gasly (and other drivers) were effectively penalised for speeds measured incorrectly. The reinstatement put Gasly back to P3. McLaren's Oscar Piastri dropped to P5. Red Bull's Isack Hadjar dropped to P4. Both teams are now arguing that the stewards exceeded their authority when they accepted Alpine's right-to-review case in the first place. Gasly currently holds his P3 result. Whether he keeps it depends on the ICA. ### Image Post Caption ⚖️ The Monaco GP ICA verdict is STILL pending. Hearing was Aug 25. Ruling expected mid-September. Gasly holds P3 for now. McLaren + Red Bull want it back. #F1 #MonacoGP
