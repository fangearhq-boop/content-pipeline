Hamilton's P18-to-P3 Comeback Is One of the Drives of the 2026 Season

By Marcus Cole | F1 Fanrecap | October 4, 2026

The race was going terribly for Lewis Hamilton — and then it wasn't.

Ferrari made a tyre strategy call during the early safety car period in Malaysia that sent Hamilton into the pitlane for intermediate rubber while the majority of the field stayed out. It was a gamble, and it backfired. Hamilton emerged from the pits in P18, shuffled to the back of a wet, chaotic grid with laps still to run and the track in no condition to make progress easy.

What happened next was something only a handful of drivers in the world are capable of producing. Hamilton, methodical and composed in conditions that were breaking others, began to pick his way through. Lap by lap, position by position, through spray and through the noise of a Sepang crowd watching something special unfold, the seven-time world champion climbed back toward the front.

He finished third. Eighteen places gained. One of the drives of the 2026 Formula 1 season.

A Ferrari Double Podium From Chaos

Hamilton's teammate Charles Leclerc added fourth place to give Ferrari a double podium from a race that, at the midpoint, had seemed destined to end in frustration for the Scuderia. The strategy call that buried Hamilton may have cost Ferrari a higher result, but the team exits Malaysia with two drivers in the top four — a reminder of just how strong the SF-26 remains in chaotic conditions when Ferrari's drivers can work through adversity.

Leclerc's P4, alongside Hamilton's P3, puts Ferrari back in the constructors' conversation as the calendar turns toward Singapore and the final phase of the season.

The Hamilton Factor in 2026

Hamilton's move to Ferrari was the most debated transfer in the sport when it was announced. At 41, some questioned whether the seven-time champion still had the pace, the hunger, and the adaptability to build a new partnership with a new team at this stage of his career.

Malaysia answered that question in the most direct way possible. In conditions that demanded everything — judgment, bravery, car control in the wet — Hamilton delivered. Third from eighteenth, in a Grand Prix that will be remembered for rain, chaos, and one driver who seemed to thrive in both.

What's Next

Ferrari heads to Singapore for Round 17 at Marina Bay Street Circuit, October 9-11. The Singapore Grand Prix features F1's first-ever Sprint weekend at Marina Bay, with the Sprint on Saturday and the Grand Prix on Sunday evening.

Singapore's tight street circuit has historically suited drivers and teams with strong mechanical grip and technical precision — qualities Ferrari has shown it can deliver in 2026. With Hamilton's confidence high after Malaysia, the Scuderia will arrive as genuine contenders on one of the season's most prestigious stages.
