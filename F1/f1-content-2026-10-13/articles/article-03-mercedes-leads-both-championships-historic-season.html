Mercedes Leads Both Championships in 2026 — A Historic Season Is Coming Together

By Elena Voss | October 13, 2026 | F1 Fanrecap

The 2026 Formula 1 season has delivered a rare spectacle: one team at the top of both championships with six races remaining and an essentially unassailable lead in each. Mercedes' W16 has given its drivers the tools to dominate. Kimi Antonelli and George Russell have done everything asked of them. The Constructors' Championship lead stands at 134 points over Ferrari. The Drivers' Championship leader sits 102 ahead of his teammate — who is the second-place driver.

When was the last time something like this happened? For context, the closest parallel is 2016, when Hamilton and Rosberg went all the way to the Abu Dhabi finale in an intra-team title battle. This year is less dramatic — and more dominant.

The Numbers After Singapore

After the October 11 Singapore Grand Prix, the Constructors' Championship standings read: Mercedes 578 points, Ferrari 444, McLaren 339. The gap to Ferrari — 134 points with six rounds remaining — is nearly as large as a maximum single-race score. Ferrari would need a near-perfect final stretch combined with a near-complete Mercedes collapse to close it.

In the Drivers' Championship: Antonelli 340, Russell 238, Hamilton 229, Verstappen 221, Leclerc 215. Three Mercedes drivers — Antonelli, Russell, and Lewis Hamilton — occupy the top three. The team has essentially been running away with the season since mid-summer.

What Makes This Different From 2016

The 2016 Mercedes season ended with controversy, tension, and Rosberg's immediate retirement after winning the title. This season has a different character. Antonelli's lead has been built on consistent wins and points scoring rather than one driver faltering while another capitalizes. Russell has been fast — he won in Azerbaijan, was quickest in Singapore practice — but has been unlucky: a Bahrain engine failure, a Singapore sprint DNF, and a grid penalty that turned a potential podium into a ninth-place finish.

For Mercedes as a team, the dominance in both championships simultaneously is the headline. If Antonelli wins the title and Russell finishes second, it would be one of the cleanest championship campaigns in the modern era of the sport.

What's Next

The United States Grand Prix in Austin, October 23-25, is the next opportunity for Mercedes to extend its leads in both championships. Six races after that and both titles could be wrapped up. The Constructors' one may come first — Ferrari and McLaren would need a historically unlikely sequence to close a 134-point gap. The Drivers' follows Antonelli's path through Austin and beyond. A historic Mercedes season may be just weeks away from its conclusion.
