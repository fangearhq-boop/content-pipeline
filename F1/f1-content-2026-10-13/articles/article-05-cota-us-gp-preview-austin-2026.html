US Grand Prix Preview: What to Expect From COTA as F1's Championship Battle Reaches Austin

By Elena Voss | October 13, 2026 | F1 Fanrecap

Formula 1 lands in Austin, Texas on October 23-25 for the United States Grand Prix — a race that in 2026 carries more championship weight than most. Kimi Antonelli needs to protect a 102-point lead. George Russell needs to attack. Max Verstappen arrives as the defending winner. And Circuit of the Americas, one of the grid's most demanding venues, is the stage.

There is no sprint race this weekend. Just two practice sessions on Friday, qualifying on Saturday, and 68 laps of racing on Sunday, October 25.

About the Circuit

The Circuit of the Americas is 5.513 kilometers (3.426 miles) of Hermann Tilke-designed track that blends challenges from circuits around the world. Turn 1 — a blind summit where drivers crest a hill and dive hard left — is one of the most photographed corners in American motorsport. The Esses through Turn 3 to Turn 12 test mechanical grip and aerodynamic downforce. The back half of the circuit, including the hairpin and the final stadium section, rewards late braking and strong traction.

It is not a power circuit in the conventional sense — it rewards an all-around package, clean laps in qualifying, and consistency over a long race distance. September and October in Austin can be unpredictable weather-wise, with afternoon heat and the occasional shower that has reshuffled races in the past.

The 2026 Storylines

Verstappen won the 2025 United States Grand Prix ahead of Lando Norris and Charles Leclerc. His Singapore victory last weekend puts him in strong momentum heading into Austin as the defending champion here. Red Bull's package has generally performed better on circuits with medium-high downforce demands, and COTA fits that profile.

For Antonelli, the race is a chance to extend his lead or, depending on the result, make a significant mathematical step toward his first world championship. He does not have to win — he simply needs to outscore Russell. The Mercedes W16 has been strong at complex, high-commitment circuits, and COTA suits that strength.

Russell must win. That is the simple version of his strategy. Every points finish without a victory just reinforces the mathematics that are turning against him. Austin is the time to change the calculation.

What's Next

A pre-race fan event called Grand PrixView Thursday takes place October 22 in Austin. Practice sessions run Friday, October 23. Qualifying is Saturday, October 24. The race starts at approximately 3:00 PM local time (8:00 PM GMT) on Sunday, October 25. After Austin, the F1 calendar continues through Mexico City, São Paulo, Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi. The world championship may be settled before the final race. Austin is the first place to find out.
