Next Stop Austin: F1's Americas Swing Begins October 23 at COTA

By Elena Voss

Formula 1 leaves Singapore and heads for the Americas. The next three races of the 2026 season run on consecutive weekends across the western hemisphere — Austin (October 23–25), Mexico City (October 30 – November 1), and São Paulo. It is a stretch of the calendar that arrives this year with the championship in its final chapter.

Circuit of the Americas

Circuit of the Americas sits southeast of Austin, Texas, and has established itself as one of the premier venues on the Formula 1 calendar since hosting the sport's return to the United States. The circuit's Turn 1 — a slow-motion charge up a steep elevation change with grandstands on both sides — is one of motorsport's great visual spectacles. A hundred thousand fans can see exactly what is happening as the cars crest the hill.

COTA's layout combines fast, flowing sections in the first half with a more technical, stop-start sequence in the second. It rewards cars that are fast in both high-speed sweepers and tight, low-speed corners. It punishes setups that compromise one characteristic for the other. The best cars in Formula 1 are the cars that do both well.

The Title That Could Be Decided Here

Kimi Antonelli arrives at Circuit of the Americas with approximately 93 points separating him from his closest championship rival, Mercedes teammate George Russell. Five races remain. The maximum available points are approximately 125–130, including sprint events and fastest lap bonuses.

Austin is the earliest possible venue for Antonelli to be crowned Formula 1 World Champion. If the race results and sprint results break the right way on the weekend of October 23–25, the ceremony could happen in Texas — and Antonelli, at 19 years old, would become the youngest world champion in Formula 1 history.

The American Formula 1 fanbase has grown substantially over recent years. COTA has played a central role in that growth — the atmosphere on race day is unlike almost anywhere else on the calendar. The prospect of a championship clinch in front of that crowd, at that venue, with that story attached to the driver involved, makes October 25 one of the most anticipated dates in the 2026 F1 season.

The Wider Picture

Verstappen arrives in Austin off the back of a perfect Singapore weekend — sprint win, grand prix win, pole position. Red Bull has been the fastest car at certain circuits this season, and COTA has historically been strong territory for them.

McLaren, fresh from a double top-five in Singapore after Saturday's sprint collision between Norris and Piastri, heads to a circuit that historically suits their package. The Constructors' fight may be largely settled, but individual race wins are still meaningful.

F1 is going to Texas. Eleven days from now, the championship story might have its ending.
