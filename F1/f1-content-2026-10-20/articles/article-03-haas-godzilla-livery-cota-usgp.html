Haas Reveals Godzilla Livery for the US Grand Prix at COTA

By Elena Voss | F1 Fanrecap | October 20, 2026

The United States Grand Prix at Circuit of the Americas has never been a race that does things quietly, and Haas is embracing that tradition with full commitment. The American team has revealed a striking Godzilla-themed livery for the 2026 COTA weekend — one of the most eye-catching special liveries of the season.

The COTA Spectacle Tradition

Austin's Formula 1 weekend has become one of the most culturally dense events on the calendar. Concerts, themed activations, celebrity appearances, and sponsor takeovers have made the US Grand Prix something between a race and a festival. Special liveries from teams looking to capitalise on the American audience and the COTA media spotlight have become part of the fabric.

Haas — the only American team on the grid before Cadillac's arrival in 2026 — has historically used the Austin weekend to connect with the home crowd. The Godzilla reveal continues that pattern, but on a scale that generated immediate social media buzz.

What It Looks Like

The Godzilla livery was described as striking in its reveal, leaning into the monster-movie aesthetic with the kind of bold graphic design that translates well on camera and in the grandstands. Austin's massive fan presence — the circuit regularly draws over 100,000 spectators per day — means the livery will have maximum visibility across the weekend's four days, beginning with the new Thursday preview session.

Marketing and Momentum

For Haas, the livery serves a commercial and cultural purpose. In a season where Cadillac's arrival has diluted the team's identity as Formula 1's lone American franchise, the Godzilla reveal is a reminder of the team's willingness to push boundaries in its presentation. Special liveries for individual races have become a significant revenue and brand-building tool for teams across the grid.

The car will carry the Godzilla design through all three practice sessions, qualifying, and Sunday's race — giving the livery a full four days in front of the COTA crowd.

What's Next

The Godzilla livery goes on track Thursday, October 22 for COTA's new preview day — the first chance for fans to see it in race conditions. Watch for it on the full grid panorama shots from Turn 1's iconic sweeping climb. Racing starts Sunday at 3:00 PM CT. How the car performs is one question. How it looks doing it? Already answered.
