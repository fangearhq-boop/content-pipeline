Verstappen Chases History: Can Red Bull Win Three Straight at COTA?

By Marcus Cole | F1 Fanrecap | October 20, 2026

The Formula 1 paddock arrived in Austin with a question that would have seemed absurd three months ago: is Red Bull on the verge of dominating the end of the 2026 season? After Max Verstappen swept back-to-back victories in Malaysia and Singapore, the fear is real — and COTA, where Verstappen is the most decorated active driver in the race's history, is the worst possible venue for the championship field to find out.

A Run Nobody Saw Coming

Red Bull spent the first 16 rounds of 2026 without a win — an extraordinary drought for a team that had won four consecutive constructors' championships. The breakthrough came in Malaysia at Sepang, where Verstappen ended the skid. He immediately backed it up with a Singapore Grand Prix performance that left the field stunned.

In Singapore, Verstappen led from start to finish, crossing the line 7.3 seconds ahead of Charles Leclerc. Kimi Antonelli was third. It was Verstappen's first-ever Singapore victory after nine previous attempts — and it set off alarm bells throughout the championship. Multiple outlets reported that the F1 paddock feared Red Bull might replicate their dominant form across the final six races of 2026.

COTA Is Verstappen's Court

If there is one circuit where Verstappen's opponents would least want to face a surging Red Bull, it is Austin. Verstappen won the US Grand Prix in 2021, 2022, and 2023 — three consecutive victories — and he holds the outright lap record at 1:36.169 set in 2023. His four COTA victories are unmatched among active drivers.

That three-year COTA win streak ended in 2024 and 2025, but Verstappen arrives this week in a different mood. With back-to-back wins and a team finding form, the historical advantage feels relevant again for the first time in two years.

The Championship Picture

Verstappen sits No. 4 in the drivers' standings with 221 points — too far behind Antonelli's 340 to win the title, but close enough to keep constructors points meaningful for Red Bull. A third consecutive win would put pressure on the Mercedes strategy team and, more importantly, complicate Antonelli's clinch math.

For Antonelli to become world champion at Austin, he needs to win. If Verstappen wins instead, the title wait extends to Mexico City at minimum.

What's Next

Free practice sessions begin Thursday, October 22. Verstappen's opening-lap aggression at Turn 1 is a known quantity at COTA — watch for his setup choices under the new 2026 active-aero regulations, which replaced DRS with Overtake Mode. If he goes fastest in practice, the Antonelli camp will take notice. The question isn't whether Red Bull is capable of winning here. It's whether anyone can stop them if they hit the front.
