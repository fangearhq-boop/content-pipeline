### STORY 4: Pirelli Brings C2/C3/C4 to Austin — The One-Stop Question

**Long-Form Post** (2:00 PM ET)
🔬 THE STRATEGY PUZZLE: COTA TIRES AND THE HEAT FACTOR

Pirelli has selected the C2 Hard, C3 Medium, and C4 Soft compounds for the 2026 US Grand Prix. On paper, it sets up a one-stop race. In practice, Texas heat and tire wear could throw that plan out the window.

Here's what teams are watching:

• **One-stop vs. two-stop:** The C2/C3/C4 selection is soft enough to encourage a single tire change, but wear rates in hot conditions could force some teams into a second stop.
• **Resurfacing:** Only Turns 9–11 were resurfaced for 2026. The rest of the circuit is older asphalt, which grips up differently and adds to unpredictability.
• **Rain threat:** A 25% chance of rain on Sunday could shuffle strategy entirely — particularly under 2026's active aero regulations, where Overtake Mode replaces the old DRS system.
• **No DRS:** The new 2026 regs remove the drag reduction system entirely, making tire strategy and Overtake Mode usage more critical for race positions.

With the championship on the line, expect Mercedes to play it conservative and Red Bull to gamble.
