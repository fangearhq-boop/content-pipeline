### STORY 1: Monaco GP FIA Court Hearing — August 25

**Verified Facts (HIGH confidence):**
- FIA International Court of Appeal (ICA) held a hearing on August 25, 2026, in Paris at FIA headquarters
- The hearing was behind closed doors
- The dispute involves the 2026 Monaco Grand Prix result
- FOM (Formula One Management) admitted there was an error in pitlane distance measurement, which caused average speeds to be over-reported
- Pierre Gasly (Alpine) received two time penalties for speeding in the pitlane at Monaco based on these inaccurate readings
- Gasly originally finished 3rd on the road; the penalties dropped him to 7th
- McLaren's Oscar Piastri made an additional pitstop to serve his penalty before the race ended, which meant McLaren could not appeal the penalty
- Both McLaren and Red Bull appealed the Monaco GP result
- Verdict expected after the August 25 hearing; could change Monaco race classification

**Verified Facts (MEDIUM confidence):**
- Lewis Hamilton issued a "responsibility" statement as the ICA hearing got underway
- The Piastri penalty affected McLaren's scoring in a key championship round

### STORY 2: Antonelli Grid Penalty at Monza

**Verified Facts (HIGH confidence):**
- Kimi Antonelli will take a grid penalty at the 2026 Italian Grand Prix (Monza, September 4-6)
- Penalty is due to taking a new power unit beyond the allowed season allocation
- Mercedes boss Toto Wolff confirmed the decision
- Penalty will likely exceed 10 grid places
- Antonelli's birthday is August 25, 2026 — he turns 20 years old
- Monza is considered Antonelli's home race (he is Italian, from Bologna)
- Mercedes views the fresh engine as a strategic asset for the remainder of the season
- Antonelli leads the championship with 242 points

**Verified Facts (MEDIUM confidence):**
- A new power unit beyond the allocation trigger automatic grid penalties under F1 regulations
- Mercedes strategy: absorb penalty at a high-speed track (where overtaking is easier) to gain engine advantage in remaining races
