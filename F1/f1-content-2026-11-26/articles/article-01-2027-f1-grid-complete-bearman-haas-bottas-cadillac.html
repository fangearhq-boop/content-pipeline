2027 F1 Grid Complete: Bearman at Haas, Bottas at Cadillac, All 22 Seats Filled

By Ryan Calloway | November 26, 2026

The 2027 Formula 1 driver grid is now completely set. With two confirmations arriving within the same Las Vegas Grand Prix weekend, all 22 seats for next season are filled and the offseason driver market is officially closed.

Haas confirmed Oliver Bearman will return for a second season with the team in 2027, partnering Rafael Camara in a fully British-Portuguese lineup. Cadillac confirmed Valtteri Bottas as Sergio Perez's teammate for 2027, ending one of the most closely watched driver market sagas of the year.

Bearman and Camara at Haas

The Bearman confirmation was widely anticipated but no less significant for it. The British driver impressed in his debut Haas season, and the team's decision to retain him alongside Camara — the top F1 Academy and Formula 2 talent — signals confidence in building a young, cohesive lineup into 2027.

At just 20 years old, Bearman enters his second full F1 season with the experience of a full campaign behind him. Camara arrives from one of the strongest junior program pipelines in the sport, and together the pair give Haas genuine youth and momentum as the team continues its infrastructure development.

Bottas Over Herta at Cadillac

The bigger story of the two announcements was Cadillac's choice of Bottas over IndyCar star Colton Herta. For much of the second half of the 2026 season, Herta had been the name most closely linked to the second Cadillac seat — an American driver for an American team competing in a sport that has worked hard to grow its U.S. fanbase.

Instead, Cadillac chose experience and reliability. Bottas's debut season alongside Perez gave the team a known quantity — a consistent points scorer with a decade of F1 experience who can contribute meaningfully to car development. As Cadillac builds its technical infrastructure, that institutional knowledge carries real value.

For Herta, the door to F1 for 2027 has closed. His path forward remains unclear, but his IndyCar credentials are undeniable, and the question of an American on the F1 grid will continue to follow the sport into the new season.

The Complete 2027 Grid

With all 22 seats now confirmed, every team heads into the final two races of 2026 with their 2027 lineup settled. The driver market's conclusion mid-season is relatively rare in modern F1, and it removes a major distraction for teams trying to maximize performance in Qatar and Abu Dhabi.

The 2026 season has two rounds remaining. Teams can now direct their full attention to the racing, with no contract uncertainty complicating their drivers' situations in those final crucial weekends.

What's Next

The 2026 Formula 1 season continues with the Qatar Grand Prix, beginning with practice at Lusail International Circuit on Thursday. With the driver market settled and the 2026 championship already decided in Kimi Antonelli's favor, the remaining narrative threads focus on the runner-up battle — where Max Verstappen leads Lewis Hamilton by 37 points with 50 available — and teams' final preparations for 2027 development.
