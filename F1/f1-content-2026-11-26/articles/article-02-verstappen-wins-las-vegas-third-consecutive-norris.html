Verstappen Wins Las Vegas GP for Third Consecutive Year, Beats Norris by 0.639 Seconds

By Marcus Cole | November 26, 2026

Max Verstappen claimed his third consecutive Las Vegas Grand Prix victory on Saturday night, holding off a relentless Lando Norris by just 0.639 seconds in a race that produced 35 lead changes over 50 laps. The win extends Verstappen's remarkable record on the Strip Circuit and strengthens his hold on second place in the 2026 Drivers' Championship.

Oscar Piastri completed a McLaren 1-2-3 at the top of the order, finishing third as Kimi Antonelli — the 2026 champion — came home fourth after a costly final-lap error that handed Norris second position. George Russell crashed out of the points.

A Night of Chaos and Precision

The 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix delivered exactly the spectacle that the Strip Circuit promises. Thirty-five lead changes across 50 laps produced a race that felt perpetually unsettled, with the front of the field reshuffled regularly by strategy calls, safety car periods, and the constant challenge of energy deployment on a circuit that punishes overcooking the final sector.

Verstappen navigated the chaos with the kind of controlled precision that has defined his career. While other drivers were caught out by the race's rhythms — most notably Antonelli, who misjudged energy deployment entering the final lap and allowed Norris to pass for second — Verstappen managed his pace and position to hold the win despite McLaren's pace making a compelling case for the opposition.

Antonelli's Error Changes the Final Lap

The defining moment of the race came on the final lap. Kimi Antonelli, who had been running in second, misjudged his energy deployment and lost drive exiting Turn 14. Norris, who had been pressing him throughout the closing laps, was perfectly placed to sweep through and take second place. Antonelli recovered to finish fourth, behind Piastri.

With the championship already secured, the error was a learning moment more than a crisis for the young Italian. But it was the kind of mistake that highlighted why the 2026 title fight was so decisive: across a full season, Antonelli's execution had been extraordinary. One slip in the season's final act changes nothing about the history books.

Championship Implications

Verstappen now leads Lewis Hamilton by 37 points in the battle for second place in the Drivers' Championship. With Qatar and Abu Dhabi remaining — a maximum of 50 points available — Hamilton needs to outscore Verstappen significantly across both rounds to close the gap. It is achievable in theory but demands near perfection from Hamilton and imperfection from Verstappen.

The standings: Antonelli 402, Verstappen 304, Hamilton 267, Russell 264, Norris 264. The gap between Russell and Norris means that fight for fourth is equally alive heading into Qatar.

What's Next

The 2026 Formula 1 season moves to Lusail International Circuit for the Qatar Grand Prix. Practice begins Thursday, qualifying is Saturday, and the race takes place Sunday. For Verstappen, Qatar represents an opportunity to consolidate second place in the championship. For Hamilton, Norris, and Russell, it is the moment to begin what may be a final push to reshape the season's closing narrative.
