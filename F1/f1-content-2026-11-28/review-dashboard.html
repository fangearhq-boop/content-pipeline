Mandatory 2-Stop Strategy at Qatar — What the Pirelli Tyre Limit Means
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post **Window:** 11:00 AM ET The 2026 Qatar Grand Prix is going to be a strategy puzzle — and the first piece is already locked in. Pirelli has set a 27-lap maximum tyre stint for Lusail, and the race runs 57 laps. That means every car must make at least two pit stops. No exceptions. The single-stop gamble teams sometimes try to pull off at other circuits is off the table here. This creates an open strategy canvas where the timing of those two stops becomes everything. An early safety car can flip the entire race. A well-timed overcut can gain two or three places without a single on-track overtake. Teams with strong pit crew execution and good in-race adaptability will have the edge. For the championship battle, the mandatory 2-stop adds another variable. A slower pit stop, a close call in the box, a tire that doesn't come up to temperature — all of it matters more than usual. ---
