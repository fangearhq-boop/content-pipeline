George Russell Is Third in the F1 Championship — And Still Waiting on a New Contract

By Ryan Calloway | August 14, 2026

Mercedes' open pursuit of Max Verstappen has created an awkward limbo for their own No. 2. George Russell is delivering third-place championship results with no confirmed future at the team.

By any objective measure, George Russell is having an outstanding season. He's third in the 2026 Formula 1 World Drivers' Championship with 160 points. He's behind only his teammate Kimi Antonelli and Lewis Hamilton — two of the most celebrated names in the sport. He's been Mercedes' consistent points scorer in a season where Antonelli's dominance has drawn most of the attention. And despite all of that, his future at the team is unresolved, his contract renewal delayed, and the reason is sitting six places behind him in the standings: Max Verstappen.

The Delay and Its Cause

According to reports from the paddock, Mercedes boss Toto Wolff's active pursuit of Max Verstappen is the primary reason Russell's contract extension has been postponed. Verstappen's performance-related exit clauses at Red Bull are open through October. Wolff has made no secret of his interest in bringing the four-time world champion to the Silver Arrows — he's been public enough about it that the pursuit itself has become a storyline independent of its outcome.

The implication of delaying Russell's renewal while pursuing Verstappen is uncomfortable to state plainly but worth stating: Mercedes is, at minimum, keeping its options open regarding whether Russell is their preferred long-term second driver. That assessment may not be fair to Russell's actual performance in 2026, but it's the signal the current timeline sends.

What Russell Has Delivered

Russell's 160 points and third-place standing represent exactly the kind of reliable championship contribution a top team expects from its second driver. He's been consistent across the season, competitive at circuits that haven't suited the W16, and professional in his relationship with a teammate who is outperforming virtually every expectation. Charles Leclerc — Ferrari's No. 2 alongside Hamilton — signed a contract extension through at least 2028 in June. Lando Norris is contracted to McLaren through 2027. Oscar Piastri runs through 2028. Russell is the one standout among the grid's elite drivers without a confirmed future.

What Happens If Verstappen Chooses Red Bull (or McLaren)

If Verstappen stays at Red Bull or moves to McLaren, Mercedes will need to address Russell's contract quickly and without the leverage that comes from having Verstappen as a realistic alternative. The longer the delay extends, the more likely Russell will have begun exploring his own options elsewhere — which creates a scenario where the driver they actually have loses interest in the team pursuing a driver they might not get.

Toto Wolff is one of F1's most astute operators. It is unlikely that he's mismanaged this inadvertently. The more probable explanation is that the Verstappen pursuit has a specific timeline and Mercedes believes they can resolve Russell's situation quickly once that timeline resolves. But from Russell's perspective, being asked to wait while his team publicly chases another driver is a significant ask — regardless of how the story ends.

What's Next

Verstappen's exit clause window closes in October. The Dutch Grand Prix — Verstappen's home race, seven days away — is the natural moment for either a confirmation or a denial from the four-time champion. Once that question is answered, Mercedes will need to move on Russell's contract immediately. The driver they already have is delivering championship-quality results. The sooner they demonstrate they recognize that, the better — for Russell, and for the team dynamic entering the season's final stretch.
