## Story Summaries

### STORY 1: Domenicali September 15 Calendar Decision
F1 CEO Stefano Domenicali set September 15 as the deadline to decide the fate of the Qatar (Nov. 29) and Abu Dhabi (Dec. 6) Grands Prix amid Middle East security concerns. Domenicali confirmed Qatar remains on the calendar, but F1 is "monitoring" the situation, with Imola emerging as the leading European contingency. Malaysia (Oct. 2-4) is already confirmed as a replacement race. Should Qatar/Abu Dhabi fall, the championship math would change significantly — Azerbaijan, Malaysia, Singapore, Mexico, Brazil become even more critical for Antonelli's title clinch timing.

### STORY 2: Antonelli Title Math — Singapore Earliest, São Paulo Most Likely
With 81 points clear and 9 races left (233 points available), Antonelli cannot clinch at Baku — maximum gap after Azerbaijan would be 106 points with 208 remaining. Singapore (Oct.) is the earliest mathematically possible scenario. Most realistic: São Paulo, three races from end. The 20-year-old Italian leads with 292 points; Russell is 2nd (211), Hamilton 3rd (191), Norris 4th (186).

### STORY 3: Ferrari Constructors Crisis — McLaren 52 Back
After Madring: Mercedes 503, Ferrari 358, McLaren 306. McLaren are 52 points behind Ferrari in 2nd place — a gap that has halved in two races. Ferrari's Madring disaster (Hamilton DNF, Leclerc missed VSC pit) yielded just 12 points while McLaren scored 15. Ferrari needs Baku to reverse the constructors' trend or risk losing second place.

### STORY 4: Williams "Almost Entirely New Car" at Baku
James Vowles confirmed Williams will bring a massive upgrade package to Baku — described as "an almost entirely new car." The team sits 8th in the constructors on just 11 points after an underwhelming start to the 2026 season on a car that arrived late and overweight. Carlos Sainz and Alex Albon are expected to benefit; Azerbaijan's punishing street circuit rewards car balance.

### STORY 5: Hamilton Baku Redemption
After conceding the 2026 title post-Madring (101 pts behind, 233 available), Lewis Hamilton enters Azerbaijan looking to reset. Baku is historically Ferrari-friendly — the team has excelled on power circuits — and Hamilton has never won in Baku, making this a fresh redemption opportunity. Ferrari reportedly diagnosing the brake system that failed Hamilton on Lap 7 in Madrid.
