### STORY 4: Williams "Almost New Car" at Baku

**FB Long-Form** | Window: 8:00 AM ET
Williams are coming to Baku with a vengeance — and almost literally a new car.

Team principal James Vowles confirmed the Grove-based squad will bring their largest single upgrade package of the 2026 season to the Azerbaijan Grand Prix (September 24-26). He described it as "an almost entirely new car," representing months of development work since the FW48's troubled launch.

The story of Williams in 2026 has been one of frustration. The car arrived late to testing, overweight, and has never overcome that early deficit. Carlos Sainz and Alex Albon have done their best with limited machinery — the team sits eighth in the constructors' championship with just 11 points.

Baku, with its distinctive mix of high-speed straights and technical tight sections, will be the first real test of whether the upgrade package delivers the step Vowles has been promising all year.

### STORY 5: Hamilton Baku Redemption

**FB Long-Form** | Window: 12:00 PM ET
The 2026 Formula 1 World Championship is effectively over for Lewis Hamilton.

After his brake system failed on Lap 7 of the Spanish Grand Prix in Madrid, the Ferrari driver publicly conceded the title race — 101 points behind Antonelli with just 233 points remaining in nine races.

But the season isn't over, and Baku (September 24-26) offers a compelling reset. The Azerbaijan circuit rewards power and high speed — traditionally a strong combination for Ferrari. The 6.003km Baku City Circuit's main straight sees cars reach 340 kph, a characteristic that should suit Ferrari's power unit.

Hamilton has never won at Baku in his Formula 1 career. If he can deliver a strong result there — and especially a win — it would reframe the final chapter of the 2026 season into something more than a coronation lap for Mercedes.

Ferrari's engineers are also working to understand and fix the brake failure that ended his Madrid race. What they find, and how quickly, could determine whether Baku is a fresh start or another day to forget.
