McLaren vs Ferrari: The Constructors' P2 Battle That Nobody Is Talking About

By Marcus Cole | November 11, 2026

With Mercedes having sealed the 2026 Constructors' Championship at the Brazilian Grand Prix, attention now turns to the fight for second place between Ferrari and McLaren. Ferrari lead with 510 points to McLaren's 398, but the battle is far from over with three grands prix still on the schedule.

Mercedes Clinch It, But the Fight Goes On

The Silver Arrows secured their 2026 Constructors' Championship at Interlagos, confirming what had been building across a dominant season. With 652 points, Mercedes outclassed the field and now turn their attention to supporting their drivers in the final three rounds of the campaign.

But while Mercedes' title was sealed in Brazil, the fight immediately below them is anything but resolved. Ferrari sit on 510 points, with McLaren on 398. That 112-point gap might look comfortable for the Prancing Horse, but with 129 points still available from the remaining races, McLaren's mathematical chances have not expired.

What Both Teams Need

For Ferrari, the priority over the final three rounds is consistency and reliability. Second place in the Constructors' Championship represents a strong season and an important benchmark for a team that has been chasing Mercedes across a grueling campaign. Avoiding technical failures and capitalizing on every scoring opportunity is the simplest path to locking in P2.

For McLaren, the math requires near-perfection from here. Lando Norris's win in Brazil reduced the gap in the Drivers' Championship and injected fresh energy into the team, but converting that form into a Constructors' swing against Ferrari demands consistent double-points finishes while hoping Ferrari encounters difficulties.

Key Factors in the Final Three Races

Las Vegas (November 22) is a circuit that tends to favor certain car characteristics, and both teams will be watching closely to see how their packages perform in the unique conditions of the Strip. After that, the Abu Dhabi finale and one further round will determine the final standings.

Strategy will matter enormously. The teams that make the right tire calls and avoid costly errors during races where championship points are at stake will be the ones standing tallest in the final standings. Neither Ferrari nor McLaren can afford to leave points on the table.

What's Next

As the paddock turns its attention to Las Vegas, the Constructors' battle remains one of the more compelling subplots of the 2026 season's final act. For Ferrari, it is about confirmation and preservation. For McLaren, it is about fighting until the final lap and giving their team every opportunity to close the gap.

The fight for second place rarely commands the headlines that the drivers' title does — but in a season where every point has mattered, the McLaren vs Ferrari duel deserves its moment in the spotlight. Three races remain. The story is not finished.
