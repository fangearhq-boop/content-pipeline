Verstappen Heads to Las Vegas on a Mission: P2 Championship Lead, Race Favorite Status, and Maximum Motivation

By Marcus Cole | F1 Fanrecap | November 15, 2026

Max Verstappen enters the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix as the fan-favorite race winner and the driver most motivated to perform — carrying a 17-point P2 championship lead over George Russell and momentum from his São Paulo drive from 21st to second.

When Max Verstappen lined up 21st at the São Paulo Grand Prix and finished second, the paddock collectively stopped to process what it had just watched. The Red Bull driver carved through the field on wet Interlagos asphalt with the kind of authority that has defined the most memorable individual performances of the sport's modern era. That drive arrived at exactly the right moment for a driver who needs the final three races to go his way.

Four days from now, Verstappen will line up in Las Vegas — a circuit that suits his style, in front of fans who adore him, with 25 points on offer and 17 points separating him from Russell in the battle for second in the Drivers' Championship.

What Las Vegas Means for Verstappen

The title is Antonelli's. That part of the season is closed. But Verstappen at 279 points, with Russell at 262 and Hamilton at 261, faces a final three-race sprint where the difference between defending second place and surrendering it could come down to a single bad weekend.

A win in Las Vegas, combined with a Russell result outside the top four, would put the P2 position effectively beyond reach with two races to go. That is Verstappen's best-case scenario, and entering as the +200 co-favorite alongside Antonelli, it is not an unrealistic one.

The Circuit, The Fans

The Las Vegas street circuit has established itself as one of the most dramatic venues on the Formula 1 calendar. The circuit's high-speed straights and technical sections reward the combination of raw pace and mechanical sympathy — a balance that Verstappen has consistently demonstrated on street circuits throughout his career.

The fan atmosphere in Las Vegas brings an energy unlike any other race on the calendar. Verstappen is the crowd's chosen protagonist on the Strip, and that backdrop — combined with the genuine championship stakes for P2 — creates the conditions for the kind of driven, focused performance that Verstappen has demonstrated most memorably under pressure.

What's Next

Practice begins Thursday. The question heading into qualifying Friday is whether the Red Bull RB22 will have the balance on the Las Vegas surface that Verstappen needs to attack from the front. His grid position will shape the race more than any other variable — from 21st in Brazil, the overtakes were extraordinary but costly in terms of time. From the front row or the top three, Verstappen becomes a different equation entirely.

Las Vegas, Qatar, Abu Dhabi — three races, three opportunities. The P2 championship fight that nobody planned for is the most compelling subplot left in 2026. Verstappen intends to close it out quickly.
