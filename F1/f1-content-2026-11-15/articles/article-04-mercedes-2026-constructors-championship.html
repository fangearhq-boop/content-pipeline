Mercedes Seal the Double: 2026 Constructors' Championship Clinched at São Paulo Completes Historic Season

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | November 15, 2026

Mercedes clinched the 2026 Formula 1 Constructors' World Championship at the São Paulo Grand Prix on November 8, completing the season's double alongside Kimi Antonelli's already-secured Drivers' title. The Silver Arrows finish with 652 points — well ahead of Ferrari's 510 and Red Bull's 415.

At the São Paulo Grand Prix on November 8, as Lando Norris crossed the finish line in first and Max Verstappen powered to second, the Silver Arrows secured the second title of a remarkable 2026 season. The 2026 Formula 1 Constructors' World Championship belongs to Mercedes — the team's dominant W17 concept, three world-class drivers, and a season-long execution that never gave the opposition the opening they needed.

At 652 points in the Constructors' standings, Mercedes stands 142 clear of Ferrari's 510 and 237 ahead of Red Bull's 415. The numbers confirm what the racing has been saying all year: this was a campaign of genuine superiority.

Three Drivers, One Mission

What makes Mercedes' Constructors' title particularly impressive is the breadth of contribution. Kimi Antonelli, the Drivers' Champion, delivered the top line — but George Russell and Lewis Hamilton added consistent, reliable scoring across races where reliability and clean execution mattered as much as victory.

Running three competitive drivers across the season's 23 Grands Prix requires depth that most teams cannot match. Ferrari brought Charles Leclerc and a strong second driver. Red Bull had Verstappen and Isack Hadjar. But Mercedes, lap after lap and race after race, extracted points from situations where their rivals did not. The 142-point gap to Ferrari tells that story precisely.

The W17 Concept

At the technical core of this double championship season is the Mercedes W17 — a car that arrived at Bahrain with pace across different circuit types and never lost that multi-circuit capability as the year progressed. Teams that design for one or two specific surface types often face a mid-season correction cycle. Mercedes largely avoided that, which is why their lead in the Constructors' standings built steadily through the year rather than arriving in one burst.

With three races still remaining, the team will continue pushing for race wins. Russell and Hamilton both have championship incentive to perform. Mercedes' constructors' title is sealed, but their drivers are still racing for the books.

What's Next

Three races remain: Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi. For Mercedes as a team, the goal is clean execution and maximum points from Russell and Hamilton in their respective bids for second in the Drivers' standings. For the Silver Arrows organization, the final three events are a chance to close out the year with the kind of winning performances that remind their rivals what 2027 preparation will need to overcome.

The double championship in 2026 is a defining achievement. When the season ends in Abu Dhabi, the envelope is already stamped. The question is simply how definitively it closes.
