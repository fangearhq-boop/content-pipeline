Norris Wins 2026 Hungarian Grand Prix — Piastri DNF Hands Reigning Champion First Victory

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | July 27, 2026

Lando Norris has his first race win of the 2026 Formula 1 season, and he earned it at the Hungaroring in front of a packed crowd and a watching world. The reigning world champion converted his pole position into a memorable victory, beating Max Verstappen by approximately 15 seconds in a race that delivered exactly the kind of drama that defines Formula 1 at its best.

It did not look straightforward early on. Teammate Oscar Piastri — who had been McLaren's form driver through stretches of the first half of the year — took the lead at the start and began to stretch away, leaving observers to wonder if the team order dynamic at McLaren had taken another twist. But Piastri's race ended in retirement, and from that point, Norris controlled proceedings entirely.

The Podium and Key Results

Max Verstappen (Red Bull) crossed the line second, finishing approximately 15 seconds behind Norris. It was a solid points haul for Verstappen on a day when Red Bull's season could have unraveled further — his podium keeps him mathematically close enough to remain technically in the championship conversation.

Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes) completed the podium in third place, extending his championship lead in the process. The 19-year-old was composed throughout a race that had multiple moments of instability for other front-runners.

Lewis Hamilton crossed the line in fourth place but was assessed a five-second time penalty for speeding in the pit lane. The penalty demoted him to fifth in the final classification, which in turn promoted Ferrari teammate Charles Leclerc to fourth — a frustrating result for Hamilton, who had been looking to close the gap to Antonelli at the top of the standings.

McLaren's Long-Awaited Win

Going into Hungary, McLaren had been one of the fastest teams in Formula 1 without having the results to show for it. Norris had been winless through the opening stretch of his championship defence — an unusual situation for the sport's reigning champion. The upgraded McLaren package brought to the Hungaroring evidently represented a step forward, and Norris made the most of it.

Norris started from pole after setting the fastest qualifying time on Saturday and managing to handle team and race strategy throughout the afternoon once Piastri's retirement removed the internal complexity. His final 15-second margin over Verstappen suggested he had plenty in hand at the finish.

The result was the last race of the first half of the 2026 Formula 1 season. Formula 1 now enters its summer break, with racing resuming in Belgium at the end of August.

Championship Implications

Antonelli's third place means his championship lead heading into the summer break now stands at 50 points over Hamilton. Piastri's retirement was costly for McLaren's Constructors' title challenge, but Norris's victory helped close the gap to Ferrari in the standings. The race result underscored a truth about the 2026 season: the championship battle is still very much alive, but Antonelli's consistency has given him a buffer that will require sustained excellence from his rivals to overcome.

What's Next

Formula 1 heads into its summer break following the Hungarian Grand Prix. The next race is the Belgian Grand Prix at Spa-Francorchamps, scheduled at the end of August. During the break, attention will focus on the driver market — particularly Verstappen's exit clause, which officially became active with the summer break's arrival — and on Fernando Alonso's expected retirement announcement. When racing resumes, Norris and McLaren will look to carry Hungary's momentum forward.
