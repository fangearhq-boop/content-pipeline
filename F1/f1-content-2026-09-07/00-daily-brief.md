## Story Summaries

### STORY 1: Kimi Antonelli — Italian GP Win
Antonelli started 19th due to engine penalty and charged through the field to beat Mercedes teammate George Russell at his home race. Historic: first Italian to win the Italian GP since Ludovico Scarfiotti in 1966. P19 start is second-lowest starting position to win in F1 history. Took the lead on Lap 18 after VSC pit stop for fresh mediums while Russell stayed on worn hards.

### STORY 2: Charles Leclerc — Crash and Red Flag

Leclerc walked to the medical car unaided, but later revealed he suffered vision problems from the impact. Underwent further medical checks. Major blow for Ferrari at their home race.
