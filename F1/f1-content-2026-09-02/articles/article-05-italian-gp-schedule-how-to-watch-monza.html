About Monza

The Autodromo Nazionale di Monza is the fastest circuit on the Formula 1 calendar. Average race speeds approach 264 km/h, and the long, flat-out Lesmo and Parabolica sequences test power unit reliability and straight-line speed above almost all else. The circuit runs 53 laps over 306.720 kilometers for the race distance.

Monza's low-downforce configuration reduces drag at the expense of aerodynamic grip — meaning setup decisions in practice sessions are particularly consequential. Cars that generate maximum efficiency on the straights tend to do well here, historically benefiting Mercedes, Ferrari, and McLaren more than Red Bull's typically higher-downforce packages.

What to Watch For Each Session

FP1 and FP2 (Friday): The most important story of Friday will be Ferrari's ADUO-2 power unit debut. The new engine specification promises approximately 15 additional horsepower and 0.2 seconds per lap at Monza. Compare Ferrari's sector times on the long straights in FP1 against other teams — that delta will quantify the upgrade's impact. Also watch Kimi Antonelli's demeanor and lap times; despite starting the race from the back, he still needs useful race data from practice.

Qualifying (Saturday): With Antonelli starting from position 20 regardless of his qualifying time, Saturday's grid becomes a three-way battle between Lando Norris (McLaren), Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc (Ferrari), and George Russell (Mercedes). Norris is the bookmakers' favorite for pole. A front-row lockout for Ferrari would signal ADUO-2 has delivered its promised gains.

Race (Sunday): Watch Antonelli's early laps — how quickly he can move through backmarkers and into the points zone tells you how much the penalty has truly cost him. Watch Norris's gap management at the front. And watch whether Ferrari's ADUO-2 gives Hamilton the engine advantage at the exact moment he needs it most.

What's Next

After Monza, Formula 1 heads directly to Madrid for the first-ever Spanish capital Grand Prix on September 13. The Monaco GP appeal verdict is expected around the same time. Whatever happens this Sunday at Monza, the 2026 title fight enters September with genuine drama.
