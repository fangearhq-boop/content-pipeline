Verstappen Arrives at Singapore on a High — Can Red Bull Win at Marina Bay?

By Ryan Calloway | October 7, 2026

Max Verstappen won the Bahrain Grand Prix at Sepang — the race that brought F1 back to Malaysia for the first time in nine years — and he arrives at Singapore with renewed confidence and momentum. The four-time world champion is tied on 188 points with Lando Norris, locked in a tight battle for third place in the Drivers' Championship. A win at Marina Bay could separate them decisively.

This is not the Max Verstappen of the mid-season slump. It is the Verstappen who dominated the sport for two full seasons, and it is the version Red Bull and their fans have been waiting to see return.

Why Malaysia Was Significant

The Bahrain Grand Prix at Sepang was not just a win — it was a statement. Verstappen and Red Bull had struggled for a sustained period while the Mercedes cars of Antonelli and Russell ran at the front. A victory at the revived Sepang circuit showed that Red Bull's technical development during the season break had delivered real-world results.

For Verstappen personally, Malaysia was confirmation that his pace and racecraft remain at the highest level. Red Bull's car appeared genuinely competitive on race day, and that has not always been the case in 2026.

Singapore Suits Verstappen's Style

The Marina Bay Street Circuit is a demanding venue that rewards aggression, precision, and exceptional car setup. Verstappen has won there before, and his style — attacking under braking, carrying corner speed through the technical sections, pushing to the limit — suits the street circuit environment well.

The Sprint format adds an additional strategic layer. Teams get only one practice session before Sprint Qualifying on Friday night, which puts a premium on driver intuition and the ability to dial in a setup quickly. Verstappen is widely regarded as one of the best in the paddock at extracting lap time from a car that is not perfectly set up — a skill that could be decisive this weekend.

The Norris Battle for Third

Tied on 188 points with Lando Norris, Verstappen enters Singapore knowing that a strong result widens his hold on the third position in the championship. Norris has been rapid all season in the McLaren, and their battle for P3 has given the mid-season a compelling subplot below the Antonelli-Russell drama at the top.

A Verstappen victory at Singapore — or even a top-three finish if Norris struggles — would give Red Bull significant psychological momentum heading into the final rounds.

What's Next

Verstappen and Red Bull begin their Singapore weekend with a single Free Practice session on Friday before Sprint Qualifying under the lights. Every detail matters when there is no time to correct mistakes. After Malaysia, Red Bull will arrive at Marina Bay believing they can win. Singapore will show whether that belief is justified.
