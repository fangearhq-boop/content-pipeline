### Long-Form Post Window: 12:00 PM ET This is not how Isack Hadjar planned his summer. The Red Bull driver — who took the senior seat at the start of 2026, displacing Yuki Tsunoda — picked up a wrist injury during summer break while boxing at the gym. The injury is bad enough that he's been ruled out of this weekend's Dutch Grand Prix at Zandvoort. In his place: Liam Lawson is back. The 24-year-old New Zealander stepped up to Red Bull as reserve driver and has now been confirmed for the Dutch GP race seat. Lawson actually made his F1 debut at the 2023 Dutch Grand Prix — so there's a neat full-circle element to his return at this circuit. The driver musical chairs cascade further: with Lawson moving to Red Bull, Yuki Tsunoda has returned to Racing Bulls alongside Arvid Lindblad — somewhat ironic, given that Tsunoda was the one displaced by Hadjar when the season started. Neither Lawson nor Tsunoda made it through SQ2 in sprint qualifying (Lawson P11, Tsunoda P12), so both start deep in the sprint grid. It's a strange situation at both Red Bull teams this weekend. But F1 never runs out of storylines. Get well soon, Isack. 🙏
