### STORY 1: Monaco Grand Prix Appeal — No Verdict Yet

**Long-Form Post (10:00 AM ET)**

🏛️ The Monaco Grand Prix result is STILL in limbo — and we won't know the answer for at least two more weeks.

The FIA's International Court of Appeal heard arguments on Tuesday in Paris. McLaren and Red Bull both showed up to argue against the reinstatement of Pierre Gasly to third place after the chaos at Monaco. The panel of four judges listened. Then they went home. No verdict.

Here's a quick recap of how we got here:

At the Monaco Grand Prix, Gasly was given two 5-second time penalties for exceeding the pit lane speed limit. That initially dropped him from P3 to P7. But then it emerged that FOM — Formula One Management — had measured the pit lane distance incorrectly. The speeds used to calculate Gasly's infractions were based on wrong data. The stewards rescinded both penalties and restored Gasly to P3.

McLaren (who got Piastri back to P3 briefly) and Red Bull (supporting Hadjar at P4) appealed. Now the court has to decide whether the reinstatement was correct under the regulations, even accounting for the measurement error.

The verdict is expected in roughly two to three weeks — somewhere around September 8-15. Until then, Gasly officially keeps his Monaco podium. Championship points for Gasly, Hadjar, and Piastri remain officially recorded in current positions.

This one matters. Those points could swing the Constructor championship by the end of the season.

Sky Sports raised this question directly after Zandvoort: has Norris become the biggest threat?

Here's the case FOR Norris:

Here's the case AGAINST:
- 83 points is a lot, even with 11 races left
- George Russell (183 pts) and Lewis Hamilton (183 pts) are actually mathematically closer
- Ferrari's ADUO-2 could make Hamilton the more dangerous threat at high-speed circuits

But the storyline is there. Norris. 2 wins in a row. Antonelli about to start last.

👀 The next two Sundays could tell us a lot.
