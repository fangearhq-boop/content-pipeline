COTA Preview: Everything You Need to Know Before the 2026 United States Grand Prix

By Elena Voss | F1 Fanrecap | October 15, 2026

Circuit of the Americas | US Grand Prix | F1 Race Preview | Austin

The United States Grand Prix arrives at Circuit of the Americas in Austin, Texas on October 23-25, 2026. Round 18 of 23 on the calendar, and the race where Kimi Antonelli might clinch the championship — or Verstappen might delay it again. Before any of that happens, it helps to understand the track.

The Circuit

COTA is a purpose-built Formula 1 venue that opened in 2012, the first permanent racing facility in the United States to host a Formula 1 race. The circuit is 5.513 kilometers long and features 20 turns designed to reference some of the most famous corners in Formula 1 history — Turn 1 echoes Istanbul, the esses of sectors two and three nod to Suzuka, the sweeping Turn 12 resembles Hockenheim's stadium section.

The most distinctive feature is the elevation change on the approach to Turn 1, where drivers crest a steep hill before diving into the circuit's most critical braking zone. The difference in elevation between the highest and lowest point of the circuit is approximately 40 meters — the largest of any Formula 1 venue.

Key Numbers

  • Circuit length: 5.513 km
  • Turns: 20
  • Race distance: 56 laps (308.405 km)
  • F1 contract: through 2034
  • Race start: Sunday October 25, ~3 PM Austin local time (8 PM GMT)
  • 2025 winner: Max Verstappen
  • 2025 podium: Verstappen, Norris, Leclerc

The Turn 1 Question

Every race at COTA is partially decided at Turn 1 on lap one. The combination of a long straight approach, a steep uphill run, and a 90-degree left-hand hairpin at the crest creates the most contested braking zone of the weekend. Pole position is valuable, but it doesn't guarantee the lead after the first corner — drivers from second, third, and even fourth on the grid can attempt the outside or inside line and come out ahead.

In 2025, Verstappen held his lead through Turn 1 and was never seriously challenged. In 2024, Hamilton defended aggressively before yielding the position. The first four corners of every COTA race are a miniature season-within-a-season.

2026 Context

The new 2026 regulations — with 50/50 ICE/MGU-K power split and the elimination of DRS — have changed how teams approach high-speed sections. Active aerodynamics replace the traditional drag reduction system, meaning the overtaking dynamic at COTA's long back straight is different from previous seasons. Teams are still learning how to deploy active aero efficiently in race conditions, and COTA's combination of medium-speed and high-speed corners will test every car's aero philosophy.

Grand PrixView Thursday, a new fourth day of the US Grand Prix weekend, is scheduled for October 22 at the circuit — giving fans and teams an additional day of access before practice begins.

What's Next

The 2026 United States Grand Prix weekend schedule: Grand PrixView Thursday October 22, two practice sessions October 23, qualifying October 24, 56-lap race October 25. The title might be decided here. Or it might not. Either way, COTA in October is one of the great stages in world motorsport, and this year it carries more weight than any race weekend in recent memory.
