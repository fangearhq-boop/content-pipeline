Antonelli Can Win the 2026 F1 Title in Austin — Here's Exactly What Has to Happen

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | October 15, 2026

F1 2026 Championship | Kimi Antonelli | US Grand Prix | COTA

Eight days from now, Kimi Antonelli could walk away from the Circuit of the Americas as the Formula 1 World Champion. The conditions are specific. The path is narrow. But it exists.

Antonelli leads the 2026 Drivers' Championship by 102 points going into the United States Grand Prix weekend, which runs October 23-25 at COTA in Austin, Texas. With six races remaining on the calendar, there are 150 points maximum still to be won. The title cannot be clinched mathematically before the race begins. But it can be clinched at the finish line Sunday afternoon.

The Clinch Formula

For Antonelli to become champion in Austin, three things must happen simultaneously. First, he must win the race — 25 points. Second, George Russell, his Mercedes teammate and the only driver who can realistically close the gap, must finish ninth or lower, meaning he scores two points or fewer. Third, Lewis Hamilton must finish fifth or lower.

If Russell finishes eighth — three points — the lead after Austin would not be large enough to close out the title on Sunday. The championship would continue to Mexico City.

It is a narrow set of conditions. But consider what they require from Antonelli: just win the race. The rest depends on two drivers finishing worse than expected on a circuit where Mercedes has been the dominant team all season.

What It Would Mean Historically

If Antonelli wins the title at Austin, he would do it with five races remaining on the calendar. That would place him one race short of Michael Schumacher's 2002 record: Schumacher clinched the championship with six races still to run, the earliest-ever clinch in Formula 1 history.

Antonelli would not break the record. But he would match the closest anyone has come to it in 24 years. For a 19-year-old in his debut Formula 1 season, in a car that had a target on its back all year, it would be a remarkable achievement.

"I dare to dream," Antonelli told reporters via Reuters earlier this week.

The Complication

Two factors add uncertainty to the Austin scenario. First, George Russell. He has already won this season in Azerbaijan, and at circuits that reward clean execution, Russell has shown he can make the most of track position. A strong qualifying lap could put him in a position to score heavily on Sunday.

Second, calendar uncertainty: reports in mid-October suggest there is ongoing discussion about the Qatar and Abu Dhabi race weekends, with a decision on their status expected soon. If those events were cancelled, the points available after Austin would decrease — making an Austin clinch either easier or, in some scenarios, irrelevant.

For now, the schedule stands, and Antonelli's focus is on Austin.

What's Next

Practice sessions begin Thursday, October 23 at COTA. Qualifying is Saturday, October 24. The 56-lap race starts Sunday, October 25 at approximately 3 PM local Austin time. Antonelli arrives in Texas eight days from now as the heavy championship favorite — and with the chance to end the conversation before the Americas swing even leaves the state.
