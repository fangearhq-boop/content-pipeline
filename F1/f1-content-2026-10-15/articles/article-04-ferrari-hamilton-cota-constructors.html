Hamilton, Leclerc, and Ferrari's COTA Mission: Protect Second Place and Push Mercedes

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | October 15, 2026

Ferrari | Lewis Hamilton | Charles Leclerc | F1 Constructors' Championship | COTA

While everyone watches to see whether Kimi Antonelli clinches the Drivers' title at Austin, there's a parallel championship story being decided at the same circuit that has received far less attention: Ferrari versus McLaren in the Constructors' standings, with Lewis Hamilton's position in the Drivers' top three on the line.

Ferrari is second in the Constructors' Championship with 444 points, 134 behind Mercedes (578) and 105 ahead of McLaren (339). Six races remain. On paper, Ferrari's second-place position is safe. But McLaren — with Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri both in the title conversation — is not going to stop pushing.

Hamilton's Position

Lewis Hamilton sits third in the Drivers' standings with 229 points. Charles Leclerc is fifth with 215 points — 14 behind. Between them in fourth is Max Verstappen with 221. Six races left, and the margin for Hamilton over Leclerc is less than a single race result.

Hamilton's Singapore result was ninth — six points earned after starting from the back of the grid due to a power unit penalty, a recovery drive that showcased the kind of racecraft that has made him a seven-time champion. But ninth is not what Ferrari needs from Hamilton at COTA. They need him at the sharp end.

Austin is a circuit Hamilton has won at four times, including twice with Mercedes. He knows COTA. He knows what it takes to be quick there. The question is whether the SF-26 — the most improved car on the grid in terms of development rate according to McLaren's own technical director — gives him the platform to be at the front.

Ferrari's Development Story

Ferrari has been the fastest-developing team of the 2026 season. They brought their largest single upgrade package of any constructor to the Miami Grand Prix, and the SF-26 has become progressively stronger as the season has worn on. Leclerc's Singapore podium finish — second, 7.3 seconds behind Verstappen — demonstrated that Ferrari can challenge at circuits that demand high downforce and technical precision.

COTA has similar demands. The Turn 1 braking zone, the first sector esses, and the high-load corners through the middle portion of the lap all require a car that is properly set up for mechanical grip and aero stability. Ferrari's upgraded package should suit the circuit.

McLaren's Threat

McLaren sits 105 points behind Ferrari with six races left — 25 points per race, six rounds, 150 maximum. The arithmetic says McLaren cannot close the gap if Ferrari score at all. But Norris and Piastri have shown they can score big double-points hauls, and McLaren's team principal Andrea Stella committed to bringing phased upgrades to each of the final six races.

The Constructors' title is almost certainly Mercedes'. But second place is not locked in. Ferrari will be racing with that awareness at COTA.

What's Next

The United States Grand Prix runs October 23-25 at COTA. Hamilton and Leclerc will both need strong qualifying laps to put themselves in position to score the front-of-grid points that Ferrari's campaign requires. The race is Sunday October 25 at 3 PM Austin local time. While the world watches for Antonelli, Ferrari will be watching the gap to both Mercedes and McLaren.
