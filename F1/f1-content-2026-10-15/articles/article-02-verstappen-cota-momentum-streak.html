Verstappen Has Won Two in a Row. He Won COTA Last Year. Antonelli Should Be Nervous.

By Marcus Cole | F1 Fanrecap | October 15, 2026

Max Verstappen | Red Bull | F1 2026 Championship | US Grand Prix | COTA

The easiest narrative to write about Max Verstappen heading into Austin is that he's out of the title race. He's 119 points behind Kimi Antonelli. He needs help. The title is probably going to the teenager in the silver car.

The harder truth: Verstappen is on a two-race winning streak, he's the defending COTA champion, and no driver in the history of Formula 1 has been more dangerous when his back is against the wall.

The Streak

Verstappen won the Malaysian Grand Prix. Then he won the Singapore Grand Prix on October 11, crossing the line 7.3 seconds ahead of Charles Leclerc and adding his 73rd career victory on his 250th Grand Prix weekend. He also won Saturday's sprint race at Marina Bay — three trophies in one weekend.

The Singapore win was his first at a circuit he had never conquered in his career. He led every lap from pole position. The pace was dominant. And the timing — with Antonelli taking only third place — meant the gap in the standings grew to 102 points rather than larger.

Back-to-back wins signal something real: the RB22, which struggled with rear wing instability at Silverstone and the Austrian Grand Prix earlier in the season, is working again.

COTA Suits Verstappen

Verstappen won at the Circuit of the Americas in 2025, his second consecutive win there (he also took 2024). He understands the circuit's key characteristics: the Turn 1 hill approach, the esses in sector one, the slower technical infield. Red Bull's car has historically been strong at COTA's layout.

The 2025 COTA podium was Verstappen, Lando Norris, and Charles Leclerc. That's the kind of finish — where Mercedes didn't crack the top three — that would be enormously damaging to Antonelli's title hopes if it repeated itself in 2026.

The Math That Remains

Verstappen is fourth in the Drivers' standings with 221 points. He trails Antonelli (340) by 119 points with 150 still available — six races including COTA. If he wins every remaining race and Antonelli scores zero points, Verstappen would win the championship. That scenario is not how things are going.

But winning in Austin while Antonelli struggles would force the title party to be postponed to Mexico. That is very much achievable. Red Bull's primary development may be shifting toward the 2027 car, but Verstappen has signed through 2030 and he is not racing to finish second.

What's Next

Verstappen arrives in Austin having won the last two races. He has won COTA in each of the last two seasons. Practice starts October 23, qualifying October 24, race October 25. If he wins and Antonelli falters, the world championship heads to Mexico City — and the most compelling title fight in years gets a second chapter.
