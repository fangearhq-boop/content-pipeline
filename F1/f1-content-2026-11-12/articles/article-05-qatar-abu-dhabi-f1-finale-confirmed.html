F1 Confirms Qatar and Abu Dhabi Season Finale Will Go Ahead as Planned

By Marcus Cole | November 12, 2026

Formula 1 and the FIA have confirmed that the 2026 season will end as scheduled with the Qatar Grand Prix on November 29 and the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix on December 6, dismissing speculation about potential changes due to ongoing regional tensions in the Middle East.

No Changes to the Season-Ending Schedule

Amid weeks of speculation about whether the closing rounds of the 2026 Formula 1 season might need to be relocated, Formula 1 and the FIA have issued a clear statement: the Qatar and Abu Dhabi Grands Prix will proceed as planned. FIA president Mohammed Ben Sulayem confirmed both events, putting to rest a conversation that had been gaining volume in the paddock and beyond.

Imola, which had been floated in some quarters as a potential fallback venue for one or both closing rounds, was formally ruled out. F1 leadership is committed to the Middle East finale, and the message from the sport's governing body is unambiguous.

Qatar hosts the penultimate round on November 29. Abu Dhabi, at the iconic Yas Marina Circuit, closes the season on December 6.

The Context Behind the Speculation

The concern stemmed from ongoing instability in parts of the Middle East region. Formula 1 has raced in Qatar and Abu Dhabi for several years, and both venues have strong relationships with the sport's commercial partners and governing bodies. When tensions in adjacent areas escalated, questions were raised — particularly by some media — about whether F1 might opt for the security of a European alternative.

The sport's response has been consistent: monitor the situation, maintain dialogue with local authorities and security advisors, and proceed unless there is a concrete, specific reason not to. That position has held, and the confirmation of both events signals that the monitoring process has not produced information that warrants a change.

What the Final Rounds Mean for the Season

With the Drivers' and Constructors' championships already decided in favour of Kimi Antonelli and Mercedes respectively, the final three races — Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi — carry the weight of the remaining competitive battles. The P2 fight between Norris, Verstappen, and Russell is the headline, but constructors' points, driver contract performances, and team testing initiatives for 2027 also keep the final rounds meaningful.

Abu Dhabi in particular has long served as the sport's natural endpoint — a twilight race at a purpose-built circuit that sends the season off on a visual and ceremonial high. That tradition continues in 2026.

What's Next

Las Vegas on November 22 is the next race on the calendar, followed by Qatar and Abu Dhabi. Teams and drivers will travel to the Nevada desert with the season's final stretch firmly in view. The confirmation that the full calendar will be completed is good news for fans, broadcasters, and everyone involved in the sport's commercial operation.

The 2026 Formula 1 season will end where it was always meant to end — on the shores of the Arabian Gulf under Abu Dhabi's floodlights.
