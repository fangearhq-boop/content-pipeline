Liam Lawson Closing in on 2027 Racing Bulls Seat Ahead of Las Vegas Announcement

By Elena Voss | November 12, 2026

Liam Lawson is reportedly nearing a deal to remain at Racing Bulls for the 2027 Formula 1 season, with an announcement potentially arriving before the Las Vegas Grand Prix on November 22, according to reports from The Race and Crash.net.

The New Zealander's Position Strengthened by Brazil

As the 2026 Formula 1 season enters its final three rounds, the driver market is tying up its last loose ends. Among the most closely watched situations is Liam Lawson's future at Racing Bulls — a seat that had appeared uncertain for much of the year but now looks increasingly likely to remain his through 2027.

Reports from The Race on November 9 pointed to Lawson closing in on a new deal, with his performance during the Brazilian Grand Prix weekend cited as a key factor in his favour. While Racing Bulls team principal Alan Permane had spoken of a decision arriving "in the coming weeks," the direction of travel within the team appears to be toward retention rather than change.

An announcement before the Las Vegas Grand Prix on November 22 is considered possible, which would settle a situation that has hung over Lawson since the summer.

The Tsolov Factor

The competing candidate for the seat is Nikola Tsolov, a Red Bull junior driver who has drawn attention within the energy drink company's driver program. Tsolov has been linked to a possible F1 promotion, and there were periods this season when speculation around Racing Bulls' second seat pointed in his direction.

As of the most recent reporting, however, Lawson is viewed as the frontrunner. His experience at Racing Bulls — his second stint with the team — and his consistency over the second half of the 2026 season appear to have tipped the balance. Tsolov remains in the picture for 2028 or beyond, but the 2027 seat looks to be Lawson's to lose at this point.

No official confirmation has been made by either party, and the situation remains subject to change until an announcement is published.

What 2027 Would Mean for Lawson

Lawson's F1 career has been a study in persistence. He made his debut as a Red Bull substitute driver in 2023 before getting a full season at the main team in 2025, then transferred back to Racing Bulls for 2026 — a step down in machinery but a vote of confidence in his long-term development. A confirmed 2027 seat would give him the continuity he needs to build on what has been a solid but unspectacular season.

He has not yet taken a podium finish in his career. With a contract secured and a full off-season to prepare, 2027 represents the kind of stability that could allow him to push toward that milestone.

What's Next

The Las Vegas GP marks a natural deadline for teams to finalise their 2027 lineups — the final three races of the season create urgency around commercial announcements that cannot be delayed much longer. Lawson, Tsolov, and the Racing Bulls team are likely to reach a resolution within the next two weeks.

All signs point toward Lawson. The official word, when it comes, is expected to confirm what the paddock already suspects.
