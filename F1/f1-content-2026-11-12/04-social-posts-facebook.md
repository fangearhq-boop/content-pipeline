### STORY 2: Piastri-Webber Split

**Long-Form Post — 10:00 AM ET**
📢 A major F1 management story broke yesterday, and it hits close to home for Australian racing fans.

Oscar Piastri and manager Mark Webber have confirmed they have parted ways, ending a partnership that began in 2019 — seven full years together. The split is described as mutual and respectful. Webber had already stepped back from his trackside role at the start of the 2026 season, and the two sides ultimately decided the time was right to go in separate directions.

Webber played a critical role in guiding Piastri from Formula 2 champion to full McLaren driver to race winner. That is not nothing. The bond between them shaped one of the most accelerated rises in modern F1 history.

For what comes next: Piastri already has former race engineer Pedro Matos and mental performance coach Emma Murray as part of his support team. His management structure going forward has not been announced.

A bittersweet milestone for both sides. Piastri is about to enter the prime of his career — the chapters ahead should be fascinating.

### STORY 5: Qatar and Abu Dhabi Confirmed

**Long-Form Post — 5:00 PM ET**
✅ F1's season finale is locked in.

Formula 1 has confirmed the 2026 season will end as planned with the Qatar Grand Prix on November 29 and the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix on December 6. FIA president Mohammed Ben Sulayem gave the clearest signal yet that both events will proceed, following speculation that regional tensions in the Middle East could affect the schedule.

Imola, which had been mentioned as a potential fallback venue, was formally ruled out. F1 is committed to its Middle East finale.

The last two races will wrap up the remaining battles in the standings and send one of the most dramatic F1 seasons in recent memory into the history books.
