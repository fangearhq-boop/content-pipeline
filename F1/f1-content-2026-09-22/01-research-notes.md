### STORY 1: Leclerc Engine Penalty — Baku Grid Drop

**Verified Facts (HIGH):**
- Charles Leclerc crashed at the 2026 Italian Grand Prix at Monza (high-speed Parabolica corner)
- The engine in his SF-26 was a brand new power unit (part of Ferrari's second ADUO allocation)
- The engine sustained damage in the crash; deemed unsuitable for high-power sessions (qualifying, race)
- Ferrari considering taking a new/fresh power unit at Baku, which would result in a grid penalty
- Fred Vasseur (Ferrari team principal) said Baku could be "quite suitable" for the grid drop
- Leclerc himself said he will "probably" need to take a penalty "at some point"
- Baku rationale: 2.2 km main straight, multiple overtaking zones — easiest circuit for recovery from grid drop
- Leclerc has dominated Baku qualifying historically (pole at Baku four years running prior to 2026)

### STORY 2: Williams FW48 2.0 at Baku

**Verified Facts (HIGH):**
- Williams will debut its FW48 2.0 (B-spec chassis) at the 2026 Azerbaijan Grand Prix in Baku
- The new chassis uses a revolutionary new carbon manufacturing process
- James Vowles (Williams team principal) has described the car as "almost completely new"
- Original FW48 was significantly overweight when it first debuted; the B-chassis addresses weight and structural issues
- Previous Baku upgrades included medium package at British GP, small revisions at Spa and Budapest, weight reduction at Zandvoort
- Vowles has cautioned the upgrade "won't be enough for points" but calls it foundational
- Drivers: Carlos Sainz and Alex Albon
- The FW48 2.0 chassis will also form the technical foundation for the 2027 Williams

**Sources:** Planet F1, Formula1.com, F1 in Generale, Williams Racing, BVM Sports
