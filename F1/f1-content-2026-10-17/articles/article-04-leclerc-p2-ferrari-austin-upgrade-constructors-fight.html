Leclerc's P2 and Ferrari's Austin Upgrade: The Constructors' Battle That Matters Now

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | October 17, 2026

Charles Leclerc | Ferrari | F1 Constructors Championship | Austin | United States GP

The drivers' championship belongs to Kimi Antonelli barring something extraordinary. But there is another championship still being contested in Formula 1's final six rounds, and at the moment, Ferrari have the clearest path to move up in it.

Charles Leclerc finished second at the Singapore Grand Prix — a strong result that continues a run of solid late-season form for the Scuderia. Ferrari arrive at the United States Grand Prix in Austin with a final development upgrade package. And in the constructors' championship, they sit second with 444 points — trailing Mercedes by 134 but ahead of McLaren in third.

Leclerc's Singapore Performance

Leclerc started from the front row alongside Max Verstappen and ran second throughout Singapore's 62 laps. His result — 7.3 seconds behind Verstappen — was Ferrari's best outcome of the late season. The circuit suited his driving style, and in the title-picture context, finishing ahead of both Mercedes teammates who weren't Antonelli was its own minor victory.

The Ferrari car has shown genuine pace in recent rounds. Leclerc has been extracting the maximum from it, and his championship position — fifth overall on 215 points — understates how competitive he has been in the second half of the season.

The Austin Upgrade

Ferrari are bringing a development upgrade to the Circuit of the Americas, described as a final significant development push for the 2026 car. If it works as intended, it could help Ferrari extract more from COTA — a circuit with a mix of high-speed sections, technical sequences, and heavy braking zones that rewards a well-balanced car.

The upgrade matters not just for outright lap time but for the constructors' championship picture. Every point scored at a Ferrari 1-2 or strong double points finish closes the gap to Mercedes and protects the position over McLaren.

The Constructors' Fight

Mercedes leads the 2026 Constructors' Championship with 578 points. Ferrari are second with 444. The gap is 134 — technically closable across 150 remaining points, but only in extreme circumstances. Mercedes would need to have multiple poor rounds while Ferrari were nearly perfect. That is unlikely.

The more realistic battle is Ferrari versus McLaren for second place in the constructors' standings. McLaren are currently third, and in a championship where prize money and development budget for the following year are tied to final standings, the gap between P2 and P3 matters significantly.

What's Next

Ferrari head to Austin for the United States Grand Prix (October 23-25) with the upgrade package and Leclerc in strong form. The Circuit of the Americas has historically produced competitive races; last year's event was won by Max Verstappen. Ferrari will be hoping Leclerc can use the upgrade to make it a very different result this time.
