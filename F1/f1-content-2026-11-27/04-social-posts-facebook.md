### STORY 3: Perez Grid Penalty at Qatar

**Long-Form Post** (12:00 PM ET)
Window: 12:00 PM

⚠️ PEREZ STARTS QATAR WITH A 5-PLACE GRID PENALTY

Sergio Perez arrives at the Qatar Grand Prix in a difficult position. The Cadillac driver is carrying a five-place grid penalty that will be applied after qualifying, forcing the team into creative strategy work.

Qatar makes this especially complicated. The Lusail Circuit demands a minimum two-stop race this weekend — Pirelli has set a 27-lap maximum stint per compound, which over a 57-lap race forces every driver to make at least two tire changes. Starting from further back already limits strategic flexibility, and the two-stop requirement means Cadillac will need to find time through the pits rather than track position.

It is the latest challenge in a season that has been a rebuild for Perez after his high-profile Red Bull exit. The Cadillac project is a long-term investment, and races like Qatar — where the deck is stacked — tell you a lot about a team's development.
