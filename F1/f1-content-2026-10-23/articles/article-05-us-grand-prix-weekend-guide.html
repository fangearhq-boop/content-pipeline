US Grand Prix Weekend Guide: Schedule, Stakes, and Everything to Watch at COTA

By Marcus Cole | F1 Fanrecap | October 23, 2026

The United States Grand Prix weekend is here. With a world championship potentially on the line Sunday afternoon at the Circuit of the Americas, Austin has become the most important stop on the 2026 Formula 1 calendar. Here is everything you need — the schedule, the standings, the contenders, and the storylines to follow from first practice through the final lap.

The Schedule

All times Central Time:

  • Friday FP1: 12:30 PM CT
  • Friday FP2: 4:00 PM CT
  • Saturday FP3: 11:30 AM CT
  • Saturday Qualifying: 3:00 PM CT
  • Sunday Race: 3:00 PM CT

There is no sprint race at COTA this season — the format is a traditional three-day weekend.

The Championship Standings

Drivers' Championship heading into the US Grand Prix:

  • No. 1: Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes) — 340 points
  • No. 2: George Russell (Mercedes) — 238 points (-102)
  • No. 3: Lewis Hamilton (Ferrari) — 229 points (-111)
  • No. 4: Max Verstappen (Red Bull) — 221 points (-119)

Three races remain after this weekend: Qatar and Abu Dhabi follow the United States Grand Prix. A total of 150 points are still available.

The Title Clinch Scenario

Antonelli can become the 2026 Formula 1 World Champion on Sunday if:

  • He wins the race
  • Russell finishes ninth or lower
  • Hamilton finishes fifth or lower

If all three conditions are met, Antonelli clinches at 20 years and 61 days old — the youngest world champion in Formula 1 history, more than three years younger than Sebastian Vettel's record from 2010.

The Key Storylines

Hamilton and Ferrari: Lewis Hamilton has won at COTA before and arrives as one of the pre-race favorites. Ferrari's pace in qualifying and race conditions has been strong in recent rounds. If Hamilton can secure the front row and fight for the lead, the title stays undecided past Sunday.

Russell's last stand: George Russell needs a perfect weekend, starting with a strong qualifying position. He has to win the race while having Antonelli struggle — a difficult combination to manufacture.

Verstappen the spoiler: Max Verstappen has won the last three US Grand Prix races at COTA. He cannot win the championship this year, but a race victory would prevent Antonelli from clinching in Austin. His car setup on the resurfaced Turns 9–11 section will be key.

Colton Herta in FP1: American driver Colton Herta gets a home drive in the Cadillac during Friday's first practice, replacing Sergio Perez for the session. One of the most anticipated local storylines of the weekend.

The 2026 Regulation Changes at COTA

Two factors make this year's COTA race different from previous editions. First, the Turns 9–11 section has been resurfaced — new tarmac creates a different grip and tire degradation profile that no team has raced on yet, meaning Friday practice data is unusually valuable. Second, the 2026 F1 regulations replaced DRS with an active aero system called Overtake Mode. Instead of a driver-operated flap, the 2026 system manages aerodynamic efficiency automatically based on circuit zones and driver deployment. The change affects how overtaking opportunities develop and how teams approach their race strategy.

What's Next

FP1 runs today at 12:30 PM CT, followed by FP2 at 4:00 PM CT. By Friday evening the initial pecking order will be clear. Qualifying Saturday will set the grid. And Sunday at 3:00 PM CT, the race begins — potentially the race that ends the 2026 Formula 1 championship and installs the youngest world champion in the sport's history.

It is, in every sense, the most important weekend Formula 1 has to offer right now. All of it is happening in Austin.
