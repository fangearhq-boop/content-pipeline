### STORY 3: US Grand Prix Preview

**Long-Form Post**
The Circuit of the Americas opens for practice on Thursday. If you want to understand what the drivers and engineers are working with, here's your guide.

COTA is 5.513 kilometers long with 20 corners and a race distance of 56 laps — 308 km total. It sits in Austin, Texas, and has hosted the US Grand Prix since 2012.

Turn 1 is the circuit's defining feature: a blind uphill approach into a tight hairpin, immediately followed by a series of high-speed sweeping corners through Sector 1. Getting Turn 1 right is essential for lap time and race strategy. It's also where races have been won and lost on the opening lap.

The back section of the circuit has a long straight that rewards top-speed cars and creates DRS overtaking opportunities. The final sector is a series of fast, flowing corners that put a premium on mechanical grip and aerodynamic balance.

COTA rewards all-around cars — strong traction, good high-speed aero, and reliable braking. It's a track that has suited Red Bull well in recent years, which is why Verstappen holds the lap record at 1:36.169 set in 2023.

The 2026 US Grand Prix is a standard format weekend — no sprint race. Practice Thursday, qualifying Saturday, race Sunday October 25. Start time TBA.

COTA gives Red Bull a genuine chance to end their two-year win drought at this specific circuit. And Verstappen, more than any other driver, knows how to make that happen.
