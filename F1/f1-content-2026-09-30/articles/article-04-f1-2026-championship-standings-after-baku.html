F1 2026 Championship Standings After Baku: Antonelli Leads 302-236

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | September 30, 2026

Fifteen races into the 2026 Formula 1 season, Kimi Antonelli holds the Drivers' Championship lead with 302 points — 66 ahead of Mercedes teammate George Russell, who won the Azerbaijan Grand Prix to narrow the gap.

The Standings After Round 15

No. 1 — Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes): 302 points
The championship leader recovered from P16 to finish P5 in Baku, limiting his losses despite Russell's 25-point win haul. With 66 points of breathing room and eight races left, Antonelli is firmly in control. He needs roughly 95 more points to become mathematically unreachable by Russell.

No. 2 — George Russell (Mercedes): 236 points (-66)
Russell won Baku to reduce his deficit from 81 to 66 points. Three victories in 2026. But he needs wins and Antonelli's misfortune — and those can't be manufactured.

No. 3 — Lewis Hamilton (Ferrari): 199 points (-103)
Hamilton is consistent but at 103 points back, his title chances depend entirely on a collapse from both Mercedes drivers.

No. 4 — Lando Norris (McLaren): 186 points (-116)
The reigning champion's title defense took a serious blow in Baku. At 116 points back with 8 races remaining, he needs Antonelli to stop scoring entirely.

No. 5 — Charles Leclerc (Ferrari): 179 points (-123)
Leclerc was fourth in Baku. Fighting for third in the championship, but the title gap makes his own challenge increasingly nominal.

What's Next

The 2026 F1 season resumes at the Singapore Grand Prix on October 9-11. Eight races will determine whether this championship is decided in a sprint or a procession — and Baku has given us every reason to keep watching.
