George Russell Wins the 2026 Azerbaijan Grand Prix in Baku Thriller

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | September 30, 2026

George Russell delivered his finest drive of the 2026 Formula 1 season on Saturday, holding off a relentless Max Verstappen by just 0.196 seconds to win the Azerbaijan Grand Prix at the Baku City Circuit.

It was Russell's third victory of the season and one of the most dramatic races of the year — a chaotic afternoon that saw six retirements, multiple safety car deployments, and a finish that went down to the final corners.

Russell Manages the Chaos

Starting from pole position, Russell built a measured lead through the early laps. The Baku City Circuit at 6.003 kilometers rewards clean laps and punishes mistakes. Russell gave nothing away until Verstappen mounted a charge after the last safety car restart.

The two-time former world champion closed to within striking distance, making the final moments genuinely tense for Mercedes. But Russell kept his composure, defending the inside line and crossing the finish line first by 0.196 seconds.

Verstappen and Hadjar Complete Red Bull Double

Max Verstappen finished second for Red Bull. Isack Hadjar completed a double podium for Red Bull in third on his return from injury. Charles Leclerc took fourth for Ferrari. Kimi Antonelli completed the top five after starting P16 due to a grid penalty — one of the most impressive recovery drives of the season.

Chaos Behind the Leaders

The race's defining moment came when Franco Colapinto locked up at Turn 1 and triggered an incident involving Alpine's Pierre Gasly and McLaren's Lando Norris. All three retired. With six total retirements, Baku proved once again it demands both skill and survival instinct.

Championship Picture After Baku

Russell's win moved him closer to Antonelli — from 81 points down to 66 — but with eight races remaining, the path to the title remains steep. Antonelli's 302-point total and his P5 from P16 recovery suggests a driver who is not about to hand this championship away.

What's Next

Formula 1 heads to Singapore for Round 16 on October 9-11 at the Marina Bay Street Circuit. Russell arrives with momentum. Antonelli arrives with the points lead. And Norris arrives knowing he must win to stay mathematically relevant.
