Norris Hit Out in Baku: Title Defense Takes a Brutal Blow

By Elena Voss | F1 Fanrecap | September 30, 2026

Lando Norris came to Baku needing points. He left with none.

The reigning Formula 1 world champion was eliminated from the 2026 Azerbaijan Grand Prix on Lap 36 when Franco Colapinto locked up on the approach to Turn 1 and collided with Alpine's Pierre Gasly and Norris. All three drivers retired.

The Incident

Colapinto's lock-up came at one of the most unforgiving braking zones on the calendar. Turn 1 at Baku leaves almost no room for error, and the consequences of a braking mistake are immediate. The Alpine driver lost the front end and had nowhere to go, collecting Gasly first and then Norris.

The Championship Cost

Before Baku, Norris was 106 points behind Antonelli with nine races to go. After Baku, the deficit is 116 points with eight races remaining and roughly 208 maximum points still available. To win the championship, Norris needs Antonelli to score no more than 92 points across those eight races — well below Antonelli's 2026 average of nearly 21 points per race.

Oscar Piastri finished P14, scoring nothing. McLaren left Baku without a single championship point from either driver.

The Colapinto Question

Attention turns to Colapinto. His error eliminated two drivers and directly affected the championship standings. The stewards assessed the incident. In a sport where margins matter, the weight of this sits on the Alpine driver regardless of formal verdict.

What's Next

Norris heads to Singapore (October 9-11) with his back against the wall. The Marina Bay Street Circuit is a track where McLaren has performed well. A Norris win combined with an Antonelli non-score would cut the gap to 91 points with seven races remaining. Anything less and the 2026 championship is essentially decided.
