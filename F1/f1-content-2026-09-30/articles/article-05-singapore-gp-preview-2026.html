Singapore GP Preview: Norris Must Win, Antonelli Must Survive

By Elena Voss | F1 Fanrecap | September 30, 2026

After the drama of the Azerbaijan Grand Prix, Formula 1 takes its next step at the Singapore Grand Prix — October 9-11 at the Marina Bay Street Circuit. The 2026 edition arrives with the championship very much still in the balance.

Why Singapore Matters

The Marina Bay Street Circuit is unlike any other on the Formula 1 calendar. A night race through the heart of one of Asia's most dazzling cities, it demands high downforce, mechanical grip, and exceptional braking stability through its 23 corners. Historically, Singapore has been a leveler — teams that dominate elsewhere can struggle here.

Norris's Last Stand?

For Lando Norris, Singapore is essentially a must-win. After his zero-point Baku retirement, his deficit is 116 points with eight races to go. McLaren has performed well at Singapore in recent years, and Norris is a driver who raises his level under pressure.

A Norris win combined with an Antonelli result outside the top 6 would cut the deficit to around 91 points with seven races left — still difficult, but it would extend the conversation into autumn.

Antonelli's Calculation

Antonelli doesn't need to win Singapore. He needs to outscore Norris enough to keep the championship gap insurmountable. If he can finish in the points while Norris wins, he may still be on course for a potential title clinch before the season ends.

Russell's Momentum

George Russell arrives in Singapore on the back of a Baku win. He's 66 points behind Antonelli — closer than Norris, but still requiring a similar combination of wins and misfortune to close the gap.

What's Next

Free practice begins Friday October 9. Qualifying Saturday October 10. Race under the lights Sunday October 11. After the chaos of Baku, Singapore represents another pivotal chapter in the 2026 F1 season.
