Rafael Camara to Haas for 2027: Brazil Is Sending Two Drivers to Formula 1 for the First Time Since 2016

By Marcus Cole | F1 Fanrecap | October 16, 2026

Rafael Camara is set to join the Haas F1 Team for the 2027 Formula 1 season, per multiple reports, completing one of the most anticipated junior driver promotions of the current driver market cycle.

The 2025 Formula 3 champion and Ferrari academy graduate would replace Esteban Ocon, whose contract with Haas expires at the end of 2026. Combined with Gabriel Bortoleto's confirmed seat at Audi — the team that will run as Sauber under full Audi branding in 2027 — Brazil will have two drivers simultaneously competing in Formula 1 for the first time since 2016.

Who Is Rafael Camara?

Camara is a Brazilian driver who has risen through the Formula 1 junior ladder under Ferrari's junior academy program. He won the 2025 FIA Formula 3 Championship, which positioned him as one of the most credible promotion candidates on the grid heading into 2026.

He is currently racing in Formula 2 with Invicta Racing, accumulating the experience and superlicense points required for a full F1 seat. His Ferrari academy backing has provided both resources and a structured development path — and the Haas connection is a natural one, given Haas's long-standing partnership with Ferrari as the team's engine supplier.

Haas's Decision

Haas team principal Ayao Komatsu said in late September 2026 that the team's 2027 lineup would be confirmed "within a couple of weeks." The choice between candidates — which reportedly included Camara, Leonardo Fornaroli, Ryo Hirakawa, and Jack Doohan at various stages — appears to have resolved in Camara's favor, with his F3 title and Ferrari backing proving decisive.

Ocon's exit from Haas leaves one seat open. The team retains its other driver — Nico Hülkenberg, whose future beyond 2026 has been reported as confirmed — which means Camara would join an experienced teammate who knows the team's technical and organizational culture.

Brazil's Return

Brazilian drivers have been absent from the Formula 1 grid since 2016, when Felipe Nasr and Felipe Massa competed simultaneously. The arrival of both Camara at Haas and Bortoleto at Audi represents the largest Brazilian presence on the F1 grid in nearly a decade.

Bortoleto, the 2024 Formula 2 champion, locked in his Audi seat earlier in 2026. He and Camara represent a new generation of Brazilian F1 talent emerging through the European junior ladder — a tradition with deep roots in a country that produced Ayrton Senna, Nelson Piquet, and Felipe Massa.

What's Next

An official Haas announcement is expected imminently — the team's timeline of "a couple of weeks" from late September puts the confirmation window squarely in October. Once confirmed, Camara will transition from Formula 2 to preparing for his F1 debut in 2027 alongside the Haas technical team.

The 2027 F1 grid is nearly complete. Brazil will have a seat at both tables when it is.
