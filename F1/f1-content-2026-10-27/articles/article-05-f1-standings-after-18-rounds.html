F1 Championship Standings After Round 18: Antonelli 365, Verstappen Eliminated, Mercedes vs. the Field

By Elena Voss | F1 Fanrecap | October 27, 2026

With five races remaining and 125 points on offer, here is the complete picture of where every driver and constructor stands heading into the Mexico City Grand Prix.

The 2026 Formula 1 season has 18 rounds in the books and five remaining. Kimi Antonelli is 123 points clear at the top of the drivers' standings. Max Verstappen is mathematically eliminated. And in the constructors' championship, Red Bull and McLaren are locked in a tight battle for third that could define the technical narrative of the year's second half.

Here is the complete championship picture heading into the Mexico City Grand Prix.

Drivers' Championship — Top 5

Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes) leads with 365 points. The 20-year-old Italian has won nine races this season — a number that would be the high-water mark for any season in recent memory — and enters Mexico City needing only to finish ninth or better to become the youngest Formula 1 world champion in the sport's history, breaking Sebastian Vettel's record of 23 years and 134 days set in 2010.

George Russell (Mercedes) sits second with 242 points. A 5-second penalty at COTA dropped him one position and, in doing so, deprived Antonelli of the title clinch in Austin. Russell's own championship mathematics are essentially cosmetic at this point — he cannot close a 123-point gap in five races under any realistic scenario — but he remains focused on securing second place ahead of Lewis Hamilton.

Lewis Hamilton (Ferrari) is third with 241 points, just one point behind Russell. The gap between second and third in the standings is as tight as it has been all season. Hamilton has had strong performances at Ferrari in his first year with the team, and the Mexico City circuit is one where he has historically been competitive.

Max Verstappen (Red Bull) is fourth with 236 points and is now mathematically eliminated from championship contention. Charles Leclerc (Ferrari) is fifth with 233 points, one step below Verstappen, and is also unable to reach Antonelli's total regardless of results.

Constructors' Championship

Mercedes leads the constructors' standings with 607 points, a margin that reflects their car's dominance throughout the season. Ferrari is second with 474 points — a significant gap to the top but a comfortable cushion over the teams below.

The genuinely contested battle in the constructors' is between Red Bull and McLaren, both of whom sit on approximately 355 points. That tie means every remaining race between their drivers carries direct championship significance. Red Bull have Verstappen, who is eliminated from the drivers' title but fully motivated to contribute to the constructors' haul. McLaren have Lando Norris, who has been consistently quick throughout the year. Five races at the season's end will decide third place in the constructors' — and neither team intends to give it up lightly.

What's Next

The remaining 2026 F1 season calendar: Mexico City (October 30–November 1), Brazil, Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi. The championship cycle moves quickly from here — races are closely spaced, and the constructors' battle between Red Bull and McLaren will intensify with every passing round.
