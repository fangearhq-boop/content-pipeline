Norris Wins Brazilian GP as Mercedes Seal 2026 F1 Constructors' Championship

By Ryan Calloway | November 10, 2026

Sunday in São Paulo delivered Formula 1 drama in abundance, and it delivered a title for one of the sport's most storied manufacturers.

Lando Norris drove a commanding race to win the 2026 Brazilian Grand Prix at Interlagos for McLaren, while Mercedes sealed the 2026 Formula 1 Constructors' Championship with George Russell fourth and Kimi Antonelli fifth. It was a race shaped by an early incident from the championship leader, impeccable management from the eventual winner, and a stunning charge through the field from Max Verstappen — who went from 21st on the grid to second at the flag.

Norris Takes Control After Antonelli Spins

Kimi Antonelli had been leading the race when he lost control at Turn 1 on Lap 20, spinning and dropping through the order. That handed the lead to Norris, who had been lurking and waiting for exactly such an opportunity. Once in front, the McLaren driver managed the race with the kind of strategic patience and tire management that has made him one of the most complete drivers in the paddock.

The conditions were demanding — wet at the start and gradually drying — but Norris was composed throughout. He crossed the finish line ahead of Verstappen and Red Bull's Isack Hadjar to claim his second win of the season and McLaren's most significant result of the second half of the year.

Mercedes' Constructors' Title

For Mercedes, Sunday was an achievement worth celebrating. Russell in fourth and Antonelli in fifth — despite the spin — was enough to seal the 2026 Constructors' Championship for the Silver Arrows. It is a remarkable turnaround for a team that has rebuilt its technical direction, and they clinched it at one of Formula 1's most atmospheric venues.

The constructors' title is the team prize, the ultimate measure of a constructor's season-long performance, and Mercedes have earned it comprehensively. Their dual-driver approach — the experience of Russell combined with the explosive rookie season of Antonelli — has been the defining partnership of 2026.

Full Podium and Top 10

Behind Norris and Verstappen, Hadjar completed the podium in third for Red Bull. Russell and Antonelli were fourth and fifth for Mercedes. Oscar Piastri finished sixth for McLaren. Liam Lawson (Racing Bulls) was classified seventh, and Charles Leclerc (Ferrari) eighth after his fifth-place penalty was applied. Lewis Hamilton (Ferrari) finished ninth, and Nico Hulkenberg (Audi) rounded out the top ten.

What's Next

Formula 1 travels to Las Vegas for the next round on November 21, airing on Apple TV. With the Constructors' Championship settled, attention will turn entirely to the Drivers' title. Antonelli holds a 111-point lead with three races remaining — a gap that makes his coronation a question of when, not if. Las Vegas could be the venue where it happens.
