Las Vegas GP Preview: Norris With Momentum, Antonelli Poised for History on the Strip

By Marcus Cole | November 10, 2026

Two weeks after one of the most dramatic Grands Prix of the season, Formula 1 moves from the tropical heat of São Paulo to the neon-lit streets of Las Vegas for the penultimate flyaway stretch of the 2026 campaign.

The 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix takes place November 21 and airs on Apple TV. It arrives carrying the sport's defining narrative of the season: Kimi Antonelli, 111 points clear of Max Verstappen with three races remaining, could become the 2026 Formula 1 World Champion on the Las Vegas Strip.

Antonelli Approaches the Title

The title mathematics are essentially settled. Antonelli leads Verstappen by 111 points, and with the maximum points available per race standing at roughly 25, no one can overhaul him in the remaining rounds. Las Vegas could be the venue where the celebrations happen — a world championship ceremony on the Strip in front of one of Formula 1's most globally visible stages.

For Antonelli, the focus will be on a clean race that makes the numbers official. He has had a season defined by consistency and speed, and there is no reason to expect that to change in Las Vegas.

Norris Arrives With Momentum

Lando Norris heads to Las Vegas as the Brazilian Grand Prix winner, riding a wave of momentum that he will want to convert into another strong result. McLaren has been competitive throughout the second half of the season, and Las Vegas' technical street circuit demands the kind of precision and aggressive setup work that suits their car.

Verstappen, whose performance in Brazil — charging from 21st to 2nd — reminded the paddock that he is still one of the sport's very best, will be looking to continue that momentum at a circuit where he has been competitive in previous editions.

The Circuit

Las Vegas hosts Formula 1 on its famous Strip, a street circuit that runs past some of the city's most recognizable landmarks. The layout demands precision through the tight sections while rewarding straight-line speed on the long stretches past the casinos. Night racing adds an aesthetic dimension that has made Las Vegas one of the sport's most photographed venues.

What's Next

Mark November 21 on the calendar. Apple TV has the broadcast rights, and with a potential championship celebration on the horizon, this is not a race anyone with an interest in Formula 1 will want to miss. Norris with momentum, Verstappen with pride on the line, and Antonelli on the verge of history — Las Vegas will deliver.
