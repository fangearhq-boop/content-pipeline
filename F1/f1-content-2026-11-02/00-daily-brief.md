Kimi Antonelli secured the 2026 Formula 1 World Drivers' Championship at the Mexico City Grand Prix on Sunday, becoming the youngest F1 world champion in history at 20 years and 68 days old. Finishing third while Max Verstappen won the race and Lewis Hamilton took second, Antonelli wrapped up his maiden title with four rounds to spare.
