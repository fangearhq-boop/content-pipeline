### STORY 1: Dutch GP Sprint Weekend — 3 Days to Zandvoort, History on the Line
- **Tier:** 1
- **Category:** F1 Race Preview / Dutch Grand Prix / Zandvoort Sprint
- **Summary:** The Formula 1 paddock has just three days until the most anticipated weekend of the second half of the 2026 season. The 2026 Dutch Grand Prix (August 21-23) at Circuit Zandvoort marks the first-ever Sprint weekend at the circuit — and very likely the last Dutch GP ever, with the race confirmed off the 2027 calendar. The format: Friday August 21 brings the only practice session (FP1, 12:30 CEST) followed immediately by Sprint Qualifying (16:30 CEST). Saturday August 22: Sprint race (12:00 CEST) then Grand Prix Qualifying (16:00 CEST). Sunday August 23: 72-lap Dutch Grand Prix (15:00 CEST). With just one practice hour, teams have no margin for error. Zero 2026-car data exists at Zandvoort heading in.
