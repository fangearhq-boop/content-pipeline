F1 Paddock Notebook: Piastri Splits with Webber, Lawson Stays at Racing Bulls

By Marcus Cole | F1 Fanrecap | November 13, 2026

Between-race updates: Oscar Piastri parts ways with manager Mark Webber, Liam Lawson looks set to remain at Racing Bulls for 2027, and three rounds remain in the 2026 F1 season.

Formula 1 is in an inter-race week between São Paulo and Las Vegas, but the news cycle never stops. Here are the stories from the paddock worth tracking heading into the season's final chapter.

Piastri and Webber Part Ways

Oscar Piastri has confirmed his split from manager Mark Webber. The two have worked together since the Australian driver's junior career, with Webber — the former Red Bull Racing driver and two-time Le Mans winner — serving as a guiding hand through Piastri's progression into Formula 1 and his rise to race winner status with McLaren.

The parting is significant, though Piastri has characterized the transition as amicable. With Piastri entering what will be a defining contract period in his career, the management change signals a new chapter in both his professional life and his Formula 1 trajectory. His next management team has not yet been announced.

Piastri remains one of McLaren's most valuable assets and a key figure in the team's future planning, regardless of who represents him off the track.

Lawson Set to Stay at Racing Bulls

At the other end of the paddock, Liam Lawson looks increasingly certain to retain his seat at Visa Cash App Racing Bulls for the 2027 season. Team principal Alan Permane is understood to favor keeping the New Zealander, who has impressed in his second full season in the midfield.

Red Bull's motorsport adviser Laurent Mekies has reinforced that Racing Bulls makes its own driver decisions independently — an important point given the complex relationship between the Red Bull program's two teams. If Permane's preference holds, Lawson will partner a Red Bull junior through the new season.

Three Rounds to Go

The 2026 Formula 1 season enters its final stretch with Las Vegas (November 20-22), Qatar, and Abu Dhabi remaining. With Kimi Antonelli's Drivers' Championship confirmed and Mercedes having clinched the Constructors' title in Brazil, the remaining rounds will settle the final standings behind the season's champions.

Second in the Drivers' Championship between Lando Norris and Max Verstappen remains unresolved, as does the final shape of the Constructors' standings.

What's Next

The Las Vegas Grand Prix weekend begins November 20 as Formula 1 swaps the rain of Interlagos for the desert heat — and neon glow — of the Strip. With three major paddock stories already developing off-track, the final weeks of 2026 promise to be as eventful in the garage as on it.
