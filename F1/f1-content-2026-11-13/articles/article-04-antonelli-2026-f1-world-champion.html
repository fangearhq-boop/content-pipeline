Kimi Antonelli Is the 2026 F1 World Champion — The Math Says So

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | November 13, 2026

With 390 points and an insurmountable 111-point lead over Max Verstappen, Kimi Antonelli has clinched the 2026 Formula 1 Drivers' Championship — the first title of a career that is only beginning.

The numbers leave no room for debate. Kimi Antonelli is the 2026 Formula 1 World Champion.

After 20 rounds and a season that has seen the young Mercedes driver compete at a level that few expected this early in his career, the mathematics are conclusive. Antonelli holds 390 points. Max Verstappen, in second place, has 279. With three races remaining — Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi — only 75 points are available. No combination of results can allow Verstappen to close a 111-point deficit.

A Historic Achievement

The title represents something more than just a first championship. Antonelli arrived in Formula 1 carrying enormous expectations — the successor to a legacy, in a car built to win, with the resources of one of the sport's greatest teams. Delivering a Drivers' Championship under those conditions is a statement of exceptional quality.

His consistency throughout 2026 has been the defining quality of his title campaign. While others have been brilliant in flashes — Norris has three wins, Verstappen has produced drives like his São Paulo recovery — Antonelli has accumulated points with the relentlessness of a champion. He leaves Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi as a formality and a celebration.

Where He Stands

At 390 points after Round 20, Antonelli's championship total reflects both his own excellence and the quality of the Mercedes package that has also clinched the Constructors' title. The two titles together make 2026 a complete season for the Silver Arrows.

Verstappen, in second at 279 points, has had a remarkable season in his own right. The Red Bull driver's recovery drives and consistent podium finishes have been a highlight of the year, but Antonelli simply had more in every sense — more consistency, more points, and ultimately, more of what it takes to win a world title.

What's Next

The final three races will serve as a coronation tour for Antonelli. Las Vegas on November 20-22, followed by Qatar and Abu Dhabi, offer him the opportunity to push his points total higher and cement the margin of his title win for the history books.

For now, the story is simple: a new champion has emerged in Formula 1. At just 20 years old, Kimi Antonelli has arrived on the sport's biggest stage — and he isn't going anywhere.
