Las Vegas GP 2026 Preview: 50 Laps on the Strip with Everything Still to Fight For

By Elena Voss | F1 Fanrecap | November 13, 2026

Formula 1 arrives in Las Vegas on November 20-22 for the 2026 Heineken Las Vegas Grand Prix — 50 laps of the Strip Circuit with the No. 2 championship spot still undecided and Lando Norris carrying momentum from São Paulo.

The neon is on. The countdown has started. Formula 1 returns to Las Vegas.

One week after Lando Norris dominated the São Paulo Grand Prix, the 2026 F1 field reassembles on the Las Vegas Strip for the penultimate championship battle of the season. The 2026 Heineken Las Vegas Grand Prix runs November 20-22 on the 6.201 km Strip Circuit — 50 laps of one of the most unique venues in motorsport.

The Storylines Going In

Kimi Antonelli arrives as the confirmed 2026 World Champion, with 390 points and a 111-point lead that cannot be closed mathematically. But the Silver Arrows driver has no intention of coasting — podium finishes in Vegas would add to Mercedes' already-sealed Constructors' title margin and reinforce Antonelli's season-long dominance.

Lando Norris heads to Las Vegas with three wins in 2026 and momentum from his São Paulo victory. The McLaren driver is locked in the battle for second in the championship and will need a strong weekend to hold off Max Verstappen.

Verstappen, as always, is the wild card. His 21st-to-second recovery in Brazil was proof that he is in peak form heading into the final rounds. The Las Vegas Strip Circuit has historically suited high-downforce setups, and Red Bull Racing will be working to optimize their package for the unique demands of the venue.

The Circuit

The Las Vegas Strip Circuit winds through 6.201 kilometers of some of the world's most iconic real estate — past casinos, hotels, and entertainment venues that make the event as much spectacle as sport. The track features long, fast straights that reward power units, combined with tight technical sections that test chassis balance and driver precision.

There is no Sprint race this weekend — just the full 50-lap race distance under the Nevada night sky, with qualifying on Saturday setting the grid.

What's Next

Las Vegas is the penultimate chapter in the 2026 season story. After the Strip, the circus moves to Qatar and then Abu Dhabi for the final act. While the Drivers' Championship is settled, the battle for second place between Norris and Verstappen — and the Constructors' standing behind Mercedes — will make every remaining lap worth watching.

The lights go out on November 22. The Las Vegas Grand Prix is seven days away.
