Norris Dominates São Paulo GP as Mercedes Clinch Their Ninth Constructors' Title

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | November 13, 2026

Lando Norris converted pole position into a commanding 14.6-second victory at Interlagos on November 8, while George Russell and Kimi Antonelli's fourth and fifth place finishes sealed Mercedes' ninth Formula 1 Constructors' Championship.

It was a race of two distinct narratives. Up front, Lando Norris was flawless. Behind him, the Silver Arrows quietly secured their place in Formula 1 history.

The 2026 São Paulo Grand Prix ended with Norris crossing the line in 1 hour, 35 minutes and 23.504 seconds — 14.6 seconds clear of a hard-charging Max Verstappen, who had recovered from 21st on the grid to finish second. But the team story belonged to Mercedes. George Russell's fourth place and Kimi Antonelli's fifth delivered the points Mercedes needed to clinch the 2026 Constructors' Championship, adding a ninth title to their extraordinary dynasty.

Norris in Control

Starting from pole position, Norris managed the tricky wet-dry-wet conditions at Interlagos with the calm and precision that has defined his best performances in 2026. This was his third victory of the season, and it showcased his growth as a complete racing driver — capable of leading from the front, managing tire strategies under pressure, and extending his lead when necessary.

The 14.6-second winning margin flattered how controlled the victory was. Norris was never truly threatened for the race lead, even as the late rain reshuffled the strategies behind him.

Mercedes Make History

Nine Constructors' Championships. The number is almost incomprehensible.

Mercedes' run of dominance in Formula 1 — punctuated by periods of competitive challenges — has produced a legacy unlike any other in the modern era. Russell's measured fourth-place drive and Antonelli's fifth in difficult conditions provided exactly what the team needed to close out the championship at Interlagos.

The two-driver combined effort reflects the strength of the Mercedes package this season. Antonelli, the confirmed Drivers' Champion, and Russell have delivered consistently throughout 2026, and the constructors' title is the collective reward for a season of excellence.

Podium and Key Results

Isack Hadjar completed the podium in third place. Lewis Hamilton finished ninth for Ferrari, with Charles Leclerc receiving a time penalty that affected his race result. The full field points will shape the final constructors' standings as the season heads to Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi.

What's Next

Formula 1 travels to Las Vegas next — the penultimate chapter of the Strip Circuit story, with the race running November 20-22 at the Las Vegas Grand Prix. For Norris and McLaren, it's an opportunity to consolidate the team's standing. For Mercedes, it's a victory lap. For Max Verstappen, it's one more chance to remind everyone why he's still the most dangerous driver on the grid.

Nine titles. The Silver Arrows roll on.
