NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post The 2026 Singapore Grand Prix is making history before the race even starts. For the first time ever, Formula 1's Marina Bay Street Circuit is hosting a Sprint weekend. Here's everything you need to know about how this weekend works — and why it's different from any Singapore GP you've watched before. **The schedule (Singapore Time / Eastern Time):** - Friday Practice: 4:30 PM SGT / 4:30 AM ET - Friday Sprint Qualifying: 8:30 PM SGT / 8:30 AM ET - Saturday Sprint Race: 5:00 PM SGT / 5:00 AM ET (21 laps) - Saturday GP Qualifying: 9:00 PM SGT / 9:00 AM ET - Sunday Grand Prix: 8:00 PM SGT / 8:00 AM ET **What's different:** Instead of three practice sessions before qualifying, drivers get exactly ONE hour of practice before Sprint Qualifying begins. That's it. You have to read the car, understand the tyres, and understand the night circuit's characteristics in a single session. Singapore at night is already one of the most demanding weekends on the F1 calendar. The heat, the humidity, the 23 corners, the barriers right at the edge of the track. Now add the sprint format — reduced preparation time and two competitive sessions before the race even starts. This is the sixth and final sprint of the 2026 season. And the grid for Sunday's race will be set by Saturday night's qualifying — not the Sprint result. ### Image Post Caption Tonight is the first-ever Sprint Qualifying at Marina Bay.
