### STORY 5: F1 Calendar September Deadline

**Long-Form Post (8:00 PM ET):**
F1's season-ending calendar is in genuine uncertainty — and we'll know more by September.

Qatar (November 29) and Abu Dhabi (December 6) are still on the books, but both are under review given the ongoing Middle East conflict that already cancelled Bahrain and Saudi Arabia in March.

F1 CEO Stefano Domenicali has been direct: "Nothing will happen before September." That means the mid-September deadline is when the sport will decide whether those two rounds — which typically decide the championship — can proceed.

If they can't go ahead, F1 has confirmed it will end the season in Europe. Imola is named as the leading replacement venue, with potentially Las Vegas emerging as another option.

The championship battle between Antonelli, Hamilton, and Russell could be decided somewhere very different from where anyone expected.
