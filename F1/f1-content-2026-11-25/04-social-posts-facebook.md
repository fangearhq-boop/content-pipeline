**Long-Form Post**
**Window:** 8:00 AM ET

Meanwhile, Camara handled the incident with composure and impressed enough to earn confirmation of his Haas seat for both remaining 2026 races and a full-time drive in 2027.

It is a complicated situation for Perez at Red Bull. A grid drop in Qatar, combined with the growing speculation about his future at the team, adds pressure to what should be a straightforward run to the season's end.
