One Seat Left: The 2027 F1 Grid Is Almost Set as Racing Bulls Confirm Lawson and Lindblad

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | November 25, 2026

The 2027 Formula 1 driver market is almost fully resolved. With Racing Bulls confirming Liam Lawson and Arvid Lindblad for next season, 21 of the 22 available grid positions are now filled. Only one seat remains unannounced.

That seat is at Haas. The team has confirmed Rafael Camara for 2027 — his continued presence following his Las Vegas debut was formalized this week — but the second slot remains open. It is widely regarded as Ollie Bearman's to lose.

Bearman as the Final Piece

Bearman has held the second Haas seat throughout 2026 and has been a consistent presence in the team's midfield operation. No announcement has been made, and no explanation has been given for the delay, but the strong expectation across the paddock is that Bearman will be confirmed before the season ends.

If Bearman is confirmed, the 2027 grid is complete — a relatively rare moment of near-total clarity heading into a new season at a point in the calendar still two races from conclusion.

Cadillac's Open Conversation

The other ongoing storyline in the driver market involves Cadillac. The American team's second seat appears to remain unresolved, with Colton Herta linked as a potential debut driver for the 2027 season.

A Herta signing would carry significant weight beyond the sporting result. An American driver in a full-time Formula 1 seat at an American constructor would represent a storyline the sport has been building toward for years. Cadillac has the narrative incentive to make it happen, and Herta's name has been circling F1 paddock conversations long enough to warrant being taken seriously as a candidate.

Valtteri Bottas's status at the team into 2027 remains unclear, which provides the opening for a lineup change that Herta could potentially fill.

The Rest of the Grid

Away from the last open slot, the 2027 lineup is defined by a mix of continuity and fresh faces at the junior end. Max Verstappen's extension with Red Bull runs through 2030, anchoring the top of the grid. Isack Hadjar is confirmed at Racing Bulls alongside the newly announced Lawson, giving the Red Bull program a clear developmental path at both team levels.

Lawson and Lindblad represent a significant shift at Racing Bulls. Lindblad, one of the more anticipated young talents in the Red Bull junior program, moves into a race seat alongside a proven race winner in Lawson, who already scored points and victories during his initial stint in the series.

The rest of the announced lineup reflects the broader market consolidation that tends to happen this late in the season, with most teams having locked in their pairings months ago.

What's Next

The Haas announcement is expected before or during the Qatar weekend, which begins Thursday at Lusail. A Bearman confirmation would close the final open chapter of a driver market that has otherwise moved quickly and cleanly by modern Formula 1 standards.

The Cadillac situation may linger into the off-season, particularly if the team is still negotiating. Either way, the 2027 grid is close enough to completion that the remaining pieces feel like formalities rather than genuine uncertainty — with the possible exception of what Cadillac decides to do with their second seat.
