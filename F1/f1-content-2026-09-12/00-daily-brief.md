### STORY 2: Ferrari Closes the Gap at Madring — Can Leclerc or Hamilton Take Pole? (T1 FOLLOW UP)

A front-row lockout would mark a major shift in the championship dynamics with 10 races remaining.

### STORY 3: Madring Deep Dive — What Makes F1's Brand-New Madrid Circuit Special (T2 NEW)
The Madring is F1's newest circuit, making its debut this weekend for the first time. Built around the IFEMA Madrid exhibition center, the 5.47km hybrid street/purpose-built circuit features 22 corners, two tunnel sections, and its signature turn: La Monumental — a 550-meter banked corner at Turn 12 with a 24% gradient reaching 13.5 degrees of banking. F1 is racing in the Madrid region for the first time since 1981.

### STORY 4: George Russell's Madring Mission — Stopping Antonelli at His Own Circuit (T2 FOLLOW UP)

Russell trails by 66 points in the championship and needs to accumulate points consistently through the final 10 rounds to have any realistic title chance. A pole and win in Madrid would be a critical step. His last pole came at Barcelona-Catalunya earlier this season.
