Esteban Ocon's Haas Seat Is Under Threat — And Oliver Bearman Is Why

By Elena Voss | F1 Fanrecap | August 7, 2026

F1 / Driver Market / Haas / Esteban Ocon / Oliver Bearman

The summer break is when Formula 1 teams make their difficult conversations official. For Haas, that conversation may be about whether Esteban Ocon has a seat in 2027.

Through eleven races of the 2026 season, Ocon has scored three points. His teammate Oliver Bearman — a 20-year-old in his first full Formula 1 season — has scored approximately six times that amount. Haas is reportedly already looking at alternatives for the 2027 seat alongside Bearman, with Rafael Camara and Leonardo Fornaroli named as candidates by Motorsport.com and PlanetF1.

The Bearman Problem

It is not quite fair to call this the "Bearman problem" for Ocon — it is a Bearman success, and the consequences happen to fall on Ocon. Bearman has been quietly excellent this season. A teenager who made his F1 debut under emergency circumstances in 2024, he has transitioned into a full season without the growing pains that often characterize first-year drivers.

His approximately 18 points stand against Ocon's three across the same eleven races. That is not a gap that reflects different fortune or machinery — they drive identical cars. It reflects how each driver has approached and extracted performance from the same package.

Former Haas boss Guenther Steiner, who has as much insight into what the team values as anyone outside its current leadership, has publicly offered what was described as a "blunt verdict" on Ocon's Haas future. Steiner's bluntness is well-documented. A negative assessment from him carries weight.

The Ocon Context

Esteban Ocon is not a driver without credentials. He has race wins in his career, most famously at the 2021 Hungarian Grand Prix for Alpine. He is a known, proven Formula 1 commodity — which is precisely why his 2026 performance at Haas is striking. He is not a rookie discovering his limits. He is a veteran failing to produce results that justify his seat against a rookie who is producing them.

The nine remaining races of the season give Ocon a meaningful window to change the narrative. But nine grands prix is also a limited amount of time to close a points gap of this scale while simultaneously demonstrating that the team's investment in keeping him was correct.

The Wider Picture

Ocon's situation is part of a larger driver market story. Reports suggest nine Formula 1 seats are under threat heading into summer break negotiations. The pattern is consistent: younger, cheaper drivers — particularly those with backing from manufacturer academies — are displacing established names whose results do not justify their cost.

Camara and Fornaroli represent that category. They are talented, young, and hungry for an opportunity. Ocon once was those things too. The sport does not care about what you were.

What's Next

The Dutch GP on August 21 begins the second half of Ocon's audition. Watch his qualifying and race performances over the final nine rounds relative to Bearman's — the gap in those numbers will determine whether Haas makes the call to move on before the 2027 contract window closes. Any driver market announcement from Haas before the Abu Dhabi finale in December should be treated as a significant signal.
