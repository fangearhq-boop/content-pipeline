### STORY 1: FERRARI UNVEILS SCHUMACHER TRIBUTE LIVERY FOR MONZA ITALIAN GP
**Tier:** 1
**Status:** NEW
**Summary:** Ferrari revealed a special Michael Schumacher tribute livery for the SF-26 to be run at the Italian Grand Prix (September 4-6, Monza). The design is inspired by the F310, featuring red and black detailing and gold wheels. Race suits feature seven stars (one per Schumacher world title) plus his personal logo on Hamilton's and Leclerc's suits. This marks 30 years since Schumacher's first Ferrari season. Ferrari also plans to debut its ADUO-2 power unit upgrade (+15hp) at Monza.
**Why it matters:** The Schumacher tribute is a massive marketing and emotional moment at Ferrari's home race. The ADUO-2 PU gives Hamilton a shot at a podium while Antonelli starts at the back from his grid penalty.

### STORY 5: MONACO APPEAL UPDATE — VERDICT STILL PENDING, DECISION EXPECTED MID-SEPTEMBER
**Tier:** 2
**Status:** FOLLOW UP
**Summary:** The International Court of Appeal (ICA) heard the Monaco Grand Prix appeal from McLaren and Red Bull on August 25 in Paris. No verdict has been issued. A decision is expected in approximately 2-3 weeks from the Aug 25 hearing, putting the ruling around mid-September. The case concerns Pierre Gasly's P3 finish and FOM's admission of a pitlane distance measurement error. Gasly currently holds P3 pending the ruling.
