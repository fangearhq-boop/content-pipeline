More than two months after the Monaco Grand Prix, the controversy over Pierre Gasly's third-place finish still has no final resolution.

The International Court of Appeal heard arguments from McLaren and Red Bull on August 25 in Paris, but no verdict was issued. A decision is expected in approximately two to three weeks from the hearing date — placing the likely ruling around mid-September, as F1 heads into the run of back-to-back grands prix following Monza.

Gasly — driving for Alpine — was penalized and then reclassified, ultimately holding onto P3. McLaren and Red Bull believe the ruling was incorrect and that the error-based penalty shouldn't stand.

If the appeal succeeds, Monaco race results could be adjusted, which means championship points shift retroactively. In a season where 59 points separates the leader from second place, any redistribution matters.

Gasly holds P3 for now. The ICA holds the pen. And the verdict could land right in the middle of F1's most critical stretch of the season.
