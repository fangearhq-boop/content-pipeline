Some F1 Team Staff to Skip Qatar and Abu Dhabi Finale Over Safety Concerns

By Marcus Cole | November 22, 2026 | F1 Fanrecap

Reports emerging this week indicate that some Formula 1 team staff have told their employers they will not travel to Qatar and Abu Dhabi for the season's final two rounds due to safety concerns. The reports, which surfaced around November 20, add an unusual backdrop to what should be a straightforward championship finale.

No teams have been named publicly in the reports, and the number of staff members involved and the specific nature of their concerns have not been confirmed. The situation represents a developing story that will likely require clarification from teams and the sport's governing body before the paddock convenes in Qatar on November 27.

Context and Implications

Formula 1 regularly navigates the complexity of racing in regions with varying security situations and political climates. Teams typically have established protocols for assessing risk and accommodating staff who have concerns about travel to specific destinations.

Qatar has hosted grands prix since 2021 and Abu Dhabi has been a mainstay of the calendar for more than a decade. Both venues have consistently delivered well-organized events, and F1 has not indicated any change to the schedule for either race.

For teams managing staffing in the paddock, any significant reduction in personnel at a venue — even among a subset of team members — creates operational challenges during the compressed final weeks of a championship-deciding campaign.

What's Next

The situation will likely become clearer as teams begin traveling to Qatar this coming week. If the reports prove accurate and a meaningful number of team staff elect to remain at home, the story could grow beyond a footnote in the season's final chapter. F1 Fanrecap will continue monitoring developments ahead of the Qatar Grand Prix on November 29.
