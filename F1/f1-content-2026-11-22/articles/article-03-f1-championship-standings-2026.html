2026 F1 Championship Standings After Las Vegas: Antonelli Champion, Battle for P2 Goes to Qatar

By Elena Voss | November 22, 2026 | F1 Fanrecap

Kimi Antonelli is the 2026 Formula 1 World Champion. That much has been settled for weeks. What the Las Vegas Grand Prix confirmed is that the battle for every other position in the standings remains intensely, almost mathematically unpredictable as the season enters its final two rounds.

Here is where the championship stands after Round 21 at the Las Vegas Strip Circuit:

Drivers' Championship

Antonelli leads on 402 points — an uncatchable total with only 86 points remaining. Max Verstappen's victory on Saturday moved him to 304 points and second place, 98 behind the champion but 37 clear of third-placed Lewis Hamilton on 267.

The positions behind Hamilton are where the tension lives. George Russell and Lando Norris are both on 264 points — tied for fourth. Three points separate Hamilton from the two drivers chasing him, with Norris's second-place Las Vegas finish closing the gap significantly.

With 43 points available to the race winner in each of the remaining two rounds, any driver from second to fifth could realistically finish the season in any position from second through fifth. This is what a live championship battle looks like at its most compressed.

Constructors' Championship

Mercedes leads the constructors' standings on 666 points, a margin that reflects their season-long consistency and Antonelli's title-winning campaign. Ferrari holds second on 517 points, though that gap — 149 points — means the constructors' title is also settled.

The fight that matters is between Red Bull and McLaren. Red Bull leads on 440 points; McLaren has 431 after Norris and Piastri's Las Vegas double podium closed the gap to just nine. This is a battle that will almost certainly go to Abu Dhabi to be resolved.

What's Next

The penultimate round is the Qatar Grand Prix at Lusail International Circuit on November 29. Every driver from second to fifth in the championship will need to maximize their points haul. Every team from third to fourth in the constructors' standings faces the same pressure. Two rounds left. Everything still to play for.
