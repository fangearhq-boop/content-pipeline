Verstappen Wins Las Vegas Grand Prix for Third Time, Norris and Piastri Complete McLaren Double Podium

By Ryan Calloway | November 22, 2026 | F1 Fanrecap

Max Verstappen claimed victory at the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix on Saturday night, crossing the finish line in 1:27:39.624 to claim his third win on the iconic Strip circuit. Lando Norris finished 0.639 seconds behind in second, with Oscar Piastri completing a McLaren double podium in third — just 1.529 seconds off the pace.

Verstappen controlled much of the 50-lap race with the composed authority that has defined his championship-era performances, holding off a sustained McLaren challenge and managing a chaotic race that featured retirements, penalties, and the kind of drama that only Las Vegas seems to produce. Kimi Antonelli, the confirmed 2026 World Champion, finished fourth.

Verstappen Moves to Second in the Championship

The victory brings Verstappen's season total to 304 points, moving him 37 clear of Lewis Hamilton in the battle for championship runner-up and firmly into second place in the standings. With two rounds remaining — Qatar on November 29 and Abu Dhabi on December 6 — Verstappen has positioned himself as the most likely challenger to Antonelli's crown, even if the title itself is long decided.

The result continues Verstappen's dominance of this particular venue. His three Las Vegas victories reflect both his technical proficiency in managing Red Bull's machinery on the circuit's long straights and his ability to execute flawlessly when the pressure is highest.

McLaren Double Podium Tightens Constructors' Fight

Norris in second and Piastri in third was the tactical outcome McLaren needed. The double podium haul leaves the team just nine points behind Red Bull in the constructors' standings — 431 to 440 — with two races remaining. The gap is small enough that McLaren could realistically overhaul their rivals heading into Abu Dhabi.

Pierre Gasly finished fifth, Franco Colapinto sixth, Lewis Hamilton seventh for Ferrari, Carlos Sainz eighth, George Russell ninth, and Charles Leclerc tenth to round out the top 10. Arvid Lindblad and Sergio Perez did not finish, while Alex Albon did not start.

What's Next

The F1 circus moves to Lusail International Circuit in Qatar for Round 22 on November 29. For Verstappen, the target is consolidating second in the championship. For McLaren, the constructors' fight is very much alive. And for Ferrari, after another difficult weekend in the desert, the pressure to rediscover performance before the season closes is mounting rapidly.
