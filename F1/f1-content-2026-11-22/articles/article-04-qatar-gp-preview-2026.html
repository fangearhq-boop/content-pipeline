Qatar Grand Prix 2026 Preview: What to Watch at Lusail as Championship Climax Approaches

By Ryan Calloway | November 22, 2026 | F1 Fanrecap

Formula 1's penultimate round of 2026 heads to Qatar's Lusail International Circuit on November 29, and the stakes could not be higher. With five drivers separated by 40 points in the battle for second through fifth in the championship, and Red Bull holding just a nine-point advantage over McLaren in the constructors' standings, the Qatar Grand Prix carries enormous significance before the season closes in Abu Dhabi.

The Lusail Circuit

The Lusail International Circuit sits on the outskirts of Doha and is one of the more physically demanding venues on the F1 calendar. The track spans approximately 5.4 kilometers, runs clockwise over 57 laps and 16 turns, and is raced under floodlights — producing the kind of dramatic imagery that has become synonymous with F1's Middle Eastern finale stretch.

The extreme heat at Lusail places significant demands on both drivers and machinery. Tire degradation is typically high, strategy is a critical variable, and the physical toll on drivers through 57 laps in those conditions can determine who has the energy to execute in the closing stages. The lap record of 1:23.196, set when F1 first raced here in 2021, reflects the circuit's ability to produce fast, challenging racing.

What to Watch

The championship battle between Verstappen, Hamilton, Russell and Norris is the primary storyline. Verstappen needs to stay ahead of Hamilton and the two drivers tied on 264 to cement second place. Hamilton, just three points clear of Russell and Norris, must qualify well and avoid the kind of midfield start that cost him in Las Vegas.

McLaren's constructors' fight with Red Bull is the other major narrative. Nine points between them entering Qatar means a strong double points finish from either team could effectively settle the question before Abu Dhabi. Ferrari, meanwhile, needs to respond after back-to-back difficult weekends and demonstrate that their machinery can perform at the front when the setup comes together on a different type of circuit.

What's Next

Free practice begins Friday, November 27. Qualifying follows Saturday the 28th, with the race starting at 16:00 GMT on Sunday the 29th. After Qatar, the final race of the 2026 season takes place at Abu Dhabi on December 6, where whatever remains undecided will be settled.
