F1 Season Finale Calendar in Limbo: Domenicali's Mid-September Deadline Looms

By Elena Voss | F1 Fanrecap | September 8, 2026

FOM CEO Stefano Domenicali says a decision on whether Qatar and Abu Dhabi will host the 2026 F1 season finale is coming by mid-September, with Imola emerging as the leading European contingency option.

The 2026 Formula 1 season has already been reshaped by geopolitical events. Now its final chapters remain uncertain.

FOM CEO Stefano Domenicali has stated publicly that a decision on the season finale venues — Qatar and Abu Dhabi — will be made "by mid-September." The backdrop: ongoing US military operations near the Strait of Hormuz and broader regional instability in the Middle East have made the logistics and security framework for those events increasingly difficult to guarantee.

With the Madrid Grand Prix underway this weekend, the mid-September window Domenicali referenced is essentially now.

What's Already Been Cancelled

The 2026 F1 calendar has already seen significant disruption. The Bahrain Grand Prix and the Saudi Arabian Grand Prix have both been cancelled from the original schedule due to regional security concerns. The Bahrain race was rerouted to the Sepang circuit in Malaysia, taking place October 2-4 in what would have been an administratively complex reorganization of facilities, logistics, and broadcasting arrangements.

The Saudi cancellation had no direct replacement. Its slot on the calendar was absorbed, reducing the season total from its original planned number of rounds.

The question now is whether Qatar and Abu Dhabi will follow a similar path, or whether the security situation stabilizes sufficiently to allow both races to proceed as originally scheduled.

Qatar and Abu Dhabi — The Remaining Middle East Rounds

Qatar's Lusail International Circuit is scheduled to host the Formula 1 Qatar Grand Prix on November 27-29. The Abu Dhabi Grand Prix at Yas Marina follows December 4-6 — the traditional season finale, which carries the weight of championship celebrations, end-of-season driver movement announcements, and the emotional conclusion to the racing calendar.

Both venues are confirmed on the current calendar. But confirmation and certainty are not the same thing in 2026's geopolitical environment. FOM's security protocols and logistics teams require substantial advance planning, and the mid-September decision deadline reflects the minimum lead time needed to either confirm the events or execute contingency arrangements.

The Imola Option

According to multiple reports, Imola is the leading contingency option if one or both Middle East rounds need to be replaced. The Autodromo Enzo e Dino Ferrari in Imola, Italy — which returned to the F1 calendar in recent years and has always been a fan favorite — has the infrastructure, the FIA homologation, and the logistical capacity to host a Grand Prix on shorter notice than most alternatives.

Portimao's Algarve International Circuit in Portugal is also cited as a possibility. Like Imola, it has hosted F1 races in the modern era and retains the infrastructure required for a Formula 1 event.

Either option would mean an unusual European conclusion to the F1 season — a departure from the traditional Middle Eastern sunset finale that has become synonymous with championship celebrations in recent years.

What's Next

Domenicali's mid-September deadline means the announcement could come as early as this week, during or immediately after the Madrid Grand Prix. If Qatar and Abu Dhabi are confirmed, the season proceeds as scheduled with those rounds on November 27 and December 4. If replacements are announced, Imola or Portimao would likely appear on the revised calendar within days of the decision.
