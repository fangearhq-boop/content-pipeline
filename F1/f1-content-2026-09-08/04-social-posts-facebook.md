### STORY 1: Leclerc Cleared for Madrid

Breathe easy, Ferrari fans. Charles Leclerc has been cleared to race in Madrid.

For a driver of Leclerc's caliber — and for a Ferrari team that desperately needs points after Monza — this is the best possible news heading into Spain.

### STORY 2: Madrid Grand Prix — Madring Debut

**FB Long-Form Post**
Window: 12:00 PM ET

This weekend, Formula 1 does something it hasn't done in years: race on a completely new circuit for the first time.

The Madring at IFEMA Madrid makes its Formula 1 debut this weekend (September 11-13), hosting the inaugural Madrid Grand Prix. The purpose-built hybrid circuit sits at Madrid's famous IFEMA trade fair complex and represents Spain's second Grand Prix venue alongside the long-established Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya.

The circuit was designed to handle the demands of the modern F1 car — combining high-speed sections with technical chicanes and multiple overtaking opportunities. It passed its FIA track inspection earlier this year and has been described as one of the most spectator-friendly circuits on the calendar, with grandstands offering sightlines across multiple corners simultaneously.

For fans in Spain and around the world, this is a moment to mark: a brand-new Formula 1 venue opening its gates for the very first time.

Free Practice: Friday, September 11
Qualifying: Saturday, September 12
Race: Sunday, September 13

But Russell starts the Madrid weekend motivated and hungry. He knows the championship window is narrowing. Expect an intense intra-team battle at Madring.

### STORY 5: Season Finale Calendar

**FB Long-Form Post**
Window: 8:00 PM ET

The 2026 Formula 1 season has already lost two rounds — Bahrain and Saudi Arabia were cancelled due to Middle East instability, with the Bahrain event rerouted to Malaysia's Sepang circuit in October. Now, the season finale venues are under the spotlight.

FOM CEO Stefano Domenicali has said a decision on whether Qatar (November 27-29, Lusail) and Abu Dhabi (December 4-6, Yas Marina) will remain on the calendar will come "by mid-September." The backdrop: ongoing US military operations near the Strait of Hormuz and broader regional uncertainty.

The leading contingency option is Imola, Italy — a beloved venue that always draws strong fan sentiment and could host a replacement race on relatively short notice. Portimao in Portugal is also cited as a possibility.

The mid-September timing means the announcement could come as early as next week, during or immediately after the Madrid Grand Prix weekend.

Stay tuned. The 2026 F1 calendar is still being written.
