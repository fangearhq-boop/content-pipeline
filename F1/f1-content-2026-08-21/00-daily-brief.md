## Top Stories

### STORY 1: Dutch GP FP1 — Ferrari Leads at Zandvoort, Sprint Qualifying Underway (Tier 1)
Ferrari topped the timing sheets in the only practice session of the Dutch Grand Prix weekend — a 60-minute FP1 on Friday morning at Zandvoort. Mercedes was also in the mix. With only one practice session before Sprint Qualifying (16:30 CEST), teams are working with near-zero setup data on the new 2026 cars at this circuit. Sprint Qualifying results to follow later today. This is the final Dutch Grand Prix at Zandvoort — the circuit exits the F1 calendar after this weekend.

### STORY 2: BREAKING — Hadjar Out With Wrist Injury, Lawson Races Red Bull, Tsunoda Returns (Tier 1)
Isack Hadjar suffered a wrist injury during the summer break — sustained during a boxing gym session — and has been ruled out of the Dutch Grand Prix. Liam Lawson steps up to race the No. 1 Red Bull seat. In the most remarkable subplot, Yuki Tsunoda — who was displaced from Red Bull to make way for Hadjar at the start of 2026 — makes a surprise return to the grid, racing for Racing Bulls alongside Arvid Lindblad. A dramatic reshuffle at Zandvoort.

### STORY 3: Verstappen's 2030 Extension — Quotes, Context, What It Really Means (Tier 2)
Max Verstappen announced his Red Bull contract extension through 2030 on Thursday, ahead of his home race. Verstappen: "I am really pleased with the contract extension. We have the best people and I'm excited to keep working together with everyone to get back to the top again." Team principal Laurent Mekies: "Having Max continue with us and retaining the best driver on the grid is fantastic news." The extension kills exit clauses that had fueled 18 months of speculation.

### STORY 4: Carlos Sainz Williams Multi-Year — Why He Chose to Stay (Tier 2)
Carlos Sainz signed a multi-year extension with Williams Racing for 2027 and beyond, choosing the Grove-based team over credible alternatives including Audi and Aston Martin. The decision is a vote of confidence in team principal James Vowles' long-term project. Sainz joined Williams in 2025, scored two podiums in his debut season, and helped the team finish 5th in the Constructors' Championship that year. Teammate Alex Albon also extended one day earlier.

### STORY 5: Antonelli's 50-Point Lead Entering Round 12 (Tier 2)
Kimi Antonelli leads the 2026 F1 Drivers' Championship with 219 points heading into the Dutch Grand Prix — 50 clear of Lewis Hamilton (169 pts, Ferrari) and 59 ahead of George Russell (160 pts, Mercedes). With 12 races remaining and 316 points still available, the title is not decided, but Antonelli's consistency — 6 wins, 6 poles, 9 podiums in 11 rounds — has established him as a clear favorite to become F1's youngest world champion.
