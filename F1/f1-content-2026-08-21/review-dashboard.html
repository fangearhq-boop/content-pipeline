NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Window: 12:00 PM ET The most analyzed, debated, and speculated-about contract situation in recent Formula 1 history has finally come to an end. Max Verstappen is staying at Red Bull. Through 2030. He announced the extension on Thursday, perfectly timed ahead of his home Dutch Grand Prix. The previous deal — signed before the 2026 season — ran through 2028 and contained performance-based exit clauses that had fueled 18 months of intense speculation about where Verstappen might go. McLaren, Mercedes, and others were linked at various points. McLaren's Zak Brown had already confirmed talks "didn't go anywhere." Now those clauses are dead. Verstappen isn't going anywhere. His quote on the extension: "I am really pleased with the contract extension. We have the best people and I'm excited to keep working together with everyone to get back to the top again." Team principal Laurent Mekies responded: "Having Max continue with us and retaining the best driver on the grid is fantastic news for everyone at Red Bull, Oracle Red Bull Racing, as well as F1 and motorsport as a whole." There's something revealing about the phrase "get back to the top again." Red Bull dominated 2023 and early 2024; the 2026 regulation change disrupted them, and Verstappen himself hinted earlier this year about the possibility of stepping away from F1 over dissatisfaction with the new-spec cars. The fact that he's committing to 2030 suggests he believes Red Bull has a genuine path back to the front. This weekend at Zandvoort — his home race, the first race since the announcement — will be fascinating to watch. ---

Sainz, 32, is betting that trajectory continues. He knows Vowles. He's seen the investment Williams is making. And having won Grands Prix with Ferrari and driven alongside champions throughout his career, he presumably has a clear sense of where this team is headed. The silly season's biggest open questions are now resolved: Ferrari (Hamilton/Leclerc), McLaren (Norris/Piastri), Mercedes (Russell/Antonelli), Red Bull (Verstappen/pending), Williams (Sainz/Albon) for 2027.
