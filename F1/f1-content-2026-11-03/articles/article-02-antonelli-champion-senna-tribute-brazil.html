Antonelli Arrives at Interlagos as Champion, to Wear Special Senna Tribute Helmet

By Ryan Calloway | November 3, 2026

Thirty-five years after Ayrton Senna crossed the finish line at Interlagos to win his home grand prix, a new Formula 1 World Champion is arriving at that same circuit for the first time wearing the crown.

Kimi Antonelli, the 2026 F1 Drivers' World Champion, will make his first appearance as title holder at this weekend's São Paulo Grand Prix — and he will do so in a specially designed helmet honouring Ayrton Senna. It is the kind of moment the sport's most romantic moments are made of: a new era touching the history of the old.

The Significance of Interlagos

There are circuits on the Formula 1 calendar, and then there is Interlagos. The Autódromo José Carlos Pace has been the setting for some of the sport's most dramatic and emotionally charged moments, and its connection to Ayrton Senna — Brazil's most celebrated driver and arguably the greatest talent the sport has ever produced — gives it a weight that no other track can replicate.

Senna's 1991 victory at Interlagos remains one of the iconic images of Formula 1 history. He drove much of the final lap in gear-shift difficulties, arriving at the line in tears, unable to lift his arms for the traditional victory salute. The crowd's reaction was unlike anything the sport had seen before or since.

Antonelli will race at that circuit wearing a helmet designed in Senna's honour, marking the 35th anniversary of that victory. The tribute is both a sporting gesture and a cultural one — a young Italian champion acknowledging the lineage he is now part of.

A Champion's First Race

At just 20 years old, Antonelli is now Formula 1's youngest World Champion, a record previously held by drivers whose careers became the measuring stick for every subsequent generation. His 2026 season has been extraordinary by any measure, and arriving in Brazil with the championship already secured gives the weekend a celebratory atmosphere that few drivers ever get to experience.

But Antonelli will not be coasting. Mercedes also has the opportunity to clinch the 2026 Constructors' Championship at São Paulo, giving the team — and both of its drivers — reason to push hard from the first moment of practice.

Brazil's Passion for Formula 1

Brazilian fans have always embraced Formula 1 with a passion that few other nations match. Since Senna's era, the Interlagos crowd has greeted every champion with the kind of energy that turns a sporting event into something closer to a national celebration. Antonelli's arrival — young, Italian, wearing Senna's colours — will likely generate a reaction unlike anything his previous race weekends have produced.

There is something fitting about a new champion paying tribute to a past legend in front of a crowd that loves both the sport and the history it carries.

What's Next

The São Paulo Grand Prix begins Friday, November 6 with the first practice sessions. The race itself runs Sunday, November 8 at 12:00 PM ET. Thunderstorms are forecast for Saturday qualifying, adding an unpredictable element to an already emotionally loaded weekend.

For Antonelli, whatever happens on the track, this weekend will be one he carries with him for the rest of his career. Champions remember their first race in the crown. At Interlagos, with Senna on his helmet, the memory is already being made.
