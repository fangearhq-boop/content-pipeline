## Story Summaries

### STORY 1: Madrid Grand Prix Debut
The 2026 Spanish Grand Prix at Madring is the first Formula 1 race in Madrid and the circuit's debut on the World Championship calendar — making it F1's 81st circuit to host a Grand Prix. The race weekend begins Friday, September 11 (FP1 at 1:30 PM local / 7:30 AM ET; FP2 at 5:00 PM local / 11:00 AM ET). Qualifying is Saturday, September 12 at 4:00 PM local / 10:00 AM ET. The race is Sunday, September 13 at 3:00 PM local / 9:00 AM ET. The 5.47 km hybrid street/permanent circuit at IFEMA Madrid features 22 turns, 340 kph straight-line speeds, and the longest banked corner on the F1 calendar.

### STORY 2: Antonelli's Championship Momentum
Kimi Antonelli enters the Madrid Grand Prix leading the 2026 Drivers' Championship with 267 points — 66 ahead of teammate George Russell (201), 76 ahead of Lewis Hamilton (191), and 96 ahead of Lando Norris (171). The 20-year-old Mercedes driver has won back-to-back Grands Prix (Dutch GP + Italian GP from 19th on the grid). A third consecutive win in Madrid would put the championship out of reach for all but Russell. Mercedes leads the Constructors' Championship by 87 points over Ferrari.

### STORY 3: Leclerc Cleared for Madring
Charles Leclerc received full medical clearance from Dr. Isabel Ornaque (Madring medical director) to race in Madrid after sustaining a 23G crash at Monza's Parabolica on Lap 2 of the Italian Grand Prix, which caused right-eye vision issues. Imaging in Monaco on Monday confirmed no structural damage. Leclerc is cleared to race. He is currently No. 5 in the Drivers' Championship with 155 points — 112 behind Antonelli. Ferrari trails Mercedes by 87 points in the Constructors'.

### STORY 4: Madring Circuit Guide
Madring at IFEMA Madrid is a 5.47 km, 22-turn hybrid circuit built around the IFEMA exhibition center on Madrid's northeastern edge. Key features: the longest banked corner in modern Formula 1 (nearly as steep as Daytona's banking), public road sections combined with purpose-built permanent track, 340 kph top speeds on the main straight, and dramatic elevation changes. No F1 team has ever raced here — all data is simulator-based. Race weather forecast: sunny, 30°C with highs of 33°C on Sunday.

### STORY 5: Season Finale Calendar
FOM CEO Stefano Domenicali has pledged a decision "by mid-September" on the final race of the 2026 season following the cancellation of the Bahrain and Saudi Arabian Grands Prix. The calendar currently ends with Malaysia Sepang (Oct 2-4) and continues through November. Qatar (Nov 27-29) and Abu Dhabi (Dec 4-6) remain on the calendar. The season finale decision involves whether Imola or Portimao replaces the Gulf races as contingency options. With the mid-September window opening in days, an announcement is imminent.
