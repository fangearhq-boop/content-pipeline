Antonelli Starts 4th: Every Championship Scenario for the Mexico City Grand Prix

By Marcus Cole | F1 Fanrecap | November 1, 2026

Kimi Antonelli lines up fourth at the Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez on Sunday — a position he inherited after a Max Verstappen grid penalty moved him forward from his original qualifying slot. From P4, with 123 points separating him from the nearest title challenger, Antonelli enters the Mexico City Grand Prix in an almost mathematically unassailable position.

Here is every scenario that could unfold today, and what each one means for the 2026 Drivers' Championship.

Scenario 1: Neither Russell Nor Hamilton Wins

If any driver other than George Russell or Lewis Hamilton takes the chequered flag first — whether that is Antonelli himself, Leclerc, Verstappen, Norris, or anyone else — Kimi Antonelli is automatically the 2026 Formula 1 World Champion, regardless of where he finishes the race.

This is the simplest and most likely path. Russell and Hamilton have to not just score points, but WIN outright, to keep the title fight alive. The probability of either achieving that in a race where Antonelli himself is starting fourth and fully motivated is relatively low.

Scenario 2: Russell Wins

If George Russell wins the Mexico City Grand Prix, Antonelli still clinches the title today — as long as he finishes ninth or higher. A ninth-place finish scores two points, and two points added to Antonelli's 123-point lead would be mathematically sufficient to close out the championship before the remaining four races even begin.

Starting fourth and driving for a Mercedes team that has produced the championship-leading car in 2026, finishing ninth is extremely achievable even if Antonelli encounters minor difficulties during the race. For Russell, the burden is enormous: he not only has to win, but he needs Antonelli to retire or suffer a significant setback.

Scenario 3: Hamilton Wins

Same arithmetic applies if Lewis Hamilton takes Ferrari's first victory of the 2026 season: Antonelli still clinches with a ninth-place or better finish. The math does not change based on which of his teammates or rivals wins — it only matters whether the winner is Russell (who closes the points gap directly) or anyone else.

Scenario 4: Antonelli Wins

If Antonelli crosses the line first, he wins his 10th race of the 2026 season and claims the championship in the most emphatic way possible. A dominant champion finishing the job with a dominant win — the ideal scenario for Mercedes and for Antonelli personally.

The Only Scenario That Delays the Title

For the championship to remain undecided beyond Mexico City, Antonelli would need to retire from the race AND Russell would need to win AND accumulate maximum points from Hamilton also scoring highly — a confluence of events so unlikely that it barely registers as a realistic probability.

No credible Formula 1 analyst believes the title will still be contested after Sunday's race in Mexico City. Antonelli is not just the championship leader — he is the inevitable champion. Today is where it becomes official.

What's Next

Watch the opening laps carefully. Antonelli from P4 into a tight first chicane, with Hamilton (P3) and Leclerc just ahead, sets up a potentially dramatic opening sequence. If Antonelli makes it through the first lap cleanly and into a points position, the title is almost certainly his before the sun sets on Mexico City. Four races remain after today — São Paulo, Las Vegas, Qatar, Abu Dhabi — and they will be races for history and records, not for the championship itself.
