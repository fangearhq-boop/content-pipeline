Nineteen years old. Leading the Formula 1 World Championship. By 66 points. With three races to go.

Kimi Antonelli's 2026 season has been one for the history books. The Italian Mercedes driver broke Lewis Hamilton's record to become the youngest driver ever to lead the F1 championship — a record set when Hamilton was 22 years old in his debut season back in 2007. Antonelli did it at 19.

With 302 points, eight race victories (including his home Italian Grand Prix), and a 66-point lead over teammate George Russell, Antonelli is on the verge of becoming the youngest F1 World Champion in history.

To put that in perspective: the 2026 championship would be Antonelli's second F1 season. He's barely old enough to rent a road car in most countries. He might be about to win the biggest prize in motorsport.
