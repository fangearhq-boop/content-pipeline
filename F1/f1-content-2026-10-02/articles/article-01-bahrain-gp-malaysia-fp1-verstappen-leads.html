Bahrain GP in Malaysia: Verstappen Tops FP1 as Championship Battle Heats Up at Sepang

By Ryan Calloway | October 2, 2026

Max Verstappen led a Red Bull one-two-three in the opening practice for the relocated Bahrain Grand Prix, with championship leader Kimi Antonelli a second off the pace in fifth.

The 2026 Formula 1 Bahrain Grand Prix is being contested at Circuit de Sepang in Malaysia — relocated from Sakhir following the cancellation of the original Bahrain round in April due to regional conflict with Iran. And if Friday's first practice session is any indication, this penultimate stretch of the championship is going to be a fight.

Max Verstappen set the FP1 benchmark with a 1m37.520s lap, 0.383 seconds clear of teammate George Russell and 0.783s ahead of Isack Hadjar in the second Red Bull. Most significantly for the championship picture, leader Kimi Antonelli finished fifth — 1.060 seconds off Verstappen's pace.

The Championship Implications

Antonelli leads the championship by 66 points over Russell with three races remaining after this weekend — Malaysia (Bahrain GP), plus two more to close the season. A single practice session is not cause for alarm, but the sight of the title favorite a full second off the pace on a circuit where Red Bull and Ferrari looked strong will attract attention.

FP2 also ran on Friday, providing teams with a second data set before qualifying on Saturday. The race on Sunday starts at 8:00 AM local time (15:00 Malaysian Standard Time).

The Unusual Setting

The Bahrain GP's relocation to Malaysia represents one of the most unusual scheduling decisions in F1 history. The Sepang circuit, which hosted the Malaysian Grand Prix until 2017, returns to the calendar as a one-off host for a race branded under Bahrain's name. For racing fans, the novelty adds to an already dramatic season narrative.

What's Next

Qualifying takes place Saturday morning local time, with the race on Sunday October 4. Antonelli will need to improve significantly overnight if he is to match Red Bull's Friday pace. Russell, who won the Azerbaijan GP just weeks ago, sits second in the session and second in the championship — making every session this weekend pivotal for the title fight.
