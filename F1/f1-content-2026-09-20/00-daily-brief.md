### STORY 1: Azerbaijan GP — 6 Days to F1's First Saturday Race in Baku
**Tier:** 1
**Category:** FOLLOW UP
**Beat:** Azerbaijan Grand Prix / Baku City Circuit / 2026 Calendar
**Headline:** 6 Days to Baku — F1 Prepares for Its First-Ever Saturday Race on the Streets of Azerbaijan

The Formula 1 Qatar Airways Azerbaijan Grand Prix takes place in six days — on Saturday, September 26, a first in Baku's F1 history. The race was moved from Sunday to Saturday because Azerbaijan Remembrance Day falls on September 27, a national day of mourning for military personnel lost in the Second Nagorno-Karabakh War. The entire race weekend has shifted forward 24 hours: FP1 and FP2 on Thursday, FP3 and qualifying on Friday, and the 51-lap race on Saturday.

**Why now:** Six days out. The unusual scheduling change and championship implications at a Baku race where anything can happen make this a top story daily through race day.

**Tier:** 2
**Category:** NEW
**Beat:** Lando Norris / McLaren / Driver Contracts
**Headline:** Norris Commits to McLaren Through 2030 — The 2025 Champion Is Staying Put

Lando Norris has signed a contract extension that keeps the 2025 Formula 1 World Champion at McLaren until at least the end of 2030, with options beyond that. **Why now:** With McLaren third in the constructors' and Norris a defending world champion, his long-term commitment is the franchise piece that signals where the team's ambitions sit over the next five years.
