## Story 5: COTA History

**General knowledge (MEDIUM — use descriptive, not specific historical stats):**
- COTA opened in 2012; first US Grand Prix to take place at a purpose-built F1 circuit in the United States
- Anti-clockwise circuit? No — COTA is clockwise with 20 turns
- Famous for Turn 1 uphill blind crest, Sector 2 flowing esses, and the sweeping final sector
- Elevation change: approximately 40m throughout the circuit
- Has hosted the US Grand Prix every year since 2012 with one COVID-affected gap
