Italian GP Preview: Antonelli Starts Last at His Home Race, Norris Chases a Historic Hat-Trick at Monza

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | September 1, 2026

Three days until Monza. And the storyline heading into the 2026 Italian Grand Prix is almost too dramatic to believe.

Kimi Antonelli — the 20-year-old Italian prodigy who leads the 2026 Formula 1 World Championship by 59 points — will start Sunday's race from the back of the grid. At home. In Italy. In front of the tifosi who have waited years to cheer an Italian driver to the title.

Mercedes made the decision deliberately. A full power-unit change on Antonelli's car means an automatic grid penalty — but the team chose Monza specifically because its long straights and plentiful overtaking opportunities give their driver the best theoretical chance to recover. Fresh components will power Antonelli through the final seven races of the season. The math, Mercedes believes, works in their favor over the long term.

Whether Monza agrees is another question.

Norris Is the Favourite — and the Danger

Lando Norris has won the last two Formula 1 races. At the Hungaroring. At Zandvoort. The reigning World Champion is riding the most dangerous winning streak on the 2026 grid, and he is the 6/4 favourite at the bookmakers to make it three straight at the Temple of Speed.

Norris signed a new McLaren contract extension, announced at Zandvoort, cementing his future with the team that gave him his first World Championship. He is motivated, he is fast, and Monza's low-downforce characteristics suit the McLaren's balance better than almost any other circuit on the calendar.

If Norris wins and Antonelli struggles to recover from 20th, the maximum points swing of 25 could compress Antonelli's championship lead to as few as 34 points — with 11 races remaining. That is still a substantial advantage, but it is no longer comfortable.

Ferrari's Wildcard: ADUO-2 and the Schumacher Livery

The third major narrative of this weekend belongs to Ferrari. The Scuderia debuts its ADUO-2 power unit at Monza — approximately 15 additional horsepower delivered through a new turbocharger, revised combustion chamber, and updated Shell fuel blend. Both Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc receive the upgrade without grid penalties.

Ferrari will also race in a special tribute livery inspired by Michael Schumacher's 1996 F310 — red and black with gold wheels, marking 30 years since Schumacher's first season in Maranello. Hamilton and Leclerc will wear matching one-off race suits.

Ferrari sit 53 points behind Mercedes in the Constructors' Championship. A strong Monza weekend, powered by the ADUO-2, could accelerate their second-half charge.

The Schedule

Qualifying at Monza takes place Saturday, September 5 at 15:00 BST. Race day is Sunday, September 6 at 14:00 BST — 53 laps around the 5.793km Autodromo Nazionale Monza circuit.

What's Next

When the lights go out on Sunday, the 2026 F1 title race enters its most consequential weekend yet. Antonelli will need to thread his way through the field while managing tire degradation, traffic, and the weight of a championship. Norris will need to convert pace into points. And Ferrari will need to prove that ADUO-2 is everything they promised. Monza delivers — it always does.
