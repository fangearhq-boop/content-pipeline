Ferrari's ADUO-2 Engine Debuts at Monza — Hamilton and Leclerc Get +15hp With No Grid Penalty

By Marcus Cole | F1 Fanrecap | September 1, 2026

Ferrari's biggest upgrade of the 2026 Formula 1 season is finally cleared for action — and the timing, at the Italian Grand Prix no less, could not be more cinematic.

The Scuderia has confirmed that its ADUO-2 power unit will make its competitive debut this weekend at Autodromo Nazionale Monza. Both Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc will receive the evolved six-cylinder engine, delivering approximately 15 additional horsepower through a suite of technical improvements. Neither driver will face a grid penalty — Ferrari carefully managed their power unit allocations to ensure both cars start from their qualified positions.

What the ADUO-2 Brings

The ADUO-2 represents the first phase of a two-stage performance push Ferrari outlined earlier in the season. The key changes are:

  • New turbocharger: Revised compressor and turbine geometry improving efficiency and response.
  • Revised combustion chamber: Updated design improving thermal efficiency and power output.
  • New Shell fuel blend: A custom formulation developed in partnership with Ferrari's long-term energy partner, adding additional power density.

The combined effect is approximately 15 additional horsepower — enough to meaningfully close Ferrari's gap to Mercedes, which has dominated the power output charts for much of 2026. A second performance phase is expected later in the season, targeting a total gain of roughly 30 horsepower by year's end.

The Schumacher Connection

The ADUO-2 does not arrive alone. Ferrari will race this weekend in a special tribute livery inspired by the 1996 F310 — the car Michael Schumacher drove in his first season with the Scuderia. The design features Ferrari's signature red paired with black accents and gold wheels, marking 30 years since Schumacher's arrival at Maranello.

Hamilton and Leclerc will wear matching one-off race suits bearing the same color scheme. Hamilton, who publicly expressed a wish for an all-red Ferrari, gets the closest approximation yet in Schumacher red at the circuit where the German legend made so much of his legend.

The emotional resonance at an Italian Grand Prix is obvious. Monza is not just a race — it is a pilgrimage for Ferrari, and they have chosen this weekend to make their strongest technical statement of 2026.

Championship Implications

Ferrari sit 53 points behind Mercedes in the Constructors' Championship. With Hamilton and Leclerc both receiving the full ADUO-2 benefit, Monza represents a genuine opportunity to accelerate that chase. If the upgrade delivers what Ferrari's data suggests, both drivers should gain meaningful grid positions compared to their 2026 Monza baseline.

What's Next

The ADUO-2's first real-world test comes in Friday practice, where Ferrari will gather data on the upgrade's behavior across Monza's unique combination of long straights and tight chicanes. If the numbers hold, qualifying Saturday and the race Sunday will give Ferrari fans something new to cheer — in a livery that honors the man who made believing in Ferrari possible again.
