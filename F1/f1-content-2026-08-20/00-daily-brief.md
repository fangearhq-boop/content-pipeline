### STORY 2: Dutch Grand Prix Weekend — Zandvoort's Final Farewell Begins Tomorrow

Practice 1 for the Dutch Grand Prix begins tomorrow (August 21) at Zandvoort — the circuit's last-ever Formula 1 Grand Prix. The weekend features a Sprint format for the first time, with Sprint Qualifying Friday, the Sprint race Saturday morning (10:00 UTC), and qualifying Saturday afternoon (14:00 UTC), before Sunday's main race. Championship leader Kimi Antonelli arrives 50 points ahead of Lewis Hamilton with 316 points still available. Rain is a serious factor this weekend — Zandvoort's coastal conditions are notoriously unpredictable.
