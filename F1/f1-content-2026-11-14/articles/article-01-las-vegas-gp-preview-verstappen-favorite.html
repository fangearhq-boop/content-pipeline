Las Vegas GP Preview: Verstappen Enters as Favorite for November 19-21 Showdown on the Strip

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | November 14, 2026

With the 2026 Formula 1 season entering its penultimate chapter, the Las Vegas Grand Prix — set for November 19-21 on the iconic Strip circuit — arrives with Max Verstappen as the betting favorite and plenty of P2 championship drama still to be settled.

Las Vegas never does anything quietly, and the 2026 Formula 1 Grand Prix promises to live up to the city's reputation for spectacle. One week from now, the fastest racing machines on the planet will navigate the 3.8-mile Strip circuit, chasing race wins and championship points under the Nevada night sky.

Max Verstappen enters as the early betting favorite following one of the drives of the season in Brazil — a charge from 21st on the grid all the way to second place that reminded observers why the four-time champion is never to be counted out. With the Drivers' title already claimed by Kimi Antonelli, Las Vegas becomes Verstappen's best opportunity to convert race wins in the season's closing stretch.

The Circuit and the Challenge

The Las Vegas Street Circuit is a 3.8-mile monster with 17 turns, featuring the long straights of the Strip that allow cars to reach extraordinary speeds before braking hard for the technical sections. It is a circuit that rewards outright power but punishes mistakes — the walls are unforgiving, and an error on a flying lap can ruin an entire weekend.

Lando Norris, who won the Brazilian Grand Prix from pole last week, has already issued a pointed warning about the specific challenge Las Vegas presents for 2026 machinery. The McLaren driver noted that the 2026 cars operate within an extremely narrow tire window that makes setup and management particularly demanding at a street circuit where surface temperatures can be unpredictable.

That Norris warning is not merely competitive posturing — it reflects genuine technical concern about a car platform still being understood by its drivers and engineers. Teams that solve the tire puzzle quickly will have an enormous advantage over the course of the weekend.

What to Watch

Verstappen's form heading in is formidable — a 21-to-2 recovery drive in Brazil and the kind of qualifying pace that makes him dangerous regardless of grid position. Red Bull will want a strong weekend, and Las Vegas historically delivers close racing with the potential for drama at multiple points in the race.

Lando Norris and McLaren arrive with their new upgrade package working well, but the team must be cautious. A race win at Las Vegas would be spectacular and would continue building toward what McLaren hopes is a constructors' title challenge in 2027.

George Russell and Lewis Hamilton, running Mercedes machinery, will be fully focused on the P2 championship battle alongside Verstappen. With 17 points separating Verstappen from Russell and Hamilton just one point further back, every lap in Las Vegas counts.

What's Next

The Las Vegas Grand Prix weekend begins November 19 with practice sessions, qualifying follows, and the race fires off Saturday night local time. After Las Vegas, the 2026 season moves to Qatar before its final destination of Abu Dhabi — but the Strip deserves to be the chapter in this season that everyone remembers.

One week from tonight, the neon lights will be competing with headlights at 200-plus miles per hour. Formula 1 meets Las Vegas — and both are ready.
