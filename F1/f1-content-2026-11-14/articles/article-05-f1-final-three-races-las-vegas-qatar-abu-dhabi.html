Three Races to Go: What Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi Will Decide in F1's 2026 Finale

By Marcus Cole | F1 Fanrecap | November 14, 2026

The 2026 Formula 1 World Championship heads into its final act with Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi remaining — and while the title is settled, the P2 battle, race wins, and constructor positions give every team and driver reason to push hard to the very end.

When the 2026 Formula 1 season opened, teams and drivers faced the most significant technical overhaul the sport had seen in years. Now, with 19 races in the books, the season is heading into its final three-race sprint — Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi — with Kimi Antonelli confirmed as champion but a remarkable amount of unfinished business still to be settled.

The drama did not end when Antonelli clinched. It just shifted.

The P2 Battle

Max Verstappen leads George Russell by 17 points in the fight for second place in the championship, with Lewis Hamilton just one point behind Russell. All three can still finish as runner-up — and in a sport where championship positions carry prestige, prize money, and commercial value, second place is worth fighting for across three consecutive weekends.

Verstappen, Russell, and Hamilton are three of the most experienced racers in Formula 1 history. Each knows exactly what is at stake. Expect fierce, clean racing between them in Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi as all three try to maximize points while monitoring each other's results carefully.

Las Vegas: The Showpiece

The Las Vegas Grand Prix on November 19-21 is the season's signature event — a night race on the iconic Strip circuit that combines world-class sport with one of the world's most famous entertainment destinations. The spectacle alone makes it unmissable, but the racing quality and the championship stakes give it real weight this year.

Lando Norris enters as a contender for the race win after his Brazilian GP victory, Verstappen is the betting favorite, and the P2 championship trio will be racing with one eye on the results and one eye on the scoreboard. Las Vegas rarely produces dull racing.

Qatar and Abu Dhabi: The Curtain Call

After Las Vegas, Formula 1 moves to Qatar before the traditional season finale at the Yas Marina Circuit in Abu Dhabi. Abu Dhabi has been the stage for some of the sport's most dramatic season conclusions — 2021 being the most memorable in recent memory — and the 2026 version will have its own story to tell, even if the championship winner is already decided.

Constructor positions beyond the top two, individual race win tallies, and momentum into the 2027 season will keep every team honest through the final miles of 2026.

What's Next

Las Vegas is first — November 19-21 — and it sets the tone for how the season closes. If the Strip delivers a dramatic race with incidents and lead changes, the 2026 final stretch will be remembered as one of the best in the sport's recent history. All the ingredients are there.

Three races remain. The 2026 season is not finished yet.
