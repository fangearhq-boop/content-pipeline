Verstappen Leads Russell by 17, Hamilton Right Behind: F1's P2 Battle Heats Up

By Marcus Cole | F1 Fanrecap | November 14, 2026

With Kimi Antonelli's 2026 Drivers' Championship already settled, three legendary drivers are locked in a tight battle for the season's runner-up position — and Max Verstappen's 17-point lead over George Russell could evaporate in a single race.

The Drivers' Championship is decided. Kimi Antonelli is the 2026 Formula 1 World Champion. But in the closing three races of a remarkable season, a secondary battle is unfolding that is every bit as compelling as any title fight — the race for second place, with Max Verstappen, George Russell, and Lewis Hamilton separated by just 18 points.

Verstappen leads Russell by 17 points after the Brazilian Grand Prix, with Hamilton sitting just one point behind Russell in fourth. With Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi remaining on the calendar, every driver in this trio retains a mathematical shot at the runner-up position, and the margins are tight enough that a single mechanical failure or penalty could completely reshuffle the order.

Verstappen's Momentum

Verstappen enters the final stretch in compelling form. His drive from 21st on the grid to second place in Brazil was the kind of performance that defines elite drivers — controlled aggression, immaculate tire management, and the relentless forward progress that made him a four-time champion. He arrives in Las Vegas as the betting favorite, and his mental resilience heading into a circuit that rewards attackers makes him a difficult opponent to hold off across three consecutive races.

Red Bull will not give up P2 without a fight. The team has the experience and the machinery to deliver strong results in the closing rounds, and Verstappen's personal motivation to finish as runner-up behind only a teammate driving a superior car would be significant.

Russell and Hamilton: Mercedes' Final Push

George Russell sits 17 points behind Verstappen — a gap that feels manageable but is not. Russell has been one of the more consistent drivers in 2026, taking full advantage of Mercedes' championship-winning machinery. A strong Las Vegas weekend could cut significantly into Verstappen's lead.

Lewis Hamilton's position is perhaps the most intriguing of the three. In his debut season at Ferrari, the 41-year-old seven-time champion has demonstrated that he still has the speed and racecraft to compete at the very highest level. One point behind Russell means the difference between third and fourth place in the standings is as small as it can get — and Hamilton has never been content to settle for fourth when he can see second from where he is standing.

What's Next

Las Vegas (November 19-21) is the first opportunity for all three to gain or lose ground. The P2 championship picture will be significantly clearer after the Strip race, particularly if any of the three takes pole and leads from the front while another suffers an incident.

Qatar and Abu Dhabi follow, and the Abu Dhabi season finale has a history of delivering dramatic final-lap conclusions. Whoever finishes second in the 2026 season will have earned it in a three-race sprint against two of the finest drivers the sport has ever produced. The battle starts in Las Vegas.
