Italian Grand Prix Preview: Antonelli Starts Last as Norris Eyes Three Straight at Monza

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap

September 4, 2026

The Temple of Speed opens its gates this weekend, and the 2026 Italian Grand Prix promises to be one for the ages.

Round 15 of the Formula 1 World Championship gets underway at Autodromo Nazionale Monza today (September 4), with the race itself set for Sunday, September 6. The championship storylines heading into this weekend are extraordinary: leader Kimi Antonelli will start from the back of the grid after Mercedes confirmed a new power unit for the Italian, while Lando Norris — winner of the last two races — arrives as the betting favourite to claim a third consecutive victory.

The Championship Landscape

Antonelli, 20, leads the Drivers' Championship by 59 points over both George Russell and Lewis Hamilton, who are tied for second. The German-Italian Mercedes prodigy has been magnificent all season, but a reliability issue at the Spanish Grand Prix earlier in the year — a race-ending stoppage — meant Mercedes knew it would have to exceed its power unit allocation before the season's end.

The team chose Monza for that penalty moment deliberately. Of all the circuits remaining on the calendar, the Autodromo Nazionale offers the most overtaking opportunities, thanks to its kilometre-long straights, multiple DRS zones and heavy braking points at Variante del Rettifilo and Ascari. A talented driver in a fast car can recover positions at Monza that would be impossible at Monaco or Singapore.

Still, starting last at a home race — Antonelli is from Bologna, just an hour down the road from Monza — is not a position anyone envies.

Norris in the Spotlight

Lando Norris enters Monza as the favourite, listed at 2.50 by the major bookmakers ahead of Russell (3.25) and Hamilton and Charles Leclerc (7.50 each). The 2025 World Champion has been in superb form, winning the Dutch Grand Prix among his recent back-to-back victories and showing McLaren's MCL40 is very much in the mix across circuit types.

At 83 points behind Antonelli, Norris needs both wins and help — and on Sunday, with Antonelli starting from pit lane, that help may arrive. A maximum haul from Norris combined with an Antonelli points deficit could make this championship genuinely tight heading into the final rounds.

Ferrari and the Tifosi

Ferrari arrive at their home race buoyed by a stunning tribute to Michael Schumacher and by Lewis Hamilton, who carries the hope of delivering the Scuderia a victory at Monza for the first time in recent seasons. Hamilton is listed at 7.50 — an outsider, but Monza has a history of surprises.

The atmosphere in the grandstands this weekend will be unlike almost any other race. The Tifosi will be at full voice, and with Ferrari's special Schumacher tribute livery on display, emotions are already running high before a single practice lap has been completed.

Schedule

  • Friday, September 4: FP1 (11:30 AM–12:30 PM) and FP2 (3:00–4:00 PM) — local Italian time
  • Saturday, September 5: Final Practice and Qualifying
  • Sunday, September 6: Race — 2:00 PM local time

What's Next

Whatever happens at Monza will set the tone for the final third of the 2026 season. If Norris wins and Antonelli struggles to recover, the championship fight intensifies heading into the Spanish GP (September 11-13) and Azerbaijan GP (September 24-26). If Antonelli powers through the field to salvage strong points, his lead becomes even more formidable.

Either way, Monday morning's headlines are going to be very interesting indeed.
