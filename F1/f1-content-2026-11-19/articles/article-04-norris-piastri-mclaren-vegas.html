Norris in Form, Piastri Hungry: McLaren Arrives in Vegas With Momentum

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | November 19, 2026

Lando Norris arrived at the Las Vegas Strip Circuit as the in-form driver of the 2026 Formula 1 season's final stretch. His victory at the São Paulo Grand Prix on November 8 — leading from pole to flag in difficult, changing conditions with a 14.6-second winning margin — was his third win of the year and reinforced what his team already knows: when the MCL40 is working, Norris is unbeatable.

Beside him in the McLaren garage is Oscar Piastri, the Australian who has collected five podiums in 2026 and enters Las Vegas with fresh motivation. Both drivers are targets for race victory on Saturday night.

Norris and the Case for a Fourth Win

Three wins in 2026 — the most of any driver not named Kimi Antonelli. Norris' season has been defined by blistering qualifying pace backed by race-craft that has matured significantly from his earlier years. The São Paulo victory, in particular, showed his ability to manage tire temperatures in cold, evolving conditions — precisely the type of situation Las Vegas will demand.

McLaren's MCL40 has been an outstanding race car. Its ability to generate aerodynamic efficiency at multiple speed ranges gives it flexibility across circuit types that some rivals lack. Las Vegas was not traditionally a McLaren stronghold, but this year's machinery and this year's driver could change that calculation.

Piastri's Push

Oscar Piastri sits seventh in the Drivers' Championship with approximately 152 points. Five podium finishes in 2026 demonstrate that he belongs at the front of the grid, but a race victory has eluded him this season. Las Vegas represents an opportunity not just for a podium but for a performance that sets up the final two rounds.

Piastri has repeatedly noted his comfort on street circuits in this season. His qualifying pace has been a particular strength in the second half of 2026, and Las Vegas — where the track rewards precision over raw straight-line speed — could play to his style.

McLaren as a Team

Three wins for Norris in 2026 means three wins for McLaren. The team has found something in their car that translates across track types, and their strategic decision-making at key moments of the season has been excellent. At Las Vegas, their low-drag configuration philosophy aligns well with the circuit's demand for minimal aerodynamic resistance on the long straights.

What's Next

Tonight's practice sessions will reveal how the MCL40 handles the Las Vegas surface and temperatures. If McLaren's data looks strong on tire degradation and straight-line speed, expect Norris in qualifying contention and potentially threatening for the win.
