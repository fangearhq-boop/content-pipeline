### STORY 3: Las Vegas Strip Circuit — The Technical Challenge

**Long-Form Post (12:00 PM ET)**
Not all street circuits are equal. The Las Vegas Strip Circuit is one of F1's most technically demanding layouts — and it's not because of the glamour.

Here's what the engineers are dealing with this weekend:

🌡️ COLD TEMPERATURES: Night racing on the Strip means track temperatures can drop significantly, slowing tire warm-up and dramatically affecting handling balance in the opening laps.

💨 THREE LONG STRAIGHTS: Teams must run low-drag setups to maximize straight-line speed — but that means less downforce through the high-speed corners, which demands more from the driver.

🏎️ TIRE DEGRADATION: The Las Vegas surface evolves over the weekend as rubber builds up. Session 1 (tonight) will tell teams a lot about which compound window they're working in.

Mercedes have historically been the team best able to extract performance from cold-track, high-speed circuits. The W17 is built to perform in exactly these conditions. Red Bull will need Verstappen to maximize qualifying in a car that may be at a slight disadvantage.
