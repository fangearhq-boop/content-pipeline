Verstappen Wins Rain-Hit Singapore Sprint — Russell DNF as Hamilton Surges to P2

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | October 10, 2026

Max Verstappen claimed a measured, dominant victory in a rain-soaked Singapore Sprint Race on Saturday, capitalizing on chaos that eliminated championship hopeful George Russell from the opening lap and saw Lewis Hamilton surge to an impressive second place.

The Sprint at Marina Bay — the first in Singapore's history — was always going to be high-risk on a circuit with barriers mere centimetres from the racing line. In wet conditions, those margins tightened further. Russell, who had claimed Sprint pole by 0.120 seconds over Verstappen, led briefly before making contact with the barriers in the early laps. He was out, his Sprint race finished, and with it any hope of extending his championship lead at Singapore.

Verstappen Measured Through the Chaos

While his rivals stumbled, Verstappen did what he does best on circuits that demand precision: he was faultless. He took the lead after Russell's crash and controlled the race from the front, crossing the line 2.591 seconds ahead of Hamilton. It was the kind of performance that reminded critics who doubted Red Bull's season that Verstappen remains the most complete driver in the field when the conditions turn hostile.

Hamilton's second place was the story of the race beyond the drama. The Mercedes veteran started sixth on the Sprint grid but threaded his way through with intelligent racecraft. His 2.591-second gap to Verstappen was narrow, and he never truly threatened for the lead — but it was a commanding drive that keeps him alive in the championship conversation at 221 points.

Leclerc and Antonelli Score Quietly

Charles Leclerc took third for Ferrari, 16.871 seconds behind Verstappen, ahead of Andrea Kimi Antonelli in fourth (+17.238 seconds). With Russell failing to score, Antonelli's fourth-place finish was gold dust for the championship leader. He did not need to win. He simply needed his main rival not to score. Russell obliged in the worst possible way.

Liam Lawson brought his Red Bull home fifth (+20.632 seconds), with Haas having an excellent afternoon through Oliver Bearman and Nico Hulkenberg in sixth and seventh. Lando Norris salvaged eighth after a fraught race that included contact with teammate Oscar Piastri, who fell all the way to 14th.

What's Next

Sunday's Singapore Grand Prix could be defining for the championship. Russell starts from the back of the grid following his fifth power unit change of the season and now faces the task of recovering to a points-scoring position while Antonelli and Hamilton start further up. With six races left after Singapore, every position matters. Verstappen's Sprint win moves him to 196 points — one behind Leclerc — as the midfield battle for the lower championship places tightens further.
