F1 Championship Standings After Singapore Sprint — Antonelli 89 Points Clear With Six Races Left

By Marcus Cole | F1 Fanrecap | October 10, 2026

Andrea Kimi Antonelli sits 89 points clear at the top of the Formula 1 Drivers' Championship following the Singapore Sprint Race, with George Russell's catastrophic Saturday doing nothing to halt the Italian's march toward what would be the youngest world championship in the sport's history.

The standings after Marina Bay's inaugural sprint tell a complicated story. Antonelli leads with 325 points. Russell is second at 236. Lewis Hamilton sits third at 221, with Charles Leclerc fourth at 197, Max Verstappen fifth at 196, and Lando Norris sixth at 189.

Antonelli's Advantage

Eighty-nine points is a significant cushion but not an insurmountable one. With six races remaining — and at least one more sprint weekend in the calendar — the maximum points available sit comfortably above that gap. What makes Antonelli's position so strong is not just the number, but how it was built: he scored in the Sprint while his chief rival did not, a pattern that has repeated itself across recent rounds.

Antonelli's fourth in the Sprint was efficient rather than spectacular. He did not take risks he did not need to take. He finished ahead of Lawson, Bearman, and Hulkenberg. He added eight points to his tally while Russell added none. That composure — the ability to bank points rather than chase drama — is what separates a championship contender from a race winner.

The Chasing Pack

Hamilton's 221 points keep him technically alive, 104 behind the leader. But the gap is steep. Hamilton would need Antonelli to suffer multiple non-scores while himself winning consistently — a scenario possible but increasingly unlikely. His second place in the Sprint was encouraging; his charge from sixth on the grid showed the form that won him championships in the past.

Leclerc and Verstappen are separated by a single point — 197 to 196 — in the fight for fourth. Verstappen's Sprint win moved him within striking distance of Leclerc, who had a quiet afternoon at P3. Norris sits sixth at 189 after his collision with Piastri cost McLaren a strong Sprint result.

What's Next

Sunday's Singapore Grand Prix carries 25 championship points for the winner. Russell starts from the back of the grid, which means Antonelli — expected to start in the top five — has an immediate opportunity to extend his lead further. The remaining races after Singapore include Japan, Qatar, and Austin, where championship decisions have historically been made. For now, though, Antonelli holds the cards — and Saturday in Singapore made his hand even stronger.
