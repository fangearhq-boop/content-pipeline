F1 Calendar Confirmed: Domenicali Ends Uncertainty — Qatar Nov. 29, Abu Dhabi Dec. 6

By Elena Voss | F1 Fanrecap | September 19, 2026

F1 Calendar / 2026 Season

Formula 1 CEO Stefano Domenicali has confirmed the 2026 season's final calendar: Qatar Grand Prix on November 29, Abu Dhabi Grand Prix on December 6. The announcement resolves weeks of uncertainty about whether the season's Middle East finale could take place as originally planned, following the cancellation earlier this year of the Bahrain and Saudi Arabia races due to regional tensions connected to the Iran conflict.

Domenicali stated at the Dutch Grand Prix weekend that "the calendar is confirmed… and we stay focused on delivering the calendar we promised at the beginning of the year." The Imola contingency that had been publicly discussed as a European alternative is now formally off the table.

The Background

The 2026 season has operated under a shadow of calendar instability since early in the year. When Bahrain and Saudi Arabia were removed from the schedule, the domino question became obvious: would the season end in the Middle East at all, or would regional security conditions make that impossible? F1 had Imola standing by as a contingency venue if Qatar or Abu Dhabi proved unfeasible.

For weeks, Domenicali had been monitoring the situation and offering measured language — "we are watching carefully," "decisions will be made in due course" — without a clear commitment. Teams and promoters were in planning limbo. Both the Qatar and Abu Dhabi promoters were proceeding with preparations while aware that the confirmation had not yet arrived in formal terms.

The Dutch Grand Prix confirmation changed that. "The calendar is confirmed" is unambiguous language from the series' chief executive. Bahrain and Saudi Arabia were replaced in the calendar by races rescheduled to Malaysia for October 4 (the former Bahrain race). The net result is a 23-race season ending December 6 in Abu Dhabi — the same finish line as the original calendar.

What the Confirmed Calendar Means for the Championship

With the full remaining schedule now set in stone, teams can finalize their upgrade development timelines, their logistical planning, and their technical staff allocations. The constructors' championship battle between Ferrari and McLaren, for instance, now has a clear road map: nine rounds remaining after Baku, ending at Yas Marina. Both teams know exactly how many opportunities exist to swing the points gap.

The Qatar and Abu Dhabi circuits themselves carry championship significance. Qatar's Losail International Circuit has historically been a high-speed power-unit venue. Abu Dhabi is where Vettel famously clinched his first title in 2010, and where multiple seasons have reached their decisive conclusion. For Kimi Antonelli, if the Drivers' Championship has not been clinched before arriving in the Middle East, both Qatar and Abu Dhabi offer 26-point opportunities.

What's Next

The confirmed remaining calendar after Baku (September 26): Singapore Sprint weekend (October 5), Japan, Austin, Mexico City, São Paulo Sprint weekend, Las Vegas, Qatar (November 29), and Abu Dhabi (December 6). All 23 rounds are now locked. The 2026 Formula 1 season ends in seven weeks and one race after the Baku weekend.
