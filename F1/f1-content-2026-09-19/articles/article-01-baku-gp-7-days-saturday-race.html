7 Days to Baku: F1's First Saturday Race Hosts Every Major 2026 Storyline

By Elena Voss | F1 Fanrecap | September 19, 2026

Azerbaijan Grand Prix

Seven days from now, Formula 1 arrives in Baku for the 2026 Azerbaijan Grand Prix — and this year's event is unlike any that has come before it. For the first time in the race's history, the grand prix will take place on a Saturday. September 27 is Azerbaijan's national Remembrance Day, so the FIA shifted the entire weekend forward: media day Wednesday, FP1 and FP2 on Thursday September 24, FP3 and qualifying on Friday September 25, and the 51-lap race at Saturday September 26.

The format change is the footnote. The story is everything else. Every meaningful championship battle in the 2026 season converges on Baku at the same moment.

The Championship Context

Kimi Antonelli arrives as the sport's near-certain champion-in-waiting. The Mercedes driver leads the Drivers' Championship with 292 points — 81 clear of teammate George Russell. The earliest mathematical clinch remains Singapore on October 5, which means Baku is pressure without possibility of resolution. But what Antonelli does here — how much he scores, how much Russell takes away from the gap — will determine exactly what Singapore needs to deliver.

The Constructors' Championship is more immediate. Ferrari holds second place, 52 points ahead of McLaren. Baku's long main straight along the Azerbaijani coastline has historically rewarded power-unit performance, and Ferrari's 2026 power unit has been one of the class of the field. A strong weekend here could effectively insulate Ferrari's position before the flyaway rounds begin. A stumble could make the final nine rounds genuinely decisive.

The Circuit and the Upgrades

The Baku City Circuit is the most unpredictable race track on the Formula 1 calendar. The combination of a 2.2-kilometer flat-out blast along the Baku shoreline — the longest straight in F1 — and a labyrinthine old-town section through some of the narrowest turns on the schedule produces a race that almost never goes without incident. Cars run three abreast into Turn 1. Safety car periods are not unusual. Championship-altering crashes have happened here.

Into that mix this year: Williams is bringing its most significant upgrade package of the season — what team principal James Vowles has described as "almost entirely new car" in the FW48. McLaren is also expected to bring a bolt-on package. Ferrari and Lewis Hamilton, whose form has been inconsistent in the second half of the season, will be looking to the long straight for pace.

What's Next

FP1 and FP2 run Thursday September 24, with FP3 and qualifying on Friday September 25. The 51-lap Azerbaijan Grand Prix takes place Saturday September 26 — the first Saturday race in this circuit's history. Singapore follows on October 5, where Antonelli could make history. Seven days, and every story running through 2026 Formula 1 arrives at once.
