### STORY 4: F1 Calendar Crisis

**Long-Form Post (2:00 PM ET):**
Formula 1 already cancelled two grands prix in 2026. Now two more are at risk.

Bahrain and Saudi Arabia were pulled from the calendar in March due to the Iran-US/Israel conflict. The Middle East races that remained — Qatar on November 27-29 and Abu Dhabi on December 4-6 — have been under a cloud ever since. This week, that cloud got heavier.

F1 CEO Stefano Domenicali has confirmed there is a contingency plan in place and that the sport faces a mid-September deadline to make its call on both races. If they cannot go ahead, the 2026 season will end in Europe. Imola is among the circuits being considered as a replacement for one or both of the final rounds.

One report has already declared both races officially cancelled — which would make Las Vegas the season finale. That has not been confirmed by multiple major outlets. But the direction of travel is clear.

The 2026 title could be decided in a European autumn finale instead of the traditional Abu Dhabi sunset. That would be one of the more dramatic and unusual finishes to an F1 season in the modern era.
