Russell Dominates Qualifying as Antonelli Crashes Out at Baku: Azerbaijan GP Race Day

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | September 26, 2026

George Russell put in one of the drives of his career on Saturday at Baku City Circuit, clocking a 1m42.526s to claim pole position for the Azerbaijan Grand Prix by over 0.8 seconds — the biggest margin of the entire 2026 season. As Russell celebrated at the front of the grid, his Mercedes teammate and championship leader Kimi Antonelli was being rescued from the Turn 1 barriers after a brutal qualifying crash that ended his session before it had really begun.

Antonelli had barely been on track in Q1 when he clipped the inside wall at Turn 1, destroying the front suspension of his W17. The young Italian was able to climb out unharmed, but the damage was done. The 19-year-old who leads the world championship by 81 points will start the race from approximately P15 — one of the worst days he has experienced in an otherwise extraordinary 2026 season.

Russell's Masterclass

For Russell, Baku has delivered everything he needed heading into race day. His 1m42.526s was comfortably ahead of Charles Leclerc in second for Ferrari, with Oscar Piastri just 0.001 seconds behind Leclerc in third. Isack Hadjar qualified fourth for Red Bull, while Lando Norris rounded out the top five before going off late in the session — a move that cost Max Verstappen his final Q3 lap as yellow flags were waved. Verstappen ultimately qualified eighth.

Russell's performance was a statement. On one of the most demanding street circuits on the calendar, he was simply in a different class. This is his fifth pole of the 2026 season, and it arrives at the perfect moment: with his championship rival stranded at the back of the grid.

Grid Chaos Before a Wheel Has Turned

The starting order was further complicated by four separate grid penalties issued after qualifying. Carlos Sainz received a five-place drop after stewards found he had failed to slow sufficiently under yellow flags. Sergio Perez was handed a three-place penalty for impeding Piastri. Fernando Alonso and Lance Stroll — both Aston Martin drivers — were given major power unit penalties and will start from the back row, with the team taking new engine elements ahead of the season's final stretch.

With Antonelli also compromised, the Baku starting grid for 2026 is one of the most scrambled in recent memory. Multiple contenders are either penalised or out of position, and the strategic battle during the race promises to be enormously complex.

Championship Implications

The stakes could not be higher. Antonelli carries an 81-point lead over Russell into race day, but a bad result here combined with a Russell victory would trim that advantage considerably. If Russell wins and takes the fastest lap point — worth 26 points in total — and Antonelli fails to score, the gap drops to 55 points with 8 races remaining. That is still a significant buffer, but it is one that Russell will be desperate to create.

Russell has converted poles into wins consistently in 2026. Starting at the front of the Baku grid, with clean air and a free strategic hand, this is arguably his best opportunity of the season.

What's Next

The 2026 Azerbaijan Grand Prix gets underway at 3:00 PM local time in Baku (11:00 AM BST / 6:00 AM ET). All eyes will be on how quickly Antonelli can work his way through the field, and whether Russell can convert pole position into a race win that genuinely reopens the championship battle.
