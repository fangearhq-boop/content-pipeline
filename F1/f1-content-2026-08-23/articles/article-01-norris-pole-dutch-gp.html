Norris Takes Pole for the Final Dutch Grand Prix at Zandvoort

By Elena Voss | F1 Fanrecap | August 23, 2026

Lando Norris called it the best lap of his life. After a 1:11.163 on a deteriorating track at Circuit Zandvoort, it is difficult to argue with him. Norris claimed pole position for the 2026 Dutch Grand Prix — the final-ever Formula 1 race at this circuit — denying George Russell by just 0.102 seconds in one of the most gripping qualifying sessions of the season.

The session's decisive moment came as conditions worsened late in Q3. While other drivers retreated to the pit lane, Norris committed to one final flying lap. It was a gamble, and it paid off spectacularly. His time stood as the best of the session, good enough for pole position in a race that carries enormous emotional and sporting weight.

The Grid

Russell qualified second, 0.102 seconds off Norris's benchmark. Championship leader Kimi Antonelli lines up third — just 0.031 seconds behind Russell and 0.133 seconds off pole. Antonelli acknowledged after qualifying that a mistake at Turn 1 cost him a realistic shot at the top spot. Oscar Piastri starts fourth, completing an all-McLaren, all-Mercedes front two rows.

Lewis Hamilton qualifies fifth for Ferrari, Charles Leclerc sixth, and Max Verstappen seventh. Verstappen has been dealing with a flu-like illness throughout the Zandvoort weekend and openly acknowledged the ongoing fundamental problems affecting Red Bull's 2026 car. Despite those limitations, the four-time world champion will be looking for something memorable in his final-ever home race.

Championship Stakes

This qualifying result sets up a direct championship confrontation from the opening corner. Antonelli leads Russell by 56 points in the Drivers' Championship, but with a race victory worth 25 points and roughly 316 points still available in the season, the mathematics remain very much alive for Russell. Starting P2 just ahead of Antonelli's P3, every fraction of the first lap carries title implications.

For Norris, pole position at Zandvoort represents more than an individual highlight. McLaren has been one of the dominant forces of 2026, and a race win today would further cement their standing in the Constructors' Championship picture. Norris won this race in 2024 — repeating that result at the circuit's final edition would be a fitting tribute.

What's Next

The 72-lap Dutch Grand Prix gets underway at 15:00 CEST (09:00 ET) on Sunday, August 23. After the final checkered flag falls at Zandvoort, Formula 1 heads to Autodromo Nazionale Monza for the Italian Grand Prix on September 6 — Ferrari's home race, where the Scuderia are expected to debut their ADUO-2 power unit upgrade.
