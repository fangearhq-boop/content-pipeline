Next Stop Monza: Ferrari's Home Race, Hamilton in Red, and the ADUO-2 Engine Debut

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | August 23, 2026

As the final Dutch Grand Prix at Zandvoort concludes, Formula 1 turns toward one of the most sacred venues on the calendar. The Italian Grand Prix at Autodromo Nazionale Monza — the Temple of Speed — is scheduled for September 6, and it arrives with several storylines that make it one of the most anticipated races of the 2026 season.

Monza is everything Zandvoort was not in technical terms. Where Zandvoort's dune-side circuit demands technical precision through tight, banked corners, Monza demands pure velocity. Top speeds above 350 km/h, low-downforce configurations, and the famous Lesmo curves and Ascari chicane make engine performance the primary performance differentiator at the Temple of Speed. That distinction is central to everything Ferrari is planning.

The ADUO-2 Engine

Ferrari has been deliberately saving their ADUO-2 power unit upgrade for Monza. Reports from earlier in the pipeline period confirmed that the Scuderia brought aerodynamic upgrades — including floor and chassis revisions — to Zandvoort, while holding the engine update back specifically for a circuit where straight-line speed carries the most weight.

If the ADUO-2 delivers the performance gains Ferrari are targeting, it could make the Scuderia a genuine race-winning threat at the one venue above all others where they most want to win. Ferrari at Monza is not simply a home race — it is an annual statement of identity, received by the Tifosi with a passion that rivals any crowd in motorsport.

Hamilton at the Temple of Speed

The second major story heading into Monza is one of the most discussed in Formula 1 this season: Lewis Hamilton racing for Ferrari in red at the Tifosi's sacred ground. The seven-time world champion — the greatest driver of his generation by most metrics — made the move to Ferrari for 2026, joining the team he grew up idolizing. Monza represents his first Italian Grand Prix in Scuderia colors.

Hamilton's Ferrari debut season has produced moments of brilliance even as the team has worked to extract maximum performance from their 2026 package. He sits third in the Drivers' Championship heading into Zandvoort. At Monza, with a potentially upgraded engine and the full force of Tifosi support behind him, the conditions are set for one of the most emotionally loaded drives of his career regardless of where he finishes.

What's Next

The Italian Grand Prix at Monza runs September 4-6, 2026. After Zandvoort's emotional farewell, the championship battle between Kimi Antonelli and George Russell heads south into Ferrari heartland. With the ADUO-2 engine in play, Hamilton in red, and the Tifosi ready to fill the grandstands, Monza promises to deliver everything the sport's most passionate crowd demands.
