Haas 2027: Fornaroli Is the Frontrunner, Ocon's F1 Career May Be Over

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | August 9, 2026

F1 Team News / Haas / Esteban Ocon / Leonardo Fornaroli

The Haas 2027 driver situation has reached what appears to be its final shape. Leonardo Fornaroli — McLaren's reserve driver, a back-to-back FIA Formula 3 champion in 2024 and FIA Formula 2 champion in 2025, 21 years old — is the frontrunner for the second Haas seat alongside Oliver Bearman, with multiple sources reporting an "agreement in principle" between Fornaroli and the team. McLaren has signaled willingness to release him on loan.

That leaves Esteban Ocon in the worst possible position. Autosport described his case on July 31 as "effectively over." Haas team principal Ayao Komatsu has been sidestepping Ocon questions at press conferences for weeks. Ten years into a Formula 1 career that included four seasons at Alpine and two at Haas, Ocon may be looking at the end of his time in the sport.

Why Fornaroli

Fornaroli's credentials are legitimate. Back-to-back championships in F3 and F2 is a rare achievement that marks a driver as genuinely exceptional rather than merely adequate. He is a McLaren junior, which means the team has invested in his development and has an interest in placing him well. A Haas race seat — even on a team that is mid-to-lower field — provides the Formula 1 debut that McLaren cannot currently offer internally, with Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri blocking the McLaren seats themselves.

Fornaroli reportedly conducted a private test at Jerez as part of the process. The "agreement in principle" language suggests both sides have reached the broad terms of a deal, even if a formal announcement has not yet been made.

Camara Gets the Consolation Prize

Rafael Camara, Ferrari's junior driver, was lobbying for the Haas race seat through the Haas-Ferrari technical alliance. Ferrari's influence at Haas is real — the team uses Ferrari power units and benefits from technical support. But the latest reporting indicates Camara is being offered a reserve driver role at Haas for 2027, not the race seat.

The paddock consensus is that Camara benefits from another season in Formula 2. He is currently third in the F2 standings — strong, but not in a position that demands immediate promotion. Ferrari and Haas appear to agree that 2027 is not his year for a race seat.

Ocon and Hirakawa: Running Out of Options

Ocon arrived at Haas with three points from 11 races in 2026. Bearman has 18. The performance gap is not the only factor — Ocon came into the team on a one-year deal and has been lobbying internally for a second season — but it is the most visible one. Komatsu's unwillingness to publicly defend Ocon's seat has been read throughout the paddock as a signal that the decision has been made.

Ryo Hirakawa, the Japanese driver who received FP1 time at the Hungarian Grand Prix, was briefly in the conversation. gpblog.com described him as "out of luck" for the Haas seat. His Hungary appearance may have been his best shot at generating traction for a 2027 place — and it was not enough.

What's Next

Haas initially indicated a driver announcement in the May-July window. That deadline has passed without a formal statement, and the team has slipped into August without confirming anything. An announcement is expected before the Dutch Grand Prix or at Monza — the Italian GP has historically served as a natural announcement window for driver deals, given the media concentration and European team presence. Watch for any Haas media session at Zandvoort for the first official confirmation of what multiple sources are already reporting.
