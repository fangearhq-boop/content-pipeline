Alonso Said "After Summer." The Summer Break Ends August 17. The Decision Window Is Now Open.

By Elena Voss | F1 Fanrecap | August 9, 2026

F1 Driver News / Fernando Alonso / Aston Martin

Fernando Alonso told the media before the summer break that he would make his retirement decision "after summer." The Formula 1 summer shutdown ends August 17. That means the decision window — the one that could mark the end of one of the greatest careers in motorsport history — is now open. An announcement could land any day between August 17 and the Dutch Grand Prix weekend that begins August 21.

This is not speculation. It is the timeline Alonso himself set. And the Dutch Grand Prix now serves a dual purpose: it is both the return of racing and the first real test of the variable that Alonso has staked his decision on.

The Variable: Honda's Power Unit

Alonso's framework for the decision has been consistent all season. He described it directly: hard to retire if the car is not competitive. The opposite implication — stay if the car can finally fight — is equally clear.

Aston Martin debuts Honda's updated power unit at Zandvoort. Reports estimate 30-50 additional horsepower from changes to the combustion chamber and pre-chamber geometry. After a season in which Alonso described the current unit in battery-clip mode as feeling "less powerful than F2," that upgrade is not just a technical development. It is the data point Alonso has been waiting for.

If Honda's new spec delivers meaningful pace at Zandvoort — if Alonso can suddenly fight cars he has been watching drive away from him all year — the case for continuing into a 2027 Aston Martin season led by Adrian Newey becomes compelling. If it underdelivers, the retirement announcement could come immediately.

The Newey Factor

Adrian Newey joined Aston Martin earlier this year. The most celebrated car designer in Formula 1 history is building the 2027 Aston Martin from scratch with different regulations. Newey has reportedly told people he is "pretty confident" Alonso will be on the grid next season — a signal that, from Aston Martin's internal perspective, the case for Alonso continuing is being made actively.

A Newey-designed car is a reason to stay. That is the argument Aston Martin is making to their most famous driver. Whether it is enough depends entirely on what happens in the next few weeks — and specifically, whether the Honda upgrade at Zandvoort gives Alonso reason to believe the 2027 machine can genuinely fight for podiums or more.

The Case the Numbers Make

Alonso turned 45 in July 2026. He is competing in his 22nd Formula 1 season. He is a two-time World Champion with 46 Grand Prix victories. His Aston Martin contract expires at the end of this season. He said Barcelona — which is not on the 2027 F1 calendar — was "probably my last time" racing there. Sky Sports' Ted Kravitz has predicted retirement repeatedly. The circumstantial evidence points one direction.

But Alonso has defied circumstantial evidence his entire career. He returned from a two-year retirement with Renault in 2021. He moved to Aston Martin when few believed in the project. He is not a driver who follows expected paths. If the car gives him a reason to stay, the retirement consensus in the paddock will be wrong again.

What's Next

The summer break ends August 17. The Dutch Grand Prix begins August 21. Alonso's Honda-powered Aston Martin debuts its updated power unit in competition for the first time at Zandvoort. Whether an announcement comes before the race, during the weekend, or sometime after depends on what the car tells him. Watch every pre-race media session and every post-qualifying interview. The answer is coming.
