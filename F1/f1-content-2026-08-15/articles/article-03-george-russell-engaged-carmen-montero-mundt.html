George Russell Is Engaged: Oval Yellow Diamond, a Candlelit Proposal, and the Whole Paddock Reacts

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | August 15, 2026

George Russell is engaged to Carmen Montero Mundt.

The Mercedes Formula 1 driver announced the news on Instagram around August 9-10, sharing the engagement during the F1 summer break. Russell proposed at a candlelit dinner by the sea. The ring is an oval yellow diamond. Carmen said yes.

The announcement was met with an immediate and warm response from the F1 world. Kimi Antonelli — Russell's Mercedes teammate — was among the first to congratulate the couple publicly. Carlos Sainz also reacted. The broader paddock followed, with drivers and fans alike sharing their congratulations across social media.

Five Years Together

George and Carmen have been together since 2020. That relationship has stretched across some of the most consequential years of Russell's career — his seasons at Williams, his move to Mercedes, his championship contention in 2025, and his current campaign in 2026 where he sits third in the standings at 160 points.

Carmen has been a regular and familiar presence in the paddock throughout that time, embraced by fans and photographed at grands prix from Monaco to Singapore. For those who have followed Russell's career closely, the engagement feels like a natural continuation of a relationship that has been visible since its earliest stages.

The oval yellow diamond ring has already become a talking point in its own right — an unusual and distinctive choice in a sport where conformity often dominates off-track as much as on it.

Summer Break, Summer News

The F1 summer break consistently surfaces the personal stories that race weekends bury under technical briefings and lap time comparisons. Russell's engagement — announced during the shutdown, shared on social media, reacted to by teammates and rivals — is exactly the kind of story that travels beyond the sport's core audience.

Sources covering the announcement include GPBlog, Motorsport Week, AthlonSports, HITC, and JustJared. The engagement has been widely confirmed across multiple outlets, with consistent details around the timing, location, and ring description.

Antonelli's reaction and Sainz's response — two drivers from different points in their careers, from different teams — reflected a genuine warmth that extends beyond paddock formality. Racing is a small world. Good news travels fast.

What's Next

Russell and the rest of the F1 grid return to action at the Dutch Grand Prix, August 21-23 at Circuit Zandvoort — the final Dutch GP ever held at that circuit. Russell enters the second half of the season third in the championship, 59 points behind teammate Antonelli. The engagement news lands during a break that, for Russell, has clearly involved more than just track preparation. Congratulations to George and Carmen.
