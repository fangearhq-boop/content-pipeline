Monza Italian GP Preview: Antonelli Starts Last, Ferrari Runs a Tribute Livery, and Norris Smells Blood

By Elena Voss | F1 Fanrecap | August 30, 2026

Italian Grand Prix · Formula 1 · Kimi Antonelli · Lando Norris · Ferrari

The Temple of Speed is five days away. The 2026 Italian Grand Prix at Autodromo Nazionale di Monza runs September 4-6, with Sunday's race starting at 2:00 PM local time (8:00 AM ET). And the storylines heading into this race weekend are absolutely extraordinary.

Formula 1's championship leader will start from the back of the grid. The team with the most to prove is running a tribute livery for one of the sport's greatest icons. And the driver on the hottest winning streak of the season believes his moment has finally arrived.

Antonelli: The Ultimate Pressure Test

Kimi Antonelli enters Monza leading the drivers' championship by 59 points. What should be a comfortable buffer heading to his home race has been complicated by a strategic decision that will define the weekend before it begins: Mercedes elected to take a complete power unit change, triggering a grid penalty that sends Antonelli to the very back of the field.

The rationale is sound. By absorbing the penalty now, Mercedes secures fresh, reliable power unit components for the final seven rounds of the 2026 season — giving Antonelli the best possible machinery for a championship run-in that includes Azerbaijan, Singapore, Austin, Mexico, Brazil, Las Vegas, and Abu Dhabi. Sacrifice one weekend. Win the war.

But the execution is what matters. Starting from the back of the grid at Monza — one of Formula 1's fastest and most challenging circuits for overtaking — is genuinely difficult. The championship leader will need to thread through the field without incident while watching his rivals maximize points at the front.

Norris: The Momentum Is Real

Lando Norris arrives at Monza on the back of two consecutive victories, including a dominant display at Zandvoort for the Dutch Grand Prix. He trails Antonelli by 83 points — a gap that, with a standard Antonelli performance, felt comfortable. With Antonelli starting from the back, the arithmetic suddenly shifts.

A Norris victory combined with a poor Antonelli result could close the gap to approximately 58 points with seven races remaining — 175 maximum points still on the table. The title fight that seemed settled could be revived in a single afternoon.

Monza suits McLaren well. The circuit's long straights and low-downforce requirements have historically rewarded cars with strong straight-line speed and aerodynamic efficiency. Norris will not need much convincing that this is his weekend.

Ferrari: Home Crowd, Special Colors, New Power

Ferrari arrives at Monza with several significant advantages beyond the passionate tifosi in the grandstands. The team is running a special Michael Schumacher tribute livery — red, black, and gold inspired by the legendary F310 — to mark the 30th anniversary of Schumacher's first Ferrari season. Hamilton and Leclerc race in suits bearing the seven stars of Schumacher's iconic helmet design.

More substantially, Ferrari is debuting its ADUO-2 upgraded power unit at Monza, reportedly delivering approximately 15 additional horsepower over the ADUO-1 unit. At a circuit where power is paramount, that advantage matters. Both Hamilton and Leclerc have won races in 2026 — Barcelona and Britain respectively — and both will be motivated to deliver Ferrari a home victory in the most theatrical circumstances possible.

What's Next

Practice sessions begin Friday, September 4. Qualifying runs Saturday, September 5. The race is Sunday, September 6 at 2:00 PM local (8:00 AM ET). For Antonelli, this weekend is about damage limitation and demonstrating that the Mercedes race craft that got him to this championship lead is enough to recover from a compromised starting position. For Norris, it is the best opportunity yet to rewrite the championship narrative. For Ferrari, it is simply home — and home demands glory.
