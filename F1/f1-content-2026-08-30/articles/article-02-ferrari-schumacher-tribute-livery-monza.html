Ferrari Honors Schumacher at Monza With Stunning F310-Inspired Livery — 30 Years Later

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | August 30, 2026

Ferrari · Michael Schumacher · Italian Grand Prix · Lewis Hamilton · Charles Leclerc

In 1996, Michael Schumacher stepped into a Ferrari for the first time and began one of the most storied partnerships in motorsport history. Thirty years later, at the same circuit where Schumacher so often electrified the tifosi, Ferrari is bringing those memories back to life in vivid red, black, and gold.

The Scuderia has confirmed that the SF-26 will run a special tribute livery at the 2026 Italian Grand Prix — a design inspired directly by the F310, the car that carried Schumacher in his debut Ferrari season. It is a gesture of profound respect from one of sport's most passionate institutions, delivered at the most emotionally resonant circuit on the Formula 1 calendar.

The Design: F310 Colors at Monza

Ferrari's standard 2026 livery on the SF-26 is recognizable and modern. The Monza version is something different entirely. The F310-inspired design brings black detailing to the sidepods, contrasting against the dominant Ferrari red and finished with gold wheels — a direct callback to the visual identity of Schumacher's iconic 1996 machine.

Ferrari teased the livery with a short video posted to social media, captioned "Home is calling" — a message that works on multiple levels. Monza is home. The F310 is home. Schumacher's legacy is home.

For Lewis Hamilton, who has spoken publicly about his desire to race in an all-red Ferrari, this livery delivers that moment in the most spectacular fashion. At Monza. With Schumacher's colors. The timing is impeccable.

Seven Stars: The Race Suits

The tribute extends beyond the car itself. Both Hamilton and Charles Leclerc will wear specially designed race suits at Monza — predominantly red, featuring the seven stars that Michael Schumacher used throughout his career as a signature motif on his helmet designs. The seven stars refer to Schumacher's seven World Championship titles, four of which came during his decade at Ferrari.

This is not the first time F1 teams have produced tribute liveries for special occasions. But the scale of what Ferrari has assembled — the car, the suits, the anniversary, the location — makes this one genuinely memorable.

Why This Matters Beyond the Aesthetics

Michael Schumacher joined Ferrari before the 1996 season, leaving Benetton — where he had won back-to-back World Championships in 1994 and 1995 — for the challenge of leading a resurgent Italian team. His first season in the F310 yielded three wins. His fifth season with Ferrari, in 2000, brought the Scuderia its first Drivers' Championship in 21 years. He won five consecutive titles with Ferrari from 2000 to 2004.

Schumacher's relationship with Ferrari and with Monza was among the defining emotional connections in Formula 1 history. To mark the 30th anniversary of his arrival at the team with a tribute of this scale is fitting — both for the legacy it honors and for the reminder it provides of what Ferrari represents at its best.

Hamilton won at Barcelona this season. Leclerc won at Silverstone. Both are motivated to deliver a Ferrari victory at Monza in these special colors — and the tifosi, who will fill the grandstands in red, will be giving everything they have to make that happen.

What's Next

The SF-26 in its tribute livery will be seen in full for the first time during Friday's practice sessions (September 4), and then in qualifying on Saturday (September 5) before the race itself on Sunday, September 6. Whether it wins or not, the visual memory this livery creates at Monza will outlast the weekend — a reminder that Formula 1, at its most powerful, is sport and history and identity all at once.
