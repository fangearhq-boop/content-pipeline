Las Vegas GP Practice Preview: FP1 Tonight on the Strip as F1's Night Race Returns

By Marcus Cole | F1 Fanrecap | November 18, 2026

The neon is on. Formula 1 returns to Las Vegas tonight when Free Practice 1 begins at 7:30 PM Pacific Time, kicking off Round 21 of 23 in the 2026 World Championship. The Las Vegas Grand Prix has become one of the most distinctive events on the F1 calendar — a night race through the streets of one of the world's most famous cities, with championship implications still very much alive.

Here is the complete session schedule for the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix (all times Pacific / Eastern):

  • Free Practice 1: Thursday, November 19 — 7:30 PM PST / 10:30 PM ET
  • Free Practice 2: Thursday, November 19 — 11:00 PM PST / 2:00 AM ET (Friday)
  • Free Practice 3: Friday, November 20 — 7:30 PM PST / 10:30 PM ET
  • Qualifying: Friday, November 20 — late PST / early morning Saturday ET
  • Race: Saturday, November 21 — 8:00 PM PST / 11:00 PM ET (04:00 GMT Sunday Nov 22)

The race runs 50 laps of the Las Vegas Strip Circuit, the purpose-built street track that winds through the heart of the city.

What to Watch in Practice

Free Practice 1 serves as the first official read on where teams stand relative to each other on the Las Vegas surface. Cold ambient temperatures are typical at night, which means teams will need to manage tyre warm-up carefully — a factor that has played a significant role in past Las Vegas results.

The key battles to track in FP1: how quickly Mercedes can establish a baseline for George Russell's championship chase against Max Verstappen; whether Red Bull can replicate the speed that made them competitive in Brazil; and how Ferrari responds after a difficult Brazilian Grand Prix that included a penalty for Charles Leclerc.

The Circuit

The Las Vegas Strip Circuit was purpose-built for Formula 1, running past some of Las Vegas's most iconic landmarks. Its defining characteristic is its pair of long straights, which place a premium on power unit performance and low-drag aerodynamic setups. Teams typically run relatively low downforce configurations here, increasing straight-line speed at the cost of some cornering stability.

The circuit also features a distinctive hotel section and a hairpin that has produced some of the most dramatic overtaking attempts since the race debuted. Cold track temperatures — often dropping well below 20 degrees Celsius at night — add an additional variable to tyre management strategy.

What's Next

Tonight's FP1 is the first of three practice sessions before qualifying determines the grid. The race itself takes place Saturday evening under the Las Vegas lights. With the Drivers' P2 championship fight alive at just 17 points between Russell and Verstappen, every lap of practice data matters.
