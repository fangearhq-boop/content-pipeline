George Russell's Final Push: 17 Points Behind Verstappen With Three Races Left

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | November 18, 2026

The 2026 Formula 1 Drivers' Championship has been decided. Kimi Antonelli is the youngest world champion in the sport's history. But the battle for second place is very much alive — and it starts tonight in Las Vegas.

George Russell arrives at the Las Vegas Grand Prix trailing Max Verstappen by 17 points. After 20 races, the standings read: Verstappen 279, Russell 262. With three rounds remaining — Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi — Russell has a narrow but genuine path to overtaking the Dutchman for the season's runner-up position.

The Mathematics

Each Formula 1 race offers a maximum of 26 points: 25 for the win and 1 for the fastest lap. With three races left, that means 78 points are still on the table. Russell doesn't need a perfect run, but he does need Verstappen to stumble.

The simplest scenario: Russell wins Las Vegas and Verstappen finishes fifth or lower. That would swing 10 or more points and slash the gap to single digits entering Qatar. In a sport where anything can happen — mechanical failures, safety cars, penalty controversies — that is not a remote possibility.

Why Las Vegas Matters Most

Of the three remaining circuits, Las Vegas arguably suits Mercedes best. The Las Vegas Strip Circuit features long, high-speed straights and smooth asphalt — conditions that have historically flattered the Silver Arrows' power unit and aerodynamic philosophy.

Red Bull, by contrast, has shown greater strength on circuits with more technical, medium-speed corners. Las Vegas leans toward raw power and efficient drag reduction. If Russell is going to make his move, the neon-lit streets of Nevada are the place to start.

Verstappen's Perspective

None of this is to say Verstappen is a passive participant. The four-time world champion will be acutely aware of the gap and the threat. Verstappen has shown throughout his career that knowing he's being chased brings out his sharpest performances.

Red Bull will undoubtedly study the data from the Brazilian Grand Prix, where Verstappen finished second behind Lando Norris, and work to optimize the RB22 for Las Vegas. The championship fight may be over, but Verstappen's competitive drive is not.

What's Next

Free Practice 1 begins Thursday evening at 7:30 PM Pacific Time, giving Russell and the Mercedes team their first look at the Las Vegas Strip Circuit in 2026 trim. Practice data will be crucial in understanding where the two championship contenders stand relative to each other — and to the rest of the field.

Qualifying follows Friday night, with the race set for Saturday at 8:00 PM PST. For George Russell, every session this weekend is an opportunity to close the gap on Verstappen. The 2026 P2 battle is far from over.
