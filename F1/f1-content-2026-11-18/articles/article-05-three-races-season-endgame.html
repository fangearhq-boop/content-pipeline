Three Races, Three Chances: The 2026 F1 Season's Final Chapter

By Elena Voss | F1 Fanrecap | November 18, 2026

The title is gone. Kimi Antonelli clinched the 2026 Formula 1 Drivers' Championship at the Mexico City Grand Prix on November 1, becoming the youngest world champion in the sport's history at just 20 years old. Mercedes have also sealed the Constructors' crown, their first since 2021. The champagne has been poured, the records broken, the celebrations completed.

But Formula 1 doesn't stop for anyone. Three races remain — Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi — and among the drivers still chasing meaningful results, the battles are genuinely fierce.

The Standings After 20 Races

Here is where the championship stands entering Las Vegas:

  • No. 1 — Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes): 390 points — CHAMPION
  • No. 2 — Max Verstappen (Red Bull): 279 points
  • No. 3 — George Russell (Mercedes): 262 points (+17 to Verstappen)
  • No. 4 — Lewis Hamilton (Ferrari): 261 points (+1 to Russell)
  • No. 5 — Charles Leclerc (Ferrari): 249 points (+12 to Hamilton)
  • No. 6 — Lando Norris (McLaren): 246 points
  • No. 7 — Oscar Piastri (McLaren): 152 points

The Battles Worth Watching

The P2 fight between Verstappen and Russell is the most prominent. At 17 points, it's a gap that could change with a single race result. Each of the three remaining circuits offers an opportunity for Russell to overturn it — or for Verstappen to cement his position.

One point separates Russell and Hamilton for P3. That battle is essentially a coin flip: either driver could end the season as the third-best driver in the world, and that distinction will shape narratives heading into 2027. Hamilton finishing ahead of Russell in his first Ferrari season would be a powerful statement. Russell staying ahead of his former teammate would validate his growth as a No. 1 driver.

Further back, Leclerc sits 12 points behind Hamilton. If the Ferrari has the pace in the final three events, the entire top-five picture could be reshuffled.

What's Next

The 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix is the first of three remaining rounds. Free Practice 1 begins tonight at 7:30 PM PST on the Las Vegas Strip Circuit, with the race itself set for Saturday evening. The season will then head to Qatar before concluding in Abu Dhabi, the sport's traditional final act.

The champion is decided. Everything else is still on the line. This is how the 2026 Formula 1 season ends.
