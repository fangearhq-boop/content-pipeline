Hamilton's Las Vegas Debut in Ferrari Red: Same Strip, New Mission

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | November 18, 2026

When Lewis Hamilton walks the Las Vegas Strip Circuit this week, he will be doing something he has never done before: preparing to race here in a Ferrari. For the first time in a Las Vegas Grand Prix, the seven-time world champion will not be wearing silver. He will be wearing red.

Hamilton's move to Scuderia Ferrari for 2026 was one of the most seismic transfers in motorsport history. After more than a decade with Mercedes — during which he claimed six of his seven world championships — Hamilton made the switch that few believed he would ever make. This Las Vegas weekend is a small but telling chapter in that new story.

Where He Stands

Heading into Las Vegas, Hamilton has 261 championship points. He sits fourth in the Drivers' standings — just one point behind George Russell in third, and 18 behind Max Verstappen in second. The championship itself belongs to Kimi Antonelli, but Hamilton has personal motivations for a strong finish to 2026.

Ending his first Ferrari season as the team's highest-scoring driver would validate the move. Finishing in the top three in the championship would be a statement result. Both are within reach, but only with strong performances over the final three races.

The Circuit and the Challenge

Las Vegas is a circuit that tends to reward power and precision over raw downforce. During his Mercedes years, Hamilton was effective here, taking advantage of the Silver Arrows' strong power unit on the long straights of the Strip.

Ferrari's SF-26 is a different machine. The Scuderia has had a mixed 2026 season, with periods of strong competitiveness offset by reliability concerns and strategic missteps. The Brazil result — where Leclerc received a post-race penalty that dropped him from his finishing position — was particularly frustrating. Las Vegas is an opportunity to respond.

Hamilton's familiarity with high-speed street circuits and his experience managing tyre degradation should serve him well on the cold night tarmac of the Strip. The question is whether Ferrari can give him a car capable of fighting at the front.

What's Next

Free Practice 1 begins tonight at 7:30 PM PST. Hamilton's first laps around the Las Vegas Strip in Ferrari red will give a preliminary indication of where the team stands. Watch for his long-run pace in FP2, which will be a stronger indicator of race potential.

With three races left in 2026, Hamilton's legacy season with Ferrari is entering its final act. Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi will define how the first chapter of this new partnership is ultimately remembered.
