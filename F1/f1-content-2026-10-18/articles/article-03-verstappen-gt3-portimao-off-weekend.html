Off-Weekend, Still Racing: Verstappen Goes P9 in GT3 at Portimão

Four-time F1 champion. No race on the calendar. No problem — Verstappen found one anyway.

By Elena Voss | October 18, 2026 | F1 Fanrecap

The Race

Max Verstappen did not take the weekend off. While the Formula 1 calendar rested between Singapore and Austin, Verstappen traveled to Portimão, Portugal to race a Mercedes-AMG GT3 in the GT World Challenge Europe on October 17.

He drove car No. 3 in a 61-car field. He finished ninth.

His co-driver, Lulham, received a drive-through penalty during the race — a complication that costs lap time and shifts strategy. Even with the penalty in the equation, the car finished inside the top ten in a high-quality field.

Not the First Time

This is not new behavior for Verstappen. Over the course of his F1 career, he has used off-weekends and spare time to compete in kart races, simulation events, and touring car competitions. The pattern is consistent enough that it says something about who he is as a competitor: he does not need an F1 calendar to go racing. He just goes racing.

There is a practical argument for keeping the competitive instincts sharp between rounds. There is also the simpler explanation — Verstappen likes to drive fast cars in competition, and when a GT3 race is available on a weekend the F1 circus has taken off, he signs up.

The Mercedes Choice

Verstappen is still a Red Bull F1 driver. The choice to race a Mercedes-AMG GT3 is worth noting. In the GT3 world, the machinery choice is largely about which manufacturer program is available and attractive. It does not carry the same weight as a Formula 1 seat. But Verstappen in Mercedes kit, at any level of motorsport, is an image that draws attention.

His F1 future beyond the current season is a topic that has run through the paddock for two years. The GT3 weekend adds a data point without answering the question.

What's Next

Verstappen returns to the F1 paddock for the US Grand Prix at Circuit of the Americas, October 23-25. He sits fourth in the championship at 221 points, 119 behind Antonelli. The title is out of reach, but the season — and apparently every available weekend — still has races to run.
