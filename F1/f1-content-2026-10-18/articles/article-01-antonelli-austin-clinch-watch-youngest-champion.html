Five Days to History: Antonelli Can Win the F1 Championship at Austin

The math is there. The scenario is clear. At 20 years and 61 days old, Kimi Antonelli could break Sebastian Vettel's record by over three years.

By Ryan Calloway | October 18, 2026 | F1 Fanrecap

The Scenario

Kimi Antonelli leads the Formula 1 World Championship by 102 points. George Russell is second. There are six races left and 150 points available. The US Grand Prix at Circuit of the Americas in Austin, Texas runs October 23-25, with the race on Sunday the 25th.

The simplest clinch scenario: Antonelli wins the race. Russell finishes ninth or lower. If both of those things happen, the championship is over. Antonelli is the 2026 Formula 1 World Champion.

It can happen on Sunday.

The Record

When Sebastian Vettel won the 2010 Formula 1 World Championship at the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix, he was 23 years and 134 days old. It was the youngest anyone had ever done it in the sport's history, and the record stood through every season that followed.

Kimi Antonelli was born on August 25, 2006. On October 25, 2026, he will be 20 years and 61 days old.

The gap between Antonelli's potential clinch age and Vettel's record is not a narrow margin. It is 3 years and 73 days. In historical terms, it would reset the category entirely — not edge the record but shatter it.

Antonelli is in his second full F1 season at Mercedes. He finished third in Singapore last weekend, kept his points lead, and now arrives at Austin needing one Sunday to close it out.

What Russell Needs

For Russell, the survival math is still there. If he wins Austin and Antonelli finishes outside the points, Russell can close the gap to 77 points. That keeps the championship technically open across five more races.

But Russell needs Antonelli to finish outside the points while he wins. That combination has not happened often this season. Russell is a strong driver in the right equipment, but he is chasing an opponent who has led every race weekend that mattered in 2026.

The most likely outcome at Austin is that the championship ends there. Antonelli has been consistent enough that banking on a collapse seems like wishful thinking.

Mercedes' Remarkable Season

Antonelli clinching at Austin would also give Mercedes both championships. The team leads the constructors' standings at 578 points, ahead of Ferrari at 444. That gap, too, is substantial heading into the final six races.

Lewis Hamilton joining Ferrari this season opened the question of whether the team that dominated F1 for the better part of a decade could continue winning without its most celebrated driver. The answer, so far, is yes — because Antonelli is the answer.

What's Next

The US Grand Prix weekend opens on Thursday, October 23, with practice sessions at Circuit of the Americas. Qualifying follows on Saturday the 24th, and the race is Sunday the 25th. Antonelli has three days to deliver. The record has stood for 16 years. It might not stand through the weekend.
