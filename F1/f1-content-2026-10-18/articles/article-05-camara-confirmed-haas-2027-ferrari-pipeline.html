Câmara Confirmed at Haas for 2027 — Ferrari Academy Driver Replaces Ocon

The Ferrari-to-Haas pipeline delivers another driver. Haas says this is not what you think it is.

By Marcus Cole | October 18, 2026 | F1 Fanrecap

The Announcement

Rafael Câmara, a Brazilian driver who came through Ferrari's junior development program, has been confirmed at Haas for the 2027 Formula 1 season. He replaces Esteban Ocon, who will not return to the team beyond 2026.

Câmara is a Ferrari Academy graduate. Haas uses Ferrari power units and maintains technical ties with the Italian team. The connection between Ferrari's driver development pipeline and Haas's F1 seats is not new.

The Pattern

Haas has been an entry point for Ferrari-linked drivers before. Mick Schumacher, son of Michael Schumacher, graduated from the Ferrari Driver Academy and made his F1 debut at Haas in 2021. The path from Ferrari junior program to Haas cockpit is one the paddock knows.

Câmara follows it. Whether his trajectory runs parallel to Schumacher's — who struggled to hold onto the seat for a third season — or diverges from it will depend on what Câmara does when the 2027 lights go out.

Haas Pushes Back

Haas team officials have made clear they do not want the signing characterized as a Ferrari directive. Their position: Câmara earned the seat on merit, is the best available option for 2027, and the decision reflects genuine performance evaluation rather than an obligation to the engine supplier's development program.

That argument has limits. Haas does not independently run the most competitive talent pipeline in F1. Their ability to attract the most sought-after free-agent drivers is constrained by their position in the constructors' standings and the resources available to a smaller team. The Ferrari connection is, by design, one of their levers.

Whether that makes them a B-team is a debate the paddock will have. The label is reductive in some ways — Haas operates independently, sets its own race strategy, and competes against Ferrari at every Grand Prix. But the dependency is real, and Câmara's arrival adds another chapter to it.

What's Next

Câmara moves to the Haas seat for 2027. Ocon's destination is now the open question — where a driver of his experience and results lands after leaving Haas will be a paddock storyline through the rest of the 2026 season. For Câmara, the next step is clear: get to the grid, adapt to the F1 machinery, and give Haas a reason to keep the seat warm.
