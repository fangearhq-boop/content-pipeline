Antonelli Leads Hamilton by 45 Points Entering Hungary — The Championship Picture Before the Summer Break

By Elena Voss | F1 Fanrecap

July 25, 2026

Kimi Antonelli leads the 2026 Formula 1 Drivers' Championship by 45 points. After ten rounds — including his victory at the Belgian Grand Prix at Spa-Francorchamps on July 19 — the Mercedes driver sits at 204 points, ahead of his own teammate Lewis Hamilton at 159. Charles Leclerc holds third at 126, and Max Verstappen has fallen to seventh with 76 points.

The Hungarian Grand Prix is the last race before the summer break. What happens Sunday at the Hungaroring will either extend Antonelli's lead or give his rivals the momentum they need heading into August.

Antonelli's Historic Title Run

The championship story of 2026 is, at its core, one about age. Kimi Antonelli is 18-19 years old, and if he holds his current form through the remaining twelve rounds, he would become the youngest Formula 1 World Champion in the sport's history — eclipsing the record set by Sebastian Vettel when he won his first championship in 2010 at the age of 23.

Antonelli's Belgian GP win, where he led home Leclerc in second and Verstappen in third, extended his lead and announced to the championship that he is not a fluke. Six wins and consistent points finishes have built a 45-point buffer that, while not yet decisive, is a substantial advantage with twelve rounds remaining.

Mercedes leads the Constructors' Championship by 73 points over Ferrari — a figure that reflects the team's dominant form and their ability to score through two drivers simultaneously.

Hamilton's Challenge: Beating His Own Teammate

Lewis Hamilton's position in the championship is complicated. At 159 points, he is second — the only driver consistently within striking range of Antonelli. But the car that could give Hamilton the championship is the same car that has elevated Antonelli above him.

For Hamilton, the Hungarian GP is an opportunity to narrow a 45-point gap that, if it grows through the summer, could become mathematically insurmountable by autumn. Ferrari's practice form on Friday — with Hamilton himself topping FP2 — suggests the pace is there. Converting it into a race win and maximum points before the summer break is the mission.

Leclerc and the Battle for Third

Charles Leclerc sits third, 33 points behind Hamilton and 78 behind Antonelli. While the title gap is significant, Leclerc's position keeps him mathematically alive in the championship conversation. Ferrari's 1-2 in Hungary practice — with Leclerc leading FP1 — suggests the team has genuine pace this weekend. A strong result at a circuit that suits Ferrari could keep the title picture from becoming a two-horse race.

Verstappen: The Context Behind Seventh Place

Max Verstappen's seventh-place standing, with 76 points, is the most significant underperformance story of the 2026 season. Four consecutive years of championship dominance have given way to a year in which Red Bull's machinery has consistently underperformed relative to the new technical regulations. Verstappen's exit clause is now active, and his future beyond 2026 is the weekend's most-discussed off-track storyline.

What's Next

The Hungarian GP race on Sunday is the last championship round before the summer break. When Formula 1 returns from August, the title race will be significantly shaped by what happens at the Hungaroring this weekend. Antonelli holds all the advantages going in: momentum, points, and a car that has proven itself at this circuit in the past. His rivals — Hamilton, Leclerc, and the rest — have two sessions of a Budapest weekend left to change the picture.
