Antonelli's "Worst Weekend" Still Leaves Him 66 Points Clear in Championship

By Elena Voss | F1 Fanrecap | September 27, 2026

Kimi Antonelli called it his "worst weekend" of 2026. He crashed in Q1 qualifying, started 16th on the grid, and watched teammate George Russell win the race outright. When the 2026 Azerbaijan Grand Prix was over, Antonelli's championship lead had been cut by 15 points.

He still leads the Drivers' Championship by 66 points. With eight rounds remaining, that is still a formidable cushion — but it is no longer the comfortable margin it was seven days ago.

The Q1 Crash That Changed Everything

Antonelli's weekend unravelled in qualifying. A crash at Turn 1 in Q1 damaged the front-left corner, trackrod, and upright of his Mercedes — ending his session immediately and forcing him to the back of the grid.

For a driver who has been the picture of consistency in 2026, the moment was jarring. Antonelli was reportedly "very mad" in the immediate aftermath, though he became more philosophical by the time he spoke to media after the race. The crash was the kind of single-mistake qualifying session that can happen to any driver at a street circuit — but at this stage of the championship, every mistake carries extra weight.

The Recovery Drive

What Antonelli did from 16th place deserves recognition. A recovery drive on a street circuit with limited overtaking opportunities is one of the most demanding challenges in Formula 1 — and Antonelli delivered a fifth-place finish, scoring 10 championship points from what could have been a disastrous weekend.

That is the mark of a championship-caliber driver: when the worst happens, you still score points.

Updated Standings

After Round 15 of 23, the Drivers' Championship standings:

No. 1 — Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes): 302 points
No. 2 — George Russell (Mercedes): 236 points (−66)
No. 3 — Lewis Hamilton (Ferrari): 199 points (−103)
No. 4 — Lando Norris (McLaren): 186 points (−116)
No. 5 — Charles Leclerc (Ferrari): 179 points
No. 6 — Max Verstappen (Red Bull): 163 points

In the Constructors' Championship, Mercedes leads with 538 points — 160 ahead of Ferrari.

What's Next

The next race is the Singapore Grand Prix at Marina Bay (October 9-11), the final sprint weekend of the 2026 season. The sprint format means additional points are available beyond the standard race weekend — making Singapore the first realistic window where Antonelli can begin making a mathematical push toward the title.

Baku was Antonelli's worst weekend. The question heading into Singapore is whether it was an anomaly or the start of a trend. Based on everything he has shown in 2026, the smart money says anomaly. But Russell will be doing everything he can to make it a trend.
