George Russell Wins Baku, Cuts Antonelli's Championship Lead to 66 Points

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | September 27, 2026

George Russell delivered a masterclass at the Baku City Circuit on Saturday, winning the 2026 Azerbaijan Grand Prix by 0.196 seconds over Max Verstappen and cutting Kimi Antonelli's championship lead to 66 points with eight rounds remaining.

It was Russell's third victory of the 2026 Formula 1 season — and it came at the moment when his Mercedes teammate needed it least.

Race Result

The final classification from a chaotic afternoon on the streets of Baku:

No. 1 — George Russell (Mercedes)
No. 2 — Max Verstappen (Red Bull) — +0.196s
No. 3 — Isack Hadjar (Red Bull)
No. 4 — Charles Leclerc (Ferrari)
No. 5 — Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes) — from 16th on the grid

Six drivers retired across the course of the race, and two safety car periods brought the field together at critical moments — creating the conditions for the chaos that ultimately cost Lando Norris his race.

Russell's Dominant Weekend

Russell's Baku win was not a fluke. He claimed pole position in qualifying with a lap of 1:42.526, a margin of 0.837 seconds over second-placed Leclerc — one of the most dominant qualifying performances of the 2026 season. From there, he managed the race with the composure of a driver who has spent this season establishing himself as the only real challenger to Antonelli's title campaign.

Three wins and consistent podium finishes have kept Russell alive in the championship conversation. He trails Antonelli by 66 points, but that lead has been 81 points as recently as last week. The momentum is shifting.

Red Bull's Strong Sunday

Verstappen's second-place finish and Hadjar's third were bright spots for Red Bull, which has struggled for consistency in 2026 relative to the dominant Mercedes squad. A double podium on the streets of Baku demonstrates that the RB22 is still capable of performing at the front of the grid when conditions suit it — particularly on the long, DRS-heavy main straight where Red Bull's straight-line efficiency has shone.

What's Next

The Formula 1 calendar next heads to Singapore for the Marina Bay Grand Prix on October 9-11, Round 18 of 23. It is the final sprint weekend of the 2026 season, meaning additional points are on offer beyond the standard race weekend format.

For Russell, Singapore is an opportunity to continue eating into Antonelli's lead. For Antonelli, it is the first realistic mathematical window to begin pushing toward the title. The 2026 season is not over — and Russell's Baku win has made absolutely certain of that.
