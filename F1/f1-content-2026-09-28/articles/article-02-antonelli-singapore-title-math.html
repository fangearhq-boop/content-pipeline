Kimi Antonelli Can Clinch the 2026 F1 Title at Singapore -- Here's the Math

By Elena Voss | F1 Fanrecap | September 28, 2026

Kimi Antonelli leads the 2026 Formula 1 drivers' championship with 302 points. His teammate George Russell is second on 236. The gap between them is 66 points. Nine rounds remain. The maximum still available across those rounds is approximately 459 points.

The earliest Antonelli can end the title race is October 11 at Singapore.

How Singapore Could Do It

Singapore is running its first-ever Sprint format this year. That adds a Sprint race on Saturday worth up to eight points, on top of the 25 available for winning Sunday's grand prix. The extra Saturday points create a scenario that did not exist at earlier rounds this season.

The math for an October 11 clinch: Antonelli wins the Sprint and the grand prix while Russell finishes outside the points. That combination shifts the standings enough to put the title beyond anyone's mathematical reach regardless of what happens in the remaining eight rounds.

It is a specific scenario and it requires Russell to have a very bad weekend. But it is possible, and it only takes the right result to get there.

Why Baku Did Not Change Much

Antonelli crashed in qualifying at Baku and started from P16. He recovered to fifth by the flag, which is a strong result from such a deep starting position, but Russell won the race. The gap before Baku was 81 points. After Baku, it is 66. Russell closed 15 points in a single weekend.

The broader picture, though, remains unchanged. Antonelli was in trouble in qualifying and still managed to limit his losses to 15 points. Russell needs to close ground far faster than that over the remaining nine rounds, and Antonelli needs to continue finishing races in the top five at minimum.

The Record That Could Fall

If Antonelli clinches at Singapore, he would become the youngest Formula 1 world champion in the history of the sport. He would be 20 years, 2 months, and 8 days old on October 11. Sebastian Vettel held the previous record from his 2010 title, which he won at roughly 23 years old. Antonelli would break it by approximately three years.

It is worth noting that the record is not the point for Antonelli. He is in a championship battle with his own teammate on the same machinery. The focus is on the next race and the one after that.

Hamilton and the Rest

Lewis Hamilton sits third in the standings on 199 points, 103 behind Antonelli. Norris is fourth. Neither is a realistic championship contender at this stage given the gap, but Hamilton's presence in the top three keeps Ferrari's driver narrative alive even as the constructors title trends heavily toward Mercedes.

What's Next

Before Singapore, the field travels to Malaysia for the Bahrain Grand Prix at Sepang on October 2-4. Antonelli needs a clean weekend there to avoid losing further ground to Russell. If he stays healthy and scores in the top five, the gap heading into Singapore will still be large enough that he can afford an imperfect weekend at Marina Bay and remain on track to clinch the title at one of the season's remaining rounds. The first shot is October 11. The championship race is not over, but the finish line is visible.
