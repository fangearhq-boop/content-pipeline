Mercedes Leads the 2026 Constructors' Championship by 160 Points -- This Is Nearly Over

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | September 28, 2026

George Russell won the Azerbaijan Grand Prix. Kimi Antonelli fought from P16 to fifth. Mercedes scored 35 points at Baku. Ferrari managed 20. The constructors gap is now 160 points.

Mercedes sits at 538 in the team standings. Ferrari is second at 378. McLaren is third at 306.

What Ferrari Would Need

Nine rounds remain in the 2026 season, with a maximum of approximately 459 points still available. For Ferrari to tie Mercedes, they would need to outscore them by 161 points across those nine rounds. That means Ferrari scoring near-perfect totals on every remaining weekend while Mercedes scores nearly nothing.

That is not a realistic scenario. Mercedes has won 11 races this season. They have two drivers competing for the title. Their car has been the fastest in the field across the majority of circuits visited. The conditions required for Ferrari to close 160 points in nine rounds would demand a complete collapse from one team and a perfect run from another simultaneously.

How Baku Looked for Both Teams

Baku illustrated the gap clearly. Russell crossed the line first. Antonelli, who crashed out in qualifying and started P16, still managed to finish fifth and score 10 points. Ferrari had Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton in the race. Their combined score was 20 points. Mercedes doubled that figure before accounting for the difference in points between first and fifth.

The 35-point weekend extended the lead by 15 over a single race. Ferrari would need to reverse that trend every remaining round and then some.

When Mercedes Could Clinch

The constructors' title has no sprint math shortcut the way the drivers' championship does at Singapore. Clinching happens when the second-place team can no longer mathematically catch the leader regardless of results. With 459 points available and a 160-point gap, the window is approaching. Depending on relative scoring over the next two to three rounds, Mercedes could have the team title secured before the end of October.

What's Next

The 2026 Bahrain Grand Prix at Sepang runs October 2-4. Mercedes will send Russell and Antonelli to Malaysia with the drivers' title picture clearly in their favor and the constructors' championship trending the same way. Every additional points finish from either driver extends the lead further. For Ferrari to stay in the conversation, they need both Leclerc and Hamilton to outscore both Mercedes drivers consistently, and they need that to start in Malaysia.
