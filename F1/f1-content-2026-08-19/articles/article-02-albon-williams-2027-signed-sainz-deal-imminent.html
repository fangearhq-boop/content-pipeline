Albon Signs Williams 2027 Extension; Sainz's 'Massive Contract' Expected Before Dutch GP

By Marcus Cole | F1 Fanrecap | August 19, 2026

Williams Racing is moving aggressively to close its 2027 driver lineup, and it is doing so before the Dutch Grand Prix even begins. Alex Albon signed a one-year contract extension with the team on August 18, confirming his place at Williams for the 2027 Formula 1 season. The announcement was confirmed by Sky Sports and GPFans and represents a clear statement of intent from the Grove-based outfit about the direction of their rebuild.

But the bigger headline may be coming within days. Carlos Sainz is reportedly set to sign what GPFans described as a "massive contract" with Williams before the Dutch Grand Prix — on terms that reportedly exceed what the team would typically offer. The deal is described as being in its "final stages" by RacingNews365, and Speedweek.com headlined the anticipated announcements together, describing Sainz as the expected follow-up to Albon's confirmed extension.

Albon's Williams Commitment

For Albon, the extension validates what has been a strong run of performances relative to the car he has been given to work with. The Thai-British driver has consistently extracted more from the Williams package than the machinery would seem to allow, and the team's decision to lock him in for another year before finalizing the second seat reflects the trust built over multiple seasons together.

The one-year structure gives Williams flexibility as the team continues its push toward the front of the grid. A strong 2027 with the right driver lineup will position Williams for a more competitive place in the 2028 market — and a pairing of Albon and Sainz, if confirmed, would represent one of the more experienced and commercially valuable lineups the team has fielded in years.

The Sainz Situation

Sainz's path has been complicated by the broader silly season dynamics that have characterized 2026. With 14 of 20 seats for the 2027 grid still unconfirmed, Sainz has been waiting for the Verstappen domino — the assumption being that Verstappen's 2027 decision would cascade through the market. Williams, rather than waiting, appears to have moved decisively.

The "massive contract" framing is significant. Williams is not known for outspending rivals on driver salaries, which makes the reported terms a departure from their typical approach. It suggests Williams views Sainz not merely as a competitive choice but as a statement hire — a driver who brings both pace and the profile to attract sponsorship and attention to a team in the middle of a credibility rebuild.

No official Williams announcement on Sainz has been published as of this writing, but reporting from multiple credible outlets places the deal as imminent. The Dutch GP paddock would be a natural backdrop for the announcement.

What's Next

The formal Carlos Sainz announcement from Williams is expected within days — potentially before or during the Dutch Grand Prix weekend. Once both signings are confirmed, Williams will have one of the most interesting lineups in the paddock locked in for 2027. The wider 2027 silly season — with 14 seats still available — will continue to unspool through the second half of the 2026 campaign as the Verstappen decision and other dominoes continue to fall.
