Champion Antonelli and Mercedes Eye Strong Finish to Historic 2026 Season in Final Two Races

By Ryan Calloway | November 24, 2026 | F1 Fanrecap

Kimi Antonelli is the 2026 Formula 1 World Champion. That fact was settled weeks before the Qatar Grand Prix, and it makes the final two rounds of the season — this weekend at Lusail and then Abu Dhabi — something different from what they have been in previous years. There is no tension around the title. There is only the opportunity to finish a remarkable season with the kind of performances that belong in its final chapter.

Antonelli arrives in Qatar with 402 championship points after finishing fourth in Las Vegas. Mercedes leads the constructors' standings too, having built a campaign around consistent scoring from both cars across the full season. The constructors' title is secured, but the total at the end of the year carries prestige and legacy, and the team will push for maximum points across both remaining races.

What This Season Has Meant

Antonelli's championship is one of the more extraordinary results in Formula 1's recent history. A driver in his first full season, still relatively young by any measure of the sport, winning the title by a 98-point margin. The numbers are remarkable and the manner of the season has been equally so — consistent, controlled, rarely spectacular in the overwrought sense but relentlessly effective over the course of a year.

Mercedes built the car around his instincts and he delivered. The partnership between driver and machine that every team chases, but rarely finds this completely in a debut season, was present throughout 2026. When the season is fully closed out in Abu Dhabi, it will carry the weight of one of the sport's more complete championship stories.

Qatar as a Closing Statement

Finishing fourth in Las Vegas was not Antonelli's ideal result, but the race itself was complicated by the Hamilton-Leclerc contact and a track that produced strategic unpredictability. At Lusail — a circuit that rewards the kind of commitment and precision that has defined this year's champion — he will be looking to remind the paddock what the full package looks like.

Mercedes will have learned from Las Vegas. Strategy under Pirelli's new 27-lap tyre limit will be refined. The car will be set up for Lusail's high-speed demands. And Antonelli, free from the pressure of fighting for a title he already holds, can race with a clarity that sometimes eludes drivers who are still chasing something.

What's Next

Qatar this weekend, Abu Dhabi next. The 2026 season closes in two races. For Antonelli and Mercedes, the goal is a strong finish to a historic year — maximum points, clean racing, and a final set of performances that complete the picture properly. The championship narrative is written. These two races add the full stop.
