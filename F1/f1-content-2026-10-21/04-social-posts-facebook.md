### STORY 5: COTA in 2026 — The New Formula

**Long-Form Post:**
Window: 5:00 PM ET

The 2026 United States Grand Prix at the Circuit of the Americas is a different race than it was a year ago.

Two significant changes have altered the technical picture heading into Sunday's race at Austin:

**Active aerodynamics replace DRS.** In 2026, F1 eliminated the Drag Reduction System — the movable rear wing flap that drivers used to aid overtaking on straights. In its place, 2026's new active aerodynamic system operates throughout the lap, with an "Overtake Mode" that drivers can engage to reduce drag and boost attack capability. At COTA, where the long back straight and Turn 12 hairpin create natural overtaking moments, Overtake Mode will define the passing battles.

**Turns 9-11 resurfaced.** The COTA corner sequence at Turns 9-11 — a flowing, rhythmic set of curves — has been completely resurfaced ahead of this season's race. New tarmac means different grip levels, potentially different lap records, and — critically — different tire degradation rates for the race day strategists.

For teams and drivers who come in with strong COTA data from prior years, that data is now partially obsolete. The practice sessions on Thursday and Friday will be critical for understanding how the resurfaced section behaves.

This is Formula 1 in 2026: a fundamentally different car on a partially different circuit. The challenge for every team starts the moment they hit the COTA pit lane on Thursday.
