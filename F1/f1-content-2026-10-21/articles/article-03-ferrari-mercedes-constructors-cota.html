134 Points Back: Ferrari's Constructors' Fight Against Mercedes Enters the Final Phase

By Elena Voss | F1 Fanrecap | October 21, 2026

Kimi Antonelli's march toward the 2026 drivers' title has dominated the conversation. But below the surface of the standings, a second championship battle is very much alive — and it runs through Austin this Sunday.

Mercedes leads Ferrari in the Constructors' Championship by 134 points: 578 to 444. Six rounds remain. That means Ferrari needs to find 135 points that Mercedes cannot match over the final stretch of the season. Tall order? Yes. Impossible? Not yet.

The Ferrari Case

Charles Leclerc has been the spine of Ferrari's constructors' campaign in 2026. His Singapore podium — second behind Verstappen's dominant victory — was another example of the consistency that has kept Ferrari competitive at every round. Leclerc understands the art of bringing home maximum points when the win isn't available, and in a constructors' fight, those second and third places accumulate.

Carlos Sainz has also contributed throughout the season. Double points from both Ferrari drivers at each round is exactly the combination that keeps the constructors' gap manageable.

If Ferrari can take both drivers to the podium at COTA — first and third, or second and third — while Mercedes scores less, a genuine dent in that 134-point deficit becomes possible.

The Mercedes Reality

Mercedes' advantage comes from one dominant source: Kimi Antonelli. His season-long dominance has been the primary driver of Mercedes' constructors' points haul, supplemented by Russell's three wins and consistent scoring. When both Mercedes cars finish in the top five, they accumulate points at a rate that is difficult to match over a full season.

The constructors' fight between Ferrari and Mercedes has an inherent structural problem for Ferrari: every time Antonelli wins and Russell podiums — which has happened frequently — Mercedes takes a massive 43-point swing and Ferrari cannot close the gap even with two podiums of their own.

For Ferrari to win the constructors' title, they would need multiple rounds where their two drivers outscore both Mercedes cars combined. That hasn't happened consistently in 2026.

Why COTA Still Matters

Every championship is decided by margin. If Ferrari and Leclerc can win at COTA while Mercedes stumbles — Antonelli and Russell both outside the points, or dealing with car issues — the gap narrows dramatically and the final five rounds of the season become genuinely consequential for the constructors' title.

Leclerc at COTA is a genuine contender. He won at Silverstone earlier this season in a race that demonstrated Ferrari's qualifying pace. If the resurfaced Turns 9-11 suit the SF-26's mechanical grip characteristics and the setup work goes well this week, Charles Leclerc can be a factor at the front on Sunday.

What's Next

The constructors' championship needs Ferrari to be perfect from here. Six rounds, 300 points on offer, 134 points to overcome — it requires help. But the constructors' title in Formula 1 is never officially over until the mathematics say so, and Ferrari has the drivers to keep this fight alive through Austin.

Watch where Leclerc qualifies Saturday. Watch where Sainz starts. The constructors' subplot of the 2026 US Grand Prix weekend could be the most consequential second story of the race.
