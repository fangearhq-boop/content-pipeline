Verstappen the Spoiler: Can Red Bull Rain on Antonelli's Title Party at COTA?

By Ryan Calloway | F1 Fanrecap | October 21, 2026

Max Verstappen is not going to win the 2026 Formula 1 World Championship. The mathematics are gone — he's 119 points behind Antonelli with six rounds remaining.

But Max Verstappen can absolutely prevent Kimi Antonelli from winning it in Austin.

In motorsport, there is a role almost as satisfying as winning the championship: being the person who makes someone else wait. Verstappen, a four-time world champion arriving at one of his favorite circuits in the best form of his recent season, is the most dangerous spoiler on the grid this Sunday.

COTA Is Verstappen Territory

The numbers are stark. Verstappen won the United States Grand Prix in 2021, 2022, and 2023 — three consecutive victories at the Circuit of the Americas. His lap record at COTA stands at 1:36.169. He knows every elevation change, every brake point, every apex in Austin as well as any active driver in the field.

The 2026 technical regulations — new active aerodynamics, resurfaced corners at Turns 9-11 — create uncertainty for every driver. But experience matters even when the car is different. Verstappen's structural knowledge of COTA gives him an edge in the first practice sessions that can be converted into a qualifying advantage by Saturday.

The Form Factor

Red Bull's resurgence in the second half of the 2026 season has made Verstappen a genuine threat at every circuit. His back-to-back wins in Malaysia and Singapore — Singapore being a dominant performance where he led from lights to flag and finished 7.3 seconds clear of Leclerc — demonstrated that the Red Bull RB22 can compete with the dominant Mercedes when the setup conditions align.

COTA, with its technical demands and historically high traction requirements, may suit the characteristics that Red Bull unlocked in their recent race wins. If Verstappen qualifies on the front row on Saturday — which his COTA record makes plausible — he becomes the driver that both Antonelli and Russell must account for in their race strategies.

The Spoiler Dynamic

For Antonelli to clinch the title in Austin, he needs to win and needs Russell to finish ninth or lower and Hamilton fourth or lower. Verstappen winning the race removes Antonelli's clinch opportunity entirely — a second-place Antonelli scores 18 points, not 25, and Russell can more easily remain within range.

None of this is about Red Bull's championship hopes. Verstappen knows his title chances this year are gone. But a win at COTA is its own reward — it extends his record there, it proves Red Bull's late-season form, and it sends a message about what Red Bull can do in 2027. That motivation is real.

What's Next

Verstappen at COTA is always must-watch Formula 1. With the championship clinch scenario in play, the stakes are higher than usual. A Verstappen win wouldn't change the title race fundamentally — Antonelli will still be heavily favored — but it would guarantee the drama continues into Mexico City and beyond.

The man who cannot win the championship may turn out to be the most consequential driver of the 2026 United States Grand Prix weekend.
