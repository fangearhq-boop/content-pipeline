LeBron's 76ers Debut Is Almost Here: Training Camp, Preseason Dates and What to Expect

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | September 5, 2026

The most anticipated training camp in the NBA opens in less than four weeks — and it's not happening in Los Angeles, Boston, or Miami. It's in Philadelphia.

LeBron James. Joel Embiid. Jaylen Brown. Tyrese Maxey. VJ Edgecombe. The Philadelphia 76ers assembled one of the most star-studded rosters in modern NBA history this offseason, and on September 28-29, the full group will officially report for duty. The questions that have dominated basketball conversation all summer are about to start getting answered.

The Schedule

NBA training camps open September 28 for teams with overseas preseason commitments and September 29 for the general league. The Sixers fall into the latter group, with their preseason schedule set as follows:

  • October 8: at Brooklyn Nets — LeBron's first game in a Sixers uniform
  • October 10: at Boston Celtics
  • October 16: vs. Boston Celtics (home, Xfinity Mobile Arena)

The Sixers also signed Tacko Fall, Jameer Nelson Jr., and Saint Thomas to Exhibit 10 training camp contracts — players competing for spots with the team's G League affiliate.

The regular season opener is October 20 at Madison Square Garden against the defending champion New York Knicks — a statement game on the road, day one. Philadelphia's home opener follows on October 22 against the Cleveland Cavaliers.

The Questions Heading Into Camp

The Sixers look extraordinary on paper. Making it work in practice requires answers to real questions:

What is LeBron's role at 41? His 2-year, $8M deal is not a max contract — it's a veteran minimum arrangement that signals LeBron is coming to Philadelphia to compete, not to be the franchise. He has accepted a supporting role in theory. Whether that translates into a functioning on-court identity alongside Embiid is the central mystery of training camp.

Can Embiid stay healthy? Embiid's injury history has been the persistent caveat attached to every Sixers title conversation for a decade. A fully healthy Embiid paired with LeBron and Brown is a legitimate championship-winning combination. An Embiid managing his minutes through the postseason is a different calculation entirely.

How does the offense run? Brown is a shot-creator. Maxey is a dynamo in pick-and-roll. LeBron is perhaps the greatest playmaker the sport has ever seen. Three players who each want the ball in their hands — with Embiid demanding post touches — creates a puzzle worth watching coaches solve in real time.

The Knicks Await

Philadelphia's season opener against the defending champion Knicks at Madison Square Garden is exactly the kind of moment this roster was built for. The Knicks won the 2025-26 NBA title — their first in decades — and the Sixers are arriving in New York on opening night with a team assembled explicitly to dethrone them.

It won't be a subtle beginning. The NBA has made sure of that.

What's Next

Camp opens September 29. The first preseason look at LeBron in Sixers red, white, and blue comes October 8 in Brooklyn. Between now and then, every practice report, every scrimmage update, and every quote from the locker room will be treated as breaking news. That's the price — and privilege — of having the most watched team in the NBA.
