### STORY 2: Defending Champion Knicks Eliminated

**Long-Form Post**
**Window:** 10:00 AM ET

The defending NBA Cup champions are heading home early.

The New York Knicks lost 108-105 to Miami on Black Friday, finishing Cup group play at 1-3 and missing the knockout round entirely. Giannis Antetokounmpo made sure of it — the Heat's superstar delivered 22 points and 23 rebounds in what has to be one of the great individual performances in group-play history.

The Philadelphia 76ers won East Group B on the head-to-head tiebreaker over Miami, meaning the Heat are also out despite winning the game that mattered most. Only in a tiebreaker situation could you beat your rival, win the game, and still miss the bracket.

For the Knicks, it's a stunning reversal. They were Cup champions just a year ago. Now they're watching from the couch in December.

The good news for New York: they've been playing excellent regular-season basketball. The Cup is gone. The playoffs are still very much in reach.
