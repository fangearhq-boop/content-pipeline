NBA Cup Bracket Is Set: Mavericks Are the Only Unbeaten Team After Friday's Group-Play Finale

By Jake Torres

November 28, 2026

The 2026 NBA Cup knockout bracket is finalized, and the storylines entering the quarterfinal round are ready-made. Eight teams advanced out of group play. One of them went undefeated. The quarterfinals begin Dec. 4, the championship is Dec. 11 at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis, and every game is on Prime Video.

Dallas is the team everyone should be watching. The Mavericks went 4-0 in West Group A — the only side in the entire tournament to finish group play without a loss. They closed it out Friday night with a 113-109 win over Phoenix. That is the kind of unbeaten run that tends to carry momentum into a knockout bracket, and Dallas earned the No. 1 West seed as a result.

The Full Bracket

Here are the four quarterfinal matchups, with games scheduled across Dec. 4-5:

In the East, the Boston Celtics travel to Detroit to face the Pistons on Dec. 4. Boston enters as the East wild card after finishing 3-1 in East Group C — same record as group winner Atlanta. The Celtics qualified based on point differential, which makes the Atlantic road trip feel like unfinished business from group play.

Also in the East, the Atlanta Hawks host the Philadelphia 76ers on Dec. 5. Philadelphia won East Group B on the head-to-head tiebreaker over Miami, earning the No. 2 East seed. Atlanta, the East Group C winner, draws that matchup.

Out West, the San Antonio Spurs travel to Dallas on Dec. 4 in what is immediately the bracket's most intriguing matchup. The Spurs arrived as wild card after their comeback win over the Lakers on Friday. The Mavericks, fresh off a perfect group stage, will be waiting.

Also in the West, the Memphis Grizzlies take on the Portland Trail Blazers on Dec. 5. Memphis won West Group B with a winner-take-all victory over Minnesota. Portland won West Group C.

Dallas Has Earned Every Advantage

Going 4-0 in group play is not a fluke. The Mavericks won every Cup game they played while other contenders stumbled in close finishes, tiebreaker situations, and high-pressure finale games. Cooper Flagg has been the driving force — the second-year forward's combination of scoring, playmaking, and defense gives Dallas a legitimately two-way star at the center of their Cup run.

The No. 1 West seed means Dallas hosts a quarterfinal game against San Antonio. For the Spurs, it is an enormous challenge after the energy they spent beating the Lakers on Friday. For the Mavericks, it is a chance to prove the perfect group-play record was not circumstantial.

What Got Left Behind

Several notable names won't be in the Dec. 4-5 field. The New York Knicks — defending Cup champions — were eliminated Friday night after a 108-105 loss to Miami, finishing group play at 1-3. The Los Angeles Lakers, after losing 99-96 to San Antonio, also missed the knockout round. The Miami Heat, despite beating the Knicks, were eliminated on the East Group B tiebreaker behind Philadelphia.

The Milwaukee Bucks (2-17 overall) were knocked out of Cup contention weeks ago. The teams that survived group play are, by and large, the ones playing well enough to deserve a December stage.

What's Next

The Cup quarterfinals tip off Dec. 4 with Celtics-Pistons and Spurs-Mavericks, then resume Dec. 5 with Hawks-76ers and Grizzlies-Trail Blazers. All four games are on Prime Video. The two semifinal winners from each conference will meet in Indianapolis on Dec. 11 for the championship at Hinkle Fieldhouse. The bracket is set, the matchups are compelling, and December basketball just got a lot more interesting.
