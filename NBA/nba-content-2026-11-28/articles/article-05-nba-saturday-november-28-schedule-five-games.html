NBA Saturday: Five Games on the Board as the League Returns to Regular-Season Mode

By Marcus Cole

November 28, 2026

The NBA Cup bracket is set. The drama of Black Friday group-play finales is behind us. Saturday's five-game slate is a return to regular-season basketball — no Cup implications, no bracket positioning, just teams trying to build momentum as December approaches.

The Orlando Magic and Washington Wizards open things up at 5:00 PM ET in a matchup that sets the tone for the evening. The rest of the board fills in across the late afternoon and evening windows.

What to Watch

Teams emerging from Friday's Cup finale will be processing two very different types of games. The eight teams that advanced — Dallas, Detroit, Philadelphia, Atlanta, Boston, Portland, Memphis, and San Antonio — will be carrying energy and momentum into their next regular-season games. The teams that were eliminated will be in reset mode, working to redirect that focus toward the remaining two-thirds of the regular season.

Saturday's slate provides the first glimpse of which teams respond best to the Cup's conclusion. The transition from Cup-intensity to regular-season play is a genuine psychological adjustment. Teams that make it cleanly tend to build winning streaks. Teams that linger on results — good or bad — can drop games they should win.

Magic at Wizards — 5:00 PM ET

Orlando enters Saturday as one of the more intriguing teams in the Eastern Conference. The Magic's defensive structure under their coaching staff has made them one of the more difficult teams to score against in the league, and their young core continues to develop game to game. Washington provides an early test against a team that has had a challenging season and will be motivated by home-crowd energy.

Paolo Banchero remains the central offensive piece for Orlando — his ability to attack closeouts and operate in the mid-post gives the Magic a primary creator who can function in multiple systems. The Wizards will look to push pace and generate easy looks in transition before Orlando can set its defense.

December Is Coming

Saturday's games matter in the standings context. December arrives in three days, and the Eastern and Western Conference pictures are still very much in flux. The Cup drew a clear line between eight teams with a December bracket destination and the rest of the league now focused purely on playoff seeding. For teams outside the Cup knockout round, Saturday is about reclaiming purpose and direction after a month dominated by tournament storylines.

Five games across Saturday provide enough action to deliver a storyline or two. The post-Cup regular-season has officially begun.

What's Next

The NBA Cup quarterfinals begin Dec. 4 with Celtics-Pistons and Spurs-Mavericks on Prime Video. Dec. 5 follows with Hawks-76ers and Grizzlies-Trail Blazers. Between now and then, the regular season keeps delivering nightly basketball — Saturday's five games are the starting point of the stretch run toward December Cup action and beyond.
