There's also a business angle to this. Strawther was eligible for a rookie contract extension through October 19. It's unclear whether that deal got done before the injury news became official.

The Nuggets are still one of the West's top teams. But they're opening the year without a piece they were counting on — and in this league, roster depth gets tested early.

The Kings, on the other hand, just got the best possible draw for a season opener.
