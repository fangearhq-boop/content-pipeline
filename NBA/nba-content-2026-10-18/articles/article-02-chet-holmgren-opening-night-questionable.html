Thunder's Holmgren Questionable for Opening Night as Injury Report Looms

By Marcus Cole October 18, 2026

The Quad Contusion That Has Oklahoma City Holding Its Breath

Chet Holmgren has not played a single preseason game. A quad contusion kept him out of every exhibition, and through Saturday the Thunder were still not ready to commit to a status for Tuesday's Opening Night matchup against the San Antonio Spurs. Head coach Mark Daigneault addressed reporters on October 17: "I'll leave that to the injury report. We'll report at 5 p.m. on the day before the first game." That means fans won't have a definitive answer until Monday evening at the earliest.

Multiple sources have Holmgren listed in conflicting ways. Rotowire has him questionable with an estimated return date of October 20. CBS Sports reported a downgrade to out, though that designation may reflect outdated information. The official report Monday afternoon will settle it. Until then, Oklahoma City's starting center situation is genuinely uncertain.

What a Holmgren Absence Means for the Thunder-Spurs Matchup

This game was circled from the moment the schedule came out. Oklahoma City beat the Spurs in seven games in last year's Western Conference Finals before falling to the Knicks in the NBA Finals. Victor Wembanyama against Holmgren at center was supposed to be the premier big-man matchup of Opening Night. If Holmgren sits, that matchup disappears and the Spurs walk into Tuesday with a significant size advantage at the five.

Oklahoma City is also without Otega Oweh for the entire season. The wing guard suffered an Achilles tear in preseason and will not play this year. Losing both Oweh and potentially Holmgren for Game 1 is a significant blow to a team that was favored in some odds formats to make another deep run. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander will play, and he will lead the team regardless. But depth becomes a real concern when two of your top seven contributors are unavailable from the jump.

SGA's Ability to Carry the Load

If there's a silver lining, it's that Shai Gilgeous-Alexander has done this before. His 32.5 player efficiency rating last season led the league, and his 64% true shooting percentage put him in an elite tier of offensive efficiency. In the 2025 playoffs he averaged over 30 points per game across six series games. The Thunder built this roster around SGA as the engine, which means the team does not collapse without Holmgren. It becomes harder, particularly against a healthy Spurs team, but Oklahoma City can compete.

The bigger question is whether a game-one loss with Holmgren sidelined would set the tone for a difficult early stretch. Oklahoma City's first 10 games include four road contests. Getting the season started without a full lineup would not be ideal, but it would not define the year either.

What's Next

The official Thunder injury report drops Monday, October 19 at 5 PM ET. That's the moment OKC fans have been waiting for. If Holmgren is cleared, the West Finals rematch gets the marquee center matchup it deserves. If he's out, SGA and a shorthanded Thunder team have to find a way to steal one in San Antonio. Tipoff is 9:30 PM ET on NBC and Peacock.
