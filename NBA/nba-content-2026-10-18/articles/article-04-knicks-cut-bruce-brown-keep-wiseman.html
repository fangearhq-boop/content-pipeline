Knicks Cut Bruce Brown, Keep Wiseman as Second Apron Math Forces the Call

By Jake Torres October 18, 2026

The Decision New York Had to Make

On October 17, the New York Knicks waived Bruce Brown and formally set their 15-man roster with James Wiseman as the third center. The choice came down to the NBA's second apron threshold, which owner James Dolan has been consistent about not crossing. Carrying both Brown, a wing, and Wiseman, a big, pushed the Knicks' salary structure past that line. The cap math made the decision.

Brown played all 82 regular-season games for the Denver Nuggets last season before signing with New York during training camp. He was a depth piece with championship experience and a specific skill set as a switchable defender who does not need the ball to contribute. Losing him leaves the Knicks thinner at wing behind the starting five of Jalen Brunson, Karl-Anthony Towns, OG Anunoby, Mikal Bridges, and Josh Hart.

What Wiseman Brings

Wiseman, 24, is the insurance policy at center behind whatever starter New York runs at the five. The former No. 2 overall pick out of Memphis has spent his career managing high expectations against a fitful injury history, but at this stage of his development he gives head coach Tom Thibodeau a backup big with mobility and rim-running capability. That profile is a better fit for the system than Brown's wing position in a roster already deep at that spot.

The defending champions are not notably weaker for this decision. The Knicks were not relying on Brown for significant minutes in any competitive scenario. Pacome Dadiet and Miles Kolek are the remaining perimeter depth options behind the starters, and both have shown enough in camp to handle that role.

Could Brown Come Back?

The path to a Brown reunion exists. If New York makes a trade that changes the roster's salary structure, Brown would be a logical reclaim target. He would clear waivers quickly given his contract situation, and his familiarity with the Knicks' system from training camp would make reintegration straightforward. Teams do this regularly when the salary math shifts during the season. But for now, Brown is available, and the Knicks move forward without him.

What's Next

The Knicks' roster is finalized heading into Tuesday's Opening Night game against the Philadelphia 76ers at Madison Square Garden. The banner ceremony starts at 6:30 PM ET. Whatever depth questions exist about the back end of the roster, the five players who need to show up Tuesday will be on the floor when Jalen Brunson gets his championship ring.
