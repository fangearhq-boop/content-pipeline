Opening Night Is Two Days Away: Your Complete Knicks Banner Night Guide

By Jake Torres October 18, 2026

The Night New York Has Been Waiting 53 Years For

Tuesday night at Madison Square Garden, the New York Knicks will raise a championship banner for the first time since 1973. The 2026 title over the San Antonio Spurs closed out the longest drought in NBA championship history for any franchise still playing in its original market. The banner ceremony starts at 6:30 PM ET, and tipoff against the Philadelphia 76ers follows at 7:00 PM on NBC and Peacock.

Jalen Brunson, the Finals MVP who dropped 45 points in Game 5 to close out the Spurs, leads the entire starting five back. Karl-Anthony Towns, OG Anunoby, Mikal Bridges, and Josh Hart are all returning. The only notable departure was Mitchell Robinson, who signed with the Boston Celtics in the offseason. Otherwise, the defending champions take the floor the same way they finished last June.

LeBron James at MSG as the Villain

The storyline the league could not have scripted better: LeBron James, 41 years old, makes his Philadelphia 76ers regular-season debut at Madison Square Garden on the same night the Knicks hoist their banner. LeBron and Jaylen Brown signed with Philly this summer in what was immediately called the league's most interesting roster construction. Brown was acquired from Boston. Paul George went the other way.

James played 18 minutes in the preseason finale against Brooklyn, showing 10 points, four rebounds, and five assists before sitting the second half. He has dealt with chronic arthritis in his left foot, per ESPN's Dave McMenamin, but his participation Tuesday is not in question. Walking into the Garden as the visiting team's biggest star, on banner night for the home crowd, with all of MSG working against him from the first possession. The LeBron-at-MSG drama is baked into the night whether he plays 20 minutes or 38.

Josh Hart's Health and the Full Opening Night Picture

The one uncertainty on New York's side is Josh Hart, who is listed as day-to-day with a minor injury. Head coach Tom Thibodeau said he is not concerned about Hart missing Opening Night. If Hart does play, all five starters take the floor together for the first time since last spring's run. That continuity matters for a team that built its identity around defensive connectivity and the shared sacrifice those five guys made through the postseason.

The full Opening Night tripleheader runs all afternoon and evening on NBC and Peacock. The Celtics open at Detroit at 3 PM ET. Then comes the main event at MSG at 7. After that, the Oklahoma City Thunder and Spurs tip off in San Antonio at 9:30 PM ET for the West Finals rematch. Three games. One channel. It's the league's biggest single night of the year and the schedule delivers.

What's Next

Tipoff is Tuesday at 7 PM ET on NBC. If you're watching one game all year, this is the one to start with. The ring ceremony at 6:30 gives the garden its moment. And then the 76ers show up and try to steal it.
