Nuggets Lose Julian Strawther for Six Weeks — MRI Confirms Knee Sprain
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post The MRI results are in, and they're not what Denver wanted to hear. Julian Strawther suffered a left knee sprain during the third quarter of Colorado's preseason loss to the Clippers on October 14. The good news: no structural damage, no torn ligament. The bad news: he's out at least six weeks, which means the Nuggets won't have him back until around Thanksgiving. Strawther had developed into a reliable perimeter shooter and rotational piece for Denver over the past two seasons. Losing him for the first five-plus weeks of the schedule forces head coach David Adelman to lean harder on Christian Braun and Peyton Watson for wing minutes while also navigating depth questions throughout the rotation. There's also a business angle to this. Strawther was eligible for a rookie contract extension through October 19. It's unclear whether that deal got done before the injury news became official. The Nuggets are still one of the West's top teams. But they're opening the year without a piece they were counting on — and in this league, roster depth gets tested early. --- ### Image Post Caption Six weeks. That's the Strawther timeline. ### Long-Form Post The Knicks made their last roster decision Saturday, and it came down to one thing: the second apron. New York waived Bruce Brown to keep James Wiseman. Brown had played all 82 games for the Denver Nuggets last season before signing with the Knicks in camp. Wiseman won the final center spot on the roster, giving New York three big men heading into a title defense. Head coach Tom Thibodeau and the front office reportedly felt that carrying both Brown (a wing) and Wiseman (a center) in the salary structure exceeded the second apron threshold that owner James Dolan has been committed to staying under. The math won. Brown could potentially return if the Knicks make a trade that reshuffles their cap situation. In the meantime, Pacome Dadiet and Miles Kolek are the remaining wings behind the starting five. The full Knicks roster is set. Clippers Opening Night Roster Confirmed — Ingram, Beal, Strus, Konan Out
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post Tyronn Lue made it official: the Clippers are opening the season without four rotation players. Out for the October 21 game against the Sacramento Kings: Brandon Ingram, Bradley Beal (knee inflammation, no timeline), Max Strus, and rookie Yanic Konan Niederhauser (foot injury). That's a significant portion of the rotation gone before the first real game of the year. Cameron Christie, who played on a two-way contract, is going to get meaningful run. Babi Miller is expected to start at center. Lue and his staff are going to have to get creative with a shorter-than-expected lineup in game one. The most concerning name on that list is Beal. Knee inflammation with no timeline means there's no certainty on when — or if — he comes back healthy for a sustained stretch this year. Los Angeles signed Beal this summer expecting a major contributor. This is not the start they were hoping for. The Kings, on the other hand, just got the best possible draw for a season opener. --- ### Image Post Caption Four Clippers out for the season opener. Ingram. Beal. Strus. Konan.
