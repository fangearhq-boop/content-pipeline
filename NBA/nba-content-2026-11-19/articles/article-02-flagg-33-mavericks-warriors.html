Flagg Erupts for 33, Mavericks Rout Shorthanded Warriors 116-94

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | November 19, 2026

Cooper Flagg dropped 33 points on 11-of-15 shooting Wednesday night, and the Dallas Mavericks blew out the Golden State Warriors 116-94 at American Airlines Center to snap a three-game losing streak. With P.J. Washington adding 21 and Kyrie Irving returning to the lineup with 17, Dallas looked every bit like the Eastern Conference contender it aspired to be this season.

The 19-year-old Flagg also contributed 9 assists, 5 rebounds, and 3 steals — a stat line that underscored just how complete his game has become in his first full NBA season.

The LeBron Comparison No One Can Avoid

Wednesday's performance was Flagg's 15th career game with at least 30 points. According to Dallas team data, that is the second-most 30-point games by any NBA player before turning 20 years old — the only teenager with more was LeBron James, who had 20.

Flagg's efficiency is what separates him from most young scorers. He does not live at the free-throw line or force impossible shots. On Wednesday he shot 11-of-15, a clip that speaks to his ability to get quality looks through smart positioning and off-ball movement rather than brute athleticism alone.

Dallas posted a season-high 34 assists as a team, further evidence that Flagg's growth as a playmaker is elevating everyone around him.

Warriors in Crisis

Golden State came in short-handed and left looking worse for it. Stephen Curry is expected to miss at least another week. Jimmy Butler and Kristaps Porzingis were also unavailable. The Warriors shot just 24 percent from three-point range, a number that tells the story of a team struggling to generate quality looks without its offensive engine.

Dallas led 35-25 after the first quarter and the gap only widened. The Mavericks led by as many as 29 points and coasted through the fourth.

Irving's Quiet Contribution

Kyrie Irving missed one game earlier in the week before returning Wednesday. His 17 points were secondary to Flagg's performance, but his presence stabilizes the second unit and gives Dallas a veteran option in crunch-time situations. When all three of Dallas's key contributors are healthy and productive, this team is very difficult to match up against.

What's Next

Dallas will need to maintain this form heading into a stretch of games against sturdier competition. Wednesday's win was helped by Golden State's depleted roster, but the Mavericks' ball movement and Flagg's continued brilliance are real, regardless of the opponent. Golden State, meanwhile, faces a difficult road back to relevance while awaiting Curry's return.
