Thursday NBA Preview: Spurs Regroup, Bucks Seek Redemption in Four-Game Slate

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | November 19, 2026

Wednesday's NBA action delivered drama on multiple fronts. Thursday's four-game slate offers a chance for some of the storylines to continue and for a few teams to reset their narrative heading into the weekend. Here is what to watch.

Trail Blazers at Bucks (8:00 PM ET)

The Milwaukee Bucks play their first home game after losing to Giannis Antetokounmpo and the Miami Heat on Wednesday. At 1-14, the Bucks sit at the bottom of the NBA standings with questions about their roster, their system, and whether the trade that sent Antetokounmpo to Miami was a miscalculation.

Portland is a scrappy team with nothing to lose on the road, which could make this game tighter than Milwaukee's home crowd would want. The Bucks need a win to quiet the growing narrative around their early-season struggles.

Clippers at Spurs (8:00 PM ET)

The San Antonio Spurs saw their 12-game winning streak end Tuesday when the Detroit Pistons pulled off a road upset. Victor Wembanyama posted 25 points and 18 rebounds in that loss but could not overcome Cade Cunningham's performance or Detroit's resilience.

San Antonio will look to get back on track Thursday against a Los Angeles Clippers team navigating its own early-season turbulence. The Spurs remain one of the West's most watchable teams, and Thursday is a good opportunity to remind everyone their streak-breaking loss was an outlier, not a trend.

Knicks at Timberwolves (8:00 PM ET)

New York's Jalen Brunson and the Knicks have been one of the league's better stories through the first month of the season. Minnesota, bolstered by their frontcourt depth, presents one of the more difficult matchup problems in the Western Conference. This is a game where both teams have something to prove: the Knicks that they can win road games against elite competition, and Minnesota that their core is ready for prime time.

Warriors at Grizzlies (8:00 PM ET)

Golden State came off a blowout loss to Dallas on Wednesday, playing without Stephen Curry, Jimmy Butler, and Kristaps Porzingis. The Warriors face the Memphis Grizzlies Thursday in a matchup that could expose just how short-handed they truly are. Memphis has shown flashes of the physicality and energy that made them dangerous in prior seasons, and a win here would be a meaningful statement against an opponent missing its entire starting core.

What's Next

Thursday's games tip off at 8:00 PM ET. The Bucks and Spurs matches are the most compelling from a storyline perspective, and both results could meaningfully shift their respective narratives heading into the week ahead. Full recaps on Hoop Heroes as the results come in.
