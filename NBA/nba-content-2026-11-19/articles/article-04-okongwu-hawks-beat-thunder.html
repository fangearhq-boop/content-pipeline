Okongwu's Season-High 28, Johnson Triple-Double Power Hawks Past Thunder 117-112

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | November 19, 2026

Onyeka Okongwu erupted for a season-high 28 points and 9 rebounds, Jalen Johnson added a triple-double with 16 points, 12 rebounds, and 11 assists, and the Atlanta Hawks outlasted Shai Gilgeous-Alexander and the Oklahoma City Thunder 117-112 on Wednesday night in Oklahoma City.

The victory had no shortage of storylines. Former Thunder players Lu Dort and Aaron Wiggins, now playing for Atlanta, returned to their old home and combined for 30 points against their former teammates — a performance that OKC head coach Mark Daigneault acknowledged warmly after the game.

Okongwu Stakes His Claim

Through the early portion of this season, Okongwu has steadily established himself as one of the more dynamic big men in the Eastern Conference. Wednesday's output — 28 points, 9 rebounds, and a block against one of the league's top defensive units — was his most complete performance yet.

Nickeil Alexander-Walker complemented with 19 points, 4 rebounds, and 7 assists, and Johnson's triple-double reinforced what Atlanta's coaching staff has believed for several years: this team has the depth and versatility to compete with anyone on a given night.

SGA Can't Finish It

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander scored 34 points, continuing his run as one of the most reliable scorers in the league. But Atlanta's defense found him in the fourth quarter: SGA was held to just 3 points in the final period, and the Thunder could not generate enough secondary offense to compensate.

Jared McCain added 18 for Oklahoma City, and Isaiah Hartenstein grabbed 17 rebounds in a physically punishing performance. The effort was there. The firepower was not, and Atlanta's late 8-0 run sealed it.

The Dort and Wiggins Homecoming

The game's most sentimental thread involved two players Oklahoma City helped develop. Lu Dort, whose defensive intensity defined multiple Thunder playoff runs, and Aaron Wiggins, a reliable rotation piece during OKC's rise, were both on the floor in enemy colors Wednesday.

Daigneault said after the game that he was proud of both players and their careers. Their combined 30 points said everything about the kind of professionals they have become.

What's Next

Atlanta continues to build an identity as a team capable of winning road games against quality opponents. Oklahoma City, still one of the West's title favorites, will look to right the ship with a quick turnaround. The Thunder's reliance on SGA as the sole primary creator is worth monitoring as the season deepens.
