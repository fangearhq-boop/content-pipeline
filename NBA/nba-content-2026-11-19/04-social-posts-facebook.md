### STORY 1: Heat 137, Bucks 130 — Giannis vs Former Team

**Post 1** | Tier 1 | Posting window: 9:00 AM ET | Long-Form

🔥 GIANNIS BEATS THE BUCKS 🔥

One of the most emotionally charged games of the NBA season is now in the books.

Giannis Antetokounmpo walked back into the building — this time wearing Miami Heat red — and delivered 27 points, 14 rebounds, and 9 assists as the Heat dominated his former team 137-130 on Wednesday night.

It was Vice City Night in South Beach, and the Heat put on a show. Miami drained a staggering 22 three-pointers and won their sixth consecutive game to improve to 9-6 on the season. Meanwhile the Bucks — who gave up Giannis in a blockbuster trade — are now sitting at a franchise-low 1-14, the worst record in the NBA.

The drama added another layer: Tyler Herro, Jaime Jaquez Jr., Kel'el Ware, and Kasparas Jakucionis — the players Milwaukee received in the trade — were all on the court for the Bucks and combined for 49 points. It wasn't enough.

After the game, Giannis was characteristically measured. When asked if the win felt special: "As special as every other night."

That's championship mentality. The Heat are surging.

What's YOUR take — did Milwaukee get robbed in that trade? Let us know below 👇

### STORY 2: Mavericks 116, Warriors 94 — Flagg 33

**Post 2** | Tier 1 | Posting window: 11:00 AM ET | Long-Form

🔥 COOPER FLAGG IS THE REAL DEAL 🔥

33 points on 11-of-15 shooting. 9 assists. 5 rebounds. 3 steals. And a season-high 34 team assists as Dallas blew out the Golden State Warriors 116-94 on Wednesday night.

Cooper Flagg, 19 years old, has now recorded his 15th career 30-point game — the second most by a teenager in NBA history. The only player with more? A young man by the name of LeBron James.

Golden State was shorthanded without Stephen Curry (out at least another week), Jimmy Butler, and Kristaps Porzingis. Dallas didn't need any extra help — they led by 10 after the first quarter and by as many as 29 during the game. P.J. Washington added 21 and Kyrie Irving returned from a one-game absence to chip in 17.

After a rough three-game stretch, the Mavericks look like playoff contenders again.

Is Cooper Flagg the most exciting young player in the NBA right now? Drop your thoughts below 👇

### STORY 3: Nuggets 105, Celtics 101 — Comeback

**Post 3** | Tier 2 | Posting window: 1:00 PM ET | Long-Form

Nikola Jokic and Jamal Murray just reminded everyone why Denver is never out of a game.

Down by 17 in the first half, the Nuggets outscored the Boston Celtics 56-40 after the break and pulled off a stunning 105-101 comeback win at Ball Arena on Wednesday.

Jokic finished with 24 points, 8 rebounds, and 9 assists. Murray added 21. DeMar DeRozan delivered 18 points, including 13 in the fourth quarter, and his clutch free throws with 49 seconds remaining gave Denver the lead for good.

On the other side, Jayson Tatum scored 20 of his 24 points in the first half — then virtually disappeared (just 4 second-half points) as the Celtics collapsed. Boston was whistled for 30 fouls to Denver's 13, and Tatum drew a technical foul in the closing seconds.

Denver improved to 8-5. Boston dropped to 11-4.

What's going wrong for the Celtics in the second half? Share your thoughts below 👇

### STORY 4: Hawks 117, Thunder 112 — Okongwu

**Post 4** | Tier 2 | Posting window: 4:00 PM ET | Long-Form

Onyeka Okongwu is HAVING a season.

The Atlanta Hawks big man erupted for a career-high 28 points and 9 rebounds in an emphatic road win over the Oklahoma City Thunder Wednesday night, 117-112. Jalen Johnson added a triple-double (16 pts, 12 reb, 11 ast), and Nickeil Alexander-Walker chipped in 19.

The game had an extra subplot: former Thunder players Lu Dort and Aaron Wiggins were back in Oklahoma City for the first time since their departures — and they combined for 30 points against their old team. OKC coach Mark Daigneault was gracious in his post-game comments about both players.

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander scored 34 for the Thunder, but could only manage 3 points in the fourth quarter as Atlanta's defense clamped down at the right moment. The Hawks led by 13 at halftime, saw OKC tie it at 83 entering the fourth, then pulled away for good with an 8-0 run.
