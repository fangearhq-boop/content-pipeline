Jaden McDaniels Career-High 34, Randle Adds 29 as Timberwolves Rout Nets 142-116

By Jake Torres | November 24, 2026 | Hoop Heroes

Jaden McDaniels delivered the best performance of his NBA career Monday, scoring a career-high 34 points as the Minnesota Timberwolves blew out the Brooklyn Nets 142-116. Julius Randle added 29 points as Minnesota posted its highest scoring output of the season, putting together a complete offensive performance from start to finish.

The 142 points were a statement. The Nets, still searching for their footing this season, were overwhelmed from early on and never found a way back into the game. Minnesota's offense was in a different gear — crisp ball movement, high-percentage looks, and two players operating at their very best.

McDaniels' Career Night

McDaniels has been one of the more intriguing players to watch this season. Known primarily as a defensive contributor and an athlete who can guard multiple positions, his offensive game has steadily developed over the course of his career. Monday was a window into what he can become when everything is clicking.

Thirty-four points is a number that commands respect at any position. For a player who has historically been asked to do his work away from the ball, to step into a lead scoring role and deliver at that level speaks to real offensive growth. The Timberwolves will need that dimension from McDaniels if they want to reach their ceiling this season.

Randle as the Constant

Julius Randle's 29-point performance was equally important. With McDaniels drawing attention from Brooklyn's defense, Randle found quality looks and converted them. The two working in tandem — one elevated, the other exploiting the defensive adjustments — is exactly what Minnesota's offense looks like at its best.

Randle has brought steady, high-level production since joining the Timberwolves, and games like Monday are a reminder of how well-suited he is to this environment. He doesn't need the ball in his hands every possession to be effective, which makes him easier to fit alongside other high-usage players.

What 142 Points Means

Scoring 142 points in a regular season game is rare, even in today's pace-and-space environment. For Minnesota to reach that number says something about how fluidly their offense moved on Monday — there were no long possessions grinding to bad shots, just sustained pressure and good decisions. The Nets couldn't match it.

Brooklyn is in a rebuild and nights like this come with that territory. For Minnesota, the number is a benchmark of what their offense is capable of.

What's Next

The Timberwolves have made a strong statement with this performance, but the challenge now is consistency. Big offensive nights should fuel momentum, not become outliers. If McDaniels can sustain even a portion of this output going forward, Minnesota's ceiling rises considerably. Their upcoming schedule will reveal how repeatable Monday's effort truly was.
