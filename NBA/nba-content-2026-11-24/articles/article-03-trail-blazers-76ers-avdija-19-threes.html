Avdija Scores 31, Blazers Drain 19 Three-Pointers in 128-111 Rout of 76ers

By Marcus Cole | November 24, 2026 | Hoop Heroes

Deni Avdija scored 31 points and the Portland Trail Blazers made 19 three-pointers as a team to rout the Philadelphia 76ers 128-111 on Monday. Portland's outside shooting was relentless throughout, and the 76ers never found an answer for a Blazers offense that was comfortable from deep on every possession.

Nineteen threes in a single game is a remarkable team total — a performance that reflects both Portland's offensive design and the collective execution required to maintain that kind of range throughout 48 minutes. It was not just a hot stretch. It was a sustained attack.

Avdija's Evolution as a Scorer

Avdija's 31 points continued a stretch of consistent, high-level scoring that has redefined how opponents approach him. When he arrived in Portland, the conversation was about what he could add beyond scoring — his playmaking, his defense, his versatility. Those remain real. But his scoring is now the starting point.

Playing through Avdija creates problems for defenses because he forces opponents to choose how to guard him — back off and he hits the midrange or drives downhill; play him tight and he draws fouls or kicks to the open three-point shooter. On Monday, Philadelphia never found the right adjustment and Avdija made them pay for it throughout.

Portland's Three-Point Attack

The 19-three performance was not just about Avdija. Portland's entire shooting rotation contributed to the total, and that speaks to how the Blazers have built their offense. They play through pace and spacing, they move the ball to open looks, and when those looks are falling — as they were in Philadelphia — there is very little the defense can do to slow the tide.

The 76ers have defensive capabilities, but on a night when Portland was shooting at that efficiency, there were simply too many shooters to account for. The game turned into an exercise in Portland executing their system to near perfection.

What's Next

Portland's offensive explosion is a sign of genuine capability, but sustaining that shooting performance from game to game is the real challenge. When the threes are falling, this Blazers team is capable of beating anyone. What the rest of the season will reveal is how they respond when the shots aren't dropping — whether the defense and depth are strong enough to win ugly as well as beautiful.
