### STORY 1: Cade Cunningham 52 Career High

**Long-Form Post — 9:00 AM ET**

Cade Cunningham just had one of the most complete offensive performances in NBA history.

52 points. 11 assists. ZERO turnovers. In double overtime.

The Detroit Pistons outlasted the Washington Wizards 144-140 in a double-overtime thriller Thursday night, and Cunningham was nothing short of superhuman. He scored 30 of his 52 points in the fourth quarter and overtime periods alone. When Washington rallied from 7 down to tie it in the second overtime, Cunningham answered with a cold-blooded fadeaway that put Detroit ahead by four with 25 seconds left — and that was that.

Trae Young had 37 points and 14 assists in the loss, and Anthony Davis added 30 points and 15 rebounds — but none of it was enough. This was Cunningham's night, start to finish.

The Pistons are for real. Are you a believer yet?
