Maxey, Embiid Combine for 47 as 76ers Down Raptors 123-112

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | November 13, 2026

Tyrese Maxey scored 27 points and Joel Embiid added 20 as Philadelphia handled Toronto comfortably, while the Raptors continue to drop games in a troubling stretch.

The Philadelphia 76ers continued their steady early-season roll on Thursday, handling the Toronto Raptors 123-112 behind a combined 47-point effort from Tyrese Maxey and Joel Embiid. It was business as usual for the Sixers, who remain among the Eastern Conference's elite.

Maxey led all scorers with 27 points, showcasing the explosive offensive repertoire that has made him one of the most dangerous point guards in the league. Embiid, playing with his typically dominant efficiency, added 20 points as the duo controlled the game's pace and outcome.

Barrett Battles but Toronto Falls Short

RJ Barrett gave Raptors fans something to cheer about, finishing with 31 points in a spirited individual performance. Barrett has been one of Toronto's most consistent performers this season, but his efforts were not enough to overcome Philadelphia's overall depth and execution.

The Raptors have now lost four of their last five games, a stretch that is becoming increasingly concerning for a franchise still building its identity in what has been described as a transitional era. Toronto has the talent to be competitive, but converting that talent into wins has proven elusive.

Philadelphia Maintains Pace

For the 76ers, the win is part of a consistent body of work that has them sitting near the top of the Eastern Conference. With Maxey and Embiid healthy and clicking in tandem, Philadelphia looks capable of competing with anyone on the schedule.

The 76ers' defensive discipline was also on display, limiting Toronto's other offensive options and ensuring Barrett's big night remained an individual effort rather than a team rally.

What's Next

Philadelphia keeps rolling through a November schedule that will test their depth. If Maxey and Embiid remain healthy and productive at this rate, the Sixers are well-positioned to claim a top seed in the East.

Toronto needs answers soon. The Raptors' losing streak is not long enough to cause panic, but the pattern of close losses and inconsistent performances is worth monitoring. Barrett's continued production gives them a cornerstone to build around — but the roster around him needs to step up.

The 76ers head into the weekend with momentum. The Raptors head in looking for a reset.
