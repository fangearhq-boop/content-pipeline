By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | November 13, 2026

A loaded nine-game NBA Cup schedule arrives on Friday, November 13, highlighted by a Knicks-Pacers showdown on Prime Video and the Jazz visiting San Antonio.

After a week of thrilling results — including Cade Cunningham's 52-point masterpiece and Minnesota's dominant statement win — Friday delivers yet another opportunity for NBA action. Nine games are on the NBA Cup docket tonight, and the matchups are compelling from tip-off to final buzzer.

Headliner: Knicks vs. Pacers — 7:00 PM ET (Prime Video)

The marquee matchup of the evening brings together two Eastern Conference hopefuls with legitimate playoff aspirations. The New York Knicks host the Indiana Pacers at 7:00 PM ET in a game that will be broadcast nationally on Prime Video.

Both teams have been competitive early in the season, and games between these franchises carry extra stakes in the NBA Cup format, where group-stage points matter beyond just the standings. Expect physicality, guard play, and a charged Madison Square Garden atmosphere.

Jazz at Spurs — 8:00 PM ET (NBA LP)

The Utah Jazz travel to San Antonio to face the Spurs at 8:00 PM ET on NBA League Pass. The Spurs have been one of the more intriguing stories of the early season, and their home court at Frost Bank Center has proven to be a challenging destination for opponents this year.

The Jazz, in various stages of their own roster development, will look to find their footing against a disciplined San Antonio squad. Victor Wembanyama's impact on the defensive end makes this a difficult assignment for Utah's offense.

Magic at Bucks

The Orlando Magic and Milwaukee Bucks round out the notable matchups on a busy Friday evening. The Bucks have faced questions about their early-season consistency, and a home game against Orlando provides an opportunity to regain footing in the East standings. The Magic, one of the league's younger teams, will look to continue building off recent performances.
