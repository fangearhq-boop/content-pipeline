Knicks Banner Night Hits a Snag: MSG Ticket Glitch Sends Prices Above $1,000

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | September 18, 2026

New York Knicks

Madison Square Garden paused individual game ticket sales for the October 20 Opening Night after a technical error sent upper-level get-in prices climbing above $1,000. The Knicks have not announced a timeline for when sales will resume. The demand — while distorted by the glitch — is real. The night itself is unlike anything New York has experienced in more than half a century.

October 20 is the date the Knicks raise their first championship banner since 1973. Then, 30 minutes later, they tip off against the Philadelphia 76ers — the team that assembled LeBron James, Joel Embiid, Jaylen Brown, and Tyrese Maxey specifically to end what New York started in June.

The Banner — 53 Years in the Making

The ceremony begins at 6:30 p.m. ET. Finals MVP Jalen Brunson leads the Knicks onto the MSG floor for a celebration that a generation of New York fans feared would never come. The 2026 NBA title, won in five games over the San Antonio Spurs, ended a 53-year championship drought that had become one of professional sports' most discussed absences.

There are fans in the building October 20 who were not alive the last time the Knicks were champions. There are fans who sat through the 1994 Finals loss, the 1999 Finals loss, the long decades of middling rosters and empty springs. The banner is for all of them, in one way or another, and the building knows it.

The Game — And the Opponent

The 76ers' arrival at MSG for the Knicks' banner ceremony is not a coincidence. The NBA scheduled it intentionally. LeBron James, 41 years old and playing his first season in Philadelphia, makes his 76ers debut as the guest villain at Madison Square Garden on the night New York hoists its trophy. Jaylen Brown, acquired from Boston in the offseason, also debuts. Embiid and Maxey provide the established core. The game tips at 7:00 p.m. ET on NBC and Peacock.

The story writes itself. LeBron has spent his career being the obstacle between various franchises and championships. Now he watches the Knicks raise their banner — and then has three quarters to do something about it. Whether he does or does not, the NBA could not have scripted a more compelling opening act for the 2026-27 season.

The Ticket Problem

MSG's technical error underlines the demand the night has generated. Upper-level seats reaching $1,000 before secondary market resale suggests that primary market pricing — whatever the Knicks intended — was significantly outpaced by what fans are willing to pay. The arena's 20,000-plus capacity means thousands of fans will be there regardless. But for those trying to buy through official channels, the pause in sales has created uncertainty.

The Knicks have not released a new onsale date or communicated directly about the timeline for the fix. Given the profile of the event, a resolution before the preseason begins seems likely.

What's Next

MSG should restore ticket sales once the technical error is corrected. The banner ceremony is set for 6:30 p.m. ET on October 20, with tip-off at 7:00 p.m. ET on NBC and Peacock. The preseason begins October 3; the regular season's first game is the Knicks-76ers matchup in New York. Between now and then, watch for MSG to announce a new on-sale date and for the Knicks to release additional ceremony details as Opening Night approaches.
