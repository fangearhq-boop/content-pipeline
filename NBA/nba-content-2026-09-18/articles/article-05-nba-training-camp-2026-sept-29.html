11 Days to Camp: Everything You Need to Know About the 2026-27 NBA Season Calendar

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | September 18, 2026

NBA

Eleven days from now, the 2026-27 NBA season becomes real. Veterans report on September 28. Training camps open across all 30 franchises on September 29. After a summer of transactions, negotiations, and roster construction, basketball returns — and the storylines waiting in every locker room are stacked.

Here is a complete breakdown of the key dates, and the questions that will define the first days of camp.

The Calendar

Two teams — the Dallas Mavericks and the Houston Rockets — break from the standard schedule, reporting September 22 and holding media day September 21 for their preseason trip to Macao. For the other 28 franchises, the schedule runs as follows: veteran report date and media day on September 28; camps open September 29; preseason games begin October 3; preseason concludes October 16; and the regular season tips off October 20.

Opening Night features three games: the New York Knicks versus the Philadelphia 76ers at 7:00 p.m. ET on NBC and Peacock — the Knicks' banner ceremony begins at 6:30 — along with the Boston Celtics at the Detroit Pistons and the Oklahoma City Thunder at the San Antonio Spurs.

The Media Day Questions Every Team Is Walking Into

Media day is when the season's unresolved business gets a public forum. These are the questions every beat reporter is already preparing:

In Golden State: Does Stephen Curry walk in with a signed two-year extension, or does media day become an hour-long press conference about a contract that still has not been executed? Curry is eligible for $136.7 million over two years. As of Thursday, no deal has been announced.

In Detroit: Has the Jalen Duren standoff resolved? The qualifying offer deadline is October 1. If media day arrives and there is no deal, the Pistons spend their first public moment of camp explaining how they intend to keep their young center on a $9.6 million one-year offer — and what comes next.

In Indiana: Tyrese Haliburton returns to camp for the first time as a healthy player since tearing his Achilles in the 2025 NBA Finals. His first public appearances will define the entire Pacers' narrative through October.

In Philadelphia: How does a locker room that now includes LeBron James and Jaylen Brown function? And how does LeBron himself describe walking into Madison Square Garden on October 20 as a first-year Sixer?

The Broader Picture

The 2026-27 season inherits a landscape shaped by one of the most significant offseasons in recent memory. The Knicks are defending champions. The 76ers assembled a new superteam. The Warriors are waiting on their greatest player to sign a contract. The Pistons face losing a franchise center to a qualifying offer mistake.

And somewhere in Oklahoma City, the Thunder — the league's reigning No. 1 seed in the West for the past two seasons — are preparing another run with Shai Gilgeous-Alexander at the helm. Camp is when the intent meets the floor.

What's Next

Media day for most franchises is September 28. Training camps open September 29 league-wide. The preseason begins October 3 with a handful of games and runs through October 16 — giving fans roughly two weeks to watch new rosters take shape before the games count. The regular season opens October 20. Eleven days from now, the season stops being theoretical.
