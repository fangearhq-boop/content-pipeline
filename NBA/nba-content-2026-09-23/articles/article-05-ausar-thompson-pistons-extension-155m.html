Ausar Thompson Signs $155M Extension With Pistons — Twins Make NBA History

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | September 23, 2026

The Detroit Pistons have secured the defensive cornerstone of their roster for the next five years. Ausar Thompson, 23, has agreed to a fully guaranteed five-year, $155 million rookie-scale contract extension with the franchise — ending a summer of contract uncertainty and locking up one of the NBA's premier defenders in the peak years of his career.

The deal carries a piece of NBA history with it. Thompson and his twin brother Amen, who plays for the Houston Rockets, have now combined for approximately $363 million in contracts this offseason — making them the first brothers in NBA history to each sign a deal worth $100 million or more.

What Ausar Thompson Brings

The statistics from last season are straightforward: 9.9 points, 5.7 rebounds, 3.1 assists, 0.9 blocks, and 2.0 steals per game. That last number — 2.0 steals per game — led the entire NBA. It was not a product of gambling on weak-side rotations or taking risks that hurt the defense. It was the result of elite instincts, elite athleticism, and a defensive investment that defined every possession Thompson was on the floor.

The league recognized it. Thompson earned first-team All-Defense honors last season and finished third in Defensive Player of the Year voting. He is, at 23, the kind of defensive anchor that playoff contenders are built around — a player whose presence makes the entire team better on that end of the floor simply by virtue of what he takes away from opposing offenses.

Context: The Duren Situation

It is worth noting what else is happening in Detroit's contract landscape. The Pistons are simultaneously working to extend Jalen Duren — their All-Star center — whose October 1 qualifying offer deadline is eight days away with no agreement reached. The Thompson extension, finalized earlier this week, signals that Detroit is actively committed to maintaining the core of a 60-win team. Locking up Thompson is a statement of intent, even as the Duren situation remains unresolved.

The Pistons have now secured one of their two foundational defensive pieces. The work on the other continues.

The Twins' Milestone

The broader story here — the one that will be told beyond the basketball box score — is what the Thompson twins have accomplished together. Ausar and Amen Thompson entered the NBA in the same draft class, were selected in the top five, and have developed into two of the league's most promising young players on different franchises. This offseason, they both secured major long-term contracts: Ausar's $155 million with Detroit, Amen's deal with Houston bringing their combined total to approximately $363 million.

No brothers in NBA history have each signed contracts worth $100 million or more. The Thompson twins are now the first. That is a remarkable milestone for two 23-year-olds who came into the league together, took different paths, and arrived at the same destination: generational security and the promise of long careers at the highest level.

What's Next

With Thompson's extension secured, the Pistons head into media day on September 28 with one major piece of business resolved. Training camp opens September 29. Detroit's primary remaining storyline is the Duren situation — the October 1 deadline looms, and whether Duren shows up to media day will be the first clear signal of where that negotiation stands. For Thompson, the extension removes uncertainty and signals the start of what the Pistons believe will be a long run together. He is locked in through 2030-31 at minimum. That is what a team building around a 60-win core looks like when it secures its future.
