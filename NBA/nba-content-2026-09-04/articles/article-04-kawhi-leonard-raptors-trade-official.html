Kawhi Leonard Trade to Toronto Expected to Go Official Before Training Camp

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes

September 4, 2026

The Kawhi Leonard homecoming to Toronto is almost here.

Following the NBA's clearance of its investigation into the Los Angeles Clippers on September 2, the long-awaited trade sending Leonard to the Toronto Raptors is now free to be processed and made official. With Toronto's training camp scheduled to open September 28 in Quebec City, the clock is running — and Leonard expects to be a Raptor by then.

The Deal's Long Road

The trade was originally agreed upon on June 30, sending Leonard to Toronto in exchange for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, two future unprotected first-round picks (2031 and 2033), a 2027 first-round pick swap and two second-round picks.

The agreement was frozen on July 9 when the NBA launched its investigation into the Clippers' alleged cap circumvention — a scheme involving improper endorsements funneled through partners including Aspiration Partners and Boingo Wireless. The league concluded its investigation this week, stripping the Clippers of five consecutive first-round picks starting in 2029, fining the team $30 million and suspending owner Steve Ballmer, team president Lawrence Frank and general manager Paul Zucker.

With that chapter closed, the Leonard trade can now proceed through the normal NBA transaction process.

What It Means for Toronto

For the Raptors, getting Leonard back is the centerpiece of what looks like an aggressive window to compete. Leonard, who won a championship with Toronto in 2018-19 before signing with the Clippers in free agency, returns to a city that adores him.

The package Toronto sent to Los Angeles — headlined by Ingram and Dick — is a significant price to pay. But landing a healthy, motivated Kawhi Leonard gives the Raptors one of the 10 best players in the NBA and an immediate Eastern Conference contender.

With LeBron James in Philadelphia, Giannis Antetokounmpo in Miami and the Knicks defending their title, Toronto needed a marquee star to stay relevant. Kawhi is exactly that.

What's Next

The trade paperwork is expected to be submitted to the league shortly, with an official announcement to follow. Once completed, Leonard will undergo his physical and prepare to join the Raptors' training camp in Quebec City on September 28.

Toronto fans have been waiting since July 1, 2019 for another shot at contention. If Kawhi Leonard arrives healthy and motivated, the wait may soon feel very much worth it.
