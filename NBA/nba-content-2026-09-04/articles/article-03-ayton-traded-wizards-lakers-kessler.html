Lakers Trade DeAndre Ayton to Wizards as Roster Rebuild Continues

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes

September 4, 2026

The Los Angeles Lakers' offseason roster reconstruction keeps rolling.

The Lakers are trading center DeAndre Ayton to the Washington Wizards in exchange for guard Jaden Hardy and two second-round picks — one in 2031 and one in 2032. The move comes shortly after Los Angeles acquired center Walker Kessler from the Utah Jazz, leaving no room on the roster for Ayton.

Why the Lakers Are Moving On

When the Lakers landed Walker Kessler from Utah, Ayton's time in Los Angeles was effectively over. Kessler, 24, is a younger and more cap-friendly option at center who fits the direction the franchise is heading in the post-LeBron era. Ayton, while still a talented big man, was expendable with Kessler in place.

Hardy gives the Lakers a young guard with upside who will compete for a rotation spot. The two second-round picks add future flexibility to a front office that now must rebuild without LeBron James for the first time since 2018.

A New Chapter in Washington

For Washington, landing Ayton is a significant upgrade at center. The Wizards have been in rebuilding mode for several seasons and have accumulated young talent in the backcourt. Ayton, still just 27, gives them a proven frontcourt piece who can contribute immediately while the youth core continues to develop.

The former No. 1 overall pick's best basketball may still be ahead of him in a system that maximizes his skills as a rim-runner, pick-and-roll finisher and defensive rebounder.

What's Next

The Lakers continue to retool around Kessler and their young core as they look ahead to the 2026-27 season and beyond. Hardy will compete for playing time in a revamped L.A. rotation, while Hardy's second-round picks give the front office modest long-term assets.

Washington, meanwhile, gets a legitimate starting center — and a clearer path toward relevance in a loaded Eastern Conference.
