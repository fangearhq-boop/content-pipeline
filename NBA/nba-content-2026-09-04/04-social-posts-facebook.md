The Houston Rockets just made their biggest statement of the offseason: Amen Thompson is STAYING.

### STORY 2: LeBron James to 76ers

**FB Long-Form** | Tier 1 | Window: 11:00 AM ET

LeBron James has made his "last decision" — and he's going to Philadelphia.

The 41-year-old legend signed a 2-year, $8 million deal with the 76ers (with a player option on Year 2), teaming up with Joel Embiid, Jaylen Brown, Tyrese Maxey and VJ Edgecombe. It's one of the most star-studded rosters in NBA history, assembled seemingly overnight.

James had considered retirement before ultimately choosing to chase one more championship. He informed the Lakers he would be playing elsewhere at the start of free agency, with the Warriors, Heat and Timberwolves all pursuing him.

But LeBron chose Philadelphia — and now the East has a whole new pecking order.

The Knicks are the defending champions. The Heat have Giannis. The Raptors are about to get Kawhi. And now the Sixers have the King.

This is going to be one heck of a season.

### STORY 4: Kawhi Leonard Trade Update

**FB Long-Form** | Tier 2 | Window: 4:00 PM ET

The Kawhi Leonard era in Toronto is nearly here — again.

After the NBA cleared the Clippers investigation on September 2, the long-awaited trade sending Kawhi to the Toronto Raptors is now free to become official. The deal — Kawhi for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, two first-round picks and more — was agreed upon back on June 30 but was frozen during the league's investigation into the Clippers' cap circumvention scheme.

Training camp opens September 28 in Quebec City, and Kawhi expects to be wearing a Raptors uniform by then. For Toronto, this is a marquee acquisition that signals the franchise's intention to compete immediately.

The countdown is on. Kawhi is coming home to Canada.

### STORY 5: East Power Rankings Reshuffle

**FB Long-Form** | Tier 2 | Window: 7:00 PM ET

The Eastern Conference is about to look nothing like it did 12 months ago.

When the New York Knicks won the 2025-26 NBA Championship, it felt like a new dynasty was being born. But the offseason has completely redrawn the map. Here's where things stand heading into 2026-27:

**New York Knicks** — Defending champs and still the team to beat. No. 1 in the East.

**Philadelphia 76ers** — LeBron James + Joel Embiid + Jaylen Brown. That's three top-10 players on one roster. Philly is the most dangerous team in the conference if they can stay healthy.

**Miami Heat** — Giannis Antetokounmpo arrived via trade from Milwaukee and gives Miami a legitimate superstar to build around.

**Toronto Raptors** — Once Kawhi Leonard's trade becomes official, Toronto jumps immediately into the contender conversation.

**Boston Celtics** — Without Jaylen Brown (traded to Philly), Boston needs to reload.

The East is stacked. Training camps open in weeks. And this season cannot get here fast enough.
