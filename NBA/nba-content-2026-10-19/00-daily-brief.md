### STORY 2: Chet Holmgren Ruled OUT for Opening Night — Quad Contusion Keeps Him Sidelined
**Tier:** 1 | **Type:** FOLLOW UP
**Category:** Oklahoma City Thunder / Chet Holmgren / Injuries / Opening Night

The Thunder's official 5 PM ET injury report confirmed Chet Holmgren is OUT for Tuesday's opening night game at San Antonio. He missed all preseason games with a quad contusion. OKC faces Victor Wembanyama and the Spurs — last year's Western Conference Finals opponent — without their second-best player. Isaiah Hartenstein and Aday Mara absorb the center minutes.

**Why it matters:** OKC's title chase loses its top frontcourt piece for at least the opener. With Otega Oweh already out for the season (Achilles), the Thunder depth is tested immediately.

**Tier:** 2 | **Type:** FOLLOW UP

October 19 is the NBA's rookie contract extension deadline, and Julian Strawther's situation is uniquely complicated: he's recovering from a left knee sprain (no structural damage, out at least six weeks) suffered in the Oct. 14 preseason game.
