Paul George Opens His Celtics Era in Detroit: The NBA Season Begins at 3 PM

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | October 19, 2026

The NBA season tips off in Detroit — not New York — as Paul George makes his Boston Celtics debut against the Pistons at 3 PM ET on NBC.

The marquee moment of Opening Night 2026 comes at 7:00 PM in New York. But the NBA season actually starts four hours earlier, in Detroit, when the Boston Celtics visit the Pistons at Little Caesars Arena for the first game of the 2026-27 campaign.

For the Celtics, it is a debut game in every sense. Paul George arrives in Boston after a summer in which the 76ers traded him to acquire Jaylen Brown. He lines up alongside Jayson Tatum and Jrue Holiday — and the question of whether this particular Big Three has the cohesion to reach the Finals will begin to take shape from tip-off.

George's New Role in Boston

Paul George's career has produced All-Star selections, scoring titles, Defensive Player of the Year consideration, and multiple deep playoff runs. He arrives in Boston as a proven commodity — but his role here is fundamentally different from what he has navigated before.

With Jayson Tatum as the unambiguous No. 1 option and Jrue Holiday as the defensive backbone, George functions as the third option who is too good to actually be third. He creates in isolation, spaces the floor, and guards the opponent's best wing. It is a role designed to push Boston into legitimate championship contention.

Whether the offensive hierarchy is established cleanly in Game 1 — or whether there is early friction in shot distribution and late-game decision-making — will be something to watch all season. Tuesday's opener in Detroit is the first data point.

Detroit Gets Opening Night Respect

The decision to begin the NBA season in Detroit — with the Pistons hosting the Celtics — reflects the league's recognition of where Detroit stands as a franchise. The Pistons have built a young, competitive team that has earned its way into the national spotlight.

Little Caesars Arena will give the Pistons a true home-court opener, and the national television audience on NBC will see what Detroit has been building. This is not a game between two pretenders. The Celtics are a legitimate Eastern Conference title contender. The Pistons are playing with something to prove.

What to Watch

Beyond George's fit in the Celtics offense, this game offers early reads on Boston's defensive identity under their coaching staff's new schemes. Tatum's conditioning coming off an All-Star summer and his early comfort level alongside George tells a lot about whether the Celtics are ready from the first week.

For the Pistons, this is their chance to make a statement on national television. An upset of a Celtics squad that is still finding its footing as a unit would set the tone for a season in which Detroit plans to be taken seriously.

The game tips off at 3:00 PM ET on NBC and Peacock — the official start of the 2026-27 NBA season.

What's Next

After the afternoon opener in Detroit, attention shifts to New York for the Sixers-Knicks banner night showdown, then to San Antonio for the Thunder-Spurs rivalry rematch. The NBA's full schedule opens Wednesday, but Tuesday's Celtics-Pistons game gives the basketball world its first look at a Boston roster that believes it is built for a championship run — starting now.
