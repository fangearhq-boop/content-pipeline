**FB Long-Form (11:00 AM ET)**

### STORY 5: Kawhi Leonard — Load Management Looms in Toronto Reunion

**FB Long-Form (11:00 AM ET)**
Kawhi Leonard left $18.5 million on the table to go back to Toronto.

He waived part of his contract kicker and accepted below-maximum value to return to the city where he won a championship in 2019. The Raptors gave him a 2-year deal with a player option for 2028-29.

But basketball reality applies. Leonard is 35 years old. His career has been defined as much by the games he hasn't played as the ones he has. Managing his workload isn't a criticism — it's how he's stayed effective into his mid-thirties.

For a Raptors team still in a rebuild, the load management question takes a different shape. They need Kawhi available enough that his presence matters — to his teammates' development, to the franchise's identity, and to whatever competitive aspirations Toronto holds this season.

Training camp opens this week. The questions start now.
