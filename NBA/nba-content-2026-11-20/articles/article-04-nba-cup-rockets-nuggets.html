NBA Cup Clash: Hot Rockets Visit Nuggets Tonight With West Group A Lead at Stake

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | November 20, 2026

The NBA Cup group stage is picking up intensity, and Friday night's matchup between the Houston Rockets and the Denver Nuggets is the game to watch. The Rockets (12-4) are one of the NBA's hottest teams. The Nuggets feature Nikola Jokić, one of the most reliable forces in basketball. West Group A lead is on the line. Tip-off is 10 PM ET on NBC/Peacock.

Houston's Red-Hot Start

The Rockets have no business being this good — or at least, that is what the preseason projections suggested. At 12-4, they are tied for the third-best record in the entire NBA, keeping pace with Memphis and not far behind the Lakers and Spurs at 13-3.

Alperen Şengün is the engine. The young center is listed as questionable for tonight after suffering a corneal abrasion in his left eye. His status going into tip-off remains uncertain. Without Şengün, Houston will need to find a way to slow Jokić using a different matchup — a task that has confounded much larger teams than the Rockets.

Clint Capela is already out with a right ankle sprain, further thinning the Rockets' frontcourt options.

Jokić and Denver's Cup Ambitions

Nikola Jokić needs no introduction. The two-time MVP remains one of the most unique forces in the game — a center who orchestrates offenses, quarterbacks defenses, and finds ways to impact every possession. Jamal Murray and Aaron Gordon provide the supporting cast.

Denver enters this game with their own Cup ambitions. The in-season tournament format gives every game added stakes through November 27, when the group stage concludes and the knockout round begins. A win tonight for the Nuggets does not just move their NBA Cup standing — it sends a message about where this team is heading.

Nine Games, One Night

The full Friday slate features nine games, spread across tip-off windows from 7 PM ET to 10 PM ET. The Pacers host the Cavaliers, the Kings visit the Lakers, the Magic travel to Brooklyn, and the Hornets take on the Hawks — all part of a packed Cup group stage night.

The Rockets-Nuggets game is the marquee matchup, but there is no shortage of meaningful basketball on the schedule. With group play ending in a week, every result matters and every team is acutely aware of where they stand.

The Bigger Picture

Houston winning tonight would announce their arrival as a genuine Western Conference contender. At 13-4, they would be within striking distance of the NBA's elite tier. Denver winning would remind the league that Jokić's Nuggets, despite an uneven start, are not a team to dismiss.

Whatever happens, it is the kind of game that the NBA Cup format was designed to produce — high-stakes, meaningful regular season basketball in November. Set your reminders.
