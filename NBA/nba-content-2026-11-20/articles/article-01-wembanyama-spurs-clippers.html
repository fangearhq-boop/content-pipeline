Wembanyama Drops 32-Point Double-Double as Spurs Crush Clippers 124-112

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | November 20, 2026

Victor Wembanyama played through a painful blister and still scored 32 points. The San Antonio Spurs didn't need a healthy, perfect version of their franchise cornerstone on Thursday night — they needed the version that shows up anyway, and that's exactly what they got. Final score: Spurs 124, Clippers 112.

The win pushed San Antonio to 13-3, tied with the Los Angeles Lakers for the NBA's best record. Wembanyama's fourth consecutive double-double — 32 points, 11 rebounds — was the headliner, but this Spurs team is building into something that goes well beyond one player's brilliance.

MVP by Acclamation

Wembanyama finished 13-of-26 from the field, 2-of-7 from three, and 4-of-4 from the free throw line. He added 3 assists, a steal, and a block in 33 minutes — all while dealing with a blister that had no interest in cooperating. It is the kind of performance that separates the genuine MVP candidates from the conversation fillers.

He is now averaging approximately 25.0 points, 11.4 rebounds, 3.8 blocks, and 1.4 steals per game on the season. Those numbers are, simply put, unprecedented at this stage of the NBA year. No center in the modern era is averaging a double-double with nearly four blocks per game and scoring at that volume.

The blister detail matters. Stars playing through discomfort and delivering at this level is a hallmark of the best players in the game. Wembanyama didn't ask out of the lineup, didn't have an off night because of it, and didn't use it as an excuse when asked afterward.

Castle and Champagnie Provide Support

Stephon Castle ran the offense with authority, finishing with 21 points and 12 assists. Castle's development as a point guard has been one of the quiet subplots of San Antonio's strong start — he is improving in real time, and his ability to create for others frees Wembanyama from needing to be a primary ball-handler on every possession.

Julian Champagnie added 20 points, giving the Spurs three players in double figures on the night. The Clippers had no answer for San Antonio's depth. When a team's third scorer goes for 20 and the whole offense runs through one of the most versatile big men in league history, losing by 12 is the predictable outcome.

13-3 and Climbing

At 13-3, the Spurs are no longer a feel-good story or a development-year narrative. They are a legitimate title contender with the best player in the world at their center. The Western Conference is loaded, but San Antonio has earned the right to be mentioned in the same breath as anyone.

The next step is sustaining it over an 82-game season and into the playoffs. That question is still months away. Right now, the Spurs are simply the best team in the league, and Wembanyama is simply the best player in it. Sometimes sports is straightforward.
