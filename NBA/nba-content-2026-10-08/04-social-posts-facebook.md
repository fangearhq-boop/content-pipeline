### STORY 2: Haliburton Returns

**Post 2 (11:00 AM ET)**
Tyrese Haliburton is officially back.

One year ago, Haliburton suffered a torn right Achilles tendon that ended his season before it could begin. He spent months in rehab, watching from the sideline as the Pacers navigated without their franchise player.

Tuesday night, he walked back onto the court in Indianapolis — and the Indiana Pacers beat the Minnesota Timberwolves 123-112 in their preseason opener.

After the game, Haliburton said he feels "no limitations." Head coach Rick Carlisle will still manage his workload carefully, but the message from Haliburton himself is clear: the Achilles is healed. The work is done. He's ready.

This is a big deal. Haliburton is one of the best point guards in the Eastern Conference, and the Pacers' ceiling — with him healthy — is legitimately a top-four seed in the East. His regular season opener is October 21 at New Orleans.

The NBA is lucky to have him back. And the Pacers? They look dangerous. 🏀
