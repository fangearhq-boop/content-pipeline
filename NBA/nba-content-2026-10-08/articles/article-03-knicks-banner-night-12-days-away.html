12 Days Until Knicks Banner Night — And Tickets Are Already $1,999 to Get In

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | October 8, 2026

Twelve days. That is all that separates New York City from the most anticipated evening in Knicks basketball since Patrick Ewing was in his prime.

On October 20, the New York Knicks will raise their first NBA championship banner since 1973 — 53 years of waiting, finally over — at Madison Square Garden. Commissioner Adam Silver will present rings to Jalen Brunson, Karl-Anthony Towns, and the rest of the 2025-26 championship squad before the home opener tips off at 7:00 PM ET on NBC and Peacock.

The get-in price on secondary-market platforms is already $1,999, making this the most expensive home opener in NBA history according to TickPick. For a game at the most famous arena in the world, that number makes sense.

The Perfect Storm of a Storyline

The banner ceremony would be a spectacle by itself. But the NBA's schedule makers found the ideal opponent: the Philadelphia 76ers, arriving at the Garden with LeBron James, Jaylen Brown, Joel Embiid and Tyrese Maxey all expected to make their regular-season debut as teammates.

LeBron James, at 41, chose to join the Sixers in the offseason in one final championship chase. Jaylen Brown came from Boston. Together with incumbent stars Embiid and Maxey, Philadelphia has assembled one of the most star-studded rosters in recent memory — and they will debut that roster on the night the Knicks raise their banner.

The drama couldn't be more perfectly scripted. The defending champions celebrating their glory. The challengers announcing their arrival. All on the same night, at the same building, with the whole basketball world watching.

What the Knicks Built

The 2025-26 championship ended 53 years of Knicks suffering. No other major American sports franchise had waited as long without a title at the time of their win. The Knicks swept the 76ers 4-0 in that playoff run, which makes the Opening Night matchup carry an extra layer of meaning.

This is not just a basketball game. It is a statement. The Knicks are the champions. The 76ers — now built to take that title — are coming to challenge them on their home turf, on their biggest night.

The Opening Night Tripleheader

The Knicks-76ers game is the marquee event of the NBA's Opening Night tripleheader. Coverage begins at 3:00 PM ET when the Boston Celtics visit the Detroit Pistons. The tripleheader concludes at 9:30 PM ET with the San Antonio Spurs hosting the Oklahoma City Thunder.

But the 7:00 PM window — Knicks and Sixers, MSG, banner night — is the centerpiece of the entire evening.

What's Next

The Knicks have a few more preseason games before the real thing begins. For fans, the countdown is almost over. For anyone who has been waiting since 1973 to see a banner go up at Madison Square Garden — the wait is 12 days shorter than it was yesterday.
