The Knicks Are 22 Days From a Banner — and LeBron James Is the Opening Night Opponent

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | September 28, 2026

The New York Knicks won the 2025-26 NBA championship. In 22 days, they get to hang the banner. And the opponent they will face after the ceremony is the team that tried hardest to acquire the players who beat them.

Opening night is October 20. The banner ceremony starts at 6:30 p.m. ET. Tip-off is 7 p.m. on NBC and Peacock. The Philadelphia 76ers, freshly loaded with LeBron James, Jaylen Brown, Joel Embiid, and Tyrese Maxey, will be waiting on the opposite bench when the confetti settles.

Fifty-Three Years in the Making

The last time the Knicks raised a championship banner at Madison Square Garden was 1973. That is 53 years. Anyone who was there for that one is now well into their seventies. For the vast majority of current Knicks fans, this is the only championship they have ever seen their team win.

The ticket market reflects that. Secondary market averages are running around $2,560 per ticket as of this weekend. Get-in prices are over $1,000. That is for a regular-season game, not the playoffs. Madison Square Garden is the highest-priced arena in the league on a normal night; what October 20 will look like in that building is something that has not been experienced in more than half a century.

The Knicks swept the 76ers four games to zero in the second round of the 2025-26 playoffs. That is the backdrop for this specific matchup. Philadelphia was not just beaten. They were swept, then rebuilt entirely around the goal of not letting that happen again.

What the 76ers Are Bringing to Town

LeBron James is 41 years old. He signed with Philadelphia on a two-year, $8 million deal and said plainly on a podcast that Joel Embiid's missing championship was the primary reason. Embiid has never won a title. LeBron has four. The math there is transparent.

Jaylen Brown joined Philadelphia via trade and has been described by LeBron as "kind of misunderstood." That phrasing is deliberate. Brown has always had his detractors, and LeBron publicly aligning with him is a statement about the locker room dynamic Philadelphia is trying to build. Tyrese Maxey rounds out the top four. He and LeBron have spoken openly about their friendship as a factor in LeBron's decision.

This is not a Philadelphia team that will roll into Madison Square Garden intimidated by what the Knicks just did. They built specifically to compete with the current Knicks roster.

What's Next

Twenty-two days separate now from the banner ceremony. The Knicks open training camp Tuesday and have a preseason schedule to get through before October 20 arrives. Neither team will show anything meaningful in the preseason. Both will be ready when it counts.

The Knicks get to bask in the moment of raising that banner, and then they immediately have to defend their title against the most famous active player in the sport, who chose the opponent specifically because that opponent hurt him. That is not a soft opening night. It is exactly the kind of game that earns NBC the right to put it in the 7 p.m. national window and know every seat will be full.
