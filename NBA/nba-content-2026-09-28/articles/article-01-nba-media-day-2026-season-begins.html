NBA Media Day 2026: Cooper Flagg Got Taller, Durant Is 'Scary,' and the New Season Is Here

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | September 28, 2026

Twenty-five NBA teams held media day on Monday. Cameras clicked, reporters crowded locker rooms, and players delivered the same optimistic lines they always do this time of year. But a few things genuinely stood out.

The Houston Rockets and Dallas Mavericks went early, holding their media days on September 22 and September 25, respectively, ahead of the China Games. The remaining 25 teams gathered Monday. Training camps open Tuesday, September 29. And the Philadelphia 76ers do their thing tomorrow, too, which means LeBron James has not yet made his official debut as a Sixer. That one is for Tuesday.

Cooper Flagg Is 6-Foot-10 Now

The most concrete piece of news from media day came from Dallas. Cooper Flagg, the No. 1 pick in the 2025 draft, checked in at 6 feet, 10 inches during official measurements. That is up from the 6-foot-9 listing he carried throughout his rookie season.

An inch might not sound like much, but at Flagg's position it matters. He can legitimately guard four positions without giving up too much length at either end. He already posted 17.4 points, 8.2 rebounds, and 3.1 assists per game as a 19-year-old last season. Dallas drafted him to be the franchise cornerstone for the next decade. The fact that he is still physically developing makes that bet look smarter by the month.

Flagg did not say much beyond the standard we-are-ready lines. He did not need to. The measurement said enough.

Kevin Durant on Houston: "This Team Is Scary"

Kevin Durant arrived at Rockets media day in a good mood. He called the Houston roster "scary," delivered in that flat, certain tone that Durant uses when he actually means something and is not just working through a PR script.

Houston enters this season with legitimate Western Conference title aspirations. Durant's straight-line scoring paired with Amen Thompson's athleticism and improved perimeter game gives the Rockets something they have not had in years: a closer who can get his shot against anyone.

On his contract extension, Durant was less forthcoming. "Taking it one day at a time" was his answer when reporters pressed on the subject. Durant's extension situation has been a topic for months. He did not slam the door on anything, but he made clear that contract talk is not where his head is right now.

He also had something for Amen Thompson. When Thompson's name came up, Durant grinned and said: "About time you done talking." It landed as the kind of veteran-to-young-player ribbing that tells you these two have a real relationship. Thompson had been vocal all summer about the Rockets' championship window. Durant's response was essentially: put up or shut up, but with warmth.

What's Next

Training camps open across the league on Tuesday, September 29. Teams will spend the next two-plus weeks drilling before the preseason slate begins. The first real games that count hit the calendar on October 20.

Tomorrow also brings the 76ers media day, which is the one everyone is circling. LeBron James, Joel Embiid, Jaylen Brown, and Tyrese Maxey will sit in front of microphones together for the first time. LeBron has no introductory press conference on record yet. He has spoken about his decision on a podcast, but the official Sixers podium is tomorrow. That is the next mandatory stop on the NBA's preseason calendar, and it is the one with the most eyes on it.
