Tomorrow's 76ers Media Day: LeBron James Finally Puts On a Sixers Jersey

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | September 28, 2026

The Philadelphia 76ers held their media day last, as the final team in a 30-team process that started September 22 with Houston and Dallas. Tuesday, September 29, is the day the 76ers sit in front of cameras. It will be LeBron James's first official appearance in a Sixers uniform.

There was no introductory press conference when LeBron signed. No podium moment in Philadelphia colors, no formal welcome from the front office. He spoke about his decision on a podcast and let the media cycle run. Tuesday is the first time he will stand in a Sixers jersey and answer questions as an official member of this team.

Why LeBron Chose Philadelphia

LeBron laid out his reasoning in a "Mind the Game" appearance with Steve Nash. Three factors drove the decision.

Joel Embiid was first. "He's done everything in our league and the only thing he has not done is win a championship," LeBron said. That sentence is the entire thesis. Embiid has won scoring titles, an MVP, and Defensive Player of the Year honors. He does not have a ring. At age 32, his window is not unlimited. LeBron, who has been on title-or-bust rosters before, is here to help close that gap.

Tyrese Maxey was second. LeBron cited his friendship with Maxey as a genuine pull. That kind of factor matters. LeBron has never joined a situation where he did not feel real alignment with at least one teammate. Maxey is one of the fastest-rising guards in the league, and if the two of them have genuine chemistry, the offensive combinations available to Nick Nurse become difficult to plan against.

Jaylen Brown was third. LeBron said Brown is "kind of misunderstood," which functions as a public endorsement and a message to Brown that his new teammate sees him clearly. Brown joined Philadelphia via trade this summer and has been a polarizing figure throughout his career. LeBron's credibility with the media and around the league carries weight when he chooses to use it this way.

What Happened Last Spring

The 76ers were swept four games to zero by the New York Knicks in the second round of the 2025-26 playoffs. Not a close series. Not a series with a couple of heartbreakers. A sweep. The entire roster was rebuilt in response. Jaylen Brown came in. LeBron came in. The organization decided the core that got swept needed significant reinforcement, not a minor adjustment.

Nick Nurse has not spoken publicly about that sweep or the new roster since the offseason moves were completed. Tomorrow is his first opportunity on record to frame what the 76ers are trying to build, what went wrong last spring, and how this group is different. Nurse is not someone who avoids direct questions, and the media room will have plenty of them ready.

What's Next

Tuesday's media day is the 76ers' official starting gun. LeBron in a Sixers jersey is the image that will dominate sports media across the country for most of the day. After that, Philadelphia opens training camp alongside the rest of the league, with the preseason schedule beginning in early October.

The 76ers open the regular season October 20 at Madison Square Garden, where the Knicks will raise their first championship banner since 1973. That opponent, that building, on that night, is what Philadelphia built toward this summer. Everything from Tuesday forward is preparation for that game and everything that comes after it.
