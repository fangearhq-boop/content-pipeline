Memphis Stays Perfect: Grizzlies Crush Kings 136-105, Improve to 6-0

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | November 1, 2026

The Memphis Grizzlies are not just winning games — they are winning them with authority.

On Halloween night, Memphis dismantled the Sacramento Kings 136-105, a 31-point beatdown that improved the Grizzlies to a perfect 6-0 and cemented their status as the NBA's last unbeaten team. While the rest of the league scrambles to keep up, Memphis keeps rolling, and the gap between the Grizzlies and the rest of the Western Conference appears to be widening with each passing night.

Six and Zero

For context on just how impressive this start has been: six games in, Memphis has not been seriously challenged. Their wins have come in various ways — blowouts, steady performances, a Cameron Boozer triple-double that shocked the league earlier this week — but the result has always been the same. Grizzlies win, and usually by a lot.

The Kings, now 0-7 and still searching for their first victory of the season, were an almost too-easy opponent on this night. Sacramento's roster construction questions have turned into an early-season crisis. Their defense has been porous, their offense inconsistent, and a 31-point loss to a healthy Memphis team only deepens the concern in the organization.

What Makes Memphis Special

The Grizzlies are built on the same foundation that made them a perennial playoff threat in years past: relentless defense, pace, and the willing distribution that comes from a team that plays for each other rather than individual glory. Cameron Boozer — the franchise cornerstone — has lived up to every expectation placed on him as the No. 1 overall pick. His triple-double two nights ago showed he can do everything at an elite level.

But the Grizzlies' depth has been the real story. Different players have been the standout in each game, which speaks to a team that has internalized its identity and can execute the game plan regardless of who is being asked to deliver on a given night.

Sacramento's Early Crisis

The flip side of Memphis's perfection is Sacramento's struggles. The Kings are now 0-7, a start that has already triggered questions about the front office's summer moves and the team's long-term direction. A 31-point loss to Memphis — a team built around young energy — is not just a loss; it is a statement about the gap between where Sacramento thought they were and where they actually are.

For the Kings, November will need to be a month of answers. For the Grizzlies, it looks like it will be more of the same: winning, and doing it convincingly.

What's Next

Memphis's unbeaten run will eventually face its sternest tests as the schedule toughens and contenders find film on the Grizzlies. The Houston Rockets, at 6-1, are the team closest to keeping pace. A potential Memphis-Houston showdown later in November would be the West's most anticipated matchup of the young season. For now, though, the Grizzlies are simply the best team in the NBA — and they have the record to prove it.
