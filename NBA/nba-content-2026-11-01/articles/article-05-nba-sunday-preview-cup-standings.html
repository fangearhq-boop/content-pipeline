NBA Sunday, November 1: Celtics at Magic, Nets at Pacers — Plus Cup Standings Check

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | November 1, 2026

After a wild Halloween slate that saw Memphis stay perfect, the Rockets bounce back, and Maxey drop 38, the NBA settles into a quieter Sunday with just two games on the schedule.

But before we look ahead, it is worth pausing on where things stand after the NBA's chaotic first week — because the early picture is fascinating.

Sunday's Schedule

Two games are on tap for November 1:

Boston Celtics at Orlando Magic (3:30 PM ET) — The defending NBA champions travel to Orlando to face a Magic team that has surprised some early observers with its energy and athleticism. Boston comes in with title expectations and a roster built for another long playoff run. Jayson Tatum and the Celtics will look to assert themselves on the road.

Brooklyn Nets at Indiana Pacers (5:00 PM ET) — Brooklyn and Indiana square off in a matchup between two teams still finding their identity early in the season. Tyrese Haliburton and the Pacers' up-tempo style will test the Nets' defensive capabilities.

NBA Cup Standings: Week 1 Picture

After the opening week of NBA Cup group play, here is the early landscape:

West picture: Memphis (6-0) leads their group; Houston (6-1) is right behind. The Rockets' lone Cup game was a loss to Dallas — their bounce-back Cup win over OKC on Friday improved their Cup record to 1-1. Dallas (4-2) and Golden State (1-1) round out the early Cup contenders in the West.

East picture: Philadelphia (7-1, 1-0 Cup) leads the East after their Cup-opening win over the Knicks. The defending champion Knicks fell to 0-1 in Cup play and will need to respond. The Celtics, who play Sunday, are positioned to make their Cup group run shortly.

Cup group play continues on Tuesday, with another set of Cup games scheduled through November's Tuesdays and Fridays. The quarterfinals are slated for December 4-5, semifinals December 8-9, and the Cup Final on December 11 in Indianapolis.

What's Next

November is here, and the NBA season enters its first major stretch. With Memphis unbeaten, the Sixers at 7-1, and Houston at 6-1, the early table-setters have been set. Every game from here through November 27 — whether it counts in the Cup or not — will begin shaping the playoff picture for real. Sunday's two games are a chance for the Celtics and Pacers to make their statements heading into a busy week ahead.
