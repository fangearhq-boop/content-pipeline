Cleveland's Halloween Blowout: Cavaliers Beat Bucks 136-111

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | November 1, 2026

The Cleveland Cavaliers added a Halloween treat for their fans on October 31 — and the Milwaukee Bucks were the ones who got tricked.

Cleveland rolled to a convincing 136-111 victory over Milwaukee, a 25-point blowout that strengthened the Cavaliers' claim as one of the Eastern Conference's top teams. Donovan Mitchell was at his usual excellent self, and the Cavs' collective engine hummed from wire to wire against a Bucks team that has now started the season at a troubling 3-4.

Cavaliers Thriving

Cleveland has built a team designed to compete at both ends of the floor, and that blueprint has produced results. The Cavaliers' roster depth is one of their greatest strengths — multiple players can hurt opponents on any given night, making it nearly impossible to single out one threat and shut them down. Against Milwaukee, Cleveland's balanced attack was fully on display.

The 136 points scored speak to an offense that has found real rhythm under the coaching staff's system. The Cavs have been particularly effective in transition and in the half-court pick-and-roll game, putting opposing defenses in difficult decisions all game long.

Milwaukee's Slow Start

For the Bucks, 3-4 is a concerning number for a franchise that entered the season with genuine championship expectations. Milwaukee's offseason moves were designed to push them closer to a title — but the results so far have not reflected those ambitions.

The 25-point loss to Cleveland raises real questions about whether this group can compete with the East's elite teams on a consistent basis. Giannis Antetokounmpo's move to Miami this summer changed the calculus in the East dramatically, and Milwaukee has not fully figured out its new identity without the Greek Freak anchoring everything.

What's Next

The Cavaliers continue their November schedule with the goal of staying in the Eastern Conference's top tier alongside the 76ers and Celtics. For Milwaukee, the message is clear: something needs to change before this slow start becomes a full-blown crisis. November games do not get easier, and the Bucks need answers — and they need them now.
