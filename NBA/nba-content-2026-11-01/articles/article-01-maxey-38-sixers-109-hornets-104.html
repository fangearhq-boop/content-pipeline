Maxey's Halloween 38: 76ers Beat Hornets 109-104 in Back-to-Back Masterpiece

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | November 1, 2026

Tyrese Maxey went trick-or-treating in Charlotte on Halloween night — and the Hornets had nothing in their bag that could stop him.

Maxey poured in 38 points on October 31 to lead the Philadelphia 76ers past the Charlotte Hornets 109-104, capping back-to-back dominant performances that have turned heads across the entire NBA. The night before, Maxey had dropped 27 in a Cup win over the defending champion New York Knicks. In the span of 24 hours, Maxey put up 65 points across two road wins, and the Sixers improved to 7-1 — one of the best starts in the league.

38 Points, Zero Nights Off

The back-to-back context makes Maxey's performance even more remarkable. Most players look for excuses to ease off the gas when facing their second game in consecutive nights. Maxey did the opposite. He attacked, attacked, and attacked again, finishing as the clear best player on the floor despite Philly being on the road.

The Hornets, who entered the game hungry for their first winning moment of a tough stretch, simply could not keep Maxey in front of them. His ability to turn the corner off the dribble, get to his mid-range spots, and convert at the line has made him the most complete offensive player in the Eastern Conference so far this season.

The LeBron Factor

What makes this run all the more impressive is the context surrounding Maxey. The 76ers brought in LeBron James this offseason — one of the greatest players in NBA history — and there was reasonable speculation that LeBron's presence might overshadow or limit Maxey's role. The opposite has happened. Maxey has stepped up, not down, with LeBron in the fold. Playing alongside a legendary distributor like James has, if anything, made Maxey's job easier by keeping defenses from loading up on him.

The 76ers' 7-1 start suggests this partnership is working. LeBron brings the passing, the experience, and the clutch shotmaking. Maxey brings the scoring, the movement, and the engine that makes the whole machine run.

Eastern Conference Picture

Philadelphia's 7-1 record places them among the early-season elite in the East. With the Cleveland Cavaliers and Boston Celtics also performing well, the Eastern Conference is shaping up as a three-team race at the top. But the 76ers, powered by Maxey's historic start, have made their intentions crystal clear: they want to win the Eastern Conference.

Charlotte, meanwhile, fell to 2-5 with the loss. The Hornets continue to search for consistency and a defining identity in what remains an uncertain season.

What's Next

The 76ers return home to continue their early-season surge. For Maxey, who is averaging a staggering scoring pace through eight games, the question has shifted from "can he carry this team?" to "how far can this team go?" Sunday brings two games to the NBA slate — Celtics at Magic and Nets at Pacers — but neither team can afford to lose sight of a Philly squad that right now looks unstoppable.
