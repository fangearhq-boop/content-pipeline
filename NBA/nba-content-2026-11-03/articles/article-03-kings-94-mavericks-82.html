Kings 94, Mavericks 82: Sacramento Breaks Through With Stifling Fourth Quarter

By Marcus Cole | November 3, 2026

The Sacramento Kings needed a win, and on Monday night, they made sure they got one.

Sacramento defeated the Dallas Mavericks 94-82 at American Airlines Center, holding the Mavericks to just 8 points in the fourth quarter to secure a road victory that snaps a difficult losing streak. The Kings did their best work when it mattered most, building a lead they would not relinquish in the final frame.

Defensive Dominance in the Fourth

The fourth quarter told the story. The Kings held Dallas to 8 points on a night when the Mavericks' offense looked sharp in the first half — the two teams played to essentially even halves through three quarters before Sacramento pulled away decisively.

That kind of defensive performance on the road against a 4-2 team speaks to the Kings' competitiveness when they are locked in. The challenge now is doing it consistently.

Dallas Drops to 4-3

The Mavericks entered the game at 4-2, one of the more pleasant early-season surprises in the Western Conference. Monday's loss drops Dallas to 4-3 and serves as a reminder that even the league's best teams can struggle with consistency in early November.

Dallas managed to match Sacramento through the first three periods, putting up 18, 35, and 21 points respectively to stay in contention. But the Mavericks' fourth-quarter struggles — they managed just 8 points — proved decisive. When a team goes cold late in games, it raises questions about depth and shot creation down the stretch.

Kings Take the Long View

For Sacramento, Monday's win is a foundation. The Kings are a team with talent and a fan base hungry for sustained winning, and results like this one — tough road wins, games won in the fourth quarter — are the kinds of building blocks that define successful seasons.

The key for Sacramento will be to not allow this to be an isolated result. Consistency is what separates playoff teams from middle-of-the-pack franchises, and the Kings have the pieces to compete in a wide-open West.

What's Next

Sacramento returns home Wednesday to face the Orlando Magic at 10:00 PM ET — a chance to build on the momentum from this win immediately. The Kings will want to protect their home court and establish themselves as a difficult out for any team in the conference.

Dallas bounces back into action Wednesday as well, with a chance to reset and remind the West why they were considered a sleeper team heading into this season.
