Knicks 103, Nets 90: New York Extends Rivalry Win Streak to 15 With Fourth-Quarter Surge

By Jake Torres | November 3, 2026

The Brooklyn Nets made the New York Knicks work for it on Monday night. They pushed New York to the brink, playing with urgency and intensity through three quarters. Then the Knicks did what they have been doing to Brooklyn for nearly four years: they took over.

New York outscored Brooklyn 25-5 in the fourth quarter to turn a tightly contested game into a 103-90 victory, extending the Knicks' consecutive win streak against the Nets to 15 games. It is a run of dominance in this city rivalry that has become a defining storyline of the modern NBA in New York.

Brooklyn's Bright Spot

The Nets were not without their moments. Landry Shamet delivered a season-high 22 points and gave Brooklyn something to build on offensively. Through three quarters, the Nets had kept the game competitive and were very much in contention to snap the streak.

Then the fourth quarter arrived, and the Knicks flipped the switch.

A 25-5 run to close the game is emphatic by any standard. It speaks to New York's depth, closing ability, and the mental edge they have built against this particular opponent over time. Brooklyn simply had no answer when the Knicks turned up the intensity in those final 12 minutes.

Fifteen and Counting

The number 15 tells the story of this rivalry more than any individual stat. The Knicks have not lost to the Nets in nearly four years. Every October and November brings another chance for Brooklyn to break through, and every time, New York finds a way to deny them.

This win was perhaps more dramatic than most in the streak. The Nets had the Knicks genuinely concerned heading into the fourth quarter, which made the eventual blowout finish all the more impressive. New York has learned how to win these games even when they are not playing their best basketball for 36 minutes.

The Knicks' ability to elevate in crucial moments is exactly what separates consistent winners from the rest of the league.

Big Picture

The win adds another chapter to one of the most lopsided rivalries in the current NBA landscape. While Brooklyn has shown flashes of progress — particularly with Shamet emerging as a scoring option — the Nets face a steep challenge in reversing a four-year trend.

For the Knicks, this is exactly the type of win that builds playoff character: grinding through a tough road game, surviving a competitive opponent's best shot, and closing with dominance when it counted.

What's Next

The Nets return home Wednesday for a matchup with the Charlotte Hornets at 7:30 PM ET, a game Brooklyn will want to win before the rivalry narrative consumes another week of coverage. The Knicks are off until later in the week, giving them time to prepare for a packed stretch ahead.

Meanwhile, the streak stands at 15. Brooklyn will have another opportunity to end it soon enough. Based on recent history, it will not be easy.
