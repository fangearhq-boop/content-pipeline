Spurs 119, Jazz 97: San Antonio Wins Fifth Straight, Emerging as West Surprise

By Damon Pierce | November 3, 2026

Every week there seems to be one team quietly making noise in the Western Conference while everyone else stares at the marquee matchups. Right now, that team is the San Antonio Spurs.

San Antonio stormed past the Utah Jazz 119-97 on Monday night to extend their winning streak to five consecutive games. The final margin — 22 points — was not a photo finish. The Spurs were in command, and the Jazz, despite playing at home, could not keep pace.

Five Straight and Counting

A five-game winning streak in the NBA is not a fluke. It requires good defense, execution, and the ability to close out games. The Spurs have shown all three during this run, and Monday's result against Utah continued the pattern.

San Antonio held the Jazz to 97 points — respectable, but not enough against a Spurs offense that has been clicking at a high level throughout this stretch. The 22-point margin suggests this was not a particularly dramatic game. The Spurs were simply better, from the opening tip to the final buzzer.

A Young Team Finding Its Identity

What makes this Spurs run especially interesting is the age and composition of the roster. San Antonio is a young team still developing its identity, and five-game winning streaks at this stage of the season are a signal that the process may be further along than many observers expected.

The Spurs do not have a single player dominating headlines the way some Western Conference counterparts do. Instead, they are winning through collective effort — a balanced attack, sharp defensive rotations, and the kind of team basketball that has long been the Spurs' organizational trademark.

Jazz Fall Back

For Utah, the loss is part of a broader challenge. The Jazz are in a similar developmental phase, and while home losses hurt, Monday's result is not a death blow to their season. Utah will continue to develop their young players and look for more consistent performances throughout November.

What's Next

The Spurs will look to stretch the streak further as the schedule progresses. Wednesday's NBA slate does not include San Antonio, giving the team a brief rest before their next challenge. The question is not whether they can win five straight — they already have. The question is whether they can sustain it long enough to establish themselves as a genuine Western Conference factor before the end of November.

Don't sleep on San Antonio. This team is playing with a purpose.
