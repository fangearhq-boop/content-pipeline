Luka Dončić Drops 42 Points as Lakers Rout Heat 138-117, Five Straight 37-Point Games

By Damon Pierce | November 3, 2026

Luka Dončić is playing basketball on a different planet than everyone else right now.

The Los Angeles Lakers star turned in another breathtaking performance Monday night at Crypto.com Arena, scoring 42 points with 13 assists and 7 rebounds as the Lakers dismantled the Miami Heat 138-117. It was Dončić's fifth consecutive game with at least 37 points, a streak that has lifted his NBA-leading scoring average to 38.8 points per game.

"One of the best scorers to play this game," said Miami's Giannis Antetokounmpo, who contributed 30 points in a losing effort. Coming from one of the greatest players in the league, the praise carried particular weight.

By the Numbers

Dončić was efficient and relentless from start to finish. He shot 14-of-24 from the field and 6-of-15 from three-point range, consistently creating advantages through his combination of size, ball-handling, and court vision. The 13 assists meant that even on the possessions he didn't score, he was orchestrating damage.

Austin Reaves added 27 points to give Los Angeles a dominant one-two punch that the Heat had no answer for. The Lakers scored 138 points as a team, and it never really felt in doubt in the second half.

Los Angeles improves to 6-2 on the season. Miami falls to 2-5.

A Historically Elite Run

Five consecutive games with 37 or more points is an extraordinarily rare feat in NBA history. Dončić has scored 45, 42, and multiple other sky-high totals during this stretch, making him essentially unguardable in the current league environment.

The 38.8 PPG average leads the NBA and puts him on pace for one of the highest single-season scoring averages in modern history. With the season still in its early weeks, every game Dončić plays feels like a potential history lesson.

The Lakers' supporting cast has been strong enough to make wins happen, but Dončić remains the centerpiece around which everything orbits. His ability to pile up points while also generating assists speaks to a rare two-way offensive talent that is nearly impossible to game-plan against.

Heat Struggle Despite Giannis

For Miami, Giannis Antetokounmpo's 30-point effort was a bright spot in an otherwise frustrating night. The Heat fell to 2-5 and continue to search for consistency in the early going. The gap between Giannis's individual output and the team's collective performance underscored the work Miami still has to do to become a genuine contender in the Eastern Conference.

What's Next

The Lakers will look to build on their 6-2 start as the NBA schedule resumes Wednesday, November 4, with eight games on the slate. Dončić and company have established themselves as legitimate title contenders through the early part of the season, and there are no signs his scoring tear will cool off any time soon.

For the Heat, Wednesday brings an opportunity to get back on track against the Utah Jazz at 9:00 PM ET. Miami needs to find its footing quickly before falling further behind in a competitive Eastern Conference.
