NBA Wednesday Preview: Bucks at Celtics Leads Eight-Game Slate on November 4

By Jake Torres | November 3, 2026

Tuesday is a quiet night on the NBA calendar, but the action resumes Wednesday with a loaded eight-game slate. From a headliner Eastern Conference matchup to a late-night Western Conference showdown with Cup implications, November 4 gives basketball fans plenty to watch.

Game of the Night: Bucks at Celtics (7:30 PM ET)

Milwaukee visits Boston in what shapes up as the most compelling matchup of the evening. The Celtics have been one of the East's top teams in the early going, while the Bucks are looking to find consistency after a rocky start that has raised more questions than answers about where this team is headed.

Boston's home court is one of the toughest environments in the league, and the Celtics will be looking to continue building on a strong start. For the Bucks, this is a game that could define the tone of their November — win, and they prove their early struggles were a blip; lose, and the scrutiny only intensifies.

Western Conference Watch: Thunder at Trail Blazers (11:00 PM ET)

Out on the West Coast, Oklahoma City visits Portland in a matchup that carries NBA Cup implications for both teams. The Thunder entered the season as one of the West's elite squads, while Portland has quietly assembled a 5-1 record that makes them one of the surprise stories of the early season.

A 11:00 PM ET tip-off means this is a late-night watch for most of the country, but for NBA Cup standings and Western Conference positioning, it is a game that matters.

Other Matchups to Watch

The Philadelphia 76ers host Detroit at 7:00 PM ET in a game that will test whether the Pistons' strong start — one of the East's early-season surprises — can hold up against an established Eastern Conference power. Philadelphia has been one of the East's best teams early on and will be favored at home.

Out West, the Los Angeles Clippers visit Phoenix at 9:00 PM ET in a matchup between two teams trying to find their footing after inconsistent starts. Miami's trip to Utah at 9:00 PM ET gives the Heat a chance to get back on track after their Monday loss to the Lakers.

What's Next

Wednesday's eight-game slate sets the stage for a busy November stretch. The NBA Cup group stage is ongoing, adding another layer of stakes to Western Conference games in particular. Keep an eye on the Thunder-Blazers result for its Cup implications, and watch Boston's home performance against Milwaukee as an early indicator of how the Eastern Conference race may shape up.

Tip-off for the first games is 7:00 PM ET Wednesday. The final buzzer won't sound until late on the West Coast — but there will be plenty to talk about Thursday morning.
