Ja Morant Traded to Portland Trail Blazers — A Memphis Era Ends, a Pacific Northwest Chapter Begins

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes

July 25, 2026

The Ja Morant era in Memphis is over. The electrifying guard who made the Grizzlies appointment television for three-plus seasons has been traded to the Portland Trail Blazers, closing one of the NBA's most entertaining chapters and opening an entirely new one.

Morant, the former All-Star whose combination of breathtaking athleticism and clutch shot-making turned him into one of the league's most discussed players, heads to Portland as the Blazers continue reshaping their roster around younger, dynamic talent.

The Memphis Legacy

When Morant arrived in Memphis as the No. 2 overall pick in 2019, the Grizzlies were in the middle of a quiet rebuild. What followed was one of the more unlikely revivals in recent NBA history. Memphis developed a hard-nosed, fast-paced identity built around Morant's fearless drives to the rim, and the team made consecutive playoff appearances, becoming one of the league's more entertaining young rosters to watch.

Morant's highlights were the kind that made casual fans into devoted followers — the dunks, the pull-up threes in traffic, the fourth-quarter sequences that defied logic. Memphis basketball had an identity, and his number was 12.

That era is complete. The Grizzlies, by trading Morant, are signaling a full reset. The next chapter in Memphis will be built around a different set of players and a different vision.

What Portland Gets

For the Trail Blazers, the acquisition of Morant is the kind of swing that changes how a franchise is perceived. Portland has been working through its own transition period, and adding a player with Morant's star power and entertainment value gives the organization a genuine face for its rebuild and a box-office draw the Rose City hasn't had since the Damian Lillard era.

Morant's style of play is well-suited to Portland's preference for up-tempo basketball. He creates off the dribble, attacks the rim relentlessly, and elevates the energy level in any building he plays in. Trail Blazers fans will have plenty of reason to fill the Moda Center from opening night.

The Trade Picture

Complete trade terms were not confirmed by press time. Portland's front office has been active this offseason, and the specific compensation heading to Memphis will provide context for how each franchise values the exchange. Memphis is clearly in asset-accumulation mode; what the Grizzlies received in return will define whether this was a franchise-building trade or a straight teardown.

What's Next

For Morant personally, the move to Portland represents an opportunity to reset his narrative in a new city with a fresh fanbase. Portland's passionate supporters are known for getting behind their players with intensity, and a healthy, motivated Morant playing for a team he chose to build with has all the ingredients for a compelling story.

Ja Morant is a Trail Blazer. The Rose City gets its star. Memphis gets to start over. The NBA offseason keeps delivering.
