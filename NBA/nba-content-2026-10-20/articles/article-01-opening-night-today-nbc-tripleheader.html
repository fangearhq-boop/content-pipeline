Opening Night Is Here: Your Guide to Tonight's NBA Tripleheader on NBC

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | October 20, 2026

A championship banner ceremony, the season's most anticipated debut, and a WCF rematch all play out on one night. Here is everything you need before tip-off.

The 2026-27 NBA season begins tonight with a three-game tripleheader on NBC and Peacock, and it may be the most loaded single evening of basketball in recent memory. A banner ceremony 53 years in the making. LeBron James stepping onto the floor at Madison Square Garden in a new uniform. A Western Conference Finals rematch between two rising powers. All of it unfolds across eight hours beginning at 3:00 PM ET.

Game 1: Boston Celtics at Detroit Pistons — 3:00 PM ET

The NBA season tips off not in a marquee market but in Detroit, and the Pistons have done enough to earn the spotlight. Little Caesars Arena hosts the first game of the 2026-27 campaign as Boston brings its new-look roster to town.

The Celtics arrived at Opening Night transformed. The blockbuster Brown/George trade sent Jaylen Brown to Philadelphia and brought Paul George to Boston, where he joins Jayson Tatum and Jrue Holiday. It is one of the most intriguing new partnerships in the Eastern Conference, and tonight is the first real test of how quickly three stars of their caliber can find rhythm together.

The Pistons are no afterthought. Detroit has been building steadily, and opening the season at home against a Celtics team finding its footing gives them a legitimate opportunity to make a statement on a national stage.

Watch for: How Paul George moves within Boston's offensive system. Does he defer to Tatum from the start, or does he immediately demand a share of the ball? The answer tonight will set the tone for the entire Boston season.

Game 2: Philadelphia 76ers at New York Knicks — 7:00 PM ET

The ceremony begins at 6:30 PM ET, when NBA Commissioner Adam Silver will present the New York Knicks with their 2025-26 championship rings at Madison Square Garden — the franchise's first title since 1973. Jalen Brunson, Karl-Anthony Towns, Mikal Bridges, OG Anunoby, and Josh Hart will receive their rings in front of a building that has waited 53 years for this moment.

Then the 76ers walk in.

LeBron James, who signed with Philadelphia this offseason, makes his Sixers debut at MSG. Jaylen Brown, acquired from Boston in the trade that sent Paul George to the Celtics, is at his side. Joel Embiid and Tyrese Maxey round out a roster built to challenge New York's title defense from night one.

The dramatic irony is almost too perfect. The Knicks are celebrating the championship LeBron could not win. The team that eliminated the Sixers in the second round last spring is raising a banner as the team that beat them walks onto the floor. Whatever happens on the court, the atmosphere at MSG tonight will be extraordinary.

Watch for: How LeBron plays in that environment. How the crowd responds. Whether Brunson and Towns look sharp coming off a championship summer — or whether the ring hangover is real.

Game 3: Oklahoma City Thunder at San Antonio Spurs — 9:30 PM ET

The late game carries extra weight. The Thunder and Spurs are meeting for the first time since San Antonio eliminated Oklahoma City in Game 7 of the 2026 Western Conference Finals — and OKC is walking into Frost Bank Center without Chet Holmgren.

Holmgren has been confirmed out for Opening Night with a quad contusion. He missed all of preseason and is unavailable as the Thunder open their season against the exact team that ended it last spring. Isaiah Hartenstein steps into the starting lineup at center, and OKC must find a way to slow Victor Wembanyama without their best interior defender.

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, the two-time reigning MVP, leads the way regardless. His matchup with Wembanyama — the face of San Antonio's youth movement alongside Stephon Castle and Dylan Harper — is appointment viewing on its own merits. Add in the revenge narrative and the Holmgren absence, and this game will tell us a great deal about where both franchises stand heading into a new season.

Watch for: How SGA attacks without Holmgren's gravity. Whether the Spurs, who added veteran Tobias Harris this offseason, have added the right kind of experience around their young core.

How to Watch

All three games broadcast live on NBC and Peacock. The tripleheader begins at 3:00 PM ET with Celtics-Pistons, continues with Sixers-Knicks at 7:00 PM ET, and closes with Thunder-Spurs at 9:30 PM ET.

What's Next

Opening Night is just the first word in a very long sentence. The full 82-game slate opens Wednesday as dozens of teams tip off for the first time. The Knicks will begin their title defense, the Celtics will look to prove the George trade was worth the price, and the Thunder will need to find out how good they can be while Holmgren works his way back. Tonight sets the table. The real story starts tomorrow.
