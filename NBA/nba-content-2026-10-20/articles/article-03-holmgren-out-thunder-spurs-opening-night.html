Holmgren Confirmed Out for Opening Night: Thunder Face Spurs One Man Short

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | October 20, 2026

Chet Holmgren's quad contusion keeps him out of tonight's 9:30 PM opener at San Antonio — meaning Oklahoma City faces the team that ended their season without their second-best player.

There will be no fairy-tale revenge tonight for Chet Holmgren. The Oklahoma City Thunder confirmed Monday evening that Holmgren is out for Opening Night with a quad contusion, having missed every preseason game with the same injury. The Thunder open their 2026-27 campaign in San Antonio against the Spurs — the team that eliminated them in seven games during last spring's Western Conference Finals — without their second-most important player.

The Injury Timeline

Holmgren suffered the quad contusion during the preseason and was held out of all exhibition games as a precaution. Oklahoma City's head coaching staff indicated they would wait for the official injury report — released at 5 PM ET on Sunday, the day before the opener — before disclosing his status. That report confirmed the news Thunder fans had feared: Holmgren is out.

Otega Oweh is also unavailable, lost for the entire season after tearing his Achilles before the season opener. The Thunder entered 2026-27 with injury cloud cover before a single tip-off.

How OKC Adjusts Without Holmgren

Isaiah Hartenstein steps into the starting center role. The veteran big man provides rim protection and pick-and-roll functionality, though he is a different player than Holmgren in nearly every dimension — less versatile on offense, less dangerous as a perimeter defender.

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander remains the team's pole star. The two-time MVP has proven he can carry Oklahoma City's offense through adversity, and the Thunder's defensive system is built for collective effort rather than individual brilliance. They will need that collective effort tonight at Frost Bank Center.

OKC made significant offseason changes beyond the injury news. Lu Dort, Isaiah Joe, and Aaron Wiggins — key contributors during the team's rise — were cut, leaving a different supporting cast around SGA. How that group coheres without Holmgren is an immediate unknown.

The Spurs' Advantage

San Antonio enters tonight with a healthy roster and momentum from winning the Western Conference Finals. Victor Wembanyama leads a young Spurs core that includes Stephon Castle, Dylan Harper, and a new addition in Tobias Harris, who gives San Antonio a veteran presence capable of stretching the floor.

Wembanyama's development continued last season at an extraordinary pace. The 2026 Western Conference Final run confirmed him as one of the league's premier two-way players, and the Spurs' coaching staff has built a system that maximizes his unique skill set. Opening Night at Frost Bank Center is a statement moment for a team that believes its best basketball is still ahead.

What to Watch

SGA's efficiency and scoring load will tell the story. With Holmgren unavailable, Oklahoma City needs Gilgeous-Alexander to carry a heavier burden on Opening Night. Watch whether Hartenstein can neutralize Wembanyama's pick-and-roll threat, and whether the Thunder's perimeter defense holds without Holmgren's rim protection anchoring the paint.

The game tips at 9:30 PM ET on NBC and Peacock.

What's Next

There is no official timeline for Holmgren's return, though a quad contusion is generally not a long-term concern. Oklahoma City will issue updated injury reports before each game. The Thunder have the depth to manage early-season games shorthanded, but the longer Holmgren remains out, the more questions will arise about whether this team can repeat as Western Conference finalists. His return date is the most important medical story in the Western Conference right now.
