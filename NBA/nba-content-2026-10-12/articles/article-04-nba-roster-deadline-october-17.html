NBA Roster Deadline Is Five Days Away — Every Team Has a Decision to Make

By Jake Torres | October 12, 2026 | Hoop Heroes

The preseason is winding down and the clock is ticking. Every NBA team has until Friday, October 17 to make final decisions on players with non-guaranteed contracts. Two days after that — October 19 at 4:00 PM CT — rosters must shrink from as many as 21 players to a maximum of 15 standard contracts and three two-way deals. Then, the next morning, the 2026–27 season begins.

It is a compressed, consequential timeline, and for dozens of players fighting for one of the last spots on an NBA roster, the next five days are the most important of their professional lives.

The Deadline Breakdown

October 16 is the final day of the preseason — the last chance for players on the bubble to make a case in a game setting. Teams will use these final exhibitions not for lineup experimentation but for evaluation: who can guard a position? Who fits the culture? Who brings something specific enough to justify the final contract slot?

October 17 is the hard financial deadline. A player on a fully non-guaranteed deal must be waived by end of business that day or their salary becomes financially obligated. For teams with tight cap situations, this date forces the hand — keep a player and commit to paying them, or cut them and preserve flexibility.

October 19 is the structural deadline. Teams that entered training camp with 20 or 21 players — several did, using those early slots for evaluation — must be down to their final 15 plus three two-ways by 4:00 PM CT. That means six or more waiver decisions in some front offices over the next week.

Teams in the Spotlight

The Los Angeles Lakers entered training camp with 16 players on standard contracts — one over the final limit before accounting for two-way deals. With Walker Kessler slotted as a guaranteed center and the rest of the roster largely set, the question of who earns that final standard contract has been a camp-long story.

Dalton Knecht, who showed promise in his first NBA season, is among those mentioned as a potential cut in media reports. That does not mean his NBA career is over — a player waived before the deadline is immediately eligible to be signed by another team — but it does illustrate the difficult math that every franchise faces.

League-wide, several other organizations are reportedly searching for the right veteran at minimum salary to round out depth, with cap room available and needs identified. The waiver wire over the next five days will be active.

What This Means for the Season

Roster construction at the margins matters more than casual fans might expect. The 13th, 14th, and 15th men on a team's roster determine whether a coach has flexibility during foul trouble, injury stretches, or late-season fatigue. Teams that make the right call on the bubble player — keeping the one who fits the culture and has a specific skill — often look prescient by March.

Teams that get it wrong scramble at the trade deadline.

The final preseason evaluations this week are not exhibitions. They are job auditions with real stakes on both sides — for the players trying to hold on to their spots, and for the front offices making decisions that will shape their full season.

What's Next

By October 17, the waiver wire will be busy. By October 19, every team in the NBA will know its Opening Night roster. Then the real evaluations begin — in regular-season games, against playoff-caliber competition, with everything that matters finally on the line.

The deadline is five days away. The players on the bubble are playing like it.
