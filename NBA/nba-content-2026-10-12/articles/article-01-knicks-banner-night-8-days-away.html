8 Days: Knicks Banner Night Is Almost Here — and LeBron Is Coming to MSG

By Jake Torres | October 12, 2026 | Hoop Heroes

Eight days from now, Madison Square Garden will raise a banner that New York fans have waited 53 years to see. The 2025–26 New York Knicks won the NBA Championship. Jalen Brunson earned Finals MVP. And on October 20, 2026, the full Garden faithful gets to celebrate together — with the most loaded opposing team in recent memory walking in as the first visitors.

The atmosphere is going to be unlike anything the building has hosted in a very long time. And the basketball that follows the ceremony is, somehow, going to match the energy.

What Happened in the Finals

The 2026 NBA Finals between the Knicks and the San Antonio Spurs went five games — but it was not a one-sided series. San Antonio won Game 3 (115–111), fueled by Victor Wembanyama's 32-point night. The Spurs were alive. They had momentum. Then came Game 4.

Trailing by 29 points at one stage, the Knicks staged what several outlets described as the greatest comeback in NBA Finals history to win Game 4, 107–106. The Garden faithful would have lost their minds — except the game was in San Antonio. New York simply refused to let the series slip away.

Brunson closed it out in Game 5. In San Antonio, he scored 45 points in a 94–90 road win to deliver New York its first championship since 1973. The city of New York has not been the same since.

The Ceremony on October 20

Commissioner Adam Silver will present championship rings to Jalen Brunson, Karl-Anthony Towns, and the rest of the Knicks roster before the October 20 home opener. Then the banner goes up. The crowd at Madison Square Garden — already holding what are some of the most expensive home-opener tickets in NBA history — will be part of a moment that fans will talk about for decades.

For the older generation of New York basketball fans, this is closure. For the younger generation, it is their first live championship celebration. For the franchise, it is proof that patience, building through the front office, and finding the right cornerstone player in Brunson eventually gets the job done.

The Opponent: LeBron's Sixers

The Knicks' banner night opponent could not have been scripted more dramatically. Philadelphia's 76ers arrive at MSG with LeBron James making his regular-season debut in a Sixers uniform. LeBron, 41, chose Philadelphia this past summer — joining Joel Embiid, Jaylen Brown (acquired from Boston for Paul George and picks), and Tyrese Maxey.

It is one of the most star-studded opposing lineups to enter the Garden in years. LeBron walking into MSG on the night New York raises a banner is a storyline that writes itself. He has been on the court for championships, for heartbreaks, for everything in between. This night asks him to be the road team on someone else's night of celebration.

The game tips off at 7:00 PM ET on NBC as the second game of the Opening Night tripleheader.

What's Next

Eight days is not much time. The Knicks will play their final preseason games this week — the roster deadline passes October 17, rosters are finalized October 19 — and then the season begins. The defending champions enter 2026–27 with full confidence, a banner to hang, and a fanbase that has earned every bit of the celebration to come.

For the rest of the NBA, the message is already clear: New York is not done. The Knicks celebrate on October 20. Then they get back to work.
