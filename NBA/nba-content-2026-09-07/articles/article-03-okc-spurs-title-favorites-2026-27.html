OKC and San Antonio Sit Atop 2026-27 Title Odds — Even Above the Defending Champion Knicks

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | September 7, 2026

With training camp 22 days away, oddsboards have the Oklahoma City Thunder and San Antonio Spurs as the West's top contenders. Both squads return their core with new questions to answer.

The 2026-27 NBA championship race has not officially started — training camps open September 29, and Opening Night is October 20 — but the market has already made its call on who the favorites are.

The Oklahoma City Thunder and the San Antonio Spurs sit at the very top of NBA Finals futures boards, listed above the defending champion New York Knicks, the Boston Celtics, the Philadelphia 76ers, and the Denver Nuggets. It is a remarkable statement about how far both Western Conference franchises have risen — and a preview of what promises to be a compelling season.

Oklahoma City: Deep Core, Thinner Depth

The Thunder's case for the title begins and ends with their big three: Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, Chet Holmgren, and Jalen Williams. The trio has already won a championship together, and with a full year of experience as the face of a winning franchise, the expectation from Oklahoma City is more of the same.

The offseason was not without sacrifice. In an effort to avoid the NBA's punishing second apron luxury tax, OKC dealt Isaiah Joe, Aaron Wiggins, and Luguentz Dort — three contributors who provided valuable depth, particularly on the defensive end. The roster is leaner than it was during last season's championship run.

What the Thunder kept was significant: center Isaiah Hartenstein, wing Kenrich Williams, and two additional top-20 draft picks that add future optionality. The front office's view is that the core is elite enough to win with a shorter bench, provided the big three stay healthy and motivated.

The health caveat is the critical concern. Jalen Williams missed 49 regular-season games last season dealing with wrist surgery and four separate hamstring strains. An All-NBA caliber wing missing that much time is a problem in the regular season; it is potentially a playoff-round-ending problem if it continues into the postseason. OKC needs Williams healthy and performing at his 2023-24 level, when he averaged 24.5 points per game, for this championship expectation to be realistic.

San Antonio: Wemby's World Keeps Expanding

The Spurs were Western Conference Finalists last season and return the core of that team intact. Victor Wembanyama continues to be the most physically imposing and analytically dominant player in the sport at his age, and the organization around him has built carefully toward this championship window.

The Spurs face OKC on Opening Night — October 20, one of the triple-header games on NBC and Peacock — in what will function as a Western Conference Finals preview. The basketball world will be paying close attention.

San Antonio's path is smoother than OKC's in one respect: they did not gut their depth in the offseason and enter camp with a more complete roster top to bottom. Their question is whether the level of competition they faced in the WCF last season has prepared them to go one step further.

The East's Defending Champions Watch From Across the Conference

The New York Knicks, who raised a championship banner at Madison Square Garden during Opening Night (the Sixers and LeBron James provide the opposition in the marquee matchup), enter the season ranked third or fourth in most futures markets. That is not a reflection of doubt about their championship pedigree — it is a reflection of how good the top of the Western Conference has become.

The race for the 2026-27 title shapes up as a genuinely competitive field with no obvious runaway favorite. The Spurs and Thunder are deserving of their frontrunner status, but the Knicks, Celtics, Sixers, and Nuggets all have legitimate cases to make.

What's Next

Training camp opens September 29 for OKC, San Antonio, and every other team. Preseason games begin in early October, offering the first glimpse of how rosters have evolved. The critical moment arrives October 20 when the Spurs and Thunder meet on national television for Opening Night. Follow Hoop Heroes all season for championship contender updates and analysis.
