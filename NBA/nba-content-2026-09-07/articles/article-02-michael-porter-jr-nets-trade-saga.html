Michael Porter Jr.'s $40.8M Question: Will the Nets Trade Their Star Before the Deadline?

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | September 7, 2026

MPJ is coming off the best season of his career and sitting on a $40.8 million expiring contract in Brooklyn. The February 2027 deadline could be the most consequential trade deadline of the season.

Michael Porter Jr. just had the best season of his NBA career. And he did it on a rebuilding team.

The Brooklyn Nets forward averaged a career-high 24.2 points per game in 2025-26, shooting 46 percent from the field and 36 percent from three-point range. He is 28 years old, in the prime of his career, and one of the most versatile scorers in the league. He is also sitting on a $40.8 million expiring contract — and at the center of the most fascinating trade subplot heading into the 2026-27 season.

The Contract Is the Story

MPJ's current deal pays him $40.8 million in 2026-27 before expiring the following summer. The Nets, who are clearly in rebuild mode and not competing for a championship this season, elected not to extend him — and Porter declined whatever extension offers were on the table. That mutual decision has set up one of the most predictable and dramatic countdowns in recent NBA memory.

When a franchise star on an expiring contract enters the season on a rebuilding team, the outcome is almost always a trade. Whether it happens at the February 2027 deadline or sooner is the only real question.

ESPN's Brian Windhorst offered a note of caution in recent reporting: "I know everybody thinks that Porter's available — that would be news to me." But Marc Stein reported hearing "pushback about the Nets' willingness to part ways" at the 2026 deadline earlier this year. Those two readings are not mutually exclusive. Brooklyn may not have officially made Porter available, but the structural logic of trading him before his value evaporates is undeniable.

Who's Calling Brooklyn?

The Golden State Warriors and Detroit Pistons have been the most consistently linked suitors in trade speculation. The Warriors have a pressing need for a consistent scorer alongside Stephen Curry, and Porter's three-point shooting and off-ball scoring would fit their system. Detroit is a team on the rise looking for a star to accelerate their rebuild into contention.

But the field is likely much wider than those two. Any team with playoff aspirations, a need for scoring, and the assets to make a deal should be exploring this. An elite 28-year-old scorer on an expiring deal does not come available every deadline cycle.

The Nets' ask will be significant. Porter's value is near its ceiling right now — coming off his best season, in his prime. Brooklyn will want multiple first-round picks and likely a quality young player in any deal. The team acquiring him also takes on the full $40.8M salary, which limits the field of suitors somewhat.

The Bigger Picture for Brooklyn

The Nets acquired Porter from Denver in the Cam Johnson trade, receiving a young star in exchange for a veteran piece. If they now flip Porter at peak value for more draft capital and young players, they accelerate their rebuild timeline significantly.

A rebuild built on accumulating picks is only as good as the assets you add along the way. Porter has given Brooklyn one excellent season and elevated their trade position considerably. The smart basketball move — and the one most observers expect — is to maximize that value at the February deadline rather than let him walk for nothing as a free agent the following summer.

Porter, for his part, heads into training camp as a Net. He is a professional and will play at the same high level regardless of his status. But everyone in Brooklyn — from the front office to the locker room — knows this is likely a countdown.

What's Next

Training camp opens September 29. MPJ will be on the floor for Brooklyn, and his early-season performance will only add to — or detract from — his trade value. The February 2027 trade deadline is the defining moment in this story. Watch for reporting from ESPN and Hoops Rumors as the season progresses for the first real signals of a deal taking shape.
