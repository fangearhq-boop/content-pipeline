## STORY 1: Ben Simmons — Kings (Tier 1)

**Why It Matters:** Ben Simmons is one of the most polarizing players in NBA history. His missed 2025-26 season due to injuries kept his comeback story simmering. The Sacramento Kings, a perennial playoff-bubble team, are betting on a healthy Simmons to provide defense, playmaking, and versatility off the bench (or as a starter). At 30, this may be his last legitimate NBA opportunity. The "No. 1 pick redemption" angle resonates broadly.

**Angle for Content:** Hope/redemption narrative. Can Simmons find himself in Sacramento? Kings get upside with near-zero risk.
