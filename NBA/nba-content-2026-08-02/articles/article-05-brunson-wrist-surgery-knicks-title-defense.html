Brunson Had Wrist Surgery — The Knicks' Title Defense Has Its First Real Question

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | August 2, 2026

The 2026 NBA Finals ended with Jalen Brunson scoring 45 points in Game 5 at Madison Square Garden, putting up the most iconic performance of the Knicks' first championship season since 1973. He was named Finals MVP. The confetti fell. Fifty-three years of waiting ended in one night.

Now it is August. Brunson has undergone surgery on his shooting wrist. And the Knicks' title defense begins with a question mark they would have preferred not to have.

The Scope of the Surgery

Details on the surgery's severity and Brunson's expected return timeline have been limited. What is known: the procedure was on his shooting wrist, and it occurred in the summer following one of the most physically demanding playoff runs a Knicks player has had in decades.

The Knicks won the 2026 title in what was Brunson's most complete and dominant playoff run to date. He was the engine — the fourth-quarter decision-maker, the mid-range maestro, the player other teams had to gameplan for above anyone else on the floor. When you carry a team that far, that hard, the body carries the cost of it. Wrist surgery in the summer is not unexpected for a player who put what Brunson put into that run.

The question now is recovery. NBA training camps open September 29. Preseason games follow in October. There is time — but not unlimited time — for a wrist to heal before meaningful basketball resumes.

The Knicks Core Is Still Intact

Context matters here. The Knicks did not lose key pieces in free agency. The core five — Brunson, Josh Hart, OG Anunoby, Mikal Bridges, and Karl-Anthony Towns — is returning. Nine of the top 10 rotation players from the championship run are back. The Knicks also re-signed Jordan Clarkson, Jose Alvarado, and Landry Shamet to fill out the bench. Andre Drummond was added for frontcourt depth.

The banner goes up October 20 at Madison Square Garden, when New York hosts an opponent in what promises to be the loudest building in basketball on the most electric night of the early season. The Knicks will raise that banner as a core group that has stayed together — which is not something championship teams always manage.

All of that is good news. The Brunson surgery does not erase it. It complicates it.

The Eastern Conference Got Harder

The New York Knicks defended their title last season as the conference's dominant team. The Eastern Conference they enter in 2026-27 is more competitive.

The Philadelphia 76ers have signed LeBron James. A healthy LeBron alongside Joel Embiid and Jaylen Brown is, on paper, one of the most formidable Eastern Conference lineups in recent memory. The Cleveland Cavaliers locked Donovan Mitchell into a four-year, $273 million maximum extension and are working to resolve James Harden's opt-out as the final piece of a retooled contender. The Indiana Pacers, dangerous in each of the last two postseasons, are healthy and motivated.

The Knicks cannot defend in cruise control. They need Brunson healthy, sharp, and ready to perform at the level he reached in the 2026 Finals. His wrist surgery is a reminder that the margin between championship and disappointment is often a healthy body as much as anything else.

What's Next

The Knicks' medical staff and front office will monitor Brunson's recovery closely through the late summer. Training camp opens September 29. Banner night is October 20. The full 2026-27 schedule is expected in mid-August.

If Brunson is cleared for full practice by the time camp opens and shows no lingering effects, the conversation returns to all the reasons the Knicks are still the team to beat in the East. If his wrist limits him in any way, the window for other teams — led by LeBron's 76ers — gets wider in a hurry.

One summer surgery does not define a season. But the defending champion's most important player having a surgical procedure on his shooting hand in the offseason is worth watching carefully. The Knicks' banner is going up in October. How fully Brunson's hand cooperates will determine what comes after it.
