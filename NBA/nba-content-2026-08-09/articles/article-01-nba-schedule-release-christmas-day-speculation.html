Four Days Until the NBA Schedule Drops — and Christmas Day Speculation Is at a Fever Pitch

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | August 9, 2026

NBA Season Preview / Schedule Release

Thursday, August 13. Four days from now. That is when the full 2026-27 NBA schedule — all 1,230 regular-season games — is scheduled to drop. The NBA Cup slate arrives two days earlier, on Tuesday, August 11. And as the countdown reaches its final days, there is one part of that schedule generating more buzz than any other: Christmas Day.

The ingredients this season are historically loaded. LeBron James is a Philadelphia 76er. Giannis Antetokounmpo signed with the Miami Heat. Victor Wembanyama is entering his third NBA season in San Antonio. The New York Knicks are the defending NBA champions. Five games, December 25. The league has never had more stars to work with on a single date.

The Christmas Day Puzzle

LeBron James has appeared in 16 consecutive Christmas Day games. That streak is not ending in 2026. The question is which team the league assigns against his 76ers on December 25 — and who they pair with Giannis's Heat, Wembanyama's Spurs, and the Knicks as hosts or visitors.

The most obvious marquee matchup: LeBron and the 76ers against the Knicks. Philadelphia-New York on Christmas Day, in the Knicks' championship season, would be a ratings event. The alternative — sending the 76ers against the Celtics or Heat — would be equally compelling. The scheduling committee is not short on options.

Industry insiders have also pointed to Wembanyama vs. Embiid as a natural pairing. The two big men play in different conferences and only face each other a handful of times per season. Putting them on Christmas Day guarantees maximum exposure for the league's most compelling positional matchup.

What Drops Tuesday: The NBA Cup Slate

Before the full schedule arrives Thursday, the NBA Cup slate lands on Tuesday. The fourth edition of the Emirates NBA Cup follows a familiar structure: Group Play on Fridays from late October through late November, with additional Cup Nights on November 24-25. The quarterfinals move to local markets on December 4-5, and the championship game is set for Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis on December 11.

The Tuesday release reveals which teams fall into which groups — three groups per conference, five teams each — and what the Friday game slate looks like for the first month of the season. For teams with strong Cup ambitions, those Friday pairings matter enormously.

The Other Marquee Dates to Watch

Beyond Christmas, every rivalry game involving the new-look Eastern Conference becomes appointment television. LeBron's first road trip to New York will be broadcast nationally. His first game in Boston as a 76er. The first time he faces his former Lakers teammates. The first time Giannis plays in Milwaukee as an opponent. These are the moments Thursday's schedule will deliver.

The Knicks' banner night on October 20 at Madison Square Garden is also a massive scheduling decision. Whoever the league sends to crash that party — in the most-watched home opener of the regular season — faces a hostile MSG crowd and a national television audience. Every team's PR staff is quietly hoping for — or dreading — that assignment.

What's Next

The NBA Cup slate drops Tuesday, August 11. The full 2026-27 regular-season schedule follows on Thursday, August 13. The 81st NBA season tips off October 20, now 72 days away. Once the schedule is in hand, the focus shifts from what has happened in the offseason to what is coming this fall. Four days.
