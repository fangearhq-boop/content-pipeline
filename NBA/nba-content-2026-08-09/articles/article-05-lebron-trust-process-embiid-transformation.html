LeBron Said "Trust The Process" — and for the 76ers, That Phrase Has Never Meant More

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | August 9, 2026

NBA Offseason / Philadelphia 76ers / LeBron James / Joel Embiid

It was three words. LeBron James reposted Joel Embiid's offseason transformation photo with a simple caption: "Trust The Process!" The internet responded accordingly. For Philadelphia 76ers fans, who spent years living and breathing that phrase during the Sam Hinkie tank era, the moment was equal parts surreal and electric.

The Process has officially arrived — in the form of LeBron James, Joel Embiid, Tyrese Maxey, Jaylen Brown, and Jared Edgecombe sharing a locker room for the first time.

What Embiid Actually Looks Like

The photo that sent LeBron — and the NBA world — into a frenzy showed Embiid in noticeably different shape than fans have seen in recent seasons. Leaner. More conditioned. The kind of visual evidence that makes an offseason narrative feel real rather than aspirational.

But the most meaningful confirmation did not come from a social media photo. It came from 76ers president Mike Gansey, who told reporters that Embiid has completed "one of the first offseasons he is healthy going into it" — a full program without restrictions. For a player whose health has been the defining question of his career, that quote carries more weight than any number of workout photos.

The Process, Redefined

The 76ers' "Trust The Process" era — coined during a years-long deliberate rebuild under general manager Sam Hinkie — became one of the most recognizable phrases in modern sports. The concept was simple: sacrifice short-term competitiveness for long-term talent accumulation through high draft picks. The process yielded Embiid, Ben Simmons, and eventually a legitimate championship contender.

Now, years later, LeBron James is using the same phrase not as a call for patience, but as a declaration that the process has paid off. He signed with the 76ers on a two-year deal for approximately $8 million — not for the money, but for the opportunity to compete for a championship alongside Embiid in his 22nd and potentially final NBA seasons. "Last decision," he reportedly called it.

Five Stars, Five MVPs

The projected 76ers starting five holds five combined NBA MVP awards: LeBron (four), Embiid (one), with Maxey, Brown, and Edgecombe rounding out the lineup. LeBron and Embiid were already teammates — on the United States Olympic team that won gold at the 2024 Paris Games. They know each other's games. They respect each other's competitive drive.

Maxey has been in the gym at 6 AM. LeBron has been posting workout content before most fans are awake. Jaylen Brown has transitioned from Boston's championship culture to a new superstar-laden environment in Philadelphia. The early signals from this offseason have been uniformly positive.

What's Next

Training camp for the 76ers opens in late September. The full schedule — including Philadelphia's home opener and first road trips — drops Thursday, August 13. Embiid's health between now and October 20 remains the single most important variable in Philadelphia's championship calculus. 76ers president Mike Gansey's confirmation of a full, unrestricted offseason is the best news the fan base has received all summer. Trust the process — training camp is 45 days away.
