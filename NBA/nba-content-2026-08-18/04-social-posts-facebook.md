### STORY 5: Kawhi Leonard Trade Still on Hold

#### Long-Form Post — 7:00 PM ET

Five weeks after Kawhi Leonard's trade back to Toronto was first agreed upon, the deal remains officially on hold — and the timeline is getting tight.

The NBA's investigation into whether the Los Angeles Clippers circumvented the salary cap to pay Leonard through a now-bankrupt company called Aspiration continues. A second allegation has also emerged involving scoreboard and jumbotron manufacturer Daktronics, which supplied equipment to the Clippers' Intuit Dome — with investigators examining whether Daktronics funds were routed to Leonard beyond his official salary.

The agreed trade: Leonard heads to the Raptors in exchange for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, two unprotected first-round picks (2031 and 2033), a 2027 first-round swap, and two second-round selections.

Resolution is expected roughly six weeks after August 10 — which points to approximately September 22. Toronto opens training camp September 28-29 in Quebec City. The math is workable, but barely.

Leonard himself has said he expects the trade to clear before camp. Extension talks — a deal worth up to $123.7 million — are waiting in the wings as soon as it does. And yes, his first return to Los Angeles is already penciled in: November 2.

The clock is ticking.
