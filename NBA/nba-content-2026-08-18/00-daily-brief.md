### STORY 1: NBA Releases 2026-27 Full Schedule — Opening Night, Christmas Day Unveiled
- **Tier:** 1
- **Category:** NBA Schedule / 2026-27 Season
- **Summary:** The NBA released its full 2026-27 regular season schedule on August 13. ### STORY 2: Basketball Hall of Fame Class of 2026 Enshrined — Parker, Stoudemire, Rivers Immortalized
- **Tier:** 1
- **Category:** NBA History / Basketball Hall of Fame / Class of 2026
- **Summary:** The Naismith Memorial Basketball Hall of Fame's Class of 2026 was officially enshrined Saturday, August 15, at Symphony Hall in Springfield, Massachusetts. Candace Parker, Amar'e Stoudemire, and Doc Rivers led the class. Full honorees: Chamique Holdsclaw, Joey Crawford, Mark Few, Doc Rivers, Amar'e Stoudemire, Elena Delle Donne, Candace Parker, Mike D'Antoni, and the 1996 U.S. Women's National Team. The ceremony capped Enshrinement Weekend, which began August 14 at Mohegan Sun in Connecticut.
- **Why it matters:** A historically loaded class — two WNBA icons (Parker, Delle Donne), a franchise-altering Suns player (Stoudemire), and one of the sport's most innovative offensive minds (D'Antoni) round out a ceremony honoring the full breadth of basketball.
- **Key sources:** NBA.com, NBC Sports, Yahoo Sports, TSN

### STORY 3: Stephen Curry Extension — 11 Days to August 29 Window
- **Tier:** 2
- **Category:** NBA Contracts / Golden State Warriors / Stephen Curry
- **Summary:** Stephen Curry's extension window opens in 11 days — August 29. The Warriors can offer up to two years, $136.7 million. GM Mike Dunleavy Jr. remains "pretty confident" Curry will finish his career in Golden State, and mutual interest is confirmed by ESPN's Anthony Slater. Curry continues shooting 42% from three at age 38. The Warriors went 37-45 last season and missed the playoffs. Without an extension, Curry would become an unrestricted free agent in 2027 — the first time in his career.
- **Why it matters:** Each day closer to August 29 intensifies one of the most consequential decisions in Warriors franchise history.
- **Key sources:** Bleacher Report, HoopsRumors, Yahoo Sports, NBC Sports

### STORY 5: Kawhi Leonard Toronto Trade Still Pending — Investigation Update
- **Tier:** 2
- **Category:** NBA Investigation / Toronto Raptors / Los Angeles Clippers / Kawhi Leonard
- **Summary:** Kawhi Leonard's trade from the Clippers to the Toronto Raptors remains pending due to the NBA's ongoing investigation into alleged salary cap circumvention — specifically whether the Clippers funneled money to Leonard through a now-bankrupt company, Aspiration. The trade (agreed June 29-30: Leonard for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, two unprotected first-round picks, a pick swap, and two second-rounders) is still on hold. A second deal involving scoreboard manufacturer Daktronics is also under investigation. The NBA is expected to resolve the matter approximately six weeks after August 10 (target: ~September 22). Toronto's training camp is scheduled for September 28-29 in Quebec City.
- **Why it matters:** Every passing day without resolution affects Toronto's roster planning, Kawhi's extension timeline, and the Clippers' future.
- **Key sources:** Yahoo Sports, ClutchPoints, NBA.com
