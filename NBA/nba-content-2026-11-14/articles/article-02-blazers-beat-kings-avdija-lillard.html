Avdija Drops 34, Lillard Adds 29 as Blazers Roll Past Struggling Kings 120-107

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | November 14, 2026

Deni Avdija erupted for 34 points and Damian Lillard added 29 as Portland handed Sacramento its fifth consecutive defeat, dropping the Kings to 1-11 overall and a league-worst 0-7 at home.

Portland's Deni Avdija and Damian Lillard have quietly become one of the NBA's most potent offensive duos, and Friday night at the Golden 1 Center, Sacramento paid the price for that emergence. Avdija poured in 34 points, Lillard added 29, and the Trail Blazers pulled away for a commanding 120-107 victory that only deepened Sacramento's early-season misery.

The result gave Portland its third win in as many games and pushed Sacramento to a devastating 1-11 record — the worst mark in the entire NBA entering the weekend.

Avdija and Lillard: A Combination Defenses Haven't Solved

Avdija's 34-point showing was a continuation of a form that has made him one of the league's most improved players this season. The Israeli forward attacked off the dribble, finished at the rim, and hit enough outside shots to keep Sacramento's defense from collapsing on him entirely. Lillard, operating alongside him, was characteristically relentless in his ability to create his own shot from anywhere on the floor.

When both players are going simultaneously, Portland is nearly impossible to guard. The Kings had no answers, and their deficit grew steadily through the second half as the Blazers' ball movement stretched the Sacramento defense to its breaking point.

Sacramento's Crisis Deepens

No number tells the Sacramento story more brutally than the home record: 0-7. For a team built on home energy and a passionate fanbase, playing seven home games and losing all of them is extraordinary bad fortune — or a profound structural problem.

The Kings now sit at 1-11 overall, their fifth consecutive defeat arriving Friday night. A 1-11 start in mid-November puts Sacramento in a deep enough hole that turning around the season will require nearly flawless basketball through the winter months. The roster has the pieces to be competitive, but something is clearly not clicking, and the pressure on the coaching staff and front office will continue to mount with each passing loss.

Portland Trending in the Right Direction

Three wins in three games has Portland feeling considerably more optimistic than they did at the start of November. The Blazers' formula isn't complicated: give Lillard the ball, let Avdija attack, and trust the supporting cast to knock down open shots. When the execution is there, as it has been in this recent run, Portland is a difficult team to contain.

Friday's win represented the latest evidence that Portland's rebuild is ahead of schedule, and that the Avdija-Lillard pairing has the potential to be something genuinely special.

What's Next

Portland will look to make it four wins in a row and continue building momentum through the NBA Cup stretch. The Blazers have an opportunity to establish themselves as a legitimate Western Conference dark horse if they can sustain this level of play.

Sacramento needs answers immediately. Their schedule does not get any kinder, and a 1-11 record heading into the second half of November is a franchise alarm bell. Organizational decisions may be coming sooner rather than later if the losses continue to mount.
