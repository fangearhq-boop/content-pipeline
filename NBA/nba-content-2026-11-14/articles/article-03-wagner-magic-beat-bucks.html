Wagner's Near Triple-Double (35 Points, 12 Assists) Lifts Magic Past Bucks 107-99

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | November 14, 2026

Franz Wagner was one rebound away from an extraordinary triple-double Friday night in Milwaukee, as his 35-point, 12-assist performance carried the Orlando Magic past the Bucks 107-99 in NBA Cup play.

Franz Wagner set out to put on a show in Milwaukee, and he nearly checked every box there is. The Orlando Magic forward finished with 35 points, 12 assists, and just one rebound shy of a historic triple-double — a performance that underscored why he has become one of the Eastern Conference's most feared players this season.

Orlando beat the Milwaukee Bucks 107-99 in NBA Cup action, a win that continues the Magic's quiet emergence as a legitimate playoff contender in the East. When Wagner plays at this level, Orlando is a genuinely dangerous team.

How Close to History?

Wagner's stat line — 35 points and 12 assists — is elite by any standard. The fact that he finished one rebound away from completing a triple-double adds an almost theatrical element to what was an otherwise comprehensive performance. Throughout the game Wagner filled virtually every role the Magic needed: shot creator, playmaker, finisher, and floor general.

At 35 points and 12 assists, Wagner's night was reminiscent of the kind of two-way offensive brilliance that defines the NBA's best players. His ability to score and create simultaneously is a matchup nightmare that Milwaukee simply had no answer for over the course of four quarters.

Orlando's Statement Win

Beating the Bucks, even with Giannis and Milwaukee playing well this season, is a statement. The Magic's 107-99 final score was hard-earned — Milwaukee made runs and kept the margin from getting comfortable — but Orlando maintained control whenever it mattered most.

The Magic's defense was a critical factor. Orlando disrupted Milwaukee's rhythm throughout, and their ability to contain the Bucks' multiple offensive weapons while Wagner was creating on the other end gave the Magic a decisive edge.

What's Next

Orlando keeps building. With Wagner leading as both scorer and creator, the Magic have established themselves as a team with a genuine identity and a clear franchise cornerstone. If this level of play continues through the NBA Cup stretch and into December, Orlando will need to be in every serious Eastern Conference conversation.

Milwaukee's loss won't panic anyone inside the Bucks' organization — they remain a talented team capable of competing with anyone. But a home defeat to Orlando serves as a reminder that in the current East, there are no easy nights.
