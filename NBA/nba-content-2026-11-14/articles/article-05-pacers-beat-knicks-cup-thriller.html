Siakam, Nesmith Power Pacers Past Knicks 106-104 in Tense NBA Cup Finish

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | November 14, 2026

Pascal Siakam scored 19 and Aaron Nesmith added 18 as the Indiana Pacers held off the New York Knicks 106-104 in an NBA Cup game decided in the final seconds, with Jalen Brunson nearly pulling off a dramatic Knicks comeback.

The Indiana Pacers and New York Knicks have an understanding: when they share a floor, the game will not be settled until the last possession. Friday night's NBA Cup matchup followed the script faithfully, with Jalen Brunson's driving layup cutting Indiana's lead to 106-102 with 26 seconds remaining and threatening to turn a Pacers victory into another chapter of Knicks comeback lore.

Instead, Indiana held on. The final score, 106-104, reflected a game that could have gone either way — but it was Pascal Siakam's 19 points, Aaron Nesmith's 18, and Indiana's composure in the final minute that made the difference.

Siakam and Nesmith Carry the Load

Siakam was the floor general Indiana needed throughout the game, controlling pace and finding his spots against the Knicks' physical defense. His 19 points came efficiently, and his ability to make decisions under pressure during New York's fourth-quarter pushes proved critical to Indiana maintaining its edge.

Nesmith's 18-point contribution provided the depth New York struggled to match. The Pacers have consistently gotten contributions from multiple sources this season, and Friday's game was another example of that balanced offensive identity — something that makes Indiana particularly difficult to game-plan against.

Brunson's Late Push Falls Short

Jalen Brunson's driving layup with 26 seconds remaining made the final moments genuinely nerve-wracking for Indiana fans. Down 106-102, Brunson's basket cut the deficit to two and set up a potential Knicks possession with a chance to tie or take the lead.

The Pacers, to their credit, did not flinch. They executed on the ensuing possession, made the crucial free throws, and closed out the victory. It was the kind of late-game poise that characterizes good teams — and Indiana's ability to hold off Brunson in crunch time was the defining moment of the game.

What's Next

Indiana's Cup campaign continues to build with this victory, adding another quality win that strengthens their position in the Eastern Conference standings. The Pacers have established themselves as a consistent, well-rounded team that can win tight games — a valuable quality come playoff time.

For New York, the 104-point total is not the concern — the Knicks scored enough to win. What will sting is the late execution and the inability to convert after Brunson's layup reopened the door. The rivalry with Indiana continues to deliver, and both teams know their paths through the Eastern Conference will almost certainly cross again before the season is over.
