NBA Wednesday Preview: What to Watch on November 11

By Jake Torres | November 11, 2026

Tuesday gave NBA fans plenty to talk about — Paul George burning Philly, the Bucks continuing their freefall, and the Knicks flexing their early-season dominance. Now Wednesday arrives with another slate of games and more chances for teams to either build on momentum or dig themselves out of a hole.

The Eastern Conference Storylines to Follow

After what we saw on Tuesday, the Eastern Conference is coming into focus. The Celtics are looking like contenders. The Knicks are rolling. And the Bucks are genuinely in crisis at 1-10. As Wednesday's games tip off, watch for teams fighting for positioning in the top half of the East — the difference between a top-four seed and playing in-season tournament elimination games can shape a franchise's entire year.

Teams like the Miami Heat, Chicago Bulls, and Cleveland Cavaliers have all shown flashes of potential this season, and Wednesday represents another opportunity to separate from the pack. When the top of the conference is occupied by teams playing with the confidence of Boston and New York, every game below the cut line becomes essential.

Western Conference: Can Anyone Slow the Warriors?

The Golden State Warriors have been one of the early-season stories of the 2026-27 campaign. Stephen Curry and company have been playing with the kind of cohesion and intensity that recall their championship runs, and their extended win streak has put the rest of the Western Conference on notice.

Wednesday's Western slate provides another test for the league's emerging powers. The Los Angeles Clippers picked up a key win on Tuesday and will be looking to keep that momentum going. The Denver Nuggets, Oklahoma City Thunder, and Dallas Mavericks are all teams worth monitoring as the conference picture takes shape.

Bucks Watch: Can Milwaukee Avoid Another Loss?

Perhaps the most compelling subplot entering Wednesday — whenever the Bucks play next — is whether Milwaukee can halt a catastrophic skid. At 1-10 with seven straight losses, the Bucks are running out of time to right the ship before the season gets truly difficult to salvage.

No specific game against the Bucks can be treated as automatic, but their opponents know that this Milwaukee team is vulnerable right now. Any team on the schedule facing the Bucks in the near term has a significant opportunity to add a win.

What's Next

The NBA season is still in its first stretch, but the patterns are starting to emerge. Teams that build good habits in November — winning close games, defending with effort, and trusting their systems — tend to look very different in March and April than those that allow bad habits to calcify early.

Wednesday's slate will tell us more about which teams are legitimate contenders and which ones are still searching for their identity. Keep your eyes on the standings — the picture is becoming clearer every night.
