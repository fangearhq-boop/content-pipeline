Paul George Drops 36 on Former Team as Celtics Crush 76ers 123-101

By Jake Torres | November 11, 2026

Paul George returned to Philadelphia on Tuesday night and delivered a performance his former city will not soon forget, erupting for 36 points and six three-pointers as the Boston Celtics dismantled the 76ers, 123-101.

George Puts on a Homecoming Show

There is a particular brand of motivation that only a return to a former team can produce, and Paul George tapped into every ounce of it at the Wells Fargo Center on Tuesday night. The Boston Celtics forward — who once wore the uniform of the host Philadelphia 76ers — torched his old squad with 36 points on six three-pointers, putting on a showcase that left no doubt about who had the better end of that roster move.

From his first shot, George looked locked in. His pull-up jumpers fell with authority, his catch-and-shoot threes were crisp, and his confidence never wavered — even as the Philadelphia crowd made its feelings known from the opening tip. By the time the final buzzer sounded, the scoreboard read 123-101 in Boston's favor, and George had authored one of the more memorable individual performances of the young 2026-27 season.

Tatum Provides Steady Support

Jayson Tatum did not need to carry the load on this night, but he contributed with 21 efficient points to give Boston a potent one-two punch that the 76ers could not handle. Tatum's ability to find the right moments — filling in the gaps when George attacked and creating his own offense when defenders collapsed — showed the kind of All-Star complement that makes Boston so dangerous.

The Celtics' ball movement also stood out. This was not a team leaning on isolation play. Boston shared the ball, found open shooters, and punished Philadelphia for any defensive mistake. The 22-point margin of victory was entirely earned.

Philadelphia Struggles to Find Answers

For the 76ers, Tuesday's loss was a reminder of the challenge ahead. Philadelphia has the talent to compete in the Eastern Conference, but nights when former stars return and drop 36 on you tend to expose defensive lapses and offensive inconsistencies that more forgiving opponents might not punish. Boston forced Philadelphia into a game that grew uncomfortable early and never recovered.

The Sixers will need to regroup quickly. With the Eastern Conference already taking shape in November, there is little margin for blowout home losses.

What's Next

The Celtics head back to Boston with a statement road win on their résumé, reinforcing their status as one of the Eastern Conference's premier teams. For Paul George, Tuesday night was more than a great game — it was a defining moment in his first season as a Celtic, a signal to the rest of the conference that this team has star-level firepower up and down the roster.

The 76ers will look to bounce back and remind their fans that one difficult night does not define a season. But the Celtics, led by a fully motivated Paul George, served notice Tuesday that the road through the East runs through Boston.
