### STORY 2: Roster Cuts — Knicks Down to Two

**Long-Form Post (11:00 AM ET)**
The NBA's final roster crunch is happening right now. 🔔

The New York Knicks just waived three players — Drew Eubanks (who tore his pectoral muscle), Ochai Agbaji, and Tony Bradley — leaving one final roster spot open. The battle is down to James Wiseman, the former No. 2 overall pick, and Bruce Brown, the veteran wing who won a championship with the Nuggets.

Wiseman is the early favorite, per reports. Keeping Brown would likely require a trade that hasn't materialized yet.

Across the league, the Jazz cut Taylor Funk and Chance Moore. The Nets waived Ben Humrichous. The Hawks cut Giorgi Bezhanishvili.

The non-guaranteed waiver deadline hits tomorrow, October 17. By Sunday at 4 PM CT, every roster must be down to 15 standard contracts plus 3 two-way players. That's 18 total bodies, and right now teams are making hard decisions about whose season starts Monday and whose doesn't.

Four days. #NBA #Knicks #HoopHeroes

### STORY 3: Porzingis — Warriors' Opening Night Problem

**Long-Form Post (7:00 PM ET)**
Kristaps Porzingis is heading into the regular season without a return timetable.

The Warriors' starting center has missed all of training camp and all six preseason games with an undisclosed health issue. Tonight, Golden State plays its final preseason tune-up against the Portland Trail Blazers — and Porzingis won't be in uniform.

GM Mike Dunleavy Jr. said he isn't marking Porzingis down as out for the start of the season, but he also hasn't given any concrete timeline for a return.

That puts the Warriors in a difficult spot for Opening Night on Monday. Without Porzingis in the middle, Golden State has to find size and rim protection from somewhere. The NBA's season starts in four days, and one of the most injury-prone players in the league has given his team nothing to go on.
