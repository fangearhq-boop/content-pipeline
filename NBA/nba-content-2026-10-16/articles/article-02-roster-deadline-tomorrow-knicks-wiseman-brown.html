Roster Deadline Tomorrow: The Knicks Cut Three, and One Final Spot Remains

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | October 16, 2026

NBA Roster Moves | New York Knicks | James Wiseman | Bruce Brown

The NBA's annual cut-day scramble is happening right now, with the non-guaranteed waiver deadline arriving tomorrow and teams across the league making hard decisions about who starts the regular season on their roster — and who does not.

The most closely watched situation belongs to the defending champion New York Knicks, who waived three players Thursday and set up a final competition for the last spot on their Opening Night roster.

The Knicks' Final Battle

New York waived center Drew Eubanks, who tore a pectoral muscle and is out for the season, along with wing Ochai Agbaji and center Tony Bradley. With those three gone, the Knicks' last open roster spot comes down to two players: James Wiseman, the former No. 2 overall pick, and Bruce Brown, the veteran wing who won a championship with the Denver Nuggets.

Reports out of New York indicate Wiseman is the early favorite. Brown would likely require a trade to keep, and no such deal had materialized as of Thursday. If a trade does not happen, expect Wiseman to land the spot by the Sunday deadline.

The irony is not lost: the defending champions, four days from raising their first banner in 53 years, are still sorting out their fifteenth roster spot while preparing to welcome LeBron James and the 76ers to Madison Square Garden.

League-Wide Moves

The Knicks were not the only team trimming rosters. The Utah Jazz waived forwards Taylor Funk and Chance Moore, both of whom are expected to join the G League's Salt Lake City Stars. The Brooklyn Nets released swingman Ben Humrichous. The Atlanta Hawks waived forward Giorgi Bezhanishvili.

More cuts across the league are expected before Thursday ends and throughout Friday, as teams finalize their Opening Night rosters.

The Deadline Timeline

The non-guaranteed waiver deadline arrives on Friday, October 17. Any player on a non-guaranteed contract who is not waived by that date has their salary guaranteed for the remainder of the season. Teams will not carry non-guaranteed contracts they cannot afford past that date.

By Sunday, October 19 at 4 PM CT, every roster must be reduced to 15 standard contracts plus three two-way players. That is 18 total spots, and right now several teams are still above that number. The next 72 hours will see the final wave of cuts that shape the opening weeks of the season.

What's Next

The Knicks' Wiseman-vs.-Brown decision will likely be resolved before Sunday. If a trade bringing in Bruce Brown does not materialize, expect the team to go with Wiseman and build their 15-man roster from there. The Opening Night roster is four days away from being tested in a real game, and the defending champions want this settled before LeBron shows up at MSG on Monday.
