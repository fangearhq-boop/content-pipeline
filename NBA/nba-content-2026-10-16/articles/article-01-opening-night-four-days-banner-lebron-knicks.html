Four Days to Opening Night: The Knicks' Banner Moment, LeBron's Arrival, and Nine Hours of NBA Basketball

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | October 16, 2026

NBA Opening Night | New York Knicks | Philadelphia 76ers | Oklahoma City Thunder | San Antonio Spurs

The last preseason game tips off tonight. Four days from now, the real season begins, and the NBA has arranged Monday, October 20 into one of the most loaded evenings in recent league history.

Three games on NBC. A championship banner ceremony. A debut that the league has been selling since the offseason. And a Western Conference Finals rematch to close it out. If you are a basketball fan, there is no reason to leave your couch from 3 PM to midnight.

The Banner Goes Up First

Before any basketball is played, the New York Knicks will raise a banner at Madison Square Garden. Their first championship banner since 1973.

Jalen Brunson won the Finals MVP with 45 points in Game 5 as the Knicks closed out San Antonio to end a 53-year drought. The city celebrated in a way it had not been able to celebrate basketball since the Willis Reed era. Now those Knicks — the same core, same coach, same building — will watch that banner rise to the rafters while their home crowd does what MSG crowds do on the biggest nights.

Then, roughly 48 minutes later, they have to play basketball against LeBron James.

LeBron at MSG — Opening Night's Best Story

LeBron James is 41 years old, in his 24th NBA season, playing for his fifth franchise. He signed with Philadelphia this summer because he believes the 76ers can win a championship. His first regular-season game in a Sixers uniform will be at Madison Square Garden, as the visiting team, against the defending champions, in front of a crowd that has every reason to boo him.

The Sixers play their final preseason game tonight at 7 PM ET against the Celtics. LeBron is listed as a game-time decision — he will likely sit out to stay fresh. That is the correct decision. Four days from now, there will be no resting.

Jaylen Brown, who was traded from Boston to get here, plays his first game against his former teammates on Opening Night as well. The Sixers' Big Four of Brown, LeBron, Joel Embiid, and Tyrese Maxey tips off in front of what should be the loudest crowd of the 2026-27 season so far.

The Full Tripleheader

The night opens at 3 PM ET with the Boston Celtics visiting the Detroit Pistons. Paul George joins the Celtics after coming over in a blockbuster offseason trade; the Pistons, building around Jalen Duren, open as a legitimate young team worth watching. It is an accessible early game for fans who want to ease into the day.

At 7 PM ET, the Sixers-Knicks game takes center stage. Banner. Rings. LeBron. This is the one that sold out within hours and pushed secondary market prices past $2,200.

At 9:30 PM ET, the Oklahoma City Thunder visit the San Antonio Spurs. The Spurs eliminated OKC in Game 7 of the Western Conference Finals last June. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, the two-time MVP and the favorite to win a third, goes back to the building where his season ended. Victor Wembanyama, fresh off a five-year, $252 million extension, will be waiting. This is the best young-player matchup in basketball, and it closes the most important night of the year.

What's Next

The NBA's final preseason games run tonight, including the Warriors at Portland and the Sixers hosting Boston. Roster cuts must be complete by 4 PM CT on Sunday, October 19. Then, on Monday, October 20, the tripleheader begins on NBC at 3 PM ET and carries through the night.

Four days. The regular season arrives on Monday.
