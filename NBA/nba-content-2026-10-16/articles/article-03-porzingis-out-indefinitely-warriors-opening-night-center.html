Porzingis Still Out, No Timeline: The Warriors Enter Opening Night Without Their Starting Center

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | October 16, 2026

Golden State Warriors | Kristaps Porzingis | Injuries | NBA

Six preseason games. Zero appearances. Still no timetable for a return.

Kristaps Porzingis has been absent from every Golden State Warriors preseason game this year, held out with an undisclosed health issue that the franchise has declined to specify. On Thursday, with the Warriors playing their final preseason game against the Portland Trail Blazers at 10 PM ET, there is still no indication of when — or whether — he will be available for the start of the regular season.

What the Warriors Have Said

Warriors general manager Mike Dunleavy Jr. addressed the situation at the start of training camp, telling reporters that Porzingis is out indefinitely due to a health issue but that he is not marking him down as out for the beginning of the regular season.

That carefully worded statement offered no real clarity. It leaves the door open for a return before Opening Night while offering nothing concrete to plan around. Four days from the start of the regular season, that ambiguity is not comfortable for a franchise with championship ambitions.

Porzingis and His Injury History

If there is a player in the NBA whose health situations carry particular weight, it is Porzingis. Over the last several years, he has dealt with a left Achilles issue that delayed his Warriors debut until after the All-Star break in early 2026, a lower back spasm that took him out of a game at Detroit in March, and various other ailments that have followed him throughout his career.

The Warriors acquired Porzingis for his rim protection, his floor-spacing, and his ability to play as a modern center in their offense. Without him, they are a smaller team that has to compensate elsewhere — not an impossible task, but not a situation any team wants entering a season with championship expectations.

Tonight's Final Preseason Game

Golden State plays Portland tonight at 10 PM ET. This is their last opportunity before the regular season to experiment with lineups, test rotations, and figure out how the center minutes will be distributed if Porzingis is unavailable on Monday. Expect coach Steve Kerr to use the game to give extended run to whoever is in line to start if Porzingis does not suit up next week.

The Trail Blazers are rebuilding, so the competition level is not exactly a stress test. But the minutes distribution tonight will tell a story about how the Warriors are thinking about this problem.

What's Next

The Warriors open the regular season on Monday, October 20. Their first game on Opening Night is not part of the tripleheader, but they face a full schedule in the opening week. Porzingis's status will be the most-watched injury update in the league between now and tip-off. If he does not play Monday, the Warriors will be forced to find solutions internally — and the league will take notice of how they handle an early test without their most important big man.
