Haliburton Is 48 Hours From His Return — Indiana Has Been Waiting 16 Months for This

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | October 5, 2026

Tyrese Haliburton plays basketball on Wednesday. That sentence, uncomplicated as it is, carries sixteen months of weight behind it.

Haliburton tore his Achilles in Game 7 of the 2025 NBA Finals — a non-contact injury in a game that Indiana ultimately lost, a final act of a season that had already asked more of him than any player should be asked to give. He had carried the Pacers to that stage almost entirely on the strength of his own creation and leadership, and then, in the game that would have defined his legacy in a new way, it ended on the floor.

Wednesday is his return. October 7. Indiana has penciled in his preseason debut, and by all accounts the recovery has gone as well as the organization could have hoped.

The Long Road Back

Achilles recoveries are measured in months and milestones. The surgery comes first, then the immobilization, then the slow, painstaking process of rebuilding strength and range of motion and, most crucially, the trust in your own body that an injury like this can take from a player. Sixteen months is not a short timeline. It is the kind of absence that changes how a player thinks about the floor, about contact, about the moments when instinct and hesitation are at war.

What the Pacers have communicated publicly throughout this process has been consistent: Haliburton is doing the work, the rehab has been on schedule, and the target date of early October was never in doubt once the summer cleared without setback. His listing for the October 7 preseason game is not a surprise to anyone inside the organization. It is the destination they have been driving toward since the day of the injury.

What He Returns To

Indiana has continued to build around Haliburton's return. The Pacers have shown, in the seasons since their core was assembled, that they have the pieces and the system to compete at a high level. They reached Game 7 of the 2025 Finals. They believe, with their point guard healthy, that they can go further.

Haliburton is the engine of everything Indiana does. He sets the pace, controls the tempo, and pulls defenders in ways that create opportunities for every other player on the roster. Without him last season, the Pacers had to reconfigure their identity around what remained. With him, that identity snaps back into place.

What's Next

Wednesday evening, Haliburton takes the floor in Indiana preseason action for the first time since June 2025. The organization will manage his minutes carefully. No one expects him to play 35 minutes in a preseason game after a sixteen-month absence. What they expect — what the whole basketball world is watching for — is the moment he makes his first pass in a real game and the crowd inside the building understands that their guy is back. That moment is 48 hours away.
