Kawhi Leonard Trade to Toronto Is Increasingly Imminent — The Reunion Is Almost Here

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | August 30, 2026

Toronto Raptors · Los Angeles Clippers · Kawhi Leonard · NBA Transactions

Seven years. That is how long Toronto Raptors fans have waited. Seven years since Kawhi Leonard won the franchise its only championship, then walked out the door the moment free agency opened. Now, according to NBA insider Jake Fischer of NBC Sports, Leonard is "increasingly confident" his trade back to the Raptors will be finalized before training camp begins — and the finish line is finally in sight.

A Trade Two Months in the Making

The deal itself was agreed upon at the end of June 2026: Leonard to Toronto in exchange for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, two unprotected first-round picks (2031 and 2033), a 2027 first-round swap, and two second-round picks.

What should have been a clean, straightforward transaction became one of the summer's most prolonged storylines when the NBA opened an investigation into the Los Angeles Clippers for potential salary cap violations — specifically related to Leonard's endorsement deals with Aspiration and Daktronics.

The investigation cast a cloud over everything. Could the trade proceed? Would Leonard end up staying in Los Angeles? Would the sanctions be severe enough to unwind the deal entirely?

The NBA Clears the Path

Last week brought the most important development yet: the NBA cleared the Clippers of the Aspiration cap circumvention charge. Leonard himself had already been personally cleared weeks earlier. With the primary allegation resolved, the trade began moving toward the finish line.

What remains is the NBA Board of Governors meeting, expected in mid-September, which will determine the scope of any remaining sanctions against the Clippers organization. That meeting represents the final bureaucratic hurdle before the trade can be officially processed.

Both Leonard and the Raptors are operating under the assumption that the deal will cross the finish line before training camp opens. Toronto's camp begins September 28 in Quebec City, with overseas players reporting as early as September 22.

The Homecoming Toronto Has Been Waiting For

For Raptors fans, this is not merely a roster move — it's a moment with genuine emotional weight. Leonard arrived in Toronto before the 2018-19 season via trade from San Antonio, delivered the city its first (and still only) NBA championship, hit one of the most iconic buzzer-beaters in playoff history, and then left for the Clippers before the confetti had finished falling.

The departure stung. The reunion, if it holds, will be cathartic.

For the Raptors' front office, the calculus is straightforward: a two-time Finals MVP and four-time All-Defensive Team selection, even at this stage of his career, transforms the franchise's ceiling. Leonard is one of the league's most complete two-way players, and Toronto is betting he has enough left to push them back into contention.

What's Next

The NBA Board of Governors meeting in September is the key date to watch. Once sanctions are finalized and any remaining procedural hurdles are cleared, the trade can be officially processed — meaning Leonard will need to pass a physical before the deal becomes official.

If all goes according to plan, Kawhi Leonard will be suiting up for the Toronto Raptors when training camp begins September 28 in Quebec City. That would mark the formal end of a saga that has consumed much of the NBA offseason — and the beginning of one of the league's most compelling comeback stories.
