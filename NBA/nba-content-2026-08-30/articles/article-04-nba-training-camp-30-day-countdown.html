30 Days to Training Camp: Every NBA Move That Matters Heading Into the 2026-27 Season

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | August 30, 2026

NBA Season Preview · Training Camp · NBA Transactions · 2026-27 Season

Thirty days. That is all that separates the NBA world from training camps opening across the league on September 29, with veterans able to report a day early on September 28. In those 30 days, teams will finalize rosters, settle contract disputes, and put the finishing touches on what promises to be one of the most talent-rich seasons the league has seen in years. Here is everything you need to know.

The Latest Transactions Shaping the League

The roster puzzle pieces have been flying fast in the final days of August.

Jonathan Kuminga — Minnesota Timberwolves (2 years, $12.43M, signed Aug. 26): The former Warriors lottery pick lands in Minnesota on a two-year deal, joining Anthony Edwards and the newly acquired LaMelo Ball in one of the most exciting young backcourts in the Western Conference. Kuminga brings athleticism and defensive versatility that complements the Timberwolves' existing core.

DeMar DeRozan — Denver Nuggets (1 year, $3.88M, veteran minimum): After averaging 18.4 points per game with Sacramento last season, DeRozan quietly signs with Denver to join Nikola Jokic and Jamal Murray. He adds a reliable mid-range scorer who has thrived in isolation-heavy systems — a perfect complement to Jokic's playmaking.

Bennedict Mathurin — New Orleans Pelicans (2 years, $16M): Mathurin gets a fresh start in New Orleans on a contract that reflects both his potential and the need to prove himself in a new system.

Nick Richards — Miami Heat (1 year, $3.06M, signed Aug. 27): Richards rounds out the Heat's big-man rotation behind Bam Adebayo, giving Miami a physical backup center at a bargain price as they chase a championship with Giannis.

J'Vonne Hadley and Keshad Johnson — Miami Heat (two-way contracts, signed Aug. 28): The Heat fill out their roster with two versatile wings on two-way deals, bringing their under-contract total to 14 players.

Key Dates to Circle

The calendar is about to accelerate fast. Here are the dates every NBA fan needs to know:

  • September 22 — Overseas players and international players can report to their teams (important for Toronto, whose Kawhi Leonard trade could be finalized around this time)
  • September 28 — Veterans can officially report to team facilities
  • September 29 — Training camps officially open league-wide
  • October 3 — Preseason begins
  • October 20 — OPENING NIGHT: tripleheader on NBC (Boston at Detroit at 3:00 PM; Philadelphia at New York at 7:00 PM; Oklahoma City at San Antonio at 9:30 PM)

The Big Picture: A Season Unlike Any Other

The 2026-27 season arrives with an extraordinary cast of storylines already in place. The Philadelphia 76ers have assembled a superteam built around LeBron James (in his self-described "last decision"), Joel Embiid, and Jaylen Brown. The Miami Heat bring Giannis Antetokounmpo to South Beach for the first time. The New York Knicks — fresh off winning the championship last season — will raise their first banner since 1973 on Opening Night.

And in Golden State, Stephen Curry signed his $62.6 million extension this very morning, signaling that the Warriors are not done yet.

What's Next

With 30 days remaining, expect a final burst of signings, buyouts, and roster decisions before camps officially open. Teams with cap space remaining will look to fill the last spots on their rosters, while contenders will be putting the finishing touches on championship-level depth charts. The summer is almost over. The season is almost here.
