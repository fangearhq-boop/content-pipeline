### STORY 1: Stephen Curry Signs 1-Year, $62.6M Extension

**Long-Form Post**
Window: 9:00 AM ET

IT'S OFFICIAL — STEPH CURRY IS STAYING A WARRIOR! 🏆

The Golden State Warriors and Stephen Curry have agreed to a 1-year, $62.6 million contract extension, keeping the greatest shooter in NBA history in Golden State through the 2026-27 season.

Here's what makes this deal truly historic: Curry will become the FIRST player in NBA history to earn more than $60 million in a single season. To put that in perspective — he'll surpass what some teams pay their entire roster.

The extension window opened yesterday (August 29), and Curry didn't waste a single day. His agent Jeff Austin of Octagon confirmed the deal to ESPN's Adrian Wojnarowski almost immediately.

Why the 1-year deal? The NBA's over-38 rule limits extensions for players who would turn 38 during a multi-year contract. Curry, currently 36, is bound by this restriction. But make no mistake — the Warriors are committed to their franchise icon for as long as he wants to play.

With this signing, Curry becomes just the 4th player in NBA history to surpass $500 million in career earnings, joining LeBron James, Paul George, and Kevin Durant in that elite financial tier.

Curry has never played for another franchise in his entire career. That loyalty works both ways — and Golden State just made sure of it.

### STORY 2: Kawhi Leonard Trade to Toronto Increasingly Imminent

**Long-Form Post**
Window: 11:00 AM ET

THE KAWHI LEONARD TORONTO RETURN IS GETTING VERY REAL 🦖

According to NBA insider Jake Fischer of NBC Sports, Kawhi Leonard is "increasingly confident" that his trade from the LA Clippers to the Toronto Raptors will be finalized before training camp begins on September 28.

Quick recap of how we got here:
• End of June: Clippers and Raptors agree on trade (Kawhi to Toronto for Brandon Ingram + Gradey Dick + two unprotected first-round picks + pick swaps)
• The deal was put on hold while the NBA investigated potential salary cap violations by the Clippers
• Last week: The NBA cleared the Clippers of the Aspiration cap circumvention charge
• Now: The NBA Board of Governors is expected to meet in September to determine any remaining Clippers sanctions before officially greenlighting the trade

Kawhi and the Raptors are already operating as if the trade will happen — they're planning for his arrival at Quebec City training camp starting September 28.

Remember: Kawhi Leonard won Toronto its ONLY championship in 2019, then left for the Clippers immediately. Seven years later, he's coming home.

For the Raptors, this is a massive swing — they're getting a two-time Finals MVP and one of the best two-way players of his generation, even if Father Time is catching up.
