### Long-Form Post Window: 9:00 AM ET IT'S OFFICIAL — STEPH CURRY IS STAYING A WARRIOR! 🏆 The Golden State Warriors and Stephen Curry have agreed to a 1-year, $62.6 million contract extension, keeping the greatest shooter in NBA history in Golden State through the 2026-27 season. Here's what makes this deal truly historic: Curry will become the FIRST player in NBA history to earn more than $60 million in a single season. To put that in perspective — he'll surpass what some teams pay their entire roster. The extension window opened yesterday (August 29), and Curry didn't waste a single day. His agent Jeff Austin of Octagon confirmed the deal to ESPN's Adrian Wojnarowski almost immediately. Why the 1-year deal? The NBA's over-38 rule limits extensions for players who would turn 38 during a multi-year contract. Curry, currently 36, is bound by this restriction. But make no mistake — the Warriors are committed to their franchise icon for as long as he wants to play. With this signing, Curry becomes just the 4th player in NBA history to surpass $500 million in career earnings, joining LeBron James, Paul George, and Kevin Durant in that elite financial tier. Curry has never played for another franchise in his entire career. That loyalty works both ways — and Golden State just made sure of it. What do you think — can the Warriors make a playoff push with Curry leading the way? --- ### Image Post Caption Window: 9:00 AM ET IT'S DONE. Stephen Curry, $62.6M, one more year with the Warriors. The first player to ever earn $60M in a single season. Some legacies you just can't put a price on. 🍿 #Warriors #NBA #StephCurry
