### STORY 3: Cavaliers 127, Mavericks 117

**FB Long-Form (4:00 PM ET)**
Tyrese Proctor wasn't even supposed to be in this role. And yet, there he was Saturday night — 24 points, 11 rebounds, 10 assists — leading a battered, shorthanded Cleveland Cavaliers team past the Dallas Mavericks 127-117 in one of the more impressive individual performances of November.

The Cavs were missing Donovan Mitchell, James Harden, Jarrett Allen, Peyton Watson, Evan Mobley, Sam Merrill, and Nae'Qwan Tomlin. Seven rotation players. Against Dallas — a team that entered with Kyrie Irving and Cooper Flagg — Cleveland should have had no business winning.
