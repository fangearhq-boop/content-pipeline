DeMar DeRozan Signs With the Nuggets — What He Brings to Denver and Why This Deal Is Better Than It Looks

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | September 20, 2026

Denver Nuggets

DeMar DeRozan is going to Denver. The six-time NBA All-Star has agreed to a one-year, $3.9 million deal with the Nuggets, a veteran minimum contract that significantly undervalues what he averaged last season: 18.4 points on nearly 50 percent shooting with the Sacramento Kings before they waived him in the offseason.

The Nuggets are the team that brought him in. Nikola Jokic, Jamal Murray, and Aaron Gordon all played a direct role in the recruitment. DeRozan spoke to the Miami Heat, Washington Wizards, and New Orleans Pelicans, and chose Denver — which tells you exactly what kind of contract this is. This is not a DeRozan-takes-the-most-money deal. This is a DeRozan-wants-to-compete-for-a-ring deal.

What Denver Was Missing

The Nuggets sent Peyton Watson to Cleveland earlier this offseason as part of a multi-team trade. Watson was young, versatile, and improving — a wing with defensive upside and a developing offensive game. His departure created a hole in Denver's rotation that needed a different kind of fill: not raw upside, but proven production from a player who can create his own shot, finish in the mid-range, and function without the ball when Jokic is operating as the offense's engine.

DeRozan at 37 is not the version who was making first-team All-NBA selections in his prime. But he is still a legitimate 18-point scorer on quality shooting efficiency, and his skill set ages well. The mid-range game — the most sustainable offensive skill because it requires footwork, timing, and touch rather than athleticism alone — does not disappear at 37 the way explosion or first-step quickness does. DeRozan's signature floaters, post-ups, and pull-jumpers off the dribble remain some of the most reliable scoring actions in the league.

The Jokic Factor

Playing alongside Nikola Jokic has historically made every offensive player around him better. Jokic's passing — generationally creative, algorithmically precise in identifying the right look — creates clean catch-and-shoot opportunities, backdoor cuts, and second-chance positions that turn marginal offensive contributors into functional scorers. DeRozan is not a marginal offensive contributor. He is an elite one walking into a system built for his type of game.

Jokic's work at the elbow creates the mid-range windows that DeRozan has spent his entire career exploiting. DeRozan coming off a Jokic high-low or using a screen from Aaron Gordon while Jokic holds the defense's attention is an action opposing scouts will genuinely have to prepare for. At $3.9 million, DeRozan gives Denver a line item that is almost certainly worth three to four times its salary in on-court value.

The Championship Calculation

DeRozan is 37. The window for a championship run is not open indefinitely. He has been one of the most consistent scorers of his generation and has never won a title. The Miami Heat, Wizards, and Pelicans all offered different roles and likely more money in some cases. He chose Denver — a team that spent the last several seasons building toward a genuine title window with Jokic at the center of everything.

The calculation is transparent: DeRozan assessed the field, identified the team with the best shot at a championship, and accepted the pay cut. That is not an unusual decision for veterans approaching the end of their careers. What makes it noteworthy is that DeRozan is not a fringe contributor taking a minimum deal to find a role. He is a genuine contributor taking a minimum deal to find a ring.

What's Next

Denver's training camp opens September 29. The Nuggets' preseason schedule begins October 3. The regular season tips off October 20 with the Knicks' banner ceremony in New York. DeRozan joins a Denver roster featuring Jokic, Murray, Gordon, and a cast of role players built around Jokic's vision and playmaking. For a player entering his 18th NBA season, this is exactly the kind of situation you take a discount to reach.
