Tatum Scores 32, Celtics Win Fifth Straight with 108-97 Defeat of Magic

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | November 2, 2026

The Boston Celtics are making defending a championship look routine. Jayson Tatum scored 32 points to power the Celtics to a 108-97 road victory over the Orlando Magic on Sunday afternoon, Boston's fifth consecutive win. The defending champions have now settled into a comfortable early-season rhythm, and Tatum's consistent scoring is the engine making it run.

Tatum Sets the Tone

Tatum was efficient throughout, using both the mid-range and the three-point line to keep Orlando's defense off-balance. The Celtics' offensive scheme creates constant movement and mismatches, and on Sunday, Tatum found himself with clean looks early and often. His 32-point effort was a continuation of a strong November start that has the Celtics looking very much like a team capable of repeating as champions.

The Magic, playing at home, tried to keep pace but the Celtics' defensive integrity proved too much. Boston held Orlando to 97 points, a total that reflects the consistency of their defensive scheme under head coach Joe Mazzulla. The Celtics don't give up easy baskets, they contest everything, and they rotate quickly — hallmarks of a team that has embraced the discipline required to win back-to-back titles.

Five Games, Five Wins

The current winning streak has come against a varied slate of opponents, which makes it more meaningful. Championship teams don't pick their spots — they show up every night and find a way. Boston has done exactly that over the past five games, and Sunday's win in Orlando was perhaps the least flashy of the bunch, which is a compliment. Not every game needs to be a statement. Sometimes winning 108-97 on a Sunday afternoon is exactly what it is: business.

What's Next

The Celtics get a day of rest before returning to action with a home game. With their winning streak intact and Tatum playing at a high level, Boston is positioned to maintain its spot among the Eastern Conference's best teams heading deeper into November. The Magic will look to regroup, but on this particular afternoon, the defending champions were simply better.
