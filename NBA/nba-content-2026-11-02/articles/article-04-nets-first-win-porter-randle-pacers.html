Brooklyn Finally Breaks Through: Porter, Randle Power Nets' First Win Over Pacers 98-90

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | November 2, 2026

The Brooklyn Nets were tired of seeing the loss column grow. On Sunday, they finally put a check in the win column, defeating the Indiana Pacers 98-90 for their first victory of the 2026-27 season. Michael Porter Jr. led the way with 23 points and nine rebounds, while Julius Randle provided steady, professional production with 20 points, six assists, and five rebounds.

Porter and Randle Deliver

The Brooklyn offense ran through Porter and Randle on Sunday, and both delivered exactly what this franchise needed. Porter Jr. was physical in the paint, attacked the glass relentlessly, and knocked down the mid-range shots that had been misfiring earlier in the season. Randle, the veteran presence Brooklyn brought in to provide leadership and secondary scoring, was equally consistent, finishing the game with a balanced stat line that reflected his value as a multi-faceted offensive weapon.

Together, they combined for 43 points and 14 rebounds — nearly half the team's total output. That kind of production from two guys asked to be the veteran core of a transitional roster is exactly what the Nets' front office was hoping for when they assembled this team.

Relief for Brooklyn

The Nets have been one of the NBA's most scrutinized franchises during this rebuilding era, and a winless start to the season had amplified that scrutiny. Sunday's victory won't silence the critics permanently, but it provides a foothold. The Pacers, playing at home in Indianapolis, are not an easy out — Indiana has the pieces to compete in the Eastern Conference — and Brooklyn beating them on the road is a legitimate achievement.

The key for Brooklyn going forward is finding consistency in their defensive effort, which can be erratic. On Sunday, holding the Pacers to 90 points was a positive sign. If the Nets can defend like that more consistently while Porter and Randle keep scoring, this team can carve out some wins in a competitive East.

What's Next

The Nets return home to Barclays Center for their next contest. Sunday's win is a start, but only that — a start. In a conference with Boston, Cleveland, Philadelphia, and Miami all playing at a high level, Brooklyn has a lot of ground to make up. First win in the books. Now keep it going.
