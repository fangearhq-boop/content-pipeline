NBA Monday Preview: Suns at Warriors (10 PM ET), Cavaliers at Thunder

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | November 2, 2026

Monday brings two compelling NBA matchups to round out the week's early action. The Phoenix Suns travel to Chase Center to face the Golden State Warriors at 10:00 PM ET, while the Cleveland Cavaliers visit the Oklahoma City Thunder in an Eastern-versus-Western powerhouse showdown. Both games carry NBA Cup implications as group play continues through November.

Suns at Warriors — 10:00 PM ET

All eyes in the Bay Area will be on Yaxel Lendeborg after his stunning 37-point performance against the Clippers on Sunday night. The rookie lit up San Francisco with an efficient 13-of-19 shooting display in Stephen Curry's absence, and the question heading into Monday is whether Curry returns to action or gives Lendeborg another extended opportunity.

The Suns present a different challenge than the Clippers. Phoenix has been working to establish its identity early in the season and will push Golden State's defense in ways Sunday's opponent could not. If Curry is back in the lineup, this becomes a showcase game for the league's most accomplished active shooter against a Suns team looking to make a statement of their own on the road.

For Warriors fans, the storyline is simple: can Lendeborg follow up a career night, or will Curry reclaim the spotlight? Either answer makes for must-watch late-night basketball.

Cavaliers at Thunder

The Cleveland Cavaliers have been one of the Eastern Conference's most formidable early-season teams, and they travel to Oklahoma City to face a Thunder squad that continues to impress despite the ongoing absence of Jalen Williams, who is out long-term with a serious lower-leg injury. OKC's depth and Shai Gilgeous-Alexander's brilliance have kept them competitive, but Cleveland's combination of Donovan Mitchell's scoring and their defensive cohesion makes this a genuine challenge for the home team.

This is the kind of November game that foreshadows playoff matchups. Both organizations are building with winning as the explicit short-term and long-term goal. Monday's result won't define either team's season, but it will tell us something about where they stand among the NBA's elite.

What's Next

Both Monday night games tip off in the evening, with Suns-Warriors the late feature at 10:00 PM ET. Check back with Hoop Heroes for full recaps of both games as soon as they conclude. After Sunday's historic performances, Monday night has big shoes to fill — but that's the beauty of an NBA season in full swing.
