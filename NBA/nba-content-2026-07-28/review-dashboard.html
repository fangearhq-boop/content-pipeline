### Long-Form Post Ja Morant is heading to Portland — and the Trail Blazers are betting on a talent that has spent years being buried by injuries and poor decisions. Memphis traded the 26-year-old point guard to Portland in exchange for Jerami Grant, Kris Murray, and $1 million in cash. No draft picks changed hands. From the Grizzlies' perspective, it's a sensible transaction: they shed a player who has appeared in just 79 games over the past three seasons — a stretch defined by suspensions and time lost — and receive two useful rotation players without surrendering future assets. They have Deni Avdija, who emerged as one of the game's most exciting young wings last season. Now they add Morant, who — when healthy and focused — is one of the most electrifying guards in the NBA. New Portland head coach Micah Nori, formerly an assistant with the Minnesota Timberwolves, will be tasked with harnessing all of that talent and turning it into something coherent. This is a team worth watching in 2026-27. For Morant, the question is simpler than any roster analysis: Can he stay on the floor and rediscover the player who once looked like the face of a generation? Portland is about to find out. ### Image Post Caption Fresh start. New city. Ja Morant lands in Portland alongside Damian Lillard and Deni Avdija. Kawhi Leonard Investigation — NBA Reviewing Findings, Ruling Imminent
NOT VERIFIEDFACEBOOK
### Long-Form Post The Kawhi Leonard investigation is approaching its moment of resolution — but in the NBA, "imminent" comes with fine print. The independent law firm hired by the league has completed its fact-finding phase. The NBA is now internally reviewing those findings before determining whether Leonard, the Los Angeles Clippers, or owner Steve Ballmer face any discipline. Commissioner Adam Silver has been clear: this probe must conclude before the 2026-27 season begins. At the center of the investigation is a $28 million endorsement deal between Leonard and Aspiration Fund Adviser LLC — the financial services firm that has since filed for bankruptcy. The question is whether that deal, and potentially additional personal expense arrangements, violated NBA cap rules. If the league finds wrongdoing, potential penalties include voiding the contract or a fine of up to $350,000. But any proposed punishment must go through an assistant arbitrator under the collective bargaining agreement, and the NBPA retains the right to appeal. That process is why "imminent" doesn't mean "this week." There are steps. What is certain: the pending trade that would send Kawhi Leonard to the Toronto Raptors — in exchange for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, and draft picks — remains completely frozen until the investigation concludes. Toronto cannot build its roster. The Clippers cannot complete their rebuild. The clock is ticking toward Silver's deadline. ### Image Post Caption The NBA's outside law firm has finished its fact-finding. The league is reviewing. The Kawhi Leonard trade to Toronto is still frozen — and the whole league is waiting.
