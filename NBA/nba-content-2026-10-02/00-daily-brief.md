## Today's Top Stories

### STORY 1: Jalen Duren Signs $200M Extension With Pistons — Holdout Over
**Tier:** 1
**Category:** Detroit Pistons / Jalen Duren / Contract Extension
**Summary:** After missing media day and the start of training camp, Jalen Duren has agreed to a 5-year, $200 million fully guaranteed extension with the Detroit Pistons. The 22-year-old center was coming off a career year averaging 19.5 pts, 10.5 reb while shooting 65% from the floor, earning All-Star and All-NBA Third Team honors. The deal ends a prolonged standoff over what Duren felt was undervaluation by the team. His initial ask was reportedly close to the five-year max of $287 million ($57.4M AAV); the agreed $200M deal averages $40M per year.
**Angle:** A major rebuild cornerstone gets his money. Detroit can move forward.
