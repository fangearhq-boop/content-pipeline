### STORY 1: Jalen Duren Signs $200M Extension With Pistons

**FB Long-Form Post**
Window: 9:00 AM ET

The holdout is officially over. Jalen Duren and the Detroit Pistons have agreed on a 5-year, $200 million fully guaranteed extension — and Detroit's franchise center is staying put.

Duren missed media day and the opening of training camp as the two sides were stuck in a standoff. He earned All-Star and All-NBA Third Team honors last season after averaging 19.5 points, 10.5 rebounds, and shooting an extraordinary 65% from the floor. He was eligible for a max deal worth up to $287 million — which means Duren left $87 million on the table to take the security of full guarantees.

At just 22 years old, Duren is the cornerstone of everything the Pistons are building. The city of Detroit can exhale.

### STORY 2: Joel Embiid Full Participant at Sixers Camp

**FB Long-Form Post**
Window: 11:00 AM ET

The Philadelphia 76ers have great news as training camp rolls on: Joel Embiid is a full participant, and according to coach Nick Nurse, he "looked great."

Embiid has reportedly dropped a considerable amount of weight heading into what may be the most loaded Sixers roster in recent memory. LeBron James (41) and Jaylen Brown join Embiid in a frontcourt and backcourt combination that could genuinely threaten in the East — if Embiid stays healthy.

He acknowledged it'll be day-by-day and not expecting a full 82, but for Sixers fans who have watched their franchise center battle injuries for years, hearing "full participant" on Day 1 is a massive deal.

Can the Sixers finally make a championship run with LeBron in tow?
