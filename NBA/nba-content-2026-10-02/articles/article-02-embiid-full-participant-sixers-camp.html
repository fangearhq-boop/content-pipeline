Joel Embiid Is a Full Participant at Sixers Training Camp — And That Changes Everything

By Marcus Cole | October 2, 2026

After years of cautious returns and injury management, the former MVP is going all-in from Day 1 of camp — dropping weight, impressing coaches, and arriving healthy as the LeBron era begins in Philadelphia.

It's three words Philadelphia 76ers fans haven't heard in years: full participant, Day 1. Joel Embiid took every rep in training camp and left coaches impressed heading into what may be the most consequential Sixers season since Allen Iverson's peak.

Coach Nick Nurse called Embiid's participation "very, very good news" and said the former MVP "looked great." NBA insider Chris Haynes reported that Embiid has dropped a considerable amount of weight heading into camp — a physical transformation that has the Sixers genuinely excited.

What "Full Participant" Actually Means for Embiid

To understand why this matters, you need context. For the better part of three seasons, Embiid's training camp availability has been managed, limited, or interrupted. Load management, knee procedures, and structural concerns have followed him. A "full participant" designation at the start of October — for all practices — is not typical for a player with his history.

Embiid himself spoke carefully at media day: "I feel pretty good. We've got a plan in place, trying to check all the boxes, just taking it day-by-day. Everything is on schedule." He was honest about the unpredictability of his body, but the tone was optimistic in a way it hasn't always been.

The LeBron Factor

The Sixers brought in LeBron James, now 41, and Jaylen Brown this offseason, creating a roster that — on paper — is capable of reaching the Finals. That potential means nothing if Embiid cannot stay on the floor. His health is the single largest variable in Philadelphia's championship equation.

LeBron, coming off what scouts described as a still-elite summer of play, is reportedly working well with Embiid in early practices. The Sixers are not expected to play Embiid for a full 82 games, but the goal is to have him at full health when the playoffs arrive.

The Road to October 20

Philadelphia opens the regular season on October 20 at Madison Square Garden — Banner Night for the Knicks. There will be no gentler welcome to the season. The Sixers will walk into the most hostile arena in the sport for a nationally televised game with a city's 53-year celebration as the backdrop.

If Embiid is healthy and performing in that game, the message will be clear. The 76ers mean business this year.

What's Next

Philadelphia plays two preseason games before Oct. 20. Watch how Embiid's minutes are managed in those contests — it will tell you everything about the Sixers' confidence level in his readiness. If he's on the floor in the fourth quarters of preseason, something has genuinely changed. The LeBron era can only work if the big man at the center of it all stays upright.
