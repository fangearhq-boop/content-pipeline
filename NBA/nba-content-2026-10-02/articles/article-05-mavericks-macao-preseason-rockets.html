Mavericks Head to Macao for Preseason — Roster Set With Cisse Guaranteed

By Marcus Cole | October 2, 2026

Dallas finalizes their 15-man travel roster with Moussa Cisse's guarantee and flies to Macao for two preseason games against Houston — a rare international NBA stage ahead of the regular season.

The Dallas Mavericks are taking their 2026-27 preseason preparations to Asia, flying to Macao, China for two games against the Houston Rockets. To finalize their 15 standard contracts required for the international trip, Dallas guaranteed Moussa Cisse's $2.2 million salary for 2026-27 — the final roster move before departure.

Why Macao?

The NBA's international preseason games serve dual purposes: global brand expansion and early competitive reps for rosters still finding their chemistry. Macao, a special administrative region of China with a strong NBA fanbase, provides a ready-made audience for teams competing in Asia.

For the Mavericks, the trip also comes at a critical roster-building moment. Two preseason games against Western Conference rival Houston provide early read on matchups and lineup combinations before the regular season begins.

The Moussa Cisse Decision

Cisse, a center/power forward, saw his $2.2 million salary for 2026-27 guaranteed as part of the roster finalization. For a player on the fringe of a roster, a salary guarantee is significant — it signals the organization sees him as part of the plan, at least for the immediate preseason evaluation period.

Cisse's role will be defined by how he performs against Houston. Frontcourt depth behind the starters can be a difference-maker in a long season, and the Macao games give coaches a rare extended look in game conditions.

Dallas Entering the Season With Momentum

The Mavericks have been building toward contention through their recent playoff runs and roster development. International preseason trips are typically reserved for franchises the NBA wants to showcase globally, a nod to Dallas's rising profile.

The matchup with Houston also previews what could be a compelling division rivalry entering the season — both teams are young, athletic, and hungry for a deeper postseason run.

What's Next

The Mavericks return from Macao before their regular season opener. Watch how the team's depth players perform in these international games — for players like Cisse, preseason appearances carry extra weight when roster spots are still being decided. A strong performance in Macao can lock in a role before the season begins.
