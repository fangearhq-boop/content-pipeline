Warriors Take 21-Man Roster to Hawaii Training Camp — Preseason Starts Oct. 4

By Damon Pierce | October 2, 2026

Golden State heads to BYU-Hawaii with a revamped 21-man camp roster featuring Kristaps Porzingis and Jimmy Butler III alongside Curry and Draymond, opening the preseason Saturday against the Clippers.

The Golden State Warriors are taking their 2026 training camp to paradise — and hoping to bring back a championship mentality along with the good weather. The team has traveled to BYU-Hawaii with a 21-man training camp roster, opening the preseason against the Los Angeles Clippers on October 4 before a season that begins October 21.

The Roster: What's New, What Stays

The foundation remains Stephen Curry and Draymond Green — the Warriors' two pillars who have been at the center of four championship runs. Around them, Golden State has added veteran depth: Kristaps Porzingis brings size and floor-spacing at center; Jimmy Butler III (the son of Jimmy Butler) and Al Horford provide experienced contributors.

The returning core includes Brandin Podziemski, Moses Moody, Will Richard, and Gary Payton II. Golden State also carries two-way players LJ Cryer, Malevy Leons, and Graham Ike, as well as Exhibit 10 players Dalen Terry, Alex Toohey, and Miles Kelly filling out the camp roster.

The Hawaii Advantage

Hawaii training camps have become something of an NBA tradition — teams use the remoteness and controlled environment to build chemistry without distractions. For a Warriors team with new faces, bonding time away from the media noise of San Francisco can pay dividends once October tips off in earnest.

Curry's leadership off the court has always been noted by teammates, and the Hawaii setting gives him the stage to set cultural expectations early.

What to Watch Saturday

The Oct. 4 preseason opener against the Clippers in Hawaii will be the first look at how Golden State's new pieces fit. Watch the Porzingis-Curry two-man game, and how much floor spacing the new wings create for Draymond's playmaking. The Warriors' ceiling depends on whether their offensive scheme adapts to new personnel.

What's Next

After the preseason, Golden State returns home for their regular season opener on October 21. If they emerge from camp with cohesion and good health, they enter the year as a legitimate Western Conference contender. The Warriors' dynasty chapters have always been written in October, and this one starts in the Pacific.
