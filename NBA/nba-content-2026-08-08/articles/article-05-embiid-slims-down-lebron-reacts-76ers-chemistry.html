Joel Embiid Posted a Slim-Down Photo. LeBron Reacted. The 76ers' Chemistry Is Very Real.

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | August 8, 2026

NBA Offseason / Philadelphia 76ers / Joel Embiid / LeBron James

Before the question was whether the Philadelphia 76ers could win a championship — it was whether Joel Embiid would be healthy enough for the season to matter. For weeks, that question has been answered in words: the coaching staff confirmed his health, Embiid publicly declared himself ready for Day 1, and training camp timelines proceeded normally.

This week, the answer came in a photo.

Embiid posted an offseason picture showing a noticeably slimmer build. LeBron James saw it and reacted publicly. That moment — brief, digital, completely of the social media era — landed differently than a press conference statement or a coaching staff confirmation. It landed like truth.

Why Embiid's Fitness Changes Everything

The history here matters. Joel Embiid is one of the most talented basketball players the sport has ever produced. In his best seasons — fully healthy, dominant, engaged — he is arguably the most difficult offensive player in the league to stop. He is a seven-footer who can shoot from the perimeter, post up anyone, and draw fouls at a rate that distorts entire defensive game plans.

He has also missed, on average, more than 20 games per season across his NBA career. Knee problems. Knee surgeries. A face fracture. COVID-19. Orbital fractures. The 76ers' championship window has been perpetually linked to a body that cannot always be counted upon.

When LeBron James chose Philadelphia in free agency — calling it his "last decision" as a player — he was choosing to bet that Embiid's window of full health would align with his own. A noticeably slimmer Embiid heading into camp is the most encouraging physical evidence yet that those windows might overlap.

The Culture Around This Team

Consider what the 76ers have built before a single regular-season game:

Tyrese Maxey has been in the gym at 6 AM. LeBron James showed up and responded to being beaten to the workout by publicly endorsing Maxey as the franchise's face — "Rese for President." Now Embiid is visibly leaner and LeBron is reacting online.

These moments are not random. They reflect a team culture that is forming in real time, shaped by the kind of veteran leadership that only a player with LeBron's career can provide. He has won championships in Cleveland, Miami, Cleveland again, and Los Angeles. He knows what a team that is serious looks like — and he knows how to build it.

The projected starting five entering training camp: LeBron James, Joel Embiid, Tyrese Maxey, Jaylen Brown, and VJ Edgecombe. Those five players hold five combined NBA MVP awards. They are expected to be ready for the season opener on October 20.

The One Question That Remains

A photo is encouraging. A summer's worth of good news is encouraging. What the Philadelphia 76ers will ultimately be measured by is whether Embiid stays on the court when the games count. That is the variable no offseason content can resolve — only the regular season itself will answer it.

But in August, the answer looks better than it has in years. LeBron's arrival gave this franchise a reason to believe. Embiid's photo gave the broader basketball world a reason to look twice.

What's Next

The full 2026-27 schedule drops Thursday, August 13 — that's when the 76ers will find out their opening night opponent and the full slate of their season. Training camp opens in late September. The preseason tips off in early October, with the regular season starting October 20. Between now and then, every piece of news out of Philadelphia will be watched closely — because this is a team that, if healthy, has the talent to win a championship.
