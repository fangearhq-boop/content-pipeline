Stephen Curry's Extension Window Opens August 29 — and the Warriors Are Ready

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | August 8, 2026

NBA Contracts / Golden State Warriors / Stephen Curry

In 21 days, Stephen Curry becomes eligible to sign a two-year, $136.7 million contract extension with the Golden State Warriors. Every available signal points to the same outcome: he is going to sign it.

Multiple reports have confirmed mutual interest between Curry and the Warriors. If Curry wants the full $136.7 million — carrying him through the 2028-29 season, his age-40 year — Golden State is prepared to offer it. A September signing is widely expected. Curry has made clear he has no interest in requesting a trade, and plans to be on the roster for the full 2026-27 season regardless of how the extension talks proceed.

For a franchise that has spent the last six months watching its offseason plans fall apart in various ways, this is a rare piece of straightforward good news.

What the Extension Means

Stephen Curry is the most important player in Golden State Warriors franchise history. That is not a hyperbolic statement — it is the only defensible position. The Warriors won four championships in an eight-year span built entirely around his generational shooting and leadership. They did not make the playoffs last season without another superstar alongside him, finishing 37-45.

Signing Curry through his age-40 season is not about building for a championship immediately. It is about keeping the franchise's identity intact while the roster around him evolves. The Warriors have Draymond Green back on a one-year, $27.7 million deal. Gary Payton II returns for $3.9 million. The supporting cast is functional. The question is whether Golden State can add a piece around Curry significant enough to return to the postseason in 2026-27.

What the extension does not answer is the roster construction question. The Warriors still need to surround Curry with enough firepower to compete in a Western Conference that includes Wembanyama's Spurs, Morant's Trail Blazers, and the full field of playoff contenders. The extension gives them the foundation. The next moves determine the ceiling.

The Financial Picture

The extension structure is 2 years at $136.7 million — roughly $68 million per season, carrying Curry through age 40. Without the extension, Curry would become an unrestricted free agent for the first time in his career next summer. That scenario is not one the Warriors or Curry appear to be seriously entertaining.

ESPN's Brian Windhorst has floated the possibility that Curry could take a slight discount — leaving some money on the table to give Golden State more cap flexibility. Whether that happens depends entirely on Curry's preferences. The standard max appears to be the expected outcome, but the final number is not locked until the ink is on paper in September.

At 38 years old, Curry averaged 20.9 points per game last season in a reduced role and shot 42 percent from three-point range. He is not declining into irrelevance — he is still one of the three or four most dangerous offensive players in the sport.

What's Next

The extension window officially opens August 29. Expect a signing to be announced sometime in September, once the formal paperwork clears and both sides finalize the structure. Once the deal is done, attention shifts to what Golden State adds around Curry before training camp opens in late September. The Warriors' offseason hinges on this signing being the foundation, not the ceiling, of their 2026-27 plan.
