The NBA Schedule Drops in Five Days — Here's Everything We're Waiting to Find Out

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | August 8, 2026

NBA Season Preview / Schedule Release

Thursday, August 13. That's the date. The full 2026-27 NBA schedule — all 82 games for all 30 teams, 1,230 matchups in total — is expected to drop that day, five days from now. The NBA Cup slate comes first: Tuesday, August 11.

It is not an overstatement to call this the most anticipated schedule release in a decade. The offseason reshuffled the Eastern Conference so thoroughly — LeBron James in Philadelphia, Giannis Antetokounmpo in Miami, Ja Morant in Portland — that every team's slate of marquee matchups looks completely different than it did a year ago.

Here is what fans have been waiting to find out since free agency concluded.

The Knicks' Banner Night

October 20. Madison Square Garden. The New York Knicks will raise their 2025-26 championship banner in front of a home crowd for the first time. The question that has fueled speculation all summer: who walks into MSG to crash that party?

Banner nights tend to produce the highest-rated regular-season games of the year. The opponent matters. Whoever the league assigns to that Tuesday night slot is guaranteed a national television audience and a hostile crowd. Every team's front office has its own theory about who gets the honor.

The 76ers — now featuring LeBron James — would be the most obvious draw. So would the Boston Celtics, who were swept by the Knicks in last season's Eastern Conference Finals. The Miami Heat, freshly armed with Giannis, is another candidate. The league will maximize the moment.

LeBron's Philadelphia Debut

In 21 previous NBA seasons, LeBron James has played for the Cleveland Cavaliers, Miami Heat, Cleveland Cavaliers again, and the Los Angeles Lakers. On Thursday, the schedule will reveal when he plays his first game as a Philadelphia 76er.

That game — whenever it lands — is one of the most significant single-game debut moments in recent NBA history. Philadelphia has not had a player of LeBron's stature and public profile carry the franchise in this way. The city will be ready.

There is also the question of his road opener: when does LeBron visit a rival arena for the first time in 76ers colors? Those games — especially in New York, Boston, and Miami — will be circled in every rival city.

Christmas Day and National TV

LeBron James has appeared in 16 consecutive Christmas Day games. The league will not break that streak voluntarily. The question is which team the 76ers — with a starting five holding five combined NBA MVP awards — face on December 25.

Christmas Day traditionally features five games, each broadcast nationally. The scheduling committee must balance star power across all five slots. With LeBron in Philadelphia, Giannis in Miami, Wembanyama in San Antonio, and the Knicks as defending champions, this is the most star-loaded Christmas since 2016.

Wembanyama vs. Embiid — The Marquee Big Man Matchups

Victor Wembanyama and Joel Embiid are two of the most compelling big men in NBA history on the same 82-game stage. The dates they share a court are events. Wembanyama has won Defensive Player of the Year in back-to-back seasons. Embiid, when healthy, remains one of the most unguardable offensive players in the league.

The schedule will tell us how often — and when — those matchups occur. San Antonio and Philadelphia play in different conferences, which limits the regular-season games. That scarcity makes each one more valuable.

The Emirates NBA Cup Format

Before the full schedule drops Thursday, the NBA Cup slate arrives Tuesday. The fourth edition of the Emirates NBA Cup runs Group Play every Friday from October 30 through November 27, plus additional Cup Nights on November 24-25. The quarterfinals are held in local markets December 4-5, and the championship game is set for Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis on December 11.

The tournament format divides all 30 teams into six groups of five — three per conference — with group play counting toward regular-season records. The Group Play schedule is what fans get Tuesday: which teams fall into which groups, and what the Friday game slate looks like for the first month of the season.

What's Next

The NBA Cup schedule drops Tuesday, August 11. The full 2026-27 regular-season schedule follows Thursday, August 13. The 81st NBA season tips off October 20 — now 73 days away. Once the schedule lands, the conversation shifts from the offseason that was to the season that is coming. Five days.
