### Long-Form Post ⚖️ The Kawhi Leonard saga has entered a new phase — and it's not going away anytime soon. The NBA's investigation into the Los Angeles Clippers for alleged salary cap circumvention is now 11 months old. The probe centers on a secret endorsement deal between Leonard and Daktronics, a scoreboard company, which the Clippers allegedly facilitated to get around his salary cap number. Here's where things stand as of today: **The trade:** Agreed to on approximately June 30. Kawhi Leonard goes to Toronto in exchange for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, and two unprotected first-round picks (2031 and 2033). The deal is currently frozen — the NBA told the Raptors they'd assume the risk of any investigation outcome if they choose to proceed before the probe concludes. **The threat:** The NBPA has stated it "wouldn't hesitate" to demand that the matter go to a neutral arbitrator if the league pursues penalties without, in the union's view, sufficient evidence. An arbitration ruling would then be subject to a binding three-person appeals panel jointly appointed by the NBA and NBPA. That process doesn't have a hard deadline — which means this could realistically drag into 2027. **The most likely outcome:** Multiple insiders — including reporters at ESPN and CBS Sports — say the most likely resolution remains a negotiated settlement between the NBA and the Clippers. That path would allow the trade to finally proceed, with both the Clippers and Raptors publicly maintaining that they intend to complete the deal. So where does that leave Kawhi? He is owed $50.3 million in the 2026-27 season. He is effectively a Clipper with nowhere to play until this is resolved. Training camp opens in approximately seven weeks. The clock is ticking — very slowly. 💬 How does this end? Does the trade eventually go through, or does something break down entirely? Share your prediction. ---

### Long-Form Post 🏆 Mark your calendars: August 29. That's when Stephen Curry officially becomes eligible to sign a two-year, $136.7 million contract extension with the Golden State Warriors. Let's be clear about what this extension means: **For the Warriors:** It keeps the greatest shooter in NBA history — who also happens to be the most important player in franchise history — signed through at least age 40. Locking him in, even at $68M per year, is straightforward. **For Curry:** He is choosing to finish his career in Golden State. He has no interest in requesting a trade or testing free agency. He is not taking that risk — and the Warriors are not letting him walk. **The fine print:** Some have floated the idea that Curry might take a slight discount to give Golden State more cap flexibility to build around him. ESPN's Brian Windhorst has explored alternative extension structures. The standard max appears most likely, but the final number isn't locked until signing day. Golden State finished 37-45 last season. Draymond Green is back on a one-year, $27.7M deal. Gary Payton II re-signed for $3.9M. The roster construction around Curry for 2026-27 is still developing — but the foundation is clear. Stephen Curry. Warriors. At least through age 40. 💬 Do you think Curry takes the full max, or does he leave something on the table to help the Warriors build a contender? Sound off below. ---

### Long-Form Post 🇫🇷 The San Antonio Spurs just sent their entire basketball operation to Paris. Not just teammates — coaches and staff made the trip to join Victor Wembanyama for his summer camp in France. It is an unusual and significant organizational commitment, the kind that tells you exactly how the Spurs view this offseason: as a chance to build something real before training camp begins. Wembanyama is getting ready to return to the French national team for the first time since the 2024 Paris Olympics, where France pushed the United States to the final moments of the gold medal game. Now he's preparing for the next chapter of international basketball under new head coach Frederic Fauthoux — who took over the French program after the Paris Games in September 2024. This will be the first time Fauthoux coaches Wembanyama with the senior national team. France's August schedule: **August 20:** Exhibition vs. Serbia in Belgrade **August 23:** Exhibition vs. Serbia in Orleans **August 27:** FIBA World Cup 2027 qualifier vs. Slovenia (home) **August 30:** FIBA World Cup 2027 qualifier at Sweden (away) Wembanyama's French roster includes Rudy Gobert and Evan Fournier — Fournier making his first appearance with France since the Paris Games. Wembanyama has been characteristically understated about the whole thing. His quote on returning to international basketball: "I do not miss the NBA right now. I miss FIBA basketball." He spent eight years developing as a youth player at a basketball court in Nanterre — a court that was recently renovated, and whose reopening he attended before the national team camp. He is, in every sense, home. The FIBA window is 12 days away. The NBA season is 73 days away. For Wembanyama, August is one long competition. 💬 Can France reach the 2027 FIBA World Cup finals with Wembanyama leading the way? What's your prediction? ---
