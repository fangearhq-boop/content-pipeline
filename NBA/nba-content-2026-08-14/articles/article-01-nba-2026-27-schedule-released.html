NBA 2026-27 Schedule Released: LeBron Returns to LA on Christmas Day, 76ers Open vs. Knicks

By Damon Pierce | August 14, 2026

The full 2026-27 NBA schedule dropped Thursday afternoon, revealing a Christmas Day blockbuster, a dramatic Opening Night, and enough marquee matchups to keep fans circling dates all season long.

The NBA unveiled its complete 2026-27 schedule Thursday, and the league wasted no time burying the needle on drama. The Philadelphia 76ers — led by LeBron James in his 24th season — will visit the defending champion New York Knicks on Opening Night, and on Christmas Day, LeBron makes his return to Los Angeles. If you were waiting for a reason to care about this season before a ball is tipped, the schedule just handed you several.

Opening Night: Oct. 20

The season tips off with a tripleheader. The Boston Celtics travel to Detroit to face the Pistons at 3 p.m. ET. Then, at 7 p.m. ET, LeBron James walks into Madison Square Garden for the first time as a Philadelphia 76er — just as the Knicks hoist their first championship banner in 53 years. The Oklahoma City Thunder round out the night at San Antonio at 9:30 p.m. ET.

The 76ers' Opening Night assignment is the most significant storyline on the schedule. New York won the 2026 title — ending a drought that stretched back to 1973 — and the league has rewarded that moment with the biggest possible opponent on banner night. Expect Madison Square Garden to be the loudest arena in the country on Oct. 20.

Christmas Day: Five Games, Maximum Drama

The five-game Christmas slate is where the schedule truly delivers. The day opens at noon ET with the New York Knicks hosting the San Antonio Spurs — a rematch of the 2026 NBA Finals. At 2:30 p.m., Giannis Antetokounmpo's Miami Heat visit the Boston Celtics, marking one of the headline reunion games of the season.

At 5 p.m. ET, LeBron James and the 76ers arrive in Los Angeles to face the Lakers. It will be LeBron's first game at Crypto.com Arena since signing with Philadelphia this past offseason — one of the most anticipated individual moments on the entire schedule. LeBron spent eight seasons with the Lakers, winning one championship there; his return figures to be a standing-ovation moment even in an opposing uniform.

The night continues with Shai Gilgeous-Alexander's Thunder at LaMelo Ball's Timberwolves at 8 p.m., and Nikola Jokic's Nuggets at Stephen Curry's Warriors at 10:30 p.m. ET.

The Return Games to Circle

Beyond the marquee matchups, the schedule is rich in reunion games. Jaylen Brown returns to Boston to face the Celtics on Jan. 21, his first game back after being traded to the 76ers this past offseason as part of the deal that sent Paul George and two first-round picks to Boston. Giannis Antetokounmpo returns to Milwaukee to face his former Bucks team on Jan. 28.

The Paris game — San Antonio Spurs vs. New Orleans Pelicans on Jan. 14 — and a Mexico City contest on Nov. 7 continue the NBA's international expansion calendar.

The 76ers' Brutal Start

Philadelphia's schedule architects did not go easy on the new-look 76ers. In their first 14 games, the Sixers will face the Knicks, Pistons, Pacers, and Cavaliers twice each — eight games against teams that were among the Eastern Conference's best in 2025-26. LeBron, Joel Embiid, Jaylen Brown, Tyrese Maxey, and Jared Edgecombe will be finding their chemistry against elite competition from the first week.

Broadcast Expansion

ABC and NBC will combine to broadcast 90 games during the 2026-27 campaign — 14% more national TV appearances than last season, according to Sports Business Journal. The 76ers and Knicks are tied for the most national TV games on the slate. The league is betting heavy on the new-look Eastern Conference storylines.

What's Next

With the schedule officially out, attention shifts to training camp. Teams with overseas preseason games open camp Sept. 22; all other teams follow on Sept. 29. The Kawhi Leonard trade situation and Stephen Curry's extension window (opening Aug. 29) are the last major pieces of business before rosters are truly set. Once those dominoes fall, the countdown to Oct. 20 begins in earnest. Circle it all — this season has no shortage of nights worth staying up for.
