Kawhi Leonard Investigation Expected to Wrap Within Six Weeks — Raptors Set Their Countdown Clock

By Marcus Cole | August 14, 2026

A resolution around Sept. 22 would give Toronto just enough time to clear the trade before training camp. Kawhi himself expects to be a Raptor — and the league may finally be ready to let it happen.

The NBA's year-long investigation into the Los Angeles Clippers may be nearing its conclusion, and the Toronto Raptors are now watching the calendar with a specific deadline in mind. According to HoopsHype, the probe could be wrapped up within the next six weeks — a timeline that, counting from the Aug. 10 report, puts a potential resolution around Sept. 22. Toronto's training camp in Quebec City is scheduled to begin Sept. 29. The math is tight. The finish line is real.

What Kawhi Expects

NBC Sports insider David Fischer reported that Kawhi Leonard himself is working under the belief that the investigation will conclude before training camp and that he will be joining the Raptors. That belief — coming from the player at the center of the dispute — is significant. Leonard has been in a professional purgatory since June 30, when the Raptors agreed in principle to acquire him from the Clippers, only to pause the deal on July 9 pending the NBA's investigation. For 11 months, the law firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz has been reviewing the Clippers' handling of Leonard's endorsement arrangements and cap structure.

What a Resolution Looks Like

Sources around the league believe the most likely outcome is sanctions against the Clippers — potentially significant financial penalties — combined with clearance for the Leonard-to-Toronto trade to be completed. The trade sends Leonard to the Raptors for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, and two unprotected first-round picks. For Toronto, it is a calculated bet on what a healthy Kawhi Leonard — now 35 — can do for a franchise that already knows what he looks like in the playoffs in a Raptors jersey. His 2019 championship run in Toronto remains one of the most celebrated in franchise history.

A Sept. 22 resolution would give both teams six days before Media Day and seven before camp starts. That is narrow — but league sources suggest that when a resolution comes, the paperwork can move quickly. Both franchises have been keeping their camp rosters and preparation plans flexible in anticipation.

The Deadline Toronto Cannot Miss

The Raptors' situation is defined by urgency. The team has structured its offseason around the assumption that Leonard will be available. Missing the training camp window would not just be a logistical inconvenience — it would deprive a veteran player of the foundational work that every team needs, especially one building a new identity with new pieces. Leonard himself has not played a full NBA season since 2020-21. Getting him into camp on time matters for his health trajectory as much as for team cohesion.

Toronto has waited 11 months. Six more weeks is manageable — provided the NBA holds to its own timeline. Commissioner Adam Silver has maintained publicly that the investigation must conclude before the season begins. The Sept. 22 target makes that commitment credible.

What's Next

Watch for an NBA announcement in the window between Aug. 25 and Sept. 22. If the league delivers sanctions and trade clearance on that schedule, the Raptors will have Kawhi in Quebec City for the first week of camp. If the timeline slips — a possibility given how many times the "imminent" resolution window has moved over the past year — Toronto faces difficult contingency planning. For now, all eyes are on the league to follow through. Kawhi is ready. Toronto is ready. The calendar is the only thing left standing in the way.
