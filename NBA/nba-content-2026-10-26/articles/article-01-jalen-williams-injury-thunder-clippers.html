Jalen Williams Carried Off Court in Thunder Win Over Clippers, Diagnosis Pending

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | October 26, 2026

Oklahoma City held on to beat Los Angeles 108-101, but the night ended on a troubling note as the All-Star guard left the arena unable to walk.

The Oklahoma City Thunder beat the Los Angeles Clippers 108-101 on Sunday afternoon, extending their undefeated start to the 2026-27 NBA season — but the postgame conversation had nothing to do with the win. Jalen Williams, one of the franchise's cornerstone players, was carried off the court late in the contest with what appeared to be a lower left leg injury, leaving the Thunder and their fans holding their collective breath.

Williams was unable to put weight on his leg as trainers and teammates helped him off the floor. He was eventually carried out of the arena entirely. Following the game, Oklahoma City head coach Mark Daigneault confirmed the team was awaiting a full diagnosis and would not speculate on the severity of the injury or his timeline for return.

SGA Keeps OKC Unbeaten

Before the injury overshadowed everything, Shai Gilgeous-Alexander delivered a vintage performance. The reigning All-Star poured in 34 points to carry Oklahoma City to the victory, doing his part to keep the Thunder perfect through the young season. SGA has been among the NBA's most dangerous players in recent years, and Sunday was another reminder of why.

OKC's defense, which has been a calling card under Daigneault, did its job once again against the Clippers — holding Los Angeles to 101 points and controlling key stretches of the second half. The Thunder were dominant enough that Williams' absence in the final minutes did not ultimately change the outcome, but the injury itself may loom far larger than any single result.

Williams' History and What It Means for OKC

Jalen Williams has dealt with lower-body issues before. During the 2025-26 playoffs, he battled a left hamstring strain that limited his effectiveness against the Suns in the first round. He was later reported to be playing through discomfort heading into the Western Conference Finals before new concerns emerged. His ability to stay healthy has been a persistent subplot in what is otherwise a Thunder team with championship aspirations.

Williams had been considered one of the two-way anchors around which Oklahoma City's offense and defense are built. Without him at full strength — or at all — the Thunder's ceiling changes considerably. The team has SGA as a legitimate go-to scorer, but Williams' creation, his defensive versatility, and his ability to operate in the pick-and-roll make him irreplaceable in the system.

Oklahoma City enters the week with the NBA Cup group stage just days away. The Cup format tips off Thursday, Oct. 30, and the Thunder will want their full roster for what figures to be a competitive East-West Cup bracket that carries genuine prize money and prestige. Whether Williams will be available for that stretch remains to be seen.

What's Next

The Thunder have not announced a timeline for Williams' return, but a full diagnosis is expected in the coming 24-48 hours. The team faces the Phoenix Suns at home Monday night, and Williams' status for that game is effectively in doubt. Oklahoma City's medical staff and front office will have a clearer picture soon, and the NBA world will be watching closely.

For now, OKC remains unbeaten and one of the league's best stories — but the health of Jalen Williams may well determine what kind of season this ultimately becomes.
