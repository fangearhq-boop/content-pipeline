NBA Monday Preview: 7-Game Slate, Cooper Flagg Questionable, NBA Cup Arrives Thursday

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | October 26, 2026

A loaded Monday slate features the Celtics hosting the Bulls, the Heat visiting Dallas (with Flagg's shoulder in question), and the Bucks hosting Indiana. The NBA Cup group stage opens in just four days.

Monday brings seven NBA games and a week that will ramp up fast as the NBA Cup group stage opens Thursday, Oct. 30. Before then, tonight's slate offers several compelling matchups — and the biggest storyline before tip-off is the injury status of Dallas Mavericks rookie Cooper Flagg, listed as questionable with a mild left shoulder sprain ahead of the Heat's visit to American Airlines Center.

Here is everything to watch on Monday, Oct. 26:

Celtics vs. Bulls — 7:30 PM ET, TD Garden

Boston opens its home schedule against a Chicago Bulls squad that will be looking to establish an early foothold in the Eastern Conference standings. The Celtics, perennial contenders with their championship core largely intact, are heavy favorites — but Chicago is not without teeth, and TD Garden will be loud for the home opener.

Boston and Chicago are set to meet again Friday, Oct. 30, in a NBA Cup group-stage game at TD Garden, making this Monday contest a potential preview of that higher-stakes rematch just four days later.

Heat at Mavericks — 7:30 PM ET, American Airlines Center

Cooper Flagg's availability is the day's must-watch storyline. The Dallas rookie — who was carried in the No. 1 overall pick in the 2026 NBA Draft by the Mavericks — is listed as questionable with a mild left shoulder sprain. Flagg has already generated enormous buzz in Dallas' early games, and Mavericks fans will be monitoring pregame warmup reports closely.

If Flagg plays, Dallas has a genuine home advantage. If he sits, the Heat — a team built on veteran toughness and defensive culture — will have an easier path to a road win.

Bucks vs. Pacers — 8:00 PM ET, Fiserv Forum

Indiana's Tyrese Haliburton and the Pacers come to Milwaukee off the back of a dominant performance, and Milwaukee will be eager to establish itself as a force in the East early in the season. Fiserv Forum's crowd will be electric for this one, and both teams have the firepower to produce a high-scoring contest.

This rivalry has produced memorable playoff moments in recent years and the early-season version carries genuine significance in the Eastern Conference pecking order.

The Rest of the Slate

Cleveland Cavaliers visit the Minnesota Timberwolves at 7:00 PM ET, a quality early-season test for the Wolves coming off their dominant 117-94 win in Toronto on Sunday. The Charlotte Hornets are in Washington against the Wizards (7:00 PM ET), the Phoenix Suns visit Oklahoma City (8:00 PM ET) — where the Jalen Williams injury update will be front and center — and the Atlanta Hawks close things out in Houston against the Rockets.

What's Next

With tonight's games in the books, attention will quickly turn to the NBA Cup, which begins its group stage Thursday, Oct. 30. The Cup format features all 30 teams divided into groups, with wins during the group phase earning points toward Cup qualification. Last year's Cup winner received a significant prize pool, and the competition adds a compelling subplot to the early months of the regular season. The NBA's holiday window is a long way off, but the Cup gives early-October basketball an extra layer of meaning — and this week, the stakes begin to rise.
