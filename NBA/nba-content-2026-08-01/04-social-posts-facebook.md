### STORY 3: DeMar DeRozan / Bulls

**Long-Form Post (11:00 AM ET):**
He's back where he belongs.

DeMar DeRozan has officially signed a two-year, $30 million deal to return to the Chicago Bulls — the team where he spent his most impactful recent seasons and won three All-Star selections.

Some players just feel right in certain jerseys. DeRozan in a Bulls uniform is one of them.

### STORY 5: Zach LaVine / Kings

Zach LaVine is staying in Sacramento — and betting on himself to get healthy.

Agent Rich Paul confirmed that LaVine has exercised his $49 million player option with the Sacramento Kings for the 2026-27 season. The 31-year-old guard averaged 19.2 points per game in 39 appearances last season before a hand injury ended his year prematurely.

The Kings clearly believe in LaVine's upside when healthy. With the team still searching for that consistent playoff presence in the Western Conference, having a scorer of LaVine's caliber at $49 million is a calculated bet — not a sure thing, but a reasonable one.

One question lingers: with DeRozan departing via waiver, and LaVine's injury history, can the Kings stay competitive enough in the West to justify the luxury of his contract?

We'll find out when October arrives.
