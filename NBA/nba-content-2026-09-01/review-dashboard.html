The trade — which would send Leonard back to Toronto in exchange for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, two unprotected first-round picks (2031, 2033), a 2027 pick swap, and additional draft assets — was agreed upon on June 30. ### Long-Form Post Steph Curry just made history — and the season hasn't even started yet. The Golden State Warriors legend signed a one-year, $62.6 million extension on August 29, the first day the extension window opened. At 38 years old and entering his 18th year as a Warrior, Curry is still setting records in every direction. But it is not just the money that has Warriors fans excited. Curry himself is genuinely fired up about what is coming. "There's a lot of health coming back," Curry said, referencing the returns of Moses Moody and Jimmy Butler — two players whose injuries derailed Golden State's 2025-26 campaign and kept the Warriors out of the playoffs entirely. With Moody and Butler healthy, plus Curry operating at what most analysts still call an elite level, the Warriors could be a far more dangerous team than their playoff-absent 2025-26 suggested. Golden State opens the season on October 21 against the Los Angeles Lakers at Chase Center — a marquee opener that will give fans their first look at this rebuilt roster. Curry has never played for another franchise. He has never wanted to.
