NBA Opening Night on October 20 Will Be Unlike Anything the League Has Done Before

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | September 1, 2026

October 20 is 49 days away. Write it down. Lock it in. Because what the NBA has assembled for Opening Night 2026-27 is — without exaggeration — something the league has never done before.

For the first time in NBA history, a single broadcast network will carry three Tip-Off games in a single night. NBC will air the entire tripleheader, with all three games also streaming on Peacock. Three games. One network. One unforgettable night.

The Full Schedule

Here is what is on the menu for October 20:

  • 3:00 PM ET — Boston Celtics at Detroit Pistons: The reigning Eastern Conference runner-up Celtics open on the road in Detroit. A clean, competitive opener to kick off the evening.
  • 7:00 PM ET — Philadelphia 76ers at New York Knicks (Madison Square Garden): The centerpiece. The Knicks raise their first championship banner since 1973 — then face LeBron James and Jaylen Brown making their Sixers debuts on the biggest stage in basketball.
  • 9:30 PM ET — Oklahoma City Thunder vs. San Antonio Spurs: A Western Conference Finals rematch. The Spurs won that series last spring on their way to their first NBA Finals since 2014, led by Victor Wembanyama.

The Main Event: New York's Night

The 7:00 PM game at Madison Square Garden is the unquestioned centerpiece of Opening Night. The Knicks won the 2025-26 NBA championship — their first title since 1973 — and they will raise that banner before tip-off in one of the most emotionally charged pre-game ceremonies the Garden has ever hosted.

Then the game begins. And waiting on the other side of the court are LeBron James and Jaylen Brown, two of the biggest stars in the league, in their first game together as Philadelphia 76ers. LeBron James at Madison Square Garden — in enemy colors — for the first time is a television event in its own right. Combine that with the banner ceremony, and the atmosphere at MSG will be unlike anything seen in a regular-season opener in years.

The 76ers enter the season with LeBron and Brown as their headliners, a combination assembled to finally push Philadelphia to title contention. The Knicks, defending champions, will be looking to establish early that their banner is just the beginning. The basketball should match the occasion.

The Nightcap: OKC vs. San Antonio

The 9:30 PM Western Conference Finals rematch between Oklahoma City and San Antonio closes the night. Wembanyama — France captain, two-time DPOY candidate, and the most dominant young player the league has seen in a generation — squares off against a Thunder team eager to prove last season's conference finals loss was a fluke. This is the kind of late-night basketball that keeps fans awake past midnight.

What's Next

Training camps open September 29. Preseason begins October 3. And on October 20, everything gets real. The NBA has constructed an Opening Night worthy of a league that has rarely been more compelling. Set three alarms — one for each tip-off. This is the night basketball fans have been waiting for all summer.
