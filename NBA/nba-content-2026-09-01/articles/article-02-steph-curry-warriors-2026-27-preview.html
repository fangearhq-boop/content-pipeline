Steph Curry Is Fired Up for 2026-27 — And the Warriors May Be Better Than Anyone Expects

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | September 1, 2026

Stephen Curry did not need to sign the extension. The moment the window opened on August 29, he was ready. A one-year, $62.6 million deal — the richest single-season contract in NBA history — and the first extension signed when the window opened. That is the kind of clarity that can only come from a player who has never wanted to be anywhere else.

But beyond the history-making dollar figure, the story heading into the 2026-27 season is Curry's energy. He is genuinely, unmistakably excited — and in Golden State, when Curry is excited, things tend to happen.

A Historic Contract, A Historic Career

Curry's $62.6 million salary makes him the first player in NBA history projected to earn more than $60 million in a single season. The deal was made possible by the over-38 rule — which limits such extensions to one year — but Curry embraced the constraint without hesitation. He is 38 years old and entering his 18th season with the same franchise that drafted him No. 7 overall in 2009. He is the only Warrior he has ever been.

His career earnings are approaching $500 million. Four championships. Multiple MVP awards. The greatest shooter the sport has ever produced. And yet, heading into the fall of 2026, Curry is talking about what is still to come.

The Key: Health Returning to Chase Center

The 2025-26 Warriors season was not what anyone anticipated. Moses Moody and Jimmy Butler — both expected to be core contributors — were sidelined with long-term injuries that derailed Golden State's postseason hopes entirely. The Warriors missed the playoffs. In a season where health should have been an asset, it became the story.

Curry is not dwelling on it. Instead, he is pointing forward. "There's a lot of health coming back," he said, referencing the anticipated returns of Moody and Butler. Both players are expected to be available for the start of training camp on September 29.

A healthy Moody and Butler, paired with a motivated Curry, gives the Warriors a very different look than the team that sat at home during the 2026 playoffs. Golden State will not be picked by many as a championship contender — but the league has learned, often painfully, not to dismiss Curry-led teams until they are eliminated.

Opening Night: Warriors vs. Lakers

Golden State opens the 2026-27 season on October 21 at Chase Center against the Los Angeles Lakers. It is a marquee first look at the rebuilt Warriors roster — and a statement opportunity for Curry, who will carry all the energy of a player who believes this team has something left to prove.

What's Next

The coming weeks will bring training camp, preseason, and early-season results that will either validate or test Curry's optimism. But as of September 1, the message from Golden State's franchise cornerstone is unambiguous: the Warriors are not a nostalgia act. They are a team that, with health and Curry at the helm, will compete. The history books are already full of Steph Curry surprises. Expect at least a few more in 2026-27.
