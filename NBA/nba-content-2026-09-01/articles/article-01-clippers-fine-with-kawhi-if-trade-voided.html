Clippers 'Fine' With Kawhi Leonard Staying If NBA Voids Trade — Board of Governors Meeting Is the Moment of Truth

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | September 1, 2026

The Kawhi Leonard trade saga has lasted 11 months, generated thousands of column inches, and kept two franchises — and an entire fan base — in a state of prolonged uncertainty. Now, heading into September 2026, a new and significant wrinkle has emerged: the Los Angeles Clippers are reportedly comfortable with Leonard remaining on their roster if the NBA ultimately voids his agreed-upon trade to the Toronto Raptors.

That is a notable shift. When the trade was first agreed upon on June 30, the Clippers appeared eager to move on from a complicated situation surrounding their franchise cornerstone. The deal — sending Leonard to Toronto in exchange for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, two unprotected first-round picks (2031 and 2033), a 2027 pick swap, and two second-round picks — represented a significant asset haul and a fresh start for both sides.

Then the NBA paused everything on July 9.

The Investigation: What We Know

At the center of the holdup is a league investigation into whether the Clippers used an endorsement deal with Aspiration — a now-bankrupt California-based digital bank — to pay Leonard money that circumvented the NBA salary cap. The probe was triggered after journalist Pablo Torre reported on the arrangement, and the league hired outside law firm Wachtell, Lipton to conduct the investigation.

After 11 months, here is the state of play:

  • Kawhi Leonard has been personally cleared of any wrongdoing.
  • The Clippers — and owner Steve Ballmer — are still facing potential sanctions, which could include fines, draft pick forfeiture, or more severe penalties.
  • Because salary cap circumvention violates the NBA's Collective Bargaining Agreement, Commissioner Adam Silver cannot unilaterally enforce sanctions without risking a protracted arbitration process.
  • NBA Commissioner Silver has publicly addressed the situation following a Board of Governors meeting, but a final resolution has not been announced.

September's Board of Governors Meeting: The Inflection Point

A Board of Governors meeting is expected in September — and it is widely viewed as the moment that determines everything. The BOG is where the league's ownership group weighs in on sanctions, the structure of any discipline, and, ultimately, whether the trade can proceed as agreed.

The Raptors and Leonard have continued operating under the assumption the trade clears. Toronto's training camp opens September 28 in Quebec City, and Leonard's camp has maintained throughout that they expect him to be there. From the Raptors' perspective, the assets they have on offer — Ingram, Dick, and a substantial pick package — make this trade worth the wait.

But the Clippers' apparent comfort with Leonard staying changes the calculus. If sanctions are severe enough to threaten the trade's viability, or if the NBA imposes conditions that cause one or both sides to reconsider, Los Angeles now appears prepared to absorb the outcome without panic.

What's Next

The September BOG meeting is the clearest path to resolution. Three broad outcomes remain on the table: the trade is cleared to proceed, the Clippers are sanctioned but the deal survives, or the trade is voided entirely and Leonard returns to a Clippers roster that — at least according to reports — is prepared to welcome him back.

For Leonard, who has been personally exonerated throughout, the uncertainty is the hardest part. He spent the summer in a state of limbo, and as training camps approach, that limbo needs to end. For basketball fans on both coasts, the answer is finally close.

The September BOG meeting will not just determine where Kawhi Leonard plays. It may define the offseason entirely.
