France Is 7-1 and Wembanyama Is Locked In — The FIBA World Cup Dream Is Very Real

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | September 1, 2026

Victor Wembanyama has a record to protect. A team to captain. A World Cup berth to earn.

And through seven games in FIBA World Cup 2027 European Qualifying, France is handling all of it with authority. After dismantling Sweden 84-69 on August 30 at Avicii Arena in Stockholm — in front of 14,750 fans, a Swedish all-time record for a basketball game — France improved to 7-1 in Group L qualifying and cemented itself as one of the most dangerous teams in Europe heading into the final windows.

The Sweden Result: Wemby Does It Again

The game in Stockholm was not supposed to be a blowout. Sweden played in front of its largest basketball crowd in history, and they made it competitive — even leading 65-64 with five minutes remaining. But that is when Wembanyama took over.

The San Antonio Spurs center finished with 18 points, 8 rebounds, 3 blocked shots, and 6-of-7 shooting from the field. It was a performance that combined efficiency with the kind of defensive presence that simply cannot be replicated at any level of the game. Pelle Larsson did everything Sweden could have asked for with 23 points, but France had a weapon no other team in Group L can match.

France pulled away for a 15-point win and left Stockholm with full marks.

Captain Wemby: A Different Kind of Leader

Wembanyama was named France's captain ahead of this qualifying window — a designation that carries weight given the talent the team has assembled. At 22 years old, he is already the centerpiece of the French national program, as he was for the San Antonio Spurs during their historic 2025-26 season.

That season — 25.0 points, 11.5 rebounds, 3.1 blocks per game, unanimous Defensive Player of the Year, Spurs to 62 wins and their first NBA Finals appearance since 2014 — established Wemby as the clear No. 1 player on the planet. He then flew directly from the NBA postseason to represent France, despite admitting he "didn't expect to feel this frustrated" about leaving his national teammates at the end of each international window.

That competitive fire is what makes him a transcendent captain. He does not merely participate in FIBA windows. He shows up to win.

The World Cup Path

France's 7-1 record in Group L puts them in a commanding position. The FIBA Basketball World Cup 2027 European Qualifiers determine which nations compete for the title, and France — with their depth and their captain — looks well-positioned to secure a berth. The final windows will require continued focus, but on September 1, the path to the World Cup is clear.

What's Next

France will return to the FIBA qualifying windows in the coming months before Wembanyama shifts focus back to NBA training camp, which opens September 29. For now, France is 7-1, the World Cup is within reach, and Wembanyama is exactly the kind of captain a national program builds around. The dream is real — and the leader is ready.
