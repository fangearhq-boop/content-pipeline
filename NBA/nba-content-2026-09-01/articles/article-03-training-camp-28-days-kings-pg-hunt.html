28 Days to NBA Training Camp — The Sacramento Kings Are Still Hunting for a Backup Point Guard

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | September 1, 2026

Twenty-eight days. That is the distance between September 1 and the official start of NBA training camps — and for most of the league, the work of the offseason is done. Rosters are built. Contracts are signed. Players are conditioning in private. But a handful of teams are still finishing the job, and among the most notable gaps remaining belongs to the Sacramento Kings.

Veterans are eligible to report to their teams on September 28, with full camps opening September 29. Preseason action begins October 3. Opening Night is October 20. The calendar is moving fast.

Kings: Life Without Westbrook

Russell Westbrook's retirement left a hole in Sacramento's backcourt that has proven difficult to fill. As a veteran backup behind second-year starter Darius Acuff Jr., Westbrook offered experience, defensive tenacity, and leadership that money alone cannot replace. Acuff handled significant responsibility in 2025-26 and impressed, but no franchise builds a season around a young starter without reliable veteran depth behind him.

The Kings have been active in searching for that piece. As of September 1, that search continues. Several veteran point guards remain available on the open market, and Sacramento is expected to make a signing before training camp opens.

Other Teams Still Finalizing Rosters

The Charlotte Hornets are another franchise with work left to do. Charlotte needs to complete at least two additional roster moves before the final training camp deadline. The Hornets have leaned on Exhibit 10 contracts and two-way deals to fill their camp roster, with the expectation that a few players earn their way into standard contracts through preseason performance.

For the rest of the league, the focus is on health, chemistry, and early-practice evaluation. Veterans who are recovering from injuries will be monitored closely. Coaching staffs are finalizing rotations and strategic priorities. Rookies are trying to make impressions that outlast the preseason.

The Big Picture: A Packed Offseason Behind Us

The 2026 offseason delivered more than most. Giannis Antetokounmpo landed in Miami. Stephen Curry signed a historic $62.6 million extension. The Kawhi Leonard investigation dragged on, keeping the league's attention on legal proceedings rather than basketball. And now, with training camp 28 days away, the attention is finally shifting back to the game.

October 20 cannot arrive fast enough for anyone who has been following this offseason.

What's Next

Sacramento's point guard search is the most visible remaining piece of business around the league. Expect movement on that front — and likely a few other roster additions and cuts — in the coming weeks as teams finalize their training camp rosters. Once camp opens, the conversation shifts from transactions to basketball, and the long offseason is officially behind us.
