Tatum's 40-Point Night Ends with Overtime Buzzer-Beater as Celtics Edge Nets 125-124

By Damon Pierce

October 28, 2026

Boston Celtics | Brooklyn Nets

Jayson Tatum delivered one of the signature performances of the early NBA season Tuesday night, scoring 40 points and hitting a buzzer-beating jumper in overtime to give the Boston Celtics a 125-124 win over the Brooklyn Nets at TD Garden. The victory pushed Boston to 3-0 and extended their winning streak to start the year.

Tatum was in another gear from the opening tip. He attacked the basket, drew fouls, pulled up from mid-range, and connected from three-point range with efficiency throughout. When it mattered most — with the game tied and overtime winding down — he caught, elevated, and drained the winning shot as the buzzer sounded.

A Performance for the Highlight Reel

The night had more than just the final shot. Tatum's 40 points came across a full range of offensive actions, showcasing why he is among the most complete scorers in the Eastern Conference. His ability to create his own shot in late-game situations gives Boston a weapon that very few teams can match.

The Nets were not simply outmatched. Michael Porter Jr. gave Brooklyn a genuine fight with 27 points, and the Nets had every opportunity to steal the game. They led in the fourth quarter and forced overtime with their own strong defensive stretch. But in the end, they could not contain Tatum when the possession mattered most.

Brooklyn in Trouble Early

The loss dropped Brooklyn to 0-4, an ominous start to a season that was already expected to be challenging. The Nets have been competitive in multiple games but have found ways to lose at the end, and Tuesday night was the most painful version of that story: within one stop of a win, then undone by Tatum's buzzer-beater.

For Boston, the 3-0 start reaffirms what the organization built over the offseason. The Celtics have all the pieces to make a deep playoff run, and if Tatum plays at this level for an extended stretch, the Eastern Conference will be theirs to lose.

Boston's defense also showed up Tuesday. Despite Porter Jr.'s 27-point effort, the Celtics limited Brooklyn to just 124 points while Tatum was orchestrating 40 on the other end. The two-way quality of this group is what separates them from the contenders below them.

What's Next

The Celtics carry their 3-0 record into a home game against the Chicago Bulls on Wednesday, October 28. The Brooklyn Nets travel to face a tough matchup Thursday, desperate to end a four-game losing streak to start the season. If Tatum continues at this pace, the conversation about whether Boston is the favorite to come out of the East will only grow louder.
