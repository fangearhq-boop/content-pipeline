Trail Blazers Outlast Lakers 136-129 in Battle of Unbeatens — Dončić Drops 41

By Marcus Cole

October 28, 2026

Portland Trail Blazers | Los Angeles Lakers

Luka Dončić poured in 41 points Tuesday night at Crypto.com Arena — and still found himself on the losing side. The Portland Trail Blazers stayed unbeaten with a 136-129 victory over the Los Angeles Lakers in one of the most anticipated early-season matchups of the year: a battle between the NBA's two remaining undefeated teams.

Portland dominated through balanced scoring and relentless second-half execution, with Deni Avdija leading a Blazers attack that could not be contained. Los Angeles leaned heavily on Dončić, but without enough support to match Portland's depth, the Lakers' perfect start came to an end.

Dončić Can't Do It Alone

Dončić's 41-point outing was spectacular but not enough. He attacked the basket repeatedly, pulled up from mid-range, and connected from deep, yet the Lakers could not keep pace with an Oregon squad playing with remarkable cohesion.

Portland's win spoke to a growing belief around the league: this Blazers team is built for more than a strong start. They have size, shooting, and athleticism at every position, and they have shown early on that they can beat good teams on the road. Taking down the Lakers, even in the first week of the season, is a statement the rest of the West will notice.

Portland Stands Alone

With the Blazers' win, Portland is now the last unbeaten team in the Western Conference. Their defense held up in the fourth quarter despite Dončić's relentless offensive pressure, and their transition game made the Lakers pay for every missed shot.

Avdija, who continued a strong early stretch, was a key piece of Portland's offensive flow. He did not need monster numbers to be impactful — his movement without the ball and his willingness to find teammates in rhythm spots made the Blazers' offense difficult to solve defensively.

For Los Angeles, the loss reveals the team's reliance on Dončić as its primary offensive weapon. When the defense keyed on him — even when it failed to stop him — the Lakers did not have enough other scorers to carry the load. That will be a storyline to watch as the season develops.

What's Next

The Portland Trail Blazers take their perfect record to Sacramento on Thursday to face the Kings, looking to extend their unblemished start. The Los Angeles Lakers will look to regroup when they host the Golden State Warriors on Friday. Dončić will have no shortage of opportunities to log another 40-point night — but Tuesday proved that scoring alone does not guarantee a win against the league's best teams.
