### STORY 1: Kawhi Leonard Trade Update
The NBA's investigation into the Kawhi Leonard trade between the Los Angeles Clippers and Toronto Raptors continues with no official resolution announced. Leonard was photographed Tuesday working out alongside Raptors players in Miami and stated "I hope so...we gonna win" when asked about coming to Toronto. The investigation centers on an alleged undisclosed sponsorship arrangement between Leonard and Aspiration, a financial services company that was a Clippers partner. Until the NBA ratifies the trade, Leonard cannot participate in official team activities. No timeline has been announced for the investigation's conclusion.
