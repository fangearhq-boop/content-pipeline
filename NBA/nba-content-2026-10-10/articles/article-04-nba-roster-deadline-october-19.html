Nine Days Left: Inside the NBA's Roster Deadline Crunch

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | October 10, 2026

Every year, the NBA calendar reaches this moment — ten days before the season begins, when the league's playing population officially narrows. By October 19 at 5 p.m. ET, every franchise in the NBA must carry no more than 15 players on standard contracts and three on two-way deals.

Right now, across the league, there are players whose professional futures will be decided in the next nine days. Some have secured their spots. Many have not.

Memphis's Extreme Math

No team faces a more urgent roster equation than the Memphis Grizzlies. The Grizzlies are currently holding 18 standard contracts — three players over the limit. Three cuts are coming, and they are coming before October 19.

Memphis's situation is complicated further by Isaiah Stewart's preseason injury, which removes a cornerstone piece from the rotation picture just as the team is evaluating the final spots on its roster. The Grizzlies know what their core looks like. Figuring out who rounds out the back end of the bench — under the shadow of a hard deadline — is the immediate challenge.

How the Deadline Works

There is an important nuance in the October 19 deadline: a soft deadline exists on Saturday, October 17. That is the last day a team can waive a player on a non-guaranteed contract without owing them salary. Waiving a player on October 19 means paying two days of prorated salary. After the deadline passes, every player remaining on a standard contract is fully guaranteed for at least the season minimum.

This creates a narrow but real incentive to act by October 17 for players on non-guaranteed deals. For front offices, the math between "this player might make us better" and "this player's deal guarantees on the 17th" becomes a daily calculation this week.

The Players Waiting to Hear

Across the league, undrafted rookies, veteran camp invitees, and second-rounders with partially guaranteed deals are playing every preseason minute knowing it could be their last. Training camp performances, preseason scoring, defensive effort — all of it feeds into front-office decisions made in brief meetings between now and next Monday.

For some players, earning a spot on a roster means a career lifeline extended for another year. For others, a waiver this week means starting over — finding a two-way deal, exploring the G League, or waiting for a call after an injury opens a spot elsewhere.

What's Next

The soft deadline for non-guaranteed contracts is Saturday, October 17. The hard roster deadline is Monday, October 19 at 5 p.m. ET — the same deadline for eligible players to sign rookie scale extensions.

The 2026-27 regular season tips off October 20-21. By then, every roster in the NBA will be set. Nine days is long enough for everything to change. It is also short enough that most decisions are already made.
