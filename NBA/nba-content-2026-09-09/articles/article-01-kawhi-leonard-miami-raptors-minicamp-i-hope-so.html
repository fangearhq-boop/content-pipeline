Kawhi Leonard Is in Miami With the Raptors and He Has Something to Say About Toronto

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | September 9, 2026

Photos and video surfaced Tuesday showing Kawhi Leonard working out with Toronto Raptors players in Miami. The trade is not official. He showed up anyway. And when asked about Toronto, he said four words that every Raptors fan needed to hear.

The trade paperwork isn't done. The NBA investigation hasn't concluded. Kawhi Leonard is technically still a Los Angeles Clipper.

None of that stopped him from showing up in Miami on Tuesday to work out alongside Toronto Raptors players at an informal mini-camp — and to offer four words that will resonate with every fan who has been waiting all summer for a signal.

When spotted in downtown Miami and asked whether he is coming to Toronto, Leonard responded: "I hope so...we gonna win."

It wasn't a press conference. It wasn't a media-prepared statement. It was Kawhi Leonard, out of uniform, telling anyone who would listen exactly where his head is.

The Trade That Still Isn't Official

Toronto and Los Angeles agreed to a deal on June 30 — Kawhi Leonard returning to the franchise where he won his only NBA championship in 2019, in exchange for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, unprotected first-round picks in 2031 and 2033, second-round picks in 2030 and 2033, and a first-round pick swap in 2027.

The hold-up: the NBA's ongoing investigation into an alleged undisclosed sponsorship arrangement between Leonard and Aspiration, a financial services company that was a Clippers partner. Clippers owner Steve Ballmer and team president Lawrence Frank both received suspensions as part of that investigation. The trade cannot be officially ratified until the league concludes its review.

Because the deal is not official, Leonard cannot participate in full team activities. He is restricted to individual workouts. That is what he was doing in Miami — on the margins of the Raptors' informal group sessions, technically separate but visibly present.

Technically separate. Visibly there. Working out with the team he expects to join.

What the Photos Mean

There is no ambiguity about Kawhi Leonard's state of mind at this point. The summer has been full of reports about his desire to return to Toronto, about his conversations with Raptors players and staff, about his belief that the franchise is building something real. Tuesday's images put a picture to those reports.

Leonard was not at a neutral workout facility. He was not back in Los Angeles with former teammates. He was in Miami, alongside the players he expects to spend the season with, doing the work that August and September are designed for. The investigation may be ongoing. His preparation is not waiting for it.

The Raptors are clearly operating the same way. They organized this Miami mini-camp with the expectation — reasonable, given everything — that Kawhi will be cleared before the season begins. Raptors president Masai Ujiri has been public about the team's optimism regarding the timeline. The deal is agreed; the investigation is the formality, even if a significant one.

Training Camp Is 19 Days Away

The Toronto Raptors open training camp September 28 in Quebec City — the site of their NBA Canada Games preseason schedule. Nineteen days from Tuesday.

The ideal outcome is a ratified trade before camp opens, with Kawhi Leonard arriving in Quebec as an official Raptor. Multiple reports have suggested that timeline is possible. But the NBA investigation has no fixed deadline, and the league operates at its own pace when it comes to compliance reviews.

If the trade is not cleared by September 28, the Raptors would open camp without Leonard officially on the roster — though Tuesday's images suggest that his informal presence alongside teammates is unlikely to stop even then.

Whatever the paperwork says, Kawhi Leonard is clearly already a Raptor in every way that matters to him. The league just needs to catch up to what he already decided.

What's Next

Watch for NBA official statements on the investigation timeline in the coming days. Raptors training camp opens September 28 in Quebec City — that is the hard deadline the league and both teams are working around. If the trade clears before then, Kawhi Leonard will arrive as one of the most anticipated returning stars in Raptors history. If it doesn't, the question becomes how the NBA handles a player who is clearly committed to a team that is equally committed to him.
