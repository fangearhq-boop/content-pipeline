Lakers 127, Warriors 105: Hollywood Wins in a Dominant Home Opener

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | October 23, 2026

The Los Angeles Lakers opened their home season with a statement, dismantling the Golden State Warriors 127-105 in a game that turned decisively in the second half and gave the home crowd exactly what it came to see.

LeBron James — splitting his opening week between road duties in Philadelphia and now his first home game — showed he can still set the tone at Crypto.com Arena at 41. The Lakers' depth was the story of the night, with multiple contributors putting together strong performances in a win that sends a message across the West.

Third Quarter Turned It Into a Rout

The Warriors kept the game competitive through two quarters. Golden State's veterans know how to manage games and keep pace with better rosters for stretches. But when the third quarter began, the Lakers found another gear that Golden State simply couldn't match.

A 22-point swing over the course of 12 minutes transformed a game into a statement. The Lakers' defense tightened. The offense found its rhythm off the pick-and-roll. And the Crypto.com Arena crowd, feeding off the momentum, pushed LA to a double-digit advantage that held through the fourth.

The final margin — 22 points — overstates the game's competitiveness for three quarters but accurately reflects what the third quarter looked like. When the Lakers are locked in, they're a different team.

The Warriors' Difficult Position

Golden State entered the season with questions about roster construction and the sustainability of building around Stephen Curry as he approaches the final chapter of his career. A road loss to a Lakers team with championship aspirations is not catastrophic at this stage of October, but it's also not the start Golden State needed.

Curry will have better nights. The Warriors system generates good shots when it's running correctly. But the gap between where Golden State is and where Los Angeles wants to be was visible Thursday night.

What's Next

The Lakers are 1-1 after splitting their first two games — a road loss that started a different chapter in Philadelphia and a convincing home win over a franchise rival. The home record matters for seeding; winning at Crypto.com Arena will be a priority all season.

Golden State needs to regroup and find consistency as the schedule continues. For the Lakers, the early indication is that when they're focused and at home, they have the talent to compete with anyone in the Western Conference.

Hollywood's NBA season is underway, and the opening credits looked promising.
