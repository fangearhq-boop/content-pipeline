### STORY 2: Anthony Davis Wizards Debut

**Long-Form Post (posting window: 11:00 AM ET)**
Anthony Davis showed Washington exactly why the Wizards traded for him on Tuesday night.

In his first game at Capital One Arena, Davis scored 34 points on 11-of-20 shooting, grabbed 12 rebounds, and added 4 assists, 2 steals, and a block. The 34 points tied the Wizards' franchise record for most points in a player's first game with the team. The crowd chanted "MVP" throughout the fourth quarter.

Trae Young ran the offense with 20 points and 13 assists. Milwaukee's Tyler Herro (24) and Jaime Jaquez Jr. (22) kept it close, but Washington pulled away for a 128-116 victory.

The Wizards are above .500 for the first time in 1,423 days. The rebuild is officially over.
