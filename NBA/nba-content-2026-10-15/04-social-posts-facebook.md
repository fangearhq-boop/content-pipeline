### STORY 3: Kristaps Porzingis Out Indefinitely

**Long-Form Post** — 4:00 PM ET

The Warriors thought they'd solved their center problem this offseason. Kristaps Porzingis was the answer.

Then training camp arrived, and Porzingis didn't.

The 7-footer has been out indefinitely since September 28, when Golden State GM Mike Dunleavy Jr. announced at media day that Porzingis would miss camp due to an undisclosed health issue. Steve Kerr said earlier this week he expected Porzingis to return to the team's facility — but Kerr was clear: no timetable for a return to practice, let alone games.

The Warriors open the season in five days.

The situation has drawn comparisons to Porzingis's long history of health setbacks, and NBC Sports reported he has previously dealt with POTS — Postural Orthostatic Tachycardia Syndrome — though it's unclear whether his current issue is related.

Dunleavy said Monday he's "not counting him out" for the opener. But with the roster deadline arriving Friday and no backup center plan in place, Golden State faces a genuine crisis heading into one of the most anticipated seasons in recent memory.

### STORY 4: OKC Thunder Preview

**Long-Form Post** — 12:00 PM ET

The Oklahoma City Thunder enter 2026-27 as the odds-on favorite to win the Western Conference, and it's easy to understand why.

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander — two-time MVP, entering his ninth season at 28 years old — is arguably the best player in the game right now. OKC went 64-18 last season, the best record in the league. And while the heartbreaking Game 7 loss to San Antonio in the WCF still stings, the front office responded decisively.

They cleared out the redundant pieces — Lu Dort, Isaiah Joe, Aaron Wiggins — to create opportunities for Cason Wallace, Ajay Mitchell, and Jared McCain. The core remains intact: SGA, Jalen Williams, Chet Holmgren, Josh Giddey.

Their first test: opening night at San Antonio, 9:30 PM ET on NBC. The exact team that eliminated them last spring. On the road.

They've been thinking about this game since June.
