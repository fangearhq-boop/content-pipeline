Kristaps Porzingis Is Out Indefinitely and the Warriors Have Five Days to Figure It Out

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | October 15, 2026

Golden State Warriors | Kristaps Porzingis | Injuries | Roster

Steve Kerr has run out of things to say about Kristaps Porzingis.

This week, the Golden State Warriors head coach told reporters he expected his starting center to return to the team's facility. That's the update. Not a return-to-practice timeline. Not a first-game window. Just: the player Golden State signed to anchor its frontcourt is expected to come back to the building where the team practices.

The Warriors open the regular season in five days.

What We Know

Kristaps Porzingis missed all of training camp due to an undisclosed health issue. On September 28, Golden State general manager Mike Dunleavy Jr. announced at media day that Porzingis would not be available to start camp. The team declined to reveal specifics, calling the situation "indefinite."

In early October, Kerr told reporters he had not yet seen Porzingis and described the situation as "definitely concerning." Dunleavy was more measured, saying he was "not counting him out" for the opener — but acknowledging that there was no timetable for Porzingis to practice.

NBC Sports reported that Porzingis has dealt with POTS — Postural Orthostatic Tachycardia Syndrome — in previous seasons. The team has not confirmed whether his current issue is related to that condition or something else entirely. The lack of specificity has done nothing to quiet speculation.

The Center Depth Problem

The Warriors signed Porzingis specifically to address a center depth concern that had plagued the franchise for two seasons. His combination of size (7-foot-3), shooting range, and defensive versatility was supposed to give Kerr a legitimate anchor that the team had lacked since Kevon Looney's departure.

Without him, Kerr's options at center are limited. The team's backup center options were not designed to start. Reports from Hoops Rumors suggested the Warriors may explore a roster move at center before Friday's 4 PM CT deadline — though it's unclear whether the team wants to spend a roster spot on a player Porzingis could theoretically make redundant the moment he returns.

That's the bind: adding a center for an unclear window, or rolling into the season thin at the most important position.

What It Means for Opening Night

The Warriors open the 2026-27 season on October 20 — the same night as the Knicks' banner ceremony, LeBron's Sixers debut, and the Thunder-Spurs WCF rematch. Golden State's game is not on the tripleheader, but the Porzingis situation will hang over the team's preparation regardless of what night they play first.

The concern isn't just the opener. It's the precedent. Porzingis has missed significant stretches of seasons before — 80 games in 2022-23, a torn ligament in 2017-18, multiple injuries in between. The Warriors knew his history when they signed him. What they didn't expect was to open their first season with him unable to practice on day one of camp.

What's Next

The Warriors face a roster deadline on October 17 at 4 PM CT, by which point they may need to decide whether to add frontcourt depth. Kerr's most recent comments indicated Porzingis was expected to return to the facility this week, but no timeline exists for practice or games. The situation will continue developing in the days between now and the season opener. Until Porzingis is on the practice floor, the Warriors' center question remains completely open.
