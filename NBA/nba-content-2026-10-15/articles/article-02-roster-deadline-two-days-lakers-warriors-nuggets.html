NBA Roster Deadline Is Two Days Away: Lakers, Warriors, and Nuggets Have Decisions to Make

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | October 15, 2026

NBA Transactions | Roster Deadline | Lakers | Warriors | Nuggets

In 48 hours, every NBA front office that hasn't finished its roster math will have no more room to delay.

October 17 at 4 PM CT is the NBA's non-guaranteed waiver deadline — the moment when teams that haven't cut or guaranteed players holding partial deals see those contracts lock in. Two days later, on October 19 at 4 PM CT, rosters must drop to 15 standard contracts plus three two-way deals. The regular season starts the following morning.

Three teams, in particular, carry the most compelling decisions into the final stretch of the preseason.

The Lakers: Guaranteed Calls Still Pending

Los Angeles entered training camp with 16 standard contracts — one more than the regular-season limit — and the front office has been slowly working through the excess. On Wednesday, the team signed guard Jahmir Young and forward Aniwaniwa Tait-Jones to Exhibit 10 deals, which typically come with G League assignments and no guaranteed money.

But those moves are supplementary. The harder calls involve players on guaranteed or partially guaranteed deals, and as of Thursday, those decisions remain unresolved. Rob Pelinka and his staff have until Friday at 4 PM CT to waive or guarantee anyone with a qualifying option. The math will be done in the next 48 hours — but it may not be comfortable math.

The Warriors: A Center Problem Nobody Solved Yet

Golden State's situation is uniquely complicated, because one of its roster decisions is tied to a health situation that has no timeline.

Kristaps Porzingis — the Warriors' starting center and one of the key acquisitions of the offseason — has not practiced since training camp opened. An undisclosed health issue has kept him out since before media day. Steve Kerr said earlier this week that he expected Porzingis to return to the facility, but the coach offered zero timetable on when the 31-year-old would be able to practice, let alone play.

The Warriors are not expected to move on from Porzingis — his deal is fully guaranteed — but they may need a stopgap at center if he misses the opener. A roster move at center is possible before the Friday deadline, per reports.

The Nuggets: Walker IV Gone

Denver's most notable move this week came earlier: the team waived veteran wing Lonnie Walker IV after just two preseason games, then signed and waived wing Pedro Bradshaw, who will join the Grand Rapids Gold in the G League.

The Nuggets' deadline math is cleaner than the Lakers', but the Walker cut — a veteran who earned a spot on last season's squad — signals that Denver is getting younger and leaner heading into a title chase.

Around the League

Elsewhere, the Toronto Raptors waived two-way big man Trey Jemison after he suffered a hand injury in training camp. The Dallas Mavericks settled on big man Jorge Diaz Graham (No. 23 jersey) after cycling through several candidates for the 21st roster spot. The Utah Jazz waived rookie center Mohamed Wague. The Spurs parted with Taelon Peter, Malik Williams, and Terry Taylor. The Atlanta Hawks added Giorgi Bezhanishvili on an Exhibit 10 deal.

The rosters will look different Friday night than they do Thursday morning. The deadline has a way of forcing hands — and the hands that remain unforced by 4 PM CT on October 17 won't stay that way long.

What's Next

The non-guaranteed waiver deadline hits October 17 at 4 PM CT. Final rosters must be submitted by October 19 at 4 PM CT. The regular season begins October 20. Any team that still has roster questions by Saturday morning is doing this wrong — but the Warriors, Lakers, and several others are about to find out how the next 48 hours shape their seasons.
