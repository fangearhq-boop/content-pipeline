### STORY 2: Jaylen Brown's 76ers Debut

**Long-Form Post (9:00 AM ET)**

Brown's debut was a warning shot. The real thing comes Oct. 20 at MSG.

### STORY 5: Bucks 103, Heat 93

**Long-Form Post (9:30 PM ET)**
The first Giannis homecoming game is in the books — and it went to Milwaukee.

The Bucks beat the Heat 103-93 in Tuesday's NBA preseason, with Giannis Antetokounmpo making his first appearance in the Fiserv Forum as an opponent. After 13 seasons and two MVP awards with Milwaukee, Giannis signed with Miami this summer alongside Klay Thompson, Bobby Portis, and Tim Hardaway Jr.

The crowd gave him a mixed ovation — warm in parts, bitter in others.

On the court, the Heat's new roster is still clearly getting acquainted. Giannis is learning Erik Spoelstra's system. The connections aren't fully formed yet. That's normal for preseason. But the talent is undeniable.

When the Bucks and Heat meet in the regular season, it'll be a completely different story.

Tonight was about the feelings. Giannis in green and cream looked surreal. Giannis in Heat red is something Miami fans are just starting to get comfortable with.
