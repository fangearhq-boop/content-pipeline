SGA Launches Foundation as OKC Thunder Countdown to Oct. 6 Preseason Opener in Tulsa

By Marcus Cole | September 15, 2026 | Hoop Heroes

Before the Oklahoma City Thunder play their first preseason game of 2026-27, their franchise superstar is already doing the work that matters most to him off the court. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander announced the launch of the SGA Foundation in early September — a new nonprofit focused on youth development in Oklahoma City and his hometown of Hamilton, Ontario.

The foundation represents the next step in a deliberate, years-long effort by the two-time MVP to root himself in the communities that shaped him and the one that gave him his platform. At 28 years old, SGA is at the peak of his basketball powers and, by all indications, thinking seriously about what comes next beyond the game.

Four Seasons Above 30 Points Per Game

In case anyone needed reminding what SGA has done on the court to earn this platform: the numbers are historic. Gilgeous-Alexander has averaged more than 30 points per game in each of the past four NBA seasons. He has earned four consecutive All-Star selections and four consecutive All-NBA First Team nods. He has won two back-to-back MVP awards.

He is, without serious debate, the most dominant player in the Western Conference heading into 2026-27, and the Thunder are widely considered the No. 1 seed favorite in the West before camp even opens.

OKC's Preseason Opener: Oct. 6 in Tulsa

The Thunder announced their preseason schedule, and OKC's first game will be on October 6 against the New Orleans Pelicans in Tulsa, Oklahoma — a nod to the franchise's secondary market and longstanding relationship with the Green Country fanbase. The Thunder have periodically held preseason games in Tulsa throughout their time in Oklahoma City, and the Pelicans matchup figures to be a strong draw.

Training camp officially opens September 29 league-wide for teams not traveling internationally in the preseason. For OKC, the week between camp opening and the first preseason tip will be the first real look at a Thunder roster that still ranks among the youngest and most talented in the NBA.

The Championship Window Is Wide Open

Oklahoma City's core remains intact from the team that won the Western Conference last season. Coach Mark Daigneault enters his sixth season at the helm of a program that went from lottery to Western Conference power in four years. The roster built around SGA — deep, defensive, and versatile — gives the Thunder a legitimate path to their next championship.

The question entering 2026-27 is whether OKC can take the final step. One NBA title is on the board. The organization's stated goal is more. And with SGA in his prime and the supporting cast ready, the foundation is as strong as it has ever been.

What's Next

OKC training camp opens September 29. Watch for early camp reports on SGA's conditioning and any roster decisions at the roster's fringes. The preseason opener against New Orleans on October 6 in Tulsa will give fans their first live look at the 2026-27 Thunder. Full preseason and regular season schedule details — including opening night — will continue to emerge as league-wide planning is finalized. As for the SGA Foundation, details on its first programming and fundraising initiatives are expected in the weeks ahead.
