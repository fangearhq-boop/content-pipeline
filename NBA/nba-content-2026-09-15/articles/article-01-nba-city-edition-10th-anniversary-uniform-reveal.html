Nike Drops All 30 NBA City Edition Uniforms at Once — 10th Anniversary Remix Collection Revealed

By Jake Torres | September 15, 2026 | Hoop Heroes

For the first time in the history of the program, every NBA team revealed its City Edition uniform on the same day. On September 15, 2026, Nike and the NBA simultaneously unveiled all 30 clubs' 2026-27 City Edition designs — a landmark moment for one of the most commercially successful alternate uniform programs in professional sports history.

The reason for the coordinated drop: this is the 10th anniversary of the City Edition line, and the league is celebrating accordingly.

The Remix Concept

The design philosophy for the anniversary collection is built around the idea of a remix. Rather than asking each franchise to come up with something entirely new, Nike and the NBA gave teams a different brief: pick your best City Edition design from the past nine years and bring it back, updated and refined.

Anniversary branding runs across all 30 uniforms in the form of an iridescent, aluminum-finish Swoosh — replacing the standard flat version — and the NBA logo has been relocated from the chest to the back neck. The touches are subtle enough to feel like a premium upgrade rather than a redesign, which is exactly the point for a collection that is meant to celebrate what already worked.

The Specter Edition: A New Uniform for Eight Franchises

Separate from the City Edition reveal, eight of the NBA's most historically significant franchises are also unveiling a new fifth uniform: the Specter Edition. The Los Angeles Lakers, Boston Celtics, Golden State Warriors, New York Knicks, Philadelphia 76ers, Detroit Pistons, Atlanta Hawks, and Sacramento Kings will all wear Specter Edition uniforms during the 2026-27 season.

The Specter Edition represents a genuinely new product category — not a replacement for any existing uniform slot, but an addition. Full details on when the Specter uniforms will appear on court and their complete design specifications were expected to accompany today's broader reveal.

A Program Worth Celebrating

City Edition launched in the 2017-18 season as a way to give teams a creative alternative platform — a uniform that could reflect a city's culture, history, or design scene without being constrained by the primary and statement palettes. Ten years later, City Edition nights are among the most watched in-game branding moments in the league, and the designs themselves drive significant retail revenue.

Launching all 30 designs at once instead of dripping them out club by club through October and November is a major strategic shift. It creates a single, league-wide cultural moment and likely concentrates media coverage in a way that individual team reveals never could.

What's Next

Fans will get their first look at the City Edition uniforms on the court once the preseason begins in October. City Edition games are typically scheduled throughout the regular season, with each team expected to wear the uniform at select home games. The Specter Edition debut date for on-court use — along with retail availability — is expected to be announced separately in the coming weeks. This is one of the most anticipated uniform releases in years, and the reaction from NBA fans today will set the tone heading into a new season.
