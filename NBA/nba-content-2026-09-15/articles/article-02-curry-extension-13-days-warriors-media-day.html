13 Days to Decide — Curry Extension Clock Ticks Toward Warriors Media Day on Sept. 28

By Marcus Cole | September 15, 2026 | Hoop Heroes

No contract has been signed. No announcement has been made. And yet, the working assumption across the NBA is that Stephen Curry will remain a Golden State Warrior for the foreseeable future. The question hanging over the Bay Area is simply: when does it get done?

The next real deadline on the calendar is September 28 — Warriors media day — with training camp opening the following morning on September 29. That date has emerged as the informal signpost in the Curry extension conversation, not because of any contractual trigger, but because it represents the last moment before the basketball season begins to swallow everything else.

What's on the Table

Curry is eligible to sign an extension worth up to two years and $136.7 million. His current deal carries him through 2026-27 at roughly $62.5 million. Signing the extension now would tack on two additional guaranteed years and settle his future well before he reaches free agency.

Multiple reports, including from ESPN, have confirmed that both sides want this to happen. There is mutual desire to keep Curry a Warrior for the entirety of his career — a career already decorated with four championships, two MVP awards, and the all-time three-point scoring record. The Warriors have reportedly taken an unusual posture: letting Curry dictate the structure. Length, timing, incentives — sources indicate the team is essentially telling its franchise cornerstone to name what he wants.

Why It Hasn't Happened Yet

The delay is not a sign of friction. Sources around the situation have consistently characterized it as a matter of timing rather than sticking points. At 38 years old, Curry is entering a phase of his career where the terms of a contract carry different considerations than they would for a 25-year-old — things like performance thresholds, opt-out clauses, and legacy framing all factor in.

The full extension — if taken — would commit the Warriors to $136.7 million beyond their current obligation, cutting the roughly $130 million in projected cap space they might otherwise have after 2026-27 to significantly less. That trade-off is one Golden State appears comfortable making.

The Sept. 28 Moment

Media day at Warriors training camp in San Francisco is set for September 28, with the team then traveling to Hawaii for camp sessions. Every Warriors reporter covering the beat has flagged that date as the next signpost — not because Curry is using it as leverage, but because once the basketball season is underway, the contract story gets harder to isolate.

The expectation in league circles is that something gets resolved in or around that window. Nothing official has been confirmed.

What's Next

Warriors media day on September 28 is the next major moment to watch. If Curry and the team have not yet announced an extension by then, the press conference will be dominated by questions about his future — which both sides would likely prefer to avoid. A resolution before that date is possible; a resolution on or around it seems highly probable. The alternative — letting the situation drag past training camp and into the regular season — would be unprecedented for a franchise that has managed Curry's status with extraordinary care throughout his career. Watch September 28 closely.
