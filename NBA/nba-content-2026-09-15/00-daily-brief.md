## Story Summaries

### STORY 1: NBA City Edition Uniform Reveal — 10th Anniversary
Nike and the NBA today reveal all 30 teams' 2026-27 City Edition uniforms simultaneously — a first for the program. This is the 10th anniversary of City Edition, so each team is remixing its best design from the past nine years rather than creating something new. An iridescent aluminum-finish Swoosh and the NBA logo moved to the back neck mark the anniversary across all 30 uniforms. Separately, eight historic franchises (Lakers, Celtics, Warriors, Knicks, 76ers, Pistons, Hawks, Kings) are also unveiling new Specter Edition uniforms today. High engagement day for NBA fans — tier 1 content opportunity.

### STORY 2: Stephen Curry Extension — 13 Days to Media Day
Warriors media day is September 28, which the league is watching as the informal deadline for Curry's extension talks. Curry is eligible for up to two years and $136.7 million. The Warriors are reportedly "letting Curry decide the structure," with ESPN confirming mutual desire to keep him a Warrior for life. No deal confirmed yet. Training camp opens Sept. 29, then the team heads to Hawaii.

### STORY 3: DOJ Clippers Probe Update
The criminal investigation by the U.S. Attorney's Office in Brooklyn (EDNY) into the Clippers' Kawhi Leonard endorsement arrangement remains active and in "earliest stages." The DOJ subpoenaed Daktronics — one of three firms that paid Leonard $18M by Aug. 2021. NBA has already imposed: $30M fine, five draft picks (2029–2033), Ballmer 1-year suspension, Leonard $700K, Lawrence Frank 6-month ban, Robertson 5-year ban. Kawhi's trade to Toronto was ratified. Investigation timeline unclear.

### STORY 4: Jalen Duren Pistons Qualifying Offer Deadline
Negotiations between Detroit and Duren are at a standstill. Pistons offered 5-year/$190M (~$38M AAV); Duren wants $40M+ AAV. Pistons say they won't raise their offer before camp. The QO is worth $9.6M for 2026-27, making Duren an unrestricted free agent next summer if accepted. QO deadline: October 1 (16 days away). Duren is increasingly willing to take the QO and test UFA market.

### STORY 5: SGA Foundation + OKC Preseason
Shai Gilgeous-Alexander announced the SGA Foundation in early September, focused on youth development in Oklahoma City and his hometown of Hamilton, Ontario. The two-time MVP (ages 28) has logged 30+ PPG for four straight seasons. OKC's preseason schedule: opener Oct. 6 vs. Pelicans in Tulsa. Thunder are the Western Conference No. 1 seed favorite heading into 2026-27.
