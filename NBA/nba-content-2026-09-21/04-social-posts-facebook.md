### STORY 2: Duren QO Deadline

**FB Long-Form** [11:00 AM ET]
The Jalen Duren situation is one of the most fascinating contract standoffs in recent NBA history — and the clock is ticking.

Duren, the 22-year-old center who earned third-team All-NBA honors last season and helped lead Detroit to 60 wins and the Eastern Conference's top seed, has been unable to agree on a long-term deal with the Pistons.

Detroit's offer: approximately $35 million per year.
Duren's ask: approximately $40 million per year.
Gap: $5 million annually, or about $25 million over five years.

That's real money — and Duren is apparently willing to walk into the season on a $9.6 million qualifying offer rather than compromise. If he does that, he can become an unrestricted free agent next summer, and sources say teams like the Sacramento Kings are watching closely.

The Oct. 1 deadline is 10 days away. Detroit needs their franchise center signed. Duren knows his leverage.

Will this get done? Or does one of the best young big men in basketball play the 2026-27 season for $9.6 million?
