Group Chats and Italy Workouts: The 76ers Are Already Building Chemistry Before Camp

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | September 21, 2026

The Philadelphia 76ers do not officially open training camp until September 29. But the process of turning four elite stars into a functioning team started long before that.

Joel Embiid texted Jaylen Brown the day the trade was announced — before Brown had even cleaned out his locker in Boston. The two exchanged messages, acknowledging years of mutual respect built through playoff battles and competitive fire, and began laying a foundation for what comes next.

Brown confirmed all four stars — himself, Embiid, LeBron James, and Tyrese Maxey — are connected in a group chat. The text threads are running. The alignment, at least on paper, is real.

The Roster Philadelphia Built

The 76ers' offseason was nothing short of seismic. Brown arrived from Boston in a trade that sent Paul George and four draft picks to the Celtics — a package that gutted the defending champions' depth to give Philly its next franchise piece. Then LeBron James, 41, made the stunning decision to join the Sixers alongside the talented young core of Embiid and Maxey.

No team in the Eastern Conference enters 2026-27 with this kind of individual talent concentrated in one lineup. The question every analyst, scout, and opponent is asking is simple: Can it work?

The Chemistry Question

History offers mixed verdicts on super teams. Some — like the 2016 Warriors after Kevin Durant's arrival — found chemistry quickly and won immediately. Others — like earlier iterations of the 76ers themselves — never fully solved the puzzle despite immense individual talent.

Brown arrived at camp with a different kind of preparation this offseason. He trained with a fencing coach in Italy, working on footwork and lateral movement. He went skydiving over the Pyramids of Giza in Egypt, facing a fear of heights as he celebrated turning 30. His trainer believes his mental and physical preparation going into this season is the best of his career.

"The fit with Tyrese, LeBron and Joel is perfect," his trainer stated publicly. Whether that confidence translates to floor spacing, shot distribution, and fourth-quarter decision-making remains to be seen. But Brown's buy-in appears complete.

The Opening Night Stage

Philadelphia's four stars will make their collective debut on October 20 at Madison Square Garden — walking into the Knicks' banner ceremony, against the team that swept them in four games last spring. New York averaged 124.3 points per game and held the 76ers to 102 in that playoff series.

Nothing announces a new era louder than a statement performance in the building where you were previously embarrassed. If the 76ers' new-look roster can win that game — or even compete in it — it will send a message to the entire Eastern Conference.

What's Next

Camp opens September 29. The next month of practices, preseason games, and on-court repetitions will determine whether the group chat chemistry translates to the hardwood. The 76ers have the talent to compete for a championship. Now comes the hard part: building the trust, habits, and systems that make four stars into one team. All eyes will be on Philadelphia as the 2026-27 season begins to take shape.
