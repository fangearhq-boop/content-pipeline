### STORY 4: Shaedon Sharpe Meniscus

Portland Trail Blazers guard Shaedon Sharpe has suffered a torn right meniscus and is expected to miss approximately six months, putting his expected return around late February 2027.

Last season, Sharpe was having the best year of his professional career — averaging a career-high 20.8 points per game and cementing himself as one of the most exciting young players in the league. This injury couldn't have come at a worse time.

The Blazers this offseason acquired Ja Morant from Memphis — an All-Star guard who was supposed to be paired with Sharpe as Portland's dynamic backcourt duo of the future. Now, Morant will carry the scoring load alone as the Blazers begin a new chapter in their rebuild.

Wishing Shaedon a full and fast recovery. 🙏
