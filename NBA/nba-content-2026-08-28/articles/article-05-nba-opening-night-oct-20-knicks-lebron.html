NBA Opening Night Set for October 20: Knicks Raise the Banner, LeBron Comes to MSG

By Jake Torres | August 28, 2026

The 2026-27 NBA season opens on October 20 with a three-game showcase that the league is calling a tripleheader — and the billing is entirely justified. Banner ceremonies, franchise legends in new uniforms, and a Western Conference Finals rematch make this the most loaded Opening Night in recent memory.

The Schedule

The night begins at 3 p.m. ET on NBC with the Boston Celtics visiting the Detroit Pistons. At 7 p.m. ET, also on NBC, the Philadelphia 76ers face the New York Knicks at Madison Square Garden. The Oklahoma City Thunder then host the San Antonio Spurs at 9:30 p.m. ET to close out the evening on NBC.

Three games. Three storylines. One night.

The Knicks' Moment

The centerpiece of Opening Night is Madison Square Garden and the Knicks' championship banner ceremony. New York raises its first banner since 1973 — 53 years between championships — and does it in front of a crowd that has waited a lifetime for this exact moment.

The opponent makes it all the more dramatic. The Philadelphia 76ers this offseason assembled one of the most star-studded rosters in the league, acquiring Jaylen Brown in a trade from Boston and signing LeBron James in free agency alongside Joel Embiid. LeBron called his decision to join Philadelphia his "last decision" — a weight-of-career statement that underscores how seriously he is treating this championship push.

He makes his debut in that 76ers uniform at MSG, on the Knicks' ring night. The basketball gods do not always cooperate. This time, they delivered something extraordinary.

The Thunder-Spurs Rematch

The night's closing act features the Oklahoma City Thunder hosting the San Antonio Spurs — a repeat of last season's Western Conference Finals. The Spurs, featuring Victor Wembanyama, and the Thunder, one of the league's most talented young teams, give fans the rematch they have been waiting for from the first moments of the offseason.

It is the kind of game that on any other night would be the marquee attraction.

What's Next

Opening Night is still 53 days away, but the anticipation is already building. The Knicks will spend the weeks ahead in training camp and preseason play, savoring the final stretch before the banner goes up. LeBron and the 76ers will be preparing their own statement.

October 20 is the date to circle. The rest of the preseason is just the warmup.
