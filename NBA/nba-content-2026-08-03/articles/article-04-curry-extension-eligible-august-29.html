Stephen Curry Becomes Extension-Eligible August 29 — The Warriors' Most Important Offseason Date

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | August 3, 2026

The Golden State Warriors have had a complicated offseason. LeBron James chose Philadelphia. The roster is being rebuilt around returning pieces rather than blockbuster additions. The dynasty that dominated a decade of Western Conference basketball is in a transitional phase.

And on August 29, the most important decision the franchise makes this offseason becomes available to them: extending Stephen Curry.

Curry becomes extension-eligible with Golden State on August 29. The Warriors are expected to open talks immediately.

The Franchise Cornerstone at 38

Stephen Curry turns 39 in March. He is, by any reasonable measure, still one of the most dangerous offensive players in professional basketball — a shooter whose gravity warps defensive schemes and whose presence alone changes how opponents prepare.

The question with Curry extensions has always been the same: how long, and at what price? The answer has never been straightforward because what Curry represents to the Warriors goes beyond basketball value — he is the reason the organization became what it became, the reason Chase Center was built, the reason the Bay Area became a basketball destination.

An extension keeps him in Golden State. It signals that the Warriors believe they can build a competitive team around him for however many seasons remain at his level. It answers the biggest unresolved question in San Francisco's offseason.

The Summer So Far

The Warriors brought back Draymond Green on a one-year, $27.7 million deal — a familiar contract structure for a player who has bounced between commitment and opt-outs throughout his career. Gary Payton II signed back for one year at $3.88 million, restoring one of the team's most reliable defensive wings.

These are continuity moves. They signal that Golden State is not blowing things up. They are preserving the core of what works while navigating the loss of the LeBron target they did not land.

A Curry extension would be the capstone of that approach: a declaration that this franchise intends to compete with its cornerstone in place, that the dynasty's final chapter is not written, and that the Warriors are building toward one more serious run.

The Historical Context

Curry's extension situation is unusual in the context of NBA history precisely because there is no comparable player who has remained at this level, on this team, for this long. Four championships. Three MVPs. The unanimous MVP of 2016. The revolution in how the sport thinks about three-point shooting.

LeBron James played into his 21st season and continued producing at an All-Star level. Curry has shown no signs of significant decline at 38. The Warriors are betting — as they have for a decade — that the man is worth the investment.

No terms have been reported as of August 3. The conversation has not formally begun. But August 29 is 26 days away, and the Warriors know what they need to do.

What's Next

When Curry becomes extension-eligible on August 29, expect Golden State to move quickly. The structure of any deal — length, total value, whether it includes a player option — will define the Warriors' competitive window and their flexibility around him. The NBA schedule drops August 13-14, which will give context to what Golden State's season looks like before any extension is formally signed. Curry at 38 entering a new contract year is one of the sport's most compelling storylines heading into fall. The clock starts in 26 days.
