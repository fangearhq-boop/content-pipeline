LeBron James, Jaylen Brown, and the 76ers' Final Gamble

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | August 26, 2026

LeBron James called it his "last decision." He chose Philadelphia. And in doing so, the 76ers became the most fascinating team in the NBA before a single training camp practice has taken place.

The pieces assembled in Philadelphia this summer are genuinely staggering. LeBron James — 22-year veteran, four-time champion, the greatest of any reasonable conversation — is now joined by Joel Embiid, who is described by those close to the team as "looking healthier than he has in a long time," and Jaylen Brown, the 2024 NBA Finals MVP whom the Sixers acquired via trade. This roster, on paper, competes with any in the league.

The Ambition Behind the Decision

LeBron's path to Philadelphia was not straightforward. He reportedly nearly retired before making this choice, and his deliberation extended long enough that Las Vegas moved the 76ers' championship odds from 20-1 to 10-1 based on the speculation alone. When he finally committed to Philly, it was with clear-eyed awareness of what he was doing: taking one more shot at a title with a team built to give him a chance.

Brown arrives as the ideal complement. The Boston Celtics' departure created a ripple, and the Sixers moved aggressively to capitalize. Brown's defensive intensity, iso-scoring ability, and championship experience earned in Boston make him the kind of wing player LeBron has always deployed most effectively. He is not a deferrer — and he won't be asked to be.

Embiid is the X factor in the deepest sense. When healthy, there is no center in the league who creates more problems for opposing defenses. His health has been the constant asterisk on every 76ers projection for years. The reports out of Philadelphia this summer are encouraging, but the proof will come in January, February, and March, not in August.

What's Next

The 76ers open the 2026-27 season on October 20 at Madison Square Garden against the Knicks — Opening Night, prime time, the biggest possible stage. LeBron at MSG for what he has framed as the final chapter of the most consequential career in basketball history. The 76ers' preseason begins October 5 against the Knicks, with additional games against Boston on October 10 and 16. Every possession between now and then is a building block. The last decision deserves the best preparation possible.
