Kawhi Leonard's Trade Is Still on Hold — and Toronto's Clock Is Ticking

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | August 26, 2026

The deal has been in place for nearly two months. The Kawhi Leonard trade — sending the All-Star forward from the Los Angeles Clippers to the Toronto Raptors for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, unprotected first-round picks in 2031 and 2033, a 2027 pick swap, and two second-round picks — was agreed to in principle at the end of June. It is now August 26, and Leonard is still a Clipper.

The NBA's investigation into whether the Clippers circumvented the salary cap to pay Leonard through Aspiration — a now-bankrupt company alleged to have been a pass-through vehicle — is the reason. The investigation was performed by outside counsel and fact-finding was completed around August 10. The league has been processing the findings since then, with resolution expected around September 22.

What We Know — and What We're Still Waiting On

The NBA has cleared Leonard personally. He is not considered to have knowingly participated in any cap circumvention scheme. The sanctions, when they come, will fall on the Clippers organization. That separation is meaningful for Leonard's reputation — and for the Raptors, who are not acquiring a player under a cloud.

The Clippers have been bracing for sanctions that could include fines, draft pick penalties, or both. The Aspiration investigation is one of two endorsement deals under scrutiny — the Daktronics deal (the company that makes the scoreboard system at Intuit Dome) is also part of the probe. The breadth of what the league ultimately finds will determine the severity of any punishment.

For Toronto, the practical consequence is a 32-day countdown. The Raptors open training camp on September 28 in Quebec City at Université Laval. If the trade isn't completed before then, the Raptors face the bizarre logistical situation of preparing for a season not knowing which players will be in their locker room. They have reportedly been operating as though the trade will clear — proceeding with roster planning that assumes Leonard arrives.

What's Next

The ~September 22 resolution date is now the pivot point for everything. If the investigation wraps on schedule, the Clippers absorb their sanctions, and the trade clears within the following days, Toronto has a few days before camp to welcome Leonard officially. If it bleeds later — ESPN has warned it could push into 2027 in a worst-case scenario — the Raptors face the prospect of opening the season without him. That would be the worst possible outcome for a franchise that sold its offseason around this return. The clock is ticking.
