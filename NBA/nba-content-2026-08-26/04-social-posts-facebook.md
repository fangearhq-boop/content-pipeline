### STORY 4: Kawhi Leonard Trade Update

#### Long-Form Post

The Kawhi Leonard trade has been agreed to in principle since late June. It's now late August. And Kawhi is still a Los Angeles Clipper.

The hold-up is the NBA's ongoing investigation into whether the Clippers used a now-bankrupt company called Aspiration to funnel money to Leonard in violation of salary cap rules. The outside counsel hired to investigate finished fact-finding around August 10. The league expects to resolve the matter around September 22 — which is, notably, just six days before the Toronto Raptors' training camp opens on September 28.

The good news for Leonard: the NBA has personally cleared him. He did not know about — and was not party to — any cap circumvention. The Clippers are expected to face sanctions.

The trade itself (Leonard to Toronto for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, and multiple first-round picks) is reportedly still on track to complete once the investigation wraps. The Raptors are operating as though it will happen.

But for now, the wait continues. And every passing day without a resolution is another day of uncertainty for a Toronto team eager to reset around a healthy, motivated Kawhi Leonard.

Tick, tock. ⏱️
