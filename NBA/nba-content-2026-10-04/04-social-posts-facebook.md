### STORY 2: Warriors Fall to Clippers in Hawaii Preseason Opener

**Long-Form Post — 11:00 AM ET**
The NBA preseason touched down in paradise — and the new-look Los Angeles Clippers arrived with something to prove.

Sunday's game at Stan Sheriff Center in Honolulu gave basketball fans their first official look at the Clippers' rebuilt roster. Brandon Ingram and Gradey Dick are in town from Toronto. Rui Hachimura signed from the Lakers. Max Strus came over from Cleveland. This is a fundamentally different team than the one that closed out last season.

For the Golden State Warriors, it was a chance to shake off early preseason rust — but they were without Kristaps Porzingis, who is targeting a mid-October return, and Moses Moody (knee). The roster has the talent; the health puzzle just needs solving.

The backdrop of Hawaii made this game feel special. The Warriors held their entire training camp at BYU-Hawaii, and bringing a preseason game to the island was a reward for the local fans who showed up in force.

Real answers won't come until the regular season opens Oct. 21 — but this week gave us our first real glimpse.

### STORY 3: Kyrie Irving "No Restrictions" for 2026-27 Return

**Long-Form Post — 7:00 PM ET**
When Kyrie Irving tore his ACL in March 2025, the basketball world held its breath.

Irving is one of the most uniquely gifted players in NBA history — a ballhandler whose footwork and touch are unlike anything the game has ever seen. And ACL tears, even as rehab science has advanced, are never guaranteed returns to full form.

But everything coming out of Dallas this preseason has been encouraging. Irving spent the summer in pickup games, drawing praise from players around the league for showing flashes of his old self. And heading into preseason camp, his message to reporters was unambiguous: "No restrictions."

He missed the entire 2025-26 season. He's now 34 years old. The Mavericks have waited over 18 months for him to return. And it looks like the moment is finally here.

Dallas has serious questions to answer this season — but Kyrie healthy changes everything. 🏀

### STORY 4: Tyrese Haliburton's First Game in 16 Months

**Long-Form Post — 9:00 AM ET**
There's no moment more cruel in sports than getting hurt in the biggest game of the season.

For Tyrese Haliburton, that moment came in Game 7 of the 2025 NBA Finals. The Indiana Pacers had gone on one of the most dramatic championship runs in recent memory, and Haliburton — their floor general, their heartbeat — tore his Achilles in the crucialing moments.

He hasn't played a single NBA minute since.

Now, 16 months later, Haliburton is targeting his return to the court on Oct. 7 in Pacers preseason action. The road back from an Achilles tear is one of the most grueling in all of sports. It tests patience, mental toughness, and the body in ways few injuries do.

Haliburton has navigated all of it. He's cleared for full activity, and Indiana fans are counting down the days.

Oct. 7. Write it down.
