NBA Preseason Sunday Recap: Thunder, Lakers, Warriors Among Teams in Action

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | October 4, 2026

The NBA preseason rolled on Sunday with a full slate of games across the country and overseas, giving fans their latest glimpse at rosters still finding their footing two weeks before Opening Night. From the Oklahoma City Thunder in the Mountain West to the Los Angeles Lakers taking on Sacramento to the Warriors-Clippers showdown in Honolulu, Sunday produced a wide-angle view of what the 2026-27 league will look like.

The Thunder handled the Denver Nuggets in their afternoon matchup, a result that continued Oklahoma City's reputation as one of the league's best-run organizations. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander entered the preseason as one of the favorites for the MVP award, and every early look at OKC is a data point toward whether they can take the step from contender to champion this season.

Lakers Edge Kings in Close Finish

In Sacramento, the Los Angeles Lakers needed a late push to edge out the Kings in a tight preseason finish. With LeBron James joining the 76ers this offseason, the Lakers enter 2026-27 in a true transitional phase — rebuilding around a younger core while maintaining enough veteran presence to stay competitive. The preseason is where that identity gets stress-tested.

The Kings, meanwhile, remain one of the Western Conference's more intriguing teams — a franchise that has found genuine competitiveness in recent seasons and enters this preseason hoping to maintain that momentum. Close preseason games against rivals are exactly the kind of competition that sharpens a roster before the stakes get real.

A League-Wide Preseason in Full Swing

What Sunday's games represented, more than any individual result, was the league coming to life after a quiet offseason. The NBA preseason serves a purpose that goes beyond wins and losses: it gives coaches real film to analyze, gives players competitive reps to refine timing and conditioning, and gives fans the first concrete evidence that the long wait for basketball is ending.

This preseason has already featured games in Hawaii, Abu Dhabi, Quebec City, and Macao — a reminder that the NBA is a genuinely global product with a fanbase that extends to every corner of the world. Sunday's domestic games grounded that globalism with the familiar rhythm of American arenas and local rivalries.

What's Next

The preseason continues through the week, with more games building toward October 20's Opening Night. The Knicks' Banner Night at Madison Square Garden — the Knicks' first championship banner raised since 1973, against the LeBron James 76ers — is already shaping up as one of the most anticipated events in recent NBA history.

Until then, the preseason is serving its purpose: sharpening rosters, answering questions, and reminding everyone watching that the most entertaining season in sports is almost here.
