LeBron James Makes His 76ers Preseason Debut Tomorrow — Here's What to Watch

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | October 4, 2026

After an open scrimmage that drew close to 10,000 fans to Wells Fargo Center, LeBron James is one day away from suiting up for the Philadelphia 76ers in his first official preseason game. Sunday night, the New York Knicks come to town, and one of the most anticipated moments of the 2026-27 NBA season officially begins.

LeBron is entering his 24th NBA season, a milestone no player in league history has ever reached. He arrived in Philadelphia this offseason alongside Jaylen Brown, forming a superstar trio with Joel Embiid and Tyrese Maxey that has Eastern Conference rivals rethinking their championship odds. The question was never whether this team had the talent. It was always about how quickly they could mesh — and preseason is where that process starts.

A Lineup Unlike Any Other

The projected starting lineup for Philadelphia reads like an All-Star ballot: LeBron James, Joel Embiid, Jaylen Brown, Tyrese Maxey, and VJ Edgecombe. The 76ers will have three former or current All-Stars in their starting five, with a fourth in Maxey who has steadily built his own case for that distinction. It is a lineup designed to overwhelm opponents at every position, and head coach Nick Nurse has spent all of training camp figuring out the best way to deploy it.

Embiid, who was a full participant in all training camp practices and received glowing reviews from Nurse, enters the season with renewed energy. The big man dropped considerable weight ahead of camp and has reportedly looked as sharp as he has in years. For a player whose health has been the central variable in every Sixers championship conversation, the early indicators are as encouraging as they have been in some time.

The Weight of 24

LeBron James at 41 years old is still one of the most compelling players in professional basketball — not just for nostalgia, but because of what he is still demonstrably capable of doing. He has made a science of longevity, and his body of work in recent seasons confirms the investment has continued to pay dividends.

But reaching a 24th season is not merely a physical achievement. It is a psychological one. Each year, LeBron has found new reasons to play at the highest level. In Philadelphia, with a roster that gives him a genuine shot at a fifth championship ring, the motivation is as clear as it has been in years. The legacy stakes are immense — but so is the opportunity.

The open scrimmage earlier this week offered Philadelphia fans their first look at how LeBron carries himself in a new uniform, in a new city, with new teammates. The fact that nearly 10,000 fans showed up for what was essentially a practice told the story more clearly than any analysis could.

What's Next

Tomorrow night's preseason opener against the Knicks at 7 p.m. ET is just the beginning. The 76ers have three more preseason games before Opening Night on October 20, when they travel to Madison Square Garden for the Knicks' Banner Night — the first championship banner raised at MSG since 1973. LeBron James walking into that building as a member of the Sixers will be one of the most electric atmospheres the NBA has seen in years.

For now, tomorrow night is step one. The building will be loud. The eyes of the basketball world will be watching. And LeBron James, entering a season no one else has ever reached, will walk onto the court in a red, white, and blue uniform for the very first time.
