9 Days: Stephen Curry's Extension Window Opens August 29 — What to Expect

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | August 20, 2026

In nine days, one of the most anticipated contract signings in Golden State Warriors history becomes official — or at least eligible. Stephen Curry's extension window opens August 29, and every indication points to a deal that would keep the greatest shooter in NBA history in a Warriors uniform through his age-40 season.

The expected terms: two years, $136.7 million. The expected outcome: a signing that ends what has quietly been one of the offseason's most lingering storylines.

Why This Deal Matters

Stephen Curry has spent his entire NBA career with the Golden State Warriors. He was drafted No. 7 overall in 2009. He has won four championships with the franchise, been named NBA MVP twice (including once unanimously), and transformed how the game is played — arguably more than any player of his generation.

For the first time in his career, unrestricted free agency looms as a realistic outcome if this extension doesn't get done. Without a new deal signed before the 2026-27 season ends, Curry would enter the 2027 offseason as a free agent for the first time ever — a scenario neither he nor the Warriors want.

"I think there are two things I'm confident about with Steph," Warriors GM Mike Dunleavy Jr. told reporters. "He really, really wants to win. And he wants to finish his career as a Warrior." The language is careful, but the confidence is real.

What the Extension Looks Like

The two-year, $136.7 million max extension is the upper limit of what the league's rules allow for a player of Curry's standing at this stage. At $68.35 million per year, it would make Curry one of the highest-paid players in the league — a number that reflects his status rather than any lingering doubt about whether he can play.

At age 38 last season, Curry shot 42 percent from three-point range. That efficiency, sustained over a full season, is a reminder that his game hasn't eroded in the ways that aging typically claims players. He's not the same explosive athlete he was a decade ago, but his skill, spacing, and basketball IQ have compensated for whatever athleticism has declined.

Some reports have floated the possibility of a shorter structure — a single year at $62.6 million — if Curry prefers flexibility. But the majority of reporting points to the full two-year max as the most likely outcome.

The Warriors' Context

Golden State went 37-45 last season — a disappointing result that reflected the team's age, health challenges, and transition period. The franchise missed out on LeBron James in free agency. Draymond Green is back on a one-year deal worth $27.7 million.

Keeping Curry anchors the roster and provides the kind of franchise continuity that allows the Warriors to recruit around him. A Curry in a Warriors uniform is a recruiting asset. A Curry in free agency is a different calculation entirely.

Christmas Day brings Nuggets-Warriors to national television — the kind of marquee matchup that the league schedules precisely because Curry's games matter to casual fans in a way that only a small number of players can generate.

What's Next

The window opens August 29. Signing can happen the same day — there's no mandatory waiting period once the window is open. If both sides are aligned (and all evidence suggests they are), the announcement could come within hours of eligibility.

The NBA and Warriors both understand what a Curry extension represents: not just a financial transaction, but a commitment to one of the sport's most celebrated careers concluding on the terms everyone hopes for. A Warrior, for life.

Nine days. The countdown is almost done.
