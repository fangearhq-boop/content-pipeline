NBA Training Camp 2026: Every Key Date Fans Need to Know

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | August 20, 2026

Summer is ending. The NBA is coming back. Before the regular season tips off on October 20, here is everything you need to know about the training camp and preseason schedule for the 2026-27 season.

Key Dates at a Glance

September 22 — Training camps open for teams participating in overseas preseason games. These are the franchises heading to international venues in the weeks before the domestic preseason schedule begins.

September 28 — Toronto Raptors Media Day in Quebec City at the Université Laval PEPS athletic complex. This is a particularly significant date given the Kawhi Leonard trade situation — if the deal clears before then, Leonard could be introduced at this event. If it hasn't, Media Day gets complicated quickly.

September 29 — All other teams open training camps. This is the primary date for the majority of the league, including the Warriors, Timberwolves, Miami Heat, and every other team not on an overseas schedule.

October 3 — Preseason games begin across the league. This is the first opportunity for fans to see new rosters in action — new teammates playing together, new coaches installing systems, young players competing for roster spots.

October 20 — Regular season opening night. The headliner: Philadelphia 76ers at the New York Knicks at Madison Square Garden. LeBron James and Joel Embiid playing their first game as 76ers in New York, at MSG, on the biggest stage the regular season offers. Other openers that night include Celtics-Pistons and the Oklahoma City Thunder at the San Antonio Spurs.

76ers Preseason Schedule

Philadelphia has released its four-game preseason schedule, which serves as a useful indicator of when the LeBron James era tips off in a live game environment:

  • October 5 — Philadelphia vs. New York Knicks (home)
  • October 8 — at Brooklyn Nets
  • October 10 — at Boston Celtics
  • October 16 — vs. Boston Celtics (home)

The Oct. 5 home opener gives Philly fans their first look at the new-look roster with LeBron James and Jaylen Brown (acquired in the Paul George trade) alongside Joel Embiid and Tyrese Maxey.

The Storylines Heading Into Camp

There are several major questions that training camp will begin to answer:

Will Kawhi Leonard be a Raptor by camp? The NBA cleared Leonard personally in its investigation, and sources expect the trade to complete before Toronto's camp opens September 28. If it does, Media Day in Quebec City becomes a coronation. If it doesn't, the Raptors face an awkward situation.

How do LaMelo and Edwards fit together? Minnesota's new backcourt gets their first sustained reps starting September 29. The ball-movement chemistry, defensive assignments, and late-game hierarchy will all need to be established before October games count.

Is Giannis healthy and ready for Miami? The two-time MVP is heading into his first season with the Heat after the blockbuster trade from Milwaukee. Training camp is where he begins building relationships with Bam Adebayo and establishing himself as the anchor of Pat Riley's latest chapter.

Will Steph Curry's extension be official? The window opens August 29 — well before training camp begins. Assuming the signing happens as expected, Curry enters camp with clarity about his future. That matters for team-building and for morale.

What's Next

NBA fans have about six weeks until preseason games begin. The off-season transactions — trades, signings, extensions — are nearly complete. Now comes the part where we find out if the pieces fit the way teams planned.

Mark September 29 on your calendar. That's when the real preparation begins. Opening night on October 20 is the finish line — but training camp is where everything gets built.
