# NBA Daily Brief — August 20, 2026
**Brand:** Hoop Heroes | **Date:** Thursday, August 20, 2026

## Top Stories

### STORY 1: Wemby vs. Jokic — France Takes on Serbia TONIGHT in Belgrade (Tier 1)
Victor Wembanyama makes his first France appearance since the 2024 Olympic final tonight when France faces Serbia in Belgrade at 8 p.m. local time (2 p.m. ET). Wembanyama was named captain for the FIBA World Cup qualifier window — a massive honor for the 22-year-old Spurs star. Serbia will feature Nikola Jokic, setting up the most compelling individual matchup in international basketball. Wemby previously said: "I do not miss the NBA right now, but I miss FIBA basketball."

### STORY 2: Kawhi Investigation — NBA Cleared Leonard, Clippers Sanctions Coming (Tier 1)
The NBA found no evidence that Clippers owner Steve Ballmer funneled money directly to Kawhi Leonard, clearing a key hurdle for the long-delayed trade to Toronto. However, investigations into the Daktronics scoreboard deal — alleged to have been used to circumvent the salary cap — are ongoing. Sources say resolution with some level of sanctions against the Clippers, plus trade clearance, is expected within weeks. Toronto Raptors are operating as if the trade completes; camp opens Sept. 28 in Quebec City.

### STORY 3: Curry Countdown — 9 Days to the August 29 Extension Window (Tier 2)
Stephen Curry's extension window opens in 9 days on August 29. The Warriors are expected to offer a two-year, $136.7 million max extension that would take Curry through his age-40 season. GM Mike Dunleavy Jr. remains "pretty confident" Curry finishes his career in Golden State. All indications point to a signing, but it's not done until it's done.

### STORY 4: Edwards' Bold Take — "We're Going to Beat Them" (Tier 2)
Anthony Edwards is already talking championship with LaMelo Ball in the fold. Told ESPN he's confident the Timberwolves will beat both the Oklahoma City Thunder and San Antonio Spurs — the top two teams in the West last season. Edwards led the NBA with 157 clutch points in 2024-25, and analysts say the LaMelo addition could make him even more dangerous as an off-ball weapon.

### STORY 5: NBA Training Camp Timeline — Everything Fans Need to Know (Tier 2)
Teams playing overseas preseason games open camp on Sept. 22; all other teams open Sept. 29. Preseason games begin Oct. 3. The Toronto Raptors will hold Media Day on Sept. 28 in Quebec City at Université Laval. The Philadelphia 76ers' four-game preseason schedule tips off Oct. 5 at home against the Knicks.
