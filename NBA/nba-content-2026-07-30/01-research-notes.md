### STORY 1: Kawhi Leonard Investigation Could Drag Into 2027

**Facts (HIGH confidence — multiple independent sources):**
- NBA investigation began September 2025, now in its 11th month
- Probe examines whether Clippers circumvented salary cap via Aspiration Partners Inc. endorsement deal: $28 million over 4 years to Kawhi Leonard, plus reportedly $20 million in Aspiration equity
- Aspiration Partners also had a $300 million, 23-year naming rights deal with the Clippers
- NBA's outside law firm has completed its fact-finding phase
- Sources at ESPN, HoopsHype, Yahoo Sports, and OpenCourt Basketball (published July 29-30) say investigation could extend into 2027
- NBPA can demand CBA arbitration before any penalty is imposed — this is the key delay mechanism
- Clippers claim they were victims of fraud, not co-conspirators
- Aspiration CEO convicted and sentenced to 14 years in federal prison for wire fraud
- Commissioner Silver has publicly said probe must conclude before 2026-27 season starts (now in question)
- Proposed Raptors-Clippers trade: Toronto gets Leonard; Clippers get Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, unprotected first-round picks in 2031 and 2033, a 2027 first-round pick swap, two second-round picks
- Trade is officially on hold; NBA will not allow it to close until probe resolves
- Kawhi Leonard and Clippers both deny wrongdoing
- Sources: ESPN (espn.com/nba/story/_/id/49482792), HoopsHype, OpenCourt Basketball, Yahoo Sports, FOX LA

**Facts (MEDIUM confidence — single sources):**
- Raptors still cannot plan their 2026-27 roster with this uncertainty
- If both sides cannot agree on settlement, case goes to neutral arbitrator jointly appointed by NBA and NBPA
