Kawhi Leonard Trade Countdown: Resolution Expected by September 22 as Toronto Camp Looms

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | August 27, 2026

Two months after the trade was agreed upon, Kawhi Leonard still officially plays for the Los Angeles Clippers.

The deal sending Leonard to the Toronto Raptors in exchange for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, and a collection of draft picks was struck on June 30 — and then frozen, pending the resolution of an NBA investigation into whether the Clippers circumvented salary cap rules through sponsorship arrangements involving Leonard.

Today — August 27 — the wait continues. But there is a growing sense in league circles that the end is in sight.

Where the Investigation Stands

The NBA's probe, conducted by outside counsel from Wachtell Lipton, has examined two arrangements: a $28 million Aspiration partnership and a separate Daktronics sponsorship deal. The central question has been whether Clippers owner Steve Ballmer used these business relationships to funnel money to Leonard — effectively circumventing salary cap rules.

The good news for Leonard: he has been personally cleared. The NBA found no evidence he was a direct beneficiary of any improper payment scheme. That clears his name and keeps the trade framework viable.

The Clippers, however, are expected to face sanctions. The form of those sanctions — financial penalties, draft pick forfeiture, or other roster-level consequences — has not been formally announced, and the investigation must formally conclude before any penalties are levied and the trade can be approved.

The September 22 Timeline

League sources have told reporters they believe the investigation is "closer to completion than advertised," with sources suggesting a resolution could come within six weeks of August 10 — putting the window at approximately September 22.

That date carries added urgency because the Raptors open training camp on September 28 in Quebec City at Université Laval. If the investigation clears on or around September 22, Leonard would have less than a week to officially become a Raptor before camp begins. That is cutting it extremely close.

Still, Leonard himself is reportedly operating under the belief the trade will be completed before camp. He has maintained a level of public calm throughout the process that suggests his camp does not view this as an imminent threat to the deal's existence.

What's Next

The next 26 days will determine whether the summer's biggest trade saga reaches its expected conclusion or takes another unexpected turn. All parties — Leonard, the Raptors, and the Clippers — are waiting on the NBA's findings.

If the sanctions are levied and cleared before September 22, the league can approve the trade and Toronto can begin preparation with Leonard in the fold. If complications arise, the trade could push into October — or beyond. The ESPN-raised scenario of a 2027 resolution, while still considered unlikely, has not been entirely ruled out.

For now, Kawhi Leonard continues to wait. Toronto continues to build around the hope that he is coming. And the NBA prepares to close one of the longest-running offseason dramas in recent memory.
