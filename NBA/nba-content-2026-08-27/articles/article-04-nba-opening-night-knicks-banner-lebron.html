NBA Opening Night 2026-27: Knicks Raise the Banner, LeBron Makes His 76ers Debut at MSG

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | August 27, 2026

The NBA has delivered a Opening Night slate that earns the word "historic" without a hint of exaggeration.

October 20 brings a tripleheader on NBC and Peacock — three games, all with something enormous on the line. The defending champion New York Knicks raise their first championship banner since 1973. LeBron James and Jaylen Brown make their Philadelphia 76ers debut in front of that banner ceremony. And the Oklahoma City Thunder visit San Antonio for a Western Conference Finals rematch.

The league has outdone itself this year.

Game 1: Boston at Detroit, 3 PM ET

The opener matches the top two Eastern Conference seeds from last season — the No. 1 Detroit Pistons hosting the No. 2 Boston Celtics. Detroit's stunning rise to the No. 1 seed was the Eastern Conference story of 2025-26, and Boston's continued presence near the top ensures a marquee matchup to start the afternoon.

Game 2: Philadelphia 76ers at New York Knicks, 7 PM ET — The Main Event

This game deserves its own documentary before the season even starts.

Madison Square Garden will host one of the most layered moments in recent NBA memory. The Knicks — 2025-26 NBA champions — will raise their first championship banner since 1973, ending a drought of more than five decades. The Garden faithful have been waiting for this. Their city has been waiting for this.

And walking into that building for the visiting team? LeBron James, wearing a 76ers jersey.

James, who publicly called the decision to join Philadelphia his "last" major career choice, will make his Philly debut alongside Joel Embiid and Jaylen Brown — who himself arrived via trade — at the most storied arena in basketball. On the night the Knicks hoist a banner that represents everything James has chased for 24 seasons.

The basketball will matter. But the atmosphere will transcend basketball.

Game 3: Oklahoma City Thunder at San Antonio Spurs, 9:30 PM ET

The Western Conference Finals rematch closes the night. OKC and San Antonio played 12 times across all competition formats in 2025-26, including the regular season, NBA Cup semifinals, and a seven-game WCF won by the Spurs 4-3. The Thunder want revenge. The Spurs want to prove last year was no fluke.

Victor Wembanyama and Shai Gilgeous-Alexander are two of the best young players on the planet. Putting them on the same court to open a season — in what could be a preview of postseason basketball — is exactly what the NBA wants for its closing act of the night.

What's Next

October 20 marks the beginning of an 82-game journey, but Opening Night sets the emotional tone for everything that follows. After the banner goes up, after LeBron walks out in a Philly uniform, after OKC and San Antonio remind each other what last year felt like — the 2026-27 season will be officially underway.

Mark your calendars. Clear your schedule. This night is going to be one for the books.
