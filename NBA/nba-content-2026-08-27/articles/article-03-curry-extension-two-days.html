Two Days Away: Stephen Curry's Extension Window Opens Saturday and the Warriors Are Ready

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | August 27, 2026

Saturday, August 29 is circled on every Golden State Warriors calendar that exists. That is the day Stephen Curry becomes eligible to sign a contract extension — and all indications suggest the Warriors are not going to wait long once the window opens.

Curry, 36, is in the final year of his current deal at $62.6 million for 2026-27. The expected new extension would add two years and up to $136.7 million, carrying the greatest shooter in basketball history through his age-38 season and beyond.

Warriors GM Mike Dunleavy Jr. has made no secret of where the organization stands. He has been "pretty confident" the deal gets done, and ESPN's Anthony Slater reported months ago that there is mutual desire from both Curry and the Warriors to keep him in Golden State for the entirety of his career.

The Windhorst Wrinkle

Not everyone is urging Curry to sign immediately. ESPN's Brian Windhorst made a compelling case for Curry to delay signing an extension this summer, arguing that waiting preserves more salary cap flexibility for the Warriors to improve the roster around him.

The logic is straightforward: Curry remains under contract for 2026-27 regardless. By not extending now, the Warriors retain flexibility in 2027 free agency rather than committing cap space ahead of time. For a franchise trying to compete at the highest level, that flexibility could be the difference in adding a meaningful piece.

But Curry has been consistent in one thing throughout his career: he has wanted to be a Warrior. He has never tested free agency. He has turned down opportunities to chase maximum leverage. The likelihood that he waits for financial maneuvering reasons feels low given everything he has said publicly.

What the Extension Would Mean

For Curry, signing the extension would cement his legacy in the most literal way — a guaranteed commitment to finish his career in Golden State, barring extraordinary circumstances. He has four championships with the Warriors, two MVP awards, and the all-time three-point record. There is no unfinished business in terms of individual legacy. This is about the final chapter being written in the right uniform.

For the Warriors, securing Curry through 2028 ensures their franchise player stays home while they figure out who the next generation of championship contributors will be. The Christmas Day matchup against Denver already features Curry prominently in the league's marketing — he remains one of the most compelling draws in the sport.

What's Next

Saturday's window opening is the moment to watch. The Warriors and Curry's representatives are expected to engage quickly. Whether the deal is structured as the full two-year max or a shorter arrangement remains the last open question.

By Sunday morning, there is every reason to believe Stephen Curry will be a Warrior through his 40th year. Two days is all that stands between now and that confirmation.
