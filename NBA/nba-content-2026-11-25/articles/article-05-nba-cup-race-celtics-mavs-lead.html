NBA Cup Heats Up: Celtics and Mavericks Both Perfect at 3-0 as Quarterfinals Loom

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | November 25, 2026

The NBA Cup is entering its decisive final stretch, and two teams have done everything right so far. The Boston Celtics lead East Group C at 3-0. The Dallas Mavericks lead West Group A at 3-0. With group play wrapping up on November 27, both franchises look well-positioned to advance to the knockout quarterfinals and carry real momentum into December.

Meanwhile, the defending NBA Cup champions — the New York Knicks — are sitting at 0-2 in East Group B and facing the kind of pressure that nobody associated with the organization expected when the season started. That tension arrives at Madison Square Garden tonight when the Cavaliers come to town.

The NBA Cup is designed to generate exactly this kind of drama, and so far, the 2026 edition is delivering.

Boston and Dallas Set the Standard

The Celtics got to 3-0 the hard way on Tuesday night. Jayson Tatum erupted for a season-high 41 points against Washington, but Boston still had to sweat out a fourth-quarter surge from Anthony Davis and the Wizards before Baylor Scheierman's clutch eight-point run sealed the victory. The Celtics are 12-6 overall and playing with renewed purpose after a four-game losing streak that had their season narrative heading in the wrong direction.

Dallas made it look significantly easier. Cooper Flagg posted 24 points and 12 assists in a dominant 120-101 win over Denver in Ball Arena, and the Mavericks' 35-7 edge in fast break points spoke to how thoroughly they controlled the game. The Mavericks are 11-7 overall and playing the kind of high-energy, pace-and-space basketball that makes opponents uncomfortable. At 3-0 in Cup play, they have been one of the best stories of November.

Both teams benefit from having star players operating at their best. Tatum's 41-point night came after a stretch of below-expectation performances. Flagg is simply playing the best basketball of his two-year career. When the Cup quarterfinals arrive, neither team will be an easy draw.

Defending Champions in Crisis

The Knicks' Cup situation is genuinely surprising. New York won the tournament last season, navigating a competitive bracket with poise and emerging as one of the NBA's most satisfying storylines. This year, they cannot buy a Cup win.

At 0-2 in East Group B, the Knicks trail Miami — which leads the group at 2-0 — by two games with group play ending November 27. Even with two wins remaining in the group stage, New York would need help to claim the group and would be relying on wild card scenarios to advance. The math is not impossible, but it is unforgiving.

The Knicks' overall form is actually encouraging — a five-game winning streak in the regular season suggests the roster is healthy and the team is capable. The Cup, with its separate record and compressed schedule, has just not cooperated. Sometimes the two records diverge, and when they do, it creates exactly this kind of strange situation: a team playing well by one measure while failing by another.

What's Next

Group play ends November 27, and after that, the NBA Cup enters its knockout rounds. The Celtics and Mavericks are well-positioned to advance, though the specific quarterfinal pairings will depend on how the final group-stage games shake out.

For teams that are still alive in the standings, the next two days represent the final opportunity to secure a spot. The Knicks must win tonight against Cleveland and then again in their final group game — and need help — to have any realistic chance.

The NBA Cup has become one of the more compelling fixtures on the basketball calendar, delivering meaningful games in November when the league schedule can otherwise feel routine. With Boston and Dallas establishing themselves as frontrunners, and the defending champion Knicks fighting for their tournament lives, the 2026 Cup is living up to its reputation as must-watch basketball.

The quarterfinals are coming. The field is becoming clearer. The question now is which teams will be ready when the knockout rounds begin.
