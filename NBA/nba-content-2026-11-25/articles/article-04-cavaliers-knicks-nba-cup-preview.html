Knicks' Cup Defense on the Line: Cavaliers Come to MSG Tonight for Do-or-Die East Group B Clash

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | November 25, 2026

The New York Knicks are defending NBA Cup champions. They lifted the trophy last season and carried the title into 2026 as one of the league's signature teams. Right now, they are also 0-2 in NBA Cup East Group B and staring at the real possibility of watching the tournament they won last year end before December arrives.

Tonight at Madison Square Garden, the Knicks host the Cleveland Cavaliers in what amounts to a must-win game — not for playoff seeding, not for a road to the Finals, but for the chance to stay in the tournament they own. Tip-off is 7:30 PM ET.

The Knicks are playing well by conventional measures. A five-game overall winning streak has the team feeling good about its regular-season form. But the NBA Cup has a separate scoreboard, and on that scoreboard, New York has nothing to show. Two Cup losses have them at the bottom of East Group B while Miami sits atop the group at 2-0.

Cavaliers Come to Town With Injury News

Cleveland enters at 9-9 overall and 2-1 in Cup play — still very much in the conversation for a quarterfinal spot. But the Cavaliers will be without Evan Mobley tonight. The All-Star big man's absence opens the paint for New York's bigs, a potential advantage the Knicks will need to exploit if they're going to get this win.

For the Knicks, there is their own injury situation to manage. OG Anunoby, Deuce McBride, and Jordan Clarkson are all listed as questionable heading into tonight's game. If any of those three miss time, the depth Boston's Celtics have been celebrating becomes a need New York will have to address on the fly.

The matchup on paper still favors the Knicks, playing at home with the Cup on the line, the crowd energized, and Cleveland missing its best interior defender. But the Knicks have not performed in Cup play this season, and paper advantages mean little when the actual results keep coming up short.

Defending Champions Know What It Takes

The Knicks did not win the NBA Cup last season by accident. They navigated a competitive field, beat quality opponents in the knockout rounds, and delivered when the moment demanded it. That experience matters. Jalen Brunson and Karl-Anthony Towns have been in big moments before, and Madison Square Garden in a meaningful game is as good an environment as there is in basketball.

The challenge tonight is simpler than it looks: win the game, stay alive, and sort out the rest later. Miami's lead in the group is a problem for another day. Right now, the only task is to avoid falling to 0-3 in Cup play — a hole that would almost certainly be impossible to climb out of before group play ends November 27.

Group play ends in two days. After tonight, there is very little margin for error in what remains. A loss tonight would send the Knicks to the Cup's final game needing a win plus Miami to stumble, a combination of results that is possible but far from likely. A win tonight keeps the dream alive and returns some pressure to the Heat.

What's Next

Tonight's outcome shapes the final days of group play significantly. If New York wins, the group stays contested through November 27. If Cleveland wins, the Cavaliers move closer to advancing while the Knicks' title defense becomes a matter of regular-season record rather than tournament progression.

Beyond this game, the broader Cup picture is coming into focus. The Celtics and Mavericks sit at 3-0 in their respective groups and look like clear frontrunners. The quarterfinals will feature the kind of matchups that make mid-season basketball worth watching.

But all of that is for later. Tonight is about the Knicks finding a way at home, in the building they know better than anyone, in the tournament that belongs to them on paper. MSG will be loud. The question is whether New York shows up for the moment.
