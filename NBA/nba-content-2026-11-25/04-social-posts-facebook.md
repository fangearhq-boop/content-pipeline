### STORY 1: Celtics 131, Wizards 116 — Tatum 41 (Season-High) Snaps 4-Game Skid

**Long-Form Post**
**Window:** 9:00 AM ET

Jayson Tatum had been quiet lately, and Boston fans were starting to get nervous. Four straight losses had the Celtics looking like a team in search of an identity. Then came Tuesday night in Washington.

Tatum erupted for a season-high 41 points, burying 28 of them before halftime as the Celtics raced out to an 80-49 lead. He looked like himself again — attacking the rim, hitting threes with confidence, controlling the tempo. At one point it looked like a blowout in the making.

Then the Wizards had other ideas. Anthony Davis put up 33 points and 13 rebounds and Washington clawed back to within 117-112 in the fourth quarter. The momentum had shifted, the crowd at Capital One Arena was alive, and suddenly this felt like a potential collapse.

Baylor Scheierman had different plans. The Celtics guard scored eight consecutive points in the final minutes to put the game away for good — 131-116 final.

Paul George added 28 points and nine assists. Neemias Queta chipped in 21. Boston is 3-0 in NBA Cup East Group C play and snapped that four-game skid in the best possible way.

### STORY 2: Mavericks 120, Nuggets 101 — Flagg 24/12, Dallas 4th Straight Win

**Long-Form Post**
**Window:** 11:00 AM ET

Cooper Flagg is not slowing down. The Dallas Mavericks' second-year star posted 24 points and 12 assists in a dominant 120-101 win over the Denver Nuggets in Denver on Tuesday, keeping the Mavericks undefeated in NBA Cup West Group A play and extending their winning streak to four games.

The game told you everything about where these two teams are right now. Dallas finished with a 35-7 edge in fast break points. The Mavericks were flying in transition, turning every Denver turnover and missed shot into easy buckets on the other end.

The Nuggets actually made a game of it in the third quarter, cutting a 21-point deficit down to seven entering the fourth. But Dallas shut the door — outscoring Denver 36-24 in the final period to put it away.

Jamal Murray led Denver with 20 points, but the Nuggets shot just 40.5 percent from the field. This was a comprehensive win for the Mavericks.

Flagg, now averaging 24.6 points per game on the season, is making a compelling case as one of the NBA's best all-around players heading into December.

### STORY 3: Pistons 104, Magic 86 — Isaiah Joe Stars Without Cunningham

**Long-Form Post**
**Window:** 1:00 PM ET

Cade Cunningham was ruled out with right knee soreness just 30 minutes before tip-off. For some teams, that would be a crisis. For the Detroit Pistons, it was an opportunity.

Isaiah Joe stepped into the starting lineup and delivered a team-high 21 points. Daniss Jenkins ran the offense smoothly with 15 points and 10 assists. Detroit used an 18-0 second-quarter run — holding Franz Wagner to just two points in the first half — to seize control and never let go.

The Orlando Magic, who entered the game at 3-0 in NBA Cup play and looking to clinch a quarterfinal berth early, shot just 41 percent from the field and committed 20 turnovers. Desmond Bane's 21 points weren't nearly enough.

Detroit wins 104-86. The Pistons are 11-6 on the season and 2-1 in Cup play. Orlando drops to 7-10 and 3-1, their first Cup loss of the season.

The Cunningham injury bears watching — right knee soreness can linger — but Tuesday night showed this Pistons group has options.

### STORY 4: Cavaliers at Knicks Tonight — NBA Cup East Group B Showdown

**Long-Form Post**
**Window:** 4:00 PM ET

The New York Knicks are defending NBA Cup champions. They've been playing their best basketball of the season lately, riding a five-game overall win streak heading into tonight.

None of that matters in the NBA Cup, where the Knicks are 0-2 and staring at elimination.

Tonight at Madison Square Garden, New York hosts the Cleveland Cavaliers in what amounts to a must-win NBA Cup East Group B game (7:30 PM ET). The Cavs come in at 9-9 overall and 2-1 in Cup play. Miami leads the group at 2-0, but Cleveland can effectively end New York's tournament hopes with a win tonight.

Evan Mobley is out for Cleveland, which opens the paint for the Knicks' big men. OG Anunoby, Deuce McBride, and Jordan Clarkson are listed as questionable for New York — a potential concern for a team that needs everyone.

The Knicks won the NBA Cup last season and know what it takes to run this gauntlet. The question is whether they can flip the switch in Cup play tonight or whether defending their title slips away before December even arrives.

Group play ends November 27. After tonight, there's very little margin for error.
