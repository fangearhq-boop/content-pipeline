Joel Embiid Says He's Healthy — LeBron and the 76ers Have Their Answer Before Training Camp

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | August 5, 2026

Every major discussion about the Philadelphia 76ers' 2026-27 season eventually arrives at the same question: is Joel Embiid healthy?

He answered it this week. Embiid has publicly assured LeBron James — and the 76ers fan base — that he is healthy and ready to contribute from the first day of their partnership. That assurance came in late July, shortly after LeBron's signing was formalized, and it is the most encouraging public statement the 76ers could have hoped for entering August.

The question mattered because it always matters with Embiid. Over the course of his career, he has been one of the two or three most dominant players in the league when healthy. The qualification is real. He has missed significant time in multiple seasons. His playoff availability has been a recurring conversation. Any assessment of Philadelphia's title odds begins and ends with whether this version of Embiid stays on the floor.

The Lineup That Exists on Paper

The projected Philadelphia 76ers starting five for 2026-27: LeBron James, Joel Embiid, Jaylen Brown, Tyrese Maxey, and VJ Edgecombe.

Count the MVP awards: LeBron has four. Embiid has one. Brown was a Finals MVP-level contributor for years in Boston. Maxey has emerged as one of the best point guards in the Eastern Conference. Edgecombe is a young wing with elite athleticism. This is not a lineup assembled for marginal playoff performance. It is a lineup assembled to compete for a championship, and the Philadelphia Inquirer has already identified Embiid's health as the single biggest swing variable for whether it delivers one.

LeBron signed for two years at $7.94 million — a number that represents enormous sacrifice in dollar terms, structured specifically to give Philadelphia the cap flexibility to build this roster. Jaylen Brown arrived from Boston on July 6 in a blockbuster deal involving Paul George and future picks. The 76ers did not just add LeBron to a roster. They rebuilt the roster around a bet on this specific combination.

What Healthy Embiid Actually Means

When Joel Embiid is on the court and at full strength, he is the most versatile offensive center in the modern game. He can score in the post, hit a mid-range jumper, and step out to the three-point line. He forces every defense to make choices it cannot win. A healthy Embiid alongside LeBron — who at 41 still commands defensive attention in transition and as a secondary creator — and Maxey running pick-and-rolls means every possession has two or three legitimate threats that the defense must account for simultaneously.

The ceiling in that scenario is not just "playoffs." The ceiling is "genuine title contender" in a way that the Eastern Conference has not seen since the Celtics' run in recent years or the Bucks with Giannis in 2021. Miami now has Giannis. New York is defending a championship. But a healthy Philadelphia roster with this much star power and two-way capability would enter any playoff bracket as a legitimate threat to everyone in it.

Training Camp Is the Real Starting Gun

The early August assurances are encouraging. The real test comes in late September when training camp opens and LeBron and Embiid take their first reps together in a team setting. That is when the on-court chemistry — how their movement patterns interact, how defenses adjust, how Maxey operates in the space they create — gets its first real look.

LeBron has worked with Tyrese Maxey privately and has been in touch with the 76ers' staff throughout the summer. The relationship is already in development before camp begins. Embiid's health declaration is the piece that was missing. Now the 76ers have it.

What's Next

Training camp opens in late September. The NBA schedule drops August 13 or 14, which will reveal when LeBron makes his 76ers regular-season debut. If Embiid's assurance holds — if he shows up to camp healthy and remains healthy through October — Philadelphia will open the season as one of the most closely watched teams in the league. The era of LeBron James as a 76er officially begins not with a contract signing, but with the first practice in September when he and Embiid share a floor in a Sixers uniform. That day is coming. And right now, according to the biggest piece of the puzzle, everything is on track.
