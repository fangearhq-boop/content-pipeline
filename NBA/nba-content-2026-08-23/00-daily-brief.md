## Today's Stories

### STORY 1: Ja Morant Traded to Portland Trail Blazers — Tier 1
Ja Morant has been traded from the Memphis Grizzlies to the Portland Trail Blazers, ending his tenure in Memphis and giving Portland a franchise-altering star. The two-time All-Star and 2019 No. 2 overall pick brings elite athleticism and high-level playmaking to a Blazers team that has been in rebuild mode since the Damian Lillard era ended. Memphis turns the page on one of the most electric — and complicated — runs in recent NBA memory.

**Status:** NEW
**Key angle:** Portland's rebuild accelerates overnight — a legitimate Western Conference contender enters the conversation

### STORY 2: Stephen Curry Extension — 6 Days to August 29 Window — Tier 2
The extension window opens in 6 days (August 29), with a 2-year/$136.7 million max on the table. Warriors GM Mike Dunleavy is "pretty confident" a deal gets done, and multiple ESPN reporters have confirmed mutual interest. The stakes are unusually high: Golden State went 37-45 last season, failed to land LeBron, Giannis, or Anthony Davis, and without an extension Curry becomes an unrestricted free agent — for the first time in his career — in 2027. Brian Windhorst has floated a bolder shorter-term structure as an alternative.

**Status:** FOLLOW UP
**Key angle:** The greatest shooter ever faces the biggest decision of his career — and the Warriors need him more than ever

### STORY 3: Kawhi Leonard Investigation — All-Star Game Stays at Intuit Dome, Resolution Nears — Tier 2
The NBA has confirmed the 2026 All-Star Game will remain at Intuit Dome in Inglewood despite the ongoing Kawhi Leonard investigation. The league has narrowed its focus to a "failure to supervise" charge against the Clippers (improperly introducing Leonard to sponsors). HoopsHype reports Leonard has received no indication his contract will be voided; both he and the Raptors are operating as if the Toronto trade completes. Resolution expected around September 22.

**Status:** FOLLOW UP
**Key angle:** Keeping All-Star at Intuit Dome signals the NBA expects a resolution — not a crater

### STORY 4: Dillon Brooks Signs 3-Year, $73 Million Extension With Phoenix Suns — Tier 2
Dillon Brooks has agreed to a 3-year, $73 million extension with the Phoenix Suns through the 2029-30 season, off a career-high 20.2 points per game last season. The deal marks the evolution of Brooks from elite defensive specialist to legitimate scoring threat, and is part of a broader Phoenix push that also brought in Mark Williams, Miles Bridges, and Luke Kennard.

**Status:** NEW
**Key angle:** The enforcer turned scorer gets paid — Phoenix builds a tougher, scoring-forward identity

### STORY 5: France vs. Serbia — Orleans Rematch Today After Jokic's Belgrade Masterclass — Tier 2
Nikola Jokic posted 20 points, 9 rebounds, and 8 assists as Serbia defeated France 90-87 in Belgrade on August 20 (FIBA World Cup 2027 qualifier). Victor Wembanyama, France's captain, had 10 points and 6 turnovers. The rematch is today in Orleans. France (5-1 in Group L) looks to split the head-to-head; Wemby seeks redemption against the greatest center of his era.

**Status:** FOLLOW UP
**Key angle:** Wemby vs. Jokic, round two — the revenge game narrative is irresistible
