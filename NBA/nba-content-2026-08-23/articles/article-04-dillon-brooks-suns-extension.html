Dillon Brooks Signs 3-Year, $73 Million Extension With Phoenix Suns

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | August 23, 2026

Dillon Brooks has earned a reputation throughout his NBA career as the league's premier agitator — a physical, relentless defender who gets in opponents' heads and makes their lives miserable on both ends of the floor. Now, after breaking out as a genuine scorer, the Phoenix Suns have made a long-term commitment: a three-year, $73 million extension that keeps Brooks in the desert through the 2029-30 season.

The deal reflects the season Brooks just put together. He averaged a career-high 20.2 points per game, proving that his offensive game had evolved well beyond the defensive specialist label he carried for much of his career. His combination of physicality, shot creation off the dribble, and the mental toughness to take and make big shots in high-pressure moments made him an ideal fit in Phoenix's system.

Brooks began his career with the Memphis Grizzlies, where he quickly became one of the most talked-about wing defenders in the Western Conference. After stints in Houston and Memphis, he landed in Phoenix and found the environment where his offensive development accelerated. A career-high scoring average earned him the kind of contract that his defense alone might never have generated.

Phoenix Building Around a Tougher Identity

The Brooks extension is part of a broader Phoenix offseason push to get back into Western Conference contention. The Suns also signed center Mark Williams to a multiyear deal, acquired Miles Bridges, and added Luke Kennard to improve their wing depth and shooting. The organization is betting on a harder, more physical team identity — and Brooks is central to that vision.

At 30, Brooks enters the prime years of this evolved version of his game. Three years at $73 million averages roughly $24 million per season — fair market value for a player who just crossed 20 points per game for the first time. The Suns see him as a building block, not a bridge piece.

What's Next

Phoenix opens training camp in late September with Brooks locked in as a key piece of their 2026-27 roster. The Western Conference is as deep as it has been in years, with Oklahoma City, San Antonio, Minnesota, and now Portland all positioning for top spots. The Suns will need Brooks at his best — the scorer, the defender, the enforcer — to carve out their share of the playoff picture.

From enforcer to scorer to $73 million man. The journey of Dillon Brooks continues in Phoenix, and the next chapter looks promising.
