### STORY 2: Kawhi Leonard Trade Frozen — Dillon Brooks Says 'It Won't Happen'

**Long-Form Post (11:00 AM ET):**
The most complicated trade in recent NBA history is still frozen — and a new voice is now saying it may never happen.

Dillon Brooks, Kawhi Leonard's former teammate in Los Angeles, told streamer Neon in a conversation on July 31 that the Kawhi Leonard trade to Toronto "won't happen." He didn't elaborate. He didn't have to. The sentiment has been building for weeks.

Here's where things stand:

The NBA is investigating the Clippers for alleged salary cap circumvention connected to a $28 million endorsement deal routed to Kawhi through a green banking company called Aspiration — which also held a $300 million, 23-year naming rights deal with the Clippers. The man behind the arrangement has been convicted and sentenced to 14 years in prison. The Clippers say they're victims of fraud.

The NBA Players Association can demand CBA arbitration, which would send the case to a neutral arbiter with no hard deadline. Commissioner Silver said the investigation must end before the season begins. That deadline is now in serious doubt.

The Toronto Raptors agreed to send Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, draft picks, and more to Los Angeles. The trade was agreed on June 30. It is now August 4. Nothing has moved.

Kawhi Leonard's 2026-27 salary is $50.3 million. His entire career future hangs in the balance.

At what point does the Raptors-Clippers deal collapse entirely?
