The NBA Schedule Drops August 13-14 — Here Are the Dates Every Fan Should Be Waiting For

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | August 4, 2026

The NBA schedule is expected to drop on August 13 or 14. In most years, that is a pleasant moment for fans — you learn when your team plays, you start circling rivalry games, and you build out your season-watching calendar.

This year, it is something else entirely.

The 2026-27 NBA season has more must-see storylines stacked up than almost any in recent memory. LeBron James is making his Philadelphia 76ers debut. The New York Knicks are raising their first championship banner in 53 years. Giannis Antetokounmpo is playing his first game as a Miami Heat player. Victor Wembanyama is entering his fourth season with a $252 million extension locked in.

Every one of those moments gets a date on August 13 or 14. Here is what to watch for when the schedule drops.

LeBron's Philadelphia Debut

The most anticipated moment in the schedule announcement is simple: when does LeBron James play his first regular-season game as a Philadelphia 76er?

He signed a two-year, $7.94 million deal on July 26. He has been working out with Tyrese Maxey in Los Angeles. He will live in New York and commute. But none of that has felt completely real yet, because we have not seen him in a 76ers jersey on a basketball court in a game that matters.

The schedule will tell us when that happens. If the NBA opens the season on October 20 — as has been widely reported — there is a real possibility the 76ers are part of that opening night. A Philadelphia-New York matchup on October 20 would be the single highest-profile opening-night game in years: LeBron's debut against the defending champions, in New York, with the Knicks raising their banner before tip-off.

The NBA's schedule makers are not unaware of this. Whether they serve it up on night one is the question.

Knicks Championship Banner Night

October 20 is already confirmed as the date the Knicks will raise their 2026 championship banner at Madison Square Garden. Jalen Brunson led the Knicks to their first title since 1973. He scored 45 points in the Game 5 clincher — a 94-90 victory over the San Antonio Spurs. The banner night will be one of the great celebrations in franchise history.

Whoever the Knicks play that night is walking into a building that will be louder than any regular-season game has a right to be. Finding out who draws that assignment is one of the most entertaining revelations in the entire schedule announcement.

Giannis's Heat Opener — And the Milwaukee Reunion

Giannis Antetokounmpo spent 13 seasons in Milwaukee before being traded to Miami. At some point in the 2026-27 season, he will play the Bucks. The schedule will tell us when that is — and whether it happens early enough to feel like a fresh wound or late enough to feel like a proper homecoming.

The Heat's preseason already includes an October 3 opener in Quebec City against the Toronto Raptors, and regular-season home games to follow at Kaseya Center. When Giannis walks into Fiserv Forum for the first time wearing Heat colors, Milwaukee will find out exactly how it feels about this transition.

Wembanyama vs. Embiid

Victor Wembanyama and Joel Embiid are two of the most physically unique centers to play in the NBA at the same time. Both exceed 7 feet. Both can shoot from range, defend at elite levels, and affect the game in ways most players cannot. They play the same position. They will meet at least twice in the regular season.

Those dates, whenever they land, will be the most-watched regular-season games of the season that do not involve LeBron playing against someone who knows him well.

What's Next

The NBA schedule drops on or around August 13-14. When it lands, the full architecture of the 2026-27 season becomes visible for the first time — LeBron's debut window, the banner night opponent, Giannis's homecoming game, the Embiid-Wembanyama dates, and every other matchup that fans have been imaging since free agency ended. The preseason begins in early October. Opening night is October 20. After a summer of transactions, the waiting is almost over.
