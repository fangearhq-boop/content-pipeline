The NBA Goes Global: Blazers vs. BC London Lions Makes History, Cavs Head to Spain

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | September 8, 2026

The 2026-27 NBA preseason includes landmark international moments: Portland meets a British club for the first time in league history, Cleveland trains in Spain, and Canada Games bring the game north of the border.

The NBA's global reach just got a little wider.

When the 2026-27 preseason gets underway on October 3, several of the season's most compelling moments will take place far outside the United States. From a historic first for British basketball to a training camp in southern Spain, the league is reminding fans that basketball's footprint extends well beyond the arenas of North America.

Portland Trail Blazers vs. BC London Lions: A Historic First

The headlining international moment comes on October 12, when the Portland Trail Blazers face BC London Lions in what will be the first-ever contest between an NBA team and a British professional basketball club.

The London Lions are the most successful team in the British Basketball League, with a growing fanbase and an increasing number of international players on their roster. For British basketball fans, the game represents a landmark moment — a chance to see their homegrown club compete directly against one of the sport's established professional franchises.

For the Blazers, it is a preseason game, a chance to work out the kinks in a newly assembled roster. For the broader basketball community in the United Kingdom, it is something considerably more significant: a validation of how far the sport has grown on British soil.

Cleveland Cavaliers Head to Spain

The Cleveland Cavaliers will hold training camp in Andalusia, Spain from September 30 through October 5, using The Embassy — a high-performance sports and wellness complex — as their base of operations.

The facility has an interesting connection to the franchise: it was partly designed by Jose Calderon, the Spanish point guard who spent two seasons with the Cavaliers and maintains strong ties to the organization. Calderon's involvement helped shepherd the partnership between the Cavs and the Spanish facility.

International training camps have become a way for franchises to build team chemistry in an environment stripped of the usual distractions. Cleveland's own roster adjustments include questions around the salary cap implications of their James Harden deal and where center Walker Kessler fits following the acquisition from Utah. The Spain camp gives head coach a controlled environment to sort those details out.

NBA Canada Games and the Broader Calendar

The NBA Canada Games will bring preseason action to Quebec City and Vancouver, a regular feature of the league's preseason calendar and one that consistently draws passionate crowds from Canadian fans who rarely get live NBA basketball in their own cities.

The full preseason schedule runs October 3-11. Training camps open September 29, with veterans reporting a day earlier on September 28. The regular season tips off October 20, featuring the marquee opener at Madison Square Garden.

Between now and Opening Night, the league's international programming gives fans around the world a preview of what's coming — and gives teams the chance to grow beyond the borders that once defined American professional basketball.
