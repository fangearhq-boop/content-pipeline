### STORY 2: Kawhi Leonard Trade Pending

**FB Long-Form Post**
Window: 11:00 AM ET

Toronto Raptors fans, we have an update — and it's still "we're waiting."

The Kawhi Leonard trade from the LA Clippers to the Toronto Raptors is still not officially finalized. The NBA is conducting an investigation into an alleged undisclosed sponsorship arrangement between Leonard and Aspiration, a Clippers partner. Until the league completes its review, the trade cannot be formally ratified.

The terms are agreed: Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, two unprotected first-round picks, a swap, and two second-rounders heading to LA in exchange for Kawhi. Toronto's training camp opens September 28 in Quebec City, and Kawhi has reportedly said he expects to be there.

But right now, "expects to" is the operative phrase.

The good news for Raptors fans: there's no indication the investigation threatens to block the trade entirely. The league is working through the paperwork. The frustrating reality: with training camp 20 days out, every day this lingers is another day of roster uncertainty for Toronto.

Stay tuned.

### STORY 3: Portland Trail Blazers

**FB Long-Form Post**
Window: 11:00 AM ET

The Portland Trail Blazers just made one of the most intriguing moves of the entire offseason — and it barely made a ripple compared to the LeBron and Kawhi stories.

Damian Lillard is back. After missing most of the 2025-26 season with an Achilles injury, team doctors have cleared Dame to return for training camp. At 35, Lillard's health is the key question, but the organization is confident.

And then there's Ja Morant.

Portland acquired the two-time All-Star point guard in a trade, creating one of the most dynamic backcourts in the Western Conference. Morant, 26, is entering his prime. Lillard, when healthy, is one of the all-time great late-game scorers. Together, they give the Blazers something special.

Are they a championship contender? Probably not this year. But they are going to be some of the most fun basketball to watch in the entire league. Portland opens training camp September 29.

### STORY 4: International Preseason

**FB Long-Form Post**
Window: 1:00 PM ET

The NBA is going global this preseason, and some of these matchups are genuinely historic.

On October 12, the Portland Trail Blazers will face BC London Lions — making history as the first time an NBA team has ever played a British club. The game is part of a growing push to expand basketball's footprint in the United Kingdom, where the sport has been growing rapidly.

Meanwhile, the Cleveland Cavaliers are packing their bags for Spain. Their training camp will run September 30 through October 5 at The Embassy facility in Andalusia — a state-of-the-art sports complex that was partly designed by former Cavalier Jose Calderon.

The NBA Canada Games will also bring preseason action to Quebec City and Vancouver.

Training camps open September 29. Preseason play begins October 3. The regular season starts October 20.

It's almost time.

### STORY 5: 76ers Depth Chart

**FB Long-Form Post**
Window: 4:00 PM ET

Let's talk about what the 2026-27 Philadelphia 76ers actually look like.

Starting five: Tyrese Maxey at the point, Jaylen Brown on the wing, LeBron James at small forward, a power forward to be determined, and Joel Embiid at center.

That's a lineup that, on paper, can compete with anyone in the Eastern Conference.

The supporting cast is no joke either. Kentavious Caldwell-Pope brings championship experience and perimeter defense. Anfernee Simons provides scoring punch off the bench. Ariel Hukporti, Dean Wade, and Labaron Philon Jr. round out the depth.

The one variable nobody can overlook: Joel Embiid has played just 57 games over the last two seasons. When healthy, he is arguably the best center in basketball. But the Sixers have built this roster to win now — and "now" requires Embiid on the floor.

Opening Night is October 20 at Madison Square Garden. The Sixers open at home October 22 against the Cavaliers.

The talent is there. The health question remains the story.
