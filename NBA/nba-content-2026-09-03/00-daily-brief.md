### STORY 1: NBA Hammers Clippers — 5 Picks Stripped, $30M Fine, Ballmer Suspended
- **Tier:** 1
- **Status:** NEW
- **Angle:** NBA drops historic penalties on the Clippers following Kawhi Leonard salary cap circumvention investigation; Ballmer suspended; Kawhi pays $700K but avoids suspension; trade to Toronto now clear
- **Key facts:**
  - Investigation by Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz found Clippers funneled improper endorsements to Leonard through Aspiration Partners, Boingo Wireless, Daktronics, Lockton Insurance
  - Clippers are a prior offender of salary cap circumvention rules
  - Penalty: 5 consecutive first-round picks forfeited starting 2029
  - Fine: $30 million
  - Steve Ballmer suspended; Lawrence Frank and Gillian Zucker also suspended
  - 5 years of strict league monitoring
  - Kawhi Leonard: $700,000 restitution, no suspension
  - Dennis Robertson (Leonard's uncle/former business rep, fired by Kawhi in June) banned from NBA business dealings
  - Leonard: "no knowledge of any intent on anyone's part to circumvent the salary cap"
  - Ruling enables Leonard trade to Toronto to proceed
- **Sources:** NBA.com official press release, CNN, Yardbarker, Last Word on Basketball
