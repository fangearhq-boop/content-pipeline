Captain Wemby Dominates in Stockholm: France 7-1 After FIBA Qualifying Window Sweep

By Damon Pierce | September 3, 2026 | Hoop Heroes

Victor Wembanyama is making European competition look easy.

The San Antonio Spurs center and France national team captain posted 18 points on a dazzling 6-of-7 shooting as France defeated Sweden 84-69 on August 30 at the Avicii Arena in Stockholm, completing a qualifying window sweep and improving France's record to 7-1 in Group L of the 2027 FIBA Basketball World Cup European qualifiers.

The game drew a record crowd of 14,750 — many of whom came specifically to watch the 22-year-old phenom who is widely considered the most exciting player in professional basketball.

A Statement Performance

Wembanyama's efficiency — 6-of-7 from the floor — underscored why international opponents struggle to slow him down. At 7-foot-3 with guard-like ball-handling and the shot-blocking instincts of a generational defender, he presents challenges that simply cannot be replicated in practice.

France pulled away decisively in the fourth quarter, outscoring Sweden 20-5 in the closing stretch to turn a competitive game into a statement win. That closing surge — Wemby orchestrating, finishing, and defending — was a preview of what the Spurs expect from him heading into the 2026-27 NBA season.

France's World Cup Push

With a 7-1 record in Group L, France is comfortably on track to qualify for the 2027 FIBA Basketball World Cup. The window (August 24 through September 1) was their strongest stretch yet in qualifying.

Wembanyama, named France captain earlier this summer by taking the armband from veteran Rudy Gobert, has embraced the leadership role enthusiastically. His return to international play for the first time since the 2024 Paris Olympics was long anticipated by French basketball fans, and he has not disappointed.

Across his two appearances in this qualifying window, Wemby averaged 25.0 points, 11.5 rebounds, and 3.1 blocks per game in the 2025-26 NBA season — becoming the first unanimous Defensive Player of the Year in NBA history. Those numbers translated directly to the FIBA stage.

The Global Qualifier Picture

France was not alone in making noise during the August window. Turkey became the first nation to officially qualify for the 2027 FIBA World Cup, finishing the European qualifying window at a perfect 8-0. Serbia also swept their window, defeating Italy 76-64 to remain in strong qualifying position.

In the Americas, Canada stayed unbeaten at 8-0, with Philadelphia 76ers guard VJ Edgecombe leading the Bahamas to a 100-82 win over Panama with 25 points. The global pool for the 2027 World Cup — to be hosted in the United States — is filling up with talent.

What's Next

France's next qualifying window has not yet been scheduled for fall 2026, but with a 7-1 record, Les Bleus have significant room to work with.

For Wembanyama, attention now turns back to the NBA. The San Antonio Spurs open training camp in late September, and the 2026-27 season tips off October 20. The Spurs, coming off a Western Conference Finals appearance where they fell to the New York Knicks in five games, are a genuine title contender. Wemby and the Spurs will be one of the most-watched teams in basketball this winter.

If these FIBA performances are any indication, the leap he made in his third NBA season will continue into his fourth.
