Kawhi Leonard Trade to Toronto Finally Cleared — Expected Official Within Days

By Marcus Cole | September 3, 2026 | Hoop Heroes

The long wait is nearly over. With the NBA's investigation into the Los Angeles Clippers officially concluded on September 2, the agreed trade sending Kawhi Leonard to the Toronto Raptors is expected to become official within days — reuniting one of the game's premier two-way talents with the franchise where he cemented his legacy.

Leonard and the Raptors agreed to the trade on June 30, but the deal was frozen on July 9 when the NBA placed it on hold pending the outcome of the Clippers salary cap investigation. Now that the investigation — and the historic penalties that followed — are complete, there is no remaining obstacle.

The Deal: What's Moving

Under the terms agreed in June, Leonard goes to Toronto in exchange for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, unprotected first-round picks in 2031 and 2033, a 2027 first-round pick swap, and two second-round picks. For the Clippers, it's a youth-and-picks return. For the Raptors, it's a star with a proven championship pedigree.

Leonard, who turns 35 this season, is expected to pass a physical and be available when the Raptors open training camp on September 28 in Quebec City. According to reports, Leonard himself has been operating under the belief that the trade would clear before camp — and now it has.

Full Circle: Back to Toronto

The narrative writes itself. In 2019, Kawhi Leonard led the Toronto Raptors to their first NBA championship, defeating the Golden State Warriors in six games while averaging 28.5 points per game in the Finals. He left that summer in free agency to sign with the Clippers, and the city grieved.

Six seasons later, after three playoff exits in the second round and a Finals appearance that never materialized, Leonard returns. Toronto is ready. The city never fully healed from his departure, and the chance to see him wear a Raptors jersey again — this time potentially for the long term — carries enormous emotional weight.

For the Raptors, the acquisition signals a shift from rebuilding to contending. Brandon Ingram and Gradey Dick gave them youth and scoring, but they needed a proven star-level presence. Leonard provides that immediately.

Eastern Conference Implications

Toronto's arrival as a genuine contender reshapes the Eastern Conference picture. The defending champion New York Knicks are the clear No. 1, but Miami with Giannis Antetokounmpo and now Toronto with Kawhi Leonard push the depth of legitimate contenders to levels not seen in recent memory.

Philadelphia's combination of LeBron James, Jaylen Brown, and Joel Embiid — if healthy — is perhaps the highest upside combination in basketball. Cleveland, Detroit, Indiana, and Boston all have playoff aspirations. Getting out of the East next spring might be harder than winning the Finals itself.

What's Next

The trade is expected to be finalized in the coming days, with Leonard's physical the primary remaining step. Once official, the Raptors can begin building chemistry heading into training camp.

The early season schedule will be revealing. Toronto faces Philadelphia in the season opener on October 20 at Madison Square Garden — with the Knicks raising their first championship banner since 1973. Leonard and the Raptors will want to make a statement from day one.

After years of patience and months of uncertainty, Kawhi Leonard is finally going home.
