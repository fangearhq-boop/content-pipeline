## Today's Top Stories

### STORY 1: Kawhi Leonard — Federal Criminal Investigation Opens (T1 FOLLOW UP)
The Department of Justice has opened a criminal investigation into the Los Angeles Clippers' financial dealings with Kawhi Leonard. The NBA concluded its own investigation last week — fining the Clippers $30 million, stripping five first-round picks, and suspending Steve Ballmer for one year — and the trade to Toronto is now proceeding. But federal prosecutors in the Eastern District of New York have launched a separate probe into the arena scoreboard kickback arrangement at the center of the case. The DOJ escalation is the major new development today.

### STORY 2: Stephen Curry — Extension Decision Window Narrows (T1 NEW)
Stephen Curry is eligible for a two-year, $136.7 million extension with the Golden State Warriors and has yet to sign it. Media day on September 28 is emerging as the key looming date for both sides. The Warriors have offered Curry three options: one-year max, two-year max, or wait for free agency. Curry has committed to finishing his career in Golden State but has only said the decision will happen "at the right time." The extension defines the Warriors' competitive window for the next two seasons.

### STORY 3: Jalen Duren / Pistons Stalemate Deepens (T2 FOLLOW UP)
The Pistons have formally offered Jalen Duren a five-year, $190 million extension. Duren wants $200 million. The $10 million gap has not moved, and the October 1 qualifying-offer deadline is now 19 days away. If no deal is done, Duren is expected to sign the $9.6 million QO and enter unrestricted free agency next summer — a significant risk for Detroit's rebuild given Duren was a third-team All-NBA selection last season.

### STORY 4: Tyler Herro / Bucks Rebuild — Hometown Chip on Shoulder (T2 FOLLOW UP)
Tyler Herro, a Whitnall High School alum from Greenfield, Wisconsin, is entering his first training camp as a Milwaukee Buck with an explicit chip on his shoulder. New head coach Taylor Jenkins is establishing culture from day one. Herro said "we want to win as many games as possible" and embraces the underdog status following the Giannis trade. New supporting cast: Kel'el Ware, Jaime Jaquez Jr., Kasparas Jakucionis, Myles Turner, and Nate Ament (13th pick).

### STORY 5: Portland Trail Blazers — Lillard-Morant Backcourt Era Begins (T2 NEW)
The Portland Trail Blazers enter the 2026-27 season with one of the most exciting backcourts in the NBA: Damian Lillard returning from a torn Achilles that cost him all of last season, plus newly acquired Ja Morant (traded from Memphis this summer). The tandem gives Portland an explosive offensive pairing — but questions about spacing, defense, and health are real heading into camp.
