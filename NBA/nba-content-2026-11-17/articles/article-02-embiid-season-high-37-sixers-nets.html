Embiid Drops Season-High 37, Brown Adds 31 as 76ers Roll Past Nets 135-123 Without LeBron

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | November 17, 2026

BROOKLYN — LeBron James sat out Monday night's game with his recurring back and sciatica issue. Joel Embiid and Jaylen Brown made sure nobody noticed.

Embiid posted a season-high 37 points and Brown added 31 as the Philadelphia 76ers emphatically handled the Brooklyn Nets 135-123, sending a clear message about the depth and resilience of this squad. The two All-Stars combined for 68 points, turning what could have been a difficult road game without their third superstar into a statement performance.

Embiid at His Dominant Best

Embiid's 37-point performance was vintage — a showcase of footwork, physicality, and touch that few centers in NBA history can replicate. The All-Star center attacked the Nets' interior defense relentlessly, creating easy looks both off the dribble and from the mid-range, while also drawing fouls at a high rate. His season-high output at this point in the schedule demonstrates the kind of elite form that makes him a perennial MVP candidate.

What made the performance particularly significant was the context. With LeBron managing his health, the 76ers needed Embiid to be more than just a scorer — they needed him to be the entire offense. He delivered on that front without hesitation, taking over the game when it mattered most.

Brown Provides Elite Co-Star Firepower

Jaylen Brown's 31 points complemented Embiid's big night perfectly. Brown attacked Brooklyn's perimeter defense with his combination of athleticism and improving skill set, scoring from multiple levels and providing the secondary threat that forced the Nets to make impossible defensive decisions all night.

When defenses collapsed on Embiid in the paint, Brown punished them from the perimeter and in transition. When Brooklyn tried to stay attached to Brown on the perimeter, Embiid dominated in the post. The Nets had no solution, and it showed in the final margin.

LeBron's Absence Becomes a Footnote

LeBron James has been managing a low back and sciatica issue throughout the early portion of the season, sitting out select games as the medical staff takes a cautious approach. With a long season ahead, the 76ers are clearly managing his workload strategically rather than risking any long-term damage.

The fact that Philadelphia can win by double digits without their most famous player speaks volumes about how well the roster is constructed. When Embiid and Brown perform at this level, the 76ers don't just win — they dominate.

Nets Continue to Struggle

Brooklyn's 123-point output was not the problem — the Nets can score. Their inability to stop Philadelphia was the story on this night. The Nets' young roster continues to work through growing pains, and facing an Embiid-Brown combination at their peak is a difficult test for any team, let alone one still developing its defensive identity.

What's Next

The 76ers will look to continue building momentum with LeBron James expected to return when his back issue permits, potentially as soon as this week. The prospect of adding a healthy LeBron to a team where Embiid and Brown are both performing at an elite level is a daunting one for the rest of the Eastern Conference. The Sixers look like a genuine championship contender, and nights like Monday reinforce why this team was built to win now. For Brooklyn, the focus remains on development and finding the right combinations to compete in a rugged Eastern Conference.
