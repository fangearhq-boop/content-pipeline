## Today's Stories

### STORY 1: Klay Thompson Signs With Miami Heat — Tier 1
Klay Thompson has signed a two-year, nearly $13 million deal with the Miami Heat after the Dallas Mavericks bought out the final year of his contract. The four-time NBA champion and five-time All-Star joins Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bam Adebayo in Miami, providing championship-proven shooting to a Heat squad that lost Tyler Herro and Norman Powell this offseason. Thompson averaged 11.7 points on 39.3% shooting in 69 games last season — a career-worst — but Heat leadership views him as an ideal floor-spacer and locker room veteran for the new Giannis era.

**Status:** NEW
**Key angle:** Championship DNA meets Miami's new Big Three ambitions

### STORY 2: Kawhi Leonard Case Could Create League-Wide Salary-Cap Loophole — Tier 1
Forbes analysis warns that the NBA's ruling in the Kawhi Leonard/Clippers investigation could establish a precedent allowing teams to use sponsor introductions as a gray area to circumvent the salary cap. The Clippers and NBAPA are both preparing legal challenges to any ruling that restricts team-to-player sponsor introductions. The investigation (centered on a $28M Aspiration deal and the Daktronics arrangement) remains ongoing, with resolution still targeted around September 22. The Board of Governors mid-September meeting is the key inflection point.

**Status:** FOLLOW UP
**Key angle:** The loophole angle elevates this beyond one team's drama — it affects the entire league

### STORY 3: LeBron James Confirms 24th Season at Fanatics Fest NYC — Tier 2
LeBron James hosted a live podcast at Fanatics Fest NYC alongside Tyrese Haliburton, where he discussed his decision to return for a 24th NBA season with the Philadelphia 76ers. LeBron called Philadelphia his "last decision" in a 2-year, $8 million deal. The 76ers' title odds moved from 20-1 to 10-1 after his signing. LeBron also said he strongly considered retirement before choosing Philly.

**Status:** FOLLOW UP (LeBron/76ers signing was July 24; Fanatics Fest confirmation is today)
**Key angle:** LeBron's introspective confirmation — retirement nearly happened

### STORY 4: Stephen Curry Extension — 7 Days to August 29 Window — Tier 2
Stephen Curry's extension window opens in exactly 7 days (August 29). Reports indicate the likely structure has shifted from the 2-year/$136.7M max to a 1-year/$62.6M deal, giving Curry maximum flexibility heading into what would be his final contract season at age 38. Warriors GM Mike Dunleavy Jr. remains "pretty confident" a deal will get done, telling reporters Curry "really, really wants to finish his career as a Warrior." Without an extension, Curry becomes a UFA for the first time in 2027.

**Status:** FOLLOW UP
**Key angle:** 1-year deal structure shifts the story — it's now a year-by-year gamble for Golden State

### STORY 5: DeMar DeRozan Signs With Denver Nuggets — Tier 2
Six-time All-Star DeMar DeRozan has agreed to a 1-year, $3.88 million veteran minimum deal with the Denver Nuggets, per ESPN and NBA.com. DeRozan joins Nikola Jokic and Jamal Murray as an additional playmaker, providing Denver with a reliable mid-range scorer and ball-handler. He averaged 18.4 points over 77 games for Sacramento last season. The 37-year-old chose Denver over interest from the Heat, Wizards and Pelicans.

**Status:** NEW
**Key angle:** Veteran depth move — Nuggets quietly building around Jokic to make another Finals run
