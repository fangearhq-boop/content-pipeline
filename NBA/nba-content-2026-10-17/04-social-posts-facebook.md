The biggest unresolved decision: the New York Knicks must choose between James Wiseman and Bruce Brown for their last roster spot.

### STORY 3: Clippers Injury Crisis

**Long-Form Post (4:00 PM ET)**
The Los Angeles Clippers are entering the season without three rotation players — and no return date for the most important one.

Brandon Ingram has not played since April 29. He went down in Game 5 of the first-round playoff series against Toronto with a partially torn Achilles in his right heel. Heel surgery came on May 8. Coach Ty Lue gave an encouraging progress update on October 14, but the Clippers have not provided a timeline for his return. He will not play when the season opens October 21 against Sacramento.

Max Strus will also miss the opener with a partially torn plantar fascia in his right foot. Yanic Konan Niederhauser is out as well.

That leaves Darius Garland — acquired this offseason — as the primary offensive option, alongside rookie Keaton Wagler stepping into an expanded role. Kobe Sanders is optimistically expected to be available.

The Clippers' season is built around Ingram. Without him, they're hoping Garland can keep the team afloat until the franchise cornerstone can return. The question is when — and whether they can stay in the hunt long enough for it to matter. #Clippers #NBA #HoopHeroes

Otega Oweh has torn his right Achilles tendon and will miss the entire 2026-27 season. The Oklahoma City Thunder lose a rotation piece they were counting on, and the timing couldn't be more painful — they open Monday night at San Antonio in a Western Conference Finals rematch.

The Spurs eliminated OKC in Game 7 last June, winning on the Thunder's home court to reach the Finals. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, the two-time MVP, is healthy and ready to face San Antonio on Monday. But losing any piece of your depth before Game 1 is a blow.

The Thunder have championship aspirations. An Achilles injury is a long-haul recovery that means Oweh won't factor into any of it this year. OKC will need to find minutes somewhere else — and potentially look at the waiver wire as the season develops. #Thunder #NBA #HoopHeroes

### STORY 5: Nuggets Strawther MRI
