### Long-Form Post Today is the most stressful day of the NBA calendar for bubble players. The non-guaranteed waiver deadline hits at 4 PM CT, and every team in the league must reach its final roster by then. The biggest unresolved decision: the New York Knicks must choose between James Wiseman and Bruce Brown for their last roster spot. Wiseman, the former No. 2 overall pick, is the frontrunner. He's 24, athletic, and fits the system as a backup big behind Mitchell Robinson. Brown, a versatile veteran wing, would likely need to find another landing spot if the Knicks pass on him. Yesterday, the Knicks already waived three players: Drew Eubanks (torn pectoral muscle, season over), Ochai Agbaji, and Tony Bradley. Today's decision is the last one before the defending champions defend their title banner on Monday. Around the league, the Jazz, Nets, and Hawks all made roster cuts yesterday. Expect more moves today as the clock ticks toward 4 PM CT. For the players who don't get a call — this is where seasons end before they begin. #Knicks #NBA #HoopHeroes

### Long-Form Post The Los Angeles Clippers are entering the season without three rotation players — and no return date for the most important one. Brandon Ingram has not played since April 29. He went down in Game 5 of the first-round playoff series against Toronto with a partially torn Achilles in his right heel. Heel surgery came on May 8. Coach Ty Lue gave an encouraging progress update on October 14, but the Clippers have not provided a timeline for his return. He will not play when the season opens October 21 against Sacramento. Max Strus will also miss the opener with a partially torn plantar fascia in his right foot. Yanic Konan Niederhauser is out as well. That leaves Darius Garland — acquired this offseason — as the primary offensive option, alongside rookie Keaton Wagler stepping into an expanded role. Kobe Sanders is optimistically expected to be available. The Clippers' season is built around Ingram. Without him, they're hoping Garland can keep the team afloat until the franchise cornerstone can return. The question is when — and whether they can stay in the hunt long enough for it to matter. #Clippers #NBA #HoopHeroes
