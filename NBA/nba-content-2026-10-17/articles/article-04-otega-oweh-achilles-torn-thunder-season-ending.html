Otega Oweh Tears Achilles, Out for 2026-27 Season — Thunder Take Depth Hit Before WCF Rematch

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | October 17, 2026

Oklahoma City Thunder | Otega Oweh | Injuries | Achilles | Opening Night

Oklahoma City Thunder forward Otega Oweh has torn his right Achilles tendon and will miss the entire 2026-27 NBA season, the team has announced. The injury is a significant depth loss for a Thunder squad that opens its season in three days against the San Antonio Spurs — a Western Conference Finals rematch that already carried enormous emotional weight before this setback.

The Injury and Its Impact

Oweh's Achilles tear removes a legitimate rotation piece from Oklahoma City's roster before the season even begins. He was considered part of the Thunder's wing depth, providing defense and energy off the bench in a team that already had significant talent around Shai Gilgeous-Alexander.

An Achilles tendon rupture is one of the most serious injuries in professional basketball. The recovery timeline typically runs 9 to 12 months, meaning Oweh's season is effectively over and his status heading into next year will depend on how well and how quickly the repair heals. For Oklahoma City, the immediate concern is absorbing the loss and redistributing minutes early in a year with championship aspirations.

The Bigger Picture: Monday Night Still Arrives

Oweh's injury stings, but it does not change the narrative of Monday's Opening Night game at San Antonio. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, the two-time consecutive MVP and the favorite to add a third in 2026-27, is healthy and ready to face the team that sent him home in Game 7 last June.

The Western Conference Finals was a seven-game series that ended with the Spurs winning on Oklahoma City's court — one of the most painful outcomes in franchise history. Victor Wembanyama led San Antonio to the title after defeating the Knicks in the NBA Finals. Now, four months later, the Thunder are the first opponent on the defending Western Conference champions' schedule.

SGA has had the summer to process Game 7. The Thunder will make their statement without Oweh in the lineup.

What's Next

The Thunder travel to San Antonio for the Opening Night matchup on October 20 (9:30 PM ET on NBC). Oweh will begin his recovery and is expected to be re-evaluated as the season progresses, though returning before next season is unlikely. Watch for Oklahoma City to potentially explore the waiver wire or pursue a trade to address the depth void in the coming weeks.
