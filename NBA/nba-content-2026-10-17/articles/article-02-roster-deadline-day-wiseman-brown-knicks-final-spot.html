Roster Deadline Day: Wiseman vs. Brown and the NBA's Final Cuts Before Opening Night

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | October 17, 2026

NBA Roster Moves | New York Knicks | James Wiseman | Bruce Brown | Waiver Deadline

At 4:00 PM CT today, the NBA's non-guaranteed waiver deadline closes. Every team in the league must be at 15 players plus three two-way contracts by that moment. For the defending champion New York Knicks, it means one final decision: James Wiseman or Bruce Brown.

The Knicks have already made their big moves. They waived Drew Eubanks on Thursday — the big man was lost for the season after suffering a torn pectoral muscle — along with guards Ochai Agbaji and Tony Bradley. One spot remains. Two players are competing for it.

The Wiseman Case

James Wiseman, the former No. 2 overall pick selected by Golden State in the 2020 draft, has spent six professional seasons trying to fulfill the potential that made him one of the most coveted prospects in years. He is 24 years old, a legitimate 7-foot center with an exceptional athletic profile, and he represents exactly the kind of backup big-man depth the Knicks need behind Mitchell Robinson.

Early in camp, Wiseman was considered the frontrunner. His ability to protect the rim, run the pick-and-roll, and stay out of foul trouble makes him a reasonable fit alongside Brunson-led lineups. His development stalled in Golden State and never fully righted itself in subsequent stops, but the Knicks believe the structure of their system could help him.

The Brown Situation

Bruce Brown has been a useful NBA player throughout his career — a physical, switchable wing who can guard multiple positions and make winning plays without needing touches. He is 26 and still in his prime, and several teams showed interest in him during this preseason period.

If the Knicks pass on Brown, the expectation around the league is that he would need to find a landing spot via trade or a quick free-agent signing with another team. A player of his caliber is unlikely to stay unsigned for long. But a spot on the defending champions' roster — even a backup role — carries its own appeal.

Around the League

The Knicks are not alone in making final decisions today. The Jazz cut Taylor Funk and Chance Moore on Thursday. The Brooklyn Nets released Ben Humrichous. The Atlanta Hawks waived Giorgi Bezhanishvili. More moves are expected throughout the morning as the 4 PM CT clock ticks toward zero.

For every cut, there is a player whose season just ended before it started — or who will spend the next few days hoping for a phone call from a team with a need. The waiver wire will be active. Injuries during the early weeks of the regular season will create openings. The players released today are not necessarily done for the year.

Three Days Until It All Begins

Once the deadline passes, the defending champions will have their final roster. Their championship banner goes up Monday at Madison Square Garden before the Sixers come to town. The Knicks need every available piece — healthy and ready — when the LeBron-led Sixers arrive three days from now.

What's Next

The non-guaranteed waiver deadline passes at 4 PM CT today. Watch for the Knicks' official announcement on Wiseman vs. Brown. League-wide final rosters will be confirmed tonight. Then rosters lock until final injury designations Sunday before Opening Night tips on October 20.
