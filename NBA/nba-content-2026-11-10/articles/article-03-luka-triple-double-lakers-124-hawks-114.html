Luka Dončić Triple-Double Sends Lakers Past Hawks 124-114, Win Streak Hits 6

By Damon Pierce | November 10, 2026

Luka Dončić is not just scoring. He is running a system, and that system is producing wins.

The Los Angeles Lakers star posted 32 points, 10 assists, and eight rebounds Monday night as the Lakers defeated the Atlanta Hawks 124-114 to extend their winning streak to six games. Dončić's triple-double was the latest entry in a string of dominant all-around performances that have positioned Los Angeles as a legitimate Western Conference contender in the early weeks of the season.

Luka's Quiet Greatness

After the kind of back-to-back 42-point explosions and scoring records that made Dončić the talk of the NBA just days ago, Monday's 32-10-8 line almost felt understated. It was anything but. A player who distributes 10 assists while also scoring 32 points is not simply a scorer — he is an offensive ecosystem, capable of breaking down a defense multiple ways on any single possession.

For Atlanta, the challenge was impossible to manage. Too much attention on Dončić opened shooters. Too much space given to the perimeter and he was at the free-throw line. The Hawks were caught in a familiar trap, and they paid for it with another loss.

Six Straight for Los Angeles

The Lakers' six-game winning streak has transformed what could have been an uncertain start to the season into a statement of intent. Los Angeles at 7-2 — or whatever their record stands at now — has beaten teams convincingly and found rhythm across the roster. Dončić is the engine, but a winning streak of this length requires contributions from supporting players, and the Lakers have been getting them.

Atlanta at 124-114 was a comfortable enough margin to suggest the win was never seriously in doubt once the Lakers asserted themselves. That kind of consistent execution, night after night, is the hallmark of a team that knows what it is.

What's Next

The Lakers will continue building on a streak that has them among the most discussed teams in the Western Conference. Dončić's ability to impact the game beyond scoring — with playmaking that ranks among the best in the league — means Los Angeles has multiple ways to beat any opponent. The six-game run has established the tone; the question now is whether they can maintain it through a long and demanding schedule.

For Atlanta, back to the drawing board. The Hawks need to find answers that do not currently exist in their defensive game plan.
