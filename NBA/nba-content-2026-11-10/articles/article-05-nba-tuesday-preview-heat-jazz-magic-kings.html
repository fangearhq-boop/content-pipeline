NBA Tuesday Preview: Heat at Jazz, Magic at Kings — Two Games Worth Watching

By Marcus Cole | November 10, 2026

After a loaded Monday slate, the NBA takes a step back on Tuesday, November 10 with just two games on the schedule. Do not let the slim card fool you — both matchups carry intrigue.

Miami Heat at Utah Jazz — 9:00 PM ET

The Miami Heat head to Salt Lake City to face the Utah Jazz in the nightcap opener, and the conditions favor Miami before tip-off. Utah fell to the Minnesota Timberwolves 115-100 on Monday and will be playing the second game of a back-to-back when they host the Heat. Fresh legs matter in the NBA, and Miami will have them.

For the Heat, this is precisely the kind of game they need to win to stay relevant in the Eastern Conference standings. A road victory against a fatigued opponent is not handed to you — you still have to execute — but the opportunity is there. Miami should come in with a gameplan built around pushing pace and attacking a tired Jazz defense in transition.

Utah, meanwhile, will need to lean on whatever home-court energy they can generate. Playing back-to-backs is a league-wide challenge, but teams with deep benches and disciplined systems can mitigate the fatigue. Whether the Jazz have enough to overcome Miami's advantages remains to be seen.

Orlando Magic at Sacramento Kings — 10:00 PM ET

The late game features the Orlando Magic visiting the Sacramento Kings, two teams building toward futures they hope include playoff basketball. Both franchises have invested in young talent, and Tuesday's matchup is an opportunity to see how that development is progressing against similarly positioned competition.

Sacramento has been a competitive home team, and Golden 1 Center gives them a genuine advantage. Orlando has been athletic and defensively engaged under their coaching staff, but finding consistent offensive production remains a work in progress. This is the kind of game where rotations matter and the team that executes its half-court sets more cleanly tends to win.

What's Next

Tuesday's two-game slate is a brief pause before the schedule ramps back up later in the week. Keep an eye on how Miami handles the Jazz back-to-back — a win there would provide useful momentum heading into the weekend. The Magic-Kings matchup, meanwhile, could offer early clues about which of those two developing programs is further along in their rebuilding process.
