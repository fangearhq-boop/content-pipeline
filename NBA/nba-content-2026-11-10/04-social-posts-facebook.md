**FB Long-Form Post 1**

They were down 10 at halftime. Then Cooper Flagg decided it was his moment.

The 19-year-old Mavericks star poured in 43 points with 10 rebounds, six assists, three steals and a block to lead Dallas back from a double-digit deficit and past the Indiana Pacers 125-122. Flagg shot a remarkable 16-of-25 from the field and made all eight of his free-throw attempts.

Kyrie Irving matched Flagg's fire with 35 points of his own, and together they combined for 26 fourth-quarter points as the Mavericks outscored the Pacers 35-29 in the final frame to complete the comeback.

The numbers tell only part of the story. Flagg's 43 points established a new season high and reportedly made him just the second teenager in NBA history to record 40 or more points, 10 or more rebounds and at least five assists in a single game. Dallas improves to 7-4 and Flagg is quickly becoming must-watch TV every night.
