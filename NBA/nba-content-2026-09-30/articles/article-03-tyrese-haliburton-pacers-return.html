Tyrese Haliburton Clears the Air: "I'm Ready" After Achilles Comeback

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | September 30, 2026

The NBA missed Tyrese Haliburton. And on Monday, at Indiana Pacers media day, Haliburton made it clear he missed being in it.

Stepping to the podium for the first time in more than 16 months, the 26-year-old point guard addressed his return directly, telling reporters he's healthy, cleared to play, and ready to compete at full speed heading into the 2026-27 season. It was the clearest signal yet that one of the league's most electric players is coming back.

The Road Back

Haliburton's injury came at the worst possible moment. It was Game 7 of the 2025 NBA Finals — the Pacers' most important game in decades — when he tore his Achilles tendon. The play that ended his season also ended Indiana's championship run.

He spent the entire 2025-26 season on the sideline, watching his team navigate the year without him. It was a year the Pacers clearly missed their star. And for Haliburton, every day of recovery was a day closer to getting back to the game he loves.

Achilles recovery at the professional level is a lengthy, difficult process. The typical timeline from surgery to return is 12 to 18 months. With his injury occurring in late May or early June 2025, Haliburton's 15-to-16-month timeline puts him right in line for a full recovery heading into camp.

What He Brings Back

Before the injury, Haliburton was considered one of the five best point guards in the NBA. He's a dynamic playmaker with elite court vision, an increasingly reliable three-point shooter, and a competitor who elevated every player around him on the Pacers.

Indiana made it to the Finals in 2025 specifically because Haliburton turned his game up to another level in the playoffs. His ability to create offense and control pace was the engine that drove that run.

The Pacers built their roster around his return. Getting him back at full strength — or even close to it — transforms Indiana from a team on the fringes of the conference's elite into a genuine title contender.

Managing Expectations

Even with positive news, both the Pacers and their fans will need to monitor Haliburton's workload carefully as the season ramps up. Returning from a major Achilles injury in Year 1 requires smart minute management. Teams that rush their Achilles patients have paid for it later.

The goal is to bring Haliburton along at the right pace, keeping him healthy for April and May rather than burning through him in November. Indiana's coaching staff will manage his reps carefully through the preseason, with eyes fixed firmly on a deep playoff run.

What's Next

Pacers training camp is now fully underway, and Haliburton's presence changes everything about Indiana's outlook. The preseason begins October 3, and while it wouldn't be surprising if the team limits his minutes early, his presence at media day and confirmed clearance to play puts the rest of the Eastern Conference on notice.

The full Tyrese Haliburton return is officially in motion. The NBA is better for it.
