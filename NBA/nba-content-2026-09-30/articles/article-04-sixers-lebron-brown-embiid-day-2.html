Sixers Camp Day 2: LeBron, Brown, and Embiid Are Just Getting Started

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | September 30, 2026

Two days into Philadelphia 76ers training camp and the rest of the NBA is already paying close attention.

LeBron James, Jaylen Brown, and Joel Embiid shared the practice floor together for the second consecutive day Tuesday — three of basketball's most imposing players building the kind of familiarity that could make Philadelphia a legitimate favorite to win it all in 2026-27.

The Summer That Changed Everything

The Sixers' transformation began on July 1 when the Boston Celtics agreed to trade Jaylen Brown to Philadelphia for Paul George and four future draft picks. It was a blockbuster that reshaped the Eastern Conference power structure overnight.

Less than a month later, LeBron James — the NBA's all-time leading scorer, a four-time champion, and a 24-year veteran at age 41 — signed with Philadelphia. He left Los Angeles after eight years and chose the Sixers because he believed this was the group that could get him a fifth ring.

His media day quote was direct: "I didn't leave my family to lose in the second round." There is no ambiguity in that sentence.

What the Big Three Looks Like on the Floor

On paper, the combination is breathtaking. LeBron James is a 41-year-old who still creates offense as well as anyone in the league, capable of playing point forward and distributing to elite teammates. Jaylen Brown is a do-everything wing in his prime at 30, built for the playoffs. Joel Embiid, when healthy, is still a top-three center who changes games at both ends.

The chemistry question is real, but it's worth noting that LeBron and Brown shared time together at the 2024 Olympics, giving them some baseline familiarity. And elite players typically find ways to make each other better when the motivation is a championship.

The Embiid Factor

The largest variable surrounding Philadelphia's ceiling remains Joel Embiid's availability. The center has battled significant injuries in recent seasons and has never played a full playoff run at 100 percent. The Sixers' best-case scenario depends heavily on Embiid being healthy and dominant in April and May.

For now, he's fully present at training camp and the early reports from inside the facility are encouraging. Having LeBron in the locker room — whose professionalism and preparation are legendary — can only help the culture and Embiid's own commitment to peak conditioning.

What's Next

The Sixers open the preseason on October 3. Every game they play before Opening Night will be watched more closely than any other team's, and the first real tests will come when they face playoff-caliber opponents.

Opening Night on October 20 — Sixers vs. Knicks, 7 PM ET on NBC/Peacock — has the feel of a championship preview. The Knicks swept Philadelphia 4-0 in last year's playoffs and are returning as defending Eastern Conference champions. LeBron's debut in a Sixers jersey couldn't come with higher stakes.

Day 2 of camp is just the beginning. The real work starts now.
