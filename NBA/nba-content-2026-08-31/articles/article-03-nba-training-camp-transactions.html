29 Days to NBA Training Camp — Mathurin Officially a Free Agent, Rosters Taking Final Shape

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | August 31, 2026

NBA Season Preview · Training Camp · Transactions

The 2026-27 NBA season is less than two months away, and with training camp 29 days out, the final roster moves of the offseason are happening fast. The last 48 hours have brought a pair of notable transactions: Bennedict Mathurin is now officially an unrestricted free agent, and John Konchar has been cut loose by the Minnesota Timberwolves.

Mathurin Goes Unrestricted

The Los Angeles Clippers withdrew Bennedict Mathurin's qualifying offer on August 29, making the 23-year-old wing an unrestricted free agent. Mathurin had previously reached an agreement in principle on a two-year, $16 million deal with the New Orleans Pelicans — a decision that reflected his preference for a shorter-term deal that allows him to reach unrestricted free agency again in one to two years, rather than accepting a larger offer that would lock him in longer.

The Clippers originally acquired Mathurin from Indiana on February 5, 2026. The withdrawal of his qualifying offer moves the transaction one step closer to official, clearing the path for Mathurin to formally sign with New Orleans. After averaging strong scoring numbers with Indiana, the young Canadian wing gets a fresh start in a Pelicans system that needs frontcourt and wing scoring.

Konchar Stretched by Timberwolves

Minnesota waived veteran wing John Konchar via the stretch provision on August 30, spreading his remaining contract across multiple years to clear immediate cap room. The move is part of the Wolves' ongoing effort to finalize their roster around star Jonathan Kuminga — who signed a two-year, $12.43 million deal in Minnesota this summer — and LaMelo Ball, who was acquired from Charlotte in a major trade earlier in the offseason.

Recent Offseason Moves Worth Remembering

As camp approaches, the biggest offseason transactions have reshaped nearly every contender. DeMar DeRozan joined the Denver Nuggets on a one-year, $3.88 million veteran minimum deal, giving Nikola Jokic and Jamal Murray another dynamic scorer. Nick Richards agreed to a one-year, $3.06 million deal with the Miami Heat, completing their superteam roster build. Stephen Curry signed a one-year, $62.6 million extension with Golden State — the highest single-season salary in NBA history — cementing his lifelong Warrior status.

Key Dates Ahead

Overseas teams may begin camp as early as September 22. For all other NBA teams, veterans report September 28 and training camps open September 29. Preseason games begin October 3, giving coaches 17 days to evaluate rosters before the regular season starts October 20.

What's Next

With Mathurin's deal now on its way to being formally signed, and Konchar's cap space freed in Minnesota, expect a final wave of veteran minimum signings and two-way contracts over the next two weeks as teams lock down their Opening Night rosters. September 29 will be here before you know it — and the NBA will officially be back.
