Kawhi Leonard Trade to Toronto — NBA Board of Governors Meeting Is the Key Inflection Point

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | August 31, 2026

NBA Transactions · Toronto Raptors · Los Angeles Clippers · Kawhi Leonard

After more than two months in limbo, the Kawhi Leonard trade saga may finally be approaching its conclusion — and the NBA Board of Governors meeting in September is being identified as the critical moment that determines Leonard's future. The nine-time All-Star expects to be in Toronto by September 28 for the Raptors' training camp Media Day, but that outcome depends on the league's investigation into his Clippers contract reaching its conclusion.

Where Things Stand

The structure of the deal has been agreed upon for months. On June 30, the Toronto Raptors and Los Angeles Clippers reached an agreement: Kawhi Leonard heads to Toronto, while Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, two first-round draft picks, and a pick swap go to Los Angeles. It looked done. Then, on July 9, the Raptors paused the trade as the NBA's investigation into Leonard's Clippers contract — involving concerns about potential cap circumvention through sponsorship arrangements — introduced uncertainty about whether Toronto could face secondary sanctions.

Law firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz has been conducting the investigation, which is now in its 11th month. The good news for Leonard: he has been personally cleared by the NBA — investigators found no evidence of wrongdoing on his part. The Clippers, however, still face potential sanctions for the organization's conduct.

The BOG Meeting as the Inflection Point

Multiple reports identify the upcoming NBA Board of Governors meeting in September as the pivotal moment. This is where the league's ownership group is expected to formally address the Clippers' sanctions, potentially clearing the final hurdle for the trade to become official.

Per NBC Sports' Jake Fischer, Leonard strongly believes the trade will ultimately be completed in time for him to join Toronto ahead of camp. The Raptors' training camp begins in Quebec City with Media Day on September 28 — leaving just weeks for the BOG meeting to generate action.

Toronto has been operating with the expectation that Leonard arrives. The organization has reportedly structured its offseason planning around him. The question is no longer whether the trade happens, but when the final clearance comes through.

What the Raptors Get

If and when the trade is finalized, Toronto lands one of the most versatile two-way players in NBA history. Leonard, a former Finals MVP with both San Antonio and Toronto, brings elite perimeter defense, midrange scoring, and a championship pedigree that the Raptors' young core desperately needs. Reports have also indicated that a multi-year extension is part of Leonard's plan with Toronto — a sign that both sides view this as more than a short-term arrangement.

What's Next

The BOG meeting this September represents the most likely resolution point for a trade that has been in suspended animation for nearly two months. If the Clippers' sanctions are issued and deemed not to impact Toronto, the trade should proceed quickly. If complications arise, there is one more fallback: the league could allow the trade to close with conditions, or with Leonard returning to the Clippers for the short term.

For now, all roads point to Kawhi Leonard suiting up in Toronto red by October. The clock is ticking — training camp is 29 days away, and the Raptors are ready to turn the page.
