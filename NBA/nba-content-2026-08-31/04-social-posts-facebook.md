### STORY 2: Kawhi Leonard Trade Inflection

**Long-Form Post (11:00 AM ET)**
The Kawhi Leonard saga is entering what could be its final chapter — and the NBA Board of Governors meeting this September is the moment everyone has been waiting for.

Here's where things stand: The Raptors and Clippers agreed to a blockbuster trade on June 30. Kawhi goes to Toronto; Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, two first-round picks, and a swap head to LA. It seemed done. Then, on July 9, Toronto hit pause — the NBA's ongoing investigation into Kawhi's Clippers contract had created uncertainty about potential sanctions that could affect the Raptors.

Per reports, he strongly believes this trade is getting done before training camp begins on September 28 in Quebec City.

The BOG meeting this month is described as the key inflection point — where sanctions get handed down and the trade gets the green light. If it happens, Toronto gets their cornerstone back. If it doesn't, the league enters camp with one of its biggest stars in limbo.

All eyes on September.
