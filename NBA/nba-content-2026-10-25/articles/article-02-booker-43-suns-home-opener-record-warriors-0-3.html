Booker Sets Suns Home Opener Record with 43 as Shorthanded Warriors Fall to 0-3

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | October 25, 2026

Devin Booker had a message for the Phoenix Suns faithful on Saturday night, and he delivered it in record-breaking fashion. Booker scored 43 points — the most any Suns player has ever posted in a home opener — shooting 17-of-25 from the field and adding seven rebounds and seven assists in a 131-122 victory over a shorthanded Golden State Warriors squad.

The near triple-double performance reestablished the Suns as a team to reckon with in the West after an ugly 41-point opening-night loss at Portland. Footprint Center was electric, and Booker fed off the energy from the opening tip.

The Numbers

Booker's 43-point, 7-rebound, 7-assist line was just one assist away from a genuine triple-double — and he went 7-of-8 from the free throw line in the process. Miles Bridges added 16 points and Dillon Brooks contributed 15 as the Suns built a comfortable margin that they never surrendered.

For Golden State, rookie Yaxel Lendeborg led the way with 26 points, while Melton chipped in 25. But the Warriors were without Stephen Curry, Draymond Green, Brandin Podziemski, and Al Horford — all sitting out on load management or injury. Playing for the third time in four days, Golden State simply didn't have enough in the tank to match Phoenix's intensity.

Warriors' 0-3 Alarm

Golden State's winless start to 2026-27 is raising early-season eyebrows. Three games in, three losses — and while the roster absences provide some context, the Warriors cannot be satisfied with their overall level of play. The schedule gets no easier, and the window to correct course before losing ground in the Western Conference standings is a narrow one.

San Francisco Bay Area fans have seen this movie before: slow starts followed by eventual corrections. Whether this year's group can replicate that pattern remains to be seen, but the 0-3 hole is one they need to climb out of quickly.

Suns' Bounce-Back

The Suns came in under a microscope after giving up a 41-point loss to open the season. Head coach Mike Budenholzer had talked all week about defensive improvements, and while the 131-122 final score isn't a defensive gem, the Suns showed the kind of offensive firepower that makes them dangerous.

Booker, now in his prime years, looks as sharp as ever. When he's hitting at a 68 percent clip from the field with a near triple-double, Phoenix is a very difficult team to beat.

What's Next

The Suns will look to build on this performance and establish some early-season rhythm heading into the week ahead. The Warriors, meanwhile, must regroup and find a path back to winning basketball — ideally when their full roster is available. An 0-3 start in the NBA is hardly season-defining, but it is a situation that demands urgent attention in Golden State.
