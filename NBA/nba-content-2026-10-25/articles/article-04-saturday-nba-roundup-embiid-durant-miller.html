Saturday NBA Roundup: Embiid 28, Durant 22, Miller 34 Headline Eight-Game Slate

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | October 25, 2026

Saturday's eight-game NBA slate produced plenty of notable performances beyond the headlining overtime thriller in Denver. From Joel Embiid's continued dominance in Philadelphia to Kevin Durant showing his value in Houston, the opening week of the 2026-27 season is already generating compelling storylines from coast to coast.

76ers 127, Bucks 116 — Embiid 28

Joel Embiid dropped 28 points as the Philadelphia 76ers handled the Milwaukee Bucks 127-116. Philly is clicking in early going, and the Sixers look like a genuine Eastern Conference threat. Milwaukee, meanwhile, is dealing with the kind of sluggish start that head coach Doc Rivers will want to address quickly before the deficit in the standings becomes more significant.

Rockets 121, Hawks 114 — Durant 22

Kevin Durant posted 22 points as the Houston Rockets beat the Atlanta Hawks 121-114. Durant's veteran leadership and scoring efficiency continue to prove exactly why Houston made the blockbuster move to bring him in. The Rockets are a squad with legitimate Western Conference aspirations, and KD's ability to score in bunches — especially in tight games — makes them a dangerous team on any given night.

Hornets 115, Heat 110 — Miller 34

Brandon Miller had a massive night for Charlotte, scoring 34 points to lead the Hornets past the Miami Heat 115-110. Miller, entering his fourth NBA season, is emerging as one of the conference's most dangerous young scorers. Miami fell to 1-1 on the young season, while Charlotte's win showed the Hornets have legitimate talent capable of competing in the East.

Other Results

The Cleveland Cavaliers edged the Brooklyn Nets 125-121 in a competitive Eastern matchup. The Washington Wizards held off the Chicago Bulls 124-120 — Coby Wilson posted 30 points for Chicago in a losing effort, a sign of what that young Bulls team can do offensively when Wilson is clicking.

What's Next

The early-season picture is coming into focus after a full week of NBA basketball. Stars are staking their claims, teams are figuring out rotations, and the standings are beginning to take shape. Sunday brings seven more games, including marquee Western Conference matchups and a Lakers-Jazz tilt at 5:00 PM ET. The 2026-27 season is fully underway, and it's already delivering.
