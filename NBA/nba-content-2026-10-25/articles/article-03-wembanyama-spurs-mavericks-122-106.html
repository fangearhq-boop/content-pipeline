Wembanyama 23: Spurs Put Away Mavericks 122-106 in Texas Showdown

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | October 25, 2026

Victor Wembanyama continues to remind the NBA why San Antonio's rebuild arrived ahead of schedule. The French superstar scored 23 points as the Spurs routed the Dallas Mavericks 122-106 Saturday night in a lopsided Texas rivalry matchup that never felt particularly close in the second half.

San Antonio is making a statement early in 2026-27: this team is no longer merely rebuilding. Wembanyama's combination of size, skill, and basketball IQ is the kind of foundation that winning franchises are built around, and through the opening week of the season, the Spurs are playing with energy and purpose.

Wembanyama's Impact

The 23 points from Wembanyama came with the full package of his unique skill set on display — long-range shooting, post moves, rim protection on the other end. Opponents still haven't found a reliable answer for a player who can guard and score at every level of the game.

At seven feet, four inches with a 8-foot wingspan, Wembanyama alters shots simply by being in the area. His offensive game continues to expand each year, and at just 22 years old, he is already one of the most complete big men the league has seen.

Mavericks' Struggles

Dallas entered Saturday looking for its first real positive result of the early season and left with another loss. The Mavericks, who feature Cooper Flagg in their lineup after the No. 1 pick's impressive summer, are still working out the chemistry and rotations that will define their 2026-27 identity.

A 16-point defeat to San Antonio isn't catastrophic this early in the season, but it does underscore that Dallas has work to do before it can challenge the West's established powers. The coaching staff will look at the film and identify the areas — defensive rotations, transition defense — where the Mavericks gave the Spurs too much freedom.

What's Next

San Antonio will look to carry this winning momentum into the week ahead. The Spurs' combination of an elite two-way superstar in Wembanyama and a hardworking supporting cast makes them a genuine wild card in the Western Conference playoff picture. Dallas needs to regroup and find its rhythm quickly — the schedule doesn't ease up, and early losses can have a compounding effect on confidence in October.
