NBA Sunday Preview: Lakers-Jazz Leads Seven-Game Slate with Thunder, Knicks in Action

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | October 25, 2026

Sunday brings a seven-game NBA slate that offers everything a fan could want in the opening week of a new season: competitive matchups, star power, and storylines worth following. Here is what to watch as the early-season table setting continues on October 25, 2026.

Lakers vs. Jazz — 5:00 PM ET

Los Angeles opens Sunday's action against Utah at 5:00 PM ET. The Lakers are still searching for their preferred rotation after a busy offseason, and the Jazz represent a chance to get things moving in a positive direction. Utah is a young, athletic team that plays fast and won't give the Lakers anything for free. LeBron James and company will need to assert themselves early to avoid a slow-start trap.

Nets vs. Pacers — 6:00 PM ET

Brooklyn and Indiana tip off at 6:00 PM ET in what could be a fun offensive showcase. The Pacers pushed the pace relentlessly last season and enter 2026-27 with similar ambitions. The Nets are rebuilding but have shown flashes — Michael Porter Jr. dropped 28 points in a loss to Cleveland on Saturday, signaling he has found a comfort level in New York.

Knicks vs. Magic — 7:00 PM ET

New York hosts Orlando at 7:00 PM ET in a game with an interesting East subplot. The Knicks are coming off back-to-back losses to open 2026-27 and need to find their footing at Madison Square Garden. The Magic, meanwhile, have been one of the league's most improved teams in recent years, built around a core of young talent that makes life difficult for anyone in the conference.

Thunder vs. Clippers

Oklahoma City against Los Angeles rounds out the highlight matchups. The Thunder — with Shai Gilgeous-Alexander operating at an MVP level — are one of the West's premier teams entering 2026-27. SGA and the Thunder will look to stay unbeaten while the Clippers try to make a statement of their own in a loaded Western Conference.
