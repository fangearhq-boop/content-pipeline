Nick Nurse Says It's the Best Embiid Offseason in Years. LeBron Is Paying Attention.

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | August 11, 2026

NBA Offseason / Philadelphia 76ers / Joel Embiid / LeBron James

The viral photos got people talking. The head coach's confirmation made it real.

Nick Nurse said this week that Joel Embiid is in "optimal shape" — and then went further, offering the most meaningful qualifier of the entire 76ers offseason: "He's having a great summer, probably the first time in a couple of summers. He's in shape, he's active." Philadelphia's basketball operations president Mike Gansey was equally direct: "The first summer in a long time where he has been healthy. He's able to work normally, lifting weights and training in the gym."

LeBron James, Embiid's new teammate, saw the workout photos and responded on Instagram with a "Trust the Process" message. That's either a coincidence or the most fitting response in recent NBA history. Either way, it landed.

Why the Health Story Is the Hinge

The Philadelphia 76ers assembled one of the most talented rosters in the NBA this summer. LeBron James chose Philadelphia — a decision he called "my last" — joining Embiid, Tyrese Maxey, and Jaylen Brown to form a Big Four that carries five combined MVP awards. On paper, they are the most formidable team in the Eastern Conference.

But every projection about this team's ceiling has carried an asterisk: Joel Embiid's health. He has missed significant time in multiple recent seasons due to knee problems. A major injury in January 2026 cut short his previous year. The question of how many games Embiid plays, and how effective he is in them, is the single biggest variable in every championship conversation around this team.

Nick Nurse's comments this week address that question directly. Not with a cautious "he's feeling great" — with a specific, comparative statement: this is better than the last couple of summers. That's not bulletin-board language. That's a coach who has seen what Embiid looks like when he's limited, and who is telling anyone who will listen that this is different.

The New Motivation

For the first time in his career, Embiid is entering a season with something he's never had: a co-star of LeBron James's stature, demanding the same standard of preparation. LeBron and Maxey are already working out together under trainer Chris Johnson. The culture of early mornings and voluntary workouts — a hallmark of LeBron's career — is being imported directly into Philadelphia's offseason.

Embiid is absorbing it. The viral photos suggest a player who has taken the physical standards of his preparation to a new level. Nurse's confirmation suggests it's real, not just an offseason social media moment. Kentavious Caldwell-Pope, who signed a one-year deal with Philadelphia on August 5, adds another veteran to a roster already loaded with championship experience.

The Calculation

The 76ers have not won an NBA championship since 1983. That is not a rhetorical point — it is the backdrop against which every move this offseason has been made. The roster, if healthy, gives Philadelphia a genuine claim to being the title favorite. LeBron James at 41 and Joel Embiid at his best are a combination the league has never seen before.

Training camp opens in late September. The 2026-27 schedule releases Wednesday. When those dates snap into place, the 76ers will see when their season becomes real — and every opponent will understand what they're facing.

What's Next

The Philadelphia 76ers enter training camp in late September with their most physically healthy version of Joel Embiid in years, according to both head coach Nick Nurse and team president Mike Gansey. LeBron James, Tyrese Maxey, and Jaylen Brown are already working out together. The full 2026-27 schedule releases Wednesday. If Embiid stays healthy, this team's ceiling conversation starts from the top of the bracket — and for the first time in a long time, Philadelphia has reason to believe that's not wishful thinking.
