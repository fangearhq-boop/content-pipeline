The Kawhi Leonard Trade May Be Six Weeks From Crossing the Finish Line

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | August 11, 2026

NBA Investigation / Los Angeles Clippers / Toronto Raptors / Kawhi Leonard

For months, the Kawhi Leonard saga felt like a story without an end date. On Monday, that changed. HoopsHype reported that there is a belief in some corners of the league that the NBA's investigation into Leonard and the Los Angeles Clippers could resolve within the next six weeks — putting a conclusion squarely before NBA training camps open in late September. More importantly, the signal is coming from the player himself: Kawhi Leonard expects the trade to the Toronto Raptors to go through before camp.

Commissioner Adam Silver has made the league's position public: "This needs to be wrapped up before the beginning of next season."

Where the Investigation Stands

The NBA has been examining whether the Clippers circumvented the salary cap through endorsement deals tied to Kawhi Leonard. The primary focus has been a $28 million deal between Leonard and Aspiration — a green banking company that also held a $300 million, 23-year sponsorship deal with the Clippers franchise before going bankrupt. CBS Sports later reported that a second previously undisclosed deal, this one with Daktronics, is also under review.

The fact-finding phase of the investigation is now complete. The league is in resolution mode — meaning the open question is no longer what happened, but what the consequences will be and whether the frozen trade can be finalized.

The Trade Details

The agreed-upon deal, frozen since late June, sends Kawhi Leonard to Toronto in exchange for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, unprotected 2031 and 2033 first-round picks, a 2027 first-round pick swap, and two second-round picks. It is a significant return for the Raptors — a package that makes Toronto a legitimate Eastern Conference threat on paper, assuming the trade clears.

The NBA informed Toronto that once the trade is made official, the Raptors would assume the risk of any investigation outcome. That risk includes the possibility of Kawhi facing a suspension or the trade being scrutinized as part of the penalties. So Toronto chose to wait — and has been waiting ever since.

What Happens When the Investigation Concludes

If the Clippers are found guilty of willful salary cap circumvention, potential penalties include fines of up to $7.5 million, forfeiture of draft picks, and suspensions of up to one year for personnel involved. But the prevailing expectation — even accounting for penalties — is that the trade clears. Kawhi Leonard is owed $50.3 million for the 2026-27 season. He currently belongs to no team in any functional sense.

The six-week window reported Monday puts a resolution in the final days of September. Training camp opens in late September. The countdown, for the first time, has a visible end.

What's Next

The NBA's investigation into Kawhi Leonard and the Los Angeles Clippers is entering its resolution phase. A six-week timeline — putting a conclusion before training camp — has been reported by HoopsHype. Kawhi Leonard expects the trade to go through. Commissioner Silver has set a public deadline. The Raptors, the Clippers, and the basketball world are now waiting on a league process that appears, finally, to be approaching its end.
