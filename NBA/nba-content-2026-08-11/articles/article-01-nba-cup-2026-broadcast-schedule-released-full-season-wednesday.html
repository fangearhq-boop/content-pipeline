The NBA Cup Broadcast Schedule Is Here — And the Full 2026-27 Season Is 48 Hours Away

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | August 11, 2026

NBA Season Preview / Emirates NBA Cup 2026

The waiting is almost over. The NBA released the Emirates NBA Cup 2026 broadcast schedule on Monday — and two days from now, on Wednesday August 13, all 1,230 games of the 2026-27 regular season will be revealed at once. For a basketball world that has spent the summer tracking trades, signings, and investigations, the next 48 hours represent the moment the season actually begins to feel real.

How the Cup Works This Season

Group Play tips off Friday, October 30 — 10 days after the season opener on October 20. From there, Cup Nights run on November 6, 13, 20, 24 (Tuesday), 25 (Wednesday), and 27. Each team plays four Group Play games, two home and two away, against every other team in its group.

The knockout rounds come fast. Quarterfinals are December 4-5. Semifinals follow December 8-9. And the Championship is December 11 — at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis, the arena where the film Hoosiers was shot. It is a genuinely inspired choice of venue: one of the most storied settings in basketball history, hosting the NBA's most personality-rich in-season tournament.

Broadcast coverage includes Prime Video for select group games and all knockout-round games, alongside ESPN, NBC, NBA TV, and regional sports networks during the group stage.

The Western Conference Groups

The three Western Conference groups are set:

Group A features the Denver Nuggets, Houston Rockets, Phoenix Suns, Dallas Mavericks, and Utah Jazz — five franchises carrying varying levels of rebuild, retool, and contention energy into October.

Group B brings together the Oklahoma City Thunder, Minnesota Timberwolves, LA Clippers, New Orleans Pelicans, and Memphis Grizzlies. OKC and Minnesota in the same group sets up what could be a rematch of one of last season's most compelling Western Conference matchups.

Group C is the one every NBA fan will be watching: the San Antonio Spurs, Los Angeles Lakers, Portland Trail Blazers, Golden State Warriors, and Sacramento Kings. Victor Wembanyama. Stephen Curry. The Ja Morant-led Trail Blazers. Group C may produce the Cup's defining early storylines.
