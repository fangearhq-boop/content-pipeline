### STORY 2: Kawhi Leonard — Six-Week Resolution Window, Deal Expected to Clear

**Long-Form Post**
⚖️ The most complicated trade in NBA history may be approaching the finish line.

HoopsHype reported Monday that there is a belief in some circles of the league that the NBA's investigation into Kawhi Leonard and the Los Angeles Clippers could resolve within the **next six weeks** — putting a resolution squarely before training camp opens in late September. And for the first time in this saga, the signal isn't coming just from league observers. Kawhi Leonard himself believes the trade to the Toronto Raptors will go through before camp.

Commissioner Adam Silver has made his position clear publicly: "This needs to be wrapped up before the beginning of next season."

Here's where the investigation stands:

**The probe:** The NBA has been examining whether the Clippers circumvented the salary cap through a $28 million endorsement deal between Kawhi Leonard and Aspiration (a company that also held a $300M, 23-year sponsorship deal with the Clippers). A second previously undisclosed deal with Daktronics is also under review. The fact-finding phase is now complete. The NBA is in resolution mode.

**The trade (still frozen):** Kawhi Leonard goes to Toronto for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, unprotected 2031 and 2033 first-round picks, a 2027 first-round pick swap, and two second-round picks. Toronto was told it would bear the risk of any penalties by finalizing the trade before the probe ends — so the Raptors chose to wait.

**What happens next:** If the Clippers are found guilty of willful circumvention, potential penalties include fines up to $7.5 million, draft-pick forfeiture, and suspensions of up to one year for personnel involved. But even with sanctions, the expectation is the trade clears.

Kawhi Leonard is owed $50.3 million for the 2026-27 season. He currently has no team to play for.

The light at the end of the tunnel is visible. ⏰

### STORY 3: Stephen Curry — 18 Days and a Growing Discount Debate

**Long-Form Post**
🏀 Stephen Curry's extension window opens in 18 days. And a question is starting to simmer: will he actually take the full max?

The expected number is two years, $136.7 million — roughly $68 million per year, taking Curry through his age-40 season. If signed, it would make him the first NBA player in history to earn more than $60 million in a single season. The Warriors want it done. Curry wants to be in Golden State. Mutual interest is confirmed.

But ESPN's Brian Windhorst has floated a different idea — a shorter-term structure that would give Golden State more roster flexibility to pursue a complementary star. And a Western Conference executive said to expect Curry to at least explore a below-max structure: "He's a smart guy — he'll want to know the options and the plan."

Curry himself has said he's "absolutely" open to sacrifice. That word has weight.

The context: LeBron James chose Philadelphia over Golden State this summer. The Warriors finished 37-45 last season. Draymond Green is back (1yr/$27.7M). Gary Payton II is back (1yr/$3.88M). But the roster around Curry needs reinforcement, and how much money Curry takes — and for how long — determines what comes next.

The window opens August 29. A September signing is widely expected. The question is whether the number ends up being exactly $136.7 million. 🤔

### STORY 4: Wembanyama and France — 9 Days to Belgrade

**Long-Form Post**
🇫🇷 Victor Wembanyama is 9 days away from his first FIBA game since the 2024 Paris Olympics.

France opens the August window against Serbia in Belgrade on August 20. Then a rematch in Orleans on August 23. Then World Cup qualifying matches against Slovenia (August 27, home) and Sweden (August 30, away). It's a four-game gauntlet — and Wembanyama will be front and center for all of it.

This is the first time France's new head coach Frederic Fauthoux — in the role since September 2024 — takes the court with Wembanyama. The roster also features Rudy Gobert and Evan Fournier, reuniting a Big Three that represented France at the Paris Olympics and pushed Team USA to the final moments before taking silver.

Wemby's own words, asked about his time away from basketball this summer:

*"I don't miss the NBA at the moment, but I do miss FIBA."*

That's the sound of a player who is hungry, rested, and ready. France enters this window 5-1 in Group L of World Cup qualifying — already in a strong position. The next two qualifying matches matter, and Wemby is the reason they're a threat.

Spurs fans are watching the workload. FIBA fans are just watching the show. 🏀

### STORY 5: Embiid's Best Offseason in Years — and LeBron Is Paying Attention

**Long-Form Post**
💪 Joel Embiid is having the best offseason of his NBA career. And if you didn't know it before, you heard it this week from his own head coach.

Nick Nurse confirmed what 76ers fans have been sensing from Embiid's viral workout photos: this is different. "Looks fantastic," Nurse said. "He's having a great summer, probably the first time in a couple of summers. He's in shape, he's active."

76ers president of basketball operations Mike Gansey was even more direct: "The first summer in a long time where he has been healthy. He's able to work normally, lifting weights and training in the gym."

LeBron James — Embiid's new teammate — took one look at Embiid's physique photos and responded on Instagram with a "Trust the Process" message. That's one of the more fitting reactions in recent NBA history.

Here's why this matters more than a fun offseason headline:

**The injury history.** Embiid has missed significant time in each of the past several seasons due to knee issues, and a major knee injury in January 2026 cut short his previous campaign. His availability has been the single biggest question mark around any championship projection for Philadelphia.

**The new motivation.** For the first time in his career, Embiid is entering a season alongside LeBron James, Tyrese Maxey, and Jaylen Brown — a Big Four that carries five combined MVP awards. That's a different kind of motivation than anything he's had before.

**What's next.** LeBron and Maxey are already working out together under trainer Chris Johnson. Kentavious Caldwell-Pope signed a 1-year deal (Aug 5) to add veteran depth. Training camp opens in late September.

If Embiid is truly healthy for 60+ games, this team is the favorite. That's the bet Philadelphia is finally making. 🏆
