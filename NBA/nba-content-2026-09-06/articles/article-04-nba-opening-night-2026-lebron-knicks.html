NBA Opening Night: LeBron's 76ers Debut, Knicks Banner Raise Headline Oct. 20 Triple-Header

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | September 6, 2026

The NBA delivered a gift to basketball fans with its 2026-27 Opening Night schedule. On October 20, three games on NBC and Peacock will combine for what might be the most watched single night of regular-season basketball in years — and the centerpiece is a matchup that almost no one could have predicted at the start of the summer.

LeBron James, 41 years old and wearing a Philadelphia 76ers jersey for the first time, will walk into Madison Square Garden as the New York Knicks raise their 2025-26 NBA championship banner. In front of the most electric crowd in basketball, on the sport's biggest regular-season stage, the Sixers will try to crash the Knicks' party before the defending champions have even had their moment.

A Triple-Header Built for Maximum Drama

The night begins at 3 PM ET with the Boston Celtics visiting the Detroit Pistons — a matchup between two of the Eastern Conference's best records from 2025-26, both looking to prove they can compete without the rosters they ended last season with. Then comes the main event at 7 PM ET.

Philadelphia at New York is appointment television. LeBron signed a two-year, $8 million deal with the Sixers this summer, making his final chapter a run alongside Joel Embiid, Jaylen Brown, and Tyrese Maxey. The Sixers open the regular season at Madison Square Garden, and the Knicks will have every reason to make a statement on a night that belongs to them.

The evening closes at 9:30 PM ET with the Oklahoma City Thunder hosting the San Antonio Spurs — a rematch of last season's Western Conference Finals. OKC lost that series to San Antonio, and the young Thunder will carry the weight of that disappointment into every game of 2026-27. October 20 is the first chance to answer it.

What Makes This Opening Night Different

Opening Night matchups are often selected for name recognition over narrative weight. This year's triple-header has both. LeBron's debut with a third franchise — and fourth team — is a genuine sports media event. The Knicks raising their first championship banner since 1973 is a historical moment for one of the sport's flagship markets. And the Thunder-Spurs rematch provides the Western Conference's defining storyline before a single October game has been played.

NBC and Peacock will carry all three games as part of the league's new broadcast deal, which also includes 60 NBA TV regular-season games throughout 2026-27. Christmas Day will bring another showcase: the Spurs visit the Knicks at noon ET, and the 76ers travel to Los Angeles at 5 PM ET for LeBron's return to Staples Center.

What's Next

Training camps open September 29 across the league, with preseason games beginning October 3. For the Sixers, it will be LeBron's first look at his new teammates in competitive action — preseason begins October 8 in Brooklyn. For the Knicks, it is one final month before their championship year is officially complete and the defense begins. October 20 is six weeks away. The countdown is on.
