Michael Porter Jr. Trade Buzz Intensifies — Nets Expected to Move Him Before 2027 Deadline

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | September 6, 2026

One year after the Denver Nuggets traded Michael Porter Jr. to Brooklyn, the forward's name is circulating in trade talks once again — this time from a Nets team that appears unlikely to commit to him long-term.

Contract extension negotiations between Porter Jr. and Brooklyn stalled this offseason, and multiple reports indicate the Nets are expected to trade their star wing before the 2027 trade deadline. For a player who turned in one of the best individual seasons of any forward in the league last year, the trade market is about to be very competitive.

The Career Year That Made Porter a Hot Commodity

When Denver shipped Porter to Brooklyn for Cam Johnson and a 2032 unprotected first-round pick, the basketball world questioned the Nuggets' valuation. What happened next validated those concerns. Porter Jr. posted career highs across every major statistical category in 2025-26, averaging 24.2 points and 7.1 rebounds per game with shooting splits of 46/36/86. He is 28 years old, healthy, and playing the best basketball of his career.

For Brooklyn — a team in rebuilding mode — Porter represents something valuable but complicated. He is a genuine star-level contributor, the kind of player that franchise pieces are built around. But the Nets appear to view him as their next major trade asset rather than a cornerstone of their next contending team.

The Interested Teams

The Golden State Warriors and Detroit Pistons have both been mentioned as potential suitors. The Pistons reportedly had interest in Porter at last season's trade deadline and could return with a competitive offer. The Warriors, entering a transitional period of their own, have the draft assets and infrastructure to package a deal for a player of Porter's caliber.

For any team in the mix, the calculus is straightforward: a 28-year-old who can score from anywhere on the floor, space the floor at 36% from three, and post nearly seven rebounds per game is a proven playoff contributor. A package built around Porter could change a team's ceiling overnight.

What It Means for Denver

The Nuggets, naturally, are watching from the sideline. Denver received Cam Johnson in the Porter deal — a solid starter but a player who has never reached the star level Porter achieved in Brooklyn. The trade could become one of the more scrutinized moves of the decade if a team lands Porter for another package of assets and rides him to a championship run.

For now, Denver's front office is focused on the players in its own building. But the Porter Jr. saga is far from over, and its next chapter could define several franchises at once.

What's Next

NBA training camps open September 29 and preseason tips off October 3. Between now and the 2027 trade deadline in February, the Porter Jr. market will develop as teams assess their needs after the regular season begins. Brooklyn is not in a rush — they have until February to maximize their return. But with extension talks stalled, the clock is effectively running. Expect Porter's name to be a fixture of NBA trade discussion all season long.
