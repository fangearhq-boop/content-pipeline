Black Friday NBA Cup: Heat-Knicks and Lakers-Spurs Decide the Knockout Bracket on Prime Video

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | November 26, 2026

The NBA Cup is down to its final day of group play, and the league has arranged the perfect stage for it. On Black Friday — no games on Thanksgiving, all eyes on November 27 — Prime Video will carry two NBA Cup matchups that will finalize the eight-team knockout bracket: the Miami Heat traveling to Madison Square Garden to face the New York Knicks, and the Los Angeles Lakers visiting the San Antonio Spurs.

These are not filler games. The standings entering Black Friday are unsettled in multiple groups, and the results on Friday will determine who advances, who goes home, and who gets top seeding heading into the quarterfinals. The NBA couldn't have drawn it up better if it tried.

Heat-Knicks: Everything on the Line

Wednesday night's results made the Heat-Knicks matchup even more compelling. The Knicks won their sixth straight game, hammering Cleveland 121-102 behind Jose Alvarado's season-high 21 points and a dominant 39-17 fourth quarter. New York is alive in the Cup at 1-2 in East Group B, and a win Friday could send them to the knockout round depending on how other results fall.

Miami comes in having lost to the 76ers on Wednesday, with Giannis Antetokounmpo posting 32 points and 14 rebounds in a losing effort. The Heat need a win to stay alive in the Cup race, and they'll be facing a Knicks team riding momentum at the best venue in basketball.

Giannis at MSG in a Cup game that could send Miami home — that's appointment television. The Knicks will be raucous, the stakes will be high, and both teams will be competing with a playoff urgency that makes regular-season games in November feel like playoff basketball.

Also worth noting: the 76ers knocked off Miami on Wednesday with LeBron James posting his first triple-double as a Sixer. The Heat's Week 7 has been defined by high-performance losses, and Friday represents their final chance to salvage the Cup chapter of their season.

Lakers-Spurs: Wembanyama's Home Statement

In the West, the Lakers visiting the Spurs sets up a compelling contrast in styles and eras. Victor Wembanyama and the Spurs have been one of the NBA's most watchable teams, blending Wembanyama's generational talent with San Antonio's historically sound system. At home in the AT&T Center, the Spurs will be favored to make a statement that secures their position in the Cup bracket.

The Lakers bring their own star power, and a road Cup game on Black Friday will test their depth and execution. The Dallas Mavericks and Boston Celtics had been the top Cup seeds heading into this final weekend, but the West bracket has room for movement, and Los Angeles will want to position themselves favorably heading into December's knockout games.

What's Next

By the end of Friday's games, the NBA Cup bracket will be locked in. Eight teams — four from each conference — will advance to the quarterfinals. The knockout games will follow shortly after, with the NBA Cup Finals in December offering a $500,000 bonus for each winning player and significant bragging rights heading into the second half of the season.

The NBA's decision to skip Thanksgiving games and concentrate marquee action on Black Friday has been validated by the matchups that have emerged. Family gatherings will wrap up, the couch will beckon, and Prime Video will be ready with the two most important NBA games of the week.

Heat at Knicks. Lakers at Spurs. Black Friday. The NBA Cup bracket is on the line. Set your reminders accordingly.
