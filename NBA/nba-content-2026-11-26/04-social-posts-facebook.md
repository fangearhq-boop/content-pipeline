### STORY 3: Durant 33K Milestone

**Post 3 — Long-Form (1:00 PM ET)**
Window: 1:00 PM ET

Kevin Durant crossed a threshold last night that only four other human beings in the history of professional basketball have ever reached: 33,000 career points.

LeBron James. Kareem Abdul-Jabbar. Karl Malone. Kobe Bryant. And now, Kevin Durant.

The Rockets beat the Suns 116-104, and Durant's 20 points in that game pushed him into this exclusive club. It wasn't a flashy performance — 20 points on a night like this, with the milestone buried in the fourth quarter — but that's KD. He doesn't need the spotlight to earn his place in history. He just keeps scoring, night after night, until the numbers speak for themselves.

There will be debate about where Durant ranks all-time — always has been, always will be. But the conversation about whether he belongs among the greatest scorers to ever play is officially over. He's the fifth player ever. That's not an argument. That's a fact.

Congratulations to Kevin Durant on a remarkable achievement.
