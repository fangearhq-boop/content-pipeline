NBA Tuesday Preview: Unbeaten Pistons Host Knicks, Celtics in Brooklyn, NBA Cup Arrives Thursday

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | October 27, 2026

Four games on Tuesday's slate, with Detroit's unbeaten streak on the line against a motivated New York club fresh off a bad home loss.

Tuesday's NBA schedule is lean but loaded with storylines, headlined by an 8:00 PM ET matchup on Peacock that puts the unbeaten Detroit Pistons against the New York Knicks — a team looking to put Saturday's home loss to Orlando firmly in the rearview mirror.

Four games tonight. Here's what to watch.

Detroit Pistons vs. New York Knicks — 8:00 PM ET (Peacock)

This is the game of the night. The Pistons entered the 2026-27 season with championship aspirations after a 60-win campaign in 2025-26, and they have backed that up with an unbeaten start. Cade Cunningham has been sensational, and John Collins gives them a frontcourt presence that causes matchup problems across the league.

New York, meanwhile, enters Tuesday carrying some frustration. The Knicks dropped a home game to the Orlando Magic on Saturday — a result that stung, particularly at Madison Square Garden. They will want that back, and Detroit provides the perfect opportunity to make a statement in the other direction.

The storylines intersect in interesting ways: Detroit's perfect record versus New York's pride. Cunningham, coming off his best basketball, against a Knicks defense that prides itself on physicality. Can Detroit keep the unbeaten streak alive on the road? This one has everything.

Brooklyn Nets vs. Boston Celtics — 7:30 PM ET (NBA League Pass)

The defending champions are coming off a 35-point rout of Chicago and head across the state to Brooklyn on Tuesday night. The Nets are a young team still building their identity, which makes this a dangerous trap game for the Celtics only in theory — Boston has shown no interest in trap games so far this season.

Watch to see whether the Celtics maintain the same defensive intensity they brought to Monday's blowout. Road games on a short turnaround are where championship teams prove their habits are real.

Portland Trail Blazers vs. Los Angeles Lakers — 11:00 PM ET (Peacock)

The late game gives the West Coast fans something to stay up for. Los Angeles heads to Portland in what figures to be a competitive matchup between two teams still establishing their early-season identities. The Moda Center is a tough road environment, and Portland will be motivated to make a statement at home. The Lakers, coming off their own early results, will want to show they can win on the road late at night — the kind of game that separates contenders from pretenders over the course of a full season.

NBA Cup Group Stage Arrives Thursday

With Tuesday's games serving as the last regular-season slate before the mid-week reset, the NBA Cup group stage officially opens Thursday, October 30. The Cup format divides teams into conference groups for a round-robin phase, with the top teams advancing toward the championship game in December. Every game Thursday counts — and teams that got off to slow starts have Cup games as an opportunity to build momentum and chemistry quickly.

Several of Tuesday's teams — Detroit, Boston, New York, Los Angeles — will be among the Cup's early participants. Tuesday night's results will set the tone heading into Thursday's opening.

What's Next

After Tuesday, Wednesday is a light day before the NBA Cup opens Thursday with a full slate of Cup group games. The Cup runs through December 11, adding another layer of competition and stakes to what is already shaping up as a compelling early-season story.
