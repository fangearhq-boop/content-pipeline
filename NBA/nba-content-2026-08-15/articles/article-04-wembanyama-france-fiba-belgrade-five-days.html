Five Days: Wembanyama Returns to FIBA in Belgrade as France Locks In Its Roster

By Marcus Cole | August 15, 2026

His first France appearance since the 2024 Paris Olympics silver medal. Rudy Gobert and Evan Fournier back. A new coach. And the best basketball player in the world ready to remind Europe why FIBA misses him too.

Victor Wembanyama said it plainly: "I don't miss the NBA at the moment. But I do miss FIBA basketball." In five days, he gets to feel that again.

France meets Serbia in Belgrade on August 20 — the first of two exhibition games before FIBA World Cup qualifying begins. For Wembanyama, 22, the Belgrade game will be his first appearance in a French uniform since the 2024 Paris Olympics, where France won the silver medal. He skipped EuroBasket 2025. The wait for his return has been long by international basketball standards.

The Roster

France's 15-player FIBA roster is headlined by Wembanyama alongside Rudy Gobert and Evan Fournier — a core that makes France one of the three or four most dangerous national teams in the world. The trio last played together at the Paris Olympics. Fournier's return in particular adds a veteran shooting presence that French teams have leaned on in important moments for years.

The group will be coached by Frederic Fauthoux, who has taken over as French national team head coach. Fauthoux has not previously coached Wembanyama in an official competitive setting — the Belgrade game will be their first competitive collaboration.

The Stakes

The Belgrade exhibition on August 20 and a second tune-up in Orleans on August 23 serve as preparation for the games that actually matter. On August 27, France hosts Slovenia in FIBA World Cup qualifying. On August 30, France travels to Sweden. These are Group L qualifiers for the 2027 FIBA World Cup — real games with real consequences.

France enters the second round of qualifying in a position of strength, sitting atop Group L at 5-1. The first round was completed without Wembanyama. Now he joins, and the expectation is that France closes out its qualifying campaign at the top of the group.

Why Belgrade Is Worth Watching

Wembanyama is the NBA's All-Star and reigning Defensive Player of the Year. He signed a five-year, $252 million contract with the Spurs this offseason. He is, by most assessments, the most complete player in basketball at age 22. Belgrade will be a chance to watch him operate in FIBA's different pace, spacing, and defensive rules — and for European fans to see the player the Spurs have built their future around.

Serbia is not a soft opponent. Their national team finished runner-up at EuroBasket 2025 and enters the Belgrade game at home, in front of a crowd that will be fully behind them. Wembanyama vs. Serbia in Belgrade — with a new French coach on the sideline — is exactly the kind of international basketball moment that the offseason was waiting for.

What's Next

Five days to the Belgrade exhibition. Three days after that to Orleans. Then the qualifying games that actually count. The France national team's FIBA window ends August 30 in Sweden, with Wembanyama expected to play in all four games. After that, the Spurs begin training camp preparations and the NBA regular season countdown begins in earnest. For now, FIBA gets the alien — and Europe gets to remember what it missed.
