### STORY 2: Shaedon Sharpe Surgery Confirmed

**Long-Form Post**
SHAEDON SHARPE SURGERY UPDATE — BLAZERS FACE LONG WAIT

Portland Trail Blazers fans received the news they feared: Shaedon Sharpe underwent surgery to repair a lateral meniscus tear in his right knee, and he won't be evaluated for return until late February 2027 at the earliest.

The surgery was performed by Dr. Kristofer Jones at the UCLA Department of Orthopedic Surgery and Sports Medicine. The Blazers confirmed a six-month reevaluation window — an important distinction, because that is NOT a guaranteed return date. Meniscus repairs vary widely in recovery. February is a checkpoint, not a finish line.

Sharpe's absence is a massive loss. He averaged 20.8 points, 4.3 rebounds, and 2.6 assists in 50 games last season — a breakout campaign that had the basketball world buzzing about his ceiling. Portland brought in Ja Morant this offseason to pair with Sharpe in what was supposed to be one of the league's most dangerous young backcourts.

That plan is shelved before it even starts. Morant opens training camp as Portland's only established star. The pressure on him is enormous.

### STORY 3: Kawhi Cleared — Toronto Trade Finalization Now Imminent

**Long-Form Post**
THE KAWHI LEONARD SAGA IS OVER — HE'S GOING BACK TO TORONTO

After two months of waiting, the longest offseason saga in the NBA is finally reaching its conclusion.

The NBA cleared the Los Angeles Clippers of cap circumvention in their investigation into payments made to Kawhi Leonard through the now-bankrupt company Aspiration. With that investigation resolved, the blockbuster trade sending Leonard back to Toronto can now be officially finalized.

The deal: Leonard to the Raptors in exchange for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, two unprotected first-round picks (2031 and 2033), one pick swap, and two second-round picks. Agreed in principle June 30, put on hold July 9, now cleared to complete.

Leonard was cleared personally throughout this process — the liability belonged to the Clippers organization. Toronto's training camp opens September 28 in Quebec City.

Seven years after Kawhi's iconic corner four bounced on the rim and fell through to send Toronto to the 2019 Finals — where he won championship and Finals MVP — he is coming home.
