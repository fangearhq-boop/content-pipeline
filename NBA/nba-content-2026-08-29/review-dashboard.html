The NBA cleared the Los Angeles Clippers of cap circumvention in their investigation into payments made to Kawhi Leonard through the now-bankrupt company Aspiration. With that investigation resolved, the blockbuster trade sending Leonard back to Toronto can now be officially finalized. The deal: Leonard to the Raptors in exchange for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, two unprotected first-round picks (2031 and 2033), one pick swap, and two second-round picks. Toronto's training camp opens September 28 in Quebec City. Seven years after Kawhi's iconic corner four bounced on the rim and fell through to send Toronto to the 2019 Finals — where he won championship and Finals MVP — he is coming home.
