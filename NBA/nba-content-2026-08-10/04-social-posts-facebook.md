### STORY 2: Kawhi Leonard — Six Weeks to Training Camp, Still No Resolution

**Long-Form Post**
⚖️ Six weeks. That's how long until NBA training camps open.

And the most complicated trade in NBA history is still completely frozen.

The Kawhi Leonard situation between the Los Angeles Clippers and the Toronto Raptors has now been unresolved for more than six weeks — and according to ESPN, the NBA's investigation could extend all the way into 2027. Here's where things stand today:

**The original probe:** The investigation centers on a $28 million endorsement deal between Leonard and Aspiration — a green bank that also held a $300 million, 23-year sponsorship deal with the Clippers franchise before going bankrupt. The NBA is examining whether the Clippers used Aspiration to funnel additional compensation to Leonard outside the salary cap.

**The expanding investigation:** CBS Sports now reports a second deal — with Daktronics, an electronics company — is also under review. The investigation isn't getting smaller. It's getting larger.

**The trade:** In late June, the Clippers and Raptors agreed on a blockbuster deal. Kawhi Leonard goes to Toronto in exchange for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, and two unprotected first-round picks (2031 and 2033). The deal is agreed upon — but completely frozen while the investigation plays out.

**Toronto's dilemma:** The NBA told the Raptors they'd assume the risk of any investigation outcome if they finalize the trade before the probe concludes. That could mean completing the deal — and then having Leonard suspended, the trade unwound, or facing other penalties. So Toronto waits.

**The NBPA factor:** The players' union has warned it "wouldn't hesitate" to demand neutral arbitration if penalties are sought without sufficient evidence. Any arbitration ruling would then face a binding three-person appeals panel jointly appointed by both sides. That process has no hard deadline.

Kawhi Leonard is owed $50.3 million in 2026-27. He is technically a Clipper with no team to play for. Training camp opens in approximately six weeks.

The clock is ticking — loudly. ⏰

Let's put that in perspective. LeBron James's entire four-year rookie contract in 2003 was $18.8 million. The NBA's economics have transformed — but even in today's salary landscape, $60 million in one year is a number no player has ever reached.

Here's the structure:

Curry is entering the final year of his existing contract worth approximately $62.6 million in 2026-27. The expected extension — 2 years, $136.7 million total — locks him in through the 2027-28 season. After a 37-45 season, they need him locked in through the rebuild.

Curry, 38, has no interest in exploring free agency. Without the extension, he would become an unrestricted free agent next summer — for the first time in his career. Everyone understands that's not happening.

The window opens August 29. Expect the signature in September.

History is 19 days away.

- **August 20** — Exhibition vs. Serbia (Belgrade)
- **August 23** — Exhibition vs. Serbia (Orleans)
- **August 27** — 2027 FIBA World Cup qualifier vs. Slovenia (home)
