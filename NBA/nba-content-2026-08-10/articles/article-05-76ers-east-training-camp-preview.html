Seven Weeks From Camp, the 76ers Are Already the East's Most Compelling Training Camp Story

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | August 10, 2026

NBA Offseason / Philadelphia 76ers / LeBron James / Joel Embiid

Training camp opens in approximately seven weeks. And already, no team in the Eastern Conference generates more genuine curiosity than the Philadelphia 76ers. Not the defending champion New York Knicks. Not the Giannis-reinforced Miami Heat. Not the Boston Celtics, who remain built to compete. The 76ers — because of who they are, how they were assembled, and what they are being asked to prove — are the East's most compelling camp storyline before a single drill has been run.

The Roster, In Full

Philadelphia's projected starting five: LeBron James, Joel Embiid, Tyrese Maxey, Jaylen Brown, Jared Edgecombe. Five players. Five combined MVP awards between James and Embiid alone. A two-time Finals MVP at center. A former All-Star and championship contributor who made the move from Boston. And a young defensive talent in Edgecombe who gives the lineup athleticism at the wing.

LeBron signed a two-year deal worth approximately $8 million with a player option. He described it as "the last decision" of his career — words that, coming from him, land with full weight. He didn't choose Philadelphia for the money. He chose it because he believes this group can compete for a championship.

The Early Signs Are Encouraging

The 76ers are not waiting for camp to build their chemistry. LeBron and Tyrese Maxey have been sharing early-morning workouts at their training facility, with Maxey reportedly arriving before LeBron on at least one occasion — drawing an amused public acknowledgment from James. The competitiveness is already present, and it's between teammates.

Joel Embiid, for his part, may be entering training camp in the best physical condition of his NBA career. 76ers president Mike Gansey confirmed it directly: Embiid completed "one of the first offseasons he is healthy going into it" — a full program, no restrictions, no setbacks. For a player whose career has been intermittently derailed by injury and health complications, this represents something genuinely new.

LeBron and Embiid were Paris Olympics gold medal teammates in 2024. The familiarity between them — what the other needs, how they read the game, where each is most dangerous — doesn't start at zero in late September. It picks up from a relationship already tested under international competition pressure.

The Eastern Conference They're Walking Into

The East is legitimately loaded. The Knicks return their championship core intact and will raise a banner on opening night at Madison Square Garden. The Heat have Giannis Antetokounmpo — one of the two or three best players in the world — joining Bam Adebayo in Miami's frontcourt. The Celtics still have the infrastructure of a 2024 champion. The competition is real.

What the 76ers bring is something none of those teams have: the possibility that LeBron James and Joel Embiid, healthy and motivated, playing together with a surrounding cast that includes Maxey and Brown, could be the best roster in the league. That's not a guarantee. But it's a genuine possibility — and the genuine possibility of something historic is exactly what makes this the East's most compelling training camp story heading into 2026-27.

What's Next

Philadelphia's training camp opens in late September, roughly seven weeks from now. The preseason begins in early October. The regular season tips off October 20. Before any of that, the NBA schedule drops Thursday, August 13 — and the first thing every 76ers fan will do is find LeBron's debut date and circle it on the calendar. The process has arrived.
