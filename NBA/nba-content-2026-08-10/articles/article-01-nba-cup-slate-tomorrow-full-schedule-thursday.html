The NBA Cup Slate Drops Tomorrow — Then the Full Schedule Thursday. The Offseason Is Over.

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | August 10, 2026

NBA Season Preview / Schedule Release

One day. The NBA Cup group assignments land tomorrow, Tuesday August 11. Forty-eight hours after that, on Thursday August 13, all 1,230 games of the 2026-27 regular season are revealed at once. For a basketball world that has spent months watching trades, signings, and investigations play out in slow motion, the next 72 hours represent something different — the moment the actual season takes shape.

October 20 is 71 days away. The offseason, for all practical purposes, is ending.

What Drops Tomorrow: The NBA Cup Groups

Tuesday's release is the first piece of the scheduling puzzle: the Emirates NBA Cup group assignments. The fourth edition of the tournament follows the established format — six groups, three per conference, five teams each. Group Play runs on Fridays from late October through late November, with additional Cup Nights on November 24-25. Quarterfinals go to local markets December 4-5. The championship game is set for Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis on December 11.

The group assignments matter more than they might seem. For a team that went deep in the Cup last season, the draw can either ease or complicate the early schedule. For a new-look roster still finding its chemistry in October, the Cup schedule sets the early competitive rhythm.

The Christmas Day Intrigue

No part of Thursday's schedule release is generating more conversation than December 25. The ingredients are historically loaded. LeBron James is a Philadelphia 76er. Giannis Antetokounmpo is in Miami. Victor Wembanyama is entering his third NBA season in San Antonio. The New York Knicks are the defending champions. Five games, Christmas Day, and the league has never had more legitimate franchise-level stars to deploy on a single date.

Industry expectation points to LeBron and the 76ers against the Knicks as the marquee pairing. Philadelphia-New York, in the Knicks' championship year, on Christmas Day, would be a television event regardless of what surrounds it. But Giannis vs. Wembanyama is equally compelling. Wemby vs. Embiid — two of the most physically imposing players in the league — is a matchup that sells itself. The scheduling committee has more options than it has had in a decade.

The Marquee Non-Holiday Dates

Beyond Christmas, the schedule is loaded with rivalry anchors that will be circled the moment Thursday's release drops. LeBron's first road trip to New York as a 76er. His first game in Boston. The Knicks' banner night on October 20 at Madison Square Garden — with whoever the league assigns to crash the party facing one of the most hostile home-court environments in sports. The first time Giannis plays in Milwaukee as an opponent. These are the moments that make the schedule release an event of its own.

What's Next

Once the schedule is in hand, the offseason's storylines snap into context — and every team's season is suddenly real. The countdown is almost over.
