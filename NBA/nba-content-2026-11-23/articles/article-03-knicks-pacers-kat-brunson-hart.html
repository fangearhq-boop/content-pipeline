Karl-Anthony Towns Posts 30/13, Knicks Win Fifth Straight With 113-106 Victory Over Pacers

By Damon Pierce | November 23, 2026 | Hoop Heroes

Karl-Anthony Towns had 30 points and 13 rebounds, Jalen Brunson added 22 and Josh Hart recorded a triple-double as the New York Knicks beat the Indiana Pacers 113-106 on Sunday night to win five consecutive games and push themselves into the upper tier of the Eastern Conference standings.

The three-headed contribution — Towns dominant in the post, Brunson running the offense, Hart covering every loose ball and crashing every board — has become the Knicks' calling card during this run. When all three are operating at a high level on the same night, New York becomes a matchup problem for most teams in the league.

Towns Controls the Game

Towns' 30-point, 13-rebound performance was his most complete game of the week. His versatility — the ability to score from the mid-range, operate in the post or step out to the perimeter — created constant decision-making challenges for Indiana's defense.

His 13 rebounds were especially impactful. The Knicks have leaned on their size advantage throughout this winning streak, and Towns as an anchor on the glass gives New York a foundation that is difficult to match. When he is engaged both ways, the Knicks look like a legitimate contender rather than a team waiting for its ceiling to emerge.

Brunson and Hart Complete the Picture

Brunson's 22 points extended a stretch of consistent production that has made him one of the most reliable guards in the Eastern Conference. He is not flashy, but his decision-making in pick-and-roll actions and his ability to get to the line when Indiana's defense got physical kept the Knicks' offense humming.

Hart's triple-double was perhaps the most unexpected piece of the puzzle. His box score contribution — points, rebounds, assists spread across categories — reflects what Hart does on a nightly basis: competing for every possession, refusing to let his team take a play off. Triple-doubles tend to find Josh Hart because Hart tends to find every battle on the court.

Five Straight and Rising

Five consecutive wins in November is a meaningful statement. The Knicks entered the season as a team with expectations, and after a slow start, this streak suggests they are finding their form at the right time. Madison Square Garden has been electric during this run, and the Knicks' depth — beyond their starting trio — has held up under accumulated minutes.

What's Next

New York will look to push their streak to six as the week continues. Indiana faces a tougher road after Sunday's loss, with their own playoff positioning still very much in question. For the Knicks, the goal now is consistency — maintaining this level through the full month of December and arriving at the holiday break as one of the East's most dangerous teams.
