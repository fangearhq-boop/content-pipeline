NBA Cup Group Stage Nears End: Heat, Spurs, Knicks Lead the Race to Las Vegas

By Marcus Cole | November 23, 2026 | Hoop Heroes

The 2026-27 NBA Cup's group stage is entering its final week, and the picture of who advances to the knockout round is coming into focus. With the in-season tournament's elimination games set to begin in December, several teams have emerged as clear frontrunners — and a few others are fighting to stay alive.

The Miami Heat, riding a seven-game overall winning streak, have been undefeated in their NBA Cup group and sit at the top of their East division. San Antonio's Spurs have the best point differential in the tournament, a reflection of the blowout wins they have been delivering. The New York Knicks, in the middle of a five-game win streak, have been one of the most consistent Cup performers in the East.

The East Picture

Miami's dominance of their group has been built on the same formula fueling their regular-season run — defense, depth and Bam Adebayo's versatility at the center of everything. Their NBA Cup record has followed their overall form, and they enter the final week with separation over the rest of the group.

The Knicks' emergence as a Cup contender has been less about individual brilliance and more about the Karl-Anthony Towns, Jalen Brunson and Josh Hart combination finding its rhythm. When three different players can contribute at a high level on the same night, as they did Sunday against Indiana, New York is difficult to beat in the short, single-elimination format the knockout round provides.

The West Picture

San Antonio's point differential tells a story of dominance. The Spurs, led by Victor Wembanyama's season-long spectacular form, have not just won their Cup games — they have done so by large margins, which is a reason to take them seriously as a potential tournament champion. Dallas and Denver have also been among the West's leaders in group standings.

The tournament's knockout format rewards peak performance over consistency, which is why teams that have shown the ability to blow out opponents — San Antonio and Miami — are worth watching as the bracket forms.

What to Watch This Week

The final week of group play will determine who secures knockout round berths and who misses out. Teams on the bubble will play with added urgency, while leaders like Miami and San Antonio will try to arrive at the knockout stage with momentum. The eight-team bracket that emerges will set up December's high-stakes games in Las Vegas.

What's Next

Group play concludes by the end of November, with the knockout round beginning in December. Eight teams — four from each conference — will advance, and the eventual champion will be crowned at a neutral site in Las Vegas. With the Heat, Spurs, Knicks and several others having built real form during the tournament window, this year's NBA Cup knockout field should be one of the most competitive in the event's history.
