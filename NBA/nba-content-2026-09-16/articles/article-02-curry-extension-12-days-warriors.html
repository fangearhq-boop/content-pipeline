12 Days to Decide — Curry Extension Deadline Looms as Warriors Media Day Approaches

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | September 16, 2026

Stephen Curry and the Golden State Warriors have twelve days to formalize the next chapter of one of the great player-franchise marriages in NBA history. September 28 — Warriors media day — is the deadline for the two sides to finalize a contract extension, and while ESPN has confirmed both parties share "mutual desire" to keep Curry in Golden State for the rest of his career, no deal has been signed as of September 16.

Curry is eligible for a maximum extension of two years and $136.7 million. His current deal already guarantees him $62.6 million for the 2026-27 season — the highest single-season salary in league history. A full extension would lock him up through 2028-29 at minimum.

Warriors Are Letting Curry Lead

According to multiple reports, Golden State is taking an unusual approach to the negotiation: they're letting the 38-year-old point guard decide the structure. One year, two years, however he wants to design it. The message from the front office is clear — Curry is the institution, and the institution decides his own terms.

General manager Mike Dunleavy Jr. has expressed confidence publicly that Curry won't be going anywhere. The Warriors have never had to negotiate against the backdrop of a legitimate trade market for Curry, and that reality hasn't changed. This is about timing and structure, not whether the deal gets done.

What an Extension Means Financially

The numbers matter beyond just Curry's paycheck. A full two-year extension at approximately $136.7 million would cut the Warriors' projected cap flexibility roughly in half. Golden State has built its offseason strategy around the assumption that Curry will stay — but the precise structure of the extension will determine how much room they have to maneuver around him.

For context, a one-year extension would keep them more flexible heading into the summer of 2028, a critical free agent class. Two years provides Curry the security he's earned and the Warriors the roster stability they want, but at the cost of cap space they might otherwise deploy.

What's Next

Warriors training camp opens September 29 — the day after media day. If a deal isn't announced on September 28, camp opens with Curry in the final year of his contract and extension talks presumably ongoing under the new season's rules. Watch media day closely: Curry's tone and the Warriors' messaging will signal whether ink is imminent. If the deadline passes without a deal, the story shifts entirely.
