LeBron, Brown, and Maxey's First Workout Video Drops — Sixers Superteam Chemistry Already Building

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | September 16, 2026

Before the Philadelphia 76ers have played a single preseason minute together, the basketball world already has a preview of what their star trio might look like. Bleacher Report published video Tuesday of LeBron James, Jaylen Brown, and Tyrese Maxey's first joint workout session with trainer Chris Johnson — and by all accounts, the three looked comfortable from the jump.

The clips show the trio working through pick-and-roll reads, cutting through half-court sets, and finishing at the rim. The movement looks fluid. Nobody appears to be figuring out a new system. Whatever chemistry concerns existed before this session got a little quieter.

The Weight of What's Being Assembled

This is not a normal collection of basketball players. LeBron James, in his 24th NBA season, is chasing a fifth championship with his fourth franchise. Jaylen Brown is a Finals MVP who won it all in Boston. Tyrese Maxey is a borderline All-NBA guard entering his prime at 25 years old.

The 76ers haven't won an NBA championship since 1983 — a 43-year drought that has outlasted every other active title drought in professional sports. The roster they've assembled, adding Anfernee Simons, Dean Wade, Kentavious Caldwell-Pope, and Labaron Philon Jr. around that core, has the look of a legitimate contender if the pieces fit.

The Schedule to Watch

Training camp opens September 29. The first real public glimpse of this lineup comes October 8, when Philadelphia travels to Brooklyn for a preseason matchup against the Nets at Barclays Center (7:30 PM ET). The regular-season opener is October 20 at Madison Square Garden against the defending champion New York Knicks — a statement-game environment from night one.

The Sixers also carry a franchise-record 34 national television games this season, a reflection of how much interest their roster has generated. Every one of those broadcasts is a measuring stick for how well the pieces actually fit in game conditions.

What's Next

Camp opens in 13 days. Watch for the Sixers' practice reports and any early lineups Nick Nurse experiments with in the preseason. The Joel Embiid health situation will be the other significant storyline as October arrives — a fully healthy Embiid alongside LeBron, Brown, and Maxey would give Philadelphia perhaps the deepest superstar lineup in league history. The workout video is just the opening act.
