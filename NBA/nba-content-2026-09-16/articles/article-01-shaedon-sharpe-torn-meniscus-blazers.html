Shaedon Sharpe Tears Meniscus, Expected to Miss Six Months — Blazers Lose 20-PPG Star Before Camp Opens

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | September 16, 2026

Portland Trail Blazers guard Shaedon Sharpe suffered a lateral meniscus tear in his right knee during an offseason workout and is expected to miss approximately six months, ESPN's Shams Charania reported. Surgery was performed successfully, but the 23-year-old will be sidelined until late February or early March 2027 — a window that encompasses roughly the first 59 games of the 2026-27 season.

Sharpe averaged 20.8 points per game for the Blazers last season, making him one of the youngest 20-point scorers in the league. His development arc had Portland fans genuinely excited about where the organization was heading. Now those hopes are on hold before training camp even gets underway.

A Loaded Backcourt Takes the Hit

The immediate concern for Portland is depth — but the Blazers are better equipped to absorb the blow than most teams would be. Head coach Chauncey Billups will still have Ja Morant, Damian Lillard, and Jrue Holiday in the backcourt, an unusual collection of veteran talent capable of carrying the offensive load.

That trio alone gives Portland a legitimate chance to be competitive during Sharpe's absence. The problem isn't the near term so much as the bigger picture: losing a 23-year-old averaging 20-plus points before his breakout season potentially shifts Portland's long-term trajectory.

The Timeline and Its Implications

A six-month return window puts Sharpe back on the court in late February or early March, which corresponds to the final stretch of the regular season and, theoretically, the postseason push. If Portland can stay in the playoff race without him, his return could serve as a meaningful boost at exactly the right moment.

The harder question is what the extended absence does to his momentum. At 23, Sharpe was approaching the age when young wings tend to make the leap into All-Star conversation. A full season lost to injury is significant developmental time that he won't get back.

What's Next

Portland's training camp opens September 29. The Blazers will need to address their backcourt depth in camp and may look to the transaction wire for insurance options. Watch for Sharpe's formal injury designation and whether the team makes any moves to shore up their wing rotation ahead of opening night. His return in late winter will be one of the more closely watched individual storylines of the 2026-27 season.
