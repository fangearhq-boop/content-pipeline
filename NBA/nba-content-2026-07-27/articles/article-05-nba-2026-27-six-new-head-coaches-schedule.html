2026-27 NBA Season: Six New Head Coaches and a Schedule Finally Ready to Drop

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | July 27, 2026

With LeBron James' destination finally settled, the NBA can move forward on two fronts that have been on hold for weeks. The 2026-27 schedule — withheld pending James' free agency decision — is expected to be released within days. And as the league prepares for a season that has been reshaped by blockbuster moves, six franchises will enter it with a brand new coach on the sideline.

One-fifth of the league pressed reset on its coaching staff this offseason. The results range from experience-building transitions to high-stakes debuts that could define franchises for years.

The Six New Coaches

Tiago Splitter — Chicago Bulls: The former San Antonio Spurs center spent years learning the system that produced consistent deep playoff runs. Now he applies those lessons as a head coach for the first time, inheriting a Bulls team that just added DeMar DeRozan — one of the league's most reliable mid-range scorers — as its centerpiece.

Taylor Jenkins — Milwaukee Bucks: Jenkins takes over a Bucks franchise that traded Giannis Antetokounmpo to the Miami Heat earlier this summer. The reset is real in Milwaukee, and Jenkins will be tasked with developing the young players left behind while competing in an increasingly difficult Eastern Conference.

Dusty May — Dallas Mavericks: Perhaps no new hire carries more immediate pressure. May transitions from winning an NCAA championship with Michigan to coaching Cooper Flagg, the No. 1 overall pick in the 2026 NBA Draft. How Flagg develops under May's guidance could determine the franchise's trajectory for a decade.

Sean Sweeney — Orlando Magic: Sweeney moves from an assistant role into the Magic head coaching job, inheriting a young core that has been quietly building toward relevance. Orlando has the assets and the players to take the next step — Sweeney's job is to define what that step looks like.

Micah Nori — Portland Trail Blazers: Nori takes over a Blazers team that includes Ja Morant, acquired in a trade from Memphis. The young talent is there. Nori, who comes from a Minnesota Timberwolves assistant role, now has to channel it into a cohesive team identity.

Jamahl Mosley — New Orleans Pelicans: Mosley transitions from the Orlando Magic to the Pelicans, where his development-focused approach is expected to accelerate the growth of a young New Orleans core. The Pelicans have been one of the most intriguing teams in the league when healthy — Mosley's challenge is sustaining that.

The Schedule Is Coming

The NBA deliberately withheld the 2026-27 schedule while LeBron James remained unsigned. The reasoning was straightforward: James' decision was the single most consequential story in the sport, and the schedule's marquee matchups — opening night, Christmas Day, nationally televised rivalries — would be shaped entirely by where he landed.

Now that he is a Philadelphia 76er, those matchups can be constructed. Philadelphia against the Miami Heat (Giannis) and Boston Celtics (Paul George) will be among the most anticipated games of the year. Golden State hosting a Big Three 76ers squad for the first time. The Dallas Mavericks and Cooper Flagg's NBA debut, wherever it falls on the calendar.

The full 2026-27 schedule is expected within the next week. Marquee matchups and opening night details are typically teased first, followed by the complete release shortly after.

The Most Unpredictable Season in Years

Six coaching changes. LeBron in Philadelphia. Giannis in Miami. Ja Morant in Portland. Cooper Flagg entering the league in Dallas. The Kawhi Leonard investigation approaching its conclusion. The potential return of Kawhi to Toronto.

The 2026-27 NBA season is shaping up to be as unpredictable as any in recent memory. The power dynamics have shifted dramatically. The coaching landscapes are new. The questions are everywhere.

When the schedule drops, fans will finally be able to start circling dates. The answers to those questions will come when the games begin.

What's Next

The 2026-27 NBA schedule release is expected within days. Six new head coaches will hold press conferences and begin training camp preparations as the offseason enters its final phase. The Kawhi Leonard investigation ruling and potential press conference for LeBron James in Philadelphia are the two major storylines still outstanding before the league turns its attention fully to the upcoming season.
