NBA Preseason Tips Off Oct. 3 With First-Ever Game in Quebec City

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes | September 19, 2026

NBA

The 2026-27 NBA preseason begins October 3, and it opens with something the league has never done before. The Toronto Raptors host the Miami Heat at Videotron Centre in Quebec City — the first NBA game ever played in that city. For the Raptors, whose Canadian fan base extends well beyond the Greater Toronto Area, it is the realization of years of pressure to bring professional basketball to one of the country's most passionate sports markets.

The game is a solo opener. No other preseason games are scheduled October 3; the full schedule picks up the following day. That structure gives Quebec City its own moment — a deliberate choice by the league to mark the occasion as historically distinct.

A Canadian Expansion Push

The NBA's footprint in Canada has always been larger than its presence suggests. Toronto drew nearly four million fans to its championship parade in 2019, and Raptors games regularly sell out. But the franchise's games have been anchored to Scotiabank Arena in downtown Toronto since the team's founding in 1995.

Quebec City represents an expansion of that reach. Videotron Centre, an 18,000-seat arena, has served primarily as an AHL venue since opening in 2015, but has long been discussed as a potential NBA expansion or relocated franchise site. A preseason game there is not a commitment to that future — but it is an acknowledgment that the market exists and that the Raptors have fans there who deserve to see their team.

The Raptors also play the Los Angeles Clippers in Vancouver on October 10, giving the franchise two road games in Canadian cities outside Toronto during a single preseason. The league's interest in developing those markets further is evident in the scheduling.

The International Schedule and Macao

Beyond Canada, the NBA is sending two teams overseas before the preseason gets fully underway. The Dallas Mavericks and Houston Rockets play a two-game series at The Venetian Arena in Macao on October 9 and 11 — which is why both franchises reported to training camp on September 22, a full week ahead of the rest of the league. Their media days were September 21; all other teams hold media days on September 28.

The Macao games continue the NBA's longstanding relationship with Asia as a key international market. The Venetian Arena, with a capacity of roughly 15,000, provides a large-venue setting for what is essentially a two-game preseason road trip for both franchises.

The Full Preseason Picture

League-wide training camps open September 29, following veteran report dates and media days on September 28. Preseason runs October 3 through October 16, with multiple games scheduled across most dates in that window. The regular season tips off October 20 — beginning with the most anticipated opening night in recent memory at Madison Square Garden, where the Knicks raise their championship banner before facing LeBron James and the 76ers.

The preseason serves its usual function: evaluating rosters, integrating new players, and building conditioning ahead of an 82-game season. But this year's camp will carry an unusual overlay of contract and roster news that remained unresolved through September. Stephen Curry's extension decision. Jalen Duren's qualifying offer deadline. Tyrese Haliburton's return from an Achilles tear in Indiana. Each of these stories gets answered — or at least further defined — when camps open in 10 days.

What's Next

Most NBA teams hold media day September 28, with camps opening September 29. The Mavericks and Rockets are already a week ahead. Preseason games begin October 3 in Quebec City, with the full schedule running through October 16. The regular season tips off October 20. The 2026-27 NBA season is 10 days from its first practice.
