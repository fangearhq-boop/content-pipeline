### Long-Form Post 📋 A quick clarification on the Stephen Curry extension timeline — because there's been some confusion this week. Stephen Curry does NOT become eligible to sign his contract extension until August 29, 2026. Not today. Not this week. August 29. When that date arrives, the Warriors are expected to offer Curry a 2-year, $136.7 million deal — roughly $68.35 million per year. That would carry him through his age-40 season and his 24th year in the NBA. ESPN's Anthony Slater has reported "mutual interest" from both sides, and those close to Curry say he wants to finish his career in Golden State. But there's a genuine question the Warriors need to answer: can they give Curry a roster worth coming back for? This summer brought some answers. Draymond Green re-signed on a 1-year, $27.7 million deal. Gary Payton II is back. But LeBron James chose Philadelphia — the Warriors were reportedly in the mix — and the path to a championship isn't obvious from where they currently stand. Curry shot 42% from three last season at age 38. He's not done. He just needs the right supporting cast. Watch August 29. ---
