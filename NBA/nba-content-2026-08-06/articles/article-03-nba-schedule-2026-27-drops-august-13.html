The 2026-27 NBA Schedule Is Coming — Here's What to Expect When It Drops August 13

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | August 6, 2026

NBA / 2026-27 Season Preview

The wait is almost over. The 2026-27 NBA schedule is expected to be released this week — most likely on Thursday, August 13 — and this season's calendar promises to be one of the most marquee-heavy in recent memory.

NBA insider Marc Stein reported the schedule is "on course to be announced next week," and the pattern is consistent: each of the last three schedule releases fell on a Thursday (August 14, 2025; August 15, 2024; August 17, 2023). A league spokesperson confirmed the announcement is on track for mid-August.

What Cleared the Path

The reason this schedule took longer than usual to finalize is well-documented: LeBron James's prolonged free agency held up the entire process. The league needed to know where the sport's biggest attraction was landing before it could build its marquee-game schedule around him.

Now that LeBron is officially a Philadelphia 76er, the scheduling team can get to work. And they have no shortage of compelling match-ups to build around.

Games to Circle Now

While the full schedule won't be known until release day, several anchor dates and narratives are already set:

Knicks Banner Night — October 20, Madison Square Garden. New York raised its first championship banner in 53 years last spring, ending one of the longest droughts in American professional sports. The Garden will be electric for opening night. Expect the league to give this a national broadcast.

LeBron's 76ers Debut. James hasn't played a regular-season game in Philadelphia yet. The league will plant that opener in prime time. Whether it's opening night or the first week of the season, LeBron's first game as a Sixer will be one of the most-watched regular-season games in years.

Victor Wembanyama's San Antonio Spurs. Wembanyama just signed a five-year, $252 million extension and is entering Year 4 alongside De'Aaron Fox ($228M deal) and a roster stacked with young talent. The Spurs are a must-watch team again.

The Six New Head Coaches. Dusty May in Dallas, Adrian Griffin in Milwaukee, Torsten Splitter in Chicago, Nate Sweeney in Orlando, Willie Green in New Orleans, and Micah Nori in Portland are all starting their tenures in 2026-27. Those early-season games — especially the first home games for each new coach — will carry extra narrative weight.

The Broader Picture

The 2026-27 season is shaping up to feature one of the most dramatic East-West competency gaps in recent seasons. The East has the 76ers, Knicks (defending champions), Heat (now featuring Giannis Antetokounmpo), and a rebuilt Celtics contending from the conference title level. The West has Wembanyama's Spurs, the rebuilt Trail Blazers with Damian Lillard and Ja Morant, and a Warriors team waiting on Stephen Curry's extension decision.

The schedule will help determine who plays whom early — and who has to navigate the gauntlet of nationally televised games while the rest of the league is still finding its footing.

What's Next

Mark Thursday, August 13, on your calendar. Once the schedule drops, teams will begin finalizing their training camp plans, and the 2026-27 season will officially shift from offseason speculation to operational reality. Training camps open in late September, with the season tipping off in October.

For fans of the 76ers, Knicks, Spurs, or any of the league's major contenders — the schedule release will be the first real look at the road ahead.
