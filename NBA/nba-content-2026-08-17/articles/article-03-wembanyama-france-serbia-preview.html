Wembanyama Returns to FIBA Thursday — France vs. Serbia Preview

By Damon Pierce | August 17, 2026 | Hoop Heroes

Victor Wembanyama plays basketball on Thursday. Not NBA basketball — FIBA basketball. And given how much he says he has missed it, the world's most dominant big man is expected to arrive in Belgrade ready to remind everyone why he's the most anticipated international player since Dirk Nowitzki.

France faces Serbia in Belgrade on August 20 in an exhibition warmup — Wembanyama's first game for the French national team since the 2024 Olympic final in Paris. He's been away from international competition for nearly two years, and by his own admission, he's been counting the days.

"I do not miss the NBA right now," Wembanyama told FIBA.basketball. "I miss FIBA basketball."

A New Coach, a Familiar Mission

The most significant new variable in France's program is head coach Frederic Fauthoux, who replaced longtime coach Vincent Collet after the 2024 Paris Olympics. Fauthoux has never coached Wembanyama at the senior level — Thursday in Belgrade will be their first game together.

The roster Fauthoux has assembled is formidable. Rudy Gobert and Evan Fournier join Wembanyama on the front court and perimeter, making this arguably the most talented French squad since the gold-medal run in Tokyo. The question heading into these warmup games is how quickly Fauthoux and Wembanyama find their rhythm together.

Notably, the San Antonio Spurs coaching staff and organizational personnel traveled to Paris alongside Wemby. The franchise is fully invested in his international growth and development — a sign of how seriously the Spurs take their relationship with the most important player they've had since Tim Duncan.

The Full August Schedule

France's August window is loaded with meaningful basketball. After the Belgrade exhibition against Serbia on August 20, there's a rematch in Orleans on August 23. Then the real stakes arrive: FIBA 2027 World Cup qualifying games against Slovenia at home on August 27 and a road match against Sweden on August 30.

France enters the qualifiers already sitting at 5-1 in Group L of World Cup qualifying. With Wemby healthy and Gobert and Fournier alongside him, clinching a berth in the 2027 World Cup should be within reach — but the qualifying games count, and nothing in international basketball is guaranteed.

Why It Matters Beyond the Games

Wembanyama's FIBA return is bigger than France's World Cup qualifying campaign. It's a reminder of how international basketball has become one of the NBA's most powerful marketing tools. The games Thursday and Saturday will draw global audiences, and every moment of Wemby in a French jersey is a content goldmine for basketball fans around the world.

For Spurs fans specifically, these games are a chance to see Wembanyama in a different context — playing in the flow of international basketball, which demands more of a big man's passing, decision-making, and defensive versatility than an NBA regular season game typically does. Wemby's international performances last summer were some of the most impressive of his career.

What's Next

Thursday's exhibition against Serbia in Belgrade is the appetizer. The qualifying games against Slovenia (Aug 27) and Sweden (Aug 30) are where France's World Cup hopes will be tested. Watch for Wembanyama's early interactions with coach Fauthoux — that relationship will define France's international program for the next several years.
