# NBA Daily Brief — August 21, 2026

## Top Stories

### STORY 1: Jokic Outduels Wembanyama — Serbia Edges France 90-87 in Belgrade (Tier 1)
Nikola Jokic delivered a masterclass — 20 points, 9 rebounds, 8 assists in 29 minutes — as Serbia edged France 90-87 in Belgrade on Wednesday night. Victor Wembanyama struggled in his France captaincy debut, managing only 10 points and 4 rebounds while committing 6 turnovers in 24 minutes. Despite a spectacular windmill dunk, Wemby was kept in check by Serbia's physical defense. France's Amine Noua led the losing side with 14 points. The two teams rematch in Orleans on August 23.

### STORY 2: Kawhi Leonard Investigation — Forbes Flags Salary-Cap Loophole Risk (Tier 1)
The Kawhi Leonard/Clippers investigation continues to draw analysis as Forbes warned this week (August 18) that the NBA's final ruling could create a salary-cap loophole precedent league-wide. The Wachtell Lipton outside law firm's fact-finding is complete; sanctions against the Clippers are expected but Leonard's personal clearance has already been established. Sources say the trade (Leonard to Toronto for Ingram + Dick + picks) will ultimately clear. Resolution still expected before the 2026-27 season, though ESPN raised the possibility of a 2027 extension.

### STORY 3: Stephen Curry Extension — 8 Days to August 29 Window (Tier 2)
The clock keeps ticking. Curry's extension window opens in 8 days on August 29. The Warriors will offer a two-year, $136.7 million max; a one-year $62.6M option is also on the table. ESPN's Anthony Slater confirmed mutual interest remains strong. The Warriors are prepared to offer Curry exactly what he wants up to the max. No deal until the 29th.

### STORY 4: Edwards and LaMelo Take the Timberwolves to France (Tier 2)
Anthony Edwards organized a full Timberwolves roster trip to France ahead of training camp — workouts and sightseeing — to build team chemistry with their new backcourt partner LaMelo Ball. Edwards said: "Right now, I don't think we're thinking about winning the championship... We got LaMelo, a new player, and a bunch of new pieces. We are just trying to build it together." Trade terms confirmed: Ball + Josh Green for Naz Reid + 2033 unprotected 1st + 3 pick swaps + 3 seconds.

### STORY 5: James Harden Signs 3-Year, $97M Deal with Cavaliers (Tier 1)
Breaking: James Harden has agreed to sign with the Cleveland Cavaliers on a three-year, $97 million contract with a player option for the 2028-29 season. The Cavaliers also acquired Peyton Watson in a three-team deal with the Denver Nuggets and Los Angeles Clippers, sending Max Strus to the Clippers and picks to Denver. Cleveland separately sent Tre Mann to the Washington Wizards for Cam Whitmore and a 2027 second.
