NBA Releases Full 2026-27 Schedule — Paris, Mexico City, LeBron's Christmas Return, and Every Marquee Date

By Jake Torres | August 13, 2026 | Hoop Heroes

The wait is over. The NBA released its full 2026-27 regular season schedule Thursday at 3 p.m. ET, and the calendar that fans have been anticipating since Opening Night was teased last week is now complete. Eighty-two games. Every matchup. Every date. And a roster of marquee moments that reads like someone built the league's most compelling possible season on purpose.

The big moments have been known since the staggered rollout began: Opening Night on October 20 at Madison Square Garden, where LeBron James makes his Philadelphia 76ers debut while the Knicks raise their first championship banner since 1973. The Christmas Day five-game slate on ABC and ESPN. But Thursday's full release fills in everything else — and there is a lot worth circling.

International Games: Paris and Mexico City

The league's global footprint continues to expand. On January 14, the San Antonio Spurs and New Orleans Pelicans will play in Paris — bringing Victor Wembanyama back to France five months after the season begins and giving European fans a live look at the most talked-about player in the sport. It will be one of the most watched regular season games of the entire year.

A Mexico City game is also confirmed for November 7, continuing the NBA's investment in Latin American markets that has grown steadily over the past decade. Both international dates sold out instantly in previous years and are expected to do so again.

NBA Cup Group Stage: The Biggest Names Open the Tournament

The Emirates NBA Cup Group Stage launches October 30 with two marquee tip-off games: New York Knicks versus Philadelphia 76ers, and Los Angeles Lakers versus Golden State Warriors. The defending champions hosting LeBron James's new team on the same night that Steph Curry and the Warriors face the Lakers is not an accident. The league is making a statement about how seriously it takes the Cup.

The NBA Cup championship is set for December 11 at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis. The regular-season Cup format — six groups of five teams, with group-stage play folded into the regular schedule — has become one of the most successful innovations in recent league history.

Black Friday and Christmas Day

Black Friday's feature game is a showcase that the league has been teasing since training camp previews began: Victor Wembanyama against the Los Angeles Lakers. The 22-year-old Spurs center against the Lakers in a prime-time slot is a television event as much as a basketball game.

Christmas Day runs five games on ABC and ESPN. The defending Knicks are home to face the Spurs in a Finals rematch. The Miami Heat and Boston Celtics meet in Giannis Antetokounmpo's first Christmas in a Heat uniform — a matchup loaded with storylines on both sides. Oklahoma City and Minnesota collide in the West. The 76ers visit the Lakers for LeBron's return to Los Angeles, his Christmas homecoming to the franchise where he spent years building his legacy. The Nuggets and Warriors close out the day.

The Rest of the Calendar

All-Star Weekend is scheduled for February 21 in Phoenix. Training camps open in late September. The full 82-game picture is now visible, and the offseason of seismic moves — LeBron to Philadelphia, Giannis to Miami, Jaylen Brown traded, the Knicks defending a title — has a concrete calendar to play out on.

For fans who have been tracking every transaction and waiting for the games to become real, Thursday was the day. The schedule is out. The season is 68 days away. Everything else is just anticipation.
