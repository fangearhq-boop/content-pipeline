Luka Dončić Drops 32 as Lakers Reach League-Best 11-2, Bucks Sink to 1-11

By Marcus Cole | November 12, 2026

Luka Dončić scored 32 points as the Los Angeles Lakers defeated the Milwaukee Bucks 123-112 on Wednesday night, pushing the Lakers to a league-best 11-2 record while Milwaukee's miserable start continued with a fall to 1-11.

Luka at His Best, Lakers at the Top

There is a version of Luka Dončić that shows up on certain nights and simply takes a game over — not by running up statistics, but by making every possession feel predetermined. Wednesday in Milwaukee was one of those nights. His 32 points came at efficient clip, mixing pull-up jumpers, drives to the basket, and the kind of creative shot-making that separates elite scorers from great ones.

The Lakers' 123-112 win extended their winning streak to seven games and lifted their record to 11-2 — the best mark in the entire NBA heading into Thursday. Los Angeles has built a balanced roster around Dončić, and it shows. When their star is on, as he was Wednesday, the gap between the Lakers and the rest of the league can look very wide.

Seven Straight and No Signs of Stopping

Seven consecutive wins represents a genuine statement from a team with championship ambitions. The Lakers have beaten opponents of varying quality and style during this run, which speaks to their adaptability. They are not dependent on one style of play — they can win in transition, they can grind out defensive wins, and they can shoot the lights out.

Dončić's 32-point performance was the headliner, but Los Angeles' depth was once again on display. This is a roster constructed to handle adversity, and through seven straight wins, that construction has looked correct.

At 11-2, the Lakers are on pace for a remarkable regular season. The question is whether they can maintain this level as fatigue, travel, and tougher matchups accumulate through the winter months.

The Bucks at 1-11: A Historic Failure

The other story from Wednesday's game is harder to tell and more painful to absorb for Milwaukee fans. The Bucks fell to 1-11 — a record that represents one of the worst starts in franchise history for a team expected to compete at the highest level.

The numbers are damning across the board. Milwaukee is losing games they should win, getting blown out by opponents they should handle, and finding no consistent rhythm on either end of the floor. For a team built around proven veterans with championship pedigree, this start is nothing short of alarming.

The coaching staff and front office are facing hard questions. Is this fixable with internal adjustments, or does something more significant need to change? At 1-11, the gap between the Bucks and the Eastern Conference's elite teams is growing with each passing week.

What's Next

For the Lakers, the focus turns to maintaining their lead at the top of the standings. Teams will be gunning for them now, knowing that LA's scalp would represent the biggest win of anyone's season so far. Dončić and company will need to stay sharp.

For the Bucks, the next few games are critical. Milwaukee cannot afford to fall further behind. If they cannot stabilize quickly, questions about roster changes and coaching adjustments will grow louder. The window to salvage this season is still open — but it is closing fast.
