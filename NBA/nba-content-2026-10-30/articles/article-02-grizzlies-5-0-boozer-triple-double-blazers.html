Memphis Stays Perfect at 5-0 as Boozer Posts First Career Triple-Double in Rout of Portland

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes

October 30, 2026

The Memphis Grizzlies remain the NBA's last undefeated team after dismantling the Portland Trail Blazers 124-103 Thursday night at Moda Center. Rookie Cameron Boozer added a milestone chapter to his young career, posting the first triple-double of his NBA life: 18 points, 12 rebounds, and 12 assists.

Five games in, Memphis has won all of them. No other team in the league can say that.

Boozer's Breakthrough

The Memphis front office knew what it was getting when it drafted Cameron Boozer with the third overall pick in the 2026 NBA Draft — a versatile, high-IQ big man capable of affecting the game in every statistical category. Thursday proved that scouting report correct in the most emphatic way possible.

Boozer's 12 assists were the most striking line on his stat sheet. For a power forward to rack up a dozen dimes while also pulling down 12 rebounds speaks to his uncommon feel for the game. At every turn in the second half, he seemed to be in the right place, making the right read, executing the right play.

The 18-point total was efficient and understated, a reflection of a player who understands his role rather than forcing his game. Boozer did not need to score 30 to make Memphis better Thursday night — he needed to do exactly what he did.

Memphis Is Serious

The Grizzlies opened their season with skepticism swirling about whether their young core could translate promise into results. Five games in, that skepticism is harder to sustain. Memphis has beaten quality opponents, played with pace and purpose, and shown a defensive identity that does not waver late in games.

The Portland game was perhaps the most complete performance of the five. The Grizzlies were in control from the opening tip, building a lead that only expanded as the night wore on. The 21-point final margin reflects a systematic dismantling of the Blazers, not simply a hot-shooting night.

Portland Struggles Continue

The Trail Blazers fell to 1-4 with the loss, continuing a rough early stretch that has raised concerns about their competitive level this season. Portland has talent, but through five games the team has not found a consistent rhythm on either end of the floor.

The gap between Memphis and Portland on Thursday was stark. The Grizzlies executed. The Blazers did not. At this early stage, that kind of performance differential matters for confidence building as much as for the standings.

What's Next

Memphis now has the opportunity to build one of the most compelling early-season narratives in recent NBA history. An undefeated team with a rookie posting triple-doubles is a story the league will follow closely in the weeks ahead.

The Grizzlies' schedule does not relent — strong opponents await. But through five games, Memphis has given fans every reason to believe this is not a fluke. The West is on notice.
