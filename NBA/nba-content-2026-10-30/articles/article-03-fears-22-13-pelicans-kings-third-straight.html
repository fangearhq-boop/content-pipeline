Fears Drops 22 and 13 as Pelicans Win Third Straight, Topple Kings 132-120

By Damon Pierce | Hoop Heroes

October 30, 2026

Jeremiah Fears posted 22 points and 13 assists Thursday night as the New Orleans Pelicans dispatched the Sacramento Kings 132-120, extending their winning streak to three games. Fears is emerging as one of the most dangerous young point guards in the Western Conference, and his performance against Sacramento underscored just how far he has come in a short time.

Fears Leads the Way

Twenty points and double-digit assists from a young point guard tends to draw attention. Thirteen assists is a number that speaks to playmaking at an elite level — not just accumulating dimes on garbage time passes, but genuinely creating quality looks for teammates throughout a 48-minute game.

Fears' ability to both score and facilitate gives the Pelicans an offensive dimension that is difficult to guard. He gets into the paint at will, draws help, and then makes decisions that punish the defense regardless of which option it leaves open. Thursday, Sacramento had no answer for that dynamic.

At 20 years old, Fears is operating with the composure of a veteran. This is his second season, and he has clearly absorbed lessons from his rookie year and turned them into consistent performance.

New Orleans Builds Momentum

Three consecutive wins is a meaningful run for a Pelicans team that has often found consistency elusive. The wins have come against varied competition, which speaks to a developing resilience in the roster rather than simply catching a soft stretch of the schedule.

The Pelicans have balanced scoring contributions across their lineup, protecting themselves from being too dependent on any single player — though Fears' nights like Thursday make him the unquestioned fulcrum. New Orleans is a team to watch in the Western Conference if this run continues into November.

Sacramento's Difficult Start

The Kings fall to 1-4 with the loss, and questions are mounting about whether this roster can find its footing. Sacramento made significant investments in building a contending team, but the early returns have been disappointing. The offense has been inconsistent, the defense has not been able to stop quality point guards, and the losses have been piling up.

Thursday against Fears and the Pelicans was another evening where Sacramento's defensive scheme looked ill-equipped. Fans in Sacramento will want to see answers soon.

What's Next

The Pelicans enter NBA Cup group play as a legitimate threat in their group. Three straight wins heading into the Cup gives New Orleans confidence and momentum, and Fears' form suggests the Pelicans are capable of running the table in group play if they play like this.

Sacramento will need to regroup quickly. With the schedule not getting easier, the Kings need to find their identity before the losses become a deficit too deep to climb out of.
