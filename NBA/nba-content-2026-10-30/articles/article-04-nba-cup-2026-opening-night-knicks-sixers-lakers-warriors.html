Knicks Defend at Philadelphia

When the Knicks won the NBA Cup last season, they did it the hard way — grinding through a bracket that tested every element of their roster. They enter the 2026-27 edition as defending champions, a distinction that adds weight to every Cup game on their schedule.

Friday's trip to Philadelphia is not merely a Cup opener — it is a statement game. The 76ers have built their roster to compete at the highest level, and opening Cup play against New York in front of their own crowd is a chance to serve notice early.

The Knicks-Sixers matchup is one of the NBA's most compelling ongoing rivalries. When Jalen Brunson and New York's core share the floor with Philadelphia's frontcourt, the physical and tactical chess match rarely disappoints. Add Cup stakes and a national television audience on Prime Video, and this is the game of the night in the East.

Lakers-Warriors: A Classic Rivalry Renewed

Few matchups in professional basketball carry the historical weight of Lakers versus Warriors. Friday's game at Chase Center at 10 PM ET is the Cup debut for both Western Conference powers, and each has championship ambitions that make group-play positioning relevant from the start.

The Warriors have been building quietly with a mix of veterans and young contributors, while the Lakers carry franchise expectations that never diminish. Whether or not either team is a Cup favorite, this matchup will draw the attention of the entire league.

How the NBA Cup Works

All 30 teams compete in five groups of six. The group stage runs through late November, with Friday games serving as the dedicated Cup nights. Each team plays five group-stage games, and the group winner from each conference bracket advances to the quarterfinals in December.

Cup wins count as regular-season wins, and the financial stakes for players — including a significant bonus pool for the champion — mean rosters treat the tournament seriously. Last year's Knicks showed what happens when a team locks in for the full Cup run.

Tonight's Full Opening Slate

Eight games open Cup group play on Friday. In addition to the headline matchups, the Charlotte Hornets visit the Washington Wizards at 7 PM ET to kick off the evening's action. Each of the eight games contributes to Cup group standings, making the full night meaningful from the first tip to the last buzzer on the West Coast.

What's Next

The NBA Cup will run its group stage through the final Friday of November, with the quarterfinals and semifinals taking place in December. The champion is crowned before Christmas, giving the eventual winner time to refocus on the second half of the regular season.

For the Knicks, defending is the ultimate challenge — the target on their backs grows with every Cup win. For every other team, tonight's group opener is the first step toward taking that target away from New York. The 2026-27 NBA Cup begins tonight.
