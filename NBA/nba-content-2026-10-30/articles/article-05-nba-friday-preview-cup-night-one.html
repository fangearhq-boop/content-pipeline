NBA Friday Preview: Five Games Tonight as NBA Cup Group Play Opens

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes

October 30, 2026

The NBA schedule on Friday, October 30 features five games, with three counting toward the opening night of NBA Cup group play. The Cup officially kicks off tonight with the league's marquee tournament adding competitive stakes to every Friday slate through late November.

NBA Cup Games (Friday, October 30)

Charlotte Hornets at Washington Wizards — 7:00 PM ET
The evening opens in the East with Charlotte visiting Washington. Both teams are early in their rebuilding arcs, and a Cup win would provide a meaningful early-season lift. This game opens the official Cup group standings and could feature some of the league's younger talent stepping into a big moment.

New York Knicks at Philadelphia 76ers — 7:30 PM ET (Amazon Prime Video)
The marquee East Cup game of the night. The Knicks defend their Cup title in a hostile road environment at Philadelphia. With Prime Video carrying the game nationally, both franchises have full motivation to deliver. This is a rivalry game with Cup stakes layered on top — everything the NBA Cup was designed to produce.

Los Angeles Lakers at Golden State Warriors — 10:00 PM ET
The West's signature Cup opener. Los Angeles visits the Bay Area in a matchup that spans decades of NBA history. Chase Center will be electric, and both teams know what a Cup run means for momentum heading into December and the second half of the year.

Additional Games

Friday also features two games outside of Cup play. The Phoenix Suns visit the Utah Jazz at 9 PM ET in the Mountain time zone, and the Los Angeles Clippers host the Denver Nuggets at 10 PM ET for a late-night Western Conference clash. Both games carry standard regular-season importance but do not factor into Cup group standings.

Cup Context: Why Tonight Matters

The NBA Cup is not a side event. Wins count toward the regular season. The cash bonuses for championship players are real. And last year's Knicks run proved that a team that gets locked in for the Cup can ride that momentum all the way to late-season relevance.

Every group-play game from here through the final Friday in November shapes the bracket. Teams entering with Cup wins in hand gain a psychological and positional edge as December approaches.
