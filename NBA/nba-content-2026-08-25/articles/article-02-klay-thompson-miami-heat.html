Klay Thompson Signs With Miami Heat After Mavs Buyout — Joins Giannis in Championship Chase

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | August 25, 2026

Klay Thompson is done waiting. After the Dallas Mavericks agreed to a contract buyout, the four-time NBA champion signed a two-year deal worth nearly $13 million with the Miami Heat, choosing a legitimate contender over staying in a rebuilding situation. Thompson joins a Heat roster that already features Giannis Antetokounmpo, instantly creating one of the most dangerous Eastern Conference teams in recent memory.

Thompson's message to Dallas was clear and direct: he wanted to compete for a championship. The Mavericks, who are in the early stages of building around rookie sensation Cooper Flagg, understood. Rather than force a player of Thompson's stature through a transitional season, they agreed to absorb the remaining guaranteed money on his deal and let him leave.

At 36, Thompson understands that championship windows don't stay open forever. He has four rings from his Warriors years, but he hasn't been to the Finals since Golden State's dynasty era ended. Miami, with Giannis at center, Jimmy Butler on the wing, and now one of the greatest shooters in league history added to the mix, gives him the kind of environment where No. 5 is a realistic goal.

What Thompson Brings to Miami

The fit is straightforward and dangerous. Thompson has spent his entire career as a catch-and-shoot weapon who plays his best when he has a dominant ball-handler to work off of. Giannis Antetokounmpo is exactly that. When Giannis draws help defenders or attacks the paint, Thompson becomes one of the most lethal kick-out options in basketball — and defenses already have enough problems stopping Giannis to begin with.

Miami's coaching staff under Erik Spoelstra is renowned for integrating multiple stars within a cohesive system. Thompson has historically been one of the easiest superstars to fit alongside others — he does not need the ball, does not need plays run for him every possession, and does not create off-court complications. He is what every team building around a franchise player wants: a proven winner who makes the primary star better.

The Eastern Conference just became significantly more complicated for everyone else.

What's Next

Thompson will report to Miami's training camp when it opens and work to build chemistry with Giannis and the existing roster in the preseason. The Heat's schedule and opening-night matchup will immediately become a marquee event.

For Dallas, the focus shifts entirely to Cooper Flagg and the franchise's next era. The Mavericks are paying for Thompson's departure in cap terms, but they gain the flexibility and the clarity of purpose that a rebuilding team needs. The Klay Thompson era in Dallas was brief and incomplete — but this chapter suits him far better.
