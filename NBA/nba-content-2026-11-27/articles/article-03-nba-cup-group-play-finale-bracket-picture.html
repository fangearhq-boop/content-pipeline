NBA Cup Group Play Finale: Who Advances, Who Is Out, Full Bracket Picture

By Damon Pierce

November 27, 2026

After weeks of group-play basketball, the NBA Cup's first chapter ends tonight with 11 games determining the full eight-team knockout bracket. Black Friday delivers the highest-stakes night of the Cup's group stage, and there are storylines worth following across both conferences.

Here is the complete picture heading into the final night — who has clinched, who needs what, and what the knockout round could look like.

The Only Team Already In: Dallas Mavericks

Cooper Flagg and the Dallas Mavericks are the lone team to have clinched a knockout-round berth before tonight. Dallas went 3-0 in West Group A, and Flagg's production — 24 points and 12 rebounds in one cup game, sustained excellence in two others — has made the Mavericks the Cup's early story of dominance.

The Mavericks will find out their quarterfinal opponent tonight. As West Group A's No. 1 seed, they receive preferential bracket placement and will be watching Friday's results closely to identify their December competition.

East Picture: Celtics, Heat, 76ers Lead Their Groups

Boston leads East Group C at 3-0 with a league-best plus-68 point differential in Cup play — a margin that underscores just how dominant the Celtics have been in group-play situations. Boston clinches the group with any win tonight, though the differential advantage means they are essentially already the No. 1 seed from the East in Cup play.

In East Group B, the Miami Heat hold the edge. They advance to the knockout round with a win over the Knicks at Madison Square Garden tonight. Philadelphia controls East Group D and can clinch with a win over Indiana. If the 76ers lose, the group title race tightens.

East Group A has its own drama. The two wild card spots in the East go to the best second-place finishers by point differential. Toronto, Detroit, and Cleveland are all in the conversation.

West Picture: Spurs, Lakers, Wolves Battle for Groups

San Antonio leads West Group C, but Los Angeles can steal the group title with a road win at the AT&T Center tonight. Minnesota controls West Group B and advances with a win over Memphis. Oklahoma City has the best point differential among West second-place teams, putting them in the pole position for the West's wild card spot.

How the Bracket Works

The Cup format places the six group winners and two conference wild cards into an eight-team single-elimination bracket. Games are played at the home court of the higher seed. The Cup Final is set for December 11, and the winning team's players each earn a $500,000 bonus.

The bracket will be officially revealed tonight after all group-play games conclude. For teams that have been chasing Cup advancement since the season began, the wait ends in a few hours.

What's Next

Quarterfinal games are scheduled for the first two weeks of December, with semifinal matchups following shortly after. The full knockout schedule will be released alongside the bracket tonight. For fans watching the drama unfold on Prime Video, the next several weeks of December basketball just became considerably more interesting.
