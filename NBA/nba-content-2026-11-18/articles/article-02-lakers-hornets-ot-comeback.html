Lakers Erase 18-Point Deficit, Hold Hornets Scoreless in OT to Win 118-114

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | November 18, 2026

Los Angeles trailed by 18 in one of the season's wildest swings, then refused to give Charlotte a single point in the final 4:24 of overtime to complete a stunning comeback.

From 18 Down to Overtime

At some point on Monday night at Crypto.com Arena, it looked like the Charlotte Hornets were going to walk out of Los Angeles with a convincing road victory. The Hornets had built an 18-point lead and had the Lakers on the ropes. For a team trying to find its footing in a competitive Western Conference, a loss of that magnitude could have been a momentum-killer.

Then the Los Angeles Lakers did what championship-caliber teams do — they refused to quit. Piece by piece, basket by basket, the Lakers clawed their way back into the game on November 17, 2026. When the fourth quarter ended with the score level, Los Angeles had already accomplished something remarkable. Getting the lead back took everything they had, but erasing an 18-point hole to force overtime was itself a statement.

The final score, 118-114 in favor of LA, tells only part of the story. The real story was written in the closing minutes of that extra period.

Four Minutes of Perfect Defense

Overtime in the NBA can swing on a single play. The team that makes a mistake, gives up an easy bucket, or misses a free throw often goes home with a loss. On this night, the Lakers made no mistakes when they absolutely could not afford to.

For the final 4 minutes and 24 seconds of overtime, the Charlotte Hornets did not score a single point. Zero. The Lakers locked in defensively with a collective will and refused to allow Charlotte to convert any shot, draw any foul, or find any rhythm. It was a defensive performance that turned a close overtime game into a decisive Laker victory.

That kind of late-game defensive intensity is a hallmark of teams with championship aspirations. For Lakers fans who have watched their team grow throughout this season, this performance was a signal. This group has what it takes to defend when the stakes are highest.

What the Win Means

Comeback wins like this do more than add a tick in the win column. They build belief. When a team comes back from 18 down, the locker room learns something — that no deficit is too large, that the fourth quarter always offers a chance, and that collective defense wins games when it needs to most.

For the Los Angeles Lakers, navigating a Western Conference that includes elite competition at every turn, wins like this matter immensely. Dropping that game would have felt deflating. Instead, the team leaves Monday night with extra confidence earned the hard way.

Charlotte, for its part, had the game in hand and let it slip. The Hornets will need to review how they managed the lead and what broke down defensively in overtime. Eighteen-point advantages should not evaporate, and that reckoning will be important for a young Charlotte squad still developing its identity.

What's Next

The Lakers continue through a November slate that will further define their playoff positioning. With this comeback win behind them, LA heads into upcoming matchups with the belief that no game is ever truly out of reach.

The Charlotte Hornets will look to respond quickly, using this overtime heartbreak as fuel to prove they can hold leads and close out games on the road. Their next performance will be telling.

For basketball fans everywhere, this game served as a reminder of what makes the NBA special: in a league of elite talent, no lead is ever truly safe until the final buzzer sounds.
