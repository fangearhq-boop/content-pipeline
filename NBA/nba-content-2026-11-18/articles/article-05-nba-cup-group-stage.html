NBA Cup Group Stage: What's at Stake Through November 27

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | November 18, 2026

The NBA In-Season Tournament group stage continues through the end of November, adding an extra layer of competition to every regular season game on the calendar.

The Cup Is Still Very Much Alive

As November rolls toward its final stretch, the NBA Cup group stage is in full swing. Through November 27, every team in the league is competing not only for regular season standings but for the chance to advance into the knockout rounds of the In-Season Tournament.

Wednesday's 12-game slate is a prime example of how the NBA Cup adds urgency to games that might otherwise be viewed as standard mid-November contests. When group stage points are on the line, teams cannot afford to coast. Every possession matters a little more. The focus is a little sharper. The competition is elevated.

That is exactly what the NBA Cup is designed to do — transform November basketball into something with meaningful stakes for every franchise in the league.

How the Group Stage Works

Teams are divided into groups based on conference and division alignment. Within each group, teams compete head-to-head, with standings determined by wins and point differential in Cup-designated games. The top finishers from each group advance to the knockout rounds, which determine the teams that compete for the In-Season Tournament championship.

For teams hovering on the bubble of advancing, Wednesday night's games are critical. A loss does not necessarily eliminate a team, but it narrows the margin significantly as the group stage deadline approaches on November 27.

For teams already in strong Cup positions, wins tonight help cement their path to the knockout rounds and allow them to manage rotations and minutes with more confidence heading into December.

Why It Matters for Fans

The NBA Cup is a relatively recent addition to the basketball calendar, but it has earned its place. Fans benefit from games that carry extra meaning — the drama of a knockout elimination, the joy of advancing into a tournament, and the chance to see their favorite team compete for a title beyond the championship ring.

Even teams with long odds in the regular season standings can feel relevant through the Cup, as the knockout structure creates opportunities for compelling storylines and surprise runs. The In-Season Tournament has produced memorable moments since its introduction, and this season looks poised to deliver more of the same.

Tonight, with 12 games on the board and Cup points on the line, is an excellent night to be an NBA fan.

What's Next

The NBA Cup group stage window closes November 27. Between now and then, every game carries double weight — regular season record and Cup standing in one package. Teams that win at a high rate over this stretch will enter December with strong positioning on both fronts.

Keep an eye on the Cup standings as the results roll in tonight. By Thursday morning, the picture of which teams are on track to advance will be much clearer — and the conversation heading into the final week of group play will heat up significantly.
