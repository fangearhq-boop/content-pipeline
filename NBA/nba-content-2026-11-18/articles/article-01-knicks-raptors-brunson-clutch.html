Brunson's Clutch Four-Point Play Powers Knicks Past Raptors 111-102

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | November 18, 2026

Jalen Brunson hit a tiebreaking four-point play with 1:25 remaining and Karl-Anthony Towns posted a 16-point, 16-rebound double-double as New York picked up a road victory in Toronto.

Brunson Delivers When It Matters Most

When the New York Knicks needed a spark in a tight game in Toronto, Jalen Brunson answered the call. With the game tied in the final two minutes, Brunson drove hard to the basket, drew the foul on a made bucket, and calmly converted the free throw to give New York a four-point swing that proved decisive.

That play — a tiebreaking four-point conversion with 1:25 left in the fourth quarter — was the defining moment in a 111-102 Knicks victory over the Toronto Raptors at Scotiabank Arena on November 17, 2026. Brunson finished with 31 points on the night, cementing his status as one of the most clutch shot-makers in the Eastern Conference.

It was the kind of moment that separates good players from elite ones. When the defense collapses and the game is on the line, Brunson finds a way. He has done it time and again this season, and Tuesday's performance was another entry in what is becoming one of the most impressive résumés in the NBA.

Towns Dominates Both Ends

Brunson's heroics would not have been possible without Karl-Anthony Towns holding down the paint for three-plus quarters. Towns was relentless on November 17, finishing with 16 points and 16 rebounds — a double-double that controlled the game's interior on both ends of the floor.

Towns' rebounding kept possessions alive for New York and denied the Raptors second-chance opportunities. His scoring presence inside opened the floor for Brunson to operate in space, creating a pick-and-roll partnership that the Raptors struggled to contain all night.

The fit between Brunson and Towns continues to look better as the season progresses. The offseason acquisition of Towns was scrutinized at the time, but the Knicks have shown that bringing the big man to New York was the right move. His 16-16 game on the road is exactly the production New York needs alongside Brunson to compete for a championship.

Road Win in Canada

Road wins are never easy, and victories in Toronto can be particularly difficult. The Raptors play physical, their home crowd is engaged, and the team has the talent to hang with anyone on a given night. Winning there 111-102 while trailing at points in the game speaks to the depth and resilience of this Knicks squad.

New York's bench also contributed to keeping the game manageable in the third quarter when Toronto attempted to make a run. The Knicks' depth — a hallmark of this year's roster — allows them to weather adversity without relying entirely on their two stars.

The victory extends New York's impressive run of form heading into the latter part of November, a stretch that will determine the Knicks' standing as a true Eastern Conference contender.

What's Next

The Knicks return home to Madison Square Garden to continue a November schedule that has tested them on the road and in close games. With Brunson in clutch form and Towns operating at double-double efficiency, New York will look to maintain momentum as the NBA Cup group stage continues through November 27.

The Raptors, meanwhile, must regroup at home and find a way to prevent opposing stars from making signature plays in the closing minutes. Their next matchup will reveal how they respond to this tough loss.

For Knicks fans, the message is simple: Jalen Brunson is here, he is elite, and New York is a problem for anyone in the East.
