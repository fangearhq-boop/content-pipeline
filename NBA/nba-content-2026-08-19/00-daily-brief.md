### STORY 1: Russell Westbrook Retires From NBA — NEW

Last week Kevin Durant posted a tribute calling his career "iconic" and Jalen Williams pushed for OKC to retire his No. 0 jersey on Opening Night. This announcement makes the HOF conversation and OKC jersey retirement story even bigger.

### STORY 2: Kawhi Leonard Investigation — ESPN/NBA War of Words — FOLLOW UP

August 17 brought the most dramatic development in the Kawhi Leonard saga: ESPN (Van Natta/Holmes/Shelburne) reported the NBA "found no evidence" Steve Ballmer funneled money to Leonard. The NBA immediately hit back, with spokesman Mike Bass calling the report full of "numerous and significant inaccuracies." Separately, NBC Sports reported settlement talks between the NBA and Clippers have started. Leonard and the Raptors continue operating as if the trade will clear (~Sept 22).

### STORY 3: Wembanyama France vs. Serbia — Belgrade Preview — FOLLOW UP

### STORY 4: LaMelo Trade Details Confirmed — FOLLOW UP
