### STORY 2: Kawhi Leonard Investigation — ESPN vs. NBA

**Long-Form Post**
Window: 11:00 AM ET

The Kawhi Leonard situation just took a wild new turn — and this time, it's not about Kawhi at all. It's about the media.

On August 17, ESPN published a bombshell report: the NBA had "found no evidence" that Steve Ballmer funneled money to Kawhi Leonard through the Aspiration and Daktronics sponsorship deals.

Within hours, the NBA hit back hard. Spokesman Mike Bass said the ESPN report "contains numerous and significant inaccuracies" — a rare and aggressive pushback from the league on a major outlet.

Here's where things stand:
✅ The investigation is narrowing to whether the Clippers improperly introduced Leonard to their business partners (a "failure to supervise" violation)
✅ NBA/Clippers settlement talks have reportedly started — which suggests both sides see a path out
✅ Kawhi Leonard has received zero indication his contract will be voided
✅ Leonard and the Raptors are still operating as if this trade completes

Target resolution: around September 22 — one week before Toronto training camp opens in Quebec City.

So even as ESPN and the NBA are fighting over what the investigation actually found, the destination hasn't changed: this trade appears to be completing. It's just taking the most dramatic possible route to get there.

### STORY 4: LaMelo Trade — Full Terms Confirmed

**Long-Form Post**
Window: 1:00 PM ET

The full price Minnesota paid to get LaMelo Ball is now confirmed — and it is steep.

The Timberwolves sent to Charlotte:
• Naz Reid
• 2033 unprotected first-round pick
• Three first-round pick swaps (2028, 2029, 2030)
• Three second-round picks (2029, 2032, 2033)

In return: LaMelo Ball and Josh Green.

Anthony Edwards wasted no time delivering a "championship reality check" after the deal — expectations in Minnesota are now elevated.

But here's the thing everyone keeps coming back to: LaMelo Ball has never played a single NBA playoff game. Not one. In his entire career.

That is the defining tension of this trade. If it works — if LaMelo's elite playmaking frees Edwards to score off the ball and Minnesota makes a deep playoff run — the price looks cheap. If LaMelo can't stay healthy or the chemistry is off, the Wolves gutted their depth for nothing.

The Ringer described it as "the Wolves betting the Anthony Edwards era" on this trade. That's exactly right.

Big bet. Big upside. Big risk.

Window: 9:00 AM ET

Ten days.

That's how long Warriors fans have to wait until Stephen Curry is eligible to sign his extension — August 29 is the official window opener.

What's not in question: mutual interest is real. Warriors GM Mike Dunleavy Jr. said he's "pretty confident" Curry will finish his career in Golden State. And Curry wants to stay — he just wants the team to show it can compete around him.

The Warriors lost the LeBron sweepstakes this offseason. That failure adds urgency to getting this done. A Curry who walks in 2027 as a first-time UFA would be the most stunning free agency story the league has seen in a decade.

Ten days.
