NBA Preseason Goes Global: Quebec City, Abu Dhabi and Hawaii Headline Weekend Slate

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | October 3, 2026

This is not your typical preseason weekend. The 2026-27 NBA season has arrived on a truly global stage, with marquee matchups spread across three continents over 72 hours — from the Arabian Peninsula to the Canadian interior to the middle of the Pacific Ocean.

For fans, it is a rare chance to see multiple star-studded lineups in action before the regular season clock starts ticking. For teams, it is the beginning of the long process of learning how this year's version of the roster actually plays together under live conditions.

Quebec City Makes History Tonight

The most anticipated game of the weekend tips off Saturday night when Giannis Antetokounmpo and the Miami Heat face the Toronto Raptors at Videotron Centre in Quebec City. The contest is the first NBA game ever played in the city — preseason or otherwise — and represents the league's continued investment in Canadian basketball markets beyond Toronto.

Giannis, in his first action as a Heat player, brings the global spotlight that follows him everywhere. Alongside Bam Adebayo and Canadian Andrew Wiggins, this Heat team has no shortage of storylines for Quebec City's packed arena to embrace. The NBA Canada Games, presented by Bell, continues a tradition that has grown into one of the most popular preseason events on the schedule.

Abu Dhabi: Knicks 1-0, Game 2 Sunday

On Friday night at Etihad Arena in Abu Dhabi, the New York Knicks opened the preseason with a polished 99-84 win over the Philadelphia 76ers, setting up a rematch at the same venue on Sunday. Miles McBride led all scorers with 12, Jordan Clarkson debuted smoothly with 9, and the Knicks' defensive unit suffocated Philadelphia's offense in a convincing performance.

The Sixers, without Joel Embiid, showed enough from Tyrese Maxey and rookie VJ Edgecombe to suggest their ceiling remains legitimate. Whether Embiid is available Sunday is the storyline to monitor. For the Knicks, the priority is building cohesion and evaluating depth ahead of Opening Night against these same Sixers on Oct. 20.

Hawaii: Warriors-Clippers Sunday

The Golden State Warriors — fresh off the announcement of Stephen Curry's two-year, $116 million extension — face the LA Clippers in Hawaii on Sunday. It is the first live look at Curry alongside new additions Kristaps Porzingis and Jimmy Butler III, and the early results from BYU-Hawaii training camp have been encouraging by all accounts.

The Clippers bring their own intrigue, continuing to rebuild around younger talent after the offseason departures of Kawhi Leonard and others. Sunday's game will be a useful early read on where both franchises stand heading into the fall.

What's Next

The preseason continues through mid-October before the regular season opens on Oct. 20 — just 17 days away. Games in Abu Dhabi, Quebec City, and Hawaii this weekend give the league's most prominent franchises valuable competitive time, but the real work of building toward a championship begins the moment the calendar flips to game week.

For now, enjoy the global tour. The NBA's reach has never been broader, and this weekend is the best possible reminder of it.
