Knicks Win Abu Dhabi Opener 99-84 as Embiid-Less Sixers Show Promise in Defeat

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | October 3, 2026

The 2026-27 NBA preseason opened Friday night at Etihad Arena in Abu Dhabi with a performance that made it abundantly clear: the New York Knicks are not planning to give up their championship any time soon.

New York defeated the Philadelphia 76ers 99-84 before a packed house on Yas Island, showcasing the kind of depth and defensive discipline that carried them to the title a year ago. It was just the first preseason game — but the Knicks played like it mattered.

Knicks' Bench Carries the Night

Miles McBride led all scorers with 12 points off the bench, a reminder that New York's second unit is among the most formidable in the league. Karl-Anthony Towns added 11 points and five rebounds, while Mikal Bridges chipped in 10 points and two steals as the defense suffocated Philadelphia's offense in the second half.

Perhaps the most intriguing debut came from Jordan Clarkson, who contributed nine points in his first outing in a Knicks uniform. The veteran guard arrived in New York as a proven bench scorer and immediately showed why the front office targeted him in the offseason.

Head coach Tom Thibodeau appeared to be running a standard preseason rotation — resting key starters early, evaluating new additions, and getting the unit's timing calibrated. By those measures, Friday was an unqualified success.

Sixers Flash the Future Without Embiid

Philadelphia played the Abu Dhabi opener without Joel Embiid, choosing a measured approach to the seven-footer's early preseason workload. It was a familiar sight for 76ers fans — and a concerning one given how central Embiid's health has been to the franchise's championship aspirations for years.

In his place, Tyrese Maxey carried the offensive load and delivered 14 points with the kind of composed efficiency that has made him one of the Eastern Conference's premier guards. Rookie VJ Edgecombe matched Maxey's total with 14 points of his own — a genuinely impressive debut performance that generated significant buzz in the arena.

Edgecombe, the 76ers' first-round pick, was one of the most anticipated rookies heading into camp. Friday night suggested the hype is justified. At just 20 years old, he moved decisively, finished through contact, and showed the athleticism that scouts marveled at throughout the pre-draft process.

Abu Dhabi Rematch on Sunday

The Knicks and Sixers will meet again at Etihad Arena on Sunday, Oct. 4, for the second and final Abu Dhabi game. Philadelphia may get Embiid more involved — though the team has given no specific timeline — while New York will look to extend an early winning streak that, while preseason, still builds momentum heading toward Opening Night.

For the storyline that truly matters, both teams meet on Oct. 20 at Madison Square Garden — the Knicks' championship banner night, the LeBron James era in Philadelphia begins publicly, and the most anticipated opening night in the NBA in years. Friday's game in Abu Dhabi was just the first act.

What's Next

Keep an eye on Sunday's rematch for any update on Embiid's participation and whether Edgecombe gets another extended run. For the Knicks, the real question is whether their preseason translates into another deep playoff run. For now, they are 1-0 — and looking every bit like the team that brought the championship back to New York.
