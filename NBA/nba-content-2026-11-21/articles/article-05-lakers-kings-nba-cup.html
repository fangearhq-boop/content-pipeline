Lakers Survive Kings 106-104, Stay Alive in NBA Cup Group Play

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | November 21, 2026

The Los Angeles Lakers needed every last possession to escape Sacramento with a 106-104 victory on Thursday night, but escape they did. The two-point win in a critical NBA Cup group stage game keeps the Lakers in the conversation for the knockout round, where the in-season tournament's stakes get considerably higher.

The NBA Cup Context

Group stage games in the NBA Cup carry a unique weight that changes the dynamic of what might otherwise be a routine regular-season contest. For the Lakers, Thursday's game wasn't just about adding to their win total — it was about staying alive in a tournament where the prize money and championship implications make every game matter differently than the standard 82-game grind.

Sacramento understood the same thing, which is why the Kings pushed Los Angeles right to the wire. A two-point margin in a game like this tells you that both teams were locked in and playing with heightened urgency. The Lakers won, but it was not comfortable.

The Lakers-Kings Rivalry

These two California franchises have a long and contentious history, and even in an NBA Cup group stage game, the edge between them is palpable. Sacramento has built a roster capable of competing with Los Angeles and regularly does in their division matchups. Thursday was no different — the Kings made the Lakers earn every bucket down the stretch and had a real chance to pull off the two-point upset.

What the Lakers showed in surviving was a measure of their composure in tight moments. Close games reveal character, and Thursday's finish, however stressful, is a data point in favor of a team that can get the job done when the margin is thin.

What the Win Means

Staying alive in the NBA Cup group stage matters for a franchise with championship aspirations. The Lakers want to compete in every format available to them, and the Cup provides a pathway to hardware in November and December, before the playoffs come into view. With Thursday's win, Los Angeles remains in the picture for a spot in the knockout round bracket.

The Western Conference group stage race will tighten before the knockout round is set, and every game between now and the cutoff will carry this kind of consequence.

What's Next

The Lakers will need to keep winning in group play to advance in the NBA Cup while simultaneously managing the full schedule of a long regular season. For Sacramento, Thursday's close loss to Los Angeles reveals a team capable of competing at the top of the Western Conference's second tier — a franchise that has closed the gap on the Lakers more than the rivalry's history might suggest. The Cup conversation in the West is heating up, and L.A. will need more performances like Thursday's to stay at the center of it.
