Cavaliers Roll Pacers 128-119, Cement Eastern Conference Lead

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | November 21, 2026

The Cleveland Cavaliers keep winning. Thursday night's 128-119 victory over the Indiana Pacers was the latest in a string of dominant performances by the team that has established itself as the Eastern Conference's standard bearer through the first weeks of the season. Cleveland's balanced, efficient attack overwhelmed Indiana in a game that was closer on the scoreboard than it felt on the court.

Cleveland's Formula

What makes the Cavaliers so difficult to defend is their ability to hurt opponents in multiple ways. They are not dependent on a single scorer going off — they have the depth and system to distribute the burden and exploit matchups wherever they find them. Against Indiana on Thursday, that formula worked exactly as designed.

The Pacers came in as a team capable of putting up big offensive numbers. Indiana's pace-and-space system is designed to generate easy looks and exploit slower half-court defenses. Cleveland absorbed that pressure and still won by nine, which speaks to how well-prepared they are for different offensive styles.

Indiana's Troubling Pattern

Scoring 119 points and losing by nine is a frustrating result for any offense. For Indiana, it reflects a defensive fragility that has plagued them through the early part of the season. The Pacers can score — that's not their problem. Their problem is that they cannot consistently stop the opponent at the other end, and against elite teams like Cleveland, that leaves no margin for error.

The gap between the Pacers and the East's true contenders is becoming clearer with each week. Indiana is capable of beating average opponents, but against top-flight competition, the defensive limitations are exposed again and again.

The East's Big Picture

Cleveland is operating at a level that makes them the team everyone else in the Eastern Conference is chasing. Their defensive intensity, combined with a versatile offense that never goes cold for long, makes them a formidable challenge for any opponent. The question is not whether Cleveland belongs at the top of the East — that's established. The question is whether anyone can make a run at them before the postseason arrives.

The Boston Celtics, Miami Heat, and New York Knicks are all capable of presenting challenges, but right now Cleveland is playing with an edge that those teams haven't consistently matched.

What's Next

The Cavaliers will continue their march through the Eastern Conference with another opportunity to extend their winning ways. For Indiana, the work ahead is clear: their offense is not the problem, but their defense needs immediate attention if they want to compete with the conference's elite. Another loss to a top-five East team will only deepen the questions about where the Pacers truly stand this season.
