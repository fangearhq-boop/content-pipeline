SGA Leads Thunder Past Pelicans 126-120, OKC Keeps Pace at Top of West

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | November 21, 2026

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander and the Oklahoma City Thunder are becoming the Western Conference's most reliable force. Thursday's 126-120 victory over the New Orleans Pelicans extended a stretch of excellence that has placed OKC firmly among the West's elite, and SGA's MVP candidacy continues to build on a foundation of consistent, game-defining performances.

SGA's MVP Case

What sets Shai Gilgeous-Alexander apart from other elite scorers is not simply that he puts up big numbers — it's that he does it in the moments that matter most and elevates the Thunder's performance as a whole. When games get tight, SGA gets better. When defenses focus their schemes on stopping him, he finds ways to hurt them through his teammates.

Against New Orleans, SGA did exactly what he has done all season: controlled the tempo, made efficient decisions, and hit the shots that kept Oklahoma City comfortably in front. The Pelicans have enough talent to threaten good teams, but they had no answer for the Thunder's superstar when it counted most.

OKC's Depth and Balance

What makes the Thunder so dangerous beyond SGA is the depth coach Mark Daigneault has developed around his franchise player. OKC can hurt opponents from multiple spots on the floor, their defensive rotations are disciplined and communicative, and their young core has developed a winning culture that shows up in close games.

Thursday's six-point win over a New Orleans team that is capable of stretching defenses is the kind of victory that builds championship habits. Oklahoma City doesn't just win — they win the right way, with collective effort and minimal wasted possessions.

New Orleans' Challenge

The Pelicans came to Oklahoma City with a competitive roster and fell just short. New Orleans has the pieces to compete in the Western Conference's middle tier, but against top-end teams like the Thunder, their inability to slow SGA proved decisive. The Pelicans will need to find answers for star guards if they want to push into the West's upper echelon.

At 120 points, New Orleans' offense is not the problem. Their defensive inability to contain elite players has been a recurring theme in their losses this season.

What's Next

The Thunder will keep rolling as one of the Western Conference's most dangerous teams, with SGA's MVP campaign growing stronger with each performance. Oklahoma City's combination of a transcendent star, supportive depth, and disciplined coaching makes them a genuine title threat. The West is loaded, but right now, the Thunder are right there at the top — and showing no signs of slowing down.
