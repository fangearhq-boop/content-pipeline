Franz Wagner Hits Walk-Off Basket, Magic Edge Nets 113-111

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | November 21, 2026

Franz Wagner has become impossible to guard, and on Thursday night, he made that abundantly clear with a walk-off basket that left the Brooklyn Nets stunned. With 2.4 seconds remaining, Wagner hit the go-ahead shot to give the Orlando Magic a 113-111 victory — capping a 35-point performance that was as brilliant as it was clutch.

The Magic continue to establish themselves as one of the Eastern Conference's most dangerous teams, and Wagner is the engine powering everything.

Wagner's Historic November

What Wagner is doing this month defies easy comparison. He scored 38 points at Utah on November 11, then followed that up with 35 against Milwaukee on November 13, and now a 35-point buzzer-beater performance against Brooklyn. That's three performances of 35 or more points in a span of nine days.

Wagner's combination of size, skill, and basketball IQ makes him uniquely difficult to defend. At 6-foot-9 with guard-like handles and a precise mid-range game, he can score over smaller defenders and create space against bigger ones. Orlando has built its offense around giving him room to operate, and the results have been extraordinary.

His walk-off on Thursday was not a lucky shot or a last-resort heave. It was a designed play, executed to perfection — Wagner caught the ball in his spot, shot over a closing defender, and drained it as the clock wound toward zero.

A Game That Almost Got Away

The Magic had led for much of Thursday's contest, but Brooklyn clawed back to tie the game in the final minute. The Nets, who have been competitive in close games this season despite their record, nearly pulled off the upset that would have punctured Orlando's momentum.

Instead, Wagner delivered. The Magic held on through the final 2.4 seconds and walked off the court with another win that continues to define their early-season identity: a team that can win tight games because they have a closer.

Orlando's Big Picture

The Magic entered the season with high expectations, and they have largely lived up to them. With Paolo Banchero as the established star and Wagner as the emerging co-star, Orlando has the offensive firepower to compete with anyone in the East on a given night.

The challenge ahead is sustaining this level of play through the full 82-game season and into the postseason, where opponents will have more time to prepare and scheme against Wagner's hot streaks. But right now, in November, the Magic look like a team that could genuinely contend in the East's upper bracket.

What's Next

Franz Wagner and the Magic will look to continue their winning ways as the calendar turns toward December. If Wagner keeps this up, the All-Star conversation will only grow louder. Orlando has an opportunity to build a substantial winning streak and make a statement about where they stand in the Eastern Conference hierarchy — and right now, their franchise player is making every clutch moment look easy.
