### STORY 2: Knicks Banner Night — The Joy and the Loss

**Long-Form Post:**
Window: 9:00 AM ET

The banner is real. The loss is also real.

The New York Knicks raised their first NBA Championship banner since 1973 on Tuesday night at Madison Square Garden. Fans who have waited their entire lives for this moment saw it happen. The ceremony was everything it was supposed to be.

Then they had to play the Philadelphia 76ers, who had spent the summer assembling LeBron James, Jaylen Brown, and Joel Embiid around Tyrese Maxey specifically to challenge for titles.

Philadelphia won 106-100. Jalen Brunson had 21 points but shot just 6-for-19 from the field. Karl-Anthony Towns was held to 24 minutes by foul trouble. The Knicks couldn't hold a 10-point first-half lead.

This is what it means to be champions. Every team comes for you. The 76ers didn't show up in New York to watch the ceremony.

The banner stays. The work starts now.
