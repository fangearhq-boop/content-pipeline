Knicks Banner Night in 24 Days: LeBron's Sixers Debut, $53K Courtside, and 53 Years of Waiting

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | September 26, 2026

The New York Knicks' championship banner ceremony is 24 days away, and the event already looks like the most-anticipated single game of the 2026-27 NBA season. This morning made it even bigger.

LeBron James spoke at Philadelphia 76ers media day in Camden, NJ — his first formal presser as a Sixer — and said directly that he wants to help Joel Embiid win a title. His opening night chance to start doing that? October 20 at Madison Square Garden, walking into a building that will be celebrating the Knicks' first championship since 1973.

The Ticket Market Reflects the Moment

The numbers tell the story. Get-in price on Gametime currently sits at $2,127 — and that's for the worst seat in the building. The average secondary-market ticket now tops $2,500. Courtside seats on Ticketmaster range from $27,000 to $53,000 per ticket. No other game on the NBA calendar for opening week approaches these numbers; a comparable ticket for the Celtics at the Pistons the same night runs under $200.

The ceremony begins at 6:30 PM ET. Tip-off is scheduled for 7 PM. The game airs on NBC and Peacock — the league's showcase broadcast slot.

Why the Sixers Make It a Showdown

The Knicks swept the 76ers 4-0 in the 2025-26 playoffs. What followed that series was one of the most dramatic offseason reconstructions in recent memory: Jaylen Brown arrived via trade from Boston, and LeBron James signed away from the Lakers to join Embiid and Maxey. The team that was swept has been completely remade.

Philadelphia's media day this morning gave fans their first look at how the new Sixers talk about themselves. LeBron cited Maxey and his desire to win as driving factors. Brown will bring a two-time champion's perspective. Embiid, who has come agonizingly close to a title without breaking through, now has both a co-star and a mentor on the same roster.

Opening night is their first audition. And it happens to be on the stage where the team that embarrassed them last spring will be throwing a party.

What the Knicks Are Celebrating

The 2025-26 Knicks ended a 53-year championship drought — the longest active title drought in the NBA when they broke through. They did it with a core built around Jalen Brunson, Josh Hart, and OG Anunoby, plus key contributions from across the roster. The championship banner going up the rafters at MSG on October 20 will be the first since the 1972-73 team led by Willis Reed, Walt Frazier, and Phil Jackson.

The franchise and its fanbase have been waiting their entire lifetimes for this moment. They get to celebrate it against a 76ers team that just brought in the greatest basketball player of the era to try to knock them off. Perfect.

What's Next

Both the Knicks and Sixers hold media day September 28, two days from now. LeBron has spoken; the Knicks will get their turn to respond. Training camps for both clubs open September 29. Watch for the pregame ceremony details to be finalized and released over the next two weeks — the Knicks are expected to have a significant production around the banner raise. Then on October 20, Madison Square Garden hosts one of those rare sporting events that lives up to its billing before the first possession even begins.
