NBA Cup Night 1 Recap: Knicks, Lakers and Rockets All Lose; Saturday Preview

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes

October 31, 2026

The 2026-27 NBA Cup opened on Halloween with an appropriately spooky night for three of the league's biggest brands. The defending champion Knicks lost in their opener. The Lakers squandered a 25-point lead. The undefeated Rockets suffered their first loss. No trick-or-treat for those fan bases — just an early reminder that the Cup produces genuine chaos.

Here is the full rundown from Friday's ten-game NBA Cup Night 1 slate, and what is coming Saturday.

Friday's Results

76ers 98, Knicks 93: Tyrese Maxey (27 points) and LeBron James (20/9/8) combined for 47 points to beat the defending Cup champions. Jalen Brunson's step-back three briefly put New York ahead 90-89 late, but Philadelphia held on. Sixers: 1-0 Cup. Knicks: 0-1 Cup.

Warriors 112, Lakers 95: The Lakers led by 25. They lost by 17. Stephen Curry scored 31 and led one of the season's most stunning comebacks. Luka Dončić's 37 points were not enough. Curry also passed Dominique Wilkins for 18th on the NBA's all-time scoring list.

Mavericks 116, Rockets 111: Kyrie Irving's 35 points, 9 assists, and Cooper Flagg's 33 powered a 40-27 Dallas fourth quarter. The Rockets' undefeated start is over. Kevin Durant scored 28 in defeat.

Heat 145, Pacers 125: Giannis Antetokounmpo posted a 31-point, 18-rebound, 10-assist triple-double in his NBA Cup debut with Miami. It was not close.

Pistons 110, Nets 94: Cade Cunningham led Detroit, which improves to 5-1 and is one of the East's early surprises.

Celtics 125, Bulls 111: Boston stays among the East's elite with another wire-to-wire win.

Magic 121, Raptors 114: Orlando won its Cup opener on the road in Toronto.

Wizards 95, Hornets 79: Washington handled Charlotte at home in NBA Cup group play.

Timberwolves 127, Clippers 101: Minnesota won decisively in its Cup opener, with Los Angeles unable to keep pace.

Nuggets 127, Suns 120: Denver opened Cup play with a comfortable win over Phoenix.

Saturday's Schedule

Saturday's slate is lighter with four regular-season games — no Cup action today:

Trail Blazers at Jazz — 3:00 PM ET: Portland has been one of the West's surprise teams early. Utah looks to bounce back from Friday's Cup defeat.

76ers at Hornets — 6:00 PM ET: Philly plays the second half of a back-to-back after Friday's Cup win. LeBron and Maxey will be monitored for load management on short rest.

Hawks at Pelicans — 7:00 PM ET: Atlanta looks to build on the Okongwu-led comeback win over Cleveland. New Orleans continues what has been a strong early stretch with Jeremiah Fears in form.

Kings at Grizzlies — 8:00 PM ET: Memphis (5-0) hosts Sacramento (1-4) in a matchup that should heavily favor the undefeated Grizzlies at home.

What's Next

The NBA Cup group phase continues through November before the quarterfinals arrive December 4-5. Semifinals follow December 8-9, with the Cup final set for December 11 in Indianapolis. Teams have room to recover from opening-night stumbles, but every group game counts toward both the Cup standings and the regular-season record. Night 1 made clear that no team — however talented, however decorated — can take any Cup game for granted.
