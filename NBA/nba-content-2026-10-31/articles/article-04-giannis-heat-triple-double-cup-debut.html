Giannis Posts 31/18/10 Triple-Double in Heat's 145-125 NBA Cup Debut

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes

October 31, 2026

Any remaining questions about what Giannis Antetokounmpo would look like in a Miami Heat uniform were answered emphatically on Friday night. The former Milwaukee Buck, acquired in a blockbuster trade in June 2026, delivered a triple-double in his NBA Cup debut: 31 points, 18 rebounds, and 10 assists as Miami demolished the Indiana Pacers 145-125.

It was a statement performance on one of the biggest stages of the early season, and it signaled that the Heat's championship ambitions are not hypothetical — they are very much in progress.

The New King of South Beach

Giannis Antetokounmpo left Milwaukee after deciding the Bucks' championship window had closed. He came to Miami to win. On Friday night, he looked every bit like the best player in the Eastern Conference.

His 31 points came in a variety of ways: driving through the Pacers' defense for layups and dunks, finishing through contact at the rim, and stepping back for the mid-range jumper he has refined over the past several seasons. His 18 rebounds — an extraordinary total — reflected his dominance on the glass on both ends of the floor. And his 10 assists underscored what makes Giannis uniquely valuable: he can do everything, and he does it simultaneously.

Pairing Giannis with Bam Adebayo in Miami's frontcourt gives the Heat one of the most physically imposing pairings in the conference. If Friday night is any indication, teams that try to match up with Miami in a Cup group game are going to struggle to find answers.

Miami's Offensive Explosion

One hundred and forty-five points. The Heat did not just beat Indiana — they overwhelmed them. Miami's offense ran at a pace and efficiency that the Pacers, despite their own scoring reputation, had no answer for.

The 20-point final margin does not fully capture how thoroughly Miami controlled the game. Giannis's triple-double was the headline, but the Heat's overall offensive construction — the spacing, the movement, the relentless attacking — was a preview of what Cup opponents will face throughout group play.

What's Next

Miami opens Cup play at 1-0 and is positioned as one of the Eastern Conference's premier Cup contenders. The Heat's combination of Giannis, Adebayo, and a deep supporting roster gives them as many ways to win as any team in the conference.

Indiana falls to 0-1 in Cup play and faces the reality that keeping pace with a team built around Giannis will require a significantly better defensive effort in future group games. For the Pacers, Friday was a wake-up call that the East has a new dominant force.
