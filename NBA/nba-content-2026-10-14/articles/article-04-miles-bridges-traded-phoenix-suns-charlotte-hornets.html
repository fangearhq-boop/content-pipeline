Miles Bridges Is Heading to Phoenix — What the Suns Get Before the Deadline

By Jake Torres | October 14, 2026 | Hoop Heroes

The NBA roster deadline is Friday, October 17. With three days to go, the Charlotte Hornets and Phoenix Suns agreed on a trade that sends Miles Bridges west. In exchange for the 28-year-old forward, Charlotte receives Grayson Allen, Royce O'Neale, and a 2033 first-round pick.

The deal closes one of the more productive chapters in Charlotte's recent history and opens a new one in Phoenix, where the Suns are looking to compete sooner rather than later.

What Phoenix Gets

Miles Bridges is a 28-year-old forward who can score. He averaged north of 20 points per game in Charlotte and has the athleticism, finishing ability, and mid-range game to be a credible scoring option on a team with championship aspirations. He also defends — not at an elite level, but well enough to guard multiple positions, which is the currency teams need in a Western Conference that has Oklahoma City and San Antonio at the top.

For Phoenix, the acquisition addresses the question of who creates shots outside of the team's primary options. Bridges is the kind of player who gets 18 points in a game where nobody is talking about him, and those games accumulate over an 82-game season. He is entering a situation where the floor is already set for him, which has historically produced the best versions of players like him.

What Charlotte Gets

The Hornets receive two veteran pieces in Allen and O'Neale — experienced, low-maintenance contributors who can play immediately — plus the 2033 first-round pick. From Charlotte's perspective, this trade is about timeline management. Moving a 20-point scorer who represents a significant contract obligation in exchange for assets and flexibility is the kind of move that rebuilding teams make when they see a cleaner path forward.

The 2033 first-round pick is nine years away. By then, the Hornets' young roster will have either developed into something significant or been rebuilt again. The pick is an option on the future, and collecting options is what the first phase of a rebuild looks like.

Timing and Context

Three days before the roster deadline is not an accident. Front offices that still have business to do move quickly as Friday approaches. Phoenix's front office apparently identified Bridges as the piece they needed and moved decisively. The deal is done, and the Suns begin their 2026-27 campaign with a new scoring wing in the rotation.

Opening Night is six days away. Whatever Phoenix looks like in its first regular-season game will include Miles Bridges. That is a significant roster change completed with less than a week until the season begins — which is exactly how these transactions are supposed to work when teams are serious about competing.

What's Next

The NBA roster deadline is October 17 at 4:00 PM CT. Final rosters must be submitted by October 19 at 4:00 PM CT. Opening Night follows on October 20. Bridges will suit up for Phoenix. Charlotte will begin its rebuild with new pieces and future assets. The trade market closes in three days, but this transaction is already done.
