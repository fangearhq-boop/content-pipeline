The NBA Roster Deadline Is Friday: 72 Hours for Teams to Get It Right

By Marcus Cole | October 14, 2026 | Hoop Heroes

On Friday, October 17 at 4:00 PM CT, the 2026-27 NBA season stops accepting revisions. The roster deadline closes. Trades still happen after that, but with a mandatory 48-hour waiting period. Waivers still clear, but the window for significant roster construction is effectively over. Every team has until Friday afternoon to be the version of itself it intends to be for the next eight months.

With Opening Night six days away and three days to the deadline, the league is in its most active final window of the preseason. The Miles Bridges trade from Charlotte to Phoenix, completed Wednesday, is proof that teams are not waiting until the last minute. Others may not be so decisive.

Injury Questions That Need Answers

The roster deadline matters more in some locker rooms than others. Rob Dillingham of the Chicago Bulls had minor surgery to remove a ganglion cyst from his right wrist. The team has not confirmed a timetable for his return. Dillingham was a first-round pick with significant upside, and his availability at the start of the regular season is an open question Chicago's front office has to plan around.

In Oklahoma City, Nikola Topic — the Thunder's own high-upside young piece — had back surgery during the offseason. The team expects him available for training camp and the opening of the season, but "expected" carries weight when the games are six days away. If Topic's status changes between now and Friday, the Thunder's roster plans change with it.

What Happens After the Deadline

Trades executed after October 17 must go through a 48-hour waiting period before they become official, meaning both players can appear on game rosters. That effectively delays any post-deadline acquisitions until after Opening Night games have been played. Teams that want a piece in uniform for October 20 have until Friday afternoon to complete the paperwork.

The final roster submission deadline is October 19 at 4:00 PM CT. That is the last moment a team can cut a player, sign a replacement on a two-way contract, or convert an existing deal to a standard contract. The season begins 24 hours later.

The Bigger Picture

The roster deadline is one of those moments the NBA treats as procedural but that carries real meaning for the teams involved. It is the last opportunity to be right before the season proves you wrong. Front offices spend all summer building toward this window, and the ones that have not finished their work — or the ones waiting on medical clearance, or the ones still evaluating trade options — have until Friday at 4:00 PM CT.

The Miles Bridges trade suggests Phoenix finished its homework early. Chicago and Oklahoma City are still waiting on clarity. Thirty teams, 72 hours, one deadline.
