27 Days to NBA Training Camp: Every Story Worth Watching

By Jake Torres

September 2, 2026

Twenty-seven days. That is what separates the NBA world from the start of the most anticipated training camp cycle in recent memory. Veterans report on September 28, camps officially open September 29, preseason begins October 3, and Opening Night arrives October 20. Between now and then, there is one outstanding legal matter, several rosters still in flux, and a league-wide sense that 2026-27 could be something special. Here is what to watch.

The Kawhi Question

The biggest camp storyline will be answered before camp even opens — or it won't be, and that itself becomes the story. Kawhi Leonard agreed to a trade to Toronto in late June, but the deal has been frozen since July 9 while the NBA investigates the Clippers organization. Leonard has been personally cleared. The Board of Governors is expected to meet this month, and the working assumption is that the trade gets ratified in time for Leonard to report to Quebec City with the Raptors on September 28.

If the BOG meeting resolves the matter as expected, Toronto enters camp as one of the more intriguing teams in the Eastern Conference. If it doesn't, both franchises open camp with enormous questions about roster construction and direction. Either way, this situation gets resolved in the next 27 days — or training camp starts without clarity.

Miami's Superteam First Test

The Miami Heat assembled the league's most star-studded roster this summer: Giannis Antetokounmpo arrived via trade from Milwaukee on July 6, joining Bam Adebayo and newly signed Klay Thompson. Coach Erik Spoelstra now faces perhaps the most complex lineup puzzle of his career. How the Heat's three All-Stars share the ball and the spotlight in practice — before the games begin — will be closely watched throughout October.

Giannis becomes eligible for a $275 million supermax extension on January 6, 2027. Whether that process begins during or after the season could tell observers everything about Miami's future. For now, the Heat's opening Christmas Day matchup against the Boston Celtics is circled on every calendar.

LeBron and the Sixers Experiment

LeBron James signed with Philadelphia this offseason in a move that instantly transformed the 76ers from a team rebuilding around Joel Embiid to a genuine championship contender. James joins Jaylen Brown, acquired from Boston in a separate deal. Philadelphia's championship odds improved from 20-1 to 10-1 once those moves became official.

But chemistry is built in practice, not on paper. James at 41 and a roster full of veterans — Embiid, who was described as looking healthier than he has in years, is also on board — will need to find their collective rhythm quickly. The Sixers open on Opening Night against the Knicks at Madison Square Garden. There will be no soft landing.

Curry and the Warriors Retool

Golden State had a difficult 2025-26, finishing 37-45 and missing the playoffs. But the offseason brought optimism: Stephen Curry signed a 1-year, $62.6M extension — becoming the first player in NBA history to earn more than $60 million in a single season — and Curry himself noted that "a lot of health is coming back" with Moses Moody and Jimmy Butler returning from injuries. Brandon Williams, 13 points and 3.9 assists per game last year in Dallas, also joined the backcourt depth chart.

The Warriors are not expected to contend for a title this season, but the presence of Curry at full health alongside a healthier supporting cast makes Golden State worth tracking from the first day of camp.

What's Next

September 28 is the first date veterans can report to their teams. September 29 is when training camps formally open across the league. The preseason schedule begins October 3, giving fans their first look at these retooled rosters in game action. Opening Night on October 20 — the most historic in a generation — is 48 days away. Start the countdown.
