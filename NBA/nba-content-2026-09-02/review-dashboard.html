Whether Kawhi ultimately arrives or stays away, the Clippers wanted veteran guard depth, and Beal — who brings playoff experience and a reliable mid-range game — fits that need on a team-friendly contract. His mid-range scoring off the bench is exactly what the Nuggets needed. 📝 **Zach LaVine** exercised his $49M player option and will stay with the Chicago Bulls for 2026-27. LaVine chose security over free agency, giving Chicago continuity as they continue to build around a younger core. 📝 **Brandon Williams** signed a 1-year, $2.6M deal with the Golden State Warriors.
