**Long-Form Post (9:00 AM ET)**
The Kawhi Leonard saga is entering its final chapter — and the ending could come this month.

Meanwhile: Leonard and the Raptors continue to operate as though the trade goes through. Toronto's camp opens September 28 in Quebec City. Kawhi expects to be there.

And the Clippers? Per HoopsHype, they would be "fine" with Kawhi staying if the trade is ultimately voided. That is a contingency plan, not a preference. By all accounts, Leonard wants Toronto.

One Board of Governors meeting. That is all that stands between him and his new team.

**Long-Form Post (4:00 PM ET)**

France closed out the latest FIBA World Cup qualifying window with a 7-1 record in Group L, and Wembanyama — wearing the captain's armband for the first time — was the best player on the floor in every game that mattered.

Here is what The Alien did:

🇫🇷 **vs. Slovenia (Aug 27, Paris):** 22 points, 9 rebounds, 60% from the field. France won 92-67.

🇫🇷 **vs. Sweden (Aug 30, Stockholm):** 18 points, 8 rebounds, 3 blocks, 6-of-7 from the field. France won 84-69 in front of 14,750 fans at the Avicii Arena — a Swedish basketball all-time attendance record.

### STORY 4: Bradley Beal Signs With Clippers — Late Offseason Transactions Roundup

**Long-Form Post (7:00 PM ET)**
The NBA's 2026 offseason is winding down — and it's going out with a few notable moves.

Bradley Beal has signed a 2-year, $13.17M deal with the Los Angeles Clippers, joining a team still awaiting the resolution of the Kawhi Leonard trade. Whether Kawhi ultimately arrives or stays away, the Clippers wanted veteran guard depth, and Beal — who brings playoff experience and a reliable mid-range game — fits that need on a team-friendly contract.

Elsewhere around the league:

📝 **DeMar DeRozan** agreed to a 1-year, $3.88M veteran minimum deal with the Denver Nuggets. DeRozan joins Nikola Jokic in a bid to make Denver a legitimate championship contender again. His mid-range scoring off the bench is exactly what the Nuggets needed.

📝 **Zach LaVine** exercised his $49M player option and will stay with the Chicago Bulls for 2026-27. LaVine chose security over free agency, giving Chicago continuity as they continue to build around a younger core.

📝 **Brandon Williams** signed a 1-year, $2.6M deal with the Golden State Warriors. Williams averaged 13 points and 3.9 assists for Dallas last year and gives Steph Curry quality backup depth in the backcourt.

Training camp opens in 27 days. The rosters are almost final. The season is almost here.
