NBA Schedule Week Is Here: Cup Slate Tuesday, Full 2026-27 Calendar Thursday

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | August 7, 2026

NBA / 2026-27 Season Preview / NBA Cup

For NBA fans, this is the week you've been waiting for. The 2026-27 regular-season schedule is coming — and it's arriving in two stages.

According to Bleacher Report and historical patterns, the NBA In-Season Tournament (NBA Cup) schedule is expected on Tuesday, August 11, with the full regular-season slate to follow on Thursday, August 13. The last three regular-season schedule releases have all landed on a Thursday, making the August 13 window the most likely target.

Why This Year's Release Feels Different

Every season's schedule release generates interest, but Bleacher Report specifically noted that demand for the 2026-27 slate is "unusually high" — driven entirely by one name: LeBron James.

LeBron's decision to join the Philadelphia 76ers reshaped the league's marquee matchup calculus. His opener — wherever it falls — is now one of the most-watched regular-season games in years. His team's matchup against the New York Knicks, the defending champions, will be must-see television regardless of when it's scheduled. His return to Cleveland, Miami, or Los Angeles carries emotional weight no other offseason move this cycle can match.

The league leaned into that demand, reportedly updating its schedule release timeline after LeBron's signing was finalized.

The Games Fans Are Circling

Before Thursday's release, here are the matchups and moments driving anticipation:

The Knicks' championship banner night — expected October 20 at Madison Square Garden — is one of the most anticipated early-season events on the calendar. New York won its first championship in decades last season, and the MSG banner ceremony will be a full-scale celebration that the entire league will watch.

LeBron James and the 76ers will have multiple marquee designations. His opener in Philadelphia — and whichever road game follows — has network television written all over it. The NBA does not bury those opportunities.

Victor Wembanyama and the Spurs draw eyeballs no matter the opponent, but any early-season matchup against the Sixers or Knicks would be a scheduling gift.

The Golden State Warriors, with Stephen Curry's extension window opening August 29, also carry significant attention into the new season. Every Warriors game takes on added weight as fans wait to see what the next chapter of the dynasty looks like.

Six New Head Coaches

This cycle also brings six new head coaches making their regular-season debuts, adding another layer of intrigue to early-season matchups. New voices in Dallas, Milwaukee, Chicago, Orlando, Portland, and New Orleans mean six fan bases will be watching their teams with fresh eyes — and six opponents will be game-planning against new schemes they haven't seen before.

What's Next

Check back Tuesday, August 11 for the NBA Cup schedule, which will reveal group-stage matchups and format for the In-Season Tournament. The full 2026-27 calendar drops Thursday, August 13 — expected around 3:00 PM ET based on the last three release windows. That's when the real planning begins.
