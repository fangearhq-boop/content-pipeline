### STORY 2: Wembanyama Back in France

**Long-Form Post**
🇫🇷 Victor Wembanyama is back in France — and he's already thinking about more than basketball.

Wembanyama has been named to France's 15-man roster for the 2027 FIBA World Cup qualifying window this August. When asked about the call-up, he was characteristically direct: "I miss FIBA."

It's been nearly two years since Wembanyama helped France win the gold medal at the 2024 Paris Olympics in front of his home crowd. In between, he won Defensive Player of the Year, earned All-NBA honors, and led San Antonio to the Western Conference Finals — where he won MVP. He closed his third NBA season as perhaps the most dominant two-way player on the planet.

And yet — he misses FIBA.

Wembanyama is hosting several Spurs teammates in France this offseason for informal workouts. ESPN confirmed the group includes De'Aaron Fox, Stephon Castle, and Dylan Harper. This is what team-building looks like away from the arena.

France is scheduled to play Serbia on August 20 and 23 before World Cup qualifying games against Slovenia (Aug 27) and Sweden (Aug 30). For Wembanyama, this isn't an obligation — it's a reunion.

Meanwhile, his Spurs future is secure: he signed a five-year, $252 million extension, deliberately choosing the 25% max salary tier rather than the 30% supermax — leaving approximately $50 million on the table to help San Antonio remain financially flexible.

At 22, he's the best player on the planet. And he still misses FIBA.

And when the opt-in deadline came, he opted into his $25M player option with the Houston Rockets anyway.

He's betting everything on his recovery.

VanVleet, 32, has been one of the more underrated point guards of his generation — a player who went undrafted, won a championship with Toronto, and rebuilt himself as a legitimate starter in Houston. The Rockets are betting on him too. If VanVleet returns at or near his previous level, he gives Houston a proven, championship-tested floor general to pair with their emerging young core. If the recovery falls short, they're carrying a significant salary for production they may not get.

Training camp opens in late September. That's when we'll see where Fred VanVleet's body actually is.
