11 Days to Media Day: Curry Extension Hangs Over Warriors Camp

By Jake Torres | Hoop Heroes | September 17, 2026

Golden State Warriors

Eleven days. That is how much time separates the Golden State Warriors from their September 28 media day — and as of Thursday, Stephen Curry still has not signed the two-year, $136.7 million contract extension sitting on the table.

Both sides want a deal. Sources confirmed to ESPN that the Warriors and Curry's camp share mutual interest in keeping the four-time champion in Golden State. Warriors brass has reportedly given Curry control over the structure of the extension — an unusual level of deference that signals just how important the organization views the relationship. But mutual interest does not cash out. The days keep ticking, and the clock has no snooze button.

What the Extension Means

The maximum extension available to Curry is two years at $136.7 million — a figure that, if signed, would push Curry past $600 million in career on-court earnings, a first in NBA history. The milestone is real, but it is not necessarily the deciding factor. The structure of the deal matters just as much as the number.

If Curry accepts the full $136.7 million extension, it cuts the Warriors' projected cap space roughly in half. That is not a minor footnote — it affects every trade target, every free agent the team can pursue, every roster decision through the late 2020s. Golden State is reportedly projecting more than $130 million in cap space. A full Curry extension halves that projection. A discounted deal preserves flexibility.

Curry, 38, is in a position few athletes find themselves in: he has so much earned goodwill with his franchise that he genuinely gets to decide what comes next, on his own terms. Whether he maximizes his own payday or takes a structure that benefits the team's long-term flexibility will say something about the final chapter of his career.

The Media Day Deadline

September 28 is not a contractual hard deadline — Curry can sign an extension at any point before the regular season begins. But media day carries symbolic weight. If Curry walks into the Warriors' facility on Sept. 28 without a deal in place, the first question every reporter asks is obvious. The extension story swallows every other Warriors story until it resolves.

Training camp follows media day on September 29, with Golden State heading to Hawaii for camp. The preseason calendar runs through early October, and the regular season opens October 20. A deal at any point through November or even December is technically possible — but for the Warriors' sake, and for Curry's, getting it done before media day keeps the focus on basketball.

Where Things Stand

As of the latest reporting, no deal has been announced. No specific deadline exists within the negotiations. The public signals from both sides remain positive — "mutual interest," "letting Curry decide" — but positive signals are not contracts.

The 11-day window is not a crisis. It is, however, a narrowing. The longer this goes, the more each passing day becomes its own story. The question is simple: Does Curry want to spend media day answering questions about his own extension, or does he walk in as the face of a franchise with a deal signed and a banner chase to discuss?

What's Next

Warriors media day is September 28. All eyes will be on whether Curry appears with a signed extension or whether the first day of public access to the team is dominated by contract questions. Training camp opens September 29, and the preseason tips off October 3. If there is no deal by media day, expect the extension story to remain the dominant Warriors narrative well into October. If a deal is signed before then, Golden State heads into camp with its future secured — and its legend one step closer to $600 million.
