**FB Long-Form** | Window: 11:00 AM ET

Herro's 50 points (15-of-23 from the field, 7-of-12 from three, a perfect 13-of-13 from the line) with 11 rebounds and seven assists for the Milwaukee Bucks is now the greatest individual effort in a Bucks loss in memory. The 76ers held on 132-129, with Joel Embiid delivering 26 points and 17 rebounds and LeBron James scoring 13 of his 21 points in the fourth quarter to keep Philadelphia ahead.

Herro's night was statistically unprecedented: he is the first player in NBA history to record 50 or more points, 11 or more rebounds, seven or more made three-pointers, and shoot at least 65 percent from the field in a single game. His final three-point attempt to force overtime came just after the buzzer sounded — a matter of inches from NBA overtime history instead of an agonizing loss.

The loss extended Milwaukee's losing streak to 13 games. The Bucks are searching for answers, and while Herro gave them everything he had, there weren't enough pieces around him to convert a historic performance into a win. For Milwaukee, Saturday night is a painful reminder of how much is broken right now.
