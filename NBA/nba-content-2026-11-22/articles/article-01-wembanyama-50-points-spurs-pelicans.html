Wembanyama Posts 50 Points, 19 Rebounds as Spurs Roll Pelicans 114-91

By Jake Torres | November 22, 2026 | Hoop Heroes

Victor Wembanyama delivered one of the most stunning individual performances of the 2026-27 season Saturday night, scoring 50 points with 19 rebounds, five blocks and four steals as the San Antonio Spurs dismantled the New Orleans Pelicans 114-91 at the Frost Bank Center.

Wembanyama shot 20-of-29 from the field, hit five three-pointers on 11 attempts and converted five of six free throws. He had 32 of his points by halftime, all while the Spurs built a lead that the short-handed Pelicans — without Zion Williamson, who sat with a thigh contusion — could never seriously challenge. San Antonio was plus-35 when Wembanyama was on the floor.

Wemby Deflects the Historic Numbers

Even in a performance that left broadcasters searching for comparisons, Wembanyama was quick to dismiss the significance of his stat line. After the game, he offered a dry comment about "stat padding" — suggesting his standards for a meaningful performance are even higher than 50 points and 19 boards.

The 50-point mark equals his career high, which he set in a 2024 win over the Washington Wizards. That earlier outing came with eight made threes. Saturday's version featured a more complete two-way display, adding four steals and five blocks to a night that covered nearly every category on the stat sheet.

Pelicans Could Not Keep Up

New Orleans struggled from the opening tip without its franchise cornerstone. Jeremiah Fears provided an offensive lifeline with 29 points, and Jordan Poole added 25, but neither player could slow the Spurs when Wembanyama was engaged on both ends of the floor.

The Pelicans finished 23 points behind in a game that felt decisive well before the fourth quarter. San Antonio's defense held New Orleans to 91 points while Wembanyama's presence at the rim forced the Pelicans into uncomfortable looks throughout the night.

What's Next

The Spurs will look to build on their dominant performance as the 2026-27 season continues. For Wembanyama, Saturday's game adds another chapter to what is shaping up as an extraordinary sophomore campaign. The Pelicans will need to get Zion Williamson healthy and find answers for stopping elite big men before they can compete at the highest level in the Western Conference.
