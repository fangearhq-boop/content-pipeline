Flagg's 22-Point Night, Irving's 25 Fuel Mavericks' Late Surge Past Clippers 110-100

By Marcus Cole | Hoop Heroes | November 22, 2026

Cooper Flagg posted 22 points, eight rebounds and six assists, Kyrie Irving scored 25, and the Dallas Mavericks closed the game on an 11-0 run to put away the Los Angeles Clippers, 110-100, Saturday night at Crypto.com Arena.

Dallas looked beatable through three quarters, allowing the Clippers to stay close and manufacture enough offense to keep the game in reach. Then the Mavericks turned a switch. The 11-0 closing run, with Flagg and Irving doing the most damage, turned a tight contest into a comfortable final margin.

Flagg Continues to Develop

The rookie forward continues to demonstrate that his impact goes well beyond scoring. Flagg's six assists on Saturday showed a passing vision that expands Dallas's offensive options and makes him a genuine dual threat whenever the defense collapses on his drives. The eight rebounds gave the Mavericks interior presence at a moment when the Clippers tried to establish their own physicality in the fourth quarter.

At 22 years old and still adjusting to the speed and complexity of the NBA game, Flagg is ahead of schedule by most measures. Dallas made him a cornerstone piece going forward, and Saturday was another reminder of why that decision made sense.

Irving Provides the Veteran Anchor

Kyrie Irving's 25 points were distributed throughout the game, but his contribution to the closing run was particularly important. With the Clippers still capable of tying the game in the final six minutes, Irving made several of the decisive plays — a mid-range jumper to extend the lead, a free throw to ice it. His veteran presence alongside an emerging young player like Flagg creates the kind of dynamic that good teams sustain over the course of a long season.

What's Next

Dallas will look to continue building on this stretch of play as the season moves toward its second month. Flagg's development is the storyline that will carry the Mavericks' narrative through the year — his combination of athleticism and IQ gives Dallas a foundation to build around for years to come. The Clippers, who stayed in the game long enough to make it uncomfortable, will need to find ways to close out games against the league's better teams going forward.
