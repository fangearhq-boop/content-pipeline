Tyler Herro Scores Career-High 50 in Historic Loss as Bucks Skid Reaches 13 Games

By Marcus Cole | November 22, 2026 | Hoop Heroes

Tyler Herro authored the greatest individual performance of his NBA career Saturday night and still walked away with a loss. Herro scored a career-high 50 points for the Milwaukee Bucks, but the Philadelphia 76ers held on for a 132-129 victory that extended Milwaukee's losing streak to 13 games.

Herro finished 15-of-23 from the field, 7-of-12 from three-point range and a perfect 13-of-13 at the free-throw line. He added 11 rebounds and seven assists in an extraordinary all-around showing. His final three-point attempt that could have tied the game and forced overtime came just after the buzzer sounded.

An Unprecedented Stat Combination

Herro's performance was not just a career milestone — it made NBA history. He became the first player in league history to record at least 50 points, 11 rebounds, seven made three-pointers, and 65-percent-plus shooting efficiency in a single game. He also became only the seventh player in Bucks franchise history to reach 50 points in a game.

His previous career high of 41 points had come in December 2022 against the Houston Rockets while he was still with Miami. Saturday's output surpassed that mark by nine points and added dimensions of rebounding and playmaking that made it a truly complete performance.

Not Enough to Stop Philadelphia

Joel Embiid countered with 26 points and 17 rebounds to anchor the 76ers, who withstood Herro's historic effort. LeBron James provided the decisive push, scoring 13 of his 21 points in the fourth quarter as Philadelphia locked down the victory in the final minutes.

The Bucks, who entered the game with a 13-game skid, now extend a streak that has become one of the most difficult stories in the Eastern Conference. Despite the loss, the performance underscored Herro's growth as a go-to scorer and playmaker capable of carrying an offense on a given night.

What's Next

Milwaukee faces an urgent need to find answers beyond individual brilliance. Herro's career night illustrates the talent within the roster, but 13 consecutive losses demand systemic solutions. The Bucks must address their defensive struggles and find ways to protect leads in fourth quarters before their playoff hopes slip further away. Philadelphia, meanwhile, strengthens its position as one of the Eastern Conference's top teams.
