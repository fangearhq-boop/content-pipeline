Keegan Murray Putback With 5 Seconds Left Ends Kings' 8-Game Skid, Stuns Celtics 97-96

By Damon Pierce | November 22, 2026 | Hoop Heroes

Keegan Murray tipped in a putback over three defenders with five seconds remaining Saturday night to give the Sacramento Kings their first home win of the season and snap an eight-game losing streak, 97-96 over the Boston Celtics at Golden 1 Center.

Murray's putback came off a missed shot in the final seconds, and the Kings had been searching for exactly this moment all season — a home win to validate their effort and silence the doubts that had built through eight consecutive home defeats. Sacramento held on as Boston's desperation heave at the buzzer fell short.

A Long-Awaited Home Victory

The Kings entered Saturday night with the uncomfortable distinction of being winless at home in the 2026-27 season. The eight-game home losing streak had tested the patience of Sacramento's fanbase and raised questions about the team's ability to protect its home court.

Murray's winning play encapsulated the scrappiness required to end the drought. Securing an offensive rebound in traffic and converting through contact over multiple outstretched arms, the forward delivered exactly the kind of play that Sacramento needed. It was a moment that required both effort and execution at the precise instant when the game demanded it most.

Celtics Suffer a Late Collapse

Boston had the lead in the closing seconds and needed only to defend a final possession to escape Sacramento with a road victory. Instead, the Kings found the offensive board and Murray made the most of the second chance. The Celtics, one of the Eastern Conference's most consistent teams, were unable to prevent the putback attempt that decided the outcome.

The loss was a painful one for Boston given how close the team was to securing a road win, but the Kings' resilience in finding a way to convert when it mattered most told the full story of Saturday night.

What's Next

The Kings will try to carry the momentum from Saturday's breakthrough into their next stretch of games. Breaking the home losing streak gives Sacramento something to build on and restores some confidence in the building. For Boston, the defeat is a reminder that no road game is guaranteed, and the Celtics will look to regroup and get back on track in their next outing.
