Bridges Buries Buzzer-Beater as Knicks Edge Trail Blazers 97-96

By Jake Torres | November 22, 2026 | Hoop Heroes

Mikel Bridges hit a three-pointer at the buzzer to give the New York Knicks a 97-96 victory over the Portland Trail Blazers on Saturday night, capping a performance in which Jalen Brunson scored 30 points and kept New York in the game when the Trail Blazers threatened to pull away.

Bridges caught the ball on the wing in the final seconds and fired without hesitation, sending the Knicks bench into celebration. It was the kind of winning play the team has come to expect from a player who has steadily grown into one of New York's most reliable options in clutch situations.

Brunson Keeps the Knicks Afloat

Jalen Brunson shouldered the offensive load through most of the night, posting 30 points on a steady diet of mid-range jumpers and drives to the basket. His ability to create offense in the half-court has been central to New York's identity, and Saturday was another example of Brunson willing the Knicks through a difficult road environment.

Portland had its chances to put the game away but could not find the defensive stop it needed in the final possession. The Trail Blazers gave the Knicks life by allowing Bridges to get a clean look from beyond the arc, and the veteran wing made no mistake.

What's Next

The Knicks continue to position themselves as one of the Eastern Conference's serious contenders, and road wins like Saturday's are the type that build résumés. New York will look to maintain its momentum as the schedule tightens in the coming weeks. Portland, still searching for consistent results at home, will need to tighten its late-game execution to avoid similar finishes.
