## Category 4: Starting Rotation / FA Targets

- **Shota Imanaga**: Declined player option and Cubs declined team option after 2025 season. Cubs insider confirms he's likely leaving this offseason. His original 4-year/$53M deal had options — both sides declined. Cubs hold QO (~$22M) as leverage/draft pick option. Sources: SI/Inside The Cubs (Cubs insider piece), TBNL newsletter
- **Tarik Skubal**: Was trade deadline target for Cubs; Dodgers won the bidding. Cubs "were out pretty early in the process" per ESPN's Jesse Rogers. Source: Bleacher Report (8/2/2026)
- **Michael King (Padres RHP)**: Posted 3.23 ERA in 27 starts for Padres in 2026. Has a player opt-out for 2027/2028. Widely viewed as "2nd best starter on this winter's FA market" after Skubal. Opt-out deadline is 5 days after WS concludes. Source: MLB.com (8/29/2026 opt-out piece), Gaslamp Ball
- **Joe Ryan (Twins)**: Mentioned as likely FA target. Source: Sportsnaut offseason guide.
- **Framber Valdez**: Mentioned as possible alternative.
- Rotation hole entering 2027: Gausman (FA), Imanaga (likely gone), Boyd (FA), Horton (out until mid-2027), Steele (TBD). Potential holdovers: Edward Cabrera, Ben Brown, Javier Assad, Colin Rea.
- Sources: Bleacher Nation rotation preview, Roundtable.io (10/06/2026), MLB.com
