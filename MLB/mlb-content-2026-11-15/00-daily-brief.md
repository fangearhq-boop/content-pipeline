### STORY 1: Skubal's California Meetings Start This Week — Mets Go First
**Tier:** 1 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP (Skubal market covered Oct 30–Nov 14)
The most consequential free agent meetings of the 2026-27 offseason are underway. Tarik Skubal (2x Cy Young, 16-5, 1.80 ERA, 252 K) is scheduled to meet with six teams in California this week, starting with the New York Mets — owner Steve Cohen and president David Stearns are expected to attend in person. Jon Heyman (New York Post) says the Mets are "probably the favorite" to land Skubal. The Dodgers, Padres, Blue Jays, and two additional teams are also scheduled. Skubal's camp is aiming to close a deal before the December 1 CBA expiration. Contract projections: 8yr/$352M ($44M AAV) which would shatter Yoshinobu Yamamoto's $27M AAV record for a pitcher. No qualifying offer attached, so no draft compensation penalty for the signing team.

### STORY 2: Qualifying Offer Deadline 48 Hours Away — All 8 Expected to Decline
**Tier:** 1 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP (QO covered Oct 30–Nov 14)

Seven players remain: Randy Arozarena (OF, Mariners), David Bednar (RP, Yankees), Ryan Jeffers (C, Twins), Josh Lowe (OF), Jake Rogers, Angel Morejon (RP), and Seiya Suzuki (OF, Cubs). Historical rate: 18 of 157 players have ever accepted (11.5%). All remaining seven are expected to decline. If accepted, the team must pay $23.15M for one season. Players who decline become unrestricted free agents.

### STORY 3: Pre-Lockout Spending Spree Begins — Teams Rushing Before December 1
**Tier:** 2 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP (CBA/lockout covered Oct 30–Nov 14)
With the CBA expiring December 1 and a lockout expected, teams are accelerating moves. Free agency will pause upon lockout; the last lockout ran 99 days (Dec 2, 2021–March 10, 2022).

**Tier:** 2 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP (Sato covered Oct 30–Nov 14)
