## Story 2: Aaron Judge ALDS Watch

**Long-Form Post:**
Aaron Judge has not played since September 16. He missed the entire Wild Card Series as the Yankees swept the Red Sox -- 18-2 in two games -- without their captain.

Ben Rice hit a grand slam in Game 1. Giancarlo Stanton and Trent Grisham returned from injury to fill in. The Yankees handled their business.

But tonight is different. ALDS Game 1 at 6:30 PM ET vs. the Tampa Bay Rays -- the American League's best team with the best record in the AL. The Rays went 8-2 against New York in the regular season.

Will Aaron Judge be on the roster tonight? That is the question nobody can definitively answer yet this morning. He has been rehabbing a moderate right calf strain and expressed the desire to play outfield, not just DH, when he returns. Whether that timeline lands on October 3 is still up in the air.

The Yankees have won before without him this postseason. The question is whether they'll need to do it again.

## Story 3: White Sox First ALDS Since 2008

**Long-Form Post:**
In 2024, the Chicago White Sox lost 121 games. It was the worst record by any team in baseball since 1900. The fan base was demoralized. The stadium was empty.

Today -- less than two years later -- they play in the American League Division Series for the first time since 2008.

How does something like this happen? You hire a manager in Will Venable who believes in young players. You sign Munetaka Murakami out of Japan -- he debuted March 26, hit 24 home runs, posted a .929 OPS, and made the AL All-Star Game as a 2026 rookie. You let Colson Montgomery develop -- he hit 20 home runs this season and launched a 432-foot shot off the Astros in the Wild Card. You let Miguel Vargas anchor the lineup. You sweep Houston in the Wild Card for the franchise's first playoff series win since 2005.

Game 1 is today at 1 PM ET at Progressive Field in Cleveland. The Guardians are a serious team that went 24-11 to close the regular season. This will not be easy.

But when you spent 2024 losing 121 games, nothing about today was ever going to be easy. It was going to be earned. And it was.

## Story 4: Braves Enter Dodger Stadium

**Long-Form Post:**
The Atlanta Braves beat the Philadelphia Phillies the hard way.

They won Game 1 on an Austin Riley three-run homer off Phillies closer Jhoan Duran in the eighth inning. They lost Game 2 in ten innings. Then they flew home and put together a statement Game 3 -- Michael Harris II hit a three-run shot off Aaron Nola in the first inning, Ozzie Albies went deep off Zack Wheeler in the fourth, and Matt Olson added a solo shot in the eighth in a 6-2 win.

In doing so, they snapped a six-game losing streak in winner-take-all games. That is not a small thing.

Now they're in Los Angeles for NLDS Game 1, today at 4 PM ET on Fox. Chris Sale -- who struck out nine Phillies in 6.1 innings in Wild Card Game 1 -- gets the ball against the Dodgers. His opponent is Shohei Ohtani making his first career playoff pitching start.

This is what October baseball is supposed to look like. Two teams, two great starters, one ballpark that is going to be absolutely on fire.

## Story 5: Jackson Merrill NLDS Debut

**Long-Form Post:**
Jackson Merrill has had the kind of 2026 season that makes people stop and say -- okay, this kid is for real.

The Padres' young outfielder has been one of the best stories in the National League all year, a Rookie of the Year candidate who plays the game with an energy that has electrified San Diego's fan base. He was part of a lineup that swept the Chicago Cubs 2-0 in the NL Wild Card, including a Game 2 win helped by Gavin Sheets' clutch pinch-hit two-run homer.

Tonight he makes his NLDS debut against the No. 1 seed Milwaukee Brewers at 8:30 PM ET at American Family Field in Milwaukee. The Brewers went 103-59 this season -- the best record in all of baseball. They had an extra week off while the Padres were grinding through the Wild Card.

The Padres won the regular-season series against the Brewers 4-2. Upset potential is real. But they'll need Merrill at his best, alongside Manny Machado, Fernando Tatis Jr., and closer Mason Miller, to get there.

Game 1 is tonight. Merrill's biggest moment is about to begin.
