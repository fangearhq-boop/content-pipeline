Field of Dreams Is Six Days Away — Netflix, Kevin Costner, and 25 Hall of Famers

By Marcus Cole

August 7, 2026

In six days, the Phillies and Twins will play baseball in a cornfield in Dyersville, Iowa, and Kevin Costner will narrate the opening of the broadcast.

The 2026 MLB at Field of Dreams Game is August 13, with a 7:30 PM ET first pitch. The game will stream live on Netflix — marking the first time the event has aired on the platform — with a pregame show beginning at 6:30 PM ET. For fans who have followed this event since its inaugural year, this is the biggest stage it has appeared on.

The 25+ Hall of Famers

The pregame ceremony is the part that has no equivalent anywhere else in baseball. More than 25 Baseball Hall of Famers are scheduled to be on the field in Dyersville on August 13. MLB is describing this as the largest gathering of living Hall of Famers at a single game since the 2008 All-Star Game at the original Yankee Stadium. That year's event marked the closing of that storied ballpark, and it drew a similarly historic assembly of legends to one place.

The Field of Dreams site does not have permanent seating capacity on the scale of a major league park, which makes the concentration of Hall of Famers — and the fans who will see them in that setting — something that cannot be replicated in a traditional stadium context. It is, by design, unlike other baseball experiences.

The Game Itself

The Minnesota Twins serve as the home team for this year's game. The Philadelphia Phillies provide the opponent. Both teams have trade deadline additions that will be in uniform for the first time in an event with this kind of national attention.

Kyle Schwarber, who has hit 33 home runs this season, is one of the Phillies' most recognizable power sources. The Twins' roster includes Luis Arraez, the three-time batting champion with a .329 career average who was acquired at the deadline, and Logan Raley, also added at the deadline.

The game is played on a field carved out of the Iowa cornfields — the same site from the film, the same setting that has given this event its singular identity since it began. The scenery doesn't change. The players and teams do.

Before the Main Event

Two days before the Phillies-Twins game, the St. Paul Saints — the Twins' Triple-A affiliate — will play the Iowa Cubs — the Chicago Cubs' Triple-A affiliate — at the Field of Dreams site on August 11. It's a minor league game at a major league setting, and it gives fans in the area a first look at the field before the August 13 main event.

What's Next

The 2026 MLB at Field of Dreams Game is August 13, 7:30 PM ET, on Netflix. The pregame show begins at 6:30 PM ET. The MiLB precursor game (Saints vs. Iowa Cubs) is August 11 at the same site. More information on ticketing and logistics is available at MLB.com.
