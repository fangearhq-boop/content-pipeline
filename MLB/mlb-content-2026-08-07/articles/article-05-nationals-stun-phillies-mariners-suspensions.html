Nationals Score 7 in Extra Innings to Stun Phillies 10-4; Mariners Suspend Manager and Reliever

By Jake Torres

August 7, 2026

Washington was down to Philadelphia heading into extra innings on Thursday. By the time the 11th inning ended, the Nationals had scored six runs to turn a tight game into a decisive 10-4 victory that sent the Phillies home on the wrong end of one of the bigger upsets of the week.

CJ Abrams hit a two-run triple in the 11th inning that cracked the game open against a Philadelphia relief corps that had been holding the score close. Washington's seven extra-inning runs in total — the bulk of them arriving in the 11th — made the final score look like a rout in a game that had been anything but for most of its duration.

The 11th Inning Collapse

Philadelphia came into the game chasing an NL playoff position, and leading after regulation would have been a useful result for a team in the middle of a pennant race. The Nationals' six-run 11th inning erased that possibility. Abrams's two-run triple was the key blow, giving Washington the lead and the momentum that the rest of the inning built upon.

The result adds to Washington's win column without materially changing the playoff picture for either team — the Nationals are not in contention, and the Phillies will shake off a single loss — but as a performance, the 11-inning explosion was the kind of game that Washington players will point to when talking about individual signature nights.

Mariners Suspensions

In Seattle, the Mariners are dealing with the organizational fallout from an August 5 incident. Reliever Gabe Speier was issued a three-game suspension after hitting Detroit's Gleyber Torres with a pitch in the eighth inning of that game. MLB determined the hit-by-pitch was intentional. Manager Dan Wilson received a one-game suspension related to the same incident.

Both Speier and Wilson are appealing their suspensions. Under the appeals process, Speier remains eligible to pitch while his appeal is pending; he will not serve the suspension until a final ruling is issued. Wilson's one-game suspension is similarly on hold. The appeals timeline is typically resolved within a week of the initial ruling.

What's Next

The Phillies return to NL playoff competition with their next series following Thursday's loss. The Nationals continue playing out the second half with this upset as a highlight. For the Mariners, Gabe Speier's availability while his appeal is pending means the bullpen situation is unchanged for the immediate future; the ruling will clarify once the appeal is decided.
