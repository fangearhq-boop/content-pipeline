### STORY 1: Braves 8 Straight + Olson 897 Consecutive Games

#### Long-Form Post — 8:00 AM ET

Matt Olson's consecutive games streak reached 897 on Thursday, and he did it in signature fashion: a 422-foot solo home run off John King in the sixth inning, his 32nd of the season, that kicked off a four-run frame and helped Atlanta beat Miami 11-3.

Olson also doubled and walked twice. He has been in the lineup for 897 consecutive games, a streak dating to May 2, 2021, when he was still with the Oakland Athletics. Yesterday, he passed Stan Musial (895) to take sole possession of eighth place on the all-time list. Thursday moved him to 897.

Ronald Acuña Jr. gave Atlanta two more reasons to smile. He hit home runs No. 10 and No. 11 of 2026, including one that found its way over the wall after bouncing off a Marlins outfielder's glove. A defensive collision turned into a Braves run. Acuña's evening added to what became an 11-run game.

The result completed a three-game sweep of Miami and pushed Atlanta's winning streak to eight — the longest of their 2026 season. The Braves are playing some of their best baseball of the year.

### STORY 2: Dodgers Losing Streak / Skubal Paradox

#### Long-Form Post — 10:30 AM ET

With Skubal, the Dodgers became the first team in Major League Baseball history to have a reigning two-time MVP and a reigning two-time Cy Young Award winner on the same roster at the same time. Shohei Ohtani, the two-time unanimous MVP, was already there. Skubal joined him.

The Dodgers then proceeded to lose six consecutive games.

They were swept in three games by Boston. They were swept in three games by Chicago. They head into Friday night's opener at Arizona — Roki Sasaki against Merrill Kelly — carrying the longest losing streak of their 2026 season. Their record sits at 69-45, and they still lead the NL West, but the offense has stalled. The team left eight runners on base in the Cubs series finale.

The most expensive, most decorated roster in baseball is in a skid. Tonight in Arizona is the chance to stop it.

### STORY 4: Field of Dreams 6 Days Away

#### Long-Form Post — 6:00 PM ET

In six days, the Phillies and Twins will play baseball in a cornfield. And for the first time, you'll watch it on Netflix.

The 2026 MLB at Field of Dreams Game is August 13 in Dyersville, Iowa, with a 7:30 PM ET first pitch. This year's broadcast is on Netflix — the first time the event has streamed nationally on the platform. Kevin Costner will provide voiceover to open the broadcast.

The pregame ceremony is something different: more than 25 Baseball Hall of Famers are scheduled to be on the field. MLB is calling it the largest gathering of living Hall of Famers at a single game since the 2008 All-Star Game at the original Yankee Stadium. That's an extraordinary number of legends in one place.

On the field, the matchup has star power. The Phillies bring Kyle Schwarber, who has 33 home runs. The Twins roster includes Luis Arraez, who arrived at the trade deadline as a .329 career hitter and 3-time batting champion, plus Logan Raley, also added at the deadline.

If you want a preview of the site itself: the St. Paul Saints (Twins' Triple-A affiliate) play the Iowa Cubs (Cubs' Triple-A affiliate) at Field of Dreams on August 11 — two days before the main event.
