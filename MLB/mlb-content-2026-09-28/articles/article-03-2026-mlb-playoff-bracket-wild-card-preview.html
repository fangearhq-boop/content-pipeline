2026 MLB Playoff Bracket Set — 12 Teams, Wild Card Starts Tomorrow, Here's Everything You Need to Know

By Ryan Calloway | September 28, 2026

The 2026 MLB regular season is over. The field is set. The postseason starts Tuesday.

Twelve teams, six in each league, survived 162 games to reach October. The Wild Card round is a best-of-three format — no margin for error, no days off, three games to prove you belong. The teams that advance meet in the Division Series. The teams that don't are done. Here is the full picture heading into Tuesday.

American League

The Tampa Bay Rays finished with the best record in the AL and earned the No. 1 seed. The Cleveland Guardians, who won their third consecutive AL Central title, are the No. 2 seed. The Houston Astros, who finished 81-81 to win the AL West in historic fashion, are the No. 3 seed. The top two seeds receive first-round byes.

The AL Wild Card round features the New York Yankees (No. 4), Boston Red Sox (No. 5), and Chicago White Sox (No. 6). The Yankees enter without Aaron Judge, who is dealing with a moderate-grade calf strain and is unlikely to play in this series. The White Sox are the most remarkable story: they made the playoffs after back-to-back 100-loss seasons, the first team in MLB history to do so. The Red Sox finished 87-75 and are the steadiest team in the field.

National League

The Milwaukee Brewers finished 103-59, the best record in baseball, and enter as the NL's No. 1 seed. The Los Angeles Dodgers (No. 2) and Atlanta Braves (No. 3) round out the division winners and receive first-round byes.

The NL Wild Card features the San Diego Padres (No. 4, 91-71), Chicago Cubs (No. 5, 89-73), and Philadelphia Phillies (No. 6). The Phillies clinched the final Wild Card spot on the last day of the regular season, beating Tampa Bay 7-3 to eliminate the Arizona Diamondbacks. It is Philadelphia's fifth consecutive postseason appearance.

The Storylines to Watch

Judge's injury is the dominant question mark heading into Tuesday. The Yankees made the playoffs as a team, but their offensive ceiling drops considerably without the most feared hitter in the AL. Boston can take advantage of that.

The Brewers, at 103-59, are the best team in baseball by record but have struggled in October before. Milwaukee fans know what it feels like to have the regular season mean something and the postseason produce disappointment. That narrative follows them into every first pitch.

The White Sox-Astros matchup, if it materializes in the ALDS, is historically bizarre: a team from back-to-back 100-loss seasons versus the first sub-.500 division champion. That game did not exist in the regular season. October has a way of creating matchups the calendar couldn't predict.

What's Next

The Wild Card Series begins Tuesday, September 29. Best-of-three, with the higher seed hosting all three games if necessary. Results will be fast: the series can end by Wednesday or extend to Thursday. Division Series follow immediately after. The 2026 World Series is scheduled for late October. It starts tomorrow.
