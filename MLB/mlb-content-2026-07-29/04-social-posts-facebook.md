### STORY 4: Schwarber HR Chase

**Long-Form Post:**
Is Kyle Schwarber about to make history?

The Philadelphia Phillies outfielder is MLB's home run leader in 2026, sitting at approximately 33 home runs through July 28. He led the NL — and all of baseball — with 56 home runs in 2025. He's already been named NL Player of the Month. And at 35 years old, ESPN is openly asking whether he can approach 60 home runs this season.

The 60 HR club is one of the most exclusive groups in baseball history. Roger Maris hit 61 in 1961. Mark McGwire hit 70 in 1998. Sammy Sosa crossed 60 three times. Barry Bonds hit 73. Aaron Judge hit 62 in 2022.

Schwarber won't be confused with the power of the steroid era. But his pure pull-side swing in a hitter-friendly era makes the math interesting. If he maintains his current pace — and that's a big if — the 60 HR threshold isn't impossible.

It's early to declare 60 a lock. But the conversation is real, Schwarber is the best HR hitter in baseball right now, and two months of baseball remain.
