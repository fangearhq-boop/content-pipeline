### Long-Form Post On Wednesday, Matt Olson did something that doesn't happen often: he passed a Hall of Famer on one of baseball's most revered lists. Olson played in his 896th consecutive game as the Atlanta Braves beat the Miami Marlins 4-1, moving past Stan Musial (895 consecutive games) into sole possession of 8th place on MLB's all-time consecutive games played list. The streak began May 2, 2021 — when Olson was still with the Oakland Athletics. Musial is one of the greatest hitters in baseball history. Passing him on any list means something. Bryce Elder added to the moment with seven innings of one-hit ball. Drake Baldwin drove in two runs.
