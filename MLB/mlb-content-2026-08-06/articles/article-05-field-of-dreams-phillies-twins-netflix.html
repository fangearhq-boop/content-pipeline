Field of Dreams Game Set for August 13 — Phillies vs. Twins Stream Live on Netflix

By Jake Torres | August 6, 2026

Seven days from now, two baseball teams will walk out of a cornfield in Dyersville, Iowa, and the 2026 Field of Dreams Game will begin. For the first time in the event's history, the world will be watching on Netflix.

The Philadelphia Phillies will face the Minnesota Twins on August 13 in one of baseball's most distinctive annual settings — a custom stadium carved into an Iowa cornfield, adjacent to the property made famous by the 1989 film. The game has become one of the regular season's signature moments, and this year's edition comes with storylines to match.

Schwarber's Home Run Chase

Kyle Schwarber enters the Field of Dreams Game with 33 home runs in 2026. That pace puts him on the short list of the National League's most compelling power hitters this season, and the game in Iowa gives him one of the most visually striking settings in the sport to add to the total.

Schwarber's power has defined the Phillies' offense through much of the season. A home run in Dyersville would disappear into a cornfield backdrop that no other ballpark in the world can replicate. That image alone is worth the watch.

Arraez Changes the Equation

The Phillies also added Luis Arraez at the trade deadline, and his presence changes the texture of the Philadelphia lineup significantly. Arraez has won three batting titles. His career average is .329. He is the rarest of modern hitters — a contact artist in an era that has largely abandoned the pursuit of contact for the pursuit of launch angle.

In a lineup that already features Schwarber's power, Arraez provides an entirely different kind of threat. The Phillies also added Logan Raley at the deadline, deepening a roster that was already competitive. Philadelphia heads to Iowa with a lineup that looks different — and arguably better — than it did a month ago.

Netflix Makes It Bigger

The game's broadcast is itself a news story. The 2026 Field of Dreams Game is the first to stream live on Netflix, bringing the event to a global audience that doesn't necessarily follow the traditional cable baseball broadcast. Whether that expands the reach of the game or simply shifts where existing fans watch it, the Netflix debut marks a notable moment in how MLB distributes its premium regular-season content.

For the Twins, this is an opportunity. Minnesota will play in one of baseball's most cinematic settings against a Philadelphia club that comes in carrying the weight of significant mid-season expectations. The Field of Dreams Game is, by design, a showcase — a reminder that the sport itself is the product, and the setting can make any game feel meaningful.

What's Next

The game is August 13. Seven days. The Phillies and Twins will spend the next week preparing for a moment that is equal parts baseball game and cultural event. For Schwarber, it's another chance to hit a home run in an unforgettable location. For Arraez, it's a first look at his new team on the biggest stage a midseason game can offer. For everyone watching on Netflix — at home or otherwise — it's a reminder of why baseball still captures the imagination in a way no other sport quite does.
