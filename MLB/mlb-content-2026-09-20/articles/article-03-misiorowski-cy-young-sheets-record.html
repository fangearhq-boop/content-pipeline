The Award Is His. Now Misiorowski Is Chasing History.

By Ryan Calloway | Ballpark Banter | September 20, 2026

Jacob Misiorowski's 2026 season is already one of the most dominant pitching performances in recent memory. With the NL Cy Young all but locked up, the 24-year-old Milwaukee Brewers right-hander is now targeting Ben Sheets' 2004 franchise strikeout record.

The Cy Young Award conversation in the National League effectively ended months ago. Jacob Misiorowski, the 24-year-old Milwaukee Brewers right-hander, has put together a season that belongs in a different category from the rest of the NL pitching field. His ERA sits at 1.89. His WHIP is 0.81. He leads MLB in strikeouts at 243. His award odds have settled at -700, with Chris Sale the only other pitcher drawing any serious mention at +500.

The award is coming to Milwaukee. The only question left — and it is a compelling one — is whether Misiorowski can reach a number that no Brewer has reached in 22 years.

The Sheets Record

Ben Sheets set the Milwaukee Brewers' single-season strikeout record in 2004 with 264 punchouts in what was one of the best individual pitching seasons in franchise history. That number has stood for more than two decades, surviving Hall of Famers who passed through Milwaukee and ace-caliber seasons from multiple generations of Brewers starters.

Misiorowski has 243 strikeouts. He needs 21 more to break Sheets' mark. With seven games remaining in the regular season and the Brewers already having clinched their fourth consecutive NL Central title, the framing of these final starts has shifted entirely to the individual chase.

A Season That Belongs in the Record Books

Even without the Sheets record, Misiorowski's 2026 season has already carved its own line in the history books. He became the first pitcher in the Statcast tracking era — which dates to 2015 — to throw 1,000 pitches at 100 miles per hour or faster in a single season. That combination of velocity volume and sustained dominance over a full year is something the sport has never measured before in anyone else.

At 24, Misiorowski is doing this in just his second full major league season. The Brewers drafted him in the first round of the 2022 draft and accelerated his development through a system built to handle high-upside arms. What Milwaukee got back is something beyond what projections typically generate: a legitimate ace in the middle of a run of NL Central titles, putting up numbers that would be remarkable from a pitcher of any age.

What's Next

The Brewers have seven games left. The math on the Sheets record is straightforward: 21 strikeouts in seven starts, roughly three per outing, is well within range for a pitcher averaging more than a strikeout per inning this season. Whether Misiorowski reaches it depends on innings pitched in games that no longer have playoff implications for Milwaukee, which means the Brewers staff will need to make deliberate decisions about how to deploy their ace down the stretch.

If the Sheets record falls, Misiorowski ends the year with the franchise strikeout mark, a historic velocity milestone, and a Cy Young Award — all before his 25th birthday. That is a resume section, not just a season.
