```

The current collective bargaining agreement expires December 1, 2026. Owners are pushing for a hard salary cap set at $245.3 million. The union has rejected any form of hard cap and opened negotiations near a $300 million luxury tax threshold. That gap — and the philosophical difference underneath it — is enormous.

Commissioner Rob Manfred has said that an offseason lockout is likely to become the norm. The MLBPA is reportedly prepared for one. Prediction markets put the odds of a deal by the deadline at around 5%.

What does a lockout mean? Free agency freezes. Teams can't sign, trade, or negotiate. If it stretches deep enough into winter, spring training and the 2027 season itself could be threatened.

Owners and players have 32 days to close a deal. History says don't count on it.
```
