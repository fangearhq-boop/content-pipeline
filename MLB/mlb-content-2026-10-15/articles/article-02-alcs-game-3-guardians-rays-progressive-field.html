ALCS Game 3: Guardians Host Rays at Progressive Field, Series Tied 1-1

By Marcus Cole | October 15, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

The ALCS moves to Cleveland, and it moves earlier than expected.

ALCS Game 3 between the Cleveland Guardians and Tampa Bay Rays has been moved from its originally scheduled 6 PM ET start to 1 PM ET due to rain in the forecast at Progressive Field. The game airs on TBS. The Guardians and Rays enter tied 1-1 — Cleveland won Game 1 in a 7-3 comeback at Tropicana Field, and Tampa Bay evened the series with a 3-1 Game 2 victory behind Freddy Peralta's six scoreless innings.

Whoever wins today takes a 2-1 series lead — an advantage that has historically translated into series wins at a high rate in LCS play.

The Home Field Factor

Progressive Field gives Cleveland a tangible advantage today. The Guardians have been one of the best home teams in the AL all season, and their fan base does not treat October games like regular-season dates. The crowd at Progressive Field when the weather clears and the game begins will be among the loudest atmospheres in baseball.

Cleveland also won Game 1 on the road, coming back to beat the Rays 7-3 at Tropicana Field. That road victory demonstrated that this team is not rattled by travel or atmosphere. But the edge — particularly in a tight, well-matched series — belongs to the home team today.

Gavin Williams Takes the Ball

Gavin Williams starts for Cleveland in what is his most important appearance of the postseason so far. Williams pitched brilliantly in the ALDS Game 5 clincher against the Chicago White Sox, helping the Guardians complete a stunning 0-2 series comeback. He enters Game 3 as Cleveland's most proven October starter — and he is being called on for a must-deliver performance.

Williams' ability to get deep into the game and limit Tampa Bay's offense is the key variable for the Guardians. Cleveland's bullpen is solid, but starting pitching depth is thinner after the rotation has already been pushed through the ALDS. Williams needs innings.

Griffin Jax and the Rays' Gamble

Tampa Bay counters with Griffin Jax, the right-hander acquired from the Minnesota Twins at the trade deadline. Jax was brought in for exactly this kind of moment — a high-leverage postseason start where the Rays need a starter who can keep a lineup like Cleveland's off the board long enough for their bullpen to close.

The question around Jax is experience under October pressure. Starting in a playoff game on the road, against a lineup that includes Jose Ramirez and a deep Guardians offense, is a different challenge than anything he faced during the regular season. How Jax handles that first inning — and how he responds if Cleveland scores early — will define Tampa Bay's afternoon.

What's Next

ALCS Game 3 is today at 1 PM ET at Progressive Field in Cleveland. TBS has the broadcast. ALCS Game 4 will be scheduled for tomorrow (Oct. 16) depending on today's result. The winner of today's game is in the driver's seat for the rest of the series.
