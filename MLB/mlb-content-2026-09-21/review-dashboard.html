### Long-Form Post Three stolen bases. Six games. That's the entire equation standing between Pete Crow-Armstrong and one of the rarest achievements in baseball history. PCA enters the final week of the regular season with 47 home runs and 37 stolen bases — putting the 40-40 club squarely in his sights. Only six players have ever done it: Jose Canseco (1988), Barry Bonds (1996), Alex Rodriguez (1998), Alfonso Soriano (2006), Ronald Acuña Jr. (2023), and Shohei Ohtani (2024). PCA would become No. 7 — and the first Cub in franchise history to accomplish the feat. He already rewrote the record books this season by breaking Billy Williams' left-handed Cubs home run record of 42, set back in 1970. Now he's chasing something even bigger. The Cubs sit at 87-69 with a magic number of 1 to clinch their postseason spot. PCA could be stealing his way into history in the middle of a Wild Card clinching celebration. The stars are aligning. Three steals. Six games. Don't look away. ### Image Post Caption Pete Crow-Armstrong is three stolen bases away from joining the most exclusive club in baseball. 47 HR. 37 SB. Six games left. #Cubs

### Long-Form Post The math has officially gotten too hard. Yordan Alvarez entered the final week of the regular season trailing in two of three Triple Crown categories — and his September batting average has been ice cold at .206. The window isn't just closing. It's nearly shut. Alvarez leads the AL with a .310 batting average (Chandler Simpson of the Rays is five points back at .305).
