Misiorowski's Cy Young Case: 243 Strikeouts and 21 Away From Brewers History

By Jake Torres | Ballpark Banter | September 21, 2026

Jacob Misiorowski is 24 years old and is having one of the most dominant pitching seasons in recent memory. That is not hyperbole. It is simply what the numbers show.

The Brewers ace leads all of baseball with a 1.89 ERA, 243 strikeouts, and a 0.81 WHIP. He is the -700 favorite in NL Cy Young Award odds. His nearest challenger, Chris Sale, sits at +500 — which, in betting terms, means Misiorowski is essentially a lock. The award is a formality at this point. What is not a formality is what comes next.

Misiorowski needs 21 more strikeouts to break Ben Sheets' Brewers franchise record of 264 Ks, set in 2004.

A Season of Firsts

The strikeout record isn't the only line of history Misiorowski is chasing. On September 8, he reached 1,000 pitches thrown at 100 miles per hour or faster in a single season — a milestone no pitcher in the tracking era had ever reached. The sheer volume of elite velocity he has maintained across a full starting workload puts him in a category by himself.

Sale, Wheeler, Sanchez, and Luzardo are among those in the Cy Young conversation. None of them are seriously close. Misiorowski's ERA is nearly a run lower than the next-best qualifier. His strikeout total is the highest in baseball. His WHIP is historically low.

The Brewers, who have clinched their fourth consecutive NL Central title, gave Misiorowski the opportunity, and he has made the most of it in dominant fashion.

The Sheets Record and What It Means

Ben Sheets was one of the most gifted pitchers of his generation — a power arm who could have been a perennial Cy Young contender if injuries had not derailed his career. His 264-strikeout season in 2004 was remarkable: a franchise record that has stood for 22 years and survived every arm that Milwaukee has sent to the mound since.

Misiorowski reaching it would mean more than a statistical milestone. It would cement his 2026 season as the standard by which all future Brewers pitching seasons are measured.

With the regular season winding down, Misiorowski likely has one or two starts remaining. If he averages even 10 strikeouts per outing, the record falls.

What's Next

Watch Misiorowski's next start closely. If he logs double-digit strikeouts, the Sheets record comes within arm's reach heading into the final days of the season. With the Brewers already in — and playing for seeding and momentum ahead of the NL Central title defense — his next outing could be a franchise-record night.

The Cy Young is coming. The record could come with it.
