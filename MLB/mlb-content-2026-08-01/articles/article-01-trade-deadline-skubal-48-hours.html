ESPN: Tarik Skubal Is Getting Traded — 48 Hours to Go

By Marcus Cole

August 1, 2026

The MLB Trade Deadline arrives Sunday at 6 PM ET — and the biggest name still on the board has apparently been decided. ESPN is reporting that Detroit Tigers ace Tarik Skubal is going to be traded before the deadline, ending weeks of speculation and making the next 48 hours a race to see which contender wins the bidding.

Skubal, the two-time AL Cy Young Award winner, enters the deadline with numbers that make him arguably the most coveted arm on the market. He's posted a 2.79 ERA and 0.91 WHIP in 96.2 innings across 16 starts, with 116 strikeouts. Detroit has already begun assembling the outgoing package — the Tigers scratched starter Casey Mize from Friday's scheduled start, a signal that Mize could be part of a deal.

The Suitors

Five teams have emerged as the leading contenders for Skubal: the Los Angeles Dodgers, New York Yankees, Atlanta Braves, Chicago Cubs, and Philadelphia Phillies. Each brings a different motivation.

The Dodgers are perhaps the most desperate. Shohei Ohtani has not pitched since July 3, managing both left knee soreness and a biceps issue, and the club cannot enter October counting on him as a rotation piece. Skubal would give them an ace-level guarantee. The Yankees need rotation depth for a postseason push. The Braves have Chris Sale anchoring one of the best rotations in baseball and want to make a statement buy. The Cubs and Phillies both believe they are realistic World Series contenders and see Skubal as the arm that could push them over the top.

Detroit's leverage is real. When a two-time Cy Young winner with a sub-3.00 ERA hits the market 48 hours before the deadline, the Tigers do not have to settle for a modest return.

The Rest of the Market

Skubal is not the only notable name in play. Freddy Peralta — the Milwaukee Brewers right-hander now with the New York Mets, posting a 4.99 ERA in 2026 — was scratched from his Friday start as trade discussions around him "intensified." Padres closer Mason Miller, who has converted all 28 of his save opportunities this season while posting a 0.79 ERA and 0.79 WHIP in 45.2 innings, is generating significant interest for teams looking to upgrade their ninth inning.

The market has already moved on several fronts heading into the final stretch. Completed deals announced ahead of Sunday's deadline include: A.J. Minter to the Minnesota Twins, Logan O'Hoppe and Chase Silseth to the Texas Rangers, Jose Urquidy to the Chicago White Sox, Nate Pearson to the Kansas City Royals, Ben Halvorsen to the Los Angeles Dodgers, Austin Civale to the Chicago Cubs, and Ron Marinaccio to the Pittsburgh Pirates.

What the Deadline Means for October

Trade deadlines rarely feel as consequential as they end up being — but the 2026 version looks like an exception. Several of the teams in the Skubal sweepstakes are legitimate World Series contenders, and landing — or failing to land — Skubal could define how their October looks. The Dodgers in particular are in an unusual position: defending champions, one of the best offenses in baseball, but with a rotation that now has a significant question mark where Ohtani used to sit.

For Detroit, this is about maximizing the return on a franchise-caliber asset. Even if Skubal pitches for a contender from August through October, the Tigers are building for the future, and the package they receive will shape their next competitive window.

What's Next

The deadline clock runs out Sunday, August 3 at 6 PM ET. Expect a flurry of announcements in the 48 hours between now and then. The Skubal destination will be the headline, but do not overlook the Peralta and Miller situations — both could move this weekend and reshape postseason bullpen and rotation situations significantly.
