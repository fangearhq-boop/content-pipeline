ALCS Preview: Rays Await Winner of White Sox-Guardians Game 5

By Marcus Cole | October 9, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

The Tampa Bay Rays are in the American League Championship Series for the first time since 2020, and they are sitting at Tropicana Field waiting to find out who joins them. The answer comes Saturday night in Cleveland, where the White Sox and Guardians play a winner-take-all Game 5 at 8 PM ET on TBS, with the series tied 2-2.

The ALCS opens Monday, October 12 at 8 PM ET at Tampa Bay, also on TBS. The Rays swept their way to the LCS, eliminating the New York Yankees three games to none in the ALDS. The ALCS can run through October 20, meaning both the LCS rounds would finish before the World Series begins.

What Rays Fans Know

Tampa Bay got there by doing something they have never done before in franchise history: sweeping a best-of-five postseason series. The Yankees, who had been swept in a best-of-five only once before (the 1980 ALCS), could not find an answer for the Rays' pitching. Victor Mesa Jr. provided the biggest swing of the series, a two-run homer in the sixth inning of Game 3 that gave Tampa Bay a 4-2 lead en route to a 4-3 clinching victory.

It is a franchise that has consistently found ways to compete despite payroll constraints, and reaching the ALCS while the Yankees — one of baseball's biggest spenders — head home early is exactly the kind of October Rays fans have come to appreciate.

Who Will Face Them?

On one side is the Chicago White Sox, a team that came into October as a genuine surprise story. The White Sox won 81 games this year after three consecutive 100-loss seasons, a number that set a new MLB record for most wins by a team coming off three straight 100-loss campaigns. They came out of the Division Series taking Games 1 and 2, but Cleveland has won the last two.

On the other side is Cleveland, where José Ramírez remains one of the most clutch players in the American League. Ramírez homered and drove in three runs in Game 4, delivering the type of performance that has defined his October career. Cleveland has the crowd, the experience, and arguably the best individual player in the series. Game 5 is the rare situation where every swing means everything.

What's Next

Game 5 is Saturday, October 10 at 8 PM ET at Progressive Field in Cleveland on TBS, truTV, and HBO Max. ALCS Game 1 follows Monday, October 12 at Tropicana Field, 8 PM ET on TBS. The Rays will be ready. The question is who shows up to face them.
