NLCS Preview: Dodgers vs. Brewers — Can LA Win Its Third Straight World Series?

By Ryan Calloway | October 9, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

It is the matchup the National League built toward all season long, and it is exactly the rematch Milwaukee wanted: the Brewers, owners of a franchise-record 103 wins and the NL's best record, hosting the Los Angeles Dodgers, who eliminated Milwaukee in the 2025 NLCS and have now won back-to-back World Series titles. Game 1 is Sunday, October 11 at 8 PM ET at American Family Field, airing on FOX.

The Dodgers came through the Division Series in four games against Atlanta, finishing 3-1. Andy Pages delivered the winning two-run single in the seventh inning of Game 4 as Los Angeles won 4-1 at Truist Park to eliminate the Braves. Max Muncy added a ninth-inning solo home run to close it out. Milwaukee, meanwhile, took care of San Diego in four games as well, with Garrett Mitchell's crucial catch in the eighth inning of Game 4 preserving a 3-1 win.

The Stakes for Los Angeles

What the Dodgers are chasing carries historical weight. No team has won three consecutive World Series championships since the Oakland Athletics won three straight from 1972 to 1974. Los Angeles won the title in 2024 and repeated in 2025. A third straight would place them in company rarely seen in the modern game, and with Freddie Freeman, Teoscar Hernández, Max Muncy, and a pitching staff that has shown the capacity to close series out, they have the pieces to get there.

The Dodgers advanced by beating Atlanta 3-1, with Tarik Skubal pitching six strong innings in Game 1 and the offense delivering key hits when needed. One issue worth noting: Los Angeles had just one hit with runners in scoring position through the first three games of the NLDS against Atlanta before Pages came through in the clincher. They will need more of that in October.

The Brewers' Franchise-Best Case

Milwaukee does not rely on the home run — only 32.6 percent of its runs scored via the long ball this season, the lowest percentage in the majors and well below the MLB average of 40.6 percent. The Brewers find ways to manufacture runs, and they do it relentlessly. Jackson Chourio hit a two-run single off Mason Miller to win Game 2 of the NLDS in the ninth inning. Garrett Mitchell made a wall-saving catch in the eighth inning of the clincher. This team does the things that win playoff series.

Milwaukee also has the home-field advantage, starting the series in front of what promises to be one of the loudest crowds American Family Field has ever seen. The Brewers swept last year's NLCS one year later (this is the rematch from that Dodgers sweep of Milwaukee in 2025). The motivation is real and the roster is the best in franchise history.

What's Next

Game 1 is Sunday, October 11 at 8 PM ET on FOX at American Family Field. Game 2 follows Monday, October 12 at 5 PM ET. If this goes the distance, Game 7 would be October 19. The NLCS winner will advance to the World Series against the ALCS winner. Given what both teams showed in the Division Series, this series has the makings of a classic.
