Ramírez Homers, Guardians Force ALDS Game 5 with 9-5 Win Over White Sox

By Ryan Calloway | October 9, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

Once again, José Ramírez was the story in Cleveland. The Guardians' franchise cornerstone hit a home run, drove in three runs, and helped power Cleveland to a 9-5 victory over the Chicago White Sox in Game 4 of the ALDS on Thursday night. The series, which Chicago led 2-0 just 48 hours ago, is now tied at two games apiece — and headed to a winner-take-all Game 5.

Game 5 is Saturday, October 10 at 8 PM ET at Progressive Field in Cleveland. It airs on TBS, truTV, and streams on HBO Max. The winner faces the Tampa Bay Rays in the American League Championship Series beginning Monday.

From 2-0 to All Even

The White Sox came into this series with tremendous momentum. They won the first two games, putting Chicago on the doorstep of its first ALCS appearance in years. Cleveland responded with back-to-back victories, outscoring the White Sox 18-8 in Games 3 and 4 combined. The Guardians' offensive output has been the story of this reversal — Ramírez leading the charge, the home crowd responding with increasing intensity.

For a team that had built itself around Ramírez's relentless production and Cleveland's ability to manufacture runs, getting to a Game 5 at Progressive Field is exactly what the organization planned for. The home team will have the full advantage of a roaring crowd behind it on Saturday night.

What the White Sox Are Facing

The White Sox, who won 81 games this year after three consecutive 100-loss seasons, have already exceeded expectations in reaching this point. Winning Games 1 and 2 was remarkable. The question now is whether a team that has been rebuilding for several years has what it takes to close out a Game 5 on the road in the most hostile possible environment.

If the White Sox advance, they would be one of the more improbable ALCS participants in recent memory — a team that lost at least 100 games in 2023, 2024, and 2025 suddenly in the final four of the American League. That is a story worth watching.

What's Next

Everything is on the line Saturday night. Game 5 at Progressive Field, 8 PM ET on TBS. The ALCS starts 48 hours after Game 5 ends. There is no margin for error, no off day, no room for anything but the best performance of the season. For both teams, Game 5 is the season.
