### STORY 5: 2026 Season's Wildest Stats

**Long-Form Post:**
Before the League Championship Series begins, let's take a moment to appreciate just how strange the 2026 MLB regular season was.

The Houston Astros became the first team in major league history to make the playoffs with an even .500 record in a full 162-game season, finishing 81-81 and winning the AL West. The Chicago White Sox won 81 games — which might not sound impressive until you consider that they lost at least 100 games in each of the three previous seasons. That 81-win total is the most ever by a team coming off three straight 100-loss campaigns.

Oh, and 16 players homered in their MLB debut this season — a new record by three. The Washington Nationals set a record with 42 blown saves. And Nationals second baseman Nasim Nuñez led baseball with 47 steals despite a .529 OPS, which is basically impossible.

Baseball never stops being weird. And that's why we love it.
