Jacob Misiorowski Is Having One of the Best Pitching Seasons in Years — and Not Enough People Are Watching

By Ryan Calloway

August 14, 2026

The 2026 NL Cy Young Award is not a debate. Jacob Misiorowski, the 24-year-old Milwaukee Brewers right-hander, is leading all of Major League Baseball in ERA, strikeouts, and WHIP simultaneously. His season numbers — 1.76 ERA, 204 strikeouts, 0.73 WHIP across 133 innings and 22 starts — represent one of the most dominant individual pitching campaigns in recent memory.

His NL Cy Young odds sit at -550. He has not had a serious challenger all season. And yet, because the Milwaukee Brewers are not in a meaningful playoff race, the national conversation has not caught up to what Misiorowski is actually doing on the mound every five days.

Second-Fastest to 200 Strikeouts in MLB History

When Misiorowski reached 200 strikeouts earlier this week, he became the second-fastest pitcher in MLB history to reach that mark in a single season. The only pitcher to arrive there faster was Spencer Strider, who struck out 200 batters in 123.1 innings in 2022. Misiorowski needed 129.1 innings to get there — just slightly more than Strider, in a category defined by the very best individual strikeout seasons the sport has produced.

For context: he has now struck out 22 batters in his last two starts alone. That is not a rate that runs in the background. That is a pitcher throwing at a level that demands attention.

The Numbers in Full

The league leaders in ERA, strikeouts, and WHIP at the same time through a full season is extraordinarily rare. To lead all three simultaneously at this stage of August is the kind of seasonal dominance that defines a pitcher's legacy — a Sandy Koufax summer, a Pedro Martinez peak, a Jacob deGrom stretch. Misiorowski's 2026 belongs in that conversation.

His 1.76 ERA is more than a run better than the second-best ERA in the NL. His 0.73 WHIP means he is allowing fewer than one baserunner per inning pitched — remarkable for any stretch, let alone for an entire 133-inning season. And his 204 strikeouts through 22 starts projects to more than 250 for the full season if the pace holds.

The Brewers Context

The Milwaukee Brewers are not a playoff team in 2026. That is the primary reason Misiorowski's season has not penetrated the national sports conversation the way it deserves. The best pitching performances in history are amplified by the playoff stage — Koufax had Dodger World Series runs, deGrom had Mets postseason urgency, Verlander had October thunder. Misiorowski is pitching for a team going home in September.

That context does not diminish the performance. The ERA is real. The strikeouts are real. The WHIP is real. The Cy Young Award is voted on by writers across the league, and the numbers Misiorowski is putting up cannot be ignored regardless of the standings. He is the best pitcher in the National League in 2026. The ballot will reflect that.
