Field of Dreams Game 2026: What to Know Before Thursday's Twins-Phillies Game on Netflix

By Ryan Calloway

August 9, 2026

Four days from now, a baseball game will be played in a cornfield in Iowa that has nothing to do with competitive standing and everything to do with what makes baseball worth watching in the first place.

The 2026 Field of Dreams Game is Thursday, August 13, at the historic site in Dyersville, Iowa. The Minnesota Twins host the Philadelphia Phillies. First pitch is 7:30 PM ET, live on Netflix. It is the first Field of Dreams Game since 2022, when the site underwent a major renovation and expansion.

The Game

The 8,000-seat ballpark features 335/380/400 dimensions and the signature right-field wall that opens to reveal a cornfield pathway — and beyond it, 159 acres of the original farmland from the 1989 film. Ticketholders get complimentary parking; gates open at 3:30 PM ET. Expect significant traffic delays on the two-lane road leading to the site.

On the field, the Twins will send Byron Buxton, one of the most exciting center fielders in the game, against a Phillies lineup that features Bryce Harper and Kyle Schwarber — who enters Thursday with 33 home runs and one of the most tracked slugging seasons in baseball. This is a quality matchup behind the spectacle.

The Ceremony

The pre-game ceremony is scheduled for 6:30 PM ET and will feature more than 25 Hall of Famers — the largest gathering of Hall of Famers at a Major League event since the 2008 All-Star Game.

On the Phillies side: Mike Schmidt, Steve Carlton, and Scott Rolen. On the Twins side: Joe Mauer, Rod Carew, Bert Blyleven, and Paul Molitor. Jim Thome and Jim Kaat, who played for both franchises, will represent both clubs. Kevin Costner — who played Ray Kinsella in the original 1989 film — will appear during the Netflix broadcast.

Why It Still Works

There have been three Field of Dreams Games now, and the concept hasn't worn out. Part of it is the setting — there is nothing else in professional sports that looks like this. Part of it is the ceremony. And part of it is that the film holds up. Forty years later, "Is this heaven?" still lands.

Netflix broadcasting the game for the first time adds a new distribution channel to one of baseball's most distinctive events. The combination of the Hall of Fame ceremony, Costner's presence, and a genuine competitive matchup between two of the National League's marquee franchises makes this year's game worth clearing your Thursday evening for.

What's Next

The Field of Dreams Game is Thursday, August 13, at 7:30 PM ET on Netflix. Gates open at 3:30 PM ET. The pre-game ceremony begins at 6:30 PM ET. If you don't have a ticket in Dyersville, the game is streaming live on Netflix with no additional charge for subscribers.
