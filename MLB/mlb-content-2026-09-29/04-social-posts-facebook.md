The Pirates had not finished with a winning record since 2018 — seven consecutive losing seasons that stretched across front-office changes, rebuilds, and the kind of slow frustration that tests even the most loyal fan bases. From 2019 through 2025, they lost more than they won every single year.

Not in 2026. Pittsburgh went 15-9 in September, pushing themselves over .500 with three weeks of strong closing baseball. The final win against Detroit sealed the milestone. They finished third in the NL Central, behind the Milwaukee Brewers and well short of a Wild Card spot, but 82 wins is 82 wins — and it ends an era in Pittsburgh that dragged on far too long.

The 2027 season begins with something the Pirates haven't had in eight years: positive momentum. That's worth noting. For Pittsburgh fans — tonight, this matters.
