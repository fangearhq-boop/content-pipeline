PCA Homers in Sunday Loss, but His NL MVP Case Enters September Stronger Than Ever

By Ryan Calloway

August 31, 2026

The Cubs lost Sunday night. Pete Crow-Armstrong homered anyway.

Cincinnati won 7-5 in what turned out to be a competitive Sunday Night Baseball game. PCA led off the Cubs' scoring with a solo shot — the kind of automatic contribution that has become his signature. The loss dropped Chicago to 77-60, but it did nothing to shake PCA's standing in the National League MVP race as the calendar turns to September.

His 2026 numbers entering the final 29 games: .282 batting average, 36 home runs, 89 RBI, 97 runs scored. His NL MVP odds sit at -1100 — a number that reflects where the market stands, and where it has stood for most of the second half.

What the Numbers Say

The case for PCA is not built on one statistical category. It is built on every category simultaneously. The .282 average is well above league average. The 36 home runs place him among the NL's elite sluggers. The 89 RBI reflect how often he delivers with runners on base. And the 97 runs scored reflect how often his presence in the lineup produces offense even when he is not the one driving it in.

The combination — consistent contact, power, run production, and run scoring — is what makes PCA's candidacy unusual. NL MVP races typically favor one dominant statistical area. PCA's argument covers all of them.

The Only Real Wildcard

The one name that could shift the conversation is Shohei Ohtani, whose anticipated return to the pitching mound later this season would add a two-way dimension to his own candidacy that no other player in the league can match. A healthy, pitching Ohtani could change the calculus in a way that pure offensive production cannot address.

For now, however, the race belongs to PCA. The Cubs are tied with the Phillies for the top two NL Wild Card seeds, and Chicago's playoff positioning — combined with PCA's September production — will be the defining story of the final month of the regular season.

What's Next

The Cubs play 29 more games before the end of the regular season. PCA will be at the center of every meaningful moment — and the MVP ballot will be filled out when those games are done. The biggest immediate test: Wednesday's matchup at Wrigley Field against Jacob Misiorowski, the NL Cy Young frontrunner. How PCA performs against the best pitcher in baseball will be one of the month's defining data points.
