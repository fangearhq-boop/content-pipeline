Jackson Chourio Reaches 60 Career Home Runs at 22 — One of the Youngest Ever

By Marcus Cole

August 31, 2026

Jackson Chourio hit his 60th career home run. He is still 22 years old.

The Milwaukee Brewers outfielder, born March 11, 2004, reached the milestone when he hit his 20th home run of the 2026 season — joining an exclusive list of players who have posted three consecutive 20-homer seasons to open their Major League Baseball careers. He will not turn 23 until next March. The 60-HR mark before age 23 places him among the youngest players in MLB history to reach that threshold.

Three Seasons, Three 20-HR Campaigns

The consistency across Chourio's first three big-league seasons is the most remarkable part of the milestone. Most players spend years building toward a first 20-HR season. Chourio has done it in each of his first three years in the league, making him one of the few players in modern baseball to establish that kind of power production from the very beginning of a career.

His career trajectory — entering pro ball and immediately producing at a sustained level — is what separates the milestone from a statistical curiosity. Sixty home runs before age 23 is not a single great year followed by plateau. It is a pattern.

Career Moments Along the Way

Chourio has produced five career multi-home run games, three of them coming in the 2026 season alone. His most recent multi-HR performance came August 27 against the New York Mets — a 4-for-5 effort with 2 home runs, 4 RBI, and 4 runs scored.

The Brewers outfielder is not just a power hitter in the traditional sense. He is a complete offensive player whose home run production has been the most visible indicator of a broader developmental arc. The 60-HR milestone is the latest signpost on that road.

What's Next

Jackson Chourio will turn 23 on March 11, 2027. Between now and then, he will play the final 29 games of the 2026 regular season plus whatever postseason run the Brewers make. He enters that stretch as a 22-year-old with 60 career home runs and three consecutive 20-HR seasons — a profile that, at this age, almost no player in baseball history has matched. The Brewers are heading to the playoffs. Chourio is heading to an October stage for which his young career has been an extended preparation.
