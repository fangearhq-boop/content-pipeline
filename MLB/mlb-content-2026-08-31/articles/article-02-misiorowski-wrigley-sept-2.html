Misiorowski Takes His Cy Young Case to Wrigley on Wednesday — and a Velocity Watch Comes With Him

By Marcus Cole

August 31, 2026

Jacob Misiorowski starts Wednesday at Wrigley Field. The matchup — the NL Cy Young frontrunner against the lineup anchored by the NL MVP frontrunner — is the most anticipated pitching appearance of the second half of the season.

The Brewers right-hander enters with a 14-5 record, 1.73 ERA, 222 strikeouts, and a 0.75 WHIP. His Cy Young odds have surged to -1400. By most measurable standards, no pitcher in baseball has been more dominant in 2026. Opponents are hitting .151 against him — the lowest batting average against any pitcher in the sport. His 37.8% whiff rate ranks in the 98th percentile of all pitchers.

The Sept. 2 start sets the tone for October. What the Brewers learn about their ace between now and the end of the regular season will shape how they use him in the postseason.

The Velocity Watch

The subplot entering Wednesday: Misiorowski's average fastball velocity has dropped from 100.5 mph earlier in the season to 98.7 mph in recent outings. The numbers are still elite by any standard. But the dip has drawn attention from both the Brewers' front office and the analysts tracking his pitch data, raising questions about workload management as one of baseball's most valuable arms approaches the stretch run.

At his peak, Misiorowski threw the fastest pitch recorded by a starting pitcher in the pitch-tracking era — 103.7 mph. The gap between that ceiling and his current average velocity is a meaningful data point. It could reflect normal within-season variation. It could reflect accumulated fatigue. The Sept. 2 start at Wrigley will be one of the most closely watched outings of his season.

Wrigley as the Stage

Facing the Cubs on the road, in a nationally prominent stadium, against the NL MVP frontrunner's lineup — this is the environment where postseason reputations are made. The Brewers have the best record in baseball and are building toward a deep playoff run. How Misiorowski performs in the Sept. 2 start against Chicago's lineup will tell both the Brewers and the baseball world exactly where their ace stands heading into October.

What's Next

Jacob Misiorowski starts Wednesday, September 2 at Wrigley Field against the Chicago Cubs. The game will be one of the most-watched regular-season starts of the 2026 season. Beyond the individual matchup, the Brewers' next decisions around his workload — whether to monitor his pitch count more carefully, skip a start, or stay the course — will define the narrative around the most dominant starting pitcher in the National League for the remainder of September.
