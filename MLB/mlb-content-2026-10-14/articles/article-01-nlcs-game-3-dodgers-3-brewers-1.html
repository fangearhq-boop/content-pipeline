NLCS Game 3 Recap: Snell Dominates, Dodgers Win 3-1 to Breathe Life Into Series

By Marcus Cole | October 14, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

The Dodgers still trail. But Wednesday night at Dodger Stadium, they proved they are not done.

Blake Snell delivered exactly the kind of start Los Angeles needed in a must-win NLCS Game 3, leading the Dodgers to a 3-1 win over the Milwaukee Brewers. The victory trims the Brewers' series lead to 2-1 and shifts the momentum — however fragile — back toward a Dodger Stadium lineup that still has four games it can win at home if needed.

Snell Meets the Moment

Since returning from elbow surgery, Snell had been one of the best pitchers in the sport — dominant across eight starts, posting a 1.13 ERA that made him impossible to discount as a postseason weapon. But the NLCS is where that résumé gets tested against the weight of elimination, and on Wednesday, Snell held up.

The Dodgers' bullpen came in and used four relievers to close out the final 10 outs, handling the late innings in the clean, shutdown manner that has been a Los Angeles hallmark all season. Milwaukee had gotten far enough into a Game 2 bullpen against the Dodgers to retire 15 consecutive batters. On Wednesday night, the bullpen dynamic ran in the other direction.

The 3-1 final means the Dodgers escaped with exactly what they needed: a game. Nothing more, nothing less.

The Chourio Factor

Milwaukee outfielder Jackson Chourio exited Game 3 with an injury, a development that could have meaningful consequences for the remaining games. Chourio is one of the Brewers' most dynamic offensive players, and his status for Game 4 is a story to monitor. A healthy Chourio is a very different equation for the Dodgers' pitching staff than an absent or limited one.

The Brewers still lead the series 2-1. They came to Los Angeles needing to win one of three games to advance to the World Series. They still need one win. But the injury concern, combined with a loss, makes Thursday's Game 4 a more complicated situation than Milwaukee walked into Wednesday.

What's Next

NLCS Game 4 is Thursday night at Dodger Stadium, 9 PM ET on FS1. Tyler Glasnow is expected to start for Los Angeles. The Brewers will counter with their own starter in what could be a series-defining game.

If the Dodgers win Game 4, the series is tied 2-2 heading back to Milwaukee. If the Brewers win, they are one game from the World Series. The margin is thin, the stakes are high, and the stakes get higher again Thursday night.

Snell gave the Dodgers Wednesday. Glasnow has to give them Thursday.
