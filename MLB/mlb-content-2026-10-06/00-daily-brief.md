### STORY 2: Pete Crow-Armstrong Joins Exclusive 40-40 Club — Seventh in MLB History
**Tier:** 1
**Classification:** NEW STORY
**Angle:** Chicago Cubs outfielder Pete Crow-Armstrong finished the 2026 regular season with 45 home runs and 40 stolen bases, becoming only the seventh player in MLB history to record a 40-40 season. He joined José Canseco, Barry Bonds, Alex Rodriguez, Alfonso Soriano, Ronald Acuña Jr., and Shohei Ohtani in this exclusive group, and is the first Cub ever to join the 40-40 club. His historic season was overshadowed by the Cubs being swept 2-0 in the NL Wild Card Series by the Padres, where he went 0-for-7 with 4 strikeouts.
**Key Facts:**
- Pete Crow-Armstrong: 45 HR + 40 SB in 2026 — only 7th player in MLB history to record 40-40
