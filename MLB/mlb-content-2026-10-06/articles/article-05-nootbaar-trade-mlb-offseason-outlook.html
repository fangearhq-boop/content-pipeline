Nootbaar to Arizona, Offseason Machine Running: The 2026 Baseball Calendar Turns

By Jake Torres | October 6, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

The World Series has not been played yet. The ALDS and NLDS are ongoing. And yet the 2026-27 baseball offseason is already in motion — signaled by a notable early-October trade and the launch of the annual offseason outlook series that signals where every front office is heading before the last pitch is thrown.

Lars Nootbaar Goes to Arizona

On October 1 — the same day the final Wild Card games were concluding — the St. Louis Cardinals traded Lars Nootbaar to the Arizona Diamondbacks. The deal included three Cardinals pitchers: Brian Curley, Daniel Eagen, and Sandro Santana. The return package from Arizona was not immediately detailed in public reports.

Nootbaar has been a fan favorite and defensive standout in St. Louis during his time with the Cardinals. His departure signals that St. Louis has shifted into a roster reconstruction mode — clearing payroll or trading from surplus areas to address needs elsewhere. For Arizona, adding Nootbaar provides a player with the kind of defensive versatility and patience at the plate that plays well in October lineups.

The transaction was one of several executed around the Wild Card round. The Brewers reinstated first baseman Andrew Vaughn from the injured list on October 2. The Mariners claimed first baseman Michael Toglia off waivers from Cincinnati the same day. The White Sox activated reliever Tyler Davis on October 3. The Padres made their own roster adjustments, placing Walker Buehler on the paternity list and activating Randy Vásquez.

The Offseason Calendar Opens

On October 3, MLB Trade Rumors launched its annual 2026-27 Offseason Outlook series — a comprehensive preview of how all 30 teams are approaching the coming winter. The series arrival is a reliable signal that the baseball calendar is turning, even before the final out of the World Series.

For the 20-plus teams watching from home, the offseason strategy conversations began the moment their seasons ended. Identifying free agent targets, evaluating trade scenarios, assessing the roster through a fresh lens after a full year of data — that work is already underway. The Offseason Outlook series gives fans a window into how those conversations are shaping up at each organization.

What's Next

The offseason calendar accelerates after the World Series: free agency opens, the Rule 5 draft, the Winter Meetings, arbitration hearings, and the first wave of major signings typically land between November and January. For teams that finished watching October early — including the Cubs, Phillies, Red Sox, Astros, and others — the next few months will determine whether 2027 represents a step forward or a reset.

The postseason is still very much alive. But in baseball, the next season is always already beginning.
