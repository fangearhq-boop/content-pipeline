Brewers on the Brink of the NLCS; Braves and Dodgers Go to Game 3 Tonight

By Ryan Calloway | October 6, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

Two National League Division Series reach critical junctures tonight. Milwaukee needs one win for an NLCS berth. Atlanta and Los Angeles need to survive their rubber game to earn the chance to close out their own series. October baseball does not offer many reprieves — Tuesday night makes that clear.

Brewers: One Win Away

The Milwaukee Brewers are one win from the National League Championship Series after winning both games of their NLDS against the San Diego Padres. Game 2 was the sharper statement: a walk-off Brewers victory, 4-3, that gave Milwaukee its commanding 2-0 series lead and sent San Diego home needing to win three consecutive games to advance.

The Padres got here by eliminating the Cubs in the NL Wild Card, but the NLDS has been a different challenge entirely. Milwaukee has been the more complete team through two games, and the narrative of their walk-off victory gives the Brewers additional momentum heading into tonight's Game 3 (9:30 PM ET, FS1).

For San Diego, the situation is dire. No team in MLB postseason history advances from a 0-2 deficit without winning the next three. The Padres, behind rotation options that include Kevin Gausman and Nick Pivetta, will need one of their starters to deliver a historically good performance to keep the season alive.

Braves vs. Dodgers: Series Even, Game 3 Is Tonight

The other NLDS is less resolved. The Braves and Dodgers are tied 1-1, and tonight's Game 3 (6:00 PM ET, FS1) is a genuine crossroads for both organizations.

Atlanta won Game 2 in unusual fashion: the winning run scored on a wild pitch by Dodgers pitcher Kris Bubic that allowed Michael Harris II to come home from third base in a 3-2 Atlanta victory. It was an opportunistic win — not the clean, dominant performance Atlanta would prefer — but it knotted the series and forced the Dodgers into a situation where a loss tonight would put them facing elimination Thursday.

Chris Sale is expected to start Game 3 for Atlanta. Sale — acquired by the Braves and a key postseason piece — gives Atlanta a legitimate top-of-the-rotation arm for the pivotal game. The Dodgers will counter with their own rotation depth. The winner of tonight's game goes to 2-1 with a chance to close out the series in four games. The loser faces a must-win Thursday.

What's Next

If Milwaukee advances tonight, the Brewers will wait to see who emerges from the Braves-Dodgers series. If Atlanta wins, the NLCS would be a Braves-Brewers matchup. If Los Angeles survives, it is a Dodgers-Brewers series — or another opportunity for Atlanta to close out the Dodgers in Game 4.

Both Game 3s tonight carry elimination weight for at least one team. That is the version of October baseball that separates the sport from everything else.
