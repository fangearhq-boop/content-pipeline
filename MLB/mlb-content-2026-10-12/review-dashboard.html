The Brewers beat the Los Angeles Dodgers 2-1 in NLCS Game 1 Sunday night at American Family Field, taking a 1-0 series lead against the two-time reigning World Series champions. Here's the question heading into Game 2: can the Brewers keep relying on their bullpen to carry 4⅔ innings every time Misiorowski starts? Sunday they did it flawlessly.
