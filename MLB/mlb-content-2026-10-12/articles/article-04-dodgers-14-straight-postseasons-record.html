The Dodgers Just Tied the Longest Postseason Streak in MLB History — 14 Straight Appearances

By Ryan Calloway

October 12, 2026

They lost Game 1 of the NLCS to Milwaukee on Sunday night. But just by being there, the Los Angeles Dodgers made history.

The 2026 postseason marks Los Angeles' 14th consecutive October appearance, a streak that began in 2013 and has continued without interruption ever since. With that, the Dodgers have tied the Atlanta Braves for the longest continuous postseason streak in Major League Baseball history.

What 14 Straight Means

A postseason streak of this length requires sustained organizational excellence. Over 14 seasons, rosters turn over entirely. Stars age and are replaced. Farm systems cycle through. Trades are made and sometimes misfire. Injuries disrupt seasons. Yet the Dodgers have navigated all of it while finishing among the league's best every single year.

The streak spans the Clayton Kershaw era, the Corey Seager championship years, and now the Shohei Ohtani era. It encompasses multiple World Series appearances and two consecutive championships entering this postseason. It has required a front office that consistently drafts, develops, and acquires talent at a level few organizations in any sport can match.

The Braves Set the Standard

The Atlanta Braves' 14-year postseason streak came across a different competitive era. Milwaukee's series lead shows that even dynasties face cycles. But matching any record set over 14 years of consistent excellence is a franchise achievement that no single game result can diminish.

Los Angeles entered this NLCS attempting to win a third consecutive World Series title — a run that would be one of the most dominant stretches in modern baseball history. They came to Milwaukee and lost Game 1. But everything they have built across 14 straight Octobers still stands.

What's Next

The Dodgers trail the NLCS 0-1 and must win Game 2 at American Family Field to avoid going back to Los Angeles in a 0-2 hole. The franchise's 14-year streak of October appearances does not guarantee a championship — but it is a testament to what the organization has built. Whether they extend it to a 15th consecutive postseason in 2027 will depend entirely on what happens in the coming weeks.
