ALCS Game 1 Preview: Guardians vs. Rays Tonight at Tropicana Field — 8 PM ET on TBS

By Marcus Cole

October 12, 2026

The American League Championship Series begins tonight. The Cleveland Guardians and Tampa Bay Rays meet at Tropicana Field in St. Petersburg at 8 PM ET on TBS — two teams that each advanced in spectacular fashion and now face the last obstacle before the World Series.

This is a rematch driven by contrasting stories. The Rays made it here by dismantling the New York Yankees in a three-game sweep, allowing just five runs in the entire series. Victor Mesa Jr.'s home run in the decisive game was the signature moment. The Guardians fought through something harder: a 0-2 deficit against the Chicago White Sox, a hamate injury to José Ramírez, and a comeback that required wins in Games 3, 4, and 5 back-to-back-to-back.

The Pitching Matchup

Drew Rasmussen starts for Tampa Bay. The right-hander was one of the American League's premier starters in 2026, finishing with a 16-5 record and 2.67 ERA. He enters Game 1 as the ace of a pitching staff that suffocated the Yankees in the ALDS.

Foster Griffin counters for Cleveland. The right-hander went 15-6 with a 3.60 ERA during the regular season and was reliable through Cleveland's ALDS run. The Guardians gave him the Game 1 start for a reason — Griffin brings durability and command that give Cleveland the best chance to steal a road game.

The Numbers Favor Tampa Bay

In the 2026 regular season, the Rays dominated Cleveland in their head-to-head matchups — Tampa Bay went 5-1 against the Guardians and outscored them 20-8. Vegas oddsmakers have the Rays at -170 favorites; Cleveland comes in at +139. The over/under is set at 6.5 runs.

But postseason baseball does not care about regular-season numbers. Cleveland just proved that — they lost the first two games of the ALDS before winning three in a row. The Guardians believe they belong here, and Ramírez's performance despite injury gave this team something to rally around.

What's Next

ALCS Game 1 is tonight at Tropicana Field — first pitch 8 PM ET on TBS/truTV. The best-of-seven series continues with Game 2 scheduled for Tuesday in St. Petersburg before shifting to Cleveland. Follow the full series to see if Tampa Bay's regular-season dominance over Cleveland translates to October, or whether the Guardians' comeback momentum carries them through.
