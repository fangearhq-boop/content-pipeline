**Window:** 10:30 AM ET

Cubs are home today against Cincinnati. These games matter for PCA's award case as much as his own at-bats do.

### STORY 3: Alvarez Triple Crown August Slowdown

#### Long-Form Post
**Window:** 12:30 PM ET

Yordan Alvarez has hit one home run in August.

For most of the season, that would be unthinkable. The Astros slugger has led the AL in home runs, RBI, and batting average for much of 2026. He was looking like the first Triple Crown winner since Miguel Cabrera in 2012.

Now the field has closed. Junior Caminero (Rays) and Ben Rice (Yankees) sit at 35 home runs — one behind Alvarez's 36. Pete Alonso (Orioles) has driven in 22 runs in August alone and has pulled essentially even with Alvarez in the RBI race.

Alvarez still leads all three categories. His batting average remains his most comfortable cushion. But the dominance that made the Triple Crown feel inevitable in July has given way to something that feels very much like a race.

The backdrop makes this even more complicated: the Astros are 67-67, on the edge of missing the postseason entirely. Alvarez would be chasing Triple Crown history on a team fighting for survival. The October he'd want to top off a historic season may not come.
