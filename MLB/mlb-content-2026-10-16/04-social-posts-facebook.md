### STORY 3: Ohtani's October

#### FB Long-Form Post — 3:00 PM ET

At some point, the story of the 2026 postseason might be what Shohei Ohtani couldn't do.

In the regular season, Ohtani was brilliant: .277 average, 30 home runs, 80 RBI — playing through right biceps inflammation that shut him down as a pitcher after 14 starts (8-2, 1.79 ERA). He returned to the lineup as a designated hitter and carried the Dodgers down the stretch.

Then October arrived.

He's hitting .150 in the NLCS. Zero home runs. Seven strikeouts. He hasn't gone deep since August 18. Manager Dave Roberts hasn't benched him — and the right call isn't obvious. The talent is unquestioned. The contract ($700 million) doesn't factor into postseason coaching decisions. But the results are there on the scoreboard, and the Dodgers need more.

Tonight is NLCS Game 5. Dodgers trail 3-1. Ohtani is still in the lineup, and tonight might be his last chance to rewrite this particular October chapter.

We'll be watching.

### STORY 5: Petey Halpin

#### FB Long-Form Post — 6:00 PM ET

Before this ALCS, most baseball fans outside of Cleveland had never heard of Petey Halpin.

The rookie outfielder was a late-season addition to the Guardians' lineup, known mostly for a three-run September homer against the White Sox that gave Cleveland a big regular season win. Entering the ALCS, he wasn't the story — José Ramírez was the story. Gavin Williams was the story. The Guardians' improbable comeback after losing to the White Sox 0-2 in the ALDS was the story.

Then Game 2 happened. Freddy Peralta, the Rays' starter, struck Halpin out on a nasty 3-2 changeup in the sixth and pumped his fist walking off the mound. Peralta had gotten his man.

Then came Game 3. Halpin dug in with two men on in the eighth, the score tied. And he put the Rays' bullpen on the hill over the left field wall.

Two-run homer. Go-ahead. Cleveland wins 3-2.

It's the kind of October moment that turns a player into a name fans remember. Tonight, ALCS Game 4 continues at Progressive Field. Halpin will be in the lineup again.
