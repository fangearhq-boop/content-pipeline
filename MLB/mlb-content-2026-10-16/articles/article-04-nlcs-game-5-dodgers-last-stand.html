Glasnow and the Dodgers' Last Stand: NLCS Game 5 Preview

By Ryan Calloway | October 16, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

At some point, great dynasties end. They end with a loss on a quiet afternoon, or with a championship celebration that signals nothing more will follow, or with a single October night when the final piece falls short. The Los Angeles Dodgers are not dead yet — but tonight, in NLCS Game 5, they are playing to stay alive.

Tyler Glasnow is expected to take the mound for the Dodgers. The Brewers lead the series 3-1. Milwaukee needs one win. Los Angeles needs a win tonight, then two more in Milwaukee — an outcome that requires surviving Glasnow's start, getting the offense going, and somehow reversing the momentum of a series the Brewers have largely controlled.

What Glasnow Means to This Team

If there is a chance for the Dodgers to extend this series, it runs through their starting pitcher. Glasnow is the kind of arm that can set a tone, change a series, and give a team the footing it needs. He has done it before. The Dodgers are betting he can do it again on the biggest stage of his 2026 season.

The Brewers, for their part, will face him with a lineup built to compete in October. William Contreras, Garrett Mitchell, Brice Turang, and the pieces around them have proven capable of manufacturing runs against quality pitching in this series. They are not a team that will be intimidated by a single ace.

The Dynasty Question

The Dodgers have made the postseason every year since 2013. Fourteen consecutive appearances. That is a number that puts them in rare company — a franchise that has built a machine capable of sustaining excellence year after year regardless of roster turnover, contract situations, or coaching changes.

Streaks like that are never guaranteed. They require excellence at the front office, coaching, player development, and — eventually — a single October game when everything has to come together. Tonight is that game for the 2026 version of this dynasty.

Jacob Misiorowski, Milwaukee's ace, is waiting in the wings for a potential Game 6 in Milwaukee. The Brewers are playing with house money; they've already overachieved what many expected coming into this series. But they believe they can finish it tonight.

What's Next

NLCS Game 5 is tonight at Dodger Stadium on FS1. If the Dodgers win, the series shifts to Milwaukee with the Brewers still holding a 3-2 edge and playing at home. If the Brewers win, the World Series begins — and the opponent will be whoever survives the Cleveland-Tampa Bay ALCS.

Either way, tonight matters. Glasnow takes the ball. The stadium will be full. And forty-four years of Milwaukee waiting may end before the night is over.
