ALCS Game 3: Halpin's Eighth-Inning Homer Lifts Guardians 3-2, Cleveland Takes 2-1 Series Lead

By Marcus Cole | October 16, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

The Guardians' Petey Halpin stepped to the plate in the eighth inning Thursday night with two men on and the score tied, and he crushed a two-run go-ahead homer that gave Cleveland a 3-2 win over Tampa Bay in ALCS Game 3. The Guardians now lead the series 2-1, and ALCS Game 4 is tonight at Progressive Field (8 PM ET, TBS).

Two nights ago in Tampa, Freddy Peralta had struck Halpin out on a 3-2 changeup to end an inning and celebrated on the mound. That game felt like the Rays establishing control. Thursday night at Progressive Field felt like the Guardians reclaiming it.

The Peralta-Halpin Subplot

The best October stories have human stakes. This one has a clear protagonist and a clear antagonist, and the hero got his revenge.

In Game 2, Peralta mixed his pitches expertly, and when he got Halpin to chase the changeup on the third strike, he wasn't hiding his satisfaction. The Rays won that game 3-1. They evened the series. Tampa Bay had all the momentum.

The series shifted back to Cleveland for Game 3. Halpin was in the lineup again, and in the eighth inning — with the score tied and the season hanging in the balance — he connected on a two-run shot that cleared the left field fence at Progressive Field and gave the Guardians a lead they would not relinquish.

The crowd at Progressive Field roared. Halpin rounded the bases with his teammates already on the top step of the dugout. In a series full of moments, this was the most human one yet.

The Rays' Situation

Tampa Bay is now in a difficult position. They are down 2-1 in the series, playing on the road in Cleveland for Game 4 tonight. Shane McClanahan — the ace who would have been their most reliable option in a series like this — is out for the postseason with a forearm injury. The depth of the Rays' rotation and bullpen has been tested, and tonight it gets tested again.

The Rays swept the Yankees in the ALDS and handled the Guardians in Game 1. This is a dangerous team that knows how to win on the road. But the margin for error is narrowing.

What's Next

ALCS Game 4 is tonight at Progressive Field, 8 PM ET on TBS. Cleveland plays with the energy of a city that has been waiting for this moment. The Rays play knowing that a loss puts them one defeat from elimination.

Halpin will be in the lineup. Whatever Peralta's thoughts are on the matchup, they will remain private until the series is decided. That's October baseball: the players who step up when it matters most become the names that last. This week, in Cleveland, that's been Petey Halpin.
