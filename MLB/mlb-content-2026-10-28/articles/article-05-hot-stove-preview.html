Hot Stove Preview: What to Watch When the World Series Ends

By Marcus Cole | October 28, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

The World Series is still being played, but the offseason is already forming in the background. Contracts are being priced. Trade conversations are being mapped out. And at least one major storyline — the collective bargaining agreement between players and ownership — could reshape the entire calendar before spring training begins.

Here is what to watch when the final out is recorded.

Tarik Skubal and the Record Contract

Tarik Skubal was arguably the best pitcher in the American League in 2026. Multiple reports indicate the Detroit Tigers left-hander is expected to sign an extension or free-agent deal that sets a new Major League record for average annual value for a pitcher.

How much he gets will set the market for every other pitcher in baseball for the next several years. The contract will be a reference point in every pitching negotiation for the foreseeable future. Where the number lands — and which team lands him — is the first big offseason domino to fall.

CBA Negotiations and a Potential Lockout

Ken Rosenthal, who has tracked labor negotiations in baseball as closely as anyone in the industry, has flagged that the current collective bargaining agreement talks are not going smoothly. A lockout, he noted in a recent piece, is possible.

If a lockout materializes, it would freeze transactions, delay free-agent signings, and push the entire offseason timeline back. The last work stoppage significantly disrupted spring training and the start of the regular season. Owners and players would both prefer to avoid that outcome — but the history of these negotiations suggests that preference alone is not sufficient.

Brandon Woodruff's Health

Brandon Woodruff did not pitch in the 2026 World Series. He was removed from the Brewers' 60-day injured list for right shoulder surgery — out for the season. Whether Milwaukee wins tonight or not, Woodruff's timeline for return in 2027 is one of their most critical offseason questions.

Woodruff was one of the better starting pitchers in the NL before his injury. A healthy Woodruff in 2027, paired with Misiorowski, would make the Brewers a legitimate repeat contender. An extended absence changes the calculus significantly.

What's Next

The World Series ends tonight or in the coming days. When it does, the offseason begins in earnest. Skubal's contract will be the first major market-setter. CBA talks will either produce an agreement or produce a lockout. And teams that finished October early are already preparing their moves.

The Hot Stove season is the best time to be a baseball fan who pays attention to the business of the game. Everything is about to get interesting.
