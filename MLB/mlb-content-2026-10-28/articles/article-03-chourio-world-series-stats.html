Jackson Chourio Is Batting .500 in the World Series at Age 22 — And It Doesn't Feel Surprising

By Jake Torres | October 28, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

Jackson Chourio is 22 years old. He is batting .500 in the 2026 World Series. He has delivered a multi-hit game in every Milwaukee Brewers win in this Fall Classic. He is, by any measure, one of the most impressive offensive performers in this World Series — and the best part is that nothing about it feels like a surprise.

Born March 11, 2004, Chourio has been on an accelerated timetable since he arrived in the major leagues. He has hit for power, stolen bases, and delivered in postseason moments before this one. The World Series stage appears, against all odds, to be even better suited to him than the regular season.

The Numbers

Through four World Series games, Chourio is 10-for-20 at the plate. That is a .500 batting average against Rays pitching — including some of Tampa Bay's best arms. He has had multiple hits in every game the Brewers have won, serving as the offensive engine that turns Milwaukee's lineup from good to dominant.

For a 22-year-old to post those numbers on the largest stage in baseball is remarkable. The history books take note: he became the youngest player since Joe Garagiola in 1946 to collect four hits in a World Series game, a mark he reached in Game 3.

What He Represents for Milwaukee

The Brewers have built their roster methodically. They developed young talent through their farm system, made smart trades, and avoided overpaying for free agents. Chourio represents the payoff for that approach. He was part of a trade the Brewers made with patience and foresight, and now he is the face of a potential championship.

If Milwaukee wins Game 5 tonight, Chourio will very likely be the World Series Most Valuable Player. He will be the youngest player to win that award in decades, and he will have earned it with the most straightforward measure: production when it counts most.

What's Next

Game 5 tonight at Tropicana Field, 8 PM ET on FOX. If Chourio bats .500 in the World Series and the Brewers win their first title, this will be one of the most impressive postseason performances by a 22-year-old in the modern era of baseball.

He has five games to get there. After four, the case is already being made.
