43 Games Left. The Cubs Are 69-50 and Going to the Playoffs.

By Jake Torres

August 11, 2026

The Chicago Cubs are 69-50. They hold the top NL Wild Card spot. The Arizona Diamondbacks and Philadelphia Phillies are each 63-56 — six games back with the same number of games played. There are 43 games left in the regular season.

The math is working for the Cubs. Playing .500 baseball from here gets them in. They've been better than .500 all season.

The Gap They've Built

Six games is a real lead. It is not an insurmountable one — baseball has produced larger collapses — but it means Chicago controls their own destiny. A team that finishes 43 games at .500 wins 21.5 of them; the Cubs need to stay ahead of teams running at the same pace, or better, while maintaining their own performance.

What makes the Cubs' position stronger than the raw standings suggest is consistency. They haven't built this lead with a single extended hot streak. They've played well across a 119-game sample that now gives the 2026 Cubs one of the NL's best winning percentages heading into the final push. Consistent winning throughout a season is the profile of a team that doesn't collapse in the final month — it's a team that keeps doing what got them here.

The NL Bracket Around Them

The NL picture entering the final stretch: the Atlanta Braves lead the NL East, the Milwaukee Brewers lead the NL Central, and the Los Angeles Dodgers lead the NL West despite a rough August. The wild card positions are where the competition lives, and the Cubs have separated from the pack.

The D-backs and Phillies are effectively tied for the second and third wild card berths, which means Chicago's primary job is not to chase anyone — it's to not collapse. Win enough games and the playoff spot is theirs. The Cubs have shown all season that they're capable of winning enough games.

Tomorrow, the Dodgers visit Wrigley Field when Tarik Skubal makes his LA debut against the Cubs. That game is its own storyline — two potential postseason opponents meeting for the first time this season. The Cubs could use a win. They've been good at getting them.

What's Next

The Cubs host the Los Angeles Dodgers at Wrigley Field on Tuesday, August 12 — the same day Tarik Skubal makes his Dodgers debut — before continuing their schedule through August and September.

With 43 games left and a 6-game lead, the Cubs are on track for the postseason. The only remaining question is exactly how they get there.
