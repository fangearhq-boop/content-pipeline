Two Days Until the Field of Dreams — and Baseball Is Already There Tonight

By Marcus Cole

August 11, 2026

The Field of Dreams gets its first game of the week tonight.

The Iowa Cubs host the St. Paul Saints at 7:30 PM ET on MLB Network in Dyersville, Iowa — only the second minor league game ever played at the historic site. Tomorrow, a High School All-American Game takes place there at 2 PM ET, managed by Jim Thome and Torii Hunter. And then, on Thursday, August 13 at 7:30 PM ET, the Minnesota Twins face the Philadelphia Phillies in what is already the most anticipated regular-season event of the 2026 MLB calendar.

Dyersville, Iowa is hosting a week of baseball. Thursday is the headline — but it starts tonight.

What's Coming Thursday

The 2026 MLB at Field of Dreams game marks two firsts: it's the first game at Dyersville since 2022, and the first time Netflix has broadcast a Field of Dreams game. Pregame coverage begins at 6:30 PM ET, more than an hour before first pitch, to accommodate what is reportedly the largest collection of living Hall of Famers at one event since the 2008 All-Star Game at old Yankee Stadium.

For the Phillies: Mike Schmidt, Steve Carlton, and Scott Rolen. For the Twins: Joe Mauer, Rod Carew, Bert Blyleven, and Paul Molitor. And Jim Thome and Jim Kaat — who wore both uniforms during their careers — represent both organizations. More than 25 Hall of Famers in total will walk through the outfield corn and onto the diamond before first pitch.

That's not a ceremony. That's a reunion of the sport's history.

The Uniforms

Both teams wear throwback uniforms for the event. The Phillies will dress in gray road uniforms inspired by what the franchise wore from 1939 to 1941, with a centennial sleeve patch. The Twins will wear 1960s pinstripes — the original jersey design from the franchise's earliest Minnesota seasons.

Neither uniform looks like what these teams normally wear. That's the point. The Field of Dreams game is baseball with its history on the surface, dressed in the uniforms of franchises that were young and building something — the same arc every team at this site has come to represent.

Kevin Costner provides narration for the Netflix broadcast. The pregame begins at 6:30 PM ET. First pitch is 7:30 PM ET.

What's Next

The Iowa Cubs vs. St. Paul Saints minor league game is TONIGHT at 7:30 PM ET on MLB Network at the Field of Dreams site in Dyersville, Iowa. Tomorrow, the High School All-American Game takes place at 2 PM ET at the same site.

The main event: Thursday, August 13, 7:30 PM ET. Minnesota Twins vs. Philadelphia Phillies. Netflix. Dyersville, Iowa. Two days.
