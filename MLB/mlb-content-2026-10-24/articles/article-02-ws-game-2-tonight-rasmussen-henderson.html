World Series Game 2 Tonight: Rasmussen vs. Henderson, 8 PM ET on FOX

By Jake Torres | October 24, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

Tampa Bay entered this World Series with a postseason ace who had been nearly unhittable in October. Tonight, the Rays need Drew Rasmussen to be exactly that.

Game 2 of the 2026 World Series goes tonight at American Family Field in Milwaukee. First pitch is at 8 PM ET on FOX. Rasmussen starts for the Rays. Rookie Logan Henderson starts for the Brewers. The series is Milwaukee 1-0 after Friday's 3-2 Brewers win.

Tampa Bay cannot afford to go home to Tropicana Field in a 0-2 hole. Rasmussen is the reason they don't have to worry about that — at least, that's the plan.

Rasmussen's October Has Been Historic

Drew Rasmussen finished the 2026 regular season 16-5 with a 2.67 ERA, earning his second All-Star selection. Then October happened, and he got even better.

In the American League Division Series against the New York Yankees, Rasmussen threw eight-plus innings in Game 1, allowing one hit and striking out 10 in one of the most dominant postseason pitching performances of the decade. The Rays won 1-0. It wasn't officially a no-hitter, but it was as close as you can get on baseball's biggest stage.

In the ALCS against the Cleveland Guardians, he threw seven innings of one-run ball in Game 5, putting the Rays in position to advance to their third World Series appearance in franchise history. Across all his postseason outings, Rasmussen owns a 1.29 ERA.

The Brewers have not faced him before. That is a significant problem for a Milwaukee lineup that is making its first World Series appearance since 1982.

Henderson's Test Begins Tonight

Logan Henderson is Milwaukee's Game 2 starter, and he is walking into a challenging situation. He is a rookie who has been part of a formidable Brewers rotation all season alongside Misiorowski, but his biggest October moment came in the NLCS when he struggled in his start against the Los Angeles Dodgers.

Misiorowski won Game 1 and threw a 103.5 mph first pitch that broke the World Series pitch speed record. Henderson's assignment in Game 2 is simpler in theory and harder in practice: give the Brewers length, keep Tampa Bay's lineup at bay, and let the offense build on Friday's momentum.

Milwaukee is 4-0 at home this postseason. The crowd at American Family Field will be the loudest it has been all fall — the city has been waiting 44 years to host a World Series game, and the Brewers have won Game 1.

Series Dynamics

A Brewers win tonight and a 2-0 lead would put Tampa Bay in a deeply uncomfortable position. No team has come back from 0-2 in a World Series since the 2004 Boston Red Sox erased a 3-0 ALCS deficit against the Yankees — and that is not a 0-2, that was an 0-3. The psychological and mathematical pressure of going to Tampa Bay needing to win three of four would be immense.

A Rays win and the series resets to a virtual coin flip heading into Games 3 through 5 at Tropicana Field, where Freddy Peralta — the former Brewer who won the ALCS MVP with a 0.75 ERA against Cleveland — is expected to start Game 3 in front of what will be a electric Tampa Bay crowd.

Tonight's game is pivotal. Both managers know it. Both pitchers know it.

What's Next

Game 2 of the World Series is tonight at American Family Field. First pitch: 8 PM ET on FOX and FOX Deportes. If the Rays win, the series heads to Tampa Bay for Games 3-5 (Oct. 26-28) tied at 1-1. If the Brewers win, they will have a commanding 2-0 lead with three road games still to play. Either way, Games 6 and 7 would return to Milwaukee on Oct. 30-31 if needed.
