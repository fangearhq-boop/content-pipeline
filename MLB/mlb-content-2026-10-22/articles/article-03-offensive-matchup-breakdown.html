Brewers vs. Rays World Series Matchup: How Each Team Scores — and How the Other Stops Them

By Jake Torres | Ballpark Banter | October 22, 2026

The 2026 World Series begins Friday night, and before Jacob Misiorowski throws his first pitch in the Fall Classic, it's worth breaking down the offensive matchup that will decide this series. How do the Brewers score? How do the Rays stop them? And vice versa?

Here is the tactical preview.

How Milwaukee Scores

The Brewers built their offense around youth and postseason confidence. Four players define what Milwaukee does at the plate in October.

Jackson Chourio enters the World Series with 6 career postseason home runs — a new Milwaukee Brewers franchise record, set during this very playoff run. At 22 years old, he has been the most electric offensive player in the Brewers' lineup all October. He hits the ball hard in the air and he does not shrink on the big stage.

Brice Turang drove in 100 runs during the 2026 regular season. He hit a home run off Tarik Skubal in NLCS Game 1 and scored in every single game of Milwaukee's sweep of the Dodgers. Turang is the kind of reliable run producer who makes a lineup dangerous from top to bottom.

Jake Bauers was named NLCS MVP after hitting .348 against the Dodgers. Bauers was benched on Opening Day of the 2026 season. The fact that he is now a Fall Classic participant says everything about what this postseason can do for a player's legacy — and everything about how far this Brewers team has come.

William Contreras has 3 home runs in the 2026 playoffs and has been a reliable middle-of-the-order bat throughout the October run.

Together, these four give Milwaukee a lineup that punishes mistake pitches and keeps pressure on opposing starters from the first inning.

How Tampa Bay Stops Milwaukee

The Rays' plan starts with their starting pitching. Drew Rasmussen is widely expected to start Game 1, and if he pitches anywhere near his postseason form — a 1.29 ERA in 15 innings — he neutralizes Milwaukee's lineup by keeping the ball on the ground and limiting hard contact. Rasmussen is not a strikeout pitcher; he wins with sequencing and location.

The deeper concern for Milwaukee is Tampa Bay's bullpen deployment. The Rays use their bullpen as a weapon, not a safety net. Their matchup optimization — pulling starters early, sequencing lefty-righty advantages aggressively — is designed to take away the second and third time through a lineup. For a Brewers lineup that can get hot in stretches, that kind of constant roster churning is disruptive.

Bryan Baker locked down the ninth inning in ALCS Game 6 with a perfect frame. Freddy Peralta threw 5.1 innings of one-run ball in Game 6 before that. The Rays' late-game management will be one of the most watched tactical elements of this series.

How Tampa Bay Scores

The Rays' offense runs through Junior Caminero. The 22-year-old two-time All-Star was limited to 51 games in 2026 due to injury but posted a .265/.356/.490 slash line when healthy. His two-run home run off the left-field foul pole in ALCS Game 6 — one of the defining moments of Tampa Bay's pennant run — is exactly the kind of swing that can change a World Series.

Yandy Díaz provides veteran production and on-base presence. Jonathan Aranda had a breakout 2026 season and gives the Rays a dangerous bat in the middle of the order. Richie Palacios doubled in the second inning of ALCS Game 6 to give Tampa Bay an early 2-0 lead — a reminder that the Rays' offense can score quickly before their opponents adjust.

Carson Williams adds a young, athletic presence at shortstop and is part of the core Tampa Bay has built for the next several years.

How Milwaukee Stops Tampa Bay

The answer is simple, if not easy: the bullpen.

The Brewers' relievers have been historically dominant this postseason. A 0.38 ERA in 23.2 innings through the NLCS ranks among the best bullpen performances in the Wild Card era. DL Hall has thrown 8.2 innings without allowing a run. Aaron Ashby has 12.1 shutout innings. Thyago Uribe has been near-untouchable. Devin Megill has closed games out.

If Misiorowski gives Milwaukee four innings and hands it to this bullpen, the Rays will need to score early. Their biggest offensive threat — getting Caminero a pitch he can drive in the first five innings — matters enormously. If they can't get to Milwaukee's starters, the late innings become almost unwinnable.

The Matchup to Watch

Chourio vs. Rasmussen in Game 1. The two best young players in this series, on opposite sides, in the first at-bat that sets the tone. Whoever blinks first in that at-bat will tell us a great deal about how the rest of this World Series plays out.
