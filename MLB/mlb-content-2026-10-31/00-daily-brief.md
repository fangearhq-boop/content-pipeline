### STORY 1: Brewers Championship Parade Draws 750,000+ to Milwaukee Streets
**Tier:** 1 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP
Milwaukee held its World Series championship parade Friday, October 30, drawing an estimated 750,000-plus people to downtown streets — nearly double the 400,000 initially expected. The route ran through downtown Milwaukee ending at Henry Maier Festival Park, where a sold-out rally was held at the American Family Insurance Amphitheater. Broadcast live on MLB Network and Brewers.com.

### STORY 2: William Contreras — First Brewer to Win World Series MVP, Now a Potential Free Agent
**Tier:** 2 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP
William Contreras hit .381 (8-for-21) with 4 RBI in the World Series, threw out four base stealers, and went 10-for-13 on automatic ball challenges. He became the first Brewers player ever to win the World Series MVP award and the first catcher to win since Salvador Perez in 2015. Reports suggest Contreras, who earned $9.5M in 2026, is expected to leave Milwaukee in free agency.

### STORY 3: Tarik Skubal Free Agency — Cubs and Dodgers Lead Chase for Two-Time Cy Young
**Tier:** 2 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP
Tarik Skubal, the top free agent in baseball, is drawing interest from the Cubs, Dodgers, Mets, Blue Jays, Phillies, and his former club the Tigers. A panel of 27 insiders projects a deal averaging 8 years and $355 million; one projection puts it at 7 years, $400 million. Chicago's rotation ranked 18th in ERA last season, and ESPN calls them a "perfect fit." No deal yet as of October 31.

### STORY 5: CBA Countdown — 31 Days Until Baseball's Dec. 1 Deadline
**Tier:** 3 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP
The MLB collective bargaining agreement expires 11:59 PM ET on December 1, 2026 — 31 days from today. The owners have proposed a $245 million hard cap / $171 million floor; the MLBPA opposes any cap and wants a ~$300 million luxury-tax threshold. A lockout beginning December 2 would freeze all transactions and potentially cancel Spring Training (currently set for February 19).
