### STORY 1: Matt Olson 900 Consecutive Games

#### Long-Form Post
**Window:** 8:00 AM ET

Matt Olson has now played in 900 consecutive baseball games.

The Braves' first baseman reached the milestone on Sunday when Atlanta defeated the New York Yankees 2-1. The streak started May 2, 2021 — that's more than five full seasons without a day off, without a day on the injured list significant enough to pull him from the lineup, without anything stopping him from being in the starting lineup when the umpire calls "play ball."

Olson is now 8th on the all-time consecutive games list in Major League Baseball history. He passed Stan Musial (895) last week to move into sole possession of that spot. The names above him on the all-time list tell you everything about what kind of company he's in: Cal Ripken Jr. (2,632), Everett Scott (1,307), Steve Garvey (1,207), Joe Sewell (1,103), Billy Williams (1,117), and others who defined durability over eras that predated modern sports medicine.

Olson doesn't talk about the streak much. He doesn't need to. It speaks for itself through every day he shows up, takes his at-bats, plays his first base, and adds one more game to the total.

The next milestone: Joe Sewell at 1,103 consecutive games. That would require Olson to stay healthy through most of the 2028 season. He'll try.

### STORY 2: Field of Dreams 2026 — 3 Days Away

#### Long-Form Post
**Window:** 10:30 AM ET

Three days until baseball goes back to the cornfield.

On Thursday, August 13 at 7:30 PM ET — streaming on Netflix for the first time — the Minnesota Twins host the Philadelphia Phillies at the Field of Dreams in Dyersville, Iowa. It's the first game at the historic site since 2022 and the biggest name in sports streaming takes over coverage from the MLB networks that have broadcast previous editions.

The pregame ceremony is the real spectacle. More than 25 Hall of Famers are scheduled to walk through the cornfield and onto the diamond. For the Phillies: Mike Schmidt, Steve Carlton, and Scott Rolen. For the Twins: Joe Mauer, Rod Carew, Bert Blyleven, and Paul Molitor. Jim Thome and Jim Kaat, who played for both organizations, will also be there. The pregame begins at 6:30 PM ET.

Both teams wear throwback uniforms for the occasion. The Phillies will be in gray road uniforms inspired by what the franchise wore from 1939 to 1941. The Twins will wear home whites inspired by their original 1960s jerseys.

If you haven't secured your Netflix subscription for Thursday, now is the time. This game doesn't happen at a normal stadium.

At 3.5 games up with six-plus weeks of baseball left, they have the lead — but Detroit, Cleveland, and Minnesota are not going away. Whatever happens in September, this White Sox team has already proven that 2024 was the bottom and the organization is back.

**Window:** 3:00 PM ET

#### Long-Form Post
**Window:** 6:00 PM ET

The Cubs rolled to a 10-2 win over the Kansas City Royals on Sunday, and their place in the NL playoff picture has never looked clearer.

At 68-50, Chicago holds the top NL Wild Card spot. The Arizona Diamondbacks and Philadelphia Phillies are tied at 62-56 — six games behind the Cubs — battling for the second and third wild card berths. With 44 games left in the regular season, the Cubs have built a comfortable cushion that they'll need to maintain through a schedule that gets more competitive as September arrives.

What makes the Cubs' position notable is the distance between them and the chasing pack. Six games is not an insurmountable lead, but it's enough that Chicago controls their own destiny heading into the final stretch. If they continue to play .500 baseball — and their 68-50 record suggests they're capable of better — they'll make the postseason.

The NL bracket picture: the Atlanta Braves lead the NL East by 8.5 games and are the likely top seed in the NL. The Brewers lead the NL Central. The Dodgers, despite their recent struggles, hold the NL West. The Cubs as the No. 4 seed wild card would likely draw a road trip early in the playoffs — additional motivation to keep winning in August.
