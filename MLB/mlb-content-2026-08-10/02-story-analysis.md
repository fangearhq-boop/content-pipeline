**Key Angles:**
1. **The 900 milestone** — a concrete, relatable number that captures the achievement
2. **Historical context** — 8th all-time; passed Musial last week; Sewell at 1,103 is next
3. **Game context** — Braves 2-1 over Yankees while Olson plays his 900th straight
4. **The quiet nature of the streak** — Olson doesn't talk about it much; the accomplishment speaks

**Headline Options:**
- "Matt Olson's 900th: The Quiet Iron Man Milestone the Braves' First Baseman Just Reached"
- "900 Straight Games: Matt Olson Reaches a Milestone No Active Player is Close To"
- "Matt Olson Has Now Played in 900 Consecutive Games. He's 8th All-Time and Counting."

**Article Approach:** Lead with the milestone and its significance. Explain the streak's origins (May 2, 2021), its historical context (8th all-time, passed Musial), and what it would take to climb higher on the list. Tone: celebration of an underappreciated achievement.
