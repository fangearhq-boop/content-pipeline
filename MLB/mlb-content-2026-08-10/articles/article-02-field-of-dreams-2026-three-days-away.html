Field of Dreams 2026 Is Three Days Away: Everything You Need to Know About Thursday's Game on Netflix

By Jake Torres

August 10, 2026

Thursday. August 13. 7:30 PM ET. Netflix.

Baseball goes back to the cornfield in three days, and this time the game is on the most prominent streaming platform in the world. The 2026 MLB at Field of Dreams game — Minnesota Twins vs. Philadelphia Phillies — returns the sport to the site of the original Field of Dreams in Dyersville, Iowa, for the first time since 2022.

The Game

Minnesota hosts Philadelphia at the custom ballpark carved out of the eastern Iowa farmland that the 1989 Kevin Costner film made famous. The Twins, in their historic capacity as the nominally "home" team, face a Phillies squad that has been one of the NL's more competitive teams through the first half of the season. First pitch is scheduled for 7:30 PM ET, with pregame coverage beginning at 6:30 PM ET — both exclusively on Netflix, marking the streaming giant's first broadcast of the event.

A Netflix subscription is required to watch. The game will not be simulcast on linear television or on team-specific streaming services. This is a first for the event, which previously aired on Fox and ESPN.

The Hall of Famers

More than 25 Hall of Famers will participate in the pregame ceremony — the largest gathering at the site since the 2008 All-Star Game. The Phillies will be represented by Mike Schmidt, Steve Carlton, and Scott Rolen. The Twins will send Joe Mauer, Rod Carew, Bert Blyleven, and Paul Molitor. Jim Thome and Jim Kaat, who played significant portions of their careers with both franchises, are scheduled to represent both clubs. Pregame begins at 6:30 PM ET at the Dyersville site.

The Uniforms

Both teams wear throwback uniforms specifically designed for the occasion. The Phillies will take the field in gray road uniforms inspired by what the franchise wore from 1939 to 1941. The Twins will wear home whites modeled after the club's original 1960s jerseys from when the franchise relocated to Minnesota from Washington. The result is a game that looks like it belongs to a different era — which is somewhat the point of playing in Dyersville.

What's Next

The 2026 MLB at Field of Dreams game takes place this Thursday, August 13 at 7:30 PM ET on Netflix, with pregame coverage beginning at 6:30 PM ET. Clear your Thursday night schedule. This one doesn't happen at a normal ballpark.
