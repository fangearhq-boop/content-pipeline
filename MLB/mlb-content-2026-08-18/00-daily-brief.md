### STORY 2: Joshua Baez Faces Adjustments — Day 4 After MLB History
**Tier:** 2 | **Type:** FOLLOW UP
**Priority:** DEVELOPING

Joshua Baez made MLB history on August 15 by homering in each of his first three career at-bats (Cardinals 8, Cubs 4 at Wrigley), becoming the first player ever to do so. The 25-year-old outfielder from the Dominican Republic had 34 home runs in Triple-A before the call-up and arrived with 30 family members in the stands. Now in his fourth MLB game (tonight at Cincinnati), the question becomes how pitchers will adjust and whether the Cardinals have found a cornerstone for their 2027 rebuild. He went 3-for-4 with 5 RBI on debut.

**Key facts (HIGH confidence):** Debut Aug 15, first player ever to HR in first 3 career ABs; 3-for-4, 5 RBI, 449-foot first HR; 34 Triple-A HRs; Cardinals Aug 18 @ CIN. Source: CBS Sports, Yahoo Sports, MLB.com.
