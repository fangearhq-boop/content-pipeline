### STORY 3: Alvarez Triple Crown

#### Long-Form Post
**Window:** 12:30 PM ET

The most respected voice in baseball said the quiet part out loud.

Tim Kurkjian of ESPN, speaking this week: "At this point, I think he's going to win the Triple Crown."

He's talking about Yordan Álvarez, who enters Tuesday with a .329 batting average, 36 home runs, and 88 RBI — leading MLB in all three Triple Crown categories. His batting average lead is described by analysts as essentially insurmountable (.029 ahead of the next closest competitor). His RBI lead is a comfortable 7. The home run race is the variable — Schwarber sits at 37, one ahead of Álvarez, with Caminero at 33 in third.

The last player to win the Triple Crown was Miguel Cabrera in 2012, when he hit .330 with 44 home runs and 139 RBI. The Detroit Tigers won the AL pennant that year.

If Álvarez pulls this off, it would be one of the most significant individual baseball accomplishments in over a decade. He'd need to hold the BA lead (almost done), the RBI lead (likely), and close the gap or surpass Schwarber in home runs (the race is real).

Seven weeks left. Can he do it? ⚾

### STORY 4: Misiorowski + Brewers Best Record

#### Long-Form Post
**Window:** 3:00 PM ET

Jacob Misiorowski has done something no Brewers pitcher has ever done.

The 24-year-old set the Milwaukee Brewers franchise strikeout record this season, and he did it while putting together one of the most dominant pitching campaigns in recent memory. He is 11-5 with a 1.76 ERA and 204 strikeouts in just 133 innings — and he reached 200 Ks in only 129.1 innings, making him the second-fastest pitcher in MLB history to hit that mark. Only Spencer Strider in 2022 (123.1 innings) got there faster.

His NL Cy Young odds sit at -550 — the shortest they've been all year.

And the Brewers? They own the best record in baseball: 77-48. They host the Seattle Mariners (59-66) today as they continue to build separation in the NL Central.

Misiorowski's next start is approximately August 23. Opponents are out of answers.

Who wins the NL Cy Young this year? 👇
