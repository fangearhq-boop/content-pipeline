Jacob Misiorowski Sets Brewers Franchise Strikeout Record — And He's Not Done Yet

By Marcus Cole | August 18, 2026

Ballpark Banter / FanGear HQ

In a franchise that has produced some of the most effective pitching in National League history, Jacob Misiorowski has done something no Milwaukee Brewer pitcher had ever done before.

The 24-year-old right-hander set the Brewers franchise record for strikeouts in a single season during 2026, and he did it while putting together one of the most dominant pitching campaigns in the majors in years. He is 11-5 with a 1.76 ERA and 204 strikeouts in 133 innings. His NL Cy Young odds have settled at -550 — the shortest they have been all season and the kind of number that signals the baseball betting market has moved on from a race to a coronation.

Second-Fastest to 200 Strikeouts in MLB History

The franchise record was only part of the historical context. When Misiorowski crossed 200 strikeouts, he did so in 129.1 innings — making him the second-fastest pitcher in Major League Baseball history to reach that mark in a single season. The only pitcher to accomplish it more quickly was Spencer Strider of the Atlanta Braves in 2022, who reached 200 Ks in 123.1 innings.

The company Misiorowski is keeping is extraordinary. Strider's 2022 campaign is remembered as one of the premier power-pitching performances in the modern era. Misiorowski is not just matching it — he is doing it with an ERA nearly a full run lower than most comparable elite seasons.

The Brewers Are Baseball's Best Team

The context around Misiorowski makes his season even more impressive. He is not pitching for a losing team propped up by individual stats. The Milwaukee Brewers are 77-48, the best record in all of Major League Baseball as of August 18. They lead the NL Central by a wide margin and have positioned themselves as a legitimate World Series contender.

Tonight, the Brewers host the Seattle Mariners at American Family Field. Misiorowski's next start is approximately August 23, which means the Brewers staff will carry the load for the next five days before their ace returns. They have handled those turns well all season.

What's Next

With roughly 44 games left in the regular season, Misiorowski has the opportunity to finish this campaign as one of the historically great single-season pitching performances. A sub-1.80 ERA with 220-plus strikeouts would put him in rare air. The NL Cy Young Award, barring a complete collapse, is already his. Watch August 23 for his next turn — and watch whether Milwaukee's record holds as the best in baseball entering the final month.
