### STORY 1: Skubal at GM Meetings — Mets and Dodgers Lead $355M Race
**Tier:** 1 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP
The MLB General Manager Meetings open today in Las Vegas (Nov. 10-12) with Tarik Skubal as the dominant topic. The CBA expires December 1, putting pressure on a signing before a potential lockout freezes transactions.

### STORY 2: Teruaki Sato — Five Teams, 45-Day Clock Running
**Tier:** 1 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP
Teruaki Sato, the NPB Triple Crown winner (.306/.383/.609, 39 HR, 106 RBI), was posted by the Hanshin Tigers on November 1. His 45-day window runs until December 16 — well before the December 1 CBA deadline. Five teams are most closely linked: Dodgers, Mets, Padres, Phillies, Yankees. Contract projections range from $80M (Heyman, 4yr) to $115M (ESPN, 5yr). The main evaluator concerns are a 26.7% strikeout rate and third-base defense, though at least one report suggests teams are considering him at first base as well.

### STORY 3: CBA Countdown — 21 Days Out, Last Chance at GM Meetings
**Tier:** 2 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP
The collective bargaining agreement expires December 1 at 11:59 PM ET — 21 days from today. The GM Meetings in Las Vegas this week represent baseball's last major gathering before the expiration. The core dispute: owners want a salary cap (2028 figure: $258.2M) and $171M floor; the MLBPA opposes any cap and wants a $300M luxury-tax threshold. Deal odds are estimated at roughly 5%. The last lockout lasted 99 days and threatened spring training. With ST opening February 19, 2027, a December lockout carries real scheduling risk.

### STORY 4: Qualifying Offer Deadline — 8 Players Have Until Nov. 17
**Tier:** 2 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP
Eight players — including Seiya Suzuki (Cubs), Randy Arozarena (Mariners), and David Bednar (Yankees) — received one-year, $23.15M qualifying offers on November 2. They have until 4 PM ET on November 17 to accept. If they decline and sign elsewhere, their former teams receive draft-pick compensation; the signing team forfeits a pick. Only 18 of 157 qualifying offers in history have been accepted. With the market active, most of the eight are expected to decline.

### STORY 5: Rotation Free-Agent Market — King and Martinez Lead Deep Class
**Tier:** 3 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP
Below Skubal, the starting pitching market is unusually deep. Michael King (3.21 ERA, 32 starts) opted out of his deal with the Padres. Nick Martinez also opted out. Logan Gray and Seth Eflin declined team options. Combined with the Giolito and Canning releases, the market offers multiple tiers of rotation options for every roster need and budget level.
