Cubs 87-69: The Three-Way Tie Broke. The Race Didn't.

By Marcus Cole September 22, 2026

The Standings Today

The Chicago Cubs are 87-69 and hold the NL Wild Card lead heading into September 22. The San Diego Padres are 86-69, half a game back. The Philadelphia Phillies are 86-70, a full game behind the Cubs. Five games remain in the regular season. The Wild Card begins September 29.

As recently as September 20, all three clubs sat at 85-69. The three-way tie that had built over weeks broke in a single day when the Cubs got help — the Phillies and Padres both lost while Chicago won. The Cubs moved into sole possession of the top Wild Card spot. It is a small lead built from one good day, and it will require more to hold it.

The Padres Are Not Going Away

San Diego has won 11 of their last 12 games. That stretch is the reason they spent most of the past month tied for the Wild Card lead despite playing in a brutal division. The Padres are playing some of the best baseball in the National League right now, and a half-game deficit is not a comfortable cushion against a team on that kind of run.

The Phillies are one game back and not mathematically eliminated from the WC1 seed. Philadelphia is the defending team that entered this race as a presumed favorite and has instead spent September in a three-club scramble. They are still inside the Wild Card bracket, which is where they need to be, but they have ground to make up on both Chicago and San Diego to improve their seeding.

Why WC1 Seeding Matters

The Wild Card bracket gives the top two seeds home games in the first round. WC1 also provides the most favorable potential bracket path to the Division Series. With the Braves, Brewers, and Dodgers all securing division titles and first-round byes, the Wild Card spots are the only route left for the Cubs, Padres, and Phillies — and the seed they enter with will shape how that bracket unfolds.

All three clubs have postseason experience. All three have rosters built to compete in October. The difference between WC1 and WC3 is real, and it will be decided over the next five games.

What's Next

The Cubs, Padres, and Phillies each have five regular-season games remaining before the bracket is set on approximately September 28. The Wild Card begins September 29. Chicago currently holds the advantage, but a half-game lead over a team that has won 11 of 12 is exactly as fragile as it sounds. Every game this week counts.
