Misiorowski at Wrigley: The Cy Young Is Decided. The K Record Isn't.

By Ryan Calloway September 22, 2026

The Numbers That Won't Be Debated

Jakob Misiorowski is the 2026 National League Cy Young Award winner. That is not an opinion. MLB's own panel of voters gave him all 32 first-place votes and 160 total points — a unanimous selection. His 1.95 ERA is the best in baseball. His 20-8 record reflects what happens when the best pitcher in the league pitches for a team that is winning a division title for the fourth straight year.

The strikeout count stands at 247 through his September 20 start at Baltimore, which the Brewers won 3-0. He is the first pitcher in the tracking era to throw more than 1,000 pitches at 100 miles per hour or faster. His fastest recorded pitch as a starter is 105.5 mph. The case for him as the best pitcher in the National League this season is not a close one.

What He Is Still Chasing

Ben Sheets struck out 264 batters for the Milwaukee Brewers in 2004 — the franchise's all-time single-season record. Misiorowski has 247. He needs 17 more to break it. Depending on how many starts remain and how deep into each game he pitches, that is achievable but not automatic.

The context of where he pitches next makes it more interesting. Misiorowski is starting at Wrigley Field against the Chicago Cubs — the team currently holding the NL Wild Card lead. The Cubs need the game. The Brewers have secured the NL Central. Misiorowski is walking into Wrigley with nothing at stake for his team's standings and everything at stake for his own place in Milwaukee's history.

Sheets' Record and What It Means

Ben Sheets was one of the best pitchers in Brewers history before injuries limited his career. The 2004 season — 264 strikeouts, a 2.70 ERA — was his peak. For 22 years that number has stood as Milwaukee's franchise record for strikeouts in a single season. Misiorowski breaking it would put him in the franchise's permanent record book, not just the 2026 season's statistical summary.

He has already won the Cy Young. He has already set records for velocity in the tracking era. The strikeout record is the one remaining individual target, and he has a start at Wrigley to try to close the gap.

What's Next

Misiorowski's start against the Cubs at Wrigley Field is confirmed. How many strikeouts he records in that outing will determine how close he gets to Sheets' 264. The regular season ends approximately September 28. If he does not break the record in this start, he will need another opportunity before the postseason begins. The Brewers secured the division title; they will manage their ace carefully with October approaching. But if the record is within reach in a single start, Wrigley on September 22 is where he will make his run at it.
