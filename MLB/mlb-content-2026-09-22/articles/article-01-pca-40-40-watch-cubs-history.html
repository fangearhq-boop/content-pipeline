2 Away: PCA Needs 2 Stolen Bases in 5 Games to Make History

By Ryan Calloway September 22, 2026

The Countdown

Pete Crow-Armstrong has cleared 40 home runs. He has 38 stolen bases. With five games left in the regular season, the 40-40 club — one of the most exclusive groups in baseball history — is within reach.

He needs two stolen bases. That's it. Two steals in five games for the Cubs outfielder to join a list that has only six names on it: Jose Canseco (1988), Barry Bonds (1996), Alex Rodriguez (1998), Alfonso Soriano (2006), Ronald Acuña Jr. (2023), and Shohei Ohtani (2024). Seven members if PCA gets there. And he would be the first Cub to ever do it.

What He's Already Done

The home run total passed the 40 mark after a two-home-run night at Wrigley against the Braves, which also broke Billy Williams' Cubs franchise record for home runs in a single season by a left-handed hitter. Williams set that mark in 1970. PCA erased it more than 50 years later.

The stolen base total tells a different kind of story. He hit 38 through his speed and instincts on the bases, not just his power at the plate. Having both at that volume in the same season is what makes 40-40 rare. The home run total can be built through strength and launch angle. The stolen base total requires sustained aggression on the basepaths over 150-plus games. Doing both at the 40 threshold in the same year puts you in a different category than almost any player in the sport's history.

The Stakes Double as the Cubs Fight for a Berth

PCA's individual chase is happening alongside a team postseason race. The Cubs are 87-69, holding the NL Wild Card lead by half a game over the San Diego Padres. The Wild Card begins September 29. Every game this week has collective weight in addition to what PCA is chasing personally.

The combination — a historic individual achievement and a postseason push happening at the same time, on the same player, on the same team — is a version of the sport at its most compelling. The Cubs need him playing, and playing well, which only creates more opportunity to reach 40 steals in the time remaining.

What's Next

The Cubs have five regular-season games left, ending approximately September 28. After that, the Wild Card bracket begins on September 29. PCA needs two stolen bases in that window. History is not guaranteed. It is available. For a player who has already broken one franchise record this season, asking him to steal two more bases to add his name to one of baseball's most elite lists is, at this point, not unreasonable. The window is open.
