Jackson Chourio Just Became the Brewers' All-Time Postseason Home Run Leader

By Ryan Calloway | Ballpark Banter | October 19, 2026

Jackson Chourio hit his 6th career postseason home run during Milwaukee's NLCS sweep of the Los Angeles Dodgers.

That home run didn't just add to his total. It broke a tie and made him the all-time franchise leader in postseason home runs for the Milwaukee Brewers — the most in the history of an organization that has been playing professional baseball since 1969.

He did it in October. He's still young. And the World Series hasn't started yet.

What the Record Means

Franchise postseason records carry a different weight than regular season numbers. The sample size is smaller. The moments are larger. Every home run in October comes in a game that matters more than almost any regular season contest — the kind of game where stress compounds, where opposing pitching is at its best, where the margin for failure is thin.

Chourio's 6th career postseason home run broke a tie with William Contreras, who entered the 2026 playoffs with the same count. Contreras added three home runs in the 2026 playoffs — a strong postseason performance of his own — but Chourio's NLCS homer moved him ahead and into the record books alone.

The most postseason home runs in Brewers franchise history. At his age, with October still happening around him.

The October Profile

Chourio isn't just hitting home runs. He came into the 2026 postseason as one of the game's most dynamic young outfielders — a player whose combination of power, speed, and defensive range made him one of baseball's best players regardless of age. His NLCS performance elevated that profile further.

William Contreras has been his complement in the heart of this lineup. Three playoff home runs from Contreras, six career postseason home runs from Chourio — the Brewers aren't relying on one bat. They're built across the lineup, with Chourio as the lightning rod and Contreras, Brice Turang, Jake Bauers, and others providing depth around him.

The World Series Stage

Chourio's record was set in the NLCS. The World Series hasn't started. Milwaukee begins the Fall Classic on October 23 at American Family Field against whoever wins tonight's ALCS Game 6 between Tampa Bay and Cleveland. The stage gets bigger. The moments get more defined.

Chourio has already shown he can rise to them.

What's Next

The Brewers open World Series play October 23 at American Family Field in Milwaukee, 8 p.m. ET on FOX. It will be Milwaukee's first World Series home game since 1982. Chourio enters it as the franchise's all-time postseason home run leader — a record that will only grow if October keeps going his way.
