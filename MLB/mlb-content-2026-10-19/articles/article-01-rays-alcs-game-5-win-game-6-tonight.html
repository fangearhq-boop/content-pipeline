Rays Beat Cleveland 3-1 in ALCS Game 5, Can Clinch World Series Berth Tonight

By Ryan Calloway | Ballpark Banter | October 19, 2026

Drew Rasmussen walked to the mound at Progressive Field in Game 5 of the American League Championship Series and pitched like someone who knew he belonged on the biggest stage in baseball. Seven innings. One run. Twenty-one outs and a performance that kept Tampa Bay's World Series hopes not just alive but within one victory of becoming real.

Bryan Baker closed it out. Rays 3, Guardians 1.

Tampa Bay leads the ALCS 3-2 and comes home to Tropicana Field tonight for Game 6. One win away from the World Series — and from a date with the Milwaukee Brewers starting October 23.

Rasmussen Delivers When It Counts

Game 5 was not a blowout. The Guardians didn't disappear. But Rasmussen made them quiet, navigating Cleveland's lineup with the kind of command that suffocates even talented offenses when they're chasing in October.

He allowed just one run over those seven innings, a performance that continued what has become the most consistent starting pitching story of the 2026 playoffs. Rasmussen hasn't had a bad inning. He hasn't given games away. He's pitched like the best pitcher Tampa Bay has, which is saying something on a staff that has carried this team through two rounds of the postseason.

Baker took the ball for the final frames and didn't allow Cleveland back into it. Final score: 3-1, Tampa Bay.

How the ALCS Got Here

This series has not been one-sided. Cleveland took a 2-1 lead, winning Games 2 and 3 after Tampa Bay opened with a Game 1 victory. The Guardians looked like a team with momentum heading into the middle innings of this series.

Then the Rays won Game 4. And now Game 5. Back-to-back wins to take a 3-2 series lead heading home to St. Petersburg.

Cleveland came into this postseason having already done the impossible once — the Guardians came back from 0-2 in the ALDS against the Chicago White Sox to advance. That resilience made them dangerous in the ALCS. It still does. They aren't out of it yet. One win on the road tonight and there's a Game 7 Wednesday.

What's at Stake Tonight

Game 6 is at Tropicana Field, and the Rays will be playing in front of a home crowd with a chance to close out the American League pennant. Tampa Bay hasn't appeared in a World Series since 2020 — that year ended with a loss to the Dodgers in six games. This roster, led by Rasmussen and a deep lineup, believes this is the year.

If the Rays win tonight, the World Series against Milwaukee begins October 23 at American Family Field. The Brewers have been waiting and resting for a week since clinching the National League pennant. A 44-year wait for World Series baseball in Milwaukee will finally be over.

If Cleveland wins tonight, Game 7 is Wednesday. The Guardians have pulled off comebacks before.

What's Next

ALCS Game 6 is tonight at Tropicana Field in St. Petersburg. First pitch is expected around 5 p.m. ET (NBC Sports). Tampa Bay will have its crowd, its momentum, and — if the schedule holds — one more Rasmussen start waiting in Game 1 of the World Series if they close it out. The Rays are one win from the Fall Classic. Tonight is the night.
