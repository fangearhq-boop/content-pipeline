Cleveland's Season Comes Down to Tonight: Guardians Face Elimination in ALCS Game 6

By Marcus Cole | Ballpark Banter | October 19, 2026

The Cleveland Guardians have already done the impossible once this October. Down 0-2 in the American League Division Series to the Chicago White Sox, they won three straight games to advance. It was one of the better comeback stories of the 2026 postseason, a resilient team refusing to let the calendar end their season early.

Now they need to do it again.

The Guardians trail the ALCS 3-2 after losing Game 5 at Progressive Field on Sunday, 3-1, to Drew Rasmussen and the Tampa Bay Rays. Tonight, they travel to Tropicana Field for Game 6 — a road elimination game with their season on the line.

What Went Wrong in Game 5

The short answer is Rasmussen. Seven innings. One run. The kind of start that doesn't give a team a path to victory unless their own starter matches it. Cleveland's offense managed one run against Rasmussen and Bryan Baker. In a best-of-seven series, you can survive one quiet night. You can't survive too many of them.

Cleveland had taken a 2-1 ALCS lead after Games 2 and 3. Momentum was with them. Then Tampa Bay won Game 4, then Game 5. Two-game swing, and now the Guardians are one loss from going home.

The Franchise's Long Wait

Cleveland's last World Series championship came in 1948 — 78 years ago, when the franchise was known as the Cleveland Indians. No championship since then. The franchise became the Guardians in 2022, carrying that drought forward under a new name.

This Guardians team is good. José Ramírez is one of the best players in baseball, a reliable offensive force who performs in October. The roster has the pieces to win a championship. This season's ALDS comeback showed they have the character to do it in adversity.

But to make that drought matter this October, they have to win tonight on the road, then win again on Wednesday if they get there. Two games. Two wins. Against a Tampa Bay team that has been the better side in this series since Game 4.

The Case for Cleveland Tonight

Elimination games have their own logic. The pressure equalizes. One great at-bat changes a lineup's momentum. One bullpen inning can swing a game in a way that regular-season baseball rarely does. Cleveland has been here — down 0-2 with their backs to the wall — and they found a way.

Ramírez will need to be huge. The lineup will need to get to Tampa Bay's starter early. The Guardians' own pitching will need to match what their offense gives them. None of those things is guaranteed on the road in an elimination game against a team with home-crowd energy and one win from the World Series.

But October baseball has shown us more improbable things.

What's Next

ALCS Game 6 is tonight at Tropicana Field in St. Petersburg. First pitch around 5 p.m. ET on NBC Sports. A Guardians win forces Game 7 on Wednesday. A Rays win ends Cleveland's season and sends Tampa Bay to the World Series against Milwaukee beginning October 23. For the Guardians, tonight is everything.
