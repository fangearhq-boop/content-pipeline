15 Games to Play: The NL Wild Card Race Comes Down to Petco

By Marcus Cole | September 12, 2026

MLB Standings

Fifteen games remain in the NL Wild Card race, and the picture at the top is complicated in the best possible way. The Philadelphia Phillies and Chicago Cubs are tied for the two secure spots — WC1 and WC2 — while the San Diego Padres hold WC3 by a half game over the Arizona Diamondbacks. Nothing is settled. Everything is in play.

The date that matters most: September 25–27, when the Padres host the Diamondbacks at Petco Park in San Diego. Three games, direct competition, at the site of one of the better ballparks in baseball. If Arizona wins that series, they're likely in. If San Diego takes it, they likely survive. It is the kind of series that, in retrospect, determines who plays in October.

Cubs and Phillies at the Top

Both the Cubs and Phillies enter September 12 with the same record — tied for the first two Wild Card spots. Both programs have earned it. The Cubs have been one of the NL's best teams all season, anchored by Pete Crow-Armstrong's historic individual season and a pitching staff that has held together despite injuries. The Phillies have their own case: consistent rotation depth and a lineup that punishes mistakes.

The tie means tiebreakers matter. How those get resolved if both teams finish with identical records is a scenario worth tracking, but the more likely outcome is that one team edges the other over the final 15 games. Neither club can afford to coast.

The Padres-Diamondbacks Battle

San Diego's half-game lead over Arizona is the thinnest possible margin. The Diamondbacks are not eliminated — they are one game ahead of anyone outside the playoff picture and just two wins from jumping the Padres with the right sequence of results. The September 25–27 series at Petco is the clearest path for a one-series resolution, but 13 games between now and then could change the math entirely before that series even tips off.

For both teams, the message is the same: win the games in front of you, and the Petco series will be what it looks like right now — a decider.

What's Next

The Wild Card Series begins September 29. The bracket will be seeded by final regular season record. Every game from today forward reshapes the seeding and the matchup. Watch the daily results for the Cubs, Phillies, Padres, and Diamondbacks as the season enters its final sprint — and circle September 25–27 on your calendar. Petco Park may decide who gets to play in October.
