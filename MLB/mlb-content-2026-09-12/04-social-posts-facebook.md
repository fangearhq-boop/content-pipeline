⚡ PCA IS 4 STEALS FROM ONE OF BASEBALL'S RAREST SEASONS ⚾

Pete Crow-Armstrong enters today's Pirates series with 41 home runs and 36 stolen bases. He needs 4 more steals in roughly 15 games to join a club with only six members in the entire history of Major League Baseball.

The 40-40 club: José Canseco (1988). Barry Bonds (1996). Alex Rodriguez (1998). Alfonso Soriano (2006). Ronald Acuña Jr. (2023). Shohei Ohtani (2023).

Pete Crow-Armstrong would be No. 7 — and the first player in Cubs franchise history to do it.

He's already making Cubs history on multiple fronts. At 24 years and 165 days old, he is the youngest Cub ever to reach 40 home runs, surpassing Hall of Famer Ernie Banks by 49 days. He's the first Cub to hit 40 home runs since Derrek Lee in 2005. And at age 24, he's just the fourth player ever to reach 40 home runs and 30-plus stolen bases (joining Acuña, A-Rod, and Canseco).

The question isn't if. It's when.

### STORY 2: Alvarez Triple Crown

**FB Long-Form**
Window: 12:30 PM ET

🔥 THE YORDAN ALVAREZ TRIPLE CROWN CHASE IS HEATING UP ⚾

15 games left and Yordan Alvarez is in the middle of a historic individual season — but he doesn't hold all three American League leads yet, and that's what makes this so compelling.

Alvarez leads the AL in batting average at approximately .315. That part of the Triple Crown is his. The HR and RBI races are another story.

In home runs, Junior Caminero of the Tampa Bay Rays leads with 39 — one ahead of Alvarez's 38. In RBI, Pete Alonso of the Baltimore Orioles leads with approximately 97 — two ahead of Alvarez's 95. To win the Triple Crown, Alvarez has to finish ahead of Caminero in home runs AND ahead of Alonso in RBI while holding his batting average lead.

If he does it, he'd be the first AL Triple Crown winner since Miguel Cabrera did it for the Detroit Tigers in 2012 — and only the second since Carl Yastrzemski won it in 1967. That's a 59-year gap, and Alvarez would be closing it.

15 games. Three categories. One legendary outcome on the line.
