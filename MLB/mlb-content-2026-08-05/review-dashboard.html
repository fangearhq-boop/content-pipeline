### Long-Form Post Tarik Skubal's Dodgers debut was exactly what you expected from the pitching — and nothing from the offense. Skubal went six innings at Wrigley Field on Tuesday, touched 99.3 mph, struck out six, and allowed two earned runs. He retired his first six batters. The only real damage: a Dansby Swanson solo home run (Swanson's 17th) on the very first pitch Skubal threw in the third inning, and an oddly-bounced infield hit that plated the go-ahead run in the sixth. The Dodgers gave him four hits of support. They lost 5-1. It was the team's fifth consecutive defeat and a season-high losing streak. Dave Roberts called this trade "Skubal or nothing." The talent is obvious — Skubal is a 99 mph ace with elite command. The team around him just needs to start hitting. They still look like the World Series favorite on paper. They need to start playing like it. --- ### Image Post Caption Skubal delivered. The Dodgers offense did not. 6 IP, 6 K, 99.3 mph — and a loss. #Dodgers ⚾
