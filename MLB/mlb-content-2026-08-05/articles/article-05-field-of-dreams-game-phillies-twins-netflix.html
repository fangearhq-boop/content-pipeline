The Field of Dreams Game Is Eight Days Away — and It's on Netflix This Year

By Marcus Cole

August 5, 2026

The 2026 Field of Dreams Game is scheduled for August 13 in Dyersville, Iowa — and for the first time in the event's history, it will stream live on Netflix. The Phillies play the Twins in the cornfield this year, with an expanded and renovated venue, a loaded Philadelphia roster that just got better at the trade deadline, and Kyle Schwarber somewhere in the neighborhood of 35 home runs with a 60 HR chase conversation that is not going away.

It is an event worth planning around.

The Cast

The Phillies are the right team for this. Kyle Schwarber entered August with 33 home runs, on a pace that multiple outlets have pegged at 60 or more by season's end. He set the Phillies' franchise record with his 30th home run earlier in the season. He led the National League with 56 home runs in 2025. The Field of Dreams game, with its mythology and its nostalgia and its connection to baseball's storytelling tradition, is a perfect backdrop for a home run chase.

Bryce Harper will be in right field for the first time since 2022, a change necessitated by the trade deadline acquisition of Luis Arraez. Arraez — a three-time batting champion with a career .329 average — is now batting ahead of Schwarber and Harper in the Philadelphia lineup. The additions of Arraez and Brooks Raley at the deadline made an already-dangerous lineup more complete. The version of the Phillies heading to Dyersville on August 13 is arguably the best team they have fielded all season.

What Netflix Means for This Game

The Field of Dreams Game has moved through broadcasting arrangements since its debut. This year's Netflix broadcast represents the event reaching a global streaming audience for the first time. Netflix's sports coverage has expanded significantly in recent years; live MLB content on the platform gives the event reach that a traditional cable broadcast cannot replicate.

The game will still be a regular-season MLB contest with standings implications. The Phillies and Twins will both be playing for something. But the setting, the broadcast arrangement, and the cast make August 13 a circled date regardless of where each team sits in the standings by then.

The Twins' Side

Minnesota enters the Field of Dreams Game in the middle of its own playoff push. The Twins made headlines at the trade deadline for not doing more — sitting one game from a playoff spot and making only minor moves — but they are still a team with enough talent to compete for a postseason berth. Facing the Phillies' upgraded lineup at Dyersville will be a test on a stage larger than their usual early-August series.

What's Next

August 13, Dyersville, Iowa. Netflix. Phillies vs. Twins. Schwarber in the cornfield. Circle it on the calendar. The Field of Dreams Game has been one of baseball's genuinely good ideas since its debut, and the 2026 edition has the cast and the platform to be the best version yet. Eight days away.
