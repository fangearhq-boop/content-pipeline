Rays Win ALCS Game 6 — Tampa Bay Is Headed to the World Series

By Jake Torres | October 20, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

The Tampa Bay Rays are going back to the World Series.

Tampa Bay beat the Cleveland Guardians 7-3 in Game 6 on Monday night at Tropicana Field, clinching the American League Championship Series four games to two and booking their ticket to the Fall Classic for the third time in franchise history.

It was not the pitcher most expected on the mound for the clincher. Freddy Peralta drew the start, not Drew Rasmussen — who had been brilliant in the postseason (1.29 ERA over 15 innings) but was being preserved for a potential World Series Game 1 assignment. Peralta did the job: 5.1 innings, one earned run, five hits, and 84 pitches.

Caminero Delivers the Knockout Blow

The game turned in the seventh inning when Junior Caminero drilled a two-run home run off the left-field foul pole, pushing the Rays' lead to 5-1 and effectively ending Cleveland's night. It was the kind of moment that defines October baseball — a big swing at the right time against a desperate opponent.

Richie Palacios had given Tampa Bay the early advantage, doubling in the second inning to put the Rays up 2-0. They never relinquished the lead. Bryan Baker was handed the baseball in the ninth and put three Guardians away in order to finish the clincher.

Before the game, Yandy Díaz had declared publicly that the Rays would win. It was a statement that could have looked foolish. It didn't.

A Franchise Moment

This is Tampa Bay's third World Series appearance, following trips in 2008 (a loss to the Philadelphia Phillies) and 2020 (a loss to the Los Angeles Dodgers in six games). Both of those runs ended without a title. The Rays have never won a World Series.

Their opponent in 2026 will be the Milwaukee Brewers, who completed their own NLCS sweep of the Dodgers earlier this week. The Brewers — who posted the best record in baseball at 103-59 — are also seeking their first World Series championship. The 2026 Fall Classic is guaranteed to produce a first-time champion.

Game 1 is scheduled for Friday, October 23 at American Family Field in Milwaukee, with first pitch at 8 PM ET on FOX.

What's Next

Tampa Bay will travel to Milwaukee for Games 1 and 2 before returning to Tropicana Field for Games 3, 4, and 5. Drew Rasmussen is the probable Game 1 starter for the Rays — a matchup that looks increasingly compelling against Milwaukee's Jacob Misiorowski, who led the National League in ERA and strikeouts during the 2026 regular season.

The Rays have been built for October. The Brewers have been the best team in baseball all year. Friday night, it all begins.
