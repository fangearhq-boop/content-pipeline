Rasmussen vs. Misiorowski: Game 1 Could Be the Pitching Duel of the Year

By Marcus Cole | October 20, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

The 2026 World Series has not officially named its Game 1 starters. But the probable matchup — Tampa Bay's Drew Rasmussen against Milwaukee's Jacob Misiorowski — is worth previewing regardless of when the official announcement comes.

If those two take the mound Friday night at American Family Field, it could be the best-pitched game of the entire postseason.

Drew Rasmussen: Untouchable in October

Rasmussen had a 2026 regular season worth celebrating on its own: 16 wins, five losses, a 2.67 ERA. Two All-Star selections. A workhorse who completed 30 starts across 172 innings.

Then October arrived, and he became something else entirely.

In the ALDS against the Yankees, Rasmussen threw eight innings of one-hit baseball, struck out 10, and didn't allow a run. That performance tied Tyler Glasnow's franchise record for strikeouts in a single postseason game. In the ALCS, he gave Tampa Bay seven innings of one-run ball in Game 5, keeping the Rays' lead in the series at three games to two.

His postseason ERA across 15 innings entering the World Series: 1.29. Rasmussen did not start the ALCS clincher — manager Kevin Cash preserved him for a potential World Series assignment. If he takes the mound Friday, he will have done so on seven days of rest.

Jacob Misiorowski: The NL's Best Arm

Misiorowski led the National League in both ERA and strikeouts during the 2026 regular season, earning his second All-Star selection. He was Milwaukee's most valuable pitcher in a rotation that helped the Brewers win 103 games, and he enters the World Series with 12 days of rest since his last postseason appearance.

At 25, he is one of the most interesting young starters in baseball — a power arm with command well beyond his years. The Dodgers, who lost the NLCS, never solved him.

Like Rasmussen, Misiorowski has never pitched in a World Series game. Friday night at American Family Field would be the biggest start of his career.

What's Next

Official World Series rosters and confirmed pitching assignments are expected before Game 1. Both Rasmussen and Misiorowski are the clear No. 1 options for their respective clubs, and barring any last-minute changes, Game 1 should unfold as the matchup the schedule quietly promised.

Game 1 is Friday, October 23 at American Family Field in Milwaukee, 8PM ET on FOX. If these two take the ball, come ready for a game that could go to the eighth inning before either pitcher allows more than two runs. The Brewers have home-field advantage. Rasmussen has October momentum. Someone has to blink first.
