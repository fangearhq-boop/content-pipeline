Milwaukee's Historic Journey to the Fall Classic

By Jake Torres | October 20, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

They were the best team in baseball from April through October. Now the Milwaukee Brewers have one more series left to prove it.

Milwaukee's path to the 2026 World Series was built on dominance at every level. The Brewers won 103 regular-season games — the best record in baseball — and never slowed down in October. They swept the San Diego Padres in four games in the NLDS. They swept the Los Angeles Dodgers, the defending World Series champions, in four games in the NLCS, completing the run with an extraordinary comeback.

The Brewers face the Tampa Bay Rays starting Friday, October 23 at American Family Field. Milwaukee hasn't hosted a World Series game since 1982.

The NLCS Clincher: A Statement Game

Game 4 of the NLCS may have been the most important game in Milwaukee's modern franchise history. Trailing 4-0 against the Dodgers at Dodger Stadium, the Brewers rallied to win 6-4 and complete the sweep.

Jake Bauers, the first baseman who spent the spring wondering if his career was over, was named NLCS Most Valuable Player. He became the first Brewer ever to win the award. Jackson Chourio hit his sixth career postseason home run during the series, breaking the franchise's all-time record — he surpassed the previous holder, teammate William Contreras, who had recorded three home runs in the 2026 playoffs alone. Brice Turang, who drove in 100 runs during the regular season, contributed throughout.

Manager Pat Murphy called it the kind of team he had been trying to build since his first day in Milwaukee.

The Weight of History

The Brewers have never won a World Series. Their only previous appearance came in 1982, when they lost to the St. Louis Cardinals in seven games — and at the time, Milwaukee was still an American League franchise. That was 44 years ago. The current generation of Brewers fans has never seen their team in the Fall Classic.

The Brewers are one of a handful of MLB franchises that have never hoisted the Commissioner's Trophy. Their opponent, the Tampa Bay Rays, shares that distinction. The 2026 World Series will produce a first-time champion. That is not a small thing for either city.

American Family Field will be full Friday night. The Milwaukee crowd that watched this team win 103 games in the regular season is about to see what October baseball sounds like at full volume.

What's Next

The Brewers are at home for Games 1 and 2 (Oct. 23-24) before traveling to Tampa Bay for Games 3 through 5 (Oct. 26-28). If the series extends to six or seven games, Milwaukee hosts the finale on October 30-31 at American Family Field. All games air on FOX.

After 44 years of waiting, the Brewers have a chance to do what they could not do in 1982. The roster that won 103 games is the one that takes the field Friday night. The history is there to be made.
