2026 World Series Schedule: Every Game, Every Date, How to Watch

By Ryan Calloway | October 20, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

The Tampa Bay Rays and Milwaukee Brewers are set to begin the 2026 World Series on Friday, October 23. The Brewers host Games 1 and 2 at American Family Field in Milwaukee before the series shifts to Tampa Bay for the middle portion. All games air on FOX.

Here is the complete schedule, plus what to watch for in each portion of the series.

Games 1 & 2 — Milwaukee (Oct. 23-24)

Game 1: Friday, October 23 | American Family Field | 8PM ET | FOX
Game 2: Saturday, October 24 | American Family Field | 8PM ET | FOX

Milwaukee hosts the first two games with home-field advantage as the National League champion. The Brewers went 103-59 during the regular season, the best record in baseball. Game 1 is the probable matchup between Drew Rasmussen (Rays) and Jacob Misiorowski (Brewers) — two two-time All-Stars who led their leagues in key pitching metrics during the 2026 season. American Family Field will be at full capacity for the franchise's first World Series game since 1982.

Games 3, 4 & 5 — Tampa Bay (Oct. 26-28)

Game 3: Monday, October 26 | Tropicana Field | 8PM ET | FOX
Game 4: Tuesday, October 27 | Tropicana Field | 8PM ET | FOX
Game 5 (if necessary): Wednesday, October 28 | Tropicana Field | 8PM ET | FOX

Travel day is Sunday, October 25. The Rays host up to three games at Tropicana Field, where they've been nearly unbeatable in the 2026 postseason. Tampa Bay's home crowd has been a factor throughout October. If the series returns to Milwaukee for Games 6 and 7, it's because neither team has put the other away during this stretch.

Games 6 & 7 — Milwaukee (Oct. 30-31, If Necessary)

Game 6 (if necessary): Friday, October 30 | American Family Field | 8PM ET | FOX
Game 7 (if necessary): Saturday, October 31 | American Family Field | 8PM ET | FOX

If the series extends to six or seven games, Milwaukee regains home-field advantage for the finale. A Game 7 on Halloween would be one of the more memorable settings in recent postseason history. The last team to host a winner-take-all Game 7 at home and lose was the Dodgers in 2017.

What's Next

Both the Rays and Brewers are seeking their first World Series title. The series will be the first since 2003 (Marlins-Yankees) in which both participants are first-time champions. Every game airs on FOX with no subscriber paywall — the entire series is available on broadcast television.

Official pitching assignments and World Series roster moves are expected before Game 1. The series starts Friday night. Neither team is going home without a fight.
