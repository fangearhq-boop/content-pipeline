Pete Alonso Sits at 299 Career Home Runs — One Swing From a Milestone

By Jake Torres | September 23, 2026

The number 300 is sitting right there, one swing away.

Pete Alonso enters the final week of the 2026 MLB regular season with 299 career home runs. It is the kind of round-number milestone that baseball fans naturally fixate on — clean, significant, and symbolic of everything the New York Mets first baseman has represented since arriving in the majors.

Six Straight Seasons With 30-Plus Home Runs

Alonso's 2026 season added 35 home runs to his career total — his sixth consecutive campaign with at least 30. That kind of sustained power production is genuinely rare in the modern game. Injuries derail careers. Pitching adjustments humble hitters. Aging curves steal bat speed. Alonso has navigated all of it and kept producing year after year.

Six straight 30-HR seasons. For a first baseman who has defined himself as one of baseball's premier power hitters, this streak is the clearest evidence of his consistency.

The Milestone in Context

300 career home runs is not just a number — it's a benchmark of career achievement. Reaching it puts Alonso in a tier of sustained offensive production that speaks for itself. And reaching it during the final week of a regular season means that if it happens, it will likely happen in front of a baseball audience paying attention.

Alonso has always been a player who seems to rise to the moment. His Rookie of the Year performance was a statement. His home run derby victories were exhibitions of pure power. Career homer No. 300 would be another moment to add to the collection.

What's Next

The Mets play out the final week of the regular season, and every Alonso at-bat carries the weight of a career milestone sitting one swing away. Whether he gets it before the final out of the regular season or carries the total into a potential postseason, No. 300 is coming. The only question is when. Don't look away from Alonso's at-bats this week.
