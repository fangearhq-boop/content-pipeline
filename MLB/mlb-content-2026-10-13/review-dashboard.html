### Long-Form Post The Dodgers have not been in this position in years. Down 2-0 in the NLCS, heading home to Dodger Stadium for a game they essentially must win. Their answer: Blake Snell. Snell missed most of the 2026 season with elbow surgery. When he came back, he was transcendent — four wins, a 1.13 ERA, 50 strikeouts in just 39.2 innings across eight starts. By the end of the regular season he was the most effective pitcher in baseball. His command was sharp, his movement was elite, and opposing hitters couldn't touch him. The Brewers send Robert Gasser (5-5, 3.87 ERA, 103 strikeouts in 109.1 innings) as their Game 3 starter. He is a solid left-hander who induces weak contact and doesn't walk people. He does not, however, have Snell's ceiling. Wednesday night at Dodger Stadium feels like a potential turning point — or a coronation. If Snell is the pitcher he was during his post-surgery run, the Dodgers have a path back into this series. If Milwaukee's lineup handles him the way they handled the rest of the NLCS so far, the conversation turns to how quickly the Brewers can close it out. ### Long-Form Post There are baseball teams that know how to win, and then there are baseball teams that seem to know something the rest of the sport doesn't. The 2026 Cleveland Guardians are the second kind. In the ALDS against the Chicago White Sox — the team that made the most improbable playoff run in years, going from back-to-back 100-loss seasons to October baseball — Cleveland dropped the first two games at Progressive Field. It looked like the script was writing itself: White Sox continue the miracle run. The Guardians didn't read the script. Cleveland won three straight. Game 3 in Chicago. Game 4 at home. And Game 5 on October 10 in front of their own fans — a 2-0 shutout featuring 21 strikeouts by Cleveland pitchers, an all-time postseason record for a nine-inning game. Gavin Williams alone struck out 12. José Ramírez hit the go-ahead home run in the fourth inning. It was historic in every way. They are the 11th team ever to come back from 0-2 in a best-of-five series. The last team to do it was the 2017 Yankees — who did it against the Guardians. Then came the ALCS. Game 1 at Tampa Bay. Rays -170 favorites. Cleveland trailing 2-0 early. Guardians 7, Rays 3. Comebacks are not an accident with this team. They are a feature. ### Image Post Caption 11th team ever to come back from 0-2 in the ALDS. 21 strikeouts in Game 5 (postseason record). Now up 1-0 in the ALCS. The Guardians don't stop. 🔥
