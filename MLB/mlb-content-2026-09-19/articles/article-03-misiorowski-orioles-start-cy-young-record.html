Misiorowski Starts in Baltimore: Cy Young Lock Chases Brewers Strikeout Record

By Marcus Cole | Ballpark Banter | September 19, 2026

Jacob Misiorowski takes the mound at Camden Yards on Saturday, carrying the best ERA in baseball, a near-certain Cy Young Award, and a chase for the Milwaukee Brewers' all-time strikeout record in a single season.

There is no mystery about who wins the 2026 National League Cy Young Award. The only question left surrounding Jacob Misiorowski's historic season is how many more milestones he can add before October arrives.

On Saturday, the 24-year-old takes the mound at Camden Yards against the Baltimore Orioles — another opportunity to add to a line that already leads the majors in ERA (1.89), WHIP (0.81) and strikeouts (approximately 243). Another start in which the Brewers franchise strikeout record gets a little closer.

The Record Within Reach

Ben Sheets set the Milwaukee Brewers franchise record for strikeouts in a single season in 2004, when he struck out 264 batters. It stood as one of the defining statistical achievements in Brewers history for more than two decades. Misiorowski needs approximately 21 more strikeouts to surpass it.

With his typical workload through the final weeks of the season, 21 strikeouts in the remaining starts is entirely realistic. Misiorowski has averaged well over eight strikeouts per nine innings this season and has reached double digits in a single outing multiple times. The Sheets record is not a ceiling — it is a number Misiorowski is expected to pass.

What Makes This Season Historic

The ERA and the strikeouts tell most of the story. But the number that truly separates Misiorowski's 2026 season from anything the sport has seen is the 100-mph pitch volume. On September 8, he became the first pitcher in MLB history — from the beginning of Statcast tracking — to throw 1,000 pitches at or above 100 miles per hour in a single season. He did not just reach that threshold. He cleared it by a wide margin, throwing more than 1,050 such pitches by that point in the season.

His recorded top speed this year: 105.5 mph. The fastest pitch by any starting pitcher in the Statcast era. For context, the average fastball velocity in Major League Baseball is approximately 94-95 mph. Misiorowski is operating in a different physical universe than the pitchers across from him on any given night.

The NL Cy Young betting odds reflect that reality: Misiorowski sits at -700. The next closest contender, Chris Sale of the Braves, is listed at +500. A -700 favorite in a two-player race is not really a race. It is a formality waiting to be completed in November.

The Brewers' Championship Context

Misiorowski's individual achievements are unfolding against a backdrop of team success. The Brewers claimed the National League Central Division title for the fourth consecutive season — the first team to do so since the division format was established in 1994. They are positioned to earn the No. 1 seed in the National League, which comes with a first-round bye.

For the Brewers, Misiorowski's October availability is the most important variable. A rotation anchored by a pitcher with a sub-2.00 ERA, a WHIP under 0.85 and triple-digit velocity on every fastball is not something opposing lineups can adequately prepare for. Milwaukee's postseason ceiling rises significantly in any series that features him on the mound.

What's Next

Misiorowski's start today against the Orioles is one of his final opportunities to add to his strikeout total before the postseason begins. If he reaches 264, the Brewers franchise record is his. If he surpasses it, a new benchmark is set in Milwaukee — one that could stand for another two decades. Then October begins, and the numbers become secondary to the outcomes they help produce.
