This is what great organizations build toward: depth that doesn't flinch when the star player isn't available. The Yankees have that depth. And if Judge makes it back for the ALDS, this team just becomes even more dangerous.

Red Sox fans: tomorrow must be different. The series is best-of-3. It's not over. But Wednesday showed the gap between these two teams right now.

### STORY 3: White Sox 6, Astros 3

**FB Long-Form (12:30 PM ET)**
⚾ Let's talk about what just happened in Houston.

The Chicago White Sox won a playoff game.

Not just any playoff game. A playoff game ON THE ROAD, in Minute Maid Park, against an Astros team that has made the postseason 9 times in 10 years.

Here's what makes this so extraordinary: the White Sox went 41-121 in 2024 — the most losses by any MLB team in the modern 162-game era. They followed that with 60-102 in 2025 and 61-101 in 2023. Three straight 100-loss seasons. No team in MLB history had ever done that and made the playoffs the following year. Until the 2026 Chicago White Sox.

Opening Day playoff odds? 1.1%. They clinched September 24 by beating the Royals 9-1.

And on Tuesday night, Colson Montgomery hit a 432-foot solo home run to dead center field — his 31st of the season — in the 4th inning. Sam Antonacci and Chase Meidroth added RBI hits. The Sox scored 6 runs on the most improbable stage imaginable.

This isn't just a baseball story. It's a story about a city that didn't give up, a young roster that believed when nobody outside that clubhouse did, and an organization that rebuilt from scratch in the middle of one of the most painful rebuilds in sports history.

The Astros won't go down easy. But Game 1 belongs to Chicago. 🖤⚪

### STORY 4: Padres 8, Cubs 0

**FB Long-Form (3:00 PM ET)**
⚾ Michael King was seven outs away from immortality.

The San Diego Padres ace took a NO-HITTER into the 7th inning Tuesday night. With one out in the 7th, Nico Hoerner hit a soft infield single — the Chicago Cubs' ONLY hit of the entire game — to end the bid. King finished with 7 innings, 8 strikeouts, and multiple standing ovations from the San Diego crowd.

His no-hit bid was the longest individual postseason no-hit attempt since Aníbal Sánchez went 7 2/3 innings for the Nationals in the 2019 NLCS.

And it wasn't even the first highlight of the night.

On the very first pitch of the game, **Fernando Tatis Jr.** hit a leadoff home run — the first leadoff playoff home run in Padres franchise history. Manny Machado added another HR in the 1st inning and an RBI double in the 3rd.

By the time Hoerner ended the no-hit bid, San Diego had a 7-0 lead. The final was 8-0.

The Cubs, who had the best record in the NL Central during the regular season, were held to one hit. They now face Kevin Gausman (on the mound tonight at 9PM ET) in a must-win Game 2.

The Padres aren't just alive in October. They look like a World Series team.
