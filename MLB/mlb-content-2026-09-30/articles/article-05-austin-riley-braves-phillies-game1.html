Austin Riley's 8th-Inning Three-Run Homer Lifts Braves Past Phillies, 5-3

By Jake Torres | September 30, 2026

With two outs in the 8th, Riley crushed a three-run home run off All-Star closer Jhoan Duran to break a tie game. Atlanta takes the series lead. Philadelphia — one of the NL's best offensive teams — faces must-win Game 2 at 1:00 PM ET.

At some point in every playoff series, someone makes a play that defines the narrative. In the 2026 Wild Card Game 1 between the Phillies and Braves, that play came from Austin Riley in the 8th inning, and it changed everything.

The Phillies had battled all night at Truist Park in Atlanta. One of the most dangerous offensive teams in the National League, they had stayed in the game deep into the middle innings, and the score was close when Philadelphia turned to Jhoan Duran in the 8th. Duran, who had been one of the best closers in the American League during the regular season, was brought in to hold the game.

Riley hit his first pitch into the stands for a three-run home run.

The final score was 5-3. Atlanta leads the series, 1-0.

The Moment and Its Weight

Riley's home run wasn't just timely — it was the kind of at-bat that crystallizes why postseason games are played differently than regular-season games. Two outs. A tie game or tight deficit. One of the game's best closers on the mound. And Riley put a charge into a ball that the crowd at Truist Park will remember for a long time.

The third baseman has a history of performing in big moments, and Tuesday's home run added another chapter to that reputation. He has been one of Atlanta's most consistent contributors for years, and when the Braves needed someone to step up in a tight Game 1, Riley was the answer.

Philadelphia's Perspective

The Phillies are not a team that folds. They have one of the best lineups in the National League, a deep bullpen, and a roster full of players who have been through postseason battles. Philadelphia's decision to go to Duran was a reasonable one — he is that good, and the Phillies needed him to hold the game.

It didn't work. One swing from Riley changed the math entirely.

Philadelphia now faces Game 2 on September 30 at Truist Park at 1:00 PM ET. It is a must-win situation, but the Phillies have the talent to win in Atlanta. They need a better performance from the offense against whoever the Braves start, and they'll need to protect late leads better than they did in Game 1.

What's Next

Game 2 is today at 1:00 PM ET in Atlanta. Both teams know what's at stake. A Braves win closes out the series and sends them to the NLDS. A Phillies win forces a deciding Game 3. The Braves won Game 1 on the strength of one of the most compelling individual moments of Wild Card round. Now they need to close it out in front of their home crowd.

Austin Riley has already done his part. Who steps up next?
