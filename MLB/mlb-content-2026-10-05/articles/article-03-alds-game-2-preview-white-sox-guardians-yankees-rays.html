ALDS Game 2: White Sox Look to Go Up 2-0 While Yankees Try to Survive Without Judge

By Ryan Calloway | October 5, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

The American League Division Series continues Sunday with a pair of games that will define the direction of both series. The Chicago White Sox, coming off a Game 1 win, host Cleveland at 5 PM ET on truTV. An hour later, the New York Yankees, looking to even a series they lost the opener of, face the Tampa Bay Rays at Tropicana Field at 8 PM ET on TBS. Tyler Glasnow is expected to take the mound for Tampa Bay. Aaron Judge remains unavailable for New York.

White Sox vs. Guardians — 5 PM ET, Progressive Field

Chicago leads the ALDS 1-0 after a dominant Game 1 performance that leaned heavily on the White Sox pitching staff. Hagen Smith, 23, started that game and the organization had to like what it saw. The White Sox enter Game 2 in position to swing the series decisively in their direction — a 2-0 lead in a five-game series is a position of enormous advantage.

Cleveland is not a team that rolls over. The Guardians have won close games all year and play a brand of baseball that grinds opponents down. Their lineup will make things uncomfortable at some point. But the White Sox have the home crowd, the momentum, and a rotation that, while young, has been effective throughout the postseason.

This game feels like a swing moment. A Chicago win puts the Guardians in a difficult spot. A Cleveland win levels the series and resets everything before the scene shifts.

Yankees vs. Rays — 8 PM ET, Tropicana Field

New York is in trouble. The Yankees lost Game 1 and now face a must-win situation on the road, in a building that has always been difficult for visiting teams. Tampa Bay is getting Tyler Glasnow — an ace-caliber pitcher on a night when the Rays need to go up 2-0 and hold serve before heading to Yankee Stadium.

The bigger story for the Yankees is Aaron Judge. The New York captain has been dealing with a calf strain and remains unavailable. A lineup without Judge is a different lineup, and the Rays know it. Glasnow can be overpowering when his fastball and curveball are working together. New York's offense will need to manufacture something without their most important hitter in the order.

If the Yankees lose tonight, they return home having to win three of the next four games. A 2-0 deficit against Tampa Bay, with Glasnow having pitched one of those games, is a challenging hole to climb out of.

The Bigger Picture

Sunday afternoon and evening will give the American League picture much more clarity. Either the White Sox go up 2-0 and position themselves for a potential series clinch, or the series remains tight. Either the Yankees get a road win to level their series, or they return home backed against the wall.

Both games matter. Both have championship stakes attached from the very first pitch.

What's Next

Games 3 and 4 of both ALDS series shift to the home stadiums of the teams currently trailing — Progressive Field for Cleveland is no longer a factor, as Game 3 moves to Guaranteed Rate Field in Chicago, and New York prepares to host Tampa Bay at Yankee Stadium. Scores and reactions from tonight's games will drive all of Monday's coverage. The American League playoff picture sharpens tonight.
