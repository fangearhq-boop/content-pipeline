Misiorowski Strikes Out Seven Straight to Open, Posts 12-K Game in Brewers' 11-2 Win

By Marcus Cole | Ballpark Banter | August 23, 2026

Jacob Misiorowski delivered one of the most dominant performances in Milwaukee Brewers history on Sunday. He struck out the first seven Colorado Rockies batters consecutively — a Brewers franchise record — and finished with 12 strikeouts in five innings as the Brewers demolished the Rockies 11-2 at American Family Field.

By the end of his start, the statistic that defined the afternoon was not the strikeout total but the velocity. Misiorowski threw 33 fastballs at 103 miles per hour or above. Per CBS Sports, that is more than twice the previous single-game record since pitch tracking began.

The Historic Opening

Misiorowski retired the first 14 Colorado batters he faced, 12 of them via strikeout. The run of seven consecutive strikeouts to open the game established a new Brewers franchise record immediately.

The only blemish came at batter number 15: a solo home run by Cole Carrigg — struck on a 102 mph fastball. It was a reminder that even at historically elite velocity, a mistake pitch will occasionally connect. Carrigg earned every inch of it.

After the Carrigg homer, Misiorowski continued to work efficiently. His final line — 5 IP, 1 H, 1 ER, 0 BB, 12 K, 83 pitches (59 strikes) — reflects a starter who was in command of his stuff from the first pitch to the last.

Where This Ranks in the Record Books

Sunday was Misiorowski's ninth game this season with 10 or more strikeouts — tying the Brewers single-season record previously set by Corbin Burnes in 2022. That record now stands as a co-mark, with the season still ongoing and Misiorowski in the rotation every five days.

The velocity story is the element that separates this start from even other elite pitching performances. Throwing 33 pitches at 103 mph or above in a single outing is not just a personal achievement — it is the kind of number that reshapes what analysts believe is physically possible in a major league game. The previous record had held since pitch tracking systems became reliable enough to count them.

The Cy Young Case

Misiorowski entered Sunday as the -550 favorite in Cy Young Award odds. He entered with a 1.76 ERA, 204 or more strikeouts, and a 0.74 WHIP. Sunday added 12 more strikeouts to a season total that is building toward one of the most impressive individual seasons in Brewers pitching history.

The Brewers are now 80-49, the best record in baseball. Their ace is having a season that defies easy comparison. The Cy Young conversation at this point is not about whether Misiorowski wins — it is about whether 2026 ends up being one of the all-time single-season pitching performances in the modern era.

What's Next

Misiorowski's next start comes in approximately five days as the Brewers continue their stretch run toward the NL Central title they have already clinched in the standings. He is 9 games into double-digit strikeout performances this season with weeks remaining. The franchise record is already his. What else he adds before October is still being written.
