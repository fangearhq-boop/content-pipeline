### STORY 1: Pete Crow-Armstrong — The 40-40 Chase

**Facebook Post**
There are 6 players in baseball history who have hit 40 home runs and stolen 40 bases in the same season: José Canseco, Barry Bonds, Alex Rodriguez, Alfonso Soriano, Ronald Acuña Jr., and Shohei Ohtani.

Pete Crow-Armstrong of the Chicago Cubs could become No. 7.

Entering tonight's game, PCA sits at 42 home runs and 37 stolen bases. Three more steals and he joins one of the most exclusive clubs in the sport. He is also the first Cub to reach 40 home runs since Derrek Lee did it in 2005 — more than two decades ago.

The Cubs are holding the NL Wild Card 2 spot, so every game matters for both reasons. Keep watching, folks. History is on the line.

### STORY 2: Yordan Alvarez — Triple Crown Slipping Away?

**Facebook Post**
Yordan Alvarez leads the American League in batting average at .311. That's the first leg of what could be the rarest individual achievement in baseball: the Triple Crown.

The problem? He doesn't lead in the other two categories. Tampa Bay's Junior Caminero leads in home runs. Baltimore's Pete Alonso leads in RBI. And Alvarez has been in a prolonged slump since August 2 — hitting just .242 with 3 home runs and 12 RBI in 35 games.

With approximately 12 games left, the window is closing fast. The last player to win the AL Triple Crown was Miguel Cabrera in 2012. Alvarez has the talent. He doesn't have much time.

### STORY 4: Jakob Misiorowski — Cy Young Frontrunner

**Facebook Post**
Jakob Misiorowski is 24 years old, and he is having one of the most remarkable pitching seasons any Brewers fan has ever watched.

The numbers: 19 wins, 8 losses, a 1.95 ERA and 236 strikeouts — all three of those lead Major League Baseball. He is the Cy Young Award frontrunner at -700 odds, with his closest competitor at +500.

But the stat that may define his 2026 season most: Misiorowski is the first pitcher in the Statcast tracking era — going back to 2015 — to throw 1,000 pitches at 100 miles per hour or faster in a single season. That has never happened before.

He also needs just 28 more strikeouts to break Ben Sheets' franchise record of 264, set in 2004. With several starts remaining, that record is very much within reach.

What a season. #Brewers
