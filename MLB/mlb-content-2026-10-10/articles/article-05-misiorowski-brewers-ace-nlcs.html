Jacob Misiorowski: The Brewers Ace Who Earned His NLCS Moment

By Ryan Calloway | October 10, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

Before Saturday night, before Game 1 of the NLCS against the Los Angeles Dodgers, you should know the name Jacob Misiorowski. He went 11-5 with a 1.76 ERA in the 2026 regular season. He is the reason the Milwaukee Brewers won 103 games and earned the No. 1 seed in the National League. And he is pitching in the biggest game of his career tomorrow night.

In a postseason that has already been defined by narrative — the 81-81 Astros, the Rays sweeping the Yankees, the Dodgers chasing a third straight World Series — Misiorowski has been the steady hand at the center of everything Milwaukee does. The Brewers have Trevor Megill to close games. But Misiorowski is how they get there.

The 1.76 ERA in Context

A 1.76 ERA over a full MLB season is exceptional in any era. It requires not just talent but durability, consistency, and the ability to make adjustments when opposing hitters start to understand what you're doing. Misiorowski did all of it in 2026, and the result was one of the better pitching seasons in the National League in recent memory.

The Dodgers' lineup is among the best in the sport. Shohei Ohtani. Freddie Freeman. A rotation of professional at-bats that will test every element of Misiorowski's repertoire. This is the exam he has been preparing for all year without knowing exactly what form it would take.

Why Milwaukee Gets Overlooked

The Brewers play in Milwaukee. They do not have the media market, the brand sponsorships, or the coast-to-coast fan base that drives national attention toward the Dodgers, the Yankees, or the Cubs. They build quietly, develop pitching carefully, and win games in ways that do not always generate highlights.

The 2025 NLCS was a loss to the Dodgers. That result gave this team a reference point: they know what it takes to compete in October against Los Angeles, and they know where the gaps were. Misiorowski returns to that stage in 2026 as a better pitcher than he was a year ago. The results say so. The 1.76 ERA says so.

What's Next

NLCS Game 1 is Saturday, October 11, at American Family Field in Milwaukee, 8 PM ET on FOX. Misiorowski is expected to start for the Brewers. Whether he faces Snell, Yamamoto, or — if Roberts makes the call — Ohtani on the mound for Los Angeles, he will be the story of the evening. Pay attention to Misiorowski. He has earned it.
