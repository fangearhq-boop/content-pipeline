NLCS Preview: Dodgers vs. Brewers — Snell's Return, Misiorowski's Moment, and the 3-Peat Chase

By Marcus Cole | October 10, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

The National League Championship Series begins Saturday night in Milwaukee, and the matchup delivers exactly what October should: a defending champion going for three straight, an underdog city that has built something worth rooting for, and pitching staffs that are capable of winning any game on any night. Los Angeles Dodgers vs. Milwaukee Brewers. Game 1 at American Family Field, October 11, 8 PM ET on FOX.

The Brewers own the No. 1 seed in the National League. The Dodgers have Shohei Ohtani, Freddie Freeman, and one of the deepest rosters in baseball. This series will be decided in the margins — bullpen usage, lineup construction, and which ace shows up sharp when the moment is biggest.

The Snell Factor

Blake Snell missed significant time in 2026 following elbow surgery to remove loose bodies. His return to the Dodgers' rotation was one of the postseason's most anticipated storylines, and so far he has delivered. Snell started NLDS Game 2 against Atlanta — a signal from Dave Roberts that the ace is ready and fully trusted in high-leverage playoff situations.

Yoshinobu Yamamoto is also projected in the NLCS rotation. The Dodgers' depth, even accounting for Miguel Rojas' back and hip injury that kept him out of NLDS Game 4 (he remains on the active roster), is the defining feature of this group. Los Angeles did not get to a 3-peat conversation by being fragile.

Shohei Ohtani's pitching status is the remaining question. Roberts said publicly there are "no limitations" on Ohtani, but no specific NLCS pitching assignment was confirmed ahead of Game 1. If Ohtani pitches in this series, it changes the conversation entirely.

The Brewers' Case

Milwaukee's argument starts with Jacob Misiorowski, whose 2026 season — 11-5, 1.76 ERA — was one of the best in the National League. The Brewers have closer Trevor Megill providing elite late-game security. And Milwaukee won 103 games to earn the No. 1 seed, meaning they also have the home field advantage for the first two games and for Games 6 and 7 if the series goes that long.

The Brewers reached the NLCS in 2025 and lost to the Dodgers. This is a rematch. They know the opponent. They know what it takes to compete with Los Angeles. The 2025 loss did not break this team; it gave it a blueprint for what needs to be different.

What's Next

NLCS Game 1 is Saturday, October 11, at American Family Field in Milwaukee at 8 PM ET on FOX. Games 3 through 5 shift to Los Angeles, with Games 6 and 7 returning to Milwaukee if necessary. The series will determine which team goes to the World Series seeking either a Dodgers three-peat or Milwaukee's first pennant in multiple decades.
