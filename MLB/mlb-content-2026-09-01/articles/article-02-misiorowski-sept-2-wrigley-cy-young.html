Misiorowski Takes the Mound at Wrigley Tomorrow. The Cy Young Race Watches.

By Jake Torres

September 1, 2026

Jacob Misiorowski pitches at Wrigley Field on Wednesday, and the matchup has been circled since the schedule dropped. The 24-year-old Milwaukee Brewers left-hander — 13-5, 1.73 ERA, 222 strikeouts, 0.755 WHIP — faces the Cubs, whose lineup includes the NL MVP frontrunner in Pete Crow-Armstrong and one of the deepest offensive cores in the National League.

It is the most anticipated pitching matchup of the second half. And it happens in front of a Wrigley crowd that will not be quiet about it.

What Misiorowski Has Done

The numbers are historic. Misiorowski leads the NL in strikeouts with 222 across his 2026 starts. His 1.73 ERA is one of the lowest single-season marks in the NL in a generation. His 0.755 WHIP is exceptional at any level. Advanced metrics confirm the underlying quality: 240 ERA+, 2.14 FIP, 13.2 strikeouts per nine innings.

Two historic markers stand out. He is the first pitcher since Doc Gooden to open a season with 25 consecutive starts allowing three earned runs or fewer. He is also the second-fastest pitcher in MLB history to reach 200 strikeouts in a single season. Those are the kinds of comparisons that do not get applied casually.

The third MLB.com Cy Young poll of the 2026 season placed Misiorowski at the top of the NL race. There are no serious challengers at this stage of the calendar.

The Velocity Question

One subplot has followed Misiorowski through the second half: a documented drop in his average fastball velocity, from 100.5 mph earlier in the season to 98.7 mph in recent starts. The Brewers' front office has taken note. Misiorowski has not offered significant public comment, and his results have not declined.

The velocity dip is not a crisis. A pitcher who averages 98.7 mph on his fastball is still among the hardest throwers in professional baseball. But in the context of a Cy Young race with 28 games remaining, any deviation from peak form invites scrutiny. If the Cubs' lineup catches anything below his best, the Sept. 2 start becomes a data point in a different kind of story.

The Matchup Context

Misiorowski's start places him directly against the team with the NL's most watched player. PCA's August — 14 HR, 1.098 OPS — makes the Cubs lineup a legitimate test. From a national-audience standpoint, this is the pitching-vs.-offense storyline the final month of the season is built around.

The Brewers have effectively wrapped up the NL Central. The Cubs are competing for Wild Card seeding. Both teams have something at stake. Neither is playing loose baseball heading into September. The Sept. 2 start is worth watching on multiple levels.

What's Next

Jacob Misiorowski starts at Wrigley Field on Wednesday, September 2. He enters the start 13-5 with a 1.73 ERA, 222 strikeouts, and a 0.755 WHIP — the NL Cy Young frontrunner with 28 regular-season games remaining. His velocity, his ability to navigate the Cubs' lineup, and his pitch count through six or seven innings will be among the first questions answered when September's rotation logs begin to fill in.
