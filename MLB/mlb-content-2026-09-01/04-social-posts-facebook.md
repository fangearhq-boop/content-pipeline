### STORY 3: Alvarez Triple Crown

**Long-form post:**
Yordan Alvarez is leading all three American League Triple Crown categories entering September.

Batting average: .322 — 14 points ahead of Chandler Simpson.
Home runs: 36 — one ahead of Junior Caminero and Ben Rice.
RBI: 91 — tied with Pete Alonso.

The most vulnerable category is home runs. One HR from either Caminero or Rice could put them ahead. Alvarez had a slow August in the power department, and that gap has closed from where it was in July.

If Alvarez can hold all three leads across the next 28 games, he'd become the first Triple Crown winner since Miguel Cabrera did it in 2012. That's 14 years without a winner. That's how rare this is.

He's 29. This might be the peak of his career. September decides whether it becomes historic.

**Caption:**
.322 BA. 36 HR. 91 RBI. Yordan Alvarez is leading all three Triple Crown categories. 28 games left. The last winner was Cabrera in 2012. ⚾

### STORY 5: Chourio Among Legends

**Long-form post:**
Jackson Chourio is 22 years old and already in legendary company.

With his 20th home run of the 2026 season, Chourio became only the 17th player in MLB history to post 20+ home runs in each of his first three big-league seasons. The others on that list include Ted Williams, Eddie Mathews, Mickey Mantle, Frank Robinson, and Orlando Cepeda — all Hall of Famers.

He's also the second player in Brewers franchise history to accomplish the feat, joining Ryan Braun.

Chourio was born in March 2004. He's been in the major leagues for three years. And he's already doing things that most players never get to put on a resume, let alone at 22.

**Caption:**
20+ HR in each of his first 3 seasons. Ted Williams territory. Jackson Chourio is 22 years old. #Brewers ⚾
