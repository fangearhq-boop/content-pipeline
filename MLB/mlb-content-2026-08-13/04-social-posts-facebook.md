## STORY 1: Field of Dreams Game Day

**Long-Form Post 1**
Tonight is the night. The Field of Dreams Game is finally here.

The Minnesota Twins host the Philadelphia Phillies at the permanent stadium in Dyersville, Iowa — first pitch at 7:30 PM ET, streaming exclusively on Netflix. Pre-game coverage begins at 6:30 PM.

This is the third edition of one of baseball's most cinematic events. After a three-year hiatus to rebuild the original temporary venue into a permanent stadium, the Field of Dreams Game is back — and the 2026 edition is the biggest yet.

Hall of Famers Mike Schmidt and Steve Carlton will represent Philadelphia in the pregame. Joe Mauer and Rod Carew will be there for Minnesota. Kevin Costner — the star of the 1989 film that started all of this — will appear in the Netflix broadcast.

And the uniforms: the Twins will wear their 1961 inaugural season pinstripes, the very first look they wore as a Minnesota franchise. The Phillies will be in their 1939-41 gray road jerseys, complete with a centennial patch. No robot ump tonight either — MLB confirmed no ABS challenge system is installed at Dyersville.

It's baseball at its most deliberately beautiful. And tonight it streams to a global audience on Netflix for the first time.

## STORY 3: MLB HR Race — Four-Way Tie at 35

**Long-Form Post 3**
The home run race in 2026 just got a lot more interesting.

As of August 12-13, four players are tied at the top of the major league home run leaderboard with 35 each: Kyle Schwarber (Philadelphia Phillies), Yordan Alvarez (Houston Astros), Matt Olson (Atlanta Braves), and Junior Caminero (Tampa Bay Rays).

That's four players. Four different teams. Four different positions. All at 35.

Schwarber — the NL leader — has been chasing history all season. He hit 56 home runs in 2025, leading the National League. Earlier this year, he became just the 18th player in MLB history to reach 20 home runs in a team's first 49 games. And tonight, he's playing in the Field of Dreams game in Dyersville, Iowa, under the Netflix lights, wearing 1939-41 Phillies road grays.

The race is wide open. Any of the four could pull away in August.

## STORY 5: Skubal 0-1 in Two Dodger Starts

**Long-Form Post 5**
Tarik Skubal is still looking for his first win as a Los Angeles Dodger.

In two starts since his trade from Detroit, the two-time AL Cy Young Award winner has been solid but unlucky, then solid but shakier. Start one (August 4 at Wrigley Field against the Cubs): 6 innings, 4 hits, 2 earned runs, 6 strikeouts, 85 pitches, a loss. The Dodgers managed only one run. Start two (August 10 at Dodger Stadium vs. the Royals): 5 innings, 4 hits, 3 earned runs, 6 strikeouts, 95 pitches, no-decision. The Dodgers rallied to win 6-5 without him.

Personal record: 0-1. Team record in his starts: 1-1.

There's nothing catastrophically wrong here. Skubal is posting six strikeouts in both starts. But command of the fastball has been "spotty" by his own admission, and the Dodgers gave him no run support in his debut. His next start is coming.

For context: Skubal recorded his 1,000th career strikeout on July 29 while still pitching for the Tigers. The milestone belongs to his Detroit career. The next chapter is still being written in blue.
