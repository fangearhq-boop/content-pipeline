Tonight's the Night: The 2026 Field of Dreams Game Is Finally Here

By Ryan Calloway

August 13, 2026

For weeks, the Field of Dreams Game has been the event on the calendar — previewed and re-previewed, the throwback uniforms examined, the Hall of Famers confirmed, the no-ABS ruling dissected. Tonight, the buildup ends. The Minnesota Twins host the Philadelphia Phillies in Dyersville, Iowa, with first pitch at 7:30 PM ET. Pre-game coverage on Netflix begins at 6:30 PM.

It's the first MLB regular-season game ever streamed on Netflix. And in Dyersville — where a 1989 Kevin Costner film about ghosts and second chances gave baseball one of its most enduring myths — the 2026 edition of this event carries the weight of all that setup.

The Setting

The game is being played at the permanent stadium built roughly a quarter-mile west of the original movie-site diamond. The new venue holds approximately 8,000 fans, including bleachers, with a right-field wall that frames windows looking out onto 159 acres of Iowa cornfield. This is the third edition of the Field of Dreams Game — the first was 2021 (White Sox vs. Yankees), the second was 2022 (Cubs vs. Reds), and then a three-year hiatus while the temporary setup was replaced by a structure meant to last.

The permanent stadium is the difference this time. The original Dyersville site was always temporary, a pop-up in a cornfield. What exists now is an intentional venue, built to host this game for years to come.

The Uniforms

Both clubs will suit up in throwback designs that lean into the vintage aesthetics of the event. The Minnesota Twins will wear their 1961 inaugural season pinstripes — the original blue-and-red "Twins" script on the chest, with a vintage navy cap carrying the classic "TC" logo. These are the uniforms the franchise wore in its very first year of existence in Minnesota, before a single winning season had been played.

The Philadelphia Phillies will be in their 1939-41 gray road jerseys, complete with a centennial patch on the sleeve. The design is simple and early-era, the kind of uniform that looks like it belongs in a black-and-white photograph rather than a Netflix broadcast.

That contrast — pinstripes from the early 1960s against flannel-gray jerseys from the late 1930s — is part of what the Field of Dreams Game does that no other event in the baseball calendar attempts.

The Hall of Famers

The pregame scene in Dyersville tonight will include some of the sport's most recognizable figures. Mike Schmidt and Steve Carlton will represent the Phillies organization — a franchise built on those two pillars across the late 1970s and early 1980s. Joe Mauer and Rod Carew will be there for Minnesota. And Kevin Costner — the star of the 1989 film, the voice of the tradition — will be part of the Netflix broadcast in some capacity.

These aren't ceremonial cameos. The players and figures gathered in Dyersville tonight carried actual weight in the sport. The event earns the Hall of Famers it gets.

One More Detail: No Robot Ump

MLB confirmed earlier this week that the Automated Ball-Strike (ABS) challenge system will not be installed at the Dyersville stadium for tonight's game. Neither team can challenge ball-and-strike calls via ABS.

The Phillies, reportedly, have declined to use the ABS system throughout the 2026 season anyway. In the most-watched game of the regular season, on a Netflix platform new to live baseball, they'll play under purely traditional umpiring — the same way they've been playing all year.
