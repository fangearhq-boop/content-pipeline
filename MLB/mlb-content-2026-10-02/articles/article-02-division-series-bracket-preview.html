The 2026 Division Series Is Set -- Here Is Your Complete Bracket and Saturday Schedule

By Jake Torres

October 2, 2026

Ballpark Banter

The Field of Eight Is Finalized

The Wild Card round is complete. All four series have been decided, and baseball now knows exactly who will compete for the final four spots in October. The 2026 Division Series begins Saturday, October 3, with a four-game slate spread across both leagues. Here is everything you need to know about the bracket, the matchups, and where to watch.

American League Division Series

AL1 Rays vs. AL4 Yankees -- Game 1: Saturday, Oct. 3, 6:30 p.m. ET

The New York Yankees swept the Boston Red Sox 2-0 in the Wild Card, outscoring them 18-2 over two games -- and they did it without Aaron Judge appearing in either contest. The Tampa Bay Rays earned the AL's top seed and come in rested after watching the Wild Card from home. The Yankees carry momentum, but face a steep climb against the league's best team over five games.

AL2 Guardians vs. AL6 White Sox -- Game 1: Saturday, Oct. 3, 1 p.m. ET, TBS, Progressive Field

The Chicago White Sox swept the Houston Astros 2-0 (6-3, 7-3) for their first playoff series win since 2005. They now face the Cleveland Guardians, who went 24-11 from August 20 onward -- one of the best late-season records in baseball. Cleveland did not stumble into the Division Series; they earned it with elite pitching and timely hitting down the stretch.

National League Division Series

NL1 Brewers vs. NL4 Padres -- Game 1: Saturday, Oct. 3, 8:30 p.m. ET

San Diego swept the Chicago Cubs in two Wild Card games and now travels to Milwaukee, where the Brewers hold home-field advantage as the NL's top seed. The Padres have the offensive firepower to compete, and the late-night Saturday start sets up one of the most anticipated games of opening weekend.

NL2 Dodgers vs. NL3 Braves -- Game 1: Saturday, Oct. 3, 4 p.m. ET, Fox

Atlanta eliminated Philadelphia 6-2 in Game 3 of the Wild Card and now faces the Los Angeles Dodgers, who hold home-field advantage as the NL's second seed. Home runs from Michael Harris II, Ozzie Albies, and Matt Olson powered the Braves on Wednesday, and that lineup depth will face one of baseball's best rotations in the Fox afternoon window.

Wild Card Round Recap

Four series were played. Three were quick. One went the distance.

The Padres were the most efficient team in the Wild Card round, dispatching the Cubs in back-to-back games without ever allowing the series to become complicated. The Yankees did the same to the Red Sox, winning by a combined 18-2 and never requiring Aaron Judge to play a single inning. The White Sox followed the same formula against Houston, winning comfortably in both games to snap a two-decade drought without a postseason series victory.

The only series that required three games was the Braves-Phillies matchup, a back-and-forth contest that Philadelphia appeared to have flipped in its favor after Alec Bohm's 10th-inning walk-off in Game 2. Atlanta answered emphatically on Wednesday, and the result was a decisive elimination that sent the Phillies home and the Braves back to the Division Series.

Saturday Schedule at a Glance

Four Division Series games are scheduled for Saturday, October 3:

  • AL: White Sox at Guardians, 1 p.m. ET, TBS, Progressive Field, Cleveland
  • NL: Braves at Dodgers, 4 p.m. ET, Fox, Los Angeles
  • AL: Yankees at Rays, 6:30 p.m. ET, Tampa Bay
  • NL: Padres at Brewers, 8:30 p.m. ET, Milwaukee

What's Next

The 2026 Division Series begins Saturday, October 3, with all four series opening on the same day. Fans will have baseball from early afternoon into the late-night hours, with something at stake in every game. Each series is best-of-five, meaning teams that win Game 1 on Saturday will carry a significant advantage. The next step toward the World Series starts this weekend.
