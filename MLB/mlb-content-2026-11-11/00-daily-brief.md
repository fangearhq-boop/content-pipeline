### STORY 1: Skubal Meetings Begin — Six Teams, West Coast Preference, Record Deal Looming
**Tier:** 1 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP
AP reported Tuesday that Tarik Skubal is set to meet with six teams, including the Mets, Dodgers, and Padres. Three additional teams were not named. MLB insider Jim Bowden said on Sunday that Skubal "prefers the West Coast," pointing to the Dodgers, Padres, Angels, and Diamondbacks as natural fits — though Bowden added the Mets could overbid everyone. The Cubs are "badly" pursuing him per ESPN's Passan, and the Yankees are "thinking about" a push. ESPN's Kiley McDaniel projects an 8-year, $352 million deal — an average annual value of $44 million that would shatter Yoshinobu Yamamoto's $27M AAV record for a pitcher. The CBA expires December 1, creating urgency for all parties.

### STORY 2: Rangers Exploring Corey Seager Trade — $157.5M Deal, Full NTC Complicates
**Tier:** 1 | **Classification:** NEW
The Texas Rangers are planning to explore Corey Seager trades at the GM Meetings in Las Vegas, per Ken Rosenthal and Evan Grant. Texas GM Chris Young wants the 2027 payroll to look "very similar" to 2026 ($193 million), and Seager's contract — $157.5 million over five years remaining — is the primary target for relief. Seager hit .227 with a .736 OPS in 2026, career lows. He has full no-trade protection. Teams named as possible fits: Yankees (though Joel Sherman pushed back on the fit), Blue Jays, Red Sox, Dodgers, and Braves.

### STORY 3: Teruaki Sato Market — Padres Emerge as Latest Contender
**Tier:** 2 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP
The Padres have emerged as the latest team linked to NPB Triple Crown winner Teruaki Sato, per The Athletic (November 10). The previously reported contenders — Mets, Yankees, Dodgers, and Phillies — remain in play. Sato was posted November 1 by the Hanshin Tigers, opening a 45-day window that expires December 16. Contract projections range from Jon Heyman's $80 million/4 years to ESPN's $115 million/5 years. The critical complication: if a deal is not finalized before December 1, the CBA expiration freezes negotiations and Sato must return to Japan.

### STORY 4: QO Decision Week — 8 Players Have Until Nov. 17 to Decide
**Tier:** 2 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP
Eight players tendered $23.15 million qualifying offers have until 4 p.m. ET on November 17 to accept or reject. The list: Seiya Suzuki (Cubs), Randy Arozarena (Mariners), David Bednar (Yankees), Jake Bauers (Brewers), Trevor Rogers (Orioles), Ryan Jeffers (Twins), Brandon Lowe (Pirates), and Adrian Morejon (Padres). Historically, only 18 of 157 players offered qualifying offers have accepted. All eight major names are expected to decline. Acceptance triggers a one-year stay; rejection means the signing team forfeits a draft pick.
