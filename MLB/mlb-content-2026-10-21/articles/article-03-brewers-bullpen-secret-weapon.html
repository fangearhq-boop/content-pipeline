The Brewers' Best-Kept Secret: A Bullpen With a 0.38 ERA in the Playoffs

By Ryan Calloway | Ballpark Banter

The national conversation about the Milwaukee Brewers centers on Jacob Misiorowski — the NL Cy Young frontrunner with 339 strikeouts and a 2.51 ERA in 2026. That's fair. He's the best pitcher in baseball.

But if the Brewers win the 2026 World Series, the relief corps may deserve equal credit.

The Numbers Are Historic

Through the NLCS, Milwaukee's bullpen posted a 0.38 ERA in 23.2 innings — the 4th best postseason ERA for any bullpen in the Wild Card era. That's not just good. That's a historically rare performance at the most pressurized stage of the baseball calendar.

The four names at the center of it:

DL Hall has thrown 8.2 innings in the 2026 playoffs without allowing a single run. That's not a small-sample fluke — Hall has been used in critical leverage situations throughout October and delivered every time. Aaron Ashby has logged 12.1 shutout innings in postseason play. Thyago Uribe posted a sub-2.00 ERA in each of the last two regular seasons and is the team's primary bridge arm. Devin Megill handles closing duties.

Why This Unit Matters More Than Normal

Most World Series teams lean heavily on their ace. Milwaukee's plan requires the bullpen to carry more than half of every Misiorowski start. With the ace averaging just 4-4.5 innings, four-plus innings of every game belong to this group.

And they've handled it with a 0.38 ERA.

The Rays would love to grind through Misiorowski's limited outing and feast on a tired bullpen. Tampa Bay's lineup is patient, contact-driven, and designed to wear down pitching staffs. But there's no fatigued Brewers bullpen waiting for them this October.

What's Next

Game 1 of the World Series is Friday, October 23, at 8:07 PM ET at American Family Field in Milwaukee on FOX. If Misiorowski exits after four innings — as expected — watch how quickly this bullpen can extinguish any Tampa Bay momentum. The Fall Classic may turn on exactly that.
