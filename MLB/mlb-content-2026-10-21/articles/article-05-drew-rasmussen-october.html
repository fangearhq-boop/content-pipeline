Drew Rasmussen's Historic October: The Most Dominant Postseason Pitcher in Baseball

By Jake Torres | Ballpark Banter

When the argument for Tampa Bay winning the 2026 World Series is made, the first name that comes up isn't Junior Caminero's big swing or the Rays' patient lineup. It's Drew Rasmussen.

Through 15 innings in the 2026 postseason, Rasmussen has posted a 1.29 ERA. He's been the most dominant starting pitcher in baseball this October — and on Friday night, he gets the biggest stage of his career.

The ALDS Statement

In ALDS Game 1 against the New York Yankees, Rasmussen was untouchable. He carried a no-hitter through more than six innings against one of the best lineups in the American League. The final line: 10 strikeouts, a 1-0 Rays win, and a statement performance that set the tone for Tampa Bay's entire postseason run.

That game wasn't a fluke — it was Rasmussen operating at the level his regular season had hinted at. His 2026 numbers: 16-5, a 2.67 ERA, and a second All-Star selection. The ace-caliber stuff was there all along. October confirmed it.

Why He's Tampa Bay's Best Chance at Winning Game 1

The Brewers are home at American Family Field, where they went 4-0 in the 2026 playoffs. That's a significant advantage. But if Rasmussen pitches anything like he did against the Yankees — deep into the game, limiting contact, putting zeros on the board — he can take the crowd out of it early.

Milwaukee will counter with Jacob Misiorowski, the NL Cy Young frontrunner. The matchup of Rasmussen versus Misiorowski on the Game 1 mound is as good as October pitching gets.

What's Next

Game 1 of the 2026 World Series is Friday, October 23, at 8:07 PM ET at American Family Field in Milwaukee on FOX. Drew Rasmussen starts for Tampa Bay. Game 2 features Framber Peralta facing Milwaukee's Shintaro Henderson. If the Rays are going to steal a game in Milwaukee to start this series, Rasmussen is the reason they can.
