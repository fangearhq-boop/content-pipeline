Misiorowski Gets 12 Days of Rest Before World Series Game 1 — Milwaukee's October Blueprint

By Ryan Calloway

October 18, 2026

Jacob Misiorowski has 12 days to get right before the Milwaukee Brewers need him most.

The 22-year-old right-hander, who led the Brewers' rotation with a 1.80 ERA and 252 strikeouts during the regular season, is set to start World Series Game 1 at American Family Field on Friday, October 23. First pitch is scheduled for approximately 8 p.m. Eastern on FOX. The Brewers' home field will host the first two games of the Fall Classic, marking Milwaukee's first World Series home game in 44 years.

The Benefit of Rest

Misiorowski's NLCS outings were effective but efficient. He needed 94 pitches in 3⅔ innings in Game 1, and the Brewers' bullpen — led by DL Hall and Trevor Megill — picked up the remainder while holding the Dodgers in check. The pattern held across the series: Misiorowski got through the early innings, and Milwaukee's deep relief corps handled the rest.

With 12 days between his last start and Game 1, the mechanical concerns that produced those short outings may reset entirely. A fresh Misiorowski — throwing his high-velocity fastball with full movement — is one of the more difficult matchups in postseason baseball. MLB.com has described him and Logan Henderson as a "dynamic 1-2 duo" in the rotation, and Henderson is expected to start Game 2 on Saturday, October 24.

Home-Field Advantage

The Brewers went 103-59 in the regular season, the best record in the National League, and earned home-field advantage for the World Series. American Family Field will host Games 1, 2, 6, and 7 of the series. Games 3, 4, and 5 will be played at the American League champion's park — either Tropicana Field in St. Petersburg or Progressive Field in Cleveland, depending on who wins today's ALCS Game 5.

The Brewers will play their first two World Series home games before traveling to face their opponent in what promises to be a hostile environment. Coming home for Games 6 and 7 — if the series goes that far — gives Milwaukee a significant structural advantage.

44 Years in the Making

The last time Milwaukee appeared in the World Series was 1982. The Brewers lost to the St. Louis Cardinals in seven games. Since then, entire generations of Milwaukee fans have watched other cities celebrate championships while their team rebuilt, retooled, and came close without breaking through.

American Family Field will be loud on October 23 in a way that hasn't been heard since the Reagan administration. Misiorowski, who was born in 2004, will pitch in front of a crowd that has been waiting longer than he's been alive.

What's Next

World Series Game 1 is Friday, October 23 at American Family Field in Milwaukee. First pitch approximately 8 p.m. Eastern on FOX. Misiorowski starts for Milwaukee. The American League opponent is determined tonight when the Rays and Guardians play ALCS Game 5 at 5 p.m. ET in Cleveland. The full World Series schedule runs through a potential Game 7 on Saturday, October 31.
