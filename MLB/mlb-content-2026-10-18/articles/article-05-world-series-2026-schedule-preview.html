2026 World Series: Your Complete Schedule, Broadcast Guide, and Story

By Jake Torres

October 18, 2026

The 2026 World Series is five days away.

The Milwaukee Brewers — winners of the National League Championship Series after sweeping the Los Angeles Dodgers in four games — will host the American League champion beginning Friday, October 23, at American Family Field. The AL opponent is determined tonight when the Tampa Bay Rays and Cleveland Guardians play ALCS Game 5 at 5 p.m. Eastern. Everything you need to know about the Fall Classic is below.

Full World Series Schedule

All games are on FOX at approximately 8 p.m. Eastern. Game 1 in Spanish broadcasts on FOX Deportes and Univision. Streaming available on FOX One and FOXSports.com with a TV provider login.

Game 2: Saturday, October 24 — American Family Field, Milwaukee

Game 5: Wednesday, October 28 — AL Champion's Park (if needed)

If the Tampa Bay Rays win tonight's ALCS Game 5, Games 3 through 5 will be played at Tropicana Field in St. Petersburg. If the Cleveland Guardians win, Games 3 through 5 move to Progressive Field in Cleveland.

The Milwaukee Story

The Brewers haven't played a World Series home game since 1982 — 44 years ago. The last time Milwaukee hosted the Fall Classic, Ronald Reagan was president, Michael Jackson's "Thriller" hadn't been released yet, and most of the current Brewers roster wasn't born. The city has been waiting a long time.

The Brewers earned home-field advantage by posting the best record in the National League: 103-59. They'll open the World Series at American Family Field with a crowd that has been counting down this moment for four decades.

Jacob Misiorowski — who led the Brewers rotation with a 1.80 ERA and 252 strikeouts in the regular season — starts Game 1. Logan Henderson, described alongside Misiorowski as MLB.com's "dynamic 1-2 duo" in the Milwaukee rotation, starts Game 2 on Saturday.

Why This Series Matters

Beyond the matchup, this World Series carries the weight of two compelling narratives.

For Milwaukee, it's a franchise that has never won a championship — that came closest in 1982, losing to St. Louis in seven games, and has spent the decades since cycling through rebuilds without another trip to the Fall Classic. This year's 103-win team swept one of the most expensive rosters in baseball on the way here. If the Brewers win, it ends the longest active title drought in the National League.

The AL story won't be settled until tonight, but either outcome — a Rays title that would be the franchise's first, or a Cleveland championship 75 years in the making — carries its own weight.

What's Next

Tonight's ALCS Game 5 at Progressive Field airs at 5 p.m. Eastern on TBS and HBO Max. The World Series begins Friday, October 23, at 8 p.m. Eastern on FOX. The series runs through a potential Game 7 on Halloween, October 31, at American Family Field in Milwaukee.
