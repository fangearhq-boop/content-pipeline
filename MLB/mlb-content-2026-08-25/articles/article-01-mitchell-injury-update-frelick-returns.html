Brewers Get Best-Case News on Mitchell — Forearm Contusion, No IL, Frelick Returns

By Marcus Cole | Ballpark Banter | August 25, 2026

Twenty-four hours after the play that had Brewers fans holding their breath, Milwaukee has the outcome it needed. Garrett Mitchell's post-game X-rays following Sunday's Little League Classic came back negative. The official diagnosis is a right forearm contusion. No IL placement has been announced. And Sal Frelick, who has been on a rehabilitation assignment for a right shoulder strain, is expected to return to the roster during this week's series against the Mets.

The Brewers needed both of those things to be true at the same time, and today they are.

What Happened in Williamsport

Mitchell was hit by a Tyler Mahle fastball — 94 mph — on the right forearm while squaring to bunt in the fourth inning of Sunday's Little League Classic at Historic Bowman Field. He stayed in briefly to run the bases after the hit by pitch, but he was eventually removed from the game. The Brewers described the news as "best-case given his history" — which communicates something about the level of concern in the clubhouse before the X-ray results came in.

A right forearm contusion for a position player is not nothing. There's bruising, potential swelling, and a recovery timeline that needs monitoring. But it is the outcome the Brewers were hoping for when they waited on results. No fracture. No structural damage. The worst-case scenarios are off the table.

Frelick's Return Adds Depth

The timing of Sal Frelick's return from his Triple-A rehabilitation assignment is not accidental. The right shoulder strain that sent him to the IL had been a season-disrupting injury, and the Brewers were cautious about rushing him back. The August 25-27 series against the Mets provides the right context — competitive games against a non-division opponent with enough runway left in the season to integrate him before October arrives.

Frelick's return does something specific for Milwaukee beyond raw roster depth. It gives the Brewers a capable outfield option in case Mitchell needs any additional rest as he recovers from the contusion. You don't have to shut Mitchell down to manage the situation prudently. You have another player who can handle the workload if needed.

The Bigger Picture

The Brewers are 81-49. They have the best record in baseball. Jacob Misiorowski is the runaway NL Cy Young favorite. The NL Central is effectively settled. This organization is building toward October with the presumption of home-field advantage throughout the postseason — and now they're doing it with their outfield intact.

Mitchell played through a scary moment in a game that had national television attention and the eyes of the baseball world on it. The result — a contusion, no IL placement, and a returning outfielder in Frelick — is about as good as the Brewers could have scripted for the 48 hours after the Little League Classic.

What's Next

The Brewers continue their series against the Mets through August 27. Mitchell's status will be monitored day to day as the contusion heals. Sal Frelick is expected to be activated during the series, pending final roster decisions. Jacob Misiorowski's next start is expected around August 26-27. Milwaukee's 81-49 record remains the standard by which every other team in baseball is measuring itself.
