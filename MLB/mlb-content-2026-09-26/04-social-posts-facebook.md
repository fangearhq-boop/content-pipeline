Saturday's schedule: Rangers at Twins, 3:10 PM ET. Astros at Oakland, 8:40 PM ET.

If Houston wins Saturday, it's over. If Texas wins and Houston loses, the race goes to the final day Sunday. Watch Saturday night closely. ⚾

### STORY 2: Yordan Alvarez Triple Crown

#### Long-Form Post — 10:30 AM ET

Yordan Alvarez is doing something that hasn't been done in the American League in 14 years — and the final weekend of the season will decide whether he gets there.

Alvarez enters Saturday's games with a .311 batting average, 40 home runs, and 102 RBI. Those are numbers that, in most seasons, would make him the runaway AL MVP. In 2026, they might make him the first Triple Crown winner since Miguel Cabrera turned the trick with the Tigers in 2012.

The complication: Junior Caminero of the Tampa Bay Rays leads the AL with 41 home runs, putting Alvarez one HR behind in that category. For Alvarez to win the Triple Crown, he'd need to hit at least one more HR than Caminero over the final two games — while maintaining his leads in batting average and RBI.

No Houston Astros player has ever won the Triple Crown. The franchise's entire history, from the Colt .45s through four decades of Astros baseball, has produced some of the greatest hitting seasons in baseball history. Alvarez winning would add his name to a very short list — the last player to win the AL Triple Crown before Cabrera was Carl Yastrzemski in 1967.

The race is tight. The games matter. Watch Alvarez and Caminero this weekend. ⚾

This matters enormously for New York. Judge is the Yankees' most dangerous hitter, the face of the franchise, and the player opposing pitchers fear most. A Wild Card Series against Boston with Judge in the lineup is a completely different equation than one without him.

The regular season ends Sunday. The Wild Card Series begins Tuesday. There are very few days between those two moments. Expect an update from the Yankees sometime this weekend. 🗽
