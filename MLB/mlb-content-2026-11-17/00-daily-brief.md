## Today's Top Stories

### STORY 1: QO Deadline Day — Arozarena Declines, Suzuki Set to Follow, Full Free Agency Opens
**Tier:** 1 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP (QO deadline previewed Nov 12–16; TODAY is the climax day)
Today at 4 PM ET is the qualifying offer deadline — the last day for all seven remaining players to accept or decline their $23.15M one-year offers. Arozarena has confirmed his decline per BeisbolFR.com reports. Suzuki's agent confirmed he will decline at the GM Meetings. Bednar, Rogers, Jeffers, Lowe, and Morejon are all expected to follow suit. With Bauers already signed ($58M/4yr Red Sox), this may be the last QO cycle in MLB history — CBA expires December 1 and the MLBPA has signaled it wants QOs eliminated in the next agreement. Full free agency opens for all seven players at 4 PM ET.

### STORY 2: Skubal at the Finish Line — Mets Favorite, $400M Deal Must Come Before Dec. 1
**Tier:** 1 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP (Skubal covered every day Nov 11–16; California meetings ongoing)
California meetings are ongoing. The Mets are "probably the favorite" per Jon Heyman (New York Post); a high-powered Mets contingent made an in-person pitch in Orange County. Six finalists remain: Mets, Dodgers, Padres, Blue Jays, and two unnamed teams. Dodgers GM Brandon Gomes confirmed interest. Contract ceiling: $400M per ESPN; $44M AAV floor shatters Yamamoto's $27M record. Skubal turns 30 this month and wants to sign before the Dec. 1 CBA expiration. Clock is ticking for the biggest pitcher contract in MLB history.

### STORY 3: Owners Meetings Day 1 — Labor Gap Wide, 14 Days to Freeze or Deal
**Tier:** 2 | **Classification:** FOLLOW UP (CBA countdown covered Nov 12–16; owners meetings start today Nov 17-19)
Day 1 of the November 17-19 owners meetings. CBA expires December 1, exactly 14 days from today. Owners are pitching a salary cap and 5-year maximum contract length. MLBPA has repeatedly refused a cap in any form. No CBA progress reported from any outlet. Commissioner Manfred has pledged a lockout if no deal lands by Dec. 1. Last lockout (Dec. 2021–Mar. 2022): 99 days. The 2027 season is at risk.

### STORY 4: Jazz Chisholm — Blue Jays Frontrunner, 11 Teams Engaged, in-Person Meetings This Week

Personal meetings expected this week.

### STORY 5: 14 Days to Freeze — The Pre-Lockout Sprint Is On
**Tier:** 3 | **Classification:** NEW STORY (synthesis of pre-lockout transaction pace)
The CBA expires December 1 at 11:59 PM ET — exactly 14 days from today. If a lockout follows (widely expected), all transactions freeze. That means teams have 14 days to complete the offseason moves they can't finish during a work stoppage. Key unsigned players: Tarik Skubal (biggest pitcher FA in history), Jazz Chisholm (11 teams), Yoshinobu Yamamoto (posted Nov 1, 45-day window closing Dec 16), all 7 QO players now fully free. Teams most pressured to act before the freeze: Cubs (need an entire rotation), Orioles (need SP), Yankees (replacing Chisholm), Red Sox (locked in with Bauers, need SP). Expect the next 14 days to be the most active transaction period since the 2021 lockout.
