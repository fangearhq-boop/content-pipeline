Ohtani Expected Back in Dodgers Lineup Today — Pitching Return in 2026 Ruled Out

By Marcus Cole | September 7, 2026

Los Angeles Dodgers

Dave Roberts says Shohei Ohtani is expected back in the Los Angeles Dodgers lineup Monday against the Cincinnati Reds, ending a four-game absence that raised the volume on what had already been a concerning late-season stretch for one of baseball's most watched players.

The return as a designated hitter comes with a caveat that has been building for weeks: Ohtani will not pitch again in 2026. The combination of left knee soreness, right bicep discomfort, and neck issues has convinced the Dodgers and their medical staff that activating him on the mound this season carries more risk than any potential postseason benefit can justify.

Roberts was direct about it. The 2027 rotation is where the pitching version of Ohtani re-enters the conversation.

The Hitting Slump That Preceded the Absence

Ohtani's bat has been a significant concern independent of his injury status. Since August 18, he is 7-for-51 — a .137 batting average across that stretch, accompanied by 20 strikeouts. That is a severe slump for any player; for Ohtani, it has coincided with the physical discomfort that ultimately kept him out of four consecutive games.

Whether the physical issues caused the slump or the slump revealed the physical issues is the kind of chicken-and-egg question that does not fully answer itself until the player is back in game action. His return Monday will provide the first real data point since September 3.

What the Dodgers Need

Los Angeles is safely positioned in their division at roughly 82-57. The playoff seed is secure. What the Dodgers need from Ohtani in September is a productive DH who can round back into form before the Wild Card Series begins September 29.

His presence matters structurally — the lineup protection he provides shifts how opposing pitchers approach the batters around him. A fully engaged Ohtani in the lineup is a different offensive threat than a Ohtani managing physical discomfort through at-bats.

The four-day rest appears to have been the plan, not the problem. Roberts scheduled it, communicated confidence in a Monday return, and managed expectations around the pitching side clearly and early. What comes next is how quickly Ohtani's bat catches up to his health.

What's Next

Ohtani returns to the Dodgers lineup Monday vs Cincinnati. With roughly 21 games remaining, the focus shifts entirely to his offensive production. The Dodgers will be cautious — no unnecessary physical risk with the postseason this close — but expect an active, present DH as Los Angeles builds toward October. His pitching availability in 2027 will be addressed after the season.
