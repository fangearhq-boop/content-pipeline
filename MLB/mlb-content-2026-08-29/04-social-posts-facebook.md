### STORY 5: Chourio Milestone Chase
**Tier 3 — FB Long-Form + FB Image Caption**

**FB Long-Form Post**
Window: 6:00 PM ET

Jackson Chourio is about to do something that has only been done three times in National League history.

The 22-year-old Milwaukee Brewers outfielder (born March 11, 2004) needs just one more home run to join Orlando Cepeda, Cesar Cedeño, and Andruw Jones as the only NL players to reach 60 career home runs, 50 stolen bases, and 200 career RBIs — all before their 23rd birthday.

He doesn't turn 23 until March 11, 2027. He still has all of September.

Chourio's August 27 game — 4-for-5, 2 HR, 4 RBI, 4 runs in Milwaukee's 8-2 win over the Mets — was the performance that put him within one swing of history. That was his fifth career multi-homer game and his third of the 2026 season.

What makes the milestone remarkable isn't just the number — it's the combination. Home runs, stolen bases, and RBIs are three different skill sets. To rack up all three in volume before age 23 is genuinely rare. Cepeda (1950s-60s), Cedeño (1970s), and Jones (1990s) were three of the most explosively talented young hitters the league has ever seen.

Chourio is playing to join that list. And he needs one home run to do it.
