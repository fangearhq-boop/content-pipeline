Phillies Fall, Padres Hold: The NL Wild Card Race Has 14 Games Left

By Ryan Calloway September 13, 2026

Phillies Lose in 11

Saturday in the National League: the Atlanta Braves beat the Philadelphia Phillies 6-5 in 11 innings on a Drake Baldwin walk-off double. The Phillies hold one of the top two NL Wild Card spots alongside the Cubs. They are not in danger of falling out of the field from one loss. But gut-punch extra-inning defeats in September are the kind of result that fuel anxiety in a fanbase watching every game with playoff implications in mind.

The Phillies still hold their position entering Sunday. What Saturday's loss does is serve as a reminder that the NL Wild Card field is not settled, and that no team in it is immune to a bad night that costs a game they needed.

Padres Hold the Third Spot

The San Diego Padres hold the third and final NL Wild Card position entering Sunday. They are half a game ahead of the Arizona Diamondbacks, who are fighting for the same spot. The Padres have not been able to create significant separation from the D-backs — the race has stayed within one game for most of the past two weeks.

The most consequential series left on the NL Wild Card schedule is September 25-27, when the Padres host the Diamondbacks at Petco Park in San Diego. Three games between the two teams competing for the same playoff spot, with roughly one week remaining in the regular season. A Padres sweep would effectively close the race. A D-backs sweep would force a decisive final week. A split would leave both teams fighting into the final days of September.

What Fourteen Games Means

The Wild Card Series begins September 29. With 14 games left, the teams currently in the field have more runway to lose than to gain — but they also have more runway to collapse. The margin between being in and being out is small enough that a three-game losing streak at the wrong moment can reshape the entire picture.

For Cubs and Phillies fans, the primary concern is maintaining what they have while watching the Padres-D-backs situation resolve itself. For Padres and D-backs fans, every result in every game between now and September 28 matters — their own and each other's.

What's Next

Sunday's results across NL Wild Card matchups will update a picture that is still in motion. The Phillies need a bounce-back. The Padres need to protect their half-game cushion. The D-backs need to keep the pressure on. And the Cubs, comfortable in their Wild Card spot, are watching the race that could determine who they face on September 29. Fourteen games from now, three of four teams in this conversation will be playing October baseball.
