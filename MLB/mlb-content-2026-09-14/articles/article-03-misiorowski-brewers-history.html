Jacob Misiorowski Is Pitching the Greatest Season in Brewers History

By Marcus Cole | September 14, 2026

At 24 years old, in his second full Major League season, Jacob Misiorowski is doing something the Milwaukee Brewers have never seen — and something that has never been done in the tracking era of Major League Baseball.

Misiorowski is 19-8 with a 1.95 ERA, the best among all qualified starters in MLB. He has 236 strikeouts. He is the heavy favorite for the National League Cy Young Award. And on September 8, he became the first pitcher since tracking began in 2008 to throw 1,000 pitches at 100 miles per hour or faster in a single season.

One thousand pitches. All of them triple digits. It has never happened before.

The Numbers in Context

A 1.95 ERA ranks among the best single-season marks in recent Brewers history. Through 25 or so starts, Misiorowski has maintained that figure in a season where even elite pitchers often see their ERA tick upward in September.

His strikeout rate speaks to overpowering stuff. Elite power pitchers typically accumulate 200+ strikeouts in a full season — Misiorowski has 236, and the season isn't over. More significantly, his fastball velocity has stayed at historic levels even as the season has extended into September, a time when most hard throwers see a natural decline.

The 1,000-pitch milestone at 100+ mph is its own category. Most MLB pitchers with elite velocity throw a few hundred such pitches in a full season. Misiorowski doubled that before September even arrived.

Chasing Brewers History

Ben Sheets set the Milwaukee Brewers' all-time strikeout record with 264 in 2004. Misiorowski, at 236, needs 28 more strikeouts to break it. With the Brewers in position to win the NL Central and hold the No. 1 seed in the NL playoffs, Misiorowski will get more starts — and more opportunities.

Sheets' 2004 season is remembered as one of the best in Milwaukee history. Misiorowski is threatening to surpass it, in wins, ERA, and now potentially strikeouts, in the same campaign.

The Cy Young voters will need to split the difference between this and any compelling AL campaign, but in the National League, there isn't much competition at the top. Misiorowski is the clear frontrunner.

What's Next

Misiorowski's next starts will be crucial for both the Brewers' playoff seeding and his personal milestones. The NL Cy Young race appears largely settled in his favor. The Brewers' franchise strikeout record is within reach. Watch his next start for evidence of whether the September workload is affecting his velocity — or whether, like everything else this season, he's the exception to every rule.
