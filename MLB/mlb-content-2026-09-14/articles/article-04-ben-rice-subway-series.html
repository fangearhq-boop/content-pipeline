Ben Rice Puts Yankees Ahead in Subway Series Rubber Game, Career-High 38 HR With 13 Games Left

By Jake Torres | September 14, 2026

NEW YORK — Ben Rice hit a solo home run in the first inning Sunday afternoon, and the New York Yankees rode Cam Schlittler's dominant six innings to a 2-0 victory over the Mets in the Subway Series rubber game, clinching the September series.

The win lifted the Yankees, and the home run lifted Rice to 38 on the season — a career high. The first baseman set his previous best at 26 home runs last year, only his second MLB campaign (his debut was June 18, 2024). He now needs two more in 13 remaining games to reach 40 home runs, a threshold no one expected him to reach when spring training broke.

Schlittler Continues Cy Young Case

Cam Schlittler was the story on the mound: six innings, one hit, no runs. His AL Cy Young campaign has been built on outings exactly like this one — quiet, dominant, and efficient. Schlittler and Toronto's Dylan Cease are in the tightest battle in either league for the pitching award, and Sunday's performance added another data point to Schlittler's case.

Austin Wells added a solo home run to provide the Yankees' second run, giving the offense all the support Schlittler would need.

Judge Chasing 400 Career Home Runs

Aaron Judge returned to the lineup on September 8 after a 99-day absence due to a stress fracture in his rib — an injury that cost him the heart of the season. He came back with 17 home runs in 2026 and a career total of 385, needing 15 more to reach the 400 milestone.

Judge is a three-time AL MVP (2022, 2024, 2025) and the American League's all-time single-season home run record holder (62, set in 2022). Reaching 400 career home runs with 13 regular-season games remaining and a potential postseason run ahead may be a tall order this October, but the goal is now visible.

For the Yankees, the immediate focus is September. Rice's 38 home runs give New York a power narrative alongside Schlittler's Cy Young push. Both stories play out over the next two weeks — and potentially longer if the Yankees advance in the postseason.

What's Next

The Yankees have 13 games remaining in the regular season. Rice needs two home runs to reach 40, Judge needs 15 to reach 400 career, and Schlittler is polishing his Cy Young case with every start. Watch Rice's next game for home run No. 39 — at this pace, it's coming soon.
