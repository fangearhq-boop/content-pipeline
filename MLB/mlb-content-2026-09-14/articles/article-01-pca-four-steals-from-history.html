Pete Crow-Armstrong Is Four Stolen Bases from Baseball's Most Exclusive Club

By Jake Torres | September 14, 2026

Four stolen bases. That's all that stands between Pete Crow-Armstrong and the 40-40 club — one of the most exclusive in baseball history.

Heading into Monday, PCA sits at 41 home runs and 36 stolen bases. He has 97 RBI. He is the center piece of the Chicago Cubs lineup and, with approximately 13 games remaining in the regular season, he is within realistic striking distance of joining a list that has only six names on it.

Jose Canseco did it in 1988. Barry Bonds in 1996. Alex Rodriguez in 1998. Alfonso Soriano in 2006. Ronald Acuña Jr. in 2023. Shohei Ohtani in 2021. Pete Crow-Armstrong would be No. 7.

Making Cubs History Twice Over

The 40-40 achievement would also carry particular weight for Chicago. No Cub has ever hit 40 home runs and stolen 40 bases in the same season. Beyond that, PCA's 41 home runs already make him the first Cubs player with 40 home runs since Derrek Lee in 2005 — a 21-year drought at that particular threshold.

That home run total, combined with a stolen base pace that speaks to his elite speed and baserunning instincts, is the product of a two-way skill set the Cubs have rarely seen in center field. PCA plays a deep center field, covers enormous ground defensively, and is now producing historic offense.

Four more stolen bases in the remaining schedule would complete a season that already ranks among the best in Cubs history regardless of the final stolen base total.

The Cubs' Playoff Picture

Chicago enters the final stretch locked in as the No. 4 seed in the National League playoff bracket. The Cubs (81-64) hold the NL Wild Card No. 2 spot and will face the Philadelphia Phillies in a best-of-three Wild Card Series beginning September 29.

The Brewers — who lead the NL Central — will receive a first-round bye along with the Dodgers, meaning Chicago must survive the Wild Card round before advancing. PCA's contributions in those games could be every bit as important as his regular-season numbers.

In September 2026, the individual and team storylines have merged. PCA is four steals from history, the Cubs are two weeks from the postseason, and the most electric player in baseball is wearing pinstripes — North Side pinstripes.

What's Next

The Cubs play 13 more regular-season games before the Wild Card Series on September 29. PCA needs four stolen bases in those games — a pace he's more than capable of sustaining. Watch closely every time he reaches base. History is right there.
