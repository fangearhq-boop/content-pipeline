Freddy Peralta Faces His Former Team in World Series Game 3 Tonight at Tropicana Field

By Jake Torres | October 26, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

For eight seasons, Freddy Peralta was a Milwaukee Brewer. Tonight, he tries to beat them.

World Series Game 3 is set for 8 PM ET at Tropicana Field in St. Petersburg, broadcast on FOX. The Brewers lead the series 2-0 after dramatic one-run victories in Games 1 and 2 at American Family Field. The Rays need a win tonight to extend the series, and Tampa Bay is turning to the pitcher who knows Milwaukee's lineup better than almost anyone in baseball.

A Different Kind of Homecoming

Peralta's path to this moment is one of the more unusual stories of the 2026 season. He came up through Milwaukee's system, made his major league debut as a Brewer, and spent eight seasons working in that rotation. Then in January 2026, he was traded to the New York Mets. By the trade deadline, the Rays acquired him, and he became one of the key pieces of their run to the World Series — as an ALCS MVP and now a Game 3 starter.

Manager Kevin Cash held Peralta out of Games 1 and 2 in Milwaukee, a deliberate choice to save him for Tropicana Field. Peralta has posted a 2-0 record and 1.08 ERA this postseason with 12 strikeouts across his starts. His Rays won each of his final nine regular-season starts. The logic of saving him makes sense.

Gasser Counters for Milwaukee

Robert Gasser takes the mound for the Brewers, and he's been one of the more unheralded stories of Milwaukee's postseason run. In nine innings across two starts, Gasser carries a 1.00 ERA. His NLCS Game 3 start against the Dodgers was a gem: five shutout innings, allowing only three hits.

After Games 1 and 2 went to the Brewers, Milwaukee has the luxury of going to a pitcher with established postseason success and a legitimate track record in pressure starts. Gasser isn't a name most casual fans know — yet — but his results this October have earned his place in a rotation that just kept performing when the stakes got highest.

First WS Game at Tropicana Since 2008

For Rays fans, tonight is more than just Game 3 of a World Series. It's the first Fall Classic game played at Tropicana Field since 2008, when the Rays lost the World Series to the Philadelphia Phillies in five games. That was the franchise's first pennant. This is the third.

The Rays are 3-1 at home this postseason, and the combination of their home-field record and Peralta's familiarity with the venue makes Tropicana Field the right place for Tampa Bay to try to start a comeback.

What's Next

Game 3 tips off tonight at 8 PM ET on FOX. If the Rays win, the series shifts to Game 4 on Tuesday, also at Tropicana Field. If the Brewers win, they can close out the series in Game 4 with a chance to clinch their first championship. The Brewers are listed at -750 to win the series.

Whatever happens tonight, Freddy Peralta's story is one of the defining narratives of this Fall Classic. Eight seasons in Milwaukee, traded in January, acquired at the deadline — and now taking the mound to try to keep Tampa Bay's season alive against the team that made him.
