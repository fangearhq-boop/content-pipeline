Tarik Skubal Is a Los Angeles Dodger: The Trade That Changes the World Series Race

By Marcus Cole | August 2, 2026

It's done. Tarik Skubal — the two-time AL Cy Young Award winner — is now a member of the Los Angeles Dodgers after being acquired from the Detroit Tigers ahead of the 2026 MLB Trade Deadline.

The return to Detroit: outfielder Zyhir Hope, the Dodgers' No. 5 prospect and a Top 25 overall name on MLB Pipeline; right-hander River Ryan, the No. 7 prospect in Los Angeles's farm system and ranked No. 68 overall; and right-hander Brady Smith, who checks in at No. 17 in the Dodgers' system. It is one of the richest prospect packages moved at any trade deadline in recent memory.

Why Los Angeles Made the Move

The Dodgers entered the summer with Shohei Ohtani as the centerpiece of a rotation that was supposed to be among the most dangerous in baseball. Then Ohtani's return to the mound stalled. He has not pitched in a game since July 3, managing concurrent left knee soreness and right biceps irritation, and manager Dave Roberts confirmed this week that Ohtani will not pitch in at least another week to 10 days — and will not take the mound on the current road trip.

Los Angeles could not afford to wait. Skubal steps into a rotation that still features proven arms but needed insurance at the top. The Dodgers identified their window and paid for it.

What Skubal Brings

Skubal's 2026 line at the time of the trade: 2.79 ERA with a 31 percent strikeout rate in 16 starts for the Tigers. He is not a rental in the loose sense of the word — he is a pitcher at the peak of his powers being moved mid-season without any extension agreed. Skubal will be a free agent after the 2026 season, meaning this is an all-in move by a Dodgers organization that has decided its window is now.

The Dodgers had company in the bidding. Five teams were reported as serious suitors before the deal closed: the Yankees, Braves, Cubs, and Phillies all had interest. Los Angeles won the race, and the prospect cost reflects how competitive that process was.

What Detroit Gets

Tigers general manager Scott Harris was not trading from a position of weakness. Skubal is at the top of his market, and Detroit maximized the return accordingly. Zyhir Hope, the headliner, is the kind of outfield prospect that teams rebuild around. River Ryan brings high-upside arm potential from one of the deeper farm systems in baseball. Brady Smith adds a third piece with real organizational value.

Detroit wasn't going to win with Skubal this year. Extracting three legitimate prospects — headlined by a Top 25 overall name — while the market was at its most competitive is exactly what a well-run front office is supposed to do.

What's Next

Skubal joins a Los Angeles roster that is now the clear favorite in the National League and perhaps the strongest overall World Series contender in either league. The trade deadline closes Sunday, August 3 at 6 PM ET — more deals could reshape the landscape before then, but the Dodgers have already made the move that matters most. The question for the rest of baseball is what, if anything, can compete with a healthy Skubal in October.
