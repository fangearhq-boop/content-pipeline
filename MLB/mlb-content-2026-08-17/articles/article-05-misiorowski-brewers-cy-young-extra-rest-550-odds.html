The Brewers Are Protecting Misiorowski — and the Cy Young Case Is Already Closed

By Marcus Cole | Ballpark Banter | August 17, 2026

Jacob Misiorowski will not take his next start on normal rest. The Milwaukee Brewers, managing one of the most dominant pitching seasons in recent memory, are giving their ace extra time between outings. His next start is expected around August 23.

The decision is the right one. Misiorowski is 11-5 with a 1.76 ERA, 204 strikeouts in 133 innings, and a 0.74 WHIP. He is the heaviest NL Cy Young Award favorite in the sport at -550 odds. He reached 200 strikeouts faster than any pitcher in MLB history except Spencer Strider in 2022. The Brewers are not going to risk their ace in September by skipping this workload management step in August.

What the Numbers Actually Mean

A 1.76 ERA is not just good — it is historically significant. A 0.74 WHIP through 133 innings means that opposing hitters are reaching base at a rate that most pitchers achieve in a single dominant outing, not across an entire half-season of work. The 204 strikeouts represent one of the fastest accumulations of punchouts in modern MLB history.

Misiorowski has been the best pitcher in baseball this season. The NL Cy Young Award race, such as it is, has been effectively decided for weeks. The -550 odds at the major sportsbooks reflect a near-certainty that barring a catastrophic late-season collapse — which his track record provides no reason to anticipate — Misiorowski is the award winner.

The Strikeout Record to Watch

With 204 strikeouts through 133 innings, Misiorowski is on a pace that almost no starter has sustained in the modern era. The Brewers have been careful with his workload throughout the season, but the accumulation of punchouts has happened at a rate that outpaces virtually every recent rotation anchor.

The second-fastest to 200 strikeouts in a season belongs to a short list. Spencer Strider reached that mark fastest in 2022. Misiorowski is the second name on that list. Whether he can push his final strikeout total into the upper 200s before the season ends will be the remaining individual narrative worth following, with the team and award conversations already settled.

What the Extra Rest Means for the Brewers

Milwaukee is playing in a competitive NL Wild Card race, and how they manage Misiorowski through September has implications beyond the individual award picture. A healthy Misiorowski in October is worth more than a fatigued Misiorowski who took every start on normal rest through September. The Brewers have made this calculation before with their pitching staff, and the decision to delay his next start reflects an organization that understands the difference between accumulating regular-season starts and building toward a postseason run.

What's Next

Misiorowski is expected to start around August 23. When he takes the mound, it will be his next opportunity to extend his strikeout total and reinforce what has already been an award-winning season. The Cy Young Award will be announced after the regular season ends; the betting market has been clear about the expected outcome for months.
