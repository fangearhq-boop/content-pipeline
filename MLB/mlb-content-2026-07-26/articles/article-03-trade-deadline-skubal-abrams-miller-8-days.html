Trade Deadline in 8 Days: Tigers May Keep Skubal, Abrams and Miller Markets Active

By Ryan Calloway | Ballpark Banter | July 26, 2026

Eight days to the August 3 deadline at 6 PM ET, and the biggest name on the trade board may not be moving after all.

Tarik Skubal has been the consensus top prize in the 2026 trade market since the All-Star break. Two Cy Young awards. The best available starting pitcher by a significant margin. The Dodgers, Braves, Phillies, and Rays all expressed interest. But the Detroit Tigers have won 25 of their last 40 games since June 1, and the growing sentiment among rival executives is that Detroit will hold.

The Skubal Situation

The Tigers' approach has shifted. They are "at least mulling" a trade per Bleacher Report, but multiple rival executives told reporters they believe Detroit will not move Skubal before August 3. The franchise's thinking: they can win the AL Central, and trading their best pitcher eliminates that possibility. Keeping Skubal gives them every chance to make a run.

The Dodgers were the most aggressive suitor. A package built around Emmet Sheehan, Justin Wrobleski, and James Tibbs III was discussed as a possible framework — controllable pitching and offensive upside. But the Tigers' recent success changed the math. A team that is 25-15 since the start of June does not trade its franchise ace unless the package is overwhelming, and the Dodgers have less leverage than they did a month ago.

If Skubal stays, the entire trade market reconfigures. Teams that were waiting to see if he became available must now look at other options.

CJ Abrams

The Washington Nationals are not shopping CJ Abrams, but they will listen. That distinction matters in trade talk — "not shopping" means they have not made calls, but they will take them. The Yankees, Brewers, and Red Sox have all made contact.

Abrams is a two-time All-Star shortstop with cost-controlled years remaining — exactly the profile contending teams want at a premium defensive position. Washington is rebuilding. If the price is right, they move him. If it is not, they hold him through the offseason and reassess.

Mason Miller

The San Diego Padres' situation with closer Mason Miller is genuinely unclear. ESPN's Buster Olney has reported that multiple executives around the league believe a trade is inevitable. The Athletic's sources say Miller seems likely to remain in San Diego. Both camps cannot be right.

The most often cited suitors are the Pirates, Mariners, and Yankees. Miller was a 2026 All-Star with team control through 2029 — a closer at that price and age is a significant asset at any deadline. The Padres' willingness to move him depends on whether the return meets a high bar for a core piece.

The Dodgers as Buyers

Los Angeles finished the 2024 and 2025 seasons as World Series champions. They are 66-38 in 2026 and have the payroll and organizational depth to pursue an unprecedented three-peat — something no team has done since the 1998-2000 Yankees. The Dodgers are described as planning to be "greedy" at this deadline. Whether Skubal is available or not, they will be active.

What's Next

The MLB trade deadline is August 3 at 6 PM ET. All transactions — trades, claim waivers, contract purchases — must be completed by that time. Eight days remain. The Skubal situation will become clearer within the next 72 hours as the deadline pressure builds. Abrams and Miller could move at any point. The Dodgers, Rays, Yankees, and Red Sox are all positioned as buyers. Sellers include the Angels, Nationals, and Mets depending on the deal.
