MLB Hall of Fame Induction Day — Beltran, Jones, and Kent Enshrined at Cooperstown - **Tier:** 1 - **Angle:** The 2026 Baseball Hall of Fame induction ceremony takes place today at Clark Sports Center, Cooperstown — the first Sunday of HOF Weekend, July 24-27. Jeff Kent — the all-time leader in home runs by a second baseman (351 as a 2B, 377 career) and the 2000 NL MVP — was elected by the Contemporary Baseball Era Committee last December.

The Chicago Cubs outfielder broke the previous record — held by Kevin Kiermaier — when he recorded his 33rd career five-star catch (a play with 25% or lower catch probability) on July 21. He got there in fewer opportunities than any other player in Statcast history. At 24 years old, having made his MLB debut in September 2023, PCA has surpassed every outfielder who came before him in this specific measure of defensive excellence. This is the best center fielder alive — and his offense is growing to match.
