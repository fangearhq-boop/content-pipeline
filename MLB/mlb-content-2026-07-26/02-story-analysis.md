### STORY 1: MLB Hall of Fame Induction — Beltran, Jones, Kent

**Tier:** 1
**Classification:** NEW
**Priority:** Lead story — happening today

**Why it matters:**
Three Hall of Famers enshrined today. Beltrán is among the greatest switch-hitters in baseball history, a nine-time All-Star who anchored the Mets' best years. Jones may be the best defensive center fielder who ever played — 10 consecutive Gold Gloves. Kent holds the all-time HR record for second basemen. All three represent different eras and playing styles, but all three clear the Hall of Fame standard by any measure. Record 61 current Hall of Famers attending.

**CORRECTION from yesterday's pipeline:** July 25 brief identified only Beltran and Jones as inductees. Jeff Kent is also a 2026 inductee — elected by the Contemporary Baseball Era Committee in December 2025. Previous pipeline missed this.
