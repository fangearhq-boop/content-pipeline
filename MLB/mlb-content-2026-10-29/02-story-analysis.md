### STORY 1: Brewers Win World Series — First Title in 57 Years

**Angles:**
- Historic angle: The franchise's entire 57-year history — 1969 (Seattle Pilots), Milwaukee Brewers since 1970, two prior WS appearances (1982 loss, never made it back) — leading to this moment.
- Comeback angle: The Rays won Game 4 in dramatic fashion; Milwaukee came back to seal it in Game 5. Showed the Brewers' mental resilience.
- Team-building angle: This championship is the product of years of player development (Misiorowski homegrown), smart front-office moves, and continuity.

**Headlines:**
- Milwaukee Brewers Win First World Series Title in 57-Year Franchise History
- The Wait Is Over: Brewers Are World Series Champions
- How Milwaukee's 4-1 Game 5 Win Ended 57 Years of Waiting

**Best angle for article:** Historic context + Game 5 description with key contributors (Misiorowski, Pratt, Megill, Contreras).

### STORY 2: William Contreras Named World Series MVP

**Angles:**
- Two-way excellence: He dominated both at the plate (.381) and behind it (4 CS, 10-for-13 ABS), which is rare for a WS MVP.
- Character angle: His slow home run trots became a cultural moment — confident, theatrical, beloved by Brewers fans.
- Pat Murphy quote angle: "If people aren't noticing Contreras" — even his own manager was signaling he was the best player before the MVP was announced.
- Venezuelan star angle: Contreras follows in the footsteps of Latin American catchers who have starred in October.

**Headlines:**
- William Contreras Named World Series MVP After Dominant Two-Way Performance
- The Slow Trot Champion: Contreras Wins WS MVP
- How Contreras Became the Brewers' Postseason Star — and the 2026 World Series MVP

**Best angle for article:** Tell the story of his postseason arc — Dodger series, WS, MVP — through the lens of his .381 average and defense.

### STORY 3: Misiorowski Masterpiece / Cy Young Case

**Angles:**
- By-the-numbers angle: 252 K, 1.80 ERA, 174.2 IP in the regular season, then under 1.00 postseason ERA. This is historic.
- 103.5 mph in WS G1 — fastest pitch in Statcast-era World Series history.
- Calf cramp in G5 adds drama — he was dominant when he left, and the bullpen finished it off.
- Cy Young case: Two-time reigning winner Skubal no longer with the Tigers. Who else challenges Misiorowski?

**Headlines:**
- Misiorowski's Numbers Make the AL Cy Young Case Undeniable
- The Miz's 2026 Season: 252 K, 1.80 ERA, and Now a World Series Ring
- Jacob Misiorowski's 5 2/3 Scoreless Innings in Clincher Make the Cy Young Conversation Simple

**Best angle for article:** Season stats + postseason numbers, framed as the definitive Cy Young case.
