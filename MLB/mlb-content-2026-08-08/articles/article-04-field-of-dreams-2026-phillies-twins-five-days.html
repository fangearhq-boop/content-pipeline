Five Days Until the Field of Dreams — The Phillies and Twins Head to Dyersville

By Marcus Cole

August 8, 2026

In five days, the Philadelphia Phillies and the Minnesota Twins will play a baseball game surrounded by Iowa cornfields. The Field of Dreams Game is scheduled for August 13 at the iconic venue in Dyersville, Iowa. Netflix has the broadcast rights. First pitch is 7:30 PM ET. Kevin Costner — whose film gave this event its name and its emotional anchor — will be involved in the broadcast.

The game has become one of the best nights on the MLB calendar. The venue, the setting, and the pregame ceremony reliably produce something that looks different from any other baseball broadcast of the year.

The Hall of Famers

More than 25 Hall of Famers are expected to attend the pregame ceremony in Dyersville — the largest single gathering since the 2008 All-Star Game at the original Yankee Stadium. Among those confirmed: Mike Schmidt, Steve Carlton, Joe Mauer, and Rod Carew. Schmidt and Carlton are the heart of Phillies history — two of the greatest players at their respective positions in the franchise's existence. Mauer and Carew represent the Minnesota thread: Mauer with his 2009 batting title and MVP, Carew with three decades of Minnesota-anchored hits and seven batting titles.

Having 25+ Hall of Famers on the same field before a major league game is not something that happens anywhere else in the sport. The Field of Dreams Game has created a space for that gathering to occur in a context that feels appropriately cinematic.

Before the Main Event

Two days before the Phillies-Twins game, the same venue in Dyersville will host a MiLB game between the St. Paul Saints and the Iowa Cubs on August 11. The Saints are the Triple-A affiliate of the Minnesota Twins. The Iowa Cubs are the Triple-A affiliate of the Chicago Cubs. The precursor game has become part of the Field of Dreams week — a reminder that the location has weight at every level of professional baseball, not just when the major league clubs arrive.

The renovated venue holds approximately 8,000 fans. Every seat is expected to be filled for both the MiLB and MLB games. The setting is unlike any other in professional baseball — temporary, intentional, and built around an aesthetic that prioritizes the sport's history over its infrastructure.

What's Next

The Field of Dreams Game is August 13. Netflix broadcast, 7:30 PM ET first pitch. The MiLB precursor game between the St. Paul Saints and Iowa Cubs is August 11 at the same Dyersville venue. Tickets, schedule, and broadcast access information are available through mlb.com and the Netflix sports platform. Schmidt, Carlton, Mauer, Carew, and the full Hall of Famer lineup will be announced officially in the days leading up to the game.
