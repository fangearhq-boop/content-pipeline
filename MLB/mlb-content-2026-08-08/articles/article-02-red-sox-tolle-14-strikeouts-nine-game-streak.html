Payton Tolle Fans 14 in Six Innings as the Red Sox Win Their Ninth Straight

By Jake Torres

August 8, 2026

Payton Tolle threw the best game of his career Thursday night at Fenway Park. He struck out 14 batters in six innings of work against the Oakland Athletics, touching 99.5 mph and producing a performance that left the Athletics with no real answers. The Red Sox won 13-1. The winning streak reached nine games.

The streak began as a story about a team playing well. Nine wins in a row has turned it into a story about a team that might actually be the best in the American League right now.

What Tolle Did

Fourteen strikeouts in six innings represents a career high for the right-hander by a wide margin. The velocity — 99.5 mph — was the headline, but the pitch mix was the substance. Tolle was generating weak contact on the pitches that didn't end in strikeouts and locating consistently throughout the outing. The Athletics, currently one of the lower-scoring teams in the American League, had almost nothing to work with.

The 13-1 margin of victory was the most lopsided Boston win of this streak, which has included several tight victories. The blowout is significant not because it inflated the streak's statistical picture, but because it gave the bullpen a night off at a point in the season when preserving relief arms matters.

The Rutschman Factor

Adley Rutschman, acquired from the Baltimore Orioles at the trade deadline, could begin a rehab assignment this weekend. Rutschman's arrival was viewed as a significant addition for Boston — a switch-hitting catcher with postseason experience and a track record of producing at the plate and managing a pitching staff at the major league level. His presence in the active roster picture, coming in as the streak hits nine games, adds another dimension to what Boston is building for the final two months.

The Red Sox are in the American League East race, where every win in August has a direct effect on the standings picture heading into September. Nine consecutive wins do not just improve Boston's record — they close gaps, create pressure on other teams, and establish the kind of momentum that carries a roster through the stretch run.

What's Next

The Red Sox continue their homestand after the Athletics series. Rutschman's rehab timeline will be updated through the official team channels and official MLB transaction reporting. The AL East standings, updated daily at mlb.com, will reflect where Boston stands relative to the division leaders. Tolle's next start will be one of the most watched pitching appearances on the schedule — a 14-strikeout performance at 99.5 mph generates attention, and the next outing will tell how sustainable Thursday's performance is at the level he reached.
