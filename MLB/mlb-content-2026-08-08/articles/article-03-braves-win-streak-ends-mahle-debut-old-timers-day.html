The Braves' 8-Game Streak Is Over — Old Timers' Day Brings Sale vs. Cole Today

By Ryan Calloway

August 8, 2026

The Atlanta Braves needed 10 innings, a walk-off sacrifice fly, a 2-run double in the 9th to tie the game, and a Tyler Mahle debut to produce the game that ended their eight-game winning streak. Ryan McMahon's sacrifice fly off Michael Kinley in the 10th inning gave the New York Yankees a 3-2 win Thursday at Yankee Stadium. Ben Rice had tied the game moments earlier with a 2-run double off the Braves' bullpen in the 9th.

The streak was the Braves' longest of the season. It ends with Atlanta still in a strong position — the run from the club that won the 2026 WCWS Finals established this team as a genuine NL contender — but the Yankees earned this one and the rematch is today.

Mahle's Debut, Olson's Streak

Tyler Mahle made his Braves debut Thursday. The right-hander, acquired by Atlanta in a move designed to add depth to a rotation that head coach Brian Snitker knew would need it in the second half, was pressed into service in this game. His performance will be evaluated in full context as he gets more starts and settles into the Atlanta system, but getting his first outing behind him in a meaningful game against the Yankees is experience that matters.

Matt Olson hit his 33rd home run of the season in this game, extending his consecutive-games-with-a-hit streak to 898. The streak, which dates to May 2021, has become one of the most quietly remarkable individual accomplishments in the sport. Olson hitting in 898 consecutive games means he has produced in games spanning five seasons, multiple playoff runs, and now another stretch in which the Braves are among the most complete lineups in the National League.

Old Timers' Day: Sale vs. Cole

Today's rematch at Yankee Stadium takes place on Old Timers' Day — one of the most tradition-rich dates on the Yankees' annual calendar. The on-field ceremonies honor former players before the game. The game itself features Chris Sale starting for Atlanta against Gerrit Cole for New York. Sale has been one of the premier arms in the National League this season; Cole is the Yankees' ace and one of the faces of the American League. The backdrop of Old Timers' Day adds weight to an already compelling pitching matchup.

Whether the Braves bounce back and restart a streak or the Yankees extend their own run will play out this afternoon. For Atlanta, the ending Thursday night was disappointing but not damaging — a team that won eight in a row has earned the right to absorb a tight loss and come back.

What's Next

Today's Braves-Yankees game at Yankee Stadium begins in the early afternoon as part of the Old Timers' Day celebration. Starting pitchers and first pitch times are available at mlb.com and through the Yankees' official channels. Mahle's next scheduled start for Atlanta will be announced by the team.
