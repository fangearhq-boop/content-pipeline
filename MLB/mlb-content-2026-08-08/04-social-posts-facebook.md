### STORY 4: Field of Dreams 2026

#### Long-Form Post — 7:00 PM ET

Five days from now, the Phillies and Twins will play a baseball game in a cornfield in Dyersville, Iowa — and it's going to be one of the best nights of the baseball calendar.

The 2026 Field of Dreams Game is set for August 13. Netflix has the broadcast rights, with first pitch at 7:30 PM ET. Kevin Costner will be involved in the broadcast in some capacity. On the field before the game, more than 25 Hall of Famers are expected to attend — including Mike Schmidt, Steve Carlton, Joe Mauer, and Rod Carew. MLB officials have called it the largest gathering of Hall of Famers at a single baseball event since the 2008 All-Star Game at the old Yankee Stadium.

Before the major league game, there's a MiLB precursor worth watching: the St. Paul Saints take on the Iowa Cubs at the same venue on August 11, two days earlier.

The venue has been renovated and holds approximately 8,000 fans. Every seat will be full. The field will be immaculate. And for one evening, baseball will look exactly like the game it has always wanted to be.
