ALCS Game 4 Is Tonight: Guardians-Rays Resume After Friday Rain Postponement

By Ryan Calloway | October 17, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

ALCS Game 4 was supposed to happen Friday night. Rain in Cleveland had other ideas.

The Guardians-Rays series resumes tonight at Progressive Field — Saturday, October 17, first pitch at 8 PM ET on TBS. Cleveland leads the series 2-1. The winner of tonight's game either secures an ALCS berth with one more win or ties the series heading into Game 5 on Sunday. One team is going to the World Series. The other is going home.

What the Delay Changed

A rain postponement in October is not just a scheduling inconvenience. It changes pitching plans, rest cycles, and the mental preparation that goes into a winner-take-all game. Both teams had an extra 24 hours to think about what a Game 4 loss would mean — Cleveland with an opportunity to put the series away, Tampa Bay trying to avoid going down 3-1 against a team that just watched the Brewers sweep the Dodgers in four.

The pitching matchup for tonight: Nick Martinez starts for Tampa Bay, facing a Cleveland team that will treat Game 4 as a bullpen game. Shawn Armstrong is expected to lead the Guardians' bullpen committee approach. A bullpen game carries risk — the Guardians are asking multiple arms to navigate the Rays' lineup across nine innings without the security of a defined starter.

The Series So Far

Cleveland has controlled this series by executing in the right moments. Petey Halpin, the rookie outfielder who hit a 2-run homer in Game 3 to give the Guardians a 3-2 win, emerged as one of the stories of the ALCS. He had struck out against Freddy Peralta in Game 2, came back in Game 3, and turned a one-run deficit into a lead. That kind of individual momentum carries weight in a short series.

Tampa Bay has shown it can compete. Peralta's six scoreless innings in Game 2 were a reminder that the Rays' pitching — even without Shane McClanahan, who is out with a forearm injury — is capable of shutting down a lineup. The Guardians have not made this easy for themselves despite leading 2-1.

Tonight's margin for error is thin on both sides. Cleveland wants to avoid a series tied at 2-2 heading into Sunday. Tampa Bay needs to win to get there.

What's Next

If Cleveland wins tonight, the Guardians advance to the World Series and head to Milwaukee for Game 1 on October 23. If Tampa Bay wins, Game 5 is Sunday at 5 PM ET at Progressive Field — still in Cleveland, still on TBS. The Brewers are already in the building, watching and waiting. Whoever comes out of the ALCS will have earned their shot at Milwaukee's 103-win team. Tonight at 8 PM ET is the next step toward figuring out who that team will be.
