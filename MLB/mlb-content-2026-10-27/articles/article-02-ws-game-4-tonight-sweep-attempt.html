World Series Game 4 Tonight: Milwaukee Seeks Sweep, Tampa Bay Faces History

By Jake Torres | October 27, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

For the first time in franchise history, the Milwaukee Brewers are one win away from a World Series championship.

Game 4 of the 2026 World Series is tonight at Tropicana Field in St. Petersburg. First pitch is scheduled for 8 PM ET on FOX. Milwaukee leads the series 3-0 after a dominant 10-1 victory on Monday, and a win tonight would complete the first World Series sweep since the 2012 San Francisco Giants.

For Tampa Bay, the task is far more daunting than any deficit the franchise has faced before.

The Brewers' Path to History

Milwaukee has been the most dominant team in this World Series by any measure. The Brewers have outscored the Rays 18-4 across three games. They have gotten a strong start from a different pitcher each night — Misiorowski in Game 1, Rasmussen went in Game 2, and Gasser in Game 3. Their bullpen is rested after throwing only a handful of pitches in the eighth and ninth innings of Monday's blowout.

The Brewers are not a team that shows cracks. They led the National League with 103 wins in the regular season, swept the Dodgers in four games in the NLCS, and have been methodical through every round of the postseason. They have the pitching depth, the lineup, and the momentum. Tonight, they have the chance to close it out.

Tampa Bay's Only Option: Win Four Straight

The Rays need to win Game 4 tonight, Game 5 on Thursday, and then win both games in Milwaukee next week. All four wins need to come against a team that has beaten them in every game this series. It has never been done in World Series history.

Tampa Bay's best hope starts with pitching. Griffin Jax is expected to get the Game 4 start, bringing a 1.88 ERA over his last six appearances into a must-win situation. The Rays also have Freddy Peralta available — the former Milwaukee ace who won ALCS MVP with a 2-0, 0.75 ERA performance against Cleveland — who has not pitched since Game 3. If Jax can keep the game close into the fifth or sixth, Tampa Bay's bullpen has the depth to compete.

The Weight of History

In 43 instances across World Series history where a team has taken a 3-0 series lead, all 43 have won the championship. The closest a team came to a comeback was the 2020 World Series, when the Rays themselves were in Tampa Bay's current position — down 3-1 — and lost in six games to the Dodgers.

The 2004 Boston Red Sox came back from 3-0 in the American League Championship Series, but that was a League Championship Series, not the World Series. In the Fall Classic, no comeback from 3-0 has ever happened.

What's Next

Game 4 of the 2026 World Series is tonight at Tropicana Field, 8 PM ET on FOX. Griffin Jax is expected to start for Tampa Bay. Milwaukee's starter has not been officially announced. A Brewers win clinches the first World Series championship in franchise history. A Rays win keeps the series alive for Game 5, which would also be at Tropicana Field.

History says this series ends tonight. Baseball has a way of ignoring history on occasion. Either way, it's going to be worth watching.
