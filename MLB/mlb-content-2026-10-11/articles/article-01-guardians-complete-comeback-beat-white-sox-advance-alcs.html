Guardians Complete Historic Comeback, Beat White Sox in ALDS Game 5 to Advance to ALCS

By Marcus Cole | October 11, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

The Cleveland Guardians are going to the American League Championship Series, and they got there the hard way. Down 0-2 to the Chicago White Sox after losing both games at Progressive Field, the Guardians won three consecutive games — the last one Saturday night at home — to complete one of the more improbable comebacks in recent postseason history. José Ramírez made sure they had a chance every time Cleveland took the field.

Teams leading 2-0 in a best-of-five postseason series go on to win 83 of 93 times historically. The White Sox had the momentum, the lead, and the math. Cleveland had Ramírez.

Playing Hurt, Playing Like Ramírez

Ramírez went into the ALDS still managing the effects of left hamate surgery he underwent in June. Hamate injuries affect hitters' grip and power at the exact moment a postseason run demands the most from them. None of that showed in Game 4, when Ramírez hit a home run and drove in three runs in a 9-5 Cleveland win that forced Game 5. He was at the center of every important offensive moment in the series.

At 33, Ramírez has cemented himself as one of the most reliable October performers of his generation. This comeback adds another chapter to that reputation.

Historical Context

The last team to come back from a 0-2 deficit in a best-of-five postseason series was the 2017 New York Yankees — and those Yankees did it against these same Cleveland Guardians, who were the Indians at the time. Baseball's sense of irony arrived nine years late but it arrived. Gavin Williams started Game 5 for Cleveland. The Guardians closed it out at Progressive Field in front of a crowd that had endured the most anxious week of the 2026 baseball calendar.

What's Next

Cleveland faces the Tampa Bay Rays in the American League Championship Series, with Game 1 on Monday, October 12, at 8 PM ET at Tropicana Field in St. Petersburg. The Rays swept the Yankees 3-0 in their ALDS and are rested and waiting. The Guardians are battle-tested but tired. That matchup begins Monday on TBS, truTV, and HBO Max.
