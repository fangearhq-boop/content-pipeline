2026 Division Series by the Numbers: Comebacks, Dominance, and the Stars Who Defined the Round

By Jake Torres | October 11, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

Both Division Series are complete. The League Championship Series begin tonight. Before the next round takes over, here is what the numbers from the first round tell us about the teams that survived — and the ones that did not.

The Comeback: 10 of 93

Teams that lead 2-0 in a best-of-five postseason series go on to win 83 of 93 times. The Cleveland Guardians just joined the group of 10 teams in history that have erased a 0-2 deficit and won the series. The last team to do it before Cleveland was the 2017 New York Yankees — who pulled it off against these same Guardians (then Cleveland Indians). It is rare, it is documented, and it just happened again at Progressive Field.

The Sweep: Five Runs in Three Games

The Tampa Bay Rays held the New York Yankees to five total runs across their three-game ALDS sweep. To put that in context: the Yankees scored fewer than two runs per game against a Tampa Bay pitching staff that did not blink once over three October nights. Victor Mesa Jr. provided the offensive punctuation, hitting a two-run home run in the clincher while a fan interference call added a layer of drama that the final score did not need. The Rays' pitching depth entering the ALCS is the most complete unit remaining in the American League.

The Player: José Ramírez

Ramírez had left hamate surgery in June. Hamate fractures limit grip strength and swing mechanics — exactly what a hitter needs in October. He played every game of the ALDS anyway. In Game 4, with Cleveland's season on the line, he hit a home run and drove in three runs in a 9-5 win that forced Game 5. At 33, Ramírez continues to be one of the most reliable October performers in baseball. His numbers in elimination games and late-game situations are a separate conversation from his regular-season value — and that regular-season value is already All-Star caliber.

The Pitcher to Watch: Misiorowski

Jacob Misiorowski finished 2026 with a 1.76 ERA. He pitches Game 1 of the NLCS tonight at American Family Field. There is a reasonable argument that he is the best pitcher left in the postseason — and a reasonable argument that most of the country does not know his name yet. By the time this NLCS is over, they will.

What's Next

The NLCS begins tonight in Milwaukee (8 PM ET FOX) and the ALCS starts Monday in Tampa Bay (8 PM ET TBS). The World Series opens Friday, October 23. Four teams remain. Two are built on dominant pitching. Two are built on surviving everything baseball threw at them. That is the setup for the best two weeks of the baseball calendar.
