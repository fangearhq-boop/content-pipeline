NLCS Game 1 Tonight: Ohtani, Misiorowski, and the Pennant Race Begin at American Family Field

By Jake Torres | October 11, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

For the second consecutive October, the Los Angeles Dodgers and Milwaukee Brewers are playing for the National League pennant. Game 1 of the NLCS starts tonight at 8 PM ET at American Family Field in Milwaukee, televised on FOX. The Dodgers are two-time defending champions. The Brewers went 103-59 — the best record in the National League — and are hosting a championship series for the first time in franchise history.

The last time these two teams met in this round, the Dodgers won. Milwaukee spent all of 2026 building toward a different result.

Misiorowski vs. a Historic Rotation

Jacob Misiorowski takes the mound for Milwaukee in Game 1. The right-hander finished the 2026 regular season 11-5 with a 1.76 ERA — numbers that would define almost any other pitcher's season as the best in baseball. For the Brewers, he is the reason they entered October with the NL's top seed. This is his first NLCS start, and American Family Field on a Sunday night in October gives him the setting his season has earned.

For the Dodgers, the rotation has an element it did not have last October: Shohei Ohtani is pitching in this series. After carefully managing his workload through the regular season, Ohtani is in the rotation for the NLCS. Tyler Glasnow is also available. The Dodgers' ability to rotate Ohtani, Glasnow, and Blake Snell through a seven-game series gives them a pitching depth that no team in baseball can match.

Milwaukee's Moment

The Brewers won their last pennant in 1982. That was 44 years ago. Every Milwaukee player in tonight's starting lineup was born after that season. For this city and this franchise, the NLCS is not a routine exercise. It is the program's ceiling being tested. At 103-59 with home-field advantage and their best pitcher on the mound, the Brewers have positioned themselves as well as any team could.

What's Next

Game 1 starts tonight at 8 PM ET at American Family Field. Game 2 follows Monday at 5 PM ET, also in Milwaukee. Games 3 through 5 shift to Dodger Stadium in Los Angeles, beginning Wednesday, October 15. The World Series starts Friday, October 23. For the Brewers, every pitch tonight carries 44 years of waiting. For the Dodgers, every pitch is part of a three-peat attempt that no team has completed since Oakland won three straight from 1972 to 1974.
