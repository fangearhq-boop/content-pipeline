ALCS Preview: Guardians vs. Rays — Comeback Kids vs. Sweep Kings Begin Monday

By Ryan Calloway | October 11, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

The American League Championship Series starts Monday, October 12, at Tropicana Field in St. Petersburg, with Game 1 at 8 PM ET on TBS. The matchup: the Tampa Bay Rays, who swept the New York Yankees 3-0 in the ALDS without breaking a sweat, against the Cleveland Guardians, who clawed back from a 0-2 deficit to beat the Chicago White Sox in five games. Two teams. Two completely different paths to the same place.

How Each Team Got Here

The Rays were dominant from start to finish against the Yankees. Victor Mesa Jr. homered in the clincher. Tampa Bay held New York to just five total runs across three games — a performance that bordered on historically dominant pitching. Drew Rasmussen (3.28 ERA) anchors the rotation. Junior Caminero, Yandy Díaz, and Jonathan Aranda anchor an offense that produces from top to bottom.

Cleveland's path was exactly the opposite. The Guardians dropped the first two games at home, putting their backs against the wall. They rallied for three straight wins, the last at Progressive Field on Saturday night. Parker Messick, who posted a 2.68 ERA in 20 starts this season, is expected to start Game 1. José Ramírez — playing through hamate surgery effects — has been the team's offensive engine and emotional heartbeat throughout.

The Key Matchup

The Rays won the regular season series between these two teams, 5-1. They outscored Cleveland 20-8 across those six games. History favors Tampa Bay. But the Guardians that arrives at Tropicana Field is a different team than the one that lost five of six to the Rays in the summer — hardened by a five-game elimination series and playing with a confidence that comes only from surviving the impossible.

Watch for Cleveland's ability to neutralize Caminero, who has been one of the most dangerous right-handed hitters in the American League. If Messick can manage the Tampa Bay middle of the order and Ramírez can stay healthy, this series could go seven.

What's Next

ALCS Game 1 is Monday, October 12, at 8 PM ET at Tropicana Field on TBS, truTV, and HBO Max. Games 1 and 2 are in Tampa Bay. Games 3 through 5 shift to Progressive Field in Cleveland. The World Series begins Friday, October 23. One of these two teams will be there.
