### STORY 2: Misiorowski Velocity / Cy Young
**Tier 1 — FB Long-Form + FB Image Caption**

**FB Long-Form Post**
Window: 10:30 AM ET

Two storylines are colliding around Jacob Misiorowski right now — and both are worth paying attention to.

**The good news:**

His Cy Young odds have surged to -1400. That's not a typo. Chris Sale is the runner-up at +750. Misiorowski has a 14-5 record with approximately a 1.73 ERA, and he has already reached 200 strikeouts in just 129.1 innings — the second-fewest innings in MLB history to reach that mark in a single season.

**The concern:**

His average fastball has dropped from 100.5 mph to 98.7 mph. In his most recent start against the Mets, only 9 of 95 pitches touched 100+ mph — a number that stands out for a pitcher who made his reputation on triple-digit velocity. Former Twins slugger Trevor Plouffe publicly urged the Brewers to monitor his workload, noting this is Misiorowski's first full MLB season and he has already nearly doubled his previous pitch-count total from a prior campaign.

His next start: September 2 at Wrigley Field against the Cubs.

The Brewers will want their ace healthy for October. The question is how carefully they manage these final weeks. 🍺 #Brewers
