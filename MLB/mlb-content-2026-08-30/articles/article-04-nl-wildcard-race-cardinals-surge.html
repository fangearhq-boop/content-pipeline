NL Wild Card With 30 Games Left: Cubs Lead, Cardinals Surge, Race Is On

By Marcus Cole

August 30, 2026

Saturday's 17-5 Cubs win over the Reds did more than validate Pete Crow-Armstrong's MVP case. It pushed Chicago's Wild Card lead to a more comfortable margin — and it came at exactly the right moment, because the team below them in the standings is making a run.

Here is where the NL Wild Card race stands entering the final month:

  • Cubs: 77-59 (WC1)
  • Phillies: 75-60 (WC2 — 2 games back)
  • Cardinals: approximately 73-62 (WC3 — 5 consecutive series wins)
  • Padres: 72-63

Thirty games remain for most teams.

The Cardinals Problem

St. Louis has won five consecutive series and carries a favorable remaining schedule into September. Redbirdrants.com ran an analysis of their remaining schedule in the context of the Wild Card standings — the conclusion is that the Cardinals' path to October is genuinely viable if they continue playing at this level.

The threat is real. Chicago sits four games clear of St. Louis with a manageable lead, but the Cubs' remaining schedule carries some of the toughest opponents in baseball — including a home series against the Brewers, who have the best record in the majors. Every Cubs loss matters more when the team behind them is playing .700 ball.

Chicago's Goal: Avoid the Brewers

The Cubs' internal calculation is not simply about making the playoffs — it is about where they seed. Milwaukee holds the best record in baseball and is the projected No. 1 Wild Card seed, which means the team in the No. 2 or No. 3 seed would draw the Brewers in the opening round of the postseason.

The Cubs need distance from the Phillies to ensure they can choose their matchup rather than being assigned one. A two-game lead over Philadelphia is comfortable for now, but it is not a position that allows for many late-September stumbles. The Phillies have been consistent, and their addition of Luis Arraez at the trade deadline added depth to a lineup that already had plenty of firepower.

What's Next

September begins with 30 games left for most NL Wild Card contenders. The Cardinals' current streak makes their games against the Phillies and Cubs in the final month the pivotal matchups to watch. For Chicago, the goal entering September is simple: hold the lead, protect the seeding advantage, and stay healthy. The final Wild Card standings will be determined by how each of these teams responds to the intensity of a September pennant race.
