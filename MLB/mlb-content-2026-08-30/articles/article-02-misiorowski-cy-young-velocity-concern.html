Misiorowski's Cy Young Odds Hit -1400 — But His Velocity Dip Has the Brewers Watching Closely

By Jake Torres

August 30, 2026

Jacob Misiorowski is the NL Cy Young Award favorite by a margin that has almost no precedent. His betting odds now sit at -1400, per FanDuel and Bleacher Nation's most recent tracking. Chris Sale is the closest competition at +750. Cristopher Sanchez is further back at +1100.

The number tells you the market has essentially declared this over. The concern is what happens between now and October.

The Velocity Dip

SI.com ran a detailed piece this week headlined "Brewers Jacob Misiorowski's Fatigue Cause For Concern? Fastball Takes a Dip." The data behind it is specific: Misiorowski's average fastball has dropped from 100.5 mph to 98.7 mph. In a recent start against the Mets, only 9 of 95 pitches reached 100+ mph — a number that stands out for a pitcher who built his reputation on consistent triple-digit velocity.

Former Twins designated hitter Trevor Plouffe added public pressure, calling on the Brewers to carefully monitor Misiorowski's workload going forward. The reasoning is straightforward: this is Misiorowski's first full MLB season, and he has already nearly doubled the pitch total he accumulated in any prior campaign. That is a significant jump, and first-year starters who make it into September with heavy innings accumulation require careful management.

The Milestone

The velocity narrative exists alongside a milestone that underscores just how dominant Misiorowski has been. He reached 200 strikeouts in just 129.1 innings — the second-fewest innings in MLB history by a pitcher to reach that threshold in a single season. The combination of strikeout rate and run prevention (14-5 record, approximately 1.73 ERA) makes his case a near-certainty barring a dramatic collapse.

The 0.76 WHIP and the strikeout numbers put his season in a tier by itself in 2026. No other NL pitcher has approached his combination of wins, ERA, and strikeout volume.

September 2 at Wrigley

Misiorowski's next start is projected for September 2 at Wrigley Field against the Cubs — a matchup that will draw enormous attention given both teams' playoff positioning. The Brewers hold the best record in baseball and are cruising toward a division title. The Cubs sit at 77-59 and need to protect their NL Wild Card lead.

The Brewers' decision about how aggressively to push Misiorowski through September will define how they approach the final month. Managing a 1.7-win lead in the win column while preserving their ace for October requires exactly the kind of careful balancing act that October contenders have to get right.

What's Next

Misiorowski starts September 2 at Wrigley Field against the Cubs. The Brewers have not publicly addressed the velocity concern, though the level of public scrutiny is likely to prompt a response before that start. Watch his pitch usage, the percentage of pitches hitting 100+ mph, and the Brewers' announced plan for his workload heading into September. If the velocity stabilizes, the Cy Young is a formality. If it doesn't, the conversation shifts to how the Brewers protect their most important pitcher.
