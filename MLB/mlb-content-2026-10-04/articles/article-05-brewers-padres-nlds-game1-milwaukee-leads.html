Brewers Hold Off Padres 3-2 in NLDS Game 1, Milwaukee Takes 1-0 Series Lead

By Jake Torres | October 4, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

The Milwaukee Brewers defeated the San Diego Padres 3-2 on Saturday in NLDS Game 1, taking a 1-0 series lead that nobody outside Wisconsin fully expected. The Brewers — a franchise built on pitching depth, bullpen usage, and fundamentally sound baseball — played exactly to their identity in a tight, contested game that came down to one run in the end.

The margin was narrow. The statement was not.

Milwaukee's October Identity

The Brewers have earned a reputation as a franchise that wins more than their market size and payroll should allow. They do it through development, through smart roster construction, and through an organizational commitment to pitching that has produced competitive teams in a division that includes the Cubs and Cardinals.

In the playoffs, that identity either holds up or it doesn't. Against San Diego — a team with experienced October players and a lineup that produced well throughout the regular season — the Brewers needed their pitching-first approach to deliver. It did. Three runs against the Padres and a two-run margin were enough for a Milwaukee staff that has learned how to navigate the leverage innings of a playoff game.

What San Diego Faces Now

The Padres came into this series with recent playoff experience and a lineup built to beat quality pitching. Losing Game 1 at home — especially by a single run — puts immediate pressure on a team that cannot afford to fall behind in a five-game series. A 2-0 deficit going back to Milwaukee would functionally end the Padres' October before the calendar reaches its second week.

San Diego has the talent to respond. Their bullpen is capable, their offense dangerous, and they know how to win close games. But Game 1 belonged to Milwaukee, and the Brewers will carry that momentum into whatever comes next.

What's Next

Game 2 of the Brewers-Padres NLDS is Sunday. San Diego needs a win to even the series and prevent Milwaukee from returning to American Family Field with a commanding lead. For the Brewers, a 2-0 series lead would put them one win from the NLCS — a result that would represent one of the most significant postseason achievements in the franchise's modern history.

Milwaukee entered this series as the team nobody picked. After Game 1, they have everything they need to keep confounding expectations. The Padres have Game 2 to prove those expectations wrong.
