2026 Division Series Day 1: White Sox, Rays, Dodgers and Brewers Each Win Game 1

By Marcus Cole | October 4, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

Saturday was a full day of Division Series baseball, and the results were unambiguous: every team leading coming out of the seeding process grabbed Game 1. Four games, four different narratives, and four series now standing 1-0 with momentum pointed clearly in one direction heading into Sunday's Game 2s.

Here is where things stand after the 2026 Division Series opened on October 3.

American League: Both AL Underdogs Win

In the American League, the Chicago White Sox blanked the Cleveland Guardians 3-0 to take Game 1 of their ALDS matchup. It was a dominant, wire-to-wire performance from a Chicago team that has rapidly emerged as one of baseball's most remarkable turnaround stories. The White Sox — who lost a record 121 games in 2024 — are now winning playoff games against one of the AL's most consistent franchises.

The Tampa Bay Rays, meanwhile, edged the New York Yankees 1-0 in the other American League matchup. The Yankees came in without Aaron Judge, who suffered a calf strain in the lead-up to the postseason, compounding an already difficult year that included a rib fracture in late April that cost him more than three months. Tampa Bay's pitching staff made one run stand up from the first pitch to the final out.

National League: Defending Champions and Underdogs Both Win

In the National League, the Los Angeles Dodgers defeated the Atlanta Braves 5-3, taking Game 1 of their NLDS. Los Angeles, the defending World Series champions, showed no early-series jitters, jumping ahead of a Braves team that arrived having just dispatched the Philadelphia Phillies in the Wild Card round.

The Milwaukee Brewers held off the San Diego Padres 3-2 in the other National League game, taking their own 1-0 series lead. Milwaukee's pitching-forward identity held up when it mattered, limiting a Padres lineup that entered the postseason with one of the most dangerous offenses in the NL.

What Saturday Told Us

Four Game 1s produced no major upsets by series seeding — but each result told its own story. The White Sox, despite their underdog status in the broader national narrative, have been one of baseball's best teams since the summer. The Rays have the pitching depth to neutralize any lineup, regardless of who is or isn't available. The Dodgers remain the standard in the National League. And the Brewers are capable of taking down a superior opponent with fundamentals, pitching, and timely hitting.

Every series is competitive. None of the 1-0 leaders can be considered safe. And the four teams trailing face the immediate pressure of Game 2 on their home turf with elimination not yet in play, but the series narrative already shifting.

What's Next

Game 2s are scheduled for Sunday, October 4. The Guardians host the White Sox in the afternoon slot. The Yankees host the Rays in the evening. The NLDS Game 2s for both the Dodgers-Braves and Brewers-Padres series are also on Sunday.

If the Day 1 results hold as a pattern, the 2026 Division Series is going to be a closely contested, drama-filled postseason round. Four 2-0 series would produce an October unlike any in recent memory. All four trailing teams will have something to say about that on Sunday.
