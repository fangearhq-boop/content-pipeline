### STORY 2: White Sox Blank Guardians in ALDS Game 1

**Long-Form Post (8:00 AM ET)**

🔥 FROM WORST TO OCTOBER WINNER — the Chicago White Sox story is unbelievable.

Two years ago, the Chicago White Sox lost 121 games — an MLB record. They had previously lost 100 or more games in three consecutive seasons. No team in baseball history had ever done that and then reached the postseason. The White Sox did it in 2026.

On Saturday night, they blanked the Cleveland Guardians 3-0 in ALDS Game 1. A roster where seven of nine starters have less than two years of MLB experience is now winning in October. Munetaka Murakami leads a young core that has somehow transformed the most embarrassing stretch in franchise history into something worth watching in the fall.

The White Sox clinched their playoff spot September 24 with a win over the Kansas City Royals. They were a feel-good story then. They are a genuine October threat now.

If this team wins this series, it might be the greatest single-franchise turnaround in modern baseball history.

Who believed in the White Sox? 🙋 ⬇️

### STORY 3: Rays Edge Yankees 1-0 Without Judge

**Long-Form Post (3:00 PM ET)**

The Rays beat the Yankees 1-0 in ALDS Game 1. It wasn't just a great game — it was a statement.

Aaron Judge, the face of this Yankees franchise, has had one of the most difficult seasons of any superstar in recent memory. He broke a rib making a diving catch in late April, was placed on the injured list May 31, and missed more than three months of the season. When he finally returned September 8, it felt like the Yankees might have found their moment. Then came a "moderate grade" right calf strain before the postseason. Yankees manager Aaron Boone confirmed Judge would miss the Wild Card round entirely.

Without their captain, New York needed pitching, defense, and a different brand of baseball. The Rays — who spent years making October difficult for the Yankees in the early 2020s — needed only a single run and a shutdown performance to take Game 1.

The question heading into Game 2: when does Judge return, and can the Yankees survive until he does?

### STORY 5: Brewers Hold Padres 3-2 in NLDS Game 1

**Long-Form Post (10:00 PM ET)**

🏆 MILWAUKEE IS PLAYING OCTOBER BASEBALL — and they're winning.

The Brewers defeated the San Diego Padres 3-2 in NLDS Game 1 on Saturday, taking a 1-0 series lead that no one in the national conversation is quite ready to take seriously yet. They should be.

Milwaukee has built one of the most consistent winning cultures in the National League over the past several years — small market, pitching-forward, smart front office decisions. When they get to October, they're dangerous in ways that bigger-market teams aren't built to handle.

The Padres entered this series as a team with October pedigree and home playoff experience. The Brewers entered it as the road team that nobody believes in. After Game 1, that narrative is upside down.

Game 2 is Sunday. If Milwaukee wins again, this series becomes a conversation.

Brewers fans — are you believing yet? ⬇️
