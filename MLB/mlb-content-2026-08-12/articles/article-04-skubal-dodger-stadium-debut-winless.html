Tarik Skubal's Dodger Stadium Debut: Still Winless in LA, But the Dodgers Are Rolling

By Marcus Cole

August 12, 2026

Tarik Skubal has won back-to-back American League Cy Young Awards. He has not won a game since being traded to Los Angeles.

The two-time Cy Young Award winner made his Dodger Stadium home debut Monday night against the Kansas City Royals — and came away with his second no-decision since arriving at the trade deadline. The Dodgers won 6-5, so at least there is that. But Skubal remains 0-for-2 in terms of picking up a win in Dodger blue.

What the Line Looked Like

Skubal went five innings, allowed four hits and three earned runs, struck out six, and threw 95 pitches. MLB.com described him as battling "spotty command of his fastball" — a phrase that would have felt absurd attached to his name just a few months ago, when he was arguably the best starting pitcher in the American League.

Skubal himself called the home debut "special to me," which suggests the emotional weight of pitching at Dodger Stadium for the first time registered even if the results weren't what he was used to delivering. His teammates, according to reporting from Dodgerblue.com, reacted positively despite the uneven outing.

Context: The Dodgers Needed This Win

The backdrop of Monday's game matters. The Dodgers had dropped 8 of their previous 9 games entering Monday — a stretch that had raised legitimate questions about the team's ability to maintain its position in the NL standings. Winning 6-5, regardless of how Skubal pitched, broke that skid. The offense gave him enough to work with.

Detroit traded Skubal to Los Angeles at the August 3 deadline, receiving a package of top prospects in return — a price that reflected how highly regarded Skubal is across the league. The trade made sense for both sides: Detroit got future pieces for a rebuilding effort, and LA got a two-time Cy Young winner for a postseason run. He has not pitched his best baseball since arriving, but two starts is an extremely small sample.

What's Next

Skubal will take his next turn in the Dodgers' rotation in the coming days. Los Angeles will continue its NL West campaign with postseason positioning at stake. Schedule and roster information is available at dodgers.com and mlb.com.
