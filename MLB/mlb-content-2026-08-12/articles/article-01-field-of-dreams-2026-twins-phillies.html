Field of Dreams 2026: Twins Meet Phillies in Iowa Tomorrow — Netflix Exclusive, No Robot Ump

By Marcus Cole

August 12, 2026

Tomorrow night, baseball returns to the cornfield. The 2026 MLB Field of Dreams Game is Thursday, August 13, first pitch at 7:30 PM ET, with the Minnesota Twins hosting the Philadelphia Phillies at a new permanent stadium in Dyersville, Iowa. It streams exclusively on Netflix — the first MLB game ever broadcast on the platform — with pre-game coverage beginning at 6:30 PM.

This is the third edition of the Field of Dreams Game. After a 2021 debut (White Sox vs. Yankees) and a 2022 follow-up (Cubs vs. Reds), the event went on a three-year hiatus while the original site's temporary setup was replaced by a permanent stadium. The new venue sits about a quarter-mile west of the original movie-site diamond, with a right-field wall that features windows framing the surrounding 159 acres of Iowa cornfield. Capacity runs around 8,000, including bleachers.

The Uniforms Make It Special

Half the appeal of the Field of Dreams Game is the visual — and this year's throwback uniforms deliver. The Philadelphia Phillies will wear their gray road jerseys modeled on the franchise's 1939-41 look, complete with a centennial patch. The Minnesota Twins will suit up in their 1961 inaugural season pinstripes — the original blue-and-red "Twins" script on the chest, paired with a navy cap carrying the vintage "TC" logo. These are uniforms from the franchise's very first year of existence in Minnesota.

Both designs match the nostalgic aesthetic that makes this event unlike anything else on the baseball calendar. They're not gimmicks — they're baseball history worn on the field of a Kevin Costner movie.

The Hall of Fame Guest List

A confirmed group of Hall of Famers will be in Dyersville for the event, with the Phillies represented by Mike Schmidt and Steve Carlton, and the Twins represented by Joe Mauer and Rod Carew. Kevin Costner — the star of the 1989 film that created this entire tradition — will be a part of the broadcast in some capacity.

The Surprise Ruling: No ABS Challenge System

MLB confirmed on Tuesday that the Automated Ball-Strike (ABS) challenge system will not be installed at the Dyersville stadium for Thursday's game. Neither team will have the ability to challenge ball-and-strike calls.

That ruling carries an unusual subplot involving the Phillies. Philadelphia has reportedly refused to use the ABS challenge system throughout the 2026 season. Whether by principle or preference, the Phillies have opted not to challenge calls via ABS when the option was available to them. Tomorrow, in the most widely-seen setting of the regular season, they'll play the game under purely traditional umpiring — which is exactly how they've been playing all year anyway.

It's a quirky detail that adds a layer to the broadcast storyline. The game was already cinematic. The no-robot-ump confirmation makes it a little more interesting.

What's Next

The 2026 Field of Dreams Game airs exclusively on Netflix on Thursday, August 13. First pitch is at 7:30 PM ET, with pre-game coverage from Devon Park beginning at 6:30 PM. For scheduling, roster, and broadcast information, visit mlb.com and netflix.com.
