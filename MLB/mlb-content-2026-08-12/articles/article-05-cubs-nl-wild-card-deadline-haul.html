Cubs 69-50, Five Games Clear: The Trade Deadline Haul Is Paying Off

By Jake Torres

August 12, 2026

The Chicago Cubs bought at the trade deadline, and it's working.

At 69-50, the Cubs lead the National League Wild Card by five games over both the Arizona Diamondbacks and Philadelphia Phillies, who sit at 64-56. With 43 games remaining, Chicago has built a lead that is real but not insurmountable — the kind that demands they keep playing well through September without a significant stumble.

The players acquired at the August 3 deadline have contributed quickly. Kevin Gausman arrived from the Toronto Blue Jays to anchor the rotation. Clay Holmes came over from the New York Mets, along with outfielder Tyrone Taylor. Braxton Garrett was acquired from the Miami Marlins. Ryan Zeferjahn joined from the Los Angeles Angels. Together, they represent one of the more aggressive single-deadline overhauls in recent Cubs history.

What Gausman and Holmes Are Providing

Gausman and Holmes made their Cubs debuts around August 8-9, with early returns described as encouraging across multiple Chicago outlets. ChiSportsNation credited Gausman with delivering in his debut, and Holmes with "showing why the Cubs wanted him." When high-profile deadline acquisitions produce immediately, it validates the front office's willingness to move assets and confirms to a clubhouse that the organization is committed to winning now.

CC Sabathia, analyzing the Cubs on NBC Sports, said the team "did enough at the deadline to hold onto the top Wild Card spot." That is the straightforward frame for what Chicago did: they identified where they needed help, acquired it, and those players are already showing up.

The Wild Card Math

Five games is a healthy lead, but in August it represents about two and a half weeks of one team going on a run while the other goes flat. The Diamondbacks and Phillies are not going away. Chicago has the depth now to withstand individual bad weeks — the Gausman and Garrett additions give them rotation length, and Holmes adds an elite reliever to a bullpen that needed it.

The Cubs beat the Kansas City Royals 5-4 on Monday to maintain the cushion heading into the second week of August. The post-deadline roster is coming together.
