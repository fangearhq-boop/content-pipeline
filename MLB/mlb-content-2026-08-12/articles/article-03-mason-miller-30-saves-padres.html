Mason Miller Reaches 30 Saves for the First Time — Padres Edge Brewers 3-2

By Ryan Calloway

August 12, 2026

Mason Miller hit a milestone Monday night that no season of his career had reached before. The San Diego Padres closer recorded his 30th save of the 2026 season — a career first — as San Diego held off the Milwaukee Brewers 3-2 at Petco Park.

The save was not entirely clean — Miller walked a batter and the Brewers threatened — but when pinch-runner Brandon Lockridge was thrown out trying to steal second base, the inning ended and the milestone was locked in. Thirty saves in a season, for the first time in Miller's career.

Where He Stands in the Majors

With 30 saves, Miller is tied for second in Major League Baseball, trailing only Bryan Baker, who leads the majors with 34. It's a meaningful position for a closer who has been one of the more dominant late-inning arms in the National League this season.

The 30-save threshold has long served as an informal benchmark for closers — it represents the number that separates a capable reliever from a truly elite one over a full season. Miller reaching it for the first time adds a career-milestone dimension to what has already been a strong year for the Padres' bullpen.

How Monday's Game Unfolded

Jackson Merrill provided the offensive punch that made Miller's save possible. Merrill hit a two-run home run for San Diego, giving the Padres a margin that ultimately proved sufficient. Miller took the ball in the ninth with a 3-2 lead, navigated the walk, and finished the job after the Lockridge throw-out closed the door on Milwaukee's final threat.

What's Next

The San Diego Padres continue their push in the NL standings. Miller will look to build on his career-best save total through the remainder of the 2026 season. Padres schedule and standings information is available at padres.com and mlb.com.
