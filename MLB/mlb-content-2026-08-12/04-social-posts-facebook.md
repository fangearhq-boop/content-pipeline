### STORY 1: Field of Dreams — Tomorrow on Netflix

**Long-Form Post — Window: 8:00 AM ET**
Tomorrow night, baseball goes back to the cornfield.

The 2026 MLB Field of Dreams Game is Thursday, August 13 — Twins vs. Phillies in Dyersville, Iowa, at 7:30 PM ET. This one streams exclusively on Netflix, making it the first MLB game ever broadcast on the platform. Pre-game coverage begins at 6:30 PM.

This is the third edition of the Field of Dreams Game. The first two were played in 2021 (White Sox vs. Yankees) and 2022 (Cubs vs. Reds) before a three-year hiatus while the venue was rebuilt. The new permanent stadium sits about a quarter-mile from the original movie-site diamond, with right-field windows that look out onto the surrounding 159 acres of Iowa cornfield.

The uniforms alone are worth tuning in for. The Phillies will wear their 1939-41 gray road jerseys with a centennial patch. The Twins will suit up in their 1961 inaugural pinstripes — the original blue-and-red "Twins" script on the chest, navy cap with the vintage "TC" logo — from the franchise's very first season.

Hall of Famers on hand include Mike Schmidt, Steve Carlton, Joe Mauer, and Rod Carew. Kevin Costner will make an appearance in the broadcast.

One fresh wrinkle: MLB confirmed Tuesday that the Automated Ball-Strike (ABS) challenge system will not be installed at the Dyersville stadium. The Phillies have reportedly refused to use ABS throughout the 2026 season anyway — so tomorrow, both teams get a game under traditional umpiring, in the sport's most atmospheric setting.

First pitch: 7:30 PM ET on Netflix. ⚾

### STORY 3: Mason Miller — 30th Save

**Long-Form Post — Window: 3:00 PM ET**
Mason Miller reached a career milestone Monday night.

The San Diego Padres closer recorded his 30th save of the 2026 season — a career first — as San Diego held on to beat the Milwaukee Brewers 3-2. Miller worked around a walk and struck out a batter; a Brewers stolen base attempt ended the inning when pinch-runner Brandon Lockridge was thrown out trying to take second.

Jackson Merrill's two-run home run earlier in the game provided the margin that made it matter.

With 30 saves, Miller sits tied for 2nd in the majors, behind only Bryan Baker's 34. The Padres closer has been one of the most reliable late-inning arms in baseball this season — and now he has a career-first number to show for it.

### STORY 4: Skubal — Dodger Stadium Home Debut

**Long-Form Post — Window: 6:00 PM ET**
Tarik Skubal has two Cy Young Awards and still can't get a win in Dodger blue.

The Los Angeles Dodgers beat the Kansas City Royals 6-5 on Monday night, but Skubal came away with another no-decision in his Dodger Stadium home debut. His line: 5 innings pitched, 4 hits, 3 earned runs, 6 strikeouts, 95 pitches. MLB.com described him as battling "spotty command of his fastball" throughout.

It's Skubal's second start since being traded from the Detroit Tigers, where he won back-to-back American League Cy Young Awards, and he's 0 for 2 in terms of picking up a win in Dodger blue. Skubal said the start felt "special to me" despite the results.

There's context worth noting: the Dodgers had dropped 8 of their previous 9 games entering Monday. This win — messy as Skubal's start was — snapped that skid. The offense gave him something to work with.

He'll keep getting chances. The Cy Young wins aren't going anywhere. ⚾ #Dodgers

### STORY 5: Cubs — Wild Card Lead, Deadline Delivering

**Long-Form Post — Window: 10:00 PM ET**
The Chicago Cubs are starting to look like a real postseason threat.

At 69-50, the Cubs lead the NL Wild Card by five games over the Diamondbacks and Phillies (both at 64-56). What makes this especially interesting is who they added at the August 3 trade deadline — and how quickly those pieces have clicked.

Kevin Gausman arrived from the Toronto Blue Jays. Clay Holmes came over from the New York Mets, along with outfielder Tyrone Taylor. Braxton Garrett was acquired from the Marlins. Ryan Zeferjahn joined from the Angels. That's an aggressive overhaul of a rotation and bullpen that was already performing.

CC Sabathia, analyzing the Cubs on NBC Sports, said they "did enough at the deadline to hold onto the top Wild Card spot." Early returns support that view: Gausman and Holmes debuted around August 8-9, and both delivered.

With 43 games remaining, the Cubs hold the position they built all summer. ⚾ #Cubs
