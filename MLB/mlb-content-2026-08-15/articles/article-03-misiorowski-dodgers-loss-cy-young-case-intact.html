Misiorowski Lost to the Dodgers 3-1. His Cy Young Case Didn't Move an Inch.

By Ryan Calloway

August 15, 2026

Jacob Misiorowski traveled to Dodger Stadium on Friday night, faced the most dangerous lineup he will encounter all season, and lost 3-1. He's now 11-6. And he is still the overwhelming NL Cy Young Award frontrunner.

The Milwaukee Brewers right-hander entered the start carrying a 1.76 ERA, 204 strikeouts in 133.2 innings, and a 0.73 WHIP — numbers that lead all of Major League Baseball across all three categories simultaneously. The Dodgers gave him a loss, not a demolition. The season-long case for the 24-year-old is built on a foundation that one night in Los Angeles cannot dent.

What the Numbers Say

Misiorowski's strikeout total already tells a historic story. He reached 200 strikeouts faster than any pitcher in MLB history except Spencer Strider, who accomplished it in 123.1 innings during his 2022 Cy Young season. Misiorowski hit the mark at 129.1 innings — a gap of less than six innings separating him from the fastest-ever pace. He is well inside the historical conversation for single-season dominance.

Leading all of baseball in ERA, strikeouts, and WHIP at the same time is extraordinarily rare. The pitchers who accomplished that combination in modern baseball are a short list of elite performances from elite careers. Misiorowski is putting his name on that list in 2026, and it's happening in Milwaukee — a market that rarely gets this kind of pitching spotlight.

The Win-Loss Problem and Why It Doesn't Apply

The Brewers are out of the playoff race. They have been for weeks. That means Misiorowski pitches his remaining starts without run support that postseason urgency might generate. The team's offensive limitations in 2026 have hurt his win total — 11 wins in a historically dominant pitching season is a quiet record of what might have been on a different roster. Cy Young voters in 2026 are aware of this. They have been since Pedro Martinez's win total was lower than his ERA+ implied it should be. Misiorowski's NL Cy Young odds at -550 reflect how the baseball world is reading his 2026.

The Dodgers beat him. The box score shows a loss. The ERA is 1.76. The strikeouts are 204. The WHIP is 0.73. One of these things matters most for October's ballot, and it isn't the win-loss column.

What's Next

Misiorowski's next start will come in five days, adding more innings and strikeouts to what is already a historic 2026 season. With six to eight starts remaining, he could finish with a sub-1.80 ERA, 225-plus strikeouts, and a WHIP that might end up as one of the lowest in modern MLB history.
