2026 Wild Card Round Recap: Three Sweeps, One Thriller, and a Bracket Nearly Set

By Jake Torres

October 1, 2026

The 2026 MLB Wild Card round is almost over — and it has delivered exactly what October baseball promised: dominant performances, stunning comebacks, and one final winner-take-all game that the entire sport is watching tonight.

Three of the four Wild Card series have already ended in sweeps. The one remaining — Phillies versus Braves, Game 3, 8:15 PM ET tonight in Atlanta — is the kind of game that defines careers and breaks hearts. But before that happens, here's what the first act of the 2026 postseason produced.

The Sweeps: San Diego, Chicago, and New York

The San Diego Padres were clinical against the Chicago Cubs. Two games, one Gavin Sheets pinch-hit homer in Game 2, and a combined offensive performance that made the NL's top regular-season offense look helpless at times. The Padres are in the NLDS. The Cubs are going home.

The Chicago White Sox wrote the postseason's most compelling subplot. Coming off a historic 121-loss 2024 campaign, this young, inexperienced team swept the Houston Astros out of the playoffs. Colson Montgomery's 432-foot homer — struck despite batting just .099 in September — became the image of the whole round. First playoff series win since 2005. The rebuild is ahead of schedule.

The New York Yankees were the most dominant team of the Wild Card round by raw numbers. Without Aaron Judge, they outscored the Boston Red Sox 18-2 across two games. Ben Rice homered multiple times. Cam Schlittler was untouchable in Game 1. Judge is targeting an ALDS return vs. the Tampa Bay Rays, and the Yankee machine barely missed a gear without him.

The Thriller: Philadelphia Forces Game 3

Atlanta won Game 1 at Truist Park. Philadelphia stared at a potential 0-2 deficit and responded with back-to-back home runs in the eighth — Kyle Schwarber (447 feet), Bryce Harper (right behind him) — and then a walk-off homer from Alec Bohm in the tenth. Series tied. Game 3 tonight.

Schwarber's 447-foot blast stands as the longest home run of the 2026 postseason through the Wild Card round. It was a swing that changed the entire tenor of a series and kept the Phillies' season alive for at least one more game.

What's Next

Tonight's Phillies-Braves Game 3 (8:15 PM ET, Truist Park, NBC/Peacock) will complete the bracket. The winner joins the Padres, White Sox, and Yankees in the Division Series round.
