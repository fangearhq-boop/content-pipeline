Back-to-Back Home Runs, a Walk-Off, and a Game 3: Phillies Force the Decider in Atlanta

By Marcus Cole

October 1, 2026

Kyle Schwarber hit one 447 feet. Bryce Harper hit the next one. And just like that, the Philadelphia Phillies were alive.

Back-to-back home runs off Dylan Lee in the eighth inning erased a deficit the Braves had spent most of the night building, and then Alec Bohm delivered a walk-off home run in the tenth to complete a stunning 4-3 Philadelphia comeback. The NL Wild Card Series is tied 1-1, and tonight at Truist Park, the whole thing gets decided in a winner-take-all Game 3.

The Phillies were facing elimination in Atlanta on September 30. They left with momentum instead.

Schwarber, Harper, and One Unforgettable Inning

Down multiple runs entering the eighth, Philadelphia needed something dramatic. What they got was historic.

Schwarber led off the inning against Lee and drove a fastball to dead center field. The ball traveled 447 feet — the longest home run of the 2026 postseason to that point — and suddenly the deficit was cut. Harper stepped in next and didn't wait long before sending Lee's next pitch into the seats for a back-to-back shot. The game was tied.

Atlanta held serve through the ninth. In the tenth, with the season on the line for both teams, Bohm came up with two outs and a runner on base courtesy of the automatic runner rule. Lee's delivery arrived. Bohm turned on it, launched it over the fence, and Philadelphia went from trailing in a near-elimination game to celebrating a series-tying victory in a matter of a few at-bats.

Game 3 Tonight at Truist Park

The winner-take-all game takes place Thursday night in Atlanta. First pitch is scheduled for 8:15 PM ET on NBC and Peacock. The Braves will have their home crowd behind them and the benefits of pitching on their terms. The Phillies will have the memory of what they just did, which may be the more powerful asset heading into a must-win game.

Atlanta took Game 1 at Truist Park before losing the second game in extra innings. The team that wins tonight advances to the NLDS — while the loser goes home after a heartbreaking end to a long season.
