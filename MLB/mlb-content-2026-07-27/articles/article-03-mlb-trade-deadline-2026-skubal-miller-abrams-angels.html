MLB Trade Deadline 2026: 7 Days Out — Skubal, Miller, Abrams, and the Angels Fire Sale

By Ryan Calloway

July 27, 2026

The MLB Trade Deadline is seven days away, and the market has officially begun to move.

The Red Sox kicked off the summer with the first significant deal of the cycle, sending starting pitcher Connelly Early to the Washington Nationals in exchange for third baseman Curtis Mead, 25. More will follow. Here is a look at the biggest names and storylines as August 3 approaches.

Tarik Skubal — The Biggest Prize That May Stay Home

There is no pitcher more coveted at this deadline than Tarik Skubal. The two-time AL Cy Young winner has been excellent in 2026 despite a brief absence — 14 starts, 82.2 innings, a 2.83 ERA. The Tigers are 27-16 over their last 43 games and maintain a slim playoff hope.

But the more you listen to the people around this situation, the more it sounds like Skubal stays in Detroit. He has said he is "never been more motivated" and wants to finish what he started with the Tigers. Rival executives increasingly believe Detroit will hold. If a deal does happen, the Dodgers are the most commonly cited destination — they have the prospect capital and the championship ceiling to justify the price.

Mason Miller — The Closer Everyone Wants

Mason Miller has put together one of the most dominant relief seasons in recent memory. The San Diego Padres closer owns a 0.89 ERA across 40.2 innings pitched and has converted all 25 of his save opportunities. The stuff is elite. The track record is building.

According to SI.com, rival executives are "convinced" the Padres will move him. The price will not be cheap — Miller represents multiple years of team control for a reliever who looks like a franchise-defining closer. Whatever it takes, there will be no shortage of buyers.

CJ Abrams — The Shortstop on the Edge of a Decision

The Washington Nationals sit at 52-52 — a record that puts them in an uncomfortable position at the deadline. They are not clearly sellers. But their playoff odds entering the final stretch of July are below 10 percent, and the state of their pitching staff may ultimately push them toward moving pieces.

CJ Abrams, 25, is the name that will bring the most back. He is an elite shortstop under team control for three to four more years. The Yankees and Brewers have shown interest, per MLB Trade Rumors. The Nationals are not actively shopping him, but they are "willing to listen" — which in trade deadline language is a very particular kind of signal.

The Angels Are Open for Business

Interim GM John Mozeliak, who replaced the fired Perry Minasian, has made clear that the Angels are sellers. Three names lead the shopping list. SS Zach Neto leads the American League in runs scored with 69. SP Jose Soriano has a 3.43 ERA across 21 starts and is under team control through 2028. LHP Reid Detmers has a 4.16 ERA over 20 starts with similar contract security through the same year.

Any of those three would improve a playoff contender. All three together would represent one of the more impactful trade deadline sellers in recent years.

What's Next

Seven days in the trade deadline calendar is a long time and a very short time. The deals that define the second half of the 2026 season will be made in the next week — and the landscape of the pennant races may look very different by August 4.

One thing is clear: there is talent available. The buyers who move first, move smart, and move decisively will be best positioned when October arrives.
