Cubs Take WC1, Padres Have a Date at Petco: NL Wild Card With 10 Games Left

By Marcus Cole | Ballpark Banter | September 16, 2026

Chicago moved into NL Wild Card 1 after beating Atlanta on Tuesday. In nine days, the Padres host the D-backs at Petco in a three-game series that could determine who plays in October.

The National League Wild Card standings, as of the morning of September 16, 2026:

Wild Card 1: Chicago Cubs. Wild Card 2: Philadelphia Phillies, 0.5 games back. Wild Card 3: San Diego Padres, with a 2.5-game cushion over the Arizona Diamondbacks.

Milwaukee holds the No. 1 seed and a first-round bye at 93-57. The Dodgers hold the No. 2 seed and a bye. Atlanta is No. 3. The NL Wild Card Series begins September 29. Approximately 10 regular-season games remain for every team still fighting for position.

How the Cubs Got Here

Pete Crow-Armstrong went 4-for-5 with a home run Tuesday against the Braves, and Chicago won 7-3 to move into Wild Card 1 by a half-game over Philadelphia. The margin is thin, and the Cubs' ability to hold their position over the final 10 games will depend in part on whether the Phillies are simultaneously stumbling — but Chicago controls their side of the equation. They have the roster. They have the momentum. And they have the best player in the National League providing a nightly reason for optimism.

The battle between Chicago and Philadelphia for Wild Card 1 and Wild Card 2 is the kind of race that makes September baseball worth watching. Half a game separates them now. That gap could flip daily for the next two weeks.

The Series to Circle

On September 25, the San Diego Padres host the Arizona Diamondbacks at Petco Park for a three-game series ending September 27. This is the most consequential series in the National League stretch run.

San Diego holds Wild Card 3 with a 2.5-game lead over Arizona. If the Diamondbacks win the series at Petco — or take two of three — they close the gap to within one game or tie it. If San Diego wins the series, they extend the lead and potentially put the D-backs away with just two or three games remaining on the schedule. The stakes could not be more direct: one team will likely know whether they are going to October by the time that series ends Sunday, September 27.

What's Next

The NL Wild Card Series begins September 29. The three Wild Card teams will host their opponents in best-of-three series before the NL Division Series against Milwaukee and Los Angeles. The September 25-27 Padres-D-backs series at Petco is the last major appointment on the calendar before the bracket resolves. Mark it. The teams playing in October will begin to come into focus the moment that Sunday night final goes final.
