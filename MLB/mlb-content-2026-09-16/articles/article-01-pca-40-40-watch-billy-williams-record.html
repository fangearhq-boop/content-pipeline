42 Home Runs, 37 Stolen Bases: PCA Ties Billy Williams' Record and Closes In on 40-40 History

By Ryan Calloway | Ballpark Banter | September 16, 2026

Pete Crow-Armstrong went 4-for-5 with his 42nd home run Tuesday, tying a 60-year-old Cubs franchise record. Three stolen bases separate him from baseball immortality.

On Tuesday night in Atlanta, Pete Crow-Armstrong went 4-for-5 with a home run off AJ Smith-Shawver, and the Chicago Cubs beat the Braves 7-3 to take over the NL Wild Card 1 spot. The home run was his 42nd of the season. It tied Billy Williams' franchise record for most home runs in a single season by a left-handed Cubs hitter — a mark that had stood for six decades.

PCA now has 37 stolen bases. He needs three more. The 40-40 season — 40 home runs and 40 stolen bases in the same year — is three stolen bases away.

What He's Chasing

Only six players in Major League Baseball history have ever hit 40 home runs and stolen 40 bases in the same season. Jose Canseco did it first, in 1988. Barry Bonds followed in 1996. Alex Rodriguez in 1998. Alfonso Soriano in 2006. Ronald Acuña Jr. in 2023. Shohei Ohtani in 2024. That is the complete list.

Crow-Armstrong would be the seventh. He would also be the first Chicago Cub to accomplish it — and only the second player in the modern era to finish a season with both totals above 40 after already reaching the 40-HR mark first. No Cub has ever hit 40 home runs in a season since Derrek Lee did it in 2005. PCA did it in September, with games still to play.

What Tuesday Looked Like

Four hits in five at-bats, including the home run that tied Williams' record. Crow-Armstrong now has 102 RBI, a number that reflects not just his power output but his consistent presence in the heart of a lineup that won 7-3 against a team still fighting for postseason position. The Cubs moved to NL Wild Card 1 after the win — a direct reflection of what PCA has meant to this team's second half.

He has recorded what multiple sources describe as seven four-hit games that included a home run and multiple extra-base hits — a combination of events that tracks as historically rare in the modern era. The consistency is not accidental. It is the product of a player who arrived as a toolsy prospect and has developed into the best player in the National League.

What's Next

The Cubs have approximately 10 regular-season games remaining. Three stolen bases in 10 games is entirely achievable — PCA has 37 in a full season, which means roughly one stolen base every four games across a 162-game schedule. He has the speed, the instincts, and the coaches who will give him the green light when the moment arrives.

Each at-bat from here carries a different weight. If he reaches base, the crowd holds its breath. If he takes off for second, the entire building lifts. That is what appointment baseball looks like. And right now, Pete Crow-Armstrong is the most compelling appointment in the sport.
