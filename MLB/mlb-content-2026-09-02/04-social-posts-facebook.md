But here's the story inside the story: PCA is two home runs and eight stolen bases away from joining the 40-40 club.

At his current stolen base pace, he projects to add 9-10 SBs before October. The home runs are the variable — he needs two in 27 games, which is well below his August rate of 14 in one month.

The MVP is essentially decided. The 40-40 is the story now.

### STORY 3: Alvarez Triple Crown — The HR Race Could Break Either Way

**Long-Form Post:**
Yordan Alvarez is doing something only Miguel Cabrera has done in the last 14 years: leading all three American League Triple Crown categories with a month to play.

His numbers entering September: .322 batting average, 36 home runs, 91 RBI. He leads:

— BA by 14 points over Chandler Simpson (.308)
— HR by one over Junior Caminero and Ben Rice (35 each)
— RBI tied with Pete Alonso (91)

The batting title is the most secure. Fourteen points is a sizable cushion. The RBI race is a coin flip — Alonso is right there. But the home run race is the most dangerous category for Alvarez.

Both Caminero and Rice hit 35 home runs entering September. A single hot week from either player flips that category. If Alvarez loses the home run title, he loses the Triple Crown. And with 27 games left, 35-to-36 is not a safe margin.

The last Triple Crown winner was Miguel Cabrera in 2012. Alvarez is trying to join that list.

### STORY 5: You Should Know Ben Rice's Name

**Long-Form Post:**
The AL Triple Crown race has three names, and two of them are familiar: Yordan Alvarez (Astros) and Junior Caminero (Rays).

The third one is Ben Rice.

Rice is 27 years old, plays first base and designated hitter for the New York Yankees, and debuted in the major leagues on June 18, 2024 — just over two seasons ago. He has 35 home runs in 2026. One behind Alvarez. Tied with Caminero.

Baseball-Reference shows Rice with 68 career home runs total. More than half of them — 35 — have come this season.

This is a breakout year happening in real time, and Rice is doing it in New York, where every swing in September carries extra weight. If Rice goes on even a moderate hot streak and Alvarez cools down, the AL home run title goes to the Bronx.

Whether that happens or not, Ben Rice is a name September baseball is about to teach you.
