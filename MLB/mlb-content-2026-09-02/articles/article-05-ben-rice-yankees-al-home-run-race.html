Ben Rice Has 35 Home Runs. The AL Triple Crown Is One Swing Away From New York.

By Marcus Cole | September 2, 2026 | Ballpark Banter

The American League home run race entering September has three names on the leaderboard. Yordan Alvarez of Houston leads with 36. Junior Caminero of Tampa Bay is at 35. And at 35 home runs, tied with Caminero and one behind Alvarez, is Ben Rice of the New York Yankees.

If you have been following the AL Triple Crown conversation, you know Alvarez. You probably know Caminero. Ben Rice is the name that September baseball is about to teach you.

Who Is Ben Rice?

Rice is 27 years old, born February 22, 1999. He plays first base and designated hitter for the New York Yankees and made his MLB debut on June 18, 2024 — just over two years ago. He has accumulated 68 career home runs across two-plus seasons in the majors. Thirty-five of those 68 have come in 2026 alone.

That context is important: Rice is not a ten-year veteran on a final surge. He is a player in the early stages of an MLB career who has found a breakout season at age 27. His power has been real, consistent, and occurring in the most scrutinized media market in professional baseball.

The Implications of a Rice September

Yordan Alvarez is chasing the first AL Triple Crown since Miguel Cabrera in 2012. He leads all three categories: .322 batting average, 36 home runs, 91 RBI. The batting average is his most secure lead — 14 points ahead of Chandler Simpson. The RBI race is tied with Pete Alonso at 91. But the home run category is the one that could fall first, and Ben Rice is one of two players in position to take it.

A Rice multi-HR game would put him ahead of Alvarez in the HR race. A continued surge through the final 27 games could mean the Yankees first baseman finishes September as the AL home run champion. That would not only end Alvarez's Triple Crown bid — it would add a significant individual accomplishment to Rice's breakthrough 2026 season and give the Yankees' offense a September storyline of its own.

What's Next

Rice and the Yankees have 27 games remaining in the regular season. Whether the home run race tightens further or Alvarez extends his lead will be one of the defining statistical storylines of September. For a player two years into his MLB career, Ben Rice entering the final month of the regular season as a Triple Crown threat in the AL's tightest individual race is the kind of breakout moment that rewrites how fans see a career.
